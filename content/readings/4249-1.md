# TEXT OF READING 4249-1 F 54

**Reading [[4249-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/15/1930  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 12015

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self, Mrs. [4249], through Mr. and Mrs. [[Morton Blumenthal|Morton Blumenthal]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [4249].  Now, we find specific conditions, and the results of the effects these created in the system, that as which the physical force labors against. The condition, as we find, has reached such a stage that the very vital forces of the life, as related to physical, is being attacked itself, and only that as may EASE [[Physical Body|the body physical]] may be added to the system at the present time.  Will there be the RESPONSE in the physical TO the applications, then such segregation may be made as that operative forces might be made to be helpful for the body, but not UNDER existent condition - for the fissure is filling with fluids that take from the vitality itself, and the NATURE of same is to spread and involve other tissue throughout the whole of the portion thus attacked.  It has not, as yet, gone THROUGH - but continues to FOLLOW rather the muscular wall, as the first tissue involved shows.  While it would be necessary to use those of the narcotics to ease the body, to produce segregation, or to stop the inroads as may be found, as WE see - while not reducing the whole amount OF the narcotic, were we to use this internally we might find BENEFITS coming FROM this, WILL the [[Physical Body|physical body]] respond to same.  This as each dose, taken internally:  Prepare capsules, and make only a few of same at the time - this in each dose:

Oil of Eucalyptol............2 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp........1 minim,
Canadian Balsam..............1 minim,
Codeine...................1/60 grain.

This may be taken once or twice each day.

2. Half an hour after taking this, we would apply the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray over body, especially in the abdominal and sacral region - see?

3. Manipulations given rather in the form of the [[Massage Therapy|MASSAGE]], will also assist in aiding the properties given, the drainage set up, the assistance to coagulation in that assimilated, and would be helpful to the body.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  In what doses should the narcotics be continued, and how often?
(A)  To meet the needs of the conditions.

5. (Q)  Any special diet?
(A)  There has been very good outline for the diet, as has also for the necessary elimination from the rectum, or from the alimentary canal.  The condition is one of those that cause the fissures in the rectal muscular forces to be involved in the condition.  We would use the oils that DO NOT irritate, also those that STIMULATE the tissue - as the pure [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], or the white oils - or they may be mixed.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mrs. Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]]
