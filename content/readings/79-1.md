# TEXT OF READING 79-1 F 50 (?)

**Reading [[79-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/29/1928  Sex: F  ReadingID: 188

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of October, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [79].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of Mrs. [79] ..., Ala., her associations, and the circumstances and conditions surrounding this body.  You will advise her as regarding change of location or surroundings and any other counsel or advice that will be of help to the body at this time.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, Mrs. [79], also those conditions, those associations, circumstances, that surround the present life, in the varied conditions that have been brought about by the changing circumstances in the surroundings.  These, as we find, at times make or bring about a longing, a lonesomeness, an unsatisfied condition, and the feeling that changes would bring to the body more acceptable things.  This is more from conditions through which the body passed before these changes took place, or came about.

3. In making a change, many various things, conditions and circumstances, are to be considered.  Well were the body to - in its own environment, in its own surroundings, among its present associates - make SOME changes, yes - in location of living, yes - in SURROUNDINGS in the location, yes - very good - but remain among those that have made, will, and do, make life worth while in the social and in the physical sense, for the present, for there will come the time when other changes will take place.  Rather BUSY self with the assistance that may be given others, in varied conditions. Interest self in many of the various conditions that are NEEDED to be looked after in the present surroundings; and in two years there will come the changes that will make many more surroundings different.  This is not as that, that would be troublesome, nor that which the body may WORRY about - yet these will come, with the natural change of events through that called time.  Keep in touch closely with the loved ones. [This perhaps refers to her daughter's child who was latter born and was such a constant joy and pleasure to Mrs. [79]].  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Will the body understand just what is meant by change from present location?
(A)  Body should understand just what is meant; that the large place, the surroundings that are in a measure responsible for the loneliness that comes - change from these, see?

5. We are through for the present.
