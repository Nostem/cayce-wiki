# TEXT OF READING 4106-1 F 36

**Reading [[4106-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/2/1931  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 11804

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of April, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4106] through Mr. [5544].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [4106].  Now, we find some conditions that cause distresses in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These in part are incident to the age and GENERAL existent conditions; the disorders in the present being accentuated by these conditions.  In the general, these - as we find - have to do with the eliminations of the system, and the effects as created from same.  These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, Mrs. [4106] we are speaking of:

2. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this disturbed by the activity of drosses of the system, which make for that separation that is apparent in the lymphatic circulation, and the general or internal circulation, so that the returns to the system - or as the blood is thrown to portions of the system, slow or inactive in its return.  Hence the alterations in the pulsations at times, as well as those conditions incident to easy fatigue - when any exercise, mental or physical, is taken.

3. IN THE NERVOUS SYSTEM, pressures, as well as changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], make for THESE reactions - and are affecting both cerebro-spinal and sympathetic; and WITH these deteriorations - or these effects of deteriorated forces not being eliminated, or dross forces not being eliminated - makes for those disorders as are apparent.

4. IN THE FUNCTIONING ORGANS THEMSELVES, these - we find there are those of a specific nature as effects from these conditions; while in the present these are not wholly functional, not wholly organic, these are rather the effect or combination of both.

5. In making corrections for the disorders, WE would first give those properties for the system that would change the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the system - then we may have a basis from which we may give or add that to the system to bring about a much bettered condition.  We would prepare, to be taken internally, first as this:

6. To 2 ounces of simple syrup, we would add:

Tincture Valerian.......................1/2 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya.........................1/2 ounce,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium........40 minims,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium.........60 minims,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]].........................20 minims,
Tincture of Stillingia...................20 minims.

Shake solution together before the dose is taken.  We would take half a teaspoonful every three hours, except when body is resting - see?

7. We would also use those of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations to clarify the system, as related to pressures created in the intestinal system which make for pressures in the lower hepatics, kidneys and bladder.  We will also find this will relieve those conditions where hot and cold flushes, heaviness in feet, unable to sleep as the body should.

8. We would also add (after this has been taken for the first five days) those of the GENERAL manipulations, osteopathically given, FOLLOWING - that is, give the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments after the body has been thoroughly relaxed, either by those sweats or applications of heat along the spine - a general treatment, see?  until the whole of the medicinal properties have been taken.  These we would give at least twice each week.

9. Then we will give those changes as necessary.

10. The [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] we would give once each week, see?  It would also be well to add, after the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], those of an intestinal antiseptic.  This may be in the form of any that are alkalin in their reaction.  They may include those of Milk of Magnesia, or Milk of Magnesia combined with - or that taken in one dose and the Milk of Bismuth taken in another. Also, as on the third day, those of the phosphates, or carbonate phosphate - carbonate hydrates - that will make for the relieving of pressure in the digestive system.

11. Then, when these have been taken for the first period of the medicinal properties, we will give further corrections. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  As indicated from the conditions, those had BEST be applied or outlined by one giving the manipulations - as for the effect same have upon the general system, as well as the digestive organism.  We are only making a basis for that as may be added later for a GENERAL debilitation, as well as those specific conditions - as are seen and indicated from that semi-functional, semi-organic.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
