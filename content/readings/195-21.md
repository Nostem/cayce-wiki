# TEXT OF READING 195-21 M 42 (Realtor & Mfr. of Automotive

**Reading [[195-21]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/16/1926  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 734

Parts) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195] and Mr. Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
2:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the data that I hold in my hand, as has been compiled and written regarding the work of Edgar Cayce.  You will tell us whether this is in its proper shape and form (when corrections are made) for distribution. Or, you will tell us any corrections that should be made in same, how same should be distributed, and any other information regarding same that would be for the benefit of the propagation of the phenomena as manifested through Edgar Cayce.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the data here.  Corrections and printing in a form for distribution would be necessary.  As to the data itself, this is very good in many respects.  There may be many corrections made.  However, this is that as is necessary to be given out, given to those who, in one manner or form, have been in touch or contact with the work in any way. Through same there will be found that it will be requested from many that much OTHER data concerning same be distributed from time to time.  And THIS, as we see, should be added even in this.  Then distribute, as given, to those who may be, or who have been in any manner, directly or indirectly interested in that force as is exercised in the phenomena manifested through Edgar Cayce.  See?

3. As to that as may be given for assistance:  As we see, this had best be given as the conditions command or need.  First, as given, commence.  Then these should be prepared and distributed, with that additional, as WE see at present.  We are through.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/30/26 Mr. [195] wrote:  "Read over the history of your
> experiences and found it very interesting, but there are one
> or two suggestions I would want to make if you had certain
> ideas in putting out such a booklet.  Will go over these when
> I see you, which I hope will be soon."
> 
> R2. 3/16/26 EC's letter:  "You know we were talking about that
> little pamphlet [when you were here].  I sent a copy of it to
> my friend M. B. Wyrick, so he proceeded to have it
> published."  [See 4905-55, Par. R3 for Cayce Introductory
> Booklet.]
> 
> R3. 5/22/26 Mr. [195] wrote:  "Again let me say the Cayce
> pamphlet is just what is needed.  Get last two issues of
> Collier's - Evangeline Adams' articles.  We should get up one
> on [[Reincarnation|Reincarnation]] and tell your story."
> 
> R4. 3/15/27 Mr. [195] obtained 195-41 on his manuscript entitled
> AN EVENING WITH THE SUPERNATURAL, a promotional piece for the
> EC work, especially on the Life Rdgs.
> 
> R5. 8/14/26 Mr. [195]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> "I have your several letters of recent date and enjoyed very
> much that reading on former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of Dr. [5618]
> [5618-8].  It will be a pleasure to meet him and I hope that
> he will be there when I arrive.
> 
> "The readings I suggested [195-32, 195-33, etc.] can wait
> until my arrival.  Hope to leave here on about the 26th if
> possible and at the same time convenient to you...
> 
> "The more one reads the different Life Readings the deeper
> the thoughts become.  When we have had a chance to read
> many Life Readings and study them from all angles we will
> ask at a reading the questions that evolve from our study
> and I am sure a different vista will be unfolded to us from
> which, maybe, a formula or [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] may e presented in such
> a way depicting life such that it will be a work of great
> benefit to humanity.
> 
> "My kindest regards to Cayce household."  Sincerely [195]
>
