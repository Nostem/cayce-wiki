# TEXT OF READING 619-3 M 50

**Reading [[619-3]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/28/1935  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 4236

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of August, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - through his wife, Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [619].

2. The conditions arising in the present, as we find, are rather acute; from toxic forces in the system; being reflex from a condition existent in the COLON area, where there are toxic forces, though these have been cleansed in a manner.

3. So first we would begin with high enemas to cleanse this whole portion of the system.  Make these high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|COLONIC]] irrigations, not just small enemas!  They should be HIGH enemas!  Give one the very first thing; then after the second day give another; continuing in this manner but gradually getting farther apart, until we cleanse this portion of the system.

4. We would add also with same the manipulations to set up general drainages in the system; specific centers being, then, those in the upper dorsal area, only coordinating the coccyx centers and the vagus centers with the alleviating of the pressure in the upper dorsal area.

5. We would first paint those areas in the right shoulder and arm, and in the portion of the hip, with a solution made from three parts Laudanum to one part Aconite.  THEN apply over the same areas a saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in packs.

6. Be mindful of the diet that rather predigested foods are used for a few days.

7. And we will find these conditions will disappear.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is the trouble in the left leg?
(A)  Neuritis!

Do these things as indicated, and we will find we will remove these disorders.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

WESTERN UNION

Give high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].  Stimulate drainages through upper dorsal, only coordinating coccyx vagus centers.  Paint shoulder area with solution three parts Laudanum one part Aconite, then apply hot [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] packs.  Use predigested foods.  Full information special.

(Directions were enclosed for preparing [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs.)
