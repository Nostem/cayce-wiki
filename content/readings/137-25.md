# TEXT OF READING 137-25 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-25]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/3/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 415

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 3rd day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made through his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                     [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I might ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of this body, with the [[Dreams|dreams]] that come to the body.  Many we have had before.  These come as the interpretation of physical conditions as are carried through mental forces into the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and correlated there with conditions from cosmic forces that bring experiences and conditions to the physical mind in [[Dreams|dream]].  Ready for dreams.

3. (Q)  Night of November 2nd, or morning of November 3, 1925, at home.  "Saw an old man with a V-shaped beard."
(A)  This the presenting to the mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those conditions in an emblematical form, meaning and giving then, much money will be in changed hands through the acts of the individual, as is seen in V-shaped beard - soon, see?  Not a loss, not a gain, save as through that channel from which this comes, see?  Or as would be given, there will be monies to the individual through another individual soon, see?

4. (Q)  "Saw Southern Railway and it might have been some indication of selling, and that I ought to sell."
(A)  This rather that of the correlation of mental conditions and not made clear to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through that change in conditions as is seen in this special stock at present.  We do not see at present it being a sale, yet with the breaking off WOULD BE, for we would find the change then coming, see? for it has not been [[Destiny|destined]] as yet, for the spirit of forces we find not set.  The conditions regarding this enter into another phase, which, as we see at present, means still the rise in Southern Stock to 22 - 122, see?

5. (Q)  That is all the first reading.
(A)  There may be much given in relation to those combinations that are being set as regards to L & N and Southern combinations being made.  These may not be given through these channels as yet, for it would be giving advantage over one and giving to ones who have not as yet obtained that necessary to gain the full information of same, so we are through for the present.
