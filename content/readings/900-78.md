# TEXT OF READING 900-78 M 30

**Reading [[900-78]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/8/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5281

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 8th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                Room ..., 
2:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                ..., West Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are some conditions in the body at the present time of which it should take notice for the best conditions for the body.  While there are very good functionings throughout the whole system, yet we would find that, was the body to take more of those properties as will be found in Petrolagar (the plain, see?), this will give better eliminations through the traverse [transverse] colon and prevent the assimilation of toxins in the system, that we see some signs of in the dull, leadened condition as comes to lower limbs and feet and through the hips.  This, as we see, is the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the kidneys and the toxins that come from condition in the traverse [transverse] colon.  This should be taken at least two teaspoonsful twice each day, until a whole quantity or bottle, is taken.

3. Keep in the right thinking way for the body, and keep that nominal exercise necessary for physical and mental balance in the system, and we will find the body will keep in that way in which it may develop in mental and spiritual forces.
