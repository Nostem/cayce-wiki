# TEXT OF READING 3527-1 M 34 (Clerk)

**Reading [[3527-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/5/1944  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 11012

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [3527], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. Lydia J. S. Gray.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Washington.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3527].

3. As we find, these are disturbances.  While the body may become very much perturbed, we find that these problems may be met in self - if there is the consideration of the coordination between spiritual and mental things, and then living them, and with the expectancy of using and knowing that what may be used is as much of God as self may make of same in its manner of relationship to self and others.

4. Hence we have a pathological and a psychological condition to be met.  For there are those periods when these seizures arise, from lesions in the lacteal duct area and in the 1st and 2nd cervical, when the body is almost possessed by its own self in relationship to those problems it is meeting in self.  And this is not good.  For these influences ordinarily leave the body-mind in those states of discouragement, of hating self, of wanting to blame everything on everybody else - and then condemning self also.

5. These must be met in self first - by the spiritual attitudes, spiritual concepts.  Know that in self alone ye may not meet such, but that as ye forgive - even yourself so may ye be forgiven by Him who is life, light and immortality.  So may the [[Healing|healing]] influence of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness come to be a part of the individual self.  Know that He has meant, "I stand and knock."  Open - don't close the door to that [[Christ Consciousness|Christ-Consciousness]].  Don't put Him away.  Don't shut the door.  Let Him enter, that He may abide with thee.

6. For as the promise is, "I and the Father may abide with thee."  Thy best self, and the way to get rid of that which has disturbed you, will abide with you.

7. Then physically apply for three days in succession each week, for at least an hour at the time, the hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the area of the liver and lacteal duct.

8. Also at the same time apply the hot [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs (using an electric pad) to the base of the brain, extending to the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical.  Put these on cold but apply the electric pad to make the body absorb same.

9. After the three-day series of the Packs each week, take internally at least two tablespoonsful of Olive Oil to eliminate the poisons and accumulations.

10. We would also follow each three-day series each week with a good massage thoroughly given, by a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].  Correct those areas of the coccyx, 4th dorsal, 4th cervical and 4th lumbar.  With the breaking up of the lesions in the first cervicals, there should be the reducing of the periods of disturbance and a change in the activity to the locomotory centers, so as to bring better conditions for the body.

11. But do first things first.  Don't condemn anyone nor self. Don't feel sorry for self - that's the best way to condemn, for you condemn everyone else!

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Please explain the trance-like state which follows one of these spells.
(A)  Read what we have just given.  It is almost possession! Do these things as we have indicated.

14. (Q)  What will cure the rectal trouble?
(A)  Do these things outlined - we will correct these disturbances.

15. (Q)  Would some particular vocation be more beneficial than another?
(A)  Do as suggested and there will be much for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to do, and it will think along a different line entirely!

16. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have name of one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(1/5/44 GD's note:  See directions for Packs [which were enclosed].)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 10/14/43 Letter from Mrs. Lydia J. Schrader Gray:  "Last
> Friday evening [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]], Lorraine and I called on Dr. Lorimer
> and his wife.  Your ears must have been burning - for our
> conversation was of you.  How we became acquainted - the
> Readings and what they meant to us - and of course the book,
> [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].  It's a [[Revelation (Book)|revelation]] to Dr. Lorimer and he
> has loaned it to several friends and other ministers.
> 
> "It's going to CHANGE some of his thinking - but he is open
> and eager.  Suddenly he said - 'I'm moved to tell you about
> a young man who came to my study at the church.  He's not a
> member of our church and I think he heard me speak - at the
> University.  Anyway - he came and told me he was at his wits
> end - at the end of the road.  For ten years he has been
> afflicted with attacks that [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] be epileptic and
> they come usually once a month and leave him in a trance like
> condition for a week afterwards.  Anyway - he cannot hold any
> position - he's been fired from one [[Book of Job|job]] after another because
> of his absence.  The attacks fortunately occur at home -
> usually when he is in bed.  The young man says - 'he cannot
> live a normal life - he can't think of marrying and life as
> it is isn't worth living.'
> 
> "He is now thirty four and this has been going on for
> ten long years.  Well - Mr. Lorimer wondered if he should
> acquaint the young man with your 'Life Story', thereby
> offering a RAY OF HOPE.  We all said, 'by all means do so'
> and if he or his family cared to meet us personally we would
> do everything in our power - to encourage that hope and give
> him added confidence.
> 
> "We met last night - in the pastor's study!  He's a fine
> young man clean cut - dark hair and dark eyes.  Truly -
> he reminds me of [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]].  They look SO much alike.
> His voice is quiet and you know that he has suffered.
> 
> "His parents have spent their all on doctors for him - he's
> been dehydrated, gone on a soybean diet - etc., etc.  After
> reading the case on epilepsy, on page 386 of There is a
> River, he became filled with hope for the case seems so much
> like his own in that the spells came on - not during youth -
> or rather CHILDHOOD, but in their twenties, so that he feels
> his spells have nothing to do with HEREDITY - as his parents
> know of no epilepsy in the family anywhere.
> 
> "We all hope that after you have heard from him that in some
> way you can consider him as an EMERGENCY case, for naturally,
> his life is at a stand still - unless he can be cured - for
> he cannot seek a position - while these spells continue - and
> to wait for over a year!  might just seem too much - or too
> long under the existing circumstances.
> 
> "Have you by any chance reserved some of your time for just
> such emergency cases??  If your wonderful gift could only
> be shared by others so that you ALONE would not have to
> hear this great burden...you must of course, protect and
> guard your own strength.  Mr. Lorimer is of course deeply
> interested and he says, he'll preach about your great gift
> from the pulpit, if this boy receives aid.  He introduced me
> to a man who wants to own the book - a Mr. Robbins, he said,
> Mr. Lorimer took the book back before he had finished it.  We
> were at the Church Supper and Lorraine said - 'many an ear
> was bending' towards our conversation.  We were speaking of
> you and your work.
> 
> "The young man's name is [3527] - born Feb. 3rd, 1909.  You
> will hear from him direct and I hope and pray that he has
> been guided to us at just the right moment."  [Remaining
> portion of 10/14/43 letter is under 1770-8, Par. R5.]
>
