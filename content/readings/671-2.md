# TEXT OF READING 671-2 F 5

**Reading [[671-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/8/1935  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 4447

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [573], through Study Group #7 of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:32 to 11:39 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Yes, we have the body here, [671].

2. (Q)  Why has the growth on her eye never disappeared?
(A)  The lack of the assimilations or drainages set up in the system, with the stimulating of the circulation as indicated by the [[Vibration|vibrations]] not only of the massage of the oil but those stimulations necessary along those centers in the upper dorsal and through the cervical area for stimulating the circulation for its absorption into the system.  DRAINAGES must be set up by this stimulation, if the absorption would take place.

3. (Q)  Was the medicine prepared right?
(A)  Prepared right.

4. (Q)  Is it necessary to remove the tonsils?
(A)  If there are those stimulations in the dorsal - upper dorsal, 3rd to 4th to the 1st and 2nd and through the cervicals, we do not find it would be necessary.

5. (Q)  Is her hearing good?
(A)  As indicated by the necessity of stimulating this circulation, all of the organs of the sensory system are affected; and it is NOT a hundred percent - about seventy- eight.

6. (Q)  Should these stimulations be given osteopathically or just a general massage?
(A)  As we would find, these would preferably - NOW - be made osteopathically.

7. (Q)  How often should these be given?
(A)  At least twice a week, until at least sixteen have been taken.

8. (Q)  Should the medicine be continued?
(A)  Not continued if the stimulations osteopathically and the general assimilations are kept normal.  This would be necessitated through, of course, adhering to the proper diets for proper assimilations.

9. (Q)  Any other treatments?
(A)  Do these; these will be sufficient in the present.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copies to Mother (one for Dr. Richardson) "      " Ass'n file
