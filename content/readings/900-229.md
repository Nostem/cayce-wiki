# TEXT OF READING 900-229 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-229]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/28/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5432

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., and [137], of ... St., both of [[New York City|New York City]].  You will also have the [[Dreams|dreams]] these bodies had on the dates which I will give you, and you will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies and the enquiring mind - [900] - [137], [[New York City|New York City]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  [900]'s, Monday Night, April 26, 1926.  "[[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] & Western 152 3/4."
(A)  In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that of the correlation of the body conscious mind as regarding information, and as to conditions as to exist in an advance of this particular stock.  Then, when - as is seen in the next numbers as are seen regarding this same particular stock - that when the attacks are made on rails, this particular stock will be among those that will decline. Then we have as this:  First the rise, then the decline; with the general attack, which begins about Wednesday - Thursday - Friday, see?  this week, with some advances again; then a deflation period, see?

4. (Q)  Wednesday Morning, April 28, 1926.  "[[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] 148."
(A)  This as given, the showing of the decline to follow, see?  that is, an advance and the decline.  Both are seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], for there is, as has been given, the attack to be made on rails, same as there has been on industrials.

5. (Q)  "Saw elemental composition of the nature and character of my mother.  It seemed that she was explaining her environment in the light of all the other studies I was making."
(A)  In this there is seen [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] gains some concept of that elemental force over which there is in the mind of man so much discussion - that of the environment and of the other conditions as go to make up the condition of man; hereditary conditions and environment.  These, as is seen, must be explained to the various forces, as is seen, especially in the writing of the entity and the study of same, in the light that the entity is giving, that the [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] forces [See 900-194] is rather of the environ-mental and of hereditary both.  Then - as is seen in the last and first analysis as is given here through these forces as are visioned in this of the mother in the elemental forces - that elementally the environment and heredity means the same as "Is thought memory or memory thought?"  [See [3744] series] or "Is environment of the resuscitated forces or is heredity resuscitated environment?"  More of these will be presented to the entity from time to time.

6. (Q)  [137]'s, Wednesday Morning, April 28, 1926.  "Saw Hudson Motors at 101."
(A)  This presents to the entity that of the change as is to come in the quotations of this particular stock, for in the light of that as is seen in numbers, and that as is apparent on the Exchange in the price - that is, at present we find this stock 65 - one - one, see?  Then we see the price 101 - one - one again, being two.  That is, some advance - still the weakness to occur, FOLLOWING some little advance, when the change is made from the attack on industrials to that on rails, see?

7. (Q)  "Saw Atchison 103."
(A)  This again shows - as has been shown in the attack on rails is to reduce the general market on rails - many of these showing much decline.  This may be expected in Thursday, Friday, trading, see?  one and three again becoming four again see?  a weakening.

8. We are through.
