# TEXT OF READING 3751-2 M 2

**Reading [[3751-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/18/1922  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 11269

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 18th day of October, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                      [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. FEM:  Mr. Cayce, would it be possible for you to tell us about the condition of [3751], ... Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]?

2. EC:  [Repeats question].  We have had this body before.

FEM:  Yes, sir.

3. The conditions that have existed in this body are being changed somewhat from those we found before.  There hasn't been sufficient atropine given into the system, nor at the proper time to produce the proper alleviating of the exciting forces in the system.  This should be injected again, together with the manipulations that we are carrying out, and this should be gentle manipulation along those vertebrae governing the eliminating forces; that is, the liver and the kidneys, if we would remove them from the system.  We are through.

4. Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about this reading you have just given.  You are feeling fine, and all the organs of your body will function properly.  Now, just wake up in 2 minutes feeling fine and all right, and free from any headache.
