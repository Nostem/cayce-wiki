# TEXT OF READING 550-9 M 69

**Reading [[550-9]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/27/1940  Sex: M  Age: 69  ReadingID: 3878

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of February, 1940, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [639], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Fla.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - as we find, there are a great many changes.  Some have been for improvements.  Some as we find are causing a great deal of uneasiness and unpleasantness for the body at times in the present, - but many changes from those as we have had here before.

3. We find that there are acute disturbances with the prostates, that cause inflammation at times, and unpleasantness and uneasiness through the area of the bladder, as well as some disturbance with the kidneys.  These necessarily make for a variation as to the respiratory system, as well as the pulsations, as well as the effects of congestion which has existed through cold throughout the body.

4. In the present, we would relax the body with Wet Heat and then have the gentle manipulations.

5. If the condition through the prostate becomes more severe, use the Elliot Appliance in reducing the inflammation; but where there has been and is the acute condition, relax first with Wet Heat and then have the gentle manipulations.

6. The use of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] will aid in equalizing more, or in bridging better equilibrium in the blood pressure, as well as eliminating poisons that cause upsetting of the whole system by their reaction through the activity of the nerves as well as the functioning of the organs of the system as related to circulatory forces, assimilating and digestive forces.

7. Be mindful that there is not overeating, nor too much sweets especially.  Have plenty of raw as well as cooked vegetables in the diet.

8. Of course, baths in hot and cold water, with the periods of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], will also be helpful.

9. The use of the Radio-Active Appliance will aid also in keeping the extremities, as well as the general portions of the body, more in coordinant activities in the extremes of the body.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file

(If your Naturopath is not acquainted with the Elliot Machine, he can secure information concerning it from: Dr. I. [[Daniel|Daniel]] Shorell, 8 East 64th Street, [[New York City|New York City]].

Folder was enclosed regarding Radio-Active Appliance.

At your convenience, please let us know just what Mr. [550]'s condition is, so that we may have something to compare with our information.)
