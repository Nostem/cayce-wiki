# TEXT OF READING 592-2 M 69

**Reading [[592-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/22/1934  Sex: M  Age: 69  ReadingID: 4129

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 22nd day of June, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self through Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Oklahoma. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Had we followed the suggestions that were given, irrespective of the information, we might have conditions quite different.

2. As it is - it IS.

3. For those conditions that have been indicated in this body, that was [592] we would find that the use of heat applied through the rectum - in the area of the activities in the prostates - would not only reduce the swelling, the pain, but would destroy those forces that bring ABOUT the FORM of infectious forces that arise from the strains and from the former conditions - and indiscretions - that arise from such conditions.

4. This would be well to be remembered.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [257]
"    " Ass'n file

([257], we are only sending a copy to you; for it would appear from the above that Mr. [592] has either passed on or is beyond being aided.  Let us know.  GD)
