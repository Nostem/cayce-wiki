# TEXT OF READING 24-2 F 16

**Reading [[24-2]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 16  ReadingID: 80

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [24], this we have had before.  Conditions are changed in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that as we have had before.  Most of these are for the betterment.  It would be well were there made, through manipulations and adjustments of those derangements - they may be called, for they are not alignments - they are OUT of alignment - through the lumbar, portion of the dorsal and cervical; so that the whole general SYSTEM would react with the eliminating system.  We will build the body to a better condition.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  About twice each week.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Lydic
