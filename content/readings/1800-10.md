# TEXT OF READING 1800-10

**Reading [[1800-10]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/29/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8124

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard.                   [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the Radio-Active Applicator as was prepared [See 538-12] for Mrs. [538].  You will answer the questions regarding same, as I ask them of you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the applicator as was prepared for Mrs. [538], and similar batteries that may be made for the use of the ills of the human family, for, as we see, these will lend assistance to the human family in many ways and manners, creating an equilibrium necessary in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] for it, the body, to function more normal in every manner.  Also those specific conditions wherein it becomes necessary to add to the human system those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of metals or of other ingredients, that the system (human system) may be made to function more normal, through the assistance received from the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of other ingredients that are applicable to the needs of the human body, for with any condition that becomes abnormal in the human body, it, the condition, is the lack of an equilibrium produced by the lack of that necessary to produce that [[Vibration|vibration]] in that portion of the organism of the human body that becomes distressed or dis-eased, see?

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is the percent of carbon steel in this battery?
(A)  Fifty-seven and five-eights percent (57 & 5/8%).

5. (Q)  Would it be necessary in preparing these for the rubber container to have holes in it at all?  Would this work as well without holes as with holes?
(A)  Work as well without holes as with holes - only necessary that the applicator be longer in the solution, so that the cold may act on the steel and produce the vibration necessary, see?

6. (Q)  What should be the thickness of the charcoal gasket, between the walls of the rubber container and the steel?
(A)  From quarter to three-quarter (1/4 to 3/4) around same, see?  That is, in the position or formation of steel, unless made half round would make a condition wherein that on edges would be thinner than on sides, see?  Of course steel could be prepared in a manner, when they are placed together with the glass between them, that the whole, with the steel or the gasket, would be quarter to three-quarters (1/4 to 3/4) around.  Just so it does not become bunglesome, see?  Would make a more symmetrical article were the steel prepared in that manner.

The thickness of the glass, we see, we would use the single strength glass, see?  This does not matter, but two pieces should be used, see?  the single strength - sixteenth to an eighth of an inch (16th to 1/8th inch) thick, see?  each piece.

7. That is all the questions.

8. There may be much said regarding these applicators.  The charcoal should be in bottom and in top, same as on sides, see?  that we may have [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] set up, or the prevention from any contact with outside forces as would apply to steel to prevent short circuiting, see?  Charcoal should be pounded or ground and placed in container, to be sure, see?  Where holes are made in same, it would act, of course, more quickly, yet not necessary, for within fifteen-to twenty minutes, when the applicator is placed on ice, we would find the battery giving off that charge necessary to create the equilibrium in the human body, giving that form of rest or recuperation as is found in sleep.  Hence this is best be applied to assist through same - sleep, see?

9. We are through for the present with this.
