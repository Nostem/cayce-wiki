# TEXT OF READING 3190-3 M 47

**Reading [[3190-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/5/1926  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 10577

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3190].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.  You will answer the questions I ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those impressions as concerning this body, [3190], here.  We have had this before.

3. Now, we find there are many of the physical conditions that are improved in the body from that as we have had; while there are some that are somewhat exaggerated, produced by the condition as has been brought about by the congestion as has been shown in the system.  The accentuation is especially in that of the digestive system, and that as gives off the reaction through the nerve supply of the body.

4. Then, to bring the nearer normal condition for this body, we would follow more closely yet those suggestions as have been given, in that of that as to be taken in the system, for the general condition in the digestive system and for the correction of those conditions along the cerebro-spinal system.  We would do that, for we will find these will bring the nearer normal condition for the body.

5. The weight, as we find, will be increased by this, for the detraction that has come, as we have given, is in the leaving off of those conditions as would produce the better assimilation, those that will produce the better activity to the whole general system, especially in that as refers to the kidneys in their action, and to the generatory system, for the replenishing of the rebuilding forces in the body gives the better incentive for the system throughout.

6. Then, we would follow those conditions as have been outlined for this body.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  In former reading, regarding trouble in lumbar region, was tried as per instructions, but has returned.
(A)  Then, we should have these conditions corrected through those adjustments as have been given, for this we find is again the reoccurrence of the inclination as was set in the system, as in times back, as has been outlined for this body, [3190].

8. (Q)  How many [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments will be necessary?
(A)  At least eight thorough adjustments and treatments.

9. (Q)  Does the body need the Violet Ray?
(A)  Needs the Violet Ray.

10. (Q)  How many hours of sleep does this body require at night, and what are the best sleeping hours?
(A)  Eight - and these may be made at whatever time the body so desires, see?

11. (Q)  What method should be used to increase the body's weight to normal?
(A)  Those of the general conditions for the system.

12. (Q)  What will be the outcome of certain conditions that have been decreasing the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for the last month or two?
(A)  Overcome these conditions through that as has been suggested that would bring about the normal condition for this body.

13. (Q)  Would the migrating of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] meet with greater success than it has in the past?  If so, what would be the exact location?
(A)  Along the East coast of the present country, we would find would be the better, see?

14. We are through with this reading.
