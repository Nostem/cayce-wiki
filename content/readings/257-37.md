# TEXT OF READING 257-37 M 35 (Sales Mgr., Hebrew)

**Reading [[257-37]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/10/1929  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 1309

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce and Thos. B. Brown.]

Time of Reading 
4:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             St. Louis, Mo. 
(Body and enquiring mind - questions)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [257] - this we have had before, also those conditions as surround the body as respecting that as uppermost in the mind of the body; and there are those conditions that are baffling in a nature, yet these have been given as warnings aforetime to the body.  The approach as will be made at present is through those that distribute the INCOMES of the company desired to be purchased; also through the BANKING interests, that are interested in the undertaking and are ANXIOUS in that the changes as may be presented by the body should come about. Very easy would it be for the body to say too much to those that are direct representatives of the individual that is out of city, yet these conditions and negotiations can ONLY be made with that that is as the representative through the FINANCIAL institution that is handling the situation.

2. Then, follow those out and we will find that in the time as set there may be begun those negotiations as will bring about the proper termination of the conditions desired.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is Mr. Orr the banker referred to?  and shall I take him into my full confidence?
(A)  Not as to the full representation; but that that may be accomplished - yes.  As to who is to accomplish it, and THROUGH whom it may be accomplished, NO.

4. (Q)  Who is the party referred to who handles the disburse- ments of dividends?
(A)  The lawyers for the banking interest.

5. (Q)  I have followed instructions -
(A)  Not wholly.

6. (Q)  (Continuing)  telephoned Mr. Geo. Logan, Attorney for Mr. Grove - (A)  Not wholly.  Too much said over the telephone as to conditions.  Follow rather those lines as have been given.

7. (Q)  Who is the other lawyer?
(A)  Find that through the banker!

8. (Q)  Any other information or advice for this body?
(A)  Follow these instructions as given here, and we will find these will come as has been given.  Do THAT.

Copy to Self - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file
