# TEXT OF READING 5453-11 M 32

**Reading [[5453-11]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/3/1930  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 13642

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made by themselves - Mr. [5453] and wife [1730].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [5453] and [1730], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and the enquiring minds of [5453] and [1730], present in this room, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding all phases of their lives.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies - [5453], [1730] - their enquiring minds.

3. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the bodies - each - there needs be that care and attention as has been given as warnings for each, that the physical may function the more near normal, that the developments of each - and the combination - may develop the more.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is [5453] to be given the opportunity to continue his work in the Research Department of the ..., or associated in that capacity with [294] and his work?  Is so, when?
(A)  That is dependent upon the movements or activities of many as related to the same.  It is the INTENTION in the present that some DEVELOPMENTS in this direction would be carried on.  It is the intention of SOME that it would be carried on through other channels.  These conditions, then, will depend upon the activities of those that control same in the present.  As to how soon or when this will be determined, this we find will be determined in April or May of the coming year.

5. (Q)  Where should [5453] apply in order to obtain a remunerative position now?
(A)  In those channels in which the body is best adapted for the giving of service for such remuneration.

6. (Q)  Will the plan discussed by [5453] and Willard Ashburn last night regarding the business now known as the Benstock Drug Co. be carried out as discussed?
(A)  This, we find, would the body [5453] be able to raise capital of one to two thousand dollars, might be carried out to a successful termination.

7. (Q)  Advise [5453] regarding this as to a partnership arrangement, welfare of family, etc.
(A)  In this there are many conditions to be considered. While there is the necessity that more than one individual be interested in same, through channels already being set in motion the greater portion of necessary capital and associations may be had.  One that would be congenial and of the same purport of mind as [5453] would be WELL to be associated as partner.  One that becomes of other MINDEDNESS would NOT be so well.

8. (Q)  Could [5453] obtain a position in [[Florida|Florida]] at this time? If so, would it be best for him and his family to go there?
(A)  That depends upon the situation as is felt MOST within self.  As we find, through the same channels or associations as body has had there may be - at this time - those associations made that would be satisfactory in a manner, and body could so adapt self as to be READY for any changes as might come for further associations in the endeavors in which the body - and bodies - have labored.

9. (Q)  Would it be well for [5453] to attempt to locate with the [[Atlantic University|Atlantic University]] through Dr. Brown?
(A)  Would only be a very TEMPORARY connection, and would not prove satisfactory to [5453].

10. (Q)  What particular significance did Dec. 1st., 1930, have regarding the life of [5453], as was mentioned in a previous reading?  [See 5453-3, Par. 8.]
(A)  A settling of many ideas or [[Ideal|ideals]] as the body has held in abeyance for many moons, and - as has been given through those of the [[Astrology|ASTROLOGICAL]] associations, environs, and those conditions which are made for - as the active forces of will, or of allowing circumstances to overcome - using, as the body has, that of the environs that would make afraid to be stepping stones FOR development - has meant much.

11. (Q)  When will economic conditions throughout the United States be improved and the present depression be at an end?
(A)  Beginning with the spring, there will be - in various portions of the country - a change in economic conditions. Another year will bring those of a greater disturbance in MANY centers.  Then, unless there is the adoption of other conditions, a still GREATER disturbance in this present land. Then, in thirty-four or five, these will gradually adjust themselves to the uphill, as it were, for the general conditions.

12. (Q)  Give advice to both [5453] and [1730] which would be beneficial in helping them in their life together.
(A)  Study to show selves approved unto God, rightly dividing the words of truth, being not afraid to dare to do that which is as to the dictates of thine own conscience.  In doing same, keep selves unspotted from the world.

13. (Q)  Should they remain at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]?
(A)  That will depend upon circumstances and associations as may be made.

14. (Q)  Advise them regarding the care and upbringing of [5454].
(A)  Apply that in the life, the lives of each, as to be ENSAMPLES - as well as in precept - as regarding those dependent upon, associated with, or connected in ANY manner. These are well pleasing in the eyes and in the upbuilding of SELF and the divine influences in life.

15. (Q)  Would [5453] be able to obtain position in his profession in Charleston, W. Va., Norfolk, Va., or Washington, D.C.?
(A)  Better in Washington, D.C.  This, however, would be better taken with someone of affluence, than in any direction, and would NECESSITATE the civil service examination.

16. (Q)  In the Internal Revenue Department?
(A)  Internal Revenue Department, or counter-department of the Federal service.

17. (Q)  Considering the advice given, circumstances and conditions at the present time, what is the best thing for them to do at the present time?
(A)  Make ready to do that which is chosen to do!

18. (Q)  Any other advice that will be of benefit?
(A)  They'll have their hands full if they'll do that!

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self Two copies to Ass'n office
