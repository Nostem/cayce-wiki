# TEXT OF READING 470-27 M 49

**Reading [[470-27]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/8/1939  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 3454

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through wife, Mrs. [1100] Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we find the temperature persists, - the cold, the congestion has not been broken.

3. We would bathe the lower extremities of the body, especially, in tepid or hot water.

4. We would take a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]].

5. We would take internally, again, -

Five grains of Asafoetida, One Alophen Pill, Two Aspirins, and A jigger of Whiskey;

with a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as the body is ready for rest after taking all of these.

6. On the morrow take AGAIN a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]], massaging alcohol especially DOWNWARD along the spinal system.

7. Do that.

8. These should purify, should cleanse the system.

9. These would be taken together, of course, the Alophen, the Aspirin and all.

10.  Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Should the alcohol rub be given today also?
(A)  Tomorrow will be the preferable, - or it may be given after the tepid bath today, especially from the waist around the body, you see, then massaged across the small of the back and the WHOLE of the back may be very well.  But on the morrow it would be well again.

Only liquids, of course, and semi-liquids should be the diet.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
