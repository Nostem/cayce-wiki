# TEXT OF READING 1068-1 F 40

**Reading [[1068-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/26/1935  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 6212

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Warshawsky home, 3046 Webb Avenue, Detroit, Michigan, this 26th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the husband - Dr. [1063], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1068] and Dr. [1063].]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1068] present in this room.

2. Now, as we find, while the body is very good in many respects, there are disturbances of those natures the correction of which made in the present will give not only the better physical functionings of the body, relieving distresses that arise from various disturbances that are caused by these reactions, but prevent later a great deal of disturbance for the body.

3. For there are those [[Physical Body|physical forces]], as we find, that produce an improper separation in the blood supplying to the system those elements necessary for not only carrying on those functionings of reproduction in [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], in ganglia, in nerve force and in the energies of the organic functionings, but as make for the very activity of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] with the mental and spiritual forces of the body itself.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them in the present with this body, [1068] we are speaking of, present in this room:

5. First, IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY OF THE BODY:

6. As indicated from these very activities from those portions of the body from which the cellular forces of the blood obtain their formation and cycle, as it were, of atomic structure about the cells in the blood supply, here we find conditions that cause a deficiency in this.  So, the effect to the body is in those portions where there are the natural weaknesses, either from overactivity in this or that direction or from exposure that may occur under stresses or strains of various activities that produce for some particular portion of the body at times the disorders.  Also the effect is in the body as to the general replenishing.

While there is the continuation of activity in the body towards producing normalcy, there is not the proper rebuilding.  Yet Life in its manifestation in an individual activity of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] in the material world finds itself able, because of its very nature, to take from the food values, the emotions that arise in the [[Mental Body|mental body]] and its environment, that which - combined with the proper activity of that assimilated in the body - produces the sufficient elements for not only the resuscitation but the reconstruction of itself - that goes on nominally throughout a body in the continual cycle of recuperation and reconstruction that occurs in the body through the various cycles of activity in the system itself.

7. Then, the variations as we find - consistent with those things indicated - are that the plasm between the white blood supply, the red blood or red blood cellular forces and the leukocyte or warriors in the system, BREAKS UP - as it were - before a COMPLETION of its proper activity.  Hence there is brought to various portions of the body those reactions as in this:

8. At times through portions of the mental forces, when there are those continued or strained activities, there comes the reaction as of a dullness - and then later there may be the recurrence of headaches, or there may be the pains through those portions of the shoulders, as through the brachial center.  Again these may recur through the throat, the larynx, and to the soft tissue of the face at times.  These may produce a stinging pain for a moment, or it may be relieved by any condition or influence that causes a reaction in the SYSTEM as to produce a greater number of the leukocytes or warriors in their flow to those portions of the system - and it may pass on.

9. Again from physical reaction there occurs, as through the solar plexus, a heaviness across the diaphragm area.  Or this again may occur through the lumbar axis and through the variation it produces through the activity of the organs of the pelvis, and the undue strain at times produced upon same. These are but effects, caused by the various cycles of application or rejuvenation in those particular portions of the body.  While the cycle of the circulatory force is nominal or normal for the pulsations through the circulatory system, the EFFECT as produced by the lack of the ability of the structural portions to produce the atomic forces that circle, as it were, about each of the globes or the molecules that produce the structural portion of the cell, is that the plasm BREAKS in its activity.

10. WHEREVER these conditions occur there may become a disorder to the body itself.

11. These, as indicated from the very nature of the body, very nature of the disturbance in the body, are constantly warring one with another.

12. For in the beginning, when there were the first separations of this body for its own individual activity, and the numbers of the cellular forces that form about the center from which the radial activity emanates, there was sufficient number for the continuation through its period of expression - in this particular activity of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or [[Soul|soul]] and body in this material world.

13. But there came periods in the earlier portion of the developing of the body, in its early teen ages, when there was a STRAIN - brought about by impoverishments to portions of the system.  And then this condition first began.

14. As there were added those influences or hormones that made for an increase in portions of the circulation, then the greater development began to show or become effective in itself.

15. Now, as the body in its very activity reaches those periods when the activities through the body-influence and bodily-forces function with the general forces of the body itself, we find these breaking down occasionally.  More often lower than the necessary influences for the completion of a rebuilding in these directions.

16. Hence we find in portions of the body [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] when there should be alkalinity.  We will find them working opposite at times.  For we may find perfect alkalinity through the testing of the salivary secretions, and yet through the eliminations in the kidneys an excess of acid.  Again we may find the secretions or activities through the gastric flow of the stomach itself in an acid condition.  Again we will find the eliminations through the kidneys, or through those activities from the bladder itself, producing a greater quantity of alkalinity than should be expressed.

17. These are indications of those unbalanced conditions as we find that arise from a structured condition existent in the lacteal ducts themselves; those in the right portion as coordinate with the activities from the secretions from the liver, the pancreas, the spleen.  Not so much as a [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLAND]] reaction, but the inability of the blood cells themselves to form in their proper ratio or coordination through the activities of the system; though, to be sure, WITH these changes the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the organs themselves become involved in the condition.

18. The NERVE FORCES of the system suffer more from specific or central ganglia than from a general reaction through the system, as would be indicated in some conditions.  For the relaxations and the stimulations that have been brought to the body, through the forms of making for coordination in the areas about the locomotories and where there has existed for a time the jamming, as it were, on the upper cervical areas, these have been helpful - and the body has been relieved of these pressures and the distress taken away.  But all of these at times suffer under stress, so that dis-ease - rather than disease - arises from those weakened portions in the cycle of activity of organs and of the coordination with the nervous system itself.

19. IN THE ORGANIC FUNCTIONINGS, brain forces are very good.  The activities of the sympathetics to the sensory organisms of the body at times show lapses in the ability for the circulation to care for the drosses.  But in the main these react nominally.

20. In the throat, the bronchi, the lungs and the larynx, here we find at times a sympathetic condition has been indicated - through the mucous membranes in the head, through the face, and in the throat itself becoming excessive in their activity.  And the flow is usually indicated by an acid condition.  Yet these may be found to react sympathetically to increase the circulation to those particular portions - but usually to the detriment of the eliminations in some other portion.  But these gradually rebuild through REST periods, or through stimulation to the cycle of activity about the central portion of the nervous system.

21. Heart's activity is irregular at times, yet the pulsation in the present - and the pressures - are near to a normal condition, considering the conditions that have been indicated as causing the disorders through the circulatory forces.

22. The digestive forces of the body:  Here arises, as indicated, a portion of the disorder; through the abilities of assimilation.  Certain characters of food disagree immediately at times, yet at other times - with an excess of the necessary influences for the producing of balance between the lactic acids and the acids themselves - most anything would digest!  Yet at other times it turns upon the system. For the ducts through which the oils and fats are carried as an assimilated force are the area from which and in which the lesion exists, or the condition in the lacteal area - as indicated.

23. Hence at times over the right shoulder, and through and under the right shoulder blade, there becomes a specific condition that disturbs; that is not relieved by the general activity. Neither are there the abilities for the corrections in the activities to the vagus center from these portions to become as effective as ordinarily.  But heat or low electrical forces may aid.

24. In the activities of the spleen, the pancreas, the liver in their coordination:  Under the general conditions these are better than would be ordinarily considered, though the activities from the gall duct and the secretions of same at times give distress.  But these are not the main conditions, as indicated; for this lies in the lacteal duct area itself, owing to the conditions that arose from a period when there were such distresses upon the system by impoverishments to portions of the body, which caused this condition to become localized there.

25. Through the activity of the digestive forces as related to the upper portion of the intestinal system, we have at times periods when there are the excesses of gases that may form for the UPPER portion.  However, with the lower portions cleansed, these pass off.  Yet these are reactions from the lack of the plasm or of the circulation of the lymph and emunctory activity through the digestive system; owing to these conditions indicated.

26. As given, the pelvic organs at times suffer under these stresses or these impoverishments; making for an unbalancing in the manner of the eliminations through their normal system.

27. Then, in adding to the body that as we find would make for corrections, that would bring about the normal CYCLE of its activity:

28. We find, as indicated, the body is of an electronic nature, with the various cycles moving about same in the formation in the ratio as to that from which it first occurred - or first began its movements.

29. In this body, then, they have lost their ratio to an extent, owing to the strictures as indicated in the manner in which the assimilations form with the secretions from the structural portion of the divisions in the blood supply; bringing about much of those conditions indicated.

30. Then, add to the body the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from or through the lower galvanic reaction that forms with metals in the body, passing the current through same.  Also have the stimulations to the centers from which all the radial activities of these are made to coordinate with the locomotory center, or through the 9th and 10th dorsal; and coordinate same with the brachial center, the lumbar axis, and those throughout the cervical forces as coordinant with drainages to the system.  And we will find these conditions gradually stimulating themselves to more and more a normalcy throughout the body.

31. Add, then, the Plain [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], making the activity to the body from the attachments in this manner:

32. The smaller copper plate would be attached first, to the 9th dorsal center - or between the 9th and 10th dorsal plexus. The larger nickel plate would be attached last, over the lacteal duct center - or that which combines a portion of the umbilicus plexus and the gall duct area - but the lacteal duct there.

33. Let this current pass through the body for twenty minutes each day, for a period of three weeks.  Then rest a week. Again apply for another period of three weeks.  And then a rest for two to three weeks.  And then apply again.

34. And we should begin to see then NORMALCY recurring through the body, and the tendencies as indicated in other portions of the body being gradually eliminated.

35. Do that.

36. The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or the manipulations, as we would have osteopathically, would be given two to three times a week. Stimulate specifically the 9th and 10th, or 8th, 9th and 10th dorsal centers; coordinating the lumbar plexus, the brachial plexus, and the cervical center, with that stimulation given.

37. Use the electrical forces, as indicated, of the Plain [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]; one anode copper, the other nickel; these passing through the system create a nominal or normal balance, for they become effective with - or ATTUNE the atomic [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body to their normalcy.  Just as one would attune an instrument to accord or coordinate its chords with other portions, producing the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that become harmonious; just so do they become harmonious in the body.

38. Ready for questions.

39. (Q)  What causes the spastic sigmoid and the incompetent ileo-cecal valve?
(A)  The inability of the proper coordination, as indicated, through the plasm where there are the necessary reactions for these to carry on their proper function.

For, as indicated, in the activity of each organ of the system, or each activity of the body, whether mental or physical, ENERGIES are used; and there is the necessary balance for conserved energies that act in its atomic forces. And these breaking up at times, as indicated, produce these spasmodic reactions in various portions of the body.

40. (Q)  What causes inability to retain ideas expressed in reading matter?
(A)  The same as indicated from the first.  The balancing between the mental forces of the body and the active forces of the [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL body]].  For these become as plasms carried through the nerve energies themselves, in what is known as the white and the gray matter, in their reflexes from the system acting the same upon the reflexes to the sensory organism - as obtained through comparisons by seeing, the sense of smell, the sense of taste, the sense of hearing, as they respond to same.  The plasm BROKEN, that makes for the replenishing of same, makes for that which becomes as a lapse, as it were, of retaining mentally - or of retaining the activity.  For the whole CYCLE of same is not completed. Hence those detentions, of those reactions that occur under such conditions.

These made to become coordinant or harmonious, through the attunement to the various portions - and especially to the centers from which the radial activities of all the functionings of the system are carried out, we will have normalcy.

41. (Q)  What causes the fine rash on face?
(A)  As indicated in how that at times this comes and goes, by the super amount of [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] that is left in portions of the system; and then recurrent conditions in other portions make for this to be carried away - or stimulations are such that same is eliminated, and becomes as nil in the reactions in the superficial or sympathetic circulation.

42. (Q)  Is there any [[Healing|healing]] action in the Oxydonor treatment, and will it help this body toward normal balance?
(A)  There are [[Healing|healing]] properties in the Oxydonor treatment. But as we have indicated - here are the variations:

In the Oxydonor treatment we ionize, as it were, or stimulate through this activity to the various centers.

Now, the condition existent here is not that these are not or have not been stimulated.  For the application of this, as indicated, has AIDED.  But there is the lack of attunement. So that the plasm between that assimilated, and the activities of the organs of the body in producing the proper balance to keep an attunement, is broken - through this condition as indicated.

For, a low current of this type of electrical vibration as indicated, through the Plain [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], passing through the main plexus - or the solar plexus, that radiates through the area as indicated - in the 8th, 9th and 10th dorsal, or that more specifically through that between the 9th and 10th dorsal center, (being a CONSTRUCTIVE and not a destructive force) will make for the greater ATTUNEMENT; harmonizing all of these - hence, as we find, will be the more beneficial.  Not only will this application create harmonious coordination in the activities of the body physically, but mentally.  Hence the abilities, the opportunities for expression, will become the greater.

43. (Q)  What causes the frequent urination?
(A)  As indicated through how the very activities in eliminations cause oft the reflex actions to the portions of the body itself.  The assimilation and elimination as carried on by what may be termed the positive and negative forces in these functionings, respond to same by a contraction in the sympathetic reactions in the organs.  Thus at TIMES such conditions are produced.

Do those things as indicated, and we would find better conditions.

44. (Q)  What is the action of the Oxydonor?
(A)  It STIMULATES the ganglia through which ionization takes place.  Or it stimulates those centers through which the life forces are carried on.  In many conditions this is helpful, where there is debilitation or an overtaxation mentally or physically - where such are caused by PHYSICAL conditions as indicated here, however, this form of rejuvenation as indicated is the more perfect.

Do these.

45. We are through for the present.
