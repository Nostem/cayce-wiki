# TEXT OF READING 294-162 M 56

**Reading [[294-162]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/17/1933  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2121

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at Lillian Edgerton, Inc., 267 Fifth Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]], this 17th day of November, 1933, in accordance with suggestion made by HLC that at the end of 446-1 he would give advice for his own health.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [446] and [444].]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                     [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Periodic suggestion for EC's betterment)

1. EC:  Congestion exists in the larynx and the upper portion of the bronchi circulation.  Increase circulation by suggestion.  Then apply light that is penetrating to the mucous membrane activity to relieve congestion.  Also give an eliminant that is more alkalin than acid in eliminating same through the alimentary canal.

2. (Q)  What type of eliminant?
(A)  As would be found in Milk of Magnesia and Milk of Bismuth.

3. (Q)  Where should the light be applied?
(A)  Over the thorax and over the 3rd and 4th cervical area.

4. (Q)  For how long?
(A)  Until congestion is relieved.  Depending upon the type of light used, and how penetrating it is.  If it's ultra- violet, two and one-half minutes.  If it's the sun ray light of high voltage, low amperage, five to ten minutes.  If the sun light with the blue ray, three to six minutes.

5. (Q)  [HLC gave the suggestion for increasing the circulation.]
(A)  That's very much better.

6. (Q)  [11/17/33, 9:10 P.M., following 447-1, HLC again gave the suggestion for EC's health.]
(A)  We have given what should be done!  It would be wise to do it.  Take the alkalin eliminant!

7. (Q)  [11/18/33 Following rdg. 445-2 HLC gave the suggestion.]
(A)  We would follow that given, until there is created sufficient of that bacilli in the body to override the cold and congestion.

8. (Q)  Should other sun light treatments be given?
(A)  Rather the antiseptics that have been indicated, and the alkalinity in the intestinal tract, for the excesses would be eliminated through these channels.  Unless sweats were used; and the conditions do not warrant that in the activities in the present.

9. (Q)  [11/21/33 Following 275-35 HLC gave the suggestion for EC.]
(A)  Not so good.

10. (Q)  What should be done for the body?
(A)  Remove congestion, temperature and cold, through channels that have been outlined.

11. We are through.
