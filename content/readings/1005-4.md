# TEXT OF READING 1005-4 M 19

**Reading [[1005-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/26/1928  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 6064

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of October, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - [1005].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. [5618] and Mrs. [760].]

Time of Reading 
7:30 A. M.                                     Lexington, Va. 
(Check Physical - Special reference eyes)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find there are conditions with the eyes that give trouble.  This is a form of astigmatism, especially in the left.  The better condition (this is produced in part by nervousness, strain, and congestion, which centralizes the condition) to meet the needs would be to take those exercises especially of the head and neck, in a CONSISTENT way and manner, and have GLASSES fitted for the CONDITION at the PRESENT; not use those that have been used, however.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Where would be the best place to have the glasses fitted, from where he is now located?
(A)  Roanoke.

3. (Q)  Who would be the best one to go to in Roanoke?
(A)  Oculist!

4. (Q)  Any special one?
(A)  Good one!

5. (Q)  Any other physical condition that should be corrected at this time?
(A)  Be careful about the cold and congestion, and the poor eliminations.

6. (Q)  Should he take the head and neck exercises once or twice each day?
(A)  Better were they taken twice each day, before retiring and upon arising - three times each side - to front, to back - and circle three times.  Be systematic about it.  Not just routine.

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this reading.
