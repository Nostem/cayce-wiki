# TEXT OF READING 243-11 F 51

**Reading [[243-11]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/3/1931  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 1095

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [243].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [243] - this we have had before. In many respects conditions are improved from that as last we had same here.  There are those conditions existent in the upper dorsal area that bring to the system in a general manner, with the mental forces of the body, those that make for ACIDS in the system - a tendency for irritation to the muco-membranes of the throat and the nose, pains in the eyes, and a DIZZINESS in the back portion, rather than even the front portion, though that at times - and top of the head - gives worriment.

2. With the application, as we find, of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] of evenings, especially over the lower LUMBAR - and then to the upper portion, going AWAY FROM the central portion of body; beginning in the 4th lumbar go towards the central portion of body; beginning in the central portion go towards the brachial centers, and to the base of the head, and especially in the 4th, 5th and the 1st and 2nd cervical - see?  until it almost jars the teeth with the [[Vibration|vibrations]], these given just before retiring will make for better rest.

3. Take internally those of an ALTERED [[Vibration|vibration]] in Citrocarbonate, Milk of Magnesia, Milk of Bismuth.  These, as we find, will tone the stomach.

4. Beware of too much sugars or too much pastries or too much of any condiments; not too much MEATS.

5. These would bring about a better condition.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Why the burning sensation in joints and sometimes in the fleshy part of body?
(A)  Too much acid.  Hence those absorptions as are given in that of the Milk of Bismuth, with those of Citrocarbonates that will act upon the LYMPHS of the system and the emunctory circulation; while those properties of the Magnesia will make for better eliminations.  When these occur, we find there has been the tendency for the alimentary canal to be somewhat choked, and tendency for acid in system.

7. (Q)  How often should these be taken, and in what manner?
(A)  Take 'em in the mouth! Take 'em every day, one or the other of them - alternate them!

8. (Q)  Why the enlargement of joints in the hands?
(A)  Most of it is hard work and worry, and acid in system! The LYMPH PRODUCING those - hence the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to carry these AWAY FROM the central portion and distribute them to their NORMAL eliminations, see?

9. (Q)  Why the catch in left knee at times?
(A)  This has been strained.  Hence the tendency or weakness in that direction.  With the removal of these, as has been outlined, and with the care and attention as has been given for the system, we will find bettered conditions.

10. (Q)  Why the blur over my eyes at times?
(A)  Those pressures in the secondary cardiac plexus area; that, and remember - the body ISN'T as young as it used to be!  In the eliminations, we will find that those carrying away of the used forces will assist in clarifying these conditions.  Would be well, too, that occasionally - once or twice each week, before retiring (and be sure it's after a thorough treatment with the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] so the body won't wake up!), apply a [[Poultice|poultice]] of OLD Irish potato, well scraped, bandage over eyes and let remain until morning - see?  then wash same with a weak solution of an antiseptic for the system.  THIS will clarify same.

Also we will find that these properties used as drops occasionally in the eye will ASSIST in clarifying same:  A little [[Baking Soda|soda]] and a little salt in about three times the quantity of water, see?

11. (Q)  Why the tight feeling in head at times?
(A)  This, as has been given, is from those centers in the dorsal area that have tightened up by the pressures, and that bringing to the STOMACH of an ACID condition in the blood, and those forces in the LYMPH that make for that in the head and soft tissue of face - see?  The snuffing of Dobell's solution would be well, into head, to clarify head.

12. That is all the questions.

13. Cream of Tartar, Salt and [[Baking Soda|Soda]] - equal parts; five times part water, with this luke warm, snuffed into the nostrils, or used as a gargle, will clarify the mucus membranes, will ease the head PRESSURE, and noise as comes from LYMPH pressure - as it does in THIS body - see?  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
