# TEXT OF READING 333-11 M 59

**Reading [[333-11]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/6/1942  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 2546

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [333], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.                        ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Many changes have been brought about with this body since last we had same here.

3. As we find, these are rather disturbing conditions and of an acute nature, but need not necessarily cause overanxiety - if those precautions are taken as may be indicated for this body in the present.

4. There has been a tendency for the body to have a great accumulation of poisons or toxic forces through the body. And with the upsetting that has come from those things which have caused a contraction through the abdominal area, pressures have been brought about in the solar plexus center.

5. Thus there is caused the dizziness, the swelling in portions of the body.

6. Have immediately, as soon as practical, - first, - very carefully given, - a good, thorough [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] to cleanse the system.  Take plenty of time, but use plenty of water.  Do have it body temperature, and do put [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt in the water to be injected.  Put a heaping teaspoonful of table salt in a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to each half gallon of water used, but use as much as three to four gallons, - not all at once, to be sure, but in the cleansing - and be sure to have it body temperature, not hotter, not colder.  Then in the last rinse water, put at least two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the gallon of body temperature water, see?

7. Let the body rest thoroughly after same; keeping quiet; not sitting up wholly, but with the heat applied, and the head plenty high, give a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  Rub away from heart and head, and to the central portions - or the eliminating portions of the system; from the head and arms downward; from the feet upward; stimulating this solar plexus center especially, with heat.

8. Do that, and - with this repeated in about ten days - we SHOULD bring better conditions for this body.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air-Mail-Special - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note:  If you have not been able to locate the proper hydrotherapist for giving the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] afterward, get in touch with Mrs. Louis B. Poss, 212 Woodland Terrace, Jefferson Park, [[Alexandria|Alexandria]], Va.  She has gotten wonderful water treatments, she says, at the Washington Sanitarium and Hospital in Tacoma Park; and perhaps has discussed the Readings with some of those in charge.  Phone her and get the name of the person or persons to approach.)
