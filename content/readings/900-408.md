# TEXT OF READING 900-408 M 33

**Reading [[900-408]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/12/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5611

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have the physical condition of this body and the enquiring mind.  You will tell what doctor he should go to to get the correct treatment of Ichthyolate.  You will give the physical condition, and any other suggestions that will assist this body.  You will answer any questions I ma ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body and the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  Also the conditions as need the attention and correction through the application of forces as needed for that in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

3. There are many such applicators of these forces.  [GD's note: Named over several doctors - couldn't make out the names.] Craig, or Craigmore (?) would be satisfactory.  While there may be some changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], yet these conditions as we find need the attention.  Craig.

4. (Q)  C-r-a-i-g?
(A)  C-r-a-i-g.  We are through for the present.
