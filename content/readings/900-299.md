# TEXT OF READING 900-299 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-299]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/11/1927  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5502

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Halcyon Hotel, [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]], this 11th day of February, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the ways and manners as have been outlined, and as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] so brings them to self these may be used by self - for in that way and manner is there seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] one of the manifestations of that force as is being studied, used and manifested, through the entity's mode of action. Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Morning of Sunday, Feb. 6, 1927.  Voice:  (as I was thinking of [[Ajax|Ajax]] Rubber Stock)  "Wait for the next rise - then go in if he buys into the stock."
(A)  This, as is seen, that of the correlation of the body conscious mind, with various conditions as regarding conditions relative to a given stock, and - as is seen - wait for the rise, and as the next rise begins, begin with that as directly from the stock - rather than of combinations as may be hindered, broken, or handled in a way and manner as to be harmful to many; for with this particular stock there have been many combinations, and there will be other combinations, and combinations that were of necessity to handle the stock in the way as is desired to control as much of same as possible.  Hence go directly for the stock, see?

4. (Q)  This is directly in line with recent events, and is it that we should wait until a reaction impending is over, then, if the party who put us in before, again creates activity - buy [[Ajax|Ajax]] again?  Correct?
(A)  Buy [[Ajax|Ajax]] again - correct; but independent of groups, see?

5. (Q)  In line with this, [137] saw a curb man buying rights while others sold the stock at a profit, indicating what?
(A)  Just that as has been given, that these other combinations will of necessity need as much outside stock as possible, and the control of same, to carry the points as will allow to be ascertained by the various groups.

6. (Q)  Voice:  "Wheeling & Lake Erie 29."
(A)  This the indications of that as is seen in the combination of the numbers in their relative condition to each other - the weakness as is seen in two (2); coming through to the entity in that same way and manner as of a correlation of body conscious with existent conditions as respecting particular stocks, but presented in this manner, see?

7. (Q)  Now this stock has caused a greater furrow than any stock since Stutz & Piggly Wiggly.  Its sensational rise has started the economic world and shaken the exchange to its roots.  What is here indicated in regards this interesting stock?
(A)  The weakness as will be seen, see?

8. (Q)  Impression that Western Maryland R.R. stock would go to

9. (Q)  Voice:  "[[Colorado|Colorado]] Fuel 40."
(A)  Again the correlation of conditions as respecting that in which there is to be a great deal of change; yet, as is seen - as indicated in the numbers given - only a double security, as it were, of the weakness as is to be experienced through this particular stock - in four - naught.  Although advances as have been given in special ones will be seen, and is seen, yet in these - with their multiples of the various elements as are exercised in figures to the entity, give their showing in the expression of figures given.

10. (Q)  Again an indication of a downward reaction in the market?
(A)  The downward reaction, see?

11. (Q)  What indicated regarding [[Colorado|Colorado]] Fuel Stock?
(A)  The downward movement - as doubly so in this particular one as given.

12. (Q)  As I was reasoning this predicted reaction, Voice: "Four Point Reaction." (A)  Four point reaction.  Again the double indication of that as is to be seen, see?  Doubly so - doubly so.

13. (Q)  General market reaction of 4 points down?
(A)  General market reaction of four points down; with many much lower, many gaining - some new high records in the day's or in season's tradings, see?  yet the general market a four point down.

14. (Q)  Voice:  "You had not faith in it, so it turned and destroyed you."
(A)  That as is seen given to the entity as to that from within self.  As is seen, the doubts arising again gives voice to that as expressed from within.

15. (Q)  How not faith?
(A)  That as is given - the fear from within self, see?  for (as is as comparison) as was given to Abraham, that it was counted to him for righteousness' sake - and his faith, knowing not where, was counted as such - so as in this. The entity finding fear within self, it becomes as a redoubling to the entity as regarding such conditions - [900] this is speaking of.

16. (Q)  [[Atlantean Destruction|The destruction]] meaning the Ajax episode?
(A)  Not necessarily; though this is pointed to by the entity as that seen in self, and as to the doubt as arose in same - yet fear of expressing that as seen by entity caused or brought consternation to self for not speaking - consternation to others for what happened, see?

17. (Q)  Meaning if [137] and I had heeded [[Intuition|intuitive]] warnings the shabby treatment [137] received at hands of another would not have happened?
(A)  Would not have happened - just as has been outlined here, see?

18. (Q)  Monday morning, Feb. 7, 1927.  A room in [[China|China]].  [[China|Chinese]] had food and were preventing whites from eating.  Some whites seemed to have made money and I paid off one big tall fellow who had been demanding it.  Then I gave back the money.  The man paid sought to get out but was forced out of one exit. As he went out he said that he hoped to get away alive.  I heard music and they told me it was a death march and indicated that he had been taken.  I saw Chinamen fling hungry white men about, who sought the food (ham it was) for their starving and enslaved selves.
(A)  In this there is seen for the entity much of those conditions wherein the entity, through the mental and body conscious mind, has sought - in its building from within mental forces - as to how entity might not act or give information concerning such conditions as are shortly to be, and as have been enacted, in this land - and the entity having dwelt mentally upon the possibilities of such conditions, and the manner in which same might be averted, finds self face to face with conditions that to the entity are as true, and ARE true in fact.  Then act upon that [[Intuition|intuitive]] force, as to how this may be prevented, see? Meaning - the entity has mentally devised how much bloodshed, much hard feelings, much of the troublesome conditions might be averted.  Action would prove the better valor to the entity!  in proving self, in proving those conditions in which the entity has delved mentally.

19. (Q)  Any bearing on the present [[China|Chinese]] situation?
(A)  As given - certainly!

20. That is all.

21. We are through for the present.

42.
(A)  Hold impression until -  As has been given, when the entity receives impressions as regarding various conditions relative to stocks, there is to be presented to the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of the entity those conditions, through correlations of body conscious and that experience through which the entity passes, or has passed, as respecting information regarding same.  Wait on these, as has often been given.  Be not too quick to grasp at an impression without the corresponding conditions to the entity to verify same.
