# TEXT OF READING 3854-4 M CHILD

**Reading [[3854-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11470

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by his mother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                         ..., Ill.

1. EC:  Now, we find there are changes in this body since last we had it here.  While those centers that supply the sensory organism have not worked through, or the tentacles of centers in elimination as produced by the sympathetic nerve system, and which had been hindered by the holding of thought and condition in the body as transmitted to same; still the condition in the system is bettered, and we find that the centers are more thoroughly equalized in pressure, and that by the creating of the correct [[Vibration|vibration]] in the system the nerve centers that give the vocal organs their functioning normal, and the auditory or ear forces may be, and can be, carried to the perfect [[Vibration|vibration]] and connection by keeping that [[Vibration|vibration]].  This has not been proper in the system as heretofore.  We would now, with the present condition, prepare this, in this manner and form.  To one and one-half gallons of rain water, add:

Heavy Sea Salt...................1 pound,
Plain Charcoal...................1 pound,
Copper Sulphate..............1 1/2 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid C.P............1 1/2 ounces,
Zinc, ordinary, common..........11 drams.

2. This we find will charge the solution, but the solution for the treatment of the body should be charged with electrical voltage, high voltage, low amperage, 120 volts, or 110 volts either, depending on direct or alternating, with 30 cycles, 4 amperes, until the discharge from this solution is at least 5 1/2 ohms, or charge the solution with this charge, this voltage, this amperage, for 30 minutes while being charged and while in use.  There should be a one pound opaque container of [[Iodine|iodine]] suspended or in the solution.  The poles shall be one of copper, the other of nickel and silver.  To these poles there would be attached two small wires, preferably insulated copper wires.  To the ends of these wires attach plates.  To the positive a small copper plate, to the opposite pole the large silver plate, very thin.  The positive will be attached to the first and second cervical, the negative to the umbilicus, for thirty minutes each evening.  Remove poles from solution when not in use, but have them in solution for at least thirty minutes before charge is given the body.  Then let the neuropathic forces be accorded the system in such a manner as to dilate the blood supply from the lower and upper cervical region, direct to the auditory and vocal organs.  Do that.  In this manner we will find we will bring the normal forces to this body.
