# TEXT OF READING 1561-16 M 59

**Reading [[1561-16]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/26/1940  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 7575

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by Dr. [2246], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:35 to 7:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled by the Carmerson Corp., in Smith County, [[Texas|Texas]], midway between Big Sandy in Upshur Co., and Winona in Smith Co.  Also you will have before you the information which has been given thru this channel concerning this well, together with the wire from Dr. [2246] which I hold in my hand.  You will tell us how they should proceed, and what the answer to this wire should be.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations here of Carmerson Corporation; conditions existing in the present operation; together with the information which has been given, and the request here.

3. Then, we would consider all that has been given concerning operations in this particular location.

4. As we find, the coring has been in formations that should indicate the presence of production.  There IS production in this location.  Do not abandon same if it is possible to continue, until there is the production in paying quantities.

5. The variation as to depths that have been indicated, - we find that these do show there is the presence of production. It should be reached, if there would not become a whole loss.

6. We would continue operations.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Continue the coring?
(A)  As has been indicated, - core until there is an entire change in structure.  Then drill if necessary.

9. (Q)  Please give about the number of days necessary to continue, that they may know.
(A)  This will depend upon operations.  For, as given, this should occur ANY time - the completion of well, see, - that is, to production; for they are IN formations that should produce.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. [2246] - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

Day Letter sent as follows:  CORE UNTIL ENTIRE CHANGE IN STRUCTURE  THEN DRILL IF NECESSARY  COMPLETION TO PRODUCTION SHOULD OCCUR ANY TIME  FOR CORING IS IN FORMATIONS THAT SHOULD PRODUCE  FULL REPORT AIR MAIL.

> 
> Carmerson Plantation, Big Sandy, [[Texas|Texas]]
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> Arctic Crescent,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> My dear Friend:
> 
> When I telegraphed you that we had reached the Georgetown
> Lime at about 5500 feet and that the electrifying test of
> Schlumberger, (very accurate and precise), had revealed the
> presence of salt water back a few feet in the Woodbine sand,
> although it did not appear in cores we took, it meant that we
> could drill no further for three reasons, first that there
> could be no oil sand below sand containing salt water till we
> had penetrated beneath the Georgetown Lime which is several
> hundred feet thick and costly to drill, second, that the rig
> was not stout enough to drill deeper and thirdly, that we had
> run out of money already.
> 
> I planned to write you at length at once, but my nervous
> reaction was so great and my disappointment so intense that I
> collapsed physically and could write no one.  I am now about
> normal once more and I shall try to pick up the threads of
> life once more and fit them to the pattern I have to make.
> 
> [...], Mrs. [2174] and I still believe entirely in the
> messages which have come through you as a channel and feel
> that we have not known how to interpret them accurately, no
> matter how contradictory they may appear, as we read them, to
> the facts as we found them.  We took 24 cores over 500 feet
> and each one was very carefully tested for oil and water but
> neither was found, although the electric test did find the
> salt water.
> 
> I should love to come to your Congress but it will not be
> possible for me.  [...] and I shall probably motor to our
> home and fetch his wife and two children to spend the summer
> here.  Mrs. [...] flew home Monday night and [...] and [...]
> left nine days before that.  Please have a statement sent to
> me of the readings we owe for, that I may send on to [[New York City|New York]]
> for a cheque to be sent to you.  Dear love from [...] and me
> to you two splendid persons.
> 
> Many thanks for the excellent photo prints you sent me.  I
> have divided among those who are in the Groups.
> 
> Faithfully yours, [1561]
