# TEXT OF READING 3618-1 M 36 (Army Major)

**Reading [[3618-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/27/1944  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 11117

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Major [3618], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Born March 3, 1907, in Boston, Massachusetts. 
Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the record here of that entity now known as or called [3618].

3. From here we would desire to give that which may be helpful for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  And yet we find the entity of such a temperament in the present that unless stands are taken or decisions made, it would be doubtful as to whether that which is possible with this entity might be fully and completely indicated in the present.  It would be necessary that he grow to it, and make decisions before there is given the complete possibility of the entity.

4. For here we have one who may be even a greater genius than he who was called the wizard of the electrical forces or energies, even than Steinmetz.  As to decisions, then, there must first be conclusions by the entity as to whether those abilities in which the entity may excel are to be used for constructive or destructive [[Purpose|purposes]].

5. For, as indicated, or given by Steinmetz' boss, materially, whatever electricity is to man, that's what the power of God is.  Man may in the material world use God-force, God-power or electricity, to do man's work or to destroy man himself. Ye did it before - will ye do it again?

6. The entity was among the [[Sons of the Law of One|sons of the Law of One]] using those energies in the periods in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] that destroyed [[Atlantis|Atlantis]], that destroyed mankind.

7. What is thy [[Purpose|purpose]] in the present?  Do ye have [[Ideal|ideals]] spiritually?  Do ye desire that ye may be controlled of power and might, or do ye desire power and might that ye may glorify thy Maker?

8. These determine in thyself; not merely by saying "Yes, I'll be a good boy."  But live it for a year, and come around sonny, and we'll tell you.

9. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self - (in case you wish to send one to Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 244 Madison Ave., [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.) Copy to Ass'n file
