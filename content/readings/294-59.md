# TEXT OF READING 294-59 M 48

**Reading [[294-59]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/9/1926  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2018

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC, present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body-mind and the enquiring mind of the body, EC.  This we have had before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]] come to the body in that way as has been given, and these should be studied in the way as has been outlined for same.

4. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

5. (Q)  Friday Morning, 1/8/26.  "Regarding booklet, etc."
(A)  In this there is seen how that the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces take or reason with the mentality of the body regarding conditions which are held in the body-mind concerning what has been given that should be done pertaining to certain conditions.  And the results of same are seen in how many people seek, many people ask that these be given to groups, that they may study some of the phenomena.

Then, as is seen, when these are presented in the way and manner as readable to peoples, then there may be expected much from the peoples who know and hear.  Then be prepared to meet those conditions and give of the best in service to all. For the preparation should go forward concerning the data as seen, and the result will come, as has been given.  [See Source File Key #4905 for Cayce booklet published March 1926, under 4905-53.]

6. We are through with this reading.
