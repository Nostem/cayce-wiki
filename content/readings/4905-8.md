# TEXT OF READING 4905-8

**Reading [[4905-8]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/4/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12933

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by self, Madison Byron Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; Linden Shroyer, Steno.  [GD was in [[Alabama|Alabama]] for the funeral of her father.]]

Time of Reading                            Oil Well, 
12:00 Noon [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                    Bosque Co., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  Now you have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on the land owned by Madison Byron Wyrick, in Bosque Co., [[Texas|Texas]]. You will give the conditions of operations at the present time and tell what to do at this time to successfully bring in this well.  You will answer any questions relative to this that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we have this here.  We have had this before, you see.  We find that the operations are progressing very nicely.  The baling has not been completed as yet.  When this is carried to the sufficient quantity and in this case should be very little left except to drill with.  It will only be necessary to drill through this black lime to the break and bring the production.  This should only require a very short while - 2 days - 3 days unless some trouble is experienced with the drill line.  Be sure that stem is well set and that all bits are thoroughly set in stem for this is hard substance and will require a very slow, steady, not too long stroke, but that necessary to make progress that they may feel their way into production.
