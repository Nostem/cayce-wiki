# TEXT OF READING 4905-52

**Reading [[4905-52]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/6/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12977

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of January, 1926, which came involuntarily, without suggestion being given to obtain it, following three readings, a physical for Mrs. [325], a mental-spiritual volunteered for Mr. [551] and a business reading for Mr. [4278] in [[Texas|Texas]], familiar with the [5628] series.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               Bosque County, 
12:10 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, we have those conditions as surround the operations on [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]], on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick.

2. The conditions, as has been given, may be carried out in the way and manner as has been outlined for same, and would prove a great deal more less expensive and more to the betterment of conditions in the future, by handling in that manner than by attempting to drill up, or to use any corrosive acids or sublimates to rid the conditions, for these, as we find, do not give the best effects to future operations or materials used after such has been used in such operations, see? Then carry out that as has been given, for we would find the successful terminations in this, and very soon, and unexpected to many.

3. We are through for the present.

> way.

> saw, and I know he is anxious to clear the hole.
