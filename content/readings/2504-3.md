# TEXT OF READING 2504-3 M 63

**Reading [[2504-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/2/1929  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 9416

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of September, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2504].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. and Mrs. [[King David|David]] Levy, Mrs. C. G. Hopkins, Miss Mildred Davis, Mr. [2504] and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                               [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            Va. Beach, Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2504].  This we have had before.  Now, we find, while changes have taken place, and many of these for the betterment, there is not wholly the improvement at THIS time as there SHOULD have been, in the physical changes.  These are shown especially in the manner in which the system fails to eliminate those drosses and the excess of those conditions as have been cast into the system by the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces set up.  Then, that lacking has been the necessary stimuli through the manipulations as give to the various centers that govern eliminating forces of the system, as to keep these conditions gradually on the improve. In other words, the body had reached where it was rather at the standstill than on the improve as should be.  Keep, then, more consistently those suggestions for the full corrections. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Can anything more be done to relieve the sluggishness of the liver?
(A)  We would occasionally use those of packs of oil - [[Castor Oil|castor oil]] - over the WHOLE of the liver, at NIGHT.  Hot packs, see? Once in ten days should be sufficient, would the proper stimuli be given along the centers of the cerebro-spinal that govern the excretory and secretive functioning of the liver. Those of ASSIMILATING elements are very good, but to enliven the reaction of the blood forces through the organ proper, STIMULATE same by such packs.

3. (Q)  Is satisfactory progress being made in restoring the use of hands and arms?
(A)  Save as has been outlined.

4. (Q)  Any other suggestions for this body at this time?
(A)  Rather keep those as have been given, CONSISTENTLY given.  Let the manipulations, as has been suggested, be kept with more activity but not so severe as to cause pain to the body - taking the time to relax the various centers as are necessary, and taking the time to give the BODY - mentally AND physically - time to RESPOND to those stimuli as created. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
