# TEXT OF READING 4108-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4108-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/11/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11807

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of June, 1923, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [4108].

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno. Mr. [4108].]

Time of Reading 
6:50 P. M.                                      ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, for the body as we have here, [4108] now here in this room:  The [[Vibration|vibrations]] as given for the body have not been applied either in medicinal properties or the exercise proper; hence we have the reaction of the [[Vibration|vibration]] in the body, and it is producing the local condition that becomes aggravating to the system.  We would take those properties as given if we would correct these conditions.

2. For the local condition, take this until the others are beginning to be assimilated in the system:  Take of the weed or vegetable commonly known as the Horehound weed, see.  Take three (3) ounces of the green or five (5) of the dried, place in six (6) ounces of water, distilled or rain water, not of the ordinary tap water.  Reduce by simmering, not boiling to one-half the quantity.  Add to this one (1) grain of common Alum, one-twentieth (1/20) gr. of Heroin.  The dose of this would be teaspoonful every three hours until the relief is shown here, then once each day until assimilations begin from the properties given as stimulation to the body.  Do that.  We are through.
