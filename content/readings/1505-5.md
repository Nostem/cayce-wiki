# TEXT OF READING 1505-5 F 37

**Reading [[1505-5]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/4/1942  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 7384

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of September, 1942, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [341], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1505].]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [1505]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in the main, conditions are developing very satisfactorily.  There are no changes as we would suggest, save of evenings when tired we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] across the lower portion of the back with a little peanut oil.  This would not be as an attempt to make adjustments or anything, just to ease the tiredness in that area.

4. (Q)  Should the [[Calcios|Calcios]] be continued?
(A)  [[Calcios|Calcios]] should be continued in about the same proportions as has been given.  We wouldn't increase the amount and we wouldn't decrease.

5. (Q)  Would it be advisable to do anything for the heartburn?
(A)  Occasionally a little [[Baking Soda|soda]] water will help, or better still the Upjohn's Citrocarbonate.  This would be helpful in keeping a better balance in the chemical forces through the stomach and digestive system.

6. (Q)  Any suggestion as for the diet?
(A)  Keep a well balanced diet.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
