# TEXT OF READING 5371-1 F 25

**Reading [[5371-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/19/1944  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 13512

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5371] - recommended by Father-in-law - Dr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, conditions physically are very good with this body.  As for those which pertain to the ability to be a part in the procreation, we find that because of disturbances which have existed, there is a lack of the functioning of the creative organs or generative organs, and thus little activity from the ovaries.  These, then, make for these conditions where there is the inability for conception.

3. As we find, this may be materially aided.  It will require a little period of time.

4. There would be first the relaxation osteopathically of the activity of the organs of the pelvis, through the sacral and lumbar areas and extending to the 9th dorsal, then co-ordinate from the brachial center to the 1st cervicals.  We would take about six adjustments.

5. And then we would prepare and take these properties.  Prepare two solutions, one with Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]], 20 grains in one ounce of distilled water.  This is one solution.  Then in one ounce of distilled water put two grains of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium.  This would be the other solution.

6. Each morning, before a meal is taken, for ten days in succession put in a half of glass of water one drop of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] and one drop of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution.  Stir and drink immediately.  Keep this up for ten days, then leave off for ten days.

7. These, as we find, would then be taken again, and by the time these have reacted through the ten days there should be sufficient activity in the body-functioning for conception for the body.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. We are through with this reading.

Three copies to Self (one for father-in-law and one for the [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 7/5/44 Letter:  "My husband and I have been married for 3
> years; for the past 2 I have tried to become pregnant but
> without success; however at first my tubes were closed and
> my womb was tipped, though now the tubes are open and I'm
> wearing a ring to support the womb.  A physician claims that
> I do not ovulate regularly.  I have had shots to stimulate
> the ovaries and also taken special douches...  We both would
> love to have our own child and we are in a position to take
> care of one properly, so if you could tell me when I might be
> 'receptive' or suggest anything at all you think might help,
> we will follow it to the letter...  My husband was checked
> and found to be o.k."  [Her father-in-law is a medical
> masso-therapist.]
