# TEXT OF READING 5529-2 F 64

**Reading [[5529-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1927  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 13787

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5529].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:40 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had here before.  The general physical condition in the forces of the body show the normal changes as occur through the various conditions as have presented themselves to the body through that of the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in their activity throughout the body.

3. There has been well outlined for this body that of the conditions necessary to be warned against, and of that to do in the physical way to keep this [[Physical Body|physical body]] in an active way and manner.  These we would follow out rather closely; not severely, but consistently and persistently, and with much attention to that of the diets that will react properly to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this system.

4. In the mental attitude of the body we find many changes as come about from time to time.  This, as we see, gives more trouble to the body than the actual physical forces of the system.  In these changes that occur from time to time, the body finds that there are many whom the body doubts that are sincere, yet their actions under the questioning of others does not always appear so to this body.  Be well were the body to make up it its OWN mind as to which of these the body would heed, and follow that, see?  for the body will find self in the position - with the continuation of those conditions as have existed for some times past - where soon the body would be without the assurance from assistance from either - for the ones far away do not hold toward the body as they should.  Rather heed to those close and present at hand.

5. We are through for the present.
