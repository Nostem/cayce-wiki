# TEXT OF READING 2885-2 F 44

**Reading [[2885-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/15/1943  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 10127

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2885], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. [2772].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time; go over the body carefully, examine it thoroughly; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2885].

3. As we find, there are tendencies and inclinations, rather than definite conditions; though there are those things that without correction may necessitate that measures be taken to remove accumulations - in the organs through the pelvis.

4. These are conditions as we find them with this body, [2885].

5. The blood supply indicates a tendency towards a lack of balance between the correct numbers in red blood and white blood; or a variation, and thus at times a quickened pulsation.

6. There are in the cerebrospinal system, as we find, disturbances that hinder the correct condition between the nerves of the sympathetic and the nerves of the central nervous system.  Thus the conditions that cause disorders at times to the area of the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and at others a disorder towards the circulation between liver and kidneys.

7. These, as we find, are in the areas of the 2nd and 3rd dorsal, and in the 7th, 8th and 9th dorsal.

8. These corrected, as we find, - with a low form of electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] with same, - will bring the bettered condition and better coordination throughout the body.

9. We would apply these with some [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and [[Massage Therapy|massage]], though the corrections should be about once each week, the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] about once each week.  Each time the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] would be included, but the correction only once each week. When the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] would be given, it would include the electrical treatment AND the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  This should be given through the hands of the manipulator being charged with electrical forces, see, - and through the areas especially of the 8th and 9th dorsal to the 2nd cervical.

10. Do these and we will bring bettered conditions for the body.

11. The exercise should be in the open as much as practical.

12. As to the diets, - those things that will add to the body through the period of treatments especially, - extra amounts of calcium and the Vitamins A and D.

13. Do that, if we would make better conditions for this body.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Who would you suggest to make these corrections?
(A)  Whoever is chosen by the body.  Reilly's would be well.

16. (Q)  What should the hydrotherapy include?
(A)  A very light sweat, then fume, and the hot and cold water - sitz baths.

17. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file

(At your convenience we'd appreciate a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.)
