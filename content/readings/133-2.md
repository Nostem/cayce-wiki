# TEXT OF READING 133-2 F 48

**Reading [[133-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/29/1928  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 300

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by self, Mrs. [133] and daughters.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., West Va. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  Now, while some of the conditions have been accentuated in the physical reaction of the body from that as we have had here before, there are at the present time bettered conditions apparent through the applications of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up by injections in the system.  These have better prepared the body for those conditions that may be administered.  While these may be continued, we do not find that these would be other than temporary relief, unless there was added that which would bring the nearer basic forces for coordination of [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body to function nearer the normal and more perfectly.  Hence we would apply rather THIS for the body:

2. At least twice each week we would give the thorough massage of the cerebro-spinal system and across the abdomen and groins, and especially about the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] region, properties of Balsam of Sulphur.  This may be cleansed after the massage with a weak solution of [[Baking Soda|soda]] or salt water, and rubbed vigorously; not sufficient to produce abrasions, but sufficient to draw the circulation to these parts to which the Balsam of Sulphur is applied and massaged into.  In this manner we will set up the better basic [[Vibration|vibration]] for the system and assist the secretions, serums, and blood so created within the system to meet and combat with those elements that have caused and have produced those disturbances and those deflections of nerve reaction in this body.

3. EACH day, in the evening, to bring better rest, we would massage over the WHOLE body - that is, beginning from the base of the brain to the tips of the toes - equal parts of olive oil and tincture of myrrh and [[Sassafras|sassafras oil]].  Massage this gently into the system, so that the capillary circulation picks up - in its internal reaction - from such gentle massaging.

4. Do this, and we will find - were this kept consistently for thirty to sixty days - a great improvement shown in the abilities of the locomotions for this body.

5. The diet as has been outlined may be alternated to meet the needs of the conditions of system.

6. It is NECESSARY, to be sure, that the intestinal tract be kept nominally open.  Do not use the cathartics any more than necessary; preferably use the Russian White Oils and high enemas carrying either the oil of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an antiseptic in same.  These only resorted to, to keep the system cleansed from those poisonings necessarily arising from non-activity of the forces of the body through these channels, by its incapacitated forces in locomotion.  Do those.  We will find we will bring better conditions for this body.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Have any of the treatments accorded the body been of benefit?
(A)  As has been outlined.

8. (Q)  Give condition of the kidneys and bladder.  Should the left kidney be removed?
(A)  Not until these conditions as have been given for the body have been tried for the body, and - as we see - the response will be such that the removal would not be necessary.

9. (Q)  What causes the bearing down pain on the bladder?
(A)  The pressure produced from the condition in the transverse and ascending colon.

10. (Q)  What can body do to get rid of the rheumatism in legs, hips and feet?
(A)  Do that as has been given.

11. (Q)  Will the body be able to do her own work?  If so, how long before she will be able?
(A)  When [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are set aright in the body, that to which the body desires to apply itself may be accomplished through its own [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  Obtain that correction necessary for the carrying out of the desires of the physical body, through the application of the mental and physical forces, to those as have been given for the correction.  We are through with this reading.
