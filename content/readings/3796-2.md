# TEXT OF READING 3796-2 F ADULT (Psychologist)

**Reading [[3796-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/9/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11366

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [3796] - Involuntary; that is, volunteered, without suggestion being given to obtain it, immediately following a Physical Reading for Mrs. [4620].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Maine.

1. EC:  Now, we have before us those conditions that have to do with those of [3796], ..., Maine.

2. The condition that surrounds [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], this body, at the present time is the loss of confidence in self and self's ability to meet the conditions as arise.  Then, as is seen, this lack of confidence is transmitted, as it were, to those who do hear the message as is given by this body.  This, then, turns as a detractive influence, rather than attracting the necessary members for the class; yet we find that the message will, in the next two to three days, bring greater results, for the body is taking counsel with self.

3. Apply, then, that teaching as is being given, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will find there will come the greater conditions for this body, the greater successes for the body, in the present place, though there are better places in the surrounding country for this work.  Those nearer the ports, a little later, will prove much better, see?

4. We are through with this reading.
