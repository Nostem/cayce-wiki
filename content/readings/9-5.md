# TEXT OF READING 9-5 F ADULT

**Reading [[9-5]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/15/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 37

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of August, 1924 in accordance with request made by her brother, Mr. [8].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give in detail the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further relief of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find there is considerable difference in the body at the present time from that as we have had before.  While those conditions as have been given have not been carried out, there have been others that have in many ways benefited the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the body.  This we find has changed conditions very perceptibly in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the body.  This has in many ways benefited the body, yet has not reached that condition that produces, or is the seat of the trouble, as we have given.

4. Now these are physical conditions as we find in this body:

5. Beginning here at the head we find where the depression or where operation has been performed on this portion of body. This has relieved the pressure for the time, and with the correct eliminations set in system, to meet the existing conditions, this could be brought to mean a great deal more to the body.

6. In this manner would this application of the eliminations be carried:

7. Through that of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces in system, in conjunction with electrical forces in [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over the centers that receive the incentive for stimulating the excretory functioning of the body, and by taking in the system properties in that of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and gold, in this quantity:

8. To 15 ounces of distilled water add 10 grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] and 15 grains of [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]].  Shake well together.  The dose would be 5 drops in half a glass of water twice each day, morning and evening.  This we will find will so enliven nerve tissue, and especially that in the creative plasmatic cells, and receive the greater enlivenment or incentive through the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], and give each in its action in system the incentive to eliminate used forces and prevent the formation of that bacilli that produces inflammation in the system.  Also stimulating the nerve and muscular tissue in right portion of body, so that the condition in other side of body will come to function nearer the normal condition.  Also relieving pain in the body.

9. Then the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces should be applied to the system every other day very full, every other day nearer the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of muscular forces, that we may keep the centralization and proper eliminations set in this system, with the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] applied, not above the cervical, or above the first dorsal, but below and over all portions of the body twice each day, thirty minutes after the gold has been taken in the system.  Let the application be with not too strong a current, but be applied for at least ten to fifteen minutes each day.

10. With the pressure as found from the condition in head, brain centers are involved that make and cause the pressure on the sensory system, affecting or producing the condition in the left side, which in its reaction causes in the sensory system the condition, or the nontentative action of light on optic forces, and the dilation of the pupil to retain the reflected rays shows that this only needs to be removed and the eliminations so carried and removed from system without forming pressure and producing inroads in the system, until the body will be able to adjust itself and will overcome the condition, save where scar tissue produces pressure direct on brain forces that govern the return reflection as made in eye proper; that is, the one most affected at present.

11. The condition in the throat and bronchials at present is a local condition entirely, and with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] will easily be adjusted to the normal forces.

12. Lungs, bronchials and digestive forces, very good.

13. Heart's action very good at present.  The force of stimulation will raise the pressure on blood supply and produce an abnormal condition in heart's action.  This is not a detrimental condition but will be necessary under existing conditions, as well as the diet and digestive tract must be looked after while these conditions are being accorded the system.

14. The rest of the body we find in functioning organs very good, save as affected by those nerve centers in the cervical and dorsal region.  That in conjunction with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pituitary]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] shows the effect in the hypogastric, pneumgastric and in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] in the lower end of brain forces where the medulla oblongata enters the brain proper. This, with the contraction and relaxation of centers along the cerebrospinal system will produce [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] for elimination rather than having them to so excite tissue until the circulation in its attempt to correlate condition creates so much of the leucocyte forces in system, and then inflammation sets in, weakening all nerve centers, blood supplies eating up the resistance in the body, [9], we are speaking of, but follow persistently, consistently, in that line and channel as has been outlined here for this body, ever keeping the mental forces in that manner of content with conditions, and satisfied only in the spiritual elements as enter in with the fortitude of conditions, which is the gift of Him.

15. Then keeping ever the mind and the spiritual forces in that manner that realizes the forces and elements that are present in the mind and heart of His creatures is only the manifestation of His love and His way of giving to those who would develop in this plane the way of understanding.

16. Then follow in that straight and narrow way, applying those material forces necessary that the physical may manifest [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit forces in the better way, and in that way that gives the greater understanding of His manifested forces in earth's spheres.

17. Do that.  We are through for the present.
