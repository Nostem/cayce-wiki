# TEXT OF READING 4804-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4804-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1917 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/19/1917  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12787

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 19th day of July, 1917.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:58 to 9:16 P. M.                   Montgomery, [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  In this body here we have conditions existing that were produced by trouble in times back - trouble with the nerves - nervous break down.  Trouble that exists now caused by treatment of the same - nervous strain.  Incapacitated by inactive functioning of most all the functioning organs of the body.  Organic trouble is not so pronounced as abnormal functioning.  Depleted nervous condition of the whole system, the body is able to get about at times, but nearly always is under the weather.  There is a lack of red blood corpuscles, white are very good, leukocytes very inferior in action, hence any abrasion is hard to heal, none now.  Important functions of the body produced by nerves especially through the sympathetics.  The action of the 5 senses, good.  To improve this condition, there are many ways, two ways we have here now - to reduce or assist the body to a better condition - it can be bettered and improved to normal.  Electrical forces as applied to the centers of nerves - static electricity, first to the 1st and 2nd cervicals, 9th and 10th dorsals, not through the lumbars or cardiac plexus.  Do not disturb centers of the cerebro-spinal; that is one way, then we have the medicinal properties - take into the system oil of white pine and sweet milk.  White pine one ounce mixed with 8 ounces white sherry, dose, dessert spoonful twice a day, morning and night before meals, take the milk just after the medicine - warm from the cow, not strained or separated from the cream - can be iced if preferred, better not.  After this till one 8 ounces are taken, then with Santogen taken in the milk, (compound magnesia, arrowroot, strychnine, digitalis).  No need for operation in this body.  Lack of power to walk properly due to trouble at coccyx in conjunction between sympathetics and cerebro, governing locomotories of the body.  The power to walk is there but the brain does not carry through the nerve forces.
