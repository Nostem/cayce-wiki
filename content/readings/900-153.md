# TEXT OF READING 900-153 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-153]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/29/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5356

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I might ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, of [900], with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to this body.  These are, as we see, the co-relation of those forces as are being manifest through the individual and may be used in the manners as have been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], using same to guide, direct, in the ways as the developments come.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Thursday morning, Oct 29, 1925.  The Voice:  "[[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Gulf 1 1/8 and 1."
(A)  This the co-relation [correlation] of conditions as are existent in the prices as seen, see?  The general trend of down in market would bring the selling off, see?  This then would be the low point to which it would reach before advancing to much higher points in the market, see?  for nearer the 23rd or 4th of November, we will find many of these high - very high, see?  unprecedent in the quotations, meaning 71, see?  71 1/8 - 71 see?

4. (Q)  What (1 1/8 and 1) does it mean?
(A)  71 - 71 1/8, see?

5. (Q)  Does this mean that 61 1/8 or 61 will be the low in price?
(A)  Be the low in price, before the advancing, see?

6. (Q)  As given in the reading, [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Gulf is going to 73, to 101, and then break down again.  The stock is now about 62, having been purchased by [900] at 68.  Is this stock going back to 73 and then up to 101?
(A)  Just as given, see?

7. (Q)  Any other information that would assist [900] regarding this stock?
(A)  No.  This is as given, see?  This the numbers as the decrease will be shown, see?  and the general trend or rising then, until the latter days of November, when reach HIGH, see?

8. We are through with this reading.
