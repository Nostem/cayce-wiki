# TEXT OF READING 3789-1 F 7

**Reading [[3789-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/22/1926  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 11357

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by her father, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find those abnormal conditions as have to do with the normal physical development of this body, these conditions have to do with that of the prenatal conditions; yet these conditions may be assisted and brought nearer to the normal forces for the body, and the mentality of the body is at that change in physical development where advantage taken of this change at the present time would be better than DELAYED conditions, for the abnormalities may turn from the physical to the purely mental, with the changes as come about with the body attempting to bring about the equilibrium in physical force, so with the physical equilibrium brought about the mental equilibrium would be left, as it were, dormant.

4. The trouble, then, being improper coordination throughout the system, produced by undeveloped [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], especially those of the nature that have to do with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] called ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - the thyroid, the pineal, the endrin [adrenal]. These all show their lack of development.  The effects of these conditions are apparent in the physical and mental action of the body.

5. To meet the needs of these conditions at the present time will require care, attention, persistence, and consistent effort on the part of those in charge.

6. The body needs be given then, under the direction of a physician, those properties as are prepared from thyroid secretions, see?  for this will necessarily have to be changed as conditions develop, and as the normal forces exercise or exert themselves toward normal development - requiring, then, one that will also give, in the sleeping or restful state, those suggestions in that direct positive manner of the development necessary for the physical and mental being to be awakened to that innate effort of the innate being, see?  that is, in this manner:  At least once each day, for ten to fifteen minutes, as the body lies to rest, to sleep, the constant talking to the body, in that subdued yet positive manner, of [[Physical Body|the body physical]] building to meet the needs of the body in a physical and mental manner, for the perfect development of body mentally and physically, and add to this, whoever directs this - mother or father, or both - one one time, on the next - the consecration of self in [[Prayer|prayer]], that the power will be awakened in self to awaken this being in its best self.

7. During this whole period - that this is being given (these suggestions and the gland treatment), give the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, of correct adjustment and alignment of every vertebra, of every muscular tissue center in the body, should be correct in every sense.  That is, perfect alignment in the cerebro-spinal system - perfect relaxation of the body in every center from which the cerebro-spinal gives its nerve nutriment to the glands in the throat, in the base of the brain - FOLLOWING OUT this line to the coccyx even, and especially that stimulation in the lower dorsal and upper lumbar - to the lower centers, see?

8. After this has been given - [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments - for fifteen to twenty days, add with these other treatments (all kept up, see?) those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be produced on the body, and in the normalcy of the development, as are found in the Radio-Active Appliance, for this creates correct metabolism and correct coordination of the nerve system.

9. Do this, consistently, persistently, and we will find the body will develop to the normal forces, being backward only about one to two years - by the time it reaches fourteen to fifteen years old.

10. We are through for the present.
