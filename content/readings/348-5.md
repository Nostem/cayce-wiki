# TEXT OF READING 348-5 M 45

**Reading [[348-5]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/13/1929  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 2712

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [340].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Ill. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body [348] - this we have had before. The body shows considerable improvement from that condition as has existed with same, and needs only to keep up the eliminations in the proper direction as has been outlined; that is, keeping down the acidity - not becoming overbalanced in an opposite direction, to be sure, but the added forces as the system builds from the proper assimilation of the foods taken, and the reduction of irritation produced in the digestive system, aids the body in storing energy.  This is needed as much as any condition.  In the water, we would keep adding those properties of the elm, also of the other conditions, as has been given for the body.  The ELM should be taken at LEAST three or four times each day - just the pinch in the glass of water, stirred thoroughly.  The saffron once to twice each day, that the irritation may be kept down and PREVENTING any re-occurrence.  Well, too, [if there were] were small quantities of olive oil taken for the system to store energy for the whole intestinal tract.  Just the quantity as may be easily assimilated by the body.  The manipulations, as has been outlined, should be kept occasionally for the body, so that the ENERGIES as are exercised by the system - both as to the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] - may have the proper distribution of USED forces, as well as the proper building up of those replenishing elements that must take place in the physical functioning of a system. Then keeping these much in the way as has been outlined, altering only to meet the needs of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], we will find the conditions gradually - but consistently - on the improve.  Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
