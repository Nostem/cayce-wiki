# TEXT OF READING 4209-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4209-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/4/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11965

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4209], via Mr. William Iams.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are abnormal conditions that may be corrected.  These conditions have to do with various disturbances as have been created in the functioning of organs, caused by a condition existent in the blood supply, produced by poor eliminations, and assisted, or aiding in causing disturbance by a catarrhal condition as existed in portions of system, produced again by congestion in organs and drosses not removed from system.

4. These, then, have produced these conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [4209] we are speaking of.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this shows un-uniformity in its circulation - that is, in pressure, in respiration, and in its ability to find full coordination through the eliminating and assimilating system.  Hence the dullness, the tendency toward the condition as exists in extremities - swelling, perspiration, and such conditions.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find this produces pains along the centers, especially of the locomotories, these coming in the sciatic region - that is, along the lower portion of the lower limbs, upper fore portion of of the upper extremities. These conditions are reflexes, rather than specific conditions.  Hence medicinal properties taken in the system for these conditions only offer temporary relief, if relief at all.  Only sedatives, then, giving relief.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, the seat or the beginning of the trouble beginning with that change in the functioning of the organs through the pelvis region, and the cold, congestion, as was taken caused the distress in the lungs, in the stomach, and in the intestines, from cold, congestion, to the mucus producing portions of all of this portion of system.  A little irritation, a little fever occurred.  Hence congestion as was seen throughout the system.  The general health apparently good, yet the general debilitation showing how the inroads of this congestion internally, producing a catarrhal condition in the functioning of the organs, caused this distress in the body.

8. To meet the needs of this condition at the present time, we would use these two means to remove this condition.

9. First we would take in the system these properties:  Prepare this as the carrier:  To one-half (1/2) gallon of distilled water, add six (6) ounces of Wild Cherry Bark.  Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one-half (1/2) the quantity. Strain.  Then add six (6) ounces of sugar, first dissolved in four (4) ounces of hot water.  Reduce again to the original quantity, see?  that is, taking out the four (4) ounces of water added, by the simmering or slow boiling.  Then we would add:

Syrup of Sarsaparilla................1 ounce,
Fluid Extract Yellow Dock Root.....1/2 ounce,
Tincture of Stillingia.............1/2 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya....................1/2 ounce.

Cut two (2) drams Balsam of Tolu in two (2) ounces of pure grain alcohol.  Add this to solution.  The dose would be (shaking the solution before each dose is taken) one-half (1/2) teaspoonful three (3) times each day.

10. At the same time, and until the body is entirely relieved, use the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator across the lower portion of the lungs, or just above the solar plexus region - wearing this most of the time, except of evenings, see, when it may be laid aside - recharging same at least once each day in as strong light or sunlight as may be had, charging for at least twenty (20) to thirty (30) minutes each day.

11. Do this, and we will bring the better conditions and remove this condition from the body.

12. The medicinal properties acting as the eliminating centers need stimulation.  The assimilating centers need that action as will give rebuilding forces.  The [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] acting as the equalizer between the nerve system and equalizing the blood supply and cleansing same through the stimulated organs of elimination.

13. We are through for the present.
