# TEXT OF READING 2504-10 M 64

**Reading [[2504-10]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/7/1930  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9423

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Wigal.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2504] - this we have had before.

2. Now, we find the conditions will need measures that will remove the pressure as prevents the activity of the kidneys, and emptying of the bladder, on account of the enlarging or engorging of the prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]]; the necessity of the removing or catheterizing the conditions becomes necessary.

3. We would find that the bowels should be kept a little more active, and that by giving an enema - with small quantities of the suds, as have been used - castile suds - and following same we would make an injection of about a half a pint of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], with one-half dram of opiates in same, in the form of the relieving of this pressure.  Also we would find that, were this used also in bathing the parts over which the packs are put - and these packs should be kept up, not allowed to just ease - but use those of the laudanum over the portions, and then apply the saturated solution of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]]. This, as we find, would reduce the swelling, and enable the conditions to be reduced.  Unless this IS reduced, there will be the necessity for other measures.  DO this.

4. Ready for questions.

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Wigal
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
