# TEXT OF READING 953-16 M 53

**Reading [[953-16]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/6/1925  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 5869

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 6th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [953].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                            ... Street, 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.           [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding the physical condition and his personal business affairs.

2. EC:  Now, the general physical conditions as we find in the body are nearer to a normal condition than we have had here before.  While there are those conditions as may be brought to a condition of exaggeration of functioning of organs that have the inclination or incentive to over function, these, as we find, are nearer the normal functioning in their [[Vibration|vibrations]] at the present time than we have had before.

3. The medicinal properties as have been kept in the system from time to time, these may be reduced in their quantity and their frequency, though we would keep that of the properties of the ambrosia weed, with its solution, in the system more than the other properties.

4. The adjustments or the manipulations for the system, strengthening the muscular forces, the nerve supplying branches, that assist the system in functioning, may be given farther apart but not discontinued as a whole, for we find the system responds through the muscular and the nerve plexus to which the stimulus is directed by such manipulation and adjusting.

5. As to the diets, these, as we see, may be kept in accordance with the conditions of the body as it progresses from time to time, for the body is a very good dietician at present itself, see?

6. In the [[Vibration|vibrations]], we would set those as are found in the Radio-Active forces of Iron, with the Ice Water, as has been given, and from time to time this may be taken, whenever the body feels headaches or pains, or the tired, restless feelings.  These, as we find (the [[Vibration|vibrations]] we are speaking of), will assist the body in keeping the equilibrium throughout.  We would do that.

7. (Q)  To what quantity should the doses of medicine be reduced, and how often taken?
(A)  Half the quantity, or half the number of times taken, save of the ambrosia or ragweed.  This we would reduce, but about every third dose skip, see?

The adjustments may be taken once in two weeks, or twice a month, or such matter.  Let the body adjust same to its own feelings.

8. (Q)  Regarding business:  Should I continue, as at present, or should or will I change?  If so, when, and in what line should I go, if I should drop my present employment?
(A)  In this, as has been given for the body, there are many lines of endeavor in which the body would succeed, other than that of the present, see?  Any that has to do with the controlling of individuals, see?  Any manufacturing line, so that it would give the body outside employment, close to the ground, or close to nature, or such as would be of an entire nature of association with soil and earth.  Any of these may be, and would be, good for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

We do not see a change just at the present, though we see there are many possibilities for the body for changing from the present, see?  These, as we find, may lead the body, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], far afield from present endeavors.  The body will succeed in any of the lines as have been given for the body, either in study, either in control, either in directing operations in oils, either directing operations in research work, or directing operations in manufacturing of materials as prepared by research work.  Any of these, as we find, would be beneficial, physically, mentally and financially in the end, see?

Now, as regarding the associations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], this body, financially, with well as being drilled on the property of this body, in this we find that there will ultimately be the success for the operations here, either in the successful drilling into the production or of bringing production from those fields and that area close to same by the shot in the hole, see?  for, as we find, with this, these still show the area is in that of the producing stratum, and with the completion of these stratums [strata] there will be found the production.

As to the changes as come with these from time to time, this as we see at present is from that of the location of the present hole, for these strata enter in, in the manner of the anticlinal, and at a slope wherein the drilling operation in present hole is passing obliquely, at times vertically, through the stratum in which the area of production is; for we find, as given, this has passed through on two sides.  We would not relinquish operations on these until this was a successful producing well.  From the incomes as will be derived by these operations, in their eventual actions, we find the means of progressing in any line the entity may desire will be a reasonable action of same.  [See [4905] series.]

9. We are through.
