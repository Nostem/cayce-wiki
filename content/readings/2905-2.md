# TEXT OF READING 2905-2 F 12

**Reading [[2905-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/8/1943  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 10162

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs.  [2936], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2905].

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Thus far there has apparently been no improvement. Have the treatments by Dr. Coulter and Dr. Reilly been given correctly?
(A)  We would in the present leave off the treatments by Dr. Reilly and continue with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, for the time being or until the period when the body is to be in the open.  Some corrections have been made that will indicate helpfulness with the body.  The upsetting of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, however, through a part of the activities, has apparently prevented the improvements that should be. [3/9/43 Had [[Germany|German]] Measles and had to interrupt treatments temporarily.]  And with these in the present we would leave off the electrical forces.  For, these add to the stimulation which will be gained by the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultraviolet]] in the out of door activity for the body.

Continue the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, though, as just indicated, until time for the camp experience; then go into the camp life.

5. (Q)  Should I wear my glasses all the time as I have been doing?
(A)  Wear the glasses, especially when in the bright sunlight, or when reading by dim light.

6. (Q)  Should I continue using the same oculist, and shall I have my glasses changed now or wait until the end of the treatment?
(A)  Wait until the end of the treatments, or at least until returning from the camp.  If these treatments are carried out, we find it will not be necessary for much change, but these may be tested in the fall.

7. (Q)  How can I strengthen and control my nerves?
(A)  Through the corrections that should be made, that are being made, there will be given better resistances in the body.  Keep these up for the period indicated.

8. (Q)  Can anything further be done to improve my eyes?
(A)  The head and neck exercises are the best for the eyes in the present.

9. (Q)  Shall I have orthodonture work done now or in the fall?
(A)  In the fall - those corrections could be made the better.

10. (Q)  Any other advice for the body?
(A)  Keep more in the spiritual attitude of hopefulness in the body-mind and in the body-unfoldment in these directions.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother (two copies; one for Dr. Coulter)
"    " Ass'n file
