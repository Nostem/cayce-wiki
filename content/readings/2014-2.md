# TEXT OF READING 2014-2 M 19

**Reading [[2014-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/30/1939  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 8538

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Dr. W. D. Taylor.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., N.J.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, while there are not the outward [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of a great deal of improvement, there ARE changes being evinced, - through the physical reactions as well as at times a great torment in the mental reactions.

4. These indicate changes as may be wrought by the administering of those suggestions which have been indicated; that - as there is better coordination brought between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system - there will be a much nearer normal reaction.

5. For some of those conditions which cause anxiety and disturbance, - namely, the poor eliminations, - we find that more of the lesions and tensions in the right side should be broken up, just below the liver and gall duct area, tending towards the caecum, and to that as will stimulate greater peristaltic movements.

6. We find that the administering of [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil or Usoline, and the taking of Senna Tea each evening, may be expected to, and will, give a better evacuation and elimination.  Begin with about a teaspoonful of the Oil twice a day, - this may be gradually increased as is found necessary.  As for the Senna Tea, - put about six to eight Senna Leaves in a teacup of hot water and let steep for an hour or an hour and a half; then strain off and take the whole quantity.

7. To aid in stimulating the breaking up of the disturbances through this area indicated, as well as to aid in relaxing other portions of the body, we find that the application of the saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in Packs will be most beneficial.  These should be applied for at least twenty to thirty minutes, and then a full gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the area with [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]; then sponging off in lukewarm water.

8. The rest of the applications we would keep in much the same outline as we have indicated.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Should he be waked earlier in the morning, so as to make it easier for him to eliminate?
(A)  It would be more beneficial, if there is not too much of antagonism encountered by such.  It is better to have a regular routine for the body, in EVERY way.

11. (Q)  How can his coughing while eating be controlled?
(A)  This will necessarily have to be the result, or the effect of the other applications, and by suggestion.

12. (Q)  Would you advise scolding or hitting him, when he is so uncontrolable? or what method would you advise?
(A)  Patience, kindness, gentleness, ever; not in that of scolding or tormenting at all.  But in cajoling, and in kindness and in patience, these are the manners.

Remember, these conditions are for [[Purpose|purposes]].  While they become very trying to the individuals who attempt to administer to the needs of the body, know that these are purposeful in thine own experience also.

13. (Q)  Is this stubborn, fresh and disobedient attitude due to his ailment?
(A)  Due to the ailment; else there would be other measures indicated.  And in the building up of the body, there must be the response to kindness and gentleness and love, - more than to force, power, might, hate or scolding.

Do that.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Taylor) Copy to Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs.)
