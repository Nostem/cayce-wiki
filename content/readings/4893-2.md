# TEXT OF READING 4893-2 F 50

**Reading [[4893-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/28/1922  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 12909

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 28th day of December, 1922, in accordance with request made by [4893].

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:15 A. M.                             [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Alabama.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before. Rather serious conditions have arisen in this body; these caused more from the atmosphere and the reactions in the system from those properties that have been taken into the system, and with the application of the [[Vibration|vibration]], and with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] becoming congested has made the reaction to the locomotories and lymphatic circulation more tense.

2. Rest, heat, more [[Vibration|vibration]], with the massage over the body will be the best to give to this body at the present time. Electric blanket would be good for it to keep it wrapped in.

3. Give plenty of water into the system, so we will have a reaction to the emunctories and something to be acted upon. Do that.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Where can the electric blanket be obtained?
(A)  Here.

6. (Q)  What is the form of this electric blanket?
(A)  Use it as already formed - to the vibrations of the body.

7. (Q)  Shall she continue taking the medicine that has been prescribed before?
(A)  Take the gold, yes.

Be well to keep the intestinal tract so that it will assimilate - that is, keep it clean.  Do that through natural means, as the body is inert, rather than through medicinal properties taken to incite the gastric juices of the system.

8. (Q)  What natural means are to be used?
(A)  By the diet, and the enemas and vibration, rather than medicine, which would overtax portions.

9. Do as we have outlined.

> HOW LONG IS NOW?

> By Horton W. Amidon
> On December 28, 1922 a second reading was given to a fifty
> year old woman who was afflicted with arthritis.  From the
> section giving treatments, the following extract is taken:
> "...  Electric blanket would be good for it (the body) to
> keep it wrapped in."
> 
> No one present had ever heard of an electric blanket so the
> entranced Edgar Cayce was asked:
> 
> "(Q)  Where can the electric blanket be obtained?
> (A)  Here.
> 
> (Q)  What is the form of the electric blanket?
> (A)  Use it as already formed - to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the
> body."
> 
> This caused wonderment, especially when no electric blanket
> could be found.  Had the unconscious mind of Edgar Cayce
> goofed?
> 
> Following this reading (4893-2) a memo was inserted by [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]]
> Davis Turner on January 20, 1958.  We quote:  "On 12/26/57
> General Electric Company answered our inquiry re date
> electric blankets were put on market:  "...The first General
> Electric Automatic Blankets were sold commercially around
> the year 1936, and not quite as far back as the 1922 date
> mentioned in your letter, and you might be interested in
> knowing that the General Electric Company did pioneer this
> product..."  We repeat the question, Had Edgar Cayce goofed?
> This is a natural question for the conscious mind to raise
> since it is difficult to think in terms of the way the
> unconscious mind functions.  To give an analogy - some years
> ago a father went into the bathroom one night and on turning
> on the light discovered his blind son, who was thirty,
> shaving with a straight razor.  "Hey!" he exclaimed, "do
> you want to cut your blooming head off?"
> 
> In this incident where the unconscious mind of Edgar Cayce
> was giving information, we have a beautiful illustration of
> at least one aspect of the working of the unconscious mind.
> The people around Edgar Cayce were well aware of the fact
> that his unconscious mind had a wider perception of things
> that were happening in space than did their conscious minds.
> Also, that his unconscious mind could perceive all that was
> going on within the person for whom he was giving a reading
> was an evidence of another kind of expanded awareness.  But
> that the unconscious mind has greater awareness time-wise
> than conscious minds was perhaps not so readily understood.
> Who knew in 1922 that an electric blanket would be invented
> and on the market in a few years?  To the unconscious mind
> with its greater range of perceptiveness in time, a few years
> in the future like a few years in the past is looked upon as
> the here and now.  This understanding of the functioning of
> the unconscious mind clears up to a degree the mystery of
> precognition.
>

> R8. 3/7/80 GD's answer:  I'm sure that is absolutely correct!
