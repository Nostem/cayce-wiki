# TEXT OF READING 3679-1 F 56

**Reading [[3679-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/19/1944  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 11183

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3679], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:55 A.M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - it's cold!

3. Yes, we have the body here, [3679].

4. As we find, there are many conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] that are very good with this body.  Yet there are disturbances, which at times upset the whole physical organism.  These are physical expressions reflexing from a subluxation which has long existed in this body.  While this has been of a minor nature, there will be found a definite lesion between the 5th and 6th dorsal centers.  With this removed, and then measures taken to aid the liver and gall duct to react to the impulses of the circulation and the reflex action from cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerve system to the area from the ganglia at this center, we would find much better conditions for this body.

5. These, then, are the manners in which we would begin the applications:

6. First we would begin applying [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs for an hour and a half, twice each day, for at least three days in succession each week, over the liver and the gall duct area. Saturate three to four thicknesses of old flannel with the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] and apply to the liver and gall duct area, extending to the caecum area.  Apply it warm and then use an electric pad to retain the heat - just so it is not too uncomfortable.  Do this for three days - the same three days each week.

7. At the end of the three days, begin to take internally small doses of Olive Oil; about a teaspoonful three to four to five times each day.  For, if this is in the beginning causes belching or regurgitation reduce the quantity and keep on taking it, at least every other hour during the waking state.

8. The next week have the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs again, twice each day for the three days.

9. Keep up the Olive Oil until there have been thorough eliminations and until there are indications that there has been a draining of the gall duct area.

10. Then have at least eight to ten [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, with particular reference to the 5th and 6th dorsal centers, coordinating the 3rd cervical, 9th dorsal, and the sacral and lumbar axis.

11. Do these and we should bring better conditions for the body.

12. After eight to ten [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments have been made, then empty or drain the gall duct osteopathically.

13. In the matter of the diet - through the period that there is the draining, we would have particularly semi-solid or semi-liquid foods, preferably.  Then a great deal of raw vegetables, prepared in many different ways, oft prepared with gelatin.  Not too much meats, and no fats; not even butter during the first two to three series of these treatments.  Soups and broths, fish, fowl and lamb, but never any foods fried.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Has some minor dislocation in the spinal column gradually caused the auditory nerves to become paralyzed?
(A)  This is rather a prolapsus of the Eustachian tube, but with the corrections in the dorsal area, setting up better drainages in the gall duct and liver area, we should find these corrections aiding the disturbances in the ear.  It would be well, when the corrections or drainages are set up, that the Eustachian tube also be corrected osteopathically.

16. (Q)  What causes the very pronounced snap, or cracking noise at the base of skull whenever the head is turned to either the right or left?
(A)  This comes from the disturbance in the lower dorsal or at the 5th and 6th dorsal, which does not coordinate with the 3rd cervical centers.

17. (Q)  How can relief be obtained from the almost constant pain from one or two vertebra located between the shoulder blades?
(A)  By the corrections to set up drainages for the conditions that are causing neuritic reactions in nerves and muscles.

18. (Q)  Are nerves inflamed or bone infected in nasal region?
(A)  This is a sinus disorder from a catarrhal condition that causes the prolapsus.

Do these things and we will have better conditions for this body.  The head noises will be relieved and drainages will be set up in the liver and gall duct so that areas throughout the body will be improved.  That causing the gradual loss of hearing arises from a liver and gall duct disturbance.

19. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Loose-leaf book in Library for indexing and study regarding deafness

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Castor Oil Packs.)

>

> of questions, also give us the exact address at which you
> will be at the hour.
> 
> "Hoping then to be of a real help, I am,"
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce
> 
> B3. 2/4/44 [3679]'s letter to EC:  "As the date for my physical
> reading draws near, which is the morning of Feb. the 19th, I
> find myself seriously considering just how best to ask my
> questions in order to present each disorder that I so
> earnestly seek relief from.
> 
> "As stated in my first letter, deafness and the accompanying
> head noises are my chief affliction.  However there are some
> conditions existing which I believe should have prior
> consideration in the reading.
> 
> "Specialists have told me that the auditory nerves have
> gradually become paralyzed and this has caused me to wonder
> if some minor dislocation in the spinal column might not have
> caused this.  However, to my knowledge I have suffered no
> remembered injury of that nature, altho I have some trouble
> centered in the upper part of the spine.
> 
> "My first question would be:  1. What causes the very
> pronounced snap, or cracking noise at the base of the skull
> whenever the head is turned to either the right or the left?
> and what can be done to correct this condition?  2. How can
> relief be obtained from the almost constant pain I suffer
> from one or two vertebra located between the shoulder blades.
> 
> "Before asking the next question I should like to make this
> explanation.  For more than four years I have had a nagging
> pain in the bridge of the nose.  This pain resembles
> toothache so much that I have sacrificed many good teeth
> thinking the trouble was there.  This pain is always most
> severe upon awaking in the morning.  It extends up into the
> forehead and also around the inner side of the left eye.
> Specialists and x-ray fail to find anything that might cause
> it.
> 
> "The nasal passage - left side - is frequently entirely
> closed but there is little or no evidence of mucous.  It is
> more like a fluctuating swelling.  The sinus seem to be free
> from infection, and I wonder if it is possible that the
> nerves are inflamed or if the bone is infected.  This pain
> causes me to have a constant scowl which is most
> embarrassing.  My next question would be as follows:  3. What
> causes the pain in the left nasal region, and what treatment
> is suggested to correct this disorder?
> 
> "I experience great difficulty in remembering - especially
> what I read.  This means I lose many valuable things that
> should be held in memory.  The dimming of that faculty,
> together with the difficulty I experience in using one word
> when another one entirely is meant, may simply indicate
> 'age', altho I refuse to believe that.  I feel confident if I
> were relieved of the head noises and these few pains, I would
> be quite normal.
> 
> "My last question would be this:  4. What relief can you see
> for my deafness and the head noises?
> 
> "This seems to be a very big order, but I do not know how to
> cover the case in a briefer form.  I trust it is clear enough
> for your needs.
> 
> "I shall be here in my own room at home the morning of
> February 19th in [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]], and shall await with
> keen interest a report of the Reading.
> 
> "I do want to thank you for the various publications I have
> received, all of which I have greatly enjoyed reading.
> 
> "With very kindest regards, I am..."
