# TEXT OF READING 1563-2 F 54

**Reading [[1563-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/9/1938  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 7584

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1563], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1563] and Lucy Clifton.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [1563] this we have had before.

2. As we find, the general conditions show a great deal of improvement.  However, there are still times when the lack of the elimination of drosses from the system and they, the drosses, having been stirred up, tend to make for periods when there is some little dizziness; also the body becomes easily tired, and there is a general "gone" feeling - and more weakness then usual.

3. These conditions as we find necessitate, then, that there be rather the specific periods for the use of eliminants that would produce a greater activity through the gall duct and through the duodenum; arousing not only the liver activity but causing the activities from the pancreas, spleen and especially the gall duct to be more active.

4. We would continue with the adjustments occasionally.

5. We would find much improvement and it much better for the body to take the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] as indicated, when the body is ready to retire.  For the reactions upon the body of this being given with the periods of manipulation and adjustment DO NOT work as well as if they were separate - or given more often; not necessarily deep, but the activities of these would be more in keeping with that for helpful forces and would make the body rest much better.

6. For the eliminant, we would use Zilatone in periods of two or three days at the time; one tablet after the morning meal and two tablets after the evening meal - about thirty minutes after the meal; and at least a full glass of water taken after same.

7. Then keep the rest of the activities as to the diets, and as to the general applications in periods of a week to ten days of each; that is, of the manipulations AND the adjustments - and rest about twice as long between same.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What causes slight hemorrhage from womb region, evident for last four days?
(A)  As indicated, such effects are the attempts of the system to eliminate the excretory functionings of the bodily forces.  Hence the use of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] at periods when the body may rest from same will make for a much better change in the system; eliminating much of these disturbances and coordinating better with the activities of the system.  Also the stirring of the activities of the alimentary canal will relieve those tensions also.

10. (Q)  Has growth on neck of bladder healed properly?
(A)  This is healed so far as the general conditions are concerned, very well.  The activities of the eliminating system need to be kept more in accord or attune with the general activities of the body, to keep same from making either an adhering or so much accumulation as to cause this to re-open.

11. (Q)  Is there a cancerous condition in female organs or bladder?
(A)  No.

12. (Q)  Has diet been sufficient?
(A)  This should be kept in the order and in the ways as indicated.

13. (Q)  Why does periodic digestive trouble continue?
(A)  As indicated, there is the need for the arousing of the activities of the digestive forces as related to the liver, the pancreas and spleen - and especially the gall duct.

14. (Q)  If body continues acid what can I do to overcome it?
(A)  Make more and more the alkalin diets, or those activities in which there will be produced more of the eliminating of the causes; by the stirring to activity of the pancreas AND the spleen with the liver.

15. (Q)  Have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments been complete and given properly?
(A)  These have been very well, but as indicated it will be better if there will be the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] applied by SELF, rather than following the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

16. (Q)  How often should they be given?
(A)  As indicated, have a week when there would be two or three given; then rest at least two weeks before they would be given again.  These taken in periods in this way and manner will be more beneficial than a continuous reaction from same.

17. (Q)  Should the vaginal applicator of the violet ray be used?
(A)  We would not, in these conditions.

18. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  These we would keep, and if there is a continuation of the drainages from the womb or from the bladder, we would use a douche with a little [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in same - the proportions being, for this body, about two teaspoonsful to a quart and a half of water.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
