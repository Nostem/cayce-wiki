# TEXT OF READING 1826-4 F 48

**Reading [[1826-4]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/30/1939  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 8193

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there are a great many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body since last we had same here. Some have been for improvements, and some tend to arouse the effect of the poisons from the body itself to still produce temperature through the system.

4. Thus the allowing of the blood stream to remain in an inactive force weakens the body, and the resistances of same.

5. As we find, still there would be advised the use of the hot and cold applications each day until there is no more temperature, - but now applied more particularly over the areas from the lower part of the cervical to the dorsal and lower lumbar areas.  Of course, the packs would be first very hot, then very cold; and these left on - each one - about ten minutes; and kept up each day until at least six or eight of each have been applied.  Then afterwards (each day) we would have a gentle rubdown with an equal combination of Olive Oil and Peanut Oil, for the absorption of this would be advised for the superficial circulation.

6. Also - but not more than once a week - we would use the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray, - this broken, however, with the green light - or the heavy green glass between the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] AND the body.  This as we find would tend to make for purifications of the blood stream by the reaction of light thus broken on same.  This should be used for a minute or a minute and a half, and - or course - thirty-eight to forty-two (38" to 42") from the body; while the green glass should be about eighteen inches (18") from the body - in between the body AND the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]], see?

7. We would also use the enemas for the reduction of temperature as well as better purifying the system; but these should be carefully administered, - not too often but sufficiently often to keep the alimentary canal and especially the colon cleansed.  Use a mild alkalin antiseptic in the water injected.  As we find, the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] is the more preferable; the proportions being a tablespoonful to a quart and a half of water.

8. These as we find offer the better conditions for this body in the present, - [1826].

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What is the source of the poisons that cause the temperature?
(A)  Those infectious forces through the organs of the alimentary canal.

11. (Q)  What causes pains in upper part of the right thigh?
(A)  The pressures upon the nerve forces through the poisons from the colon area.  This is a reflex disturbance, and not a source of trouble.

Do these, as indicated, for the better conditions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Brother
"    " Ass'n file
