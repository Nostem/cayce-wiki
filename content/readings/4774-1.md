# TEXT OF READING 4774-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4774-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1910 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/7/1910  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12752

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 7th day of November, 1910.

[Edgar Cayce; Al C. Layne (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. ACL:  You have before you [4774] who is in the building here he is before you right now.

2. EC:  [4774], yes we have him here.

3. ALC:  Alright, now I want you to go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with him, if anything.  Well what do you find?

4. EC:  Trouble, more or less, through the whole system at times.  We have here that most of the trouble is from reflex action.  We find the trouble is here, we have trouble with the eyes, some with the nasal passages and a little inflammation from the larynx.  The trouble you see, is most from the nerve center, produces indigestion, produces a lack of nutriment from the system to throw off the supply from the pancrean juices here produces an action of tissue at other times.  Leaves into the system too much for the capillary and pains in the joints, through the shoulders, through the head, along the spine, through the hips, sick at his stomach, stretchy and achy.  That is from the condition we have of the indigestion or lack of nutriment to digest what is taken into it and arises as it were of the juices in the stomach and back into the throat, out through the passages - a belching up gases from it - it should pass off the other way.  A reflex from that is more or less of the inflammation through the pelvis, especially, through the kidneys, of the throwing off of this lack of gases.  A throwing of these gases into the stomach and lying there and fermenting in the intestinal tract and causes inflammation.  We have the effects from this into the facial nerve, around the eyes, around the chest, that is from the capillary circulation, you see.  The lying of tissue in the form of mucous is taken up by the lymphatics capillary system and eliminates the supply from the rest of the system all through it.  It does not produce so much pain in the head only at times it is very painful.  The eyes hurt, burn.

5. (Q)  What treatment would you suggest?
(A)  Take something into the stomach to sweeten it.  Magnesia and Calcium Chloride into the stomach itself, it will produce nausea.  Take enough of the Calcium Chloride with enough of the Magnesia to get the system to allow the secretion organs to secrete the mucous from the system as it were it dries up.

6. (Q)  Any other treatment you would suggest?
(A)  That will make him sick enough at times.  Remove the trouble from the system.  This reflex from this to capillary, to the lymphatics, to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] here, you see, through the throat, through the eyes around here.  These pains through the head, along the spine is caused from this action here, same thing we have here.  The reaction from the pelvis causes pains here in the hips, across second lumbar.  Feet ache, feel tired.

7. (Q)  How about the liver?
(A)  The secretion from the stomach here - the liver is one pole of the system of the body and the kidney is the other - inflammation in one produces an opposite action on the other. That is what we have here that produces this enlarging of the lobes here of the liver, a torpid condition of the liver, in taking up the secretion.  The kidneys excrete, the liver secretes and throws out to the digestion and the non-digestion of the food, produces the trouble we have in the liver.  That is, from the condition we have existing, is the trouble we have there.

8. (Q)  How about the vocal chords?  Have we trouble there?
(A)  The trouble in his vocal cords is from the same trouble we have here.  These forces in the solar plexus nerve centers from lack of the forces lack within the vocal cords within themselves - a lack of these forces to supply to the vocal cords themselves.

We have the sympathetic, we have the cerebro-spinal and then we have the nerves themselves or the vocal cords or the vocal box, right here in the throat; we also, have an expanding of the tissue here (or cartilage) which produces the sound as it is thrown from the lung and we then have the posterior muscles that govern the sound.  Then we have the exterior muscles here which throw or cause a drawing of this box, causes a straining of this from the lack of nutriment. Produces a straining of the vocal box and a reaction from these forces, a taking care until we produce a normal action of this force which will help these strains.

9. (Q)  Would you suggest any local treatment for this?
(A)  Reach these forces here and then we will have it in a better condition for local treatment.  Remove the condition and then we can remove the trouble, eliminate the effect. Then you will be in a better condition for local treatment.
