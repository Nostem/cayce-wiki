# TEXT OF READING 538-58 F 59

**Reading [[538-58]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/4/1939  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 3794

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of October, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [538].

2. As we find, there is a great deal of improvement in some directions; yet there is the tendency for the spreading of the infectious forces.

3. WE would - after four or five days - repeat the diet as was indicated.

4. And, over the areas that have been BETTERED, as WELL as about the edges to prevent spreading, we would use the [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]].  This, now, will tend to make for better conditions.  The too quickly drying effect of the [[Camphor|Camphor]], or [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]], would have driven this in, but - now that there is a bettered condition in the growth or the humor of the blood itself - this may be used with impunity.

5. Do that.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Should I still use the Resinol and D.D.D.?
(A)  Very good.

8. (Q)  Would it be better to use bananas or some other fruit for the diet?
(A)  Use the bananas and the buttermilk.  Then continue, of course, with a little of the buttermilk; and don't eat too heavily of ANY foods that are too acid.

9. (Q)  Should I continue the Yeast tablets during the three day diet?
(A)  If necessary for better eliminations, continue with same throughout the period of the diet.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
