# TEXT OF READING 5504-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[5504-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/19/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13742

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Pa. 
(Check Physical - questions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [5504].  This we have had before.  There is not a great deal of change since last we had same, save that of the conditions as have been given as the conception taking place.  There should be, for the proper conditions to be brought about in the system, the administration of those conditions as have been outlined for the body as respecting how the physical conditions might be brought into their proper relationship one with another in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, that THESE conditions might come about normally - else we may not expect for the full time, or for the proper developments for the mother and the offspring; for with physical defects there must come the continued dread, the continual ordeal of the FEAR - as brings the improper reflexes to all physical organisms of the system; while, with correction of these conditions through the manner as has been outlined - through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, or through the [[Massage Therapy|masseur]]'s manipulations for the physical conditions - these corrections may be brought about and the portion where there is dread and fear in the system will be carried then to its NORMAL expectancy, AND there would be brought that which the BODY DESIRES in its inner self for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in self and of the offspring.

2. Then, DO as has been outlined, and we will find the better conditions for the body, and WITH these there will become the reduction of those troublesome effects as have existed with the system.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

> I certainly trust things are going the nicest kind for you.
> I would like to see you and talk with you.
> 
> With kindest personal regards, and trusting that we may be of
> service to Miss Anderson, I am..."
> 
> R3. 1/12/31 Husband's letter:  "Some time ago the writer sent you
> a check for $10 and requested a reading on a Miss Anderson.
> You later advised me that you would prefer that Miss Anderson
> write in direct and request this reading.  However, in the
> meantime Miss Anderson has completely regained her health
> and does not see fit to join your Association.
> 
> I requested in my last letter that you return my check but to
> this date have not heard from you.
> 
> I trust your Hospital is coming along in good shape and
> that you had a very prosperous year.  I will also take this
> occasion to wish you every success in your venture for the
> year 1931."
> 
> R4. 1/21/31 EC's letter:  "In answer to your letter of the 12th,
> I am taking this matter up with the [[New York City|New York]] office and hope
> to be able to send you a check in the near future.  I am
> sorry there has been this delay, but I assure you it is
> unavoidable on my part.
> 
> Hoping that everything is going the very best for you, I
> am..."
> 
> R5. GD's note:  This was just prior to closing of the Cayce
> Hospital.  All funds which had been sent in for appointments

> R6. 1/28/31 EC's letter to [5504]'s husband:  "We are at last

> reading.
> 
> Should we ever be able to be of service to you or yours,
> please let us hear from you."
>
