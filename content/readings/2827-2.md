# TEXT OF READING 2827-2 M 62

**Reading [[2827-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/21/1942  Sex: M  Age: 62  ReadingID: 10028

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - [2827], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mrs. Melvern Gimbert.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [2827] at home, approximately 14 mi. from ..., N.C. (going on ... Rd. from ... to ... then left on ... Rd.), mail route R.F.D. #... You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are improved from those as we have had here before.

4. Those conditions that disturb in the eliminations, - we would change these somewhat.

5. We would use the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the abdominal area and the liver area, occasionally; that is, for at least an hour to two hours at the time, two or three days each week. Apply these warm, and keep warm for the hour or two hours at the time, see?  over the liver and abdominal area.

6. Following such periods of using the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, take internally the Senna Pod Tea; prepared by using about four or five Senna Pods in a cup of hot water and allowing it to steep for at least two hours - then drinking the whole cup of tea, see?   This would be taken each time after using the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.

7. If the eggnog disagrees, leave out the "nog" and use only milk and malted milk in same, or the yolk of an egg.

8. Do rub the back, especially, each day with Peanut Oil.

9. When it is at all practical, keep in the open air and in the sunshine; though keep the face or eyes shaded and do not be in too much draft.

10. These we would keep.

11. We would also keep the Brandy where evaporation may take place; that is, sufficient corking and sufficiently close to heat to cause evaporation, more than it has been heretofore.

12. The foods we would keep up, so long as they agree with the body.

13. A little exercise in the open will be well as the body feels capable of doing so.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What is causing pain around heart?
(A)  It is from overeating, or overfeeding at times, and lack of proper eliminations.

16. (Q)  Does the eggnog cause the pain in chest after taking it?
(A)  No; but if it disagrees with the body, and he doesn't want to take it, don't take it!

17. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do the things as we have indicated for the body.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Mrs. Gimbert) Copy to Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Castor Oil Packs.

The Senna Pods or Leaves may be secured from Peoples Drug Store in Norfolk.)
