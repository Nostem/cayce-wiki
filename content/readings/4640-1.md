# TEXT OF READING 4640-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4640-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/25/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12557

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - through Mr. Edwin D. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [4640].  Now, we find specific causes for differentiations, or those conditions as bring about unpleasantness in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and functioning of the body, as related to those conditions as exist in the FUNCTIONING of the system as related to the blood supply and its unbalanced condition, and as related to the nerve system - ESPECIALLY as to centers in the cerebro-spinal and their junction with those sympathetic nerve forces of the body. These affect, as we find, the distribution or eliminations in body, the causes being a combination of the influences coal tar and mercury derivatives have had upon the body.  The effect, as signify the causes, are through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - and their ability or non-ability to function, or to produce their proper derivatives for the sustenance of the physical rejuvenations in a nominal manner.

2. In meeting the needs of the conditions, necessary - in the one, that an antipathy for those conditions produced may be used in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of system - and in the other one, that as in sympathy - or synthetic [sympathetic] reaction - to the conditions produced in system.  Hence, we will find that those conditions as would bring about the nearer normal reaction will come through the functioning of the system as related to [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as may be received into the system through those centers in the body as regulate the central nervous system and circulatory system.  Through, then, the application of the battery forces that are CHARGED, and that will carry - in an anode - those of the silver and of the gold; using sufficient of the manipulations, OSTEOPATHICALLY given, as to produce proper distribution of the changed forces through the nerve systems and muscular forces, as will cause the proper equilibrium to be built or brought about in the body here, [4640].

3. In the matter of the diet - these, as we find, must be those that do not make for sugars, or too much of same; nor too much of alcoholic effect in the body THROUGH those activities as are created in the manner when fermentations are taking place in the body.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  How often should the battery be applied?
(A)  Put the body under those conditions where it may be applied at all, and these may be given!  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Wigal
