# TEXT OF READING 623-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[623-4]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/16/1938  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4258

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of November, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [623], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Here we find there has never been the full application of all those suggestions which were made some time back, in ELIMINATING the sources.  And NOW the effect of the toxic forces through the system is upon the extremities and especially in the joints and tendons.

4. As we find in the present:

5. We would take, periodically, the Eno Salt, - to act as an eliminant and as a purifier for the system.  This would not be such a drain upon the blood supply and the lymph system as to cause the body to lose TOO much weight, or as to be a great strain upon the body.  Take a heaping teaspoonful in a glass of water morning and evening for five days.  Leave off for three days.  Then repeat.  Leave off then for five days.  Then repeat again.  Leave off again for three days. Then repeat again, - and so on.

6. And be mindful especially of the diet, that fats or fried foods are not a part of the diet.

7. For as we find, if the body is consistent and persistent, we will eliminate the conditions.

8. Until these general conditions are set up to correct the effects, by the continued or consistent use of these properties as indicated for eliminants, we would apply the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs when there is pain in the joints, as the knees or the hips or the feet or the hands or the arms. These will eliminate the disturbance, and aid in setting up better lymph circulation.

9. But the needs are for the flow of the general eliminations and a purifying of the bodily force and functionings through the manners indicated.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What causes soreness and fullness in abdomen or colon?
(A)  As indicated, there is a dilation of the colon through the lack of the proper eliminations.

Even with the taking of the Eno Salt regularly, we find that the use of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations would be MOST helpful for the body.  But use a saline and [[Baking Soda|soda]] solution for such.  Have the water body-temperature; not hotter, not colder, and it will thus not become a strain upon the system.  The proportions would be a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and a heaping teaspoonful of salt to each quart of water used; thoroughly dissolved in the container of water used.

12. (Q)  I should lose weight.
(A)  Then, as indicated, do these, - and it will not overdo this, see?

Do these for this body, and we will bring better conditions in every way.

13. We are through for the present.

[During above rdg. EC dreamed of HLC getting fan letters on his "[[Initiation|Mysteries]] of the Mind" broadcast.  See detailed [[Dreams|dream]] under 294-189 Reports.]

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
