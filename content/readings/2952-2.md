# TEXT OF READING 2952-2 M 40

**Reading [[2952-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/28/1943  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 10220

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [2952], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               In his office, 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  [Apparently approaching the location of the body] "Yes - what a mess!"  [GD's note:  Mr. [2952] was a corset mf'r - any pertinence?]

3. We have the body here, [2952].

4. As we find, there are responses in the body.  Still, there is a great deal of aggravation from the nerve centers that apparently have not responded as yet - or their reactions, and the voluntary and involuntary connections especially in the nerves of face and neck.  But these have been a long time developing.

5. We would only add:

6. Take greater quantities of the B-1 Complex Vitamins, see? Take the B-1 Brand, and keep these; though not overdosing self.

7. Keep the system cleansed.

8. Do not add too much of electricity, but do give a very mild form of electrical treatment about every other period of the massage and [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment, see?

9. These, as we find, should bring better conditions.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What dosage of the Vitamins each day?
(A)  As indicated, B-1 Complex Brand.  This means one tablet; don't double it because it is helpful.  But take just one tablet each day.

12. (Q)  Have all former treatments been properly applied at Reilly's?
(A)  These are very good, but we wouldn't leave them off - rather make them a little bit farther apart, - keeping them up for the time being.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Reilly) Copy to Ass'n file
