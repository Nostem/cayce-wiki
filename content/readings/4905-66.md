# TEXT OF READING 4905-66

**Reading [[4905-66]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/11/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12991

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give us the condition of operations at the present time, telling how these operations may proceed to make a success of this well, giving anything of which the operators should be warned, and answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations here.  Now, we find these are only needing to be careful in the drilling of the last portions of this before entering the lime in which new hole is to be made.  There will be encountered some showings of the first portion of pump lost in hole, and these will cause some troubles in hanging of tools; though when this is reached use more of the acid and this may be taken from the hole without trouble.

3. In the new hole there will be found, when the bottom is reached (where the new hole begins), that the position is some different from that as was encountered before these troubles began.  There is some showing of the oil formation, and thirty-one (31) feet should carry through this lime, or to that break where the production will be encountered.  Then the taking of the production will only be necessary for the success of operations - that is, handling the conditions as is necessary under the pressure as will be encountered in this depth, the quantity and quality of the production, see?

4. Keep this, then, in the way as given, for success will crown the efforts of those that labor therein, even to that amount as has been given - fifteen hundred for each one dollar invested in these operations.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How far from where they are at present before they will encounter the pump?
(A)  They will encounter the pump in the next ten (10) feet.

6. (Q)  How much acid should be used?
(A)  That as has been used for these operations heretofore, see?

7. We are through with this reading.

> My dear Mr. Wyrick:-

> I didn't get this off quite as soon as I wanted to, but did

> though that this will help out.  It would seem that prospects
> are a little bit brighter than they have been.  I'm expecting
> to have real good news from you real soon.  Hope you'll let
> us hear from you whenever it's possible.
> 
> We are all getting along so-so, and keeping awfully busy.
> Hope this finds you feeling good and everybody happy.
> 
> With kindest personal regards from all,
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
> 
> R2. 12/14/26 EC's ltr. to HLC:  "...Morton sent me $100 -
> yesterday.
> 
> I don't think Morton means to make it hard, though Mother
> [GC] is very much of the opinion, I think that Morton got
> "miffed" at what I said about a letter I wrote, and is taking
> this manner of showing me that he's going to run it or not
> play at all.  While I don't feel that way - Mother may be
> right.  Anyway, it makes it rather hard for a holiday season.
> But we'll manage along some way until this well comes in.
> 
> Mr. Wyrick has gone to [[Texas|Texas]].  Things are progressing and
> might come off before Christmas, if the reading can be relied
> upon.  Had a wire from the people in Fla. wanting us to come
> down right away.  I haven't answered it but will by letter.
> Of course we couldn't do such a thing right now.  They claim
> they didn't locate the treasure they were looking for.  I
> also had another letter from a man in Fla., wanting me to
> come right away.  He proposed to pay all expenses - wanted me
> to locate some oil.  He gave me rather a full outlook of the
> oil situation.  It looks good to me, though you can very well
> imagine what Mama and mother had to say when the fellow
> enclosed a copy of a letter that he wrote to A. D. Noe -
> insisting that Noe get in communication with me at once and
> bring me to Fla. with him.  I haven't heard from Noe as yet -
> possibly won't.  But it makes the outlook for the oil in Fla.
> look good.  This man claims one well a day or 2 ago - blew
> the crown block off the top of the rig..."
>
