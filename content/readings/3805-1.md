# TEXT OF READING 3805-1 M 15

**Reading [[3805-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/24/1944  Sex: M  Age: 15  ReadingID: 11379

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

In the office 
Time of Reading                               of ... Co., 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., S.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3805].

2. As we find, there are disturbing conditions which prevent the better physical functioning of the body.  These conditions are somewhat improved in the present.  The characters of administrations for the control of the suggestive forces, or mental and spiritual attributes of the body, are well.  These we would continue.

3. There are in the physical or pathological conditions, however the sources of the troubles.  In the cerebrospinal system, through the lumbar and the lower dorsal area, there have been those hindrances in times back - by lack of activity of the lymph, blood and nerve supply - that have caused plethoric conditions in colon, thus engorging same.  The fecal forces have remained so long as to cause the activities to become nil, or to cause the lack of control with the removal of the disturbing conditions.

4. These, then, as we find, should be the characters of administrations in the present, combined regularly with the suggestive and spiritual directions for the body in its control of its activities among peoples, among associates, as well as for physical conditions:

5. We would continue the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.  These should be taken regularly, two each week, for at least sixteen treatments; with special reference to the lumbar-sacral and throughout the dorsal areas or those centers that control not only the activities of the colon in the caecum and transverse area but the nerves and muscles that control the sphincter centers in the lower portion of the descending colon.

6. Occasionally cleanse the colon so that there may be, with the change of the circulation of nerve and blood supply, a replenishing and rebuilding of the nerve and muscular forces controlling the activities of the colon, the sphincter muscles of the anus and through the lower portion of colon.

7. When the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations are given, the first solution injected should contain a heaping teaspoonful of table salt and a heaping teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to the gallon of water body temperature.

8. In the last rinse water used, do put at least a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to each gallon of water body temperature.

9. Do these, and then let the body live normally in its associations and activities, and we will build a normal reaction through the body-forces.

10. As to the frequency of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, these should be given whenever there is a feeling of fullness in colon or a lack of proper nutrition; or once every two weeks or twice a month.

11. In the first series of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, keep these twice each week, regularly for at least sixteen treatments. Leave off then for a period of three weeks to a month and then have another series.

12. Thus we will make better conditions for the body.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under Case number for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 7/3/43 Letter from mother:  "He has suffered for many years
> from an enlarged colon, with other possible complications,
> which cause him to behave as if he had no control over his
> bowel movements.  When he was 9 years old his father & I
> discovered his condition, but doctors assured us he would
> outgrow it.  This has not been true and now, in addition, he
> has developed what might be termed a 'shame complex' if there
> be such a thing.  In other words he is ashamed of his habit
> and has withdrawn within himself.  Last year I took him to
> Johns Hopkins where they took about 7 lbs. of fecal matter
> from his colon which had become impacted there.  Now he is at
> home where a local doctor is doing the same thing for him.
> I cannot and will not believe that there is not some basic
> cause for this trouble which, once discovered, cannot be
> removed."
> 
> B2. 8/5/43 Letter from mother of [3805] to EC:  "I do wish you to
> keep March 24th for me.  It is very disappointing that it
> will take such a long time, as I consider the condition
> serious in the life of a boy of 15.  A major operation has
> been suggested but we are going to avoid it if possible on
> account of the shock, as he is already suffering from a
> handicap which is disturbing enough.
> 
> "I would appreciate it exceedingly if the reading could be
> given sooner.  In the meantime I shall thank God for your
> gift."
> 
> B3. 3/8/44 Letter from [3805]'s mother to EC:  "I recently wrote
> to you concerning the rdg. for my son scheduled for the
> morning of March 24, and asking for advice on the type of
> reading, also for two subsequent dates for rdgs.  I trust
> that I will get an answer soon.
> 
> "I have 7 persons prepared to pray and [[Meditation|meditate]] during the
> hour, so as to aid you as much as possible."
