# TEXT OF READING 5286-1 F 29

**Reading [[5286-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/29/1944  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 13422

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5286], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by an Alice Bailey Group.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, those desires and hopes of the body.  These, we find, are very well, provided the body will maintain a consistent attitude towards the principles and activity of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-faith in the concrete and not abstract.

3. As for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], the weakness in the nerve tensions through the body has come from periods when there has been opening of centers of the body without direction to the use of the energies that have been and are created in and through the kundaline forces as they act along the spine.

4. Thus, we would give the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments once each week and each time use the radio-short-wave treatment to stimulate the better connections between sympathetic and cerebrospinal nervous systems, especially in the areas of the brachial centers.  [[Hydrotherapy|Hydrotherapy]] should include the [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]], the Bubble Bath and a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with equal portions of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil.

5. These do, and keep the balance in the diets so as to supply energies to the nerve forces of the body.

6. Not too much of starches nor too much of sweets, lest there be a further exaggeration of the activities of the pancreas and spleen upon the digestive forces of the body.

7. These, as we find, if they are adhered to, will bring better conditions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What should be used in the Fume Bath?
(A)  Two teaspoonsful of witchhazel to a quart of water.  Not much of the dry heat, practically all of this should be of the fumes.

10. (Q)  Am I able to manage the care of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] by right mental polarization and by the decentralization and forgetfulness of the little self?
(A)  This may be better done when there are better coordinations between sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems. Don't overtax the imaginative body to the detriment of the physical being.

11. (Q)  Am I making any real progress in my effort to achieve such decentralization?  ["I am trying to focus on the Plan and not on my reactions to it."]
(A)  As has been just indicated, there is progress made but at times to the detriment of the nervous forces.  We would, then, correct the physical being and the mental and spiritual forces may manifest the better.

12. (Q)  Am I right in supposing that I shall have a slight usefulness as an occult student and future interpreter of spiritual values?
(A)  This may be possible, it is practical in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], provided there are correct values given in thy study and in thy judgments.

Doing these, we will make the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and the body the better channel for manifestation of forces within as well as without self.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two Copies to self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, RCA Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|New York City]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
