# TEXT OF READING 369-8 F 26

**Reading [[369-8]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/3/1931  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 2849

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 3rd day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss, [369].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [369], Ida West, Sarah Hesson, Edna Mae Hewett, Annie, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - we had it under a different name before!  [GC used [369]'s nickname instead of full given name as had been used previously.]

2. In many respects we find conditions are well, though there are irritations in the duodenum and in a portion of the intestinal system.  There are those conditions there that tend to make for dizziness, sick headaches, pains in the eyes at times, conditions through the intestinal system as of gas - as of irritations.  These, as we find, come from those irritations as exist in the 6th, 7th and 8th dorsal plexus.  There has been some impingement there.  With a good cathartic, and about six to eight thorough manipulations osteopathically, we will have conditions eliminated.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Have my eyes responded to the eye operation?
(A)  The conditions in the nerve system have not been to the best for the conditions in the eyes, in the present.  With the proper manipulations given over the nervous system, we will find a better response in the eye operation.

With those that would be used for the cathartic - These we would NOT take until at least two manipulations or treatments had been given, see?  Then we would take as this; this as the dose:

Podophyllum.............1/4 grain,
Leptandrin..............1/2 grain,
Sanguinaria.............1/2 grain,
Cascara Sagrada.........1/4 grain.

Let one day be between each dose, taking three as a series - see?

Drink PLENTY of water during the time this is being taken.

4. (Q)  What causes my stomach to be so distended?
(A)  Gas!

5. (Q)  What can I do to reduce my stomach?
(A)  Take the cathartic and it'll reduce it alright!

6. (Q)  What causes sores in mouth and under tongue from time to time?
(A)  This is as the indication from the irritation in the duodenum and in the stomach itself, see - this from acid in system.  After these have been taken, the cathartics, it would be well that occasionally there be taken that of Milk of Magnesia, Milk of Bismuth, alternately - a teaspoonful as a dose, of evenings.

As to the matter of diets, beware of those that are too acid-producing for the system.  Not too much meat, none of sea foods, and little of those that are too heavy in system - see?

7. (Q)  What caused recent pains in lower portion of back?
(A)  Strain on the system from those adjustments needed in 5th, 6th and 7th DORSAL area.  These are opposite from where these would be, because these in the back were in the lower dorsal - 12th, and 3rd and 4th lumbar.  These are reflexes, the attempt of the hepatic circulation and lower portion of body to ADJUST itself to the strain.

8. (Q)  Could Dr. Crews give the manipulations alright for this body?  [Gena L. Crews, D.O.]
(A)  With the conditions as exist for the eyes, we would have rather one that gives more a general treatment and not specific.  Dr. Crews may give the treatment CORRECTLY for a GENERAL, but as for specific that would be most helpful for the eyes - and for the adjustment of the conditions with that as has been attempted in the OPERATIVE forces in same, one that would be more specific would be better.

9. (Q)  Dr. Richardson?  [Martyn L. Richardson, D.O.]
(A)  Dr. Richardson would be excellent!

10. We are through with this reading.
