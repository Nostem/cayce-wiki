# TEXT OF READING 5741-12

**Reading [[5741-12]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/28/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 14212

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of July, 1927, in accordance with request made by Mr. [943].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] that has been drilled on Section 19, Township 54, Range 35, Dade County, [[Florida|Florida]], and you will please advise us if it is possible to plug this well and follow instructions as given sometime ago.  Give information in detail as to what is necessary to do to obtain results.

2. EC:  This we have had here before.

3. Now, we find operations thus far have proved as has been given regarding the obtaining of production - the production, as given, in small quantities - yet paying quantities - would and could be obtained from these operations, were instructions as have been given - as MAY be given - followed closely; yet we find there are those circumstances, those conditions surrounding the operations that would even make THIS hard to be carried out in a proper way and manner - for we find there have arisen many of those conditions that bring contrary-wise activity by individuals, and the desire to destroy rather than that someone else might obtain any benefit from any operations.  These, however, would be the ways and manners to obtain production here:

4. First there will be found the necessity to raise casing to at least twenty-two hundred (2200) feet, see?  This the depth from the top.  Plug hole below this, see?  Then raise casing again, after hole is plugged, to seventeen ninety-nine (1799), see?  Then REAM from this to the twenty-two twenty-five (2225) feet.  Here we will find production will be encountered, where showings were seen, from eighteen seventy-five to twenty-two twenty-five (1875 to 2225), this giving sufficient of the stratum in which the production will be obtained.  Necessary - if water gives obstruction or trouble here - that packers be used on casings when suction or pumps are used to start the flow.

5. Do that, for the production to be obtained - yet be careful of the information as is given, for there are those that would hinder such operations being carried out.

6. We are through for the present.

> Dear Mr. Cayce:

> you concerning the above question."
>

> Southern peninsula of Florida.
> 
> No. 2. Did the mid-continent plunges cause the Florida uplift
> and does the oil migrate into the State on these plunges and
> did the Ouachita plunge and the Red River fault have anything
> to do with oil migration into the State of Florida.
> 
> No. 3. What caused Lake Okeechobee and reef on southern part
> of lake and the many lakes thorough the central part of the
> State.
> 
> No. 4. What caused the Caloosahatchee river and the New river
> fault.
> 
> No. 5. What caused the many islands and reefs on the Eastern
> side of the southern end of the Florida peninsula and the
> many breaks and waterways inland on the western part of the
> southern end of Florida peninsula.
> 
> No. 6. Does and oil and gas structure exist in and near the
> city of Miami, Florida, and if so, give cause of the
> formation and if possible a location to drill for oil or gas.
> 
> No. 7. Is Biscayne Bay and Miami River a fault.
> 
> No. 8. Does oil exist below a depth of 4000 feet and if so
> give in detail where the big sand or limestone formation is
> located with reference to section, range and township and
> amount of production.
> 
> Mr. Cayce, if we can work something out of this will protect
> you as far as an interest is concerned in the sale of the
> book.  I believe that we can do some good for the State and
> for ourselves at the same time.  Please advise me just what
> you think of the proposition and if you think well of it
> might give it a try at one or two readings.  We must locate
> the oil pools in the State and get busy on our world
> proposition for that means more to us than all the money in
> the world and particularly to the people of the world.
> 
> With kindest personal regards to you and family and Miss
> Davis.
> 
> Sincerely yours, [943]
