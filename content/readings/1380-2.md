# TEXT OF READING 1380-2 M 28 (Head of Manufacturing Dept.,

**Reading [[1380-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/12/1937  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 7089

Hebrew) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of October, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1380], new Associate Sponsored Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by:  Dr. [[Paul (Apostle)|Saul]] B. Sells, Dr. Gardener Murphy and Dr. Lucien H. Warner, of the Department of Psychology, Columbia University, [[New York City|New York City]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born July 8, 1910 in [[New York City|New York City]]. 
Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 (?) A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  (At beginning when reaching birth date at 1910, said "Oh-o-o-uuu!" as in a voice of horror or dread.)  Yes, yes - [1380] - Yes, yes - Not now - Not now -

2. We are through.
