# TEXT OF READING 4050-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4050-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/11/1944  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11744

This psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [317], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

About a mile from 
Time of Reading                             the city limits, 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M.               southwest of 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  These as we find are very serious disturbances. There are segregations and inflammations, as is understood, of that nature such that there is the need for stimulating the activity of the central nervous system and blood supply, so that there may be a destruction of cellular forces that are as ravages upon the vitality of the body, and thus the building of resistance in lungs, heart, liver, kidneys.

3. As for the desire to maintain the activities of body, mind and [[Soul|soul]], these have run their course well.  The administrations that are being made with the x-ray are very good.  It would be found that if there would be administered before the x-ray treatments are given, whenever they are given, one-eighth grain of Animated Ash, these would be more beneficial.

4. Keep the body quiet.  Keep the body-building forces for the system.  Keep good eliminations.

5. These offer the better conditions for the body.  We wouldn't change anything other than to add the Animated Ash at least not longer nor not shorter than fifteen to twenty minutes before the x-rays are given.  Put the dry ash on tongue and take a drink of water.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What can be done to relieve the pain?
(A)  As has been indicated, the activities of the Animated Ash in the system will do more to relieve the pain unless the cobra venom is administered hypodermically, though with the age and the low resistance, it would be mighty hard to adapt this body to the use of cobra venom.

8. (Q)  Is there anything better than the codeine for sleep?
(A)  Nothing better that is not too much habit-forming and that doesn't require the large quantities to be taken more often.  Do keep the stimulation of eliminations.  These will be better for the body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Son
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(4/11/44 GD's note:  See explanatory article [which was enclosed] regarding Animated Ash.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 3/30/44 Son [317]'s letter:  "Last spring and again last
> summer Dr. Putney, of Staunton, operated on Dad's face
> for skin cancer.  The [[Healing|healing]] has never been complete.  We
> believe that if Dr. Flanagan, of Richmond, had not started
> X-ray treatments about the first of this year that the
> continued hemorrhages would have proved fatal.  Some
> improvement has been made since (we are planning to go to
> Richmond April 1st for the last treatment, we hope), but
> the condition could be better.
> 
> Here are some specific questions:
> 
> Should the X-ray treatments be continued?
> What other treatment is advised?
> Is the diet right?
> What can be done to relieve the pain?  Is there anything
> better than the codeine for sleep?
> Any other advice?
> 
> Some of these questions may seem to overlap, so I will leave
> it to you good people to use them as you see fit.  Please let
> me [know] if there is further information necessary.
> 
> My sincere thanks for your kindness."
