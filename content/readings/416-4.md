# TEXT OF READING 416-4 M 28

**Reading [[416-4]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/12/1934  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 3131

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 12th day of July, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [416], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [416]; this we have had before.

2. Considerable improvement is indicated in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  While there are still some weaknesses or tendencies in the activity of the nervous system as related to those pressures in the throat and the activity between the cerebrospinal system and the sensory system, these now need rather a period of rest for recuperative forces.  Then later, if it becomes necessary - through the burning of the eyes an fullness of the throat - about a third of the dose of the treatments, by manipulations and effective active forces in the body, may be repeated.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Any further treatments suggested to keep the system in a good condition?
(A)  Not in the present, save in keeping eliminations near normal and in keeping the activities such as not to STRAIN - of course - the throat or the eyes or any of the sensory system, you see.

5. (Q)  When should the change in glasses be made?
(A)  After the second period of treatments - if it is to be had.

6. (Q)  Then how long after wearing the new glasses before they should be discarded entirely?
(A)  In two to three months.

7. (Q)  Any further advice for my better general welfare?
(A)  Be consistent in all the activities.  Do not overstrain, or try to test to see what might come; but rather live the normal life, not necessarily the dilatory - but the normal life.

And we will find that the individual who takes rather the course wherein the general activities are to keep the body mentally, physically and spiritually well-balanced, is the individual who brings the greater results in the experience of each body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two Copies - 1 for Dr. Richardson Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
