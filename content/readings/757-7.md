# TEXT OF READING 757-7 F 46

**Reading [[757-7]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/20/1934  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 4615

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of December, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [757], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Calif. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [757]; this we have had before.

2. We find conditions are very good, considering all those reactions, activities and influences that have been effective and active in the body.

3. As we find, if there will be taken those precautions that have been given - and that are portions of the body's experience (referring to, or resorting to the applications of the light, the various elements that have been given from time to time), we will keep the resistances such that we will bring the better and better conditions for this body.

4. For the rash that appears on body, on face at times, and especially for the deep headaches that arise at times from the general depletion of the strength and vitality, we would use a compound to be massaged into the portions where the irritation is shown - and at the base of the brain to the lower cervicals.  Mix THOROUGHLY together one ounce of Iodex with ten grains of Animated Ash.  Very gently and very thinly massage same into or on the portions of the body indicated. This will materially aid the body.

5. Well that there be the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations occasionally to keep the drosses and toxic forces from affecting the body in its disturbed circulation at times.

6. Do these, and - as we find - we will have the better conditions for this body, [757].

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Would the pain and soreness in lip clear up?
(A)  As indicated, if these properties are used about such portions of the body.

9. We are through with this reading.
