# TEXT OF READING 137-72 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-72]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 462

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [137-71] given on April 11, 1926, on [137], and you will answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given here.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  As given, Atlantic Coast Line will rally a little bit, then deflate greatly to 151.  Is this rally now taking place - the advance yesterday?  Will it continue to advance today? How high in price will it go?  How long will the rally continue?  When will the deflation in this stock occur - the big deflation?  How will this downward movement of Atlantic Coast Line stock affect other stocks, as Hudson Motors? Will they be affected and go down with it - Hudson Motors?
(A)  In this, as there has been given, there are many conditions regarding same to be considered, for, as seen, rails will be attacked, especially, on the next deflation, or the next attempt to put stocks down, see?  Atlantic Coast Line may NOT be affected first, and it MAY be affected most, beginning about Thursday, this week, see? or, WITH the change in conditions in various portions of the country, the various reports as regarding steel, various reports as regarding the combinations, may bring the large boost, and beginning from the present advance until the May deflation period.  The Motors will be affected, not so much by Atlantic Coast Line's variation, but as to the general conditions, and we will find a change coming at the present time, an advancing, with Thursday and Friday - some either - high or low conditions appearing again, see?

As to Allied Chemical, as we find, the change is to appear Thursday, especially.  All, as we see, are on the advance, yet Thursday or Friday may see an attack and a slow trading for the week end period, see?  The other rails all coming much as has been given, for, as given, we would find, after the 12th, a turning period, see?  As to what the advance will be in Atlantic Coast Line, we do not find this going very much higher than at the present quotations now, yet there may be some smaller advances, but greater deflations.  These should be gained through these sources of the individuals as conditions present themselves from time to time, see?

Hudson Motors, as we find, will be attacked again, even in today's trading; yet we find from the 64 price an ADVANCE until at least ten to fifteen points are gained, before a MATERIAL decline, and should steel reports, and those combinations as are being made with this special stock, be perfected, we will find an advance immediately, in the next week, to the 106.  All industrials have been on the advance. All industrials will be attacked again by the selling in the latter portion of this week, see?  Rails - not so much attacked as the others.

These informations concerning these should be gained more from the individuals or specific stocks given, and their information taken, see?  than as to who is handling, and how, and when, and what will be done with same.  Not approaching these from the right channels here at present.
