# TEXT OF READING 4388-1 F 75

**Reading [[4388-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/18/1932  Sex: F  Age: 75  ReadingID: 12227

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 18th day of October, 1932 in accordance with request made by Dr. A. J. Foerter, Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, H. L. and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  This, as is applied to that attendant with this body.

2. Those stimuli that have been given for the internal applications for this body are well.  Those mental aptitudes and attitudes as have been suggested that the body should maintain at all times will be of the greater strength and aid to sustaining the body, for its own conditions as well as assisting the body in meeting those needs in the body it ministers to at this time.

3. The body would find that a great deal of aid would come in bathing the lower limbs each evening in very warm water, and massaging them from the knees down with this preparation as put together in this order and manner:

To 2 ounces of Russian White Oil, add:

Olive oil ...............1/4 ounce,
Rub Alcohol................2 ounces,
Witchhazel...............1/2 ounce.

Shake this together and massage sufficient that will be taken up by the absorption that comes from the opening of the pores of the lower limbs and feet by the heat of the water as applied.

4. It would be well that at times there be a quantity (whatever may be well dissolved) of heavy coarse salt in the warm water used for the bathing of the limbs; then repeat the other as it is necessary.

5. Ready for questions.

6. That is all regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Foerter
