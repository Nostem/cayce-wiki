# TEXT OF READING 341-25 M 20

**Reading [[341-25]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/8/1927  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 2664

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by his mother, and self.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. [341].]

Time of Reading 
11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of this body, and will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now we find there are some exaggerations of those weaknesses as have been given for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, regarding congestion for the system and the effect same produces on the organs of the body.  The dullness in reaction, the congestion from cold - these all are, as seen, the effects of that bacilli as would be called malarial condition, produced by the non-eliminations of the body.

4. Well, then, that the liver be stirred sufficient to have full activity, and that the spleen respond to these actions.

5. With those properties, then, as have been taken in the system, we would follow with at least two (2) tablespoonsful of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]].  Then flush the system.  Do not attempt to flush the system until the activity of the body has been renewed through this exercise of the excretory system, by the attempt to meet the needs of the acid produced by [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]]. Flushing may be assisted by enemas, to prevent nausea and headache - but renew with this same, those conditions given in Asaphoetida [Asafetida] pills and the Aspirin.  This - though body needs rest, physically and mentally, until the body is flushed well, for the reaction to the system is not good, with the streptococci bacilli showing in the blood and in lung.

6. (Q)  What should be taken to flush the system after the oil has been taken, and how long after the oil has been taken?
(A)  Take the oil to-day.  Flush the system in morning with those properties of the salts, pluto, honeyida water [Hunyadi Water], or phosphates.

7. (Q)  How soon should the glasses be changed?  or is it necessary to change same?  and are the eyes improved?
(A)  Eyes are improved.  Change the glasses by the 1st to 10th of November, THIS year.  Keep glasses cleaner will be better for the eyes too!

8. We are through with this reading for the present.
