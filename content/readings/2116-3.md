# TEXT OF READING 2116-3 F 41

**Reading [[2116-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/6/1940  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 8770

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of July, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2116], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are very much improved from that as we have had here before.

4. All of the poisons from the toxic forces of the body have not been entirely removed, yet these show a great deal of improvement in this direction.

5. We would continue with those applications suggested, as to the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and [[Massage Therapy|massage]]; and at least one or more of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.  There is still indicated some toxic forces and mucous through the colon.  The irrigations need not be given oftener than to remove this condition.

6. As we find, the general treatments as well as the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] may gradually be taken farther and farther apart.  The [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should not be oftener than six weeks apart, and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and a general treatment once in ten days or the like should be sufficient.

7. This with the general reaction by the general activity, - that is, the exercises of not too strenuous a nature at the time of the treatments, - will also be well.

8. Keep the diets much in the same line as has been indicated.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Shall I continue with the inhalant as prescribed in my first Reading?
(A)  If there is the least disturbance in the nasal passages or the soft tissue, it would be well to continue this when needed.  It will also keep down those tendencies to be affected by the dust, as well as the pollen of certain weeds or flowers that cause disturbance.

11. (Q)  Shall I continue with taking the [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] with Iron?
(A)  This, too, will depend upon the general appetite and strength.  It may be left off for the time, and then begun again if found necessary.

12. (Q)  Is there anything else I should do or not do?
(A)  As has been indicated, do these things; which have been removing the disturbances and will aid in creating the better balance in the system.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of further suggestions [which was enclosed] for Reilly.)
