# TEXT OF READING 4823-1 M 12

**Reading [[4823-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/21/1932  Sex: M  Age: 12  ReadingID: 12812

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of June, 1932, in accordance with request made by his father, Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[King David|David]] Levy.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            Brooklyn, N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, there are abnormal conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These, in the manner, while the body is yet developing, are of comparatively long standing as to causes of same.  In times back there was an injury to the body that made for later developments of pressures that have hindered in the coordination of physical energies, as exercised by organs of the system.  Hence those recurrent conditions that make for spasmodic conditions of the nature that make repressions in the system.  In the activity of same, these produce a reaction in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as affect the assimilations.  Hence there are hardened conditions produced in the sympathetic cardiac plexus, or secondary, also in the region about those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] where digestion takes place in the system.  These affecting the respiratory system makes for the reducing of the blood supply, under the strain that has existed, as well as making a form of condition in the system of the nature that prevents normal developments.

2. In those of pressures for the digestive system, these make for those reactions from those pressures in the sympathetic system, as produce those reflex spasmodics that cause the congestions to be reactive in the system.

3. In meeting, then, those conditions, as we find, while these may be aided, this will require some systematic consideration of both repressions that are produced in the body and those changes for the general physical conditions that may arise in the varied conditions as they appear from time to time.

4. In the beginning, then, we would add first as these:

5. Use the Radio-Active Appliance, the applications being made in this manner:  Both plates or anodes should be of the size at least as large as a dollar, or larger on the one that is attached over the lacteal area, or that just below the liver and the end of the umbilicii, toward the right and toward the lower portion of the duodenum.  The one attached to the secondary cardiac plexus, or the 3rd and 4th dorsal plexus, would be attached first.  Then the one over the other area the last.  This should be given at least thirty to sixty minutes each evening as the body is to rest.

6. Should this produce nausea, or an over reaction in either the jerking or spasmodic conditions, either from those that affect the lung or the brain's reaction through the sympathetic system, these would be reduced in period - but continued in their activity.  Be sure that it does not short circuit by filling too much of the water in the container in which the appliance is placed.

7. In the matter of the diet during this period, we would make it that most easily digested by the system, and that is blood and nerve building; or, as in this:

8. Mornings - citrous fruits and nuts.

9. Noons - fruit juices that are seasonable, followed with arrowroot or junket, or the like.

10. Evenings - rather the malted milk, dry milk, and the meat juices.  These taken in small quantities and more often are better than large quantities.

11. Do this for thirty-six days, and then we would give further instructions.

12. This, as we find, will require time, patience, persistence - but should bring about a much nearer normal condition for the body, [4823].

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Will the boy be cured?
(A)  That depends upon whether they are to do, or how the reactions take place in system.

15. (Q)  About how long will it take?
(A)  Depends upon how the body responds, and how consistent and persistent the applications are.  As will be seen, it will be necessary for several changes - and would require some months, but it should be worth it for those whom the body is dependent upon, as well as for the building up of the body.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [...]
"    " Cayce File
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [[King David|David]] Levy
