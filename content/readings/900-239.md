# TEXT OF READING 900-239 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-239]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/5/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5442

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ..., Deal, N.J., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come in that way and manner as have been outlined.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Tuesday Night, June 1, or Wednesday Morning, June 2, 1926.  Saw a baby or young child and a turn in a road, also food such as ice cream for the child.  My [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] faculties reasoned (voice) that I prepared this food for the child but [140], my sister-in-law, got it instead.  I still saw the turn in the road.
(A)  In this there is seen those conditions as had and as have been weighed in the mental forces of the body-conscious mind as regarding food for body, for preparation of coming child, and this as is seen - the conditions present that the roadway presents a possible turn in conditions as respecting a condition as regarding a coming child and, as is seen, there is presented that another gets the condition, rather than being prepared by the body, see?  for these conditions are as lessons in various ways, and will, through this presentation, present many questions to the mind - that is, as is seen, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, or the body, feels or appears to experience the seeing of the action of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]], or of an unconscious condition becoming conscious in the actions of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], see?  and the questions, then, and the lessons as will come from same, will bring, as it were, to the mind, this turn in the WAY and MANNER of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s THINKING, in a body-mind way and manner.  Just as the food as might be prepared for the body would be the change, or would be that which would build, or that which would be harmful, or that which would be good, see?  for we must come to the first premise in thinking, in reasoning concerning thinking - Mind the Builder.

4. (Q)  Thursday Night, June 3, or Friday Morning, June 4, 1926. I got out of a train at a station and noticed that I could not get aboard again because the doors of the cars were closing.  Disappointed and dismayed, I turned for help and advice and the porter told me it was alright because the train would back up into the station again (after pulling out) but on another track on the other side of the station. Reassured, I went downstairs, intending to cross over to the platform on the other side of the station.  I noted crowds going my way and mulling about.  I became bewildered or unsure of my way.  I asked the way to the other track or platform and they pointed the way underneath a tunnel that led to the other side of the road.  I went there and upon reaching asked a man there the way, being polite and saying I was sorry.  Someone made fun of me, calling me young because I was so polite asking my way in this manner, "I'm sorry, but will you direct me to the other side - to the tracks on which the train will return?"  They pointed to a stairway leading up to the other side, and feeling sensitive to criticism, I went up the stairway but found a sudden change - no station. Again I asked for the station and they said it was 3 BLOCKS UP.  "It's too late now," I said.  "I can't make that train any more."
(A)  In this there are presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] many various conditions as respecting the mental condition of the body mind as concerning conditions taking place in the physical sense.  In the first there is seen that as regarding conditions respecting the stocks as pertaining to rails.  As the decline comes in this first few days, there will become the troubles or consternation, as is seen by entity's finding the way closed, or the losses to self by being closed on the condition in which the entity had intended to act, see?  and in the attempting to right the condition there would come more consternation, by becoming fearful of the circumstances that would attend with such sudden changes as would be enacted.  The solution - then don't act that way.

In the next there is seen that the severe criticism is given the entity at times, as resulting toward the action of self towards other individuals, as concerning the mental attitude, the thought and study which the entity is making.  Then, with this self CONSCIOUSNESS in same, if allowed to enter in where the bewilderment would come from the fear of being called curious for being polite, or doing that which the entity knows to be right, there would follow, then, that condition as is seen by the entity's self, when he realizes that it is too late to accomplish that whereunto he had first set self and [[Purpose|purpose]] and allowed earthly criticisms to hinder. As is seen regarding rails (return to the first form as seen in this), there will come first the two to three days - Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, see?  with many changes, gradually falling off, see?  Then the change for the better, for, as has been given, the entity must act just in the opposite direction from what he had first intended to act, see?

5. (Q)  "Numbers 113 - 111."
(A)  Southern Railroad.  We are through.
