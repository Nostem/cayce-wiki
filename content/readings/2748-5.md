# TEXT OF READING 2748-5 M ADULT

**Reading [[2748-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/1/1942  Sex: M  ReadingID: 9890

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the brother - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [257] and Mrs. [903].]

Time of Reading                      Brooklyn Jewish Hosp., 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time. Room 802, Brooklyn, N.Y.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As indicated, there is still resistance being builded in the body - it is still in the body at present.

4. Conditions are quite serious, though, yet.  There has not been that relaxing of the centers that cause the tie-up in the abdominal area, in those sections or areas where infection has caused the trouble.  That there has become the inflammation in the wall, or the peritoneum, makes for greater distresses.

5. Unless there can be drainages kept sufficient, this may eventually cause sepsis poison - which would be fatal to the body.

6. If they will give the body the opportunity to respond, DO relax the area indicated.

7. We are through.

Copy to Brother - Air Mail, Special, Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
