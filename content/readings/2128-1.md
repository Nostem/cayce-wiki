# TEXT OF READING 2128-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[2128-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/29/1940  Sex: M  ReadingID: 8787

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of February, 1940, in accordance with request made by the son - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Messrs. [5417] and [2040].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions arise from general debilitation, - as from toxic forces and the effects upon the functioning of organs as related to the vital energies of the body.

4. We find that it would not be well to change from those applications which are being used.

5. Then, we would only suggest that there be greater drainages set up, - from the toxic forces, to eliminate same.

6. And as there is so little resistance, there needs to be greater stimulation for the activities throughout the body.

7. These as we find, with those things being administered, would be suggested as best for this body at the present time.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What method would you suggest for greater eliminations?
(A)  Not so much of sedatives, - that is, keeping away from these as much as possible, - and better stimulation to centers along the cerebrospinal system.

This as we find may be best had through [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and massage.

10. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mr. [...] [Son] (one copy for Hydrotherapist) Copy to Ass'n file (At your convenience please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, also an outline of the treatments being administered.  This is valuable for our files, so we may have something with which to compare our information.)
