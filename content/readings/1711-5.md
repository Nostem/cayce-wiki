# TEXT OF READING 1711-5 F 62

**Reading [[1711-5]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/2/1943  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 7881

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - [1226], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  We don't find the body here.  We have those impressions regarding this body.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is the Radio-Active Appliance working properly?
(A)  When prepared in the manner indicated; that is, for its activity upon the body.  The Appliance in itself is correct, and when applied correctly will aid in equalizing those disturbing forces and the unbalanced circulation in the body.

5. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do keep those mental attitudes towards constructive thinking; knowing and realizing that all [[Healing|healing]] is of the spiritual source, and thus must be generated within the body itself, by the coordinating of mental attitudes, spiritual [[Ideal|ideals]], as to bring the manifestations of such influences in the material body.

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
