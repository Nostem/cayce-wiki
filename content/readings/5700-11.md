# TEXT OF READING 5700-11 F ADULT

**Reading [[5700-11]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/9/1928  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14148

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - [5700].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Missouri.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  Many conditions we find with the body are improved from those as we have had here before.  Much better conditions exist in many ways in the system.

3. To bring about the full correction of those conditions and relieve the body from the pressure as is caused in portions of the system by the tautness and by the lesions as have formed, we would apply that of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] over the body, following same by the completion of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces through that of the Violet Ray over the frontal and back portion - while that of the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] would be applied over and along the cerebro-spinal system, throughout the central portions of nerve centers, and plexuses in the extremities, arms, and all along.  The Violet Ray would be applied principally along the frontal portion and over the back portion.  The [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] would be used from about twenty to twenty-five minutes, the Violet Ray should be used from thirty to forty minutes.

4. Do that.  We will bring better conditions for this body.

5. We are through for the present.
