# TEXT OF READING 900-341 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-341]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/17/1927  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5544

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I might ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, are those presentations through the various channels as have been outlined for the body, and are applicable in the affairs of the individual in the way and manner as same are presented.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  Morning of Sept. 17th.  Gary talking to me:  "The break in cotton is what caused the flurry on the stock exchange - but watch steel."  Correlate this with that given night before - that I could buy it back cheaper.
(A)  In this we find there is presented that which the body consciousness may study, and find that same is as of any good business reasoning with existent conditions, when many issues of various stocks have reached a top-heavy position, and with that reasoning again there is seen, in that one presented, that changes are to be made in the policies or in the handling of same, and while - as has often been given this as the criterion of the exchange price, as has also been given these with industrials or motors would form the greater basis for changes again, save in Baldwin.

In the correlation of the two is that the activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] respecting these, having been satisfactory, then watch and listen - watch and listen - for that as will be given to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] for the better handling of these various condiions as will be presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] from time to time.  Act only when that direct command is to act.  Then act sure and quickly.

5. We are through with this reading.
