# TEXT OF READING 3230-1 M 56

**Reading [[3230-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/21/1943  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 10632

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3230], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3230].

3. In giving the interpretation of the causes and the manner of correcting the disturbances with this body, we find that - if the body will accept same - there are other things and conditions in the mental and [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] also that should be taken into consideration; else why correct a physical being or body only to use for self's own indulgence.

4. For, the sources of much of those disturbances that are a part of the physical condition in the present arise from a disregard of many of those things pertaining to the answering "Am I my brother's keeper?"

5. These, then, should also be taken into consideration.

6. While there are pathological disorders it is well that a body-mind, and a body-physical mind and being, be not only good but good for something - other than just considering self.

7. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body here, we find that these in the main are the result of the toxic poisonings - those conditions produced in the liver as well as that disturbance between the liver and the kidney circulation.  These are the basis of the inflammatory condition produced in the lymph and muscular forces of the abdominal area, as well as in the limbs and in the arms and shoulders.

8. As we find, - we would have a consistent course of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments; these including light sweats - more fume sweats than dry heat, and the fume sweats varied.  These should be given about twice each week, if the heart and the general circulation will be taken into consideration - as to how much heat is applied and this watched closely.  It will reduce these conditions.  The Fume Baths should be alternated between Witchhazel one time and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (or [[Iodine|Iodine]]) the next time.  A teaspoonful of Witchhazel would be used in the pint of water boiling in the Cabinet to produce the fumes. Also a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] would be used, or if [[Iodine|Iodine]] nearly a teaspoonful - though this should be used in another container - and will discolor much more.

9. Following each [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] rub down thoroughly with this combination of oils:

Nujol or [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil (as the base).....4 ounces,
Pine Oil...................................1/2 ounce,
Oil of Pine Needles..........................1 ounce,
Peanut Oil...................................2 ounces,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root......................1/4 ounce.

While these will tend to separate, shake them together before they are applied to the body, and apply over the afflicted areas - all the body will absorb - this is the abdomen, the feet and limbs and shoulders, and especially across the diaphragm area.

10. Do not take any carbonated drinks or any malt drinks of any character.

11. Do keep up these applications.  When the general swelling has been reduced, after about three to four weeks, then apply [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs on those areas of the limbs and feet.

12. About five days before beginning the Packs, start taking internally three drops of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken.  After the five day period, leave off five days and then take again, and so on.

13. Do keep these in unison of [[Purpose|purposes]], hopes, desires, as of [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] in and through the activities of the body.

14. We will bring help, if there is the raising of self to that point of accepting self as being responsible for something, responsible for self and its activities as a helpful influence in and among its fellow men.

15. Do that.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What should be done for loose bowels?
(A)  All of these conditions will be cared for when those applications are made.  When the massages are given, include a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] occasionally.  This will take care of the general conditions there; provided there is not added any of those fires of nature in those forms that have been indicated.

18. (Q)  Hay fever?
(A)  This again, with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], will be kept down - by the use of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments in the manner indicated.

19. (Q)  Give suggestions for remedying the generally weakened condition?
(A)  This treatment suggested will weaken more at some periods, especially if there is a breaking over.  But it will soon make for much improved conditions.  For the diet of strengthening foods will be helpful for the body.

20. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for physiotherapist) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing.

(We would suggest that you send a copy of this reading to Dr. H. J. Reilly, physiotherapist, RCA Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., NYC., and ask him to refer you to someone in your locality who can follow these treatments.  Dr. Reilly has had considerable experience with carrying out suggestions made in Mr. Cayce's Readings.  Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs.

If Atomidine is used for the Fume Baths, it may be obtained from The Atomidine Co., Atten. Harold S. Bisey, 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y.  It sells for $1 a bottle.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/7/43 [3230]'s letter:  "For 25 yrs. I have been troubled
> with what the doctors called 'Gouty Arthritis' but 2 yrs. ago
> my intestinal tract, always sensitive, gave a royal kick up,
> causing a 4 months dysentery - in bed - accompanied by
> attacks of the so called gouty-arthritis.  All in all I did
> not leave my bed for 8 months.  Naturally all this has left
> me in an extremely weakened condition which is just now being
> aggravated by my usual Spring attack of Hay Fever - so-called
> - and will be followed about Aug. 15th by the Fall session,
> of course it may not but it has for the past 9 yrs...  I am
> crippled in no way - the unbalance seems all to be systemic."
> 
> B2. 9/12/43 Letter:  "By medication and diet we have managed to
> keep matters in hand and carry on, but it seems these
> troubles have finally taken their toll so that now I can work
> only a few hours a day, then my vitality and strength is used
> up."
> 
> B3. Questions submitted:
> 
> 
> 1. Please comment on and give suggested remedies for each of
> the following chronic conditions:  Gouty-arthritis?
> 2. Loose bowels?
> 3. Hay Fever (Spring and Fall attacks)?
> 4. Give suggestions for remedying the generally weakened
> condition?
