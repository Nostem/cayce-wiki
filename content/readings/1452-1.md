# TEXT OF READING 1452-1 F 38

**Reading [[1452-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/9/1937  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 7240

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of October, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1452], through her cousin, Mrs. [...], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. While there are physical disturbances, these as we find have been produced as much by the mental attitude - to self, to conditions, to surrounding influences.

3. And these then need not only a physical but a spiritual approach to the applications for relief and help to be brought the body.

4. As is SOMETIMES understood, the physical will deteriorate through MENTAL suggestions to self.

5. And the self-condemnation has produced in those portions of the cerebrospinal system such activity upon the nervous system as to produce a reaction that is contrary to the activities of the nervous body.

6. Hence those periods of melancholia, those periods when there is the determination for self-effacement in one manner or another.

7. This has produced in SPECIFICALLY the centers in the upper dorsal and through the cervical, and especially in the vagus area, such reactions that there becomes an incoordination between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic or vegetative nerve systems of the body.

8. Then, under the VARIED activity, this produces disturbances in the reaction of the organs that become involved by the too much flow of impulse to the system.

9. Thus we find there are those overflows often to the ducts that magnify and manifest sadness, sorrow, and self-effacement; in that the condemnations which arise make for the dislike of activities that are necessary for the expression of any NORMAL, cooperating, balanced system of the spiritual activities in a [[Physical Body|physical body]].

10. In the approach then, first as we find, from the very nature of the reactions, medications - as medical reactions, in the form of any bromides, or those that would make for a degeneration of the feelings to the system - would only allay; but produce those conditions where softening of the reflexes from brain forces to the activities through the system would set up in such natures as to become WHOLLY destructive to the [[Physical Body|physical body]] and its reactions.

11. Then, this should be rather the physical approach:

12. We find that the body should be in such an environment, and with such applications as may be had in an institution such as Still-Hildreth; or the applications such as may be received there to stimulate activity in associations with a constructiveness in the mental reactions.

13. These would necessarily come slowly at first, until there is the arousing of the mental reactions to the body that there are those individuals who DO care!

14. Then the spiritual realization that God IS and that He cares; even though the darkness of hate overshadows thee, even though there are the determinations of those that may create surroundings that become heart-rending.

15. As an individual, as an active force within self it may be aroused to an activity in which there may come yet those awarenesses of His presence abiding IN thee AS an individual; and that the physical activities of self - physically, mentally, materially - must be such as to give expressions to those influences that applied in the experience of each [[Soul|soul]] bring the consciousness of well-being and not of degeneration in ANY sense physically, mentally or spiritually.

16. In such an attitude, then, let there be sought those environs such as indicated [Still-Hildreth]; and keep self in accord with a study and an application of that studied as to the development of [[Soul|the soul]] through any given experience.

17. For, He has not willed that any [[Soul|soul]] should perish but has with every [[Trial of Jesus|trial]], every sorrow, prepared a way, a means of overcoming.

18. And instead of the conditions that exist in the environs physically, mentally and spiritually, becoming stumbling-stones to thee, they may become stepping-stones to a larger, a more abundant life of service in His Name.

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  Any special treatment that would be suggested at Still-Hildreth's?
(A)  That there may be those applications of the mechanical nature for making the better associations and connections through the areas indicated, for a more perfect NERVE reaction; and that the activities physically and mentally be directed in constructive and spiritual study AND application.

21. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mrs. [...], [1452]'s cousin] Copy to Ass'n file
"    " be sent to Still-Hildreth [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] Sanatorium, Macon, Missouri, Atten. Dr. A. G. Hildreth, when writing for particulars about their taking the case
