# TEXT OF READING 3980-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[3980-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/26/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11662

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3980].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find that the general physical conditions of the body are on the improve, and that the body needs only keep in that way and manner as has been outlined for the body.  We find, now, it would be necessary for the full round, as it were, of the treatments as have been accorded for the system, for the awakening of those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system that will produce the fertile fetus for the system, see, and that reproduction may take place; for the general condition of the body is improved, and with that injection, as have been given for the system, and these repeated, with the general condition applied for the building of the system, we would find this would bring those conditions about.

4. Then, we would only repeat that as has been given.  Do that. We are through for the present.
