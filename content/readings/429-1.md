# TEXT OF READING 429-1 M 39 (Rabbi)

**Reading [[429-1]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/11/1930  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 3220

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Rabbi [429] via Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [429], Apt. ..., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].  You will answer the questions which we will ask you regarding [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s associations and contemplated connections in various phases of his life.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [429], and those conditions as surround the body, physically and mentally.  Also the various connections, associations, and relations.  These are varied in their aspects, but - in the physical must the body take the warnings, that the physical may be able to be that channel through which the mental MAY manifest.  These should be given due consideration.  Those conditions as exist in the soft tissue of the head and throat; especially those conditions in antrum and in the throat proper; also those relationships with same, as respecting the digestive system, and the MENTAL reactions that the body makes towards physical relationships must be kept in accord with that as is attempting to be made manifest by the body-mental, the body-physical.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] accept the ..., [N.J.] invitation if extended to him?  [Pulpit left vacant by death of Rabbi [4159?]]
(A)  Should accept!

4. (Q)  If so, would it be necessary or advisable to abandon The [..., N.Y.] Synagog?
(A)  Not abandon it in IDEAL, but in fact - in the present, or under those conditions; for two factors may not be served at once, but a single PRINCIPLE, a single IDEAL, may be carried throughout.

5. (Q)  Regarding marital relations, should [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] marry the one with whom he has been keeping company?
(A)  The one who has been an associate in portions of the work, whom the entity has relied upon [his sec'ty] - the one who has been active in welfare work, the one who is competent and capable and able to be of aid to the body, mentally and physically.

6. (Q)  When should an engagement take place and be announced?
(A)  At once!

7. (Q)  To what extent and in what details are the objections of his family justified?  [His mother and sisters?]
(A)  Not at all!  [9/6/30 See 259-2, Par. 9-A--12-A in re Rabbi [429] and his sec'ty's marriage, their association with Mrs. [459] and her work, etc.]

8. (Q)  To what extent should entity be governed by his older sister's judgement?
(A)  Only in that in which the relationships have existed, do exist, as respecting that as regarding the appreciations for that as has been done for same.

9. (Q)  How can entity help in the development of [[Atlantic University|Atlantic University]] at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]?
(A)  Not at all at present.

10. (Q)  Would entity be of service in lecturing to the salesmen of the [257]'s Furniture Co., or would an entry into the financial field be preferable?
(A)  Wouldn't enter into either one!  Tend to the business taking on in hand!  Do that well first!  Do not attempt to smear self all over so many things!  but be COMPETENT, ABLE, to handle that in hand!  When the offer is made, the hands will be full!  Won't need to be spread out over too many fields!

11. (Q)  How could entity arrange to keep up The ... [N.Y.] Synagog while partially supporting himself at ..., [N.J.]?
(A)  [[Ideal|The IDEAL]] as is IN same may be propagated by the teachings and by the lecturings, that will come in accord WITH those efforts and with those activities of the entity.

12. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Keep self straight first, and then the entity will be able to advise and counsel with others; remembering, that - that as is within CANNOT be of a canker and expect to have a liberal, or even a normal growth from without.  Do not be two-faced in any service.  Do not be the appearance in one way and the inner life not satisfactory to self.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
