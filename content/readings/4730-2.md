# TEXT OF READING 4730-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4730-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/23/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12698

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23rd day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4730].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                     Clifton, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are many conditions in this body that are improved from those when we had them before.  Still there are some conditions that have to do with the nerve system and with that producing or causing this condition in the system that have not been brought to the proper or normal conditions in the body.

2. We would, to correct these conditions from the present forces as shown and exhibited in the system, take these as medicinal properties in the body, and make those adjustments in the form of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces as would be applied in the lower dorsals and in the upper lumbar region.  These we find give distresses to the system and to the functioning of the organs of upper digestion in the intestinal tract.  These adjustments should be taken at least six to ten of these, or until the condition is adjusted in reference to these centers, as shown in the system, taking these properties:

3. To one gallon of rain water, add:

Sarsaparilla Compound, or
Sarsaparilla Syrup Compound.......1 1/2 ounces,
Leptandrin...........................10 grains,
Sanguinaria..........................10 grains,
Fluid Extract Calisaya................2 drams,
Essence of Celerina...................3 drams,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]......................1 dram,
Oil of Peppermint.....................1 dram.

Reduce this by slow boiling or simmering to one quart. Strain and keep the dross skimmed from solution while being prepared.  Add to this 2 ounces of grain alcohol, with 1 dram Balsam of Tolu cut in it, and added to before added to solution 15 minims Tincture of Myrrh, and with solution 1 dram Balm of Gilead.

4. The dose would be two teaspoonsful in little water twice each day, morning and evening.

5. Keep meats from the system, save fowl, fish or wild game.

6. Do that, [4730].
