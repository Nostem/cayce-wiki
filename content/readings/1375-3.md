# TEXT OF READING 1375-3 F 60

**Reading [[1375-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/13/1937  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 7072

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of June, 1937, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1375].

2. As we find, there are definite changes for the better.  The inclination for the superficial circulation to throw off poisons from the system, as indicated by the rash upon portions of the body, indicates that the disturbance is being broken up.

3. Hence there would be, then, precautionary measures as respecting same.

4. Bathe off affected portions with a very weak solution of Bichloride - one tablet (0.475 gm) to one quart of water; gently; and be sure to destroy the cotton or batting that is used for doing this.

5. Then apply Cuticura Ointment.

6. Let a little more time elapse between the use of the Stupes, but do not discontinue same.

7. Use a little tea made from the [[Mullein|Mullein]], internally - preferably made from the flower, but this is early in the season for same.  The proportions would be about half a dram to a quart of water.  Let it steep like tea.  Take about an ounce of this once a day, internally.  This will be helpful for the general condition of the inflammation throughout the intestinal system.

8. When conditions have increased - that is, been aided - until there is no discharge from the rectum, even after stools (which as we find, with the use of those properties indicated, should be within the next ten days now) - use a SMALL rectal applicator of the Violet Ray.  This should not cause too great a disturbance in the insertion of same, and use only for about three-quarters of a minute in the beginning.  It may be gradually increased.  Do this in the beginning about three times a week.

9. And if we keep up the rest, we should bring [[Healing|healing]] - with the way this has started - or an elimination of the condition.

10. The Ash has aided.  Use it - not too often, but sufficient for the accumulations to produce better circulation by releasing oxygen.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should the [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes be applied directly to body now, or wait until the rash has disappeared?
(A)  As indicated, these must be applied but wait for longer periods between the applications; and a gauze may be used between the [[Mullein|Mullein]] and the body.

But after each use (for this tends to draw), sponge off with the weakened solution of Bichloride; then apply Cuticura Ointment.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [601]
"    " Ass'n file
