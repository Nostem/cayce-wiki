# TEXT OF READING 137-32 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-32]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/16/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 422

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by, or through, his brother, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] which come to the body are as those conditions which we have given for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], and may be used in the way and manner given, correlating same with the physical experiences in body-mind transactions as relating to same.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Wednesday evening, November 11, 1925, at home, ..., [[New York City|New York City]].  "St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] R. R. Stock go up to $50 per share."
(A)  This, as we see, is again that presentation to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of those conditions regarding a commodity in which the physical mind has given some attention and thought, and in the interpretation of same, this stock, we find, is a buy, and refers to that of the Preferred, which will advance first, and also the Common, which will advance, see?  As to the number, as given, use in the way and manner as has been seen referring to numbers.  When advance is five and ten points (5 and 10 points) then sell, see, or close transaction - see?

4. (Q)  Does this mean the common or preferred stock of St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] R. R. ([[Chicago|Chicago]] Milwaukee & St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]) will go to $50 per share?  Which, the common or preferred?
(A)  Both, as given, will be on the advance, see?  Be satisfied by the fifteen point advance, see?

5. (Q)  Does this mean we should now purchase this stock for the rise to this price?
(A)  Purchase this stock for the fifteen point advance, see? Both will advance.  The Preferred more than the other, yet in proportion near the same, see?

6. (Q)  Thursday evening, November 12.  "Saw a fire escape which to me meant a way out, and seemed to refer to [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Gas & Electric Stock we are now long of - own.  Horace Block had given me an order to sell his stock and I had that in mind. The fire escape seemed to be under a great strain, but was steady under the pressure.  A man, who was long of this [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Gas stock and who is a member of the N.Y. Stock Exchange, was jumping on the fire escape to test its strength.  The fire escape withstood its test."
(A)  This then, as we see, is emblematically given, as to how [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will make in the transactions on [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Electric Gas, see?  through the heavy trading of those members that deal in large blocks, and the efforts, especially of Horace Block will bring the successes to this entity, see?  Then follow the leads as are being used through those specific forces of the entity's application of self as regarding this stock, see?  That is, when the entity finds these various ones, members of Stock Exchange, are long, be long with them, see?  when the sale comes short, be short with same - see? for this will find, we see, to one and five-sixths, we see - see one and five-sixths (1 & 5/6).

7. (Q)  "Another [[Visions|vision]] I don't remember.  Recall and interpret for me."
(A)  This, we find, was the [[Visions|vision]]:  The ladder that is reaching from one window to another, and this presents to the entity a way, or a means in which the climbing to the points wherein there will be the profit to the entity in those same stocks.  Take them when they have the profit, see?

Now, we find there are other [[Visions|visions]] that have come to this entity as relating to the various stocks held by the entity. Entity, [137], should take into consideration, when these informations are given as regarding same, same is related (the information) to the time the entity sees the visions, see?  for as seen in this - 14th of November, see, in evening - there is seen a paper, with various quotations - 14 - 12 - 11, and this seems to be printed rather in red than in the regular colors, see, and the quotations from same seem to have been supplied by someone whom the entity does not see - see?  This representing the information, then, as given here.

8. We are through with this at the present.
