# TEXT OF READING 5691-2 F 4

**Reading [[5691-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/17/1928  Sex: F  Age: 4  ReadingID: 14126

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Pa. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  There is not perceptible much change in the physical conditions of the body at present from that as we have had before; yet the change is just in that as the consistency has been with the application of those conditions as were given for the first of the correction for the body.  There is seen that there are periods when the coordination in system - the retraction or reaction to the [[Mental Body|mental body]] - apparently shows change; yet in a general way and manner, little or none - and at times apparently it grows less.  This is a lack of the proper handling, or proper consideration, or proper consistency with that as has been given.  Would the conditions be assisted, as has been outlined, it will require a very persistent and consistent action on the part of those that would build into this [[Physical Body|physical body]] that of the proper coordination between the mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]], that the retraction and reaction as come from the mental being of the body, the normal functioning of the physical being, coming in coordination with the mental and brain reaction and building up within the forces those that will build this connection between the sympathetic, and cerebro-spinal and sensory retraction in body.  The added forces of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as given will bring this about.  Now is needed the consistent use of same, and adding WITH that, that of the consistent manipulating of the centers as especially govern the central nerve system; also that of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]]'s connection with the brain forces in the 1st and 2nd cervical, and in the 9th dorsal and the 4th lumbar.  These centers need especial attention.  Manipulated osteopathically - that gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the body at least every other day.  Do these consistently for the next thirty days, and we will find considerable improvement in this body, [5691].  We are through for the present.
