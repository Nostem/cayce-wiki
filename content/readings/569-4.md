# TEXT OF READING 569-4 F 45

## Text

Date: 7/31/1925  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 4012

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 31st day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [569].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]]; Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                     ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with special reference to the condition in the ear, the right ear, with suggestions for the improvement of any or all of these conditions.  You will answer any questions relative to these that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find many conditions are improved in a great many ways in the body, yet there are those abnormal conditions that need correction and that should have had those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as were given, to prevent the centralization of used tissue or drosses as comes from the condition in system where the eliminations are improperly divided in system.  Hence we have the excretions as are shown in inflammatory tissue as we see in portions of the body, ear especially.  This is a centralization of the condition being eliminated in the upper portion of body, and centralized. The [[Radium|radium]] applicator forces, as we see, would, with the [[Vibration|vibration]] it creates with the blood and nerve system, have minimized and produced a better equalization throughout the system, producing a coordination in eliminations and adding life-giving forces, rather than allowing tissue to become involved in the condition produced by drosses remaining and producing cell force in themselves.  This, as we see then, would need the double strength, as it were, of such application at present time, though we would find this would be of assistance to the body were this used.

3. Then, for the specific conditions as exist in the body at present time, we would keep those of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces to assist in eliminations, but making a more general condition, taking those properties of salts of tartar, or of properties that are of a saline nature that keep the intestinal tract open, using the antiseptics as are being used as local application to cleanse the broken tissue that it may heal properly.  Keep away from meats or of those properties that give tissue that building force that creates of the cellular force in itself.  [GD's note:  Warning of cancer]  Do that for the body at the present time.  We find the general condition, in eyes, the condition in the general part of system, that bacilli as is being carried in the body, as has been given before, is lessened at the present time, for it has become centralized.  These conditions and these properties for the system will assist in eliminating and bringing the body nearer to normal forces.  Sulphur and salt of tartar may also be used.  Should not get the body wet at such times, or feet wet, or too much exercise for the body. The body MUST rest more.  Keep off of feet more while these properties are being taken.

4. (Q)  What proportion?
(A)  Small quantities and under supervision of physician, for we do not wish to turn the sedimentary conditions to that of an intestinal disturbance, yet we must eliminate more through the intestinal tract and increase circulation through liver and intestines, that we may throw off drosses and so eliminate poisons from system that are accumulating in a localized condition, yet these must be watched, for according to diet and the disposition of the mental forces towards conditions would the change come.  Hence, under the care and attention of physician should these eliminations be kept. Keep off of feet and from too much exercise, while this is being taken - but use applicator.
