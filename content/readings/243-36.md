# TEXT OF READING 243-36 F 62

**Reading [[243-36]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/23/1942  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 1120

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Artic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [243], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                              Millinery Dept., 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find, while there are improvements, there are times also when the conditions with the kidneys and bladder prevent the thorough cleansing, - when there are indiscretions due to overactivity or incorrect eating, or the lack of taking proper adjustments.

3. Thus, the distress is continued to be caused by the pain through the lower area of the kidney, also in the area of the urinary canal.  This brings nervousness, anxiety and disturbance.

4. We would include the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]] in the diet at least twice a week, cooked in its own juices, - that is, in Patapar Paper, so that all the juice of same may be stirred or mixed with the bulk when this is eaten.

5. We find that this will add sufficient of those properties necessary to aid in keeping down inflammation through the kidney area.

6. The massages should be kept occasionally, but should be made consistent.  These, as we find, with those precautions indicated as to not overtiring the body or getting the feet and limbs out of their proper relationship to the general activities, should bring the better conditions.

7. These are the better for the body in the present.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
