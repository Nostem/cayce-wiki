# TEXT OF READING 2649-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[2649-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/15/1942  Sex: M  ReadingID: 9711

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self through Mrs. [845], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2649].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical functionings.  While the conditions are rather acute in the present, we find that these arise primarily from a constitutional or a long standing disorder.

4. These, as we find, have more to do with the digestive influences in the present.  For, there has been caused an expansion - from the lacerations and the inflammation in the pyloric portion of the stomach itself.

5. Thus, throughout the duodenum there is a great deal of anxiety, the inability to retain foods; for there is a great deal of uneasiness throughout the whole area.

6. The acute condition arises from congestion, from some cold, as combined with the general condition from which there have been the greater disturbances.

7. In the present we find that there should be given a thorough relaxation osteopathically; this with the special reference to setting up drainages from the stomach AND duodenum. Especially stimulate, then, those areas in the 4th, 5th and 6th dorsal, with a GENERAL reaction to the secondary cardiac centers.

8. Give the body internally the Yellow Saffron Tea.  This would be made by putting a teaspoonful of the American Saffron in a crock (as a teapot) and pouring a pint of boiling water over it, allowing it to steep for at least five to ten minutes.  Drain off or strain, and use this quantity during a twelve hour period - taking it when water is desired.  And drink plenty of water!

9. The diet should consist principally of broths and [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], with milk or soft toast and the like.

10. Do these, and the body should within a few days regain strength.

11. But continue the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, also the aid to the gastric flow (the Saffron Tea), until the disturbances are eliminated.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations be given?
(A)  About every other day until at least ten or twelve are given; rest from them a period of some two weeks, and then take six to ten more.

14. (Q)  Who would you recommend to give these?
(A)  Richardson.

15. (Q)  Will this treatment relieve the condition referred to as being existent in the pylorus?
(A)  This is what it is given for!  For, you see, there is a swelling there in the present; and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxation followed by the adjustments, with the relaxing with the properties indicated, should allow these conditions to pass away.

16. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self through Mrs. [...] (one for Copy to Ass'n file                     Dr. Richardson)

(See Associate Blank and directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)
