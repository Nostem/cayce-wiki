# TEXT OF READING 3043-1 F 48

**Reading [[3043-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1943  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 10360

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3043], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are conditions that prevent the better physical functioning of the body.  These are rather a complication of disturbances.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [3043]:

5. From the effects of a deep cold, combined with some intestinal disorder, there has been produced inflammation that has upset the coordination between the lymph circulation and the central circulation, also the nerve impulses.

6. So, we find a great deal of trouble through the intestinal tract; forming gas in the lower portion of the duodenum after almost each meal, a great deal of discomfort, and the reflexing of these to the circulation between the heart, liver and lungs.

7. At other periods through the lower portion of the abdomen, and through the liver area, there are tendencies for contractions.

8. These, we find, are the sources - and the disturbances with this body.

9. In the blood supply irritations are produced at times, oft, to the superficial circulation - areas on various portions of the body, extremities as well as the torso itself, becoming irritated; while we find an irregularity to the circulation or to the pulsation itself.  There is a great deal of disturbance in the soft tissue of face, head, neck. Also this disturbance between the lymph and the impulses as supplied by nerve forces of the circulation of the blood force in varied areas through the body.  Especially over the abdomen these produce cold areas, even to the touch; showing the irregularity of the circulation.

10. As we find, all of these disorders, and the effects that have been produced in the functionings of the varied systems of activity in the body, should be taken into consideration in making applications that would prove the more beneficial for this body.

11. First, we would take internally a preparation made in this manner:

12. To 2 ounces of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin as the base, we would add:

A fusion of Wild Ginseng...................1 ounce,
A fusion of Wild Ginger..................1/4 ounce.

Take a teaspoonful of this each evening when ready to retire.

13. Also, when this is begun, begin having at least once a week a mild [[Hydrotherapy|fume bath]] or sweat, using in the fumes Witchhazel.

14. Follow this with an [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] light treatment, preferably with the projection of the green glass between same and the body.  Stay under the light some ten to fifteen minutes, five minutes on the front side and five on the back, or such a matter.

15. After the light treatment, have a thorough rubdown, preferably with Peanut Oil.

16. Keep these in their regular order, being mindful of the diet.

17. Occasionally, when the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment is given, - say once in twenty days, unless it is necessary oftener, - have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].  Do use [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt in the first waters, in the proportions of a teaspoonful heaping full of salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to each gallon of water.  In the last or rinse water injected, use a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the gallon of water, as an intestinal antiseptic.  These will prevent irritation, and these should rid the system of these disturbances.

18. When there is acute pain through the abdomen, apply a [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Pack.  Use some three thicknesses of flannel, preferably old rather than new flannel, well saturated with the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], and apply directly to the skin.  This preferably as we find, in this case, would be applied over the right side, extending from close under the ribs to almost the caecum area.  Then we would apply to this the electric pad, even though the Pack should be put on hot.  Let this remain on an hour.

19. DO NOT take any carbonated drinks of any kind.  Carbonated waters are not good.

20. DO use a good deal of sea food, a great deal of leafy vegetables, and the character of foods that are body and blood building; as celery, lettuce, radish, carrots, beets, beet tops, water cress, - all of these should be taken quite often; not every day, to become tiresome for the body, but these will be found to be most helpful.

21. Ready for questions.

22. (Q)  What causes the migraine headaches?
(A)  This is from the stomach.

As we find, these applications should get rid of a great deal of this disturbance.

23. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, RCA Bldg., Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., who can follow the directions for the Fume Baths, etc.) "  " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/16/43 Letter:  "I have awful neuritis all over me, and one
> migraine after another, which seems to upset my digestion.
> Some doctors say my spastic colitis gives me the neuritic
> pains and heads, and others say that my sinus upsets and
> poisons everything."
> 
> B2. 5/2/43 Letter:  "You might be interested to hear I had
> various x-rays taken last Wednesday and the verdict is
> I haven't gallstones.  It is a great relief to me to know
> that, as you can imagine.  All the trouble seems to come
> from a spastic colon, which sets up colitis and a nervous
> tummy, and so stops the gall from functioning properly.  When
> the attacks come on, I feel thoroughly unhealthy and it also
> gives me neuritis.  Dr. Rosenhain is going to get in touch
> with a N.Y. M.D. I have chosen, and give him a history of
> my case.  At present, Dr. Rosenhain has given me a sedative
> to calm the tummy, also a mild cathartic.  I am not in favor
> of sedatives, but suppose for a few days they wouldn't hurt.
> I prefer to be wide awake.  But perhaps the spastic business
> might quiet down a bit.  I wish I hadn't to wait until June
> 16th for the Rdg., because I am so anxious to hear what you
> prescribe for me.  I feel you will diagnose my case
> thoroughly.  So few medicines I can take, and the doctors
> find me a problem."
