# TEXT OF READING 758-33 M 9

**Reading [[758-33]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/26/1931  Sex: M  Age: 9  ReadingID: 4650

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, temporarily in Room 2135 of Hotel Victoria, [[New York City|New York City]], this 26th day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by his parents, Mr. [417] and Mrs. [1192].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [417] and Mrs. [1192] and Mr. [279] and Mr. Bloom.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 L.I., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [758] - this we have had before.  Now, we find conditions have not been so good in the physical functionings of the body.  There have been those conditions both [[Astrology|astrological]] as well as those in the physical functioning that have produced more incoordination than we have had for some time back.  This makes for that tendency of the incoordinating in the upper cervical to the reactions of the nerve and muscular forces in the [[Physical Body|physical body]], producing - with its reaction - a congestion in the eliminating through the stomach proper; so that BOTH disorders are aggravating to the physical condition.  We find that, will there be [if there will be] precautions taken as to the diet - and not over exciting the body in any manner, increasing the dose of the Mayblossom Bitters - not sufficient to make for that irritation as may come to the intestinal system - and with a more relaxation through the manipulations in the LUMBAR and lower DORSAL area - the body would be bettered.  Will be well also that for periods - Now this, as we find, will be necessary to change occasionally - but those of the no salt diet, and THEN periods when there IS and OVER quantity, as it were, of the salt - see?  that is, when the body desires same, let this be taken in its original state, rather than in the foods, as he would taste sugar, or as he would take on tongue.  We will find that during those periods when there was NO salt taken in the foods, which won't be at any time, with it used in this manner, see?  but when it is not given to take dry, well that very small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] be given - this will clarify the conditions.  Do not give more than three drops at a time, though the dosage on the bottle may call for ten.  Just give three drops ONCE each day.  Do this for five to ten days, then skip a period of at least three to five days, see?  Ready for questions.  Do not, with the manipulations that are given, overtax those in the CERVICAL. Will there be [if there will be] the proper manipulations given in the lumbar, the sacral, and the lower dorsal, FIRST, there will not be so much tautness when the manipulations in the UPPER dorsal AND cervicals are given, see?  because some tautness remains there.  While those that are given in the upper dorsal will aid that condition where there has lately appeared rather an incoordination in the [[Visions|vision]], where these were as squinting more than usual, or more blinking when attempting to FOCUS the eye.  This indicates that pressure in the cervical region, see, but - as has been given - to relax, in THIS body, best for the cervical not to have too much manipulation, but more in the lumbar and dorsal where the INCOORDINATING begins in this body!

2. (Q)  When the body gets these attacks, why does his mouth twitch to left side?
(A)  For the contraction along those of the sympathetic system, which makes for the incoordination through the central nerve system, is weaker on THAT side.  Hence the more CONTRACTION there, see?

3. (Q)  What was meant in previous readings when it said many will call [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] blessed?
(A)  For the peace, harmony, and GOOD [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the body, will bring into the lives of others.  Hence, as has oft been said, those that would lend to the aiding of this body, this body-consciousness, to gaining his normal equilibrium, physically AND mentally, are doing MUCH for their own development.

4. (Q)  In what way will this body give pleasure to those who are interested?
(A)  In the many ways and manners that, physically and mentally, and through the music of the body, bring joy, peace, and UNDERSTANDING, to those directly interested.

5. (Q)  Is present school good for body, and is it advisable to continue sending him there?
(A)  The associations of others, as has been given, is ALWAYS good for the body, provided there is not the attempt for FORCE TOO much MENTAL strain EVER for the body.  Let it be a natural growth, for the MENTAL abilities are there - just dormant, or inactive in the present.

6. (Q)  When will they become active?
(A)  As the corrections, as the coordinations, become more and more nearer the normal.  As to just how soon this will be, as we have given, the greater CHANGE will come WITH the change in the physical conditions of the body, as the change from childhood into those of adolescence, and this will be an anxious period, but properly cared for in the present will bring much bettered conditions that have been said by many couldn't exist.

7. (Q)  Should battery treatments be continued again?
(A)  The battery treatments are EVER HELPFUL to the body, when they are put so as to make an even balance in the circulation of the body, or [[Vibration|vibrations]].

8. (Q)  Have they been given correctly?
(A)  These have been very good.  There have been excesses of other conditions and more strain in this cervical region than there should have been put!

9. (Q)  Would Dr. Bernhardi in Jamaica be the doctor to administer the treatments, or would it be best to have Dr. [[Grace|Grace]] Berger?
(A)  Dr. Bernhardi may DO it, will he [if he will] conform to those suggestions, or the outline as has been given!  If he will not, then we would take it to the other!

10. (Q)  Should this body have codliver oil?
(A)  This is always good for DEVELOPING bodies, for it carries those vitamins that are not found in but very few foods.

11. (Q)  How can this body be built up, so as to ward off colds and stomach upsets that come so frequently?
(A)  As we find, if we will follow very closely along this outline as has been given, these will ward off these conditions.  The VITALITY low, makes easy access for cold or congestion, or for the cold germ.  With the vitality, or with the nerve reactions coming, without coordination, makes for stomach upsets, as do foods that are of the natures as has been outlined.

12. (Q)  Should more food be given?
(A)  If it's kept well balanced, more food will be well for the body - just so it is able to take care of the digestion. The assimilations are good.  The weakness lies in the abilities of the lacteals to DIGEST an over-abundance, but being kept well balanced will gradually make for bettered conditions, as will these things that have been given aid in creating elements in the system, in all the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system, a bettered condition.

13. (Q)  Are present glasses correct, or should eyes be examined now?
(A)  Let's rather follow these suggestions for ten days or two weeks, then if these have not made sufficient corrections for the condition, then we would have the eyes examined and glasses changed.  We are through with this reading.  (After GC said "That's all the questions.")

Copy to Mrs. [1192]
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
