# TEXT OF READING 670-9 M 24 (Museum Apprentice, Protestant)

**Reading [[670-9]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/26/1939  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 4442

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of September, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [670], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [670], who seeks information as to where and how it would be best to go about seeking employment.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [670].

3. In considering the desire for employment, and in keeping with that which has been suggested as to the channel or branches of activity in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would find the greatest outlet, and more harmonious activities in the experience:

4. As we find, through the same channels and branches in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has been engaged in research in the natural history and the archaeological research should be found the proper activity, - through those same offices in which there has been the training and the developing for such work.

5. Continue to be insistent, to be consistent in the application of self; and whether there are the returns in the immediate or not, insist upon being associated, affiliated and connected in such a way and manner that there may be the offices, or office of filling the place in any of such research; for these activities will continue, and especially as may be associated in any portion of the land where there has been the indication of the activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that particular field.

6. Be insistent, then, with the connections that have been made. STAY there, BE there; that there may be the better opportunities for the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 11/14/39 Letter to EC from [670]:
> 
> ..., Md.
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> Arctic Crescent,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> "While the reading of Sept. 26th, 1939 which I received from
> you was fairly complete.  I feel that it was perhaps a
> mistake on my part that I did not submit a few questions.  At
> that time I had just returned from [[Mexico|Mexico]] and I did not
> understand my situation as fully as I do now; for that reason
> no necessary question occurred to me.
> 
> "As the situation stands now I can see more than one
> opportunity for employment; I have made some connections
> with the art department at the Smithsonian and I am now
> engaged in some temporary work for Dr. Sterling in the Bureau
> of Ethnology; looking forward to the time when he will find
> it possible to employ me.  At the present time I am not
> getting any compensation.
> 
> "Now some doubt has arisen in my mind, as to whether or
> not I am doing the right thing.  Perhaps I should stay
> with Dr. Stewart of the department of Anthropology.
> 
> "As some doubt has arisen as to the proper proper
> interpretation of your last reading I feel the need of
> another check reading, in the hope that it will indicate the
> direction in which I can most satisfactorily and righteously
> direct my efforts.  I feel sure that a few questions will
> make it possible for me to benefit more fully from your last
> reading.
> 
> "I know you will help me as always and I sure do appreciate
> it.
> 
> "Mother and Dad send their regards.  Let us know how you and
> yours are getting along.
> 
> "Wishing you all the best, I remain,"
> 
> Sincerely, [670]
> 
> "P.S.  Please find enclosed list of questions."
> 
> R2. 11/21/39 See 670-10.
