# TEXT OF READING 2974-1 F 38

**Reading [[2974-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/19/1943  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 10257

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2379], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., North Carolina.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2974].

3. Now as we find, conditions here are rather in that state where it might be said that the body is not so very ill, and yet not well at all.

4. And the feeling of indisposition has much to do with the incoordination of the circulation between physical impulses, or organic disturbances, and the activity - on the reflexes - to the sensory system.

5. Thus we find those conditions in which there is the lack of coordination of [[Vibration|vibrations]] given off by the central circulation.

6. And the nervous system reflects or responds to that condition.

7. Thus we have those disturbances in heart, lungs, liver, kidneys.  Not that all are diseased, but this circulation being at a dis-ease, we find these reflexes:

8. The tendency for insomnia at times, a tendency at times to be easily upset; feeling easily hurt, "toucheous," wanting to be alone - and yet seeking something that it does not find.

9. This as we find arises primarily from a condition that exists in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body.

10. Then, if the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] were purified - or set in activity as to cause proper functioning, by proper stimulation in those centers from which impulses are received for coordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic systems - we would find the body responding.

11. And then, with that attitude of forcing its self somewhat to become more active in the creative or helpful forces, we will bring much bettered conditions for this body.

12. As to the manner in which there may be brought about these corrections:

13. First, we would take internally one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken, for five days.  Then leave off five days.  Then take two drops each morning for five days.

14. Then leave off.

15. And begin then with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, with particular reference being given to the lumbar axis, 9th dorsal, 6th, 7th, 4th and 5th dorsal, and throughout the cervical system. These adjustments should be given rather in the order given, but relaxing the tautness through the sacral and the lumbar areas, and then relaxing - not making adjustments but relaxing - through the 9th dorsal area, extending, of course, to the 10th, 11th and 12th also, - and then relaxing through the area between the shoulders, on down to the 6th and 7th dorsals; then we may make corrections necessary through the upper portion of the spinal column.  We would take these twice a week, for at least ten to twelve of such adjustments.

16. In the diet, - beware of combinations in which there is too much of starches or too much of white breads, or macaroni or cheese, or white potatoes.  Have more of the whole grain cereals.  Use brown bread, a great deal of sea food.

17. And keep better eliminations.

18. The exercises we would take such as walking in the open.

19. And these as we find will prove the manners to remove those general tensions and inclinations for the accumulations of tautness in the muscular forces, in extremities, and the general "all over" feelings that are experienced by the body.

20. DO take up some special hobby.  Do entertain, and be entertained, more.

21. And we will bring better conditions for this body.

22. Ready for questions.

23. We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file

([[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is manufactured by The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Co., 9 South 8th Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y., and sells for $1 a bottle.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].  Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose, so we may ask him for a report on your case at the proper time.)
