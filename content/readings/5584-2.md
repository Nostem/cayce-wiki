# TEXT OF READING 5584-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[5584-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/21/1929  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13902

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - [5584].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5584].  This we have had before. Now, we find quite a change in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that as we had before, and these, as we see, are for the betterment of the physical functionings of the body; and now, while we find the conditions are allayed, there should be, and should have been, that which would have set the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system, such that there would not be the reoccurrence of this condition for the body.  Then DO as has been given!  We are through with this reading.
