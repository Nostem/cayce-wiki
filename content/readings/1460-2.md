# TEXT OF READING 1460-2 M 18

**Reading [[1460-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/7/1942  Sex: M  Age: 18  ReadingID: 7251

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self through his mother, Mrs. [3914], Associate Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born December 8, 1923, Suggsville, [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Kansas.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '39 - anxieties, disturbances - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [1460].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, these we choose with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this may prove a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling the entity to better fulfil those [[Purpose|purposes]] and abilities with which it entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

5. Not, then, from the material angle alone.  For, [[Soul|the soul]]'s entrance into a material manifestation is that [[Soul|the soul]] may find - through the application of [[Soul|the SOUL]] [[Ideal|ideal]] - those closer relationships to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

6. For, God hath not desired that any soul should perish, but constantly gives opportunities for the application of those tenets and truths that are awarenesses in the experience of each entity; that it may find itself.

7. To be sure, there are urges latent and manifested in the experience of each soul, through the manner the application has been made of the varied [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth.  These leave characteristics, or individualities, that - with the character and with the individuality - create or bring personalities, or manners in which the individual entity meets varied problems.

8. The [[Purpose|purpose]] of such information, then, is to point out, to indicate to an entity, those faults, those virtues; that the entity may be forewarned, and thus - with the knowledge and the opportunities in the earth - apply self, for spiritual and soul development.

9. These are the characteristics that one finds with this entity.  As to what the entity does ABOUT such is the problem, is the living experience of the entity.  Thus the necessity of each entity, as for this entity, to have [[Ideal|an ideal]] - and then live up to that [[Ideal|ideal]]; not only in the material plane but in the mental and spiritual consciousness also.

10. Know what ye believe and the author of thy belief; knowing, or being persuaded by thine own consciousness, that the ideal, - spiritual, mental or material, - is able to keep self, to keep that, to keep the way that will lead to the closer relationship to God, in Christ-[[Jesus Christ|Jesus]].

11. The entity is one affable, and makes self felt in its relationships to others; yet there are the tendencies to bluff itself through, in many a circumstance.

12. Naturally, from experiences in the earth, the entity is a student of, and one interested in, humanity.  Thus the entity is tenderhearted; one easily moved by distress in any relationships with others.  This, to be sure, may become a stumblingblock or a steppingstone, dependent upon the manner in which the entity may use same.

13. From such also we find great amounts of [[Intuition|intuition]].  The entity KNOWS without knowing how or why it knows, and yet all the more reason why the entity should be well-grounded in an ideal; else there may be taken what may oft appear to be the easier way for the moment, forgetting the final development of such activities.

14. One that may use its [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]], then, for either weal or woe; woe if used as a bluff only; weal or betterment if the guiding forces in self are tempered with that promise in the way, the truth, the light, and ONLY in spiritual and NOT in ENTITY'S consciousness or guidance.  For, these may become very, very disturbing if there is not the grounding in those forces that make for the closer walk in spiritual things.

15. In most any field of activity might the entity succeed; that as would have to do with mechanical appliances (as will be seen why), or the greater force - if the entity would prepare self, and become grounded in spiritual understanding - may be through the expending of self as a lecturer, or a writer. But, unless there is preparation spiritually, mentally, materially, these may become disturbing, distressing, destroying - if attempted without preparation.

16. We find that the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]] have little influence, owing to the manner in which the entity has thus far applied itself; though these as latent influences are indicated.

17. Mercury, - a good mental mind, one that can remember or recall easily, if the entity sets itself to such.  Thus it might become a wonderful student, once it had FOUND itself and become determined in the course to pursue.

18. In Venus we find the affability, the ability to direct or control others; though not necessarily a leader, save as in mental abilities.  And with this tendency of "bluff," there necessarily may become a questioning, unless self has been and is kept grounded in spiritual things.

19. We find in Jupiter the congeniality, the ability to interest others - as groups or masses.  Also from Jupiter we find influences in its mechanical ability to apply self.

20. As to [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - while not all would be given here, we give those urges that are the more manifested or prominent in the present experience of the entity.

21. Before this the entity was in the [[France|French]] land, when Mesmer indicated to the scientific world those influences of the power of suggestion over minds, over matter, over disease of body or mind.

22. The entity then was closely associated with that entity, in the name then Marcheler.  The entity gained, the entity lost through the experience; gained in knowledge, gained in material things for the time.  But when such abilities and knowledge were applied to the gratifying of self's own indulgences, these brought material deterioration and mental disturbance, - and conditions oft in the present of attempting to bluff self through.  Yet high mental aspirations, high mental abilities are a part of the consciousness of the entity from those experiences, - but it will require that more courage, more physical determination, more spiritual application be exerted, - not only in self but in the dealings with others.

23. Before that the entity was in [[Promised Land|the Promised Land]], during those periods when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the earth; though the entity was among those of the [[Rome|Roman]] peoples in charge of the varied activities for the distribution of the varied groups through the land.

24. Thus the entity was given to sea service, yet came under those influences of the activities that later were a part of [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]'s relationship, and others of the disciples that were travelers in the later periods of that experience.

25. Thus the entity learned of spiritual imports.  The abilities as manifested by [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]], by [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]], came under the entity's sojourn in the latter part of the experience, when there was the full acceptance, because of the wreck of the entity's crew, when there came the knowledge that these were inner forces of the soul, of the mind-soul.

26. Then in the name Petromus, the entity gained in the latter portion, and aided much in the dissemination of truth, of understanding, of the bettering of the conditions materially, spiritually, of many peoples through that era.

27. As to the present application from those latent urges, - study more and more those periods recorded in the Book, and they will become more and more a part of self, if there is the giving of self to same; not materializing, other than living, living self in the truth and the way.

28. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, when there were those periods of [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]] and of return.  The entity was among those put by the King in charge of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] during the [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]], as well as in those periods when there was the return to the spiritual direction there.

29. The entity then aided in that establishing of the mechanical activities, not only for lifting, hoisting, and the varied mechanical appliances, but aided in the vocational guidance to many.

30. Then the name was Ele-mer-en.

31. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, - these influences, to be sure, are latent and manifested.  Apply self by first analyzing thine own purposes, thine own desires.

32. Then know Who is the author of same.  Self glorified, or is it to be to the glory of God?  Unless to the glory of God, have ye chosen wisely?

33. To be sure, only a few of the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] have been given, but these are the ones that have the greater urge in the present.

34. Ready for questions.

35. (Q)  What should be my life work?
(A)  As indicated, either as a teacher or lecturer, OR in some mechanical appliance - in the working of some mechanical appliance.

36. (Q)  Where should I be located in civilian life?
(A)  That, too, must be the [[Free Will|choice]] of self.  Under those directions of someone, or more, who has an interest in the SPIRITUAL welfare first, THEN the material welfare.

37. (Q)  Should I enter the U. S. Service, or wait to be drafted?
(A)  As we find, the entity MAY - if it chooses - find work, or find a greater service in mechanical ways that will be as great a service as in conflict areas.

38. (Q)  What branch should I seek to enter, if I do this?
(A)  Any of those having to do with MECHANICAL training, or the handling of machines of varied natures.

39. (Q)  Am I very psychic, and should I attempt to develop abilities in this direction.
(A)  As indicated.  This depends upon the use to which such would be put, or the purpose to which these unfoldments would come.

40. (Q)  Should I make out application concerning money due me from Washington?
(A)  To be sure.

41. (Q)  Would it be best for my stepfather, [...], to legally adopt me?
(A)  It would.

42. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self, through mother
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [264]

[Question not asked:  Can advice be given thru this approach as to my nerves, and as to constant pain in head, and colds in head - apparently resulting from broken nose of years ago? Cannot breathe through nose.]
