# TEXT OF READING 4066-1 M 57

**Reading [[4066-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/17/1944  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 11762

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 and 11:30 A. M. 
Taken 10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Oregon.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Nabisco - yes - unless they do something rather soon for this body, we will find the heart block or the arterial block will cause a stroke.  For there are clots.

3. Unless the body keeps quiet, and keeps off of the feet most of the time, these conditions will be rather serious - even in the use of these things we will suggest for the body.

4. Don't take sedatives unless it is necessary.  But those that will keep the heart better in its activities are well, administered only under the direction of a physician who will be in accord with those things suggested here; else the quantities may cause more damage than help.  Rather than strychnine, then, that would dilate the heart, we would advise just the opposite - that causes the pumping of the heart, or the drawing of the circulation through.

5. But do take eliminants, mineral salts; such as, in the combinations of Eno Salts or Sal Hepatica; small doses two or three times a day until there is the entire elimination of the poisons that accumulate in a dilated colon, all the way across the transverse colon.  Take a level teaspoonful of the Sal Hepatica, or the Eno Salts, two or three times a day, for two or three days.

6. Then have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

7. Each evening before the body retires, have a thorough massage using cocoa butter as a means to stimulate superficial circulation.  Rub from the tips of the toes, the tips of hands, and the top of the head, towards the central portion of the body.  Keep these regularly.

8. Do not have heavy foods.  Liquids or semi-liquids are preferable.

9. Doing these we may bring bettered conditions for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  How long will he be able to carry on in business?
(A)  Unless something is done pretty soon, he won't be able to carry on very long!

12. (Q)  Is there any hope of a cure?
(A)  While there's life there's hope, but there must be persistence, consistence and faith in the divine, that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has a purposefulness in its experience in the earth.

13. (Q)  Is the trouble all in the clogging of the arteries?
(A)  This is a result not the cause.  The more of the trouble is in the overengorgement of the body, the lack of proper activities and the colon condition.  Do, though, as indicated and we will bring help for the body.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self (Air Mail)
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
