## Text

Date: 10/23/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12160

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 23rd day of October, 1921, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4337].

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] (?), Conductor; (?), Steno. Mrs. [4337].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this body here before, you see.  [Earlier Rdgs. not in E.C.F. files.]

2. Now there are a good many changes in this body, of the effect of the condition, from what we had here before, but these now are the conditions as we find here in this body, Mrs. [4337] as we are speaking of here, at this time:

3. First, with the circulation, the blood supply in the system, we find the rebuilding of the cellular forces follows the normal condition that it should be for a body in this condition.  There are conditions that we find that are abnormal, that are normal to other bodies.  This is the lack of the force sufficient to rebuild or carry out the forces necessary as we have had within the pneumogastrics in the stomach to produce to that portion of the system needed to rebuild it.  This is better than we have had here before. The blood supply, to restore or rebuild in the system, has inhibited within the system itself through the nerve forces. The action with the blood supplying energy of the body, now of the leucocytes, are sufficient with the character of the hemoglobin as to cause coagulation within the body or the system.  This is abnormal to this condition.  It is normal to other bodies.

4. Through the nerve forces within this system, we find as this condition exists, there is a lesion in the lumbar region produced by overtaxed condition, produced some time before. This has been external or internal.  The nerve ends as being on ends, at the second lumbar ganglia, the lesion that is formed much by the condition of the system, through this and the sympathetic system, produces the headaches, producing through the ganglia [word(s) missing] the gastric juices in the stomach producing the nervousness throughout the body.

5. This condition effects all the organs of the system, all ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] through the sympathetic system.  These become affected as it is an excess created in the system.  We find that they have been benefited through the forces by creating more of the warriors in the system to combat the nerve forces to changes taken in the system, that is, here as with the action of foods or properties taken into the system, is not acted on with sufficient lactic foods in the system, this causing an acid condition to pass in the whole intestinal tract to create too much nerve energy to take up the excess having been created by the nerve energy by suggestion in the system.  The system creates more lactic foods in the system to assist nature to remove.

6. We would reduce in the system, by relaxation, the members to have perfect circulation, and we would apply to the system, the proper [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] through the system to cause the circulation to be carried through the proper channels to trunk portions of the body as is needed to meet with that taken through the brain, lungs and solar plexus by a charged battery.  Apply to the system as would find in this:

7. To one and one-half gallons of rain water, add four ounces willow charcoal, one-half pound Blue-Stone copper sulphate, three drams sulfuric acid, six drams of zinc.  Put these in a glass jar, and inside of this jar there will be place another glass jar containing six ounces of [[Iodine|iodine]].  This charges naturally itself, but should be charged by electricity of low voltage, three amperes, six cycles, one hundred and ten volts for thirty minutes, that is, to give the charge of the application of this will carry the [[Iodine|iodine]] to the nervous system.  Attach wires to outer jar and to the inner jar.  This will be charged to the ankle and to the wrist but to opposite sides, that is, attach to left ankle and right wrist and right ankle and left wrist, each time, each evening for a period of twenty minutes.

8. Do this and you will find relief in the body.  In three to four months body will be in perfect health.
