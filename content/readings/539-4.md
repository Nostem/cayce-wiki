# TEXT OF READING 539-4 M 57 (M.D.)

**Reading [[539-4]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/17/1933  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 3812

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of January, 1933, in accordance with request made by his son [...] and by Edgar Cayce himself.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those impressions and those conditions that disturb this body, [539]; these we have had before.

2. As we have given, the body well understands much of the disturbing forces in the physical functioning of the body, and - as many an individual - makes better applications and suggestions for others than for self.

3. That there is a disturbance in the physical functioning of the body that causes a form of anemia (though the body appears in a robust condition), the CIRCULATION is so impaired by the attempts to meet the needs OF a large body; and necessitating the flow of blood - under the existent conditions - in a much more EFFECTIVE manner than ordinarily, the EXTREMITIES - especially - suffer.

4. The taking of properties [Kofron tablets?] that would aid in creating within those portions of the system the arising of an excess of the red blood cell, and there continuing to be the lack of the elements in the hormones of the hemoglobin to carry that proper and necessary element for the corpuscle itself to be of the normal size, constituent and activity, has ONLY CLOGGED the system rather than AIDING the system.

5. Thus, a very slow process, to be sure, but - as we find, were there added those elements in the iodines - or in [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], as given [in 539-3 on 10/21/32] that would aid the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system to the stimuli of secretions, so that those properties necessary may be created to SUPPLY the hemoglobin - and SUPPLY the corpuscle body itself that element, that constituent FOR its activity, then we may confidently expect - and will find - that there will be the gradual increase in the circulation.  Then the necessity of CLOGGING the system, either with foods or with properties, as to make divisions and thus calling on the structural portions of the system - and the leucocyte or the warriors in the system - to perform the functioning of making those divisions necessary in the system, will become less and less; so that the activities of the general system will be aided, thus reducing this tendency of the heaviness in the spleen, the fluttering condition of the heart, this deadness in the extremities - as in feet, limbs, hands, head, neck at times; and making for a more NORMAL condition for the system.

6. We will find also that the plain Radio-Active Appliance, used by the body in aiding the [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the circulation to the extremities, will be MOST helpful, most beneficial.  To this PARTICULAR body, as we would find, for this to be used for twenty to thirty minutes about TWICE each day would be more effective than to be used for an hour or an hour and a half at ONE period in the day.  Each time attach to the opposite sides of body; that is, to the inside of wrist, inside of ankle, on opposite sides of body - but attach the anode first to the wrist at each time.

7. Do this.

8. Be mindful that the diet is kept so that the eliminations are somewhat above normal and more alkalin than acid.

9. Ready for questions.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
