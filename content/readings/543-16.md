# TEXT OF READING 543-16 F 23

**Reading [[543-16]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1932  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 3849

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 8th day of January, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [543], on Active Membership of her father, Mr. [1212], in Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [543], Sarah Hesson, Mildred Davis, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          417 - 22nd Street, 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [543] - this we have had before.  We find that the more the body-consciousness in able to gain and hold that the consciousness of the body is being rid of those disorders, the more EFFECTIVE becomes that as is being administered for the correction of the disorder.

2. As has been outlined in various conditions as concerning this body, these conditions, this is an altered [[Vibration|vibration]] being met as of old, [[[Karma|Karma]]? - See 543-11] and the body- consciousness, body-mental, body-physical, should be persistent, be consistent, and those also about the body - in their treatment of, their approach to, their associations with, in the same mind; for, "Where two or three seek in My Name," He has given, "there will I be also."  Is this consciousness to be awakened, then seek often - ALL - that would minister with the body in gaining that understanding, that the ministrations as given will bring about a more perfect accord in this material body, and it - the cure - will come to pass.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should any change whatever be made regarding any part of the treatment?
(A)  Those treatments as are ready for ministration may be given in the manner as outlined, provided there is still kept that which must meet the whole condition.

4. (Q)  Does that refer to that medicine which I hold in my hand?  [Lafayette M. Hunter, M.D.]
(A)  Any, so they keep that which has been given, in the making of which BRINGS the change.  [Passion flower fusion - as in 543-11?]  This will only allay, or keep that as has been given - these work together, for it's the same as being administered - or has been.

5. (Q)  Would [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations be better than the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments at the present time?
(A)  Would be better that the drosses of the system be removed.  Remember, as has been given, it is more essential that there be the eliminations effective than in MOST bodies. Would be better that at least once a month, or three times in two months, a high irrigation or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] be made in preference to the manipulations as have been administered.

6. (Q)  Any other advice for this body?
(A)  It isn't so much advice, as counsel and cooperation the body needs!  Do as we have given!

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to File
"    " Ass'n
