# TEXT OF READING 796-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[796-1]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/19/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4839

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [796], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Study Group #9 of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, [796].

2. Now, as we find, there are many conditions that disturb the better physical functioning of this body.  While we find there are periods and times when the body feels fairly well, these are rather the periods when the exaltations through the activities of the mental and emotional self override or overcome for the moment the physical distresses in the body. Then with the returnings of the activities in the physical cycle of activity the distresses arise from a very poor assimilation, with the superacidity that is caused by the lacerations in the duodenum portion of the stomach itself proper, congestions in the liver and those activities that tend to make for the very poor circulation through the digestive forces in the jejunum itself.  Thus there is produced a very slow peristaltic movement through the colon, causing the distresses or pains at times in the right portion or caecum area, those tendencies for the constipation such as to produce inflammation through the kidney and bladder area. Thus poisons are reproduced, and the effects upon the blood supply.  At such times we often have the retention activities, as it were, in the heart - that make for the shortness of breath; periods when the activities through the lower limbs - and pressures that exist in the lumbar area, especially in the vortex of the pelvic center itself - produce numbness in the lower extremities, especially in the left limb; tingling sensations and cold feet, a numbness whether walking or sitting.  These occur from the poor circulation and from the conditions that have been indicated as existing in the system.

3. Then the effect produced upon the whole body is very much in a positive reaction to that felt by the body during the periods of quiet and [[Meditation|meditation]] (as in the present), when other conditions seem to make for little or no difference in the conditions of the the body.  Yet when the body becomes the more conscious - not that there are not physical conditions, but when the body becomes the more conscious of those activities of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] - the distresses are brought to many portions of the system.

4. Then, in meeting the needs of the conditions, as we find, it will necessitate that there be created for the balance in the system that which will not only remove the causes but make for the ability of the organs disturbed in their functioning to become resuscitating in that the organs and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] secrete for the resuscitating of the vitale and vitality throughout the body itself.

5. As least two or three times each week, then, we would have a GENERAL massage; this preferably following the use of the steam cabinet to make for a reaction through the superficial circulation, and those tendencies to make for the heating of the body; not to such a temperature as to become weakening to the body, but the general massage should follow it. Preferably this should be two or three times each week, and at such times make the specific adjustments in the coccyx end of the spine and in the 4th lumbar and 12th dorsal.

6. Also we would be mindful of the diet, that those foods are taken which create more of an alkalin-reaction for the body. Not that these are the only things the body may eat, but this would be given as an outline:

7. Mornings - citrus fruit juices with whole wheat toast, coffee, cocoa, Ovaltine - these altered from time to time. Occasionally, once or twice a week, we would change to the gruels or the breakfast foods that are preferably cooked - especially the whole wheat and barley combinations; and with these would be used preferably the dried milk or skimmed milk if the animal milk is used, but only that which has been acted upon by the heat for the preparations of same for the body itself.  But do not use citrus fruits AND cereals at the same meal!  At times there may also be taken fruit, stewed fruits; but do not use bananas or apples raw - either of these may be had if they are cooked and used at this meal.

8. Noons - preferably the juices of vegetables or meats or the combination of same; and at other periods - two or three times each week - there should be only a raw green vegetable salad taken, combining such as carrots, onions, peppers, radish, lettuce, celery, tomatoes and the like, with which there may be used - if desired or preferable - an oil or a combination salad dressing.

9. Evenings - leafy vegetables and those that grow above the ground.  Have at least two of the leafy vegetables to one that is of the pod or starchy nature.  If rice is used, only use the brown rice - or that which has not been polished. The beans should be preferably those that have less of the starch activity and more of the gluten activity.  If potatoes are used at all, eat only the jackets and that close to same - in a very small quantity.  No sweet potatoes or any vegetables prepared with sugars.

10. After each meal we would, for at least four or five days during the week - but be consistent and persistent with it, after each meal, take half a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth and six to eight drops of Essence of Lactated Pepsin stirred in a whole glass of water.

11. We find also that it would be most beneficial to take a tablespoonful of Milk of Magnesia in the evening just before retiring; especially during those periods when the corrections and treatments are being made.

12. In making application for those things that disturb the body as related to the condition on the face, close to the eye and on the cheek, we would use a compound for massaging, prepared in this manner:  To 1/2 ounce of Iodex add 5 grains of Animated Ash.  Mix these THOROUGHLY together.  This combination, then, would be applied very gently.  For the condition may become of a very destructive nature unless it is ridded from the system.  DO NOT use caustics!  Do not use electric needles on same!

13. Do these things and we find we will bring the better conditions for this body of [796].

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See letter [which was enclosed] to [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] or Physiotherapist.

If everything pertaining to the treatment is not clear, let us know.  GD.)
