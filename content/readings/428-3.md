# TEXT OF READING 428-3 F 47

**Reading [[428-3]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/19/1931  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 3207

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [428].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - Mrs. [428] - this we have had before.  Now, we find in many respects the body shows improvements from that as we have had before.  In others we find acute conditions arising at times, especially the inability of the body to apparently hold its strength or vitality.

2. We would find that more of the manipulations would be beneficial, if they are of such nature as to keep the drainages well balanced in eliminations, and not overtaxing kidneys too much.

3. As much as possible, the exercise that is taken - though do not overtax the body - SHOULD be in the open.

4. Well to change the diets at times to meet the needs of eliminations, as respecting the digestible or indigestible conditions that come about with the changes.

5. The more air or oxygen, the quicker will be the proper rejuvenation of the blood supply.

6. Do that.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Could more [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] correction be done?
(A)  As given, more of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations would be well, if they are kept nearer and even balance or accord in the ELIMINATIONS of the system, GRADUALLY working towards more CORRECTIVE measures along the cerebro-spinal system - but not too much at ONCE!

8. (Q)  What is cause of congestion in veins of left side of pelvis?  What treatment?
(A)  Just as outlined, keep the eliminations more in accord one with another, through the type of manipulations and adjustments made, and we will REMOVE this pressure.  Where these are indicated, well that the rubber bands - or the taping be used for the assistance in the body's being corrected in its muscular and structural nature, that these may be taken care of through the upbuilding.

9. (Q)  In addition to ultra violet ray would any other ray be beneficial?
(A)  The green ray will be helpful, even more than the Infra Red - that is, the ultra violet with the green prism acting over the body.

10. (Q)  Is present diet best?
(A)  As indicated, change these diets to meet the changes necessary for the better eliminations AS the corrective measures are made osteopathically - for these will affect the DIGESTIVE system, and - as given - make changes to meet these changes, see?

11. (Q)  Shall body continue to take the medicine?
(A)  Take the medicine.

12. (Q)  Any further treatment to benefit bladder trouble?
(A)  The changes as indicated.

13. (Q)  How much walking in fresh air is necessary for body?
(A)  At LEAST as much as an hour and a half to two hours out of every twenty-four should be in the open.

14. (Q)  How much rest should be taken?
(A)  At least eight hours each twenty-four.

15. That is all the questions.

16. We are through for the present.
