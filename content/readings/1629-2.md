# TEXT OF READING 1629-2 M 54

**Reading [[1629-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/6/1938  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 7709

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the wife - for him - Mrs. [4434], through his, Mr. [1629], Associate Membership in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                             Charity Hospital, 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. New Orleans, La. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Under the existent conditions, the environs and surroundings, we could not - we would not - change the material applications.  For the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and activities, with the tendency for the esophagus to close, and for the activities of the digestive system to rebel, are too much in keeping with that which is being used to induce same to better activity.

3. But self, those about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] who look to and who feel the abilities for the seeking of aid through spiritual sources, spiritual activities - that the life and the experiences may thus be made more in keeping to fulfil the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is in this material plane - this alone in the present may be able to overcome those physical disturbances. [[Prayer|Prayer]], [[Meditation|meditation]], DEPENDENCY upon the promises and the activities of the mental self for resuscitating forces from the divine within, as in coordination with those who have the abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present at heart.

4. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Wife
"    "  Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/20/38 Wife [4434]'s letter to EC:
> 
> New Orleans, La.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce.
> 
> Mr. [1629] passed away Aug 8 11 P.M.  He was in the hospital
> over two months the immediate cause of death was abscess on
> lung, complicated with stricture of esophagus anemia and
> stomach trouble.  We did every thing we could for him.
> 
> I feel the poor boy is happier now, not to be hampered with
> his [[Physical Body|physical body]].
> 
> Sincerely Mrs. [4434]
> 
> R2. 8/23/38 EC's letter to [4434]:
> 
> 
> Dear Mrs. [4434]
> 
> Appreciate your letting me hear - sorry to know of the
> passing, but [1629] has been such a sufferer, and know he is
> safe in the care of a Divine conscious.
> 
> Feel there is another helper over there, trust the end was
> easy and that all is well.
> 
> Thanking you with all His blessings attending you
> 
> Sincerely [signed] Edgar Cayce

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/30/38 Wife [4434]'s letter to EC:
> 
> New Orleans, La.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> No doubt you remember some correspondence & acquaintance with
> [1629], [[Alabama|Birmingham]] Ala.  He asked me to write you, to see if
> you could help him some.  As he needs the help badly.
> 
> He has been in N.O. La. - 5 or 6 wks.  Been in the Charity
> hospital over three WEEKS.  He is mere skin & bone, like a
> skeleton.  Suffering with stricture of the esophagus they fed
> him with infusions of saline & glucose thru his veins, and
> now, they are giving him the nasal feedings milk & eggs thru
> his nose.  He is much better last few days.  But he has been,
> & IS a very sick man.
