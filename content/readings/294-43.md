# TEXT OF READING 294-43 M 48

**Reading [[294-43]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/25/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2002

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]] and Mrs. [136].]

Time of Reading 
4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. [900]:  You will have before you the rdg. taken on interpretation of EC's [[Dreams|dream]] 10/20/25 [294-42], and that given in this rdg. in which it stated that the sooner Johnson Bransford, Whiteford R. Cole and [900] unite in their physical enterprises, the better.  You will tell us how this may be gone about and what [900] may do at the present time to effect such a meeting and bring about such a united action.  You will also answer any questions which I might ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  [900] has only to await the appointed time, for Johnson Bransford and Whiteford R. Cole will seek him out personally for other considerations, and then the circumstances and conditions may be brought to that point where these may be joined into an association.  This, as we see, will occur between now and January the first, 1926, see?

3. (Q)  Would it be advisable at that time to mention the stock readings, the manner in which [900] became a stockholder in L & N and Nashville, Chattanooga, of which Cole is president?
(A)  Very good to mention same, for it will be along lines similar to this in which [900] will be sought by Bransford and Cole, see?

4. (Q)  Will you give any other suggestions that will help [900] in relation to this pending event?
(A)  These men, their associations, will be of assistance to [900] in gaining much of the necessary expression of self and assisting him in gaining the equilibrium necessary for spreading out information toward others as pertaining to seeking out of truths through [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] and phenomena, see?

5. We are through for the present.
