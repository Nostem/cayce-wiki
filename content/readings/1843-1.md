# TEXT OF READING 1843-1 M 6

**Reading [[1843-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/16/1939  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 8221

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by Mr. [2413], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [5417].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Bellevue Hosp., 
Time of Reading                              26th St. & 1st 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief for this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find, it is too late for any IMMEDIATE change.  Or to attempt to rectify or change immediate applications would be only futile.

3. If there is the ability of those administering to stop the hemorrhage to the brain before there is entirely the loss of activity through the system, when the body has quieted there may be given something that might aid in restoring the equilibrium (provided the body is out from under the present environs, of course), sufficient for the mental reactions to be such as for the body to care for self.

4. As to the locomotary forces that will be hindered, and some of the organs of the sensory forces, - these - even if life IS preserved - may be destroyed in such a manner as to be only partially active.

5. Hence as we find it would be in the present useless to attempt to change conditions.

6. Let those administrations that are being used, then, be followed.

7. And for those who are the closer to the body, let there be much [[Prayer|prayer]].  But - promise not to thy Maker that you would not fulfil in thy daily dealings with thy fellow man!

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mr. [2413] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file (At your convenience we would appreciate a history of this case to the time of obtaining the Reading.  This is valuable for our files, so we may have something with which to compare our information.)

## Reports

> R1. 3/17/39 EC's letter to [2413]:
> 
> Dear Mr. [2413]:
> 
> Wish there was much more optimism in the information than
> there was - know we will be glad to try and help when ever we
> can - will appreciate if you will let me hear from the little
> man - know am [[Prayer|praying]] the for best and I believe in [[Prayer|prayer]].
> 
> May God's blessings be with every one about the boy.
> 
> Sincerely Edgar Cayce
> 
> R2. 8/14/39 Mr. [2413]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Dr. Edgar Caycee
> Asso. for Research & Enlightenment
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Dr. Cayce:
> 
> Pursuant to your reading on [1843] on March 16th, and your
> further correspondence of March 17th, I am very anxious to
> have you follow this up and enlighten me in the best way you
> can, and in this connection I am giving you the following
> information.
> 
> [1843] is a little boy six years old, my nephew.
> 
> He was born in shock, after a short hard delivery, and oxygen
> administered.  He thrived well, but at the age of 2 1/2 years
> showed a difficulty in behavior.  He went to the Kindergarten
> in public school at the age of 4 1/2 years, and at that time
> the Child Guidance in the public school recommended his
> removal from school because of lack of co-ordination, and
> suggested a private school for retarded children.  His
> parents tried that without success.  At the age of 6 he was
> sent to Bellevue Hospital, Psychiatric Institute for
> observation, and they claim injury at, during, or before
> birth.  He was then sent to Letchworth Village which is an
> institution for mental and retarded children, where he now
> is.
> 
> What are your suggestions with regard to this child.  Could
> you give us a follow-up reading on this case.
> 
> Your first reading seemed to fit his case exactly.
> 
> Thanking you, and anxiously awaiting your reply, I am
> 
> Very truly yours, [2413]
> 
> R3. 8/18/39 Mr. [2413]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Dear Dr. Cayce,
> 
> Answering your letter of August 17th regarding the reading
> for [1843].  I take notice that you can give me the morning
> of the 26th, 10.30 to 11.30, E.S.T. for this.
> 
> [1843] is in Letchworth Village, Thiells, [[New York City|New York]].
> 
> What I want to know is, are they helping him?
> Will he benefit by being there?
> How long should he stay?
> Will his mental condition improve?
> Could he possibly outgrow this condition?
> What would you suggest with regard to his condition and
> treatment?
> Anything you could help us with would surely be appreciated.
> 
> Please take note also that Letchworth Village is a public
> institution.
> 
> Thanking you, I am
> 
> Very truly yours, [2413]
> 
> R4. 8/26/39 See 1843-2.
