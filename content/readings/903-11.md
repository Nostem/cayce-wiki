# TEXT OF READING 903-11 F 26

**Reading [[903-11]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/9/1929  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 5684

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 25th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of January, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thos. B. House and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 N.Y. City.

1. GC:  You will give complete information as to the mental, physical and spiritual of Mrs. [903], ..., N.Y. City.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround same.  These we have had before.  There is the normal developments as respecting the body of [903], also of the child.  The normal activities may be expected, with the continual and continued activities of the body as respecting care for self and of the body dependent upon same.  The full time, as has been given, may be expected - unless there were changes from present conditions and indications.  Normal, nominal, exercise of the body in the ways and manners as designated should be adhered to by the body for the best for self and for the body dependent upon same.  Last week of January or first week of February there may be expected the completion of this time.  [Baby son, [759], was born 9:13 A.M. on 2/15/29.]  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is body normal and in the proper condition to deliver issue now expected in good health to mother and baby?
(A)  These have been given.

4. (Q)  What should the child be named?
(A)  That in keeping with the conditions and surroundings as respecting same.

5. (Q)  What may be expected from this child - in its mental and physical development?
(A)  These conditions have been outlined for both bodies - the father, the mother - and these may be expected, that with that same character of care, love, forbearance, as is given for same, may be expected in return - and in that attitude with which the conceiving, the duration of expectancy, the manner of mental attitude towards same, in that air, in that atmosphere, in that expected, may the development of the mental and physical be grounded.  Then, with that care - as has oft been given as respecting that necessary for the perfect foundation in a mental and spiritual atmosphere - will the offspring come forth as the result of that given by each.

6. (Q)  Should mother be warned of anything for protection of self and baby?
(A)  Only those as respecting those same conditions, or tendencies, as has been outlined.  These at present show a NOMINAL reaction, but care as regarding same should be kept in mind - as to the activities of the physical respecting that body dependent upon same.

7. (Q)  Will it be born easily and properly?
(A)  Just as has been outlined!  Nominal - normal.

8. (Q)  Should father of child take trip to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] Saturday?
(A)  If the body so desires, it may.  Be not far from this body dependent upon the body for those cares, those little endearing portions that to the body means the physical, mental, reaction as is EVER apparent from such conditions, such associations, and such developments - for while there is seen that the REGULAR order of conditions in the [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]] of man's inception and experience into the earth's plane is taking place, yet just in so far as this - the all important event, occasion, body, mind, and [[Soul|soul]] - is to each upon which the dependency of that brought forth is enacted - those are as FOUNDATIONS of that brought forth.  We are through with this reading.
