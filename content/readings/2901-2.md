# TEXT OF READING 2901-2 M 33

**Reading [[2901-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/12/1926  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 10155

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2901].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., S.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body, and you will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find the general physical conditions of the body very good.  There have recently been some minor conditions that have made the individual feel somewhat indisposed. Not ill to any great extent.  Just a disturbance with the digestion, and congestion, produced by cold, and the disturbance throughout the system.  Phlegm in throat.  Some little headache.  Pains through eyes and head.  Some over tired feeling.  These are those conditions as we find with the body, though, that it should be warned of:

3. In the digestive system, there are those conditions that the incentive of a normal digestion is disturbed in this, especially through the region of the 8th and 9th dorsal vertebrae, and the plexuses of and from these centers disturb, especially, the pancreas and the intestinal digestion.  Corrections, then, should be made of these conditions, else there may be later expected trouble with kidneys and liver, by overtaxing the system and the eliminations becoming clogged.  Though the body healthy and the general resistance good, this would bring about disturbances, through overloading the system with congestion attempting to be eliminated through normal channels, giving then an over taxation, either through the kidneys or the liver, depending upon the cycle of the functioning at the time.

4. To overcome this, then, we would correct, by [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, these conditions in this dorsal region. This corrected, we will find the general condition of the body much improved.  In capillary circulation, this being overcharged, we have a superfluous amount of energy taken up. Hence the condition as exists in this capillary circulation.

5. Teeth also need some attention, especially those on left side in both lower and upper molars.  Local attention by the dentist will save the body much trouble.

6. Do that, and we will bring better conditions for this body, [2901].  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  You will please tell the body what to do to restore the hair and and prevent from getting bald.
(A)  Keep better circulation, by the correction of those conditions in the dorsal region.  This will stimulate circulation to exterior and interior portions of body and eliminations will become normal, assisting many portions of the body, and the hair especially, but for the specific condition to prevent further development of that attacking the deeper circulation in capillaries about the hair, apply equal parts of crude oil with [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] into the scalp about once each week, for three to five weeks, cleansing same after such [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with a weak solution of alcohol.  This we will find will rid the scalp of this trouble and, with the increased circulation through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, give more hair for the head.  Do that.

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through for the present.
