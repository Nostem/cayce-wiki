# TEXT OF READING 288-20 F 22 (Secretary, Protestant)

**Reading [[288-20]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/24/1927  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 1911

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [288].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
7:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [288], present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [288].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come through those channels as have been outlined for the body, and may be applied in the way and manner as has been given for same.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Saturday morning, Oct. 22, 1927.  "Lincolns or some visitors coming or leaving.  Miss [635] studying a reading, saying she could be just as enthusiastic and 'sold' on it as she had previously been indifferent.  We went out to get a prescription filled.  Later I was walking to school with someone and found SEVERAL fountain pens scattered along the way - later it seemed they were pistols.  Passed some kind of contraption that was going to cause an explosion - ran past it (by myself then) and called back to a little boy to run the other way instead of crossing street and coming on. Turned and went another way, running through a muddy road - everything seemed dark and threatening - passed some people who looked at me and seemed to be in sympathy, or understood that I was making some supreme effort."
(A)  In this emblematical way and manner we find the various conditions of the mind of the body, in its evolution, as it were, respecting various conditions in the mental mind of the body.

In that first [[Visions|vision]] respecting reading and the information as given, that the body-consciousness of self may gain - will it apply through those informations given in readings - that to meet the needs of self, and, as seen, this is the prescription for SELF.

In the walk, and pens, and pistols, representing that of the mental attitude towards individuals whom body has written to, and the change as may come with same by harsh words in the way of that called, or as will produce explosives in the minds of some.  ["Words once spoken can never be recall'd."]

In the muddy road, and the sympathy felt, that with the clarifying of self's own mental attitude there will come that sympathy, knowledge, understanding from others that is NOT as pity, but of glory, or lauding the efforts.  APPLY these in the life, for these apply to the mental building of the body.

4. We are through.
