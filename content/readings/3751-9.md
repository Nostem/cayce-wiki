# TEXT OF READING 3751-9 M 3

**Reading [[3751-9]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/20/1923  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11276

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]] on the 20th day of April, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                    [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  The conditions are somewhat better than when we last had it, yet the outline as given has not been followed out, you see, so do not have the elimination as properly as we should or would under the conditions and [[Vibration|vibrations]] and applications as we have given, see.  Do those.

2. (Q)  Why are the conditions in this body not as good as when you had it before?
(A)  We have just given they are better, you see.

3. (Q)  Why do the parents of this body think he is worse than when you had it before?
(A)  They don't.

4. (Q)  Is the Plantain Root being made up properly and properly applied?
(A)  Not of sufficient strength as we have given, you see, to corral or SERIGATE [segregate] the conditions sufficient, see.

5. (Q)  What druggist should they have to put this up correctly?
(A)  They have one.  He will fix it.

6. (Q)  What kind of electric [[Vibration|vibration]] is indicated in previous reading?  Should this be [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] with the amperes out and how often and how long a time at each treatment should it be used and should it be used over the entire body?
(A)  We have given the kind and form and where it shall be used, how much and how long.  Follow those out.  It is not the best for the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] to be used when the other [Abrams Machine?] is possible to be done, see.  Without that possibility the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] would be the next best thing to use.

7. (Q)  Will they know just how to use this other treatment, the Abriam [Abrams] treatment?
(A)  Yes, the one who gives the treatment will understand. This body would not, see, it is in the developing stage, child, see.

8. (Q)  Should this Abriam [Abrams] treatment be used over the entire body?
(A)  No, you see, it has been given how it should be used. This is [[Vibration|vibration]] and comes to these centers here, where the body is affected.  They will be used in conjunction with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment and the injection, just as we have given.  They are to affect the perfect elimination and SERIGATION [segregation?] and afflicted portions of the cellular forces in this body.  Do as we have given if we would bring the best results to this body.  We are through.

9. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, will you please answer one more question?
(A)  We are through.

10. (Q)  Would it be possible for the tumor or abrasion on the left eye to be removed by a surgical operation?
(A)  We are through.
