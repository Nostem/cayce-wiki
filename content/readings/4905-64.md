# TEXT OF READING 4905-64

**Reading [[4905-64]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/16/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12989

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will tell us the conditions of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] and the operations at the present time, telling us how this may be made a successful producing well.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find while there has been caused much trouble by the conditions as have existed with the casing, and those conditions surrounding the stemming off of the obstructions, and of those things that hindered the producing or making hole, the operations at the present time show some little trouble from that as had first appeared might be overcome - still, this will be, and can be, overcome by the DUMPING of the concrete - or of the more water in the concrete, and by drilling these out as already have shown, see?  and as being done in part.  Then we will find sufficient of this will have taken care of obstructions, so that they do not move by the drilling operations, and as we find there are only the small depth from the bottom of where it was last drilled to the production.  As we see, it will, still, be made a producing well, by carrying on in the way as has been DETERMINED by the operators - will they but carry on.  Do that.  For the production is there, and this may be reached.  Drill out obstruction in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] - in the casing, see?  Pour more, or more water with that as is dumped in the lower part, see? Set - drill through, for we will reach production, will this be but drilled.

4. We are through.
