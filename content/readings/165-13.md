# TEXT OF READING 165-13 M 52 (Engr., Mfgr., Educator,

**Reading [[165-13]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/24/1929  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 655

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [165].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce, Messrs. [257], [4995], Louis Francis and Mrs. [136].]

Time of Reading 
1:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ill.

1. [900]:  You will have before you [165] ... Ill, and [4995] who is present in this room.  You will answer the questions that I will ask you regarding these two bodies and together with their associates in the Health Room, Inc., and the program that corporation has planned to carry out, and any of the developments of that company.

2. EC:  We have those conditions as surround the individuals, also much of that as may be developed from experimentations, and those that may give health to individual conditions through the efforts of such conditions.  These, in part, we have had before.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is our set-up for holding company for health room experimentation correct?
(A)  Correct, especially in [[Purpose|purpose]] and intent as present. There will be, of necessity, changes or developments, some change in the manner and in individuals' associations and experimentations.  As has been given, first there should be the beginning, the associations of individual effort in mind and [[Purpose|purpose]] for such work or such developments; for, as has been given, through these sources, these channels, may come those conditions that will be helpful and beneficial, and much good may be accomplished through the efforts of these. Then we would say, for a beginning, correct.

4. (Q)  In what direction can we best work to get an income from our efforts to support the work?
(A)  These may come through one of, or more than one channel. After the first experimentations there will be the lights, that may be used or dispensed individually, and there will be also those incomes from the use of health rooms, as well as of individual application towards same.

5. (Q)  That is, subsidiary corporations may exploit the patents for monetary gain, as well as health [[Purpose|purposes]]?
(A)  Correct.

6. (Q)  If possible, should we employ one of the following - and if so, which one of the following - to operate our laboratory?  The following being:  C. Dantsizen, Schenectady, N.Y.; E. E. Carlton, of the General Electric, and R. E. Carter, Flushing, L.I.
(A)  E. E. Carlton would be the better individual to be associated in that capacity.

7. (Q)  What work should [4995] do now, in the beginning?
(A)  In the beginning, as has been outlined, there should be first the consideration of those conditions existent in lights that are at present in use, and with that as may be given those experimentations to be made as to that which will give that which will commit most for the individual conditions that are to be brought about.  This the work as to be first considered, as should first be given.  Get that! That is, the various forms of lights used as health lights, see?  The consideration of the usages, the purposes of each one.  These have, as has been given, their good points, their bad points.  These may be overcome, see?

8. (Q)  What work will be most helpful to the [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] in this connection?
(A)  The health room, through which the various forms of lights as are to be produced will be experimented DIRECTLY with on the conditions individually, and the results shown.

9. (Q)  Will the most helpful ray [[Vibration|vibrations]] be of simple or complex spectrum?
(A)  Simple!

10. (Q)  What metallic elements used in producing [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] will be most used?
(A)  Those will necessarily need experimentation for, but they are those elements that occupy as numbers - 1, 16, 32, 64 - Molecule elements as go to make up the waste forces, and of the electric units, see?

11. (Q)  Do these numbers represent the position in the atomic scale?
(A)  In atomic scale.

12. (Q)  Will the new substitute for ice cream and frozen foods that [4995] has a patent to make prove successful and desirable for eating purposes?
(A)  They would!  There will necessarily be some changes in these, as the form of the operation is brought into effectiveness - but as a whole, these will prove beneficial, will also prove effective, will also be of monetary gain.

13. (Q)  Is the principle of the machine sound?
(A)  Sound.

14. (Q)  Is it advisable at the present state and under present conditions to exploit as well as develop this?
(A)  It should!

15. (Q)  How may this new company assist, and should it have any part in the developing and exploiting of this machine?
(A)  Dependent upon whether these and such conditions have been and are to be considered a portion of the work of the holdings company.  Would these be used, then the company SHOULD - and it should be exploited.

16. (Q)  Will very much further experimentation and developments have to be made in [4995]'s machine to perfect it, so this will produce the food he wants it to?
(A)  Not a great deal more.  Only a little time - dependent upon how much time is given, as to how soon same may be developed.  If the principle is correct, as is understood by the individual, it merely comes to the application of same. The principle, as given, is correct.  Merely changes in manner of application of the forces as produce the same in the form as is desired, see?

17. (Q)  Is it advisable for [4995] to cooperate in investigating [165]'s patents at an early date?
(A)  He should!

18. (Q)  Taking into consideration [165]'s patents, is there anyone of the light lamps we might best start with, such as the Alpine Ray?
(A)  This has been gone thoroughly into, and the two as should be developed first have been given.  WE haven't changed!  Do those!

19. (Q)  What part of the [900] & [137] Bros. play, or best play, in the organization?
(A)  In the introspective, and also in the financial end and business handling.

20. (Q)  What part should [257] play?
(A)  That, as has been given, may be applied in many different ways - but most will be talk for [257], and the application of those forces as induce the interest necessary for development into the conditions, see?  That's as necessary as the other, but must be done PROPERLY - the same as the other.

21. (Q)  Is there any further advice the Forces would give to [165]?
(A)  As has been given, as developments and experimentations progress, as they start - or an application is being made, THEN may the individual application be made and information given.  BEGIN.  Commence - and then we may assist.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
