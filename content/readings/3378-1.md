# TEXT OF READING 3378-1 F 42

**Reading [[3378-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/29/1943  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 10819

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 29th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[California|California]]. 
(The following information came at the end of another reading 
without the suggestion being given by [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]]).

1. EC:  Now we have the body, [3378], ..., [[California|California]].

2. As we find, conditions are very serious.

3. As for material aid, there may be added that which will only assist in attuning the body to those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that are accorded in the supplications and [[Prayer|prayers]] of those dear ones about this body.

4. Then prepare this physically.  This is not to be construed as a cure, but as an assistance in aiding the body to find [[Healing|healing]] for the lungs - that are being gradually dissipated by the infectious forces, or the nature of sarcoma infection in body:

5. Prepare Plantain.  Cut with scissors, not the heavy leaves but the tender portion of Plantain in very small pieces or strips.  Prepare about a pint of same by measure, when cut, and pack into a pint container.  Put this quantity in a pint of cream, poured from milk that has settled.  Let this be heated and then spread on gauze.  Apply front and back, over the lungs.  Change this at least once each day when practical.

6. Do apply about twice each week the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Light with the green glass projected between the body and the Light; the Light about thirty-seven inches from the body (only the Mercury Light being used), the green glass fourteen inches from the body.  Apply this a minute or a minute and a half at the most.  This apply, if possible, to the frontal portion of the body, shading the eyes with the leaden pieces, even for the moment.

7. Do that and keep the [[Prayer|prayers]], keep the hopes.

8. We are through.

Two copies to Husband - (Air Mail Special - Wired also) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(Please sign, fill out and return the Membership application [which was enclosed] to complete our files.)

> sending the money home etc.  Now [3378] office installed
> an air conditioner in the office and a blessing in disguise
> discharged me from the navy.  When I came home I found her
> unwell and a trip to the doctor told us she had pleurisy and
> after a month a biopsy was taken and a "Button" test made
> which I told, there were tumors on the lung and that my
> wife would die within a year.  You can readily understand my
> having take our troubles up with some twenty doctors and all
> have shaken their heads and simply say take codeine pills and
> deaden the pain.
>

> his forgiveness and behaving surely some ones [[Prayer|prayers]] would
