# TEXT OF READING 5089-3 F 50

**Reading [[5089-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1944  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 13217

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [5089], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

(Sherry- 
Time of Reading                               Netherland 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time. Hotel, 5th Ave. 
at 59th St., 
[[New York City|New York City]].) 
Sterling, Utah.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [5089], nee [...], born October 13, 1893, in ..., Rhode Island, who is now at the ... Hotel, corner ... Streets, ..., Penna.  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] seeks a mental and Spiritual Reading, with information, advice and guidance as to the development of psychic faculties and the meeting of problems arising in the mental and emotional life.  You will answer the questions she submits, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind of that entity now called [5089].

3. In giving advice and counsel, much of the activities we have indicated in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].  If there would be first periods of this entity making a daily practical application of suggestions made, much more might be given the entity as respecting the hows or the wherefores of the applications given or suggested.  For unless there is the attempt on the part of the body-consciousness of the entity to make applications of those suggestions, and it finds in the suggestions a response for helpfulness, little more may be given.

4. Know in self, first this:  Thou art body, mind and [[Soul|soul]], a three-dimensional individual in a three-dimensional consciousness.  Hence ye find the Godhead, to a consciousness of an individual in the earth plane, is three-dimensions: Father, Son and [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]].  Each are individual, and yet they are one.  So with the body-consciousness:  the body, the mind and [[Soul|the soul]].  Each have their attributes, each have their limitations, save [[Soul|the soul]].  Nothing may separate the soul from its source save [[Free Will|the will]] of self.

5. Then apply in self those things first:  The analysis of self, and know the entity's own [[Ideal|ideals]], spiritual, mental and physical.  Use them for a period of twelve months.  Then call again, there will be much as may be given this body.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
