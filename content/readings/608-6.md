# TEXT OF READING 608-6 F 3

**Reading [[608-6]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/11/1929  Sex: F  Age: 3  ReadingID: 4207

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [635].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Fla. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body [608] - this we have had before. Now we find there are many changes since last we have had same.  Much of the condition as is existent at present comes from a STRAIN the body has received, that brings about the congestion of the hepatic circulation, causing reflexes of the nature that accentuates the activity of the blood supply, creating much of the same reflex as would be found from malarial conditions, yet these are rather the drosses of an inactive force as is not producing proper eliminations from the system, though bearing much of the same nature to the system as would be produced through that of the malarial effect, or affect, in the system; causing nausea, temperature, high hepatic circulation in lower portion of body.

2. Now, to meet the needs of these we would first give that of the proper manipulations through the lower portion of the cervical and dorsal region; also the corrections gently given through the lower part of the lumbar and sacral regions; massaging IN the body - after such manipulations - those of cocoa-butter, see?  This will assist the system - from the absorption as is given there - that of the reduction of the temperature; and begin, after the second such manipulation is given (which should be every day for at least three or four, but after the first treatment of such), give those of Castoria in broken doses until there is the FULL reaction from the LIVER and the digestive system.

3. Do not over feed the body for a few days until these corrections are made.  Only those of vegetable soups or the juices of same, or of chicken broths, may be given without bad effects.  Use preferably Graham crackers, or the whole wheat bread, with such - see?

4. Do that, and we will bring about the normal forces for this body.  These manipulations should be given osteopathically, for there are some corrections in the sacral and lower dorsal to be made.

5. (Q)  Any special [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in [[Florida|Daytona]] Beach -
(A)  Anyone that will do the work properly.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
