# TEXT OF READING 5483-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[5483-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/24/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13700

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of March, 1931, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...] through Miss [349], Mrs. A. M. Pope and Mrs. [959].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [5483]''s mother, Mrs. [959], Mrs. Pope, and others.]

Rosehill School, 
Time of Reading                      Chester Heights, Penna. 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(GC gave Physical, Mental and Spiritual Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5483], and those conditions that surround the body.

2. In the considering of betterments for the physical and the mental forces of the body, the conditions necessarily must be considered as they exist in the present.  There needs be, for the proper consideration for aiding the body-physical, something of the history, or the conditions that have existed as the body has developed to the age or conditions that surround the body.  Had these been considered as the developments began, there should have been a near normal body by the second cycle.  Since the passing of the third cycle, these make for a different condition then to be met for an improvement in the mental reactions for the body; for the beginning of the condition exists from those conditions, environs and circumstances surrounding the body as it developed before the birth.  In especially that condition where properties and conditions that were lacking in the body as developed, made for a change in the central nerve supply and nerve system that made for the undevelopment of those conditions in the upper portion of the body, though not related in that condition or period to the mental forces; but with the retarding of those developments in the imaginative forces, where cerebro-spinal and sympathetic especially makes connection for those in the central nerve system as related to the organs of the sensory system, and the necessity of changing those reactions that occurred in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that were brought about in periods to those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that affect both genitation and the activities to the brain forces, through those of that [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] at the base of the brain - in the 1st and 2nd cervical area - acting with those of the medulla oblongata in the brain's reaction itself; we have not an atrophy, but a SMOOTHING of the brains themselves in their activity WITH the developments.

3. Now, to act WITH the physical conditions of the body, we will find it will become necessary to make those proper connections in the cerebro-spinal centers - especially in the upper dorsal and the whole of the cervical area - that make for the nominal flow of blood forces that act both as upbuilding and of removing the used forces in system, and supply to those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] themselves that operate through system from those glands in the genitive system, with those at base of brain, as well as those in the head themselves.  These should be kept COORDINATING one WITH another; that is, the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system - see?

4. As these [[Vibration|vibrations]] and those connections are made in the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic system (the corrections we would make osteopathically), the vibrations we would set with a low form of electrical forces carrying into the system the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], alternated with Nitrate of Silver - which puts stamen in the nerves themselves AS RELATED TO the glands of the system.

5. In these changes we will find first these conditions to become existent:  There will be a change in the body's reactions at the menstrual periods.  Then there will be the nausea as follows with same - but these reactions we will find, while will be gradual in the aiding of creating an equilibrium and a balance in system, will aid to bring about a condition in the physical and material body as to make for a more active, more worth while, a more reasonable and a more reasoning forces within the activities of this body, [5483].

6. These batteries, or low form of electricity, we will find that in supplying the gold - this should be applied at least once each week, applying the small anode (first) to the 1st and 2nd cervical center, then the larger anode (which passes through the gold) applied to the umbilicus in the upper and to the right portion of same - that we may have those changed vibrations direct to the central nervous reaction from that portion of system.

7. In the application when the Nitrate of Silver is used, we will apply the small anode (first) to the upper and to the left side of umbilicus - see?  and the larger anode (carrying the silver) to the 4th LUMBAR center.  This should be at least once each week, but at least be two days between the applications of those vibrations for same - see?

8. These should come from the PLAIN wet appliance, NOT charged, - for as they CHARGE themselves they will carry the proper vibrations for the system.

9. We WOULD - after the use of same for three to four weeks - make a new solution, both in the vials carrying the solutions and the jars from which the vibrations emanate.

10. We would also give internally (Now, this will not change from those things that have been given at present) the gold made in THIS solution:  Take 15 grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], add to 15 OUNCES of DISTILLED water.  Keep this as one solution.

11. Take 10 grains Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]], add to 10 ounces of distilled water.

12. Each day as the body is ready to rest or to retire, give 3 drops (in the beginning) of the [[Gold Chloride|gold solution]] and 5 drops of the Bromide solution - that is, the Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] - in half a glass of water.  Do not mix until they are prepared to be taken as medicinal properties - see?

13. Keep the intestinal tract open.

14. In the applications for the MENTAL forces in the body, as these changes come about, we will find that the suggestions as are given to the body - both in a normal state and especially as the body rests - will be much more responsive will they awaken WITHIN the body those of the SPIRITUAL ABILITIES of a body.  These should be given in a positive, but not in a forceful manner - that is, insisting or MAKING - so that it becomes rote!  but rather as that NECESSARY, which must be felt as much by the one giving suggestions as one feeling them; for without the giving in the proper manner there CANNOT be the proper reception.  Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Will she ever have complete control of her nerves, so that she can live at home?
(A)  We should find that, with these applications that have been outlined, within six to nine moons this should be possible.

16. (Q)  Is there any special line of study or work that she can be trained in?
(A)  This, as we find, while will be necessary as the reactions come, these had BEST be followed more closely with that as develops WITHIN the BODY'S forces itself - see? that is, as the body mentally and physically is awakened to the potential forces within SELF, these will seek to find expression in the various fields of expression ABOUT a DEVELOPING body and mind.

17. (Q)  What has caused her tooth to fall out?  Will she lose others the same way?
(A)  If the conditions exist as caused this, from the lack of the circulation through the upper portion as has been indicated - this one of the first BEGINNINGS, and while this a long ways from the beginnings, yet these are as signs or marks OF that as has been indicated.  With the releasing of the pressures, and stimulating those that cause the feeling out of nerve centers towards their proper connections, these conditions SHOULD disappear.  Otherwise, there may be the necessity for local applications.

18. (Q)  How often should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  Tuesdays and Thursdays, or that ratio.

19. (Q)  What is condition of eyes?  Does she need glasses?
(A)  As may be seen from disturbances in the cerebro-spinal, with those of the sympathetic system through the body as related to the sensory organism, these are dimmed or hindered.  While under the present or existent conditions it would be rather hard to fit, or to prepare glasses - LATER, in thirty to sixty days, these should be much easier done.

20. (Q)  What happened when the body was eight months old?
(A)  That severing of those forces in the pineal's reaction, as caused by that that PRODUCED the retention of energies to the upper portion of body, producing those of the dryness and the conditions that came about the throat and the head, as well as the contraction in the nervous system.

21. (Q)  Are there any other suggestions?
(A)  Follow these as outlined for the present.  Then, in sixty to ninety days, we would give those changes and the necessary changes for the BETTERED conditions - or bettering OF conditions for the body, [5483].  We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother (2 extra copies sent later)
