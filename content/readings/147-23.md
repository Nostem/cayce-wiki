# TEXT OF READING 147-23 F 51

**Reading [[147-23]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1925  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 625

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find that the preparation as has been prepared or renewed in charge for Mrs. [147], of ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], is nearer to that correct [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary for the assistance to the body.

2. The glasses in their change should be examined again, under the present condition of the body.  Test the [[Visions|vision]], see?  We will find the necessary changes may be easily made.  Should be made by changing the lens from one eye to the other.

---

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/7/25 She wrote:  "Both your letter and Reading received
> yesterday.  Thank you very much for both.  I was so glad the
> battery was somewhat near correct.  I assure you I shall do
> all it is possible for me to do to keep it that way and also
> keep up the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments until you say 'enough.'
> 
> "I am going today to have my glasses attended to.  It is
> almost impossible to get any one to do a thing differently
> from what they decide to do.  If you try to explain to them
> they just think one knows nothing and go right on in their
> own little rut.  They even go so far as to tell me (if not in
> plain words insinuate) that I don't know what I am talking
> about.  Probably not but I do know when I can see and when I
> can't; and that is the principal thing with glasses.
> 
> "I shall keep you advised often as to my condition.  Hoping
> and trusting I may have a GOOD report next time and thanking
> every one of you again and again, I am"
> 
> Most sincerely, [147]
> 
> R2. 7/16/25 She wrote:  "I went to the occulist who made my
> glasses and he would not consider changing the lenses.  I
> then went to an optician and told him what I wanted done.  He
> tested my eyes and has made me other glasses.  I got them
> yesterday.  They blur - not so much, I think, as the others
> but are not clear.  I doubt very much if he did what you
> suggested but I had no way of knowing.  I am trying them to
> see how they will be.  I think they are magnifying too much.
> Seems that way to me.  Possibly I am wrong but I can't help
> but feel they have given me glasses too strong.
> 
> "Have had the battery remade and recharged.  Also a cover for
> it.  If I could only get others to carry out your
> instructions as I do there would be some results.  I only
> trust they have this time.  If it wasn't for the confidence I
> have in you I think I would lose my mind.  I try so hard to
> get them to do things as you tell me.  Don't know whether
> they don't try or can't.
> 
> "I am trying to think every thing is correct and I shall soon
> have some results in my hearing.
> 
> "With kindest regards to all of you, I am"
> 
> Most sincerely, [147]
> 
> R3. 7/17/25 She wrote:  "I have just had my [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]
> treatment.  The Doctor says I should not wear these glasses
> as they magnify too much.  That I should go back to the ones
> I have been wearing for the last two months.  I am quite
> certain that instead of reversing the lenses they have just
> made them magnify more.  That does not prevent them from
> blurring and even though it did in a short time I wouldn't be
> able to get glasses I could see with at all.  Of course this
> is only MY opinion.
> 
> "I was talking with a friend of mine last night and telling
> him what a time I had in getting the battery properly
> charged.  He suggested my using a small Radio battery, if it
> would be as effective.  I don't suppose it would be the same
> but am just asking for your advice.  If I could use that I
> would always know just what it was and not have to trust
> those electricians.
> 
> "I want to keep you informed to the very best of my ability
> that is why I am writing again today.
> 
> "Thanking you once more for all you are doing, I am"
> 
> Sincerely, [147]
> 
> R4. 8/6/25 She wrote:  "I have again had the battery renewed.  I
> trust it is correct and has been.
> 
> "Am wearing the same glasses, the first ones I had made.
> Can't see any better with them.  If I only knew they were the
> correct glasses and weren't injuring my eyes.
> 
> "Have taken 13 [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] treatments - two a week - the
> Doctor said I was not responding to the treatments as I
> should and wanted to test my blood which was done.  They said
> the blood showed I had serious spleen trouble; that I only
> lacked 1/10 of 1% of something (I didn't know what they
> meant) of having Pernicious Anemia; that I had a bad case of
> dry catarrh - that it was the catarrh that was causing my
> deafness, eye trouble etc.  [Hypertrophic rhinitis?]
> 
> "Isn't that a staggering array of ailments (If I really have
> all of them) in connection with my stiff leg and other minor
> ills?
> 
> "They have some kind of battery they want to use on me
> together with the Osteopathic treatment.  Have used it once
> - said I should start immediately.  It is applied between my
> shoulders.  If you think it will interfere with my own
> battery, or is no good any way, which I doubt myself, I
> shan't take any more."
> 
> R5. 8/7/25 EC volunteered (without an appointment being made)
> her Ck. Physical Rdg. 147-24.
