# TEXT OF READING 900-213 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-213]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/25/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5416

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and the [[Dreams|dream]] and [[Visions|vision]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Tuesday Night, March 24, 1926, while reading Bible. "Two calling.  The word 'Detroit.'"
(A)  This, as we see, the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] reasoning, physically, conditions as regarding certain stocks, and, as is seen, there is given, through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], the solution of a condition, that, whereas certain interests are attempting to control the stock of the Hudson Motors, that the call as is and has been made is for the actual delivery of the stock.  The changes as have come will, then, necessitate the advance in price for the delivery of same, for this will be found in the way and manner as has been given.

4. (Q)  Does this mean that those in Detroit are calling stock sold, or stock that they bought, or are they buying stock, or both?
(A)  Buying stock and calling for that bought.  Both then, see.

5. (Q)  Wednesday Morning, March 25, 1926.  "Call your stock. Don't lend it out!"  The answer came, "Stock will cost you

6. We are through for the present.

90."
(A)  This, as seen, the reasoning of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] with the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and is as a condition concerning the existent conditions regarding this particular stock.  Hence that as has been given, why portions should be delivered, and that is sold, see, and the low price taken advantage of, see?
