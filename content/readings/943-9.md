# TEXT OF READING 943-9 M 37 (Promoter, Protestant)

**Reading [[943-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1929  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 5809

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [943] and Dr. H. A. Moore.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Clark, Mr. [943], Dr. Moore, and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and the enquiring minds of [943] and Dr. H. A. Moore, present in this room, with reference to making examinations with the English oil finders for the location of shallow oil in the counties of Dade, Monroe, Collier and Hendry, in [[Florida|Florida]].  You will give whether or not the information given by the oil instruments can be relied upon - and, if possible, explain the operation in detail of this instrument.  You will give the best location for shallow oil wells in the above counties, giving the depth and quantity.  You will also give any further advice that will be of assistance to those bodies in locating and developing this field.  You will answer any questions which we will ask regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies, the enquiring minds, [943] - Moore - oil finder - [[Florida|Florida]] - oil production - conditions surrounding same.  Here we have a large ORDER all in one. The instrument as an indicator of oil may be relied upon. The operations of same are upon the atomic theory of energy as related to the gravity as created in rotation of the earth's forces as related to that as causes the PRINCIPLE of operation; that is, the instrument will give the radial forces of oil when same is operating through the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as will be effective with the [[Vibration|vibration]] as sent off FROM the petroleum production, or from the atomic forces as are created energy in gravitation with the rotation of the earth.  Hence, pointed incorrectly there may be incorrect findings - but under the proper conditions and surroundings, correct.

3. As to oil productions in [[Florida|Florida]] - Hendry, Dade, Monroe, Collier counties - many of these we have had here before. As has been given, and as will be indicated by the findings of this instrument, these should be worked out along THIS line, see?  The radial center from which this should be begun would be in Dade County, and in Section 54, Township 19, in the northwest quarter of the southeast quarter of same. Here we have almost the axis of the producing area.  When it, the area, is guided from this in the shape of a curve, that would be indicated at less than an arch - or horseshoe shape - and, as is seen on the inclines as are from the north and east, and north, according to the curve, see?  staying on the north side of same we will find the radius coming in various depths in different areas - from the 3800, 4000, up to 1600 and to 900 feet, VARYING according to the dips as are indicated from this radius.  Work these out, for this would be more satisfactory - then stay right with it, stick here and you would get it - for if the instruments are to indicate, let it INDICATE those places as show the greater radiation, for this can be made the largest producing area in the American continent.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is the best time of day to use the instrument?
(A)  From eleven A.M. to one P.M.  This being the axis of radiation, offers that of the abilities to not only judge the depth (by the shadow given) but to the QUANTITY as would be indicated through same, see?  The radiation coming from such at these periods.  NEVER when the decline is in the axis of more than forty-five, see?  We have two others reasons why this instrument - this same instrument - as to the factors in this particular area:  These should be tested, for their best operations, between that of March 24th and of July the 30th.

6. (Q)  Any further information that would be of assistance to these bodies?
(A)  Beginning here as an axis, work in one direction until there is shown that it reverses upon itself.  Then beginning from the opposite radius, work to the same point.  This will give the defined area in which production may be found, and it will be a large pool - as well as the various stratums as come in such pools and areas.  Then locate.  Then dig.  Then use that gained properly, else there may be that reverting upon self - that talents given not applied correctly brings damnation to self's own development.  We are through.
