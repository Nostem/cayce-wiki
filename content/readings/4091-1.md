# TEXT OF READING 4091-1 F 15

**Reading [[4091-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/9/1930  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 11787

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made by her mother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. and Mrs. [...], [4091]''s parents.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4091].  Now, we find the body very good in many respects.  There are conditions in the physical that, are they not corrected in the present, must bring disorders that are more aggravating than just indications in the system at present.

2. These conditions, as we find, have to do with pressures existent in the cerebrospinal system, affecting the organs as well as the nervous system.

3. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [4091] we are speaking of:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find shows evidences of a character of anemia, produced by pressures in the system, affecting the distribution of that assimilated in the body. This affects directly rather that in the character of the plasm as produces for the body more of the red blood cells. Hence the numbers in the red SOMEWHAT deficient at times; NOT that of a metabolism thrown wholly out of balance, other than for the nervous forces affecting same, as we shall see.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, here we find the seat or the cause of the disorders.  This, as we find, comes from specific conditions existent in the cerebro-spinal centers, namely: Those in the end of the coccyx itself; those in the lower portion of the dorsal area, at its junction with the lumbar area.  These produce that disturbance as makes for irritability at times; as makes for that condition as respecting those of the periods, that should be - and comes in the system - as affect the organs of the pelvis; as affects at times those of the headaches and the eyes; as affects in others those of the pains across the diaphragm region of the body.  These are reflexes; these to the head are reflex; those to the forces as makes for irritability are reflex.  The greater disorder is in the lower end of coccyx, SYMPATHETICALLY that of a circular or circulatory lesion in the 12th dorsal and 1st lumbar area.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, brain forces are good.  These show well developed forces for discernment, and especially of discernment as regarding minutia or detail.

7. In the organs of the sensory system, reflexly we find through the nervous system - especially those of the sympathetic - tendencies to be that of jerky at times, as indicated to the eyes, to the speech, to the auditory forces AT TIMES; not always.

8. In the throat, bronchia and lungs:  Throat shows the effect of a lower lesion disturbance, as to that of the circulation through the five nerve centers, and at times these produce an aching.  In the lungs and bronchia - these are normal, save for circulation.

9. Heart's action good.

10. The digestive organs show disturbance sympathetically at times, not always.  These would not be considered seats or causes of these disorders.

11. Liver and digestive system show defect through nervous reaction.  Hence the necessity of making corrections before there are used those properties as would cause a congestion or an anemia that would become of a specific, or affecting the organs directly themselves.

12. Hepatic circulation slow, produced most by that of the nerve disturbance in the coccyx and sympathetically in lumbar.

13. In the whole of the system, these - as we find - may SOON be relieved, will those conditions be adhered to as will bring an alignment for the cerebrospinal nervous system, with any form of low electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] force that will aid the body in gaining a normal equilibrium in a nerve distorted center.

14. These, we find, would best be through the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], used with the manipulations AND corrections for the cerebrospinal system, used during the same period, for three to five minutes.

15. Also, as the body rests, at least two or three times each week, use those low [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance - PLAIN - attaching same in a circulatory manner; that is, right ankle, left wrist - left ankle, right wrist - in the anodes.

16. In a few weeks a NORMALCY should occur.

17. In the matter of diet through such treatments, those of a nature that are nerve and blood building - not JUST for weight or for filling up, but a WELL BALANCED diet.

18. Keep the alimentary canal in a nominal or normal eliminating manner, that - as corrections are made - and drosses are thrown in system, they do not find lodgement or cause disorders in other portions of body.  Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Where can she get the best treatment, in order to follow these suggestions?
(A)  From those in sympathy with those sources or channels that may aid in making for the corrections, see?

20. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be given?
(A)  In the beginning, or for the first two weeks, twice each week or three times each week.  Later they may be made only twice each week, or once each week, dependent upon the responses, see?

Do that, as we have outlined.

We will find that those of the low [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces should be applied, as given, two to three times each week for one hour. These may be in evening, as the body rests, see?

Those of the manipulations should have corrective forces, especially in coccyx and lower dorsal and upper lumbar region.

21. We are through for the present.
