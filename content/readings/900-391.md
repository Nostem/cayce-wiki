# TEXT OF READING 900-391 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-391]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/1/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5594

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.

2. In and with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body [See 900-390 on 5/29/28] there are those conditions of which the body should be mindful, as has been given.  Keep up the inhalant, and well would it be for the body to use the gargle of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] alternated with Listerine - both at full strength.  Also well that the massage of the cocoa butter and Cocoa Quinine be used on the throat and upper portion of chest, temple, antrum, and the region about the eyes, nose, ears, all of the soft tissue portion of head - and keep the bowel open.  Drink more water.  Because this produces activity of kidneys is well, for these need to be keep flushed while condition is improved and eliminations begin set up.  [6/4/28 See additional information in re health in 900-392.]

3. For [[Dreams|dreams]] - ready for the interpretation.

4. In those just seen, as has been given, when these come direct, it - the interpretation - is to act, for in action comes understanding.

5. In that respecting those of the Union, this - as indicated - that there are soon to come many changes in this, and, as related, this refers to combines as will have to do with radio and kindred elements; for this has been referred to before in changes coming about in this line.  Telephone, radio, Western Union and such lines, will receive great impetus in the changes as will come about - and these will come about before September.  So this the opportunity, in the slumps that occur from time to time, to act.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] buy Western Union at the present time?
(A)  Only when there are instructions, "Now Act."  This is at PRESENT rather that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should concentrate on these activities.  Those directly referring to other stocks or conditions when given to act, as Fox and rail, these are act at present.

7. (Q)  The body should buy these today?
(A)  In the act there is the understanding.  Do as given.

8. (Q)  It seemed then Gary [Elbert H. Gary] appeared and said, "Tomorrow buy Fox.  Buy plenty of it, and N.Y. City Railway, B.R.T., etc."
(A)  Just given.  That direction when coming from such sources means act in that manner.  Just as given.
