# TEXT OF READING 1713-3 F 25

**Reading [[1713-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/8/1923  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 7885

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce, in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 8th day of November, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see.  Now we find this body is growing more beautiful, physically, mentally and spiritually beautiful, by the physical better than it has been, you see.  We need to keep much of the same conditions as we have given.  The exercises, not so much medicinal properties, but sufficient for the body to keep its mental and spiritual attitude toward itself and others, to keep its vital forces going throughout the whole system.  Those conditions in the side where the stitches or the pains, as it were, come at times, are only of the sympathetic nature at the present time.  The body should come to rely on its own self, in creating - itself - that necessary vital force to give out the forces necessary to keep the body physically fit.  It is well for this body to know and feel the highest [[Vibration|vibrations]] in its self-development, you see, for with all the forces through the spiritual self, the development of [[Physical Body|physical forces]] must come with the creation of cellular force sufficient to give the best [[Vibration|vibration]] to the whole body.  Body only needs to keep this as its thought:  the development of the physical force, through properties given as treatment, as the body determines in its own mind and force that which is necessary for it to keep.  The body is improving; keep it up.

2. We are through.
