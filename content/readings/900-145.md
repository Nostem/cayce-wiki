# TEXT OF READING 900-145 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-145]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/19/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5348

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Estella Smith.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, of [900]. This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], the [[Visions|visions]], as are presented to the body are for the development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in that full well-rounded manner in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may gain the understanding of the phenomena of life, with the various phenomenized conditions as are projected into the physical world of those conditions that pertain to physical, as man, and as pertain to the spiritual in relation to man and man's existence in and out of the earth's plane.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Sunday Morning, October 18, 1925, at home, Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City.  "Numbers 14 and [or] 15."
(A)  These, as we see, are the psychological conditions presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] for study of same and these pertain to [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as may relate to man's action, man's condition, as is seen in an earth plane.  One being a unit, see?  two double, with half power; three with that Universal Force; four weakening with conditions; five made stronger in a unit force; six in the multiple of the three dimension; seven with mystic forces as apply in many manners; eight in the double weakness and strength BOTH of four; nine completing in its relation - see?

Then, as to how these may be studied, in a manner that will be understandable to the mind, the development, as is reached and seen in the entity, [900]; as is seen now in strength, as is given in each numerical number as is used by man in the physical plane, we find this now, to associate same with stock relations, are as these: When a stock reaches, we will say, two or three; that is, sixty-two or sixty-three, fifty-two or fifty-three, the advance begins to five, see?  the lower coming then to that multiple, as is seen in descent to fifty-eight - fifty-seven, see?  running back and forth each way and as given, as the entity grasps the conditions as relate to such, then may be seen, for again, as this may be seen, let each letter in the name, [900], represent all numerical values in the line, then add same together and keep reducing same by addition until the numbers are given, then we see, know, what numbered day of week, what numbered hour of the day, what day of the month, may be that in which the mystic forces, the forces of multiplication, the forces of loss, or of each as they come in their relation, may be seen, see?

4. (Q)  To what do these numbers 14 and 15 refer?
(A)  These numbers refer to these, as has been shown.  Then for the study of the entity and not as an indication to anything definite, save as is worked to the individual mind, see, in its relation to those.  As is given, number of self's, number of brother's, number of mother's, number of wife's name, of each in their relations, see?

5. (Q)  Words:  "[[New York City|New York]], New Haven and Hartford."
(A)  This, as we see, relates to that which will be presented to the entity in the near future, especially as is correlated with numbers as will be worked out by the entity in these stocks, these relations to the present conditions existing with same.

6. (Q)  Now we come to what seems a correlation of the above two, with something more.  Someone came to me for advice regarding prices of stock and I gave him what I thought was good advice.  It seemed that I got this advice from the psychic source.  Down in the Street I heard the man I had advised call up another stock broker and ask that stock broker advice about stocks.  The broker told him that the oils were a short sale, that they were going down in price.
(A)  This, as we find, is the reasoning rather of the physical mind than with that of existent conditions.  That is, the body-mind attempting, with existent conditions, with advice or information as has been given, trying to reason same physically, see?

7. (Q)  I dreamed I was short Pan Pet B (which I am not.  In matter of fact, I am long Pan Pet B).
(A)  (Interrupting)  Which is very good, see, at the present time.

8. (Q)  This did not seem to co-relate.  I said to myself: "Well, as everyone is going to sell Pan Pet, then I want to buy it.  I don't see the necessity of following their advice anyway."  I seemed to be trying to co-relate the advice of Pan Pet Stock received from psychic source and that from the broker's office.
(A)  This, as we see, again that attempt of the physical mind, or of the body-mind, acting in that sense as of a physical reasoning with conditions, just as the condition of desire reasons with the body when desiring that which the body knows, physically, not good, morally not right, or spiritually a destructive force; yet the flesh being weak, see?  the conditions arise, as it were, to reason when the tempter presents self, doubt becoming the first fruits of same.

Then, as we see, the conditions are ready to arise with the conditions and the entity has the opportunity, as it were, to more fully study that information as has been given and to present same to self, to others, in a manner, in a way, that will be in keeping with that lesson as the entity has gained through this individual stock, see?  Then wait on those conditions and USE same in the way and manner as has been given the entity already.  Going up and going down with same, see?  for it is near at hand when a change shall come with this particular stock, varying much more than it has heretofore.  Down and up, up and down, then settling toward the first days - 9th to 10th, see?  fullness 9, see?  10 a unit added with Forces, making an open unit, see?

9. (Q)  Does this indicate that Pan Pet Stock is sale at a special price of 53,
(A)  (Interrupting)  "63, this should be."

10. (Q)  (Continuing)  that I am using information received from psychic force wrongly in being long on the stock?
(A)  As given, this is as the lessons, see?  and as have been given to the entity, the entity may follow same up and follow same down, see?  for we will find the advance and the descend in both, see?

11. (Q)  Does it refer to all the oil stocks except my holdings in Pan Pet?
(A)  It refers to oil as general, Pan Pet as the lesson.

12. (Q)  I entered a room where there were many people, and saw a man asleep - lying there asleep.  It may have been Edgar Cayce.  I was still worried about Pan Pet and the oil.  Many were looking to me for advice at the same time.  Then I said: "Oh, well, I can't converse with a sleeping man as easily as if I could talk to him, even though not a word is spoken, where I get my advice - from Guide."  Seems the others didn't ask the understanding to get that advice.  Then a little boy dressed in rags entered.  I felt sorry for him and putting my hand on his head said, "I'll take you in and love you."  Then a man who seemed to be the child's father came in and the boy pointed him out to me.  I said to him:  "I want to take your son and care for him."  Then the man answered:  "I can support him and bring him up, if I had my health."  Then he showed me a paralyzed arm.  I felt terribly sorry and thought I would like to use my power to convert the man again with sleeping man, and then talk with these people and explaining in language they would understand what they could do.  Then I saw the sleeping man in what [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] be a department store window and crowds of people there looking through the glass window at this phenomena.
(A)  In this we find many various phases of a mental condition, with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and the cosmic forces all related in the conditions, see?  As is seen in the body viewing man in that state wherein the Universal Forces; that is, the combination of mental, of cosmic, of spiritual forces combined, see?  and information then through same, this sleep as is seen by individuals, who through dream reach mental, spiritual, cosmic forces, and with the correlation of same may gain knowledge through which the physical, the mental, the material, the moral, the WHOLE of man may be benefited, see?  As the entity views same, we find correlating that through which the mental-[[Physical Body|physical forces]] has passed regarding conditions of stock quotations, in which the entity is already interested, physically, see?  With the desire to obtain same, the spiritual forces also present to the entity the inability to make others, who are dependent upon self, the entity, for information as regarding same, understand the phenomena as is being exhibited or seen, or through which the entity may obtain that information necessary to be satisfied in mind and know same correct, see?  and the entity then reasons with self as concerning same, until that manner in which the entity may see brought to many that channel through which assistance may be rendered to all; that of education or knowledge for the young, assistance to those halt, maimed or in distress physically or mentally, for in reaching the One of the Forces, see?  through that Universal understanding, then the connection, as is gained in numbers (again), as is gained in the mental, as is gained into the cosmic, as is gained into the spiritual understanding; these, then, may be brought as lessons and as understandings to individuals.

As is again viewed, then, by seeing the entity, or body, in window, this showing one phase of that manner of presenting same to people.  Immediately there is seen that the appeal in such a manner is to that portion of the mental which does not reach to either of those necessary for the perfect understanding, but rather to the mystery, or to the understanding of those who only seek the moment's understanding of any condition; that rather of the extravagant - not of that Voice as is seen in "I will take you and help you," or that as is seen.  "Were I whole I could supply that necessary for his sustenance, in physical and in the mental and in the moral." Then, these are given that the entity may understand through what source, through what channel that building of that necessary, that many may gain the full knowledge of the help and assistance that may be rendered to many, for as is seen, with the entity's abilities of the understanding already reached in material manner, in physical manner, in spiritual forces, the entity may present the understanding to each from that obtained from the man asleep, whether in window or whether in room, among many or among few.  Assist same to give the understanding to him who seeks that knowledge that will bring them in the relation to those Universal Forces, in the relation to God, that will give the more perfect understanding of the phenomenas of life, of that which may be sought and that which may be gained, by coming in contact, as it were, with the God that dwells within each and every individual.

13. (Q)  Now the body [900], with the mind, physical mind, interprets this as an indication that he should bring those people that need help to Edgar Cayce, that the phenomena may help all spiritually, morally, etc., as it has helped [900], furthermore will not only be the means of bringing this to the people but also of interpreting to them the meaning of the readings, as he did [551] for example.  Is there anything that the body may do right now that may bring about this condition?
(A)  Present to the people who have sought, who do seek, that message as would be given about "I have proven it!  I have found Him, my Lord, my God."

14. We are through for the present.
