# TEXT OF READING 601-9 F 49

**Reading [[601-9]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/5/1935  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 4162

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [601].

2. We do not find any fracture.  There is, of course, the muscular strain; and the injury or bruise on the end of the elbow and at the shoulder.

3. The conditions should be relieved by the application of the combination which has been indicated for such conditions (that is, three parts Tincture of Laudanum to one part Aconite), and the massage with the oil, followed by the application of heat over same - from an electric pad or from salt bags kept heated.

4. Also the massage with the oils as indicated would be well.

5. These will make for the easing of pain.

6. And soon we would have adjustments by the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

7. These, as we find, will bring about the better conditions.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery - Also wired
"    " Ass'n file

(We will be in N.Y. after Tuesday, % Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 44 W. 77th St. So sorry about your accident.  Are you coming back to N.Y.? Love.  GD.)
