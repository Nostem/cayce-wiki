# TEXT OF READING 5060-1 F 73 (Widow, Presbyterian)

**Reading [[5060-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/21/1944  Sex: F  Age: 73  ReadingID: 13181

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5060], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Dr. [2288].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5060]; this we have had before.  [No copy of previous readings on file.]

3. As we find, there are definite disturbances here.  These relieved, we will find that the general welfare will be improved and many of the disturbances will be materially aided.

4. Through the periods of activity the action of the gall duct area has become, as it were, full of sediment; not so much stones but sediment; little activity of the excretory functionings as to supply sufficient quantity of the bile for the digestion of foods or oils for the body.

5. For an hour each day, for five days in succession, apply [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver and gall duct.  Use at least three to four thicknesses of flannel saturated with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]].  Apply these directly to the body and when covered, to prevent soiling linens, apply the electric pad.  Keep these sufficiently warm as to cause the radiation to be absorbed through the area.

6. After the first pack is taken, begin taking small doses of Olive Oil, a teaspoonful every four hours.  Keep this up for the five days.

7. Then leave off the Packs about a week, and then have another series, and so on.

8. After the first series (not before) we would begin with relaxing osteopathically the whole length of the cerebrospinal system.

9. Thus we will aid and prevent the headaches, nausea, the shortness of breath that occurs at times, make better eliminations, better activities through the whole system; gradually enabling the body to have the gall duct drained through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] method, you see.

10. Keep away from fats and heavy meats.  Fish or fowl may be taken in moderation, but not fried.

11. Do increase the amount of the raw foods that would be taken, especially carrots and celery.  These we would have oft prepared with gelatin, by being grated or cut and prepared in gelatin, or at times it would be well to juice these and just drink the juice.

12. These as we find will bring bettered conditions.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Are the kidneys involved?
(A)  Only sympathetically.  There is the attempt of the kidneys to carry on part of the elimination that should be carried on by the activity of the liver.  This causes the muscular pains and the neuritis at times, but with the setting up of drainages as should be done with the type of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments indicated, we will find that these conditions will gradually clear.

15. (Q)  Can the rectal trouble be relieved, and how?
(A)  We would use this combination as an injection, for the disturbance in the sphincter muscles and nerves from which the hemorrhoids arise:  To 1 ounce of Glycerine add 2 drops of Carbolic Acid.  Stir or beat this very thoroughly, as with mortise and pestle.  Then add 2 ounces of Usoline or Nujol. Use this in an ear syringe.  Inject half of this amount at the time, in the rectal area.  Do this and then apply the second quantity about a week to ten days later.  This should be sufficient.  But if there should recur the condition, prepare in the same proportions and manner, and use some six to eight weeks later.

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Let us have the name of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

> B1. 1/26/44 Letter:  "Some time ago I wrote you for my sister
> Mrs. [5059].  I was writing for her for she cannot see well
> enough to write herself.  I am Mrs. [5060], and we both knew
> you during the days you lived in Bowling Green.  You gave
> a reading for [[Lord's Prayer|our father]], Mr. [4939], and also for a Mrs.
> [4940] in our home.  Afterward we were present in a reading
> for a blind girl, a Miss [4941], and also a reading in search
> of a lead mine on the [4941] place.  In all these readings,
> I served as stenographer, and from then till now have had
> profound faith in this wonderful power which you possess, and
> have always believed, and do now, that the Lord is trying to
> give the world something wonderful through you and except for
> the denseness and the bigotry of the mind of man, the world
> would have more understanding of it by now.
> 
> "We have read '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]', and Mrs. [5059] had it
> reviewed in the Current Events Club by a friend as her
> program.  Because of her eyes, she was not able to do it
> herself.  It was greatly enjoyed.  Dr. [4946] by the way, was
> present.  I told in that meeting the things I had seen you
> do.  We have recently been so interested in your work for
> Dr. [2288], a nephew.  They are busy trying to get him off to
> [[Arizona|Arizona]].  At present he is hardly strong enough to start, but
> is gaining a little.  Forgive this lengthy chat...  Enclosed
> find check for membership in the ARE; also $10. check for
> a reading for Mrs. [5059], my sister.  This is as we have
> understood the requirement from your literature.  My sister
> Mrs. [5059] has almost lost her eyesight.  Doctors hold out
> little hope and say there is little that can be done.  If
> only we can find a remedy in time, before she goes blind.  At
> present nothing is being done.  She is taking [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]], her
> own decision and judgement.  I understand you are far behind
> in your readings.  We must not ask for or expect special
> favors, for there is urgency in every case, but we shall be
> so glad when you get to her reading that we may have more
> hope.  I have the belief that you are under direction of the
> Lord and are walking in His will."  Sincerely yours, [5060]
>
