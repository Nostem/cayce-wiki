# TEXT OF READING 900-397 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-397]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/7/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5600

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.              Deal, N.J. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  [[Dreams|Dreams]], [[Visions|visions]], as come to the body come in those ways and those channels as has been outlined for same.  (In undertone)  So many crowd in and say something - it's hard to get here, you see - so many - so many - come from the sea!  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  Sometime ago.  I was thinking of those individualities that control Cayce while the physical submerged, of that one, or those who prevent too great a pressure or disorder by the many spiritual individualities who would express themselves physically.  It must be that spiritualities that have stored medical experience do the controlling, I thought.  Then when I myself submerged my physical faculties my consciousness detected a spiritual individuality addressing me, saying: "Yes.  One of the controls is in [[Germany|Germany]]."
(A)  Well, that's right!  (In undertone)  Such a crowd of - This will make the head ache!

3. You will dismiss these suggestions from your mind.  (Gave suggestion for him to wake up.)
