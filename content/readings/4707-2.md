# TEXT OF READING 4707-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4707-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/5/1911  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12663

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 5th day of June, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno. [4707].]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M.                                          ..., Ky.

1. LBC:  You have before you [4707] who is in this room with you.  Go over her carefully, examine her thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with her, and what her condition is now.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had her here before.  No difference between it and what we had before.  No change in the condition proper.  Of course we have it producing effects, and we have some change, of course in the material part of the body, or building forces in the blood supply, but no changes here in the lesions formed, or difference in the condition of the upper cortex of the stomach.

3. (Q)  What is the cause of this trouble?
(A)  The condition of the stomach.  The upper orifice of the stomach, the outside, or cortex portion, has become hardened. The condition existing in the body itself is from time back, and has produced this condition.  Loading at times of the stomach, or taking into the stomach to produce a dilation of the walls, or excess of gastric juices formed, and force from here produces the stomach condition or blood supplies from the 6th and 7th dorsal, and that goes to there and then we have the condition of the contraction of the muscular force, same as we had produced here from time back from the 11th and 12th dorsals, producing the drawing condition to the lower extremities.

4. (Q)  What is the condition in the pelvic region?
(A)  These are reflexes or cortex conditions from the condition we have from the lumbars.  They are only reflexes from the condition we have here now.  The condition first began here from the condition in the stomach.  We had the condition then from the 11th and 12th dorsal from the 3rd and 4th lumbars, producing the condition we have through the pelvis.  The condition of the pelvis now is retrograded from the condition we had before.

5. (Q)  What treatment would you prescribe to relieve this?
(A)  Same things as we had before.

6. (Q)  What type of electricity do you prescribe?
(A)  Static.

7. (Q)  Which side of the body needs treatment most?
(A)  The right side.

8. (Q)  What kind of clay do you prescribe:  red clay or white clay?
(A)  White clay.

9. (Q)  How would it be prepared?
(A)  It is already prepared.

10. (Q)  How often should she be treated with electricity?
(A)  Every other day.

11. (Q)  Put the clay to her spine?
(A)  From the 2nd cervical and the ninth dorsal. Applications at the time of rest, producing a drawing, or a cortex producing a reaction or retarding or restimulation to the effects to the nerve supply at the time of repose.
