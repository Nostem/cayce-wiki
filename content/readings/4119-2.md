# TEXT OF READING 4119-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4119-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/28/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11820

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4119].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                          ... S. LaSalle St., 
11:00 A. M.                              [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois.

1. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in this body throughout the physical are very much improved from those as we had here before.  That condition we find in the sensory system in the auditory ganglion, the roaring, or disturbance in ear, is produced by the lack of elimination in the system, and in that as eliminations has caused congestions in the system. The Eustachian tubes have become clogged, and show some inflammation.  These cleansed either by local treatment or by massage and [[Vibration|vibration]] from electrical forces, or violet ray, will remove this condition from the system.  Either will do this properly and relieve this condition in system.

2. Or it may [be done] by changing the properties in the system; that is, take now those properties as found in sanatogen, used with milk.

3. And take head and neck exercises night and morning.  Head forward three times, back three times.  Circle to the left six times.  Circle to the right six times.  This will also remove this congestion and clear the system through eliminations and [[Vibration|vibrations]] being properly accorded in the system.

4. Do one or the other of these, if we would bring the body to the normal and best.
