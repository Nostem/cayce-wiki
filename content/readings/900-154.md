# TEXT OF READING 900-154 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-154]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/31/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5357

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
9:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... Street, N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the date which I will give you. Also - [900-150] the reading given last Sunday morning for this body, Oct. 25, 1925, regarding L & N and Southern Railroad stock.  You will give the interpretation of these [[Dreams|dreams]], co-relating them with the information as given, and answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, and the enquiring mind, [900], here, with the [[Dreams|dream]] and the reading as given regarding L & N and Southern Railroad and other information.  Ready for dream.

3. (Q)  Saturday Morning, Oct. 31, 1925.  "I saw the number 200 - that divided by some number, the result of division multiplied by a number, the result of which equaled 145."
(A)  This an attempt of the body-mind to co-relate and gain that key to figures, as has been outlined in the manner to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and as seen, this co-related with conditions may be gradually gained by entity.  Well this be worked out, as it will come again - Sunday Morning, November 1, 6:45.

4. (Q)  "Saw the number 126."
(A)  This, as we see, the co-relation again of numbers, but in this instance refers to those conditions as exist concerning L & N Railroad stock, at which figure there was given to one as how to proceed regarding same.  L & N again we find will, to-day, Saturday, reach that figure.  Well that same be taken in then by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], see?  and those concerned with same, for we find L & N will eventually, in the change as given, come to those figures as given.  Only acting now in the manner as given, going beyond that even thought by anyone concerning same.  As we see, co-related with those conditions as given in last Sunday's dream, for we find that L & N will recede and rise again.  Well that same be sold at the high figure for the receding price, but taken in at the present price - 126, see?  The L & N and Southern both have acted in a manner as first given regarding these stocks, yet we will find Southern advancing to the 122 and then receding again, for this will happen. Hold those stocks for advance and make those combinations and turns with the L & N, as given.  Then taking advantage of same in the new turns, as we see will occur, see?  for this will recede, then going to 145, see?  Again, receding, then going still higher.  In these numbers and changes we will find the key to that as has been given in numbers, see?

5. (Q)  In the reading last Sunday, it was given L & N would go to 117 Friday, then advance to 130.  When should we sell stock short and at what price?
(A)  When the advance has begun to recede.  This we will find will be many weeks yet, or several weeks, before this general advance begins.  (the following in undertone - scarcely audible)  Division of 20 - with 104 and 5 - 9 and 10 and 5 -

6. (Q)  Does this mean that [137] and I take in the stock we are short for the account at 126?
(A)  126, which will occur TO-DAY, Saturday, see?

7. (Q)  The voice:  "Corn Products and Cream Cheese."
(A)  This gives that connection, as it were, to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of the advance in the Corn Products and in the Dairy Products, for as both are seen, and as has been given, these will relate to food values, see?  Then both would be a buy at the prevailing price, see?  Rather begin though Monday than Saturday.

8. (Q)  As given, Southern R. R. would go to 122 and L & N 117 -
(A)  (EC breaking into first sentence)  This will go to 122, and L & N will go to 117.

9. (Q)  (Continuing after rest is read)  Instead L & N has gone over 130 and Southern to 114.  What is advisable at the present circumstances for [900] and Edgar Cayce to do with Southern R. R. stock they are long and L & N they are short?
(A)  Make the combination or change at 126 on L & N, for the change, or when the new change will come, hold for the long prices on Southern Railroad, for we find this will advance to the 122.  Do that, see?

10. That is all the questions.

11. We are through.
