# TEXT OF READING 2197-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2197-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/12/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8909

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by cousin, Mrs. [...], via Miss [288].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                       Brice Hospital, Ward 4, 
3:00 P. M.                            Tuscaloosa, [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Now, we find with this body the physical is very good throughout.  There is in the physical those obstructions to the indentations of the action of the nerve system that produces aberrations and hallucinations in the manifestations of the mental, and the physical becomes abased with these conditions.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body. First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, there are abnormal conditions.  Most of this, however, is a normal force, for that that has been accorded the system as sedatives to the nerve centers.

4. The nerve systems in the physical we find that depression first caused in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|Lyden]] [Leydig] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] that pressed, or indentations made on the perineurial and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve center connected with the Lyden [Leydig] gland.  This then gives the hallucinations in the [[Vibration|vibration]] to the brain center or through the cerebellum oblongata, you see.  In the impression as this receives, there comes those conditions of melancholia, of self-destructive forces, of aberrations, of depression as received and hallucinations to all the functioning of the sensory organism, through which these nerve connections find manifestations with the pineal nerve in its course through the system.  The excess of these [[Vibration|vibrations]] are producing the indentation or the lack of those in the sensory system.  These finding manifestations abnormally.

5. IN THE ORGANS OF THE SYSTEM, we find most function properly. Those organs of the pelvis function abnormal, there having been suppression through the functioning of the genital organs, producing first obstruction in the gland, and to bring the normal to this body would be necessary by operation, to remove these glands in the pelvic organs of genitation [genitation - gestation?].  This will remove the pressure, and with the submerging of the whole sensory system the control over the physical may be brought to the normal functioning.

6. Do that.  The sooner the better.  This may be accomplished by Gilbert in Baltimore.
