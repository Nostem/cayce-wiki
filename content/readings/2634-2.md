# TEXT OF READING 2634-2 F 23

**Reading [[2634-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 9680

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. 
Taken March 5, 1942,                          At home in 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are a great many improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]], but much yet needs to be accomplished before the body would be able to carry on in its best physical, mental and spiritual manner.

4. True, the sources of the infection in the blood supply are abated.  But the result of [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]], or the creating of sufficient from the vital energies of the leucocyte to destroy same, has left definite conditions that are yet to be reckoned with.

5. To be sure, through the lungs, the liver, the kidneys - the sources through which those destroyed bacilli are to be eliminated - are to be kept most in their state of ability not only to combat these influences but to create sufficient for the supplying of necessary elements throughout the body to replenish the supply of red blood cells.

6. For, a natural form of anemia exists from same.  And the inclinations for pains in joints, even in the bone structure of extremities themselves, give distress at times.  Also there is the arising of a temperature occasionally, especially in the evenings; and those tendencies of night sweats, and then the tiredness afterwards.

7. These arise from the inability of the body to at all times cope with the conditions, though resistances are gradually being builded.  But it will require a great deal of patience and persistence, unless there is added that which may aid in more quickly establishing a better equilibrium.

8. We would, then, administer these; and if these are adhered to, we do not find the necessity of injections.  While these are helpful, to be sure, they carry with same a great deal of necessity for precautions that may not always hold good.

9. Then, as soon as practical, begin with these; to be sure keeping up all other treatment until ready to begin these:

10. Take each evening a teaspoonful of ACIGEST stirred in a glass of raw milk.

11. Every other day, at the noon meal, take at least sufficient [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] to spread upon or to butter nicely a whole wheat cracker.

12. We would begin IMMEDIATELY with taking the [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]] as medicine.  Do not take more than a teaspoonful at a time, but take it regularly every three hours.  Not that the body should be aroused through the night to take same, especially, but if awake give it during the evening or night.

13. DO have, after the sponge bath of morning, a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the whole cerebrospinal system, and of the limbs and especially the joints of ankle and feet, knee, and across the sacral areas of the back and through the hips, with an equal combination of Peanut Oil and Witchhazel.  These do not mix easily, but shake together, pour in a saucer, dipping the fingers in same to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body.

14. Do keep up good eliminations, and at least once a week have a good, [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] to keep the colon cleansed.

15. With the regular diet, do have plenty of the whole wheat cereals, - these preferably of evenings rather than of mornings; these warm, with milk or cream, - not too much sugar.

16. Of mornings have plenty of citrus fruit juices, with rice cakes, corn cakes or the like, with honey.

17. At the noon meals have vegetable soups and the like.

18. In the evening or at the noon meal, stir half of a yeast cake in a small glass of tomato juice and drink.

19. These should supply sufficient of all the vital forces and energies, until there has been sufficient activity of the [[Calcios|Calcios]] (or calcium) for strengthening the body-forces in lungs, heart, liver, kidneys, and sufficient activity of the Acigest to so purify and cleanse the system as to take away the destructive forces that may become active in the body-influences unless eliminated.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  What should be done to prevent gas and to stimulate better eliminations?
(A)  The type of the diets, including all of these suggested, should eliminate these disturbances.

22. (Q)  Is the blood stream clear of the strep infection?
(A)  If the blood stream were entirely clear we would not have the disturbance through the circulation as to cause these conditions.  As indicated, the sources of infection are allayed, not entirely eradicated.   Most arise from DESTROYED bacilli - and must not be allowed to find lodgment, either in the organs or in the extremities.

Hence the use of those properties given.

Do these consistently, for at least six weeks - and then we would give further instructions.

Keep the mental attitude of [[Prayer|prayer]], of being helpful, being good - not only passively but actively.

23. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note:  Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]] are both manufactured and distributed by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, Long Island, N.Y.  Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form best assimilated, and sells for $1 a bottle.  Calcios is free calcium in a form best assimilated; it comes in a syrupy paste that sells for $2.50 a jar.

Let us know when these treatments have been followed for a six-week period, so we may make an appointment for a Check Reading to give further instructions.)
