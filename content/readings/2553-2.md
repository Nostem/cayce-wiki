# TEXT OF READING 2553-2 F 26

**Reading [[2553-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/22/1923  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 9552

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:20 P. M.                                      [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find conditions are somewhat changed in the body from those as we had before, though those suggestions as given for the betterment have not been carried out in this system.  The lack of the vital forces necessary to produce the storing energy, as it were, through the circulation still show their subnormal condition in the body, hence the condition in the blood supply is still below the normal.  The mental forces in conjunction with the nerve systems are better than we had before.  The reaction in the system is better.  The vitality in the body, that, that gives the vitality forces necessary have not improved except as the nerve energy has been more equally distributed over the system, though the body in its entity [entirety?] is weaker than we had before.

2. We would give those properties more to furnish carbon in the system, and to give more vital energy to the blood supplying forces creating more of the red blood forces throughout the body, more outdoor exercises, more open air for the body. Let the medicinal properties for the system be those through the food values than of medicinal properties direct, for with the energy received in the air and water for the system, the body will be better than by addition of forces to be overcome.  Let be in the diet for the last one meal of the extract of beef, rather than of the flesh itself, or that of beef and wine would be well for this body - Beef, iron and wine with rather the extract of juices of the beef, also, with this action, we let them be taken together, you see, that is, the dosage of iron, beef and wine, taken before meals of beef juices.  Then, carbons in the form of green vegetable matter - not overloading the system at any time, but keep the assimilation well in the body.  The [[Vibration|vibration]] of a light nature over the system would be very good, the electric forces or that as found in the violet ray forces over the length of the spinal column.  We will equalize centers and give vitality to the body.  Do that.

3. (Q)  What are the conditions of the body's teeth?
(A)  There is one that needs some attention.  That is local conditions.

4. (Q)  Which one is it?
(A)  One of the molars here.

5. (Q)  Should it be pulled or filled?
(A)  Be better if it was removed.

6. (Q)  How often should this beef, iron and wine be taken?
(A)  We have given it.  Only one dose each day before the meal of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] itself.  The [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] preferred to the flesh itself.  Better if made fresh each day.

7. (Q)  What are the conditions of this body's lungs?
(A)  Very good, when we consider the condition of the blood supply throughout.  Not organic conditions - neutralized conditions of the blood forces, hence the necessity of that, that produces red blood cells.  The beef is blood and muscle builder.  The iron, the stimulation in the blood supply.  The wine as the assimilator for the system and acts with the juices of the duodenum and pancrean forces.

8. (Q)  Should the beef be made into a broth?
(A)  The beef prepared in this manner:  Select that of fresh steak or meat, little fats, cut in cubes or small particles. Put inside of glass jar, no water.  This set in water and boiled until the juice itself is extracted from this meat. Take out, press juice out, then season, with salt and cayenne, no black pepper.  Use only the juice, not meat.

9. (Q)  Is the beef, iron and wine a prepared medicine?
(A)  Wyeth's Beef, Iron and Wine.  One dose half an hour before the meal of extract of beef and crackers or small broken bread, you see.  Cereals, fruits and vegetables. Only this for the body, no meats, no fats.
