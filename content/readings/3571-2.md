# TEXT OF READING 3571-2 F 4

**Reading [[3571-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/23/1944  Sex: F  Age: 4  ReadingID: 11059

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Would removal of tonsils and adenoids help relieve the condition?
(A)  Removal of tonsils and adenoids would only relieve some tensions.  Yes, we would remove tonsils and adenoids, but we would not depend upon this only to bring back speech or the associations and connections in sympathetic nervous system and the cerebrospinal, but follow through with those things suggested for correcting the conditions.

4. (Q)  Will she, in time, learn to talk?
(A)  Provided the corrections are made as indicated.

5. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
