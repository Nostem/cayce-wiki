# TEXT OF READING 4906-5

**Reading [[4906-5]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/22/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13000

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of January 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4906].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Section 8, Township 14 North, 
Time of Reading                Range 14 E. in 
3:00 P. M.                     Okmulgee County, Oklahoma.

1. LS:  Now, you have before you the lands located in Section 8, Township 14 North, Range 14 East in Okmulgee County, located about seven miles North of the town of Morris, and is in the section East of the one on which you have just given a reading [See 4906-4].

2. EC:  This, we find, is an extension of the same pools that will be encountered in seven, though in this the structural conditions and the handling of the oil and quantity will be increased.  Not the heavy, or as heavy pressure in gas, but sufficient to make this a productive proposition in this field.  Do not get too far North or over the north side of anti-cline, else the structural forces, through which drilling would be necessary, would cause some trouble in drilling.  These, we see, will be encountered with the greater depth and in heavier sands than that to the West. Drill this.  We will find we will produce thousands of barrels of oil of high grade from this territory.

3. We are through.
