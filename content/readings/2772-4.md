# TEXT OF READING 2772-4 M 37

**Reading [[2772-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/8/1943  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 9925

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [2772] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2772] this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  The [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in my neck seem swollen in the morning when I arise, especially on the left side.  Is this true; if so, from what and how can it be cured?
(A)  This is true, or the FULLNESS appears; from an unbalanced condition which has existed through the body, and that tends to revert to the disturbance between the circulation of heart, liver, kidneys and lungs.

If the suggested treatments are kept up occasionally, for the creating of a normal balance, this should disappear.  Or if there would be used the Radio-Active Appliance, this would be beneficial in equalizing the circulation; this used of evening when ready to retire.  Through the manner this instrument is constructed, this is not using other than the [[Vibration|vibrations]] or the electrical forces of the body, charging or creating in the Appliance the activity for the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to form a magnetic field ("feel"?) and thus equalizing the balance in the extremity circulation.  Use the attachments to the wrist and the ankle, opposite sides of the body; but use the same anode or connection as it circulates about the body; keeping these very clean, for the radial activity of the body in its circulation produces a film upon the plates as they are attached to the body.

This will require a little longer, of course, but the Appliance would keep down this tendency.

5. (Q)  Am I allergic to sea food; if so, which kinds?
(A)  As we find, not allergic to sea foods; it is rather allergic to combination of foods that are taken with sea foods.  These cause the tendencies for a quick superficial circulation.  It is the excesses of an activity of the vital forces that come from sea food combinations.

6. (Q)  What combination has this effect with sea foods?
(A)  Any sort of sweets or any of those foods that are of an acid nature.

7. (Q)  Am I allergic to any other type of food?
(A)  Only as to combinations, as we find.  Certain types of berries, if combined with other foods.  Any foods that carry quantities of potash with same, the body would tend to be allergic to; with combinations that are at variance to potash.

8. (Q)  What caused the numbness and tingling sensation of my right arm, shoulders and fingers?
(A)  This unbalanced circulation in the central circulation, see?

9. (Q)  I have a devitalized upper left second bicuspid tooth. Is it causing any pathology and therefore harmful to my health?
(A)  We do not find it so.

10. (Q)  Why do I fear water so that I do not swim far in water over my depth?
(A)  This is from a condition that existed in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] in very young childhood.  Suggestions to self, or forcing of self to overcome such, might discharge this from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] influence.

11. (Q)  Is this why I am afraid to dive or jump into the water even if I am only one foot above the water?
(A)  The same.

12. (Q)  Why have I had several colds this winter, even after having taken inoculations against colds?  Didn't it give me an immunity?
(A)  Inoculation is merely the attempt to create a condition in which it is necessary for the system to produce the certain form of virus to encase the devitalized cold germ injected.  There is an unbalanced condition still existent, which is part of the general disturbance that was first indicated by the conditions in the central circulation, and these do not adhere to such activity.  For, injections are more to the superficial while the causes of the acidity or the unbalance is from the deeper circulation.

13. (Q)  What is causing me frequent slight headaches lately?
(A)  These as we find are from an unbalanced condition in assimilation, or digestion.  Watch the diet more, or better, and keep in accord with that which will agree the better with the body - and these should disappear.

14. (Q)  What is causing me mental unrest and as a result disturbing [[Dreams|dreams]]?
(A)  This unbalanced condition, - not wholly coordination between the superficial circulation, or the circulation that is the more active in the sensory forces, and the deeper circulation to the organs of the body.  Thus the activity of the mind, or the sympathetic and cerebrospinal force of mind, causes [[Dreams|dreams]], see?

15. (Q)  What brings on sneezing attacks to me?
(A)  This we do not find other than the environment of the body at the time.

16. (Q)  What special environments cause the sneezing?
(A)  Odors.

17. (Q)  Why am I gaining weight?
(A)  The diet.

18. (Q)  Why am I so fatigued at the end of a day?
(A)  This is overanxiety of the body in many different directions.  It is dependent upon the expectancies of the body after a day's work, a to how much fatigue there is.

19. (Q)  Should I move my office to a new location why my lease expires?
(A)  This had best be determined by circumstance that is a part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experience, and should be chosen from the reasons of the own individual entity.

20. (Q)  Would a change in location be better or worse for me as far as increasing my clientele?
(A)  Again this should be chosen by the conclusions of the body-mind itself.

21. (Q)  Shall I apply again for enlistment in the Navy or shall I continue my civilian dental practise?
(A)  This should depend upon the [[Ideal|ideals]] and choices of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as respecting same.  If there is the innate desire for the manifestation of [[Ideal|an ideal]] as respecting service of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to a cause, a [[Purpose|purpose]], [[Ideal|an ideal]], make application.  If it is for what may be said, or for a selfish motive, or for the satisfying of ego alone, do not.  But remember, - be true to thine own ideal.

He, though, that stays and waits may also serve.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
