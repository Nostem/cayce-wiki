# TEXT OF READING 1561-6 M 58 (Mining Engineer)

**Reading [[1561-6]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/7/1938  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 7565

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1561], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of Mr. [1561], ..., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., who seeks information, advice and guidance as to his interest in the release of metals and minerals.  You will consider at this time the Carmerson Corporation which owns and holds leases on some three thousand acres of property located in Smith County, [[Texas|Texas]]. You will advise Mr. [1561] if he should devote his time and energies in endeavoring to interest capital in this Carmerson Corporation's oil properties in [[Texas|Texas]].  You will answer the questions he submits, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, Mr. [1561]; also the interests in nature's storehouse, and the specific activity in the operations of Carmerson Corporation in its search for oil.

3. In giving advice or counsel to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] such as this, under the conditions and circumstances, many conditions are to be taken into consideration.

4. First - the validity of the operative forces of the Carmerson Corporation.  There should be in this connection, as we find, a little better comprehension by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of the policies and [[Purpose|purposes]] of the directors of this Corporation.

5. If it is the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] for the Corporation to remain an independent corporation, and to develop its holdings to a productive area, and then dispose of same to those that hold the greater manner or way of outlet for the use of same, it is well to interest self.

6. If the [[Purpose|purpose]] is that it is to remain an independent and OPERATIVE corporation, then there becomes too many loopholes, too many ways in which those whom the body might impress or whose confidence he might gain - for them to invest in same - to lose such.  And this would mean to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] the loss of self's OWN comprehension, self's own respect in a way and manner.

7. Hence as we would give, first - such as this should be determined in the mind of the body before it makes too great an effort, or becomes too insistent in obtaining funds for the carrying on of the project there.

8. That there is production to be obtained in the deeper areas of the whole of the Corporation makes, as we find, without a doubt a good proposition.

9. The policies and the manners of handling, then, become rather the problem for the Corporation.

10. As to the desires, the interests of the entity in all the products of nature's storehouse - as minerals, as oils, as ANY of the products that are a part of this - as we have indicated, these are [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which the entity may gain the greater opportunities for self-expression.

11. As to how and in what manner these are to be USED, when obtained, is the part of the entity's problems.

12. Again, in considering the proposition of the entity interesting itself in the Carmerson Corporation - in the PRESENT these are advisable from one angle, - that is, the needs of the entity in its immediate activity in and about the home environ and surroundings; as well as among its friends, associates and activities in the immediate present.

13. But as to how great may be the entity's interest SHOULD be dependent upon the final POLICY of those who DIRECT or carry on with this Corporation.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  If this policy is found satisfactory to the entity, should he devote his time and energies in endeavoring to interest capital?
(A)  As we have indicated!

16. (Q)  Will this venture be profitable for the purposes determined upon?
(A)  As we have indicated, if the policy is of one nature - No!  If it is of another nature, Yes!

17. (Q)  Can this financing be done and can names and addresses be given of one or more persons who would be interested in financing this venture?
(A)  Who is to do financing?  The entity or the universal sources which have only the interests that ARE of a constructive nature?  These would NOT be given!  These are a part of the entity's work!

18. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Look into self as to purposes, as to policies - and if it is that as ye would have done to YOU, then hold fast!

19. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
