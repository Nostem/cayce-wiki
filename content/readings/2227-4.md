# TEXT OF READING 2227-4 M ADULT (M.D.)

**Reading [[2227-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/5/1942  Sex: M  ReadingID: 8947

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [1866], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Grant Hospital, 
Time of Reading                              Room 425, 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.       [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - the body is becoming very much run down, very weak.

3. Owing to the unbalanced forces through the circulation, the arterial walls are leaking, thinned.  It is thus that the blood is being discharged, or from which hemorrhage is coming.

4. It would be well if there would be the administrations of the blood that has been developed by the hospital in Vanderbilt; the oxblood that in its activity forms the coagulating forces to relieve and to stop this hemorrhage.

5. Then, afterwards, it would be well that small doses of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] - not tea, not soup, but [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] - be administered every two hours; not more than half to three-quarters of a teaspoonful.

6. These offer the opportunities of allaying, of retarding, and building the system for resistance.

7. Do that, but do it at once.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Wife - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file

Wired also:  ADMINISTER IMMEDIATELY OXBLOOD FORMATIONS DEVELOPED BY VANDERBILT TO PRODUCE COAGULATION  FULL INFORMATION AIR SPECIAL.

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)
