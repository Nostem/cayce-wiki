# TEXT OF READING 900-219 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-219]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/7/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5422

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come in the various manners as have been outlined from time to time, and should be studied, or used, in the way and manner as have been outlined.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Tuesday night, April 6, or Wednesday Morning, April 7, 1926.  "Joe Wallack called me up on the telephone and said 'Buy 20 shares of Coca Cola at 143 or 44,' and I answered 'Why?'  'Is that the stock going up?'  'Yes,' he said, 'its going way up.'  I asked, 'Is 143 or 44 the right price to buy?'  He said, 'Well, handle it any way you want.  Buy it anyway, at any price.'"
(A)  This, as we see, the mental forces seeking out some definite information, through the various channels, and there is the answer from one whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would, in ordinary manner, consider as good advice.

In this as given, we do not find that this will advance to any great height, or any great advance.  While there will be some, but, as is seen from the amount of stock told to buy, the small quantity will indicate the small advance, even though there is said to be a great advance coming.  Just that condition of the mental forces seeking to gain, from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], that of the existent conditions, and the mental answering rather than the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], for we see a physical reasoning rather than [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] reasoning, in the manner same is presented, see?

4. (Q)  Does this mean that Coca Cola is going up, and at what price, and when, should it be bought?
(A)  There may be some minor advance.  We do not see a great advance in same.  Then, the handling or buying of same, while the industrial and manufacturing, or commodity stocks, are on the advance, in the next two and three days, the advantage of same should be taken, for, as is seen in the advance of this class of stock, in the last day or two days, especially in yesterday - there will be farther advances in same to-day, to-morrow - then easing off, see?

5. (Q)  Then he said "American can may also be bought at about

6. (Q)  Will American Can be one of the leaders on the up side, and when should this be bought?
(A)  Be one of the leaders, but be just as much one of breaking as one of the leaders.

7. (Q)  "I told [137] about this, particularly Coca Cola and American Can, and he waved me aside, saying:  'Why should they go up?'  I said, 'Because I saw them go up through the subconscious.'  He said, 'Well, you have seen that before and they have not gone up, and besides, supposing that failure is reported in a few days?'  'We will buy our stock back, and besides Coca Cola and American Can will go down.' I didn't know what to do."
(A)  In this there is seen that of the physical reasoning or mental reasoning, and visioned from the subconscious forces, as is seen that reference is made to same, and as the questioning of the subconscious is made, there is then seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that neither [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] understands the full questioning or the full meaning of the information as is given or gained through same, as is seen in the reasoning as is attempted in this questioning, and the trouble feeling there comes by one questioning the entity as to the actions as should be taken regarding that as has been seen in the subconscious.  Now, as we have for the reasoning of the condition as exists in this special two stocks, or information as is received might be termed, though subconscious, a secondhand reasoning, for not the individual sees the stock to be advance, but someone outside calls and informs the entity that such and such is to take place. Now, then, the entity should reason regarding these conditions, respecting these stocks, in its own mental PHYSICAL way, rather than from the subconscious reasoning, as is shown by that seen IN the dream, see?

8. (Q)  "I asked Moe Wallack what should we do with the Hudson Motors we are short, and he said we ought to buy it in at 2, 3 or 4 points lower, and I told that to [137] and [137] said, 'Suppose that failure comes on - then what?'" (A)  This again the same way or manner of reasoning through the subconscious, or through the dream condition, for, as we find, instead of same being presented in this manner, or through the subconscious, as would be termed, we find same coming through a body mind reasoning of that of being told by one that tells someone that TELLS someone to do some things, see?  and, as these are reasoned in this manner, the entity would do well to study how or what the variations, as have been given from time to time is the action as regarding subconscious information and that as of body mind and physical reasoning, though it be seen in sleep, [[Visions|vision]], or dream, and as pertaining to this individual, stock, for we see the reasoning GOOD, see?

9. (Q)  Will the failure be announced?  If so, when?
(A)  We do not find this as yet, for, as has been given, should the various customers, the various individuals, stand by, with and for, these ones concerned, why SHOULD it be announced?  unless the crash comes again in another circle - then we would find it would be.  As to when, this is not seen as here.

10. (Q)  What had we best do regarding Hudson Motors report? When had we best do it?
(A)  This, as we see, should be reasoned from that standpoint of the information as has been given from all sources, for, as is seen here, there will be the turning point between now and the close Saturday, or on Monday - 12th, see?  and the variations, for we find advances have occurred in the last day - two days; yet we will find breaking away again, UNLESS these conditions, or crashes, come, as is seen MAY happen, or may not happen, depending upon those conditions of the various groups as have been given.

As to what should be done, then, respecting this, reason same from that of the [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces of each individual concerned in same.

11. (Q)  "On top of all of our troubles, somebody else lost money - $800.  It seemed that I could make that up in Coca Cola and American Can."
(A)  This, as we see, a [[Physical Body|physical body]] reasoning, see, from various conditions as have been presented in the mental forces of the entity in the recent days, and conditions. Now, there are visioned, through the conditions as have been presented in the mental mind through [[Visions|vision]], that possibility of a recuperation, even for others that seem to have been under pressure.  These, then, as given, may be used for a quick short turn, but we do not find any great advance immediately.  See?

12. (Q)  What seems to be indicated in these two stocks?  Some big advance?  What and when will this be?
(A)  There will be some advance in the next day - two days.

13. (Q)  "I saw Newirth, a bootlegger.  We were both at a railway station.  He advised me not to drink the liquor that would be sold on that train, that he was going to make it right in the one car, and it would be better if I didn't drink it.  Then he got on the train and I got on the car next to his, and the train broke right at that point, and his end pulled out of the station one way, and we stayed in the station, and I noticed how quietly they pulled out.  I turned around to look at our side, my train, and noticed the engine was pointing in the opposite direction, and our train was right long - many cars."
(A)  In this there is again presented that condition as has been reasoned for the entity in the various ways and manners, as [[Visions|vision]], dream, or action of the mental mind, body mind, and subconscious action are reasoned before and for the entity.  As the idiocrasies [idiosyncrasies] are seen in this condition, at this present condition of the train, the manufacture, the talking to one who would manufacture such on and under such conditions, and the way in which the divison is made, as is seen, shows that to the entity as how the reasoning of various conditions, as are presented through the vision, dream or subconscious, must be weighed, gathered, seen, measured, taken aright, that the best may be gained.

14. We are through for the present.

43.  That will go up too."
(A)  This again one of those very active stocks at the present price, and the same condition exists as pertaining to the other, and this may be taken for a quick or a short turn over, see?  that is, do not buy either for a long hold, but for a quick turn, of either American Can or Coca Cola to be bought, if they ARE to be bought.
