# TEXT OF READING 4483-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4483-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/25/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12351

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4483].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with this body, and these conditions are somewhat changeable in the effects as are produced in the functioning of the organs of the system, on account of the seat, cause, or that which has first produced, for effects of conditions have been combated with, rather than the seat of cause of the trouble.

3. These, now, are conditions as we find them in this body:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this is irregular in its pressure, or in its effectiveness in the system, as to the cause of existent conditions.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this very much the seat of the trouble. While many of the organs are involved, yet the seat, the cause, the first troubles, began through this, and through the highest effect, or the highest [[Vibration|vibration]] in the nerve system, in the beginning of the trouble, arose.  As it has (the trouble) been lowered in the various [[Vibration|vibrations]] of different organisms in the system, different effects have ensued from same.  Hence, we have, in more than one plexus of the cerebro-spinal system, effects of this condition as began in the system through that of the generatory organs of the system.  This effect has produced in the digestion, as we see, a tie-up, as it were, in the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus - has produced in the secondary cardiac this condition as is produced to the pulmonary system, and to the whole of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], or ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body. Hence a debilitation in most of the incentive for the functioning of the system, and the listlessness as is produced in each organ's center, see?  That is, as we see from the atomic forces of the body, each organ takes from the blood supply (which, as we have already seen, is defective in the building in the system, through the condition as is produced in the central portions of the body), and, as each has taken its organ, its centers, to supply, by its division in the various functionings of the system, the incentive being low, the debilitation has been low, for the incentive of the nervous system, and the lack of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s interest in the ability of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to meet and cope WITH the condition, has been much in the trouble, for often has the body become discouraged with self, with self's position, and with self's position, and with the conditions under which the whole body has labored.  See?

6. All the organs, then, as we see, are under that effect, or the strain as is produced by this condition.

7. To overcome this, then, in the body, first we would give that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] must first awaken within self that necessity of self's applying that possible within self to meet the needs of the body's building - that is, declare a [[Purpose|purpose]] within itself - set a goal, aim to that, reach it, master it - and self is first to be mastered.

8. In that which will assist in doing this, will be that which will give the greater incentive for the whole organism of the body to build, or that which is, as is seen, easiest for same TO do, see?  Then, that which will do this will be that which will bring the greatest equalizing condition throughout the system, which will be by using this as an inhalant for the respiratory system and the blood supply of the body, which will be found in this:

9. To 4 ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol.............................20 minims,
Benzoin................................10 minims,
Benzosol...............................15 minims,
Balsam of Fir...........................5 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...................5 minims,
Tolu in solution (Tincture of Tolu)....20 minims.

10. Put this in glass container with glass stopper, at least a six to eight ounce container.  Shake the solution before inhaling.  Inhale this at least three to four times each day.  Deep inhalations into the lungs through the mouth.

11. And apply each evening to the body, first to the wrist, then to the ankle, with the positive pole - the negative pole being attached to the umbilicus, see?  in which there will be the alternation of the Silver and of the Gold, see?  of the Radio-Active Appliance, see?  In this solution as made, there would be one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of water. In the Silver solution, there would be one grain of Silver to one ounce of the alcohol, see?

12. (Q)  One grain of Silver to one - (A)  (Interrupting)  One grain of [Nitrate of] Silver to one ounce of alcohol.  The anodes should be kept clean, see? When the Silver is used in the container, the positive pole and the negative poles are opposite - the positive then being the one attached to the ankle, and the Gold attached to the wrist, see?  This should be taken at least an hour to an hour and a half each evening, see?  The body will rest better. Then change will be seen in the whole general system.  Do not overload the system with food, or diet, but let it be in keeping with good judgement, as the body can apply to self. Do that.

13. We are through for the present.
