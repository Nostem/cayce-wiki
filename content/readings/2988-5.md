# TEXT OF READING 2988-5 F 38

**Reading [[2988-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1944  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 10284

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [2988], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are acute conditions respecting the optics. These, as we find, we would for the present keep reduced with applications which would cause relaxation.  But if the inflammation continues in the eye, we would operate to remove that which has caused the greater distress.

4. As to the general health of the body:

5. We find that the body is beginning the changing periods. Thus the injection of the male hormones would be well for the body.  This is to be done under the direction of a physician.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What has caused condition in left eye?
(A)  This is from an injury and inflammation.  We would keep down these conditions with ice or such, unless this continues.  If inflammation should continue, operate.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file "   for Indexing
