# TEXT OF READING 2744-1 F 25

**Reading [[2744-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/4/1928  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 9878

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of April, 1928, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2744] via Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes.  Now, we find there are those conditions more of which the body should be warned, and precaution should be taken in time concerning conditions apparent with the body, than that which gives such distress at the present time. While there are those conditions that bring unpleasantness for the body, these are more of the secondary nature, and were those of the eliminations looked after, or precautions regarding same taken, and those proper corrections made, the body would find better resultant conditions with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  While there are such conditions that are abnormal and need correction, such as the position of the womb and the effect same creates in the system, these are not the greater conditions to be warned against.  These may be corrected by the local treatments; that is, those abnormalities, and by the relaxing and correcting of the system through proper manipulations osteopathically given, and through packs that would bring the near normal position. These need attention, and from thirteen to fourteen (13 to 14) such treatments osteopathically would bring corrections.

3. Those as have to do with the eliminations are those that the body should be warned about most.  With the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation necessary for the correction of condition in pelvis region, and for the effect as is created as secondary conditions in the hypogastric, these would be assisted by the general treatment as is brought to bear for other corrections also, but that necessary added for the system to bring about the proper reaction would be the use of the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], which will add to the blood supply and so bring about the proper relation between the nerve centers, as corrected THROUGH the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation, and the organs as are involved, as well as the capillary and lymphatic circulation, that shows the beginning of those effects created by improper eliminations in the system.  The age and the conditions are at that point where corrections need to be made, following along these lines as suggested, rather than for that which may be given as internal medicines; for these other existent conditions will only build that which later would bring detrimental effects to the body.  With the diet as would be taken (which would be given by one making such corrections), we will find much better conditions being brought to the body, Mrs. [2744].  Do that.

4. We are through for the present.
