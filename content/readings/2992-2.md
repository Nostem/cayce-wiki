# TEXT OF READING 2992-2 F 55

**Reading [[2992-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/9/1943  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 10291

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2992], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2992].

3. As we find, the body is recuperating from the effects of applications for the removal of disturbances in the abdominal areas.

4. These are not entirely easy as yet and bring about weakness. There must be precautions taken at this particular period, else adhesions may form that may later cause a great deal of trouble.

5. While the circulation is gradually returning to normal, and the nerve flexes and reflexes are gaining, we find that it would be well to apply - about twice or three times a week - the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the whole abdominal area. Dampen a cotton cloth with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and apply when the body retires.  This should extend down low over the pubic center, and this will aid in regulating the activity for the kidneys and bladder, preventing the tendency towards inflammation.

6. About twice a week take internally five drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in half a glass of water to [[Alkaline Diet|alkalize]] the system and to act as an antiseptic in absorbing through the alimentary canal.

7. Also as an absorbent for the gas and the conditions through the intestinal tract, we find that it would be most helpful to take charcoal with honey (such as prepared by Kellogg's).

8. For the disturbance in the reflexes to the organs of the sensory system, we would take the head and neck exercises morning and evening.  We would take these very easily; that is not too swift - but, sitting erect, bend the head forward twice, very slowly, twice backward, twice to the left, twice to the right; then circle right to left, and then left to right.  Remain quiet for a time after this.  These exercises if taken regularly will tend to strengthen the muscular forces of throat, head and neck, and will aid in the eyes, the hearing, and be a general stimulation for the body.

9. In the diet do take more often carrots and beets.  Eat the carrots grated raw, also made into a salad with gelatin and the gelatin itself will be well for the body.

10. Keep strengthening foods; that is plenty of sea foods, plenty of vegetables of the leafy variety, including watercress often.  Lettuce and celery should be taken often.

11. These will be better for the body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What is causing the pain in my right shoulder?
(A)  This is a neurotic pain or neuritic.  The cleansing of the alimentary canal, the taking away of the poisons from the system by the absorption as well as with the applications externally, should relieve this.

It would be well to cleanse the colon by the use of enemas or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]]; not too severely, not too much at a time, but do keep the colon clean.

14. (Q)  Are there signs of any recent [[Healing|healing]] of a serious internal trouble?
(A)  As has been indicated.

15. (Q)  Will this [[Healing|healing]] be permanent?
(A)  This depends, just as indicated.  Unless there are measures taken we find that adhesions may form that may make the condition become worse.  But take the precautions as indicated.

If these local applications will be followed by a gentle oil [[Massage Therapy|massage]], it would be well; such as an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|mutton tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|turpentine]] and [[Camphor|camphor]].  Not too severe, - just apply these oils and then wipe off.  Don't apply on flannel or the like.  Just [[Massage Therapy|massage]] gently into the area over which the Glyco-Thymoline Packs have been applied, and then wipe off the excess with a dry cloth.

Do these.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
