# TEXT OF READING 136-14 F 21 (Bride)

**Reading [[136-14]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/15/1925  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 321

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:10 P. M.                                       ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to this body.  These, as we see, may be used to the edification of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] into that of how spiritual laws are manifest into the physical world.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Monday morning, October, 12, 1925, at home.  "I was in a gymnasium back in New Orleans and a basketball game was in progress.  I noticed all of my fraternity sisters in the back seated and watching the game.  I recall my dress distinctly and noticed they all had on uniforms alike of white dresses and blue ties.  I went back to them, talked to them and sat on my friend P. S.'s lap.  I saw P. S.'s black hat with large feathers particularly, because it was the exact same as my hat."
(A)  In this we find the emblematical conditions being presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

The gymnasium is presented as truths that are being studied by the entity, that are being heard by the entity.

The seeing of others in suits of the fraternity shows the general trend of thought in every character by the entity as well as by others, and the seeing of blue and white representing the pure and true, as is seen in the various studies, see?

The seeing of an individual represents that the individual feels towards that entity, that position held by the entity itself, see?  for it sat in a lap of same, yet hat in black with feathers being the same, shows how that study to the body would mean the same conditions as being met.  Lesson then:  Study that in hand, for it brings one to that perfect understanding, as seen in satisfaction and dissatisfaction in knowing the conditions as must be meted out, see?

4. (Q)  Tuesday morning, October 13th.  "I was out some place and got into a private car - somebody's automobile.  We drove along and I noticed all the shades of the car windows were down.  Then the chauffeur attacked me and I screamed with fright.  He threatened to run the car into a tree or into some accident if I did not keep quiet.  Two men in a small car came up to help me and as I screamed, the chauffeur let the car slide slowly over a precipice into what seemed water - but I felt the car slowly sink, and was panicky within."
(A)  This, as we see, representing, through correlation of mental forces with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], conditions that may arise within the home, see?  for with the troubles arising, the entity may feel that same panicky condition and as if the entity were sliding into darkness, into water, into that unknown, coming from mis-understandings, see?

Then as this is presented in care in driving, in the actions of subordinates, these actions will occur through those of the head of household, see?  Then in the manner, seeing how same is presented, be on the guard, and when such occurs, laugh it off!  It means nothing then.

5. We are through for the present.
