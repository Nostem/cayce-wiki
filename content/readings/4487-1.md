# TEXT OF READING 4487-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4487-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/18/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12355

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of July, 1927, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195], Dr. [5618], and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of Miss [4487], of ... St., ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on July 8th, as follows:  "Dreamed I was at a party with [195].  Starting home I asked him not to take a drink. He went in the kitchen and poured out a very large drink. It then seemed that my father was holding the glass of liquor poured out, and my sisters were there and a serious argument ensued.  My mother appeared and tried to settle the argument."  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this [[Dreams|dream]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, Miss [4487]. In presentation of [[Dreams|dreams]] to the body there is seen there may be gained the better understanding, would [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] apply those various phases of consciousness as are active during that period called dream, [[Visions|vision]], or the activities of the phase of the mental forces of a body.

3. In this [[Visions|vision]] as is seen, there is presented that there is one whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has much confidence in, much faith in, indulging in an act that is not in keeping with the ideas of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; yet the greater trouble as is presented is through the SOURCES of that one whom the entity later sees taking same.  The presentation, then, of the disturbance as arises from same, and the intervening of the mother in the way and manner is as of from what source there may be gained by and for the entity that knowledge, that understanding, that manner in which the situations as confront self as respecting the father's indulgence may be had, and these may be obtained through the assistance of [195].  Do that. We are through.
