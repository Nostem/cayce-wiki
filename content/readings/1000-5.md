# TEXT OF READING 1000-5 F 41

**Reading [[1000-5]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1930  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 6021

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self, through her sister, Mrs. [1192].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1192], Mrs. Hewett, Drs. Lydic and Parker, and Mrs. Dozier and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  In many respects the body shows improvements.  The WORRY of conditions produces the greater distresses with this body. Would there be applied as corrective measures those, now, of the plain battery forces as are obtained from the [[Wet Cell|wet cell]] (plain) - these attached to the wrist and the opposite ankle, beginning with only twenty minutes each day, increasing five minutes each day until an hour may be taken - we would find better conditions in the body.  When these are given, it will be found necessary that there be days when it would be well NOT to give; that is, two, three or four days, then skip a day - or three days and skip a day - maybe the next five days and skip a day; for the NAUSEA as would be recurrent from same (unless very mindful of the diet) would irritate the body.  This will act very much as a sedative for the whole nerve system, by equalization in same.

2. Well that, during such time, a gentle [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] massage be given.

3. The packs would be well.  These may be obtained BEST by the use of the sweat baths, not too long given, and RUBBING the body down afterwards with dry sand; not so much as to irritate the body, but to increase the capillary circulation; then sponging off with the lukewarm water or shower, and rubbing well with alcohol.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What causes pain in left side of throat and lower jaw bone?
(A)  Sympathetic conditions only.

5. (Q)  What can be done to relieve swelling and coating on tongue?
(A)  Change the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system!  This has just been given!

6. (Q)  Were eggnogs harmful?
(A)  Not very good!

7. (Q)  Is there any danger of recurrence of severe pain suffered two years ago?
(A)  Not if the body is brought back to the proper revibratory forces in system, and this has been outlined for the body.  We would do that if we don't want it to re-occur! We are through for the present with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mrs. [1192]
