# TEXT OF READING 488-3 M 19

**Reading [[488-3]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/14/1930  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 3591

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and mother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, Mrs. [255], Misses [275], [276], and Mr. [488].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, the body is here.  Very good are those conditions for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  In the reactions through those of the lower extremities, for the feet, for the digestive system, these need a DEEPER manipulation - not break it, of course, but dig deep into the tissue in the sacral and be rid of that FLATTENING as occurs in the sacral and the lower lumbar.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What is correct weight for body?
(A)  He can make it most anything he wants to, if he will diet himself in the proper manner.

3. (Q)  How much will body still grow?
(A)  How much does he want to grow?

4. (Q)  When will body be able to go home?
(A)  He can go home when he chooses, but will he be READY to go home - will be different!  The corrections, as we find, SHOULD be made in those deeper manipulations in lumbar and sacral; of course BALANCING the rest of the system with those of the deeper manipulations.  Don't just dig in and leave it there, but carry out the impulses throughout those of the centers leading from the lower plexus.

5. (Q)  What effect has sacharin [saccharin] had on body?
(A)  The body active with too much sweets of another NATURE with those, isn't good.  Don't try to put everything in the stomach that would tend to form differentiations in their activity.  The activity in system of sacharin [saccharin] itself is good, for the ACTIVITY of same, as given, is for that of the corrective forces with the activities of the gastric juices, especially in the duodenum.

6. (Q)  How can mother help body?
(A)  Better that the body help mother!  and that the body take more consideration for the duty that is innate with each activity, and the findings of the mind itself - and the mother would be able to help the body!  but will the body live alone for self, and irrespective of the duty and of the PRIVILEGE as is - Act as if it was a PRIVILEGE, and the DUTY active forces WILL be bettered.  CONSIDER that well.

7. (Q)  When will he be ready to go home?
(A)  When these corrections are made.  These should be in at least three to five - Deep, but don't get them too close together, because he would be limping!

8. (Q)  Does beef contain iron?
(A)  Depends upon how soon it is used after being slaughtered.  Depends upon the characterization of the preparations.  The JUICES contain iron.  The meats contain little.

9. That is all the questions regarding this body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self to send home
