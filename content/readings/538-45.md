# TEXT OF READING 538-45 F 57

**Reading [[538-45]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/16/1937  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 3781

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of September, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [538]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, the specific or acute conditions show some improvement.  Yet there are still those inclinations from the activities through the genital system for irritation at the portion or the end of the clitoris.

3. Then we would keep a few more of the manipulations.

4. We would use the low electrical forces as created by the bodily forces in the Radio-Active Appliance; making the attachment of one of the anodes to the pubic bone, the other to the 4th lumbar center.  This take each day for about four days, rest a period of about four days.  Take for thirty minutes at each application

5. (Q)  Which plate should be attached to the body first?
(A)  That at the pubic bone.  This is preferably attached not with tape but adhesive.

6. (Q)  What is the condition of the kidneys?
(A)  These show some improvement, as indicated.

We would keep a well-balance in the alkalines and acids; not too much now of the lemons but more of a combination - lemon, orange, lime, about half of each at one period during the day.

7. (Q)  Should I continue with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douches?
(A)  These are very well.

8. (Q)  Is once a day too often for these?
(A)  Not if the temperature of the water used is body-temperature.  Too hot or too cold, this becomes too often.

Three more of the manipulations would be well, and then a rest period and no more unless there is a return of the condition.

9. (Q)  How long should I continue with the Appliance?
(A)  Until the conditions in the clitoris have abated.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
