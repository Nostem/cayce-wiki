# TEXT OF READING 4524-4 M ADULT

**Reading [[4524-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/10/1911  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12405

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 10th day of November, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Jno. C. King, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M.                                      ..., Mo.

1. LBC:  You have before you [4524], ..., Mo.

2. EC:  Yes; we have had him before.

3. LBC:  I want you to go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly, and tell us what his condition is now.

4. EC:  His condition is better than he has before.

5. (Q)  What conditions do you find now?
(A)  Now we find a different condition here at this lesion of the formation or hardening of the stomach, here.  The action of the gastric juices into this produces this spasm as we have over the body here sometimes; the action in conjunction with that of the circulation, or through the hepatic circulation to the secondary cardiac in its forcing the blood back to the body as we had before, or into the circulation to the carried to the heart, purified in the lungs and carried back to the circulation again.  We haven't so much of that: that is, the lack of gastric juices in the stomach, that is, we haven't as much of the gastric juices.

The better unison of the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal forces with the eliminating or active principle of the nerve forces in this through the body has brought about a better condition.  The cleansing and keeping the stomach and intestinal tract in better order has assisted to keep it down.  The condition as we have through the lumbars here, the reflex conditions, is some better.  More of a perfect unison of circulation; a better condition through the body.  The forces we had to the upper portion of the body, or to the cervical and upper lumbars, are still in the same condition: we have a tautness, as we have had, through the body.  The condition through the arms and shoulders is the same as we had.  That to the head is some better; not so much of the headaches, but we still have the fullness of the saliva in the mouth, through the action of the salivary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  We still have too much of the belching, or the inactivity of the stomach in carrying off the particles that should go through the dross instead of back up.  We still need to keep the body under the care of the forces we had before.  We need more salines in the system.  Any gluten or proteids taken should be with plenty of salt, and we should have the salt rubbed along the spine here at times, with the manipulation we should have over the body, with a reverse of the electrical coil, or that is, which acts to the ends of nerves to pick them up to the action from the forces, or act directly through that of the solar plexus, celiac, cardiac and secondary cardiac and along the spine.

6. (Q)  Any other treatment that you would prescribe now?
(A)  We need some of the bromides, of course, still in the system, but not too much.  Keep the digestion open; the bowels open; digestion acting freely through that, not of these that choke the hepatic or liver, nor of stringent to the kidneys, but that acts of a free nature through all. Some suggestion to the body; keep the body in perfect control of himself through the suggestion, not too much of a humored nature, to rely on himself and trust himself. Raise the standard of thought as here in self, and put more confidence in self.

7. (Q)  Should he have the manipulation along the spine?
(A)  He should have the manipulation for the relaxation of these forces all along the spine.  Not necessarily the manipulation over the whole body.

8. (Q)  Then the electricity along the spine?
(A)  Electricity and salines in the system.  Watch the digestion and the diet that is taken into the system.  Plenty of salines at all times; not enough to sicken, but well seasoned with salt.

9. (Q)  Will he be cured?
(A)  If he will follow this out he will be bettered and he will be cured.  He will follow this up until we can eliminate through the stomach and intestinal tract this condition we have had from times before; eliminate the dross matters from the system through the proper channel until we have no return of the vertigo forces to the heart action.  There is no organic trouble, but we have forces of dizziness to the head; loss of control of self, or vertigo; no unison of the cerebro spinal and sympathetic forces at the 3rd, 4th, 5th and 6th dorsal.  These come in conjunction with the secondary cardiac through that of the venous circulation.

10. (Q)  About how long will it take?
(A)  It depends on how the treatment is to thoroughly control the material mental and physical man.  You see the whole system in the body here, in removing the troubles or eliminating the actions of forces of the body, works in cycles of building of forces in the body.  The action of forces at some times works in one part of the system, while renewed forces of growth in the body will help eliminate those to other parts of the system, as in this.  The growth in this has been of a nature to growing of the condition instead of eliminating.  We have it reversed; that is, to keep these into the proper channels and eliminate them from the system by the proper channels, and not leave the system to another force to be destroyed.
