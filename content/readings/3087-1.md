# TEXT OF READING 3087-1 F 25

**Reading [[3087-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/10/1943  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 10438

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. W. C. Tingle.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [3087]''s husband.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, physically and mentally, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3087].

3. Now as we find, there are conditions that are preventing normal reflexes and reactions in the nerve forces - and the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the body.

4. This has to do primarily with nerve obstructions in the body, and the effect this pressure has upon the mental and the physical reactions in the body.

5. Then, these are the conditions as we find them with this body, [3087] we are speaking of:

6. The blood supply indicates the nature of the disturbance exists in the suppression of nerve energies, because of the manner in which there is the coordination of the activity of heart, liver, lungs and kidneys.  For, the variations in the functioning of the liver and the kidneys indicate obstructions in the lower portion of the cerebrospinal system.

7. In the nerve forces of the body, - this inability of coordination with the mental and physical self arises from those subluxations which exist in the sacral and lumbar area, causing a lesion in the lower dorsal and third cervical.

8. This suppression of energies has caused the functionings of the pelvic organs to act in those manners in which there is a suppression of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, as related to the tubes and the ovaries themselves.

9. Thus we have a suppression that arises from the supplying of energies to the activity of these organs.  Thus we get those reflex reactions to the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system, as to produce the lack of coordination between the mental - or the reasoning self - and the physical self in the body.

10. The ORGANIC conditions are only sympathetic.

11. As we find, to aid - or to correct these conditions, - we would first give that there be the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manner used to correct those segments in the sacral, the lumbar and the dorsal areas; coordinating the cervical area with those corrections made in the lower part of the body.

12. Have the adjustments twice each week until there have been made at least ten adjustments.  Such an one as Irvin we would get to make these adjustments.  MOVE especially the vertebrae in the lumbar axis and in the sacral and coccyx, and in the 9th dorsal, and in the 6th dorsal and 3rd cervical.

13. After the second treatment for the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections in the body, - begin with the use of the low electrical force through the ordinary Dry Cell Appliance; to supply those energies needed for building up the destroyed tissues in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] forces of the pelvic organs.  Prepare this, though, with the ability to charge the low electrical forces through [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] - in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water (and we would use at least three ounces of the solution).  This would be attached to a cord FROM the APPLIANCE, or the Dry Cell Appliance, to the body.  Apply this TO THE CENTRAL section of the Dry Cell Appliance, with a large nickel plate. The copper plate would be attached to the outside section. These plates would be attached to the body for half an hour once each week - once each week - for at least ten weeks; but attach to the body on the same day and the same hour each week.  The copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar. (Let this be pointed out to the body by the one making the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections.)  The larger nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially, would be attached to the pubic center, or where the Appliance would coordinate with two of the bursa supplying lymph flow through the pubic center.

14. This done, we will find bettered conditions coming for this body, and the mental and physical reactions attaining more to normal conditions.

15. Do that.

16. Ready for questions.

17. Do this, and we will find better conditions for this body.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mr. Davenport (one for Dr. C. W. Irvin, Bankers Trust Bldg., Norfolk, Va.) Copy to Ass'n file

(The Dry Cell Appliance may be prepared for you by Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., Phone 35323.)
