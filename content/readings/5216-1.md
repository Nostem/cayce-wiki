# TEXT OF READING 5216-1 F 64 (Librarian, Protestant)

**Reading [[5216-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/12/1944  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 13352

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by daughter - Mrs. [5264], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, these are quite serious disturbances with this body.  As we find, these are from inflammation of the whole intestinal tract, with those formations in the lacteal ducts of accumulations which, when emptied, cause a great deal of fluid that is not good.  This, as we find, cleansed from the system, would leave it so weak, as it is most of the time when these accumulations break, and there is at times blood in same.

3. We would prepare a grape [[Poultice|poultice]], fresh grapes, leaving the hulls and pits in same.  Apply this about twice each day for two or three days at a time, and then rest a period from this and use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs, using only [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] or Lavoris as an intestinal antiseptic.

4. Also give the radio-short-wave treatments for stimulations that will allow the poisons to be eliminated.

5. Have the foods that are easily assimilated, as a great deal of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], liver and liver extract.  Take these in small doses.  Do not give hypodermically.

6. Slippery Elm Bark, if this is prepared in a tea and allowed to be cooled and drunk, would be well.  Also Yellow Saffron prepared by putting a pinch between the thumb and forefinger, and this put in a cup and boiling water poured on same.  Let stand for 30 minutes, strain, cool and drink.  This take about every day and we will make better conditions for the body.

7. Do keep the spiritual attitude differently.  Don't condemn, don't blame others, for it is within self.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  How should the Elm be taken and how often?
(A)  Elm may be taken every day, just so that there are the eliminations and this doesn't ferment in the alimentary canal, but it will be helpful for the conditions through the alimentary canal, especially in colon.  A pinch put in a glass, pour boiling water and let stand for twenty to thirty minutes, then put in an ice-box until it is cool and drink. It will be as slime, of course, but this is what's needed in the alimentary canal.

10. (Q)  What causes heart disturbance?
(A)  The pressure by the gas in portions of the colon, in the transverse and descending colon area.  This would be found in the abdominal area.  These are not heart disturbances but sympathetic from pressures of gas on the hypogastric.

11. (Q)  What can her daughter [5264] do to help her?
(A)  We haven't the daughter, but help can come by consistency, [[Prayer|prayer]], earnestness in wishing to be of help; then doing something about it.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.  See directions [which were enclosed] for [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)
