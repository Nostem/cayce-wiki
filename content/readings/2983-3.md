# TEXT OF READING 2983-3 M 7

**Reading [[2983-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/19/1944  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 10275

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time. ..., [[Florida|Florida]]. 
[Taken 12:10 P. M.]

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are some changes.  Though these may appear very slight as yet, there is a very good change being made by the manner of the manipulation.  But there needs to be the moving of the segments in the sacral and the lumbar area, so that the body-circulation is established through these areas.

3. To be sure, there are variations in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces. These are being aided by the character of the massages and the diets.

4. But as we find, if there is more particular attention given to the lumbar and the sacral, and the 3rd cervical, we will get better conditions for the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] being administered properly?
(A)  The attachments plates should be kept a little bit cleaner.  Polish these with the emery paper each time when taken off and each time before attaching.  Take the connections out of the bottles when not in use.  By remaining in same these tend to dilute and to form such coatings as to prevent the complete effect of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] produced in same.  Put the connection in bottle and connect to the Appliance about twenty minutes before it is attached to the body.

The oil rubs are very good.

Keep up the vitamins for the body.

7. (Q)  Has sufficient progress been made to indicate that this body will be normal by the time puberty is reached?
(A)  This will depend entirely upon whether or not there is a more thorough effort made to move the locomotory centers, and these even to the points of hips also.

Do these things.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file
