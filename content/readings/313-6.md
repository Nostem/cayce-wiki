# TEXT OF READING 313-6 F 48 (?)

**Reading [[313-6]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/13/1934  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2369

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 13th day of June, 1934, in accordance with request made by the husband, Mr. [228], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Fla. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [313]; this we have had before.

2. Some conditions, as we find, are improved from that we have had here before; others show a taxation in the system and thus make for conditions that bring disorder and distress.

3. These, though, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [313] we are speaking of:

4. Still we find there is a lack of the quantity and quality in the blood supply.  Assimilations do not take place properly.  While there is some improvement in the position of the stomach proper, this is still somewhat tipped - and sufficiently so that it gives the tendency for fermentation before digestion, or prevents the reaction of the gastric forces in the system in a proper way and manner.  This, with the formations of gas as it produces, is the reflex condition to the throat and bronchi that has been affecting the system through the general strain that has been in the system recently, from toxic forces or malarial reaction in the blood supply from these toxic forces in the body.  First the toxic forces, the reaction to the liver and the elimination; the toxic forces produced reflexly from the position of the stomach or the fermentations, and these in their reaction produce the poor circulation to throat and head.  This makes for that tired, depressed feeling; dull headaches, languid - not lazy but LANGUID, and the tendency for inertia.  The nerve pressure from the position of the stomach, as well as the organs of the pelvis or the womb itself, makes for the irritation in the nerve system; tending for those conditions when at times the least thing worries or irritates the body. The dipping of the womb makes for the depressions at periods when there should be the eliminations through the activity in the system.

5. In the present we would use the deep manipulations with the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] in the upper dorsal and cervical area; resting at times with the feet very much higher than the head; at times bandaging the body so as to hold the position of the stomach more in place - which would be preferably done with bandage about the body, rather than most of the belts, as we find.  Also we would have the deep manipulation with the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] over the lumbar and sacral area, with the position of rest, which will make the tendency for the activity of the organs of the pelvis to be nearer in accord.

6. We would change somewhat from the [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] that has been taken at times, for we would alter this considerably.

7. It would be well to use [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Tincture of Myrrh as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the lumbar and sacral, as well as the upper dorsal area as indicated for the manipulations with the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].  This we would do each evening for ten to fifteen minutes.  Heat the oil and add the myrrh, equal parts - about a tablespoonful and a half of each.  Only prepare sufficient to use at each period, and only [[Massage Therapy|massage]] into the body that the body will absorb.

8. We would take internally the Saffron Tea - Saffron Tea; and a compound put together in this manner:

9. To 2 ounces of Simple Syrup, add:

Tincture of Valerian..............1/2 ounce,
Elixir of Calisaya................1/2 ounce,
Syrup of Rhubarb..................1/4 ounce,
Tincture of Stillingia............1/4 ounce,
Tincture of Capsici.................3 minims.

10. Shake the solution well together before the dose is taken, which would be half a teaspoonful about twice to three times each day.  As we find, this will make for a relaxing and a digestion and the eliminations throughout the system.

11. Do this.  Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Are there any other organs out of place besides the stomach?
(A)  As indicated; and we have indicated how these may be best brought to their proper position.

13. (Q)  What diet is it now best for me to follow?
(A)  An alkalin reacting diet, but a general normal diet that will make for blood and nerve and body building; but keep more in the ALKALIN reaction.

14. (Q)  Is "Nature's Mineral Food" I have taken beneficial to me?
(A)  Is not harmful, yet - as we find, with the correction of these activities as related to the nerve system and position of the stomach - a general diet and this combined occasionally with same will be well.

15. (Q)  Any other advice for my better welfare?
(A)  In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], as we find, if we will follow these, we will bring much better conditions for the body, [313].

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(A letter was enclosed to druggist - also Alkalin reacting diet list.)
