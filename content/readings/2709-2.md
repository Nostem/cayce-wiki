# TEXT OF READING 2709-2 M 27 (Lawyer)

**Reading [[2709-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/19/1928  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 9822

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of March, 1928, in accordance with request made the by self - Mr. [2709].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2709] and wife [2710], and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                        ... Central Park West, 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [2709], present in this room, the physical health of the body, also business associations and relations of this body; also his home relations and relations with his wife's people. You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [2709], here.

3. Now, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these, we find, very good throughout.  There are those existent forces that the body should be warned concerning, rather than of existent forces that are not being met with at the present time. These have to do with the pressure of the blood supply and the action of assimilation in the system that show tendencies towards conditions that would bring - were these not corrected - trouble with the eliminating centers of the body, and reflexly the pressure abnormal to the body.  This is caused from the conditions existent in the 9th dorsal plexus where the impingement produces improper distribution of nerve energy for the pancreas, spleen and hepatic circulation.  At present this causes little distress, but - allowed to remain - would give distresses by the time the body reached the thirty-fifth year, or in the fifth cycle.  This may be corrected by [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, properly given, and then be careful of the diet until the system is properly aligned throughout with the changes brought about by this correction.

4. In the business relations, these are very good, but some cause to the mental reaction of body distresses.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Regarding the promotion of sale or consolidation of the machinery factory, will it go through?  What can I do to make it go through?  Am I associated with the proper people?
(A)  This we find SHOULD go through with this association, unless there is brought to bear some other influences than that present.  We will find that this will go through before September - not much before.

6. (Q)  Regarding the purchasing of a hotel, shall [257] and I plan to put this through together?  What is the best procedure?
(A)  This very good.  While conditions are not altogether favorable at the present time for this venture, as conditions develop and as peoples are gathered here, this would be well. As to procedure, there are many varied industries and varied lines that are being interested in the sector and in the development of same.  These may ALL be interested in the venture.  Keep true to all of them!

7. (Q)  Regarding deal involving silk, is this possible or is it not worth while bothering with?  Are the people who presented the proposition to me reliable?
(A)  These are reliable, and it's worth bothering with! for there are many possibilities in this field.  While many changes are taking place in the structure and construction of the commodity through these lines or channels, yet the peoples, the manner, the place, the way, the conditions connected WITH same, all have more than ORDINARY possibilities for large returns - if used aright.  [See 900-385 on 5/19/28, Par. 5-A advising Mr. [900] not to invest in Mr. [2709]'s American [[Peru|Inca]] Co. rayon silk manufacture stock.]

8. (Q)  Is there any special branch of law I should follow?  If so, what is it?  What can I do to improve my law practice?
(A)  Particularly that branch as pertains to contracts, corporations, or international law.  To improve same is to apply the knowledge already in hand; for in application one finds the way and the manner of gaining more ability for WHATEVER may present itself.

9. (Q)  Should I enter politics?
(A)  Not a good politician!

10. (Q)  What will be my most successful years?  and days of the month?  About what numbers does [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] revolve, or most favorable?
(A)  In numbers to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] - while each and every body has those factors that have to do with numbers, but - don't rely too much on numbers for this body!  for it reasons too far ahead for that called numbers, or [[Numerology|numerology]].  For this body TWO is the greatest number, yet when reasoned, is seen to have both strength and weakness, see?  Hence we will find in years, all that have to do with the divisibility of two, four or eight, are the years through which most of the misunderstandings, misdirections, or conditions that trouble the mind, arise in.  When ordinarily these should be the best!  Yet those divisible by six AND two, or six and nine, will be the better years.  Make all contracts on Monday! Make all closed deals on the 2nd, 12th or 22nd.  Now these, as we see, vary from that just given, yet these are numerical conditions in this body's life.  Watch it - it will be seen!

11. (Q)  About our marriage, what can I or my wife do to make our marriage a lasting success?  and how can we help each other?
(A)  Let that as was set in the promise one to another (which to each are very sacred) be kept first and foremost in the life as related one to another.  Be true one to another in being true to self; for, as has been given, one true to self will not be false to another.  Remembering that each are combined one with another, and keep the [[Purpose|purpose]], intent, the faith as set before each; for what e'er may betide anyone, that of [being] true to promise made brings that peace and satisfaction that is never shaken by any of the vicissitudes of life.

12. (Q)  What policy should be taken in reference to business affairs with my wife's family?  and to improve harmony between us?
(A)  Let that same loving indifference that is part and parcel of each - the wife - the self - remain.  Never use harsh methods that will bring self-condemnation in any manner.  Let thine self know thou hast done wisely by doing the proper condition and thing, EVER.  This will bring harmony in no uncertain manner; for so doing, one brings those conditions that CANNOT be combated in ANY manner. This indifference is not ordinary indifference, but loving indifference - that of knowing the heart, the mind, the [[Purpose|purpose]] in self, is correct, and in keeping with that that is progressive in every sense.

13. We are through.
