# TEXT OF READING 4913-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4913-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/6/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13022

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 6th day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made 6/26/25 by Mr. [953].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time or Reading 
2:35 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                    ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will have before you Mr. [4913] of ... Street, ..., Ill.  You will tell us the psychic ability of this body and whether he can give correct information.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] has an extra amount of that force necessary to give information from and throuqh [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]].  There are times, however, that the body forces the issue through other influences and gathers the action of psychic from deductive rather than inductive forces.

3. We are through.
