# TEXT OF READING 900-211 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-211]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/23/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5414

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900] of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, [900]. This we have had before.  Now, we find the [[Dreams|dreams]] are the interpretation of those conditions that are correlated in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], of those conditions in the mental and body mind, and may be used by entity in way and manner as have been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Monday Night, March 22, or Tuesday Morning, March 23, 1926.  "My mother and I went into my uncle's store.  They were quarreling.  There seemed to have been a fight over something.  I had on overshoes.  They warned me to be careful and not soil the linen.  I told them the reason I was taking losses on the market was that grain prices were too high; namely, it was not my dealing but that the grain being so high it made all the other stocks decline."
(A)  Now, in this, we see this presented in the manner as given, for the mind of entity has taken into consideration conditions as might, could or would arise, were certain conditions to happen, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds self, even though rebuked in a manner, attempting to justify self in self's action and in self's view taken at certain times.  This, then, in its interpretation, or lesson from the interpretation of that as is seen in the mind, that though there may be the feeling of resentment to self for the present condition, there will come the justification of the condition, or the enactment of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in the way and manner as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has and does act, for though the condition in grain has produced certain conditions of unsatisfaction in many places, there will be found the advance in this (grain), as well as in those other stocks in which the uncle is interested, see, for this will advance.

4. (Q)  Has this any indication that Stewart Warner is going down instead of up?
(A)  Will go up, as we see, to that price as has been given, though the entity may, to justify self, when there IS the profit in this stock take same, rather than to endanger self in being rebuked for a lose.  Next two or three days, see, this will happen.  Be up, we see, to 90 - 85 - 83 - 82 - 83 - 84 - 86 - 89 - 90.

5. (Q)  It seems I went to a horse race, but on the race program they had railroad time tables, and with particular reference to the I. C. Railroad.  Someone said to me that Ben Block had the complete schedule of the I. C. Railroad and knew just what route to take.  Then I saw the C & O and it made some connection with the L & N, and it seemed I was making some roundabout way on the L & N.  Then I saw Southern at 137.
(A)  In this there is again the correlation of thought, idea, and of body mind concepts, with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of entity, and, as is seen, the pooling of groups of stocks, as were and is known by a certain individual, and through whom the entity may gain much information from, for these especially, C & O, L & N and of Southern, will advance, while as that of I C will be in the GENERAL, THROUGH the changes as are to be made; for, as we find, these are conditions as are existent in this combine:  There have been in the past those combinations in which there were to be much of a chance in these roads, especially, see?  Now, with the changes as do appear through the handling of same, there will again come that combination wherein, before these changes are made, prices on these rails, especially, will advance, for a closer union is to be made with these four roads.  Hence the four as seen, and much inside information may be GAINED by the entity from that one as is given that knows the routing of this road, especially; though, as is seen, this seen on race track card.  Then, the manner in which this is seen (race track - horse race) will be seen the manner in which this will be handled - rushed, fast, furious, see?

6. (Q)  There is reference made here to four railroads.  Tell us what is the significance.
(A)  The closer union as is to be made with these four specific roads, and, as is seen, specific prices given on the one, which is as the criterion on many of the others; though, as we see, this (the Southern) will advance in its price, and the others also, in their various ways and manners, see?

7. (Q)  Saturday Night, March 20, 1926.  I saw Cayce asleep. The Voice spoke and said:  "You see, [900], there is a Christ in each and everyone of us, and [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] speaks to us from within through [[Law of Moses|the law]] of relativity of force.  That is how this is done."
(A)  As is seen in this, the Voice or the inner self presents to the entity that of the living experience of the study of which the entity has given much time, thought and study, for this is as a truth, and the more the entity studies along this line of presented thought, the greater, better, bigger, concept there will be gained of the living Christ, in the lives, hearts, of men, and the MANNER of His Spirit dwelling within the earth's plane, and through same may a greater concept be gained by the entity of the issues of life. Then, study and study, and study.

8. We are through with this reading.
