# TEXT OF READING 3750-1 F 52

**Reading [[3750-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1927  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 11267

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3750], via Miss Lucille [[David Kahn|Kahn]] and Mrs. [266].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Oklahoma.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes.  Now, the physical conditions of this body are not satisfactory at all, and WE find there are specific conditions that have brought about much of this trouble, and that by careful building up of the body, and making those corrections as are necessary in the physical structure of the body, this body may be brought, yet, near to normal conditions.  While many conditions are of secondary nature, there are also those SPECIFIC conditions that at present do not apparently show, and the result is that more often have the effects been treated than the cause.

3. Now, these are the conditions as WE find them with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, Mrs. [3750] we are speaking of.

4. Just at the present time we find there are some pains to the left side of the head that are causing some trouble.  These are of a secondary nature, but are produced from some conditions that have been given for the body in the diet, that produces a digestion that is unbalanced.  These will be overcome, though, by those properties that are being administered at present.

5. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, we find, first:

6. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, there are evidences in the blood stream that all of the organs of elimination do not function in unison, and that from time to time (depending upon the cycle of functioning) various conditions arise that are indicated by the disturbance in THAT portion of eliminating system; such as is seen as with liver, with the kidneys, with the capillary circulation, with the swelling in extremities and in portions of the body - these all are as indications, and the circulation necessarily assumes the responsibility of attempting to carry refuse forces to other portions of the system to be eliminated.  Hence tissue becomes involved in various portions of the body, with the result that the exterior or muscular forces become inflamed in various parts of the body.  The body is as sore to the touch in various portions, and this, as we see, shifts about from time to time.

7. The functioning, then, of the blood stream proper is very good, but the incentives as are exercised in various portions of the system do not coordinate.  Hence the disturbance as is created in same.  The effect, of course, is at times to the heart's action, yet no organic condition there.

8. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this, too, we find under stress and strain, especially in the centers and ganglions of the cerebro-spinal nerve system, and more specifically in those ganglions in the lower end of the lumbar and the solar plexus connection with the sympathetic system.  Hence the inactivity and the incoordination between the locomotory and the sympathetic and the cerebro-spinal system in this portion of the body - that is, the nerve reflexes are such that in the lower portion, or in the locomotion, the body finds often that voluntary reaction is as of an involuntary movement and that involuntary becomes voluntary - that is, the reaction to conditions existent.

9. With such inflammation as is created in disturbed elimination, and with this extraordinary condition as exists in the nerve system, especially in that portion of the body wherein reflexes are to many portions of the mesenteric system that functions directly with many of the organs of the torso of the body, is it a wonder that the body assumes, then, those conditions of distraughtness in the functioning of these organs?

10. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, brain forces are very good.

11. The throat, bronchials, lungs, larynx, all show the effect of a disturbed circulation, yet without organic conditions, as does the heart's action, and the digestive system - though digestive system, as given, at PRESENT in that of a disturbance produced by indigestion in the intestinal tract, or intestinal indigestion, headaches resulting from same.

12. In those structural portions of the body wherein the body suffers under strain, we find this was produced in times back by those conditions that were added to by other conditions that brought on the disturbance.  In the coccyx we find that the bending of one of these vertebra, or muscular or cellular forces there, produced such pressure as to cause most of the trouble as is being experienced by the body.  This, as we see, was from an accident in which the body fell, or sat down most too hard.  Then, with the condition that formed when inflammation was produced in the eliminating centers, there were disturbances brought to this portion, which added to, forming a lesion, which is of a lateral nature, which affects, then, sympathetically and directly, the tissue and the muscle, and the action of these portions of the body throughout the lower lumbar and sacral region.  Hence the disturbed condition, and the addition of properties to overcome the various EFFECTS of this produced in the system has kept constant irritation, and with the system attempting to eliminate and set about to overcome conditions has kept a constant warring within the body of those elements that would bring [[Healing|healing]] forces or correction of conditions for this body, Mrs. [3750].

13. Now, as we find, these would of necessity take care and attention to make a correction wherein there is seen that vertebra, cellular tissue, has formed in such a way and manner that the body, rather than [[Vibration|vibrating]], has become as bony structure.  Not that this may not be overcome, yet it would require time, care, attention, persistence, and consistent usage and correction of those conditions existent - which would be in applying that which will gradually bring about correction of the disturbed conditions in sacral and coccyx, and the manner to do this would be to first give in the system those properties that will create the extra secretions necessary for the oils and tissue to be added in system, that, with the exercising of these portions of the body, it may take on that [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to create new active forces in body, and at the same time keep the body in the general physical condition in such a way and manner that the system eliminates nearer through its normal channels, and the rest of the body is kept in that way of a general good healthy [[Vibration|vibration]] to the rest of the body, see?  By, then, applying those properties in which we will find a saturated solution of pure apple vinegar and salt, to the sacral and coccyx region.  Every day let these properties be applied, just as warm as the body can stand, see?  and then, when this has been applied in this manner for at least ten to fifteen (10 to 15) days, begin the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and correction of those conditions as are found in this portion of the body, correcting same through [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] adjustments, see? Keeping in the body small quantities of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] and [[Baking Soda|Soda]], see, which would be prepared in this manner - in this proportion prepared, see?  One grain of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of distilled water, see?  In another solution we would place ten grains of [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] and one ounce of distilled water.  The dose would be first two drops twice each day - two drops of the [[Gold Chloride|gold solution]] and four drops of [[Baking Soda|soda]] solution, put in half glass of water; increasing the gold solution one drop every day, and the soda in proportion, see?  until there are eight drops of the gold being taken twice each day.  Then skip a day.  Then begin over again, taken in those proportions as given, see? increasing the soda as the increase is to the gold; stopping off and then beginning all over again with the two drops, see?

14. At the same time we would have those vibrations as are applied in the ultra [[Violet Ray|Violet ray]], see?  These would only be taken once each week, PREFERABLY on Tuesdays, see?

15. When these have been carried out for thirty days, we would give further instructions - for the body is only being PREPARED, as seen at present, but the gentle corrections must be begun in the manner as has been given.

16. We are through for the present.
