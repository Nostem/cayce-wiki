## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/24/21 Mrs. [265]'s letter:  "To Whom It May Concern:
> I wish to state that I have known Mr. Edgar Cayce for about
> twenty-five years, sometime before he discovered his
> psychological power.  Am acquainted with his work in
> relieving and restoring many people, including himself.
> Have heard him give many readings when patients admitted he
> described conditions and symptoms better than they could
> themselves.  I wish to mention especially Mrs. [...].
> Another, Mrs. [4962], whose case her doctors failed to
> understand.  Mr. Cayce diagnosed correctly this case,
> giving treatment which was entirely successful.  Another..."
> 
> R2. 3/27/73 GD's note:  Today in going through some old letters
> of [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]]'s dating back to 1906 in Bowling Green,
> Ky., when she was a member of a Pen Pal Club, I came across
> this letter from Mrs. [4962] to Edgar Cayce as follows,
> dated
> 
> Feb. 19, 1908, [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.
> 
> "Dear Edgar:  Words will not begin to express my thanks
> to you for the kindness you have shown in giving me the
> readings.  You can't imagine what a load you have lifted off
> of my mind.  I have suffered so much pain bodily and a great
> amount of mental strain until I was almost worn out.  If ever
> I can in any way do you a kindness in the future, all you
> have to do is let me know, and I will be more than glad to do
> so.  I called [husband [1103] at work] and told him what
> [his sister [760]] had telephoned me that you said, and he is
> much relieved as myself.  We both thank you.  Tell [760] and
> [538] that [1103] said he would call them up whenever he had
> anything new to report."  [4962]
> 
> R3. GD's note:  Nine days later, on February 28, 1908, her son
> [419] was born.  Apparently her condition was connected with,
> or because of, pregnancy?  I heard that she died when the
> little boy was still very young and he was brought up by his
> father's sister, Mrs. [1795].  She, Mrs. [4962], had an older
> child, a girl [1312].  The only one living in her family now
> is her granddaughter [852], who does not know or remember any
> of the details.
