# TEXT OF READING 433-5 F 45 (Widow, Hebrew)

**Reading [[433-5]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/1/1931  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 3232

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 1st day of December, 1931, in accordance with request made by self, Mrs. [433], Class A Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you Mrs. [433], nee [...], born in [[Russia|Russia]], who is now at Room ... Hotel, ..., Brooklyn, N.Y. You will give the exact birthdate of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present physical plane, giving the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] with the universe, and universal forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities latent and exhibited, in the present life.  Also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will also answer the questions this entity has submitted as to its future welfare in this plane.

2. EC:  We haven't the beginning!  Mrs. [433] - we have the body here - nee [...] - We don't find it!  We have the body and those conditions as surround the body in the present!  There are many of those conditions builded.  It must have a beginning!

3. (Q)  How may the body get the necessary information to get the life reading as desired, or where?
(A)  At least the place, and near the year!  There should be SOMETHING to begin with!

4. We are through with this condition in the present.  Seek SOMETHING!

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
