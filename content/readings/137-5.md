# TEXT OF READING 137-5 M 26

**Reading [[137-5]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/2/1924  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 395

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 2nd day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by his brother, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Archie Adlman, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], and [900].]

Time of Reading 
10:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                 [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  Now you have before you [137], who was at his apartment, ... Street, [[New York City|New York]], on the fourth floor, at nine A.M. November 2, 1924.  You will give a spiritual and mental reading, giving us the names of his spiritual guides.  Also tell us if he is carrying out the suggestions properly to develop the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]], or what he may do to better develop these forces.  You will also answer any questions I might ask you relative to these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see, with the mental and the spiritual forces in and with this body or entity.

3. In this we find the exceptional forces manifested in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] through the mental and the spiritual forces, and the body prepares the mental well for [[Soul|the soul]] development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. We would not give change for the mental or spiritual development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]; only be thou faithful unto the end.

5. As to the guides in this entity, we find the exemplification of the forces innate in the developments in earth plane guiding the present entity's mental forces, through the spiritual nature as developed upon the earth plane.  Hence the ability of the entity to develop the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] to the consciousness of the conscious and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] minds of the entity, for the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] are the projection of [[Soul|soul]] development in the earth plane.

6. The manner in which the body goes about these developments at this time is very good, only the body should not attempt to consciously prevent the conscious losing itself in sleep or slumber, for through this we will find the first action of the psychic making the physical manifestation to the conscious mind, and with those impressions gained in such condition use those at once, and the conscious will find the developing of the psychic or latent forces in the present earth plane and may be able to use those manifestations for the development of self and of others.  This is the correct way to develop the forces, [137].

7. (Q)  Has the body not definite guides in spirit sphere?
(A)  Development sufficient, as given, that the entity's guide the innermost conditions in the physical plane.  Hence the ability of manifesting through the psychic forces, as given.

8. (Q)  How will psychic manifest in the physical?
(A)  First through the lapse of consciousness, which the body should not warn or fight against when entering the silence, and through such lapses will the first development show. There have been two already, only one acted upon.  Act immediately.

9. (Q)  What were these two?
(A)  One came only as names.  Again came as actions in the daily labors.

10. (Q)  Will [137] be able to give psychic readings like Edgar Cayce?
(A)  The development is beyond those conditions as given by Edgar Cayce, for they will become conscious conditions to be acted upon by the conscious mind; that is, the impressions received in the Borderland, and be able to bring same to consciousness from the physical standpoint.

11. (Q)  What was the name that came to him in this first?
(A)  He has that.  He acted upon it.

12. (Q)  What was the second?
(A)  Second not acted upon.  Had to do with stocks.

13. (Q)  Has he already acquired these psychic powers?
(A)  Developing them.  Do not go against those conditions, would the development be to the better.  Act upon but do not take advantage of others, using same rather to assist and develop the latent forces in entities upon whom the forces direct the entity's endeavors.

14. (Q)  How may he use this psychic power to assist in the work of Edgar Cayce?
(A)  By entering into these conditions, with the questions necessary, before any group who may develop the powers and forces.  They will work well together, one beyond or above the other, you see.
