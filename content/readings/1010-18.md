# TEXT OF READING 1010-18 F 71

**Reading [[1010-18]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/28/1942  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 6105

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1010], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 10:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1010].

3. As we find, while conditions are not entirely satisfactory, many influences are very good.  We find that the disturbance of the circulation, - fluttering of the heart, the [thrombose] thrombus, the condition between heart and lungs and heart and liver, - arises more from the toxic forces in the alimentary canal.  While this has been cleansed in periods, there are still - through portions of the colon - those conditions and tendencies that are inclined to cut off this circulation, or retard it; thus causing a fullness of same.

4. If there would be a gentle relaxation osteopathically, or chiropractically, or neuropathically, we find that this would be beneficial.  This should be especially in the area of the 6th and 7th dorsal, particularly; but we should keep the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations rather regularly, until there is no indication of mucous from the colon.

5. The we would do.  And as we find, being precautious as to not overtaxing the body, being mindful that there are not too much meats taken, these should keep down the disturbances.

6. Fish and fowl as meats, but not pork or beef.  A little lamb. These not fried, however.  No fried foods of any kind.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What must I do for insomnia?
(A)  Use the Radio-Active Appliance.  This will bring rest, - if the attachment plates are kept clean or polished, and if there is not a short circuiting of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] created by the body in the Appliance.

9. (Q)  Nervousness?
(A)  As indicated, this is part of the general condition. Have the relaxation osteopathically, or neuropathically, or chiropractically.

10. (Q)  For the lame, sore feet?
(A)  Use an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Tincture of Myrrh, heating the oil to add the myrrh.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this into the feet and limbs in the evening when ready to retire, and in the morning when the feet are bathed [[Massage Therapy|massage]] this again into the feet.  This will aid in keeping better circulation.

11. (Q)  Are the rubs I am taking helping me?
(A)  These are helping, but there are disturbances that arise as indicated from the colon.

12. (Q)  Any other advice at this time?
(A)  Keep the general attitude for creative influences in and about the body.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(10/28/42 GD's note:  Did you ever use the Radio-Active Appliance, or only the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]]?  I'm enclosing a little folder regarding this.  If you still have your [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] you might make an exchange for the Radio-Active.  GD.)
