# TEXT OF READING 1409-6 F 61

**Reading [[1409-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/19/1938  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 7151

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of December, 1938, in accordance with request by the self through Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the weakness and the lack of vitality, - and the inability of the system to produce sufficient of vital forces produces the great weakness in the body.

4. The use of the Beef Juices as a tonic or medicine is MOST advisable, - as well as the use of egg cooked with spirits frumenti and very little sugar added; though some milk in same, of course, as strengthening forces.

5. And we would advise that at least three Codiron pellets be taken each day for the building of resistances in the system to cold, and the added vitamin forces as will arise from the use of same for better activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.

6. This we would do.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Any advice regarding the rest of diet?
(A)  This had best be in accord with the appetite - though do not overcrowd the body at any time.

Use irrigations preferable to cathartics, always, for this body.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self through Mrs. [303]
"    " Ass'n file
