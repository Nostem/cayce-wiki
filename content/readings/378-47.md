# TEXT OF READING 378-47 M 57

**Reading [[378-47]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/29/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2929

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
5:35 to 5:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Let not thine heart be troubled.  Thou hast made the way open.  Then keep the [[Purpose|purposes]] that are willed in self and in self's activity as related to the material, the mental, and the spiritual influences in thine activity.

3. For, He is the way, the life and the truth.  No man may approach those things that will be for the constructive influence in his life if they are not founded in the principles He has given, "Love thy neighbor as thyself; do good to those that despitefully use you!"

4. Feel in thine inner self that thou art being used, and willing to be used by Him - irrespective of the slights, the slurs that may be poked at thee.

5. For, what gaineth [[Soul|the soul]] that maketh or exalteth itself above its fellow man, by implication, by word or by deed?

6. But rather let thine yeas and thy nays be in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Consciousness.

7. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file
