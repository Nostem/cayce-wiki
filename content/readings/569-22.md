# TEXT OF READING 569-22 F 54

**Reading [[569-22]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/9/1934  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 4030

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 9th day of July, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self, through Mrs. [538], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [569]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are somewhat improved.  While the eliminations have been increased and the toxic forces in the general system have been eliminated so as to make for better reactions, especially in that assimilated for the supplying of the blood, there has been a localizing of conditions in portions of the body - that calls for or produces distresses.

3. If the treatments that have been indicated are in the main followed along, and the adjustments in the upper dorsal and the cervical area COORDINATED to produce better drainages from the head and neck, we would find - with the keeping of the eliminations in an excess - the disorders or disturbances in the head would be MATERIALLY eradicated.

4. We would, then, continue with the use of the Ash as indicated.  We would use the violet ray just after this has been taken, some ten to fifteen minutes after taking the Ash, using it for three to five minutes especially over the frontal portion of lungs and over the lower portion in the back and along the spine - from three to five minutes.

5. The diathermy we would keep for periods sufficient to so enliven the nerve system and the impulses along the blood flow as to enliven the walls, as it were, of the nerve and the blood supplying or accompanying activity along the CENTRAL nerve system.

6. For the local condition in the head and ear, we would cleanse these with a mild antiseptic and use the compound of St. Jacob's oil in same, which will remove the stress - but the drainages should be set up by the manipulations and the corrections - upper dorsal, from the 4th to the 1st, and throughout the cervical area, as to make for DRAINAGES from the system, or from the head INTO the general system through the circulation relieving the pressures and giving the flow of blood supply to that portion of the body, see?

7. The teeth that show the greater disorder in the LOWER, we would then take out some of these; not all of them, to be sure, but those where the pockets are showing that the pus accumulates; and the drainages will aid in carrying these conditions away.  Local applications will not remove all the disorder there.  Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is bedtime the best time to take the Ash?
(A)  Well, we would take it before going to bed; be things to be done after taking it, and these best can be done not in bed but before you get in bed!

9. (Q)  How long should the Al-Caroid and Bile Salts be continued?
(A)  As long as necessary to keep the eliminations, so that they WILL make for an increase in the eliminations, you see. Of course, the powders will be taken after meals, to reduce the acidity.  The Caroid will be taken for the eliminations to be increased, and to keep the bile ducts or the activities of the pancreas, spleen, acting WITH the eliminations in the system; assisting also - with the Caroids - to make for cleansing the kidneys.

10. (Q)  Should diathermy treatment be shortened from twenty to fifteen minutes?
(A)  This would be alright, provided that these are kept until - we don't want to overstrain these - it would be better to take them for periods, and then have rest periods.

11. (Q)  How many should be taken before rest periods?
(A)  About half the number.

12. (Q)  Are the adjustments and manipulations being given too strong?
(A)  As indicated, these should be more for setting up drainages from the head and neck, and these may be done better by coordinating the pressures and the segments in their positions from the 4th dorsal to the 1st cervical; for where there is a tendency for a pressure to prevent the normal flow of a revivified or revivifying circulation it allows accumulations, as indicated in the areas where distresses are shown in the present.

13. (Q)  Would it be better to have any of the teeth treated?
(A)  As indicated.  Some of the lower ones should be removed, and others should be treated.

14. (Q)  Which special lower ones?
(A)  As indicated, those where the pockets or pus show and are not being absorbed or eliminated by the applications for the eliminating of disorders in the system.

15. (Q)  Is the body in a condition now to begin having some teeth removed?
(A)  When these conditions in the head are alleviated somewhat.

16. (Q)  Any other advice that would be of help or benefit to the body?
(A)  The diet, as outlined, should be alkalin rather than acid forming.

Be consistent, and be persistent.

17. We are through with this reading.

[7/9/34 Immediately following above, 5756-13 was volunteered with messages from deceased members of [569]'s family and others.]

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
