# TEXT OF READING 1631-1 F 56

**Reading [[1631-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/7/1938  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 7711

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1631], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1620].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, Mrs. [1631].

2. Now as we find, there are very definite and specific disturbances.  While these are in their reactions a bit different, we find that they should respond - with care and persistence; even under the long standing existence of the disturbances.

3. The reactions where there is the causing of the stiffening of joints, the activities especially in the extremities, the arms (the right, the more), the lower limbs - these all arise from infectious forces.

4. The hindrances and pressures produced by the accumulations from poisons, and the arthritic reaction, are an effect.

5. We find that the cause arises from adhesions that come from conditions about the gall duct and the umbilicus plexus as related to the lacteals' activity; and especially in the action of the ducts through which the activity of fats is carried on for the digestion.

6. Hence we find that all of those things that consist of oils or fats cause an increase, or greater disturbances for the body.

7. These then arise from this pressure produced between the nervous systems by the activity of the assimilating forces upon the locomotories and the centers and the muscular forces of the body.

8. The attitudes of the body that have been kept, especially in the constructive thinking, the desire for self to be useful for a [[Purpose|purpose]], the keeping from the mind attitudes of resentments and all such, have been and are the saving graces for the body as it were.

9. As we find, under the supervision of such an one as Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, these would be the activities wherein we may bring for this body the eliminating of the causes, a reviving of the bodily forces and functions, and an activity to be almost near to normal - if there will be consistent and persistent activities, and the following of the diets that would be necessarily a part of these whole conditions.

10. It must be remembered by the body, and those assisting in making the applications for corrections, that this is rather an insidious character of disturbance.

11. Then, because there are great improvements, and because the conditions begin to show alleviation of the disturbances, do not become negligent as to the applications; nor revert to those things that the desire or the appetites might call for in ways and manners; else we may produce greater inflammatory forces, as to cause or gain a return of even greater disturbances than exist in the present.

12. First we would begin with the use of heavy [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the area from the lower portion of the right rib area, or the portion of the lobes of the liver, entirely to the caecum area - on the right side, you see; extending to at least the middle portion of the abdomen.  These would be applied at least for an hour and a half each day, for four to five to six days - preferably in the evening; as hot as the body can stand same.

13. After each evening's treatment with these, the body would be sponged off with warm [[Baking Soda|soda]] water; so that the oil may be removed, and the absorbent forces that naturally are thrown off from the body in poisons that may be removed in such a way and manner.

14. Then, following the [[Baking Soda|soda]] water sponge bath, there would be a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] given over the whole area, especially in the right side.

15. And as these treatments are being followed, there would gradually be made a correction of a subluxation which exists in the lumbar axis.

16. After the five to six days of the Packs, we would begin then with a five-day course of the Zilatone tablets.  Take one tablet about half an hour after the morning meal, drinking at least two glasses of water with same.  Then after the evening meal take TWO tablets, drinking at least two glasses of water when taking same.  Keep these up in this manner for at least five days, see?

17. Then we would have a rest period from ANY of the treatments, for a period of about a week.

18. Begin then with small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; two drops in half a glass of water upon arising, before any meal is taken.  In the afternoon take two more drops, in half a glass of water. In the evening just before retiring take two more drops, in half a glass of water.  This procedure would be kept up for five days.

19. Then on the next evening, after the five-day course of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], we would take a bath in almost a saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]]; this as warm - not hot but as warm as the body may well stand; and should be sufficient to almost cover the body in a tub or container, see?  that is, there should be used as much water as necessary to almost cover the body, and put as much [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in same as will be dissolved in that amount of water.  Lie in this for thirty minutes; of course, keeping the head out - but practically all the rest of the body under the water.  Stay in the bath for at least thirty minutes, or until the water begins to cool.

20. Have a thorough rubdown after this bath.  Then have a thorough rubdown with [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] as a rub, after the body is well dried.

21. Occasionally, a general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] would be given the body WHILE IT IS IN THIS BATH, see?

22. Then, after the entire treatments have progressed to the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath - or after the Epsom Salts Bath has been given, not before - we would begin with the low electrical forces of the Diathermy.  The application would be made to the plexus from which the gall duct and the liver excretory forces receive their impulse, in the cerebrospinal system, you see, and OVER the gall duct center.  About five of these treatments would be given, but two days elapsing between.

23. After about two weeks it may be necessary again to repeat the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] course AND the Epsom Salts Bath.

24. But if these are followed consistently, persistently, with that same prayerful attitude which has been a part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experience, we should rid the body of these disturbing conditions.

25. As indicated, throughout the period the diet should be watched carefully.  Keep away from fats.  Vegetables and fruits should form the principal part of the diet, or practically ALL the diet - especially during these treatments.  Never any fried foods.  Not quantities of eggs; very seldom should eggs be taken, just occasionally - and then only the yolk, not the white of the egg - and preferably boiled.  No fried foods of any nature.  Roast chicken or baked fish may be taken occasionally, but not during the periods when so many of the applications are being made. Fruits and vegetables, both raw and cooked.  No coffee, no tea.  Cocoa, or any CEREAL DRINK may be a portion of the diet.

26. Ready for questions.

27. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one copy for Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, 205 West 57th St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file

(GD's note at the time of the Reading:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)
