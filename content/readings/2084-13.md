# TEXT OF READING 2084-13 F 14

**Reading [[2084-13]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/8/1942  Sex: F  Age: 14  ReadingID: 8706

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of September, 1942, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [2841], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Conn.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2084], this we have had before.

3. As we find, the upset that has recently come about is partially the emotional body, that affects the functioning of the organs.

4. Thus, it has produced those acute conditions.  To be sure, these might have been averted if there had been made those administrations as were last suggested.

5. Yet, of course, these emotional upsets become a part of the condition, and will necessarily have to be the change in the attitude of the body itself.

6. The chill and the emotions arise in the sensory forces of the body.  And with those disturbances that exist between the liver and the kidneys, it produces this reaction.

7. Hence in the applications of those natures in the diet, there should be the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]] to supply the insulin as to keep down shocks to the nervous system; thus creating a better coordination between the liver and kidneys.

8. Thus, we would have this particular character of artichoke as a part of the diet.  Use it for at least two to three days in succession; then about every five days, until there has been the time for the reaction from the mechanical ([[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]) adjustments that have been suggested.

9. These, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, are to be stimulations to the upper portion of the body; that is, in the dorsal and cervical area - the upper dorsal and cervical area, and a relaxation from the 7th and 8th dorsal to the end of the spine.

10. These, as we find, - with the precautions not to overexercise, but sufficient to keep a normal circulation and keep the proper eliminations through the alimentary canal, should bring back the better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Any change in diet?
(A)  Only as has been indicated.

13. (Q)  Please outline the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments for Dr. Coulter?
(A)  As has been indicated.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Father, Air Mail, Special (one for Copy to Ass'n file                       Dr. Coulter)
