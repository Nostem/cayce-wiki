# TEXT OF READING 4367-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4367-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/10/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12200

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 10th day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4367], via Mr. C. S. Willmott.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M.                                    ..., Oklahoma.

1. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions in this body physically that may be corrected.  We have those conditions that may be assisted and give better [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system, and add to the improvement of the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4367], we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find those conditions that have to do with the rebuilding forces in the system and the blood carrying the form of bacilli that has come to be a portion of the general condition in body, produced from bacilli from the exterior or that has been contracted in the system itself, and shows its effect on the functioning of organs in the system, especially those in the pulmonaries and the tissue in the organs of the pelvis.  In the quantity of blood supply normal for conditions in the system.  Over supply of white blood and the under supply of rebuilding in tissue and in vitim necessary to restore or to make restored energy in the system.  Hence the weak condition the body finds when overtaxed in any physical manner or way.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, many centers have become involved in conditions, especially those in the lower lumbar region, and reflexly those in the first and second cervical region.  In the nerve proper the perineurial sheath of many have become inflamed.  Hence the pains across the small of the back, or those that assist in the functioning of the Fallopian tube, and the distress as is caused to one of the ovaries proper. Hence all the nerves of gestation and genitation have become excited above their normal condition, and produces the reflex pains that cause the headaches and the condition through the digestive tract, through the pneumogastric and hypogastric centers, causing pains at times in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of stomach, and at other times this same nerve condition produces the excess of secretions as thrown from the action of kidney themselves.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS PROPER, brain force normal for conditions, yet often indiscreet with will and mental forces for the betterment of conditions not meeting the needs of the body in the normal way.

6. In the organs of sensory system here we find some conditions that have become the normal condition for the body that may be assisted, yet not corrected in whole.

7. Lungs show effect of the blood condition, as does bronchials and larynx in the present condition of congestion there.

8. Hepatic circulation high.  Digestive tract affected through the nerve system, especially hypogastric.  Liver the conditions there producing the excess of the hepatic circulation in the pelvis and the organs.  There we find the greater seat of the trouble.  The vagina and the womb proper shows the excess of blood that has been thrown to these portions of the system to build the broken tissue as produced there by irritation.  This may be corrected in part.

9. To bring the best to this body, we would take into the system this:  To one gallon of rain water, add:

Sarsaparilla Root...........6 ounces,
Black Root..................2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root............2 ounces,
Mandrake Root..............15 grains,
Buchu Leaves...............30 grains,
Elder Flower................4 ounces.

10. Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain. While warm, add:

Balsam of Tolu..............1 ounce, cut in
Grain Alcohol...............6 ounces.

11. The dose would be two teaspoonsful four times each day.

12. The electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] as that in [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] should be used over the whole exterior portion of body, and the Electrode of [[Vibration|vibration]] used for three minutes in the vagina.  This should be given at least once each day.

13. After medicinal properties and the electrical vibration has been taken for seven days, begin the deep manipulation as found in that of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment.  This will bring about a condition that the condition as thrown off in pelvis may be eliminated from system.  Do that.

14. (Q)  Should this body have its womb removed, and what effect would it have?
(A)  No.  Do not have it removed.  Create the condition as we have given.  Then treat the womb locally when the condition has been prepared in system so this may be done with impunity.

15. (Q)  Is it possible for the body to have any more children?
(A)  When corrected, it might.  With the condition, no.
