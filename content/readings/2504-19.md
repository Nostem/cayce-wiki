# TEXT OF READING 2504-19 M 64

**Reading [[2504-19]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/8/1930  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9432

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Gena Crews and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2504] - this we have had before. In many respects we find the body comparatively at ease, and the general conditions no worse in any respect.  There are times when distress is felt from anxiety and general debilitation is in the functioning of the organs.  These, as we find, are holding - as might be said - their own, with gradual improvements in the general condition.

2. We would keep much of that as has been outlined for the body from time to time.

3. In the matter of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as come for the system, these we would keeping a general manner as has been given.

4. We would keep those applications in the massage, both as to manipulations and corrections, as well as those exterior applications.

5. We would keep many of those that have been outlined as internal forces for the building up of the system in a general manner; that is, as the gold, or those that will act with the general assimilating system.

6. The electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, these should be kept for the body, and we will find these will continue to be much in the same way and manner.

7. The exercises of the body, for the strength to be gained in the system, will be found most by the massage of a neuropathic nature, which would be the most helpful; that is, beginning at extremities and massaging with the oils TOWARD the body, not FROM same.  Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What would tend to eliminate increase in stiffness?
(A)  Just been given!

9. (Q)  Would change to higher altitude and colder climate be beneficial?
(A)  Be beneficial, were all of those applications as given followed.

10. (Q)  Should small doses of citrate potash be used to clear up cloudiness of urine?
(A)  Provided not too large doses are given, as to produce an irritation to the functioning organs, or as related to the adrenal or endorine (endocrine?) [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].

11. (Q)  What dosage and how often?
(A)  Very minute doses, that should be given under the direction of physician who is WATCHING the conditions!

12. (Q)  Should vinegar and salt be continued?
(A)  Acetic acid in water would be better than salt and vinegar for the condition in hands or portion of the body, but the ELIMINATIONS must be kept high if it is to be used, in a manner of the sweats or tub baths, see?

13. (Q)  What proportion would be used?
(A)  One to ten.

14. (Q)  Is present schedule of medication and treatment good for the body?
(A)  This has just been outlined!  and gone over!  Just look at that!

15. That is all the questions regarding this body.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
