# TEXT OF READING 1031-3 F 45

**Reading [[1031-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/21/1939  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 6145

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1031], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   At home in 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are little changes of importance since last we had the body here.

4. Yet as we find, there is a great deal of pain when the body attempts to move about, or when there are activities of the nature that require the use of the locomotories.

5. As we find, it would be more in keeping if the body would arrange, for at least a period of two to three weeks, for the treatments under the supervision of Backman as indicated; the hot and cold water treatments, with just sufficient of the massage to assist the circulation.

6. And begin immediately with the use of the other applications suggested.

7. Then, if there is the suggestion that further treatments are necessary as indicated through the Backman method, let THAT source make the suggestions for those in close proximity of the body, see?

8. These as we find would be the most advisable ways and manners for the body to carry on.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Would it be possible for me to take the hot and cold water applications at home, without having to make the trip to N.Y. to Dr. Backman?
(A)  As indicated, better that for the first three weeks, at least, there be arrangements made that these may be given by one well versed in such treatments.

11. (Q)  Could this be done if I visited my sister in Philadelphia?
(A)  Unless you carried Backman with you, what would be the use of visiting Philadelphia?  It is the specific type of treatment that Backman gives which is necessary, you see, as we have indicated for this body in the present.

12. (Q)  Could you suggest any order in my day of activity which would help?
(A)  This will be better given after there has been the following of the Backman applications, as indicated, for at least three weeks.  Then this may be better indicated, see?

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
