# TEXT OF READING 4476-1 F 5-1/2

**Reading [[4476-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/10/1924  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 12343

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 10th day of September, 1924, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...], recommended by [21]''s mother].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                            ... Blvd., 
12:50 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.           [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find in this body there are many abnormal conditions, and the body shows the effect of some conditions that relate to prenatal conditions.  There are some local conditions that affect the body also.  In the general condition we find that of incoordination being the greater disturbing element in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as affect the body at present.  In the effect these create in the system, we find this has been of a more serious condition than at the present time.

4. To gain the better control over present conditions would be first to overcome through the correct adjusting of those conditions where impingements defer and bring the incorrect incentives to the functioning of the system.  In this manner and form we would find the body would best meet the needs of this condition from the physical standpoint, for we find the body in the developing stage, and in that condition that the retraction will be easier to bring about from the present developing stage than if allowed to run for any length of time beyond the first cycles.

5. This then would be that proper, to take the body to such an one as would be found in the practise of [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] forces, or [[Vibration|vibration]] as accorded the system through such treatments. We would give one as Robert Saxmann, 7300 Princeton Avenue. In this we will find one that will understand the conditions and that necessary for the direction of the conditions to be met, and through these channels we will find the better forces accorded this body, that will bring nearer to the normal developing of this body, [4476].  We are through with this body for the present.
