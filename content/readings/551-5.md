# TEXT OF READING 551-5 M 28 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[551-5]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/13/1925  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 3884

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 13th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [551].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. W. L. Jones.]

% W. C. Moore, 
Time of Reading                           35 Wall Street, 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.          [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you [551], who is located with W. C. Moore, 35 Wall Street, [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y., and you will give the prospects for success in his present business associations.

2. EC:  The changes in the associations of the body, as have been given, we see are in part now the fact in physical way. The associations offer those prospects, those conditions, as have been given as would bring to the body much of this world's goods, the specializing in an individual market, and one that offers many changes and one that will require a great deal of study on the part of [551].  One, though, that handled correctly will, in the next turns, make much of the success the body desires, provided the body uses same in that channel and manner in which it is in keeping with those conditions as have been set for the body, and as has been given that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should apply those of the worldly goods toward, see?

3. The change, as we find, brings also those of the conditions of which the body had been warned.  Then, in this particular relation, beware of those connections as might be made through this association with individuals in [[Chicago|Chicago]], see? While information, while the status of conditions as regards this relation of business in which the body is now engaged, the means, the basis of the operations is produced in the greater part by the Liverpool and [[Chicago|Chicago]] connections with same, Duluth reports as we find in storage, and Kansas reports, will have the greater effect upon the coming market. The active one will be December and May.  We would say would be success.  Handle properly and in the manner as directed.
