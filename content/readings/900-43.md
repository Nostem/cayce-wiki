# TEXT OF READING 900-43 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-43]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/5/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5246

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                               ..., Street, 
5:15 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you the readings given on March 4, 1925, on the bodies [900] and [137].  As given in these readings, the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] of the two individuals has been, and is, correct as regards stock market information received by [137] and [900] from the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]].  You will answer the following question in this; so that information the mind and body of [900], present in this room, seeks may be perfectly clear to him:  Does the correct intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] of these two bodies permit their use of the stock market information to buy and sell securities on the N.Y.  Stock Exchange, based on the information received from this source?

2. EC:  Yes, we have the reading and information as given in same here.  Now we find the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] of each individual correct.

3. As to the use of the information as given, there has also been given in these readings, and in this information given as to the conditions regarding such information, and as how such information may be used.  This then becomes the individual factor, for the individual's use and purpose.

4. As has been given, when the intent and purpose of the use of any information is correct, and that same influence guides, guards and directs the use of such information, then we have the correct results to each one that is concerned with such information.  For, as had been given, as has been seen in conditions surrounding just this character, this nature, this usage of information obtained through such source, when the intent and purpose of the individual is adhered to and carried out, the end is correct.  Not so used, the results are the proof of the real intent and purpose was not correct in the beginning.

5. As we would give as an example:  In information obtained by, through, the forces as manifested in the body [137], we find when used in the intent and purpose as is set in the individual, results satisfactory.  When not, results unsatisfactory.

6. As we have seen, and see, as another example, when information as is received by individual through the forces as are manifest through the body Edgar Cayce, when such is used as banter, as an example of superiority in intent or in mind's reactions, results unsatisfactory.  As has been given, this information as regarding this individual sale, this individual purchase, correct.  Individual sale must be used through the intent and purpose of the individual and answer same for self individuality.

7. We are through with this reading.
