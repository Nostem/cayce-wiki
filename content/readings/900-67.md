# TEXT OF READING 900-67 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-67]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/7/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5270

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Street, 
11:40 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [900], of ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had regarding [Miss [4353]] of [[New York City|New York]], at his apartment ..., ... St., New York City, on the night of May 6, 1925 or early morning hours of May 7, 1925.  You will give the interpretation of [[Dreams|dream]], correlating same with conditions and circumstances, and tell the body [900] what should be his actions regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [900], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had in the early morning hours of May 7, 1925, as regarding [Miss [4353]] and the dream as is given of the condition as has existed, does exist, between the bodies [900] and [Miss [4353]], which in dream becomes rather that of the mental state as is created by co-relation of past incidents in the life of each individual and those as do appear in the future.

3. In the interpretation then we find those conditions of which the body, [900], has been given the understanding of.  Those, as we see, are to be treated in the light of the spiritual relation as exists between the mental or the higher forces of each individual.  The conditions as have existed recently in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for the body, [4353], should be taken into consideration.  As to the actions of the body, [900], regarding same, best that those be held in this position: The body should offer the assistance in the mental frame, yet not urging same on the body of [Miss [4353]].  Direct never through other individuals.

4. (Q)  Should he then see the body, [4353]?
(A)  Should the body [4353] desire that he see her, then do so.  Not until she does.

5. (Q)  Any other suggestions for [900] regarding this?
(A)  Let not those entanglements come in with such conditions that would bring about self-condemnation in any manner, either before or after of conditions.

6. We are through.
