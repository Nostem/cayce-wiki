# TEXT OF READING 5152-1 F 26 (Methodist)

**Reading [[5152-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/23/1944  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 13286

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - through his mother, Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Kansas.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, a very lovely person!  We have the body here, [5152].

3. The disturbances are indicated in the conditions on face, and especially lips, as the result of the great amount of acidity in the system and the tendency for the use (or the necessity for the use of that) for greater eliminations through alimentary canal.  These are the sources or causes of this.

4. As we find here, it will require patience, persistence, if we would eliminate this character of acidity from the system.

5. As we would find, we would begin and take at least three or five glasses of water carrying [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  This is not all the water to be taken.  Drink about six or eight glasses, of water daily, but in at least three of these put five drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

6. Also we would have [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations to eliminate the sedatives which have been parts of the consciousness through the activity, or lack of activity, in the gall duct area, so as to make proper coordinations between the general conditions which exist through the body.

7. We would do these, and use over the abdominal area, as well as the area along the right side, [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs. These we would do regularly.  Do this about two days in succession and rest a period of two days between each series. These should be taken for at least an hour.  Saturate a cotton cloth, three to four thicknesses with Glyco-Thymoline.

8. Do this, and keep the diet towards those things that produce better eliminations.

9. These applications should include a thorough massage given twice a week.

10. Follow through with these and we will find changes for the better.

11. Do keep the general mental attitude in the way of being not only worth something, not only being good and worth something, but be constructive, creative in thy thinking; and we will bring much better conditions for the body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  How often should the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] be given?
(A)  About eighteen to twenty days apart.

14. (Q)  How often should the Packs be given?
(A)  Two days each week.  Two days and then rest two days.

15. (Q)  What can be done to boost her morale and overcome nervousness?
(A)  Keep those things of better spiritual interpretation and when there are bettered conditions in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, we will find the temperament of the body much improved.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self through Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyco-Thymoline Packs.)

> any information you could give me about the irrigations.

> as I understand it, can be given by a member of your family.
> It isn't something, though, that you want to hurry through
> with, but follow the suggestions pretty thoroughly.  Not only
> is the massage to be given along the spine and over the rest
> of the body, but particularly over the abdomen, to correct
> the circulation through those areas.  If it is impractical

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 10/9/17 in Kansas; single; occupation not indicated.
> 
> B2. 12/43 Mother's letter:  "I am in receipt of your letter
> saying I could have a reading for my daughter on 5/23/44.  If
> we can do no better, we will have to accept that date, but
> I would like so much to do something sooner.  She has a
> peculiar condition of the lips.  She has been to Mayo
> Brothers and to 2 other specialists.  They all agree she will
> probably get well, but do not say when.  The trouble started
> just a year ago.  She would like so much to get work, and
> aside from the disfigurement of the face, she is physically
> able.  You can imagine also what this does to her morale..."
> 
> B3. 5/4/44 Mother's letter:  "...She will be here at home in ...,
> Kansas.  The questions are as follows:  (1) Can she be cured,
> and how?  (2) What has caused her trouble?  Her morale has
> gotten so low, and she has become so nervous, that I would be
> glad to have any suggestions pertaining to her welfare.  If
> you desire any information, I will send it to you.
