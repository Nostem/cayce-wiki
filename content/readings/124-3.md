# TEXT OF READING 124-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[124-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/1/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 287

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois, this 1st day of May 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; Agnes Jenson (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now there is much change in this body from that we have had before.  The body is receiving from the system these properties that come nearer meeting with the conditions through the system.  Though not all elimination necessary to carry off the poisons created in the system are being cared for, hence with the overtaxed nerve centers as we have given we find some trouble with the nerves and ligaments about portions of the body that show and give expression of the effect created in the system by the unbalanced condition or perfect incentives for the body, see.  Keep closer to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as we have given in the proper centers and places, and we will find the better conditions will develop for the body, and remove the source of some toxins produced in the system that we find with the teeth, you see.

2. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, regards to the body of [124] what treatment would you prescribe regards her limbs and feet?
(A)  Just as we have given.  Remove that producing the cause and give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the portions of the body that govern the circulation and elimination through the lower limbs and feet, see.  The inhalation to give the incentive to the blood supply.  The [[Vibration|vibration]], necessary to give the correct incentives to eliminate and remove the conditions producing the toxins.  Do that.  We are through.
