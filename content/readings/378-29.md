# TEXT OF READING 378-29 M 57

**Reading [[378-29]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/2/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2911

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by daughter, Miss [275], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
11:25 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will, in the light of Divine guidance, aid in assisting [[Soul|the soul]] of [378] to seek to do that which is best and in keeping with that his Maker would have his [[Soul|soul]] WILL to do at this time.

2. EC:  [In undertone, "[[Jesus Christ|The Christ]]!"]  Yes.  Let not thy heart be troubled.  Ye believe in God.  Ye know that in doing, being that which is just and right in thine own experience, ye bring to your [[Soul|soul]] peace, to your understanding enlightenment.

3. Keep the heart and mind in accord and attuned to those promptings that will guide thee.  For, His ways are not past finding out.  Yet each soul must experience in itself that necessary for its awareness of His Spirit bearing witness with thy spirit, thy spirit being the gift of the Maker that thou mightest bear witness with Him that has brought and does bring to all that fullness of strength to meet every emergency in the experience of a soul that is in the consciousness of that which surrounds it in its transition or change; or that which makes for the awareness of His presence bearing witness with thee that thy soul is in accord with the life-giving flow that is ever present in that which holds to the [[Oneness|oneness]] of His expression in material, in mental, in spiritual realms.

4. That we may change in our mental attitude should be the awareness of all.  For, change shows - and is - the essence of growth.  For, ye grow in the knowledge, in the understanding of that which makes the awareness of His presence abiding in thee.

5. Faint not, but let that mind be in thee and thy being that was in Him that gave, "I go to prepare a place for you, and where I am ye may be also."

6. Send that thou hast in materiality made as a manifestation of the mind that is in thee.  For, the love of the Father will sustain thee; and that love, as it will be manifested in the lives, the activities, the hearts, the presence of thy fellow man, will bear thee up.  For, like begets like.  If ye will but put your trust in Him, then He may trust thee.  Do ye doubt, do ye fear the voice of men more than the voice of thy conscience, of thy soul?  The BODY is temporal, the soul eternal!  Do not that which would make thine soul even fearful.  Rather give place to that which might be fearful in body to that which would make thee fearful in the inner self. For, He is in His holy temple, even in thee.  All life is an expression of that divine love, as much in the warped body or mind as in the beautiful.  For, it gives the expression of the desire, [[Free Will|the will]], to make manifest HIS Spirit in the earth.

7. Let thy will be His will, thy ways His ways, His ways thy ways.  Not in doubt or in fear; not in doubting as to the strength of those that would aid thee in finding thyself. For, He is willing.  Be thou willing that He, the God, [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], the love, will guide thee!

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [275]

"    " Work Book
