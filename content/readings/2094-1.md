# TEXT OF READING 2094-1 M 70

**Reading [[2094-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/12/1940  Sex: M  Age: 70  ReadingID: 8737

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of February, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2094], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [2091].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here, [2094].

3. As we find, there are rather the complex disturbances with this body, and these affect the system in rather the combination of disturbances; though they may appear to centralize in one disturbance.

4. We find that with specific corrections of conditions, many of these combinations will also be corrected; and thus the general disturbance of disability, or inability for the more perfect health, will be overcome, - so that better general conditions will result.

5. These as we find are disturbances with this body, [2094] we are speaking of:

6. The blood supply indicates a form of anemia, as involved through the lack of the proper purification of the blood stream through oxidization in lungs.  Yet these are a part of the combination.

7. Also in the blood stream we find the tendency for a disturbance through the pancrean secretions; and thus an effect to the blood as related to sugar in the urine.  This saps the strength of the body, as it were, especially in relationship to the lymph or the emunctory-lymph circulation.

8. Hence there is some disturbance at times with the prostates, which is a sympathetic condition to the combination of complex disturbances.

9. As we find, in making corrections that would overcome these troubles, - there are those subluxations which exist in the 6th, 7th, 8th, and a portion of the 9th, dorsals.  These have gradually slowed the circulation, and the effect to the organs has been to produce a disturbance to lungs - as related to circulation and throat, to the pancreas as related to the hepatic circulation, and the general weaknesses which are indicated at times through the system.

10. First, we would have corrections osteopathically made; with special reference to that area indicated; coordinating the lumbar axis, as well as the coccyx end of the spine, as well as the dorsals, with the upper dorsals and throughout the cervical area.

11. These corrections osteopathically should be made twice each week until at least fifteen have been taken; then rest for at least ten days or two weeks, and then have another series of twelve to fifteen, - at which time they may be taken once a week, or three times in two weeks.

12. We would add to the diet those effective activities for the blood stream, particularly.  Hence, we would take internally, at least twice each day, the [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] or its equal; or preferably the White's [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] Tablets; two twice each day.

13. Also twice each week we would eat (with the regular meal) a [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]]; one about the size of a small hen egg, not larger; and not oftener than twice a week.  This should be cooked in its own juice, as in Patapar Paper, and may be mixed with a little salt and butter if desired.  Keep this up regularly in this manner for at least ten or twelve weeks.

14. This, as combined with the general diet which has been outlined, should bring for the body the better and nearer normal conditions.

15. When there are the nervous spells, and the tendency for the lack of proper rest, or the overtiring that comes from exercise at times, use the Radio-Active Appliance as an equalizer.  Take it for thirty minutes to an hour.  If it is for insomnia, or nervousness, or the hot and cold flushes, take it for an hour.  If it is for tiredness, take for thirty minutes, - but, of course, lie prone during the time it is taken; and use that period as the period for creative, constructive or spiritual [[Meditation|meditation]].

16. And these should bring better conditions for this body.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  What climate is best suited for my health?  What location, what part of the country?
(A)  As we find, it is best to follow the locations as the climate changes.  In the winter, we find that [[Florida|Florida]] would be very well.  In the middle of the season, or in the early spring, somewhat farther north, - the Carolinas or Virginia. In the mid-summer, - the St. Lawrence or Maine.

19. (Q)  Is the treatment I am now taking proper and beneficent, and likely to cure me?
(A)  If it had been entirely, we would not have suggested additional applications!

20. (Q)  Is it possible for me to recover the large financial losses I have suffered since 1937?  If so, by what means?
(A)  These as we find may depend upon some of the associations and some of the investments as may be made.  But these should NOT be allowed to become a hindrance to the improvements.  For, as indicated by the spiritual meditative influences which should be a part of the applications, the attitude should be rather that if it is the better, and that the self may magnify or manifest the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it came into experience in the present through such recuperations, and not merely for physical satisfaction or self-indulgent satisfaction, then it will be.  Or, rather, put the self in the hands of, and in the mind with, the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], - to which each [[Soul|soul]] owes its being, its [[Purpose|purpose]], and its allegiance.

21. (Q)  Are my financial advisors the proper and best ones?
(A)  These are very well.

22. (Q)  Shall I travel abroad again?  If so, when?
(A)  This as we find will depend upon the recuperative forces as are brought about through the activities.  As we find, if it is so desired, it may within two to three years travel abroad again.

23. (Q)  Shall I be able to continue my work for human betterment?
(A)  This again, if it is for the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] and [[Purpose|purposes]] and desires, and not for self-glory, may be maintained.

These are to be determined, to be sure, within the own inner self.

24. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> inclined to be irritable.  My skin is highly sensitive.  I am
