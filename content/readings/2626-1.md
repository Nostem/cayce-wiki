# TEXT OF READING 2626-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2626-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/19/1941  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9671

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. Robert R. M. Emmet, 1 [[Grace|Grace]] Church St., Rye, [[New York City|New York]], this 19th day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2626], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1523].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:15 to 8:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are very serious.  Overanxieties in some respects, too much application of those activities that hinder or submerge the influences for replenishing. Accumulations that form by overactivity in the mesenteric system.

4. Thus the hindrances in the ability of the functioning of the assimilating and distributing of assimilated forces through the body.

5. While the heart and the activity of the liver, even of the kidneys at times - under the stress, are very good, all - of course - become involved, as does the whole of the viscera.

6. As we find, these offer the better opportunity for improvement:

7. We would keep, for at least three days at a time, and this each week - three days at least in each week, the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs about the whole of the abdominal area.  Let these extend from the area just below the ribs to the lower portion of the abdomen.  These we would apply so that the body will absorb all that is possible.  Keep on for an hour and a half or two hours.  Apply warm and then apply heat, an electric pad preferably.

8. Following the use of the Packs, especially, but also EACH day, and just after sponging off the body following the Packs on the days that these are given, we would massage the body thoroughly with Pure Peanut Oil.  This should be over all of the cerebrospinal system, the limbs, the neck and shoulders. Massage over the limbs, the neck and shoulders, as well as thoroughly along the cerebrospinal system - all the body will absorb.

9. Then, after the Peanut Oil rub, apply grain alcohol for strength, at least a twenty percent solution, - applying this over the same areas, you see, but especially the neck and shoulders.

10. Give the body doses of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] every two hours, but take some little time in sipping - not gulping same.

11. Do this, and keep the other foods being used - those that have already been administered, that are easily assimilated, but that carry properties that are body building.  These are preferable.  Eggs cooked with a little rum (by pouring the rum over the raw egg, you see), with milk, are also very good - and would be stimulating.

12. Do apply the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] for thirty minutes each day, alternating in the carrying of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] (in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of Distilled Water), [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] (commercial strength), and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (in the proportions of one ounce to two ounces of Distilled Water).

13. The attachments would be made as follows:

14. When using the Gold, - attach the copper plate at the 9th dorsal, and the nickel plate - through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially - to the umbilical center.

15. When using the [[Camphor|Camphor]], - the copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar, while the larger nickel plate - through which the [[Camphor|Camphor]] passes vibratorially - would be also attached to the umbilical center.

16. When using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] - on the third day, you see - attach the copper plate at the 2nd dorsal, or between the shoulders, and the nickel plate - through which the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Solution passes vibratorially - also to the umbilical center.

17. Continue rotating the applications in this manner.

18. In making the Appliance for carrying the charge, use; to the gallon and a half of Distilled Water:

Copper Sulphate...............2 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid C.P.............1 ounce,
Zinc.........................30 grains,
Willow Charcoal.............1/2 pound.

19. Do these, and after at least four weeks - or in thirty days - we would give further instructions.

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs and Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

See sheet [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], also extra copy of specifications to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., who will make up this Appliance for you.

When you have followed all the directions for thirty days, let us know so that we may make an appointment for a Check Physical Reading to give further instructions.)
