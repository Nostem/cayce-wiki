# TEXT OF READING 4463-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4463-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/27/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12324

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 27th day of October, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stallings, Brazelton & Hale Court Reporter (?), Steno. [4463].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep.  (Pause)  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep and all of the organs of your body will function properly.  You will hear me and answer all of my questions.  You have tonight before you the body of [4463], and the body is present in this room.  Now, Mr. Cayce, tell me the physical and mental ailments that you find within this body that you have before you, and if an operation is necessary please tell me, and if not, tell me what will relieve the conditions within this body which you have before you, [4463], present in this room.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, the abnormal conditions as we find in this body are principally those of functioning conditions rather than organic.  They have to do with the circulation, the nerve force, the intestinal tract, and was begun principally from indigestion that has affected the system and has become the seat of the troubles, and the others, we find, are mostly reflex, though there are at present other organs involved, that at present does not come directly from these conditions, or that is the digestive forces.  Now, these are the conditions as we find in this body, Mrs. [4463] we are speaking of.  The blood is impoverished, both on account of the condition existing in the liver and the hepatic circulation, caused by the blood carrying some of the conditions as expressed by these organs themselves, that is, as the circulation passes through these organs, liver and kidneys, there should be certain poisons eliminated from the system.  Also, as the lungs clarify the blood forces we find the conditions here are involved to such an extent as for the blood not to become wholly carbonized before going back through the system.  Hence, the effect in the system of feeling at different times as if different organs were involved each time.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

3. So we have as the cycle of the individual organ involved by such a condition approaches the incentive of the functioning of the organ itself, begins to express or to show in the nervous system the condition given off to the system, that is, the body.  There is a lack of sufficient red blood cells in the body carrying carbon forces.  There is sufficient number of white to meet broken or used forces in functioning organs, or else with the conditions existing the body would collapse in to coma state, for we find with taxation of nerve and muscular forces this body soon becomes fatigued, you see.

FEM:  Yes.

4. Short breath, fast heart, heavy tired feeling to the extremities, weighty expressions through the locomotories, overtaxed farther the head gives the expression of swimming or dizziness.  See?

FEM:  Yes.

5. In the nerve forces we find the brain through the cerebellum and through the nerves of the brain direct good.  The body is well balanced in this respect.  The sympathetic nervous system is overtaxed, for with the plethoric condition existing in the lungs themselves and the extra amount of blood that at times comes to the larynx and the bronchials, showing this condition, these nerves - sympathetic system - becomes overtaxed, giving stimulus through the muscular force to supply to the body the correct incentives.  See?

FEM:  Yes.

6. The organs of the throat, the larynx and the bronchials show the effect of this condition as we have given, both from muscular, [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] and nerve force.  The lungs themselves present a plethoric condition throughout their whole functioning tract.  Seldom the body breathes sufficiently deep in the lungs to give all of the force necessary.  That would be one of the things we would suggest first for this body to always do, - breathe deeper.  See?

FEM:  Yes, I see.

7. Especially early mornings.  The stomach itself presents the condition as of being often entreated to give the correct incentives for intestinal functioning throughout; that is, it shows through its nerves and ganglions and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that it has often been, as we would term, reproved for its condition. See?

FEM:  Yes.

8. Through the right lobe of the liver, where we find the functioning of the secretive forces has given to this organ, they do not function always proper, while the excretory forces at present, though they have not always been so, function better than those in the other lobe.  The kidneys present only that condition as of a hepatic circulation of rather slow action, for at times they become overtaxed, and again do not secrete sufficiently of the fluids needed to be eliminated through this organ.  See?

FEM:  Yes.

9. Now, these are the conditions as that are present in this system.

10. (Q)  Then, Mr. Cayce, give us the remedy to clarify all of these conditions.
(A)  Then, Mr. Cayce, give us the remedy to clarify all of these conditions.  We will reach those here.  The action of the nerve throughout the cerebrospinal nervous system working in coordination with those of the sensory organism of the body can and will have, if properly directed, a great influence over the whole system, not that the conditions existing are not real, existing forces within the body, yet they may be controlled a great deal by the mind of this body itself, if properly adjusted or trained or given the correct [[Vibration|vibration]].  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

The body should train its mind along a given set of rules to require this thought and development of mind along such a channel.  Take in to the system such proprieties as will assist the physical to reach its equilibrium and that will aid the mind forces to assist in overcoming the conditions in the body.  Such a line of thought or study would be that as found in Unity.  Such medicinal properties as this we would take, that it is - for the condition existing - and that it has come spasmodically in the bronchials and larynx, we would take Calcidin, one tablet, as it is compounded, which is as this Calcidin, - [[Iodine|Iodine]] compounded for the system, acts directly on the respiratory system.  As that for the condition in the system, helping to produce the proper incentives to the rest of the body, we would take this: Plain Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]], half a teaspoonful in a half a glass of water each morning before breakfast.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

When this is stirred together, we would put into this five drops of Syrup of Sarsaparilla.  Every third day add two drops of a ten percent solution of [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium.  Do this, with the mental training - will bring perfect balance to this body within nine moons.  We are through.

11. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, this body has one particular question that it wants you to answer.  Will you please tell her, or tell this body what the condition of her bladder is?
(A)  Mr. Cayce, will you please tell this body what the condition of her bladder is.  The effect as given, in the hepatic circulation, where the action of the liver and kidneys come in their conjunction, or action over the system, the opposite or the negative pole in this body, the kidneys over function at times, you see.

FEM:  Yes, sir.

As we have given here.  They produce then, with the amount that is being thrown off by the kidneys, the excess of matter as tried to be eliminated from the system, that the change of the condition that would be expressed in the system over the functioning of this organ itself, by the action in the system of this, will change the condition as exists through the bladder from the irritation of the secretions as thrown off. The irritation, as produced in the bladder, comes from the secretions as thrown off by the kidneys in this overtaxed condition from the condition of the liver.  See?

FEM:  Yes, I see.

Both must be in perfect balance to bring the system to this perfect correlation in its functioning of the organ.  Organic conditions - only functioning conditions.

12. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, this body wants to know, then, if this treatment will reduce the high blood pressure.  Then you will be through.
(A)  Mr. Cayce, this body wants to know, then, whether this treatment will reduce the high blood pressure.  Then you will be through.  As we have given here, the condition of the blood and its action over the system was produced by the conditions through the hepatic circulation, the condition of the liver itself, the overcharge or the condition produced to the kidneys themselves, and the action through that of the larynx, which creates the condition at the cardiac plexus center, produced high blood pressure, you see.

FEM:  I see.

That is where they reach or show in the system itself. Correction of the conditions through the hepatic circulation through that of the larynx or the forces as governed by that, that we are giving the system the Calcidin, to retard and give the pressure corralled in its proper form - this will relieve the conditions as we have given.  See?

FEM:  Yes.

To reduce specific pain within the body at the times that we have either an expression to the kidneys or to the liver, for their action becomes one from the other through the action of the hepatic circulation, you see.

FEM:  Yes.

We would take only those of heat to produce the respiratory circulation.  The body can control its whole system through its mind forces, if it will govern itself in such a way. The medicinal properties will only act those through the functioning of the organs themselves as it given through physical force.  Use much of the mind too.  See?

13. Yes.  Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about this reading you have given.  You are feeling all right, and all of the organs of your body will function properly.  You are feeling good.  Now, wake up in two minutes feeling fine and all right, and without a headache.
