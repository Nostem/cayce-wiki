# TEXT OF READING 257-233 M 48 (Sales Mgr., Hebrew)

**Reading [[257-233]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/18/1941  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 875

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of June, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Arthurdale Inn, 
Time of Reading                              Arthurdale, 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  West Va. 
[He was there 
June 17, 1941.]

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, while conditions are not changed a great deal, they are better in many respects.

4. We would follow very closely those suggested applications, and be mindful of the warnings indicated.

5. If the body overtaxes self, or through neglect does not do those things indicated as oft as suggested, then there may be expected the bad after effects.  For, these mild applications made farther apart than indicated, with the character of disturbance in the body, there may be an increase in the disturbance - rather than there being sufficient of the properties to eliminate or allay same.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is my present course of treatments at Reilly's doing what they are expected?
(A)  When they are done, they are very well; but be mindful of what has just been indicated.  Taking less than the amount indicated is dangerous!

8. (Q)  Is the green light properly given?
(A)  This is very good.

9. (Q)  Why occasional pain over wound?
(A)  This is more from weakness and the low resistances in the system.

10. (Q)  Is peanut oil best rubbing oil?
(A)  It is the best rubbing oil if sufficient of it is given for the body to absorb, for it becomes rancid less often than others.

11. (Q)  Is peanut oil taken internally as beneficial as [[Olive Oil|olive oil]]?
(A)  Peanut Oil is a lubricant and not a food as [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] is.  In some conditions it would be very well.  As an eliminant it is very good.  As a food value in some conditions it is more harmful than beneficial.

12. (Q)  What is my physical condition now?
(A)  As just indicated.

13. (Q)  Diet?
(A)  Keep to those things outlined.

14. (Q)  What exercise now for this body?
(A)  Too oft the body takes too much exercise of the wrong kind!

15. (Q)  Would present locality be good for health and mind of body during summer?
(A)  If so desired, and if the mountains is what is desired! Choose for self.

16. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Better keep close to those things and those warnings that have been indicated, or else be sorry!

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
