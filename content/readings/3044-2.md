# TEXT OF READING 3044-2 M 43

**Reading [[3044-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/30/1943  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 10363

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3044], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Mo.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. There are great changes in the body since last we had it. But we would follow those lines that have been indicated for this body; for there must be other corrections made.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Shall I permit another operation to take place for sinus in the upper region?  (The lower left side has been operated on and the infection stopped from that source.  There is still some trouble apparently over my left eye.)
(A)  Either that or use the Elliott Machine as the corrective sources; that is, so that heat may be applied in such measures or degrees. But under these stresses we find that operative measures would be the better.

Be sure there are better conditions for coagulation.  And we would have the operation.

6. (Q)  Are my stomach disorders due to sinus?
(A)  The greater portion we find due to the drippings that cause the irritations and infectious forces.

7. (Q)  Can my left eyesight be improved through the operation?
(A)  This will be improved too, from the rest as well as from the corrections made there.  Yes, it will be helped.

8. (Q)  Are there any other physical deficiencies of which I am unaware?
(A)  With the rest and with those corrections, if there is the full response, we find that all of the disturbances with this body in the present should be corrected.

9. (Q)  What would you recommend I do to regain some weight and generally improve my health, including eyes and weak stomach?
(A)  These will necessarily come after the building up processes in the activities.

When the body is on the mend, take once each day at least four ounces of gingerale with two ounces of top cream in same.

Take plenty of outdoor exercise, morning walks, and we will build up the body.

10. (Q)  Are my [[Intuition|hunches]] to be followed in matters requiring decisions?  Are these dependable?
(A)  If there is the attuning of self to spiritual forces, yes.  If these are taken only from the physical-mental self, no.  But the [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces of this body are good. Cultivate them.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n file
