# TEXT OF READING 900-75 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-75]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/26/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5278

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
3:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                [[New York City|New York City]].

1. LS:  You will have before you the body of [900], and the enquiring mind of this body, and you will answer questions I will ask you regarding [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on night of May 22nd, or early morning hours of May 23, 1925.  You will answer, as I ask the questions, in a manner understandable to the mind of [900].

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, as we have had here before, with the [[Dreams|dream]] and [[Visions|vision]] as had on evening of 22nd and early morning of 23rd, May, 1925.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  "I saw steel sell at 120.  Then a voice said:  'Now analyze the conditions.'"  Steel today, Saturday, sold down to 118-1/4.  Does this mean steel will again sell at 120?
(A)  Steel will again sell at 120.

4. (Q)  So many wide and varied things must be considered in analysis of Steel.  First their great surplus, substantial financial basis, then Gary's optimism about future business, but his assertion that something is wrong with present business.  Then much depends on whether the right people are buying the stocks and finally I have the [[Visions|vision]] of an impending slump.  How should I analyze Steel in the light of these many other factors?
(A)  The factors as entering into the [[Visions|vision]] as received is not of Gary's or individuals' buying; being, as has been given, the factors entering in the making of the impressions as received by [900].  And these have been explained to the body in how one may enter from the purely physical (such as is reasoned in that just given) and how same may enter as the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] action of conditions, as steel will again sell at 120, or such as is given in other visions as have been related.  Act on these.

5. (Q)  Give that explanation that will make this vision of Steel selling at 120 clearer to the mind of [900] such that he may use the information given him about Steel in an advantageous, businesslike manner.

(A)  As has just been given.  The co-relations [correlations] must come within the mental forces of the mind of [900]. Just as has been given and acted upon from that viewpoint, for as has been given, conditions with the individual, [900], may be such as to project physical and physical alone in such reasonings.  Again there may be such as the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces project same.  There may be the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and the [[Superconscious|super-conscious]] forces.  As to when these come from these sources, the individual must do the judging.  We are through with this.

6. (Q)  (Reading second [[Dreams|dream]])  "It seemed the blood rushed from my head as many times before in such visions.  I seemed to realize I was in bed in an upset room such as my own now is. Gradually my bed started to move slowly at first and I said: 'I trust and serve the Lord my God and Father!'  At that the bed swung around - I seemed to extend somewheres - yet something was troubling me terribly and I repeated my faith in the Father.  Suddenly, in a large bed opposite me I saw my Father and Spiritual Guide.  Some thought seemed to be conveyed to me and partly in fear but much more in anguish I cried out:  'Ma!  Ma!  Ma!'  I wanted to run to her or have her come to me, but she didn't answer.  My father still was there.  Heartbroken that my mother didn't answer I started to sob and cry.  'She doesn't answer - she won't come,' I said leaning towards my father, trying to see him better.  Then sobbed the harder.  Slowly my bed moved back and quietly - the vision complete, I awakened."

You will give the lesson and the interpretation of this dream.
(A)  In this we see the visions of that as has been explained, given, often to the individual and enquiring mind of [900].  The entering into the Borderland in that the moving of the physical, giving the moving of the subconscious forces into that which may often bring anguish, when attempted to be explained by or through, or for, physical conditions.  And the attempt to induce others may bring the same anguish, unless brought in the manner and why as has been given.  The ability of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to retain the connection with the spiritual forces in the Borderland being that development to which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has been able to guide self, and shows the greater forces that may enter in.  For, there will be brought to the consciousness the peace and quietude as was felt in the presence, as it were, of these spiritual forces.  Then, as has often been given, "Be thou faithful, would thou enter in the joy and peace," no matter the expense or the spending of self in any and every relation, the joy of eternity different from the physical moment.

7. We are through.
