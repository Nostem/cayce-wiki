# TEXT OF READING 3146-1 F 17

**Reading [[3146-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/10/1943  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 10514

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] LaClair.)]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Pa.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3146].

3. As we find there are disturbing conditions which have prevented the nearer normal functioning of the body. These have to do primarily with [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] conditions.

4. The source of these we find is a pressure that caused the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] to become overactive.  This has caused an unusual growth in the structural portions of the body; to the joints, tendons; which has caused a drain on the system.

5. With the operative measures and the submerging of the activities of the coordinating forces between the sympathetic and cerebral spinal-nerve consciousness, these were arrested. Yet spasmodic reactions were resultant, because the sources of irritation had not been removed.

6. These at present prevent activity within structure; as the thigh, the rib, and the areas through which there is the formation of certain plasma cells or functioning.  Hence, while there is a form of anemia, it is the character that causes rather tendencies in the ability of the glands in the stomach to produce sufficient for the activity in the pineals.  Thus we find the imaginative body overactive.

7. We find that these may be materially aided, but will require these characters of application.  These should be done under the direction of, or by, an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] of the "first water". We would suggest such an one as Dr. Dick in Philadelphia.

8. These once begun, there would be the setting up of the corrections that must be made in the first, second, and third cervical, the second, third, and fourth dorsal, and through the lumbar area, the sacral, and especially the sacro-ileac plexus.

9. Each week there should be the treatments with the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]], using the green light projected between the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] and the body, this for about a minute and a half to two minutes once each week.

10. For thirty minutes each day at least, use the Radio Active Appliance.  This period should be used for [[Meditation|meditation]], for preparing the body in its spiritual and mental meditations.

11. The massage and correction should be done about twice each week.

12. A series of eight to ten of these adjustments should be had, then a period of rest from them of at least three to four weeks, then a period of having six to eight more, which should be sufficient for these corrections, but the Radio Active should be used daily throughout the periods, keeping the attachment plates very clean, polishing them with the emery paper each time when taken off and each time just before attaching to the body.

13. Do these and keep a nominal, active life.  Keep a normal diet, stressing rather the yellow vegetables and yellow fruit with plenty of sea food throughout the period.

14. These we find should bring normal forces for this body, for it is an exceptional entity in its ability to apply itself in this material world.

15. Do that.

16. Ready for questions.

Two Copies to Mother (one for Dr. Marion Dick, 5041 Spruce St., Philadelphia, who has had considerable experience with carrying out directions given through the readings) Copy to Ass'n File
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

>

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 7/11/43 Mother's letter:  "Until 4 yrs. ago she seemed to
> be perfectly healthy in every respect; then she grew very
> rapidly for about a year but did not put on the weight to
> correspond with her growth.  This did not seem to affect
> her except in strenuous gym exercise, but we had the usual
> routine check-up made and she was decidedly underweight.
> Then in the fall of '41 she had her appendix out and seemed
> to gain for a while after that.  In June of '42 and from then
> on until Dec. '42 she would have periods of severe pain in
> her left side.  In Nov. of '42 she had a complete physical
> examination, was in the hospital and had as complete an
> examination as possible - everything was in good condition
> organically.  Dec. 4th she came home from school with a
> severe headache which lasted off and on until Dec. 14th when
> she stayed home and in bed nearly all of the time.  Dec.
> 23rd we took her to the hospital, unconscious, following 2 or
> 3 spasms.  Dec. 25th she regained consciousness and neither
> the unconsciousness nor the spasms have been repeated.  She
> was not back in school until Feb. 16th.  Since then various
> things have been tried.  There is a strong history of allergy
> in the family and the first of June we began to give her
> a serum but it made her much worse, in fact she is just
> now recovering from it.  It's a progressive sort of thing
> affecting first one set of nerves and then another.  At
> present her eyes are affected.  In spite of it all she
> graduated from H.S. as valedictorian of her class, yet I
> would not say she studied hard.  These are the theories which
> have been advanced, any one or all may be right but we would
> like to really know, or at least know if we are on the right
> track.  We have had five physicians and a masseur.  1. All
> agree that she has grown too rapidly, is very active mentally
> and physically and her muscles, nerves and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] have
> probably not kept up with her rapid physical growth.  2. All
> want her to put on weight.  This she does not do in spite
> of a good appetite.  3. A psychosis - one Dr. holds to this
> idea but the rest who know her better and the masseur sees no
> evidence of this, as she has a healthy, happy attitude toward
> life herself.  4. Complete nervous and physical fatigue -
> reason unknown.  5. Allergy - our allergy specialist has
> found nothing so far but the allergy in the family makes this
> idea have sense.  6. An infection which we cannot find, or
> a brain pressure - which has been ruled out.  7. Adolescent
> development which time will remedy.  This is a complete
> [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]], as much as I can give physically.  She's
> underweight, has periods of excessive weakness, aches,
> headache, poor action at times of her 'smooth' muscles.
> Emotionally & socially has a nice balance, is religious in a
> wholesome way, cheerful even thru this whole year of illness.
> Socially has had problems which apparently did not upset her.
> We have moved frequently so that she has been in 4 different
> schools since she was in the 8th grade, has not had close
> friends the last 2 yrs. in school but has had in the
> vicinity, so her week-ends have been very busy and happy when
> she was able for it.  Mentally she has completed the college
> prep. course in H.S. and also typing & shorthand, likes to
> study - really enjoys it..."
> 
> 1. (If [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] is suggested)  Will massage do as well?
> (We have an excellent masseur with some medical training &
> some psychology.  He works in cooperation with many of the
> doctors here.  If there are any special treatments indicated
> will you indicate where they may be obtained.  I am not at
> all sure of the reaction of our doctors to this and in case
> it is negative I would like to know of doctors who would
> cooperate.)
