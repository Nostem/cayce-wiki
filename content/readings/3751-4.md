# TEXT OF READING 3751-4 M 3

**Reading [[3751-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/13/1922  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11271

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]] on the 13th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:19 A. M.                                    [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, the conditions as we find in this body, [3751] we are speaking of, we find the treatments are bringing to the cuticle and to the respiratory system much of the poisons that should be eliminated from the system, and that the medicinal properties that are given to cause the lymphatic forces to eliminate inwardly are working to the betterment of the body, with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces as have been applied to the system.  The body will continue to improve.  The body should have deeper manipulation, especially, about the solar plexus center and over and about the liver, and the ilium and sacral plexuses should be stimulated, also.

2. For the abrasions, or for the blemishes that are showing over the body, the drain for these, we would apply this lotion, [rather] than some that have been used (Well to use the antiseptic as has been used, but they would be better to be the opposite from the acid) - use this:

3. To (that is, these are the proportions to make these in) - six ounces of Plantain, that is, the common Plantain root and stem, preferably, in the green state as would be found at this season of the year, to one pint of water, simmer until reduced almost to a jelly, strain off, yet mix enough of the roots, see, that are cut up in this to make the solution almost jelly, add sufficient quantity of the oil of butterfat (that is, the butter with the milk and all impurities cooked out by heat, and of course, the salt or any other ingredient) to make a salve, you see.  Apply night and morning to these broken places, you see.  Do that.

4. The [atropine] injections will only be given when we see the elimination is not giving properly to the capillaries or until we see the system is cleansing.  Give that just above the umbilical center.
