# TEXT OF READING 3863-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[3863-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/3/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11490

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3863].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes.  Now, we find the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are somewhat improved from those as we have had here before, yet we find those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have been given that were to be set up within the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, by carrying out those suggestions as were given for the improvement and correction of these conditions, have NOT been carried out in the body.

3. In these disturbing conditions, we find these are produced by the incoordination of the system; that is, in the distribution of the replenishing or rebuilding forces in the system wherein there has been caused weakness, and where tissue has become so involved through this weakness as to produce that stress and strain on forces of the system.  This application of this [[Vibration|vibration]], with the correction of the incentives of nerve energy in system, would create that necessary to bring about the strengthening of the walls of the tissue, and in correcting those things that hinder the perfect coordination and cooperation of conditions in this body.

4. Would the body then, receive that as has been given for the correction, the body should - in toto - carry out those suggestions as have been given.  Only a portion will not bring the necessary correction for the body, for that stimulation in the blood supply and the electrification in the nerve energy is as necessary as that relaxing or as tissue being given the incentive.  Unless that incentive is accompanied with the rejuvenation necessary, no stability may be created in the benefits as are received.

5. Then, do that as has been given.  We would not change from those suggestions as have been given.  Only carry same out in their full complete way and manner.

6. We are through for the present.
