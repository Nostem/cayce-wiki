# TEXT OF READING 2235-1 M 68

**Reading [[2235-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/31/1929  Sex: M  Age: 68  ReadingID: 8958

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2235].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2235].  Now, we find there are complications of conditions that affect the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These must be met, as we find, to aid the body to better physical health, in an orderly manner - and consideration given to the various other defects in the physical functioning, organic disturbance, the eliminations and the assimilations for the system; for not only are the organs themselves involved in the conditions, AS organs, but also the functioning of other organs as, also, the blood supply and the organs that clarify same, is MOST in distress.

2. Then, to meet the immediate needs for this body, we would first use as this:  Those of the carbon ash, given once each day - one-quarter grain in carbonated water, or the water that is used, half carbon, half plain - and the quarter grain of the ash stirred in same before taking, and we would take this at least in the middle of the day, as near as possible.

3. We would then, every OTHER day, apply those of the ultra violet rays.  It will be necessary to continue for a time some of those elements given to ease the body.  These, as we find, are gradually losing their strength, but when these [[Vibration|vibrations]] are given, will the body respond to these, we will find that there will be loosened in system more of the oxygen, and this will allow the vitality to re-establish itself better.

4. For the diets, we would use principally those that are predigested, blood building, and carrying as much iron as will be well assimilated by the body.

5. Do that.  Then, in twenty to twenty-six days, we would give further instructions for this body, [2235].  Ready for questions.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mr. [165]

> 
> The chamber already has attracted a number of business and
> political leaders, including Sen. Everett M. Dirksen, R-Ill.

> saw the hyperbaric chamber.
> 
> In hyperbaric treatment, patients breathe pure oxygen from
> masks while they lie in a chamber where air is pumped to
> three times its normal density.  The high pressure causes the
> oxygen to effortlessly dissolve in body-tissue, resting the
> heart and providing a remarkable economy in the metabolism of
> the body.
> 
> "The tissues absorb 22 times more oxygen in the
> three-atmosphere pressure of the hyperbaric chamber,"
> Dr. Abbott said.
> 
> Oxygen is normally [[Teleportation (Atlantean)|transported]] through the body by red blood
> corpuscles.  Under hyperbaric treatment, according to Dr.
> Abbott, "you could probably live without red cells all
> together."
> 
> The chamber at St. Barnabas, twin steel tubes 45 feet long
> and 12 feet wide, provides what hospital president Anthony
> Scala calls the most complete hyperbaric facility in the
> world.
> 
> During a week at the hospital last month, Sen. Dirksen
> underwent five normal hyperbaric minutes of breathing pure
> oxygen.
> 
> Dirksen's treatment was for a lung disorder, and when he left

> 72-year-old senator told a news conference the treatments
> had given him a lift.
> 
> U.S. Sens. George Smathers and Richard Russell are scheduled
> to check in for treatments later this month.
>

> who said he hopes to build a much larger hyperbaric center

> 
> Scala said Reynolds also hopes to construct hyperbaric units
> across the nation in conjunction with old-age housing.  Scala
> said the aim would be to give daily treatments to the
> elderly, resting and reviving their body systems.
