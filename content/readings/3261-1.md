# TEXT OF READING 3261-1 M 13

**Reading [[3261-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/4/1943  Sex: M  Age: 13  ReadingID: 10670

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [3900], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3261].

3. As we find, these are disturbing conditions here - as they are the effects of a temperature produced in the body of a very low form, from congestions in the liver and incoordination between liver and kidney circulation.  Thus poisons throughout the system, the tendency for lack of appetite - or the excess of same that upsets the body, and a more specific condition of infection where there have been those abrasions that cause a great deal of disturbance.

4. We would prepare a compound to be taken for purifying the blood.  While this is not as a blood condition, there are poisons in the system from lack of proper eliminations. Combine these ingredients:

Sulphur...................1 level tablespoonful,
Cream of Tartar...........1 level tablespoonful,
Rochelle Salts............1 level tablespoonful.

5. Mix these thoroughly together, very thoroughly.  Of morning before the meal is taken, stir a level teaspoonful of this mixture in a glass of water and drink it.  Not very pleasant but he can take it.

6. Do this for three mornings, leave off then, and wait for another week.  Then take it again for three days, and wait another week, and take it again, and so on.

7. In the meantime have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments made where there are subluxations in the upper dorsal and at the 3rd cervical areas.

8. For the conditions in the knee, or between knee and ankle or on the ankle, use Ichthyol Ointment to clear this disturbance.

9. In the diet keep closer to the semi-liquids; not too great quantities of meat, but fruit, vegetable soups - all characters, and whole grain cereals - oatmeal, Maltex and such.  Use Cream of Wheat with Wheat Germ on same.  All of these use in abundance for the body.

10. And with these we should clear these conditions.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is the extreme nervousness and sleeplessness a purely physical thing that can be remedied with medicines?
(A)  Purely physical.  Correct those disturbances in the areas indicated, but this will have to be done osteopathically.

13. (Q)  Is the trouble he has with his eyes itching and burning caused from the chronic sinus trouble he has.
(A)  Caused from the kidney trouble as well as from the sinus, but all of these things suggested will help set up drainages.  The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections should set up better drainages.

As a spray in the sinus or in the nasal passages, use - in a De Vilbiss atomizer - an equal combination of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and distilled water.  Put the atomizer in warm water and let it be sufficiently warmed too before using; not hot but warm.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file

(Note:  While the Sulphur is in the system, be sure the body does not get his feet wet or get caught in the rain, or the like.  Not that he shouldn't bathe, but don't get damp in the weather - this would be bad, while the Sulphur is active in system.)
