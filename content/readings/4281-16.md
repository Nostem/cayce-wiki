# TEXT OF READING 4281-16 F 8

**Reading [[4281-16]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/23/1927  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 12076

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [4280].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               Griffin, Ga.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  We have the body - we have had this before.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What causes the distressing cough this body has, and what can be done to stop it?
(A)  The after effects of the cough - whooping cough - and the weakness of the resistance in the body.  To relieve this condition, use this as an inhalant, using same whenever the cough is at the worst, see?  or just after such a coughing spell, which - as we see - occurs when the body lies down or soon after getting up.

To four (4) ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol...........................30 minims,
Rectified Creosote....................5 minims,
Benzoin..............................10 minims,
Balsam of Fir........................10 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp................10 minims,
Tincture of Tolu in solution.........50 minims.

Keep the solution in a container twice the size of the quantity, with a glass cork.  Shake solution each time before the inhalation is taken in the throat.  This will act as a restrainer, and will also heal the tissue irritated by coughing, and act as an antiseptic for the bronchials and lungs.  Deep breathing - taking this into the bronchials and upper portion of lungs - the gas off of same that forms by shaking this combination together.  Do that for the cough.

4. (Q)  What can be done to build up her blood?
(A)  Use the manipulations osteopathically to distribute and to give the proper incentive from all centers that radiate from the cerebrospinal system to the blood building centers of the body, using as a stimulus - with this those - [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the [[Radium|Radium Pad]] - X.  [GD's note:  Regular strength, not Double X]  This will build the blood, clarifying same, and stimulating the nerve system with the manipulations.  Then the food, of course, should be considered, for THIS is from which the blood is built.  This has been given often.  FOLLOW those suggestions as have been given concerning same, using that which will ASSIMILATE with the body.  Very little of the fats are to be given until better assimilation is seen, but this will be found to improve when the system eliminates better under the rebuilding forces of these two [[Vibration|vibrations]], or three [[Vibration|vibrations]], as will be set in the system.

5. (Q)  Should the pad be worn continually?  and on what part of this spine?
(A)  On the solar plexus, and worn continually, or that is, during the day, when the body is up - and this should be charged, or laid in the sunshine, at least a few minutes twice each day - otherwise be worn continually, and may be laid across the breast, or the chest, at night - which would - as the radiation from same - be well there, and assist the replenishing of the vibratory forces through the lung proper and bronchials.

6. (Q)  What can be done to gain weight for this body?
(A)  Better assimilation will gain weight for the body.

7. (Q)  How much below normal is her blood?
(A)  The below normal is very little, save as the element to replenish in the system.

8. (Q)  Is her whole condition below par?
(A)  No.

9. (Q)  Will magnetic treatment cure her?
(A)  Those vibratory forces as have been given will be the best for the body at the present.  Magnetic forces will be reactive through these conditions.  Do that as has been outlined for the body.

10. (Q)  Would electric magnetism do her any good?
(A)  Rather that of the vibratory forces from [[Radium|radium]] will assist this body.

11. (Q)  Should the body try to study one hour each day?
(A)  Be WELL for the body to study one hour each day.

12. (Q)  Would it be harmful for her to take up the study of music at this time?
(A)  No.

13. (Q)  Should she eat any butter at all?  Would this affect the ringworm on her knee?
(A)  Eat no butter at all.  Apply [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] for the ringworm on the knee.

14. (Q)  Will B. B. Culture benefit her?
(A)  Only in the manner and the way as has been outlined in the general diet for the body.

15. (Q)  Would it harm this body to go barefoot in Griffin?
(A)  Too late to go barefoot in Griffin!

16. That is all the questions.

17. Follow these as have been given, and we will bring the better conditions to this body, [4281].

18. We are through for the present.
