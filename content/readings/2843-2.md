# TEXT OF READING 2843-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[2843-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/7/1933  Sex: F  ReadingID: 10057

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1933, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2843], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her son Mr. [311].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [311] and H. L. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2843], this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, while there are conditions that disturb the body in the present in some respects the conditions are bettered, and the ministrations for the body are in that direction which will bring about better conditions; though there should be, as we find, for conditions that have developed - or that are the outcome of other disorders which have arisen, that administered to make more helpful, easy and sure correction of the conditions.

3. These are conditions, then, as we find them in the present:

4. In that form of anemia, that prevents the circulation being as active or effective in the system as it should be, still in some portions there is shown the reaction as indicated, in that disorder which comes more as spasmodic reactions from the bronchi and throat, as well as making a fullness as related to the heart's action at times.

5. This, then, will be materially aided by there being added in the system those elements to make for an impulse in the circulation through the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], that make for the creating of the proper elements or homones [hormones] in the blood stream itself for greater resuscitation, and the practical divisibility of the red blood cells themselves in circulation.

6. The effect also of these properties would be to aid in clarifying those distresses that are caused in the lower hepatic circulation, or the functioning of the kidneys as related to eliminations, and to clear those strains that are caused in the bladder at times.

7. These properties (which, of course, would be added to what is already being administered, see?) would also aid in clearing the circulation to the lower extremities, and make for an easier activity of the body in its locomotaries.

8. They (the properties) would be in the form, then, of [[Iodine|iodine]]; or [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in small quantities - three to five minims taken each day, but divided into two doses; that is, two to three drops in the morning and two in the evening, see?  Take it in this manner for three to five days in succession, then leave off for two to three days and then begin again.

9. Adding this with the other ministrations, we would find the greater aid for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  How much anemia do you find?
(A)  As indicated, this is somewhat improved; but those homones [hormones] - or that element in the blood stream which makes for the proper division and distribution of the red blood supply, or the circulation through the system where these have been affected by [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that are slow or lazy in their activity - will be added by taking these properties.

12. (Q)  Any bronchial trouble?
(A)  Rather reflex, as indicated.

13. (Q)  What makes it so easy for her to choke?
(A)  The inactivity of the respiratory functions as related to the secondary-cardiac plexus reflex.  With the manipulations and the massage there, in this area, the condition is being aided - but will be assisted MORE thoroughly with the addition of the element in the system to produce the impulse from [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]' activities.

14. (Q)  Tell condition of lower vertebrae?
(A)  These conditions are on the improve; these have been more to the side than lower, in the lateral way - a parallel.

15. (Q)  How is the bladder?
(A)  As indicated, there are irritations from the lack of proper secretions in the glands that make for the circulation through this portion of the system.

16. (Q)  Any prolapsus of the colon?
(A)  This is much bettered than we have had it.

With the manipulations, or with those applications that make for the better activity of the eliminations - whether those in the circulatory system or otherwise, which will strengthen and aid with that which is being given to the MUSCULAR activity and along the impulse of the nerve and blood supply, these conditions will be corrected.

17. (Q)  Any suggestions for improving gums or plate?
(A)  Use the same solution, diluted two to one, and massage the gums with it.

18. (Q)  Meaning [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?
(A)  Meaning [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?

19. (Q)  Any suggestions so plate will not slip?
(A)  Well, when the conditions in the gums are clarified there will be a much better activity for the plate staying in place, see?

20. (Q)  Any other suggestions at this time?
(A)  We would follow these, and we will find the responses to be much better.

21. We are through for the present.
