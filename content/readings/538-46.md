# TEXT OF READING 538-46 F 57

**Reading [[538-46]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/4/1937  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 3782

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of October, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [538].

2. As we find, conditions are gradually on the improve.

3. Keep up those applications that have been indicated through those [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the bodily forces.  We would alter somewhat the applications, making these in periods of eight days but the four days as indicated in the same manner as given in the use of the Appliance; then four days alternating the circulation to the extremities of the body.

4. These unless they are kept may become, of course, of a malignant nature; but do not irritate or bruise if possible. Keep the ointments in the manner indicated, and we may bring eventual dissimulation or distribution of the energies without their becoming localized.

5. Be consistent and persistent.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  After using the eight days, then rest four days or rest eight days?
(A)  Use four days as has been indicated, through the body, see?  Then begin for four days circulating the body; right wrist, left ankle; left wrist, right ankle, see?  Then the next day first to the left ankle and then the right wrist; the next to the right ankle and the left wrist.  These for four days, see?  Then rest the four days.  Then begin THROUGH the body four days, four days to the extremities; keeping tab that these are circulated properly.

8. (Q)  Have the pressures been removed in the lumbar area?
(A)  They have been removed somewhat.  We would at least once a week STILL have the manipulations, and these not so much now for removal as distribution; that is, setting up a stimulation along the ganglia for ELIMINATIONS.

9. (Q)  Am I using the Appliance correctly?
(A)  As indicated, use it THROUGH the body four days, to the extremities four days, circulating the body; resting four days; beginning again four days THROUGH the body, four days circulating the body.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
