## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/21 Affidavit:
> 
> THE GEO. E. BENNIE MANUFACTURING CO.
> 
> MANUFACTURERS,
> IMPORTERS - WHOLESALE
> COMMISSION MERCHANTS
> $700,000,000 CAPITAL EMPLOYED
> 
> Nashville, Tenn.
> 
> State of Tennessee,
> County of Davidson,
> 
> Personally appeared before me a Notary Public for an in said
> state and county as shown above, Mr. H. P. Salter [2903] of
> the George Bennie Mfg. Co. who upon being duly sworn states
> that he is personally acquainted with Mr. Edgar Cayce of
> [[Selma Alabama|Selma Ala]]. and Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] of Lexington Ky. and that
> upon their recent trip to Nashville Tenn. they gave me the
> following readings with results as will be shown.
> 
> That he owned two hundred units of stock in the
> Pritchett-Thomas Oil Co's William Walters lease [3777-5] at
> Bowling Green where they were drilling for oil and that said
> readings advised no oil in paying quantities would be found
> where drilling although drilling was near producing well on
> adjoining property and although [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] has been drilled out
> it has turned out just as Mr. Cayce said it would and against
> the expectations of the entire Company.
> 
> Again said Salter and friend Williams thought there might be
> oil on Tennessee land [3777-6] that might be produced cheap
> and Mr. Cayce's reading in giving the location of oil on the
> property gave locations of buildings, lots, a broken down
> gate, ravines and other data in saying just where the wells
> should be put down to get oil in quantity that only one who
> was familiar with the place could have told.  Mr.  Williams
> thought he must have been reading his mind but for data given
> that Mr. Williams did not know of until he later made a trip
> and found conditions as Mr. Cayce had described.  Mr. Cayce
> had never been within a hundred miles of the property.
> 
> Mr. Cayce gave a reading on Mr. Salter's wife and not only
> described her condition and treatment but said where she
> should go for treatment, - Still Hildreth [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]]
> Sanatarium at Macon Mo. and it is his understanding that even
> Mr. Cayce did not know the name of such institution until the
> matter was later investigated.
> 
> Mr. Salter has known Mr. Cayce for the past 18 or 20 years
> and not only considers him to be a a man of most wonderful
> powers but a gentleman of the highest integrity.
> 
> [signed] H. P. Salter
> 
> Sworn and subscribed before me at Nashville Tenn. this 22nd.
> day of January.
> 
> [signed] A. G. Duffy
> 
> [notarized]  My commission expires Oct. 9, 1920
