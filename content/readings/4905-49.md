# TEXT OF READING 4905-49

**Reading [[4905-49]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/16/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12974

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of December, 1925, involuntarily; that is, volunteered without request or suggestion being given to obtain it, following a [[Dreams|dream]] interpretation for Mr. [137] and a physical for Mr. [304].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               Bosque County, 
10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, we have here the conditions as surround the operations in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick, in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

2. The conditions as have been produced in the operations, by water and caving (on account of conditions as have been given), these have caused some delay, and are trying to the temperament of all concerned.

3. The conditions as regarding everything surrounding - the tools, in this has been some unsatisfactory conditions; yet we find these, with persistence, with care and attention, should carry out these operations to completion, and that these should be able to be terminated now in the next few days, with a successful bringing in of the large producing well, see?  for in the next few feet in this black lime should be encountered that break in which the sands carry that formation - six to eight (6 to 8) feet.

4. We are through for the present.

>

> pump until we were ready with fishing tools ready.
> 
> On last Friday night Leach and White were positive they had
