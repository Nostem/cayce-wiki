# TEXT OF READING 3034-2 M 41 (D.C.)

**Reading [[3034-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/5/1943  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 10350

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3034], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [3034] who is now at ..., N.J., born March 25, 1902, in Odense, Denmark.  You will answer the questions he submits as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those environs, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of that entity [3034]; this we have had before.  Some of the suggestions made have been acted upon; others - [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is being quite stubborn about.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Would it be advisable for me to leave for [[California|California]] in October Month this year, to take Post Graduate studies in some special subject I am interested in, and feel would benefit me in my Profession, and also in the meantime seek Employment in a Factory out there?
(A)  Be most advisable.  In the aeroplane factory would be well, as this is part of the environ or [[Vibration|vibrations]] for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; and those pertaining to its practice would be well.

5. (Q)  Near what place should I locate, and what day and month would be best for me to leave for [[California|California]]?
(A)  As we find near the aeroplane factory.  Necessarily this would require locating in that same environ.  In October - do it before the 26th or you won't do it at all.

6. (Q)  What day and Month and Year would be the best to open my Office and practice in [[California|California]]?
(A)  That depends very much upon many other conditions.  For the duration, if begun here, you will keep it.  And then a time after that when there has been the completing of the Post Graduate courses, begin the service to others.  But in the application of self, do not attempt to live upon but live for others.  If you live upon others, you will be a failure. If you live for others you may be a success.

7. (Q)  Would the Town "[[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Grove" in California, be the best place for me to open my Office and practice?  It is not far from Los Angeles, if not, what Town or City would be best in California for me to Open my Office and practice in?
(A)  Not necessarily.  This would be very good but not necessarily best.  Ventura would be very well.

8. (Q)  Would it be advisable for me to use my Endo-Nasal and Aural technique and also Octozone Therapy, beside Electro-Therapy?
(A)  These would be very well, but do not cover such a broad field that you do not become thorough in any.  Either be acquainted with the use of all or make a specialty in some special conditions.  All of these have their place.

9. (Q)  In what should I specialize?
(A)  Electro-Therapy!  If this is thoroughly understood, we may do away with most other types of treatment - besides the Mechano-Therapy.  Mechano-Therapy and Electro-Therapy should be those that will cover the broader field of service to men.

10. (Q)  Would it be advisable for me to use the 12 different Cell Salts remedies in my practice?
(A)  When these are necessary.  These are confusing to self - until you thoroughly understand them or understand them much better as to their place in your practice.

11. (Q)  Would it be advisable and useful if I prescribed and gave the "Homeopathic" remedies to my patients besides the regular treatment?
(A)  Remember what has just been given.  If thoroughly understood, the Mechano-Therapy and Electro-Therapy should cover the broader field.  To be sure, there are many instances where Homeopathy is indicated, we find that if this is to be used it will require more years in preparation.

12. (Q)  Will I inherit any money this year, if not, when will it be?
(A)  We do not find this is to be settled for some two or three years as yet.  There will be an inheritance, or should be, provided or dependent upon what the stabilization of the Government is - and not until these conditions are entirely settled will there be any great amount, as we find.

13. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  As indicated; get [[Ideal|the ideal]]:  He that would be the greatest success will be the servant of all, and not the boss or the commander but working with and for others.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file (If you go to Los Angeles, you might like to get in touch with our chiropractic friend and member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] A. Miller, 105 No. Van Ness Ave., Los Angeles, California.)
