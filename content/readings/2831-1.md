# TEXT OF READING 2831-1 F 34

**Reading [[2831-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/23/1942  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 10038

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [722].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Oklahoma. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, the disturbances are the effects of an unbalanced condition in the nature of a prenatal activity.

3. Yet these are such that we may prepare the body for those things that may be helpful and beneficial to the body, if there is the wholehearted cooperation with those suggestions as may be made here.

4. The drawing in the limbs and the disturbances in the circulation all arise from this chemical unbalance.

5. In the preparation of the body for those applications as may be best made, - we would give internally once every other day an eighth grain of ANIMATED ASH.  Empty this dry on tongue and then swallow with a little water.

6. Ten minutes later, after the Ash has been given, you see, apply the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray Light; this the Mercury Quartz, NOT the Carbon Light.  This should be at least thirty-eight to forty Inches (38" to 40") from the body, with a GREEN light - or glass projected - between this and the body - at least fourteen inches (14") from the body, so that certain rays - even from the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] - are broken up.  This would be applied for not more than a minute or minute and a half, to the back - and also along the lower limbs.

7. In the diets, be sure to include all of those especially of Vitamins A, D, C and G.  These are those as we find the more deficient in the body.

8. Do not be too active, as to cause overtaxation, but sufficient to prevent the body from becoming too much in that way of giving way to the disturbances in the system - or to those distresses - at times.

9. Follow these applications for thirty days; that is, for at least fifteen of the doses of Ash followed by the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] applied in the manner outlined.

10. Then we would give further instructions for the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What was the original cause of the chemical unbalancing?
(A)  In the body of the mother, as we have indicated.

13. (Q)  Should present treatments be discontinued?
(A)  This is to be chosen by the body.  Read what we have given here.  If there is the full coordination or cooperation with these suggestions given here, we may bring helpful influences.  But the choices are to be made by the body.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] explaining the nature of the Animated Ash and where obtained.

If you have trouble locating a green glass, a foot square of green glass may be ordered for $2.50 from American Plate & Window Glass Co., Springfield Ave., East Orange, N.J.

At your convenience, we'd appreciate having a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so that we may have something with which to compare our information.

Let us know when you have followed these suggested treatments for the period indicated, so that we may make an appointment for a Check Physical Reading to give further instructions - without additional cost to you.)
