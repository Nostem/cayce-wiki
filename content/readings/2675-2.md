# TEXT OF READING 2675-2 M 25 (Government Clerk)

**Reading [[2675-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/4/1925  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 9766

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made the by self - Mr. [2675].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.        [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further improvement of same, and then will answer the questions regarding the physical and mental condition of this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are many changes in the physical and mental forces of this body since last we had same.  The conditions that are abnormal in the present [[Physical Body|physical forces]] have to do, somewhat, with the conditions as we have given here before, in the emunctories and the excretory system of the body, and the effects of the affectations in the right portion of the abdomen and the liver show how these conditions are manifesting themselves in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of body.  This, as we see then, a lack of the coordination in [[Vibration|vibration]] as is necessary for the system to eliminate through its proper channels.

3. Then, to assist this condition in the body at the present time, to overcome these conditions, the body MUST pay more attention to the diet of the body, until the system is able to adjust itself to the proper [[Vibration|vibration]] in each organism of the system.

4. Then, we would first add, then, to the system these properties in this manner:

5. Make and take five capsules of each of this consistency and proportions, see?

Podophyllin..........1 grain,
Leptandrin.........1/2 grain,
Sanguinaria..........1 grain,
Cascara Sagrada....1/2 grain,
Dover's Powder.....1/2 grain.

6. These should be taken every second evening, until we have had a full action from the entire intestinal tract.

7. After these have been taken, take then one full dose Citrate Magnesia, that we may cleanse the system thoroughly, see?

8. After this, begin with the diet of the formations of whole wheat, of gruels from same, at least one meal a day to consist only of the full whole wheat, either rolled or in mash of in the form so the body may take same, for we need the effect of this reaction in the system.  Little meats, save chicken, fish or broths, with a great amount of green vegetable forces, especially of those that grow ABOVE the ground.  Potatoes, either sweet or irish, may be taken in small quantities.  Not much stimulant, especially of tea. Do not take tea.  Milk, coffee or chocolate, may be taken in moderate quantities.

9. Also add to the body, after these have been taken, the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are found in the battery formation as has been given, such as would be called in the Radio-Active forces of Carbon, Iron and Water.  Follow this.  These should be taken at least one hour to two hours each day, following the diet and taking sufficient outdoor exercises, or indoor exercises, as we will keep the body reacting with the properties as are taken internally and through the action of the diet as is given for the body.  This, we will find, will bring the normal forces and conditions for this body, [2675].

10. (Q)  What is the remedy for the mental hallucination, loss of personality, will, and self-control, latest development of nervous symptoms?
(A)  The conditions as have been prescribed, especially in battery formation, to produce the equilibrium throughout the whole nerve system and prevent the action of the forces through centralization in affectations of system.  Also we would apply those forces as are found in the study of psychological subject forces or matters, see?

11. (Q)  Is the new work upon which I am entering on the first, clerk in Internal Revenue Office, compatible with my present mental and physical state, and is it justice to myself and work to continue?
(A)  These may be made so, for this is rather a state of the mental reaction of the body, which, as we would see, might be reached through the conditions which show those of the compatibility of the body to meet such conditions in its mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  Physical forces may be MADE to meet.  Mental forces may be brought to that state wherein the reaction would be very good for the body, and to which the body, through its other channels, may acquire that necessary for better mental development.

12. (Q)  Should I, in the interest of my present mental and physical condition, discontinue my present plans for the night study of law?
(A)  No.  Continue same, in the moderate way.  Do not overtax mental or physical, else we would find the reaction disagreeable both to physical and mental forces, but, as given, the daily labors of physical reaction may be made to react to the studies of law in a manner, and especially in that phase pertaining to commercial laws, as regards the effect of the governmental forces through which the body labors at present has with individuals.  In this [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] might become very proficient and successful, see?  That is, corporation law or tax law, or commercial law combining both.

13. We are through.
