# TEXT OF READING 2606-1 M 3

**Reading [[2606-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/22/1941  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 9645

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mrs. Agnes M. Sanford, recommended by Mrs. A. L. Eggleston.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. In giving the interpretations of the disturbances which exist here, many things, many phases of man's experiences, of the physical activities, of mental and material manifestations, might be indicated or illustrated.

4. All the care, all the help that may be given will not be amiss for those about the body.

5. That there is lack of the full completion of physical manifestations is indicated from the lack of coordination in the mental and the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

6. Each day we would use oils as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]]; one day the Oil of Lobelia, the next day Pure Peanut Oil.

7. The Oil or Lobelia would be massaged only from the base of the brain to the higher point of the cervical area - or to the 3rd, 4th or 5th cervical.  Not too much of this, but gently massaged - so as to be absorbed - to the joint of the shoulders, see?

8. The Peanut Oil would be massaged more particularly along the whole area of the spine, from the base of the brain along the spinal column to the end of the spine; gently, thoroughly, all the body will absorb.  Also [[Massage Therapy|massage]] this across the diaphragm and the abdominal area.

9. Also we would use the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying alternately the Gold and [[Camphor|Camphor]], one used one day, the other the next; making the attachment each day for twenty minutes, one day with the Gold, the next day with the [[Camphor|Camphor]].

10. When using the Gold (in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of Distilled Water - and use at least six ounces), - the copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal center, while the larger nickel plate - through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially - would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus; which will be one finger from the navel center to the right, two fingers up, on this body.

11. When using the [[Camphor|Camphor]] (Spirits of Camphor, commercial strength - 6 ounces), - attach the copper plate to the 2nd and 3rd dorsal center, while the nickel plate - through which the Camphor passes vibratorially - would be attached also the to umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, the same as with the Gold.

12. Change each of these solutions every thirty days (that is, after fifteen treatments with each, you see).  Also re-charge the Appliance every thirty days.

13. Each day, as the applications are made, let it be at periods when the body rests or sleeps.  Do these with, prayerful expectancy.

14. Doing these physical administrations, with [[Prayer|prayer]] - and with those [[Healing|healing]] [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may come from such an one who has administered these to the body, we may bring responses.

15. In ninety days we would given further instructions.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  This is very well, and will be changed as the body and mental developments progress.

Do these as indicated in the present.

As oft as practical have the [[Prayer|prayer]] and the [[Healing|laying on of hands]].  This should be through the 9th dorsal and the FRONTAL portion of head.

18. (Q)  What produced this condition?
(A)  These questions are to be left.

Much might be given, but these conditions are being met.  Do not trouble their minds, but keep [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness. All [[Healing|healing]] comes through spiritual forces.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(See sheet [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], also extra copy of the directions to be sent to:  Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., who will prepare one of these Appliances for you.)
