# TEXT OF READING 592-3 M 71

**Reading [[592-3]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/6/1935  Sex: M  Age: 71  ReadingID: 4130

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [592], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., also by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

... Place, 
Time of Reading                                Apt. 810, 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes; yes.  We have the body here, [592]; this body we have had before.

2. Conditions are very serious with this body.

3. While there might be some opportunities for the body through operative measures, as we find, it would only be an allaying for a time; neither do we find it probable that there would be even a response after the anaesthesia.

4. The conditions are becoming more and more aggravated through the inflammation that arises from the activities in the prostate, and the effect that this is producing upon the urethra and the eliminations through the bladder.  And it's gradually going backward, backward into same; making then for only that which may be done to alleviate the pain.

5. So, as we find, the greater aid is offered in the present through the use of the Elliot Machine with the rectal tubes for the applying of heat to the conditions in those portions of the body.

6. And keep the body as quiet as possible.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Where is the blood coming from that passes in urine?
(A)  As indicated, through the walls - from those conditions in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] there.  Prostate, and the effect upon the organ - or the bladder FROM the kidneys' activity, but through the urethra.

9. (Q)  What prospects has the body of regaining his health?
(A)  Not any!

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [257] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(Pamphlet was enclosed explaining theory of Elliot Machine. Find out from Dr. Shorell who to contact in [[Chicago|Chicago]] for obtaining such treatments.)
