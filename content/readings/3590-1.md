# TEXT OF READING 3590-1 F 57 (Housewife)

**Reading [[3590-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/30/1943  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 11082

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3590], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born February 23, 1887, in Leon, Iowa. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Minn.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of that entity now called [3590].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, these we choose from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] with the desire and hope that this information may prove to be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. While the entity grows weary at times at the seemingly unattractive surroundings, we find that the entity in its dress should make itself the attractive surroundings for others.  While there are those about the entity who are influenced and make remarks, the entity may through such efforts on its part make a great deal of impression for that the entity itself holds as [[Ideal|the ideal]] - spiritual, mental, material.

5. The suggestion of the entity, even as to how individuals may in the present environ or surrounding cultivate those things as to grow mentally, will make for a change in the attitude of this whole community.  For that's how the community looks up to this entity.

6. As to that, there should be so much time for recreation, so much time for social activities, so much time for improvement of mind, so much time for the improvement of body, so much time for rest.  These set in motion in this community would bring for the entity the unbounding praise of many, as well as the good such would do in ANY community - if there were those so fitted as this entity to impress (and make it stick) such a program for any groups of people.

7. In analyzing urges from the [[Astrology|astrological]] activity, we find Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Mercury.  This is not indicating, because of the manner in which each planet occupies its position, that Mercury is belittled in the mental aspirations or activities, but rather that the activities, conditions and diplomacy controlled through Mars and Jupiter are the more prominent in the activity of the entity.

8. The abilities to be the leader in the community is not too much for the entity to think or feel, or for it to act either!  It doesn't have to brag or boast about it, for then the effectiveness is lost and it becomes self-[[Purpose|purpose]]; but in the activities of Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, we may find the helpful influences of a [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] applied in the diplomatic manner that is a part of the experience will bring good in the experience and in the environs of the entity.  And these will be sound judgments and be unfoldments and developments for the entity that will be sowing the seeds of that the entity sets as its ideal.

9. Apply that in the daily experience, then, and we will find life becoming more interesting, more worth while.

10. And what is life?  God manifested in the material plane.  For it is still in Him that we live and move and have our being. Thus life as a material manifestation is the expression of that universal force or energy we call God.

11. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, these have been quite varied and quite far apart.  While all may not be given, these will indicate the activities of the entity:

12. Before this the entity was among those in the early settlings of the land, when there was the general trek towards new fields of activity.

13. The entity was an associate of the pioneer peoples, in the name then Evelyn [[Arizona|Prescott]], and the entity journeyed much.

14. In the present there are the abilities to keep the records of activities in changes wrought in every form of human experience.

15. But the greater abilities for growth lie in the constructive influence the entity may have in the lives of others as these changes are wrought in their experiences.

16. Before that the entity was in the English land during those periods when there was the breaking up of the tribes of Israel [Zedekiah period?  2nd Chron. 36:10?].

17. The entity was a granddaughter of Hezekiah the king, and among those who set sail to escape when the activities brought the rest of the people into servitude in the [[Persia|Persian]] land.

18. Then the entity was among those who landed and set up the seat of customs as indicated in the altars built near what is now Salisbury, [[England|England]] [Stonehenge?].  These were the early traditions carried into those activities.

19. Then name then was Elemeshia.  The entity gained in the ability as a leader, in the ability to influence others, the ability to control the activities of individuals in such a way as to make for the forming of moral habits and ideas in their material experiences.

20. Before that the entity was in the [[Yucatan|Yucatan]] land when the people settled there who came from [[Atlantis|Atlantis]].

21. The entity was a priestess among those who set up a form of activity there; being also then associated with records that were attempted to be preserved from the old order of things in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]].

22. We find that the entity established and added to, or detracted from, the circumstance decided upon as the expedient one for the change in clime and in surroundings.

23. The name then was Queetel, as would be translated.

24. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

25. These are limited only according to the entity's [[Ideal|ideals]].  The abilities are efficient, especially in record-keeping and in activities in which it may contribute to the welfare of a household, a town, a community, in the way of [[Ideal|ideals]] set not only for the young but for those who are establishing homes or businesses or becoming active in political or economic conditions through the land.

26. Ready for questions.

27. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

> the possibility of sending thots thru space is possible when

> are presented by science, so his book should be a great help
> in that way.
>

> am sending for your book "A [[Search for God Study Groups|Search for God]]" and several of

> thot.  I noticed that one of the comments regarding the book

> 
> I have hesitated to ask you to give me a reading when I did
> not have the money to pay for it as I have always paid my

> greatly in need of the reading and am looking forward with
> great eagerness to the time when I may get it.  And I am more
> than willing to co-operate in any way I can.  And as I am so

> possible for me to have a "Mental and Spiritual" one too that
> would be really grand as I feel I am at a cross road and if I
> could find myself it would be such a help.
>

> prepared for groups?  I have no one to study with but maybe
> I could use them alone.  If you think I could I would be
> very thankful for them for I want to advance in knowledge
> and understanding.
> 
> Could I please have a sample copy of your monthly
> publication?
> 
> I am filling out an application for Associate Membership
