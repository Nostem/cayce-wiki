# TEXT OF READING 5195-1 M 67 (Real Estate Agent, Hebrew)

**Reading [[5195-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/5/1944  Sex: M  Age: 67  ReadingID: 13331

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the son - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body; and [[Soul|the soul]] would take leave of same.  There are many experiences in a life's journey in the earth that are much more serious than that man calls death, when the trust of [[Soul|the soul]] and heart of the man is in the Lord, who doeth all things well.  There is little interpretation-physical [that would be given] of the disturbance, and it is gradually progressing in the inabilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to do for self, think for self. Even yet there is in the consciousness the desire that those, for whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has been responsible for their being in the earth, should take note and prepare while they may to meet their God.

3. For the time cometh, as it must to this body, when no work is to be done but ye must stand before the judgment bar of thine own conscience, as must each [[Soul|soul]], and determine as to whether in the light of the knowledge, in the light of thine opportunity, ye can as thy friend, thy God say, "I have dishonored no man, I have taken naught from my brother, but what I restore fourfold."

4. And remember, as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] goes to its long rest, there are those whom he has entrusted with an obligation.  Do not disregard the counsel.  For he has been and is viewing those past opportunities that soon shall come no more in this life. There are the warnings, heed them!  For the Lord will not always smile on those who disregard the warnings which have been and are being now, here, made to those whom this body would have heed the warning.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Son, Mr. [...]
"    " Ass'n file

> 
> B3. 12/11/43 Mr. [257]'s letter:  "Attached is a letter from
> [5195]'s son, Mr. H. I. R.  He has asked me a dozen times
> about writing you regarding his father.  This entire crowd
> came to hear you while you were in [[New York City|New York]] lecturing, and
> all were turned away because there was no room for them.  I
> am attaching the questions I would like to have asked.  His
> father is always at home and in bed.  It seems that cases of
> this kind keep coming up all the time.  This time I hope to
> bring it to the attention of the doctors in [[New York City|New York]], who
> have been hounding me ever since you left town...  (1) Please
> give a general Physical Reading, giving the cause and cure of
> this condition which is affecting the whole body.  (2) Can
> this condition be stopped where it is?  (3) Is there any
> specific treatment that will stop this condition, wherever it
> is found, which seems to be unknown to the doctors?  (4) What
> immediate treatment can be given to prolong the life of this
> man?  (5) What diet should be taken while the treatment is
> given?  (6) How long should treatment be given?  (7) Who is
> the proper doctor to handle this case?  Give name.  (8) Is
> this a hereditary condition, or one that is contracted?  (9)
> Please advise us how we can pass this information on to
> medical associations to prevent the ravages of this disease
> in other people.  (10) Is there a name for this disease, so
> it can be further investigated for welfare of humanity, and
> recognized in its first developments?"
