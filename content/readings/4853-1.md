# TEXT OF READING 4853-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4853-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/30/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12857

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of April, 1931, in accordance with request made by her sister, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround same, [4853].

2. In giving that that may be of aid in the present, in correcting the disturbances, we find it will be necessary that the causes of the disorders be understood - and these conditions, as we find, may not be pleasant to some.  There existed a prenatal condition from the condition of the sire, or father, that brought into the system those effects in the nervous system as related to the genitive organs and genitive relations those forces as made for a weakening of the associations or connections in the nervous system; so that, as the body reached that age wherein nature was to discharge in the system those forces as made for [[Creative Forces|creative energies]] in the system, these failed to respond in their proper way and manner.  This brought about a suppression of the whole nervous system, especially as related to the sympathetic system, making for those of imaginations, screaming out in the night, suppression of the urine, again an over-activity of same; nausea arising during those periods when there was an over-activity of the gastric forces in the digestive system themselves - this produced by the call upon nature's forces in the body to supply those necessary elements to create those needs as were forced to be active through the organs themselves' functioning in this particular developing force of the body itself.  THESE, as we find, HAVE brought about - DO bring about - those tendencies in the present. Unless there be supplied those forces in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] or make-up in the body itself, not only to SUPPLY the needs but to destroy that tendency of the muco-effect in the plasms of the nervous forces to the organs of genitation, and effect to the brain centers and to those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as act with same - in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]], the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pituitary]], the adrenal, the secretions in those of the active forces in assimilation - or the lacteals; ALL of these must be supplied, with a changed [[Vibration|vibration]] - and will we be able to create same in such a way and manner as to bring about coordination, we may overcome those activities that produce for the system those effects in the body as are acting on the sensory system, so that the body has those spells of wanting to be alone, desiring even to destroy self, desiring to tear and break things, desiring to be rid of the eyes or the watching of others, imagining people are watching the body when they are not; these are natural results from the character of the disorder, as which the body itself NOT responsible!  To go deeper into the conditions, then, it would be asked WHY, within the body. These would be with the metaphysical forces, as has been meted by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or the body itself - and these might be seen from the approach from a different angle.

3. To meet these in the present, necessary - then - that those who may apply those forces in the activities of a human body take warning by the conditions as exist in this body, and aid THIS body in gaining a normal equilibrium in its mental balance, in its physical balance, in its reaction throughout.

4. These, as we would find, would be the manners to approach this in the present:

5. This will of necessity be a persistent, a consistent, application.  Do not begin unless there is the full expectancy and determination to see this through to where the body may be its normal self again, even in its earliest teen ages.

6. In the applications, then, we would apply those forces as may be found in the [[Wet Cell|wet cell]] appliances, that carry one of nickel the other of copper in the anode - see?  and these are to pass through the [[Gold Chloride|gold solution]] in one treatment and through the silver solution in the next treatment, and these would be given, we will say, on Tuesdays the [[Gold Chloride|gold solution]] and on Thursdays the silver solution - THAT ratio, see?  In the applying same to the body, we would apply that anode first as has NOT the medication or medicinal properties passing through same in its [[Vibration|vibration]], to the 11th and 12th dorsal area.  That carrying the solution, apply to the umbilicus center - and this should be taken for - in the beginning - thirty minutes, each one.  After the tenth or twelfth day, the time may be increased to sixty minutes.

7. If these are given in this ratio, then on Mondays and on Wednesdays there should be the thorough manipulations and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the whole body; these given that the capillary circulation, structural portions of the body - be given osteopathically, but as a GENERAL [[Massage Therapy|massage]] throughout, making the corrections as will be necessary from time to time along the cerebrospinal system, but carrying same to the extremities as well as to the torso of the body and to the radial axis of nerve plexus - following them all out.

8. Keep the eliminations good as related to the alimentary canal, these more by diet and by the use of the excavations - or those of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations - than by medicinal properties or cathartics.

9. The diet should be that of a general one, keeping stamina and stability through the whole system.

10. Keep this for - as the one or first application from the plain battery itself will last - thirty-six days; THEN we would give the necessary changes for the corrections in this body.  Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What caused her derangement of mind six months after she was married?
(A)  The same effects as seen from those conditions existent in the system, only become accentuated by the activities of the imaginative system - through the forces as has been seen in system, from incipiency of the conditions.

12. (Q)  What caused her to fall into comas during and after pregnancy?
(A)  This same reaction, with the system attempting to adjust itself.  This, as seen, not produced from - that is, in the system itself, see - not produced from that as the more violent stage of those conditions in a body, but that that is more OFTEN considered as slight and of little moment.

13. (Q)  What is causing her extreme nervousness and melancholia at the present time?
(A)  The inability of the system to adjust itself for proper coordination, as has been outlined.  Do as we have given if we would bring the conditions.  Be persistent, be consistent, and we will - within the next twelve to eighteen months - bring about a near normalcy for this body.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Sister, Mrs. [...]
"    " File
