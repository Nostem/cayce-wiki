# TEXT OF READING 1788-12 M 4

**Reading [[1788-12]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/5/1943  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 8080

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [480], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here, [1788]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, the acute conditions that have existed in the system have been improved.  These were produced, we find, by a combination of disturbances; first the inflammation in the tonsils and the adenoids, and the drainage to same as the body swallows or attempts to swallow.  These upset the digestive system, causing an inflammation and high temperature.  These have improved because of cleansing of the body.

4. In the present, we would use Caldwell's Syrup of Pepsin as an antiseptic and an eliminant.  Give about half to three-quarters of a teaspoonful two or three times a day, or about three hours apart, until there are good eliminations from the alimentary canal.  Leave off two or three days (keeping the intestinal antiseptic and the massages, which we will indicate), and then take again - until there are good eliminations.

5. As an intestinal antiseptic we would give a few drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]; not more than six drops at a time, twice a day should be sufficient, in water.  Keep this up until the odor of same may be detected in the stool.  Leave off a few days and then take again.

6. If there is trouble with the face and throat, we would wrap about same a cotton cloth (two or three thicknesses) well saturated with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], and keep it on for an hour and a half to two hours.  This will aid in reducing the inflammation.

7. And we would have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments to set up drainage.

These should, if drainages are set up properly, - from the neck and head, - prevent, or make it unnecessary for operative measures; unless there is cold or congestion that would cause reinfection in same.

8. Do these, and we will have better conditions for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What can be done to stimulate appetite?
(A)  When we have corrected the conditions with the liver and the digestive system, there will naturally be the inclination for the body to eat.

In the diet we would give whole wheat grains as cereals, whether dry or cooked.  We would give plenty of good vegetables that are well cooked in their own juices.  Plenty of sea foods, and these - with fowl - will be the better meats for the body.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file
