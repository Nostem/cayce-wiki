# TEXT OF READING 649-4 F 21

**Reading [[649-4]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/29/1938  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 4362

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of September, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self, through mother - Mrs. [379], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  (In undertone - "There's a block here in some way!" Long pause:)

3. Yes, this is getting rather serious.  We don't find the body but the impressions are that the conditions are NOT so well!

4. As we find, the disturbing conditions are rather acute. There is a spasmodic reaction of the lower portion of the epiglottis.  This causes through the stomach area the inability of assimilation, causing a heaviness through portions of the lungs - or the lower part, or at the breast bone or the palate portion - such as to almost cut off the breath at times.

5. WE would have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] ADJUSTMENTS made here, to correct those impingements in the cerebrospinal system that cause contractions in the area.

6. It is well for the time that there be rather the use of predigested foods and those that are very stimulating to the activity of the digestive system; such as gruels, heavy or whole wheat cooked cereals, egg and milk and spirits frumenti.  All of such as these.  Of course, plenty of citrus fruit juices - if remaining.

7. Then, make those corrections and set up better eliminations.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What doctor would be suggested to make these corrections?
(A)  One in sympathy with the Work, as well as a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

10. (Q)  Are climatic conditions in Washington, D.C., agreeable to my body?
(A)  These are NOT so much the disturbance as the subluxation, as we find, in the cardiac area of the cerebro-spinal system - or the areas that govern the epiglottis and the cardiac portion.

11. (Q)  What has caused me to lose weight?
(A)  Lack of assimilations.

12. (Q)  Is smoking bad for my body?
(A)  For this body, smoking is VERY bad.

13. (Q)  Is there any patent medicine that would be a good body builder for me?
(A)  It's not "patent" but the Codiron would be MOST beneficial for the body; two tablets morning and evening - these are preferably taken while taking the meal.

Of course, only have semi-liquids until the full corrections are made.  But do not even then have fried foods so much.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note to Mrs. [379]:  We would suggest that you send the Reading and have [649] go to:  Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, 1707 Columbia Rd., Washington, D.C., who is a good [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] and who has given us reports on several cases followed according to Reading suggestions.

Codiron is manufactured by The Lawrence Laboratories, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.  It comes in tablet form and sells for $1 a bottle.  Our Meredith Drug Co. at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] expects a supply of it this week-end, as they've ordered some.  You may save time by having it sent from here.)
