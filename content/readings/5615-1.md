# TEXT OF READING 5615-1 F 29

**Reading [[5615-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1930  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 13957

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5615], via [257] and [903].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5615], Mrs. Fannie [[David Kahn|Kahn]], and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [5615], present in this room.  You will give a mental, spiritual and physical reading for this body, with advice for the use of talents and personality to aid her in making a success of her life and those depending upon her.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [5615], and those conditions physical, mental and spiritual which surround this body.

3. First, in consideration, this body should take care regarding the physical conditions of the body, that it does NOT cause worry, anxiety, on the part of self and others - for there is existent in the physical functioning of the body such a condition that, unless taken in time, will prove very stubborn to handle; yet, taken in time the conditions may be relieved and eliminated, eradicated in such a way as to give the body the best physical functioning as may be had through this experience.

4. In the physical functioning we find conditions existent with the respiratory system, the lungs themselves, that - unless taken in time these will prove to be the greater force, and much trouble may come from the spread of this bacilli in the system; for, as is seen in the vitality - this under strain, not only through the physical but through the MENTAL also, so that there has been at times almost the nervous collapse. This has sapped the vitality until the resistance as is exhibited is weakening under this strain.  Not that the body is ILL, or even at dis-EASE at ALL times, but MOSTLY - when considered form the distressed condition as may arise in the system; for, with the reduction in the vitality, with the unbalancing of the vitims [vitamins] as are necessary, with the hemoglobin becoming deficient, with the inability of portions of the leucocytes to meet the needs, and the still less reduction in proper coagulation in the system - this must soon, unless taken in time, REDUCE the vitality.

These are seen in the character of the circulation, in the tendency of the cold, clammy conditions existing - especially of NIGHT; of the inability of the body to find perfect coordination, either in thinking or in resting; the inability to REST, even, and relax properly, is as a WARNING FOR the system of the USE as is being put to the vital forces of the body.  The tendency of the distress as is seen in the unbalancing of the system in the hepatic circulation.  Hot internally, cold hands and feet, circles appearing in the face, the limbs feeling heavy at times, the hands clammy, the feelings as if nothing particularly HURTS, but UNPLEASANTNESS all over.  These are that manner in which such conditions take hold and have made inroads before there is REALIZED that such conditions have arisen.

5. Then, the first consideration for [[Physical Body|the body physical]], the body mental, the body spiritual - the physical or material successes must FIRST depend upon the body's regaining its full equilibrium in the physical sense.  This may be accomplished by the proper CHARACTER of foods, vitims, that are taken in the proper relationships of the system as related to revitalization of the blood stream, even of that ALREADY in operation - in the adding to, through proper assimilation and through the proper application of self, mentally AND physically, the needs of the present situation.

6. Then, we would begin with as much rest OUTDOORS as may be made possible, and MAKE it possible to have a great deal. In other words, it may be taken as "rough-it" for a time. Plenty of the vitims as may be had in liver, tripe, and in codliver oils.  As much of OLIVE oil as the system will well assimilate.  Eggs, but only the yolks of same should be taken for this body at the PRESENT period.  Later they may be taken more, or the whole - where stimuli is needed for the digestive forces in the system.  Also meat JUICES, but not too much of the body of same.  But as much of the tomato juices, of those of the calves' liver, of the tripe - beef tripe or mutton tripe, or hog tripe - ALL of these carry that NECESSARY vital force as to meet the needs of that that may be created IN the blood as to COMBAT the conditions existent, and BUILD vital forces.

7. The exercises should be principally those as will keep an equilibrium in the system.

8. A gentle massage, especially following those of the bath - that should be well rubbed with COLD water along the spine. THEN the exercise of the limbs following same - but much rest.

9. In the exercising of the mental forces of the body:

10. These should be kept in accord with that as may be ACCOMPLISHED BY the body.  In the mental forces there is seen that loss of confidence in others has WEAKENED that in self, or the loss of that as may be said - as [[Ideal|an ideal]]; and only IDEAS are now the outlook, and these make only for - here a little, there a little - "this I think, this I don't."  But rather set before self [[Ideal|an ideal]] [[Purpose|purpose]] to be attained by self, for self.  Not as of selfish motive, but rather that the abilities, the capacity of service for self and others, may be gained.

11. We also would use, for [[Physical Body|the body physical]], those of an antiseptic inhalant - that will clarify the conditions in the system as related to the mucus-producing portions in throat, bronchia and lungs - taking in the system occasionally, as a cleanser - not as medicine, even - those of small doses of plain [[Baking Soda|soda]]; NOT as is common [[Baking Soda|soda]], but rather the rectified - [[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]] - and occasionally, say five drops each week, [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  This will CLEANSE the system and take up much of the residue in such a manner as that the eliminations may be made near perfect.

12. In fifteen to sixteen months the body would be physically fit to take the life even seriously.  Do not take the conditions so serious as to WORK at it.  Rather PLAY at getting physically fit.

13. In the material conditions in life, consider first that as is necessary that the mental and the spiritual may have a place of manifesting; for [[Physical Body|the body physical]] is truly the temple of [[Soul|the soul]], the dwelling place of the mind, of the [[Mental Body|mental body]], through this material plane, and needs same for physical manifestation.  Care FIRST and FOREMOST for these, finding in this development in self that to which the body mental, the body physical, may even worship.  Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  To what definite vocation should entity apply itself in order to achieve success?
(A)  Dependent upon what is finally decided by the body, mental and physical, as what constitutes success.  FIRST make the success of a body physically WHOLE, and in same find a bodily, mentally, efficiency - a body spiritual awakened to the possibilities in each; and in same there would come the success.  The success in a home is the highest success as may be achieved by ANYONE.  In the dependence upon those in whom the body mental, the body physical, may trust - and in the trust find comfort, peace, and understanding.  This BEING destroyed in part.  Find it first in self, and that as is meted will be meted to you again - for He is faithful, who has promised that "He that abideth in Me, I will abide in him.  Be my people, and I will be your God" has ever been, and ever WILL BE, the criterion, [[Ideal|the IDEAL]], for one seeking success.

15. (Q)  Shall my baby and I continue living in ..., [[Texas|Texas]], with my family?
(A)  For sixteen months, yes.

16. (Q)  Should the body marry the one she has in mind?
(A)  When this has been accomplished first in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - and this should be, before such conditions would even be thought, or sought, or even considered - will they have been the same?  First seek self, to know self - self's relations, and service that self may accomplish.  THEN we may consider the outside.  Accomplishing something in self FIRST!! Gain an understanding of self's relationships, of self's relationship to the divine as may be manifested - and FIND an IDEAL!  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
