# TEXT OF READING 608-5 F 2

**Reading [[608-5]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/3/1928  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 4206

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [635].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Fla. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the impressions of the individual here - [608].

2. Now, there are changes in the physical conditions of the body since last we had same here.  There are evidences of improvements in the condition.  Still there exists those conditions that continue to cause distress in the assimilating and assimilation in system, bringing distraughtness in the hepatic circulation.  This has especially to do with that portion of the system in the ascending and in the beginning of traverse [transverse] colon.  The cause or seat of trouble, however, radiates to that of those ducts and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] where division in that assimilated takes place.  Hence there is with the condition that occurring at times, though not so prevalent at the present, nausea, temperature, high blood pressure even, for the individual - see?

3. To meet the needs, then, with those conditions as are being applied - for we wouldn't change these much, see?  Only add these to same.  We would give occasionally, when nausea occurs, equal parts of distilled water, lime water, and cinnamon water - see?  little sips of same.

4. We would also give, at least twice each day, three to five drops [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

5. Should there be pain, or a hardening of the region about this portion where trouble occurs; that is, in the right side, in the ascending colon, see?  apply hot packs, or very warm packs, just so it will not burn the body, of olive oil, see?

6. As for the diet - this should consist principally of gruels, cereals, zwieback, milk - but preferably use dry milk rather than cows' milk.  The dried milk, see?  This necessarily will need to be regulated according to the conditions as arise.  Little orange juice occasionally will not be harmful.

No apple or fruits of other characters, though, may be used as yet.  These, as we find, would bring the normal forces - reducing the conditions in system.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is the body not at this address?
(A)  The impressions are here.

8. We are through with this reading.

> the bowels move 6-7 times daily.  She has absolutely no
> appetite..."
