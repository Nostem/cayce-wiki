# TEXT OF READING 2299-12 M 4

**Reading [[2299-12]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/2/1944  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 9056

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set Bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there has not been followed out those suggestions that have been recently given, but we would continue with them in the present.

3. Give the body an enema about every other day.  Do not use cold water, but tap water, body temperature.  Follow this enema with alcohol rubs.  Obtain Calcidin tablets, one grain from Abbott & Company, [[Chicago|Chicago]].  These will aid in clearing the system and adding to the body the calcium and [[Iodine|iodine]] necessary for bettering the circulation.

4. Apply [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver area about one hour each day for two days, then give internally two teaspoonsful of Olive Oil after the second day.  Apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Pack with at least three thicknesses of flannel, saturated with the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], and then apply the electric pad over same. This should stir the liver into activity.  These are what is needed to remove the tendency for strep.

5. Do have the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] through the system, and if possible, gargle or spray the throat with same.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What dosage of Calcidin should be taken?
(A)  As has been indicated.  Put together at least five grains in two teaspoonsful of hot water, not boiling but just so it is thoroughly dissolved.  Every two or three hours give a sip.  Not a spoonful, not a half-teaspoonful but about one-third of a spoonful, just sip, see?  About every three hours apart until the whole quantity is taken.  Keep this warm.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Castor Oil Packs.)
