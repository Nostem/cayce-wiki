# TEXT OF READING 5600-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[5600-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/30/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13927

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [202], her employer.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. [202].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Body and reading yesterday afternoon - questions)

1. EC:  We have the information as given, also the body.  In changes, there are LITTLE.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is Dr. Oxley the one to properly perform this operation? Or should someone else do it?  Just which one would be best to perform this operation?
(A)  There are MANY that might, or could, or would, perform such an operation better than Oxley.  Though the body IS experienced, the experience is not of the efficient kind in THIS particular case or condition.  There are those that understand the condition better, and would be better in this particular case.  Why?

3. (Q)  And who understands the case better?
(A)  Those who have followed the case more closely.

4. (Q)  Why?
(A)  In this condition, as given, there were those conditions that, the services - or the activities, rather than service, in the particular condition of the body Oxley, would produce, as given, dissension.  Too centralized in self.  We are through.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
