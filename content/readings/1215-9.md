# TEXT OF READING 1215-9 M 19

**Reading [[1215-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/26/1939  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 6757

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of October, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1348], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there is continued improvement in the physical, as well as in the mental attitudes of the body.

4. We find that the present surroundings and environments are most conducive to better conditions.  The whole atmosphere is good.

5. There needs to be kept much of that which has been indicated for this body, if there would be the continuation of the coordinating of the nerve ends that have been delayed and subjugated in their activity.

6. Then, we would keep the present environs for the periods especially through the coming next three to six months. These as we find would be the better for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is the new doctor as efficient as the other one?
(A)  As we find, if there is the proper approach, just as efficient, just as helpful; and may be made MORE so if there will be the suggestions that the mechanics of the body ([[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] methods) be brought into play in making better outlining for improvements.

9. (Q)  What about Dr. Fransson?
(A)  As indicated, the present environs are the better.

10. (Q)  Have you any other suggestions?
(A)  Just keep those attitudes and those determinations to make the opportunities, that are being afforded through the activities, the most helpful influence in the experiences of all who may know or contact the body.  As he helps others, he will help himself.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
