# TEXT OF READING 1180-5 M 33 (Oil Well Driller)

**Reading [[1180-5]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/25/1924  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 6595

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made the by self - Mr. [1180].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                Wichita Falls, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, as to the operations as being handled by Cecil S. Ringle in Wichita County, [[Texas|Texas]], send at once this wire:

2. "Information unavoidably delayed.  Locate at the place where stake is located, near gully."

3. In these, as we will find, there will come some discussion and digression from original conditions regarding these developments.  However, these, when carried out in a proper manner, would prove successful.

---

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 11/6/24 [1180]'s letter to EC:
> 
> HUCKINS HOTELS
> Huckins Bros. Mgrs.
> 
> Kemp Hotel
> Wichita Falls, Tex.
> [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] Huckins, 3rd
> Manager
> 
> Written at Wichita Falls, Tex.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I will not attempt to write a letter tonight - but will let
> you know what I have done.  I will give you full particulars
> in a letter soon as am very busy.  Now about eighteen hours
> for ... [?].
> 
> We continued the hole to seven hundred feet without a single
> rainbow then in order to go twenty feet farther we would have
> not [had?] to set pipe and go to as much expense about as it
> had already cost, then as we had failed in our expectation
> to that depth then there being thousands of wells in this
> country and a sand between the depths of seven and nine
> hundred is almost an unheard of thing.  Then the depth test
> near by looked so much like a dry one that we were unable
> to sell any acreage leaving us with all the expense.  We
> thought it best to move back onto a good contract where
> we could be making something until we can get a cleared
> location.  Now Mr. Cayce this is just to let you know what
> we have done.  A long letter and retort will follow.  Now I
> have now lost confidence in you and wish to try again soon.
> 
> I am as ever yours of old and in a hurry as usual [1180]
> 
> R2. 11/13/24 [1180]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Wichita Falls [[Texas|Texas]].
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce:
> P.O. Box 463
> [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I am afraid you will think I have quit you but no not yet.
> I have been awful busy day and night, then not feeling any
> the best then to I have been wanting to go into detail with
> a long letter relating to your readings, and our dry hole
> then I have come to the conclusion what is the use we cannot
> expect them all to be big ones, as for me I am used to
> disappointments.  That did not hurt us any we paid the bills,
> and moved on and have made it all back on contract work
> already so why worry.
> 
> I feel sure you do not think we put any thing over on you now
> if you so desire I will make you a full report of the entire
> operations.  We still have the leases with a dry rotary hole
> close to the South line, but other people are contemplating
> the drill - of another on the North East if they do and we
> can sell the lease for enough to get out, we will and I shall
> let you know about it.
> 
> Some how I did not feel exactly right with your readings
> while I was drilling that hole they seemed to be confused
> to me but I am not able to explain in a letter so will wait
> until I see you.  But then as for the depth the only reading
> I saw that gave any depth was the one taken at your home
> while I was there that gave the depth from 528 to 589 which
> is in line with the production in that section, so in
> starting our hole we started it big enough to go from six
> hundred to eight hundred feet but we were able to get exactly
> seven hundred feet which was good, and generally conceited
> as deep enough for any shallow sand.
> 
> I would certainly like to see you and have a long talk but
> the trip costs so much, then I am so very busy, and then to
> if you are still interested as I am we can try another one
> before I come back as one is no test.
> 
> Now Mr. Cayce in reply to your last letter in regards to my
> using your information, for my own use I wish to again assure
> you that none of it leaves my hands and no one shall receive
> any use of it except those directly interested in my Company
> that does there part toward the financing, like Mr. Benton
> did on this one on the Evans.  Now I wish to ask one thing
> that is for you to take a reading at your earliest
> convenience, on these questions.
> 
> Q. What was the reason we did not get oil in this well?  Was
> it any fault of mine should we attempt it again if so how
> should we carry on the work?
> 
> Can I successfully, finance your institution, by drilling
> after your readings if so how?
> 
> Now if you see fit to consider these questions you may of
> course remodel them as you think best then if in any way I
> am lame in my part I wish to know it, and I assure you there
> will be no ill feelings.  I know I am not perfect but am
> willing to try.
> 
> With reference to your last letter of Oct 29th, I am
> certainly proud to know the interest you are taking in me and
> your willingness, to help me in any way you can but in return
> I want to solve some method in which I may be able to assist
> you in carrying out your plan.
> 
> Am I not giving you interest enough or have I some one with
> me that is not worthy or should I carry this end entirely by
> myself?  Now if you can offer any suggestion I will gladly
> do what I can with it and as soon as I can get more acreage,
> in shape and you are still with me I shall try it again.
> I cannot see why you should be up there in your present
> financial condition, and able to give such information as
> you do on so many other things.
> 
> Then with me here with the equipment and able to drill a hole
> so quick that we should not succeed when we wish to, that we
> might be able to do good, then others make millions that does
> themselves harm, and no one else any good.
> 
> P.S.  Nov. 18 here I find this letter not finished or mailed
> so will send it on I am feeling fine again now and hitting
> the high places in my new Ford.  I just rec'd your last
> letter of the 11th and will endeavor to answer it soon will
> close this for now and run along across the prairie about
> ten miles to a well we are on please give my best to all
> the family I am as ever  Your same [1180]
> 
> R3. 10/21/25 See 1180-6 on land in Rio Arriba Co., [[New Mexico|New Mexico]].
