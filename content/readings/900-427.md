# TEXT OF READING 900-427 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-427]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/7/1929  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5630

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 7th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y.

1. [900]:  You will have before you the reading taken yesterday at 5:20 P.M. [900-426], on [195], [137] and [900].  You will have before you question #7 and the answer given to question #7.  You will give us the answer for [137], and [900], and for [195], individually.  You will give these three the answer to question #5, as it was given yesterday.

2. EC:  As given:  [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]], husband of [[Virgin Mary|Mary]], mother of the Lord, [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].  [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]], father of Jose and Jude.  [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] the carpenter.

3. (Q)  Does this mean Joseph, the father of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] and Jude?
(A)  Joseph - the question asked - Joseph, the husband of [[Virgin Mary|Mary]], the mother of the Lord, [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].  Joseph, the father of Jose and Jude.  Joseph the carpenter.  We are through with this reading.
