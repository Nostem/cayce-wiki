# TEXT OF READING 438-2 M 40

**Reading [[438-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/21/1935  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 3253

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of May, 1935, in accordance with request made by the wife, through his active Membership in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Holy Name 
Time of Reading                                Hospital, 
10:45 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    Teaneck, N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [438].

2. As we find, with reference to those injuries in the shoulders, the head, and those conditions that are the more DISTRESSING in the upper portions of the body:

3. While we find there is no internal bleeding in the present, there are those injuries especially in the upper dorsal and cervical that will require special precautions and special attention to be given.  That is, let the body rest, you see. Do not attempt to move it or to make for changes, that are UNNECESSARY, until there has been sufficient time for the system to adjust itself to the distressing forces that have occurred in the extremities and in those specific portions of the body itself.

4. Keep the body very quiet, then, for eight to ten days.  Then we may find that there may be the applications in other directions that may aid in making for the prevention of the scar tissue or lesions in the spinal cords or spinal segments giving distress later.

5. Just keep those necessary precautions about the face, about the upper portion of the body, and the extremities - that, of course, need local attention; keeping the antiseptics nominally, and - as we find - these should come along alright.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Any specific antiseptics that would be suggested?
(A)  These are being used; just keep those precautions. Nothing better than the iodoform used in the present, with - of course - the local antiseptics when the dressings are made.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [718] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(Let us hear in eight to ten days, or when he may be moved, so that we may give suggestions for recuperative and corrective treatments to prevent later disorders.  GD.)
