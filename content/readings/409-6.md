# TEXT OF READING 409-6 F 19

**Reading [[409-6]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/23/1929  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 3049

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. and Mrs. [[Rome|Rome]], Mrs. Strauss, Mrs. Goldburg, Dr. Hansen, and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
2:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, conditions are continuing to show improvements.  There are some alterations in the effects being created.  A couple of times, at least, the adjustments for the weights have NOT been proper.  These tend to pull or to irritate tendons, rather than acting with the muscular reaction from tendon ends.  Would be WELL that occasionally this be attached ABOVE the knee.  Continue with those massages, those adjustments, and rub the whole of the limb with that of the equal parts of oil and myrrh.  Correct the conditions in mouth.  Keep the intestinal tract clear, an ALKALIN reaction in same.  Use the body cautiously, but use same in a SYSTEMATIC way and manner, and we will find the conditions CONTINUING to improve.  Keep the rest in the manner as has been given.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is bone gradually going in place?
(A)  Gradually taking its position, as it will remain.

3. (Q)  How long before it will be normal?
(A)  Dependent upon the applications and how the body applies itself, and what accidents would or may happen to the body, and how the care is taken of the whole system.  That's the same as to say when will a fly be swatted or when will it miss being hit!

4. (Q)  What causes stiffness in the knee joints?
(A)  It's just been given.

5. (Q)  Would it be harmful for patient to be taken to dentist?
(A)  We have just given that it should be!  Why would we say it would be harmful?  We are through for this time.

Copy to Dr. Berger (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital) Copy to Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Parker
