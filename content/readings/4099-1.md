# TEXT OF READING 4099-1 M 44

**Reading [[4099-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/10/1944  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 11797

This psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [4099], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. 
Taken 10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find the incapacitating of the locomotories in this body has been produced by pressures created on the spinal nerve and the locomotory centers themselves.

3. It will require patience and persistence but, as we find, if there will be used the low electrical forces that will build nerve energies for the body and the use of a massage that will strengthen the abilities to supply energies to the nerve and muscular forces in the lumbar area and throughout the limbs we may gradually regain better use of the limbs.  While this condition may not be wholly removed, the body should be able to be near to those activities in which it can control the whole locomotion; though the weakness through the areas where there were those injuries will last some time.

4. First have prepared the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying the [[Vibration|vibration]] of gold into the body, in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water.

5. The large plate through which the solution passes vibratorially would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus.  This will be the width of four fingers from the navel center to the right and four fingers up from that point.

6. The small copper plate would be attached to the lower portion of the 9th dorsal.

7. Make the attachments for thirty minutes each day.  Every thirty days recharge the Appliance and change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]].  While the Solution will not have been taken from the bottle, there will be sufficient condensation as to cause the Solution to be insufficient to carry the proper amount of gold to the body-force.  Thus change it.

8. Do keep the attachment plates very clean.  Do take the connections from the solution when not in use.  Do attach the connections to the Appliance and to the Solution about twenty minutes before the attachments are made to the body.

9. Each day following the use of the Appliance, give the body a thorough massage from the 9th dorsal to the toes and feet. Massage away from the body entirely, so that we carry the circulation in that direction.  Massage all the body will absorb of the equal combination of Olive Oil (heated) and Tincture of Myrrh.  Give this massage in a rotary motion, especially over the lower portion of the spine and the areas where the sciatic centers come down the limbs, especially around and under the knees, along the sciatic center, along the areas of the bursae of the feet and the calf of the leg or lower portion of the limb.

10. This will require several months but it will be worth it.  We would keep active in what might be called the trade or piddling or that in which the body as it sits in its chair, or wheel chair, may work with for the time being.  This will enable the body to do work along mechanical lines and in the repairing of various things of that nature in such a way as to be the gainer mentally, physically, spiritually.

11. Do keep the attitude creative.  Know from what source all [[Healing|healing]] must come.  For it is not of self, it is the practical use of elements of which the individual man is made and in which the constitution is lacking.  These must be added.  All power, all force is one source - the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]] or God.  Without such there is little hope.  In using these there is hope.

12. Do these things consistently and persistently, not as something just to be hurried through with but be persistent, be consistent and we will bring better conditions for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Any other suggestions that would enable me to support my family?
(A)  Do these and you'll find support, trusting in Him and not in self.  But use thyself, for you can do much work.

The massages may be given by the wife if this is preferable. This is preferable, provided the desires are a part of the experience of each.  This is the better way, but don't hurry through with it.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Assn file "   under case number for indexing

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, ..., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)
