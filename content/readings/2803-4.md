# TEXT OF READING 2803-4 F 24

**Reading [[2803-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/26/1943  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9990

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs.  [2803], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Phyllis Goodall and Herman Theaman.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Maryland.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions and anxieties that have gradually arisen with the changes which have been wrought in the activities of the body.

3. These are NOT good.  There ARE those tendencies for the activity of the liver, especially in the right lobe, to produce excess gastric flows that are depriving the lower portion of the body of the nerve energies - particularly from the lower locomotories.

4. Thus the painful experiences with the organs of the pelvis at periods, as well as the conditions in the lower limbs, knees, and in the calf of the leg.  And these contractions cause great pain to the body.

5. We would begin with these:

6. The rubs we would keep up.

7. And we would have, osteopathically, the breaking up of those tendencies for tautness in the sacral and in the lumbar axis.

8. For the liver, for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], prepare a [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] and a Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution to be taken internally.  Keep these separate until the dosage is to be taken.  These, of course, are poison, but in the manner taken will be beneficial and not poisonous.

9. The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be one grain of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of distilled Water.

10. The [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution would be four grains Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to one ounce of distilled water.

11. Once each day, put first one drop of the Gold Solution and then two drops of the Soda Solution in half a glass of water, and drink immediately.

12. Keep this up - for at least a whole period - that is from one menstrual period to another.

13. These will change all the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in the body, as well as - especially - the activity of the liver.

14. Do put the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver area an hour each day for three days.  Then take internally half a teacup of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].  Rest for at least a week, and then do this again.

15. In the periods when there is great pain, paint the pubic center - using a small brush - with a combination of four parts Tincture of Laudanum to one part Aconite.  Then apply a heavy pack of salt, heated, in a sack, you see.  This will relieve the contraction that is caused in the tubes and the pain through the vagina and the ovum's activity.

16. It will be necessary to obtain from a doctor, or by a doctor's prescription, the four parts of the drug from which morphia is made, and the nitrate combined with same.

17. Gradually these conditions will be relieved by the taking of the properties internally, the corrections osteopathically, and the rubs.  But for the first period the pain may be rather severe.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Is there a curvature in my spine?
(A)  There is rather a heaviness, not a curvature.  But this is in the area of the sacral and ileac plexus.

20. (Q)  Did the automobile accident last March have anything to do with this condition?  [7/26/43 Sister-in-law, [1523]'s report on the accident in March referred to, as she also was in the car at the time:  "[2803] was thrown forward, her head hitting the windshield and bouncing her back in the seat, thereby apparently injuring or throwing the coccyx out of line.  We all went to the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] the next day, but no thought or attention was given to the coccyx area."]
(A)  It caused the condition here in the sacral plexus, you see.

21. (Q)  How may I regulate my bowel movements without the use of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] every other day for proper eliminations?
(A)  The activities that have been indicated will change these considerably, but it is better to take the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] until the general activities are changed.  For, the use of the Packs and the oils should purify the system, and thus stimulate the peristaltic movement through the alimentary canal.

22. (Q)  What form of exercise would you advise?
(A)  Walking; combined with the regular home work.  But walking is the best.  But this should be systematic and consistent, and should be done early of morning or late of evening.

23. (Q)  Is an operation necessary?
(A)  For what?  There's nothing here to be operated for!

24. (Q)  Please suggest an affirmation.
(A)  LORD, LET THAT WHICH IS BEST FOR ME - THAT I MAY BE OF THE GREATER SERVICE - BE MY [[Purpose|PURPOSE]], MY HOPE, AND MY ACTIVITY NOW.

25. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n file
