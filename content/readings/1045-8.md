# TEXT OF READING 1045-8 F 38

**Reading [[1045-8]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/5/1936  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 6170

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of March, 1936, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., also by Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Hannah and [[Mount Ararat|Noah]] Miller.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1045]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, the body physically is very weak from the inability of the blood supply to attain the vitality from the circulatory force sufficient to sustain the activities of the body; through the alteration in the manner through which the air or oxygen passes through the lungs.

3. It would be well that the oxygen tank be used in the present, as we find.

4. And as there is given a little strength through same, use more of the Ash with the violet ray - [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]].

5. Use the inhalations from the charred container, rather than otherwise.  This inhalation, of the [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] from a charred container rather than from the wet or from the heating of same, will make more improvement.

6. We would use a quarter grain of the Ash once each day.  Use the tank oxygen first.

7. Keep up the GENTLE manipulations to prevent any portions becoming segregated or stoppages being brought in the circulation.  These given once or twice a day, osteopathically, will be helpful.  Work from the limbs TOWARD the body, rather than FROM the body.  In the body itself, stimulate gently the secondary cardiac plexus area as well as the vagus center; which will aid in alleviating that fullness.

8. For strengthening internal food values we would give the juice of crushed fresh grapes; any character of grapes so long as they are not those fermented or those that have been canned.  This, as we find, with little sips of the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] as indicated, will be the necessary nourishment in the present.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  How should the Yogurt be prepared for this body?
(A)  Yogurt is prepared by making a curd that passes through the system to absorb and aid in the eliminations.

Use in milk, then, as a curd - and take very small sips for the body.

Yogurt is a preparation from honeycomb, you see, that acts to take caseins from milk that are injurious to the system and helpful to the intestinal tract.

This is needed more later than in the immediate, you see; for these emergencies must be met - in this lack of oxygen supply for the body.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to [Husband]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [1100]

DAY LETTER:  FIRST USE OXYGEN TANK THEN QUARTER GRAIN ASH DAILY, [[Ultraviolet Light|ULTRA-VIOLET]].  MANIPULATIONS ONCE-TWICE DAILY FROM LIMBS TOWARD RATHER THAN AWAY FROM BODY, STIMULATING GENTLY SECONDARY CARDIAC AND VAGUS CENTERS.  GIVE SIPS JUICE FROM FRESH CRUSHED GRAPES NOT FERMENTED OR CANNED.  ALSO [[Beef Juice|BEEF JUICE]].  INHALE BRANDY FROM CHARRED CONTAINER.
