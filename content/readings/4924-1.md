## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/45 Report by Mr. Tom Baugh of [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky:
> 
> "When Edgar Cayce lived in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]] I knew him well and
> heard him diagnose many cases, some of persons who lived here
> and others of persons from all parts of the United States.
> Nearly every case, when instructions were followed, showed
> results.  I made an electrical apparatus from his diagnosis
> for use by his patients.  I made one for Miss [243], the
> first one I made that gave good results.
> 
> "I will tell you of one case that many people here remember:
> [4924], a city policeman at the time, was in bed several
> months.  Mr. Cayce diagnosed his case and had me construct an
> electrical device, many parts of which I had to order from
> [[New York City|New York]].  I made it, took it to his home and showed his wife
> how to use it.  In two weeks time [4924] brought it to my
> store and said he wanted someone else to have it in order
> that it might help others.
> 
> "I made different types to suit various diagnoses and shipped
> them from Maine to [[Texas|Texas]].
> 
> "It's a funny thing to me that Edgar Cayce, when awake, did
> not know the first thing about electricity.  He couldn't even
> make a connection on a battery.  Yet in a trance he seemed to
> know all electrical terms and would even tell the types of
> electrodes to use."
