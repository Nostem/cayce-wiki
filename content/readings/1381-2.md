# TEXT OF READING 1381-2 F 29

**Reading [[1381-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/10/1927  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 7091

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by self, through Mr. [165].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Mr. [165].]

Time of Reading 
1:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer any questions regarding same that I might ask you.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find the general conditions surrounding this body are much different from that as we have had here before.  In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body there is not the great deal of change.  In the mental attitude of the body, THIS we find has changed much, and this has - and will have - much to do with the physical reactions as will emanate from the efforts and from the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Those conditions as disturb the body in the physical sense need the greater local attention through the efforts of the body, rather than that of the outside influences - for with the body building and making from within that proper reaction for self, there may be builded most any condition in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body the body would desire; for, as has been given respecting these conditions with the physical forces as are seen applicable in this body, the mind [is] strong, and the mind [is] the builder.

4. In those things, those conditions as are necessary then for the full correction, the body - through that proper attitude towards that as brings about the changes - may alter or mar the various reactions as will be builded in the system from same.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What can be done to prevent miscarriages?
(A)  The mental attitude of the body towards these conditions is the greatest influence, as has been given to the body, as respecting these conditions.  There has been so long builded in the whole entity that disregard for some responsibilities in this direction, that easily we find the body responding to that as is innately at times felt in the body.  To add physical effects to this condition would be to demoralize, as it were, the inner activity of that creative energy towards the resuscitation of life through the efforts of the body.

This in the physical sense may be added as that as will induce - through the activities of the physical body - that which would prevent such:  Just after the menstrual period the body use the stupes as this:

To a gallon and a half of hot water, add:

Tincture Lobelia..........1/2 ounce,
Tincture of Myrrh.........1/2 ounce,
Tolu in solution............1 ounce.

and expose the vagina to the steam or fumes from same.  This will act as the reaction forces to the body to bring about a strengthening of the portions of the genitive system that will bring better conditions through the mental forces and the physical forces of the body.  Do that for at least three periods before there is the act of regeneration in system, and we will find the difference in the condition.

6. (Q)  What should be done for her serious throat trouble?
(A)  This may be healed the greater through the physical application of that mental force within self that is the recuperating or building forces for any physical body, for we find not all bodies will respond to that mental reaction regarding the physical forces.  In THIS body these WILL respond, will the body but WILL self towards the various physical conditions as are supplied to a physical body through concentrated effort on the part of self to add to the body that proper rejuvenation through the proper attitudes towards life, resuscitation of life, rebuilding of life from within self - THESE will add the greater conditions to the condition in any portion of this body, and to throat also.

7. (Q)  No operation is required?
(A)  No operation required, with the proper attitude of self towards self and the [[Healing|healing]] powers that will come from within through this proper attitude.

8. (Q)  What can improve the home happiness?
(A)  Only that as comes from within.  That as is given out by self to make for the better elements of happiness.  Happiness being then the state of mind - towards any condition, see?

9. That is all the questions.

10. To add to the physical [[Vibration|vibrations]] of this body, to bring about a more active coordination with the elements of treatment as applied to self, we would add as this through that with which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may apply to self:

11. Each evening as the body retires for that intro-retrospective - application of the physical being to self add physically those [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the body as come from the Radio-Active Appliance.  This will assist the body in gaining a better reaction to the whole physical forces of the body.  Not as a curative - but as the effect created in the nerve impulse to react in that created by the introspective treatment as applied to self.  Do that.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
