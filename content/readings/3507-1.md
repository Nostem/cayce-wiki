# TEXT OF READING 3507-1 M 27 (Naval Officer [Deceased?])

**Reading [[3507-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/13/1943  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 10990

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mr. [5367] and Harmon Bro.]

Born March 27, 1916, 12:01 A. M., Redlands, [[California|California]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as or called [...] - in the present not a prisoner, but in the land of the living though quite ill, but attended by natives.  He should recover.

3. In giving the influences in the experiences of this entity, many conditions, many experiences are to be considered - from that termed the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, though this is a poor term for the meaning of the consciousness of the entity, as well as the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] of the entity in the earth's plane that are a portion of the personality of the present entity.

4. These we choose from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this information be a helpful experience for the entity; enabling the entity to better fulfil the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

5. As will be seen in the material [[Incarnation|sojourns]], the entity's life follows a pattern that should hold through this present experience.

6. When the [[Astrology|astrological]] sojourns are considered or the consciousness other than three-dimensional as correlated with the material sojourns, we find:

7. The entity is one having a pleasing personality; one with a great deal of political tact in its activities.

8. One that has a manner or mien in keeping with the tenets "Live and let live."  This does not indicate that the entity is extravagant in any sense, but it is rather as the version of the entity in that "As ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them."

9. Astrologically we find Mercury, Venus, Jupiter and Mars as the ruling influences with the entity.

10. One with high mental abilities.

11. One with those characteristics of the Jupiterian, being rather of the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] and always taking the part of those less fortunate than self.

12. One well given in affection, and making friends in its activities one with another - which is standing in well also in the present environs.

13. In Mars, while anger is a part of the experience at times, the entity has control over self well in such relationships. And again we would give that one without a temper is not worth much, but one not able to control the temper is worth a great deal less.

14. In its activities, the entity is ever busy in that having to do with economy or associations with other lands, and especially will this be true if there is the completion of physical conditions in the present and the return to that ye call civilization.  But he is doing a very good [[Book of Job|job]] of civilizing, even though unconscious has been his part often of late.

15. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, we find that these have been quite varied; but as indicated the entity is one given to a real pattern.

16. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity during the reconstruction period.

17. Then in the name William Cowper, the activities were well known in writing, the contributions to the [[Purpose|purposes]] and [[Ideal|ideals]] in those periods.

18. Before that the entity was in [[England|England]] during that period that followed the early Crusade.

19. The entity was active in reconstruction in the land where there were great differences of opinion as to political and religious tendencies.

20. Then in the name Carlson Carlington, the entity contributed to the activities that brought about a greater unity of purpose in those groups which the entity had joined.

21. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when there were those periods of spreading the tenets and teachings, where the efforts were united in those groups from the Mongoloid land, the Golden City, the land of Saad, and [[Egypt|Egypt]], when there was a uniting of purpose for the cause of one.

22. The entity acted in the capacity of preparing the tenets to which each group would subscribe, according to the activities of each of the groups of that land.

23. The entity's name was Tek-elar-ken.

24. Before that the entity was in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] just before the first [[Earth Changes|upheavals]].

25. The entity was among the [[Sons of the Law of One|children of the Law of One]] as opposed to the activities of the [[Sons of Belial|sons of Belial]].

26. The entity acted in the capacity as the prince to the ruling forces in the land, and ever stood for those tenets of truth, for the upbuilding, for the regeneration of those peoples that were partly as things - or those not capable in mind to care for their own activities.

27. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain and how:

28. Keep first those tenets as from the beginning in the activities, that in the present may be in giving the truths of life, light, liberty, union of purpose.  These are to be the activities, the work then of the entity in the present, and may there be those hopes fulfilled in the entity's presenting the causes of other lands to those that will be in authority during the periods of reconstruction following the present activities.

29. Ready for questions.

30. (Q)  May we be given the name of the land where he now is?
(A)  This will not be given in the present.  If there is the completion, the gaining of health, we find that by the 15th of January or the 1st of February, 1944, there should be word received from him.

31. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Father

> B1. 7/11/43 Wife's letter:
> 
> Dr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|The Association]] for Research and Enlightenment,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Dr. Cayce:
> 
> I just finished reading [[Thomas Sugrue|Sugrue]]'s book of your life, "There
> is a River".  It is needless for me to say how much I enjoyed
> reading it.  I thought perhaps you could help me, as you have
> helped many others.  Here is my problem and if you feel I am
> worthy helping, I would enjoy hearing from you.
> 
> My husband [3507], born March 27th, 1916 in Redlands,
> [[California|California]] at 12:01 a.m. was supposedly killed in action on
> November 13, 1942 in the Solomons area.  He graduated from
> the Naval Academy in 1938 and was one of the top men of his
> class.  Why I mention this is because he was very brilliant
> and I always tried to improve my self, so as to be on the
> same plain as he.  But I was never able to attain this goal.
> 
> I am very interested in obtaining his life reading as well as
> my own.  I was born on May 7, 1916 in [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]] in the
> early evening.  My maiden name is [...].  We have one child
> [...] born March 20th, 1943 in Long Beach, [[California|California]] as
> 12:01 p.m.
> 
> There is some question as to whether my husband is really
> dead.  I have yet to find any one who saw his body before the
> ship USS Atlanta was sunk.  After talking to people who were
> there, we have come to the conclusion he was either blown
> over the side of the ship into the water, or his body was so
> badly mangled it was not recognized.  I do not think of you
> as a fortune teller and so I am not asking you to tell me
> whether he is alive or not but I am interested in trying to
> get my self in the same plain his [[Soul|soul]] is in.  I might add,
> that we both were Sunday school teachers at the Central Union
> Church, in Honolulu.
> 
> If there is anything you can do to help me I will be
> sincerely grateful and will always remember you in my
> [[Prayer|prayers]].
