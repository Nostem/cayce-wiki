# TEXT OF READING 5670-1 F 64

**Reading [[5670-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/12/1928  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 14081

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5670].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                    ... St., 
12:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have here the body [5670].  There are conditions that may be aided and the nearer normal condition brought about.  There are conditions that have reached such stages as would be hard to be, and would hardly be, brought to a normal condition through THIS experience.  The conditions as may be corrected and assisted, while of long duration, and while they have much to do with the debility and disableness as exists, yet with the bringing about of resuscitation and recuperation for portions that have been allowed to come under distress through this gradual accumulation of forces in the system, and the setting up of outside forces as compared WITH the physical functioning of the body, these corrections then will, and would, bring MATERIAL aid in the ease as would be brought to the body, and as to the abilities of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] to function nearer in a normal manner.

2. The condition has to do, then, with the accumulation of forces as used forces, or poor eliminations and the effect as has been brought on the nerve and the ganglia of the body, as well as disturbances in the organ functioning of portions of the body.  These then causing distress through the locomotaries of the system, and the inability of the body to carry on in NOMINAL way.

3. In meeting, then, the conditions - first, while there has been considerable attention given to the diet of the body, and these at times apparently aided the system and made for a time bettered conditions, yet the seat or the cause - as was produced by the disturbances at the time the first were brought into the system - has not been reached.

4. Then, to begin with, we would be careful of the diet.  Meats should be banned.  Only vegetables.  And little of those that carry the heavy proteins, or protein producing forces in the body.  Rather those of the green vegetables, or of those creating much iron for the body; as some fruits of the character that carry same; also of the vegetables as carry much of same.

5. And the body should begin with such properties as Occy-Crystine and set up eliminations in such a manner as to create the more non-acid, or the more active forces through non-acids for the body.

6. Also applying those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be found in that of the Radio Appliance carrying Gold.  These will add to the functioning of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in such a manner, with the diet and with the eliminations set up, with at least twice each week deep manipulations osteopathically to equalize those conditions as are set to work to produce elimination and to bring about resuscitation in properly distributed forces through the system - applying the Radio Appliance to the umbilicus and to the wrist one day, to the ankle the next - on OPPOSITE sides of the body, see?

7. Begin with the Occy-Crystine (tablespoonful is the maximum), beginning with the minimum dose of half teaspoonful in two glasses of water.  When the tablespoonful is taken there should be at least three or four, or more, glasses of water taken with same, see?  Preferable not to use the local water in drinking.  Preferable to use that either of a softer water, or this local water may be boiled.

8. Do that.  When these have been carried out for thirty days, we would give further instructions.  We are through for the present.

> be here to see me today in regards to the Radio-Active
> Appliance.  Thanking you many times I am respectively..."
> 
> R2. 1/13/29 Letter:  "...It will be thirty days on the 17th since
> I started to follow your suggestions on my treatments and
> must say in some ways I am not so sore and have less pain in
> my hip, but my knees are still as sore as ever, but of course
> I do not expect much in this short time.  I have, I think,
> done fine in regard to my diet.  Now in this time I have only
> tasted meat three times.  No potatoes at all, no sweets or
> pastry of any kinds mostly green vegetables and kraut and
> rye and cornbread.  I have followed up the use of the Radio
> Appliance with the exception of I think three evenings, for
> my husband used the electric current on me and one day I went
> to take my treatment and I had so much electricity in me when
> Doctor Lydic went to treatment me the sparks from my body
> made the Doctor jump so he laughingly told me I would have
> to let my batteries run down.  In other words he told me not
> to use the Appliance for a while.  Doctor Lydic treats me
> tomorrow which will be nine treatments.
> 
> "Now, Mr. Cayce I feel I owe it to you to tell you my husband
> [5656] is a physician, and he has done everything he could
> do, then he took me to other physicians.  Also had X-rays
> made showing the arthritis very bad and it has all been
> brought on by constipation for a number of twenty years.
> So last June Doctor [referring to her husband] bought me
> an Ionica Belt that I have used three times a day.  Until
> I started your treatments.  It is a magnetized affair that
> I connect to the current.  Shall I still use it or give it
> up for a while?  Hoping to hear from you soon, I am very
> respectively..."
> 
> R3. 2/8/29 EC made an appointment for a check reading for her to
> find out what further treatments were necessary.
> 
> R4. 2/21/29 Letter:  "...I am enclosing my card for my reading
> also wish to say I am better in a great many ways.  I do not
> suffer the pains like I did and am sleeping better.  I have
> been keeping up all treatments with the exception of the few
> days I told you of in my recent letter.  I have only broken
> the rules three or four times in regards to meat and only
> a bite at one meal but would not be six ounces in all this
> time.  Will the stiffness leave my back, hips and knees,
> also the soreness?..."
>
