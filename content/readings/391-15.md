# TEXT OF READING 391-15 M 24

**Reading [[391-15]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/8/1935  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 2992

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [391], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Tamiami Trail & 
Time of Reading                              Hill St., 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Ft. Myers, Fla. 
(Body and enquiring mind.  Questions regarding his business 
and material welfare)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [391]; those conditions and the material associations.

2. As we find, while there have been those conditions and circumstances that have made a great deal of anxiety on the part of the body and those associated with same in the activities there, this - as the seasons begin, as the changes come about - should make for a most satisfactory arrangement; enabling the body not only to make for recuperations that appear at present to be investments without returns but more besides.

3. So, let the body be patient.  And make the stress upon the foods and service that would be WHOLLY in keeping with [[Law of Moses|the law]].  The associations and activities, then, would be in not only the hamburger and hot dog but also a chicken dinner might be prepared and used as a specialty.

4. These should, unless there are the anxieties allowed that would cause the body to endeavor to make for other changes, bring about the DESIRED results.

5. Be courteous; be kind.  And make for placarding the routes that come to those directions.  This at not a great expense; it can be done by those about the body - just boards or placards that might be put along the highway, in the trail and in those entering from the city itself and those connections.

6. And you will find these will be most satisfactory.  Forty dollars a day gross should be a very small amount as the seasons are underway.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How long will it be before we get the average winter business?
(A)  By the 15th or the 20th of the month it should BEGIN. See, there is NOT the great amount of travel on those activities until there is some settled condition of those things that have brought and do bring about turmoils and anxieties on the part of some traveling through these portions.  Hence by the 20th we should find a sufficient to more than balance the activities, or to meet the needs.  By the 15th of December, then, until the first of March or 15th of March, we should find most satisfactory results.

9. (Q)  Should we keep the place during the summer months and let someone run it for us, or should we sell out at the close of the winter season?
(A)  As we find, it would be better just to close for the summer season.

10. (Q)  Will we be able to get our $400 back, that we invested in it, before next summer?
(A)  As indicated; you should get not only that but much more.  More than twice that much should be CLEARED above all investments!

11. (Q)  Is this the location spoken of in the previous reading?
(A)  This is a very good location.

12. (Q)  How much rent should we pay here?
(A)  It is not an excess in what has been, if same will be kept as indicated.

Follow those suggestions.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

>

> over 2 wks. longer.  I've borrowed $165 to keep us going
> until the season got here but it's all gone and we can't pay
> our rent, lights and car payment...  So I don't see anything
> to do but sell what interest we have in the car for $100 or
> whatever we can get for it and let [934] go home [to [[Ohio|Ohio]]]
> and [2111] and [...] to Ala., and I intend to look for a [[Book of Job|job]]
> somewhere.  I have a fine of $15 that I owe for a mess I got
> into about a slot machine.  I can't pay that, so I might get
> my room and board for about a month at least.  This lovely
> state of Fla.  I hope I never see it again, if I ever get out
> of it...  I hate to send you a letter like this, but it isn't
> just the customary blues, it's just as things are.  I'm $40
> behind in the bank on cks. and rent, lights, fine and car
> payments due.  What can I do?  If you think the readings
> would cover a condition like this, I certainly would like to
> have one.
> 
> "Regards to the rest of the family."  Sincerely, [391]
> 
> R3. 1/6/36 EC's reply:  "I have yours of the 30th.  I'm sorry
> indeed to note that you are discouraged and disappointed in
> your undertaking there, and feel that you have got to give
> up.  I have been so busy the last day or so that's impossible
> to write, owing to the emergency readings; for we have
> scarcely had anything else each day since the 1st; even on
> the first day and ever since there has been an emergency
> reading, so that we have had to put off the regular routine.
> 
> "But I believe you will agree with me, and I hope you will
> bear with me and listen at me, [391], for just a minute.  I
> feel, of course, responsible in a way and manner for your
> having gone to [[Florida|Florida]].  I feel a bit responsible even for
> your ever getting married.  Yet I am sure you realize, from
> your knowledge and study of human experience, it isn't
> knowledge alone, it is the wisdom in the application of the
> knowledge that may be given you that makes for good or for
> bad.  Now, one may give you an understanding or a knowledge,
> but the wisdom and the application of same is up to you
> yourself.
> 
> "But hold on there for a time, [391], anyway.  I have the
> feeling that you are going to find that it is very much like
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]; the real season there will not be until
> possibly the middle of January and until the first weeks of
> March.  That's the one thing, first!  Even if you have to
> borrow more money from Mr. Tepper or somebody else, CARRY ON!
> DO NOT, above all things, send [934] home.  Stay with her;
> keep her with you through this period.  It's going to be best
> for you and you'll think more of yourself, and you'll think
> more of everything that you're going through!  And she will,
> I'm sure.  If it becomes necessary that you lose the car, or
> that some of the rest of them go home, do it - but you stick
> it out, and keep her WITH you above everything else!  For you
> are passing through a time and a period that only happens
> once in a man's life, and if you don't make the most of it
> you'll regret it the rest of your whole experience.  But I
> just feel like it's going to work out.  Don't give it up!
> Hold to it!  Don't try to run off and leave whatever
> discouraging things you have!  MEET 'EM!  Anyone can do fine
> when things are going dandy!  It shows the real man and the
> real worth of a fellow that can meet the emergencies when
> things are going all wrong.
> 
> "I'm sure you've made a lot of friends there.  You may have
> run up against some tough propositions also, but you can meet
> them a whole lot better together, [391], than you can by
> going off and leaving each other.  And I'm sure that [934]
> will agree with me.  Talk it over.  Let this thing that is
> happening bring you closer together than you have ever been
> in your life!  Let it knit that thing it is intended to knit
> in the experience of every individual - the love and the
> knowledge that comes from knowing you are responsible for the
> creating of a channel for a [[Soul|soul]] expressing itself in the
> material world!
> 
> "It's a big proposition, yes.  But God has promised those who
> call that He will hear.  Let it make YOU, then, closer to
> Him.  He won't turn His back on you, no matter how bad you
> have been or FEEL you have been.  Because you haven't been
> bad at all.  You know OF Him, and you know you are a channel
> through which He is manifesting at this time.  Then ACT like
> it!  Don't try to dodge this issue!
> 
> "You know that if I can be of a help through a reading, I'm
> only too glad to try.  Possibly the circumstances have been
> such that what is needed is for me to talk to you like a
> dutch uncle.  And maybe you think it's none of my business.
> But it's because I DO think a lot of you, think a lot of you
> both, that I'm saying the things I am.  I've had the
> experience, I know what it means.  And we have had the
> experience of the readings through the last thirty-five
> years.  I know, from the good that has been brought into the
> experience of individuals, the work is worth while.  I can't
> be fooled that long, and I couldn't fool the thousands of
> people that claim and testify that it has been valuable to
> them.  Now YOU make it valuable in your life!  The reading
> may tell you the thing to do for your best development,
> materially, mentally, spiritually; it may give you the
> knowledge, but the DOING is up to YOU!
> 
> "If you have to borrow MORE money to meet these things, then
> DO IT!  But HE will arrange it, if you will do the right
> thing.  You know, all the increase that may come comes from
> God.  YOU can't change it; you can only do that which will
> make yourself a channel through which He can work - which is
> DOING RIGHT!  Maybe you think I'm foolish, [391], but take
> this as it is meant, and stick to it there.
> 
> "If you find that you have to ask Mr. Tepper again for a
> loan, then tell him the whole situation, [391]; and that
> you've got to stay together, and that no doubt the
> proposition there will let you "break even", if nothing more.
> And it'll be a whole lot better.  Tell him you have GOT to
> stay together, and WHY.  And that's the thing you MUST do!
> If not, you'll regret it the rest of your life.
> 
> "Drop me a line when you have the opportunity.  I'm not
> preaching to you, I'm talking just PRACTICAL sense!
> 
> "Love to you and [934]."
> 
> Ever the same, Edgar Cayce  [EC:GD]
>

> to get out and the time is about up.  What we're trying to do

> mother and the expenses won't be very much.  The whole
> trouble now is trying to get enough money to get up there on,
> and it's not easy.  The place is empty of stock and has been
> several days, with no money to buy more.  The idea of
> borrowing more is out of the question.  If I couldn't make
