# TEXT OF READING 2553-4 F 26

**Reading [[2553-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/10/1923  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 9554

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 10th day of October, 1923, in accordance with request made the by Arthur H. [[Arthur Lammers|Lammers]], of [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], October 10th, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Mamie Rosenberg (?), Steno. Mrs. [2553]''s husband, Mr. [5759], also present.]

Rosedale Hospital, Rm. 28, 
Time of Reading                    4429 Nicollet Ave. So., 
11:05 A. M.                        Minneapolis, Minn.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  There are a great many changes in the body since last we had it here.  Most have been for the worse, for they have not followed out the instructions as given here, you see, to correct and localize this condition before the operation was performed.  Hence we find that the cellular forces within the system carrying the germ of the poison as produced, have become above and beyond the power of control within the system and have set up or established that condition which will be destructive to the physical of this body.

2. Relief from the pain as is suffered by this body may be obtained more through other properties then that as is being given at the present time, for we only need to give rest to the body and be able if we are to create within the body the resistance (within the self) rather than within the physical, for it is beyond the physical at the present, you see.

3. For this we would give that as carries the heroin, 22nd of a grain, and that of the eucalpytol, with the rubbing in these portions of the body, - about the arm pits, the throat, across the diaphragm and down the right limb, - those as we would find in a strong solution of hydrastic.  This will be absorbed for the system.  This will give more relief.

4. Give with the water that is taken in the system as in nourishment, that with elm, yellow saffron and bismuth, equal parts to twenty percent of the water as taken.

5. That is all at present.

6. (Q)  Shall another operation be performed?
(A)  Not necessary.  No more than the first was.  Only hindrance to the system.

7. (Q)  Shall her husband remain in Minneapolis?
(A)  That has nothing to do with this body we are speaking of.

8. (Q)  Shall this body be removed to [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]]?
(A)  Impossible at present.
