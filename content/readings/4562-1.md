# TEXT OF READING 4562-1 F 65

**Reading [[4562-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/14/1924  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 12449

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4562], via Mrs. [2481].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4562] and Dr. [235].]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in this body in the physical there are some abnormal conditions that may be corrected. These have to do with the functioning of organs as affected by the eliminations in the system.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4562] we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find in this that there is carried that bacilli as is being absorbed from conditions existing in other portions of system, and as the needs of the system are such that the leukocyte forces are not able to combat with these that the return of these conditions to tissue and organs in system in system at times give some unpleasant conditions to the functioning of the organs.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find is very good, yet in some centers there is that overtaxing that shows how the eliminating centers are affected.  The normal elements in the nerve system are so controlled by the mental that the ability of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] to control the functioning of the system is well exemplified in the nerve system in this body, and in so far as the whole system can be controlled this is above the normal in this body, yet with the continual pressure as created from physical exertions the system still absorbs these toxins that at times becomes detrimental to the system in that the body does not create sufficient of the leukocyte to overcome, and when this is enacted in the nerve system at times comes to the detriment of other portions of the system, in the physical sense only.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find in the cerebro-spinal very good, save as some centers are taxed, as we have in the 9th dorsal center.  In this plexus do the portions of the branch ends from this center show the conditions existing in trunk portion of body.

6. In brain center proper very, very good.  One controlled more through its own will, active with the mental capacity of the ability, than is ordinarily found.

7. In the organs of the sensory system, especially, do we find the manifestations of the mental forces and of the spirit elements entering into that functioning of the organs direct.

8. Lungs, throat and larynx very good.

9. Heart action subjugated to the condition of circulation.

10. Digestive tract in times back we have had conditions here unsatisfactory.  Very good at the present time, for with the mental attributes governing the conditions appetites are governed more and nearer correctly.

11. In the spleen, pancreas, duodenum and liver do we find the show in these of the condition existing in the region of the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], from which the system absorbs toxins in the system, and these organs in their functioning as physical organs, and with the mental acted through the mental of the body finds its manifestations, though they show the conditions existing from the physical plane.

12. In the intestinal tract in times back we have had conditions that have not been good; that is from digestion.  These have been improved to some extent.  Not as much inflammation and more of the secretions are given in the system to prevent this portion of the system forming toxins.  Still do we have some inflammation produced at times in the lower hepatic circulation or the kidneys, and the bladder, have strained from the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of system with toxins.

13. Then to give the better condition to the body, for we find reflexes from these to the lower limbs at times, and to bring the normal incentive to the system with that as contained and performing in the physical through mental forces of this body, we would add those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be accorded the system in a specific exercise for the system, and of the [[Vibration|vibration]] received in [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] forces over the whole colon tract and across the diaphragm, using only three minutes each evening, before resting.

14. As for the exercise, let these be for the upper portion of the body of mornings and for the lower portion of the body of evenings.

15. Do this.  We will bring much good to this body, and add many days of usefulness and service to others.
