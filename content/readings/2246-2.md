# TEXT OF READING 2246-2 M 73 (Professor, Retired)

**Reading [[2246-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/18/1944  Sex: M  Age: 73  ReadingID: 8972

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the foster son, Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

In Coats-Gafney 
Time of Reading                               Clinic, 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.        Tyler, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, conditions are not so good.  Cold, or the excess of the attempt of system to create lymph for the conditions through the limbs, has drained the strength and vitality of the body.

3. We find that these conditions are being handled as well as might be.

4. We find it would prove beneficial for the body to have added the normal dose of those properties administered by the homeopathic profession for phlebitis or dropsical conditions - cimex lectularius.

5. Do that.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Any further suggestions for care of his leg?
(A)  Only those precautions to protect from the breaking of the cuticle itself.

8. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet to build up resistance.
(A)  Those foods that are easily assimilated are the better. The resistance, though, is very low.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - ..., Tyler, [[Texas|Texas]]
"    " Mr. [...]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
"    " Mr. [2091]
