# TEXT OF READING 1505-8 F 37

**Reading [[1505-8]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/3/1943  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 7387

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [341], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - same place.

3. As we find with this body in the present, there only needs be added a well-balanced vitamin addition or supplement.

4. Owing to the manner in which the body is under a strain, mentally and physically, there is the tendency to under eat, or to be neglectful in certain activities.

5. Hence, as we find, we would add the Purity Cross Vitamin tablets.  Take one every OTHER day, when ready to retire.

6. These should bring a better appetite, better assimilation, better eliminations, and make for strength and resistance in the body - from the iron and the vitamins as will be assimilated by the use of this in the system.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Husband) Copy to Ass'n file
