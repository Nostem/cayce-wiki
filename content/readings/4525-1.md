# TEXT OF READING 4525-1 M 14

**Reading [[4525-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/18/1926  Sex: M  Age: 14  ReadingID: 12406

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [...], via her brother, Mr. [5086].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are some specific conditions, some minor conditions, and some of those of the nature that should be corrected, for the better development and for the normal functioning of the body.

4. In these changes, as will be made through the adjustments of those conditions as need correcting, we will find there will be many various minor conditions to overcome.

5. In the cause, then, we find this as the greater trouble - these also the effects as are produced by this condition.

6. In some time back there was that condition brought about wherein, in the 2nd and 3rd lumbar, and in the 12th and 11th dorsal, there is a subluxation between these two - one to the side, and one subluxated, or one down.  These cause pressure on the nerve system.  Hence that peculiar condition as has existed as regarding the digestion, as regarding the eliminations in the system - the dull, laggard feeling felt at times, the inability to find anything exactly agreeing with the body, acting in the way wherein never quite satisfied, yet ever desiring to do the best.  These are reflections from the refractory reaction of these subligations, as they act on the nerve system, on the digestive system, on the circulation, and the general forces in the body - never causing, as it were, any DIRECT distress, save as the dull mean headaches, and trouble with the eyes as come from time to time - yet these, as we see, are of a reflex nature.

7. Then, to overcome the condition, we would, through the correct adjustment of these conditions, chiropractically or osteopathically, bring about the normal straightening, or normal condition, in the cerebro-spinal region - keeping the diet in the way wherein the general rebuilding will be for better blood, purer blood, better eliminations, and we will find the body, through the normal exercise as is ever necessary for the development of any [[Physical Body|physical body]], will be brought about in such a way that the normal forces will manifest and show themselves through such conditions.

8. The adjustments should be at least eight to ten, or until these are corrected properly.

9. Do that.

10. We are through for the present.

> easy to make cry.  I talk to him about it and HE comes to me

> being cross sometimes it seems, I feel so badly and for the
> same I reason I cannot control myself about crying."  Now
> this is not his disposition, for he was born even tempered
> and of a sweet, gentle, disposition and very affectionate.
> Just as thoughtful and polite to me and other people as he
> can be, and will always put himself last to do for others.
> As I have said about his appetite, he has never been a body
> to eat everything.  He likes lots of milk, sweets and little
> delicacies.  Eats very little meats, bread and vegetables.
> I have tried to force flesh and muscle making eats on him by
> denying him his likes, but I believe he would actually starve

> 
> Of course you know I have not been able to indulge them in

> as amusements and pleasures and an allowance of money each

> swims, bicycles and etc. that the boys of today enjoy, while
> all of his running mates do enjoy these things, and in a way
> it seems to be a disappointment to him, so he is getting so
> now he does not seem to care to be with other boys as he used
> to.
> 
> If he passes this term at school, he will be ready for High
> School in the spring.  I believe he is at the age where

> large enough to understand, and he has a horror of such, and
> I have watched him carefully along this line.  I notice that
> he doesn't seem to be made up, just exactly right, not as his
> younger brother who is 2-1/2 years younger than he is, and I
> wonder if a slight operation is necessary, and also that his

> 
> I believe I have told you about all I can think of about
