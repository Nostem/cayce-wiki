# TEXT OF READING 3409-1 M 26 (Motion Picture Operator, Army

**Reading [[3409-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/15/1943  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 10865

Sgt.) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3409], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Born May 10, 1917, (St. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] Hospital) in Brighton, 
Mass. 
Air Corps Section, 
Time of Reading                           APO 951, 
4:00 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.      % Postmaster, 
San Fran., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane; giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3409].

3. In analyzing [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we have them here, we choose from same that which if applied in the activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will give a better interpretation of that [[Purpose|purpose]] for which the entity entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

4. There are virtues, there are faults.  We would magnify the virtues, we would minimize the faults.  This, too, should be the policy of the entity.  For we find in the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects as well as periods of manifestation in the material plane, there are tendencies on the part of the entity to become rather severe in criticism.  Not merely of others, but tending to make the same criticism of self, or to feel sorry for self - which is the severest criticism of all.

5. For, unless there is the ability in each entity, each [[Soul|soul]], to appreciate self and its relationships to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], what opportunity is there to have others to think much of yourself also?  For like begets like.  These are unfailing, in spiritual, in mental and in material aspects. These change not.  They are unchangeable laws.  Like begets like.

6. So in interpreting the experiences of the entity in all of its phases, study to show self approved unto God or unto thy ideal.  This is saying, then, choose [[Ideal|an ideal]] in spiritual aspects of your life; not merely saying as ye read the tenets of this group or organization "These are good - I accept these," No!  Those are the tenets of others.  Read thyself. Set not only in mind but on paper what the entity believes. For what the entity promises itself, this entity will fairly well fulfill - unless it becomes slack in those urges as we find that conflict with the mental processes of the entity.

7. For mind is the builder.  This keep ever before thee - the attitude of mind.  If you expect to make a failure who else is going to expect you to succeed?  It must be within yourself.  For as has been indicated, think not who will come from heaven or who will come from over the seas to tell you, but know it is within thine own heart, thine own conscience. For thy body is indeed the temple of the living God.  And if ye choose Him, His Son as thy companion, as thy ideal - if He be with thee, who can be against thee?  And what does it matter if they are!  They are naught!  For with Him all things were made possible that have come into material experience, whether in mind, in gas, in matter or materiality.  All come through the knowledge that is in Him. Thus as you associate with Him, as ye associate ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]] with Him, ye grow in [[Grace|grace]], in knowledge, in understanding.

8. And as He has given, "In all thy getting, my Son, get understanding."  This is putting proper emphasis in the proper places, and do not become sidetracked by things that would pertain to material or spiritual alone, or things of the body or things of the heavenly force.  For you grow to heaven, you don't go to heaven.  It is within thine own conscience that ye grow there.  For there first must come peace and harmony within thy [[Purpose|purpose]], thy ideal, thy hopes, thy desires.  Thy wishes even must be in harmony with thy ideal if you would make the experience in the earth of value to thee.

9. [[Astrology|Astrological]] urges are oft conflicting in the entity's experience.  For there are those things which appeal, and then the entity finds that it becomes doubtful.  Hence the great injunction to find in self first the [[Ideal|ideals]] - spiritual, mental, material; and not just hearsay.  Put them down in black and white.  Put them down in the three columns, and you'll rub them out daily - if you study it - and replace them; and then find that they all will be in one column.  For the Lord, thy God is one.  But put them down.  Put names to them.  For in names we give limitations, to set metes and bounds.  And as ye grow, these limitations must be expanded.

10. Do that.

11. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, while not great in number these have been quite varied and bring to the entity's experience those opportunities - if there is the use of same in those directions in which there are the latent and manifested urges.

12. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity during the early settlings in the land, but when there had come those expressions of missions, sayings, ghosts, activities that bespoke of spirits.

13. These are things, and yet they take literal form at times - and become very active.  These disturb the body, in physical, in mental or spiritual things.

14. Thus the injunction to analyze self, to set down the ideals and ideas, to know what you believe and who is the author.

15. The name then was Walker Smyth.  The entity gained, the entity lost.  Hence the injunction to be mindful of judgments and to be just as patient with others as you want others to be with you.  Be as forgiving to others as you want others to Be forgiving to you.  For unless you would forgive thy brother, that you see and know in the flesh, how may ye expect thy heavenly Father to forgive - whom you have never seen, or may ever see - unless ye prepare the way here and now.  Then, do things not to be seen of men but to be seen and judged of God.  For His judgment and His justice is right.

16. Before that the entity was in Russia, being active in the grain fields and with foods and things pertaining to bodily needs.  The entity entered into the preparation of such activities, gaining through these because of the distribution of same to the needy.

17. These brought periods of material and spiritual combats.  For the earth is the Lord's and the fullness thereof, and they that hoard same - whether it be bread, wood, gold or what - are only cheating themselves in cheating the Lord.  For thy brethren are thy obligation.  For thou art thy brother's keeper.

18. In the experience hard lines came to the entity because of the individuals who rose against the entity.

19. The name then was Kurt Kertzheimer.  From that experience we find in the present that the entity should be mindful to keep the way open for the more perfect understanding of what the Lord would do, with thee.

20. Before that the entity was among those children of promise that journeyed through the wilderness, being among the leaders or the fathers in those groups.

21. The name then was Ben-sur.  The entity was helpful to many of those who forgot the ways.  Though the entity was among those born in the wilderness, before entering into the promised land the entity became among those who were the leaders in its own family - the children of Simeon.

22. The entity gained, for he was active in those periods when the lawgiver summed up his service of the one hundred and twenty years in the preparation and the application, and the expectancies, and when the leader [[Joshua|Joshua]] - the patient [[Joshua|Joshua]], the one who followed closely in the way that would give to the individual (who would study) the life and interpretation of the Son of man.  These in the earth activity were much alike ([[Joshua|Joshua]] and [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]]) not as combative, as in the warrings, but in spirit and in [[Purpose|purpose]], in ideals, these were one.

23. Thus the entity gained much.  Thus may ye use the Son of man, [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], as the ideal in the present, and find a new meaning - if there is the studying and the paralleling of the life of Jesus and of Joshua.

24. Before this the entity was in [[Egypt|Egypt]] when activities were crystallized in regard to vocational preparation.

25. The entity was among the Natives that were set in positions attending to the minerals, the mines and the cares of same.

26. Hence these things become a part of the entity's desires and hopes in the present.  These may be used aright, these may be applied in the experience.

27. The name then was Shap-ast-an.

28. As to the abilities of the entity - these are only limited by the desires and purposes, if there is the correct choosing of the ideals.

29. Then study to show thyself approved unto thy ideal, a workman not ashamed rightly dividing the words of truth, and keeping self from condemnation - of others or of self.

30. Ready for questions.

31. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
