# TEXT OF READING 5610-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[5610-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/31/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13949

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of July, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5610].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Sarah Hesson and Mrs. [5610].]

Time of Reading                              417 - 22nd St., 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            Va. Beach, Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, as we find, there is a great improvement in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  As we find in the auditory forces, the conditions show that form of treatment and the breaking up of the lesions in the manner as had been outlined for the CORRECTION of same, and with the proper treatments to give the nerve and muscular forces their proper DIRECTION in the resuscitating forces in the direction should bring about a near perfect, normal condition in this direction.  These, then, we would keep in that manner as has been outlined.

2. In the general condition as has been indicated in other portions of the system, these ALSO show for improvements, and WE would give - the whole condition shows great improvement. Keep that outlined.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Have the two finger operations been successful?
(A)  Been very successful, and only need that continued use of the manipulative forces as given, to prevent adhesions or lesions from forming again, so that there is LITTLE or no scar tissue, or that the Eustachian tubes, or the connecting forces or directions to same are kept in alignment - see?

4. (Q)  Is Dr. Richardson treating ears and giving [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments correctly?
(A)  As has been OUTLINED, these are on the improve.  If they had been wrong we would have told it!

5. (Q)  How often should they be given?
(A)  Two to three times each week.  The treatments should be given to keep the alignments for the conditions as have been altered or changed, see?

6. (Q)  Why are noises in left ear worse than ever at times, also cracking in ears when swallowing?
(A)  This the retraction, or the reflection of the conditions as are being broken up, and from the very NATURE OF the condition PRODUCE THAT reaction!

7. (Q)  Will the noises disappear, also my hearing be entirely restored?
(A)  As soon as the conditions - We must remember, there are nerve ENDS that MUST form in their proper relationship and connections to MAKE a perfect auditory reaction.  When these have had the opportunity, and the incentive through those connections, and the creating of the better physical conditions in the body, we will have a normal reaction from same.

8. (Q)  Will it be necessary for body to continue treatments from a doctor in N.Y.?  How soon?  Or if so, when?
(A)  Not if it will wait until it gets these conditions properly adjusted!  Of course, if it goes along off without them, and just do anyway, it will be necessary!

9. (Q)  What percentage of hearing will I have?
(A)  What percent does it desire!

10. (Q)  Has the body been subjected to too much sun treatment?
(A)  This may be given too much, or too much may be taken. So far, there has not been damage done.

11. (Q)  How would you suggest this be taken?
(A)  From twenty to thirty minutes twice a day.

12. (Q)  Is diet, also eliminations, satisfactory?
(A)  These are very good.  There are times, or conditions that arise at times, that it will be necessary to alter something in the diet, or to have the activities to keep the balance in the eliminating system.  In the present, these are very good.

13. (Q)  Any further suggestions for treating dark spots in eyes?
(A)  Keep that as has been outlined, and that is being applied for same.

14. (Q)  Any further suggestions for treatment for this body?
(A)  Follow those lines as has been given, and as is being outlined for the body.

15. (Q)  How long should the body take treatments from Dr. Richardson?
(A)  Until the condition clears itself and the body has the opportunity to ADJUST itself TO those conditions as HAVE been and ARE necessary for the proper relationships and connections.  These can be told BEST BY the reaction of same in the body, see?

16. That is all the questions.

17. We are through with this reading.
