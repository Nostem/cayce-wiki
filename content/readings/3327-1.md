# TEXT OF READING 3327-1 M 20 (Army Air Corps Cadet)

**Reading [[3327-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/1/1943  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 10756

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) [3327]''s parents, Helen Brown, Katherine Hardy and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.     [[Texas|San Antonio]], [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3327].

3. Now as we find, while the body is very good in most respects, there are disturbances that - without there being some assistance given in the soft tissue of face, nasal passage or the second antrums - may cause a great deal of disturbance; that may bring conditions upsetting the abilities of the body through the sensory forces to continue with these activities.

4. For, these in the present are beginning to affect the Eustachian tubes.  These in turn would cause the inner ear to become affected, as well as producing pressures upon the optic forces.

5. These are the specific disturbances as we find them with this body.

6. As to administrations we would suggest for aid, - we would use the heat treatment that may be had through the Elliott machine - at least one hundred and eighty degrees of the heat.  Use this about every other day for three to four weeks.

7. Also we would relax osteopathically those ganglia from the 3rd cervical to the base of the brain.

8. Thus we may relieve these disturbances, prevent deeper infections and destroy all of the sources of the plethoric condition that closes and thus holds those tendencies for formation of catarrhal disturbance through the soft palate, the antrum, the Eustachian and the ear area.

9. Do these and we will have better conditions for this body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Can this Elliott machine treatment be obtained in or near this locality?
(A)  There are three in [[Texas|San Antonio]]!

12. (Q)  Any suggestion about what causes this trouble?
(A)  AS indicated, catarrhal condition in soft tissue of the inner antrum.

13. (Q)  If possible, would it be advisable to move him from the immediate surroundings?
(A)  Only if necessary for the carrying out of these applications suggested.  For, the injections will NOT allay these troubles.

Do that, as indicated.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Mother Copy to Ass'n file
