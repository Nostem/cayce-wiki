# TEXT OF READING 2955-1 F 64

**Reading [[2955-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/6/1943  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 10223

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office, of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2955], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her son, [...], through Mr. [1156].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, [2955].

3. As we find, the disturbing conditions that cause the greater distress are the lack of the vitality in the nerve forces of the body to keep the resistances in the reactions through the locomotory centers of the body.

4. This, we find, is not from those disturbances ordinarily considered the source of such disorders in the body, but rather from minor subluxations which have caused a suppression in the supplying of energies to the body from the assimilated conditions, or from the assimilations of the body.

5. These, then, are the disturbances as we find:

6. In some time back there was a wrench, or a suppression in the areas of the 3rd, 4th and 5th dorsals, that has gradually slowed the circulation through the arms.  This has also involved the nerve coordination between the 3rd and 4th cervical, with the nerve supplies and the bursa in the areas in the ganglia to the lower part of the lumbar axis.

7. Hence we find a slowing of the circulation, as combined from these subluxations, and the depleting of those pressures in the blood supply, as to cause insufficient activity to the nerve and blood force to the extremities of the body.

8. These, then, exist:  There is a slowing of the circulation in heart, liver, lungs and kidneys.  This tends to form poisons, or toxic conditions, that alter or change about; slowing the eliminations through the alimentary canal.

9. In the specific conditions it produces tiredness to the body, the acute reactions in organs of the sensory system; the reflexes to the arms and hands, the lack of the ability to grasp or to hold things.

10. We find in the lumbar a heaviness across the lower portion of the sacral area and the limbs, the hips, the knees and the feet at times giving disturbance or troubles.

11. These are the conditions as we find with this body.

12. Also there has been a slowing up in the functioning of the organs throughout the system.

13. As we find, in making administrations, these we would apply:

14. We would have at least eight to ten [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, but have these only once a week.  This will not be in agreement with most of the osteopaths, but do it in this manner if you would have the best results.  These would be once a week, say on Tuesdays.

15. Also once a week, - say on Fridays, - we would have a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] by a [[Massage Therapy|masseuse]]; using the Fume Baths with Witchhazel; not the dry heat cabinet, you see, but the wet heat - from the fumes of Witchhazel in the cabinet and settling over the body.  Following such a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]], [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body with a little pine oil in the water that may be used in the rub along the spine and over the body.

16. Along with the massage, it would be well to have at least one or two [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations - during the period of taking these, you see.

17. The corrections osteopathically are to be made in the areas indicated; especially in the upper dorsal areas, and then coordinate the cervicals; then the correction in the lumbar and sacral areas, coordinating the upper dorsals and cervicals with those corrections.

18. And these, as we find, especially with one of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments and one of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments with massage each week, for at least ten weeks, should bring much better conditions for the body.

19. Take one on Tuesdays, the other on Friday, or that ratio. DO NOT take them, for this body, on the same day.

20. Do use the Radio-Active Appliance to cause rest for the body.

21. Do take the combination of vitamins such as the Purity Cross brand.  While this is quite a combination, if taken properly it will be very beneficial.  Take one each day for about three days in succession, then leave off for three days. Then next take one on one day and leave off for the next. Then take one each day for three days, leave off three, and so on.  Preferably take these in the evening, when ready to retire.

22. Do keep up the eliminations.

23. In the diet, have plenty of the whole grain cereal; plenty of citrus fruits, but not at the same meal with the cereals.

24. Eat plenty of liver, fish and fowl.  These should be taken some each week, two or three times a week.

25. Complying with these, we will make better forces for this body, [2955].

26. Ready for questions.

27. (Q)  What causes the nervous stomach condition?
(A)  Lack of proper assimilation and, from various centers of the body - through the hypogastric and pneumogastric, the calling for supplies for the body when there isn't sufficient supply.  Thus we have a nervous indigestion.

Do these, and we will make for better conditions for this body.

28. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] and physiotherapist who carries out the suggestions) Copy to Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance, which may be ordered from The Appliance Co., Atten. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va.

Please let us have the name of the osteopath and physiotherapist you choose for following these directions. If near Philadelphia, we would refer you to Dr. Marion Dick, 5041 Spruce St.  If near N.Y., we would refer you to Dr. Geo. N. Coulter, osteopath, 18 East 41st St., and Dr. H. J. Reilly, physiotherapist, 1250 Sixth Ave.  Either of these might refer you to someone in your vicinity.)
