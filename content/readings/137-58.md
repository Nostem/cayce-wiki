# TEXT OF READING 137-58 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-58]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/28/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 448

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretations and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137], with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to this body from time to time.  These, as we see, may be used in the way and manner as have been given for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Wednesday night, Jan. 27, or Thursday morning, Jan. 28, 1926.  Saw Southern at 116.
(A)  This, as we see, is that as has been given as regarding the advance in this stock, and, as we see, when this has advanced near this price, then be out of same - today, January 28th, see?  for we will find a falling off in these in the latter part of this week, and much lower on the early portions of next week, with many conditions changing as regarding many stocks, before the rise, which will come in the early portion of Spring, and changing of many conditions, see?

4. (Q)  Should we sell it there, or should we be out at any price today?
(A)  Sell it there, or somewhere near, so there is a profit in that as held.  Be out today, see?

5. (Q)  Saw [[California|California]] Petroleum between 33 - 5 and 6.
(A)  This again the advance as will come - today, 33 - 34 - 35 near 36.  Sell when these advances show.  Be out of these today, as WE see it, see?

6. (Q)  Will it go to these prices, 33 - 35 and 36, today?
(A)  33 - 34 - 35, see?

7. (Q)  If it does not go to these prices, should we sell it anyway?
(A)  Sell it anyway, so long as there is a profit in same.

8. (Q)  Saw some weakness in the market indicated by Woolworth. Saw that selling at 206.
(A)  This, as we see, presenting to the mind that correlation of conditions as are felt, from information as has been given the body, and from that as is experienced by entity in the conditions as are existent.  This, as we see, indicates the falling off of this individual stock.  Well, then, that today, tomorrow, this may be sold for a lower level, see? 2 - 4 for 6 points, see?

9. (Q)  Should we sell Woolworth for lower level?
(A)  Sell Woolworth for lower level.

10. (Q)  Does it mean that the break is going to begin at the end of this week, as given?
(A)  End of this week, as given, as we see it at present.

11. (Q)  Just what is indicated regarding the Kresge Stores, as given in reading [137-56] of January 25, 1926?
(A)  As given in this, with the splitting up of the stock of Kresge Stores, in the reduction of the valuation of each certificate, or giving three for one, there will come the decline in these, from thirty to forty points, see?  So, when this, as is given, in the second, third week (from present, see?); that is, 8th to 9th, 10th, 12th February, begin the sale of such stocks for lower levels for, as we see, these are conditions as are existent regarding the conditions in the Stock Exchange:

There is much uncertainty in many quarters of the various conditions, as is being produced by the many changes as are taking place.  Then, with that general condition as is induced into the affairs, as it were, of man, through the changes to take place soon in the influence in the world's condition, many changes will be felt, many conditions will fall away, and the general trend is to be a downward movement of the prices.  Some swinging very low.  Others in a dilatory sort of way or manner.  Others showing exceptional ability to hold up.  These, as we see, as have been given, in the specific conditions, and ways and manners.  Then, in those specifics, as we have seen, these are as some of the conditions to be watched, or some that will act as the criterion for the fluctuations:

Steel - U.S. Motors - General. Grains - Corn. Utilities - those as of department stores, see? (except of some individuals - sugar and such).

These will be as criterions.  That is, the manner in which they act, the general conditions under those heads will be reacting in the same manner.

12. We are through for the present.
