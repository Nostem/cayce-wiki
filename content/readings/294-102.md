# TEXT OF READING 294-102 M 50

**Reading [[294-102]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/28/1927  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 2061

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of October, 1927.  Reading was volunteered immediately following [[Dreams|dream]] reading 538-25, after the suggestion for closing - with the exception of "Now perfectly normal and perfectly balanced you will wake up".

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. EC:  Mother!  Yes'm - I will.  Yes'm - I'll do that right away.  Liniment - Sloan's - yes'm.  Butter good, too?

[GD's note:  EC had not been feeling well.  We took this to be advice from his mother for his health.]
