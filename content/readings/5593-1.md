# TEXT OF READING 5593-1 M 66 (Salesman)

**Reading [[5593-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/24/1929  Sex: M  Age: 66  ReadingID: 13917

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5593], via his daughter & son-in-law, Mrs. [267] and Mr. [317].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [5593] and wife [422], Mrs. [267], and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [5593], present in this room, and you will answer the questions which we will ask you regarding his business associations and relations, and questions regarding the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [5593], present in this room.  Also business associations and relations.  In considering the business associations and relations, many various phases of the body's endeavors are to be considered.  Those present relations, while there are apparently some unsavory and unsatisfactory conditions, the body will find that - with the proper consideration given the other's abilities - the associations will prove most beneficial to continue in the present relations.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is it wise and safe to place unbounded confidence in my superior, C. J. Hendryx, who is manager of our Atlanta beanch?
(A)  As given, there may be placed all confidence in these relations.

4. (Q)  Is my future secure in his hands?
(A)  As long as that same relationship exists, as has for some time, this IS secure.  There may be CHANGES in personnel, and the relationships with others, yet this - unless other conditions than those seen at present arise - would NOT alter this relation.

5. (Q)  How can I make myself more valuable to my employers, E. E. Atkins & Co.?
(A)  By the continued study of the needs of the conditions to be met, and by applying self in direction of meeting same, knowing that with what measure one metes it will be measured to them again; for, in such relations as that where one seeks to renew and continue service in the direction of being of aid, be sure that condition also seeks you - and, as this is expanded upon, there will come the knowledge and the means, and the ways, to endear and to hold self in that proper relation.  BE sincere.

6. (Q)  In the event of my resignation from my present position, what kind of work should I take up?
(A)  Why resign?

7. (Q)  Is it wise to invest in St. Regis Paper Company's stock at present?
(A)  Not at present, with the changes as will come in the new relationships that are apparent to take place.  In April or May, THEN the investment would be much better.

8. (Q)  Regarding the [[Physical Body|physical body]], what will relieve the morning headaches I so frequently have?
(A)  The better would be for the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], taken once a month, for three to four months - for this is produced by the pressure in the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus, in the poisons as accumulate from the colon - as does also this affect, sympathetically, that center from which the radiation of the auditory and [[Visions|vision]] are radiated, and these will aid the sensory reaction, or make more acute the hearing, the eyesight, the feeling, and the whole system as related to same.

9. That is all the questions.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
