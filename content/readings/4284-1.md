# TEXT OF READING 4284-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4284-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/9/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12084

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Meridian, [[Texas|Texas]], this 9th day of April, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:00 A. M. [P. M.?]                              ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions in this body.  Some are abnormal to other bodies, that to this body have become normal conditions. These that may be corrected have to do with the nervous system and with the assimilation and elimination and functioning of the circulation, especially, through the corpuscle circulation.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body:  First, in the circulation the blood, we find is low in the rebuilding properties to give the vital force necessary to meet the resistance in the system - the red blood cells being below the normal in count, the white and the lucosite [leucocyte] being predominant in number and quality.

3. The nerve forces in the system, we find the cerebrospinal forces without the perfect coordination with the sympathetic and sensory organism of the body and about the ganglia of the seventh dorsal, first and second dorsal and the cervical in the fifth and third, the [[Vibration|vibrations]] do not give to the circulation with its depleted condition, the correct incentive to eliminate properly in the system so the flow of the circulation to the organs of the sensory system becomes involved producing the condition in the functioning of the organs, the incoordinating force both in sound and in [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to make the proper disposition of used forces through the system, the pains to the head at times and the condition in eyes and ears give the physical expression of this condition.  The lesion, costal, about the seventh dorsal producing this condition has been produced by the condition in the nervous system from the upper lumbar region, see, the first condition producing this coming from the condition existing in the false pelvic region.  With the change of the [[Vibration|vibration]] and the exercise to the body, both through stimulation and through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] brought about the condition now existing in this region and gives the expression to the sensory system through the incoordination of the sympathetic, cerebrospinal and sensory system, see.

4. Through the functioning of the organs themselves in the functioning of the brain force itself proper, we find the flow of circulation produces the condition there that impressions received are not able to be physically carried out, see.  Through the organs of the sensory system, the ears suffer most with the eye giving the reflex forces through the muscle and nerve tissue governing those.  The body is easy to take cold or susceptible to congestion of the mucus functioning of the organs both of the larynx and of the head itself, see.  The lung forces are very good for the condition as exist in the blood supply.  There is a plethora condition in the cells of the lungs itself, though not organically affected.  The digestive tract is often abused with its condition of the blood supply and the functioning of the organs of the system.  The liver, the hepatic circulation, the pancreas in its functioning often becomes overcharged, see.  The liver is engorged, extending lower than normal. The kidneys, overactive in their functioning in eliminating secretions overtaxed by the condition in lymphatic circulation.

5. To give the correct incentives to the nerve forces, to carry out their functioning in this body would be first to remove the condition about the seventh dorsal by deep manipulation, see, such as that by [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, see, then to give the vibrations necessary to overcome the destructive forces of the cellular blood force in the spleen, those that would be about the eighth, ninth, seventh, sixth dorsal here, the vibrations of the electrical force, either through that of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] or of electronic forces.  Do that.

6. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, have you anything further to say that will perfect a cure in this case?
(A)  Do as we have told you.  We are through.
