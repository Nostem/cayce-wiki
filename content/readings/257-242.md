# TEXT OF READING 257-242 M 48

**Reading [[257-242]]** · Series: 250-261 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/22/1942  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 884

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.                [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those disturbing forces that have caused the anxiety.

3. We find that these conditions arise rather from a form of swelling produced in the lower portion of the duodenum, rather than in the intestinal tract itself - as yet.

4. From the conditions which have existed, naturally the anxieties are caused.

5. But this should disappear soon if there will be osteopathically those stimulations to the upper dorsal, and even to the lower cervical; the stimulations for relaxing this portion of the anatomical forces.

6. Take internally small doses of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] several times a day; three to four drops (not more than that) in a glass of water, as an intestinal antiseptic.  This would aid.

7. Have Dobbins remove these pressures in the dorsal and lower cervical, and we will find relief from these disturbances.

8. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self, Air Mail-Special (one for Dr. Dobbins) Copy to Ass'n file
