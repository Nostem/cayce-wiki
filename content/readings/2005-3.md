# TEXT OF READING 2005-3 F 8

**Reading [[2005-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/28/1942  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 8526

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother Mrs. [1947], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2005].  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  To which of the innoculations should she be submitted, if any, that are to be encouraged next month, - typhoid fever, small pox, diptheria, and whooping cough?
(A)  Whooping cough and diptheria should be those for this body.

4. (Q)  Any further suggestions about hearing?
(A)  If those suggestions are kept as we have indicated for the activities of the body, these should bring the better general and normal developments.

5. (Q)  What causes the urinary disturbance at times?
(A)  We do not find such a great disturbance.  Most of this becomes as SUGGESTIVE forces.  But if there will be the use of those properties combined, an equal combination of the [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]], massaged at least once or twice a week over the kidney AND the liver area, it will aid.  Do this at night, after a bath.

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
