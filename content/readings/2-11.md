# TEXT OF READING 2-11 M 49

**Reading [[2-11]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/8/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 12

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Here we find conditions continually on the improve, and marked changes are seen in the physical conditions and physical reactions.  The body should be in the open more, provided there is the proper shading for the eyes - so that the physical activities, physical exertions that will assist (in being in the open) in bringing about those proper coordinations or relations between the lymphatics, or the sweats of the body, that will become natural with the exercising in the open - provided he is kept much in the sun, but shading the eyes well when such is done - but the light, the heat, the activity, the radiations that are thrown off from same will be very beneficial.  Put him in a bathing suit, wet him, and put him in the sand!  provided the shades are kept over the eyes - see?  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How long before body will see improvement in sight?
(A)  He sees marked improvement now, already!  but this will continue to improve.  Let's change these conditions about, and we should see a DIFFERENCE and a better marked improvement in the next three to five weeks.

3. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments now?
(A)  Every other day.

4. (Q)  Is the proper treatment being given to the teeth at present?
(A)  These have never been properly attended to.  The drainage will assist, but - as WE have seen - that one needs REMOVING!  [See 2-9 and Reports.]

5. (Q)  What dentist can he go to, to have this tooth removed?
(A)  Any one that will look at the condition in the proper way and light and manner.  One that will cooperate with that undertaking as is being attempted to be accomplished for the body. [GD's note:  Dentist later found it.  See 2-9 Reports.]

6. That is all the questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mr. [2]'s wife
