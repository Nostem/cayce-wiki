# TEXT OF READING 4747-1 F 57

**Reading [[4747-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/12/1932  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 12718

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 12th day of September, 1932, in accordance with request made by her cousin, Mrs. [4784], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. Ernest W. Zentgraf.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and Kathleen Kenny.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [4747].

2. Now, as we find, the conditions physically are rather acute. There is a complication of disorders.

3. Under the existent circumstances and conditions that surround the body, we would not make any changes in the immediate applications for the betterment.

4. The complications are from a disturbed functioning of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] with the heart's action, and the hardening of muscular and sinew forces in extremities; yet those applications that are being made should bring some relief.  When the conditions are improved, so there may be begun those that would make for the reaching of the basis or cause of the disorders in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - that affect the distribution of elements in the making up of that assimilated, and not be detrimental to the heart's action, then we may give through these channels that which should be of aid.

5. We will find a change in the next six days.  On the ninth day we may make those suggestions for the aiding of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [4747].

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [4784]
"    " File
