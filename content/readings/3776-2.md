# TEXT OF READING 3776-2 F 67

**Reading [[3776-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/5/1923  Sex: F  Age: 67  ReadingID: 11315

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of July, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 P. M.                                          ..., Ky.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  While the conditions in the body are a great deal different from those we have had before, the condition at the present time as we see is a slowing up of the heart forces as produced by improper elimination through the system, and by congestion as is caused through portions of the body, the hindrance in the circulation in the heart forces produced by congestion over the system that as shown in the lower portion of the body where injuries have shown in the system and a shock to these that cut off elimination in the kidneys and affect this system throughout.  The other organs, the lungs, bronchials, we find much of the same forces as we have had before.

2. To adjust conditions we would have that of the [[Chiropractic|Chiropractic]] forces as applied to the body to dilate or expand through the lung forces, so contract the muscular forces in and about the solar plexus, to stimulate through the cardiac forces, the heart action, and reduce by relaxation that in the abdominal region and that, that affects the locomotories and this portion of the body.  Stimulate circulation both through this force and through that of forces as would be reached in dire necessity, or stimulate through the heart forces themselves, you see, but through the other forces would be better than through medicinal forces.  Do that.

3. (Q)  How often should the [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] treatments be given?
(A)  Necessary that they be given pretty regular until some improvement is shown, if we do something for this condition through this means.

4. (Q)  How long should she take the treatments?
(A)  Until there is no necessity.
