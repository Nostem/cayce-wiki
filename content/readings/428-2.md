# TEXT OF READING 428-2 F 46

**Reading [[428-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 3206

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [428].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [428] - this we have had before.  The conditions and changes as have been brought about in the system are very much in the position as where sediment in a container in water has been stirred up for elimination.  In the administrations of those properties as to alter or change [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system, these have aided in that - with the LITTLE of the manipulations as given with same - have only set about ready for changes.  Now, as we first outlined, there are specific centers in the cerebro-spinal system that need not only adjustment, but SUFFICIENT of manipulation and treatment as to cause a change in the direction of impulses FROM those centers as have been in the position of action of impingements.  Not as those of out of place, but muscular forces in a lateral form of the condition as to CAUSE this misdirection.  These, as we find, are most from the 9th and 10th dorsal to the upper cervicals, in this particular body.

2. Now take those of the manipulations AND adjustments as will make for the CORRECTION of these conditions, so that - as drainages are set up in system - the corrective forces, or those of the drosses as STIRRED up - will be eliminated from the system.  These, as we find, will eliminate this tendency of the tightness about the middle of the body, this fullness as appears in face, in eyes, in neck - the tendency in the throat of the too much secretion, the fullness or the heaviness in feet - these, as we find, will disappear. Be well were there applied, when the manipulations are given, those of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, centered on the 9th and 10th dorsal center, segregating the rest of it, or shade same over the centers there - for two to five minutes.  This following every OTHER treatment.  See?  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Give cause and treatment of gas in intestines.
(A)  This from acid, as comes from those repressions in the dorsal regions.

4. (Q)  Give cause and treatment of bladder disturbance.
(A)  Same cause.  Same treatment.

Do as we have given, and we will find these will disappear.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
