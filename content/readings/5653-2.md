# TEXT OF READING 5653-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[5653-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/19/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14050

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5653].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - the conditions, as we find, become more and more acute with this body.  While there is an aiding with those packs, will there be added with them the medicated ash - quarter grain at a dose every third day, and in thirty minutes following same with an application of the ultra violet light - these will prolong this present experience the greater.  Do that.

2. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n Office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/19/31 EC's letter when sending the reading:
> 
> "I am very sorry to know that you are not getting along so
> well.  We were able to get a check-up reading today, which I
> am enclosing.  This medicated ash prescribed in the reading,
> we have wired to [[Chicago|Chicago]] [to Mr. F. F. Bradley] - where it is
> made asking them to send you a package at once.  It may come
> put up in the eighth grain capsules.  If it does, you are to
> take two of them - which would be a quarter grain - every
> third day.  Don't take it in the capsule, but empty the ash
> out in water and drink it.  I don't know whether there is a
> doctor anywhere near there who has an ultra violet light or
> not, but you should have these light rays applied after each
> dose of the medicine.  Keep up the packs, and let us hear
> from you."
> 
> R2. 6/25/34 Mr. [3983]'s letter:  "Mrs. [5653] is enjoying good
> health now."
