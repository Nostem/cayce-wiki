**Reading [[2065-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/20/1939  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 8633

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of December, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through mother - Mrs. [...] new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [...], brother of Mrs. [394].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. HLC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2065].

3. Now as we find, disturbing conditions here arise from prenatal influences, - which have affected and do affect the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system as related to the reproductive forces in body, as well as in the circulation to the superficial portions of same; affecting the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] through the EXCESS of [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] reaction, and a DIMINISHING of the activities through the adrenal and the effect upon pineal.

4. These as we find arise from an insidious influence of a prenatal nature.

5. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body:

6. The blood supply indicates the disturbance in the glandular forces, especially as related to thyroid, - the activities indicated in those conditions of the growths upon portions of the body being affected by such conditions.

7. The activities as related to this, as indicated, effect the variations we have in the pulsation and as to the circulation between the upper and the lower hepatic circulation, as well as that to the heart and the lungs.

8. The activities through the nerve forces of the body have become as breaking portions, especially in the area of the vegetative nerve plexus, or from the 3rd and 4th dorsal to the upper portion of the body.

9. These as we find show reflexes in the NATURE of the disturbance finding expression IN the superficial circulation to hands, arms, and especially face, neck and head.

10. All of these, then, are arising from those disturbances indicated.

11. Applications that have been made have only been as temporary reliefs, and at the expense of some other portion of the reflex functioning system.

12. As we find, then, while it will require a little time, and patience and persistence in the application - not only the very strict reactions for portions of the system, but consistency in the mental as well as physical reactions to the disturbances - we may bring the better conditions for this body.

13. Then, first:

14. We would begin taking small quantities internally of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; one minim (drop) in half a glass of water each morning before a meal is taken.  Take for five days, leave off five days, - then take again for a whole period of at least thirty days.

15. And do not rest in bed too long of a morning.  Arise rather early and be active in the open air, in the early morning, as much as practical from the general weather reactions.

16. Begin also at the same time taking the [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]] from [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] through the low electrical forces of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  These would be taken each day for thirty minutes. The attachments would be made in this manner:

17. The small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal plexus, while the large nickel plate (through which the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] - commercial strength - would pass) would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, that is, two forefingers from the navel center toward the right side.  Make these attachments each day for thirty minutes, for THIRTY DAYS.

18. Then, after that period, have the Appliance recharged; THIS time taking through same the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Solution (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water).  The small copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar plexus, and the large nickel plate (through which the vibrations of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would pass) would be attached to the gall duct area, or the distance of the plate ABOVE the center to which the plate had been attached for the Atomidine vibrations.  The Gold Solution would be renewed at the end of fifteen treatments, you see.  Make these attachments each day for thirty minutes, for THIRTY DAYS.

19. At the same time the Gold Vibrations are begun, begin also with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections through those areas specifically of the lumbar and sacral center, 9th dorsal center, and from the 3rd and 4th cervical to the 6th and 7th dorsal; these being made in this manner:  Begin with the lower ones first, then the 9th dorsal, then in the UPPER cervical and downward to the 5th and 6th dorsals.  These would be given twice each week until twenty-six such treatments had been taken.

20. Now, the conditions through the first periods will appear to be more aggravated, - that is, the rash or the breaking on the face, the scalp and the head, these will appear more inflamed.

21. During these periods, use upon same an ointment carrying Stearate of Zinc and Cocoa Butter; a ten percent solution in two ounces of Cocoa Butter, well mixed.

22. After the first series of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, leave off the Solutions - either being taken internally or vibratorially - and take a series of Fowler's Solution of Arsenic.  Take this in a very sincere manner, but begin with one drop in a little water each day for five days, then increase to two drops each day for five days, then increase to three drops each day for five days.  Then increase to five drops each day for five days.  (Note the difference from the three to five drops!)  Then leave off entirely for at least two weeks.  Then begin again with the one drop, increasing in the same way and manner.

23. After this second series of the Arsenic is completed, then have another series of fifteen adjustments, osteopathically made.

24. During this second series of fifteen [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, take internally the one minim of Atomidine each day, and also use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] each day carrying the Chloride of Gold Solution.

25. DO NOT use electrical vibrations for the body other than through the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] in the manner indicated for carrying the vibrations of the medicinal properties into the system!  No direct electrical current!

26. In the matter of the diet, - keep more to vegetables and fruits than so much of meats or the like.  Eat all forms of vegetables, but not too much combination of such as corn, potatoes, rice or white bread, - no two of these at the same meal.  Any one of these may be taken separately, occasionally, or regularly as desired, but not two of these at the same meal.  Have plenty of leafy vegetables, and juices of fruits, as well as juices of vegetables, rather than meats.  These should form the basis of the food values for the body.

27. Do these consistently, - we will remove the causes and correct conditions.

28. Ready for questions.

29. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Osteopath, when you are ready for his treatments.  Let us know the name and address of the Osteopath you choose, so we can put him on our mailing list.

Our local druggist carries Atomidine, and if you cannot obtain it let us know and we will have it forwarded.

See article [which was enclosed] regarding the Wet Cell Appliance.)
