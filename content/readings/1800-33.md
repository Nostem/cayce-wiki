# TEXT OF READING 1800-33

**Reading [[1800-33]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/14/1941  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8147

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by Mr. Marsden Godfrey, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                        2709 Lafayette Blvd., 
11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you the Radio-Active Appliances, present in this room.  Please comment on them, advise if they are correctly made, and give suggestions for betterment.

2. EC:  These are inanimate.

3. As we find, these are well constructed, and in keeping with those suggestions made.  They are only active, to be sure, when they are connected with a body.

4. It would be well if they were dipped in a paint, or a lacquer, to the distance that the Appliance should be in the ice - whatever color is desirable; any would be well, but blue would be very good, if heavy enough, - and then red from there up.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  How far on the Appliance should the ice come?
(A)  Within an inch and a half to two inches of the top, just so that should it settle from ice in bottom of container it does not come over top of the Appliance.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Marsden Godfrey
"    " Ass'n file
