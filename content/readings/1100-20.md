# TEXT OF READING 1100-20 F 43

**Reading [[1100-20]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/9/1938  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 6307

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1100], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there have been some indiscretions as to dampness of the feet; and with a little superacidity there is a severe contraction through the bronchi and the whole of the lymph for head, throat and bronchi.

4. There needs to be a stirring of the liver, and the reduction of the inclination for temperature through a good enema.

5. And with such activities we find we should make for the ability of the body to soon adjust itself.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What should be done to stir the liver?
(A)  Take a cathartic of some nature that is acceptable to the body.  As we find, a vegetable compound is preferable to mineral for the body.

8. (Q)  What can I do to build resistance against head colds?
(A)  Keep the normal [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] and alkalinity, by occasionally taking the test with litmus paper - both from the urine and from the spittle.  Use the blue litmus, see?

When there is the inclination for [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], use any of the sodas or their derivatives (citrocarbonates) as would make for producing a better balance.  Thus we will find the colds will be eliminated.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail, Special - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
