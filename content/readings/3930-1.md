# TEXT OF READING 3930-1 F (AGE ?)

**Reading [[3930-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1920 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1920  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11567

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Selma, Ala. (?), this 3rd day of July, 1920.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno. [3930] and others (?).]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Now these are the conditions as we find them existing in this body, [3930] here in this room.

2. We have conditions existing in this body that have to do with the physical, mental, and spiritual bodies of this body.  The conditions that we have to do with deal with the body from the time of conception, through the spiritual side of the life of the body, even to this time.  These conditions have to do with the physical and mental bodies as we find them at the present time.

3. These are the conditions that we have to do with in this body that are abnormal.  There has been, from time beginning, a lesion that was formed at the left side of the spinal column at the sixth cervical, which causes an undeveloped portion of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], giving to it, or taking from it, the life which goes to make up every portion of the body as exercised through this part.  This condition disturbs the spinal nerve centers, and also affects the lymphatics.  The body in an attempt to form a union between the nerve forces lying dormant and with the live active forces of the body, produce a hallucination of the brain, causing a spasmodic condition of the vertebrae at this point, leaving portions of the body in a lifeless, dormant, and inactive condition, save through the spiritual forces of the body.

4. To relieve the abnormal conditions existing in this body, which can be done in part, is to remove the lesion at the left side of the sixth vertebrae through absorption and stimulation, and form a union between the inactive forces and the rest of the body.  To assist in eliminating these conditions in the body, we would first give into the system this -

[[Iodine|Iodide]] of [[Baking Soda|Soda]]..................2 grains,
Dover's Powders.................3 grains,
Red Prussiate of Potassium......1 grain.

5. No other bromides will be needed in the system, let these take the place.  This to be given every other day for three or four weeks.  After this time we begin with a treatment known as the Chiropractic treatment to the Spinal Column, and by this treatment stimulate to action the forces lying dormant.  By this treatment we find that the body will be greatly relieved, equilibrium will be established, and the body will be brought to a normal condition.
