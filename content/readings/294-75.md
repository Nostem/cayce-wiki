# TEXT OF READING 294-75 M 49

**Reading [[294-75]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/23/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2034

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. House, Tommy House, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you, and you will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of this body, EC.  This we have had here before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]] come in and through those channels as have been given for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].  These should be taken and used in the way and manner as has been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. For oft [[Dreams|dreams]], [[Visions|visions]], present to the individual those conditions existent in the mental forces or body-mind. Through mental and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] they give that experience, either in emblematical or direct way and manner, in which many changes may be seen, many conditions averted, and many advantages taken of experiences seen in same.

5. Ready for dream.

6. (Q)  Sunday Morning, 8/22/26, regarding Miss [288] and my father getting married.  [See 288-15 on 4/27/26 warning her.]
(A)  The conditions presented in this and in that which follows, appear uncomely; presenting, then, to the forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], that through such associations, through such relations, there might come those conditions that should be warned of, that only good may come from such sources, for "Let not thy good be evil-spoken of."  Take the warning then.

7. (Q)  Monday Morning, 8/23/26, HLC going away to school and coming back married to Miss [4586].
(A)  In this there is presented other conditions, as is seen - lessons, learning, associations, and close associations.

In this emblematical way and manner, then, it is seen that through those channels, if these were taken in the right way and manner, and through the way and manner as presented, there may come those associations, those relations that would bring about good and well-being for many who may be interested in such work as the entity is engaged in.  [See 4586-1, Par. R4 in re HLC entertaining Miss [4586] in VB.]

Take THIS warning and follow therein.

8. We are through for the present.
