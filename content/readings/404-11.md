# TEXT OF READING 404-11 F 52

**Reading [[404-11]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/1/1942  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 3035

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [404], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [404], Esther Wynne, Frances Y. Morrow and Helen Godfrey.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [404] born October 25, 1889, in Twillingate, Newfoundland.  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [404].

3. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] in such a manner that it may be drawn into a chart as a beneficial influence for the entity, signs and symbols may be indicated.  Much will depend upon the artist's concept, in making the more beautiful figures and designs, symbols and signs, that are taken from activities physical, mental and spiritual; to indicate activities through varied periods.

4. For, an [[Aura|aura]] chart is the expression of the manifested efforts of an entity in its relationships to others in the material plane; thus indicating characteristics, intents and [[Purpose|purposes]] of an entity.

5. For this entity, - first we would draw that to indicate a piece of parchment, or paper torn from a record.  And all of these emblems, these signs, would be upon this piece - as indicated in the jagged outline around, and these upon same.

6. In the center of this, at the bottom, put a view of a mountain, with the circle as of the roadway AROUND the peak, or to the peak - in stone color, or the like.  Much of the mountain peak would be verdant, with the top capped with snow.

7. About two-thirds the way up indicate the altar, and as the dwelling place - this in gray stone, but with the fire upon same.  No figure would be about the altar.

8. This would indicate that period of activity when there was being sought safety from the flooded waters from [[Atlantis|Atlantis]], in what became the Incan land.

9. Upon either side - indicating the symbols of the entity's activity:

10. In the lower right corner put the cross of the compass and the square, - this in gold; the compass crossing the square, to be sure one pointing up, the other down.

11. About this put a red star, on this the blue side, and the symbol or sign of Virgo.

12. Upon the left put the compass and the square; this in blue, and the gold star above same (these, to be sure, not too large, but as a balance for the whole drawing).

13. The signs here would be Sagittarius, only two of these - and these upon the points - that is, Virgo and Sagittarius, or near the points where the square crosses the compass.

14. These would indicate the period, the activities, and the manner of relationships to the associations through the period.

15. Next above this put a scene.  In the distance would be seen a fringe of palm trees, with the Nile or the water course, with the pyramid and the sphinx in the distance.

16. This would indicate the period, and the activities indicated upon either side of same; Mercury upon the left - in white and gold; with the sign of Aries or the ram's horn this in three figures - black, not too heavy, indicating the severity, or the activity of the individual in its mental aspirations towards those activities through the period of [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] influence.

17. Upon the right put the symbol of Venus, - this in blue; while the activity indicated by the sign of Gemini would be in three figures, top and at either side.

18. These would be interpreted as the place AND the activities through the mental AND characteristic forces innate and manifested by the entity through the experience.

19. Above this, indicating the central portion of the chart, we would put - in the center - the brazen altar, as indicated in the wilderness, and the fire upon same; this, to be sure, in red - with the incense or the smoke above same.

20. Upon the left side of this would be the candelabrum with the seven lights, or the seven spirits, or the [[Seven Churches|seven centers]], or the light to which the entity in its activity gained. This, to be sure, would be in gold - save the candle and the flame from same, the candle being white and the flame coral red.

21. Upon the right side of the altar put the cross with the brazen [[Serpent (Eden)|serpent]] on same; this, to be sure, in the wood or oaken color, - [[Serpent (Eden)|the serpent]] in the brazen color.

22. Upon the left - as the activity - would be the sign of Venus; this in green but light.  About same would be the three phases of the moon - the left side the new moon, the right side the old moon, in the center above the full moon; indicating the figure or the face in each symbol.

23. Upon the right side, or beyond the figure of the brazen [[Serpent (Eden)|serpent]] (and these should be central figures vignetted into the whole experience), put the sign of the Sun, and about same (centralize this with the white figure and it bright, though not gold in color - the rest of the figure) put the symbols of Pisces, three in number; these in the gray-blue figure.

24. These would indicate the period, the activities and the interpreting of same in the experience of the entity.

25. The figure above this would be the three-masted schooner, indicating the Norse land and the venturing and daring of those about the entity; indicating a considerable amount of sea.  And upon the sides of same put, in the one the moon in the new phase - this upon the left, and this in silver and gold, edged the outline of the face; and upon the right side the old moon in silver and outlined in gold.  The boat would be in gray and blue, with the sea-green and the clouds. The clouds would be fleecy, not the sheep but the fleecy cloud.

26. As to the attainment, above, - the central figure would be the heavy cross; this not set direct but upon an angle, not too much to the left or the right, but this the golden cross, with the crown and the wreath or garland of roses in a wreath about same, - extending from - though not touching the top of the cross, towards the crown upon either side of same. Let there be HIS star in gold above the cross.

27. This would indicate the attaining of the entity in the cross, in the crown as may be attained by the complete trust in Him.

28. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self (two copies; one for Miss Wynne)
"    " Ass'n file
