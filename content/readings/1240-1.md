# TEXT OF READING 1240-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[1240-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/14/1931  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6812

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his room in the Hotel Victoria, [[New York City|New York City]], this 14th day of August, 1931, in accordance with request made through Mr. [279].

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. A. C. Preston and wife, [279], and others.]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
9:15 A. M. Daylight Savings Time.                  ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, as we find with this body, conditions are very serious.  There is that infection which attacks the locomotories of the body in their actions, and a contraction to the cerebro-spinal nervous system proper, affecting naturally all activities in the system as related TO the cerebro-spinal action, either as to brain forces or as to locomotion.  While the central nervous system is not so much under distress, there are inflammations in some at the present.

2. With the applications as are being made, these SHOULD be effective; save there ARE those conditions that there should NOT be as much of the activities ABOUT the BODY as there has recently been, for the responses of the body mentally under strain is not WELL for that inflammation as occurring in the central system.  We would not change from what is being applied.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is this a special type of the disease now prevalent?
(A)  Of A disease now prevalent.  It is a type of same.

4. (Q)  Is there any special treatment that we can offer the medical science for the assistance of this prevalent condition of the specific disease?
(A)  That possibly may BE, but it would require working from conditions before there were applications made; for, as is given, all is being done that may be, will they prevent too much activity - else we will find conditions cut off.

5. (Q)  What is the medical term for this disease?
(A)  Paralyses - Infantile.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. Bulova
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
