# TEXT OF READING 1835-1 F 59

**Reading [[1835-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/2/1939  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 8205

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1835], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1602] and Mrs. [1833].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Jefferson Hosp., 
Time of Reading                            9th & Sansom Sts., 
10:40 to 10:50 A.M. Eastern Standard Time. Philadelphia, Pa.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body - and those conditions that are disturbing forces and influences with same.

3. As we find, rather than a great deal of material or physical application, the needs here are the care and the attention as may be given by those about the body, - in making such an intercession as to arouse within the body-consciousness the relations of the body-mental and spiritual to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or Influences; and the desire on the part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to so apply same in this material experience as to become and to be a channel through which this consciousness, this awareness may be helpful in the experiences of others.

4. With this awareness aroused, through those about or acquainted with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], or who are capable and willing to show that true application of that as He gave, "Love one another," these as we find are those influences, those forces

that may bring the better physical, mental and spiritual forces about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present, - [1835].

5. Then, let those who care for same use every effort to bring about this, in the experience of this body in the present.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
