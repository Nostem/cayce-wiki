# TEXT OF READING 487-13 M 9

**Reading [[487-13]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/29/1927  Sex: M  Age: 9  ReadingID: 3572

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by his father.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for improvement of same, and you will tell if the body is responding properly to the treatments as are being accorded.

2. EC:  Yes, we find the body here in a better condition than that as we have had before; yet there is still that need of the deep manipulation along the lower portion of the cervicals to make the necessary response to all the face, neck and head nerve responses for the body, for the body needs that of the perfect coordination in centers radiating to the upper portion of the body.  This will respond faster than through such manipulation, and the preventing of cold and congestion by the diet and by the activities of the body. Do that.

3. (Q)  Is it necessary to give the manipulations further down the spine?  [By M. L. Richardson, D.O.  See 487-13, Par. R2.]
(A)  Just as given, not only the neck and head but to the lower portion of the cervicals and even to the solar plexus radiation - for eliminations are as necessary throughout the whole system as in the upper portion of the body, for eliminations are from those conditions as are known - through respiratory system, kidneys, liver, and the rest of the body. The natural tendency, as has always been shown, the kidneys will have to be looked after.  The tendency is towards those conditions, see, of disruption through this channel.  Hence stimulation for coordination throughout eliminations of body will assist even in that of the radiating forces to all of the lymphatic circulation.  Gentle massage over the WHOLE body will assist the lymphatics, see?

4. We are through for the present.
