# TEXT OF READING 5555-4 F 51

**Reading [[5555-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/7/1929  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 13838

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Jacobs, Mrs. [[Rome|Rome]], Drs. Berger, Hansen and Parker, and Mr. Blanchard.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5555].  The body is on the improve, the physical conditions of the body show better coordination.  The eliminations and the ability of the system to dissolve or to assimilate should be kept in much the same direction as has been begun.  These conditions, as have been given, may be added to or taken from, as the changes come about in the system, see?  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What causes extreme weakness on moving about?
(A)  That's a very foolish question for one studying the case and following it!  for it is as this:  When there has been BUILDED, as it were, a DAM in the system, or in the stream - and you remove it - would it be weak?  or would it be just as heavy and as strong?  See?  There has been the weakened condition of the system, that those forces as go to make up the tissue or the cell tissue of the system may be builded - from the PROPER correction - these were broken down by the non-activities of the digestive forces, or the juices in the system, and these altered.  As these were broken down, NATURALLY the result was to take from the system the strength, that the vitality may be builded in the PROPER direction, see?  Then, as these are followed - just as has been given that these should be followed, along in the line as has BEEN outlined for it - when it becomes necessary to add to the system for the alimentary canal to be eliminated, add!  As it isn't necessary, decrease the amount!  As the stimuli is necessary, add!  As it isn't necessary, decrease! We have the proper directions!  Let's keep them!  Don't block them with something else!

3. (Q)  What causes pain in face at times?
(A)  The reaction from that of the 5th cervical center. Study it there and you will see!

4. (Q)  Outline specific diet.

(A)  This should be followed in the course of that necessary to break down those conditions as have been builded as barriers, and to build up that as will add strength and vitality without adding bulk and fat.  Do that.  We are through.

Copy to Dr. Berger
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Son
