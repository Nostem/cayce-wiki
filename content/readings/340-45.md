# TEXT OF READING 340-45 F 54

**Reading [[340-45]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 2637

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [340], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   ... Ave., 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - As we find, there needs to be more of the properties that have been heretofore indicated, in the combination of the Ginseng, Wild Ginger and the like. These will purify the conditions through the intestinal system, as well as take away those reactions or regurgitations that cause the tendency for a cough. [See 340-23.]

3. It will also relieve those tendencies for pressures upon the ends of the nerve system.

4. Take occasionally the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath and the rubs afterwards with Peanut Oil.  [See 340-44.]

5. Do this for this body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What causes frequent headaches?
(A)  From the intestinal system.

8. (Q)  Would Vitamin B tablets be better for me than the Adiron I am now taking?  If so, what kind and how often?
(A)  As we find, we need to clear the system somewhat before either of these would be responsive in the body.  THEN we would take the B-1 Complex tablets, see?

9. (Q)  How may I best obtain regular elimination?
(A)  Serutan would be very good for this body, provided it is combined at times with the agar and the oils; that is, these alternated at times, see?

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(11/3/42 GD's note:  A copy was enclosed of Ginseng formula 340-23.)
