# TEXT OF READING 3710-1 F 42

**Reading [[3710-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/3/1926  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 11216

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3710] via Mrs. [4691].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those conditions that are abnormal with the body that may be assisted and helped a great deal, and by care, persistence, and by the body keeping eliminations as near normal as possible, bring much good for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. Now, these are conditions as we find them with this body:

5. The general physical conditions are very good, save where there has come an obstruction in cell tissue in the lower portion of the tongue, and this produces that which causes the distress to the body in the mental way and manner, and the coughing, hard breathing, and the existing disturbance at times in the intestinal system.  Hence we will find that it will be necessary to keep those conditions for the body that will keep the system in as near the true antiseptic system as is possible - that is, not allowing any drosses from the system to accumulate in any of the intestinal tract, and not allowing any of the discharge from phlegm as thrown from the affected parts to cause any irritation in any other portion of the body.

6. The diet, the general exercise of the body as has been kept for the system, is very good.  Those properties as have been given the body to cause rest will ONLY cause rest, but these weaken the condition in system, and when these other properties are used in the sufficient manner to give ease, keep away from these sedatives as much as is possible, though the body need not allow itself to become racked with pain to the point where it cannot endure the condition, for the mental and physical agony both would be severe, straining on the system, and produce toxins in the blood and in the general system that would prove detrimental to the body, but first we would use this:

7. Use the heavy [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] ever across the solar plexus, see?  At least five minutes each day rest, lying prone, and lay pad across the throat, see?  Just about where the [[Adam and Eve|Adam]]'s apple would be on man, or at the lower portion of the tongue, see?

8. Take Petrolagar continually - that is, tablespoonful once to twice each day, using the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] Petrolagar, see?

9. Prepare an inhalant of this nature and character, which will relieve this cough, and which will reduce the irritation and assist the appliance that [[Radium|Radium]] gives to the body to carry out disseminating the conditions, allowing the body to bring about better resistance in the body:

10. To four (4) ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol....................15 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp..........5 minims,
Canadian Balsam................5 minims,
Benzoin........................3 minims,
Tolu in Solution..............40 minims.

Dissolve or place in this - in this carrier that it will dissolve one-sixtieth (1/60th) of a grain of Heroin, see?

11. Inhale this whenever the body feels the tendency to cough. Inhale through the mouth.  Keep solution in a glass stoppered container, at least three times the size of the quantity of solution.  Shake solution before it is inhaled inhaling only the gas as forms from the fumes from this, see?

12. Do that, and we will find we will bring better conditions to this body.

13. The condition, as is seen, is not WHOLLY malignant, for it sluffs off, and may be removed by absorption, provided the system keeps the resistance as will prevent this from becoming set up in a form where the condition builds within itself.  A broken tissue and irritation at present. Laceration that has become self-infected.

14. We are through for the present.
