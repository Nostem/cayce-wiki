# TEXT OF READING 4691-3 F 56

**Reading [[4691-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/7/1925  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 12641

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4691].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At her store, on the 
Time of Reading                        South side of square, 
11:15 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.       ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.  You will answer any questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find the body in many ways shows the improvement in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of same.  While there are many conditions not entirely satisfactory, and many that cause distresses to the body at times, the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are being created in the system by the applicator and the medicinal properties are showing the effect on the system and assisting same.

3. We would, to assist the medicinal properties in having their proper effect, and to assist the eliminations and [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are set in motion by the action of [[Radium|radium]] forces in the system, keep the intestinal tract nearer open by taking sufficient quantities of the mineral oils (Usoline preferred), and by taking enemas necessary to cause evacuation from the intestinal tract.  We would use higher enemas, gradually increasing this until we can form in the system those incentives for evacuation at a normal, nominal, time, see?  These should be taken at least once each day, keeping the properties in system as have been given, being careful of the diet, that we keep in the right [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] for the rebuilding in the system.  Keep these up.  Paying attention and care to that as given, and we will bring the better conditions for this body.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is the condition of the heart and what causes the distress to the body in that region?
(A)  This is rather that of the pressure created by the return [[Vibration|vibrations]] on the pneumogastric plexuses, than organic heart condition.  The heart condition as is produced is the lack of nerve coordination.  Hence the vibration from [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], and the evacuation necessary from intestinal tract, the same as is shown in the muscular and nerve condition in the pelvic organs where distress is caused by the kidney in its functioning.  These, as we see, are being assisted. Hence the rest that the body needs at times for same to contract the normal conditions.  These, we see, have been of long durations and are rather effects of conditions in nerve and muscular system than of organic functionings, see?

5. (Q)  What causes the hoarseness through the summer?
(A)  This, as we see, is produced by the congestion as is caused in the nerve system that supply the pulmonaries and vibrates with the muscular forces as produce the in-breathing and the ex-breathing, or inhalation and exhalation, see? This also is seen in capillary circulation, system attempting to produce an equilibrium.  Hence the splotches as shown over exposed parts of the body, see?  system trying to throw out poisons.
