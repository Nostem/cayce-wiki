# TEXT OF READING 420-5 M 61

**Reading [[420-5]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/15/1941  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 3170

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [420], through wife, Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Myra H. O''Dell and mother.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there have been improvements; yet there has been a series since we have had same here when there were some setbacks, - owing first to some cold, some strain on the body, and not full or deep enough adjustments in the areas indicated.

4. We would not change the applications suggested, save that the body be a little more persistent - and a little more careful about getting the feet wet.

5. Be mindful of the diet.

6. And we should continue to now make for more improvements.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should I take more [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  These should have been a little deeper.  Hence we would have not too much of a stimulating but more of a setting up of drainages and eliminations, see?  Give these a little deeper.

9. (Q)  What should be done for the pain in left arm?
(A)  This should be removed by the adjustments and the precautions as to eliminations.

10. (Q)  What is the pain in left side of chest and shoulder?
(A)  Congestion from the cold that has existed.

11. (Q)  What should be done for the worn out feeling?
(A)  REST!  Or, the precautions as to the general conditions.

Also we would take ADIRON, especially through the rest of the cold or damp weather.  This will materially aid in giving more vitamins, more vital energies and forces for the body. Take one each morning, one at noon, and one at night, see - with the meals.

12. (Q)  Are the vitamin capsules given by the Company where I am employed beneficial to my body?
(A)  We would use ADIRON in the place of these, for this body.

13. (Q)  What can be done for better sight?  Do glasses need changing?
(A)  There should be a little bit more precaution about keeping the glasses clean, or the better exercises about the head and neck when the adjustments are made, as well as the head and neck exercises.  They do not need changing as yet.

14. (Q)  Are corrections needed in kidneys?
(A)  None other than may be done by correcting of the general conditions indicated.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Extra sheet was enclosed for Dr. Hudgins [F.C. Hudgins, Jr., D.O.].)
