# TEXT OF READING 294-82 M 49

**Reading [[294-82]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/29/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2041

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 29th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by Edgar Cayce himself.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the morning of 9/29/26, regarding a [[Dreams|dream]], the interpretation of same, and talk with [228], etc.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this.

2. EC:  Yes - this, as we see, is the condition as presented in same:

3. Oft has [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] felt that communication should be established with that entity, because of and through that association, that influence which has ever dwelt between individuals as seen from the earth's plane.  This has passed as a [[Dreams|dream]] rather than as action.  In action, then, it would bring more satisfactory conditions to each.  Then act.  [See Life Rdg. 228-2.]

4. We are through.
