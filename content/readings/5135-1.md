# TEXT OF READING 5135-1 F 33 (Stenographer, Catholic)

**Reading [[5135-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 13268

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5135], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [2602].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for the help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, these are unusual circumstances which disturb the better equilibrium of this body.

3. As we find, there is a subluxation which exists in the 3rd, between the 3rd and 4th, and some adhesions or lesions in the 2nd of the dorsal segments.  These have deflected the circulation to face, head, ears and nose especially.  For there is some infection, through the irritation produced by the hindrance of the lymph circulation, through the soft tissue and in the area of bone structures; that is more of a gristle than bone infection; which has caused period when there is swelling, irritation, breaking out on face almost as if it were solid with eczema.

4. These are from poor circulation, and the carrying of infection through portions of the body causes an upset throughout.

5. Do at least once each day have packs over nose and face with at least three or four thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

6. Do have the short-wave electrical treatments.  These should be about once a week, yet we would have corrections neuropathically or osteopathically in the area where the subluxation exists.  These should be about twice or three times a week.  Do not use the short-wave of a greater strength than three amperes.  These should be over the area where there are the hindrances in the circulation to the face and head.  These should not be of longer periods than about twenty minutes, or less time in the beginning.

7. Do keep close to a diet consisting of less meats; though fish and fowl may be taken in moderation.

8. Do these things and we will make better conditions for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Would a change of climate be beneficial?
(A)  This would be beneficial, but if you'll keep your hands from these conditions on the face, and do those things indicated, we will make better conditions for body.

11. (Q)  Do you recommend the continued use of nose drops?
(A)  NO!  These have been the partial cause of the infection.

12. (Q)  Would you suggest a change of diet?
(A)  Only as indicated.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Rathbun) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 1/12/44 EC's letter:  "I have a letter from Mrs. [2602]
> including an application for membership for yourself and the
> request for a Physical Reading...  I appreciate your
> interest, and I hope you may ever thank Mrs. [2602] for her
> introducing you to the work..."
> 
> B2. 4/14/44 Letter:  "...I shall be at my place of work ...,
> Conn., during the reading period, which time I will keep in
> [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]] as you suggested.  Following is a list
> of questions [in addition to those at end of 5135-1] I should
> like to have answered, if possible:  (1) What is the cause of
> my nose trouble?  (2) Do you recommend an operation?
> (3) Please make other suggestions which might help to improve
> my nose condition.  (4) What is cause of very dark circles
> under my eyes?  Do you think these are due to kidney
> trouble; what would you recommend to improve this condition?
> (5) Every winter there is a touch of eczema or what looks
> like it all over my face which seems to get worse each year.
> At this time it is impossible for me to use water or soap.
> What causes this?  Please recommend something to improve this
> condition.  (6) Am a sufferer of constipation.  Please
> recommend something to improve this condition.  (7) Menstrual
> period very short (about 2 days); also very little.  Do you
> think this is due to anemia?  Please suggest something to
> correct this condition."
> 
> B3. 5/11/44 Mrs. [2602]'s letter:  "...She is terribly
> disappointed that you were not able to get the reading for
> her, and asked me to write you...  She is really desperate
> and is finding it increasingly difficult to go to work and
> earn her living, but cannot afford to give up her work.  She
> counted so much on the results of the reading that I know
> that she is terribly discouraged..."
> 
> B4. 5/18/44 Letter:  "Thank you very much for your letter making
> my appointment possible on Monday 5/22...  Thank you again
> for your consideration."
