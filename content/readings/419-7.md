# TEXT OF READING 419-7 M 29

**Reading [[419-7]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/30/1938  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 3164

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of January, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [419], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Highway Patrol 
Time of Reading                               Station, 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now as we find, while there is not the excruciating pain, the causes of same through the pelvis and through the coccyx area FROM the strain have NOT been entirely removed.

2. The body should have a series of adjustments; not just one or two until the body feels better, but have at least six to eight or ten - these taken every other day until that number have been taken.  This should remove the source of the disturbances and remove the cause of inflammation, and the inclination for a choking or stoppage through the caecum area.

3. Do that, if we would rid the body of the causes of the disturbance.

4. Ready for questions.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
