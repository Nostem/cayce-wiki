# TEXT OF READING 2619-2 F 59

**Reading [[2619-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/30/1943  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 9661

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes - still conditions are very serious with this body - lack of self-control.  And, as we find, these are being helped in a manner, but not sufficient stress is given to keeping up proper eliminations.

3. The massages should be given as we have indicated, daily.

4. DO use the hand massage as well as the hand magnetic treatment; that is, placing the left hand on the back and the right hand in front on the shoulder and through the hips, as to give the magnetic treatments.  These will be beneficial.

5. Also the low [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] will be most helpful, using the sponge applicator.

6. Don't overfeed the body.  Don't torment the body by trying to make it walk.

7. Keep in the open as much as possible.  But do not overstrain the body.

8. These, as we find, will bring the better things for the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is the place where she is now residing hindering her recovery?
(A)  It CAN be made to be helpful, if there are the applications made as we have suggested.  It CAN be!

11. (Q)  Would you advise having her teeth treated now?
(A)  In a little while, if there are the improvements in the ability of the body, but don't TORMENT the body too much.











2619-2

12. (Q)  What causes eyes to be blurred - which has occurred since starting the treatment suggested in first reading?
(A)  Because you haven't given the treatment just exactly as it was indicated!  If you will do it as suggested, adding those things indicated here, we will find better conditions.

Do that.

13. We are through for the present.

Two copies to sister, Miss [...] (one for Mrs. [...]) Copy to Ass'n file
