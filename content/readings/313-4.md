# TEXT OF READING 313-4 F 46 (?)

**Reading [[313-4]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/6/1932  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 2367

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, in Pinewood on Lake Drive, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of April, 1932, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [228], Active A Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Fla. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before, [313].  In many respects we find conditions physical generally on the improve.  While there are still some conditions that prevent normalcy through the general physical system, these - as we find - should gradually adjust themselves with the keeping at least of a portion of that outlined for the body.

2. In the functioning of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system, these - as related to their cleansing themselves - are now near normal. Hence we would leave off those properties that excite or produce a stimuli to same in their functioning, [That is, [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]] but adding those that are of the same characterization that may be assimilated from the character of the food taken.

3. Those that will aid in the replenishing of those forces as make for the creating of more red blood, more plasm, through the releasing of the vitamins necessary to make resistance and the activities of the functioning organs near normal, assisting in increasing the weight, these we would continue - in the White's [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] tablets.  Take one or two after each meal.

4. As for the conditions in the teeth, that molar as indicated - as we find - shows a leakage, and would be well for the dentist to make an X-Ray of same - and we will find this should show the indicated disorder.  As for the rest of the teeth and gums, these will be well occasionally to have those properties that will keep the mouth cleansed from any of those forms of accumulations that come either from acidity in system, through the breath, or through foods.  Not indicating that the body doesn't wash its teeth, but that film that is an irritant, that - with the acids of system oft becomes a place for germs.  Then, once a week, or three times a month, we would massage these thoroughly with Ipsab.

5. In the use of the [[Vibration|vibrations]], electrically driven, these - we find - will be well to be given, especially at times, over those portions where the injury, or the shaking up of the body in some times back, through accident, causes or produces - when the barometer is low, or the atmospheric pressure changes suddenly - those of aches, or heaviness, and the like.  These we would give, possibly not EVERY day - but whenever the necessity of same is required, of evenings; these would be well to be kept up, and they will give better strength and vitality to the system, allowing the body to rest much easier, relieving the pressure generally over the system, and KEEPING an even balance.

6. In the matter of the diet, we would not alter or change a very great deal, save that there should be more OFTEN taken those that carry [[Iodine|iodine]] in the system; such as the oyster, or the shell fish, or those of especially certain characters of fish that are of the deep sea nature, but not those of the nature that carry only certain portions or quantities - but as the mackerel, or as the plummet [?], or jackfish, or the like - these are very good.  Those that are of the fresh water, or of coarse flesh, not so good - but principally shell fish, oyster, lobster, or crab, or the like.  As for vegetables, those that carry the [[Iodine|iodine]] - as of lentils, beans, peas, radish, salsify, onions; and salads of lettuce, celery, and those activities that make for a CLEANSING of the blood stream.  All of these will be well. Not over eating, but keeping an even balance.  Quantities that will be assimilated of olive oil will always be well for the system.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is the cause of the small rising that comes from time to time in the nostril, and what should be done for this?
(A)  When this gives disorder, use an ointment of the nature that is one percent Yellow Oxide Ointment.  With the [[Vibration|vibrations]] given, with the systems eliminating properly, we will find this should disappear entirely.  It is from re-infectious forces.  Hence, as from acid in system - and is from the same characterization or nature as is indicated as respecting the mouth and throat, see?

The [[Vibration|vibrations]], in the manipulations that are given with the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], should be in the 3rd CERVICAL, and the 1st and 2nd dorsal, for the nasal condition.  Of course, coming on up from same to each side of the neck and around to the forehead - see?

9. (Q)  Was the second molar upper right examined by the dentist the one that should be extracted?
(A)  This has been given.

10. (Q)  What reason should we give the dentist for wishing it extracted?
(A)  This has been given.

11. We are through with this reading.
