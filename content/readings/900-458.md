# TEXT OF READING 900-458 M 34 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-458]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/26/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5661

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you [195], [137], and [900], and their personal plans, both in business and in respect to a contemplated winter vacation.  (This vacation must include in consideration [136] and [142].)  Also plans for [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] of National Investigators, and the educational division thereof.  You will answer questions pertaining to this that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions and those associations, and those relations as individuals, as associates, as [[Purpose|purposes]].  These, as has been given, in many respects are most beautiful, and the efforts and the labors on the part of these individuals, as individuals, have not and WILL not go unrewarded in material, in mental, and in individual satisfaction, WILL those [[Purpose|purposes]], those intents, be kept. Then, keep the faith - my son - that called into being the associations, and keep that [[Purpose|purpose]] that has bound the efforts of thine self, thine brother, thine friend, thine neighbor, thine wife, and thine son.  In those relations there comes not only that of the paternal, the friend, the neighbor - but those also of the teacher, the exhorter, the co-laborer with those of the world, that NEED more of the understanding of that stem as is left in Judah, that is KNOWN in Israel, that is abroad in the land.

3. As for the material conditions, these - as we find - are planned in a most opportune manner at present.  Though there appears on the present horizon those of shadows, as pertaining to the indulgence - as some would term - into such conditions; yet there is the dawn of a NEW understanding, a new relations, will self and those about self but harken and take cognizance of those relations and conditions that have been, are BEING, brought to the mental and spiritual selves in the present day.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Will [195]'s motor be operating by December 15th?
(A)  18th.

5. (Q)  What is the name of the author, and the name of the book recommended to [195], on the new theory of [[Vibration|vibrations]], in the reading of Sept. 30th [22nd]?  [195-66]
(A)  Sandes (?).

6. (Q)  Sandes (?) is the name of the author?
(A)  Sandes, name of the author.  Henry Holt, [[England|England]], publisher.

7. (Q)  The name of book is "New Theory of [[Vibration|Vibration]] - ?
(A)  Sandes - Henry Holt, [[England|England]], publisher.

8. (Q)  Now, from the personal and the educational standpoint, and for the good health of all concerned, everything then considered, would it be well for [195], [900], [136] and [142], to go abroad this winter?
(A)  Would be well.

9. (Q)  Would the baby's health suffer enroute, or in the southern part of [[France|France]]?
(A)  Would not.

10. (Q)  Would our plans to go on the "[[France|France]]", January 11th, be the best sailing for us?
(A)  Be good.

11. (Q)  May we anticipate a smooth crossing at that time, January 11th, for about 14 days?
(A)  Very good.  Not overly severe.  No, rather that of the smooth crossing.

12. (Q)  The return, February 26th, on the "Il de [[France|France]]", from Havre to [[New York City|New York]] - what may we expect on that crossing?
(A)  Well, until the latter days - and then NOT an over severe crossing.

13. (Q)  Will the weather in Niece or Canne be good - that is, warm and sunshiny - during February and the latter part of January?
(A)  This may be expected to be very good, save for a few days of rain around 27th and 9th of January.

14. (Q)  If it could be arranged, would be well for [195] and myself to visit [[Egypt|Egypt]]?
(A)  Would be well, for there - with the application of that necessary for the better understanding - much good may come to each.

15. (Q)  Would it be better that the baby - that's [142] - be not [[Jesus in Egypt|taken into Egypt]]?
(A)  Would be better.

16. (Q)  Now to return to the educational plans, or conversation in Washington - [165], [900] and Dr. [204] are those plans feasible, and is Dr. [204] serious in desiring to enter the undertaking?
(A)  Plans are not only feasible and plausible, but are farther reaching in their scope of bringing a better understanding to many, than felt at present.  [204] is sincere, and will prove worthy of the confidence and purpose as is set forth in that purpose at that meeting.

17. (Q)  Was there not something of [[Greece|Greece]] - ancient [[Greece|Greece]] - in that meeting?
(A)  MUCH - of that as was between the leader and that one aiding that leader in the accomplishing of the purpose in that period.  Just as these in this day may aid each in putting forth the best in that materially manifested, so may that be accomplished that will resound much greater in this present day, generation, than in the siege - or in the taking of Troy.

18. (Q)  To what extent was [137]'s actions on the floor during the great turmoil Thursday due to cosmic influences?
(A)  As was promised, there would not be the forsaking of the way - for, take not thought, but in that day will I put into thine heart that necessary for those that would rail against thee are not capable of defeating thee when my son keeps his mind, heart, and being, in the keeping of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]].

19. (Q)  Are there any suggestions or references for my lecture, "Heaven and Hell" to be given tomorrow?
(A)  Keep that same [[Visions|vision]] of the application of the body being the temple of service, that is either raised to that purpose in service or debased into that other division of man's OWN will.  We are through.

Copy to [900]

"    " Datastician
