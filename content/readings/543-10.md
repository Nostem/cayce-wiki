# TEXT OF READING 543-10 F 22

**Reading [[543-10]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/9/1931  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3843

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of July, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [543].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [543] - this we have had before.  Now, we find there are continued changes going on in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

2. As to the EFFECTIVENESS of the properties being administered, this may easily be seen from the reducing of the attacks; yet the effect same is producing upon the whole MENTAL and nervous system of the body is also apparent.  [Medicine from Dr. Harry L. James, M.D., of Western  Medical Corp., [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.]

3. As to the changes, or the alterations OF the vegetable compounds as are being given, the effect may NOT be given until there is known or seen the changes as will be MADE from time to time.  WE would continue with the present applications until there is the satisfying of those upon whom the body is dependent for those administrations for the welfare OF the physical and mental being; yet we would be very careful that there is not added with these administrations ANY drug that becomes habit-FORMING, or that the body-physical becomes dependent upon same for its own responses, or in USING - rather let the hypnotics than those of the narcotics be used; though BOTH must be harmful if persisted in.  Necessary that the changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] be effective by a change in the reactions from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the reproductive system, as has been seen.  That which will produce in the body a tendency for suppression ALONE of the functioning of these [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] cannot be expected TO be a permanent [[Healing|healing]] for the body.  These must either be those that will not only suppress, but destroy or alter the activity OF the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - see?  Then, these may be expected to be helpful.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  How long should she take the medicine before it gives the desired effect?
(A)  That cannot be given as long as there are continued changes in the quantity, or the calibre, or kind OF properties - see?  For what a body will do within themselves is a matter of their own will; while in THIS case [[Free Will|the will]] is active by those administering same.

5. (Q)  Will it obliterate the attacks entirely?
(A)  That depends upon what changes are made.  As it is suppressing same, so there is not a narcotic used, it WILL! If it's a hypnotic, it WILL relieve.  If it is changed, or made to a narcotic, it will not!

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents
"    " Self
"    " File
