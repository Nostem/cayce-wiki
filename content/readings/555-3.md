# TEXT OF READING 555-3 M 36

**Reading [[555-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/30/1934  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 3908

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of July, 1934, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [540], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Abbie Kemp, Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body; this we have had before. Conditions are improved somewhat in the present from that as was indicated from the specific conditions.  The temperature is much lower.  There has not been sufficient of the eliminations through the alimentary canal as should be, so we would begin with another dose of the Castoria, you see; half a teaspoonful every half hour until a whole bottle is taken. We would also be mindful that the cold and congestion that makes for the heaviness in the lungs does not localize here. DO NOT add those things that would clarify this, but clarify the general system and the pressure here will be removed.

2. Now we will find, beginning about noon, spirits frumenti with egg and the like will be helpful in maintaining a better equilibrium without a heaviness to the head.

3. We would also soon use the semi-liquid foods now.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Why is body unable to move on bed?
(A)  Stiffness from cold and congestion.  Hence as indicated, remove these pressures more through the alimentary canal.

We would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body thoroughly, of course.  Keep heat applied to the solar plexus - yes, even with the temperature; that is, outside, not OF the body - on the 9th dorsal this would be helpful, see?

6. (Q)  Is treatment having desired effect?
(A)  Doing very good.  Response is very good.

7. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these, as we find; and the body should, by the morning, be feeling a great deal better and be sitting up.

8. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [540]
"    " Ass'n file
