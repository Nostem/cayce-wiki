# TEXT OF READING 8-2 M 33

**Reading [[8-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/30/1924  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 30

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 30th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [8].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:45 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find there are some abnormal conditions in this body and these may be corrected.  They have to do with the lymphatic circulation and with specific conditions existing in the system, by the absorption of external bacilli in the system, and the effect these have produced in the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body. First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find with the pressure as is produced in the second dorsal vertebra a diverting of the blood supply from the pulmonaries to the heart, and the actions of the distributing through eliminating forces in the system from this in such a way and manner that the lymphatic circulation becomes involved and produces in the bronchials, the throat, the nasal cavity, the extra secretions as are thrown in the system and made manifest by the temperature, by the extra secretions as discharged in the system through action of bacilli created by the meeting of these conditions with those of external forces in the system, and this brings distress to the organs affected by this condition, as we have had in times back the extra flow of blood to the organs of the throat and upper portion of the larynx, in the tonsils and in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] region.  This inflammation created in the system produced by this overflow and without the system eliminating the dross in used portions of the system here.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find as this:  In the dorsal region we find the condition there of a lesion, which diverts the circulation through secondary cardiac plexus forces, or the secondary nerve and muscular forces as conduct the blood supply from heart through lungs and the return through the aorta artery.  In this condition then we affect the sympathetic nerve system in its connection with the sympathetic that governs the organs of the sensory system, affecting both the larynx, in the vocal box, and the eye and the organs indirect and direct.  The conditions are not of the aggravated or of a pre-disposition, but of the condition as produced by a congestion first and a lesion following, producing this condition in the system.

5. The organs of the system themselves direct very good. Functioning under the strain of the conditions.

6. Then to remove and to relieve the condition in the system for that of the local condition as has been exercised and shown in the system, in the throat, in the larynx, in the bronchials, use a solution of dioxide, to use as a wash or gargle, following this with the twenty percent solution [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], interchanging, or one with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and again with Listerine, as antiseptics, both acting in this manner:  One as an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]], the other as an acid, so that the absorption of the various conditions as produced by this will equalize and minimize the effect produced in the intestinal tract, through that portion inhaled or taken in the system.

7. For the condition in the dorsal, remove by deep manipulation; and until the condition is removed entirely take only once each week one, one-eightieth of a grain* in the system, through the intestinal tract, that of Alophen.

8. Do that.  When this condition is removed, we will find we will relieve the body of the strain produced by this present floating lesion.



* [4/15/76 GD's note:  Mr. Ingram of Ingram Pharmacy says the l/80th has no meaning for him; he thinks EC meant just one Alophen pill.]
