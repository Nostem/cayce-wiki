# TEXT OF READING 667-12 F 24

**Reading [[667-12]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/11/1939  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 4428

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [667], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [667] and sister.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [667].

3. There is a great deal of change and difference in the general physical reactions of the body in the present from those as we have had here before.

4. Some, of course, have been changes in the mental, some in the physical, and in the general attitudes of the body itself.

5. As we find in the present, there needs to be the greater consideration given to the lack of the vitality and strength to the body.  These as we find are in such an indeterminate manner in the present, - as the pains through the small of the back, through the areas across the lumbar.  Here we should have some adjustments.  Not a great deal would be necessary, but two or three PROPERLY given should be sufficient.

6. Then, take Codiron AS a tonic.  This for this body would be taken one tablet in the beginning at each meal taken, - not after or before, but DURING the meal, - for the first ten days.  Then take two at each meal for the next ten days. Then leave off for five days, and then begin with the one tablet at each meal for ten days.  And then for the next ten days take two at each meal, and so on.

7. These should bring a great deal of strength and vitality, for they are those influences that should cause a more and a better supply of blood, which is lacking, - which causes the fullness or the pains about the heart, the chest, the lack of the ability to sleep or rest well of evenings, and the general tired, achey conditions of evening or of night.

8. Do these and, as we find, we would bring the better influences and forces for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Are the kidneys alright?
(A)  The stress that is produced there is from the pressures that should be removed by the gentle but deep manipulations through ALL of the cerebrospinal system.  Some three or four SHOULD make for proper corrections, if they are properly done!

11. (Q)  What causes pain around the heart?
(A)  As has just been indicated, weakness and lack of the proper assimilation and eliminations, and the general debilitation.

12. (Q)  Are there any tubercular germs present in the body?
(A)  We do not find tubercular germs.

13. (Q)  Are cigarettes harmful to the body at this time?
(A)  For this body, cigarettes are HARMFUL!

14. (Q)  Are any of these conditions brought on the body by childbirth?
(A)  The weak conditions, to be sure, or lack of the blood supply.

15. (Q)  What should the diet be?
(A)  All those foods that are body-building.  Plenty of fish, fowl and lamb, and every sort of vegetable that agrees with the body.

16. (Q)  Whom would you suggest to make the corrections?
(A)  Whoever is desired by the body.  But choose one that will give a thorough, general treatment, making the corrections in the lumbar and sacral areas.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
