# TEXT OF READING 293-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[293-1]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/6/1933  Sex: F  ReadingID: 1959

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 6th day of March, 1933, in accordance with request made by Miss [630], through Miss [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading                             Mission Hospital, 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. Ashville, N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find that that may be accomplished most for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] may be through the concerted effort of [[Prayer|prayer]], that those ministering may bring that in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] which may be in keeping with that which will create about the body, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], those influences for the better developments for resuscitation, for the awakening in every way for the entity.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Any treatments?
(A)  That that has been given, that those ministering may minister that as necessary.

4. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  There should be the concerted effort on the part of all those that would aid in this direction.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [630]
"    " File
