# TEXT OF READING 4199-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4199-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/13/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11948

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 13th day of October, 1922, in accordance with request made by the self, Mrs. [4199].

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stallings, Brazelton & Hale, Court Reporters, Steno. Mrs. [4199].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                    [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala.

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep.  (Pause)  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep, and all of the organs of your body will function properly.  You will hear me and answer all of my questions.  You have before you the body of Mrs. [4199], who is present in this room.  Now, Mr. Cayce, I want you to tell me the physical and mental ailments that you find within this body that you have before you, and tell me if an operation is necessary.  If so, please tell me; and, if not, tell me what will relieve the condition within the body which you have before you, Mrs. [4199], present in this room.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  The abnormal conditions as we find within this body have to do with the distribution of forces as received from the nerve and muscular incentives that give the action to voluntary and involuntary muscular forces in portions of the body.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

3. It has to do with the lymphatic circulation.  Now, these are conditions as we find in this body here.

4. Through the blood supplying forces we find the blood of a very good character, so far as the blood forces are concerned, yet it carrying out of it that which gives to some portions of the body as it picks up through the circulation that which causes too much of the force carried to the capillaries, hence to the extremities, to those portions governed directly by the emunctories and the lymphatic circulation.  We find that it produced pain, - contraction of joints of the portions that are affected by action of the lymph in this circulation through the capillaries.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

5. The contraction at times of the mucous sacs carrying that portion of the distributing forces needed to cause the action of the joints.  See?
FEM:  Yes, sir.

6. As we have here in the knee; as we have here in the arm at times; as we have through portions of the body where we have the joint action of the forces in the body.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

7. This producing from or being produced from forces picked up in the hepatic circulation; or we have an auto-intoxication picked up from the system and distributed and is now being absorbed by the capillary circulation and not eliminated through the capillary eliminating channels or the pores of the skin as much as it should, nor is it being distributed through the forces as it should be distributed from the liver and the kidneys and by the blood's circulation through them. See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

8. The number of the red blood corpuscles being at present sufficient to care for this, the action of the leucocyte or white blood, being below the normal condition within this body, Mrs. [4199], we are speaking of here.

9. Through the nerve forces we find that the condition existing in the muscular forces, the nerves have become lax through the extremities.  Yet, through the cerebrospinal centers, that is, along the spine from the first dorsals to those of the sacrum, we find rather tautness.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

10. It is the opposite condition from what we find through the extremities, that is, at times the body feels achy along these portions as they extend to direct points, to the extremities, through the locomotories that affect both the lower limbs and those of the arms and to the outer portion. Reflexes from the solar plexus show the condition existing through the capillaries.  See.

FEM:  Yes, sir.

11. It is nerve forces we are speaking of now.  The action of the sensory forces, in this system is above normal.  The action of the spinal forces, the mind action, as we have given, is good.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

12. It has not affected any of the force of the mind forces, even of the physical suffering of the body, through which it has been so needed.  See?
FEM:  Yes, sir.

13. But it has kept on an even keel or balance, so that this condition existing through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], the functioning of the organs themselves throughout the body that have not a normal force considering the condition being shown through the toxin forces, that is, the digestive tract shows the effect of this.  At times we find it is hard to digest certain things that at times have agreed entirely with it. See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

14. Then again, we find things that have not agreed with it that would be best for the system at times.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

15. To relieve these conditions as we find in this body here, we would add that to the system that will assist the blood supply in eliminating these toxins from the system.  We would give that into the system that would carry these forces through their proper channels or excite, as it were, the mucous forces and the lactic forces to their proper action.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

16. Then we would give that which would take these or eliminate these from the system.  We would give this into the system. First we would give into the system two doses of medicinal properties, this being in each dose:

Podophyllin.......................1 grain,
Senna.............................2 grains,
Sanguinaria.......................2 grains,
[[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]]...............1 grain.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

17. After this has been taken into the system, the two doses two days apart, see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

18. Then we will give into the system a good amount of water sufficient to flush the whole system throughout.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

19. Then we will begin to give this to the system:

[[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]].................15 grains,
Carbonate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]].................6 grains,
dissolved in fifteen ounces of water.

The dose of this would be five minims or drops in a half glass of water.  This would be taken four times each day. Just before the evening dose is taken, the body should be treated across the lumbars and all across the region of the locomotories and under the knees with electric [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

20. That is, as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] force to this.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

21. Then when we have suffered pains to these portions of the body as we had at times, see, we would apply heat as found in common table salt, with the body well bathed with pure apple vinegar.  Dry this into the joints of the body here by salt heat.  This will give rejuvenated forces to this body.  If so followed out as we have given here, we will find that we have relieved these conditions existing here in the body of Mrs. [4199], we are speaking of, see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

22. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, Mrs. [4199] wants to know after this treatment is taken if she can walk up and down the steps without pain.
(A)  Mrs. [4199] wants to know if she can walk up and down the steps without pain after this treatment is taken.  We have given the things to eliminate these things from the system.  Follow this as we have given.  We will find that we will be relieved of this condition existing in this system, to be able to walk up and down the steps.  Follow this as we have given here.

23. We are through.

24. Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about this reading that you have given.  You are feeling fine and all right, and all of the organs of your body function properly.  You are feeling good.  Now, wake up in two minutes, just feeling fine and all right.
