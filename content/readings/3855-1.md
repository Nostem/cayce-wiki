# TEXT OF READING 3855-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[3855-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/9/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11472

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at Altoona, Pa., this 9th day of May, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:00 P. M.                                          ..., Pa. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions in this body.  All may be corrected through the proper treatment to the conditions in the system. The troubles have to do with the coordination of the nerve forces in the system, and the effect as reached by this improper condition in the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3855], we are speaking of:  In the beginning we find the effects as produced in the system at present date back some time to conditions as existed in the body, there formed by the reaction of a bacteria in the functioning organs and the effect it produced over the system, inactivity between the action of the voluntary and involuntary forces and of elimination through the ordinary channels or natural forces and as that, nature attempted to rebuild in the body to perform the functioning of those that were hindered by the action of this bacillus or bacteria.  In the blood supply of the body we find the blood circulation from the inactive forces of a full coordinating circulation throughout, hence the elemental forces to replenish building tissue through the system is lacking in the first constituents of the blood itself.  This condition is a natural condition from the effects of the body, see.  The forces through the white blood supply makes that of secretions perform in abnormal ways over the system in the attempt to bring the condition about that would give the necessary life-giving function to the nerve and muscular forces about the body to perform the involuntary forces which gives then that of the improper conditions in portions of the body, that is, developments abnormal in portions of the body with the rest of the system here, as we have here.

3. The nerve forces here, the seat of the trouble, we find the centers along the whole spinal region are out of accord with the functioning of the muscles to carry the forces to the body, as we have at the brachial force, the second lumbar force and the sacral force, that is, the plexus, as it would be termed, of each does not coordinate with the mental forces to produce the proper functioning of the limbs in any of their action.  The forces all become secondary rather then of volition or involition and only the sympathetic nerve energy that keeps the functioning organs in the voluntary forces give the life to the body, either through mental or [[Physical Body|physical forces]], see.

4. In the functioning of the organs we find the underdevelopment of the body and only as we have given the abnormal development to portions by poor sympathetic forces to the specific organs as their cycle of development has progressed with the physical, see.

5. To give the proper incentive to this body we would first begin with that, that would give the incentive to the central portion of body and gradually build the nerve energy to the portions of system and cause the proper flow of nerve energy through the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary for this individual ego or I, see.  These would be found in this:

6. To two gallons of rain water in a glass container, add:

Heavy Sea Salt...............1 pound,
Plain Charcoal...............1 pound,
Copper Sulfate...........1 1/2 pounds,
Zinc........................10 ounces,
Sulfuric Acid C.P........1 1/2 ounces.

This we find would charge itself, but must be charged from those of electric forces of high amperage with low voltage. While being charged there would be suspended in this solution an opaque container holding one pound of [[Iodine|iodine]].  This would be charged for thirty minutes, see, both poles being of copper.  The ohms must reach as high as five see.  The poles are then changed to one of copper, the other of nickle or silver, the negative being silver or nickle, see; small wires are attached to these poles, these ends in turn attached to a plate about the size of a half dollar, copper; this, each evening, the plate would be attached to this body at the tenth dorsal for twenty minutes, see.  The reaction will show on the third day, see.  After the second solution has been used, for it must not be allowed to go below two ohms for use before it would be renewed again, see - in or during the use of the second solution, the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the whole system over that known as the Neuropathic forces should be applied to the body.  This will gently and consistently bring about the development of the proper coordination in this body in the control of all nerve and muscular forces and development in the physical use of mental manifestation of self throughout.  Do that.

7. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, who will prepare this solution for the treatment?
(A)  Do as we have given.  Anyone can do it.

8. (Q)  Can the treatment be given in the home?
(A)  It is the home we will give it in.  The body is the home.  The treatment is at the home.  We can give it anywhere the body is, in the evening just before the hour of rest for the body, see.

9. (Q)  What about the diet for this body?
(A)  Give those to the developing body, and with the conditions as are carried, see, only those of the developing [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Do that, if we would bring the correct incentives to this body, [3855].  We are through.

> of the brace, and he called to his grandmother to look that

> us because up to the present time the treatment has acted as
