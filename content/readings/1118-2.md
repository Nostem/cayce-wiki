# TEXT OF READING 1118-2 F 37

**Reading [[1118-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/11/1936  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 6377

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March, 1936, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., New Jersey. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. There are improvements indicated in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body; as better equilibrium is being established in the coordination between the reflexes of the sensory forces of the body and the sympathetic and cerebrospinal reactions.

3. As we find, those applications or injections that HAVE been used for the nasal passages are still necessary.  And even when those solutions are used that we have indicated for taking the place of these (that CREATE normalcy - the new solutions - through their very activity upon the system), it will be necessary to continue such injections occasionally but gradually diluting the EFFECT of the cocaine reaction in the system itself.

4. As these injections are in the nature of a stimuli that produces or induces reactions that are habit-forming, or required, it will be necessary that their reduction from the system be partially psychological as well as pathological.

5. We would continue the use of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and adjustments osteopathically, in the upper cervical and particularly through the upper dorsal and coordinating the general system; until the system responds more and more to normalcy.

6. In preparing a spray of the Mono-icthyol, or mononized Icthyol, we would use ten (10) parts of the Icthyol to one (1) part of the Ammonium.  And as the Ammonia in its very nature is an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]], and FORMS most in the gaseous form, HEAT BOTH to their boiling point without boiling BEFORE ADDING!  Hence we would have a different type of an application when it is reduced by water AND alcohol AS a spray.  For while Ammonium is soluble in water and Icthyol soluble in the alcohol, these prepared in the manner indicated may be cut or reduced by a very much weakened solution of alcohol - grain alcohol, of course.  And in this manner it may be used for this body, as we find.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What proportion of alcohol and water to the Icthyol and Ammonia, after it is combined as indicated?
(A)  This would come one (1) to forty (40).  The first, one (1) to ten (10).  In the reduction, one (1) to forty (40); that these may be applicable in a spray, and may be yet effective in their activity upon the system.

And, as indicated, the injections will have to be GRADUALLY reduced, both as to their strength and as to periods - for the very cause or reason shown.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [...] [Husband]
"    " Ass'n file
