# TEXT OF READING 1713-23 F 46

**Reading [[1713-23]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 7905

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1713], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Pennsylvania.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are a great many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. If the body would follow those suggestions that have been heretofore made, we would find improvements.

5. But ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is the source of my trouble and how can I become better?
(A)  This has been given long since.  It is the incoordination between the superficial and the deeper circulation; that is, the superficial circulation as from the lymph and emunctory activity, and the deeper circulation - or heart, liver, lungs and kidney circulation.

7. (Q)  Am I following the right diet or is this taking away too much?
(A)  This is a very good diet when it is adhered to, but the body breaks over quite often.

8. (Q)  What is causing excessive [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]]?
(A)  The lack of bringing proper coordination between the superficial and deeper circulation.

We would take Acigest to allay the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]]; a teaspoonful in a glass of raw milk twice each week - say Tuesdays and Fridays - at the evening meal.

9. (Q)  Are whole wheat toast and Ralston cereal alright for me to eat?
(A)  They are alright but do not eat these with citrus fruits at the same meal.

10. (Q)  Are raw vegetables harmful to me?
(A)  Certain vegetables are alright.  Prepare them oft with gelatin or various types of salad dressings that are not acid-producing, and we will find these will be well.  Grate, slice and prepare them in varied manners.

11. (Q)  What causes occasional blood in stool and how can it be stopped?
(A)  This arises from a hemorrhoid condition far in the rectal area.  Use this combination of oils as an injection: Add two drops of carbolic acid to one ounce of glycerine. Beat this thoroughly together.  Then add one ounce of Usoline or Nujol.  This should make sufficient for at least two or three injections.  Inject this into the anus in such a way that we will have control of the conditions and relieve the areas.  Use an ear syringe for the injection.

12. (Q)  Is it necessary to take [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  Not necessary, but what do you want to do about it? You can take exercise that will be as helpful, owing to other circumstances surrounding the body, but it will take much longer.

13. (Q)  What is wrong with my [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system?
(A)  It is a condition between the superficial and the deeper nerve supply that upsets the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.  But if the regular exercise is taken and the Acigest is carried through properly, we will change this.

14. (Q)  Are my eyes getting stronger or weaker?
(A)  Both; that is, there are times when they are reacting to the superacidity, that they are weaker.  When the body comes back near to normal they get stronger.  It is well to use glasses, though be sure that these be properly fitted.

15. (Q)  What causes left shoulder and arm to bother me?
(A)  This is neuritic, from the pressure of poisons in system.  This should change with taking regularly the Acigest and keeping good eliminations.

16. (Q)  Are the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] tablets I am taking good for me?
(A)  Not especially.  They are not adding much, not detracting much.  They make for drosses that are sometimes not easily eliminated.

17. (Q)  How can I get better coordination in metabolism?
(A)  By having a regular exercise for the body.  Take the regular setting-up exercises and the Acigest that has been indicated.

Do this and we will find better conditions.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
