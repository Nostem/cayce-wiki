# TEXT OF READING 635-5 F 52

**Reading [[635-5]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/2/1934  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 4321

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of October, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [635], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Florida|Florida]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [635]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, there are considerable changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body since last we had same.  Many are for the improvement; others that have arisen (and that are a portion of the natural consequential changes in the body in its development or experience in this period) bring about very detrimental conditions.

3. And, as we find, these can be materially aided.  However, we find a contributing cause to the disorder is the lack of the dissimulation of the energies that were released in the body through the use of the oil packs.

4. (This aside; but make it in big notice for all to whom you send information:  EITHER DO ALL THAT IS SUGGESTED THROUGH SUCH INFORMATION OR DON'T DO ANY OF IT, for you may bring detrimental conditions to yourself - even as this body has!)

5. In changing the eliminations through the alimentary canal, to relieve those pressures from the activities of a congestion in the ducts of the digestive area, or the bile ducts, the gall ducts, the lacteal ducts, the activities in the lobes of the liver, these were to be distributed by the stimulation of the cerebrospinal system with the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] along the ganglia of the system, see?  This produced in the alimentary canal an assistance towards elimination, but the nerve effects or the congestion from those portions that should have had their stimulation for the proper distribution of energy as released from the whole portion of the cerebro-spinal system did not!  Consequently we have, through the ducts of the system, along the cerebro-spinal system, a contraction to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] area - the ducts that run through the medulla oblongata into the portion of the cerebellum itself, that makes for a contraction to the very brain centers themselves.

6. We would use in the present more of the oil packs over the liver and caecum area, but we would first paint the spine - from the base of the brain to about the 3rd dorsal (that's the whole cervical area, you see) - with laudanum and aconite; half an ounch of laudanum with twenty minims of aconite.

7. We would use the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the electrically DRIVEN [[Electric Vibrator|VIBRATOR]] over ALL of the cerebrospinal system.

8. We would put hot salt packs across the hips and up and down the spine from same to about the 9th dorsal (using coarse heavy salt in bags, heated); and we will find relief in these.  They would be used only when pain is severe.

9. The [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] treatments should be taken each EVENING when the body is ready to retire.

10. The application of the oil packs would be taken when there is the tendency for gas or the feeling of fullness in the stomach, bad taste in the mouth, a little nausea or headache. Any of these are indications that there needs to be a stimulation to the ducts of the duodenum, the pancreas, the lacteal ducts, the gall ducts, the bile secretions, the lobes of the liver, the activity through the caecum, the activity through the veriform appendix, through the jejunum, the activities along the colon area itself - all of these need stimulation; and this stimulation is the better for this body.

11. Be mindful and careful of the diet also.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Is the same condition producing dizziness and nausea?
(A)  Produces the dizziness and nausea, as has been indicated.

Do as we have outlined.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
