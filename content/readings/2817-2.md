# TEXT OF READING 2817-2 F 66

**Reading [[2817-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/31/1942  Sex: F  Age: 66  ReadingID: 10010

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2817], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, while some general conditions are improved, it will take a little time for the response to those pressures through the shortness of breath, quickness of the pulsation.

4. And with the administrations, if it does not clear a great deal within the next ten days, then it would be well - under the direction of a physician - that there be given the X-Ray treatments through the lungs; and we may dissipate the accumulations there, and aid in the better recuperative forces being builded in the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Do I have a goitre?
(A)  There are accumulations in the thyroids.  These will, or should, respond to the administrations indicated - unless there is the dissipation through the better eliminations being set up.

Do these things indicated.  If there are not better results in ten days, then have - under the direction of an X-Ray specialist - those treatments for the lungs particularly.

Do that.

Keep plenty of the [[Iodine|iodine]] products as food, and the administrations of small quantities of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] will aid; one drop each morning before the meal is taken for five days, leave off five days, and then take again, - and repeat this for several weeks.

7. We are through for the present.

Two copies for self (one for Dr. Smith) Copy to Ass'n file
