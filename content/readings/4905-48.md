# TEXT OF READING 4905-48

**Reading [[4905-48]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/8/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12973

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading as given on [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick, this reading given on Dec. 5, 1925.  You will describe to us the stratum through which the drilling has progressed in this angle and tell us when, and in what stratum, we may expect to find this production you give is now on sides and bottom of this hole.

2. EC:  Yes, we have that information as transcribed on well on lands of Madison Byron Wyrick.  The stratum and formations as are seen in this operation are in that nature of the double anticlinal form, and the operation or hole passing in that as might be termed in the ridge of same, in the relation to those pools in oil that lie to the South and East in the various stratum or level in which, or through which, these operations have passed, making the various levels as have been given as where the level that production would be reached.  In first the twenty-nine hundred (2900) feet, the double production to the South being of the first or last of the cretaceous forms and first of the bend.  That in the next in the forty-two hundred (4200), being that in level of the central or low bend.  In the formations then as are to be found in the lower portion of operations, we find that in the two folds, as given, twice the drill or hole has passed through the same formations, in almost identical same depths.  In the present, at the sixty sixty-six (6066), we find the entering into the last of the black lime formations, in which the break into the gray in the much shorter depth, and the production in this as turns into the double incline and the cone shape formation of the pool proper.  So, we will find in this stratum, the half in depth of the last black lime and a third in depth of gray, before encountering the pool of oil itself.  Drill through to this.  We will produce oil in this hole.  The depth to drill as yet, as we see, then, thirty-eight and a half (38 1/2) feet.  We are through with this reading.
