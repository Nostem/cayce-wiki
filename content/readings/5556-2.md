# TEXT OF READING 5556-2 F 63

**Reading [[5556-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/12/1927  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 13844

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5556].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5556].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement and relief of same, and you will answer any questions regarding same that I may ask you.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  We find the body shows much change since we had same here, the greater portion for the improvement of same.

3. Those properties as have been given and applied for the body should be kept much in the way and manner as has been outlined for the body.

4. In the manner of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as should be set up by the manipulation, should this be changed, the body may take those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the electrical driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] instead, would the body desire rather to use that form of [[Vibration|vibration]], but with this we would use the massaging thoroughly of the lower limbs, that give some distress, with the saturated solution of vinegar and salt - pure apple vinegar.  Pour as much salt in same as the vinegar will dissolve, see?  and massage this into the pores of the body on the limbs, especially about the knee and about the hip or the joints. This do just before the vibratory forces are applied, and this preferably should be done just before retiring each evening.  This will be, in a manner, the same reaction as from manipulation.  Hence will require CONSIDERABLE time to give the body the thorough treatments.  This should be given almost next the skin, see?

5. As for those properties as have been given in that of that to ELIMINATE - through that of the Toris Compound - these we would, for the time being, keep up - alternating in the way of preparing same in the manner given.

6. Keep up also, WHENEVER necessary - or whenever the condition is chest shows any tightness - those of the Calcidin tablets, see?  taking one at the time.  May be several taken in one day, or may be only one or none, according to the FEELINGS of the body as respecting the asthmatic condition.

7. The Violet Ray we would apply over much of the body, as has been given.  Never too long, but as is seen this is beneficial to the body.  Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should the body take Eade's gout and rheumatic pills?
(A)  If these had been necessary we would have given them. Does the body need these as an eliminator, they may be taken - but would the diet and the other properties be applied as given, this would - as we find - not be so necessary; but for eliminations these may be taken occasionally.  They are not hindrances, and are more of ELIMINATORS than of preventatives.

9. (Q)  Has the Violet Ray caused any burn?
(A)  No.  Only the body should not allow same to be used when there's too much moisture, or too long in one place. The body may use talc powder over the portions where the appliance is applied, and we will not have burns.

10. (Q)  How long at a time should the vibratory method be applied?
(A)  From thirty to forty to sixty minutes, for these should hunt out each center along the cerebro-spinal system, with the ball - the flat or the sponge [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] used over the limbs, arms, face, chest, but the hard knot, or the ball [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] used in each vertebra along the spine very thoroughly with body lying prone, see, on face - for these will assist and give relief to the body, as has been outlined.

11. We are through with this reading.
