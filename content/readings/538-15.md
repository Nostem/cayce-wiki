# TEXT OF READING 538-15 F 45 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[538-15]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/16/1925  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 3751

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [538].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [569].]

Time of Reading 
10:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [538] of ..., Virginia, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [538], here. This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] as we see come for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s edification, and once having obtained that relation with such spiritual forces, well that entity, for its best mental, physical, financial, conditions, pay attention or regard same in that same manner in which same have been given to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], for through same the issues of life may be understood and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] more able to apply same in its life, physically, mentally, morally, spiritually, see?  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Thursday night, October 15, 1925, at home.  "Dreamed [288] and I were in a large station waiting for a train to go on a trip.  The station seemed to be like the Pennsylvania Terminal in [[New York City|New York City]], underground.  We were both dressed nicely and felt that we were.  Then it seemed [288] (or I was not sure then it was her) was going to be married, and we were waiting for the young man.  While waiting, the train came up and we felt we had to catch that train, so we started and I told [288] that he would follow, but in some way I became separated and while trying to get to the train, through a dark passage, a man came after me, and I woke hollering."
(A)  In this we see that correlation with those conditions as have been presented in the dreams as have been given through, by, [900].  And this refers, then, directly, to that of Long Island trains as were seen in his to Palm Beach, see? and this gives rather then the trading that may be expected in the coast line ships that go from the [[Florida|Florida]] and Bermuda coast to Long Island, see?  This, as is seen with the two in station, brings the message to the one in waiting, which was [900], see?  and the marriage, or thinking of the one or someone being wedded, brings the financial conditions in relation with this.

Then the entity should act in that way as to give such information to [900] as regards coast-wise shipping trade that will be on the advance, see?  for this changes very fast.

4. We are through for the present.
