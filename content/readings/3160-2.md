# TEXT OF READING 3160-2 F 65

**Reading [[3160-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/18/1943  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 10533

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3160], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. While there are not great improvements, there are those tendencies - and there needs to be only the keeping very consistently towards those things that have been suggested for the body.

4. Be consistent, be persistent, be prayerful, and use that ye have attained.

5. Find less faults with others, and others will find less fault with thee.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is the body [[Healing|healing]] properly?
(A)  It is, as indicated, being on the improve.

8. (Q)  Any suggestions for further treatments?
(A)  Just be consistent with those applications that have already been suggested.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
