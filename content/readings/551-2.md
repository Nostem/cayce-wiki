# TEXT OF READING 551-2 M 27

**Reading [[551-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/3/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 3881

This Psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [551].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, telling us if the treatments are being accorded properly and giving any further suggestions for the improvement of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find the general physical conditions in body are responding to those conditions and [[Vibration|vibrations]], and treatments that have been and are being accorded the system.

3. There is at present time some show of retraction in the system that has been a little unsatisfactory to the body. This, we will find, will be necessary for the body to pass through to gain that full physical equilibrium for the best manifestations in the physical conditions of the body.

4. Then, keep those suggestions as have been given for the body, with persistence and consistent to the mental, physical and moral attributes of the body, adhere to those suggestions as have been given for the best manner in which the physical may develop and manifest, and thereby prepare itself for the best mental and physical and spiritual development in the material world.

5. The change in the [[Vibration|vibration]], as is found necessary with consistency of physical reaction from mental and moral conditions in body, were necessary, these as given in a consistent manner.  With the whole entity's relations, physical, mental and moral, applicate those things necessary for the best physical development.

6. We have no changes to make, or suggestions, other than has been given, to offer.

7. We are through for the present.
