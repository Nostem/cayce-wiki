# TEXT OF READING 137-110 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-110]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/26/1928  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 500

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come through those channels as have been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and are to be applied as given, in the way and manner as has been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  Afternoon of June 25, 1928.  Saw a little child sliding down the roller coaster.
(A)  This, as is seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], represents those conditions in the mind as respecting those elements that go to make up existent circumstances, and are to be applied in the manner as the body has, or is acting respecting same at the present time.  See?

3. (Q)  Does this refer to radio?  If so, correlate it with what has been given about radio and the merger before September.
(A)  That the conditions are sliding, as it were, little by little, for the contemplated merger - see?  that is, stock going down, then the general rise that comes before such conditions enter.

4. (Q)  Is it a good time to buy radio?
(A)  Good time.

5. (Q)  Recent date.  [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]], our chauffeur, took [900] and me out in my car into the woods.  He pulled up and ordered us gruffly to get out.  I grew frightened at his ugly manner. [900] wanted to get out on the street side, but I was afraid and was urging [900] that we get off on the sidewalk side.
(A)  This, as has been given, is respecting the care which should be taken regarding associations of those not trustworthy.  While that as seen does not indicate to the mind other than precautions; yet it would be well that not too much confidence or latitude be given such as indicated through this individual, see?

6. (Q)  Had I best not hire [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] again in the fall, due to his not being the type to trust and put such a responsibility in? That is, is this a warning to me along these lines?
(A)  Best not.

7. We are through with this reading.
