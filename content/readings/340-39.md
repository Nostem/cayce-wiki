# TEXT OF READING 340-39 F 50

**Reading [[340-39]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/30/1938  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 2631

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of May, 1938, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [243], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Evelyn & Anna Gimbert, Ethel R. Harris, Maybelle R. Morris and Mae Verhoeven.]

Time of Reading                                  ... Avenue, 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, conditions are not so good.  There are the inclinations for the accumulations from inflammatory forces through the digestive system; as well as through the pulmonary system; producing an accumulation that becomes in the form almost tumorous in its nature.

3. Under the present conditions we find it would be more preferable to have the attention of the physician of the [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] school, but the man rather than the lady - for this particular body in the present; for it may become necessary for operative measures and his associations or connections would be better.

4. We would apply the combination of the opiate influences upon the body; that is, three parts Laudanum to one part Aconite; just below the breast bone, you see - or across a portion of the abdomen AND just below the secondary cardiac plexus; or about the 4th and 5th DORSAL areas.

5. THEN apply HEAT, preferably the heavy SALT - rather than the saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]]; for there is needed the SALT effect, or calcium.  Apply this over the same areas of the abdomen as well as in the back.

6. Do these as we find for the present.

7. (Q)  Is Dr. Shackleford the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] physician referred to?
(A)  A man rather than a lady.  Shackleford would be very well, and the connections are better.  [James R. Shackleford, D.O.]

This is rather serious.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Wired (Sent Special)
