# TEXT OF READING 215-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[215-1]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/15/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1036

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of October, 1923, in accordance with request made by his daughter.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Mayme Rosenberg (?), Steno. Mr. [215]''s daughter.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                   Brooklyn, N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now as for conditions in this body and that producing them, the abnormal conditions and the effect as is produced in this system at the present time.  Now, we have conditions to be met with and we have the incentives within the system producing and correcting conditions at the present time.  So, for conditions as are, and as being produced, we will have to give the relation between existing and conditions produced and being produced. So, take these into consideration, see.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body of [215], we are speaking of.  First, in the blood supply in the body, we find the incentive for increased circulation and for building to the red blood, also for the cellular force to meet resistance, - yet we find the system lacking in these conditions, yet the incentive as is necessary at the present time to meet the condition at the present time, see?

3. In the nerve force of the body, we find as this:  The body in times back has been rather temperamental, see, and the activity of the mental force often manifested in the physical-mental action, and in the physical actions of the body, to the detriment at times, not always, to the blood in its incentive for nerve reaction within the body.  Internal forces, we are speaking of.  At the present time, subjugation of these to the subliminal forces is producing a different effect upon the system, see.  There are centers in the cerebrospinal system that show this effect.  That of the pneumogastric digestion has been involved, though we find at the present, these centers in the [hypogastric?] centers, with the pneumogastric centers giving off the infusion necessary to counterbalance the condition at present, see. This, with the center in the 7th dorsal region, where congestion is met between the perineurial, pneumogastric, cerebro-spinal, sympathetic and sensory systems, the greater center.  That shows the reaction of these conditions within the body.  Hence, from this we have the sympathetic conditions produced in the capillaries, that is in the skin [proper] property, in the nerve reaction, heart force, head, larynx, stomach, intestines and spasmodic conditions at times through the forces expressed in the lower end of the hepatic circulation.

4. The organs themselves proper, we find, functioning normally at present, for the condition has been assisted within the system and the correct incentives for the equalization of [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary for each integral force expressed in the cellular forces of the body, both physical, mental and [[Soul|soul]] forces being in their active force, irrespective of organic condition, - that as giving expression through functioning organs, see.

5. Brain forces of body good.  Organs of the sensory system reflect at times an over-secretion in much of the eliminating.  Throat, larynx, excretory functioning, above normal.  Incentive for proper correction.  [[Glands (Esoteric)|Thyroid]] very good. Lungs, expression of the blood forces, as is shown in system from time to time.  Spasmodic conditions at times produced upon the condition of the 7th dorsal.  Heart forces very good at present.  Repression has been the reaction in the systems in times back.  Digestive forces good, the expression at the present time of renewed energy and [[Vibration|vibration]] through the condition in the pneumogastric forces at the present time. These may or may not be kept in balance, depending upon [[Vibration|vibration]] accorded the system and how applied.  Liver, pancreas, duodenal, upper portion of intestines where digestion is completed in system, as it were, of renewed vibration, though laggard in its expression or manifestation in the system, see.  Corrected from former formers [?], yet not active in its normal force.  Hepatic circulation above normal.  Kidneys not very good, though we find the vibration in the nerve force as expressed from those centers that govern the circulation to the kidneys in and with the hepatic proper, give their reaction, and hence we have the spasmodic action proper of kidneys in their emunctory functioning.

6. To keep and give the correct vibration at present, with the present conditions, we would keep much of the stimulation to excretory system and add this, for this add:  Give the rest necessary for the subliminal forces to gain their control within the system and thereby meet the needs of the physical at present.

7. Two bars of carbon tempered steel, one-half inch wide, one-quarter inch thick, four inches long.  Fasten together with non-conductors between.  To these would be attached small copper wires, better if insulated.  To the other end of the wires attach two small copper plates, so that they may be, these plates, easily attached to this body, of [215], we are speaking of.  [Radio Active Appliance]

8. When ready for use each evening, for one hour, these would be attached, these plates, to the body, one on the left ankle, the other on the right wrist, and vice versa, each evening, see, right ankle and left wrist, - left ankle and right wrist, see.  When in use the steel bars would be placed in very cold water or ice water.  The action of this within the system is to create an equalization of nerve force, both in the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic and in the circulation for the body, and keep in the tendency as the body is at present, - do that.

9. Do as we have given, if we would keep the body in and toward the balance within the system of those conditions for the correction and of the keeping correct those with the spiritual, mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body of [215] ..., Brooklyn, N.Y.  We are through.
