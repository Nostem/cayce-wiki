# TEXT OF READING 2824-2 M 1 MONTH

**Reading [[2824-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/10/1942  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10020

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the father - [...], of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2824]''s father.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2824].

3. As we find, while the conditions might be said not to be entirely perfect, but don't mess with 'em now!  For the regular activities, developments and all, are very good.

4. In another three weeks it will be necessary to increase the strength of the feeding.  Do add a little bit of the Lime Water to the formula; this will aid.

5. And a little later, a little [[Calcios|Calcios]] will be well.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  How much Lime Water should be added to the formula as it is?
(A)  Depends on how much is made at a time.  What would be equivalent to half a teaspoonful to the bottle.

8. (Q)  Should this be put in the milk when the formula is prepared, or added to each bottle when it is to be taken?
(A)  It is more efficient if added to each bottle.

9. (Q)  Should anything be used on the eyes except the boric acid solution?
(A)  Use plenty of that, and wash them at least three to four times a day!

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
