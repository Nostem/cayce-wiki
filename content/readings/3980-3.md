# TEXT OF READING 3980-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[3980-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11661

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 8th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3980].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find the general condition in this body somewhat improved from that as we have had here before.  While there are conditions still abnormal, and while the depressions as have come to the body recently, through taxation mentally, and through the temperature and strains under which the body has labored, the body is improved from that as we have had, and we only need to carry out those suggestions as have been given for the body, taking the adjustments necessary for the system to function nominally, taking those injections as will give the incentive for the system to function normal, taking those properties, those exercises, those outlines of treatment as have been given.  Follow those in a consistent manner with the physical attributes of the body, taking into consideration the conditions surrounding the body, and we will bring the normal conditions for this body.

Do that.

> 
> 
> 115 West 35th Street,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mrs. [3980]:
> 
> Yours of the 2nd was forwarded me here.  I'm very glad to

> nicely, and I certainly am in hopes that you continue to do
> so.  I'm surely glad to know that the suggestions as were
> given for you have proven such that it gives you faith and
> confidence in the work that we are trying to do, for it is
> our desire for people to have this confidence - not so much
> in us as individuals, but as for the work that we are trying
> to do, for I believe it is the awakening of the truth of the
> Spirit of God that is within each and everyone of us that
> is only trying to manifest itself through ourselves, as
> individuals, and if this work can awaken these possibilities,
> then we are succeeding in what we are trying to do.
> 
> We shall certainly be very glad to make an appointment for
> your friend, [4890], and I certainly hope and trust that we
> shall be able to give those suggestions that will prove most
> beneficial to her.  You will see from the enclosed card and
> date and hour as set, and as you know, it is necessary we
> have her full name and the address at which she will be
> on the date and hour as is given, and have her, please,
> return this card at once, that we may know that this is a
> satisfactory time for her.
> 
> Thanking you for your interest and assuring you that we are
> glad to hear from you at any time, and you know if anything
> arises that you would like to ask us about, we shall
> appreciate your letting us hear from you.
> 
> As you will notice, we have moved from [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], to
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].  Not just to be moving about, but for some
> time the work itself has given this as the most satisfactory
> place to work from.  So we are led to believe that we will
> be more able to serve mankind from here, and we trust in a
> better and more satisfactory manner.
> 
> With kindest personal regards to yourself and Mr. [3981].
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
