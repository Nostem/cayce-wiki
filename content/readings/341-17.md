# TEXT OF READING 341-17 M 18 (Student, Protestant)

**Reading [[341-17]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/10/1925  Sex: M  Age: 18  ReadingID: 2656

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [341].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [569].]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [341], of ..., Va., together with the Life Reading as given for this body on February 28, 1925, in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], especially the appearance second from the present one.  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] at that time as one, Edgar the 3rd, Standard Bearer. [341-8].  As given in the Life Reading, well that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] understands much of each appearance in the earth's plane, that this may be weighed with the present development that lies within the sphere of mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  You will give such information concerning this appearance, the second one from the present one, that may be of help in the developing of the entity along every line.  You will also give any information concerning this appearance that will aid the entity, [341], in solving the question of his life's work.  You will give any other advice that may help the entity in deciding the life's work and in better understanding the [[Dreams|dreams]] and [[Visions|visions]] as come to the entity, and in maintaining the correct mental balance to keep away from that madness of Mars as warned of in the Life Reading. You will answer any questions that I ask you regarding any of this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given, and the relation of the entity, [341], with those Universal Forces, especially that manifested in that period in earth existences known as Edgar the 3rd, Standard Bearer in Crusade - or Edicut [?]. In this we find the entity, then, in the royal house of then Bavarian Prince, and in the Crusade the entity was the Standard Bearer at the age of nineteen.  The life to the time the entity left for the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] was in that of training and of the development for the position of the one to lead the peoples.  In the second Crusade we find the entity, in entering the hill at the base of same, that overlooks [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]], the entity then lost the physical life from that of the slingers from the wall, see?  and [[Crucifixion|the Cross]] Bearer [See 78-1 on 5/23/25] [See 1005-2 on 7/23/25] upholding the entity - yet in the suffering and the entertaining in the house of those by whom the entity fell, in the study of other religious forces, through special application, brought many changes in the life, [See 849-1 on 6/7/27] yet this innate force as is manifest in oppression, and through suppression, of the desires of self is manifest much in the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], see?  In the developing forces, the urge to study all phases of the religious viewpoint, and an understanding as would be gained by few raised in the environment of the Moslems' point of view, see?  In the keeping then from the influence in Mars, as is seen from this [[Incarnation|sojourn]], and this inclination toward that of destructive forces to [[Physical Body|physical body]] from firearms, we find in the madness of the desire would the greater destructive force be manifest, see?  Only, then, in the twenty-fourth year in the earth plane during this [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  Very good.  Strong of body, strong of mind, strong of will; yet broken much in oppression in the conditions that surrounded same in that land.

3. We are through for the present.
