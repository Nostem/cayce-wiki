# TEXT OF READING 482-10 F 54

**Reading [[482-10]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/4/1939  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 3550

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of May, 1939, in accordance with phone request through sister - Mrs. [340], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the conclusions that have been reached by those examining the body are in accord with the conditions.

4. If the temperature continues, the sooner there is the exercising of those influences to remove the disturbing conditions the better.

5. Complications may arise.  Do not hesitate to use adrenalin should it become necessary; for this would work with this body, even thought - as understood the lung conditions make for a diffusion or a confusion in this direction.  As we find, this would work with this body, if it becomes necessary.

6. (Q)  What is the condition of kidneys?
(A)  This as we find is s sympathetic condition, but an involvement from the temperature.  And the activities of all the influences necessary would, of course, be HARD upon this condition; but if there would be used the extracts or the juices themselves - of the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]], even as a dose of same - it would be most beneficial, - even during the periods of the development for the system.

7. (Q)  What is condition of uterus?
(A)  Very much inflammation, and needs those conditions to correct the system's strain by the flow.

8. (Q)  Do those in charge thoroughly understand the conditions?
(A)  As just indicated, the conclusions are in keeping with the conditions as we find that exist.

9. (Q)  Are operative measures advisable, then?
(A)  As has just been given.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

Wired:  Doctor's conclusions correct.  If temperature continues.  Proceed.  If complications arise, don't hesitate to use adrenalin.  Full information special.
