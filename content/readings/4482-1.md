# TEXT OF READING 4482-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4482-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/2/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12350

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by self, through Miss B. B. Sidelman.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4482], and those conditions as surround the body.

2. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] we find there are disturbing factors that need that awakening, for the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] to function in the material way, as well as those in the spiritual manner; for when physical conditions have been builded by disorders to that extent to where they become active forces with active principles within themselves, then the necessary elements for that awakening - that the balance may be brought to a [[Physical Body|physical body]] - must come from without, as WELL as from within, that the consciousness of a well-being may be awakened within that body-consciousness.

3. These, as we find here, are from poor eliminations that have existed in the system for times back, and having builded those conditions which make for - in the body-functioning - those places where the activity of toxic forces are active WITHIN the body-functioning, make for distresses in the form of neuritis and rheumatism in the organs themselves.  These elements and these conditions that have been named, or as are named, become then active forces for a body, with their resultant reaction in a physical body.

4. We would find were the diet, and the reactions from things taken in system, more of the alkalin - with those added [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the high electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] - these will bring about a better physical condition for the body.

5. While these are active in the system, well that there be kept that continued attitude of SEEING the body replenished, rebuilded, in a mental, a spiritual, and a material way and manner.  This held by the body-consciousness as seeing these things accomplished, will aid ALSO in the correcting in the physical forces of the body.

6. These we would apply as in this manner:  We would take alternately - that is, one dose of one one day, one dose of another the next day - those properties in Milk of Magnesia, the next day Milk of Bismuth, the next day the Phenolax.  We would take EACH day, also, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Violet Ray|VIOLET ray]], this taken in the beginning for three to five minutes - for the first day - particularly over the cerebro-spinal system, following OUT those centers where the disorders in the lower limbs and in the extremities of the body cause disorders at times; gradually increasing this one to two minutes until at least fifteen to twenty minutes of the vibration may be taken.

7. These, we will find, will bring for the physical body the nearer normal elements and activities for the body-consciousness, for the mental forces of the body, for the physical body itself, [4482].  Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Will the electric treatment I have been using be of any real help in relieving the painful condition?
(A)  Taken in the manner as has been outlined here, with the properties as bring about the better eliminations for the system, would - as we find - be more effective.

9. (Q)  Is there any real success in my work to be attained?
(A)  As the body is aided physically, as the body is able to see itself gradually getting PHYSICALLY better and sees that as is the desire of the heart builded in the lives of those whom the body contacts, so may there be success for the body in bringing those necessary elements in the material life to see the success necessary for body, and physical, and mental, and material and spiritual development.

10. (Q)  Will the "Blue Bird" of happiness ever make its nest on my path?
(A)  This depends upon self.  As there is prepared, by the activities of self in the lives and consciousness of others, that that brings happiness to others, so will it bring those things that make for happiness, peace, love and understanding in the heart and the mind of the body.

11. We are through for the present.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 3/18/31 Letter from [4482] to EC; from Hotel Somerset, 150
> West 47th St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].:  "I have heard so much of you through
> Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], and Miss Sidelman, that, at the suggestion of the
> latter, I am writing to ask if you will extend to me the help
> in which I am of such dire need.  For the past five years
> I have been under the influence or control, of the most
> malignant and cruel forces - ill-health - an unsuccessful
> operation, deafness - and worst of all, continued and
> enforced idleness, with no means of support, and I am just
> about at the end of my endurance - unflagging energy and
> effort are useless - every door is barred - hurts,
> humiliations, insults, disappointments, in every direction.
> I have made a name for myself - in the theatre - in [[England|England]],
> [[France|France]], S. [[Africa|Africa]], and [[Australia|Australia]], and America.  But in spite
> of my admitted reputation and standing as an artist (and
> teacher) I cannot find work of any sort.  If you will give me
> a reading at your earliest convenience, and trust to my good
> faith and honesty to pay you the very moment I can earn it -
> I will appreciate it, from the depths of my heart.  With
> grateful thanks for the anticipated help, I am..."
> 
> B2. 3/18/31 Letter from Miss Sidelman to EC re. forming new
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]]:  "There is also a Miss [4482] who is sorely in need
> of help and I know that a reading is the only thing that can
> help her.  Unfortunately, finances prevent.  There should
> be some provision made, at the time the new organization
> is formed, to provide for cases of this kind, - people whom
> a reading would help, to get on their feet, physically,
> mentally and financially, and who would consider it a
> moral obligation to pay as soon as they could."
> 
> B3. 3/21/31 EC's letter to Miss Sidelman:  "I am also in receipt
> of a letter from Miss [4482].  Please understand me when
> I say this - but such cases as Miss [4482] are mighty hard
> to handle.  You should be able to realize this better than
> anyone else, under the circumstances, for I'm sure you know
> there is no one we would rather help than you or yours - yet
> we have made not only one but several attempts to get these
> readings for your parents, and each time it has proven a
> failure.  Now why, I do not know - but there is SOMETHING.
> It ISN'T the money part, no - but there's something else.
> Now, in Miss [4482]'s case, the press of the moment is going
> to be so great that unless we could tell her where she could
> get a [[Book of Job|job]] the next day, I'm afraid a reading would mean very
> little to her, and unless we were to maintain something of a
> bureau for aiding individuals in this direction, it would be
> mighty hard to handle.  Don't understand that I am kicking
> against people being referred to us, but people in such a
> position as this need something else besides a spiritual
> reading - they need more of the material things of life.
> Just how to balance these together is going to be one of the
> main problems at this meeting.  To be sure, this is only to
> be a pre-organization meeting, to set out the real [[Purpose|purposes]]
> of an organization to be formed later.  There are so many
> phases to be considered, and we want to strike that happy
> medium where we can be of the most service to the MOST
> people, irrespective of our individual interests - for
> they are to be thought of last."
> 
> B4. 3/21/31 EC's letter to Miss [4482]:  "I am just in receipt
> of yours of the 18th, and I will have to admit I hardly
> know how to answer you at this time.  We are just in a
> state of transformation ourselves as regarding the manner of
> presenting the information to the public, in a way that it
> will be of the most service to the most people.  Until the
> 6th of April our time is wholly and completely taken up.
> After that time I feel it is going to be necessary for me to
> be away for a few days; consequently, it would be the middle
> of April before I could make a definite appointment.  Now,
> I would like very much to try to be of help.  I do not know
> just how much we can help, but I would like very much to try.
> I know that for one in the circumstances you intimate, to be
> put off makes you inclined to feel we are trying to shift the
> burden of responsibility.  I want to assure you that is not
> my [[Purpose|purpose]], but so many things are crowding in just at the
> present time I can't say otherwise.
> 
> "If you will write me again sometime after the 12th - or
> if it is possible for you to arrange to talk with me when I
> am in N.Y. the week of the 6th - perhaps we can arrange an
> appointment.
> 
> "I appreciate Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] and Miss Sidelman talking with you,
> and I assure you I would like to help if it is possible..."
> 
> B5. 4/6/31 Miss Sidelman's letter:  "In regard to [4482], I
> believe that what she needs is a physical reading to help
> her deafness, and to find out one thing - just why it is she
> cannot get a [[Book of Job|job]]; she's really desperate, but I am trying to
> help her as much as I possibly can.  She is looking forward
> to meeting you, but I have explained the situation to her,
> just what to expect."
> 
> B6. 5/24/31 Letter verifying appointment:  "I would like to know
> if my physical condition, due to ineffective operation,
> will ever improve.  Should I attempt to wear a supporter, or

> shall be for your kind help."
>

> much not being able to keep the appointment for your reading,
> for we realize how anxious you are, but you know how such
> things are.  When Mr. Cayce goes along for several weeks and
> gives out so much of his energy to others, he just has to
> take a few days now and then to recoup.
> 
> "Just as soon as he is able to be at his desk again, he will
> write to you and make another appointment.
> 
> "Hoping that you will understand and bear with us..."

> Time.  As you know, it is necessary for us to have your
