# TEXT OF READING 3470-1 F 53

**Reading [[3470-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/21/1943  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 10946

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3470], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in CORONET.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3470].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  These have to do with the nerve forces of the body, the control of the nerve energies.  There has been some deterioration, and in the locomotory centers, we find that impulses become involuntary that should be voluntary or there is a form of agitans.  These are results from some [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance and the lack of proper elements supplied to the body through the assimilation.

4. We find that operative measures for this disturbance would not be helpful or beneficial.  However, there may come material aid by those applications that may be helpful to this nerve reflex condition indicated in locomotories and throughout the trunk portion of the body at times.

5. Then, these are the applications we would make in the present:

6. Every third day we would have a light [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]], preferably using Witchhazel.  This we would not use with any dry heat in the Cabinet, only the [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]].  Use two teaspoonsful of Witchhazel to the pint of water in the croup cup or an open kettle boiling in the Cabinet, as to allow the steam to settle over the body.

7. Afterwards we would have a brisk rubdown, not too severe but a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], especially in locomotory centers - 3rd dorsal, 6th dorsal - 3rd cervical to the 6th dorsal, the 12th dorsal to the end of the spine.

8. Following this [[Massage Therapy|massage]], we would use this combination of oils to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] into these particular areas though it would be well if these were applied to all portions of the body for a better superficial circulation.

Nujol or Usoline................6 ounces,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].......................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil......................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied)...........1/2 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root.........1/4 ounce,
Oil of Pine Needles...........1/2 ounce.

9. These should be added in the order named.  These will tend to separate.  Shake together when ready to use.  Pour what is to be used in an open container.  Do not pour in hand from bottle, and do not pour back into the bottle that not used. Massage these thoroughly into the body.

10. Also twice each week we would use the Radio-Active Appliance to equalize the circulation.

11. Twice each week we would use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying one time [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Solution, the next time [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].

12. Make the attachment for thirty minutes each time.

13. When the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] is used (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water), attach the small plate to the 9th dorsal.

14. When the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] is used (commercial strength) attach the small plate to the 2nd dorsal.

15. Each time the large plate, through which the Solution passes vibratorially to the body, should be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center - which on this body will be two fingers from the navel center to the right and three fingers up.

16. Each of these applications should also be followed by a massage.

17. Do these, and we will bring better conditions for this body.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Do you advise having teeth extracted to help condition?
(A)  No.  Use Ipana toothpaste, and massage the gums with this.

20. (Q)  What causes burning sensation in left groin?
(A)  The activities to the locomotory center, disintegration.

21. (Q)  What causes my head to shake, and is there any cure for it?
(A)  Beginning of the agitans, as given.

Do these things indicated - and we will have better conditions.

22. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for the masseuse) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(12/21/43 GD's notes:  See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., for preparing [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in your individual case.  Also article explaining theory of the Appliance.)

> seem to be unable to overcome my trouble, though I am certain
> that my faith in the [[Healing|healing]] power of God has helped me in
> many ways.  When I read about your wonderful work in CORONET,
> I remarked to my family, 'This at last is God's answer to
> my [[Prayer|prayers]].  I know Mr. Cayce can cure me.'  I wrote you
> immediately, seeking your help.
> 
> "All my life I have had an interest in psychical research,
> and enjoy reading any books which come within my reach.  I am
> looking forward with great pleasure to receiving your Monthly
> Bulletin, Monthly Research Paper, and printed booklets.
> As my knowledge of psychical research is very limited, I
> probably would not be much help to you as an active member
> at present, but later I do hope to become one.
> 
> "I am enclosing application for Associate Membership,
> together with check for $20.00.  I am very disappointed that
> I may have to wait so long for a reading.  I note that in
> emergency cases you do make an exception, and although I do
> not want to crowd in ahead of others in poor health, I do ask
> you to believe me when I tell you that this is an emergency
> case.  It doesn't mean life or death however, but it does
> mean excruciating pain, which only an operation or torturous
> local treatments every few months will alleviate.  This
> special kind of operation would leave me in bad shape for the
> rest of my life.  Therefore I have fought against it for the

> not know.  So you see, Mr. Cayce, I do need your help as soon
> as possible.  I promise to cooperate with you in every way
> possible.  I truly believe that with God's help, you will be
> able to locate the cause of my trouble, and thereby work a
> complete cure.

> and the wonderful work you are doing."
