# TEXT OF READING 3493-1 M 56

**Reading [[3493-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/22/1943  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 10976

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. R. Lee Page.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mr. [3493]''s wife and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3493].

3. As we find, there are definite disturbances that prevent the normal functionings of the organs and the body's activity at times.  These have to do with the eliminating systems and the effects these, combined with a subluxation, produce upon the central blood supply and the activity in the heart, in the veins and especially in the thrombose area.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body:

5. The blood supply indicates a disturbance with the central circulation, and there are those tendencies towards plethora in the area between the circulation of heart, lungs and liver.

6. We find that these have their causes from impulses received from the central nerve supply that coordinate between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous system at the lower portion of the 9th dorsal.  This pressure there has caused a plethoric condition in the ascending colon area.  And if this were released, the body would gradually be allowed to build back to much nearer a normal pressure and activity in the coordinating of impulses from the sensory system and the reflexes in the brain centers.

7. We would have, then, at least two or three [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations; these scientifically given.  Preferably, these should be given by one such as may be had at Reilly's.  These may be given locally, but not as well.

8. These [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be given about ten days to two weeks apart, and there should be taken at least three.

9. Also those pressures in the 8th and 9th, and 9th and 10th dorsals should be relieved osteopathically, with a good massage and relaxing following same.  But with the first [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, corrections should be made in the pressures.

10. Be careful with the diet.  Refrain from any carbonated waters, or heavy meats - as beef, or quantities of any red meat.  No hog meats of any character for the body.

11. The general massage and the kneading of the colon between and during those periods of giving such treatments will aid in bringing better conditions.

12. Do that.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What is condition of the kidneys?
(A)  These are not disturbed save reflexly in the present. if the system is cleansed and better diet habits are formed, these should not become functionally disturbing.

Do these things and we will find better conditions for this body.

15. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Wife (one for the hydrotherapist and [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(Locally we would refer you to Dr. Vincent H. Ober, Bankers Trust Bldg., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., who is an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] and hydrotherapist.  If and when able to go to [[New York City|New York City]], we would refer you to a physiotherapist, who has had wonderful results in following these Readings:  Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 6th Avenue, [[New York City|N.Y.C]].)
