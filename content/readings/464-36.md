# TEXT OF READING 464-36 F 66

**Reading [[464-36]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/15/1942  Sex: F  Age: 66  ReadingID: 3417

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th Day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [464] - Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [464]; this we have had before.

3. Conditions are not so good.

4. Unless there are greater precautions taken, as we have indicated, these disturbances will bring about weaknesses that will be very aggravating and very detrimental to the body.

5. We would not take more of the vitamin pills until the liver and colon have become more normal in activity, - until there have been at least two more of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, but don't make these too close together.  Have these about ten days apart.

6. The taste from the mouth and stomach should indicate when the bile has begun to flow normally through the system.

7. If it is necessary to produce more of such, we would use the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver for at least two days before the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] are given - even though it may be terrifying in hot or warm weather.

8. At the time the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] is given, do have a massage especially with the oils.  The type of sweat should be preferably that taken in water, not in dry cabinet; reclining in a tub of hot water, you see, or warm water - with the Pine Oils in same; and then a thorough massage afterwards.

9. When better conditions are set up, begin taking the vitamins but NOT in the juices or at the same time juices are taken, for this upsets the body.  Do that.

10. We are through for the present.

> about of a nature that would not improve anyone's health.  As

> against.  I know I am over sensitive and imagine a lot.

> to claim his own He won't leave me here alone.  Although I
> don't know what He could find to do with me, I am just hoping
> He doesn't forget me, for I don't want to stay here and have
> to battle with this sickness and the world all alone.  Are
> you ready to go when the trumpet sounds?"
