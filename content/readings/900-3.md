# TEXT OF READING 900-3 M 29

**Reading [[900-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/4/1924  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5206

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the New Southern Hotel, Parlor L, [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of September, 1924, in accordance with phone request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno.]

Time of Reading                           ... Broadway, 
11:30 A. M.                               [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GD:  You have before you the reading given September 2nd, 1924, [900-2] on [900], % Wm. E. Lauer & Company, 74 Broadway, Room 401, [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.  Has the specific lead been given as mentioned in this reading, and is it the one made by [[Lucius|Lucius]] Greve?

2. EC:  Yes, we have this reading and this body here.  We find the specific lead has been made, that one made while the body, [900], was considering and studying the conditions as outlined in the reading given.  This we find is that one offered through [[Lucius|Lucius]] Greve - G-R-E-V-E - yes, this is correct.  Follow that lead.  We will have some unexpected developments.  We will find through this great amount of monies will come to this body, [900].

3. Do that.

4. We are through.
