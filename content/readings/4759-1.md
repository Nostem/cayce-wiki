# TEXT OF READING 4759-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4759-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/6/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12732

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [715].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, these conditions, as we find, are rather serious; yet taken while there is the opportunity of aiding the system to rebuild, may bring the better results for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  While the tissue is being attacked, and while the temperature as is caused by these conditions in the tissue, this may be overcome by giving the system the opportunity to throw off the refuse forces, even from diseased tissue.  These then, now, are conditions as we find them with this body.

3. The blood supply indicates the character of the conditions existent in the pulmonaries.  This is shown by the nature of the resistance as being attempted by the blood stream, as well as by that shown in the blood stream that is being attempted to be eliminated.  Hence the cause of temperature. While the condition in the lung is serious at present, cell continuing to break, more tissue becoming involved; yet there IS sufficient resistive force in the body to add to and build upon to meet the needs YET, would the body arise, as it were, to meet the needs of these conditions.

4. The nerve system shows the laxness as brought about by temperature, by the tiredness and by the whole condition existent.

5. To meet the needs, then, at present, we would add as this:

6. First much of that as is being done is very good, and would be gradually changed - but we would, to bring the better resistive forces and add to the system to meet the needs of the body, use these as a whole for the body:  First, then, the diet:

7. Little of spirits Fermenti with raw egg and milk, at least once a day.  As much of the fruit and nut diet as the body may assimilate.  This should be alternated from the changes as arise from time to time.  As much [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and codliver oil as will assimilate.  First would be at least half a teaspoonful two or three times each day.  These may be taken half an hour or an hour apart, see?

8. Use as internal medicine, properties as this - taking only one capsule each day.  Preferably this would be taken at evening.  This the dose for each capsule:

Eucalyptol.....................1 minim,
Canadian Balsam..............1/2 minim,
Rectified Oil of Turp........1/2 minim,
Codeine.....................1/60 grain,
Tolu Gum.......................1 gram,
or solution.................2 minims.

9. Also use as an inhalant to clarify the lung proper, and the throat:  Prepare pure apply brandy in charred container - at least half a gallon of same in a gallon or two gallon container, charred.  Keep this where evaporation will take place, but keep it well corked - and inhale into the lung the fumes from same at least three times each day.  When it is first begun the body may only take at least two to three deep inhalations.  As time goes on this may be increased. Not sufficient to strain the system, but inhale sufficient that the body feels the effect of same tingling, as it were, throughout the whole nerve system.  Do that.  As changes come about we would give those necessary things for the improvement of this body, [4759] we are speaking of.  We are through for the present.
