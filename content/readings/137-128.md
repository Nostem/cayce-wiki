# TEXT OF READING 137-128 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-128]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/22/1930  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 518

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce and Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind, business associations and 
relations.  Questions on all phases.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had before.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What caused me to buy those stocks at such a bad time?
(A)  As has been given, there are conditions in which the [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces are to be relied upon wholly by turning to the within of self.  There has also been given that the manifestations of one who has aided, and does give information gained through the same character, or the sources that one obtained same, with the combined attunement of others, of which the self is a part; in either or both - or quick change may be expected, but when these are taken as combine, or when they are only partially taken from each, there may be expected to be the lessons that should be gained, in that as HAS just happened.  As has been given, when there is the so necessity OF those conditions being in accord, turn to the within.  [See 137-127, Par. 22-A on 2/16/30.]

3. (Q)  Is it not intended for me to work with Mrs. [3734] and her experiences?  If yes, how?
(A)  As has been given, the experiences of the body - both OF the bodies that form the group - MUST be IN accord, FOR the perfect understanding or the completion of that wherein the information may be to the advantage, or properly understood.  JUST as has been given.

4. (Q)  What is immediate and future forecast for the stock market?
(A)  As is seen in the present, there are considerable refinances in some direction; also the combinations are the order of the day.  In the present situation, these rumors of both natures will have an unsettling effect, both as to the SUDDEN changes and as to the AMOUNTS in sudden changes. Hence that character, or characteristic - until such are made more complete - of the whipsaw variety, and of unstableness as to AMOUNTS or VOLUME of change.  Some periods being enormous, and others VERY slack - but in industrials and in rails, that are BEARING the brunt of these changes or alterations, will be the conditions - or the ones in which the greater change would come.  Through the present, and in the next month or six weeks, the unstabled condition.  In April and May there SHOULD be - unless sudden changes occur - rather the STABILIZING, and the gradual advancing of those in these lines that show the stabled change, whether refinanced or whether of the combine being more complete.

5. (Q)  Will I be able to cover my present short stocks at a profit?  If so, when?  If not both stocks, will I be able to cover either at a profit?  Which one?
(A)  Both, as we see, unless changes are brought about, may be done in the next five days.  Then there will be rises again, and whipsawing or changes.  Especially is this seen in that of the COMBINING of those as has been given, in Radio, Tel, and Western Union and Westinghouse.  These, and the tentative combine perfection, may be expected to MAKE a CONSIDERABLE CHANGE - either for the better or for the loss, ESPECIALLY in May.  Considerable whipsawing in the near future.  Those of the industrials, or of the chain store, or such, may be expected to DO very much the same; for with the combined efforts being brought about from many sections, will have its influence upon PRICES, and price change. While it will have LITTLE effect upon the amount of dividends declared, or upon the ACCUMULATION of same, or holdings of same in too great volumes in certain or specific centers.

6. (Q)  What is interpretation of Mrs. [3734]'s shamrock experience and my inference of St. Patrick's Day, presented to Mrs. [3734] three successive nights?  [GD's note:  Mrs. [3734], his and [900]'s telephone operator, who had recently 'developed' psychic sensitivity, was Irish-Catholic].
(A)  Seen three times, and that emblem presents the threefold, and the three days in which there will be the threefold change.  More will be presented to self and to the body seeing same, as concerning this.  Be warned, and attune self for the CORRECTION, or CORRECT condition as is to arise about that period, or CENTERING about that date.

7. (Q)  On my desk in office, ... I have attempted to chart the days of next week according to [[Numerology|numerology]].  Have I done this correctly?  If not, what is error?  How shall I correct it? Is my deduction of their meaning according to [[Numerology|numerology]] correct?  If not, what do numbers mean?
(A)  Depending upon the phases of [[Numerology|numerology]], for these to some bring an entirely DIFFERENT [[Vibration|vibration]].  Well were the body to compare with self's interpretation as worked here, that as understood BY Lopez [Vincent Lopez - GD's notes: See Vincent Lopez books later published by Citadel; "Lopez Speaking" in 1960, "Numerology" in 1961.  9/29/41 Mr. [257] wrote EC that Vincent Lopez had published a book on "[[Astrology|Astrology]]" and was a writer every week for American [[Astrology|Astrology]] Magazine.] and his teacher, Michaelson (?), as well as of Mann's [Henry B. Mann's "Introduction to Algebraic Number Theory," [[Ohio|Ohio]] State?] [[Astrology|astrological]] influence of interpretation, even OF numbers, in and through certain periods.  This, as seen, in some manners would dis-QUALIFY numerology for this body.  Then, why so construct for self, other than for the KNOWLEDGE of same and for a MEANS of, through which the various manifested forms of force may speak or manifest to self?  but depend upon, for an understanding, that as is gained by [[Intuition|INTUITIVE]] forces.  Not that both offer an ensample of what force and power may manifest, or show forth in a material plane of cosmic or universal forces, but depend UPON the sources from within, and use THESE, and as that set FORTH, rather as a MEANS of interesting self, expansion of self's understanding, and a means of other FORCES or SOURCES operating THROUGH, and not the source itself, or the manner in which the source operates, be the last word.  Let THAT be in self!  Mann, then, the astrological interpreter, and Lopez and Michaelson (?) those, aided with that of self's interpretation, will give a NEW concept.  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
