# TEXT OF READING 2913-1 F 25

**Reading [[2913-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/30/1928  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 10171

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of August, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [2913], via friend Mrs. [2710].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2710], Miss [2913], and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body very good in most respects. Rather would, or should, there be warnings as respecting physical conditions for the prevention of conditions that might arise from existent forces as are exhibited in the physical functioning of the body at present.  These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [2913], present in this room.  First:

2. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find near nominal in most respects.  There are hindrances in the normal circulation in portions of the central system, especially to that above the subluxation as existent in the 2nd dorsal.  This produces, then, a slowing up of eliminations in a portion of the body, and is one of the warnings as may be given the body.

3. In the elements and constituents of the blood, these are good - save at such times when the cycle of circulation is such that these conditions become overcharged, as is seen when congestion is produced, either from the contraction through cold or through any activity that will bring an overactive force of the sympathetic nervous system above this subluxation.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this very good, though in THIS there is the greater activity toward those that would be subject to changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the system; for through the subluxation as exists in the 2nd dorsal, we find there are reactions from same in various portions of the body that gives improper incentive in the functioning of tissue as radiates from the activity of this nerve plexus or ganglion.  This, as we see, to the upper portion - through the sympathetic and sensory system - affects the vocal forces, or the auditory forces, or the upper portion of the body, and we have that of a full or THUMPING, as it were, to all of the sensory organs of system at such reactions.  This, then, (the fullness as occurs at times to the body) is not a dis-ease, but a discomfort; not disease, but improper incentives for the radial action of the nerve centers. In the LOWER portion, through that of the sympathetic and central nerve system, there comes from this same subluxation those conditions as exist in the functioning of the system towards the absorbing, through the action of assimilation, those properties that at times the body finds disagrees, or indigestion occurring.  This, only slight attack has EVER occurred as yet; yet there is the feeling of the fullness through the action of the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus, through the activity of the ganglion as is controlled through the action of the secondary cardiac plexus.  Hence the body finds times when the appetite is good, but the minute it begins to eat it is immediately full.  This, then, is a reaction - rather than the conditions that NEED correction.  These are effects and NOT the cause of condition.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS, brain forces very good. Exceptional is the body mentally in its abilities in lines that has to do with discrimination, whether of character, characteristics, or of the elements that make for sudden decisions; and when the decisions are made, JUSTLY so, hard to change!

6. In the functioning of the organs of the sympathetic system with that of the sensory system, these, as given, function under the impulse of mis-directed conditions through a subligation existent in the 2nd dorsal plexus.  This subluxation caused by non-activity through conditions existent in times back.  Hence there is seen there were those forces existent that brought about these conditions that affected directly the brachial plexus, or that condition that produced tingling to the whole of the radial forces of the plexus.

7. In the throat, bronchials, larynx, here we find again effects of the conditions as are seen from the slowing up of circulation, REMOVING used tissue from portions of the body.  Not that the condition existent is of the nature that needs peculiar attention, or specific attention; for it is not diseased; discomfort rather than even dis-ease, except occasionally when the condition arises in these regions.

8. Lungs good.

9. Heart's action nominal.

10. Digestion affected through the action of a subligation, as given.  Pancreas and liver only at times when highly sensitive to outside conditions, do we find affections to these portions of the body.  The digestive system, as given, save in the lower portion of same, there needs be those attentions from the reflex forces that will give the near nominal, normal, reaction in same.  Kidneys and the organs of the pelvis show the reactions as occur through a high sensitive reaction in the nerve system, especially of the sympathetics, though not organically deranged there.

11. To remove the conditions and to bring near nominal or normal reaction, would be to correct that condition as exists in the dorsal and cervical region through application of heat and manipulation osteopathically, or chiropractically, so that the proper adjustments are made, and by adding those properties that would assist the body in the circulation to bring about a normal flow, or to give the antiseptic that CLEANSES the system especially in the tissue of the sensory system - the inhalant as has been given, applied nominally, or when such treatments are being given.  Three to five should bring a normal force for this body, [2913].  Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in N.Y. City would be best to make these corrections?
(A)  There are many there.  As we find, Dan Morrison, or any of those that are acquainted with the activities of the force THROUGH which information concerning same is given.

13. (Q)  The body has been told it needs a high climate.  Is that necessary now?
(A)  From existent conditions in the subluxation in the region and the effect this has produced on the central system, these natural inclinations are such as would indicate such conditions.  Make these corrections and the body may be a DIVER if she desires!  We are through for the present.
