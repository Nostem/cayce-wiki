# TEXT OF READING 1561-14 M 59

**Reading [[1561-14]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/23/1940  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 7573

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by Dr. [2246], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
5:35 to 5:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] now being drilled by Carmerson Corporation, on its property in Smith County, [[Texas|Texas]], midway between Big Sandy, in Upshur County, and Winona in Smith County, together with the drilling operations now in progress and the geological formations, especially those concerning the production of oil, and the information previously given regarding these operations through this channel.  You will answer the questions regarding these operations, and information.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations here - Carmerson Corporation - Smith County, [[Texas|Texas]]; the conditions which exist in the present, and the information that has been indicated.

3. As we find, there are the indications in the present operations.  While this in the present is at the lower depth indicated in previous information, and indicates fault, this is rather that through which there may be indicated productive operations.

4. We would continue with the coring until there is the entire change that should indicate the producing sands of sufficient to make the productions in the manners that have been indicated.

5. Continue the coring, then, in the present.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Please explain why coring from 4725 to 4906 has not shown production area as indicated from last information it would?
(A)  As just indicated, in the last depth indicated in previous information there are the natures indicated as related to production sands.

Continue the operations, then, until the harder sands - with the PRODUCING sands - are reached.

8. (Q)  How much farther should that be?
(A)  Whether it's six thousand, ten thousand, twenty thousand, reach the producing sands!  (Left out of original copy)

As indicated, there is production in this operation, - if there is the drilling to same, - as has been given.

We would core through these formations until the harder sands are reached, in which production should be found.

Do that.

9. (Q)  Then, should they drill?
(A)  This will RATHER be where, in the harder production, you see, casing is set - as has been indicated.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. [2246] - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

Wired as follows:  CONTINUE CORING  FULL INFORMATION AIR MAIL
