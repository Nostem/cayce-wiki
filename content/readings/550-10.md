# TEXT OF READING 550-10 M 69

**Reading [[550-10]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/14/1940  Sex: M  Age: 69  ReadingID: 3879

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of September, 1940, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [639] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                             At the Hospital, 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Fla.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - quite serious.

3. Little may be offered as changes from those being administered in the present.

4. For, these - unless there is better coordination in the heart's activity - are close to separations.

5. Keep the body quiet.

6. Keep away from too much sedatives, - but it is necessary for the stimulation of draining the heart's activities.

7. As soon as practical, have more of the neuropathic adjustments.  The body responds best to these.  If practical, do these.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [639] - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
