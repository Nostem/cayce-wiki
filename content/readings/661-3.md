# TEXT OF READING 661-3 M 21

**Reading [[661-3]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/30/1935  Sex: M  Age: 21  ReadingID: 4406

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 30th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [607], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [661]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there needs to be more, now, of those corrections with the application of ELECTRICAL forces along the lower end of the spine to prevent any return of those tendencies for the accumulation in the end of the spine area; else we will have the tendency later on for the system to accumulate such poisons in various centers and cause stiffness to the extremities, or else settling in some organ to make for distress.

3. We would have about ten to fifteen more [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] adjustments, FOLLOWED by the violet ray that makes for only charging the body, see?  this is to the superficial, to be sure; it may be held in the hand, or - preferably - hold it in hand one time and the next time place it on the foot, in opposite portions of the body.

4. (Q)  How often?
(A)  Each time the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are given; following same.

5. (Q)  Would an operation be suitable for the body now?
(A)  The operation may be made, but if these treatments are taken PROPERLY it should not be necessary.

6. (Q)  What causes the bleeding?
(A)  The tendencies for the irritation to continue to arise from this portion of the body.

7. (Q)  Any other suggestions at this time?
(A)  As we find, these will make for corrective forces for the body.

Do that.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Dobbins) Copy to Ass'n file
