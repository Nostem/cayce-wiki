# TEXT OF READING 4120-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4120-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/1/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11822

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of July, 1924, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [4121].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                At her home, 
8:50 A. M.                                     ..., Pa.

1. EC:  Now, we find there are some differences and changes in this body since last we had it here.  In some manners and ways the body is better.  In others we find the corrections have not been made and the general incentives for the system to function through, and under the influence of those conditions keeping then much of the same conditions producing the distress forces in the body.

2. Over portions of the system at the present time, we find that with a reaction in the nerve plexuses, hypogastric and pneumogastric, that we have had some distress through the digestive tract and the organs, as well as the headaches and the pains through lower portion of the chest.  These we find being produced by acute conditions arising from the same specific conditions as are preventing the perfect equilibrium throughout the system.

3. We would for the present condition take these properties in the system, and with the [[Vibration|vibration]] by [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] forces correct the conditions in the spine, and with the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] relieve these strains by giving the incentive necessary for the body to bring the normal functioning.

4. Medicinal properties, taking these:

Tincture of Valerian..............4 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium...............5 grains,
Bromide of Potassium.............20 grains,
Elixia Calisaya...................4 ounces,
Tincture of Capsicum or Extract...2 minims.
Sufficient Simple Syrup to make 11 ounces.

5. Dose would be teaspoonful every three hours until the condition is relaxed by the sedatives and by the relaxing in the dorsal and lumbar regions by the manipulation, which should be taken at least every second day.

6. With the [[Vibration|vibrations]], electricity in [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], at least three to five minutes each evening.

7. Do these, as given.  We will bring the body to the normal conditions.
