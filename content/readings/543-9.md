# TEXT OF READING 543-9 F 22

**Reading [[543-9]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/20/1931  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3842

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of April, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [543].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [543], Sarah and Lu Hesson.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [543] - this we have had before.  There are indications of improvements in the general conditions of the body.  We would keep for the present that line, that manner of treatments as have been and are being administered for the body.

2. We WOULD add some TO those administrations in the form of the saltpetre as WAS given.  This we would give now, that there may be - with these changes that are taking place - that preventative of the activities as respecting the functionings of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] from which the disturbance or the disorder arises in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|lyden]] [Leydig] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and in those [portions] of the system as related to same.

3. We would keep those of the manipulations occasionally, with those of the local applications that will prevent the irritations for the system.  The manipulations should be more in the upper dorsal and in the cervical regions, LOOSENING those tautnesses as are indicated in the upper cervical and in the 1st and 2nd dorsal region.

4. Be very mindful of the diet, that the eliminations in the alimentary canal are kept FULLY active.  Not as an excess, but rather as a complete elimination from the system, and when these do not function normally we would have prepared as THIS to be taken, to aid in this direction, and these will be found to COORDINATE with those things, those properties being administered.  Have six to ten capsules made - this in each capsule - see?

Podophyllum....................1/2 grain,
Leptandrin.......................1 grain,
Sanguinaria....................1/4 grain,
Senna..........................1/8 grain.

5. This will be found to be very active.  While it may produce nausea the first time, provided there has been too much of sweets or of the fruit or vegetable salts in system, but will be found to be a most effective property for the stubborn conditions in this direction that cause much irritation. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  How many light treatments should be taken each week?
(A)  Two to three should be sufficient.  Two of the manipulations, and let these be the day before, then in between, and the day following such treatments.

7. (Q)  Is diet being followed out properly?
(A)  As suggested, be mindful and watch the diet as concerning eliminations.  Do not eat, or PERSIST in eating those things that tend to PRODUCE constipation.

8. (Q)  Is body responding as it should under present medicines being taken?
(A)  We have given that we should keep up with these for the present, at least, ADDING those as given.  Make a half a dose of the saltpetre, see?  and this will be taken at least ONCE each day.

9. (Q)  Should canned corn be eaten?
(A)  Not so well.  The FRESH corn is good.

10. (Q)  Should whole wheat bread be eaten?
(A)  Brown bread is better for the body.

11. (Q)  Is body getting enough exercise?
(A)  Take as much exercise, not to weary the body - for the body should rest of mornings, to be sure.  BEST that the body arise as soon as it awakes, then rest during the morning, see?  This will be better for the body.  Take as much exercise in the open air as is well for the body; that is, so it doesn't tire it too much.  Keeping the mind active, and keeping the body active in the better forces for the system.

12. (Q)  How many times a week could nuts be eaten?
(A)  [[Almonds|Almonds]], and such, may be taken - but not the walnuts or those that carry too much of the fusel oils.

13. That is all the questions regarding this body.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to File
