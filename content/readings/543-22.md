# TEXT OF READING 543-22 F 26

**Reading [[543-22]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/16/1935  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 3855

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [543], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis [referred to below as the ''observer''].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:50 P. M. 
[GD's note:  Since a copy of 543-22 is not on file with 
Edgar Cayce Foundation or elsewhere according to available 
records, the following is a sheet from Mildred Davis' 
"observation book," which she kept over a period of 
approximately two years.  It gives a brief of the reading 
along with her observations.] 
File No. 543-22 
Date:  Jan.,16, 1935 
Time:  P.M. 
[[Vibration|Vibration]]:  Good 
Individual Present:  No 
Personal Reaction to Reading: 
Attitude of Psychic:  Passive.  Looked and acted tired still. 
Much confusion in boys getting off to town. 
Others Present:  Psychic; Conductor; Steno; Observer; 
"Yes, quite a difference in being directed and directing," 
after suggestion was given TWICE, after locating body.

1. Phenomena:  Only slight changes in cause of condition that prevents normal functioning.  Some aid has been brought body.

2. Treatment:

Continue Maypop Bitters. [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs to caecum, lacteals, on right side (directions). Yeast (new activative - Fleichman's - XR?) Alcaroid after heavy daily meal. Caroid Tablets Exercises; Diet, alkalin (suggestions). Mental attitude - toward Divine.  Treatment in Durham Hospital, 1932, not helpful to body. Salt [[Peter (Apostle)|peter]] may be taken later.

3. Reports:

"We are through with this reading."  There had been another, but psychic appeared tired so it was not included. Complained of headache.  Frowned upon awaking, but soon became cheerful.
