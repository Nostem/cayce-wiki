# TEXT OF READING 195-28 M 42 (Realtor, Mfr. of Automobile

**Reading [[195-28]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/4/1926  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 741

Parts, Accessories) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of May, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [195], and the enquiring mind of this body, of ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [195].

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to this body in the way and manner as we have given.  That is, when the body-conscious mind has weighed in the inner self there is presented, through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] experiences, conditions as regarding same.  These are presented in an emblematical manner, as for the benefit to the conscious mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

4. (Q)  "I dreamed I was associated with some man in a new idea of dispensing oil or gasoline through some unit ownership of the dispensing pumps handled by operating company on a royalty basis.  There was some lawsuit on between myself and someone (I don't know who).  Suit was over some misrepresentation or breach of contract and just as I was assured of victory in lawsuit the other party won through some predetermined technicality."
(A)  In this we see there is presented a condition wherein [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has given time, thought and study, to the engaging in some business concerning such an arrangement of dispensing oil, gas or gasoline, and that, if [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] were to enter into same, under the present existing conditions, with the papers, contracts, in their present condition, there would ensue from same lawsuits that would become detrimental and prove a loss to the entity.

Then, the lesson is as the warning to the entity, [195], not to enter into this condition, for there will come losses through same.  For, as has been given, there would be given to the entity those understandings and [[Visions|visions]] and dreams when there was the approaching condition of loss or gain, so do not enter these at this time.

5. (Q)  Is Mr. Sherman capable of putting over in [..., 0hio] and other places as planned the unit ownership and cooperative handling of these dispensing devices?  Will he make a success here?
(A)  (Interrupting)  Capable of putting over such, but under the present conditions, neither in ... nor in any other place would be made the success as has been set out, see?  Not that the idea isn't correct.  The man's principle isn't correct as yet.  The warning is to [195] and not to the conditions as to the man.  Do not enter into same, unless there may be expected, and the entity is prepared to meet, law suit and losses in same.  Be warned, for, as we have seen, as we have given, many such things, such conditions, for gain and the prevention of losses, will be presented to the entity through [[Visions|vision]].

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " EC file

Wired also:  DO NOT ENTER INTO THE ARRANGEMENT REGARDING DISPENSING MACHINE UNTIL YOU SEE REPORT.
