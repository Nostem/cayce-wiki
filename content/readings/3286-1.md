# TEXT OF READING 3286-1 F 27 (Religious preference:  "None"

**Reading [[3286-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/11/1943  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 10706

(Jewish parentage)) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3286], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]], and Mrs. [2982] and Mrs. [3051].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3286].

3. Conditions here, as we find, are the after effects of an infection and a very high temperature in the body; thus destroying much of the superficial and sympathetic circulation.  While nerves of a nature are indicated through the limbs and knees, and the hands and wrists, this is rather of a superficial nature, or not coordinating with the central nervous system directly.

4. The attitudes that have been held by the body have been and are helpful, yet these conditions - being [[Karma|karmic]] - must be met by the body; in adding to the being and [[Soul|soul]] of the body, patience, kindness, brotherly love, long suffering and the like.

5. Physically help may be gained by the use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying gold, silver and [[Iodine|iodine]] alternately.  This would require three different connections to the Appliance. Attach the Appliance for thirty minutes each day, one day using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water), the next day using Nitrate of Silver Solution (in the proportions of one ounce of a two per cent solution in two ounces of grain alcohol) and the next day using [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] - which is [[Atomidine|atomic iodine]] (in the proportions of one ounce of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in two ounces of distilled water).  Alternate these in this manner:  First the gold, the next day the silver, the next day the Atomidine and so on.  Do not allow the plates or connections to remain in solution when not in use.  Wipe them off each time.  Each of these solutions should be changed after it has been used fifteen times.

6. The charging solution for the Appliance should be changed every thirty days.  Make the charge regular strength.  Do not use Charcoal in this solution.

7. Each day the large plate through which the solution passes vibratorially, should be attached to the umbilical center - two fingers from the navel to the right and one finger up from that point.

8. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the small plate would be attached at the 9th dorsal.

9. The next day when using the Silver Solution, apply the small plate to the 2nd dorsal.  The third day when using the Atomidine, attach the small plate to the 4th lumbar or lumbar axis.

10. Each day following the Appliance massage the body around the waist, across the hips, over the shoulders and down the arms, and down the limbs, using this combination of oils; adding these in the order named:

Russian White Oil as the base.........6 ounces,
(or Nujol or Usoline)
Oil of Pine Needles...................2 ounces,
Olive Oil.............................1 ounce,
Peanut Oil............................2 ounces,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras Oil]].......................1/2 ounce.

These will separate, but shake together each time they are used, pour in a saucer and massage into the body over the areas indicated, all that the body will absorb.

11. Do keep the braces when attempting to walk.  Do use the supports.  Keep the braces on wrist if there is activity with same.  To be sure, remove same when the treatments are applied.

12. Be persistent, be consistent, be instant in [[Prayer|prayer]].

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Which foods are detrimental to this attainment, and which are conducive?
(A)  Abstain from too great a quantity of starches or any quantity of sweets if meats are taken.  Meats should consist of fish, fowl, or lamb, but none ever fried.

15. (Q)  What must I do to attain perfect physical strength?
(A)  As just given but remember the help must come within self, through self, and making self right with [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

16. (Q)  How can I overcome fear of falling?
(A)  Putting your trust in Him, who is life.

17. (Q)  What must be done to my right knee to strengthen its muscles and keep it from unlocking?
(A)  Keep up the rubs as has been given.

18. (Q)  Are the exercises I do now beneficial to me?
(A)  These will be easier to be done if these massages are followed properly.

Do as outlined.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] according to specifications in your particular case.  Also see article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of this Appliance.)

> express that perfection.
