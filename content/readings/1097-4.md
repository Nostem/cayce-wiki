# TEXT OF READING 1097-4 M 46 (Bank Liquidating Agent,

**Reading [[1097-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/9/1929  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 6282

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [1097], via Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Linden Shroyer and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                  ... Bank, 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind - questions regarding business 
associations and relations)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [1097].  This we have had before.  Many changes in the mental, physical, social and financial way, have come to this body since we had same here before, and as the body's NUMBERS would indicate, many changes are YET to come into the experience of the body, in the business, social, and financial manner. Changes of scenes will even be greater than they have in the experience the body has had since last we had same here.  Applied in the correct way and manner, much may be accomplished by the body, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], through the experiences as come to same.  While there may have come, and do come, disappointments in the material sense; yet will the body keep self in that same mental attitude that has so long influenced the body in its relationships to others, in that of being acting for others in so many varied respects, advising, counseling, aiding others to understand their relationships with their fellow man, the body will find these will be returned TO self in no uncertain way and manner, and there will come the peace and understanding that comes only from service, and in the applying of self and self's abilities to the strengthening of the [[Purpose|purposes]] of life in the individuals whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] contacts.  There will be, in the not too far distant future, opportunities in DIFFERENT channels.  In the State in which the body at present labors, will recognize that service as has been rendered to same through the efforts of the body, and OFFER - through one of two channels - other propositions for the body.  There, too, will come, from a distance, the offer of the position as comptroller, by an organization that is worthy of being considered.  As has been indicated to the body, in larger fields of activity the body will find the opportunities for the advancements in the material, the financial, and in the business affairs.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What position, if any, will I be offered in the near future?  By whom?
(A)  By the State, and by others in other lines of endeavors.

3. (Q)  When will the offer be made?
(A)  June, July, and September.

4. (Q)  Should I resign my present position?  and accept the offer that will be made me from other sources?
(A)  Not at present.  Wait until all of these have been at least broached in such a manner that the body may choose between the three.

5. (Q)  Will I give satisfaction and be successful in the new line of work?
(A)  As the body has labored, CONTINUE in that as has been given, and satisfaction will result.  [1097] we are speaking of.

6. (Q)  Will myself and family enjoy good health and prosperity should we locate in a large city?
(A)  In the East, yes.  IN the South or Southeast, no.

7. (Q)  What city would be the most advisable?
(A)  [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], Pittsburgh, or [[New York City|New York]].

8. (Q)  Will the Federal Farm Bureau at Washington offer me an appointment as one of its Examiners for [[Texas|Texas]]?
(A)  It will.

9. (Q)  What other steps should I take in order to expedite an appointment as an Examiner for the Federal Farm Bureau at Washington?
(A)  Only keep up those fences (as would be termed in common parlance) that have been begun to be builded through those channels through which such considerations are given individuals who have rendered a service to the State. That meaning, then, through the REPRESENTATIVES OF State, in their VARIOUS offices.

10. (Q)  What is the business forecast for my future?
(A)  Very good.

11. (Q)  What steps are most suitable for me to take in order to come-back as a successful business man?
(A)  The application of self's abilities toward that in hand, and as the various conditions and advancements arise, apply self in that manner as befitting that understanding the body has respecting same; for in application of self, knowledge and understanding and ABILITY shows forth; even as the light that is set of a hill.  While these may be darkened by varied conditions as arise in the experiences of a body from period to period, yet holding forth as steady - and as using the knowledge aright, that is in hand, those of the elements that give the knowledge of application are ACTIVE in action.  In staleness one finds the ability to USE that as is known in any direction to become as a straw in the hand, and unfit, unuseful, in the battle of life.  Life is a battle, in that each must put on the whole armor and be invulnerable to those LITTLE things that do so hinder; and let not self nor self-exultation become a STUMBLING block to thine endeavors.  We are through.
