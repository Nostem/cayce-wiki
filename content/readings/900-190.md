# TEXT OF READING 900-190 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-190]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/1/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5393

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Street, 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, that come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], are in that way and manner as have been given, and may be used, and SHOULD be used, by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the way as has been given for same.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Thursday Night, Jan. 28, or Friday Morning, Jan. 29, 1926.  Voice:  "THE MARKET IS A SALE SATURDAY."
(A)  This, as we see, the indication of co-relations of information as has been gained, through the conscious conditions, of those relating to the prices on the market, and the conditions as have been given as information to the mind, weighed with those of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], until it brings to the consciousness of those conditions, as is seen, for a sale for same, see?

4. (Q)  Does this indicate we should sell more General Electric short Saturday?
(A)  Sell any stock short, but as has been given, there will be so much change in these, best that these be deferred until that as given.

5. (Q)  As given, General Electric is "Much too High," and we accordingly sold at 334.  Where should we sell more - at what price?
(A)  This, as we see, should be rather followed as that as has been given regarding General Electric, or any other stocks, see?

6. (Q)  If it is a sale, to what level will it go - i.e. at what price should we re-purchase?
(A)  Sell, or be out of same at the low level, as will be reached about the 8th to 9th.

7. (Q)  Voice:  "You will be taken care of - this has been granted you."
(A)  Again we find the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces correlating with those conditions as have been carried in the mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], by that as has been spoken, by that as has been thought or studied on, and is as of those inner forces replying, or giving the answer to those - physically, mentally, morally, financially - would the entity depend upon these - be taken care of.

8. (Q)  Saw myself holding down eye of Mrs. [139].  Impression again that I should read anatomy - that the divine within would point many ways to cure, hidden to physical mind of doctors - that I might give the sign of being One with Him - to heal the sick and ease the suffering.
(A)  This again those same correlations, as we see, as are thought, studied, by the entity, and as these are given in these [[Visions|visions]], of how the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of entity may better be prepared to meet those conditions as may arise in the experiences of the entity, regarding every phase of the phenomena as is manifested to the entity, see; then study such as is found in Gray's Anatomy, or in that of Tarbell's. These will give that information, as will be seen - little by little; not try to comprehend all at once - little by little; for the more we find as to the entity's conception of as to how phenomenized forces are manifested in the physical world, more and more may the study of physical conditions bring to the mind the better understanding of how the phenomena itself may be made manifest in the individual, see.

9. We are through with this reading.
