# TEXT OF READING 862-6 M ADULT

**Reading [[862-6]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/8/1937  Sex: M  ReadingID: 5125

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of October, 1937, in accordance with request made by his niece, Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find with this body, [862], rather acute conditions have arisen - from these sources or causes:

3. With the keeping in the system of those activities and properties as would purify and cleanse the blood stream from impurities, there was a RENEWING through the activity.

4. Then the breaking OVER, or taking into the system properties that were at variance to those things indicated for the body, produced rather an acute condition; which has arisen and is shown in the lower extremities, where foreign substance from old injuries makes for conditions of accumulations about same.

5. Hence we would find the NECESSITY of operative forces that there may be the removal of not only the sac but of the properties themselves.

6. And then the applications and the keeping of those things for purification of the body and of the system itself.

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is the foreign object which the X-Ray shows?
(A)  As has been indicated, something from outside. A portion from the old injury.  Metal.

10. (Q)  Is this condition Synovitis?
(A)  Call it what you will!  It is that which has been indicated; the overtaxing of the system when there was a renewing, and it is the fluids of the system gathering about or localizing in the attempt to ward off conditions - but they have become so exercised as to segregate.  And as inflammation arises it is necessary, as has been indicated, for operative forces.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [601]
"    " Ass'n file

Wired to Mrs. [601]:  NECESSARY TO REMOVE NOT ONLY SAC BUT FOREIGN SUBSTANCE BY OPERATION.
