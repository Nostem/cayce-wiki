# TEXT OF READING 5462-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[5462-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/28/1929  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13663

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by his father - Dr. [...] [a dentist], recommended by Mrs. [1192].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1192], [5462]''s father and Mr. Hyman Levy.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5462].  Now, we find the conditions as are disturbing in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body have to do with improper coordination in the physical functioning, and the relationships between those activities in the changes as are wrought in [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  The incoordination may be of many characters.  In THIS individual, as WE find, it being a specific condition having to do with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], as in its connection through the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal system, as relate to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] and lyden [Leydig?] glands, and the effect as produced is the abnormal developments in physical functioning in different directions, and the EFFECTS as are produced produce abnormalities in varying degrees IN the system as respecting same, so that the coordination between that as may be called the impulse or white tissue in nerve centers and nerve ganglia, and that of the gray as in ITS activity in the variations as are produced in the system - these are the disturbing forces as WE find with the body, and in meeting the needs of the conditions these may be altered by the variations in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as may be set up IN the system through the administering of those elements that, having their metallic effect upon the system, being absorbed in the proper proportions for the system to create the different changes in the system, and then by the administering of those elements or [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as would change or alter these, as to the effect they may produce in the system - by the stimulation through the various ganglia OF the system, coordinating between the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic system - these, then, would be taken into the system through those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be carried in the Radio-Active Appliance, as would carry both Gold - in a state wherein assimilation of the vibratory forces may aid in the tissue as is related to the active forces between the white and gray tissue - ALSO that would carry those of the silver in the nitrate state, through the same, that would strengthen and produce the better metabolism in the system, as is related TO the forces as govern all of the SENSORY system - so that the coordination between sympathetic and the sensory organisms, as related to their vibratory forces in system, would be changed.  These should be given, then, in their regular alternate manner - that is, the vibratory forces from Gold for at least three to five days.  Resting one to two days.  Then those vibrations of the silver nitrate, this being carried through the same channels, or through the same vibratory forces as would be created from the low form of electrical current that passes from and to the body through such an appliance.  The Gold should be made in the ratio of two grains to ONE ounce, in this particular vibration.  Each case, or each application, should be of at least thirty to sixty minutes duration, followed - in at least three to five hours after such - by the massage, not a treatment, but a MASSAGE following the lines of the nerves as would be used, or is used, by those in the neuropathic profession.  Do that, if we would gain any aid to this system.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What proportion should the nitrate of silver be?
(A)  In the same proportion as in its standard - though we would reduce the ordinary by one to twenty, changing same with that in which it is soluble.

3. (Q)  What diet would be best for this body, and what foods should be avoided, if any?
(A)  The diets should be principally those that are of body building, and be sure that the system is kept more alkalin than acid - so that the developments as come through the application of mental forces as may be developed with the application to those in the system, as related to the nerve systems and the glands, have  their development in a normal manner.  Changing these according to that as is assimilated. Nothing that becomes heavy, or NOT assimilated, should be continued in system.  Be sure that, with the applications and the anodes - that they are kept in a state of normal preservation, shining or rubbing with some substance as to clear same; attaching both to the 2nd cervical center in the small anode.  For the Gold, the larger anode, or the nickel anode, of the larger dimension, should be attached to the umbilicus.  In attaching the SILVER solution, this the last one attached - should be attached to the 10th dorsal center.

4. (Q)  What is to be done in the event of a reaction?
(A)  This is to be met by the normal - Use consistency with those applications for the system.

5. (Q)  Are breads to be avoided?
(A)  Those of the brown or whole wheat may be used, but white or soggy of any character should be avoided.

6. (Q)  Is insulin of any value?
(A)  Value to the system in as to the character of the changes produced in the activities in the system, regulating the effect of sugars for the system - but in the application of these vibrations as are changed - THESE would take care of any condition as regulating these conditions.

7. (Q)  What would be the dosage of insulin?
(A)  One Hundred centimeters.  We wouldn't use insulin WITH the VIBRATIONS set up.

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
