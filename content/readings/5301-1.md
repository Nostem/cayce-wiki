# TEXT OF READING 5301-1 F 42

**Reading [[5301-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/26/1944  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 13437

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [5301], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  These, as we find, are very aggravating conditions, for the disturbances arise from the breaking of the activities between the sensory system, its circulation, its lymph activity and the organs of the central nervous system and eliminating system.

3. Thus the conditions which exist in the eyes and more in the portions as the lids than the optic itself; producing redness, constant itching as well as a general nervous condition throughout the body.

4. These will require a great deal of patience, a great deal of persistence and care as to diet, as well as the applications which may be administered for beneficial conditions for the body.

5. First, in the diet, do not take sweets of any character.  Do not take carbonated water of any character.

6. Do set up better eliminations by having regular periods when small quantities of Eno Salts, a mineral laxative, would be taken; a teaspoonful every morning for five mornings.  Leave off for at least two weeks, and then take for five days again and so on.

7. Do have the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] which would carry to the body both [[Iodine|iodine]] and gold.  These should be prepared with the [[Iodine|iodine]] charged in such an Appliance.  This should carry the solutions for making the static electrical forces.  Use two pounds of the Copper Sulfate and other ingredients in proportion, in a gallon and a half of distilled or rain water.  No charcoal in this.  Do, when these have been prepared, suspend the [[Iodine|iodine]] in a glass bottle, preferably with a glass cork, charge, and not higher than the low amperage, for at least half an hour.

8. Then use the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Solution, one grain to one ounce of distilled water, using three ounces.

9. The larger plate should be attached to the body at the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, which for this body would be four fingers from the navel center directly to the right, and two fingers up from that point.  The smaller plate would be attached at the 2nd and 3rd dorsal center, or the brachial center, from which the nerves of the optics obtain their impulses from the central nervous or cerebrospinal nervous system.

10. Use this each day for at least thirty minutes.  Do keep the plates very clean.  Do disconnect when not in use.  Use for thirty days, then recharge the Appliance, using the same quantity and the same container, with the same solution of the Tincture of Iodine.  Change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] to a new solution, using at least three ounces.

11. Do have a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] after the use of the Appliance.  Commence at the base of the brain, around the ears, along the mastoid areas, the nerves of the throat, the larger nerve and blood supply areas, on either side of the head, 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th and 6th dorsal.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this gently but thoroughly and away from the head at each treatment.

12. Do these and then each and every other evening, have scraped Irish potato, old, not new, and apply on gauze over the eyes. Keep this on for at least one hour, then cleanse the eye with any good antiseptic solution, using cotton tuft for same.

13. Thus we will bring bettered conditions, we will relieve these general tensions.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What causes distressing dizziness for two years?
(A)  The incoordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic impulses through the body.

16. (Q)  What causes black specks on teeth at gum line, which increase rapidly in size and have to be ground out and filled?
(A)  These are from the lack of elements in the system which work from and to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] which produced the calcium which supplies circulation in teeth.  The iodine and Gold will supply elements through the manner indicated, taken in the assimilated foods to assist in supplying elements in these areas.

17. (Q)  Is the work I am doing detrimental to my health and is that the reason for my unhappiness with it?
(A)  This has been the source.  For the first of these disturbances came from such fumes which were inhaled which were part of eye disturbance.  But the unbalanced condition in system allowed the head to be susceptible to same.  We would remain in these activities until improved and then to more of office or secretarial work.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    "  Ass'n file "   for indexing

(Please see instruction sheet [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. Godfrey, 2709 LaFayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this case.)
