# TEXT OF READING 1739-5 M 36

**Reading [[1739-5]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/28/1930  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 7959

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Messrs. Levy, Benstock and [1739].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1739], this we have had before. The body physically and mentally continues to be on the improve.  Time, precaution, proper amount of mental and physical labors, or proper amount of physical and mental exercises are as necessary as applications MATERIALLY or physically for the corrections.  Keep the manipulations in such a manner as to keep the proper coordination.  Keep the proper equilibrium of the taxations from becoming centralized throughout the alimentary canal, or a tendency for that of an alkalin reaction - for developments of the body throughout are more active in the alkalin than an acid; ESPECIALLY is this true in that age of hustle and bustle.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How often should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given now?
(A)  At least every OTHER day.

3. (Q)  Are all treatments given properly?
(A)  Very good.  Do something for self too.

4. (Q)  Any further suggestions?
(A)  Just do these at present.

5. (Q)  May the body have permission to present a book on Christian Science to the hospital?
(A)  It may.

6. (Q)  Would body be doing right to try to help the body [1122] along Christian Science treatments, with her permission?  or any other patients at the hospital, only with their consent?
(A)  Do not force an issue with anyone.  PROPERLY given, might be very good.  Now the case of [1122], THIS is NOT the CHARACTER of treatment for THAT body.

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this series of readings.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
