# TEXT OF READING 5450-2 F 19

**Reading [[5450-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/25/1930  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 13622

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [5450].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Lydic, L. J. Hesson, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we have the body here, [5450].  This we have had before.  In SOME ways we have improvements; in others not much change.  There is continued that in the blood stream which forms accumulations in various portions of the system, producing those of the eruptions between the cuticle and the epidermis.

2. We would give this, at the present - see?  Prepare in this way and manner:  To 1/2 gallon of RAIN water - not distilled, but RAIN water - add:

Wild Cherry Bark..................1 ounce,
Sarsaparilla Root.................1 ounce,
Burdock Root......................1 ounce,
Yellow Dock Root..................1 ounce,
Dogfennel.......................1/2 ounce,
Cinchona Bark...................1/2 ounce.

3. Reduce this by simmering, not boiling, to 1/2 the quantity. Strain while warm and add 2 ounces of Grain Alcohol, with 2 drams of Balsam of Tolu cut in same.  This as the preservative.  Shake solution together before the dosage is taken.  The dosage should be half a teaspoonful four times each day.  Take it before the meals, but eat the meals regularly - and on time!  Do not overtax the system with too much meats, and especially of highly SEASONED foods.

4. We would ALSO use the high enemas to cleanse the colon.  At first these should be taken once each week, until at least four have been taken.  Then they may be taken once each month.

5. Do this until the whole quantity of medicinal properties have been taken.  This will last a month just a month.  Do that.

6. (Q)  Should glasses be discontinued at once?  Or be changed?
(A)  Changed.

7. (Q)  Why do eyes hurt?
(A)  Glasses!  That's why we said change 'em!

8. (Q)  What causes pains on top of head?
(A)  Pressure from conditions in colon.

9. (Q)  What is cause of red mark under right eye?
(A)  This from conditions prenatal.

Do as we have given for the present.

10. We are through with these readings.

Copy to Sister [349]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
