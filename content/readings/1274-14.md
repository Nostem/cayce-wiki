# TEXT OF READING 1274-14

**Reading [[1274-14]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/17/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6879

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1928.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. 
[Fourteenth reading given on Broad Creek Channel treasure 
Monroe County, [[Florida|Florida]].  On 2/24/32 reading was loaned to Mr. 
[943] by HLC and not returned.]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/12/28 Mr. [943]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> We spent all last week and part of the week before trying to
> locate the treasure at Broad Creek channel and it seems
> impossible to find it as we followed information given as
> closely as it is possible for human being to do.  We built
> a coffer dam around about three fourth of the forty square
> feet and with shovels and buckets bailed out the mud and
> water [See Source File Key #1274 for hand drawn map re
> 1274-14, Par. R1] down to hard rock.  Something seems to be
> wrong and I would like for you to give another reading on it
> to see just what is missing.  I feel that we are entitled to
> this one because we have done so much work on it and I still
> feel that it is there.  Parties that has [have?] been working
> with me are very much disheartened and I can't blame them.  I
> thought that if you would give another reading that I would
> go there myself without the aid of anyone else and see what
> effect that would have if any.  I would suggest that the
> question be along the following lines:
> 
> "You have before you the buried treasure near Broad Creek
> Channel, Monroe County, [[Florida|Florida]], on which you have given
> information that the treasure would be found near the center
> of a forty square feet space using a location where a block
> was found as the South East corner.  Almost three fourths of
> this space has been excavated down to hard rock and balance
> of space thoroughly probed down to the rock and nothing
> found.  Will you give the reason why [943] has been unable
> to locate the treasure according to information given and if
> information given is correct where the error was made.  Is it
> possible to give more detail information how to locate it and
> what to do to follow out instructions as given."
> 
> I will make one more attempt at trying to locate it,
> Mr. Cayce, if you will furnish me with another reading along
> the lines as above and if you can suggest anything more that
> would get us what we want add to the above suggestion.  I
> still believe in the information and would like to prove
> this.  Please give a reading this week if possible.
> 
> With kindest personal regard
> 
> Sincerely, [943]
> 
> R2. 3/22/28 See 1274-15.
> 
> R3. 2/24/32 Loaned to Mr. [943] by HLC - Not ret'd.
