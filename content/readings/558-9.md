# TEXT OF READING 558-9 F 41

**Reading [[558-9]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/9/1943  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 3950

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the brother - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.                       ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [558].  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Would Thursday morning be a good day for the operation?
(A)  If the one who is to make the operation is decided that the body is in condition for such, then it would be well. So far as the [[Astrology|astrological]] influences are concerned, this is [[Ideal|ideal]].

4. (Q)  What will be the reaction to her body?
(A)  This may not be given.  This depends too much upon whether there is agreement in the body, mind and [[Purpose|purpose]] or [[Soul|soul]].  Where two agree, and ask, it shall be done.

5. (Q)  Dr. Forman and Dr. Kassebohm [Fred A. Kassebohm, M.D.?] are the doctors.  Please advise them for any special care that should be taken.
(A)  Let them do their [[Book of Job|job]]!  We will not do it for them!

6. We are through.

Copy to Mr. [1565] - Phoned to Mr. [257], Raleigh Hotel, Washington, D.C.
"    " Ass'n file
