# TEXT OF READING 5703-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[5703-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/4/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14152

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 4th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M.                                      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions in this body, they have to do with the emunctory and lymphatic conditions throughout the system, the effects this produces in the body makes the abnormal conditions, yet they all may be reached through the correct or the proper correction of [[Vibration|vibrations]] and the effect the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]] will give over the system, just in the same manner and way this overcharge and broken tissue in the lymphatic centers produce in the body at present giving to this body the weakness, the debilitated condition, the inability to retain foods in the proper manner, the action of the digestive forces, the condition through the lungs and larynx and the high forces produced in the hepatics, temperature, we see.  By the correction of these, these will be overcome in the same manner as they are submerged or under the influences of the condition at present.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, Miss [5703], we are speaking of, present in this room:  The blood forces, while the red blood cells are deficient the number and the quality of the red blood being exercised upon by the condition as produced in the hepatic circulation, that is the eliminating forces, you see, in the liver and kidneys produced by action or by the cell force of the lymphatic forces in these organs themselves, and the inability with the conditions existing in the pulmonaries of having the blood forces correctly carbonized by the air forces with the condition existing in the lungs, more from the action of lymphatic cells, again, than of organic or foreign conditions existing.  The nerve forces are good when we consider the debilitated circulation.  The brain forces are over and above the normal and control the body a great deal.  The body reasons well with every condition before it makes it a part of itself.  That is of great assistance to this body with the conditions in the system.  The organs themselves, we find through the lungs a plethora condition rather than of a closed cell force, hence the mucus or sputum from the excess of the lymphatic action is easily, at times, thrown off, again, it, the condition, works to the opposite force. Through the digestive tract there is the lack of forces through the elemental, that is, blood supplying forces to these, to carry the normal functioning and the normal functioning towards elimination as well as rebuilding, so the body through certain cycles of organs rebuilds quickly and again, is easily overcome or overtaxed.  The condition as we find through the pelvic organs, the kidneys and those organs of the true and false pelvis are only in their weakened state of functioning, yet as organs or as organic conditions there, we only find the reflex from the whole condition over the body as produced from cells giving away through overstrain within the lymphatic centers.

3. To give the relief as necessary and to reduce the high state existing in blood forces, we would give those that would reach the system without giving strength or using the strength of the organs to propagate or give the incentive to the system.  Do this:

4. Take a two or five gallon keg, (one charred well on the inside) place in this with the keg on end, one-half gallon of grain alcohol, add to this two ounces of Oil of White Pine, two ounces of Benzosol, one-half ounce of Oil of Eucalyptus. There is to be so attached to this end of the keg that is used, a tube whereby this body may inhale gases formed off these properties, not the material itself, but the gas.  This should be taken into the lungs through the bronchials, the larynx and the lung forces, the gas, twice each day for three to ten minutes.  The solution must be shaken before the gas is inhaled off of this and must be corked tight when not in use.

5. Take into the system Yogurt, one tablet, three times each day.  Let the diet be that, that will give the vital forces to the body, principally of nuts or nut fats, eggs and milk but warm milk with the heat of the animal and not of the fire.  Do that.  We will bring the proper forces and incentives to this body, Miss [5703] here present in this room.

6. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, what causes the left side of this body's abdomen to swell?
(A)  The condition that exists through the hepatic circulation as we have given here causing the overtaxation of this portion of the body, see.

7. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, what causes this body's shoulders to ache?
(A)  The action of the blood forces as they are carried into the lungs for clarification giving the effect to the nerves of the plexus across the shoulders, see, of the brachial plexus.

8. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, can anything be done to relieve the trouble in the left eye?
(A)  Only by the rebuilding of the vital forces of the body and existing in the sensory system through the general forces in the body.

9. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, what causes the fast pulse beats of this body?
(A)  With the condition as existing as we have given here in the hepatic circulation through all the rest of the system, you see.  We have given these conditions here.

10. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, one more question, where does the blood come from and what causes it in this body?
(A)  We have just given the condition through the lung forces here with the action of the lymphatic forces in the lungs themselves, not from a broken or a closed tissue but the plethora condition of the lungs and give off to the mucus action of the body.  We have given the condition existing in this body.  We are through.
