# TEXT OF READING 5522-2 F 34

**Reading [[5522-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/7/1929  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 13779

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by Dr. J. E. Miller, D.C.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Miller, Mr. Miller, Mrs. Robinson, Mr. and Mrs. [[Rome|Rome]], Mr. Hargis, Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Drs. Berger and Hansen, and [5522]''s husband.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. Now, the general physical conditions as we find show improvements in a general way and manner.  The changes as we would give for the continuation of these recuperative forces, would be to create a more alkalin condition through the digestive system, that the eliminations as are carried on through the system do not tax the organs of elimination, nor allow dross accumulation through any portion of the system where the blood supply would pick up or carry back into circulation destructive forces.

2. Then, we would add those of the alkalin Petrolagar.  To each dose add one to two drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  These will act as alkalin reactions, with an intestinal antiseptic.

3. Keep the manipulations and corrections along the cerebro-spinal system.

4. Keep the diet much in the manner as has been followed.  Also those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as equalize the coordinating influences in the nerve system.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is the Ovaltine she has been using all right?
(A)  This very good for the system.

6. (Q)  Should Kellogg's All-Bran be used?
(A)  This is good, but would be better were there alternated conditions with these of the same nature or character, rather than producing a roughness or condition in system that would gradually bring on the fermentation, even with bran.

7. (Q)  Should the treatments be continued daily?
(A)  They should, for at least another twenty-eight days. Why twenty-eight days?  for this is a cycle of functional reaction with the eliminating systems, and with the changes as should be inaugurated, in the character of eliminations set up, and changes made by the change of [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the use of same, this should require at least that period, and then this could be judged by the reactions as have taken place.

8. (Q)  Any medical formula to be given now?  Should same one be continued?
(A)  Continue with those properties, only adding these as have been outlined, and the DOSAGE of this would be one tablespoonful - or dessert spoonful - each EVENING, see?

9. (Q)  Is patient up too much now, or should she be up more?
(A)  Not TOO MUCH, but do not overtax the strength of same. The air good, but not too much of the autumn sun - though the air excellent.

10. (Q)  Should patient attempt going to church in two weeks?
(A)  There should be sufficient recuperation for such to be done.

11. That is all the questions regarding this body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Four copies to [5522]'s husband Copy to Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
