# TEXT OF READING 136-76 F 23 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-76]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/6/1928  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 383

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by husband [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                     [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the lesson and interpretation to be gained from this, as I read it to you.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [136].  This we have had before.

3. [[Dreams|Dreams]] are of the forces as given for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].  Follow same.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  Recently.  Analysis no good.  Gin is rotten.
(A)  This, as seen, is a warning to the body as regarding the physical condition of the body created by the taking of gin into same, and used as a warning would be beneficial to the body - for the activities of same are detrimental to those weakened conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], or produce too much activity there for the condition of the body.  Being warned, be forearmed.  Not referring to the analysis as to its authoritative force respecting the properties of gin itself.  Rather that as related to the body itself.  Heed!

5. We are through for the present.
