# TEXT OF READING 1787-2 M 48

**Reading [[1787-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/2/1939  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 8066

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1787], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are indications of improvement throughout the body.

4. That there has been somewhat too DEEP an application to the areas from which the bronchi receive the impulse is indicated by the tendencies for the tissue to break under strain, or when there has been severe activity of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] and then a general relaxing of same.

5. Not that these applications are to be left off, but not quite so severe or deep in the corrections through the upper dorsal and cervical area.

6. Stimulate a little more through the 9th, 10th and 11th dorsal area.

7. ALSO we would add [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; two minims (two drops) in half a glass of water twice each day, - half or three quarters of an hour before the morning meal, and again in the afternoon between three and four or five o'clock.  DO NOT gulp this; rather SIP it.  Take at least three minutes to consume this quantity.

8. Take it in this manner for five days, leave off three days, then take again for five days.  Do this until at least six such rounds or periods have been completed.

9. These properties as we find add to the system not only the [[Healing|healing]] but purifying of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system and STRENGTHENING through the ability of the blood supply for coagulation; thus reducing the inclination and tendencies for the body to have the veins or the circulation through the lymph to break so easily.

10. The rest as we have outlined should be kept nominally.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(We have ordered one bottle of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] sent to you from our local druggist, thinking you might not be able to obtain it there.

See extra sheet of additional directions [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]].)
