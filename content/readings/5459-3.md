# TEXT OF READING 5459-3 M 54

**Reading [[5459-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/22/1928  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 13657

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5459].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [5459], of ... [[New York City|New York]], N.Y., and you will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding conditions for this body's physical, mental and spiritual betterment.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [5459].  This we have had before.  The conditions as surround this body in many ways are satisfactory to a point.  There are others existent that are partially of own creation, through the imaginations of the body, and [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of elements for correction that are abhorrent in part to the nominal activities of the body.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How can I be relieved of the head noise which appears to be in the region of the inner ear (left)?
(A)  By consistent application of those treatments as have been outlined for the body for the removal of same.  The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] and the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as will be set by the proper application of the radio-active forces.  In the condition existent, this is as has been given, the lack of proper coordination between the sensory and the cerebro-spinal system, and the [[Vibration|vibratory]] force as set is that of a sympathetic nature.  Do, then, as has been given - consistently, persistently - and with the certain knowledge that PROPERLY handled, the condition must adjust itself.

4. (Q)  How can I overcome the depression spells to which I am subject, owing to the troublesome head noises?
(A)  This is of the own creation, and to overcome such, one fills one's own mental activities and proclivities with those things that are more pleasant, or more pleasing to the ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]] of self.  Just be consistent.

5. (Q)  How can I overcome constipation?
(A)  The activities of the body are such that the inclinations are towards that of non-attention of the needs of the body.  With the diet changed in an orderly way and manner, and with the proper assimilation of foods (as will be brought about by the manipulations, as given for the correction of other condition), and the added force as is necessary to bring nominal conditions in this direction, this will be brought in the system.  Do that.

6. (Q)  How can I overcome the fears that beset me, especially about myself and my wife?
(A)  Fear is the root of most of the ills of mankind, whether of self, or of what others think of self, or what self will appear to others.  To overcome fear is to fill the mental, spiritual being, with that which wholly casts out fear; that is, as the love that is manifest in the world through Him who gave Himself as a ransom for many.  Such love, such faith, such understanding, casts out fear.  Be ye not fearful; for that thou sowest, that thou must reap.  Be more mindful of that sown.

7. (Q)  What change should I make in my present habits?
(A)  That is within the individual self.  That necessary to make one's self wholly in accord with that known by self to be the correct attitude toward self, family, neighbor, associates - social and business - and keep self unspotted from censure by the own conscience.

8. (Q)  Should I take electric treatments by Mr. Woodruff [Ph.T.?] at New Hyde Park, L.I., and board nearby for that [[Purpose|purpose]], having 3 treatments a week?
(A)  These will not hinder, nor materially aid.  Then act according to the dictates to own conscience.  As for the corrections and as for the conditions, these have been outlined.  Would the proper conditions be brought about, and if the mental elements can be brought into the attitude wherein it is known within self that these, if properly applied, will bring the correction, then do THOSE; otherwise forget it!  We are through.
