# TEXT OF READING 5554-3 M 44

**Reading [[5554-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/26/1929  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 13833

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5554].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Berger and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5554].  This we have had before. The body shows much improvement since we have had this condition here.  There are still, however, those reactions that cause the body distress at times.  This is particularly seen in the activity of the digestive and eliminating system. We would, for the further corrections, at least once each month - for two to three months - or for at least three months, or three such treatments - empty the colon.  We would, at least twice each week, or DAILY would be preferable - but at least twice each week have deep manipulations, OSTEOPATHICALLY, for the general physical system.  Each other treatment, each day, given osteopathically, should be more to produce the proper coordination between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal system; that is, stimulating - particularly - these centers:  The 4th and 5th lumbar plexus, radiating - of course - to the end of the spine, and across those centers radiating to the sciatic nerves.  Then the solar plexus center, in the 10th, 9th and 11th dorsal centers - then in the 3rd and 4th dorsal centers, and a full general massage in the 3rd, 4th, 1st and 2nd cervicals.  Pressure being brought on the nerves to the frontal portion of the head, to relieve that strain in eyes, ears, nose.  These, we would find, should - in two weeks, were they followed properly - WITH the three months' of the other treatment; that is, one treatment each month - have the body nearly normal.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 11/8/29 Letter:  "...Very glad to receive yours of the first.
> I am also very thankful for the prompt reading I received.
> It was the best I have received and no doubt there must be
> some improvement.  I went to an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] last Monday night -
> Dr. C. A. Lynch...  I thought I could not explain to him just
> the kind of treatment so I thought best to show him reading
> which I did as I know him quite well.  He seemed very
> interested and gave me quite a treatment.  It is a question
> in my mind how he could get a reading for any of his
> patients.  As I understand it, it would be necessary for the
> patient to become a member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].  Am I right?
> On the other hand, if the doctors could get a reading they
> would know better how and what to treat.  As you know,
> statistics show that more trouble is caused by wrong
> diagnosis.  Looks like there should be some way around this.
> I would like very much to spend two weeks at the hospital and
> it does seem a shame that I have not had any vacation, but
> I am not trying to let it worry me as I have a hard time
> keeping my mind clear as it is..."
> 
> R2. 11/11/29 EC wrote suggesting that he have his [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]
> communicate with Dr. L. A. Lydic who might discuss the EC
> work with him.
> 
> R3. 1/21/30 Letter:  "...This is a long time since your letter
> of November 1st, but I do want to thank you for the prompt
> reading just the same - it was greatly appreciated.
> 
> "Mr. Cayce, I sure have had my trials and tribulations since,
> but I thank God that I am able to keeping going.
> 
> "I have not been away from work one day.  Of course, I am
> grateful that I am able to work and a [[Book of Job|job]] to go to.  Since
> business became dull and the stock market crashed our
> Company, while running full, laid off 55 men in the plant and
> the plants are running just the same, but my, the additional
> work.
> 
> "I do not think it advisable for me to ask for a vacation
> now and they don't say a word.  It certainly does seem like
> a great injustice to me but I hope I can see it through.
> 
> "I have followed very carefully the last reading.  However,
> the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments would make me rather nervous at
> night.  He also put the sun light lamp on me but it did not
> tan me like other people.  I don't think my blood is up to
> standard.  My nervousness causes me to smoke quite a few
> cigarettes and I can not clear up my tongue even with a
> little medicine.
> 
> "Otherwise I feel better than last summer.  Of course I do
> not have the old pep at all and worry just a little about my
> condition at times.  I would be pleased for you to furnish
> another reading.  And I am enclosing ten dollars covering...
> 
> "I am sorry that I have not seen Dr. Lydic of [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], but as
> I said before I just hate to lay off as conditions are not so
> good.  If I felt like myself I would not mind.  Sometimes I
> think I would be far better off if I would leave the state
> of [[Ohio|Ohio]], but in the May reading is a suggestion that I not
> change locations..."
> 
> R4. 1/27/30 Letter:  "...I just learned that Dr. Lydic is now
> located at the [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]].  I am surely glad to hear
> that, yet I am disappointed as I was anxious to meet him.
> Dr. Lynch here is a mighty fine man and knows Dr. Lydic
> but somehow or other he seems to be very doubtful.  Is it
> possible you might know of some osteopath in this vicinity
> that I might call on who is interested.  I perhaps could
> arrange to take one or two treatments a week even though
> he is not located in ...
> 
> "I think Dr. Lynch is interested a little as he said he would
> make it a point to see Dr. Lydic at the monthly meeting at
> [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] but somehow or the other he was not able to see him.
> 
> "...Some of these bosses around here that thought I did not
> need any vacation are making their arrangements to go south
> on their winter vacation next month.  Of course that is
> different and when you work for someone else you must do
> as they desire and content yourself.
> 
> "Although it looks sometimes like there is no Divine justice.
> I am thankful to God that I am able to work and have work to
> go to, but I surely would like to feel stronger.
> 
> "I am very much interested in the work you are doing and I
> surely wish I knew what I could do to help it along..."
> 
> R5. 2/3/30 EC gave the fourth and last reading - 5554-4.
