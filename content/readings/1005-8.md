# TEXT OF READING 1005-8 M 22

**Reading [[1005-8]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/8/1930  Sex: M  Age: 22  ReadingID: 6068

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [1005].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [1005] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions show very much of the same nature and character in the body, generally, as we have had here before.  This in the present comes from mental and physical strain under which the body has gone, and the tendencies for the exaggeration of the conditions are the manner in which the system responds to those conditions as produce irritation in the caecum region.

2. We would have more eliminations through those of the high irrigation or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], and we would keep much of that as has been outlined in packs, as well as of the diet and the keeping of the system cleansed.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How long at a time should the oil packs be applied?
(A)  Depends upon the acuteness of the condition.  If the SYSTEM - that is, the colon itself - the caecum - if these obstructions are REMOVED, then we will find these will not necessitate LONG duration, see?

4. (Q)  Should the [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] be continued?
(A)  Be continued, as long as the system ASSIMILATES same.

Be mindful that temperature does not arise from the conditions.  Be mindful that there are no foods eaten where seeds or particles may cause irritation in the appendicial or appendix region, for these would be most harmful - see?

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
