# TEXT OF READING 313-19 F 53 (?)

**Reading [[313-19]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/26/1939  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 2382

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [313], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, in many ways there are better conditions with this body, especially as related to those activities of the gastric flows.

4. But being close to those periods of activity for the changes in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces as related to the menstrual flow, [Menopause?] we have had and do find a great deal of disturbance there.

5. We would take Tonicine in very small doses; six drops three times a day for a period of about five days, - then leave off for three days, and take again, - and so on.  Now, the dosage given on the bottle, of course, is a teaspoonful and more, but take it in this manner; six drops three times a day for five days, then a rest period of three days, and so on.

6. Also we would use the douches of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], but about half the strength of those that have been indicated heretofore; that is, half the strength of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] that has been used, see?

7. Be precautious that the body is not too much on the feet for a few days.

8. Thus we will find these will gradually adjust themselves.

9. Also it would be well at this time to increase the doses of the Codiron, for a better tonic and stimulations, see?  Take about two of the pellets twice each day, at least.

10. Do that, and we will bring the better forces for this body.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
