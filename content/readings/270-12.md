# TEXT OF READING 270-12 M 42 (Accountant, Protestant)

**Reading [[270-12]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/27/1927  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 1629

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of December, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [270].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               Room ..., 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [270], and his business associations and relations, especially regarding [[Chicago|Chicago]] City and railroad franchise. You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [270] - this we have had here before, and those associations as regarding city, franchise, railway, and so forth.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Will the [[Chicago|Chicago]] City and Connecting Railway Franchise application be approved and renewed?
(A)  After much discussion, after some injunctions have been filed and some changes have been made in the franchise, this will be again granted - which will come near February 5th to 6th, or even later.

4. (Q)  What will be the ultimate outcome of this application?
(A)  As given.

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.
