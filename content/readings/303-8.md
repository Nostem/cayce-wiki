# TEXT OF READING 303-8 F 48

**Reading [[303-8]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/6/1935  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2219

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of May, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 to 3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [303]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are somewhat changed.  Some are bettered, some apparently are very much aggravated.  But if those precautions are taken that the condition on the spine or back is not bruised, and if the applications are made for the removal of same as indicated, this will make for a great improvement in the general conditions.  For, to be sure, such causes a drain and a strain on the system.

3. We would find it would be necessary that there be a pack of gauze made, more than is just applied over same; this should be around same, you see, to prevent the rubbing of the clothing on same.  And massage same at night, or around it, with the Iodex and Ash - and over the place itself, or on the gauze, put the [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], the [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and the [[Camphor|Camphor]], equal parts of each; this would go directly over same, see? so that the discharge is taken up, and have sufficient padding that this may be taken up without causing profusion or confusion to the body.  This should be dressed at least twice a day, morning and evening at least, see?

4. For the general health of the body we would keep the tonic that has been indicated; being mindful of the diet, not too great a strain; and keep the mind in just as constructive a manner as is possible.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Are the medicinal properties compounded properly, as prescribed in the last reading?
(A)  As given in the last reading.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
