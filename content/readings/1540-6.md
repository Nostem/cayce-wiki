# TEXT OF READING 1540-6 F 45

**Reading [[1540-6]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/14/1942  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 7482

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of September, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1540], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [1540].]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Virginia.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Miss [1540]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are a great many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Most of these are tendencies towards improvement.

4. There are the needs for the mental attitudes of the body to be directed by self, as we have indicated; to enable and assist in the proper reflex actions, however, we would use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] in the manner ordinarily using the Radio-Active Appliance.  This would supply sufficient of the low electrical forces.  Attach to the right wrist and left ankle; left wrist and right ankle the next afternoon or evening.  Put on for about thirty minutes before ready for sleep, or when ready to retire, and keep on for thirty minutes.

5. Keep the anodes very clean or polished before and after using.

6. Keep the body-mind busy with doing things, and applying self for a helpful force in any manner toward others.

7. In the matter of the diet, - keep that which is well balanced, and keep the body tending toward the alkalinity

8. And we should find continuous improvements.

9. By the mental attitudes to kept, we mean:  DO NOT condemn self nor others.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is it possible for me to get my health back?
(A)  What have we been trying to talk about?  If there is doubt within self, it is not possible!

12. (Q)  Has my health been seriously damaged?
(A)  As indicated, if this is the attitude there will be no help!

13. (Q)  Are the shots I am taking beneficial?
(A)  Not necessarily so.  It depends upon the body's reactions.  If there is the feeling that they are necessary, then they will be beneficial.  Not necessarily are they beneficial.

14. (Q)  What are the best outlets for my loneliness?
(A)  Be busy in keeping optimistic and in helping somebody who is in a worse fix than yourself - and there are millions of 'em!

15. (Q)  If he knew the whole truth about things, is there any way the estrangement between [...] and me could be straightened out beneficially to both of us?
(A)  This must be determined in self.  As the conditions exist in the present, they had best remain so - until there is the opportunity for the full understanding.

16. (Q)  Is it unfair for me to ask for a part of my brother's room, where I can rest and [[Meditation|meditate]] at home?
(A)  This is that to be determined in self.  Who has God made a judge between you and your brother?  If God does not judge you, would you ask from another source to judge?  Ye KNOW the way.  Ye choose the way.

17. (Q)  Will my father and other members of the family be responsible if I fall down because they will not let me adjust myself in the part of the house where I can find rest and quiet?
(A)  YOU are responsible!  No one else is responsible for your falling down, with your understanding as ye have!  YE have an understanding!  No one else is responsible for your falling down!

18. (Q)  What understanding could be reached with my former employer?
(A)  That had best be determined when ye feel capable and willing to apply self in relationships to those activities necessary.  He'll be willing for work whenever you show self efficient.

19. (Q)  Should I sell my car now?
(A)  We have no car - we are fixing for the body!  If the body needs the car, keep it!

20. (Q)  Could I take up my friendship with [...] where I eft off?
(A)  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
