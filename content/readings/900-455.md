# TEXT OF READING 900-455 M 34

**Reading [[900-455]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/23/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5658

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. and Mrs. [[John (Apostle)|John]] F. Blanchard, Drs. Berger and Hansen, and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [900].  This we have had before.  Now, again, we have the accentuation of the system, that is under stress, through the overactivity of the nerves and mental forces of the body - and, as has been given, the body must REST, as well as work - it must play as well as labor; else there will be the accentuation of the disturbing forces in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] - for, with the stress as produced by improper eating, or eating under the conditions where the food consumed is mere poison, is bringing destructive forces to the [[Physical Body|physical body]] - as is seen in the digestive system, and as reflexly shown in those portions of the face where the accumulations of poisons are seen in the antrum and in those portions of the face.  Three to four days rest, with proper consideration to the diet, the proper manipulations, and the application of the antiseptic for that portion of the antrum as causes distress, and with the light rays, we would find the body near fit again.  DO that. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
