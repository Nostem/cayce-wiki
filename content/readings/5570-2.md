# TEXT OF READING 5570-2 F 49

**Reading [[5570-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/15/1931  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 13882

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by self, through Dr. [[Elisha|Elisha]] T. Kirk.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Penna. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5570], and those conditions as surround same.  These we have had before.  As far as the applications as were outlined have been kept in accord with that given, so far have been the changes and the betterments for the physical conditions of the body.

2. In the relationships to those disorders as disturbed the body, in the present we find that in the activities of the system as related to the heart's action and the digestive system - as comes from the aorta connection between those of the central nerve forces in system and the active forces in the system themselves - these are more of a reflex nature, from those irritations as are produced in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - in the mammary system or [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] - and in those regions of the upper or right clavicle.  These, as we find, would respond the more SUDDEN with the use of the ash, WITH the ultra [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] applied in those plexus and regions from which BOTH of these centers have their distributing forces in the cerebro-spinal system.  The dosage as would be taken of the animated ash would be an eighth grain twice each day, while the light would be given only twice each week.  On the days that the ash is given when the light is to be applied, a quarter grain would be taken an hour to two hours BEFORE the treatment WITH the light.

3. Then, keep the rest of the applications as HAS been outlined, in the manner as HAS been indicated, in a consistent and persistent manner, would there be brought the nearer or more perfect normal conditions for the body.

4. With the activities that will come in the system from the use of those properties given, we would find the pain would be eased - that is, if those of the precautions that are consistent with the conditions and that attempting to be accomplished in the system.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is the condition above the right clavicle?
(A)  This is a specific condition from the irritation in [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]].

6. (Q)  What has the aorta to do with the condition at the left of the umbilicus?
(A)  Sympathetic condition as produced from that of the nerves' reaction to those irritations in the system.

7. (Q)  Are there any indications of malignancy?
(A)  Not as yet.  These, were they to take hold - or become more centralized, might become.  Treat as has been outlined, and we will find - with the persistency, or CONSISTENCY, the RESPONSES will be!

8. (Q)  When can I expect a perfect restoration of my health?
(A)  When those suggestions as given have been completed, with a consistent and persistent manner, for a period that will allow the resuscitating forces to accomplish in the system that necessary to bring near normalcy for the body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
