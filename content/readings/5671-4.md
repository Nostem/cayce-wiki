# TEXT OF READING 5671-4 F 34

**Reading [[5671-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/16/1929  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14086

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5671], Drs. Berger, Hansen and Parker, Mrs. Hopp, Mrs. Ringle, Mrs. Greene, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround and affect this body, [5671].  This we have had before.  Now, we find the general physical condition on the improve.  We would keep those conditions as are being administered, in the WAY they are being administered, and we will find the continued improvement in the general physical conditions.

2. In that portion of the canal where disturbances have shown their effect on the system, these show improvement - both by the relaxation of the muscular forces as control the excretions from system, as well as the conditions themselves that have caused the distress in the system. The DEEPER manipulations in the lumbar and sacral, with the straightening or the relaxing of the end vertebra, or end portion of spine, will materially aid in releasing the pressure as IS being released through the changes being wrought.  Keep it then.  Why falter when there is basic results being obtained?  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What solution should be used in vaginal douche?
(A)  We would use those of the weakened solution of Creolin. One to twenty.

4. (Q)  How long before itch will disappear?
(A)  When the pressure is removed that produces the taxation to those nerves as control the system, where pressure has been created in the lower portion of the colon and the reflexes through the sacral and lumbar.

5. (Q)  Would lard and sulphur ointment be alright to use instead of Cuticura ointment?
(A)  Not as effective.  If it had been we would have given it!  We can see both of them, and the application of each. One - Here is the differentiation of it, taken from the medicinal standpoint and its reaction in the system:  That of the Cuticura carries those properties that are of a food value to the tissue as is in the exterior portion, and is soothing to the reflex action of same; while those of the sulphur and lard will be irritating, from the very nature of the carrier of the properties, that would heal, and would PRODUCE those in other portions of the system as a scab. Would you produce that on the inside?  Not many!

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. Much may be given regarding the body, but we would keep at the present those conditions as have been.  We would make DEEPER with the corrective measures in the sacral, and especially springing the coccyx up, as to relieve the pressure as has been started in part by the relaxation of the muscular forces in the lower end of the active eliminations in system.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Berger (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Dr. Parker
