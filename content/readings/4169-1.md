# TEXT OF READING 4169-1 M 22

**Reading [[4169-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/23/1924  Sex: M  Age: 22  ReadingID: 11902

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23rd day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [4121].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born November 23, 1901, in Wilkingsburg, Penna. 
Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                         ..., Pa. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  We find [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit forces complete the physical entity in the early of the morn, and that there are many influences in the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].  This we find an exceptional one, and the way as marked before [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] from the astronomical conditions will influence [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s developing in a very marked way, provided [[Free Will|the will]] does not carry the forces into improper channels.

2. One we find whose greatest influences are from that plane from which [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit forces took its last forces; that of Venus with the influence of Jupiter and of Mercury. The conditions that have to do with the constellations and the signs under which these give strength are in the mental, with the ennobling and upbuilding forces as of Jupiter.  The afflictions come under those of Mars and Neptune.  Hence the fear that will ever be in the entity's inmost thoughts respective to war or flood tides.

3. In the forces as to the building, we find as this:

4. One given to the love of group, yet one who is rather the leader of such groups.

5. One given to many affairs of the heart, and over many influences that this gives and brings, or as brought by such in the influence, [[Free Will|the will]] must be exerted to keep in the way that leads to the developing of the force in the entity.

6. One given to study all phases of the human body.  One that will do well in that profession of the doctor.  One that would find strength in giving the relief to others through that other than of the earth forces.  One that with [[Free Will|the will]], will be able to give strength, relief, both in body and mind to many others.

7. One that with the petty things of life has little to do.  One with the broader scope and the higher [[Visions|vision]].  One that will do well to keep the idealistic forces in self-development, for with the afflictions as come under those of Mars and Neptune will find the influence, without will, breaking, as it were, the idealistic forces that must be ever kept before, would this entity develop either physically, mentally, spiritually or materially.

8. One that is given to the frivolous, yet not in the petty ways, but rather the enlivening.

9. One given to sensation and the experiences as found through incentives of the natures as exhibited in forces of Venus.

10. One that will bring much joy in the life of the home, were it joined with those coming especially under the influence of the same forces, and ESPECIALLY those born in latter days of January, June or November.

11. The elements that appear in the personalities as present exhibited, we find as these:

12. Before this we find the forces as were under those in the Court Physician of Louis the 15th, and the turn towards the Jester of the higher type, expressed in the present individuality and shown in the personality.

13. In that before we find in the land of the Greeks, when the forces were in the building of the Philosophy of the then known world, and this gives the reasoning forces of the present entity, and the acceptance of those that especially give sensation to the conscious forces of the body.  This name we find in history.  That of Toabias, [Tobias?].

14. Before this in the land of the Eastern forces, and was then in that country now known as Mongolia.  From that we get the influence as is brought in the present in the love of the outdoor, with the beautiful nature surroundings.  Yulee, the name then.
