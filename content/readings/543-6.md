# TEXT OF READING 543-6 F 22

**Reading [[543-6]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/1/1930  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3839

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [543].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Gena Crews, Miss [543] and sister.  Also Elsie Swindall [nurse].]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [543] - this we have had before.  In MANY respects conditions are gradually on the improve, and will we [if we will] keep that as has been outlined in a consistent and persistent manner, keeping the eliminations above normal - so that the drosses created by any forces that use energy in body, either mental or physical, may be eliminated - changing the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces through those administrations as are given medicinally, either locally or general - and those of the manipulations as to make for better coordinations - the conditions will continue on the improve.

2. Would be well that even the eliminations through alimentary canal be through irrigations once to three times per month, that there may be no dross or auto-infection from the colon, or that the weight in colon does not produce irritation to the organs of the lower hepatic circulation, nor of the pelvis themselves.

3. The application occasionally of the violet ray, as for the QUIETING effect same will produce on the system, will be found to be beneficial.  Not too long a period of treatment, but those centers particularly along the cerebro-spinal system as revert to the pelvis organs' functioning; for remember, many of the administrations are creating an extra effluvia in these organs' functioning, and capillary stimulation will make for beneficial effects; as also necessity of local applications for conditions as arise. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Would auto-suggestion be beneficial?
(A)  As to that as should be held, this has been given - and would be most beneficial, were this SINCERELY and SPIRITUALLY suggested.  If it is to be just ROTE, don't do it!

5. (Q)  What causes pain under right shoulder and how can it be relieved?
(A)  This, that of the digestive system's want, or call for digestion as passes through that portion of the system's functioning where carbohydrates and fats are dissolved into blood stream, and ENTER under or over the right shoulder. In relieving this, ALTER the food values occasionally; that is, give those properties as will be NECESSARY to call into play the organs involved.  Fats!  No, it won't make the body fat!

6. (Q)  Is body responding properly to treatment?
(A)  It's just been given!

7. (Q)  Is body getting sufficient rest?
(A)  More rest won't hurt it!  Be more beneficial!

8. (Q)  How often may sweets be eaten?
(A)  Once to twice a week.

9. (Q)  How often may nuts be eaten?
(A)  Many of these are rather hard for those properties as are being taken to be assimilated WITH, and unless they are of the filbert or [[Almonds|almond]] don't eat them!

10. That is all the questions.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
