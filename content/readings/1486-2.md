# TEXT OF READING 1486-2 M 55 (Writer of Religious Music,

**Reading [[1486-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/21/1938  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 7339

Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of January, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1468], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., this Reading being Sponsored by Mrs. [1158], Active Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1486], who is at present at [[New York City|New York City]].  Also you will have before you the manuscript of an Operetta which he wrote several years ago, and which about three years ago he sent to Vladimer Rosing, 21 Albion Mews East off Bayswater Road, [[England|London]], [[England|England]].  This manuscript was taken to Mr. Rosing by [1486]'s cousin, [...] then resident in [[England|London]], England.  Mr. [1486] wishes to know definitely whether this manuscript is still in existence, or was it destroyed?  You will answer the questions he has submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind, [1486], and those circumstances and conditions as we find relative to the manuscript.

3. As we find, Rosing was not aware of the delivery of the manuscript, as - owing to circumstances under conditions, and the involvements that arose - this manuscript was put aside. Later the maid piled same among papers that in the moving were destroyed.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Of all Mr. [1486]'s present interests, which one should he concentrate upon?
(A)  The reconstruction as we find of this manuscript - which with some labor may be reconstructed; or the writing or playing of those things associated with those portions of his experiences which have been indicated for him.

6. (Q)  Do you think it wise for him to push his idea of a "Creator's Cooperative Corporation" and affiliate same with the American Federation of Labor?
(A)  This is very well, but will require a great deal more of straightening out of those conditions that would make the affiliation of same an effective activity.

7. (Q)  Would it be constructive and profitable for him to use his creative talent in writing and producing theatre musicals?
(A)  This as we find is very well, as has been indicated - but the reproduction of that as has just been indicated would be most worth while.

8. (Q)  Any other advice or counsel that will be helpful at this time?
(A)  Of course, there are great [[Intuition|intuitive]] influences and forces as related to the mental and spiritual influences about the body - in the very act of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

If the body will then listen oft to that within, the directions may be more in keeping with that which will be satisfying or gratifying, and constructive in the experience.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self thru Mrs. [1158] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
