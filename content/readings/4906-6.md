# TEXT OF READING 4906-6

**Reading [[4906-6]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/19/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13001

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4906].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Section 7, Township 14 
Time of Reading                     N. Range, 14 E. North of 
10:20 A. M.                         Morris, Oklahoma.

1. LS:  Now, you have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] which is being drilled in Section 7, Township 14 North, Range 14 East, situated directly North of the Town of Morris, Oklahoma, in Okmulgee County.  You will give the conditions of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] at the present time.  You will also answer the following question: Should casing be run at the depth of two thousand seven hundred seventy-two feet, to which depth this well has been drilled?

2. EC:  Yes, we have this here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find the conditions here as these:

4. On this location we find the double fold, and the sand that is or was oil bearing has been passed through above this depth.  In this place, or as at the present depth, we should with the first lime, at two thousand seven hundred seventy-eight (2778) ground measurement (not derrick) find that place to set the casing to cut off the water from above, and at the two thousand seven hundred eighty (2780) ground measurement find the first show, as given, of the oil and gas production, which will be produced from the sands below.  Do not expect to find the full production at the two thousand seven hundred eighty (2780) but the first show of oil and gas, and the casing should be set on the first lime at two thousand seven hundred seventy-eight (2778) ground measurement.

5. Do that.  Then drill in, and we will find production in this place.

6. DO THAT.

> wonderful test, and I can't help but believe that your faith

> for the proposition.  Now, as soon as you have been able to
> finish with this in Section 7, and can take up the one in 8,
> you will certainly change people's opinion of what you are
> doing, and that you are sticking on the [[Book of Job|job]] yourself is the
> proof of your sincerity in the proposition.  I am sure the
> report as sent this morning, even judged by your letter, will
> check up.  Consequently, I am looking for a wire from you
> saying you have the "precious juice" with abundance of gas
> about Tuesday next.  I certainly enjoy and appreciate your
> letters, and I have to laugh at myself sometimes at the
> confidence and faith that my wife has got in you, for some
> reason that I have never seen her have in any other man.  I
> appreciate very much your position financially, and is one of
> the reasons, as I said before, I did not want to burden you,
> for I knew you had your hands full.
> 
> As for our friend, [257], the last I heard from him he had
> a position in [[New York City|New York]] with some coat and suit house.  I'm
> going to try to write him to-day, and see if I can get in
> touch with him again.  He was just as full of [[Visions|visions]] when

> money from that one and the other.  Whether he has ever done
> anything with the furniture he was to sell or not I do not
> know.  However, I do know the furniture was not his, unless

> Again thanking you for your letter, and trusting you will
