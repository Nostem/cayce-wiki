# TEXT OF READING 5658-1 M 20

**Reading [[5658-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/22/1931  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 14060

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [959].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., W. Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5658].  In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find abnormal conditions.  These have been produced by a complication of conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], where there had been (and is still existent) allowed an unbalancing of the elements as go to make up the physical functionings of the nerves, the muscular forces, and the taxation of same reached such a point or condition as to use up the energy of same in such a manner that, with those reactions of very severe nature, CAUSES the inability of the vital forces IN the muscular, in the tendon, in the structural portions, to RESPOND to such SEVERE strain as the SUDDEN CHANGES called upon TO be adjusted in the physical functioning.  So, there is produced that that prevents the normal reaction in locomotion, in the use of the limbs. These are as of the very TENDONS themselves, were wrenched from the body, in the locomotary centers, especially - the scapula of the right arm, the pelvic of the whole body - these make for incapacitation.  Without the vital necessary elements being added to the body as to produce a bettered condition, and a virility in same that would bring about the proper circulation through the muscular forces, through the tendons, even through those of the union of the extremities to the body itself, they must gradually lose in their elasticity, and the body remain in a helpless condition; but, as we find, will there be added those forces necessary to create within the system itself those elements that will make for this proper reaction, proper functioning, through the distribution of forces as may be builded in the system, then we would have a resuscitation of the whole body - see!  These at first would be rather slow, for the first two to three weeks, then we would see a more rapid reaction in the abilities to use the body, use the limbs, use the arm.  The very nature of conditions, with the age and virility of the system, are in the body's favor, will there be kept ALSO the PROPER attitude mentally OF the body TO its condition!  Let the body THOROUGHLY REALIZE its helpless condition, but do NOT allow the mental forces - for ANY cause - to cherish, or THINK, the idea that it is a hopeless condition, or that the body IS to be a drudge on someone else.  Rather know that there are duties, there are obligations in self, that must be met, and that those elements, those forces as would make for the arousing of the spiritual forces as may be made manifest have had to choose SUCH a channel or manner to AROUSE the REAL abilities as lie innate within the body itself; for much in many ways may yet be accomplished by the body, Mr. [5658], WILL the body-mind, the body-physical, but call upon, and SINCERELY desire from within that there be brought to the body, through the application of those things that will from without arouse to the consciousness of coordinating activity of the elements from within, so that the coordinating of the mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]] will be in attune to be used as a channel of blessings to others, as well as for self.

2. Then, we would prepare those of a least two pounds of Balsam of Sulphur.  This we would take, a small amount, three times each week, and thoroughly massage same into the body, over those of the cervical and upper dorsal area, along the cerebro-spinal system, around the shoulder blade, over the top of the shoulder, under the arm, down to the elbow, across the lower part of the body - from the high waist down, or from the middle of the spine down to the knees, and UNDER the knees - see?  This would require about a piece the size of a goose egg for the whole of each massage given.  Massage this THOROUGHLY into the body over these portions given, at least THREE times each week.

3. Begin immediately with the taking of those properties of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], in water.  BEGIN with three drops four times each day.  Every FIFTH day INCREASE the quantity two drops, until nine drops are being taken - see?  Then leave off for five days.  Then begin with the three drops again.  Do not follow instructions printed, but rather as given here.

4. As for the matter of the diet, we would give those things that are blood and nerve building.  Not too much meats, and when those of the vegetables are given (which should be plenty), do not prepare same in quantities of grease.  When the fats are used, use rather those of the vegetable fats, or butter, to cook same in, seasoning same well - see?

5. Exercise the limbs, even when lying alone, as much as is possible without too much pain, and we will find in - or by the time the whole of the two pounds has been used, a change for the better.  Then we would give further instructions. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What caused this condition?
(A)  This has just been described, that an unbalancing of the system was produced by the lack of elements, overtaxing of the system for its strength, then sudden congestion, and the body not able to meet the severe demands made on it for equalization, and it's wrenched the MUSCLES and tendons from their hold.

7. (Q)  Will his condition ever affect the mind?
(A)  Not if we will do what has been given, for it'll be alright!  He will be WELL again, STRONG - as ever!

8. (Q)  He will be able to walk?
(A)  Be able to do anything he wants to!  Jump, run, walk, lift, play - do anything!  Do as we have outlined.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [959]
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
