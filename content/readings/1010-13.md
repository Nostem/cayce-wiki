# TEXT OF READING 1010-13 F 67

**Reading [[1010-13]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/6/1938  Sex: F  Age: 67  ReadingID: 6100

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of November, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1010], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1010].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that are acute in the present.  These arise somewhat from the lack of the proper eliminations of accumulations from the old disturbance in the gall duct and liver area; producing a greater stress upon the eliminating forces in the kidney area, causing pressures upon the locomotaries in the lumbar and sacral area, - thus producing contraction of the muscular forces along the sciatic nerves, drawing toes backward under the feet as it were.

4. This as we find has caused or does cause a great deal of excruciating pain when attempting to move.

5. Apply first over the lumbar and sacral area a mixture of three parts of laudanum and one part Aconite.  Then apply over same the heavy salt, preferably in bag, but the salt HEAT.  Heat the salt, you see - heavy, coarse salt, and put in bag.  Change about every thirty minutes, or every twenty minutes, until there is the relaxing of the muscular forces along the sciatic nerves.

6. As these applications begin to relax the body, then combine with same a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the sciatic nerve to the bottom of the feet and plumb to the center of the lumbar area, - using a compound for such massaging, prepared in this manner:

7. To 1 quart of Straight Gasoline (not colored), add - IN THE ORDER NAMED:

Gum [[Camphor|Camphor]]...................1 dram,
O'Ceder, or Cedar Wood Oil....1 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]............1/2 ounce.

Shake these ingredients together.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] - not rub hard, but pat and rub - over the areas indicated, see?

8. Prepare Watermelon Seed Tea.  The proportions would be a tablespoonful of crushed seed in a pint of boiling water. Steep as tea.  Drink this, strained off, during a whole day.

9. After the application of the heat AND the properties indicated (Laudanum-Aconite; then the salt heat), have a correction osteopathically or neuropathically along the whole of the cerebrospinal system.

10. Stimulate the eliminations with the combination of Syrup of Figs and Fletcher's Castoria.  Take them opposite; a teaspoonful of one every hour; first one, then the other; until there are thorough eliminations.

11. Do these and we will bring the better forces, and remove these disturbances from the body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail Special - Wired also

PAINT LUMBAR SACRAL WITH MIXTURE THREE PARTS LAUDANUM ONE PART ACONITE THEN HEAT COARSE SALT AND APPLY IN BAGS CHANGING EVERY TWENTY MINUTES UNTIL RELAXED  TAKE INTERNALLY TEASPOONFUL SYRUP FIGS ONE HOUR FLETCHER'S CASTORIA THE NEXT HOUR UNTIL FULL ELIMINATIONS  HAVE NEUROPATHIC CORRECTION WHOLE CEREBROSPINAL SYSTEM  FULL INFORMATION AIR MAIL SPECIAL

> On Fri. evening five fifteen (5:15-P.M.) P.M.  My legs

> hands, but could not do so, had no pain, no dizziness nor
> faintness, no numbness, legs just gave away, and I would
> crumble to floor.  A doctor was called in at five thirty.
> He took blood pressure - only one point above normal,
> respiration normal, pulse fast (94) no temp.  He put me to
> bed, said it was over work, and over work of my one kidney,
> (only have one).  I have been having black spots before my
> eyes until I was blinded at times, for two Mo. or more.  I
> had really worked too hard lately, and ate wrong, some meat,
> and too much protein and stood for hours on my feet at a
> time.  The Doctor told me to apply heat, and rub in something
> with ether in it, and take some tablets for the cleansing of
> my kidney, as there was pus in it.  Was AWFUL sick, am living
> on fruit juices and pot. soup.  Sent telegram to Edgar Cayce
> next A.M. rec. telegram in evening.  We immediately followed
> suggestions.  Am much better, can walk some.  Am anxious for
> letter of suggestions.  Will let you know right along how the
> care is progressing.  Enclosed is the ten dol. ($10.00) for
> check reading.  Thank you so much.  So sorry you are not well
>
