## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/22 Mr. [1868]'s letter:  "On or about March 5, 1917 I
> took a reading from Mr. Cayce on the condition of my father
> [4971] about which condition EC had no previous information.
> 
> "The reading described the disease with which my father was
> affected exactly, which was in non-technical terms a cancer.
> Mr. Cayce made a statement that the bladder, stomach, kidneys
> were affected and that the only cure would have been an
> operation by the Mayo Bros. and a substitution of the
> diseased parts from some living animal.  We were unable to
> convince my mother [242] that this course was practical so
> the disease developed rapidly and was fatal within sixty days
> of the reading.
> 
> "During the year 1916 (?) my sister [4953] was visiting in
> North Carolina and we had been unable to hear from her for
> about ten days.  After sending several telegrams and
> receiving no answer we asked Mr. Cayce for a reading.  Mr.
> Cayce told us there had been an accident and that a letter
> was then at our home explaining the matter.  We immediately
> telephoned and found that such was the case and that our
> sister would be delayed several days more from a fall while
> horseback riding.
> 
> "We [[1868] & [228]] have taken a great number of readings
> from Mr. Cayce and have found that in practically all
> instances where his directions were followed explicitly, as
> in a case under my personal knowledge of Mr. [4959] of
> Bessemer, Ala., results have been phenomenal.
> 
> "I have read a number of letters from all over the U.S.
> stating that writers have been cured by following his
> directions."
