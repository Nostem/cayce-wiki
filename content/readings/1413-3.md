# TEXT OF READING 1413-3 F 20

**Reading [[1413-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/27/1937  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 7161

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of September, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self through her mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1413]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while there are still many conditions that must be changed for the better reactions and for the corrections in the system, the body is a little bit on the improve - though there are periods of the violent reaction and the attempts of the system to eliminate poisons, as the sympathetic system becomes imbued with same through the incoordination between the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal systems.

3. Hence these changes as we find we would make:

4. First in the manipulations and [[Massage Therapy|massage]]:  Every third treatment, at least, we would make specific adjustments, now, in those centers where the cerebrospinal AND the sympathetic nerve systems react the more specifically together; notably in the 4th lumbar, the 9th dorsal, the 1st and 2nd dorsal, and through the 3rd and 4th and 2nd cervical MAKE DEFINITE adjustments by MOVING - at least - the segments in these areas.  The other treatments or daily treatments besides this we find are well.

5. Also, in the present, we would leave off the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] internally.

6. But we would apply the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying into the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the system [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], at the regular or commercial strength.  This would be used two days, then left off two days, then used two days again, then left off two days.

7. Then use the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] one day, carried into the system vibratorially in the same manner but a different container, the proportion being one grain to each ounce of Distilled Water.  Then leave off two days.

8. Then take the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] again for two days, leave off two days, take again two days, leave off two days.

9. Then the Gold again for one day, leave off two days and begin with the [[Camphor|Camphor]] again.

10. Continue in this manner.  The application would only be made for thirty minutes, and the attachments each time would be made as follows:

11. The small plate would be attached first, at the 9th dorsal; while the larger plate, through which the Solution passes, would be attached last, at the umbilicus and lacteal duct - or that area very close, or in conjunction with the absorbent activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces from the ducts of the liver and the assimilating system.

12. Do not leave the anodes or connections in the Solution when not in use.

13. Let these applications be made by that one giving the massages.  And after each treatment with the Appliance, give a THOROUGH [[Massage Therapy|massage]] NEUROPATHICALLY over the WHOLE BODY, where it is possible or practical to treat.  This as we find will aid in the system's absorbing the activities to create not only a normal balance with the correction in the centers indicated, but to make for a balance in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activities of the body as to prevent the salts of the system becoming overbalanced and so susceptible to the activities from without.

14. Keep up the rest of the treatments that have been indicated.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  What, if any, will be my reaction to an allergic shot I took about a month ago?
(A)  This as we find, owing to the general condition, has been slow in reaction; but the manipulations, the treatments that have been given are producing in the system a better reaction than if same had not been given.

Also the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the metal as well as the [[Healing|healing]] properties of the Camphor, as stimulated by the vibratory forces of the body, will prove beneficial how to the general reactions; not only for the eczema but the circulation in general, as well as the disturbance in the upper dorsal areas where specific adjustments are to be made, as well as in the cervical to the bronchi and the larynx activity for the pulmonary reactions.

17. (Q)  Is there anything that I am not doing - or doing - that retards progress?
(A)  The condition is being approached from not a palliative angle but so as to ERADICATE the conditions from the body! Keep in the ways that have been outlined.  Make the activities of the mental forces keep constructive toward ALL CREATIVE ACTIVITY; keeping in the attitude of deep meditations, especially during the periods when the Appliance is active upon the body.

There will NOT be felt any material reaction from the Appliance, or any high vibration.  There may occur at times a little nausea.  Hence do not use the Appliance over the thirty minute period, but relax the body thoroughly when these treatments are given - and let the body relax thoroughly while they are being given.

Hence the manipulations or the neuropathic manipulations following each treatment thoroughly over the system. Stimulate all centers, all ganglia, all the activities through the system, both to the lymph and the emunctory circulation where they come close to the surface in the superficial circulation - where the stimulation is so needed; that the vibrations created (through the use of the Appliance) may be DISTRIBUTED THROUGHOUT THE BODY ITSELF.

And doing these, we should find, now we should begin to see a little faster improvement.

But do not grow weary.  Know, as has been indicated for the [[Meditation|meditation]], that the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]], or God in His mercies, will give that necessary for this [[Soul|soul]] and this body, this mind, to see and know that the Lord IS Love - and is MINDFUL of the children of men pleading in a constructive manner to Him.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother (two copies; one for Dr. Nona Grapes)
"    " Ass'n file
