# TEXT OF READING 646-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[646-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/14/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4352

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of September, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, Beth Graves and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Mich. 
(Physical suggestion, in regard to her first reading of 
August 31, 1934)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given respecting [646].

2. As we find, there have been, through, some applications made for the body, and we are going AT the basis of the conditions by making the corrections in those locations that have been indicated.  These we would follow.

3. Ready for questions respecting the reading.

4. (Q)  Please explain what parts are to be strapped.
(A)  Those that swell!

5. (Q)  When and for how long a period?
(A)  When it's needed, and as long as it's needed!  As the stocking of heavy elastic, you see, and strapping same on those portions of the lower limbs where there are the tendencies for the cords and the filling of those parts of the body, or at times it maybe necessary about the torso of the body.  Use discretion, and as the conditions arise then meet them.

6. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  Two to three times each week.

7. (Q)  Also about for how long should they be continued?
(A)  Until the condition is removed!

8. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Doing this will be sufficient for the present.

9. We are through with this reading.  [GD's note:  While GC was giving the waking suggestion, EC interrupted her by a chuckling sound, as though amused - then said:  "How would people on the other side go fishing?"  He paused, laughed again, and said:  "Three of them are - as we see here."]

Copy to Mrs. [601]
"    " Ass'n file
