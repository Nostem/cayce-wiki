# TEXT OF READING 4185-3 M 42

**Reading [[4185-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/15/1929  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 11924

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [4185].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [4185] of the ... coproration of ..., N.Y., and all phases of his life.  You will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [4185] - this we have had before.

3. In the relations as exist, there is seen that with the application of those suggestions as have been given the body, there has come the greater opportunity in the way of meeting, accomplishing, and carrying out many of the conditions as has been given would be, and bring, the better elements and conditions for this body.

4. In the handling of the situations as present themselves at this time, there needs be only that same care and attention given in the various distributing channels as is being, and as has been, followed out by the body.  The policies as have been established are proving satisfactory in the greater number of instances.  Where there is dissension with the product as is being distributed, this should be turned to [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which corrections may be made in their proper way and manner.  This will better facilitate the body conduct of the business in the manner as will keep the less or the minimum of troubles coming to the special attention of the body, that so often aggravates and unfits one for the proper consideration of the larger, or the more effective distributing channels.  Then, that would be the advice and counsel, to keep the self in that way and manner wherein the service as is rendered each and every associate DEMANDS, by that service rendered, the BEST that individual or GROUP of individuals will be able to give to the body.  So act, then, in every business relation, that there may not be even a question asked as to other than a service for the individual, that will call and demand the same CLASS of service from those so served - for like begets like, and that meted will return in the manner as it has been given.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How should I guard against failure in carrying out the large contract -
(A)  Just given.

6. (Q)  What is the outlook, this year, in the matter of Majestic Radio sets and the Radio business in general?
(A)  The policies as are being pursued by the general distributing and manufacturing of this special equipment is meeting a response that is phenomenal in itself.  Will each individual as is doing this keep that same attention as is evidenced in the data and the service, there will be the still greater increase in this special make, or product. The general outlook in Radio is much of the same as is evidenced in the abilities of the individual to attain a better and better product.  Then, the outlook would be in keeping with that of general conditions, as is evidenced in what is termed prosperity among the peoples; for while the product is still in its infancy as to what may be accomplished, it is still classed among those of non-essentials - yet with the continued successes in the class of product and in the abilities of individuals to obtain same, there will be at least as good or better outlook for the present year than that in the past, even for the PARTICULAR product as is distributed by THIS body.

7. (Q)  What does 1929 hold in store for me?
(A)  That which the body would put into same.  There has come, from time to time, an INKLING - as it were - of the ABILITIES of the body to succeed, in financial, social, and in a general business way.  There has been warnings given the body concerning that, thou shall not put thine self before that from whom thou receivest that thou hast.  Keep that in mind, that it was ever true - preferring one another to self, and there will come the greater joy in making, NOT the living - but the LIFE worth while!  Not joy in the everyday but the joy in the lives of those whom the body would serve.  That will create the greater in the life, and these earthly - these material blessings, will be the RESULT of self's service, and not the end - for what profit it a man to gain the whole world and then find it dross?

8. (Q)  Shall I continue to live my life as heretofore, or what changes shall I make?
(A)  More and more grow in [[Grace|grace]] and in the knowledge of the life's purport in this mundane sphere; for it is not all of life to live, nor yet all of death to die.  Live each and every day as if the evening was to be spent with THY Creator, and in the MORNING of each day give thanks to the Creator for that thou may be able to give in service and self to others, that they may be blessed, even as the Creator would have them be - through thine effort.  Not that self is preferred above or beyond the other, for he that would be the greatest among men will be the servant of men.  Learn what that means in the life, and the life will be worth while.

9. (Q)  Shall my wife and I adopt another child?
(A)  WELL!

10. (Q)  What can I do to make those dear to me more comfortable and happy?
(A)  Give that that is the BASIS of happiness; not necessarily moneys!  but if moneys are necessary for material conditions, contribute that thou hast in hand!  We are through for the present.
