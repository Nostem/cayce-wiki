# TEXT OF READING 900-69 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-69]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/11/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5272

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... Street, [[New York City|New York City]], on the night of Monday, May 4, 1925.  You will please explain the flash of light or [[Visions|vision]] this body saw or experienced during this hour of [[Prayer|prayer]].  You will also tell us how this may be brought about again, and its significance in the life and mind of the body [900].  You will also give the interpretation of [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had during his sleeping hours of this night, May 4, 1925, as I read them to you, and the lessons this body, [900], may gain from same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind of this body, on the night of May 4, 1925.  The light as came to this mind and perception of the body is but the development of the mental and the attunement of same to those higher forces, as are seen in the co-relation [correlation] of the [[Superconscious|superconscious]] and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of the body.  While these are as those conditions partaking of the highest forces as manifest in the Universe, yet we will find these will ever become those unspeakable conditions of the individual apart. Yes, these should be sought but rarely spoken of.  As was given, "I was in the Spirit, whether out of the body or in the body I cannot tell, but was caught up to the seventh Heaven and viewed those unspeakable things".  Such then are these [[Visions|visions]] or these interpretations as will bring the awakening of the mental forces in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] when contacting the highest elements that may be manifest in a material world, coming from spiritual and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces through the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Then, to attain same, attune self to that element in supplication, in [[Prayer|prayer]], in entering into the realm of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and [[Superconscious|superconscious]] forces as would thus, through mentality of the physical when entering in [[Prayer|prayer]] to this threshold.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  The first [[Dreams|dream]].  (reading)  [Hand or arm extended with a lot of paper money, "Take the money, damn you!"]
(A)  This, as we find, is not complete in that brought back to self or consciousness, and is the presentation of both conscious and subconscious forces being enacted upon in the mental forces of the body [900].  In the presentation of the hand, that of the offering of the unseekable, unspeakable riches that will and would come by the seeking out of those truths as is set in the mind and the developing of the body at the present time, the voice an answer of the materialist who would hinder and prevent or bring condemnation through such forces.

Then, the lesson:  Bring self to the position of being able to gain the whole influence of the Divine forces, in the study of conditions as have been set in the mental forces, thus gaining the access to the storehouse of the higher realms.

4. (Q)  The second [[Dreams|dream]] was as follows:  (reading) ["Various tips on stocks ran through my mind."]
(A)  These again the elemental forces of the subconscious attempting to correlate with the conscious mind existent conditions of the mind in its waking or elemental state. These should be confined to self's development and self's usage, and not from these forces.  Only as same co-relates [correlates] with the development in self may these be used, as has been often given.  Do that.

5. (Q)  The third dream I have forgotten.  Can you recall it to me and its significance?
(A)  There are many that are called dreams passing through the [[Visions|vision]] of the in between, as it were, of consciousness and subconsciousness.  The one at this time as this:  The pole and the flag from same indicating the standard by which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] judges itself and sets for itself as the criterion for action, whether physical, mental or spiritual.  Then the action should be as in the dream.  "This will be my standard. This I will follow.  As for others, do as they may, but for me and my house, we will serve the living God."
