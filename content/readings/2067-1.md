# TEXT OF READING 2067-1 F 52 (Teacher, Quaker - Spiritualist)

**Reading [[2067-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/22/1939  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 8636

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of December, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [2067], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss Millicent Horton.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. [2067].]

Born January 6, 1887, in Boston, Mass. 
Time of Reading 
10:35 to 12:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces, giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, and that to which it may attain, and how; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '35 - changes - '34 - '31 - mental upsets - '20 - yes, a change, - '19 - 1900 - different environ - '99 - '94 - not so well - '93 - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2067].

4. These records we find are full.  Many varied phases of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here present themselves for interpretation. These, however, are chosen with the desire, the [[Purpose|purpose]], that this may be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling the entity to find something which answers within, - as to the relationships of self with the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], or God; thus using, applying the many gifts, the many abilities which are latent and manifested in the experience of the entity, through this [[Incarnation|sojourn]], for the glorification of those [[Purpose|purposes]] for which each [[Soul|soul]] enters a material experience.

5. The natural inclinations and tendencies of the entity, as will be seen, particularly from the material [[Incarnation|sojourns]], influence or urge the entity to become and be a seeker.

6. This one principle the entity must gain, or become more aware of, - if the greater understanding, the greater knowledge would be attained, or the greater good accomplished:

7. It is in the giving of that which is attained that the abilities come for greater comprehension and understanding.

8. This applies not only in the material.  For, in this present day, - though hardships make it necessary for the consideration of economic influences, and many activities have caused the entity to oft curtail its efforts in some directions, - fame and fortune will be the lot of the entity in this experience, unless there is a failing entirely in the use of that which IS in hand.

9. In considering the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, - this as we find may be said to be a composite of not only the material [[Incarnation|sojourns]], but of the sojourns in the interims between the material sojourns.

10. And these (the material sojourns) have been many.  Thus there has been confusion of many natures in this present experience.  Gradually, though, the entity has grown to comprehend, - not so much because of necessity as because of the earnest seeking and the desire to be used as a channel through which hope, help, aid, might come to others.

11. This is the greater influence for good in the experience of this entity, and it is that which is to be cultivated the more throughout this experience; YET in somewhat more of an humble manner than the mien has indicated at times.

12. For, as has been suggested, these are the composites:

13. Oft the entity is considered by others to be too sure of itself without sufficient proof of the source of the knowledge as may be presented to others.

14. Hence the interest in the occult, and the interest in spiritual things.

15. Discern well, - as the environs have proven, - the vast difference between occultism and spiritualism, and spirituality.

16. These are fields of service.  They each have their place, their part in this entity's experience, as well as in the lives and experiences of a great horde of seekers.

17. But know, as the Teacher of teachers gave, there is one proof for all, - "By their fruits ye shall know them."

18. Again, know the author of not only thy faith but of thy confidence as well; and be assured not only from personal experience but from that which such has accomplished in the hearts and the [[Soul|souls]] of men, - in that such authorities are able to measure up, - yea, to keep inviolate (through that form or manner of experience) that which is committed unto them.

19. Such knowledge, such interpretations of authorities would only be found by the answers from within.  For, "My Spirit beareth witness with thy spirit" is an immutable, an unchanging law.  And again, - the heavens, the earth may pass away, but His laws shall not pass away.

20. Hence in thy seeking, find that answer; not as from something without, but from opening the door of thy own consciousness to the promises that are sure in Him.  For ye will find that as the pattern which was shown in the mount to [[Moses|Moses]], as well as the greater pattern in the temptations to [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] upon the mount; in that indeed as He was lifted up, He draws all men (and this means women also) unto Him.

21. This then is, shall be, ever, the basis of thy faith, and not as in they of whom He spoke, "Many shall come in that day and say, Did we not thus and so in thy name accomplish among men those things that bespeak of heavenly things, and I will say I know ye not."

22. As to the influences which arise from the sojourns of the entity other than in the material manifestation, these the entity may comprehend in this manner:

23. The entity finds self a body, a mind, a [[Soul|soul]].  There are attributes of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] that may or may not be controlled by mental forces and influences.  There is the soul-body that may not be controlled by, or may be controlled by, the good or the bad influence of spirit.  These are as the shadows which were indicated in the mount by the outer court (the body), the inner court (the mind), and the still more [[Holy of Holies|holy of holies]] (the soul).  These are but shadows, and yet indicate the trend of the development, - just as the sojourns of an entity in or during interims between earthly or material manifestations indicate the trend in which the soul through VARIED experiences has sought an outlet or an expression.

24. About the earth in man's (or earthly man's) sojourn are planets (so called); and to such has been ascribed an environmental influence, - mentally applied upon the movements, the urges, the impulses.  And these are in a manner according to [[Law of Moses|the law]], "He that receiveth a [[Prophets|prophet]] in the name of a [[Prophets|prophet]] receiveth the [[Prophets|prophet]]'s reward."

25. In each experience, then, it is sincerity, hope, faith, that urges on the individual - toward the means, the manner, for the greater expression.

26. So in the experiences of this entity we find Venus, Uranus, Saturn, Jupiter and Neptune as the greater environmental influences from a cosmic or outer urge, - through the [[Dreams|dreams]] or [[Visions|visions]] of the entity.

27. Hence we find these as latent influences in the experience of this entity in this particular sojourn:

28. Beauty is conceivable to the entity in the lowliest as well as the highest.  An appreciation of such is a faculty of the imagination of the entity.  It needs, then, to be the more cultivated.  For, remember, [[Law of Moses|the law]] is as He has given, "Think on these things - [[Meditation|meditate]] on these things."  This is cultivation.  Not in those tendencies that oft arise from the Uranian forces of the extremes, so that there is the change in the experience and a knowledge "rubbed on" by self, as it were, in its associations with its fellow men, and then immediately peeled off to see what has happened.  A crude illustration, but the entity will see this oft in self, - if there is the self-analysis of [[Purpose|purposes]] and desires.

29. Men's (and women's) activities are to sow the seed.  It's God's part, and promise, to give the increase.  It is an answering again of spirit to spirit, the quickening, the enlivening, the life-giving.  For, God and love alone are life in its essence.

30. All else are but lines to those forces, for God in His love and in His wisdom has sought such as companions with Him in the movement which began from "God moved and there was light."

31. In thy movements, then, let thy thoughts, thy purposes, thy hopes, thy desires, ever be towards that of light.  Not as light of the sun, or even of the stars, - for these are but reflections of that GLORIOUS light which is in the [[Sons of God|SON of God]], who is thy light, thy Brother, - yea, thy Lord, thy God!

32. In Jupiter we find the broad [[Visions|vision]] of the entity, as well as the mighty force, power or influence as may be wielded by the entity.  Not that the entity may always be conscious of such, but that is the purpose, - that may not be seen, may not be handled, but that penetrates to the very heart and core of all activity in which man may find himself in his relationships to his fellow man, or to things, or to conditions which are apparent or latent.

33. This then the entity will do, as it applies itself in its abilities as the writer, as the interpreter of those forces which have impelled men and women in the varied climes, in the varied environs, - whether it be from the waters of the Ganges, or from the holy rocks of Horeb, or from the desert sands of Arabia or [[Egypt|Egypt]].

These - their intent and purpose - are one.  For, Know, O [2067], the Lord thy God is ONE!

34. Would that all souls could, would, comprehend that great, great sentence, - which has been, is, the motivating force in ALL who seek!  not in all who impel, but in all who SEEK to live, who SEEK to manifest, who SEEK to give expression of God's force or power.

35. For, as all power is ordained of Him, so acknowledge same in thy expressions of that which brings faith and hope and brotherly love and kindness.  For these are the fruits of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]], - that take hold upon, and bring one into, the chamber - into the [[Holy of Holies|holy of holies]]; where He makes Himself known, even to His brethren, as He did in those lands so long ago, with which ye were so closely associated or affiliated!

36. In the influences which arise innately and manifestedly from Neptune, we find the forces or powers of the earthly mother - water - as an influence; coming more in the form of the warning regarding the occult or mystic powers.  These are children of doubt and fear, - yea, they are also children of light.  But know their import.  Know that their manifestations have oft sought personal manifestation, as in the spirits of those who sought in the beginnings for personal gratification, - when the very evolution of materiality presented an opportunity.  Thus they brought to themselves confusion, as well as the necessity of man's own materiality by encasement in matter.

37. Then, seek that such be even as He gave, "a sign cannot be given - MAY not be given - to an adulterous, to a sinful generation; but only to those whose cry is oft, and sincere, 'Create within me a righteous heart, O God, and renew a righteous spirit within me!'"

38. These are not words alone, but are as that which applied in one's daily life may lead to an opening of [[Veil of the Temple|the veil]] which enters into the [[Holy of Holies|holy of holies]], - even as that [[Veil of the Temple|veil]] which was rent when His Spirit cried unto His God.

39. Then, make that God - His God - thy God.  What do ye worship? Might, spirit, power, impulse, hope, or what?  Whose power seek ye?  Thine own, or that in thy weakness ye may be made strong by His abiding light in thee?

40. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - these, as indicated, are many; they have varied much as to their forces and powers, as to how the emotions have manifested.  For, as must be the proper interpretation of same, - material sojourns are as the senses of the material body, just as the [[Astrology|astrological]] sojourns are as the mental or [[Dreams|dream]] body.  Thus they find expression oft in FEELINGS towards places, conditions, individuals, that in thine own inner self send out that radiation which (in mystical study) may be seen in the [[Aura|aura]], or in the study of a part of [[Numerology|numerology]] as to the number, or in the very emotions as may be expressed  in lines of the hand, feet or face.  For, remember [[Law of Moses|the law]], - all carry the mark in their body; just as ye bear His mark in thy forehead.

41. In these manners, then, - oft in the emotions being five rather than the three (as the earth is interpreted as a three-dimensional sphere of activity), we come to the realm through such emotions or senses - of the fourth, yea and to the fifth dimension.  Thus the warning that has been given, - that because of such emotions in self ye will find others oft question thy authority, because of thy interpreting of fourth or fifth dimensional experiences and expressing same in three-dimensional terms!

42. Only those who know God or have sought Him may interpret even His walks among men!

43. So, be mindful, - as He warned Thomas, Philip, Andrew and [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]], as well as showed to James and [[John (Apostle)|John]], that these things are oft hard to be understood by those who may only know that which answers to the sensuousness-consciousness of an entity. Not the senses as the higher realm, but sensuousness that begets (and its children are!) lasciviousness and those forces that engorge or indulge in those things that gratify appetites, and those things that become a part of many who become self-indulgent in habit forming, habit creating.

44. For, the animal man is a creature of habit.  But learn rather from such the lesson, and not become so much a part of same. For in nature and in the animal instincts we find only the expressions of a [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] of hope, and never of fear, - save created by man in his indulging in the gratification of material appetites.

45. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity, during the early experiences in the land, when there were the questionings as to those forces, powers, mandates and acts about Salem and Provincetown.

46. Then in the name Dorothy Manley, the entity suffered much for a just cause; misunderstood, misinterpreted.  For the voices as heard, the movements as experienced, even as a young girl, were hounded by those who understood not.

47. Learn ye then that lesson in the present, "Cast not pearls before dogs nor swine."  For as the dog returns to his vomit and the hog to his wallow, so will they that listen at pearls of great price yet have no thought, no mind, no purpose for same.

48. In the experience the entity found material hardships, mental satisfaction, spiritual awakening; and thus gained in all phases of its experience through that sojourn.

49. The entity was young in years when it lost its experience through the so oft being dipped, as to contract that known in the present as pneumonia, - and passed in a high fever.

50. In the present such has been the experience of the entity, to catch a glimpse oft, under similar circumstances, from and in various fields of activity, of a something that has taken hold upon self.  Remember, - these are shadows, and that the real is in Him.

51. Before that the entity was in the land [[[Palestine|Palestine]]] of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] of masters' sojourn in the earth.

52. During those periods the entity was a queen of no mean estate, and took hold upon the words of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], - though never personally coming in contact with Him.  For the entity then was the companion or wife of [[Trial of Jesus|Herod]], who sought His destruction.  Yet the entity's experience there, as Thesea, sought a closer comprehending of the [[Wise Men|Wise Men]].

53. Thus the entity will, or may in this experience find, even by the conversation with one of these - that is in the city of its own birth - the interpretations of those experiences and periods that brought about the determinations to cry out, as it did, that brought material or physical extinction - and bore in its activity the very same influences and forces that were upon the minds of many by [[Crucifixion|Calvary]].

54. For as the entity reasoned with the [[Essenes|Essenes]], as well as conversed with the [[Wise Men|Wise Men]] who came with the new messages to the world, the entity proclaimed, - yea, that pronouncement that He Himself then being announced had given, - "Others may do as they may, but as for me and my house, we will serve the living God."

55. Do not interpret this to mean those who may be in thine own physical household.  Thy house is indeed thy body, - THAT is [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God.  THAT is the whole house made to conform to [[Free Will|the will]], the way.  "He that loves me keeps my commandments," in body, in mind, in soul.

Through the experience the entity gained, the entity lost; yet those very happenings of that land were written by the entity, - as to how there were the longings in the hearts of those peoples, especially from the days of the return to the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], through the provisions of those forces that were as in answer to a law, "If ye call, I will hear, saith the Lord of hosts."

57. Hence the entity wrote concerning those peoples through those periods from the prophets to the period when the announcing had been to those groups who sought for His coming.  And these [her writings] were a part of the records (as destroyed by her own children) in the [[Alexandria|Alexandrian]] library, as well as in the 'city in the hills and the plains.' And yet, as has been before indicated, know that the message is ONE; for the Lord thy God is one, and that Jesus IS the one who was promised from that day, "...and her seed shall bruise his head."

When there was given that understanding to the Princess as to how Joseph had made himself known to his brethren, and thus had asked Pharaoh - through the pleadings of the entity - that he might seek his father in his old age, and to his brethren give good for evil, - there came another awakening to the entity in those experiences when there were such activities that the peoples brought those forces into the founding of what may indeed be called the Society of Magicians. Then, in those expressions of indifference - as ye have found in reference to those in thy own experience, of those in thy own life, that took away from thee the hope that ye sought in thy earthly self - others are brought in their proper relationship, their proper sphere of activity for greater awakening.

They each have come step by step.  For, while there is physical evolution, there is also soul growth, not an evolution, but an awareness in material things.

85. (Q)  Can you tell me what relation my former husband was to me in another incarnation?
(A)  A dipper in the experience before this; attracted to, and repelled also.  In the Palestine or [[Promised Land|Promised land]], a guard or a protector in the household of the companion at that time.
