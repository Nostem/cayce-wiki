# TEXT OF READING 1556-1 F 58

**Reading [[1556-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/28/1938  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 7552

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1556], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. Laura Canterbury.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body - and those impressions of same - Mrs. [1556], of ... Street, [[New York City|New York City]].

2. As we find, there is rather the complication of disturbing conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body; though with the proper adjustments, corrections, and the proper eliminations established, we should find the body responding and soon returning near to normal conditions.

3. These then are conditions as we find them with this body, Mrs. [1556]:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find there are indications of an intestinal toxic disturbance, as from the lack of proper activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system as related to assimilation.

5. Thus we find the liver and the spleen, rather than the pancreas, involved in the conditions.

6. As to the effect produced, we find rather the combination of effects - through the pressures created in the system - along centers where cerebrospinal and sympathetic make greater connections.

7. This toxic force, as combined with the excess acidity as produced BY the toxic forces, causes the mucous membranes of the throat, and especially the soft tissue of face, to become much involved at times.

8. Also those pressures from the poisons and subluxations in the dorsal and cervical area, combined with mental worries, oft produce depressing conditions throughout the system.

9. THE NERVOUS SYSTEM, this as indicated is involved by not only pressures produced from poisons, where there is the attempt for activity in areas from which organs receive their impulses - as in the throat, bronchi and lungs which have become involved, but from specific subluxations - as in the first of the dorsal area and through the head and neck cervicals; as the 1st, 2nd and 3rd, even to the 4th and 5th cervicals - all will need readjustments, as it were.

10. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, as we find, owing to the disturbances in the system, the organs have become involved more sympathetically than organically.

11. Hence their functionings have been spasmodic reactions.

12. IN MAKING APPLICATIONS FOR THE HELPFUL INFLUENCES AND CORRECTIVE MEASURES, THEN:

13. First we find it would be well to keep a mental attitude of hopefulness; not necessarily of resignedness, but the trust in the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] - that are the promise of each [[Soul|soul]] that would keep His ways.

14. Be in that attitude of an ACTIVE passiveness, then; knowing - as He has said - that he whom the Lord loveth He chasteneth; and that - again - the Lord loveth those who help themselves.

15. Then, for the physical activity, we would first begin with taking a quarter teaspoonful of the antacid reacting properties found in Alcaroid; at least twice each day for two or three days; after the morning meal and after the heavy meal of the day - whether noon or evening.  This will serve to make for activities through the digestive system that will produce the gradual changes for activity in the duodenum, calling for better activities through the liver AND the spleen.

16. After this is taken for three days, then take Zilatone; one tablet about half an hour after the morning meal, two tablets about two hours after the evening meal.

17. Then leave off both the Alcaroid AND Zilatone for two to three days.  Then repeat again.  This procedure is to cleanse the system.

18. At the same time we would begin with adjustments - as may be administered by the activities of Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller - EVERY day for five days; leave off a week; then take again for five days.  Leave off another week, then have another period of treatment.

19. With these kept - and the precautions taken as to the diet, keeping less of the starches and more of the vegetable forces - we will find we will bring the near to normal forces for this body.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  How is my heart?
(A)  Sympathetically irregular.

22. (Q)  My left ear bothers at times.
(A)  With the corrections indicated, we will find many corrections in the body, or the individual activities of sympathetic reaction in various portions will be improved.

23. (Q)  Have had hay fever from childhood.
(A)  The corrections, as well as the activities for the betterment should prevent the reoccurrence so much of these disturbances.

Do those things as indicated, and we will find the better conditions for this body.

24. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
