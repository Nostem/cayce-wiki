# TEXT OF READING 4906-4

**Reading [[4906-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/22/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12999

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of January 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4906].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Section 7, Township 14 North, 
Time of Reading                Range 14 East in 
2:30 P. M.                     Okmulgee County, Oklahoma.

1. LS:  Now, you have before you the lands located in Section 7, Township 14 North, Range 14 East situated directly North of the town of Morris, Oklahoma, in Okmulgee County, and is two hundred feet West of the paved road going North out of Morris on the left hand side of the road and about seven miles North of Morris.  You will give the possibilities of drilling an oil well in the eighty acres in this section of which [4906] of Okmulgee, Oklahoma, has a nine-sixteenth interest.  You will give the depth at which oil will be encountered, the quantity of oil, the difficulties that would be encountered in drilling.

2. EC:  Yes, we have these here.

3. Now, we find this location, and at the present time, now, there is some troubles (minor ones) about the operations.

4. As to the possibilities of production for oil here, we will find this will prove satisfactory.  There will be some difference in the way this is handled when the gas and oil is to be separated, for the difference in depth will make the change, and the adjoining country to the East and Southwest is very good also.  In this we will find there will be several hundred (three) barrels of oil production per day, with strong gas pressure, first showing occurring in the twenty-seven eighty (2,780) structure, and the increase in these under the sand will produce the oil and gas.  This is well.  This may be handled at nominal cost for such drilling. The water conditions will be helped.  The atmospheric, the weather conditions that are to interfere will be of assistance and better later on.  Handle these productions.

5. (Q)  Is [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] that is being drilled in the Southeast corner of the north half of the Southeast quarter of that section a good place for drilling?
(A)  We have just given it and conditions about [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]. This is the one we are speaking of.  Drill it.

6. We have finished with this reading.
