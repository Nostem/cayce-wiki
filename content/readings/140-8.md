# TEXT OF READING 140-8 F 23 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[140-8]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/18/1925  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 544

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by, or through, her brother-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, [140], here.  We have had this before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  Sunday night, November 15, or early morning of November 16, 1925, at home.  "Helen, G's Nurse (the G's are close friends of ours - Helen being the nurse of G's baby as Emma is the nurse of our baby [143]).  This Helen was run over by a motor car (in my [[Dreams|dream]]).  Emma and [143], Helen and the G's baby were returning home from the Beach when the accident occurred.  I was out visiting at the time and Emma rushed in for me to tell me that [143] was at home safe, but that she was going for a doctor for Helen.  I went for my baby, who was at our old 86th Street home and found her in the basement.  I took her out for awhile, then went home and upon examining her found no serious injuries."
(A)  In this there is given to the mind, in this manner, those conditions as respect the condition which happened in the hearing and sight of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s mind (child) and [[Physical Body|physical body]], that which caused the distresses to the body. And as the body finds in the carrying out of those conditions as given, regarding the care and attention of self to same, there becomes no harm to the body.  Another condition is presented in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds that there is the answering of the desire in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces to the physical, see?  for this is as given, the indication of conditions which WERE presented, and NOT as may be expected as an accident to anyone.  [See 143-1 on 10/31/24 in regarding warning of auto accident for [143].]

3. We are through for the present.
