# TEXT OF READING 4905-55

**Reading [[4905-55]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/18/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12980

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.  An involuntary reading, that is, without suggestion to obtain it, following a reading on the EC work [[254] series] and a physical for Mr. [953].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now we have those conditions as respecting [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] as being drilled on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick.  While the operations have been slow, we find that the running of the two-prong grab, with the slip socket, would now be able to remove obstruction from this hole.  While, should this, on account of the conditions surrounding the pulling of such obstruction, be unsatisfactory, then the cementing and drilling through would prove of the sufficient to prevent further trouble from same, provided sufficient time for hardening is given, and the full amount capable of being dumped is placed in the hole.  Through this stratum we will find only a few feet before the oil in large quantities are reached.  Patience and endurance are being tested, yet we find the results will fully compensate, as far as dollars and cents are concerned.  Rather be, then, concerned, each and everyone, as to the use that such monies may be put. Let not this become a hindrance to that development of the spiritual, for to gain the whole world and lose a [[Soul|soul]] is not worth the price.  Be thou faithful, then, unto the end, for soon there will be much work to be considered, and many dollars to be expended.

2. We are through for the present.
