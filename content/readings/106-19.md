# TEXT OF READING 106-19 F 52

**Reading [[106-19]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/12/1929  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 255

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Check Physical)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the conditions as surround this body, and may now better give that as will be helpful and assist the body than we have before.  While those conditions show a general improvement from that as has been exhibited, there still are those conditions that do disturb the body, and from time to time bring resultant conditions or effects that cause distress or uneasiness to the body.

2. We would, then, to meet the conditions as are seen, wherein the stomach, digestive system and the reflexes from same are disturbed, give those of a medicinal property that will relieve and assist this condition, as well as to keep active much of the same applications for the body in respect to manipulations for the assimilating and for the eliminating systems of the body, giving special attention to those conditions existent in the region of the 8th and 9th dorsal plexus, as well as that in the 4th and 3rd lumbar region, as well as some of the sacral centers that need attention. Keeping the body in the open as much as normal and nominally can be done.  Rest is well for the body, but rest in the manner of keeping away from crowds, and from surroundings that bring turmoil of mind, or of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and we will find much better conditions coming to the body.

3. As for medicinal properties, prepare as this:

4. To 4 ounces of simple syrup, add:

Tincture Valerian.........................1 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya.........................1/2 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium............50 drops,
10% solution Bromide Potassium...........20 drops,
Oil of Peppermint.........................5 drops,
Tincture of Capsici.......................2 drops.

Shake solution well together before the dose is taken.  Take about every 3 hours until there is the full reaction from same, and an added activity to the kidneys - half a teaspoonful at the dose, in water or plain.

5. Let the diet be much after that as has been outlined, with this as some changes:  Not much bread, but that that is taken should be of whole wheat or rye bread; only of these may the body eat and digest well.  Those of not too much starches, and pertaining principally to proteins and of the non-acid reacting in system.  Do that, and we will bring the bettered conditions for this body, [106].

6. The changes in the reaction of these are the DESIRES on the part of the individual itself.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
