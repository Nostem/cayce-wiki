# TEXT OF READING 5029-1 M 59

**Reading [[5029-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/17/1944  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 13147

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5029], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  [After repeating suggestion in an undertone]  Yes, we have the body here, [5029].

3. As we find, there are many disorders and disturbances, but most of these are pretty well understood, except as to what first caused the disturbances.  For many of these have come from exterior injuries; as the fractures in the structural portions of the body.  All of these arise from these disorders.

4. First there was the lack of proper circulation in the heart's activity, in the circulation through the central nervous system, which in the greater part arose from a plethoric condition existing in the colon; that caused the first heart disorder, and the pressures produced on same caused the first disturbance.  These, combined, make rather a serious condition.

5. If there is quiet, with persistent and prayerful application of those things suggested, we find that we may create a better equilibrium throughout the body.

6. First we would begin having [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, about 10 days apart until five or six good [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] have been taken - or continue these until there is no mucous indicated in the stool.

7. After the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] have been taken we would begin with the grape juices for the reduction of weight and for better assimilation.  Four times each day, about 1/2 hour before each meal and before retiring, take 2 ounces of [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] (Welch's) preferably, with an ounce of plain water.

8. At the same period, we would begin using the Radio Active Appliance for an hour each day, preferably in the evening. Do attach the same plate first each time.  Do keep the attachment plates very, very clean.  Cleanse before attaching and cleanse when taking off.

9. During this hour let [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] keep in [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]], with these thoughts being put into the body's own words:

KEEP THIS BODY-MIND IN ACCORD AND IN TUNE WITH THY DIVINE [[Purpose|PURPOSE]] FOR THIS BODY AND MIND AT THIS PERIOD IN ITS EXPERIENCE.  LET THY [[Purpose|PURPOSE]], THY [[Ideal|IDEALS]], THY HOPE BE MINE AT THIS TIME.

10. Each day when the Radio Active Appliance is removed, give the body a massage to stimulate superficial circulation in head, neck, through the body where the paralysis prevents the body from better activity.  Use this combination of oils:

Olive Oil................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil...............2 ounces,
Lanolin (melted).......1/4 ounce.

Begin at the side of the head, each side, going around the temple, around the mastoid area, under the chin to the point of the chin, down the neck and along the spine, along on the spinal area as well as on the sympathetic nervous system. It should require at least 30 to 40 minutes to give the body this thorough massage.  Massage at the brachial center, following out the nerves.  This should be done in the neuropathic manner; that is, following the trunk nerves along the whole torso and the extremities also.

11. Do this daily and we will find that, if the diet is kept in accord - refraining from a great deal of starches of any nature, yet just sufficient for body-fuel and body-strength - having a great deal of leafy vegetables - we will bring better conditions for this body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What should be done for tired feet?
(A)  All of this combination indicated.  The use of the equalization of the flow of circulation will aid.  While there will not be felt any [[Vibration|vibration]] from the Radio Active Appliance, until it has been kept for some little time, we find that it will equalize the circulation and bring rest to the body.  Do put the Radio Active Appliance in the ice 20 minutes before it is attached to the body.  Following the treatment with this each day, there should be the massage with the oils, which should include the feet, as well as other areas requiring superficial stimulation.

After a month or two there should be the repeating of the colonic, you see, to relieve pressures on the heart, as well as on the torso; that is, after the first series of colonics, leave off a month and then have another series.

Do that.

14. We are through with this reading.  [In most cases when EC reached the end of a reading he would say, "Ready for questions".  In this case however, he did not do so.]

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.)
