# TEXT OF READING 137-20 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-20]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/28/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 410

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 28th day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding any of these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, with the [[Dreams|dreams]] that come to this body.  These, as we see, are the correlation of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], with the psychic or cosmic forces presenting the lessons to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the psychic or phenomenized state, and may be used in the way and manner indicated if [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would gain that proper insight into the laws that govern such phenomena.  For, as has been given, all [[Dreams|dreams]] of the cosmic or [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces are for the use of the individual, when interpreted aright and are used in the correct manner.  Ready for dreams.

3. (Q)  Friday morning, September 25, 1925, at home.  "I was riding on the boat used from [[New York City|New York]] to [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Highlands (then train to Deal), and as we arrived at our destination, I got off the boat and changed to horseback.  Now many of us were in our group on horseback - [900], [140], [106], and [136] - principally [136], my sister-in-law.  As we rode along, I thought of National Dairy Products - a stock I am holding for higher prices.  I noted [136] riding beside me. We passed a lunatic asylum and I became frightened.  As we rode along, I noted that each one of the crazy inmates was kept in an individual cage.  I felt reassured at seeing that. Then we came to a man who was completely naked and seemed harmless enough in the bushes, but as we came along we frightened him, and he ran out for [136].  All the while National Dairy Products stock kept going through the back of my mind.  The crazy nude man grabbed [136]'s foot out of the stirrup and sought to pull her down from the horse. I rode over to her aid and tried to kick the attacking man and in so doing (and still thinking of National Dairy Products stock, and desperate with fright), I fell from my horse as did [136].  I awakened shaking with fear." (A)  In the interpretations of this we find many various phases of the life as may pertain to the individual and those seen in the [[Visions|vision]] or dream.  As is seen, [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] by boat meaning a definite destination that is being enacted, or as coming to pass to the individual as regards those stocks, those conditions as pertain to conditions relating in the life of others to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and the horse and riders representing then messages to all.

The crazy people, or those unmindful, or without mind, representing the unmindful condition of the markets as regards to the Dairy Products.  The reassurance in seeing the inmates each confined shows the various points in the advancement of same in the mind of the individual.  The seeing then of the nude, wild, man, is representing the wild condition that will appear on the Stock Exchange in many respects, and the entity needs be careful that this has been disposed of when this condition begins, see?

With the assistance as lent to the entity to whom the body goes to assist, there is represented conditions that will, or might, arise in the life of the entity that is seen attacked by the individual, and the entity may lend the assistance necessary, through the brotherly advice as may be given, to prevent any fault arising in the life, in the manner, in the conditions as may surround these.

Then, take note, take heed.  Be careful of the conditions which come to exist in the social and filial relations, and also those that will come to pass in the Exchange, or the wild conditions as will be seen in the market, see?

4. (Q)  Has this anything to do with National Dairy Products stock, with its future movements?
(A)  As given.  When this market begins in that wild manner, be rid of them.

5. (Q)  What has [136], my sister-in-law, to do with this?
(A)  As given, when conditions arise that might bring strained relations with a number or member of group seen, let the entity act in that capacity of the assistant to aid the entity in gaining a perfect mental equilibrium regarding conditions which are arising, see?  This represents, then, conditions that may be misunderstood by the sister-in-law, that the entity would be able, through the advice, through the kindly attention, be able to prevent the aches and pains that would arise from such strained relations.  Act then accordingly, see?  As to who, how or when these will arise with groups as seen, perhaps with all may be involved, see? as to when, when the conditions of the wild changes are apparent in the market, see?  for all will be under a strained condition.

6. (Q)  What should be her attitude and action in relation to the meaning of this?
(A)  Accept that advice and counsel given.  [See 136-12 Reports and 136-18 Reports.]

7. (Q)  "I had a distinct second dream, another one in addition to the above.  You will recall it to me, give the interpretation, the lesson it teaches and give me advice as to what I should or had better do in accordance."
(A)  In this we see again is given that intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] of the entity to make that [[Oneness|oneness]] with Universal Forces, through which the entity would be able to gain for self, assistance in a physical, mental and financial manner.  Then give self to the more perfect understanding of those laws as appertain to same.  For such instructions have been given and must be heeded by the entity, if the best would come to this developing and seeking mind, [137].

8. We are through for the present.
