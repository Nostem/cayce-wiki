# TEXT OF READING 906-4 F 62

**Reading [[906-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/30/1943  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 5715

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [920], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At the home of her 
Time of Reading                          sister, Mrs. [920], 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Many changes have taken place in the body since we had it here.  And it might well be said here, if conditions were not so serious with the body in the present, "We told you so!"

3. If there had been carried out consistently the use of the Appliance in the manners indicated for this body, we find that those growths or accumulations need not have developed through the abdominal and pelvic area.

4. We would not interfere with that being applied.  For the activity in or under the present conditions may only be maintained by keeping the body with the desire to carry on.

5. So, there should be more of the environs to encourage the body to attain to those hopes and [[Purpose|purposes]] in the body-mind, if there would be the continuation of activities in the present.

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [920] - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file
