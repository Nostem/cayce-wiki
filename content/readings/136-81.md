# TEXT OF READING 136-81 F 24

**Reading [[136-81]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/5/1929  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 388

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], and Mr. L. B. Cayce]

Time of Reading 
5:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., La. 
(Suggestion given at beginning of reading 140-30.)

1. EC:  Now in the body here of [136] we find there has been considerations and conditions through which such an experience has passed.  THIS body presents a DIFFERENT understanding of conception and of the awakening of the body to that of service it renders its Creator in such an activity; yet not YET should there come such conditions for THIS body, for there is the necessity of the more perfect understanding, that the relations as would bring such conditions are to be ONE - one in [[Purpose|purpose]], one in intent, one in the FULLNESS of time - and WITH this there may come the more perfect - more perfect - being.

2. (Q)  In other words, my own [[Prayer|prayers]] years before our present baby was born played a part in this subsequent birth?
(A)  Played a part in his subsequent birth, even AS that UNION of strength between the two bodies bringing into being may act just in the manner as has been given, as to how doubt, fear, or any element that leads to either, is a hindrance to that brought into being, that attracted into the sphere, and the activities of same throughout.  We are through for the present.
