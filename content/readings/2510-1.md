# TEXT OF READING 2510-1 M 51

**Reading [[2510-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/2/1941  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 9441

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of June, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2510], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [2413].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have those anxieties, those disturbances as regards the body, [2510].

3. As we find, - through the disturbances from an old injury, and through the unbalancing of the chemical forces of the body, - segregations, and disturbances in same, would necessitate operative measures as the better means in the present.

4. When these have been accomplished, and there is some relief from the tensions - and the poisons being thrown back into the system by the necessary cleansing of the system - before and during the periods of recuperation from such operative forces to the abdominal areas, we would then give that as might be the more helpful in attaining or in keeping a balance, and in relieving the causes; thus making for the better conditions.

5. But as we find, such proportions have been reached that the operative forces would be that necessary in the present.

6. Do that.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Mr. [2413]

(Let us know when you are recuperating from the operation, so that we may make an appointment for a full Physical Reading to give instructions from that period forward.  This would be done without any further charge to you.)
