# TEXT OF READING 2595-2 M 8

**Reading [[2595-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/9/1941  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 9619

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [573], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2595]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are somewhat different from those we have had here before, though the sources of the disturbances are about the same.

4. That in a manner there were some responses to that suggested for helpfulness would indicate that the suggestions were correct.

5. The troubles arising from poor eliminations were brought on by not careful administering of those things suggested, AND quite a diversion in diet.

6. So, the areas where coordination should be made in eliminating channels brought about tendencies for [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to swell and for the irritation to superficial circulation.

7. Too much sugars were included, and thus causing a backing up between the eliminations as should be normal from any [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance, especially between kidneys and liver.

8. Thus the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], the glands in the groins, those in the throat and the stomach give distress.

9. We would continue, or we would give consistently those things suggested, - the rubs both for the spine and the abdomen.

10. But we would now have at least a dozen [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, with special reference to those areas in the lumbar and sacral where some external injury has also contributed to this unbalanced condition; and especially in the upper dorsal and through the cervical area.

11. Have these, the first two in a week, the next week one, the next week two, the next week one, the next week two.  Have them in that order, see?

12. Also we would be more mindful as to diet.  Have plenty of citrus fruits and also the re-inforced cereals, hot as well as dry cereals, but not the citrus fruit AND cereals at the same meal.  Alternate these.  Have baked apples, but not raw apples.  These may be a part of the morning diet also, with milk or cream.  Have prunes, stewed prunes - including the combinations of prunes, raisins, dried peaches, apricots and the like.

13. Also have at least a part of one meal each day consist of green foods; raw vegetables with mayonnaise or salad dressing.

14. Liver and fowl, if flesh is taken, should be a part of the diet.  Have all kinds of vegetables, as well as the leafy or meat soups, - these with not too much grease.

15. These as we find should bring better conditions for this body, - [2595].

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What causes catch in groin?
(A)  As indicated, an injury - and the settlings of poisons from too much sugars.  Have little sugar on any of the cereals, and that used - let it be brown sugar, or beet sugar.

18. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Richardson) Copy to Ass'n file
