# TEXT OF READING 340-35 F 49

**Reading [[340-35]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/4/1937  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2627

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of April, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self, through her sister, Miss [243], Member of the Ass''n. for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   ... St., 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [340].

2. As we find the acute conditions here with this body, as we have had before, arise more from nerves and those effects that same produce upon the system; with the general conditions as arise from changing in the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system as combined with the weaknesses through the intestinal system.

3. As we find, under the direction of a physician, a combination of Bromides - or Bromidia - would be the more preferable for the body in the present; but under the direction of physician and making for an easing through the abdominal area as we find.

4. The painting of the areas with a solution of Aconite and Laudanum; three parts Laudanum to one part Aconite, and then hot salt packs - salt preferable to Salts - these would be the more preferable in the present.

5. Be mindful of the diet; and it is necessary for the body to see constructive forces rather than animosity or too great an anxiety as to general conditions.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. Packs would be used, of course, only until pain is removed. If this requires the changing once or twice during the first day or period, very good.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
