# TEXT OF READING 2-12 M 49

**Reading [[2-12]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/29/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 13

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Theodore J. Berger.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2], - this we have had before.  We are finding some changes taking place in the general conditions of the body, some retractions that have been or are rather worrying the body.  These are only retractions, and with the continuation of those conditions as has been given, as to keeping up the eliminations in system - Do not OVERDO it by too much of the light or electrical forces as applied to the system, when those are used in the sun and the sand - but when these are NOT used, these SHOULD be applied - the lights, see?  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How often? & in what order should fume baths, sand packs & [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] lights be given?
(A)  Do not use the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] when sand packs are used, see?  Sand packs may be taken every day, while the ultra violet would only be taken every OTHER day, see?  The sand packs should be with the eyes WELL shaded, see?  The conditions as for the fume baths, these are BEST given with those of the salts, as has been outlined.  These may be taken twice each week, see?

3. (Q)  Give cause and treatment of backache across kidney area?
(A)  Retraction.  Treatments are, keep up those we have been giving.

4. (Q)  Are the teeth alright now?
(A)  Not ALRIGHT as yet, but the condition or that causing a good deal of the condition has been removed.  [See 2-9 and report indicating that the dentist finally found the main trouble tooth referred to and removed it.]

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    "  Ass'n office
"    "  [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    "  Mr. [2]'s wife
