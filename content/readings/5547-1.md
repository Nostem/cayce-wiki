# TEXT OF READING 5547-1 F 6

**Reading [[5547-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/5/1929  Sex: F  Age: 6  ReadingID: 13823

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...] - and by her cousin, Mr. [...], via Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find the body here - [5547] and those conditions physical and otherwise as surround same.

2. There may be much said, and there has been much written, concerning conditions of this nature - yet these conditions and the characteristics of the condition produced in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are as varying as individual lives and as individual response to material or to mental conditions in this material world.

3. These conditions, then, are much of a prenatal character, and would be necessary to go much into conditions as surrounded the advent of the physical being into the material plane and as related to conditions as surrounded same, for the full understanding of cause of conditions, as WELL as into that of the development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an ENTITY]] in its relation to the material, as well as to the cosmic or spiritual, or psychic plane.

4. That particularly interesting to the individuals seeking that as may be helpful to this material manifestation at this time, will have more to do than with the conditions of the present.  There is seen there are improper coordination of the sympathetic, cerebro-spinal, and the plexuses' reflexes to the material body - as well as the distribution of impulses from the nerve system, as relating to coordinating of activity in the physical functioning of the body; and again we find there are reactions in the physical that apparently have little or nothing to do with these conditions that are troublesome, these being purely a physical reflex of assimilated forces.  Hence there are the combination of conditions to be considered in administering that as would bring bettered conditions for the body.  These may, as WE find, be MATERIALLY aided - yet will require time, patience, endurance, long-suffering, much change, and many contributing forces - both from the physical and cosmic forces and influences, to bring about the proper relations of the physical, the mental, and the responses of same in this body; and unless those so in charge of the conditions - mental, physical AND spiritual - intend to carry out these suggestions as may be made, NOW, and from time to time as respecting these conditions, then well that they do not even begin in part.

5. First we would give that there be the administration of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the system as are in accord with the necessary [[Vibration|vibration]] in the special physical entity, that will bring a coordinating material force in the activity of the body for the proper response of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] materially to that of the [[Mental Body|mental body]], and aid this in the power of suggestion as these administrations ARE made in such a manner as to build that as will bring PROPER coordination of same.  This, as we find, may be accorded in this way and manner:  First construct such a [[Vibration|vibratory]] recurrent machine, as this:

6. To one gallon and a half of rain water, add (This should be placed in a stone crock):

Plain Charcoal.................1/2 pound,
Plain or Common Bluestone....1 1/2 pound, then
Sulfuric Acid C.P................1 ounce, and
Common Zinc......................6 drams.

This solution, as we see, would ordinarily make a low discharge of electrical vibratory force - but for this body should be charged to such a charge as to make a DEFINITE discharge from its activity when applied to the body for a vibratory motion to be set up in the system, so that the discharge of vibration from the body itself will be in accord with the charge from the machine or battery as to make concurrent forces in system build along the vibratory centers of the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic system definite conditions in the body.  Hence we would place two poles in solution, with a wooden top covering the container - the poles extending ABOVE the top an inch or an inch and a half, one pole being of copper - quarter to half an inch in length - or breadth and thickness, the length sufficient to reach above the top of pole, the other of a nickel, same proportions in length, breadth and thickness.  This, then, should be charged with 60 cycles of a 120 volts for 35 minutes, see?  We will find the discharge at that time will be near 12 1/2 ohms.

7. Now the machine or battery is ready to be attached to the body.

8. We would annex, then, leads of copper wire (insulated) from each of the poles - of sufficient length to be easily attached to the body.  The one from the copper pole should have thin copper plate, see?  the one from the nickel pole should have thin [[Germany|German]] silver or nickel plate to attach to the body, see?  These would be attached, one to the small of the back - or OPPOSITE the solar plexus center, between the 9th and 10th dorsal.  The other would be attached to the wrist.  First the right and then the left - attaching the COPPER plate to the wrist first, then the nickel or [[Germany|German]] silver plate to the solar plexus radial center.  This should be applied at least twenty minutes each day for the first five days.  Then REST three days.  Then begin again and attach for at least THIRTY-FIVE minutes each day for at least five days.  Then rest three days, and attach again each day for an hour, until the solution or the battery shows that the discharge has equalized with the system, until there is no discharge from the battery.  Then solution should be renewed, and this kept up until another discharge has been completed. Then we will begin, after the second discharge of machine, to the coordinating of the system - with the proper [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as would be given at that time, with the proper diet that is to be assimilated in the body.

9. If this is to be carried out, begin with that as given - and we will assist as conditions progress.  This is the proper manner for the handling of this body, [5547].  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]
"    " Mr. [554] Two copies to Mother and Cousin
