# TEXT OF READING 900-297 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-297]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/28/1927  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5500

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Morning of Sunday, Jan. 23, 1927.  A bitter quarrel with [137] in which Ma took sides against me.  It got so bitter that I struck [137] and complications set in that involved the whole family.
(A)  In the body conscious mind we find there those elements in which, in this emblematical manner, is given as a correlation between the experiences of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] force and those body conscious conditions.  These, then, are as the warnings, that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may - in this understandable way and manner of the conscious mind, the body mind - be so warned, that such conditions may not arise, see?  for these conditions are but as the body conscious mind reasoning, as it were, with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  These do not prelude [neither do they allude] to any given condition - for, as is seen, there are few specific conditions felt, known, experienced, save that those conditions arise that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would not have self indulging in at the saner moments, nor would it bring down railings or troubles from all.  Hence the conditions necessary, then, that the body conscious mind keep in that way and manner as is known to entity.  This does not give any specific condition, nor need such conditions be expected to arise - UNLESS the entity entertains, harbors, same to the extent - for "Thoughts are Deeds.  They may become crimes or [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]]."

4. (Q)  I seemed to have gone to Philadelphia to see Moe Wallack and his wife.  She told me of some mother-in-law trouble. Then I rushed out to catch a train home.  On the way I looked at a time table (I saw the docks and boats at the foot of a steep hill down which I was running) and saw C & O on one side and B & O on the other.  At the foot of the hill I became lost, couldn't find the trains.  I wandered about a hotel seeking and asking my way, but none could tell me.  I came to a car yard and asked a man if a train went out with a diner and he said he could fix me up a car right there and I replied in the negative.  I said the Lehigh Valley also runs out of here and he replied dubiously that it did.  He told me to take the train that runs along the east bank of the river. It was getting late and I jumped on a train, but found it did not go to N.Y. so jumped off again.  I noticed it was seven o'clock and late and I was still seeking the right train back to N.Y.
(A)  In this we find the emblematical condition of those forces as have been carried through the body conscious mind of the entity as relating to physical conditions as regarding home and the relations of individuals.  Again we find same switched to those relations as respecting that in which the body conscious mind has been thinking, studying, wandering, seeking those relations as respecting prices and conditions on stocks - as are seen in the two specific ones as are found when the body, in its rush, approaches that that would bring it (the body) back to its normal position, or normal standing - as is viewed from the conscious mind.

Then, as the interpretation would be - that while the changes are being carried on, the entity should watch and seek, rather than being in the position of getting on and necessitating getting off by finding that the entity has taken the wrong course as regarding the buying or the selling of these particular stocks, see?  for not the indication of price is seen - rather the indication of those conditions as seen - for in seven days - seven o'clock - we will find the change set in a definite way and manner, and as the entity studies these in the body conscious mind, there will be presented those relations in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] that will guide and direct the entity in choosing and following out that which would be the best, see?

5. (Q)  Morning of Jan. 24.  Saw symbol "V" which on ticker tape stands for New Haven R.R.  Voice:  "Call Schwarz & Kaplan and buy V for them.  Buy it for all customers."
(A)  Direct command.  Then why change from that as has been given?  for, as the voice means to the individual a certain condition, then act in that way and manner!

6. (Q)  N.Y., New Haven and Hartford R.R. is selling at 46. Does this mean that this stock at this price is safe, even if the market does sell off?
(A)  Safe, even if the market does sell off - which WILL do, you see.
