# TEXT OF READING 1322-2 M 10

**Reading [[1322-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/23/1937  Sex: M  Age: 10  ReadingID: 6990

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1937, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Alexander F. Jenkins of Baltimore, Md.]

Time of Reading 
3:05 to 3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mass. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there are definite and decided improvements. These are indicated in the general health and in the quietness that is seen in the activities.

3. The mental reactions necessarily need more of those connections between the impulse and the activity for carrying out same.  These as we find may be aided and accomplished by the continued use, first, of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] Appliances as has been indicated.  This we would keep.

4. We find also, while not so often, there should be periods when there is the repeating of the applications of the Oil Packs for the breaking up of the lesions in the caecum and the areas in the abdomen.

5. The massages we find would be well to be taken, and especially these should be applied in the coccyx, the 4th lumbar, the 9th dorsal, and then in the upper cervical and upper dorsal; but the more of the application in the coccyx, the 4th lumbar, the 9th dorsal, and then in the upper cervical and the upper dorsal areas.

6. These we would keep, and keep the body in the open; taking the care and precautions necessary for its welfare, and we will find improvements continuing, and the greater increase in the mental attitudes and mental development.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Has there been the response as should be from the treatments?
(A)  There has been the response, as indicated.  This is shown in the general health and in the quietness of the body.

Keep those things indicated.

9. (Q)  Should the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] be continued in the same manner?
(A)  As has just been given, this should be continued; keeping the plates or anodes clean, renewing the application - or the solutions that are given as [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces for the body.

10. (Q)  May any advice be given as to special training for him?
(A)  This as we find will come more with the general development for the mental activities in those periods when these would be taken up again - in September or October.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
