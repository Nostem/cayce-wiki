# TEXT OF READING 3037-1 F 35 (Housewife)

**Reading [[3037-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/9/1943  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 10354

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office, of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3037], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [3037].]

Born Novembr 30, 1907, in [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]], New Jersey. 
Time of Reading 
10:35 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '40 - changing in the direction of thought - '36 - some anxieties - '31 - changes - '20 - an experience - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3037].

4. The seal of life for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would be rather of interest, and should be a part of the experience of the entity as an emblem, a reminder to the entity of the truth in that so often heard repeated and yet so few interpret:  "The Lord thy God is one Lord."

5. We find that the seal would be:

6. A book, open.  Upon the page would be, in the upper left-hand corner, the sun - in its color and brilliancy, in a vignette.

7. Below to the right would be the crescent, and just below this a very brilliant five-pointed star.

8. On the opposite page would be [[Crucifixion|the Cross]], this in relief.

9. These fill all knowledge.  [[Crucifixion|The Cross]] the way, [[Star of Bethlehem|the star]] the guiding light, the moon those various changing phases of man's consciousness, and the sun as of righteousness.

10. The book would be white and gold, and about same a scroll.

11. This we would have executed and kept as a reminder of that as may be interpreted from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] we find here of that entity now called [3037].

12. In choosing the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]], these as choose with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this be a helpful experience for the entity; enabling the entity to better fulfil those [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

13. No [[Soul|soul]] enters consciousness by chance; though there may be accidents, to be sure - as there was in creation.

14. The urges that are latent and manifested are oft interpreted as indicated in the seal, through the relationship the entity bears to the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects of material consciousness.  Not because the sun, the moon or the stars were at a certain position at the time of birth.  To be sure, these are significant - but WHO is given authority as concerning the earth, as concerning the universe, or this present solar system?

15. The [[Soul|souls]] that are aware of their being in a consciousness as they pass through the environ, or the dimension of that consciousness.  Just as in the earth it is known as three dimensions, yet man may think in an eight-dimensional consciousness.

16. Hence each of the environs about this present solar system has its part in the abilities or the awareness of each entity, as it enters same, or as it passes through, - in the same manner as each entity passing through a grade in school is subject to the lessons the mental self retains or uses, or applies, in whatever may be the problem in its relationship to other things, other conditions, or to other [[Soul|souls]].

17. Thus, as indicated for this entity, - Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, Saturn and Uranus all become a part of the consciousness or awareness, or the manner in which this entity thinks or reacts.  For, as the earth is a three-dimensional awareness or consciousness, - indicated by body, mind, soul, - so is the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] manifested or expressed in the three-dimensional as Father, Son, [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]]; while it might be manifested or indicated in many more in Jupiter, Venus, Mercury or Uranus.  For, each has its consciousness, just as each entity has its abilities, its activities.

18. As they manifest in the awareness of this entity:

19. One with an exceptional mind - beautiful mind; thinks straight; usually correct as to its judgments, and dislikes very much to be disputed.  Learn to be tolerant, and know there are other minds as well as thine.  These from their awareness or consciousness may be correct.  This ye meet oft in thy children.  For these we find in various consciousnesses to thine own.

20. In Mercury we find the high mental ability.

21. In Venus we find the love of home, beauty, and especially music.  DO give music to one of thy children.  Do instruct and follow thyself with this unfoldment in them, for it will aid in unfolding thy own awareness.

22. We find in Jupiter not only the benevolent but the awareness in the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]].  These also bring the desires and hopes.

23. In Uranus we find the interest in the unusual, the extremes, the awareness in what is ordinarily termed the metaphysical, or that having to do with the universal or unseen forces or activities.

24. Hence abilities are a part of the awareness of this entity. And from these may be indicated that which is the chart of the life, - [[Crucifixion|the Cross]], the way; the Mind then the Builder, as IS the awareness of the spiritual source of light in the sun - both s-u-n and s-o-n.

25. [[Star of Bethlehem|The star]] and the moon bring those helps, those forces, those changes, - as the BOOK is the One; not merely the individual, but that which comprises the book and the material within is of many more than three-dimensional consciousnesses to the entity.

26. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - while not all may be indicated, - we find these are the greater influences in the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]] of the entity.  For this entity, as many, is among those souls that seek manifestations or parts of what may be termed a GROUP.  Thus we find the varied individuals, the varied groups having their effect even upon the abilities of the entity.  For, as indicated from the seal, all are correlated, are dependent, are ONE in the Book. Thus they are as relative one to another.

27. Before this, then, we find that the entity was in the land of the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], during that particular period of settlings around parts of Maryland, when there were definite associations of groups as related to the spiritual growth, or spiritual unfoldment; the seeking for abilities of self-expression.

28. Thus to the entity in the present we find there must be freedom of thought, there must be freedom of expression.  And yet there is still held by the entity that as of there being some one definite thing that is more correct than another. Yet the fact, as the entity sees illustrated, as well as finds demonstrated in its experience, is that all such is RELATIVE.  For the Lord thy God is one, and He is not a respecter of persons, but rather looketh on the heart than on the outward appearance.

29. Thus the hopes, the aspirations of self have as much influence upon what is to be, as does the fact of an act today.  For, a force one spends is a part of the past, or a consciousness that must be used or rejected.

30. For, there is constantly set before thee good and evil, life and death, - choose thou.  For it is the awareness within. And as the lawgiver gave, "Think not who will descend from heaven, or who will come from over the sea, for, Lo, it is within thine own heart.  Thus, as the sources of light indicate, - either in the sun or the moon or the stars, - from whatever source these may rise, - these must come through, and have their own, Cross.  For, the way of the Cross leadeth to the more perfect understanding

31. In the experience the entity gained, for it became an instructor, a director for the young peoples, for groups. Thus in the present, not merely because of its own offspring, but there is a universal awareness of the desire to instruct or direct, not merely in the material things but in the mental aspirations and spiritual truths, to the young.

32. Here is the hope of the world - in the young.  For as you find in self, ye have encased thyself with ideas until it is oft very disturbing to be upset even by other opinions.  This ye have to meet in thyself.  Not becoming so open-minded as to forget the source of light, but others may think - and what a man thinketh in his heart, so is he.

33. The name then was Marianne Henry.

34. Before that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], during those periods when there were the activities following the life of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] in the earth.

35. The entity as a child knew [[John (Apostle)|John the beloved]].  The entity heard [[John (Apostle)|John]]'s exhortation to little children.  The entity heard and knew of the persecutions of those who had been close with [[Jesus Christ|the Master]].

36. The entity made application of those tenets and truths among the peoples especially in those lands about Gennesareth.  In the experience the entity gained.  For, the entity became - though far from the cloisters that are made today - a sister superior in a school in that land, under the aid even from the Romans.

37. Thus we find the entity capable of meeting many hard problems where others may fail.  When it touches the political or social life the entity is capable of meeting those problems and of arousing an interest in those that are in political or economic authority.

38. Use such, do not abuse such, - just as ye did in the experience then, as Jueneta.

39. Before that the entity was in the Midian land, through which the children of promise passed.  The entity harkened to those tenets of those peoples, during the periods of sojourn there. The awareness of the plague brought about in those periods, of the Midianites, and the activities of the king, as well as the [[Prophets|prophet]] turned to blessing rather than cursing.  And the entity became among those peoples - as it entered the Promise Land.

40. The entity, in the experience and in the acceptance of those changes, brought those activities that aided in the spiritual and material instructions to those with whom the entity became associated in that period.

41. The name then was Shelah.

42. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, when there were those activities of preparations for the needs for definite service, through those offices of [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful.

43. There the entity prepared as an emissary, and the entity had in those experiences the associations with the individuals from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, and the offspring that grew there were the companions of the entity.  And that individual who is the companion today was the companion through that experience.

44. Thus you find many experiences that are as of olden days, and look forward to many a new experience, even with the years of working, laboring, planning together.  Almost [[Ideal|an ideal]] association.

45. Be in that position of meeting those problems there, as has been indicated in the necessity for changes in the present activities.

46. The name then was Ist-Elah.

47. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

48. First, find self - as to its own [[Ideal|ideals]], - spiritual, mental, material.

49. Then study to show thyself approved unto that [[Ideal|ideal]], a workman not ashamed, rightly dividing the words of truth; condemning none, keeping self unspotted from the world.

50. In those fields of instruction, as in the home, as in the teaching of small groups, are the greater fortes and the outlets where greater things and experiences may come to the entity.

51. Keep the faith in self as it manifested the faith in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].

52. (Q)  What is my major lesson to be learned in this experience?
(A)  As indicated, it is in meeting those needs of being patient - even with those that worry thee much.

53. (Q)  Am I making proper headway spiritually, or has my material circumstance stilted such growth?
(A)  As indicated, this is almost ideal.  Never be satisfied, but do be content.  Press on to the mark of higher calling, and know ye have a duty, ye have an obligation, - to be ever a shining light to others.  If not the sun, the moon; if not the moon, a tiny star.  But keep a light, a hope.  And they must all be in the Cross of Jesus.

54. (Q)  Are my ideas, which I put into writing from time to time, on occult subjects, essentially correct?
(A)  In general, correct.  But keep the Cross above same, ever.

Let that mind be in thee ever as ye sought the teachings of the lawgiver, as ye found in the way, the truth, the light, as ye heard from [[John (Apostle)|John the beloved]].  And thy [[Visions|vision]] of the light will come stronger and stronger to thee.

55. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self

> WHAT IS TIME?
> [by [3037]]
> 
> Time, contrary to common presumption, does not 'fly' nor
> does it 'pass by quickly or slowly' and the truth is surely
> misconstrued when we say 'Time and tide wait for no man' -
> but rather we might complain that so far Man has not found
> it possible to stay HIS own inevitable movement THROUGH Time.
> 
> Man, physiologically, is in a perpetual growing motion called
> 'being alive' and this physiological movement is measured for
> its spatial existence or age by a man-made plan we call Time.
> This Time plan is merely a theoretical convenience,
> conceived in order to measure the space of Man's movement
> (both physiological and physical) through this material, and
> therefore dimensionally limited, existence.
> 
> Thus Time is an abstract copy of a vast yet motionless
> yardstick, reaching endlessly into spatial existence, as a
> method of measuring our real dimension of physiological
> space - a dimension each existing thing acquires by the very
> act of existing through what we call Time, but which is more
> accurately called Space.
> 
> Consequently, Time and Space are essential partners,
> incorporated by their interdependence.  Hence, in further
> reference to either the proper title to be used is
> Spatial-time.  Though their meanings are paralleled in usage
> they are distinguishable in character.  Space may be likened
> to an endless bolt of tapestry cloth picturing all material
> existence, while Time is merely the dimensional measurement
> of all that cloth.
> 
> Then the Past, Present, and Future are just imaginary points
> on this imaginary yardstick.  To say what point in Time is
> which is a purely relative matter, depending on whether or
> not one is in motion and in what direction.  'Now' is your
> Present - it is your ancestors' Future - and your
> descendants' Past!  Do you realize that it is only the
> directional quality of our Physiological Time-space dimension
> which divorces our Past from our Future?  Then imagine a
> state of consciousness free from our physiological
> properties, i.e. our material bodies, (such as after 'death')
> and you'll find that Past and Future are meaningless terms;
> in fact, such a state can't be said to involve Time at all if
> Time is but a term of measurement for physiological spatial
> existence!
> 
> All of this shows Time to be not only an abstract term,
> useful on our material plane alone, but a confining term as
> well, for does it not prejudice our conception of the
> so-called Past and Future, not allowing them credit for the
> quality of permanence they truly possess?  Isn't it only that
> ghost we call Time which marks off the Present as the only
> space existing.  Without this abstract measurement would not
> all space - Past, Present, and Future, - be one timeless
> space?
> 
> But the directional quality of material life and growth
> calls for such a measure to parallel its progress and to fit
> its limitations.  Thus, we lose sight of the forest being too
> close to the trees - which is to say that we fail to
> recognize the unity of spatial-time in material existence,
> being marked off as we are by Time itself into seemingly
> un-unified spaces called the Present.
> 
> Now supposing the Time plan had not been conceived for our
> convenience, and the terms past, present, and future, and
> seconds, minutes, and hours were completely erased from our
> knowledge - then the spatial existence of anything would be
> one whole unbroken unit or dimension, measured by nothing but
> creation and termination.  But because Time is so
> conveniently applicable to our physiological progress, this
> dimension is named the Time dimension to distinguish it from
> other spatial dimensions.
> 
> Were it possible for one of us to 'arrest' the motion of
> our own physiological train, shooting through Spatial-time,
> long enough to watch from any 'station' en route the flight
> of any other physiological train as it completely appears
> and disappears, (that is, the physiological existence of
> anything as one whole unit) the result of this unique
> observation would be 'seeing' the Time dimension of that
> existing thing!
> 
> For just as your physical self would have to get off from a
> moving train to actually see that train's spatial dimensions
> in relation to other existence, so we would have to step out
> of our own physiological motion in order to glimpse that
> dimension of Time in other physiological existence.  If a
> physiologically dead person could just 'look' at an existing
> object, that 'look' would capture the fourth dimension of the
> object viewed; for the Time dimension can spread into view
> only in contrast to the absolute halt or stoppage of Time for
> the observer.
> 
> Perhaps at 'death' when one's physiological growth is
> slackening into stoppage the dimension of spatial-time might
> possibly be glimpsed.  It is said that a person dying 'sees'
> his whole life roll before his mind's eye.  It is natural to
> believe it is the separation from the train of his
> physiological growth which places him in the role of the
> observer of that train fleetingly.
> 
> What we think of as the Future is ever existent in
> Spatial-time, we merely have not reached it as yet materially
> because the space of physiological growth intervenes.  It is
> similar to saying that though an actual train we might be
> traveling to [[Texas|Texas]] on hasn't reached there yet, [[Texas|Texas]] is none
> the less existent.  The only difference separating any points
> from the point of arrival is Space, (Time being merely the
> theoretical measurement of such space).  So we may say that
> the Past and Future are ever existent, as Spatial-time we are
> growing away from or growing towards, but the perpetual
> motion of our physiological trains keep them ever divorced
> from our Present.
> 
> Spiritually, however, there is no such obstacle as
> physiological directional growth separating one point from
> another, and thus no restricting Time element!
> 
> Here is material life, some minds are capable of overcoming
> the normal restrictions of Spatial-time during infrequent
> spells of apparent physiological hesitation - as in true
> [[Visions|visions]] and precognitive [[Dreams|dreams]] - and leaping into our future
> spatial-time.  This also argues for the existence of our
> Future before we arrive there, but though we may say it is
> ever existent it would be false to say ever present, for the
> physiological motion of all living things keeps a space of
> time (or a time of space) between the present moment and
> existent future moments.
> 
> Now if you're thinking 'where' are these Future moments,
> well - 'where' is this Present moment which you accept as
> existent?  If any moment, either past, present or future,
> is in reality just measured spatial existence (the word
> 'moment' being a child of the Time illusion remember) then
> all Time is all Space, and Future moments are merely the
> spatial existence we have not as yet grown into
> physiologically!...(If the word Physiological appears to be
> over-used here, let it be understood that the word applies
> better than any other single term to the very essence of what
> we mean by the fourth dimension, or Spatial-time.)
> 
> But to say that future spatial-time is ever existent is not
> a point for fatalists; it merely means to say the 'cloth'
> wherewith to clothe our [[Destiny|destinies]] is flung out generously
> before us, but the harmony of line and color is in the
> precious use of our own individual [[Free Will|free will]].
> 
> Conceiving of a state of spiritual consciousness - when
> once our dear, dull bodies are laid to rest and directional
> growth is physiologically halted - it would appear from all
> of this that such a state will afford relation to our
> so-called Past (in its spiritual state, naturally not its
> material state!) by virtue of the absence of the Time
> illusion.
> 
> But if you contend that perhaps Space, if not Time, will
> restrict the spiritual consciousness to a certain point in
> Space, consider this:  - Time was conceived by Man to
> measure existence in Space, but Space is only conceived by
> us in relation and contrast to the existence of matter.  Take
> away all matter - return it to its spiritual state from
> whence it came - and the term Space becomes as illusory as
> Time.  Therefore, Spatial-time is only a material dimension,
> and is an illusion and limitation of the world of matter.
>
