# TEXT OF READING 337-6 F 42

**Reading [[337-6]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/7/1929  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 2560

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by self and Dr. Berger [[[Grace|Grace]] C. Berger, D.O.].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mesdames [337], Robinson, Drs. Berger, Hansen, Miller, Messrs. Hargis, Miller, and [5417] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [337].  This we have had before.  Now, we find there is a better reaction through this system than we have had before.  A better coordination between the upper and lower circulation; that is, as has been given, where the circulation required - internally, in the vegetative system and through the mesenteric system - the capillary circulation carrying so many of the eliminations as should be carried from the body, producing in the external or capillary circulation disorders and disturbances.  The reaction as has been shown through the liver has been clearing up the blood supply, until even the body is able to get a deeper BREATH without hurt, or without feeling a flush. This, then, is creating the plain - or the position of a greater carbon and oxygen reaction for the system; or the body has just BEGUN to be relieved, where there is really the basis for the replenishing and building up of the system. Keep the work up, then!  At least for three to four more WEEKS, and we would find much bettered conditions, and the body near to normal.  DO that.

2. (Q)  Why do bloody stools continue?
(A)  Must be - When there is the eliminating of those distresses through the vegetative system, where the walls of intestines have been absorbing those forces through the feces, as carry these conditions - better that they be eliminated than remaining in the system, producing infection throughout!  Well were this to be washed, or cleansed through that of the enemas, as has been suggested.  Do that.

3. (Q)  Is present diet the proper one?
(A)  This very good.  May gradually be changed to those of the more blood building, and those that are EASY still of digestion, but more of the cooked foods, and those that will replenish the system.  Give those more, then, that carry silicon, calcium, and such.  Don't eat too many!

but sufficient that the body may begin to change in the assimilated forces, to carry the various conditions in the body.  Iron should also be included in the elements of food.

4. (Q)  Can [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given daily?
(A)  If not too DEEP.  The GENTLE treatment, with one to two thorough or deep treatments each week.  This would ASSIST, if these are not given too deep, for it will assist the capillary and lymphatic circulation.  The lymphatic centers should be followed in the daily treatments, or nearer those of the neuropathic applications would be well, see?

5. (Q)  Should powders be continued?
(A)  Continued for the time being.  Should be discontinued gradually, in three to four days.

6. (Q)  Is it advisable to use an ointment or abrasions?
(A)  Not as yet.  If we would use any, we would use those of the carbon powders.

7. (Q)  Should gall bladder be emptied again, osteopathically?
(A)  In about another week.

8. (Q)  What causes druggy taste?
(A)  If as much dross was in the system as is being exhumed in the lower portion of the duodenum and the intestines, as is seen, its bound to come back!  This is regurgitation, but the activities of the manipulation to the exterior portion, and the deeper manipulation in the upper portion of the body, the reaction of the system through the emptying of the colon thoroughly, these will be diminished.

9. (Q)  Should raw vegetables still be given at noon?
(A)  At times, yes.

10. (Q)  Should continue with [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray?
(A)  SHOULD.

11. That is all the questions regarding this body.

12. Be well, too, that the sand packs, when possible, still be given at times for this body.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
