# TEXT OF READING 4353-2 F 20

**Reading [[4353-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/20/1924  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 12181

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of October, 1924, in accordance with telegraphic request made by self - [4353].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                         ... Riverside Drive, 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will give the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief for this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we find the impressions of the body here, not the body itself.

3. Now we have had these conditions in this [[Physical Body|physical body]] before.  We find the body has been able, through the retraction in physical conditions, to overcome much of these conditions that were distressing to the body.  We find in those conditions where the body receives distress at times these conditions could and would be removed were those [[Vibration|vibrations]] accorded the body as has been outlined for this body.  Not necessary for an operation for these conditions, were the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] made to vibrate through the conditions as accorded for this body.  We would give to do that rather than through operative forces.  Be consistent, be persistent in the following out of these conditions for the body, in that manner as has been outlined.

4. We are through for the present.
