# TEXT OF READING 2025-3 F 53

**Reading [[2025-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/4/1940  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 8566

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of March, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2025], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have had the body here before.

3. As we find, there are those anxieties owing to rather the localization of those conditions which have existed as a covering of the abdominal areas, in the organs or the pelvic cavities themselves.

4. This as we find is NOT of a fibroid nature, nor is it in that form which might be called a sarcoma nature - or of the consumptive nature.  Rather it is of the fluor nature, as a swelling of the areas about the organs of pelvis.

5. Every other day, until there is little or no discharge, we would use an [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche; a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to a quart of water, body-temperature, used ONLY in a Fountain Syringe Douche.

6. Then the rest of the suggestions as we have indicated we would keep, - as to the diet, as to the use of the artichoke, etc.  Do not use too much of the artichoke, but keep this as an active force in the diet, not only for the pancreas and liver and kidneys but as a general stimulation for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]].

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is this conditions malignant in character?
(A)  As we have indicated, it is rather of the fluor nature. If operative measures were attempted, it might become so - because of the conditions which exist in the blood stream.

But if there will be more of the beef juices and the liver extract, or broiled liver itself and not too much cooked, these will aid in BUILDING the body for better strength, better resistance, and less of this disturbance.  For the body will adjust itself, if the douches are used as indicated to keep down inflammation and to act as an antiseptic from the harboring of the disturbances which arise naturally from drosses of the nature in the body.

10. (Q)  What can I do to get rid of gas in my stomach, which often keeps me from eating, and occasionally causes vomiting?
(A)  As indicated, the artichoke will aid in this especially. Use enemas rather than other things, though at times it would be very well to take any effervescent salts that is not too much of a laxative.  Upjohn's Citrocarbonate would be well.  Also the use of Charcoal Tablets as an absorbent through the system would be very well with these conditions, but vary these according to the severeness of the conditions, see?

11. (Q)  As I cannot eat enough to keep my strength, is there anything I can do to increase my weight?
(A)  Keep those things that have been indicated, and these will GIVE the strength, - the liver and [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], not as a drink but as a medicine.  And these - the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] and the liver extract - would be sipped, and not taken as a drink, but sipped very slowly.  The broiled liver, of course, would be masticated very thoroughly.

Do that.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
