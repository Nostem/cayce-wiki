# TEXT OF READING 2931-1 M 64

**Reading [[2931-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/5/1943  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 10191

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2931], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. [2772].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - unusual environment - some preparations.

3. Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, [2931].

4. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that are preventing the better physical functioning of the body.

5. We find that the disturbances are as if short circuits were produced in the centers from which coordination is established between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerve centers.  These are effects, but that is the nature of the disturbance; so that the effects are seen in the activity of the body, as in use of hands - more than lower limbs at present, though the condition may become more and more spreading (unless corrections are made) until there is what might be called the agitans, or the inability of the body-mind to control impulses of the nerve forces through the body.

6. Thus we have an easily tiring of the body, the inability to coordinate the activities.  Easily at times are things forgotten; at others the memory apparently is more acute than ordinarily.

7. This as we find arises from the effects of conditions applied in the body in many, many times or days or months back; which, in the assimilation of that taken in the body, reduces the ability of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to reproduce stamina as an active force in the ganglia along the cerebrospinal system, where the coordinating reflexes come between the superficial and deeper circulation.

8. In the main portions of the functioning body organs these are apparently not materially disturbed, though - to be sure - the activities of the kidneys, of the eliminations in alimentary canal, are not so easily controlled by the voluntary impulses.

9. Then, in making application for beneficial conditions for this body, - we would administer those forces that will assist the system to establish better coordination:

10. First we would begin with having three times a week a gentle massage following a gentle cabinet sweat; preferably let the sweat be produced principally by a [[Hydrotherapy|fume bath]], rather than the dry heat.

11. While the massage is being given, apply the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to the body, in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of Distilled Water.

12. The smaller plate would be attached one time to the lumbar axis, for twenty minutes, while the large nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center - or, on this body, four fingers from the umbilical or navel center to the right, and one finger up from where that forefinger would come.

13. Then remove both plates and cleanse, by using emery paper. Then attach again, - putting the small copper plate on the 2nd dorsal center, and the larger nickel plate - with the Solution - at the same area as before; that is, the lacteal duct.  This time apply for twenty minutes.

14. This procedure would be followed each time that the massage is given, you see, which would cover a period of forty minutes in all of having the Appliance attached - three times each week; and the massage would be given during that time and some massage afterwards, of course.  But take time in giving the massage.  These will bring stabilizing of coordination in nerve impulses to the activities of the body.

15. The Gold Solution should be changed each fifteen days.  The Appliance should be recharged every thirty days, using:

Copper Sulphate...................1 pound,
Sulphuric Acid C.P............1 1/4 ounces,
Zinc..............................3 drams.

Do not use the Charcoal in this solution.  Stir the solution well and mix for at least twelve hours before it is to be used on the body.

16. Disconnect the connections from the solution when not in use.

17. Do these.

18. And then be mindful in the diet.  Take those foods carrying plenty of [[Iodine|iodine]]; fish and sea foods several times each week.

19. Plenty of whole grain cereals.

20. Also take daily some raw vegetables, or their combinations.

21. These, to be sure, are not to be all the foods, but include these in the diet.

22. Refrain from any stimulating or over-alcohol producing influences in the body.  No brewed or alcoholic drinks of any kind are to be taken during these treatments.

23. We should find considerable improvement by the second charge of the Appliance solution.

24. Do these for this body.

25. Ready for questions.

26. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., NYC) Copy to Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  Dr. Reilly may have one of these already in his office that could be used in your case; at least he has used them and is familiar with their use.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 2/19/43 [2931]'s letter to EC:
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> "[[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]]" has been carefully read by me.  I have
> had happy days in Bowling Green, Ky., and Selma, Ala., as
> well as many other places mentioned in the book, which gives
> me a bond of friendship, although we have never met.
> 
> By a most strange series of events I have been led to your
> door at a time of deepest discouragement.  For three years
> past I have been affected with trembling of the left hand
> and arm and, to a lesser degree, in the right.  I have tried
> endless treatments by medical men, to no avail.  With all my
> heart I sincerely believe you can help me through your divine
> gift, and earnestly ask you for a physical reading.
> 
> Dr. [2772] told me how much you had helped him and his child
> [2779], and suggested that I ask you any questions that
> seemed pertinent.  I particularly wish to know (1) What is
> the basic cause of my trembling, and (2) How it can best be
> treated; (3) Why is it possible for one person by the touch
> of hands to cause the trembling to cease completely for a
> period of considerable length or, until I begin thinking...
> also (4) Why does the trembling cease when I sleep?
> 
> I feel grateful for the opportunity of subscribing to such
> a wonderful Cause, and enclose Application and check.
> 
> May the world with men like you go 'ever and forever onward'.
> 
> Faithfully, [2931]
> 
> N.B.  I would deeply appreciate having a reading as soon as
> possible, but on any day or at any hour you designate.  I
> will be at my residence.
