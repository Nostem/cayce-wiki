# TEXT OF READING 262-34

**Reading [[262-34]]** · Series: 262 · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/18/1932  Sex: M  ReadingID: 978

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 18th day of December, 1932, in accordance with request made by those present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Minnie and C. A. Barrett, Florence and Edith Edmonds, Esther Wynne, F. Y. Morrow, Mildred Davis, Annie Cayce, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Sarah and Lu Hesson.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:30 P. M.

1. GC:  You will have before you the individuals gathered here and their work on the lesson In His Presence, a copy of which I hold in my hand.  You will go over this and as I call each paragraph make any suggestions as to change, or addition of material.  You will answer the questions which will be asked:

2. EC:  We have the group, as a group, as individuals, gathered here, and their work on the lesson In His Presence.

3. Ready for questions.  (Answer "Very good" to each paragraph.)

4. (Q)  Is the interpretation of HE and Him correct, as given on the paper which I hold in my hand?
(A)  Very good.

5. (Q)  Compilers:  Please give a more complete address for the Argosy Magazine, as the paper [3976-9] sent to the address given was returned.
(A)  Argosy Publishing House, Argosy Bldg., Pennsylvania Ave., Washington, D.C.

6. (Q)  Please give street number and complete address of the Blue Goose Magazine publishing house.
(A)  As given, 57th Street, [[New York City|New York City]].

7. (Q)  [993]:  Please interpret the message I received on Dec. 13th about 6:30 A.M., when a voice said, "It is time for you to take the Holy Grail."
(A)  With the study that has been going on, in the activities of the body, the reading of the quest of the Holy Grail at the present time would bring to the body the proper interpretation of how and what the [[Visions|vision]] should mean to the body in the present; for it will be seen in the story of the quest how that it becomes necessary for the individual to partake of that which was meant by Him who gave, "Ye must eat of my body, drink of my blood."  [GD's note:  It seems the original book on the quest for the Holy Grail was written by Thomas Malory, entitled LE MORTE D'ARTHUR.]  [Many others have been written, including a Penguin Classic Book in 1969, THE QUEST OF THE HOLY GRAIL, translated by Pauline M. Matarasso.]

8. (Q)  For [1929], [GD's note:  Mrs. [1929] was the first member of Group #2 who met weekly with Esther Wynne as the teacher.] who is contemplating Study Group Work:  Please interpret her [[Dreams|dream]] of November 19, 1932, a copy of which I hold in my hand.
(A)  As we have given for [1929], there are those interpretations that come to self in the DEVELOPMENT of self, through the experiences and activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the material application of the spiritual elements of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]. So, in the [[Visions|vision]], as the application of self is to those things seen, in their essence or elements represent the developments of varied experience and activities THROUGH others; and these may be brought to a better interpretation by study along the lines as visioned.

9. (Q)  What should be the basis and fundamental thoughts to be presented in the lesson on THE CROSS AND THE CROWN?
(A)  As there has been presented in the lessons that which may be applicable in the thought and study through ANY activity of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]], so in the lesson THE CROSS, THE CROWN, there is made the definite stand of the activities OF an entity, that must come to stand as that which is first and foremost in the minds mentally, the minds spiritually, of each entity.  As given, "I am determined to know nothing among men save [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], and him [[Crucifixion of Jesus|crucified]]."  So, He, with the Cross, represents something in the experience of every entity in their activities through the earth, and has led in all of the experiences of thought in ANY of the presented forms of truth in the earth, and comes at last to the Cross.  So, this should be the central thought, the reason of the Cross, the Crown; for, as ye may be known by the Name that He has given, so must the central theme, the basis of each individual's approach be:  Not "what is my cross?" which is the usual first question in every MATERIAL mind, but rather "How may I with His aid best MEET my cross, in my approach to the Crown of Righteousness?"  and NOT as of a temporal or even a mental kingdom, but the Cross of Glory, the Crown of Glory, in, through and by His Name.

10. (Q)  Please give affirmation for this lesson.
(A)  [[Lord's Prayer|OUR FATHER]], OUR GOD, AS WE APPROACH THAT THAT MAY GIVE US A BETTER INSIGHT OF WHAT HE BORE IN THE CROSS, WHAT HIS GLORY MAY BE IN THE CROWN, MAY THY BLESSINGS - AS PROMISED THROUGH HIM - BE WITH US AS WE STUDY TOGETHER IN HIS NAME.

11. We are through for the present.

> INTRODUCTION

> Our thoughts, our words, our activities, our general outlook
> on life, are motivated by our concept of Him whom we worship.

> of His Presence abiding with us.
> 
> When we keep His Presence as a thing apart, something to be
> experienced, something to be aware of when we are disturbed
> in some manner, we lose sight of the fact that to abide in
> His Presence is the experience, the knowledge and
> understanding of all who seek to do His biddings.  His
> Presence abides with us always; for it is in Him that we live
> and move and have our being.  We must realize this, come to
> know and understand this, know ourselves to be sons of the
> Most High.
> 
> "God is Spirit," standing back of everything in creation.
> "God is One."  Everything is God; hence, God is everything.
> We cannot separate Him and His creation.  We may try to do
> so, but then we become dual, and it is then we begin to
> misunderstand.  When we separate ourselves, or think
> ourselves apart from our Maker, God, we become as "a ship
> without a rudder."

> When we have the realization of His Presence, of our [[Oneness|Oneness]],
> it is then that life takes on a new aspect.  The [[Oneness|oneness]] is
> ever existent, but it is our realization, our acknowledgment
> of its existence that works the change in us.  We are
> freewill agents in the matter; therefore, we see that whereas
> God is not a person in the sense that we think of persons, He
> is at the same time very personal.
> 
> It was this [[Oneness|Oneness]] with the Father that [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] stressed
> while on the earth plane in physical form.  "I of myself can
> do nothing; it is the Father within that doeth the works."

> is there.  All the guidance, help, supply, joy, peace, and
> whatever goes to make life worth living, is within, if we
> will just realize, acknowledge, and use our great storehouse

> "still, small voice" that we know the Presence is with us.
> Truly the [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] is "God in action."

> "Know, O ye children of men, the Lord thy God is One.  Each
> spirit, each manifestation, either in this, that, or the
> other sphere, scope, or space of development moves toward

> One - Him - God - Jehovah - Yah - All One."

> Then we should know that we are one with Him.

> "I marvel at the fact that I have no worry, and yet I have

> 
> "What does it mean to be in His Presence?  If I am a child

> "It is only when we forget God that troubles overtake us.

> PREPARATION OF SELF TO ABIDE IN HIS PRESENCE
> 
> Realizing that we are ever in His Presence, the preparation
> becomes rather the way of keeping self in the realization of
> being in His Presence.  As each individual is at a different

> breaking up of the carnal forces within self, the spirit is
> made glad, even as He in the hour of [[Trial of Jesus|trial]] smiled upon him by
> whom he was being denied."
> 
> We are prepared by acknowledging His Presence with us now,
> today.  The Lord is in His holy temple; let all the earth
> keep silent before Him.  We are prepared through living so
> that others may KNOW what we profess with our lips to believe
> is in keeping with the Presence held as [[Ideal|the Ideal]].  We are
> prepared through being living examples of that we profess;
> for, whether we will or not, we are the expressions of the
> God we worship.  That is the reason many often give others
> the wrong idea of Him, as their actions are not in accord
> with His attributes.
> 
> "We are prepared through keeping in the way of understanding,
> so there will be more and more that glorious consciousness of
> His Presence abiding with us; for, as we conquer in self that
> which makes for hindrances, our souls pass from glory to
> glory until the glorious knowledge of His Presence is ever
> before us."
> 
> Lastly, we are prepared through keeping His commands.  "If ye
> love me keep my commandments."  What are His commandments?
> "Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these,
> my little ones, ye have done it unto me."
> 
> LET US REMEMBER THAT OUR GUARD IS EVER IN HIS PRESENCE
> 
> Let us not be afraid in the Presence of our Maker.  He is
> ever willing to fulfill His promises to His children, ever
> ready to make known His ways unto those who will seek to know
> His face.  The God who wrought the beauties of nature and set
> the laws which govern the harmonious symphony of the universe
> could not have left a part of His creation without guidance,
> without a sustaining force.  The way is exemplified in the
> Son; and there are those Universal Forces ever-ready to aid,
> strengthen those who seek to travel this way.
> 
> "BOW THINE HEADS, O YE MEN THAT WOULD SEEK HIS PRESENCE.  BE
> STRONG IN HIS MIGHT.  FALTER NOT AT THINE OWN WEAK SELF.
> KNOW THAT THY REDEEMER LIVETH AND MAY THIS DAY MAKE KNOWN IN
> THINE OWN HEART HIS PRESENCE ABIDING WITH THEE.  ROOT FROM
> THINE BODY, THINE CONSCIOUSNESS, AUGHT THAT WOULD HINDER HIS
> ENTERING IN:  FOR HE WOULD SUP WITH THEE.  WILT THOU, THEN, O
> MAN, MAKE KNOWN THINE OWN DECISIONS?  WILL YE BE ONE WITH
> HIM?  THE WAY WHICH I GUARD LEADS TO THAT GLORY IN THE MIGHT
> OF THE LORD.  I, [[Michael (Archangel)|MICHAEL]], WOULD GUIDE THEE.  DO NOT DISOBEY.
> DO NOT FALTER.  THOU KNOWEST THE WAY."  [262-33]
> 
> "Glory in the Lord, not in self; not in the wisdom of the
> earth, knowing that those who partake alone of the mental may
> easily become stumbling stones in the way of many.  Let the
> [[Holy Spirit|Spirit of Truth]] separate the chaff from the wheat and enter
> into the full knowledge of His Presence, shutting out all
> those things that would hinder or cause doubt or in any way
> make afraid."  [262-32, Par. 19-A]
> 
> COME!  LET MY HEART BE LIFTED IN PRAISE AND ADORATION OF THE
> WONDROUS LOVE THAT THE FATHER SHED UPON THE CHILDREN OF MEN.
> 
> COME!  LET ALL BE GLAD IN THE OPPORTUNITIES THAT ARE GIVEN
> TO SERVE IN HIS NAME DAY BY DAY.
> 
> COME!  LET US BE JOYOUS IN THE TRUTH THAT, "INASMUCH AS YE
> DID IT UNTO THE LEAST OF THESE, MY LITTLE ONES, YE DID IT
> UNTO ME."  LET THE LOVE OF THE SON BE MAGNIFIED IN OUR LIVES,
> THAT OTHERS MAY KNOW THE JOYOUSNESS OF SERVICE BRINGS PEACE
> AND HARMONY TO OUR HEARTS AS WE SERVE.
> 
> COME!  GIVE THANKS UNTO HIM, FOR WE WOULD MAKE OUR OWN LIVES,
> OUR OWN BODIES, A DWELLING PLACE OF THE LOVE THAT THE FATHER
> WOULD MANIFEST UNTO HIS CHILDREN.
> 
> COME!  GIVE PLACE TO HIS HOLY NAME, THAT THERE MAY COME
> JOYOUSNESS IN THE HEARTS OF MEN AT THE COMING OF THE CHRIST
> INTO THE LIVES AND THE EXPERIENCES OF MANY.  [281-14]
