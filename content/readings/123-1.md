# TEXT OF READING 123-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[123-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/20/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 284

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1926, in accordance with request made by his son-in-law, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions with this body.  Some of these may be assisted, some corrected, in the physical functioning of the body.

3. The conditions first to be met are those of that invasion in the system of those conditions as are produced by that that destroys the tissue in portions of the body - face, and the tissue about the various portions of the system.

4. Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body.

5. First, we find the vitality of the system low.  Hence the blood supply is super or overcharged with those conditions that prevent the system from rebuilding or of recuperation, for coagulation is hard to take place in used tissue, or in those of broken tissue.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this is kept so subjugated that the reaction to the system is rather of the sympathetic nature, than of the normal reaction through cerebro-spinal system.

7. THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, all show the strain under which the system labors, and these vary in the various cycles of their circulation.

8. First, there would be necessary that those rays be applied to the system to assist the blood stream in being eliminated of that bacilli that destroys tissue in the building, especially in the hemoglobin portion of the blood.  Such will be found in the Red Ray for the body, or that as gives off that [[Vibration|vibration]] in its action to the nerve system to so strain or stimulate the blood supply as to give the better efforts to the body to rebuild and to meet destructive forces.

9. There should also be worn that of the high voltage or high [[Vibration|vibration]] of the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator, which will give rejuvenation to the system in the stimulus as is supplied through the metabolism of the system - that is, this will assist in aiding the weakened tissue in functioning without destroying or breaking the incentive of each functioning organ to give the best to the system.

10. Then, apply [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] first.  Do not keep the pad on when the system is being treated with the electric ray, see?  But keep on at all times when this is not being applied.  This will be found, too, good to be applied across the face, the eyes, the upper portion of the body, for at least three to five (3 to 5) minutes each day, see?

11. Do that.  Then, with the assistance this will bring to the body, we would give those conditions to be met as they arise.

12. We are through for the present.
