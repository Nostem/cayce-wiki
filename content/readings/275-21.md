# TEXT OF READING 275-21 F 18

**Reading [[275-21]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/18/1931  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 1725

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1931, in accordance with request made by self Miss [275].

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find there are many improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

2. As for the condition in the hip and the cartilaginous forces as are being gradually BUILDED in the manner as to aid in preventing farther inroads or erosion, or erasion, by those conditions as have existed there, these are gradually growing to where they are producing a very good pad, or a condition that - without INJURY, or infection of some nature - must prove most helpful and most beneficial to the activities of the body in the use of the limbs.  While, to be sure, this cartilaginous force will NOT be the same as the bone or the structural portions of body, but by the proper precautions and the proper use of same, with the continual building up, by the time there is the full development of the normal activities or the organs of the body, these should make for a most SATISFACTORY activity for the body - see?  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Are [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments being given correctly?
(A)  These are very good.

4. (Q)  How soon will walking become easier for me?
(A)  As soon as the cartilaginous forces are sufficiently padded in their position and place that these may act as a brace in the walking, see?  Now this may be accomplished.  As to just when it would be accomplished depends entirely upon how much of the system may be turned to that activity to build this in the system; that is, this cartilaginous force. We will find that those rubs, as well as those applications as will prevent those activities that may produce infectious forces, as well as those that release the carbon in system [Carbon Ash], or oxygen in system through the activity of the carbon and electrical forces - are, and will be, the beneficial effects for the system.

5. (Q)  Are light wines good or bad for me?
(A)  In moderation they are WELL for the body.  Just so the activities are not such as to become detrimental to digestion, but LIGHT wines with the digestive forces are beneficial.  An over amount of same, or activity produced by taking with improper foods - with foods that they do not COORDINATE with - is harmful for the system.  Whether the bodies would have it or not, FOODS produce that WITHIN the system that is that same fermentation that is called wines, or beer, or liquor, or ales, or such, FOR the digestion to be proper in a body!  That man over balances same, by engorging, or taking too much into the system and making a dog, or a hog of himself, is to be pitied for the man!

6. (Q)  What is cause of my hair falling out, and what can be done to correct it?
(A)  This, as we find, has been produced by some activities that have been active in the force of the body as respecting the capillary circulation.  This may be aided by stimulating same with a low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] as from the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], as used in the points through the hair, and by the use of white vaseline in same - massaged into the scalp.

7. (Q)  What is raw cereal?
(A)  Rolled wheat, rye or oats, are usually termed the RAW cereal.

8. That is all the questions on this body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
