# TEXT OF READING 774-6 F 78

**Reading [[774-6]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/23/1937  Sex: F  Age: 78  ReadingID: 4763

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of January, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [774], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [774].

2. In the present we find those effects of accumulations of poisons through the lack of proper eliminations, producing pressures that cause neuritic forces - or neuritis - in the arms, the limbs; and bringing a great deal of distress to the body.

3. As we would find, to set up eliminations will bring the quicker relief, and then with the general care of the condition we may be rid of these disturbances.

4. First we would take enemas.  Take them at least two to three days straight running.  Do not attempt to create such an activity as to strain or overtax the body, but have the water used at the temperature of the body.  Use preferably a Fountain Syringe for same.  It may be necessary to use the colon tube but DO NOT make this so as to become irritating. To each quart of water used (at the temperature of the body) dissolve very thoroughly into same a heaping teaspoonful of common table salt and a half a teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]]. And in the last quart of water used (that is, the quart of fresh water - not that with the salt and [[Baking Soda|soda]] in same) put a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

5. We would also begin to take a full teaspoonful of Eno Salts every morning before any meal is taken.  Take this for four to five days, rest four to five days and then repeat - unless the whole of the condition has already been relieved.

6. In the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations or the enemas, take one each day for three days, leave off for three to four days - dependent upon the eliminations.

7. As to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance, keep the plates clean and keep these; they will help and be much more beneficial after there is a general cleansing of the system.

8. Keep the diet rather in more of the alkalin-reacting foods. For the natural resistances in age and the inability for a great deal of activity makes for the tendency for more and more acids to be thrown into the system.

9. Do this; we will bring the better conditions for this body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What should be done for gas condition?
(A)  The same.  This is the effect of those conditions of poor eliminations.  Hence the first is given, the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.

12. (Q)  Is there any liver trouble, creating bile?
(A)  The natural inclinations for slow activity make for heaviness in those disturbances.

13. (Q)  When in pain or sleepless I have taken a capsule occasionally?  Should I continue this, or is it harmful?
(A)  When the pain is so sever these are very well, but know that they produce such a tie-up in the eliminations it makes it more necessary for the taking of that to cause the activity through the alimentary canal.

But as we find if we will do those things suggested, we will find relief speedily.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
