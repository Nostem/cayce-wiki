# TEXT OF READING 1635-3 F 5 (Protestant Background)

**Reading [[1635-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/2/1943  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 7720

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this - 22nd day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2111], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., through the aunt, Miss [288].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2111] and [1635], and baby son [2892].]

Born June 22, 1938, 5:00 A. M., Vaughan Memorial Hospital, 
[[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
Time of Reading 
10:35 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane; giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [1635].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these we give with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this may be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling those upon whom the entity depends - or those to whom there has been entrusted the developing of the entity's abilities (for they are many) - to direct the entity in those channels that it may fill that [[Purpose|purpose]] for which it entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

4. As for a seal, this would be very good - yet will require some patience in the execution of same.  Yet this should be a constant reminder to the entity:

5. There would be a shield, made to stand out from the background of a figured design.  The shield would be about six by six and a half (6x6-1/2), upon a background at least ten by twelve (10x12).  The background would be covered with the figure of the fleur de lis, about half an inch apart; this giving the background, then of one of the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], as we shall see, when there was the greater activity of the entity in a given direction.

6. Upon this shield, then, in the center of this seal, we would put a car - an auto - with the appearance of being out of control.  This would be as a warning, - not as a car being wrecked, but out of control - indicated by the expression of those handling same.

7. Upon the upper lefthand corner put a design as of a bunch of flowers - roses.

8. Upon the righthand corner of the shield put a scroll; not too large, and this covered with some design, - as of violets, pinks, or the like.

9. In the bottom of the shield, toward the base, put an open book; this indicating the life's experience - if sought - in an avocation, or as in making the life for a definite activity.

10. That on the center of the shield would be as the warning respecting wrecks, possibly through car.

11. The styles, or the figures, would indicate also the character of the work, - a designer as of wallpaper, or as an interior decorator.

12. The book indicates the abilities AND the high mental forces that are a part of the entity's experience.

13. In interpreting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]], then, - these we give with the idea of minimizing faults, magnifying virtues.  For we have rather an unusual entity; one that may go far, if the [[Free Will|choice]] is in that direction of making a career, - though, as in warning, there must necessarily be warnings respecting the associations with the opposite sex in the sixteenth to eighteenth year, especially; else there may be regrets on the part of the entity.

14. The urges are indicated in the artistic temperament, as indicated in the seal, towards those directions not exactly of commercial drawing but of design, - as wallpaper, or interior decorating; as we shall see by the activities of the entity in dealing with those things having to do with cloth, or cloth covering.  For this may be used also in the interior preparations, or decorations.  For, if given the opportunity, these are those channels or courses or sources for the greater outlet of the abilities that are latent and manifested in the experience.

15. Astrologically we find, while very determined in self and one with high mental abilities, the entity is one that can be easily reasoned with.  And the entity can be directed or controlled by kindness where it will resent that of scolding or of force, or undue pressure being put upon the activities of the entity in making requirements for this or that activity.  These may make for resentments that might cause even changes through those periods of its associations with the opposite sex.  For, being fair of body, of mind, and of activity, the entity will be sought by others.

16. Also we find indicated in Venus the material or mental faculties that will be as weaknesses or inclinations toward attraction to those of the opposite sex, as well as being attractive TO the opposite sex; not only in its ability as a companion but as the natural tend or trend.

17. While outdoor sports may be trained as a part of the entity's experience, we do not find a natural tendency in this direction.  It would be well for the health that there be the directing of same while still very young, else such will be far from the inclinations of the entity.  Hence such activities as tennis, badminton, swimming, rowing, ball games of any kind - basket ball, handball, - all of these should be the insistency, though not forced upon the entity, for the health's sake more than for any other reason. These, of course, if followed out, will bring attractions a little later.

18. But do, from the beginning, stress the abilities as the designer, as the decorator, as one making for the fitting of patterns for rooms and the like.  For these are in keeping with the [[Astrology|astrological]] urges not only from Mercury, Venus and Jupiter as the benevolent force, but also from Mars - which come as afflictions.

19. Hence where there is anger, the participation in spats or argumentations, - these become very disturbing, and will more and more become disturbing to the entity, for it likes to argue too well itself - at times.

20. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - these as we find are not at variance to the urges indicated from the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects.  For:

21. Before this the entity was in the household of the Audubons. There the entity was the daughter of its present mother, and not too long-lived [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].  For, the entity then was destroyed - or met sudden death - in an accident, in a car, or a horse and car.

22. These are latent conditions in the present experience for the entity, then; and warnings must be entertained and kept, not as to keeping the entity from such, but KNOW with whom any traveling is done - else there may be accidents in car or in travel that may make of the body a partial invalid.  These are a part of the [[Karma|karma]], unless there is kept that law of [[Grace|grace]] through which [[Karma|karma]] is NOT an actual experience.

23. Hence there shall be great stress in the unfoldment or development of the entity, upon its trust in DIVINE sources, of its care, of its intent, of its TRUST in those with whom it becomes active - not as to DARING, but as for [[Purpose|purpose]], as for hope, as for those forces of the divine nature; that the entity in its intent and purpose may be kept in a sound, perfect body to better fulfil its purpose in the earth in its present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

24. The name then was Macie Audubon.

25. Before that the entity was in the [[France|French]] land, when there were the Louises that made for especial use of the sign or symbol of [[France|France]], - the fleur de lis.

26. For, there the entity was in association with the court activity; and the entity became not only the designer for the clothing but more especially in the fittings for the homes, for the state, in the preparations for decorations of wall, for hangings, for draperies, for the coverings of chairs, for the preparations of beds.

27. And, as we find, in the present even, the entity often likes to do things ABOUT furnishings in a room - if the opportunity is given; and it will make special coverings and settings for such things.

28. These should be encouraged, these should be - as indicated - the work of the entity in this experience.  And the entity should HAVE a career, for it will have it!  even if it gets married early, it won't stay with those conditions until it has a career of some kind or another!

29. Then prepare the entity in those developments.  For these are innate, and may be made manifested - by giving the entity the opportunities in those directions.

30. There we find the entity was associated with some of its associates in the present, by its activities in its associations and connections; not as of family but as associates, as of activities in connection with those, - as may be seen by the paralleling of their activities.

31. Then the entity was known as Mademoiselle Rheulicheu; not as a relation of the Cardinal, but near that same name - though a variation in the manner of spelling.

32. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when there were those of the children of promise leaving the cities of [[Egypt|Egypt]].

33. The entity was an [[Egypt|Egyptian]], - not of the people of promise, or of the Jews - as they were even called then, or Hebrews, - but the entity as was favorable to those.  For, the closer friend of the entity was the associate of [[Joshua|Joshua]] - who was so ill when they left, or when they were preparing for the leaving, and who died on the way.

34. Then the entity was in the name Shalmahr, and the entity was among those close to those in authority and power in the land.  For, the entity's companion and its associates were in rule over those people, though the entity was favorable - as indicated - to the Jewish or Hebrew women, especially.

35. Again we find the entity was active in decorative lines, especially having to do with boats, and those things having to do with pleasure crafts; as to designs for sails, as for the carvings and some of the dressing-ups of those boats of pleasure.

36. And we will find that the entity will always be wanting to get on a boat to go somewhere!  These are a part of the experiences of the entity; wanting to go!  and yet, as indicated, be warned as to travel, especially cars - whether automobiles or trains.  It will be much safer in a plane!

37. Before that, then, the entity was in also in Egypt; during those periods when there were the preparations for the activities that made for the returning of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] to the religious authority or command.

38. The entity then was closely associated with the peoples in [[Ra Ta|the priest]]'s own household.  This brought those activities by the entity in aiding in the preparations for paper, paper making, and linens and linen making; not only from the cotton but from the materials grown in the land.

39. Thus we will find that designs in cloth, those of special weave, - will have a special interest; as there is the development by material practice of the entity in such things.

40. The entity rose to one of authority in those periods.

41. Then the name was Sat-El-Or.

42. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

43. As given, the natural tend is towards spiritual life, social life, the commercial life, - in the order named.

44. Then stress these in the developing years of the entity, - being warned in those things indicated.

45. And keep those precepts and those examples before the entity as to keep it in the right way.

46. Ready for questions.

47. (Q)  What have been the past associations with the following, and what strong points should be cultivated in present associations:  The mother, Mrs. [2111]?
(A)  As indicated.

48. (Q)  The stepfather, [...]?
(A)  As we find, more than once were there the associations here.  For, as we find, the entity was what might be called the "lady friend" during the experiences in [[France|France]], to the entity who is now the stepfather.  Also there were associations in the earliest Egyptian [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

49. (Q)  The uncle, Mr. [641]?
(A)  Associations in France.

50. (Q)  The aunt, Miss [288]?
(A)  In the activities, we don't find close associations, but we find INFLUENCES - as in the French, as well as in the experience during the periods of [[Exodus|the exodus]].

51. (Q)  The cousin, young [1208]?
(A)  In France, AND in the early Egyptian sojourn.

52. (Q)  The brother, baby [2892]?
(A)  As we find, very closely associated in Egypt.  That the only experience, as we find.

53. (Q)  The grandmother, Mrs. [1187]?
(A)  In the experience just before this there was an acquaintance, but not closely associated.

54. (Q)  For what purpose did the entity enter the present family?
(A)  For an expression - to give them an opportunity to meet that necessary in those directions indicated for the entity, and for the meeting of much in each.

55. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother [2111]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Aunt [288]
"    " Uncle [641]
