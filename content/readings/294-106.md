# TEXT OF READING 294-106 M 50

**Reading [[294-106]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/22/1928  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 2065

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of February, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement and relief of this body, and will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before.

3. The physical conditions need that correction as has been outlined in the information given; that is, so that the eliminations are set up properly in the system.  When an over acid condition arises, then congestion is easily distributed in system, so as to cause distresses in various portions of the body.

4. The body is bettered in the condition as existed from that of lesions in the right side; yet, were those baths - electric baths [sweat cabinet baths] - taken as was given for the body, and the manipulation given as has been outlined - or as has been taken by the body - and the acid kept low in system, we would find near a normal body.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  In what way may the acid be kept low in the body?
(A)  By keeping the eliminations high.

6. (Q)  Should the treatments - without the electric baths - be given?
(A)  That will assist, as has been seen.  These have set up conditions just as given, so that the adhesions as were seen and given have been relieved to a great extent.  Hence bettered conditions in the body.  The lack of perfect elimination and assimilation is produced by the lack of the system being thoroughly cleansed of poisons or accumulations from existent or formerly existent condition.  Manipulations would be beneficial, but to bring the more normal have both, see?  These may be taken in the way as has been applied - the treatments see?  The baths - in the cabinet or from electric cabinet, see?  so that the excretory system - as throws off through the outer portion of the body, or the capillary circulation - this may be brought UP, see?  through sweats, or heating the body, see?  Cabinet be better.

7. (Q)  Any other information that will be of assistance to this body?
(A)  These we would do for the physical condition, for the body is very good physically, would these be kept nearer normal.  Better condition that it has been in many days, many years, even at present, but these conditions kept near normal will give better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] through which the physical, the mental, the spiritual, may function.

8. We are through for the present.
