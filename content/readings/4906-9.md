# TEXT OF READING 4906-9

**Reading [[4906-9]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/6/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13004

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 6th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4906].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Section 7, Township 14 
Time of Reading                       North, Range 14 East, 
4:30 P. M.                            Morris, Oklahoma.

1. LS:  Now, you have before you the oil well which is being drilled in Section 7, Township 14 North, Range 14 East, located directly North of the town of Morris, Oklahoma, Okmulgee County.  You will answer all questions which I might ask you relative to this well.

2. EC:  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  At what depth should the 5 inch casing be set to shut off water at 2908?
(A)  2913, the first solid structure we have below the water line; drilling from here we will find we will encounter the oil sand, and this will be in the 2980 depth.  Drilling into this sand 6 to 8 feet when gas will be very strong.  The production from this will produce 3 to 32 hundred (3,000 to 3,200) barrels per twenty-four hours.

4. (Q)  Will this be a long life sand?
(A)  Fairly long life sand, yes.  This is below the other depths where water stratas have interfered in the sands above.  In the sands above this we have two, you see.  These, as we have given, interfered with by water that has come in from other districts.  Holes were not handled properly.

5. (Q)  Will this property be attached when well is finished.
(A)  No.  After the pipe line connections are made trouble may be expected, but this may be helped by the other developments that will be handled in same districts.

> has become of me, and whether I am ever going to write you
> or not.  Well "old man" I have been stepping on "through
> the Rye" here in Callahan County.  I have added TWO more dry
> holes to my long list and am starting another one this week.
> It seems that dry holes have a kind of nourishment in them,
> or at least I seem to thrive on them.
> 
> Cayce I returned from San Saba last night, it being my second
> trip to see Mrs. Moore, and I am glad to say that I have in
> my pocket this moment a new five year commercial lease on
> the Rocky Pasture and also Mr. Leverett's lease of 1000 acres
> adjoining Mrs. Moore's property, and about 4000 more acres
> that Mr. Boller the lawyer is getting signed up for me as I
> could not remain long enough to secure them myself.
> 
> I am very much impressed with Mrs. Julia Moore.  She sure
> is an excellent Christian character, and a most lovable old
> lady.  I took my wife and kids down with me and they enjoyed
> every moment of this stay.
> 
> Cayce I have made up my mind that I am going to drill that
> well, something tells me that its my way to success, that is,
> it means help for the poor and needy, for that's what my part
> will go for.  All I want out of it is to pay this debt of
> $150,000 that hangs on my head, and here is what I want to do
> for you, if your prediction is true, as I believe it is.  I
> am willing to drill that well on this basis, I to own all the
> leases I will finance the well, and will take out the exact
> amount of my indebtedness and interest, and then you to have
> 1/2 of all that I have, the well and all the acreage that
> remains.  I want to drill this well without selling an
> interest or selling any of the acreage, and I feel that I
> will be able to do that, for I am [[Prayer|praying]] for the well I am
> starting in Callahan County to come in good enough, that it
> will bring me enough to drill the Rocky Pasture.
> 
> Something tell me I'll get this hole down Cayce and that you
> will yet realize all you ever expected out of it, for your
> work or institution.
> 
> Cayce, I am unable to interest my Oklahoma partner in the
> deal at all so am going to fight it out by myself, and I want
> you to do this for me I suppose you can.  You take a reading
> and get a geological report in detail on that block of
> acreage telling the thing in full as Dr. Emil Boese does in
> his, and sign it Dr. Edgar Cayce - wouldn't it be fine to
> have a report written by you before the well was started
> when the well come in.  It would prove to all the world
> your power.
> 
> Say write Mrs. Moore a letter.  She would like to hear from
> you.
> 
> I am keeping Boese report and the record but am returning the
> rest of the papers.
> 
> My agreement on the Rocky Pasture is to be actually drilling
> on or before Aug 1, 1926 with all the tools rig and material
> on the ground.  Let me know Cayce if you are satisfied with
> my proposition on drilling the well and if you are not we
> will try to change it to your liking, it is only as it seems
> to me to be the fair way.
> 
> Let me hear from you, write to Mrs. Moore and don't forget
> the report.
> 
> With kindest regards to you and your family,
> 
> Yours truly  [4906]

> certainly was funny though - about two days before I received
> your letter, I DREAMED I was in Rocky Pasture and saw a well

> Yes, I'll be very glad to write to Mrs. Moore, and will do so

> As far as your offer and suggestion as to what you will do in
