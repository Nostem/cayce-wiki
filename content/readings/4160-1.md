# TEXT OF READING 4160-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4160-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/17/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11888

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 17th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by her mother.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Rosenburg.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find this body very good in many respects. There are those segments in the spinal system that have impinionments, nerve branches that produce in the system some derangements in the nerve system and the application of blood supply to portions of the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4160] we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find those conditions where the blood in its circulation to the capillaries becomes involved in the elimination.  Hence there are carried in the system those of the condition where system, attempting to alleviate the distress, brought the circulation by the derangement, as it were, in elimination.  This especially do we find in all portions that affect a portion of the inner skin, as it were. Not the exterior, for the circulation, as it were, between these becomes the deranged portion.  Hence the intense condition over portions of the body of a tingling, itching sensation, without any abrasions, except when made by the itching or irritation to the cuticle shows on the body.  This affects also all portions of the body where the roots of hair show in the cuticle.  In the quantity then in blood supply, we find that this is sufficient in the system.  Simply that derangement as produces the improper eliminations, though blood carries these properties in the system, giving to the organs of elimination a distress at times.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find in the ninth dorsal that derangement which affects the ends of the solar plexus and the branches that attach, as it were, or affect through the hypogastric plexus.  This gives the excess at times through the nervous condition as produced by this, the overtaxing to the bladder, direct to the kidneys in excretions, to the liver and to the circulation becoming a capillary circulation.  Hence the full capillary circulation at times. Again the deep seated circulation, the hepatics being then overtaxed.  This we find a lesion in the ninth dorsal segment.  In the rest of the nerve system reflexes of this condition are shown.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS PROPER, We find very good save at times when the system becomes overtaxed by the excess of elimination, that the body in its present physical condition has not adjusted itself to.  At present very good.  The taking of medicinal properties into the system only would not correct these conditions, yet we would find that it is necessary for the body to take medicinal properties with corrections made in the spinal column.  This had best be made by those of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] [[Vibration|vibration]], for the muscles, the tissue must be adjusted, as well as the segment itself removed from producing pressure.  When this is done, we will find there will be other conditions along the spine, with this change that must be kept in alignment until the system re-adjusts itself to the change in circulation and in [[Vibration|vibration]] to circulation, and may expected to cause nausea, for its connection with the hypogastric center after some several treatments have been taken.  Also melancholia and loss of incentive, as it were, to act.  These are reactions that must be accorded in the system to reach the condition necessary to bring the body normal.

6. After five to six treatments through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces have been taken, take this in the system:

Tincture of Valerian.............2 ounces,
Bromide of Potassium............15 grains,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium..............5 grains,
Elixia Calisaya..................4 ounces,
Fluid Extract of Capsici.........1 minim.
Sufficient simple syrup to make 10 ounces.

7. The dose would be half a teaspoonful (preferable in water) three times each day, and when the medicinal properties are being given each evening, give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the violet ray over the portions of system, especially where hair is needed, or wanted to be grown on the body, and along the whole length of the spinal system, or along the spinal column and at the umbilicus.  Take the vibrations over the whole system for three to five minutes each evening.  Do this. We will bring the body to its normal forces.
