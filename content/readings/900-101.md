# TEXT OF READING 900-101 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-101]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/30/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5304

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 30th day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... Road, Deal, N.J., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind of the body of [900], with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to this body.  These, as we see, with others, still bring those interpretations to the individual of the studies of the body-mind into those of the metaphysical, or of the spiritual forces, as are phenomenalized in the material world.  These from time to time, then, bring those lessons in an illustrated manner, being then the interpretations, as it were, of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] force translating those findings of the body or material mind, and the metaphysical or spiritual forces manifesting in and through same through co-relation of the action of [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or [[Soul|soul]] forces, that of the body and spirit element lying between in the elemental forces of spiritual divination of truths as may become body knowledge to an individual through study and interpretation of same, and more by applying same from time to time, for as any great truth is seen, cognizance or consciousness of same gained, without the physical application in the material world, same does not find its conscious corresponding conditions within the elements of forces as are manifested. Do that, then.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Friday Morning, July 24, 1925, at home in Deal.  "I was in bed watching some people.  First there was a lady in attractive tights.  I refused to notice her much.  Then there was a Man in tennis clothes and a goat or some animal.  The man took some milk from the animal and when I suggested that he spit it out, shook his head 'No!'  This surprised me and again I noticed the lady."
(A)  This, as we see, is that presentation to the individual mind of [900] that all the good, all the truth, all the building elements, do not through [[Physical Body|physical forces]] proceed from a given source, for all is good and purposeful in the higher realms of force and the usage of same is in accordance to the environment of the individual who applies same.  Hence that as given as first:  When a law is understood, put same into application in a material world and sense.  That is, give same to others, that THEY may apply same in THEIR lives as is to suit, fit, the needs of THEIR individual responsibilities, from the development as is and has been acquired within THEIR individual lives toward the Universal Forces - God.

4. (Q)  "The man in tennis clothes was getting ready to go out on the courts.  'You can't talk on the courts,' he said to me.  'Can't talk?'  I queried.  'How foolish and why not.' 'You can't play and talk both,' said the lady and I noticed she was now dressed and had on a large red hat.  'I'll bet you one dollar I can play, talk and beat you,' I called after the man as he left the room.  The lady followed him out.  A sailor entered and took some milk from the animal.  This was a different sort of man, I thought and motioned him to spit it out.  He shook his head 'No!'  I became utterly disgusted and a revulsion of feeling came over me.  'You are a terrible set of people,' I said."
(A)  This again the same lesson as has been given in the one preceding this, or a continuation of the same lesson, presented from the different point of view, as is seen in wager of speech and play, as is seen in change of costume, with that presenting the higher elements of red in head gear, or of the higher attributes of mind's development, as is seen in the reasoning with the various stages of man's considered development in physical or in environ-mental forces.  All good of one force.  Obtain same from all, see?

5. (Q)  Sunday Morning, July 26, in [[New York City|New York City]].  "Saw a body in the flesh and out of it.  In and out - in and out - always moving."
(A)  This, as we see, a continuation of those forces, as has been presented, wherein the body saw grave open, where the grave and all move, where the body saw self and its continuation or arise from same, the same manifestation of the same forces as are set in motion by the [[Oneness|oneness]] of the spiritual elements as may manifest and do manifest through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in a material world, for all force is of one force, and a force may be a force yet not a force, in the sense of physical reasoning.  Hence as is seen in the body and out of the body, yet all in motion, all of one body, all of one force, all of one elemental condition; that of the [[Creative Forces|creative force]] as is manifest from the spiritual from within.

6. (Q)  "Saw numbers 100 and 116 - then 100 and 116 - 100 and 116 - up and down, back and forth."
(A)  This, as we see, relating to conditions that exist in the mental mind relating to some specific stock in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has had the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] to deal therein.  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] should act upon the information, knowing that such will be, has been, is being now, the condition regarding same.

7. We are through.
