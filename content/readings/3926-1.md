# TEXT OF READING 3926-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[3926-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1920 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/3/1920  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11563

This psychic reading was given by Edgar Cayce.*

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Z.T.A. House, 
Time of Reading                                ... College, 
Unknown.                                       ..., Ga.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  The abnormal condition that we find that is working to the detriment of this spark of life that is attempting to create an equilibrium that will keep the body and [[Soul|soul]] and spirit of this body together, lies in the fact that the distributing forces that should take from what is given into the system to obtain sustenance from, has not as yet reached a state, or stage, to which the body is able to make this perfect balance, or to take from this sustenance that should propagate or give life cellular force within this body itself, that is instead of building on what is given this system, this body of [3926] it is still using the force, or spark, from the supply of nutriment given by the mother to it in his conception, and it is being the force or the action of the elements within itself, here through the digestive tract to create the building force from its own active force has not completed sufficient to rebuild through these, so we find one gradually giving away, losing rather than gaining in its sustenance to keep the spirit and [[Soul|soul]] and body together.

2. First there is need, which has been given and is being given, attention that has been necessary to keep this, that which will produce heat without fermentation in this little body, that which will make a clarification of all used forces to gain power from that which is given, one that will study and that does study well the dietetics, so thoroughly keeping its being on that which has been from propagation rather than sustenance given into the system.

3. The change from this is what will be needed.  It will be hard for those about to see what is needed to take, for very soon if that change is not made we will find there will be coming between this, that which will cause battle between the elements within itself.

4. We would take a vegetable matter, such as found in the combination known as Royal.  Combine this with the predigested foods, such as is found in Mellin's Prepared Food



* [GD's note:  7/3/20 [3926]'s uncle, [3927] obtained a Physical Reading.  This reading may also have been obtained the same time.]

for infants, under new dietetic arrangements and formula, for this age and condition.  Only one drop twice a day will be needed to give into the system.  This does not mean that care shall not be taken in watching the digestive tract, but follow this closely along with this.  It will be necessary to use more lime.  Just after the first day or two it will be necessary to add this to what has been added by the outside forces, and there will be a change within the next three to four cycles of 24 hours.  Do this if you would keep these in perfect accord.

5. (Q)  What hours during the day should this Royal be given?
(A)  Morning - Evening.

6. (Q)  Can this be obtained at any drugstore?
(A)  No, it is specially prepared by the Royal Co. of [[Chicago|Chicago]].  It is an antiseptic especially made for infants.

7. (Q)  By this treatment life will be maintained in this little body, will it not?
(A)  That depends on circumstances that must come into it. This is the thing to do at the present time.

8. (Q)  Are there any details pertaining to this Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Do as we have outlined.  The food should be that as we have outlined - from the prepared food, see?  That will act or give to the duodenum and pancrean forces and perform the proper action in the intestines.
