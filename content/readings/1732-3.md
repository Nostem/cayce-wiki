# TEXT OF READING 1732-3 F 37

**Reading [[1732-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 7936

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1732].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       Ossining, [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1732] - this we have had before.  In some respects the conditions physical are bettered.  In others there is not much change, but that as HAS been applied has changed in the manner given.  Only halfheartedly have the suggestions given been carried out by the body.  Just to apply MECHANICAL suggestions will mean little, unless it be for PURELY PHYSICAL conditions alone, that are of an exterior nature - but when, as here, there are involved those of the physical and the mental, as well as those retractions as come from both the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic system, as related to the eliminations - then these must be adhered to properly, and in the manner as given.  Will those suggestions as first given be adhered to in a manner in keeping with these as suggested, then we may GIVE further corrective measures.  Use that in hand FIRST, that one may have MORE!  We are through with THIS reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
