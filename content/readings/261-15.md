# TEXT OF READING 261-15 M 47 (Banking, Investments,

**Reading [[261-15]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/17/1935  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 924

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 17th day of August, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [261], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [261] and Mrs. [772], and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Long Island, N.Y. 
(Entity.  You will give a mental and spiritual reading for 
[[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], giving the reason for entrance into this solar 
cycle and guidance for developing and expressing to the 
highest possible degree in this life his psychic faculties. 
[[Entity (Cayce Term)|This entity]] seeks council and direction.  Answer questions.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, [261].

2. In giving that which may be helpful to the entity in the applying of self in this material experience for [[Soul|soul]] development, it is well that each [[Soul|soul]] understands [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] of an entity, a [[Soul|soul]], taking on matter that there may be the manifestations through practical application of the tenets that make for the developing of the soul and the soul-body THROUGH this USE of same in its relationships to its fellow man.

3. That the soul of each should be a companion with, of, the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], is the [[Purpose|purpose]] of each entity, each soul. That it, the soul, is in the material plane away from or at variance with, or accord with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], is evidenced by the manner of the application of things spiritual, things mental, things material in the relationships one with another.

4. The [[Purpose|purpose]] of the entity's entrance at this time or period, then, or for this activity, was that another lesson - as it were - might be gained; that there might be the opportunity for soul expression.

5. The knowledge of an activity, the knowledge of a thing, is not sufficient in itself; to have heard of, or to be merely aware of a condition.  But to make same applicable, active as a principle in the experience of the motivative forces in the soul is the [[Purpose|purpose]] in the material world.

6. For, as given, "As the tree falls so will it lie."  The body of the tree, or the body of the individual, is to become tenable for the growth, the spirit, the creative energy, the 'elan vitale' through that active force to go on and on to become one with, yet aware of its OWN identity IN, the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]].

7. Hence these are the purposes, the aims of the soul in its activity, how an entity may become aware of its soul, of its psychic force, of its relationships to the Creative Forces that these may be as practical, workable experiences in any phase, in every phase of the soul's experience.

8. It has been given by Him, the Giver of all good and perfect gifts, that if ye would KNOW thyself - if ye would know thy Maker, then KNOW thyself; by that ye mete to thine brother in relationships, or as standardized by that thou holdest in thy consciousness as [[Ideal|the IDEAL]] manner.  [[Ideal|The ideal]] manner was set by Him that gave, "If ye abide in me and I in the Father, what ye ask, THAT ye ask, WHATEVER ye ask in my name, that shall ye experience in thine own consciousness."

9. How does one, then, approach same?  The activity is as the seed sown, which has within same (every act) the fruit thereof.  For the first law is, "Like begets like."  And what ye sow, ye shall reap.

10. It is, then, how ye mete that ye reap in thine relationships one to another.

11. As He has given, it is in patience with self, in patience with thy friend, in patience with thy foe, that ye become aware of thine soul; that it, thy soul, is - in the activities thereof - prompted through the mind of same in accord with or bearing fruit of life or death, good or evil; that THOU hast set as thine [[Ideal|ideal]].

12. Then, the first law of knowing self, of understanding self, is to become more and more sincere with that thou doest in the relationships one to another.  For the proof of same is the fruit thereof.  And when thou hast found the way, thou showest the way to thy brother.

13. These be the manners; these be the ways in which each entity - THIS entity - may know that there is the access to the Father through the Son IN the self, through that which He has promised.  For the maker of that which comes into materiality as a force, as a power that is adverse to Creative Forces or to the generations of what is constructive, is man - or the selfishness in his experience.  But the ego turned in the other direction may be and is the constructive force in the experience of each soul; expressed in the I AM being one with the I AM presence.

14. Then, in making practical or concrete activity of the entity in its experience, first enter in as through the meditative forces within self; through the purifying of the body, of the mind, that it may be one and in accord, in attune with the Creative Forces from within; setting the ideal in Him who has promised to meet thee in the [[Holy of Holies|holy of holies]], in [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of thy soul, in thy own body-consciousness.

15. Thus does the self, the I AM, become aware of that presence. And in meeting day by day in the walks with thy fellow man, sow those seeds that YE would reap in thine experience.  For they, the seed ye sow, BECOME - as it were - thine OWN SELF! For each soul meets that it has meted unto the fellow man. For as He gave, "As ye do it unto the least of these, my little ones, ye do it unto me."

16. Psychic is of the soul, then, first becoming aware of its inactivity or activity toward the Creator within thine own experience.  Not as from without; for the external experiences will be the natural result when the self is attuned.  As ye attune thine own mechanical instruments to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of this or that sending influence, ye receive that which is being sent out or vibrated UPON that plane.

17. So is thy body, thy mind, thy soul, as the sending, as the receiving, as the giving forth in thine activities and thine walks before men.

18. This, then, is the manner.  Enter ye in at the gate called straight, through thine own preparation in thine own manner, and meet Him there.  For thy body is [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God, of thy living soul; thy body is [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] that must be, should be, WILL be kept holy, if ye will know thy true relationships to thy Maker.

19. Keep the trust thou hast in the ALL-Creative Forces; knowing He is able to keep that thou committest unto Him against any experience that may arise in thine own activity.

20. Keep the self in such a manner as to be circumspect in thine own consciousness.  Be true to self, making for those activities that bear the fruits of the spirit; just being kind, just being gentle, just being patient, just showing fellowship, just showing brotherly love; just bearing witness in thy walks, thy acts, thy understandings unto thy fellow man.  And ye shall know Him face to face.  For He hath promised to bring to such the REMEMBRANCES from the foundations of the earth, of the world, of the universe.  For thou wert in the beginning, even as He.

21. Ready for questions.

22. (Q)  Can the entity's psychic faculties be expressed or developed through [[Numerology|numerology]]?
(A)  As we have indicated oft, [[Astrology|astrology]] and [[Numerology|numerology]] and symbology are all but the gateways or the signs of expression.

As for this entity, as we have indicated the symbols of [[Numerology|numerology]] may be developed; but the [[Intuition|INTUITIVE]] forces that arise WITH same make for rather the safer, the saner, the more spiritual way, with the less aptitude of turning to forces from without.

For, as we see in numbers, or numerology:  One indicates STRENGTH, power, influence; yet has all the weaknesses of all other influences that may be brought to bear upon any given activity in which same may be indicated.  But it is KNOWN as strength and power; even as the UNION of self with the Creative Forces that express themselves in the activity of matter, in ANY form, is power.

Two makes for a division; yet in the multiple of same, in four, it makes for the greater weaknesses in the divisions. In six and eight it makes for the same characterizations, yet TERMED more in these that one is power, two is weakness, three is the strength of one with the weakness of two; four being more and more of a division and weakness; six being the changes that have been made in the DOUBLE strength of three. Seven is the spiritual number.  Eight indicates the commercial change.  Nine indicates strength and power, with a change.

These, then, are as INDICATIONS; and NOT other than the SIGNS of things, that may be altered ever by the force or factor from which they emanate.

Hence [[Intuition|INTUITIVE]] force is the better, for in this there may come more the union of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] with Creative Energy; thus the answer may be SHOWN thee, whether in Urim, in Thummim, in [[Dreams|dream]], in numbers, in WHATEVER manner or form. For He IS the strength of them all, and beareth witness IN thee and through thee - if ye but do His biddings.

23. (Q)  If so, how can they be used through this method for the benefit of others?
(A)  As indicated, there are many channels, many manners - as we have just given.  These are as free in themselves; the numbers, see?

As related to individuals these each vibrate to certain numbers according to their name, their birth date, their relationships to various activities.  Then when these appear, they become either as strengths or as losses or as helps or as change, or as the spiritual forces.  But, as indicated, they are rather as the signs, or the omens; and may be given as warnings, may be given as helps, may be given in any manner that they may be constructive in the experience of the individual.

24. (Q)  Any other advice that will be of help at this time?
(A)  There are many authorities (so called) upon numerology. Study same, if ye would know same; yet rely upon the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]], ever, that may make itself manifest in its own way unto thee.

Does the beauty of the rose excel the beauty of the violet or of the other flowers?  Each in its own manner has its own appeal; yet who giveth the beauty of these?  It was ORDAINED that these might be as signs, as omens, as knowledge, unto man; that he might make himself - through the desires, through the longings, through the application of these beauties - at-one with the Source, through the expression of gentleness.  As He gave, "A cup of water given in my name loseth not its reward."  Just as the beauty of the rose expressed loseth not its preciousness in the sight of the Maker, though it be trampled underfoot by a disappointed personage.

So in thine application of thy abilities of thy influences in thine experiences, make the applications in the SPIRIT of helpfulness, hopefulness.  Not of exhortation, not of aggrandizement of self nor for the laudation of self; but that His beauty, His joy, His peace, His glory, may be through thy efforts taken account of, taken thought of, be experienced by those ye meet day by day.

Thus may ye come into touch, into knowledge, into association, into relationships with all that is power, that is beauty, that is strength, in the material, the mental, the spiritual realms.

25. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n File
