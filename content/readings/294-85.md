# TEXT OF READING 294-85 M 49

**Reading [[294-85]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/12/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2044

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by self.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC, present in this room and the two [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the mornings of Nov. 11th and 12th, 1926, regarding the horses and messengers, or riders.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, EC.  This we have had before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, represent to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] the various messages that come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and in the manner of the coming represent that seen in the charger, in the slower plodder, and the racer - as well as is seen, in the sex and mating.

4. In the lesson then to be gained:  That, though many may charge, many may appear that the conditions are such as to cause worry, trouble, fear and misunderstanding - yet in the fullness of the messages will be seen that all bring to that one [[Purpose|purpose]] in fulfilling that designed through the All [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] for such to accomplish - for as seen in the conditions, all bring joy in the end.

5. Then take hope, heart - fear not, for the conditions will be brought to that in which many will know the truth through the efforts of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  See?

6. We are through for the present.
