# TEXT OF READING 900-301 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-301]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/19/1927  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5504

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce, at the Halcyon Hotel, [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]], this 19th day of February, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come in those ways and manners as have been outlined, and are to be used by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the way as has been given should be used by same. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Night of Thursday, Feb. 10, 1927.  While at [[Prayer|prayer]], Voice:  "27th to 9th."
(A)  In this there is presented that fulfilling of the period to which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has looked and does look forward with so much expectancy, and as the numbers, the time, the period, indicates, will be the full, complete, and in keeping with those conditions as are sought by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in supplication, to the Giver of all good and perfect gifts - for meriting in the action, to that extent, the entity gains that sought, through such supplication - for in action comes results. See?

4. (Q)  27th to 9th meaning days of some month?
(A)  27th of March to 9th of April, see?

5. (Q)  In reference to what?
(A)  Fulfilling of those desires, those conditions for which the entity sought in its supplication, to be guided and directed for these conditions.

6. (Q)  Morning of Tuesday, Feb. 15, 1927.  A young boy who seemed to be a younger brother wanted money and went in to what seemed a rather stern father to get it.  The boy's name seemed to be Tom.  The latter emerged from the father's room with $1.50 and went on out.  Then I went in to my mother and found her lying on a couch.  She said to me:  "Take care of Tom, [900], and you may have anything and everything you want."  I felt a sort of emotional rush and replied timidly: "You mean that I may even have my chemistry?"  "Yes, you may even have that," she replied.  "I will take care of Tom," I said.  (I felt like I would have anyway.)  I went out and followed Tom down the street.  He had built radio wires and I noticed that it was still in process of construction.  I saw the many wires stretched across like many parallel aerials. "Into this construction I will run my individual station lines," said Tom.  I saw metal pieces stretched with the end of each piece at an angle to each wire.  At another end of the wires I saw some contrivance.  Then the whole affair was decorated in gay fashion.  The stern father came out of the house, and looking at the work, asked:  "When was it started?"  I feared his displeasure and interceded for Tom, saying:  "He built it on his way out, right here."  I marveled at the work as it by this time was completely and elaborately decorated in carnival fashion.
(A)  In this emblematical way and manner, there is presented to the entity those conditions as sought by the entity, in the way the entity may so conduct self toward individuals, of gifts, or of those as related to individual association - those elements as are seen by the entity of the action, bringing to self the satisfaction in obtaining for self those lessons, those conditions, those truths, and a manner and a way to present, to deliver, to see that all gain that knowledge and understanding of same in the action of self toward individual conditions and individual elements - for, as is seen, various conditions enter in - as to the displeasure, as to the fatherly association, as to the fatherly sternness, as to the mother's giving of self to the individual's qualifications for building that which to the entity becomes as the marvel - which, as is seen, represents to the entity that - in the building into self those conditions as are pictured, in the assistance and aid, into the keeping of those truths that have been placed, and are placed, in the keeping of the entity, to be handled in the ways and manners as will bring about the better conditions for all - there comes that position, that condition, in the life, in the action of the entity, and capable of being disseminated out to many, that will bring the peace, the joy, the happiness, the understanding of conditions as relating to the giving to others an understanding in the ways of living, and of life.

The realization - as these represent wires that would radio, or radiate, to various individuals - is as the great radiation that the entity would be capable of sending out, would the entity bring self wholly, entirely, in that attitude of the abilities to disseminate, through applying lessons and laws gained by self in the study of the conditions as are being followed, see?

As is seen in those specific conditions as were seen by the entity in the study and in the application of conditions, use same, then, in that way and manner.

7. (Q)  Night of Feb. 15th or morning of Feb. 16th, 1927. Definite experience on creation of instruct and intelligence.
(A)  In this there is presented to the entity, in the correlation of conditions in the mental consciousness with that of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and [[Superconscious|superconscious]], as it were, or reasoning from the [[Superconscious|superconscious]] experience, those experiences which are to the entity as lessons upon which the theories, the practice of the entity, may be builded towards definite conditions, definite goals reached, definite application in the lives of others, in the life of self - in its everyday action, in its secret action, in its action with the fellow man.  Then use these experiences as the building for that as the entity would give to others.

8. We are through for the present.
