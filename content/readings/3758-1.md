# TEXT OF READING 3758-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3758-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/13/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11292

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 13th day of October, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stallings, Brazelton & Hale Court Reporter, Steno. Mrs. [3758].]

Time of Reading 
9:00 P. M.                                         ..., Ala.

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep.

Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep.  All of the organs of your body will function properly; you will hear me and answer all of my questions.

You have before you tonight Mrs. [3758] and the body is present in this room.  Now, Mr. Cayce, tell me the physical and the mental ailments that you find within this body that you have before you; also tell me whether she has any trouble with her hearing, or any defects that she may have, and if you find any prescribe a cure.  Mrs. [3758], present in this room.

2. EC:  Yes, where we have the body here.  Now the abnormal conditions as we find in this body - Mrs. [3758] we are speaking of - have to do with the functioning of the organs and with the effect of the functioning of other organs not affected but reflexly affecting other portions of the body; that is through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

3. Now these are conditions as we find in this body:  The blood supplying forces we find shows the effect of the nervous forces as is thrown off by the action of the nerve generating forces through the hepatics, and the action directly of the pneumogastrics on the forces as applied to the circulation through these portions of the body, the carrying in the circulation or in the part of the hemoglobin those parts picked or carried by the circulation from this part of the body, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

4. The action of the nervous forces over the system has become affected by this condition through actions to the sensory organisms as is shown in the action here of the ear or forces in the right side here, which come from the left side of the body, - see?
FEM:  Yes sir.

5. That is, at the 7th dorsal vertebra we have a lesion that forms that prevents the proper distribution of forces through the physical to care for the condition existing in the system, or has prevented the removing through the slowing of the circulation both in lymphatic forces and in blood supply from taking from used forces as is used by the [[Vibration|vibration]] in the body when sound was produced within the forces here, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

6. Also used; that the [[Vibration|vibration]] responded to the sense of the hearing, - see?

FEM:  Yes.

7. That then has formed the force or the thickening of the physical wall, so that the sound only vibrates in very slow tones to this portion of the body, - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

8. The action of the sensory system not being in accord at all times with the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the sympathetic nervous force prevents the action of the forces as we have seen expressed through the circulation in its retarded forces shown through the pneumogastrics' ends and their feeding forces from there about the digestive tract from which forces the beginning of the trouble to the ear was brought about.  The pneumogastric extends from the stomach to the base of the brain and through to the upper brain, and is the active or the dwelling forces of the spiritual forces of this body here, you see.

FEM:  Yes sir.

9. The action then between the pneumogastric and sympathetic forces may direct and assist the vibrations if properly kept in tune to revibrate to the sensory organism and produce sound again within the body; yet with mechanical forces it should be brought about to give the proper incentives to give these to the body, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

10. Just as we find that the body accepts physically the congestion as shows through the mucous forces through the throat and through the head here, you see.

FEM:  Yes sir.

11. Just so we find that with the proper vibrations these may be soon thrown off, - see?
FEM:  Yes.

12. That is the mental forces, you see.

FEM:  Yes.

13. In the brain force, that is the cranium, cerebellum, may act in direct connection with the action of the sensory organism, with the heart forces, with all of the organs producing life-giving forces within the body, through the pneumogastric force, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

14. The direct connection between the cerebro-spinal and all of the viscera of that portion of the body affected in vibratory forces in the body.  To add that of mechanical forces to this body would be to relieve the congestion or lesion now formed at the seventh dorsal, - see?

FEM:  Yes.

15. And to relieve the congestion as we find in the first and second cervical.  That we would do by vibratory forces, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

16. Then by keeping the body physically and mentally and spiritually in the balance, control the action of blood force, the action of vibratory force throughout the trunk portion of the body; then overcome by this same condition those reflex vibrations that at times affect the locomotories here in the lower extremities to where the feet become heavy and tired at times, you see.

FEM:  Yes.

17. So that the vibrations carry out the whole portions of the body.  Keep the body young as it can here will do by its own vibrations, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

18. Do that.

19. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, is there any further remedy that you could suggest to restore the hearing of the right ear?
(A)  Give the vibrations to these portions of the body as we have given, - see?

FEM:  Yes.

To the left side of the seventh dorsal and to the whole or the front of the cervicals here, you see.

FEM:  Yes.

Second and third, by vibratory force, or by deep manipulation force, - see?

FEM:  Yes.

Or that as we would find in the deep, heavy [[Massage Therapy|massage]] force, by electric driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], - see?

20. (Q)  Is there anything the matter with this body's left side?
(A)  Only reflex conditions as we have given here, you see, that affect through the action of blood supply force, and the pneumogastric throwing off the discharges as we have given, you see.

21. (Q)  There is nothing organic then.
(A)  Nothing organic; same reflex conditions that are produced in here, you see.  Correct the condition we find in the dorsal here, you see.

22. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, I don't just quite understand how you expect to correct that condition.  Please tell me.
(A)  By removing the congestion as we have given here.

23. (Q)  With what force will we remove that congestion?
(A)  Just as we have given you how to remove it:  By vibration from electrically driven forces, or deep manipulation.

24. (Q)  Would that have to be done by an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] doctor?
(A)  We have not given any doctors.  We don't need any doctors.  We told her how to use it.  We are through.

25. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about this reading you have given.  You are feeling all right, and all of the organs of your body function properly.  You are feeling good, and now wake up in two minutes, just feeling fine and all right.
