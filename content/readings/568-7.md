# TEXT OF READING 568-7 M 29

**Reading [[568-7]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/8/1943  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 4008

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self, through the mother, Mrs. [379], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.                  ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [568].

3. As we find, from the activities and the diet, there has come again the reaction to the coordinating through the nerve centers at the base of the brain, those congestions causing contraction of the nerves of the body-forces.

4. It will be necessary to be more careful about the diet.

5. We would have those lesions broken up that are tending to form again in the upper cervical as well in the area of the lacteal duct.

6. Do use more of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  Use these at least the day before the adjustments are given, and do [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the areas about the lacteal and liver and gall duct centers.

7. Do that.

8. (Q)  What about the activities during these treatments?
(A)  He may go on with his activities.

9. (Q)  What should the diet be?
(A)  It should not be too much of heavy foods, those of the pulse variety, not too great a quantity of meat; not leafy vegetables for this particular body.

10. (Q)  Any suggestions as to who should give these treatments?
(A)  Those who have been making them should do very well.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. Richardson) Copy to Ass'n file
