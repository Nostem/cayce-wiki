# TEXT OF READING 1745-7 M 49

**Reading [[1745-7]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/10/1942  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 7978

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, the general condition - or the state of the conditions in kidney - are not more serious than when last we had same here.  There are SOME improvements; though at times there is a great deal of discomfort to the body.

4. There has been the consideration, we find, of those treatments under the institution in Glen Rose, [[Texas|Texas]].

5. Here we find that not only the baths but also the character of treatments would be beneficial, if there is used ONLY the gentle massage AND the magnetic treatment.

6. Much might be said about the water and the treatments of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] nature here; also the diets.

7. DO NOT become entangled in some other subjects that are at times presented at this institution, but the treatments for the kidney disturbance WOULD prove most beneficial and MAY enable the body to eliminate same WITHOUT operative forces.

8. Then, we would entertain the considerations of such treatments, as soon as practical, at Glen Rose.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is there any other agent that will cause the body to absorb this stone?
(A)  Of course, as we have indicated, there are other measures.  These, however, would be the better agencies in the present - if there is the ability, the time, and if the body does NOT become involved, see?

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Sister, Mrs. [1100]
