# TEXT OF READING 264-6 F 39

**Reading [[264-6]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/15/1930  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 1537

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. House and Mrs. Evans.]

Time of Reading                                 At her home, 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [264] - this we have had before.  Now, we find - while there has been apparent some conditions that were of an unsatisfactory nature - the body-physical and mental is on the improve.  Drainages set up in system, calls for eliminations in some character, some form; while with the flowing of the system through those periods in which the conditions about cyst throw off, call for the accumulation of excess forces in the white blood supply, there IS the increase in the red blood supply as well.  So long as the attitude is kept as it IS as regarding self, and with the proper drainages kept, these will constantly REDUCE the conditions as cause the distress. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Then, under present circumstances, the body should not have the operation?
(A)  Not under present circumstances.  It may become necessary, should there become an increase or a hardening again of those cysts, or those conditions in the form of the cyst or tumor, but these drainages are, as is seen, REDUCING - and ABSORPTION IS taking place.

3. (Q)  Under present conditions, should the body return to the hospital for treatment, or can she follow same at home?
(A)  If she will carry out all of the treatments, she may remain at home - or she may return to hospital, as is chosen by self.

4. (Q)  Which would be best for the body?
(A)  That would be determined in self.

5. (Q)  What is blood count, or condition of blood?
(A)  This has just been given.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
