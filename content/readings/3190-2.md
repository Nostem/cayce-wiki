# TEXT OF READING 3190-2 M 46

**Reading [[3190-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/23/1925  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 10576

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23rd day of February, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [3190].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [3190] and wife, Mr. [77], and Mrs. Thompson.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. LS:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will also answer all questions which I will ask you relative to this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find the physical conditions in this body much improved from those as we have had here before.  The resistance in the system and the conditions as have been in the digestive system that had to do with eliminations and assimilations are better.

3. In the region of the mesenteric connection in lumbar region, this all shows the improvement in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of body.  In the structural forces in lumbars, we find there still some conditions where the over-taxed condition of the mesenteries and of the forces within the lumbar plexuses show strain, or we have pains in the muscular tissue, produced by the lack of full eliminations in the system.

4. The properties as taken in system as was given show how the effect was produced.

5. Now, for the present condition and to bring the reaction in the system, we would take as this:

6. To 10 ounces of distilled water, add 6 grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], with 30 grains [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]].  The dosage would be, beginning with 5 drops in glass of water taken once each day, preferably in the evening.  Increase drop by drop each day until 15 drops are taken.  Miss one day.  Then begin again with 6 drops, increasing then drop by drop to 21, then beginning with the 5 again.  This we find, with the stimulation over the system of the violet rays, will bring the resistive force in the system and create perfect eliminations through tissue and increase the system in all [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] conditions.  These we find will give the body the better condition and bring normal forces to the body, [3190].

7. Do that.

8. (Q)  Should the body skip a day between the day on which he takes 21 drops of the medicinal properties from the day on which he takes 5 drops?
(A)  Each change skip one day, that the system may have the reaction with the properties, producing their incentives in system, and beginning with the smaller quantities, once 5, next 6, that they may give their valuation in relative forces in tissue in body.

9. (Q)  What causes the eyes to water at times?
(A)  This produced by overflow through lachrymal ducts, and this we find is lack of the proper elimination through the nasal cavities.  With increased tissue in system, increased lymphatic forces in the body, increased and stimulated forces in emunctories, this will bring relief to body.

10. (Q)  What method should the body use that would give the remarkable powers of resisting physical exhaustion?
(A)  That as we have given.

11. (Q)  What special foods would give to the mind and body great strength, endurance and activity?
(A)  All of the vegetable, especially those of celery, parsnips and that of the like nature.  Celery, salsify, parsnips.

12. (Q)  What should the weight of the body be normally?
(A)  Hundred and sixty-five.

13. (Q)  Is the body under weight at the present time?
(A)  Yes.

14. (Q)  What should the body do to reach its normal weight?
(A)  Keep the system in that as we have given, so we will bring the conditions necessary for rebuilding in system, for as we find, the body has reached that condition in the general system where the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of body, the tissue in the arterial system, must be stimulated to give the correct incentives.  This we will find through properties as given, and correct living.

15. (Q)  What should the body do to relieve the itching of the rectum, and bring to the normal again?
(A)  Using those conditions as we have given for the body. Increasing the circulation in the emunctories and lymphatics gives sufficient secretions to each [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] of the system, when the incentive in [[Vibration|vibration]] through [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] is applied, to relieve the strain on system.

16. (Q)  In what way can the body control the physical through the mental?
(A)  The mental ever controls the physical, when guided in those channels that gives the awakening in the physical of the Divine in self.  In that channel may the body guide the physical through the mental, for ever will we find that the "spirit beareth witness with My Spirit," as to the control to make the mental, the physical, the spiritual in the body One with THAT FORCE giving the life in the body.

17. (Q)  What limit has the body's power of [[Visions|vision]]?
(A)  In this too we find the limitations as to the guards as set in the mental bearing the relation with the spiritual forces in self, as are inherent in each individual.  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|This entity]] may gain the greater [[Visions|vision]] for itself, depending upon the usage of gained [[Visions|vision]] as to its development, for as we see each entity has its own individual forces as are manifested through self, and as one sees the Divine manifested in the individual the insight of the individual controlled by its usage of that obtained.

18. We are through.
