# TEXT OF READING 140-17 F 25 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[140-17]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/28/1927  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 553

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by her brother-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                     NYC. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [140].  This we have had here before.

2. The [[Dreams|dreams]] - as has been given for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - present them- selves through the various phases of the body consciousness of the individual, and are to be used in the ways and manners as have been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. But we find there are many that have been presented to the body from time to time, and were the body to take those as are presented and study same - there will be found much of interest, much of profit, to the body and to the body development, and to the body consciousness of [140].

4. In the various presentations, these phases of the relation- ship with individuals - of those unseemingly, ungovernable conditions by some - are as lessons, that the body may gain that of the way, the manner, the HOW to direct self - for these are as maps, or chalked outlines for individual character presentations from time to time.

5. (Q)  Morning of September 26.  Dreamed Edgar Cayce died and we were all in consternation and excitement.  We were in great turmoil and excitement.
(A)  Presenting in this manner one of the various phases of conditions as are seen by those approaches of study and of thought, and - as is seen in this - presents for the body that of how the various conditions - that seemingly are unattractive, or untentative in a manner to the mind - may cause the greater disturbance and the greater perturbance to the mind and activities of the body, unless considered in the way and manner that is to gain a better understanding.  This then is seen in the way of the correction for self in the activities of the body as pertaining to individuals, and the individual especially as seen.  Act as the conscience - as pertaining to same - directs.

6. We are through with this reading.
