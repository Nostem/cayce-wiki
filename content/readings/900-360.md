# TEXT OF READING 900-360 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-360]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/4/1928  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5563

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and the enquiring minds of [900] and [137], of ..., N.Y. City, and their business associations.  You will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] and [137].  These we have had here before, with the business associations respecting same.

3. Many conditions present themselves to these bodies at the present time, for these bodies are finding in that material plane that success in the mental, in the physical, or in the financial, brings with same more responsibilities and more presentations of the ability to take on more responsibility; and more of the fruits of success in the mental, the material, and, the financial way, are presented from time to time - but ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Would it be well for [137] and [900] to take in a partner at this time and trade for themselves?
(A)  Rather - just at the present time - for these to make haste slowly.  While these are on the wave of success, financially and materially and mentally, yet not well that these be over burdened with the overhead under the present conditions; for, as has been presented [in 900-359, Par. 5-A] in that wherein there was seen one with another entering the closet dark - and the clothes closet rather than that of a speculative or of other nature, and the cloudiness, the troubled expression - yet when submerging, or when leaving same, other conditions seem to have been overcome and pleasantness entered, or pleasantness came from same.  Let that be rather then as the lesson.  Not just yet is the time. While to not leave the conditions in an UNSETTLED way, still defer same for the time being, and the congeniality, the conditions as would surround the change with one being considered in such a place will be found to work out more satisfactorily in every way and manner.

Then, we would defer this until at least the latter part of the summer or early fall of the present year, see?  for we find there are many conditions to be considered as respecting conditions throughout the country in this present year.  Many peoples who have been considered of a mediocre temperament or abilities will shine as leading lights in the mental and the financial world - and some of these are these associated here; in fact BOTH of these as seen here will, in THEIR WAY - each in their channel - shine as lights in the financial and in the mental world.  These had best be made as individuals, yet still with the associations as at present surround same, rather than the change, until each are more stable or more accustomed (in the common parlance) to their new surroundings, their new conditions, their new abilities, their new RESPONSIBILITIES, their new obligations in such fields.  Not that the one considered as forming such partnership would not be entirely satisfactory, yet - as given - [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] will be much better helped by some delay in this being brought about, than were it entered into in the present conditions or present surroundings.

5. (Q)  Should [2886] be considered in this light?
(A)  As given, this body [2886] very acceptable, and one that may be added to or associated with these in quite a satisfactory way and manner; yet as given, and as seen from here, these had best be postponed for the time being.  Latter part of the summer or the early fall.  Why?  Many, many conditions, many elements, enter in - and, as given, these would prove more satisfactory at such time than at the present.

6. We are through with this reading.
