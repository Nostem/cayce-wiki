# TEXT OF READING 294-206 M 65

**Reading [[294-206]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/14/1942  Sex: M  Age: 65  ReadingID: 2165

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. Edgar Cayce, Honorary Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Esther Wynne, Frances Y. Morrow, Florence Edmonds, Ruth LeNoir, Marsden Godfrey, Ruth Denney, Beverly and Riley Simmons, and Thomas Jefferson Davis.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] Edgar Cayce, born March 18, 1877, in Christian County, near [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky. You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes.  In giving the signs, the symbols, as may indicate activities which may be picturized into an [[Aura|aura]] chart, - much depends upon the concept of the artist in making such a drawing a thing of beauty, or a helpful experience for an entity.

3. In giving the interpretations of same, the influences or activities during the period of the experience of such an entity may be indicated the better, possibly, in picturizations of a place; while the manners in which such an activity was applied may be given in the signs or symbols to which the entity was attracted from such an activity in that particular sphere of consciousness.

4. Then, for this entity, we would begin first by picturizing that to illustrate the period when the morning stars sang together and the sons of God came together announcing the advent of man into material consciousness.

5. Thus, across the lower portion, extending almost the whole width of the design, - begin in the righthand side by putting the crescent, with the seven stars; the one closer to the crescent the brighter.  These, the stars, would be five- pointed, in gold and in silver.  This portion of such a drawing should be rather with the background the blue canopy of the heaven; dark, while toward the left side would come the shading to that of light, lighter, lightest - with just the very tip of the sun arising - over the expanse as of water; with heavy vegetation and growth.  The animals all would be indicated as in a state of expectancy toward the light, or the sun; from [[Serpent (Eden)|the serpent]] to the fowls of the air; indicating especially the raven and the doves in the fowls of the air, and [[Serpent (Eden)|the serpent]] upon one of the trees or shrubs indicated, and the ox, the camel.  These especially would be indicated in such a drawing.

6. Upon the left, in the lower portion, put the symbol of the sun.  Upon the right put the symbol of the earth.  Each of these would be in white and green; even the sun would be in the light green with the white and gold center.

7. Above this indicate a mountain, and the symbol or sign that is the symbol of Gemini - or the two-bodied figure, or united bodies as a figure (small), on the edge of this mountain. The vegetation here would be very verdant, in the central portion; this shading off to the left in that as of [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], - or [[Crystal (Atlantean)|the crystal]], or an obelisk with [[Crystal (Atlantean)|the crystal]] in the top.  This, to be sure, would not be too large a figure; with many figures at worship about the light that comes from this obelisk.

8. On the right side would be the fields with laborers in chains or bonds.

9. This, to be sure, would indicate the period in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] when there was the separation of the sexes indicated among things, or the thought-figures or bodies; those that had caught the [[Visions|vision]] and those still kept in bondage.

10. Upon the left put the symbol of Mercury, - this in gold and green.  About same would be the sign or symbol of Aries - or the ram - four in number; small, and black.

11. Upon the right side put the symbol of Jupiter, - in purple and coral; while about same would be four symbols of Scorpio.

12. These indicate the place, the activities, and the manner in which these were applied in the experience.

13. Above this, extending almost across the whole of the drawing (which should be in vignette, and not encircled as the first), - indicate - in the left - the trek of a group or body of people, with a leader - with a staff in hand - leading peoples; with a few beasts of burden, - as the ass, the camel, and also elephants, but few in number of each of these - rather distributed through the group and not as the mere leaders or in the front of the caravan.  Then [[Great Pyramid|the pyramid]] would be indicated in the central portion, in sand, with the top not complete; for over this would be the light as of a flame.  In the right portion put the facade of [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice, indicating within at least three crude rock-built altars.  Upon same would be the fires builded, but not a sacrifice on either of these.  Also a little farther to the right of this figure would be [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful.  This would be - and upon the columns would be - the stars - seven, indicated by a facade of many columns, the central bearing the larger star the higher in color as well as in size.  And the entrance to same would be indicated by the white and green light that would appear from same.

14. In the sides from this indicate the heart on the left and [[Crucifixion|the cross]] on the right.  No characters, no symbols would be about either of these.  This would rather be indicated in the activities as experienced by the entity through that [[Egypt|Egyptian]] [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; indicating the entrance, the activity, and that attainment in the sacrifice and in the temple service; and the heart as the manner, [[Crucifixion|the cross]] as the attainment.

15. Above this the central figure, also as a vignette, would be a series, first, of terraces of rock - and the lame man upon same, with the light as from heaven above shining upon the figure; with the raven upon one ledge of the rock and two doves together upon another.  This should be as a central or larger figure, the center the rock and the man, though the small-sized figure of the man, but indicating the lameness.

16. Upon the left put the sign or symbol of Venus, with Pisces - four, black-faced, about same.

17. Upon the right side put Uranus; part gold, part green, the gold the upper portion.  About same would be the symbol of Libra, four in number.

18. These would indicate the place of activity, and the manner in which there was the activity in the experience.

19. Above this, in the central portion to the left side put a ship or boat, with a very small mast; with at least three people in same, - one a man of middle age, one a younger man, and a girl.  Indicate them, by their dress, as orientals. Upon the right side of this, in the center, put the anchor, - this not too large yet balancing with the figure of the ship, and [[Crucifixion|the cross]] - still to the left.  The cross would be in gold, while the anchor would be in steel-gray edged with the lighter gray; with sufficient rope or cord to same to make a balanced figure.

20. This would indicate a period of the entity's activity, and of being anchored in the cross, through that period in Laodicea.

21. In the lefthand put the symbol of Mercury, and about same the symbols of Leo.

22. In the righthand put the symbol of Mars, and about same the symbols of Aries.

23. The figure above this would be a small ship, rowboat.  This, however, would be upon the right side.  The central figure would be a brothel, or as of a counter over which drink is dispersed, and the crowd about same - the dancing girl also indicated in the central figure.  And to the left would be indicated the river in which there is [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of the raft when crossing is attempted.

24. Upon the left side here put Neptune as the symbol, with the symbols of Gemini about same.

25. Upon the right put the earth sign, with the symbols about same of Libra.  All these symbols would be in black, but small.

26. As to the attaining of the entity:

27. In the top put the all-seeing eye, the singleness - the eye; - this rather large but balanced with the rest of the design or chart.  The clouds would be about same in pink or purple, the blue and green - all indicated in the lights as from the cloud; with the cross, and this leaning just a bit towards the right.  Here, under same, put the small white star.  In the edge of the clouds, extending below the eye, put the gold eight-pointed star.

28. These are the symbols, this is the [[Aura|aura]] chart, of this entity Edgar Cayce.

29. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Esther Wynne
"    " Ass'n file
