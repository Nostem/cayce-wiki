# TEXT OF READING 5090-1 F 40

**Reading [[5090-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1944  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 13219

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5090], new Associate Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., via the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Pa.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here and those disturbances which are causing mental anxiety to the body.

3. As we find, there is little or naught physically to prevent those normal activities, normal associations and results in those associations, as is the desire of the body.

4. But if the body would be the manifestation for a greater expression of the activities in this experience, do care for the body in such manners that it may be a channel for the manifestation of the glory of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] in this period of experience.  Yes, it is practical for the body to have conception and to bring forth a normal body, and if there will be through the period of gestation the practical care of the body, through the massage or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments which are necessary to keep body-development, body activities, a good, clean activity with the associations of circulation between liver and kidneys, we will have little or no trouble with the periods of activity.

5. There have been, of course, physical defects in the associations of the companion of the body.  But if there is the care and the attention this may be accomplished.

6. When there are those preparations for such, do this to insure conception, if this is desired at a special period (and those periods just before the menstrual period are the preferable for conception):  Put into a hot basin of water a sweet gum ball about the size of a small agate, or say the end of the thumb, in hot boiling water.

7. Sit over this for some ten minutes, and you needn't fear, there will be conception.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] of your [[Free Will|choice]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 7/19/43 Letter:  "I have just finished reading the book
> '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]', and I am so impressed with the story of
> your life...that immediately I felt you could help my
> husband.  I feel God is working through you...  So I am sure
> you can help us.  I am active church worker, teach and always
> have taken an active part in the missionary society.  Since
> last fall, I am a nurses' aid and give much time to the
> hospital, as I feel God wanted me to do it as my share toward
> the war effort.  My husband had a gall bladder operation 4
> years ago, and had his gall bladder removed and 2 black
> stones removed from the gall duct.  He recovered nicely from
> that operation.  A little over a year ago, he was hurt in a
> sleighing accident, had 5 ribs broken and was badly cut and
> bruised.  He recovered from that, and I felt he was going to
> be all right.  But there is something wrong, he just can't
> seem to regain his health; we have been to 5 doctors and they
> never find anything wrong.  He did give 4 pints of blood to
> the Red Cross and that caused his blood pressure to drop,
> which we are now trying to build up.  He likes to ride
> horseback and sometimes I feel he does too much of that.  I
> am careful of his diet and whatever it is he is not lazy,
> there is something holding him back.  I feel so confident
> since I know of your gift from God that you can diagnose his
> trouble and prescribe a cure...  I have worried for 15 years
> about him, and now that I read about your help, my hopes and
> faith have been renewed.  Prayerfully, I send this letter..."
> 
> B2. 8/17/43 Letter:  "In my first letter to you I asked for a
> reading to learn what causes are undermining my husband's
> health.  Since that time we have decided that I am to have a
> physical reading first, and then his reading will come next.
> My trouble is wanting children.  We have been married 15 1/2
> years.  My 4 brother-in-laws all have children, and they kid
> us and make us feel so depressed because we have no children.
> I have been to so many doctors and they say I am all right as
> far as they can see.  I do have an acid condition which I
> have been treating and this condition could keep me perhaps
> from having children.  Do you think at 39, I am too old to
> have a child?  I am 5 feet, 1 inch, and weigh 110 lbs. - have
> always been well.  I have prayed so much and can't help but
> think of God's promises to grant what we ask, as found in
> [[John (Apostle)|John]] 16:23 and Mark 11:24.  I suppose I lack the proper
> faith.  I prayed for years, then discouraged, stopped; then
> I'd pray again; but when I see women who have so many
> children, and single girls have babies, and here I am
> breaking my heart and there are many others like me...
> I think I have a right to be discouraged...  My husband was
> examined and the fault is not his.  The fault is mine, and I
> do not know what to do to correct whatever the trouble is..."
> 
> B3. 5/15/44 Telegram:  "Retel, my home address is ..., Pa."
> 
> B4. 5/16/44 On the basis of this correspondence, the following
> questions were formed by IMG:  "(1) Is it unwise to have
> children at my age?  (2) Does the acid condition, which I
> have been treating, prevent me from having children?  (3) Is
> it possible for me to have a child?"
