# TEXT OF READING 5019-1 M 34

**Reading [[5019-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/8/1944  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 13136

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5019], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [3275].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. 
Taken 3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.            [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, there are disturbances in this body. These are partially [[Karma|karmic]].  Thus there are some attitudes not merely to be assumed but to be definitely decided upon by the body itself, as to the activities towards divine influences in the body.

3. We find that these also bring a definite pathological condition.  The applications now being made will have little or no effect, even with this change of general attitude.

4. The attitude to be taken should be not merely to be good but to be good for something.  The expression of life is of the divine.  The divine is that influence or force called God. Use same, do not abuse same.

5. Pathologically there has been brought about the lacking of stamina in the nerves and muscular forces of the extremities. The greater portion is indicated in the limbs at present and in one of the arms.

6. Hence the necessity of adding to the system the mechanical as well as chemical forces that may assist the body, through that assimilated, to gain energy and strength in the nerves, muscles and tendons.  That there is becoming an atrophying of the nerves and muscular forces is because of the lack of there being carried through the system the properties for replenishing, by the lack of assimilation of properties needed to aid these muscular and nerve forces to reproduce themselves.

7. Have prepared the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to carry, by the radial activity of the low electrical forces, [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]] alternately; one to be used one day, the other the next day.

8. Attach the Appliance for thirty minutes each day.  The larger plate, through which the solution passes, would always be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus which, on this body, would be the width of three fingers from the navel center to the right and two fingers directly up from that point.

9. When using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (in the proportions of one ounce of commercial strength [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and two ounces of distilled water), make the attachment to the lumbar axis.

10. When using the [[Camphor|Camphor]] (three ounces of commercial strength [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]) make the attachment of the small plate at the brachial center, between the shoulders, or at the 1st and 2nd or between the 2nd and 3rd dorsal centers.

11. Alternate the attachments in this manner each day.

12. Each time when the appliance is removed, give the body a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with this combination of oils:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].....................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil....................2 ounces,
Lanolin (melted)............1/4 ounce.

13. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] these oils thoroughly into the spinal areas, especially along the brachial center and the lumbar axis, and to the sciatic centers and to the limbs.  Commence at the toes and gradually work up.  Take at least half an hour to three-quarters of an hour each day to give this gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

14. Prepare two solutions to be taken internally, but to be kept separate until dosage is to be taken:

15. One solution would be one grain of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to one ounce of distilled water.

16. The other solution would be two grains of Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to one ounce of distilled water.

17. When the dosage is to be taken, it would be every third day, put one drop of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] and one drop of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution in half a glass of water, stir and drink immediately.  Use a medicine dropper for this, not just guess at it.

18. Do these, keeping away from those things that carry any form of alcohol or too great quantities of starches.

19. These do and we may bring better conditions for the body.

20. Do the first things first.  Begin with reading [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5 and Deuteronomy 30.  Apply these to self.

21. Be persistent, be consistent.  For here you have the opportunity for help if you'll use it.

22. We are through with this reading.*

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing



* [Note:  In most cases when EC reached the end of a reading he would say:  "Ready for questions".  In this case however, he did not do so.]

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 11/1/43 Letter:  "...A little over a year ago I began limping
> and after visiting our family physician, he sent me to
> Neurological Institute for observation.  After three weeks
> there they diagnosed my case as Myeloradiculitis.  They
> prescribed Diathermy and Vitamin B. therapy.  This did
> not help me.  My condition grew worse and I then visited a
> neurologist who recommended that I go to Mt. Sinai Hospital
> for observation.  At Mt. Sinai they diagnosed my case as
> Amyotropic Lateral Sclerosis and prescribed Vitamin E.  I
> have been taking Vitamin E and still I have become worse.  I
> have also been to chiropractors and osteopaths, and they have
> not done anything for me.  As things are today, the muscle
> atrophy has progressed to the point where I can no longer
> walk without the aid of crutches, and have to be helped up
> from a sitting position and placed on the crutches.  Even
> then walking is very slow and unsteady.  One of my arms is
> beginning to feel the effects of atrophy.  It is weak and
> it takes quite an effort to raise it.  This atrophy is
> accompanied by fibrillations.  I have told you all I can in
> a letter and as I explained before, not knowing the working
> of your organization, I must wait for your answer with your
> suggestions..."
> 
> B2. 11/12/43 Letter:  "I am writing to you as you requested in
> our telephone conversation this morning.  Just to recall to
> your mind whom I am, I was referred to you by Mr. [3275].
> Would you please try to do something for me?"
> 
> B3. 3/18/44 Letter:  "...(1) What is the cause of my illness?
> (2) Will my present treatment help?  (3) What therapy do you
> suggest?  (4) Would a change of climate be beneficial to me?
> If there are any further instructions please let me know, so
> that my reading will be clear."
