# TEXT OF READING 294-177 M 57

**Reading [[294-177]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/3/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2136

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1934, in accordance with GC''s health suggestion for EC''s health, following 625-1.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Suggestion periodically for EC's welfare)

1. EC:  Yes, as we find, there is inflammation in the mucous membranes of the nasal cavities or passages, that makes for pressure which prevents the proper circulation through the head.  This forms phlegm in such measures as to produce stagnations in portions of the throat, and especially in those areas just above the entrance to the lung - or to the esophagus.  This needs, then, local applications with spray, or such measures as to not only reduce this but to cleanse same; such as would be prepared in this manner:

To 1 ounce of distilled water, add:

Tincture of Benzoin...................20 minims,
Eucalyptol.............................5 minims,
Tolu in solution......................20 minims.

2. Use this in a spray; keeping the eliminations through the alimentary canal, so that the circulation may be helped and aided throughout; and we will find that we will remove much of this disorder and disturbance through the body.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is the nature's salts the body has been taking good for the eliminations?
(A)  Very good.

5. (Q)  Any other advice for the body?
(A)  This we would do, and keep normal activities - and it'll be very good.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Work book "   " Ass'n file [On awakening and hearing the above read, Edgar Cayce said nothing was wrong with his nasal passages.  However, after preparing the spray and using it he discovered there was a soreness there.  His headaches were stopped almost immediately after beginning use of spray.]
