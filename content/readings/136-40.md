# TEXT OF READING 136-40 F 21 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-40]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/12/1926  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 347

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by her husband, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of New Jersey, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, you will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind, [136].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come in those ways and manners as have been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

3. For, as we find, each and every individual, who is possessed of the faculty of visioning the various conditions of experiences which pass through the various consciousnesses of the individual, is capable also of gaining those lessons and truths from same, which are as experiences or lessons, or the truth as shown to the individual through the various phases of its consciousness.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  Monday night, June 28, or Tuesday morning, June 29, 1926.  "I had some inner trouble that prevented my having a baby.  The doctors told me that to have it corrected would necessitate a slight operation.  I objected on the grounds that I couldn't take ether.  'O yes you can,' they replied, and I found myself on the operating table being given the anaesthetic.  Slowly I felt myself losing consciousness under this influence.  I felt their fingers grow lighter in touch and their voices seem further and further away.  I was unconscious of myself losing consciousness and of the latter's consummation.  Then I came to - regained consciousness, the operating having been done.  I mentioned to the nurse that I supposed now I could not have a baby. She replied that I could - very soon.  I wanted to get up and go out."
(A)  In this there is seen that condition which has been as a weight upon the mind of the individual.  And, as is seen in the [[Visions|vision]], those conditions arise as have been told [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as regarding itself; and the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] of the individual is giving to the body-conscious that experience of the condition through which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] passes to attain that condition which is, or becomes, as a matter of will with the entity, in bringing about the consummation of those conditions which are as a matter of will.  For we find in this there is the lesson seen as to that which the entity may reach through will's forces, and that which is necessary for the consummation of physical conditions for same to pass through that physical being necessary to become a portion of the material consciousness of the physical world.  And as these conditions pass, these are as the development of the mental forces of the entity, rather than of physical conditions which exist in the body.  And, as is seen, these may, and must, pass through that same mental change as the physical being would, to reach that condition in the material world for the consummation of those conditions as set for the individual entity in its at-[[Oneness|oneness]] to be able to carry out that which is set and that HAS BEEN GIVEN should be for the entity.

Then it is the mental forces that must be set, or must be operated upon, as it were, rather than the physical being. Do that.

5. We are through.
