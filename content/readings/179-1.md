# TEXT OF READING 179-1 F 13

**Reading [[179-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/19/1926  Sex: F  Age: 13  ReadingID: 685

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 19th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by her sister, Miss [...], and her mother. Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               On a farm near 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Georgia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Now, we find there are those abnormal conditions with this body, and in the present condition may be corrected, with care - persistent care.  In the existent condition there are are specific troubles, and these troubles, in the diffused manner in which they affect the nerve system, bring to the muscular forces and brain centers a reaction that is improper and abnormal for the body.

3. In the first condition, we find there are some prenatal [[[Karma|karmic]]?] conditions to be considered, and other conditions as were produced by physical conditions as were seen in the body at the time of birth.  These were those conditions, for the pressure as produced in the presentation brought to certain cervicals that non-alignment which produces a pressure, not so much on the cerebrospinal cord as on that of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] situated at the base of the brain.  This, then, is the cause of the character of repression and the variation in their severity, and the apparent cause of that as brings about the cycle of the [[Vibration|vibration]] for, as we see, the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] is affected.  This affects directly that condition as produced in the cervical and in the lumbar as was experienced at the time of birth, with the umbilicus or navel center, and this cold, congestion, this improper [[Healing|healing]] of pressures through the central brain of body, that causes the disturbance, see?

4. Then, to bring about the normal forces for the body will consist in those of CONSISTENT keeping up of these conditions as given, and of the changes as will be necessary as conditions develop, for the body will develop normally, and these will come farther and farther apart, until they will be very rare, if ever re-occurring in the system.

5. First, through the manipulation, OSTEOPATHICALLY given, correct those conditions in the cervical region, aligning same in reference to the solar plexus vertebras, as well as the upper dorsal and rest of the cervical, especially that condition in the 1st and 2nd cervical, that in the 11th and 12th dorsal.  These need be given once or twice each week, but kept for at least fifty-six (56) such adjustments and treatments.

6. Taking during that period also, and longer if found necessary, those properties as found in Mayapple Bitters - Daniels' [[[John (Apostle)|John]] B. Daniels'] Mayapple Bitters [Maypop Bitters?], see?  Take half a teaspoonful, and this should be taken after each meal, or before each meal should the body desire to make the change - at least this is to give, with the foods that are taken, sufficient of nerve nutriment to overcome that condition as is seen by cellular rebuildings that have to do with the glands, and especially of the pineal gland.  [GD's note:  3/9/26 EC's letter under 3788-2 says Maypop Bitters is manufactured by [[John (Apostle)|John]] B. Daniels, Atlanta, Ga.]

7. Taking also during this same period - though this should be begun about fourteen (14) days after the treatments and medicinal properties are given - those [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the umbilicus - see, as the negative pole, and through which the [[Vibration|vibration]] of those properties as will be taken in the system - that vibration from the Radio-Active Appliance, using the [[Iodine|iodine]] solution as the vibration for the system, as the body is near to that development as comes to one of its age - sex, see?  and this will also produce the equilibrium necessary in the system, see?  The strength of the solution - using TINCTURE [[Iodine|Iodine]], one (1) ounce, and to that add one (1) ounce pure grain alcohol, see?  using then a two (2) ounce vial for the anode through which the vibration goes to the umbilicus.  This would be placed at the right upper and right side one time, the upper and left, see, the next time.  The other anode, or positive, that would be attached first, would be one time to the ankle, the next time to the wrist, and from different sides of the body each time, see?  If begun with that on the right ankle, then the next day (for it would be given an hour each day, as the body lies down to rest, see?) it would be to the left wrist, the next day to the opposite ankle, next day to the opposite wrist - or to wrist first, the ankle next, you see - these changed about.

8. When this has been taken and followed out for at least sixty to ninety (60 to 90) days, we would give the changes as are seen, for we will find this will prove beneficial in EVERY way to the mental and normal physical development of the body.

9. Do that.

10. (Q)  What should the anode be made of that is used in the [[Iodine|iodine]]?  [See [1800] series.]
(A)  Lead, as in each and every one.  [[Germany|German]] silver or nickel for those as to be attached to the wrist and ankle, and to the umbilicus - large plate for the umbilicus, see, as has been given.  These kept polished, and the connections in proper order - using plenty of ice in the jar or crock, see - these should never be used in any except crock or glass, for the ice and anode, see?

11. We are through for the present.
