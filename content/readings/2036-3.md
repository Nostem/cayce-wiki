# TEXT OF READING 2036-3 F 3

**Reading [[2036-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/11/1927  Sex: F  Age: 3  ReadingID: 8581

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [1690].  Involuntary.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ky.

1. In the condition as regarding the child, [2036], here we find the physical conditions are in that state wherein temperature in the hepatic circulation is kept above the normal.

2. We would give broken doses of Castoria for at least two (2) days, until the system is thoroughly cleansed.

3. Then prepare this as a tonic for the body, to clarify and cleanse the blood:

4. To one-half (1/2) gallon of distilled water, add:

Dogwood Bark............. 1 ounce,
Prickly Ash Bark......... 1 ounce,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root Bark...... 1 ounce.

5. Reduce this by simmering (not boiling) to  one-half (1/2) the quantity.  Then cut two (2) drams Balsam of Tolu in two (2) ounces of grain alcohol, and add to solution.

6. Shake solution well together before each dose is taken, which should be half teaspoonful four (4) times each day.

7. Let the child be more regular in its meals.

8. We are through for the present.
