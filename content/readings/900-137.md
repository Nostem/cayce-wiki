# TEXT OF READING 900-137 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-137]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/8/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5340

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the [[Dreams|dreams]] that come to this body from time to time.  These, as we see, are in the various forms as have been given for the body and may be used in the manner as has been given may be used by the mind, body, for the good of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Voice:  "Now is the time to sell stocks."
(A)  This, as we see, the co-relation of cosmic forces with that of the mental, and this brings then to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that instruction as is seen through the experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and should be acted upon in the manner as given. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Does this mean sell out the rest Pan Pet B, not waiting for further rise?
(A)  Sell out all stocks as are held, see?  at present, for the lower level.  Then buy in at the lower level, including, as has been seen and given, those of Southern Railway and of L & N stocks, see?  for all we find will be on the temporary decline.  While with those Railroad stocks and securities, or bonds, we will find these will fluctuate, and after reaching the low levels, in the latter portion of present month, begin the steady rise again, see?  That in L & N, as seen, will have more change, yet in the mergers, or the submerging of the conditions, as given, will bring the greater changes and the higher values, see?

5. (Q)  As given, Southern Railway Common Stocks is going to 180.  If this should now be sold for a downward move, at what price should it be re-purchased?  This question is asked in order to co-relate between the two given, namely:  Southern going to 180 and "Now is the time to sell stocks." (A)  That as has been given.  The Southern would be a buy at 107, also at 104.  While the changes as we see with the general movement is down, the entity, as has been given in others, may take advantage of those variations in short numbers, using, then, the information in the way and manner as has been said that the entity may bring the good to self, rather than those detrimental conditions, by mis-application of the truths as are being set and experienced by entity in the study of spiritual and physical phenomena in a material world.  This does not indicate that it will not go to the higher point.  That comes in another cycle, see?  cycle means period of time.  Take advantage of those conditions at present, see?  These will be short, yet worthy of consideration, that the entity may gain the full understanding of the experienced conditions as are being seen through the various phases of laws governing same. Through these may be gained the knowledge of the cosmic forces, spiritual forces, spiritual laws, spiritual elements, pertaining to the physical conditions, EVEN stocks and their quotations.
