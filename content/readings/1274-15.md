# TEXT OF READING 1274-15

**Reading [[1274-15]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/22/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6880

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of March, 1928.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M. 
[Fifteenth reading given on Broad Creek Channel treasure 
Monroe Country, [[Florida|Florida]].  On 2/24/32 reading was loaned to 
Mr. [943] by HLC and not returned.]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/22/28 EC's letter to Mr. [943]:
> 
> My dear Mr. [943] -
> 
> Yours of the 12th came just at my return from a business
> trip to [[Chicago|Chicago]].  Hence the delay in answering.  Also some
> inconvenience by my not feeling equal to getting a reading,
> even when we had appointments.
> 
> Now, I'm sure from what you say, those associated with you
> have not only become discouraged but rather disgusted at the
> whole undertaking, and of course - ordinarily looking at it -
> we couldn't blame them at all.  It has apparently been a wild
> goose chase - time and money badly spent - inconveniences
> indured, all to no good - chasing a rainbow!
> 
> We tried to present this in a way that would give us all of
> the information.  For some reason, after that we have here
> was given, it cut off without giving an opportunity for
> questions to be asked.
> 
> As to whether you will be able to use this or not, I don't
> know.  I really don't know how to advise you about it.  Very
> naturally, I believe it must BE there - how it is, or why it
> is, we haven't been able to locate it - I don't understand.
> Why it won't take it up and give it to you in more definite
> terms, why it insists that you must work out what it has
> already given you, I don't know - unless it is that it
> is that it is proving, testing him, each individual that
> approaches the phenomena - testing him in his own way of
> thinking, and his own mode of action.
> 
> I know you feel discouraged about it - and I don't blame
> you.  It may be possible that its the best thing to give it
> up for the time being, or to study it over, arrive at some
> way of working it out - then give it one more fair [[Trial of Jesus|trial]].
> 
> At least let us hear from you.  We are disappointed also.
> Disappointed for your sake, as much or more than for our own.
> Please remember us very kindly to Mrs. [5571] and [4477],
> and with kindest personal regards to yourself.
> 
> Sincerely, [EC:GD]
> 
> R2. 3/26/28 [943]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]]
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> 115 West 35th Street,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Your letter and reading received with reference to Treasure
> at Broad Creek Channel and it seems from the report that
> something is wrong with individuals which I have interested
> in trying to locate same from time to time.  Now Mr. Cayce
> if you will help me by giving me the necessary readings as
> we need them I will try to locate the treasure by myself.
> I can get to Key Largo and it cost me about two dollars for
> a boat from there to the the location and I will go prepared
> to spend about one to two weeks.  I feel like you do about
> the matter - I believe it is there and am willing to do what
> the forces desires to be done before exact location is given
> and if found to dispose of the treasure as directed by the
> forces.
> 
> Suppose you give another reading with the following
> questions:
> 
> "You have before you the buried treasure located near Broad
> Creek Channel, Monroe County, [[Florida|Florida]], and [943] at ...
> Street, Miami, Florida, and much information has been given
> concerning the treasure to the above party together with
> others trying locate it.  The mind and body of [943] will
> seek the treasure without the assistance of any individual
> or individuals and the information as hereto given as to
> location has been followed as interpreted by the mind of
> [943].  Now, will the forces give definite information as
> to the location of treasure so that the mind of [943] may
> understand same.  Give definite instruction to [943] so
> that he may do what is necessary to prepare himself or
> proceed in locating the treasure as above mentioned."
> 
> Please let me have this reading by this week end if possible
> Mr. Cayce, as time is going fast and I want to do this one
> thing if it is possible.
> 
> With kindest personal regards to you and family and Miss
> Davis,
> 
> Sincerely yours, [943]
> 
> R3. 4/4/28 See 1274-16.
> 
> R4. 2/24/32 Reading 1274-15 loaned to Mr. [943] by HLC - not
> returned.
