# TEXT OF READING 569-2 F 45

**Reading [[569-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/13/1925  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 4010

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 13th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [569].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                      ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them.

2. EC:  Now, we find the general conditions and the specific troubles in the body somewhat improved from that we have had here before.  The treatments as are being accorded the eye proper are showing some results.  While this is not as fast as it would be under conditions where eliminations were increased, as were given for the manipulations to be given, as treatments were accorded, yet these we see will bring better conditions for the body, as will also the glasses when adjusted properly for the body.  While these bring some distress to the body, will build to that necessary for the condition, for we have the body on the improve at the present.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What causes the sharp pains under left arm at times, also the hard lump in the left breast [See 569-2, Par. R1], and what would relieve same?
(A)  This, we see, is produced from obstructions in the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], this produced by clogging of tissue.  This at the present time is only tissue that has been surrounded by the system attempting to eliminate same.  The manipulation as was given will assist in the elimination of this condition, with the general building up of the system.  This should, however, not be bruised or hindered in any manner in the manipulations, nor should the condition be kept from eliminating, rather causing the system to absorb same.  It also would assist the body to wear constantly the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator.  Let this be attached about the body near the 5th and 6th dorsal.  [See 569-8 on 7/15/26 asking again about breast lump.]

4. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the improvement of this body?
(A)  Keep the eliminations as near normal as possible.  Do not eat meats, rather vegetable and fruits and cereals, and those properties that make for rebuilding tissue, other than the meat properties.

5. (Q)  Are the glasses correct for the eyes?
(A)  Glasses are very good.  They were built, as [you in 569-1] were given, to the eyes.  [GD's note:  By Dr. Hugh C. Beazley, eye specialist.]

6. We are through with this reading.
