# TEXT OF READING 3480-2 M 49

**Reading [[3480-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/28/1944  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10960

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3480], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time. ..., Montana.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3480], this we have had before.

3. There have been improvements and there have been retardments. Some of those conditions which were the primary causes have been helped but there are other disturbances, as with the digestive system and the slowed circulation.

4. Changes in the environments would be well, yet these are not the primary causes.  We would find, if there would be the leaving off of some of the diets which have become obnoxious, then the purifying of the alimentary canal, the setting up of better drainages from the head and the soft tissue in ear, nose, throat and stimulating the eliminations, it will be the better manner in which to control these disturbances.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Can anything be done to relieve the nasal catarrh?
(A)  Do these which have been indicated.  Use the inhalant which may be outlined for the body in this:  Prepare a large-mouth bottle.  Put two vents into the opening of the bottle in which solution is put.  Leave a two ounce space, at least, above solution in container.  Add the following ingredients in the order named:

Pure Grain Alcohol................4 ounces,
Oil of Eucalyptus................20 drops,
Rectified Oil of Turp.............5 drops,
Tincture of Benzoin..............15 drops,
Oil of Pine Needles..............10 drops,
Tolu in Solution..................5 drops.

Shake the solution, then removing the cork from vents inhale deeply into the nasal passages - not too much in the beginning, just once or twice.  Then inhale into the throat.

But do set up better eliminations with [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.

7. (Q)  Have the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] been sufficient and frequent enough to clear up all mucus?
(A)  We would not imply such if these had been correctly given.  Do have sufficient to remove the mucus, which causes the disturbance.  This should be observed in the periods of giving [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

8. (Q)  Am I getting enough valuable food elements?
(A)  Enough valuable food elements if they are kept balanced properly.  Do have more of the B-1 and E.  These may be supplemented in the activities in Zyrone and in wheat germ oil.

9. (Q)  Would any particular climate be recommended?
(A)  [[Arizona|Arizona]], near [[Arizona|Tucson]].

10. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do keep the mental being in constructive activities.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See illustration [which was enclosed] of type bottle to be prepared for the inhalant.

Zyrone is sold by The Chattanooga Medicine Co., Chattanooga, Tenn., and sells for $1 a bottle.)

> accurately.  I'm sure that part of it is accurate and

> suffering, one very seriously.  In closing I want to say that
