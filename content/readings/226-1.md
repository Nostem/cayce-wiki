# TEXT OF READING 226-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[226-1]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/9/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1051

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Exchange Hotel, Montgomery, [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 9th day of November, 1922, in accordance with request made by the wife of Mr. [226].

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno. Mr. [226]''s wife, and Dr. Elise L. Stone.]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have had this body before.  This body is better than when we had it before, you see.  When we follow out more of the conditions as we have given we will find we will create within the system those elements to produce perfect elimination as far as it is possible to create within this body.

2. Take these properties into the system; in this manner as we have given:  Clary flower or sage, six ounces - put into a half gallon of rain or distilled water and reduce by simmering, not boiling, to one quart.  Strain and add to this, six ounces gin, then four ounces of sugar dissolved, and while warm add one ounce grain alcohol with fifteen grains of Ambergris dissolved in it, then add this to the other properties while warm.  When cold, add three drams of cinnamon, preferably in the stick, not ground, you see.  The dose of this would be a spoonful, tablespoon, thirty minutes before each meal.  Do that.  Prepare in the manner as we have given.

3. (Q)  What is the trouble with this body's right hand?
(A)  The condition from the nerve forces, and the condition from the brachial plexus to these forces.

4. (Q)  Do the teeth contribute to the cause of ill health to this body?  If so, what will be the remedy?
(A)  No.

5. (Q)  Why did the other medicine sour?
(A)  Not prepared in the manner as was given here.  These are; not what was left out of the other medicine.  Follow these.  Do not take when sour.  We are through.
