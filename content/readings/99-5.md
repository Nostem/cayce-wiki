# TEXT OF READING 99-5 M 38

**Reading [[99-5]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/7/1930  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 224

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - [99].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Wigal, and Mr. [99] and wife Mrs. [98].]

Time of Reading                           ... Drug Co., 
12:05 P. M. Noon Eastern Standard Time.   [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  In some respects we find the conditions have been allowed to become as CLOGGING for the system, especially as related to those of the elimination in the system.  As has been indicated, the great needs of this body are to KEEP the COORDINATION in the eliminating systems.  As is seen, in all bodies, not ALL eliminations are carried on THROUGH the alimentary canal - but the respiratory system, the pulmonaries, the skin texture itself, the kidneys, AND the alimentary canal, carry off the drosses.  True, that nearly fifty percent is eliminated through the activity of the hepatic circulation, but hepatic circulation - while OF the liver and kidneys - may throw into the pulmonaries, or the lymphatic circulation, that which should have been carried off in other channels.  So, the exercising of that necessary in the system to keep the coordinations is ESPECIALLY necessary when there has been existent in the system those tendencies to make for hardships to the extremities, through the changing of the circulation, through pressures as have been existent in system, so as to cause IMPULSES that carry too much circulation to the throat, head and lungs - not sufficient of circulation to the kidneys and the lower extremities.  Where segregations have existed, and do exist in lower limbs - these need, necessarily, those of local attention - but that as will COORDINATE with the eliminations through alimentary canal, and eliminations through the respiratory system, and the PULMONARIES.

2. Then, we would take those properties that keep the alimentary canal active.  Such are found in those as have been used, yet we would add more OF those of the compounds of the Sarsaparilla - at least half an ounce more in the double quantity of those of the potashes and of the salts, as is found in those of the Toris Compound.

3. We would also take more of those - that is, not increase in quantity - but just keep it up - those as act as an eliminant to the alimentary canal, in those of the salts water, or Pluto water, or of Honeyida water - which is the better. With this, we would take occasionally small quantities - eight to ten drops - of an INTESTINAL antiseptic, such as is seen in that of Glyco, or that as is found in any of those that are the ALKALIN reaction.  Such as may be seen in forms of those of the pepsins, that keep the system the more alkalin in the stomach's reaction proper.

4. Bathe the feet and lower limbs - not just to ankle, but to the KNEES - for the centers from which the RADIATION comes is from those in the actions UNDER the knees, and to the feet themselves - in a SATURATED solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]]; bathing same afterwards with that of the oil and myrrh - heating the oil and adding the myrrh, about a tablespoonful of each. This massaged thoroughly into the limbs - that is, from the knee to the feet, and over the feet; and treatments of those natures, osteopathically given, that form for the coordination of the eliminating systems.

5. In the diet, beware of meats - especially of red meats. Those of the vegetable - those of even more starches may be better taken than to much of that, that must form acid - and which produce pressure.  Ready for questions.

6. That is all the questions.

7. Do that.  We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Self
