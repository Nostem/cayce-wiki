# TEXT OF READING 1700-2 M 17

**Reading [[1700-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/12/1944  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 7844

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by Mother - Mrs. [4245], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Cal.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of the body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1700], we have had this in various ways before.

3. As we find, there are those tendencies and those disturbances which may become very serious to the better welfare physically of this body, unless something is done about same.  These, as we find, are of a complex nature. There has been continued for some periods the effects of a flu germ.  These have caused through the lymph circulation, as through lungs, nasal passages, abdominal areas, the reduction in the numbers of the leucocytes of the body. These have allowed soft tissue to become lacking in resilience or vitality in same, and hence the tendencies for the palate of the mouth to produce irritation, and there has been brought about a bronchial and a lung disturbance.

4. First, in making applications for helpfulness:  In the crown of the head pull up a tuft of hair, tied tight until it kind of hurts, but keep it there for at least two or three days; not a great quantity, but right in the center, the top of the head.

5. Begin and give the body gentle [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments with special reference to the brachial centers, with the 3rd cervical, 9th dorsal and throughout the lumbar and sacral areas.  Give them every other day, a gentle relaxing treatment; not stimulating, but relaxing.

6. Begin and take internally Acigest each day for at least five days, leave off then for a week, and then begin and take every other day a teaspoonful in a glass of raw milk, or that which is homogenized.  This preferably should be as fresh as possible, even with the heat of the animal in same would be the more preferable.  Take this of evening, at the evening meal.

7. During the noon meal, or lunch meal, take a small amount of [[Calcios|Calcios]], a quantity thinly spread on a whole wheat wafer.

8. Do these as has been indicated.  Take the [[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest on separate days, see?  But in the beginning, see, first five days take them the same day, each day.  But one at the noon meal, the other at the evening meal.

9. Keep in the open, though not too much of the sun in the midday, see?  Do that.

10. We will find better conditions for this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two Copies to mother - Air Mail - One for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] Copy to Ass'n file "   for Indexing

(6/12/44 GD's note:  Please let us have the name and address of the one [[[Osteopathy|osteopath]]] you choose.

[[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest are distributed by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Attention:  Mr. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., [[New York City|New York]].  Calcios is a syrupy paste containing free calcium easily assimilated and sells for $2.50 per jar. Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated and sells for $1.00 per jar.)
