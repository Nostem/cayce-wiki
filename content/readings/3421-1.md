# TEXT OF READING 3421-1 F 39 (Housewife, Theosophist)

**Reading [[3421-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/27/1943  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 10884

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3421], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Born September 2, 1903, in Missoula, Montana. 
Time of Reading                               ... St., 
11:25 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Montana.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [3421], at home ... St., ..., Montana, who seeks a Mental and Spiritual Reading, with special regard to the creature which she feels is troubling her.  And you will give the information which will be most helpful at this time, with advice and counsel, answering questions which she has submitted as I ask them:

2. EC:  [After repeating suggestion in an undertone]  Yes, we have the body here, [3421].

3. In giving that which as we find will be of help, the pathological as well as psychological conditions must be considered with the mental conditions of the body.

4. We find that there has been the opening of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|Lyden]] (Leydig?) [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], so that the kundaline forces move along the spine to the various centers that open with this attitude, or with these activities of the mental and spiritual forces of the body - much in the same manner as might be illustrated in the foetus that forms from conception.  These naturally take form.  Here these take form, for they have not in their inception been put to a definite use.

5. The psychological reaction is much like that as may be illustrated in one gaining much knowledge without making practical application of it.  It then forms its own concepts.

6. Now we combine these two and we have that indicated here as a possession of the body; gnawing, as it were, on all of the [[Seven Churches|seven centers]] of the body, causing the inability for rest or even a concerted activity - unless the body finds itself needed for someone else.  Then the body finds, as this occurs, the disturbance is retarded or fades - in the abilities of the body to exercise itself in help for others.

7. Pathologically, we may find this center in the reproductive [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] or activity of the body - the ovarian activity.  Here apply an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], and then apply heat. This combination may be put on a flannel cloth, or the cloth saturated with it and applied to the area.  This will tend to segregate and separate the disturbance.

8. Also corrections osteopathically are necessary, where there is a subluxation in the tip end of the coccyx area.  This was brought about at a period when there was activity in these areas, and it has remained.  Hence the distress easily enters, or even when the body sleeps at times, it becomes possessed.  When it is in deep thought it will become possessed.  This varies according to the physical activities of the body; that is, as to whether or not the body is active in spiritually constructive work.  Overactivity physically may bring it on.  Overactivity mentally may bring it on. Highly emotional activities, as in sex, may bring on the conditions.

9. All of these indicate, then, the pathological and psychological conditions.

10. When the corrections have been made osteopathically (not before), begin applying the low form of electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body - through the use of the Radio-Active Appliance.

11. And use that period for the [[Meditation|meditation]].  What should be the trend of the [[Meditation|meditation]] at such times?  [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5, [[John (Apostle)|John]] 14:2, 3, 4.  These are one, applied to self.

12. Do these.  Be consistent, be persistent.  Use the Appliance each day for an hour.  In the beginning surround self with that which is the heart and [[Soul|soul]] of [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5.  For there is the hope.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Two Copies to Self (one for the osteoath - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/6/43 Letter:  "A week or 2 ago, I ordered some books from
> The Theosophical Society, and they enclosed a review on
> '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]'.  I was fascinated by the reports of your
> [[Healing|healing]], and have ordered the book.  But this is why I am
> taking the liberty of writing to you.  For more than 22 years
> I have been constantly troubled by an elemental thing.  It
> troubles me at night, and oh, I have had so little sleep all
> these years.  It has been seen by 3 different people with
> [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyant]] [[Visions|vision]], but not one has been able to help me or
> to tell me how I may be freed from this creature.  I and my
> husband [3480], and family, have all pursued religious
> theosophy in hopes that by self purification I could lift
> myself out of this.  We have always held hopes of gaining
> freedom.  I will soon be 40 years old.  One very dear friend,
> Father T.E.M. by name, who is quite [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyant]] and a liberal
> Catholic priest, read the review of this book and has urged
> me to write to you.  Would it be possible to advise or help
> through mail?...  My family and friends are ready to make any
> sacrifice to gain my freedom...  Will you be kind enough to
> give me your honest opinion?..."
> 
> B2. 6/25/43 Letter:  "Your prompt letter was received last week
> and today the data.  I have read it over, and am answering
> immediately...  I cannot check the kind of reading, for I
> don't know.  Perhaps you can tell best what my case
> requires...  I have been troubled violently by an elemental
> thing about 23 years.  It has been constant at night and I
> have had so very little sleep.  For several months now, I
> average 1, 2 or 3 hours of broken sleep a night, and the rest
> of the time is mostly spent in walking the floor, exercising
> and [[Prayer|praying]].  Every few years, I have gone through a period
> that has been almost unbearable.  And I am going thru that
> now.  If it hadn't been for the continued [[Prayer|prayers]] of my
> family and friends, I couldn't possibly have survived.  Also
> my own spiritual efforts have helped me through this.  It
> seems that when I have these extremely bad periods, it has
> always taken someone new to help me out.
> 
> "I have traveled to Rochester, Minn. to the clinics, and gone
> to dozens of doctors.  Twice we went to ..., S.D. to a doctor
> there who is a [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyant]], and wise enough to help me.  We
> went to Calif. for help and to Wash., and had numerous Truth
> Teachers and the [[Healing|healing]] depts. of the Theosophical Society,
> Unity and the Rosicrucians work on me, without results.  It
> is so stubborn, we wonder what the source really is.  Several
> have seen the creature, and it resists [[Prayer|prayer]] but won't let
> go.  Now my [[Karma|karma]] might possibly be ripe and ready to be
> finished, and it might possibly be that you are to be the
> channel, Mr. Cayce.  It is almost too much to hope for, yet
> we still live in hopes that at some time I will be freed
> again and have rest, sleep and live a normal life...  The
> [[Physical Body|physical body]] and etheric are affected, and I just don't know
> how much the astral and mental are affected.  Our good
> clairvoyant friend could advise me, but I am so eager to send
> my letter off, I won't take time to write him, and will leave
> it to you to decide.  My husband will also send for a
> reading, for his [[Karma|karmic debt]] is as heavy as mine, except it
> is purely physical, leakage of the heart and head infection.
> I shall try to cooperate in every way..."
> 
> B3. 7/15/43 Letter:  "...We have suspected my trouble to be a
> case of vampirism and will be oh, so grateful to know the
> source and how to counteract it.  Your life story in 'There
> Is A River' impressed me greatly..."
> 
> B4. 8/2/43 Letter:  "...I am enclosing some questions for my
> reading, and my husband's application blank.  If I continue
> at the rate I am now, without any interference, I won't be
> able to stand it until Nov. 1st, for my appointment.  I am
> reaching a point of desperation, and haven't much to work on.
> The things I have used in the past to give me relief, now
> fail.  Electricity applied to the hands and feet have given
> at least temporary relief in the past, as Sloan's Liniment
> and [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] baths, but now nothing does.  My body and
> mind are so tired I can't do the spiritual work I have done
> in the past by walking the floor all night long and saying
> affirmations and trying to become more and more aware of my
> own Christ self.  You see, it 'tackles' my legs and
> arms, mostly in the joints but sometimes in the fleshy part,
> and not frequently in the solar plexus and lungs when there
> is an opening from a cold in the chest, etc.  It not only
> jerks so violently, it tears my body to pieces unless I keep
> moving and exercising, the reaction of the nerves is
> maddening.  I can't stand it unless I vigorously exercise and
> move.  So I walk all night long until my body is so weary I
> can't go any longer, and I'm mentally a blank and fall asleep
> sometimes standing up, and the minute I sit, only have to get
> up and move on.  It's a most peculiar case, most unusual.  It
> goes wild when we pray and work, and becomes even more
> violent.  My sleep has averaged for some time now, 1, 2 or 3
> hours a night, and that only from the most desperate fighting
> and walking, only to be violently awakened again at the end
> of that period.  And I haven't been able to rest days either,
> it's constant.  My poor husband, who isn't at all well, is
> straining himself and working so hard, and he is very
> powerful spiritually.  My mother and father too, work daily,
> and our dear Father T.E.M. is very powerful.  He is very
> close to the masters.  When he comes, this creature is more
> violent.  It has been seen by him and 2 others, a huge
> octopus.  Of course, I realize that nothing matters except
> our spiritual progress and I can truthfully say that we have
> all made tremendous strides from the effort put forth because
> of this.  So it definitely hasn't been in vain.  And I also
> realize the [[Karma|karmic debt]] which I and my loved ones have to pay
> off to the last farthing.  But I understand that every effort
> should be made to face ourselves.  I have no fear of death...
> 
> In fact, I would welcome the peace of it after what I've gone
> thru for 22 years.  I hate to be 'licked' for my family's
> sake...  I don't expect you to give me an appointment by
> taking it from someone else who is equally as important and
> entitled to his own...  (1) Exactly what is troubling me?
> (2) Is it prompted by someone else, or on its own?  (3) What
> satisfaction does it get from me?  (4) What did I do in past
> [[Incarnation|incarnations]] to cause this?  (5) What can I do to permanently
> free myself from this creature, and from everything else of
> an undesirable nature?  (6) Is there a solution or formula
> that can be used for the helping of others generally in such
> cases?  (7) To what should I devote myself in the future for
> my spiritual improvement to be of more service in the world?"
