# TEXT OF READING 264-36 F 43

**Reading [[264-36]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/31/1933  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 1567

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at Lillian Edgerton, Inc., 267 Fifth Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]], this 31st day of October, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Helen Quinto and R. N. Ladd.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before, [264].

2. Greatly are the improvements manifesting themselves in the better physical functionings of the body.  Well that much that has been given be adhered to, for the better physical functioning.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should any change be made in treatments to eliminate growth more quickly?
(A)  The use of the lights that are suggested or intimated would not be harmful, but beneficial; provided the manipulations following such treatments disseminate the activity of the portions thus stimulated by the sun's rays, or the heavy light ray.  Too strong would be the treatments if they were not followed with a general stimulation of ganglia adjacent to, and associated with, the cerebro-spinal activity and the sympathetic ganglia, for such violent heating or relaxing of areas in system.  We would advise the use of the lights properly, then.

5. (Q)  Is magnetic treatment being given properly?
(A)  About three times out of five, given correctly.

6. (Q)  Please give specific instructions.
(A)  The placing of the hands is not adhered to just as outlined at all times, nor the rotary motion of that hand over the condition that disturbs the body.

7. (Q)  Would it help to eliminate growth for Dr. Brenner to use Dr. Loeb's Mountain Sun Light on me?
(A)  This is as we have just given.

8. (Q)  Should #2 Spectrum Sun be used for stabilization?
(A)  Be correct.

9. (Q)  Should #15 and #5 be used to support heart?
(A)  This will require watching those activities closely.  In the present these appear to be the ones proper to be used, provided there is that dissemination of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] created by these activities in the system.

10. (Q)  Should #35 Blue Sun be used for elimination of growth?
(A)  Provided those suggestions given are adhered to.

11. (Q)  Should #23-a Yellow Sun and #14 Yellow Sun be used for kidney elimination?
(A)  No.  We would use different from this.  Those that combine better with those that support the activity of the sympathetic circulation as related to the heart.

12. (Q)  What numbers of light should be used in this?
(A)  32-24.

13. (Q)  Of the Yellow Sun?
(A)  Of the Yellow Sun.

14. (Q)  What adjustment could Dr. Brenner give me, or could the Loeb Mountain Sun light be used to give me a normal eyesight?
(A)  With the correction of the conditions in the system that come with the activities of these forces in the body, this will be made more normal without much of any adjustment save the stimulation of the centers indicated.

15. (Q)  Outline exercise which was suggested in previous reading which would help me.
(A)  These have been outlined.  Those are a portion of the exercise ordinarily given in the reducing of the pressure in the system during those periods, or just before periods of menstruation, that aid in the dilation and the activity of the organs of pelvis as related to the general eliminations in the system, and should be outlined by those that make the applications for the general activities in the changes that are being considered in the applications for treatments in the body.

16. (Q)  What treatment would help my feet?  Why do they swell and feel bruised?
(A)  Contraction of the circulation through pressure in the lumbar areas, and with the relaxing of same following the treatments as outlined, the circulation should be stabilized so as to reduce these activities in the body.

17. (Q)  What treatment would help the hemorrhoids?
(A)  Those treatments that will be through the Sun ray.

18. We are through with this reading.
