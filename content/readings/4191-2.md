# TEXT OF READING 4191-2

**Reading [[4191-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/10/1932  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 11933

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, Pinewood on Lake Drive, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1932.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce, and Gray Salter.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.

1. GC:  You will have before you the information given yesterday, March 9, 1932, through these channels, as to the exact location of Charles Augustus Lindbergh, Jr., also the enquiring minds of those who sought this information.  You will give any advice and counsel that will be of help to them at this time, and answer the questions which they have submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given respecting location of Charles Augustus Lindbergh, Jr., also the enquiring minds of those interested in recovering this body.

3. As we find, the body Charles Augustus Lindbergh Jr. is still there, in that as indicated, and in the way in which has been outlined would be the manner to gain control of same, through those that have been employed for making connections with such individuals.  The ones, or one, to be dealt WITH directly would be the leader or authority of the group, as has been given - Megleo.

4. In location - this a portion of the northeast section, or east section, of New Haven; so called from those of the activities in this particular vicinity.

5. The streets, as given - the manner and way of approach. Do that.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  As we are unable to locate Cardova section through Post Office, please inform us how to proceed to locate this section?
(A)  It's as given here, Cordova.

7. (Q)  How is this name spelled?
(A)  C-a-r-d-o-v-a.

8. (Q)  Through what official channels can same be located?
(A)  Look and see!  Here it is, here!

9. (Q)  Through what channels can we locate Scharter Street?
(A)  By going on the street - seeing the name on it!  That's the way we see it!

10. (Q)  Are there any marks on the street?
(A)  If the name's on it, why it's a right good mark!

11. (Q)  Is Cardova section name for a mill, or where did it derive its name?
(A)  As given, named from the activities there.

12. (Q)  Upon locating house, what method should be used to contact people?
(A)  This has been outlined, through those channels that have been employed to make connections or agreements with such groups.

13. (Q)  Which special one -
(A)  Those that have already been chosen!  One that knows the people, knows the kinds, better.

14. We are through.
