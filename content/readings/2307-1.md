# TEXT OF READING 2307-1 M 43

**Reading [[2307-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/13/1940  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9076

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of July, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2307], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257] & Mr. [2345].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., W. Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there are disturbing conditions which prevent the better normal physical functionings.  While these are not causing unusual distresses or disturbances in the present, they are of a nagging nature, - and without corrections in the present may bring about disturbances that would be hard to eliminate.

4. These as we find affect primarily the nervous system, and arise from [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbances.  These have been of a gradual development, as indicated in those periods when the body has been under great tension or when there has been an upsetting of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in one manner or another.

5. The blood supplies indicate a [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] disturbance as well as a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance affecting the metabolism of the system; these arising form deflections from subluxations in the pancrean area and a compound deflection from the thyroids.

6. We find that these cause periods when there is the lack of proper assimilation, proper elimination, and when there is the easily tiring; then at other periods the very high tension to the nerve forces of the body, producing fretfulness or peevishness, in a manner, - as might be called by the close associates or family.

7. These make for hard conditions not only for the body but for others about the body.

8. The causes of these are not only from the subluxations, but from the unbalancing caused by these in the chemical forces of the system itself.

9. These, then, cause disturbance through the functioning of the organs of the circulatory system; heart, liver and kidneys.

10. All of these combine to produce those reactions as we find indicated in the body.

11. In meeting the needs of the conditions as we find them:

12. First, we would begin with the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] applications from a physiotherapist who would include corrections or adjustments with same; that is, the steam baths, thorough rubdowns, and the corrections in the areas from the 9th dorsal upward; later coordinating those areas from the 9th dorsal to the end of the spine WITH the corrections made through the dorsal and cervical areas.

13. Also during the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] treatments we would have the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] to reduce the activities of poisons that are as accumulations from the toxic forces of the system; bringing a better condition for the pulsation, the blood supply, the blood pressure to nearer normal, and relieving the tension from the conditions indicated in the glandular forces as well as in the muscular forces of head, neck and shoulders.

14. Occasionally, but not too often - though consistently - we would take small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to purify the activities of the glandular forces of the body; one minim each morning before the meal, in half a glass of water, for five days. Then leave off for five days. Then take again for five days. Then leave off for fifteen days.  Then take for three days. Then leave off for five.  Then take for three more.  This should last during the periods of the adjustments, as well as the cleansing forces through the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.

15. As to the diets, - keep away from fried foods of any kind, and from rare or red meat of any kind.  Preferably use only fish, fowl or lamb as the meats.  Have plenty of raw vegetables in the diet each day.

16. Doing these, as we find, consistently, we should bring bettered conditions for this body of [2307].

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  What is condition of my teeth?
(A)  These need local attention; but with the corrections and the removal of poisons in the manners indicated, and the local attention, there should be much better conditions with these.

19. (Q)  What exercise?
(A)  Walking is the best exercise, or golf included with same.

20. (Q)  How much rest do I need daily?
(A)  At least eight hours.  It is well to budget the time so that there are periods of recreation, periods of rest, periods of labor, and periods of development of the mind, or concentration on activities that have not to do with the daily labors.

21. (Q)  What is the condition of my organs?
(A)  As has been indicated.

Do as we have outlined, if we would bring the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for this body.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Physiotherapist.

[[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is manufactured by The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Company, 64 E. Sydney Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y.

At your convenience, please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.  Also let us have the name of the Physiotherapist you choose, so we may put him on our mailing list.)
