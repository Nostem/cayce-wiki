# TEXT OF READING 2501-5 F 20

**Reading [[2501-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 9404

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at this office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by her parents, Mr. [289] and Mrs. [413].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [289] and Mrs. [413], Dr. Lydic, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  Now, we find - as we have given - some conditions are bettered. Some needed more, and need more, close attention.  Especially those conditions that have to do with the organs of the pelvis and of the mental reactions in the system.  In and with the corrections, as has been outlined, these will bring the better reactions for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and for the whole system in its reaction to the conditions about same. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What manner of treatment should be given to pelvic disturbance?
(A)  That there may be the proper release of all the forces as apply to the eliminations in system, and as to the correction as for the nerve ENERGIES as come to same. Through the lower dorsal, from the 12th dorsal; also the 4th lumbar and IN the coccyx.  This should be raised, see?

3. (Q)  Define necessary changes in manipulations for body?
(A)  These have just been defined, in these activities to be brought to the pelvic organs.  Also there should be those coordinations of these releases in system through those of the upper cervicals, where they coordinate with those activities to the brain centers themselves; RELEASING much of those forces as act with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], with the adrenal [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and with those about the central portion of the head itself - see?  STIMULATE these.

4. (Q)  What is meant by "proper precautions to be taken physically and mentally"?
(A)  That those associations of the body-physical, those activities of the body-physical, are kept in accord with that being attempted to be administrated in and through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, that the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]] and the [[Physical Body|physical body]] may coordinate.

5. (Q)  How can proper control over mental activities be accomplished?
(A)  In that of controlling as to what the body thinks, THROUGH the associations, and what the body studies IN its quiet moments, and what the body's activities are - all kept in accord.  Direct physical activities would be well for the body, and these would be well to be directed in specific channels, and in specific ways and manners.  These should be required, as much as of tasks - see?

6. (Q)  Have the necessary changes in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the glands been made?
(A)  These have been effective, and are being effective. That's why there is given that the stimulation should be now to those of the glands of the head, that the activities of same may act throughout the system.  See?

7. (Q)  Is the present environment in keeping with what is desired to be accomplished for this body?
(A)  Not at all times.

8. (Q)  What corrections would you advise?
(A)  Keep those so that they will be in accord!

9. (Q)  Will you make this more specific, so that we may understand just what is meant?
(A)  That [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of the individual with other individuals, those individuals with whom it's associated, be in accord - and their activities and their thoughts in keeping WITH that being attempted!  That's VERY specific! We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Parents
