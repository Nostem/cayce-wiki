# TEXT OF READING 482-8 F 53

**Reading [[482-8]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/1/1938  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 3548

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Miss [421], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., niece of Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. Conditions as we find have been much more serious than in the immediate.  Though there is still a great deal of disturbance, this arises from effects of old conditions as have been indicated through a portion of the digestive system, or area, or in stomach; upsetting by an overnervous state, as produced from lack of proper eliminations and assimilations.

3. In the present, for the immediate:

4. We would have the gentle, positive applications osteopathically; not chiropractically, for assisting to drain the stomach AND intestines.

5. The use occasionally of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]], or the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, would be beneficial.

6. The POSITIVE effect over this acute condition would be to use the Appliance in the manner and the way as has been formerly indicated for the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What should the diet be?
(A)  Principally the fruit and vegetable juices, or those that are easily assimilated; and not too much fat at any time, neither too much of raw milk.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Daughter - Wired also - Sent Special
"    " Ass'n file
