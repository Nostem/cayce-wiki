# TEXT OF READING 1100-36 F 47

**Reading [[1100-36]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/1/1943  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 6323

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [470], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   Hotel ..., 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Utah.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the acute conditions, the cold, as well as the irregularity being experienced with the heart activity, - much of this comes from the general altitude.

4. It would be well if there would be a correcting of these disturbances at a different altitude, see?

5. Yet as we find in the present, these will aid in equalizing the conditions:

6. Have a thorough relaxation, osteopathically, to the secondary cardiac plexus.

7. Apply at [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach a [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Pack.  Use three to four thicknesses of cotton cloth well saturated with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], and let it remain on for at least an hour - at the time.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Any advice regarding the diet at this time?
(A)  Use rather the pre-digested foods.  Not too much of heavy foods, but those that are body building.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Special - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

6/1/43 Wire:  OSTEOPATHICALLY RELAX SECONDARY CARDIAC PLEXUS APPLY AT PIT OF STOMACH FOUR THICKNESSES COTTON CLOTH SATURATED WITH [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCO-THYMOLINE]]  PREDIGESTED FOODS  FULL INFORMATION AIR SPECIAL.
