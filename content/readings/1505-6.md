# TEXT OF READING 1505-6 F 37

**Reading [[1505-6]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/29/1942  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 7385

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of September, 1942, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [341], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [341].]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [1505]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while time is fast approaching when the body should be delivered of the body within the body, all of the activities are very good.  It should be near to full time, unless some unusual incident or accident should occur to body.  And while it will not be, as might be said, entirely free from overanxiety, there should be a very normal, nominal deliverance.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is there anything which may or should be done to facilitate the timing of the birth of the child to coordinate with the [[Purpose|purpose]] and spiritual development of [[Soul|the soul]] which will enter this body?
(A)  As just indicated, this will be near normal.  Unless there are undue excitements or happenings of some kind, it should be a normal, nominal delivery, and full time.

6. (Q)  Is the time the doctor has set, around October 8th, near accurate?
(A)  As we find, between the 9th and the 11th.

7. (Q)  Would it be helpful or advisable to have a Life or Mental and Spiritual Reading through this channel for the baby before physical birth?  and through such a Rdg. could help be given in assisting or facilitating the coming of [[Soul|the soul]] for this baby's body?
(A)  (Interrupting at end of first part of question)  No.

8. (Q)  Is the body taking too much exercise and getting too tired?
(A)  As just indicated, unless there is overexertion or overanxiety, or accidents, there should be near normalcy. Do not be overtiring to the body, else we may make very unpleasant results, see?

9. (Q)  Is there any special food or type of food which should be given the body during the next few weeks?
(A)  Most of these have already been supplied.  Just whatever answers to the body's appetite.  Not such great quantities, save - of course - the natural circulation or boosting for the building up of the body in the present.  Do not have or take foods that cause such a drain on the body for the supply of nominal forces of development for the body.

10. (Q)  What special foods or types of foods should not be taken?
(A)  Those that the body finds disagree; as bananas, or too much sweets, or too much green or raw apples, - while cooked apples, if well prepared, are good for the body.

Orange juice, as long as it agrees, or citrus fruit juices of all kinds are good.  Wild game is good, fish excellent. All of these, just so they agree with the baby.

11. (Q)  Should the [[Calcios|Calcios]] be continued?
(A)  [[Calcios|Calcios]], though not too close together, should be continued.  This makes for better conditions for the body afterward.  For this is a natural drain upon the calcium. And a better circulation of the heart's activity will be maintained if there is sufficient quantity of calcium through the system at all times.  While it makes for structural portions or build of the body itself, - that is, within, - it will be better for both.

12. (Q)  Any special advice regarding the handling of the body during the time of delivery?
(A)  These activities have been put in the hands of others, and these ye will follow.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
