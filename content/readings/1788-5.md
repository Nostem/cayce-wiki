# TEXT OF READING 1788-5 M 4 MONTHS

**Reading [[1788-5]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/3/1939  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 8073

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [480], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, it is not so much the improper combination with the milk but rather the combination with other foods that causes the body to REBEL against the feeding time.

4. Here as we find, while orange juice is advisable in most cases, we would weaken or leave it off entirely in this case; and keep the right formula for the milk; and as we find there would be less spitting up.

5. It will be well for this body to be given and to RETAIN, as it were, a little Olive Oil each day; just a few drops will be most helpful for the body, - as would be, alternated with same, the Peanut Oil.  It is well that there be kept this activity so that there may be the better reaction with the whole of the hepatic circulation.  One to two drops should be sufficient each day, see?  One day give the Olive Oil, the next day the Peanut Oil, and so on.  These act with the assimilating system and are strengthening to the activity of liver AND kidneys, and thus will make for better conditions through the body.

6. Well that the Lime Water STILL be a part of the formula for the diet, or for the milk for the body.

7. Do these and, as we find, we may keep the better conditions for this body.

8. Only those combinations cause the disagreement with the digestive forces.  With the Oil taken for two to three months, we should find then the citrus fruit juices combining much better with the gastric flow through the system.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Would you suggest any change in the milk formula?
(A)  As indicated, this had best be kept as it is at present, but NOT using the orange juice; rather taking the Oil as suggested.

11. (Q)  How may he be broken from sucking his thumb?
(A)  Give him a pacifier or let him keep it as it is. Better a pacifier than the thumb, though, because this might eventually cause some deformity with the same, see?

12. (Q)  Does sucking a pacifier cause any abnormal conditions?
(A)  Some hold that it causes adenoids.  No.  It does not cause other than a period when [[Free Will|the will]] must act with the habits of the body.

13. (Q)  Why does he not sleep through the night?
(A)  It's an active mind in the one direction, and those disturbances of the assimilation in the other.  These activities changed as indicated should make for better sleeping through the evening.

14. (Q)  How may lower hepatic circulation be aided?
(A)  As just indicated, through the usage of Olive Oil and Peanut Oil; one to two drops each day, one day one, the next day the other.

15. (Q)  Any other advice for this body?
(A)  These we would keep in these directions.

We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
