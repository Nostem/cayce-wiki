# TEXT OF READING 883-1 F 62

**Reading [[883-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/8/1935  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 5180

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1851 Columbia Road, Washington, D.C., this 8th day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [883]''s sister, new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [883]''s sister.]

Milwaukee, 
Time of Reading                            Wisconsin, (moved 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  about a week ago 
to Dr. Dorn's 
Hosp.)  [Cancer 
hospital] 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [883].

2. Now, as we find, the conditions show some improvements in the general application of those things that have made the responses to the applications - while only one so far, it is [[Healing|healing]] very nicely.

3. We would not change in any sense those applications being made, but we would use with same the Animated Ash - that will relieve, or allow for the releasing in the tissue where segmentations show [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of tissue.  This would make for [[Healing|healing]] that is not being accomplished through the applications that are made.  Only in that direction would we make changes.

4. For, if these are allowed to eat further into the tissue - while they may heal, the tissue still remains involved. However, the Animated Ash released in the system will bring [[Healing|healing]] and the releasing of oxygen to the tissue itself; one-eighth grain of the Animated Carbon Ash administered each day, preferably in the evenings.

5. As we find, if there is response until two or three such applications may be made, then there will be the opportunity for the resuscitating forces to be revivified as to eliminate much of the drosses that have bedregged the system in the present.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is it possible to get fluid food through the intestines at all now?
(A)  Some is passing.  If there is the application of the Ash for the fluid forces in the circulation, there may be made greater improvements in same.  Give the Ash internally, to be sure - loose in powder form, NOT in capsule, preferably through the mouth.

8. (Q)  What do you mean by the fluids in the circulation?
(A)  The lymph circulation.

9. (Q)  Did the X-Ray pictures show the areas involved, correctly?
(A)  Showed the areas involved correctly; those from the caecum to the second fold in the jejunum.

10. (Q)  What would you advise as the best food stimulant for the body, and how should it be given?
(A)  It's necessary that this be given as injections in the present, you see; and only such as will NOT produce irritations to the fluids in the lymph circulation - which only sets up counter-irritations, you see.  This is being supplied very good in the present.

Then, as given, the only change we would add would be the Ash which is healing in itself to tissue that has been attacked by the bacilli that is indicated here; and will allow the applications to be the more helpful.

11. (Q)  If the body responds -
(A)  (Interrupting)  As indicated, you'll know by the third treatment if there is response - that's within the next nine days.

12. (Q)  Is the growth malignant or tumorous?
(A)  Tumorous, but malignant in its nature.

Do this, as we find for this body, [883], in the present.

13. We are through for the present.

Two copies to sister (one for those in charge of case) Copy to Ass'n file

(4/8/35 GD's note:  See copy of letter [which was enclosed] explaining the Ash, and where obtained.)
