# TEXT OF READING 373-2 M 53

**Reading [[373-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/12/1933  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 2877

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 12th day of July, 1933, in accordance with request made by himself through his wife, Mrs. [320], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Born October 7, 1879, at 8:30 P.M., in Frankfort [Frankfurt?] 
on Oder, [[Germany|Germany]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, that are latent and manifested in the personalities of the present entity, [373].

2. In entering, there are many phases of influences that would be of interest to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present, from the studies or reading along the varied lines of thought.

3. Hence these may be taken from any of those angles or directions, were such chosen.

4. As we find, those that may be the more helpful, applicable and practical in the present experience, would be - we would direct - from the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]], rather than from other inclinations; for these, as we find, deal more directly with the mental and material inclinations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] in most [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth.

5. For, as seen from a [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in an environment that is in the experience of this entity, in that of Jupiter; these influences deal more directly with impulses, inclinations, trend of thought, and that which produces same.  It is much as the manner of dwelling in a particular clime or country influences an entity, as the entity experiences from the Fatherland and the adopted land.

6. Hence, as the customs or the laws of varied experiences or [[Incarnation|sojourns]] wield that trend of thought in an individual's experience in earth's sojourn, so does the environ to [[Soul|the soul]] and its [[Soul|soul]] body affect impulses in an entity's activities in a material sojourn.

7. As to what the entity does about or with the application of self and its knowledge of such associations of mental thought, so does the influence affect the material life or passes rather as that which becomes passe' in an everyday or earthly experience.

8. With Jupiter as the ruling influence, then, we find these innate in the entity - without respect to will that has been applied in the experience in the present sojourn:

9. One with an adaptability to comprehend a large scope or [[Visions|vision]] of things, peoples and conditions; hence one that finds the ability to retain in the physical mind much that would pass the observation of many another associate.

10. One, then, that could or might adapt self to be an overseer in capacities that require the ability to [[Visions|vision]] or judge individual activity in a commercial or in a social, or in an economic, or in a political way or manner.

11. One that of self thinks well of self's opinions; not bigotry, in the manner that is compared with the abilities of the entity, yet may at times be considered by others as tending or a trend in that direction.

12. One that finds there have been periods in the present experience when apparently that which, without cause or reason, seems to have become good judgement on the part of self as to its relations to things, individuals or places, at other periods - the same character of judgment - fails to bring or produce or to give an understanding that has become, or is, as comprehensible as at other periods.

13. This is an inclination from the sojourn of the entity in Uranus, as compared with - or from - Jupiter.

14. Hence we find also occult and mystic forces as being a portion of the mental experience.

15. Remember, we are speaking of mental experiences (Please put this aside).

16. In this also we find that there is the influence of affability in periods of the entity's activity; at others when the entity may attempt to make for friendships, or seek to make those connections or establish definite relations, no amount of preparation apparently makes for the same experience in one as it does in another.

17. Such experiences in the present (we are reverting now to the material mind) have made for that which has developed in the entity one that is rather wary, or at times appears suspicious to others - and as if contemplating the abilities of groups, individuals or societies, to produce that which may have been even the actual experience of those who have embraced the thought of such group or associates.

18. (This is in line with that which has been given, that would make for a representative of a diplomatic relation or position.)

19. As from Venus and its relations with Mercury, from sojourns of the entity, irrespective of will, innate, there have been brought those activities late in the present experience in relation to affairs of the heart; rather, through Mercury, these have been of the order pertaining to that which may be called duty, rather than sentiment alone directing the activity of the material conditions.  Not that sentiment is not a portion of the entity's experience, but rather the character of sentiment that bespeaks of duty than of sentiment that may be from sentiment's - or sentimental - sake.

20. As to those things pertaining to the experiences of the entity in the earth (Now we turn to MATERIAL conditions):

21. In the one before this we find the entity was in the land of nativity, during those periods when there were the gatherings of those that would defend [[Ideal|an ideal]] in a foreign or in the native land - or during the Crusade era.

22. The entity then, in the name Schllenger, made for developments; being among those in the Crusade army, and GAINED through the experience.  For, with [[Ideal|ideals]] - and the mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]] attuned to carry these out, [[Soul|the soul]] and mental development must ensue; though the body-physical may suffer or may be counted, from earth standards, as a failure.

23. Rather did the entity, though, gain mentally, materially, physically, through the experience; rising in power, even in the land of the Moslems, and in those periods gained in the abilities to merit the better thought even of the persons, individuals or groups that were pitted at war against the entity and the group.

24. The entity became as a leader, and assisted in establishing relations that became helpful to both the defeated and winner in those expeditions.

25. In the present experience this thought of the Moslems is of a PECULIAR interest to the entity, as is the varied thought pertaining to spiritual life, making for an ability that is indicated through the mental forces as a director, leader, overseer and counselor to men in material affairs, in those things that deal with things and peoples, and their relationships one to another.

26. Before this we find the entity was in that period when there were the gatherings in the land known as the [[Rome|Roman]], when there was the distributing and gathering together of those that would embrace a tenet, a thought, a [[Purpose|purpose]].

27. The entity then was among those that were in the senate of that land and those peoples; hence one in power, in the name Romanolau.

28. In the experience the entity gained and lost; for the entity remained true to that to which it had ever pledged itself through the experience, yet not hardened, not wavered, by those acts or activities of the peoples through the land. The entity in MATERIAL things lost through the latter portion of experience, but gained much in the spiritual, for the entity gained in the services expended for country and for the peoples.  The losses were only in creating hates that arose with some in higher positions.

29. In the present from the experience may the entity gain in the application of LAW, as pertains to peoples in their relations one with another; and also in that which may pertain to speculation, when applied to exchange in varied lands or country - but in commodities, no.

30. Before this we find the entity was in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], in the period when there were the gatherings of the peoples for the return of the banished priest.

31. The entity then was among those that were the keepers of the Temple, and in the name Ptli-Ilo.

32. In the experience the entity lost and gained; lost during the experience when there were the attempts to be on both sides at once; gained when, with the return of the priest, the entity made personal application of self to the building up of the understanding of the spiritual laws - and of the relationships of man to man by such laws.

33. In the present, through that pertaining to history, writing, overseeing, may the entity make the channel through which the material affairs may advance, through which the soul and spiritual forces may have the opportunity for the greater advancement.  In every soul this should be a warning and a direction:

34. DO ever in self that thou knowest to be right, though it may make of self even an outcast to thine neighbor.

35. Know in whom thou hast believed, for the spirit of Right guards those who choose His way in directing the mental and material life.

36. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
