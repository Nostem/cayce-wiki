# TEXT OF READING 2977-2 F 37

**Reading [[2977-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/31/1944  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 10261

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2977], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mrs. [2977] and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2977]; this we have had before, you see.

3. As we find, there are a great many improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  It was indicated that there would be periods when there would be apparently an exaggeration of the disturbing influences in the body, from the incoordination of eliminations as to produce the irritation of the skin, as well as irritations to the respiratory system or the bronchi and larynx and to portions of the lung.

4. If there will be given consistently those applications indicated, as to the adjustments, as well as to the attitudes and the diets of the body, we may gradually eliminate the causes of these conditions from the system.

5. Then, we would repeat the entire series of treatments that have been indicated.  Don't hurry through with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, for these are not merely adjustments - but there must be the creating of coordination between the lymphatic and sympathetic nerve system and the cerebrospinal centers, through stimulating the small ganglia - yes, even the patches in those areas of the coordinating centers, or the centers where there is more specific coordination - if we would gain control of those activities of the respiratory system.

6. Hence there should be a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] at those centers, from the 6th dorsal up to the cervical and then through the 3rd cervical center especially, and the coordination in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervicals.

7. Keep these, then, and we will keep better conditions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  May I still hope to be able to have a child, or should I give up the idea entirely?
(A)  Let this rest rather with the [[Ideal|ideals]] and [[Purpose|purposes]] for same, making of self a better channel; physically, as well as mentally and spiritually.  This will renew and keep the hope for the body.

Too oft individuals are too prone to look upon conception or child birth as purely a physical condition.  Rather should it be considered, as it has been from the beginning, that life - sources of life - is from the one source.  Oft those who may yearn within their material minds for children are indeed blessed, if they were to consider all the environs to which a [[Soul|soul]]-entity would have to become accustomed.

Remember how Hannah prepared herself, and as to how others - as [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] - prepared themselves.  There are many recorded, and there are many others of which nothing is heard, and yet there was the long preparation.  For God is to each entity, individual.  He must become Father-God.  For as [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] indicates, "[[Lord's Prayer|Our Father]]."  He has become this to those who seek to be a channel through which God may bring life for a [[Purpose|purpose]].

Then make thyself a channel, physically, mentally, spiritually.  To be sure, law applies.  For in the beginning of man, in his becoming a living [[Soul|soul]] in the earth, laws were established and these take hold.  But lose not sight of [[Law of Moses|the law]] of [[Grace|grace]], [[Law of Moses|the law]] of mercy, [[Law of Moses|the law]] of patience as well. For each has its place, especially when individual [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] consider and seek, desire, that they be channels through which life, God, may manifest.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(Under separate cover, we are sending an article on Child Training, which gives quite a dissertation on the preparation for conception.  You probably will find this helpful.)
