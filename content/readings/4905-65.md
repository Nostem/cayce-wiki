# TEXT OF READING 4905-65

**Reading [[4905-65]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/17/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12990

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will tell us how the operations may be carried on in the present hole to insure success, telling us the formations and the kind of obstructions are in the hole, and how is the best way to handle this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, while the conditions as have surrounded these operations have become that nature as to be exasperating to many, yet we find conditions in the hole, or well itself, in that condition wherein - while troubles will be experienced in the operating - the success of same may be assured by the careful handling of conditions at the present time.

4. IN the handling of the obstruction, this we find is a portion of the casing, and that the width of the obstruction will increase to some extent until near half the cutting edge will show on tools.  This, then, may be handled by either continuing to pound up the metal, or by use of acid - which would expedite conditions to some extent, yet increasing the liability of there being an accident to tools.

5. The care in operation is that there be no hanging up and losing of tools by striking a portion of the hollow incasing and tools full in diameter but blunt and sharp cutting edges - that is, the dubbing of bit be shortened, see?

6. In the depth, we find there will only be a few more feet of the hard obstruction.  Then twenty-one (21) feet should bring the operations to the bottom of hole, with the less than thirty-one (31) feet of new hold to make before that stratum of lime - black lime - shows the oil in its natural state, for in or near the bottom of hole will be seen the indications of the oil seeping in.  Do not become over anxious at the renewed indications of the ultimate success, but show care and caution as gas and oil begin to rise in the hole.

7. Following these, we will make the success as has been given.

8. We are through with this reading.

> the opportunity to drop in on us sometime in the very near
> future.  We know you are very busy - but it is hard for you
> to have a moment you can call your own - but we certainly
> would love to see you, and have you with us even for a few
> hours.  I find it mighty hard to get straightened out.  I
> know time and hard work is about the only thing that will
> bring about contentment, but it seems that it isn't exactly
> mental work that I need - and I'm not situated so I can do
> much else, especially with what I am trying to do, and the
> many, many requests we have on hand.
> 
> We certainly shall appreciate your letting us hear from you
> from time to time, and we certainly hope and trust it is
> going to be possible for you to come in and see us.
> 
> With kindest personal regards from all here,
