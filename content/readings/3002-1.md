# TEXT OF READING 3002-1 F 51

**Reading [[3002-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/15/1943  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 10308

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3002], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [3002], ... [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., who seeks information, advice and guidance, in a Mental and Spiritual reading, as to her health and certain emotional problems.  You will give [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] any information which may be enlightening and inspirational, in helping her to work out these problems.  You will answer any questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of that entity now calling herself [3002].

3. In giving that which as we find would be most helpful and beneficial, those physical disorders that have been a part of the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should be analyzed.

4. There has been long standing a pressure that exists in the centers of the cerebrospinal system, associated with the organs of the sensory system.  And it is of such a nature that at various times through the body's experience hindrances have occurred to the organs of the sensory reactions in the body.

5. There have been periods of disturbed hearing, and also of what might be called imaginary hearing.  While not imagination to the body, nor as a fact as imagination, it has [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] others as imagination of the body.  And such discussions that have arisen have brought those periods when the body, as a protection for itself, has desired to be alone.

6. Also there are periods when these affectations are to the eyes, to the speech, even to the senses of touch or feeling.

7. As may be asked, why these variations?

8. Remember, all activity or emotion in the body is as a [[Vibration|vibratory]] reaction from one portion of the body to another.

It is as a system of sensitivenesses, that with any tautness as may come from an upset digestion, upset animation, - as conditions that arise from dis-ease of any nature through the system - causes the nerve impulses from ganglia to be magnified.  And these cause, in these centers that are under distress, what may be termed short circuits.

9. Thus we have frustrations to the body, periods of melancholia, and the opposite; periods of wanting to cry, and periods of excess of emotions.

10. All of these have been at times controlled by direct suggestion.

11. They are mechanical in their nature, however.  Thus, there is the need for there to be mechanical ([[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]) adjustments.  These will require some little time, and should be rather in periods of three weeks each.

12. During a three-week period take at least six treatments. Then leave off these for two weeks, and then repeat, see? Keep up these series of treatments and rest periods in this manner, and these will change - gradually the whole outlook of the body.

13. In the mental and spiritual, take a guide - as of this nature:

14. Know in Whom ye believe.  There are those forces that may arise from His Word.  Study, then, thoroughly, the 30th of Deuteronomy - as applied to self.  Analyze self in the light of that as may be obtained by application of self in those tenets and truths presented in the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]].

15. And ye will have that guide, the divine in self, where He has promised to meet thee within thine own temple.

16. But, DO have that temple attuned to correct consciousnesses by the physical adjustments; else the lights often become dim.  And these are the sources of the frustrations, the sources of those conditions indicated.  But when ye abide in His presence, and He in thine own consciousness, ye will seek to apply self in aiding others.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  What has been the cause, since childhood, of the frightful spells of tension and pressure in my head?
(A)  This pressure, as indicated, is existent in the areas where there are the sensory nerve forces - the upper dorsal and in the 3rd cervical.  Have these adjustments made by one in sympathy with the information through this channel, and you will find these will materially aid.

THEN make the greater adjustment in thy spiritual and mental self through the application of those tenets and truths in the references given, in thy relationships to others.

19. (Q)  What causes the great fear of having people, even those I love best, near me at such times?
(A)  As indicated and described as to how, under those disturbances and emotions, there is the seeking to save self. Thus the desire to be alone.   But when these pressures are corrected, and with the mental adjustments for spiritual avenues of reactions, we should find greater assistance.

It will require a little time.  Have at least three to six series of three weeks each of the treatments, and as many rest periods.

After at least the second or third series of treatments, begin then with the study of the scripture and the analyzing of self in the manner indicated.

20. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Geo. N. Coulter, 18 East 41st St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 3/28/43 [3002]'s letter:
> 
> "...I have just finished reading Mr. [[Thomas Sugrue|Sugrue]]'s delightful and
> inspiring book about you and your work.  I really almost feel
> as though I know you a little.  My mother's family were
> Kentuckians and the ones I have known were a warmhearted lot.
> [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]] was named for an ancestor of mine.
> 
> "The story of your life so completely corroborates what I
> have always felt about people who have genuine psychic power
> in a greater or lesser degree.  I have known several and they
> have all been people of the highest integrity and
> outstandingly kind and normal.  I believe any true power
> would be denied if it was misused.  It is too bad that fakes
> and charlatans have kept the world skeptical.
> 
> "I consider your gift to be a perfectly natural phenomena,
> though something as yet not understood.  You have always used
> your power for the good of humanity and it seems tragic to me
> that doctors and scientists are not studying it more closely
> and cooperating with it.  Your cures are a proof of the
> validity of your work.  I noticed in a reading you said
> infantile paralysis was a virus and doctors have just found
> that out.  Your readings might reveal a cure for cancer, and
> many other things that might be of incalculable benefit to
> mankind.
> 
> "I would like very much to have a health reading for myself.
> I am now 51 years old and since very early childhood I have
> had frightful spells of tension and pressure in my head.
> Often when the attacks are very severe, my thinking is
> blocked and my [[Visions|vision]] impaired.  During these periods I often
> have a great fear of having people, even those I love best,
> near me.
> 
> "In the course of my life I have been to doctors, osteopaths
> and a psychologist, without getting any relief.  If a reading
> revealed some clue to my trouble and a suggestion for even
> partially curing it, it would be an absolute Godsend to me.
> 
> "I could not go to Va. Beach now, but will follow any
> instructions you give me for a reading at home..."
> 
> B2. 4/13/43 [3002]'s letter:
> 
> 
> "...I shall be glad to take May 15th, 10:30 to 11:30, for my

>
