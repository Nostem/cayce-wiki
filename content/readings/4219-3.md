# TEXT OF READING 4219-3 M 47 (Realtor)

**Reading [[4219-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/23/1924  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 11982

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23rd day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4219].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [4219].]

Born November 11, 1876, in Liverpool, [[England|England]]. 
Time of Reading 
2:30 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find this present entity completed in the early part of evening, and the influences of the astronomical conditions come under those of Jupiter first, and those in Mercury and in Neptune, with Uranus in 9th house.  Hence the ultra and positive conditions that have ever presented themselves in this present entity.

2. We have many various conditions to be considered with [[Free Will|the will]] force as has been manifested in this present entity. We find there is only half, however, of the present entity's time in the past, and the influence as these conditions through the astronomical forces will bring the change, and should bring the change in the present known calendar year, and with the ultra forces in Uranus, and the ruling force of Jupiter in the present year, brings much to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in its developing during the present year, reaching its height of present year in the early weeks of December.  In the influences as these have exerted, we find the afflictions come in that of Sagittarius, Saturn afar off, and the Moon in the wane.  Hence the afflictions have been to the body of the digestion and the thighs.

3. In the assistance as will be found in the coming year, [[Free Will|the will]] with the present entity's development will go far to assist in presenting [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] wholly and acceptable unto Him who giveth all things.

4. We find in those forces as have been manifest in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s [[Incarnation|sojourn]] upon the planes that there have been several sudden deaths, or removal.  Hence the inert [innate] feeling that is exhibited that some such condition might possibly happen in present, and some conditions, when the condition in astronomical forces have been the strongest, have brought such forces in the present plane.  Sight as it were, or near to such conditions, yet with the influence in Jupiter's forces it has been prevented by will, and the developing of [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit with the entity's forces that lie inert [innate].

5. This has been one well chosen in many respects, and the forces as have been manifested in times past show forth in the conditions as circumstance appears to bring to the entity in the present sphere, though in friendships there have been those elements that were broken through the environment under which others were drawn.

6. As to the developing in the present sphere, none better could be found than in that with the forces as set before itself. Keep in that straight and narrow way that leads on to life everlasting.  Keep the body fit, that [[Free Will|the will]] and [[Soul|the soul]] forces may manifest, for the body means much to all who contact same, and in the coming three years will mean more than has in the past.

7. As to the personalities as expressed, the individuality as brought from those before:

8. The one before this we find in that of the King's son, and the counsellor and friend of the Shepherd King when the Shepherd became King - Jonathan.  [See 4219-3, Par. R1.]

9. In that before, we find in that of Poseida, when Alta ruled the earth's forces, and was then in that of Aramus.

10. In the one before, we find in the early dawn when the forces showed the Sons coming to-gether for the Glory of the earth's plane.

11. In the personalities as shown in the present, is in the first that of the love of sincerity.

12. In the second, and in that the life as earth forces knew them lost in the volcano eruption, in that we find the present forces of judgement of structural forces.

13. In the next the friendship of those who are the faithful to any cause that is just.
