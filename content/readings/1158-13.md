# TEXT OF READING 1158-13 F 47

**Reading [[1158-13]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/24/1937  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 6515

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 24th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1158].]

Time of Reading 
1:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. We would keep in the mental and in the physical activities those things that have been indicated and suggested for this body, and we will find we will keep a very close to normal reaction, and a normal balance physically and mentally.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is it advisable for me to see a doctor who specializes in [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] conditions?
(A)  Not if there are those activities, those relationships in the activities, that have been indicated.

While the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces make for disturbing activities at times, but keeping the mental and physical balance as has been outlined, with the adjustments, the activities in the physical and mental fields, the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] respond.

For the glands are that through which the relationships are kept established as it were between the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] and the [[Mental Body|mental body]].

5. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Schoelles) Copy to Ass'n file
