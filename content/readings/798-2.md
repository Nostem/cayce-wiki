# TEXT OF READING 798-2 F 49

**Reading [[798-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/5/1935  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 4844

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 5th day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the cousin - Mr. [261], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [798]; this we have had before; under somewhat different conditions and surroundings, however.

2. As we find, save keeping the applications of the appliance in order - having those recharges of same when necessary - we would not alter a great deal in the present from that which has been indicated to make for the better radial activities through the system.

3. Make the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] that will strengthen the limbs and especially the lumbar and brachial centers, as indicated.

4. We would keep the plates of the appliance quite clean, being assured that the connections of same - or in same - are secure.

5. Otherwise we would not alter in the present.

6. Ready for questions.

7. No questions.

8. Keep, then, that as indicated; making these minor changes as suggested, stimulating in the lumbar - which is just above the sacral - and that just between the dorsals, so that this tiredness is overcome; not stimulating so much as to cause irritation, but a general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] will rest the body a very great deal.

9. Keep up the rest.  Recharge the battery.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [261]
"    " Ass'n file
