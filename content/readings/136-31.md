# TEXT OF READING 136-31 F 21 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-31]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/12/1926  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 338

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                     [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [136].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to this body are in that way and manner as have been given, and should be applied in the life in the way as has been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Tuesday morning, January 5, 1926.  "My mother was well and to be married and all were celebrating.  I was sad because I could not join in the merry making because I knew she had to be operated on again and would die.  Also I was disappointed because I was not invited to her wedding."
(A)  In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those conditions which present impossible conditions in the physical to the physical mind, yet these are presented in such a way and manner that the [[Mental Body|mental body]]-mind may gain that spiritual lesson that should be gained from the conditions seen.

The mother is well - will be well.  Not through operations, but through those applications of spiritual forces that may be awakened in the individual, as to give that physical effect, as has been shown, and as may be manifested in physical bodies.

The sad condition, the condition that is seen, that the [[Physical Body|physical body]] knows of conditions that will prevent, indicates the inability of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] to understand physical conditions that have been produced by spiritual effects in the system, see?

Then the body should take this lesson:  That which is presented to the body-mind from time to time should not only be judged from the physical reasoning, but should with the mentality of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [136], be reasoned with that spiritual insight as may be awakened in the consciousness of the entity and judged according to that result obtained by such, see?

4. (Q)  Wednesday night, January 6, or Thursday morning, January

5. We are through with this reading.

7.  "Dreamed of [900] all night - can't recall exact dreams - but much of him."
(A)  In this as is seen, there is then in the mental forces of entity those conditions which bring to the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of the entity that which needs to be reasoned through such forces.  Then, the lesson [for as is seen, no definite stand, no definite point - rather a drifting condition in the stabilizing conditions of the entity].  The entity should know, realize, some definite stand, some definite attitude, physically, mentally, must be taken, that there may be the more perfect understanding, which would make each entity in themselves, and as a whole, better magnify that as is held nearest and dearest in the hearts of each.
