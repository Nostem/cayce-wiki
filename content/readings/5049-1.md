# TEXT OF READING 5049-1 F 23 (Filing Clerk, Protestant)

**Reading [[5049-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/1/1944  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 13170

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the foster mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [3356].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born October 5th, 1920, in Hartford, Connecticut. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [5049].

3. In interpreting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, we find that, if [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will apply its own will, the abilities to become a channel of blessings to many are made manifest.  Here we have an unusual entity, that has the ability, as would be termed in common parlance (as does each individual within its own self) to "make good", if it will.

4. For God hast given thee a wonderful opportunity in the companionship of thy foster-mother.  While not too many years will be granted thee in that companionship, make good while ye may, else there will come periods of regret, and such regrets become as canker to the mind and body.

5. Thy body is indeed [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God.  It has known (thy body) in this body, thy [[Soul|soul]]-body, the touch of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] of men, the Saviour of man, and this in itself should keep thee safe.  Ye must put from thy mind that of selfishness and the desire for the gratifying of the appetites of the body.  Discipline thyself.

6. While the activity in the Armed Services would be galling at times, and offer opportunities for many forms of indulgence, we find that if ye will take it seriously, ye will have the opportunity in thy experience that will bring to thee by the 3rd day of January, 1945, the happiest day of thy experience in this life.  Don't lose that opportunity, for it will be as a cycle of time, as in commemoration, as it were, of the blessings of the hand of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] upon thee, if ye choose that day to give thy body, thy promises to a companion who may become a part of thy life on that day.  Keep that body then, as it were, for that day.

7. In giving the interpretations of the urges from the [[Astrology|astrological]] angle, we find Venus, Jupiter, Uranus and Saturn are in thy consciousness.  Love and beauty are parts of thy [[Soul|soul]] and life, yet ye oft would sell that most precious to thee for a moment of selfish joy; of self satisfaction; yet there is deep within thy [[Soul|soul]] that which as from the Jupiterian influence, is noble, fine and good, and if ye will but hold to it, it will be thy crowning glory.

8. Saturn has brought, and brings, many changes in thy experiences.

9. As to [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], these have been few and far between.  Thus many things are new, yet there is a little that ye know of good and bad, and little ye may not attain if ye will put thy trust in Him, whom ye served so well in the experience before this, until thy zeal ate up thy good intentions.

10. Be not weary in well-doing, for it is those who endure to the end who find again peace within themselves.  This is thy experience, ye are not satisfied.  He can satisfy, even [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], the Christ.  In doing good to others, for others, this as ye know, is well pleasing in His sight.  This is that He indicated to thee, as is blessed thee, as Omarn; in just being good for something, to administer to the needs of others, in thy ability to bring cheer to others, and never belittling thine own self, nor thy [[Ideal|ideals]].

11. Thy [[Ideal|ideal]] is the life of the Master, that ye have dreamed of at times, and yet, like many others, ye have grown weary in watching that which appears to be good in the earth. Remember there is a way that seemeth good, but the end thereof is bitter gall.  There are those harmonies that thou hast which if applied, will bring the joy of living.

12. Before that we find them in the land of the Grecians when there were individuals chosen as models for the carvings and statuaries and paintings.  The entity then as Marty was chosen, end even she with the arms broken was modeled from Marty.  In beauty and [[Grace|grace]] and harmony, then, lose not thy hold upon thy [[Purpose|purposes]] for which these are done.

13. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, when there was the choosing of individuals as models through which the activity in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful, as well as from [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice, was carried on.  The entity then, as a grand-daughter of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]], stood out as the model for those who were in activities in the various channels of home building, or preparation for modeling activity in other lands, in the name then of Astra-al-on.

14. As to the abilities of the entity then:  these are limited only to thine own will, for He the giver of life, the blessings of the blessed, has blessed thee.  Forget it not. Live true to it, and when the opportunity is presented as for January 3rd, 1945, set that as thy wedding date.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Should I join the Waves, or some branch of the Service?
(A)  Any of these branches of the Service.  Discipline is needed, as well as determination in self, if you will be true to thy better nature.

17. (Q)  How and when was I associated with my foster-mother before?
(A)  In the experience in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]].  She was then thy mother.  Don't fail her now.

18. (Q)  Will my foster-mother be able to keep her health as long as she lives, or shall I plan to care for her?
(A)  As has been indicated, not too long.  Will ye but grant that companionship; make well of it, that there be no regrets.

19. (Q)  What has taken place in previous [[Incarnation|incarnations]] that in spite of early Christian training, education and abundant opportunities to make good, that I choose bad company and habits, instead of good, always 'kicking against the pricks' and giving those who love me anxiety?
(A)  Read that given as to that which has caused thee to wonder, or to feel or to seek gratifying activities, by belittling self.  It is self ye have hurt more than she. Ye can correct it in living that ye know she, yea even thy Master, would have thee be.

20. (Q)  Should I stay in [[California|California]], or is it best that I go elsewhere; if so, where?
(A)  It is best that you join the activity, and wherever the entity is sent, for it will not stay in [[California|California]].

21. (Q)  Why have I since childhood deceived and lied to my foster-mother whom I love, or do I love her?
(A)  As has been indicated, over-zealousness.

22. (Q)  When and where have I known [...] before?
(A)  In [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience, as well as in the [[Greece|Grecian]].

23. (Q)  Will she have care in a Christian home?
(A)  She will, if ye see to it, by thine own [[Prayer|prayers]].

24. (Q)  How may I begin to carry out God's plan for my life?
(A)  Begin by first analyzing of self.  Choosing and willing self to do His biddings.

25. (Q)  What was my relationship with [...] in previous [[Incarnation|incarnations]]?
(A)  A friend in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience, as well as one as a tempter in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]].  These may be good or bad associations, dependent upon what ye do about same.  Study to show thyself approved unto God; a workman not ashamed, stressing the words of truth, keeping self unspotted from the world.

26. We are through with this reading.

Copy to self through foster Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   for Indexing

> daughter.  Thank you."

> application for membership and request for a Life Reading
> for your foster-daughter.  The first possible period we have
> for this is the afternoon of May 1st...  I hope this will be
> satisfactory.  If you accept this period, be sure to let us
> know just where she is at the hour of the reading.  Also,
> prepare a list of questions that doubtless you would like
> to have asked.  Miss [3356] can help you, I'm sure, with the
> questions...  I hope we may be the means of real service."

> problem, what am I to do?  (2) What was the basic trouble

> home?  (3) What line of work is best suited to me and which
