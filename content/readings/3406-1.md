# TEXT OF READING 3406-1 F 59

**Reading [[3406-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/16/1943  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 10861

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3406], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through son [...], via Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

In the local 
Time of Reading                                hospital, 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Montana.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, conditions are such, and the natures and sources are such, that the greater help or aid may come through those channels that are seeking to be of help for the body.

4. For as we find, not the earthly physician but the mental and spiritual physician is needed here so that the very atoms of the body, each corpuscle, may be spiritualized to such an extent as to resist those destructive forces that would persuade body-energies to capitulate or to be destroyed by the vital energies being dwindled or weakened by the strains upon the body.

5. Then, keep in the attitude of [[Prayer|prayer]].  Keep close to those that daily offer [[Prayer|prayer]] for and with the body.  And let the body know that there must be that wholehearted cooperation with those seeking to aid the body.  Not merely in mind but in [[Purpose|purpose]] and in intent give every bit of thy strength to attune the body to those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that are set in motion by the [[Prayer|prayer]] groups.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How should I best occupy myself, when so much alone?
(A)  By reading or having the Book read to the body.  Begin with [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5, then read, or have read, the whole of the 30th of Deuteronomy, then the 23rd and 27th [[Psalms|Psalm]].  Then turn to [[John (Apostle)|John]] 14, 15, 16, and 17.

Know these not merely by heart but say them, feel them in thine own being; that these promises are all to thee - for thee to use in the ways and manners in which ye have need of in the present.

Do that.

9. (Q)  Is there something wrong with my mental attitude that retards spiritual help?
(A)  That's why these are being given here.  These should correct that attitude.  Know that these words are speaking to self, and that they are from the living God.

Do that.

10. (Q)  If the information can be given at this time, would like to know whether I should move to another state, selling my home, and if so, where?
(A)  Not in the present.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self via son
"    " Ass'n file
