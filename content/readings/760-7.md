# TEXT OF READING 760-7 F 53

**Reading [[760-7]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/12/1928  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 4687

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by Dr. [5618].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [760] and Dr. [5618].]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find there are the indications of reoccurrence of conditions that have been seen with the body before.  While these are from the effect, and that which is producing conditions now are those accentuations that were not entirely removed from the system.  Hence we find in the descending colon that pressure on the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus, causing regurgitation in the lower portion of the duodenum, and this causes the reaction in the system - see?

2. These need, then, that which will remove those conditions that cause the impactions, giving or producing the pressure on the system, then increase that incentive from those radial plexus that supply nerve energy to the region from which the regurgitation and reflexes occur; then we would remove those conditions.  First we would remove the cause, and then the effects will of themselves - by correction of the inclinations from nerve reflexes - cause the correction of conditions.

3. These, then, would be removed by that which has been given aforetime for the correction of those conditions.

4. First beginning with the oil that will be assimilated by the system; not over-crowding same, but increasing as the system will assimilate same.

5. Reducing the pressure as this is given in system, and this will aid the body in ridding those pressures in same.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What kind of oil?
(A)  Same kind!

7. (Q)  What should be the diet?
(A)  Not a great deal until this condition has been started to be removed; preferably those of pre-digested foods.

Malted milk, provided same is taken in small quantities often; this would be well until the condition has begun to be removed.  Then the normal diet for the body as the conditions are changed.

8. (Q)  What quantity of oil to begin with?
(A)  Half teaspoonful, or such.

9. (Q)  Of olive oil?
(A)  Of PURE olive oil.  Not of that MADE, or that which is mixed, but the pure olive oil.  Gradually increasing as the system absorbs same.  This character of oil, as seen, is food value for tissue that is deadened by inactivity through the form of conditions existent in system.  Should these conditions, with the stimulation, produce griping or nausea, then apply the heated [[Castor Oil|castor oil]] over this portion of the body until the nausea is reduced.  Giving OIL enema using Usoline, Nujol, or Russian White oil.

10. (Q)  How should the stimulation be given - in what manner?
(A)  That as indicated from stimulating the incentives from the nerve plexus!  Use common sense with it!  This is only given by manipulation; cannot give this through giving medicine, or destroying the incentives of the activities of the system!  As the condition is REMOVED; that is, after the cleansing of the colon, the stimulation of the system - osteopathically, or by manipulation, so that the activity of the liver begins - well that stimulus of whiskey (burned) be given; this will aid the system.  This, in this case, is NOT a costive condition, or tendency to constipate.

11. (Q)  Are there any gallstones in the bladder?
(A)  Not to amount to anything, if you remove the pressure - for you will get the conditions here.  More of impaction; though a good condition for gallstones to form.  For when there is inactivity of the system, where the secretions from the system in its excretory action are left in the duct, the natural condition is to form sedimentary conditions; as is the case in that ordinary gallstone condition.

12. (Q)  Do certain foods the body eats cause the gas to form?
(A)  No.  This is nerve reaction.  Pressure on the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexuses in the lower portion OPENS the cardiac end.  Hence the gases or regurgitation as has been given or called.  Do as we have GIVEN - we will remove the trouble here.

13. That is all the questions.

14. We are through for the present.
