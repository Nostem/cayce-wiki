# TEXT OF READING 2913-2 F 25 (Business Girl, Hebrew)

**Reading [[2913-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/6/1928  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 10172

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [2913].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2710], Miss [2913], Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Dr. Thos B. House.]

Born June 29, 1903, [[New York City|New York City]]. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present experience.

2. In entering, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] coming under the influence of Venus, Mercury, Jupiter, Uranus, and Neptune, with those variations as are presented in the effect of the square of others' influences in the present experience.

3. Irrespective, then, of will's influence, these are greater influences in the present experience. (Will is that application in self making the individuality of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in its application of the innate forces as applied to present environment, present conditions in the development of the entity.)  In that irrespective, then, of [[Free Will|the will]]:

4. One that is naturally attracted by those elements in the beautiful, and those of learning, irrespective of their application to good or evil.  Those that are of the bigness and strangeness being those most innately felt by the entity, and feared in the manner that lack of understanding would be the detrimental force in same.  As applied in will's forces, these - as seen change in the present experience.

5. One that, with Jupiter and with Venus' influence, finds that the self may be loved into the various experiences, yet never driven.

6. One that loves much and hates much, and accentuated especially in those influences in Uranian experiences.  Bad indeed would it be for such experiences to come to the entity in the months of latter part of November to the middle of February, for EVER would these remain as barriers, were there to come likes or dislikes for the body through these periods. MOST of these, as we find, have been in other portions of the influence, or year as termed.

7. In those influences as have to do with the experience of the entity in the earth's plane, these have been crowded in the experiences through the earth plane in a manner that may find greater expression, even in that innately felt, that knowledge and understanding will give the better application of those conditions as come in the present experience.

8. One that finds the mystical and occult interesting to self. These would be well to be weighed in the balance and studied with self's own understanding, for in occult much may be gained for the entity.  In the mystic little may be attained by the entity, for as in the application of these comes the better understanding of that as exists between the mysterious or mystic forces and that of occult, or knowledge OF force.

9. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and the effect same has upon the present experience:

10. In the one before this we find in that land lying between that now known as [[France|France]] and [[Germany|Germany]].  The entity then among those peoples who were in the way of the invasions through that land, and only remained in the earth's plane a short while, for the entity suffered physically under this experience.  In the name Werltz, and the entity GAINED through the experience, for the entity learned tolerance, and the ability to apply self in patience, and THIS portion seen in the present experience, that often others WONDER at the tolerance and patience borne by the entity when it not the experience of many about same.

11. In the one before this we find in the land when changes were being made in that now known as the [[Greece|Grecian]] power.  The entity then among those that studied much into [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of life and the relations of individual mind with the material experiences.  Then in the name Sylvvee, and the entity gained and lost through this experience.  Gaining in the service given others during this period.  Lost in the confidence placed in some in power and coming as a teacher to the entity through that period.  In the urge as seen, slow is the entity to place confidence in individuals, especially in the opposite sex, yet when once placed is sincere, true, and - as given - there should be the mindful conditions of such being made in a certain period of that called year, or months.

12. In the one before this we find in the rebuilding of [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]].  The entity among those that assisted in the establishing of the rule in the city, and among those setting up the home and household through the various conditions arising at this period.  Hence there is seen in the present experience the desire of orderly activity in household, home, or in office, or in whatever element the entity would be associated in, and often patience is lost with others for inorderly activity.  Well to be mindful of others in this direction.  In the name Ulane.  The entity gained through this experience, and there may be much gained through the study and application of those truths set up by the peoples rebuilding [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]].

13. In the one before this we find in that land when there were divisions in the study between the priests and the king, in the land now known as [[Egypt|Egyptian]].  The entity among those who served with those that established law in the land, and was a favorite of the priest during that period; losing in confidence in the priest and king through the divisions as arose at the time; GAINING in the latter days through that establishing of order and orderly conduct among a re-united peoples in a [[Oneness|oneness]] of service to mankind, as individual and as a whole.  In [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as may be yet established in [[Coffer (Pyramid)|the tomb]] of the king, the entity's activities during that period are STILL to be found.  In the name Iseui, and many were the models or [[Photography (Atlantean)|images]] of the entity made at that period for the service given during the latter days.  The urge in the present will be found great along this direction, for ever does the entity seek to aid in the proper knowledge and understanding; yet, as seen, fearful of the relations or the activity of others.  Study, then, along those lines as were set in [[Law of Moses|the law]] as pertaining to the peoples as returned, and apply in the present experience.

14. In the one before this we find in that period when known as Alta, in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]].  The entity among those who were of the highest development in the material application of these forces as pertain to the NIGHTSIDE of life, or the ability to apply that of forces not at present used in many ways for man's own use.  The entity may gain in material sense through the application of self to inventions, or those things that have to do with power, or through electrical or WIND application.  If money were the goal, much may come to the entity through these sources.  Then in the name Azice.  The entity among the household of AW [?].

15. In the abilities of the entity as has been seen, these cover various activities; either as the speculator for material gain; the student for the mental gain; the assistance or nurse, or teacher, as the application of that gained.  Keep before self that relation as gained through the application of the knowledge in the division period, and these will bring the understanding that gives the knowledge of self, applied to those forces that bring the at-[[Oneness|oneness]] with the [[Purpose|purposes]] of the earth's experiences.  Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  In what sphere of life would the body be most fitted, for material or spiritual?
(A)  There may be much gained in the material through the speculation, or through those of patents - especially as has to do with electrical forces, or the application OF ANY force using unseen powers.  In spiritual life comes the only DEVELOPMENT worth while.  Study, then, that as may be gained from the experience of SELF, the entity's individuality in the period given, applying same in the mental, the spiritual and material self.  We are through.
