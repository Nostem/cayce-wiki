## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 10/31/20 Letter from F. H. Dagley to [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] [?]:
> 
> Kaufman & Mayer
> Sapulpa, Oklahoma
> 
> Dear Sir:- (D.E.K.?) [[[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]?]
> 
> My Sister has just sent me the reading on the diagnosis and
> prescription for my niece Miss Kivelinger (no copy) - I am
> indeed grateful to you, for the interest manifested in her
> by you and your friend whom I shall call "The Wonder Man".
> She tells me she is improving under the treatment prescribed,
> and I am hoping she may be restored to perfect health and
> happiness again - I wish I could have used the opportunity -
> I had on the train and become better acquainted with you.
> However if you are ever near Sapulpa let me know it please -
> I lost your address but am hoping my memory will serve me
> right and you will receive this.
> 
> Again thanking you.
> 
> Am yours very truly,
> 
> F. H. Dagley
