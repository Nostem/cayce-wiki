# TEXT OF READING 900-405 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-405]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/4/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5608

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 Deal, N.J. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  [[Dreams|Dreams]] as come to the body in their various forms, presenting the various phases of the phenomena of life as is manifested through the various channels as seen, the application of same in the life and activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], or body physical, should be in the ways as have been outlined for same.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].  Many occur to the body in VARIOUS manners.  The body should not overtax self to induce the element of self expression, but rather let self be the channel through which expression may be made, applying self in the way and manner as to be the greater channel for the greater expression of greater forces, by the act OF just applying that given.

2. (Q)  Night of Sept. 2nd.  Saw [137] seated in a chair, and he seemed sick.  As I studied him, it seemed he was paralyzed or something.
(A)  Rather the warning that there are existent conditions of which the body should be warned, that the body may be mindful as concerning these little conditions that, not heeded - or not taken care of, might lead at the present time to those that would be hard to handle, causing worry and distress to many.  Then being warned as to the condition, see that such is followed rather closely.  Do that.  We are through for the present.
