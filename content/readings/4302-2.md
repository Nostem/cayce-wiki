# TEXT OF READING 4302-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4302-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/3/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12111

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in Meridian, [[Texas|Texas]] (?), this 3rd day of August, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here [present in the room?].  We have had this before, you see.  Now there are some changes in this body since last we had it here.  The body, physically, is in a great deal better condition in many ways.  There are still some aggravations in the circulation in the capillaries, and the effect as is produced by this condition. All as has been given has not been carried out in the system, else we would have had a great difference even from this in the capillary circulation.  We need to carry out the conditions as given here, and we will find we will remove this producing these from the system.  Do that.

2. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, are there any changes to be made in the treatment as given before?
(A)  No, just carry them all out you see, just as has been given.  Any changes may come after the effect of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as given in the system.  Just carry these out. Do that.

3. (Q)  Has Mr. [4302] been taking the treatment as prescribed?
(A)  Just as we have given here.  Not all, in part.

4. (Q)  What portion of the treatment has he not taken?
(A)  The [[Vibration|vibration]].

5. (Q)  Will he get the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray machine]] that he has ordered?
(A)  He should get it here, you see, for the condition that the body should have to stimulate the circulation and eliminate through proper channels and to destroy that [[Vibration|vibration]] that produces the separation of the particles as are shown in the outer cuticle.  Do as we have given.  We are through.
