# TEXT OF READING 2752-1 F 6 WEEKS

**Reading [[2752-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/20/1942  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 9895

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1942, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [4930], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via his sister Mrs. [1523].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mae Gimbert.]

At the home of 
Time of Reading                               her parents, 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2572].

3. The body is in those developing stages of attuning or changing to the physical-material environment.

4. There is little or no assimilation properly going on.  And thus the body is not only in pain but it is losing in weight and in its resistance.

5. As we find, it would be better to use the milk that we have so oft indicated for such conditions, - the Carnation Milk. For this particular body, however, make the pectin rather than with the Karo, but the formula should be weak.

6. And we would give, now, at least ten drops of Milk of Bismuth.

7. Then in four hours begin with the very small doses of Fletcher's Castoria; one or two drops at a time should be sufficient, but give several times a day - every hour or so - until the effect of same may be seen in the stool.

8. Also give with EVERY feeding, though not in the milk, at least one or two drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Continue to give this until the odor of same may be detected in the stool.

9. Give the Castoria until there is the cleansing of all those inflammatory conditions through the colon.  The Milk of Bismuth will change the color and will relieve the tension, though it may be necessary in the evening (if this treatment is begun in the late evening or morning) to give a mild enema to relieve the pain through the colon area.  This also will tend to aid in correcting the sourness in the in the colon and bowel.  Use the baby enema, of course, and put a drop of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in this water also.

10. After the bath, do dust with the powder that carries Balsam in same.  This is [[Healing|healing]].  But don't tend to dry up too much.

11. Use on the affected areas the baby Carbolated Vaseline, - this not on face, of course, but on neck and body; or the [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]] - if this is weakened, see (that is, weaken same by using [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] in same).

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What proportion of milk should be used in the formula?
(A)  As has been given - this must be through those ministrations of a physician.

14. We are through.

Two copies to Mother Copy to Ass'n file
