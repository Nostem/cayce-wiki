# TEXT OF READING 2097-1 M 42

**Reading [[2097-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/11/1931  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 8743

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by his niece, Miss [2096].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. S. A. Bisey.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body and those conditions as surround the body here, [2097].  Now we find, while the conditions are rather serious, and that the greater portion of the physical organism is involved in the disorders as have arisen, there may be those applications that will bring relief and ease to the body; and WILL there be BUILDED within the body-consciousness itself that full wholehearted cooperation of the [[Healing|healing]] that is possible from within, then we may bring about a relief and a removal of the disorders.

2. These are the conditions as we find them with this body, [2097].

3. The BLOOD SUPPLY is weak from the poor elimination and the tendencies of the assimilations to be abnormal, produced by those conditions where poisons are accumulating in the system from those arising in the digestive organ itself.  The walls of the stomach in itself are thickened; the muscular forces of same are hindered in activity.  The conditions are more as that of a CAKE, or LUMP, as is formed, rather than of ulceration or laceration - as yet.  These have been produced by exterior influences, and are - or DO they become BROKEN, this must become a condition hard to combat with.  The condition is not an operative one, for the tendons of the inflamed portions, hardened portions, have gone too deep into both the pylorus and the pylorus orifice for this to be of an help.  There are natural conditions arising in the system from this in the organs of elimination, as the functioning of the liver, of the pancreas, of the alimentary canal itself. All of these suffer under the condition, as does the systems from the POISONS that ACCUMULATE from these disturbances.

4. As we would find, in meeting the conditions in the present - as well as the body-mental force meeting those conditions necessary for the proper attitudes towards self, towards its surroundings, towards that as has been builded by itself; knowing that these may be cleansed through the forces as are innate within each individual to bring resuscitation to any tissue within the system, through THE introspection of self and its relationships to the divine within; applying in accordance with those forces as lie within, that may effect and enable the consciousness of each atomic force within the system itself to MEET the needs - THESE we would find in the present:

5. In the first we would give very small doses of the activated ash, one-eighth grain three times each day - but in the first THREE days only ONCE a day, then the second twice, then three times each day - and keep this up for at LEAST eighteen to twenty days.

6. On the third days - that is, every third day - we would add those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from that of the ULTRA violet ray, and make the application of same to the 6th and 7th dorsal center of a specific nature by shielding the light except to those centers.  This should be given at least thirty-eight inches from the body, and for not over two and a half minutes in the beginning.

7. We would also gather, in or from the country, those of the green ambrosia weed - just the leaves; not the stem or the seed (as it is seeding time).  We would CHEW this, not retaining any save the juice as comes from the CHEWING of the weed - see?  This would require two to three leaves at a time.  This may be done once or twice a day.  This would be better than the distillation of same, or from the COMBINING of this with others, under the existent condition.

8. The water that is taken, we would alternate between that of small pinches of elm in a glass of water just before taking and a weak solution of the yellow saffron.  These, will be found, will combine better with those of the ash - as well as of the electrical forces as will be applied.

9. Beware of ANY element that carries too much alcohol in same. The foods would be those that are NOURISHING, as those of the oils, those of tripe, those of codliver, codliver oils, calves' liver (not sodden, but not with grease), those of blood puddings WITH the liver would be well.  This will give stimuli and active forces with those of the system.

10. Do this for at least twenty-one days, then we may alter or change as to meet the needs of the condition.

11. Be oft in [[Prayer|prayer]], OFT in [[Meditation|meditation]], SEEING self gaining the proper nourishment, proper resuscitating forces from those elements being given to the system for its resuscitation.

12. The active forces are as these:

13. With the ash there is those elements that will allow more oxygen to be carried in the blood to ALL affected or afflicted portions of the system, giving an impulse that allows drosses and refuse forces to be more easily eliminated from the system.

14. The active forces of the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] for system merely acts as an IMPULSE to cause those centers from which the system receives nerve and blood impulse from the ganglia in the region as given (in the dorsal) to the system itself, centralizing and acting WITH the system.

15. Those of the ambrosia weed act, with its secretion by the masticating of same, with the gastric juices that come to the system from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the digestive portion of system, to make for the eliminations to be accentuated without carrying too much stimuli to any portion that produces STRAIN, or an OVER force of the lymph and emunctory activity, either in the extremities or through the digestive system.

16. Ready for questions.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [2096]
"    " Ass'n file
