# TEXT OF READING 3300-1 F 54

**Reading [[3300-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/19/1943  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10728

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3300], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. M. H. Allen.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3300].

3. As we find, there are conditions that may be aided, that disturb the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in such a way as to bring distresses to the body.  These, as we find, have to do with the eliminations, the assimilations, and the effect this impoverishment has had upon organs of the body.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body:

5. The blood supply indicates toxic conditions existent in extremities, especially as related to the circulation between the liver and the kidneys, as well as the general impoverishment that brings to the body the weaknesses and the inability to build up resistances.  This is also indicated in the liver, the lungs and the heart's activity - in a disorderly disturbance through this deeper circulation.

6. These being so low at times, the superficial or the sympathetic and the lymphatic circulation has become in portions almost nil.  Hence those conditions that irritate the superficial portions of the body.

7. In the nerve forces the pressures produced by the toxic forces are those that cause disturbances in the circulation in the limbs and also in the organs - especially through the alimentary canal and as related to the activities in the organs of pelvis.

8. As to the functionings of the organs themselves, - there has been a gradual growing on the body of the effects of poisons upon the activities of the organs.  Thus these disturbances are partially organic, but mostly functional through the system.

9. Then, in making corrections for the body, - first, we find it would be well that there be a cleansing of the system as related to activities in alimentary canal, in the functioning of the liver, in stimulating better circulation between the liver and the kidneys.  We would begin then with these:

10. Begin taking a teaspoonful of Caldwell's Syrup of Pepsin each morning.  On the fourth morning take three teaspoonsful.  On the fifth morning take only one teaspoonful.

11. But on the fifth day, do have a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]].  This should be given scientifically, to cleanse the colon throughout and to remove the poisons from the system.  This will make the body weak.  Hence do not overtax the body through the periods when these eliminations are being set up.  During these first five days, do not take too great a quantity of solid foods; preferably liquids - vegetable juices, whole grain cereals - and these cooked rather than dry cereals; citrus fruit juices and citrus fruits but not taken on the same days that the cereals are taken.  Green vegetables, yes - celery, lettuce and the like - carrots; but grate all of these.  Congeal them with gelatin, using that form of alkalines for the body. Grate them or mash or grind them saving the juices, however. Coffee may be taken if desired, but not with milk or cream in same.

12. After the five days, rest then from all applications for a day.

13. Then begin taking internally one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before breakfast for five days. Then leave off five days, and then take again five days.

14. When the second series of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is begun, then begin also (not before) with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; emptying the gall duct and eliminating the poisons; stimulating only those areas in the lumbar and sacral axis, RELAXING those areas from the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical on to the lumbar area - relaxing and not overstimulating.

15. Begin with the regular diet, and take vitamins A and D as reinforcements for the general assimilation.  Don't begin these until the system is thoroughly cleansed in the manner outlined.  Take these for a week, leave them off and take B-1 for a week.  Then alternate.  A and D would be taken together, B-1 would be taken separately; the A and D one week; the B-1 the next week, and so on.

16. Keep the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments until about twelve have been taken, and we will find conditions bettering for this body.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Do I have arthritis?
(A)  You have those tendencies, but if we will eliminate the poisons purifying the system, these conditions will disappear.  It is more neuritic than arthritis.

19. (Q)  Did removing my teeth remove the cause?
(A)  No.  For the cause is in the liver and gall duct and kidney, not in the mouth.  But if those things will be done as given, we will find improvements for this body.

Do that.

20. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], when it is time for you to begin these treatments) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

([[Atomidine|Atomidine]] might be purchased from Burrow-Martin or Peoples in [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]].  If not it can be ordered directly from the Atomidine Co., Att:  Harold S. Bisey, 1326 St. [[John (Apostle)|John]]'s Place, Brooklyn, N.Y., for $1.00 a bottle.

The [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] - we would suggest that you have this done by a professional, such as the nurse in Dr. James R. Parker's office, Banker's Trust Bldg.)
