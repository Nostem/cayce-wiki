# TEXT OF READING 900-273 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-273]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/10/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5476

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you the body [900], present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] the body had on the nights that I will give you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [900], here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find the physical conditions in this body improved from that as when last we had the conditions.  In the general forces of the body the resistance is being rebuilded by the abilities of the body mind to control the system through the sympathetic nerve system - that is, no harsh, rash conditions occur through same to create those poisons in system as is created in this plasm as is thrown into blood circulation, either by that of an expression of temper from physical, or of ANY condition that overtaxes the nerve plexuses and thus cause a drain on the lymph circulation, and through same obtains, as it were, the poisons in the system by re-absorption.  In the general way and manner, then, as has been outlined for the body in [[Physical Body|physical forces]], in the applying of physical conditions for the body, well that these be attended to, and that these be kept in a normal way and manner, as is befitting for conditions as arise - for, more and more, we will find that the [[Physical Body|physical body]] will be able to build in self that vitality through this manner, as has been set by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself, in the exemplification of the lessons and truths as are being gained from time to time, that the [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL body]] may be built strong, for the resistances as are necessary to meet the needs of the mental conditions of the body, and supply that channel through which the mental and the spiritual forces may manifest in the material world.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

4. (Q)  Oct. 7th, the number 147 1/2.
(A)  This, as we see, to stocks, and is of motors of the general nature, see?

5. (Q)  Does it refer to a past happening, or one that will happen?
(A)  Past happening - for, as we see, in the manner in which it was presented, through those conditions as existed in surrounding these at that period.

6. (Q)  The [[Dreams|dream]] of Oct. 8th, marked number one on the paper in my hand.  The dream regarding my relations with my father as regards the lack of harmony that would exist between father and son when two natures are too much alike.  I saw my father and he was trying to point out something to me.
(A)  In this there are presented to the mind of the body, through these forces of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and [[Superconscious|superconscious]] forces, as are manifest before [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in this, those conditions as have been given, that from time to time various questions as present themselves to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [900], for interpretation, leads, guides, or directions and counsel would be given in and through the combination of the forces of thought, as between self and the individual, individuality, as is presented in this presentation.  In this, then, there are seen that questions as have been presented to the entity for the study through body mind, and the seeking of a way and manner to present same in an understandable manner to the layman is sought.  Then, in this there is seen that manner in which this question may be approached, as it were, through an experience to the entity that may lead to an understandableness for the entity, so that the consciousness of same may be gained by the body conscious mind of the entity, [900].  Presented, then, through self's concentration, and aided by the concerted effort of that same force as is seen in the mind of one explaining or presenting same, as regarding the relativity of forces as are manifest in the material world through that of how that from the potency of father and son there is given that needed for the development of each, or, as it were, a scotch for the shortcomings of each, would each manifest that Spirit Energy, or that Creative Energy as is the [[Purpose|purpose]] of each entering the physical plane, see?

7. (Q)  This morning, or this morning's dream had regarding [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]], the details of which are here written.
(A)  In this there are presented the fundamentals, as it were, of many various experiences as the entity will pass through by the concerted effort of the body conscious mind, for in the presentation of the [[Visions|vision]] as is seen, these become those properties of the physical mind, obtained from a [[Visions|vision]], as it were, of the various forces as are loosed in the Universal Forces by disintegration, and by re-integration, as it were, of various forces manifesting individually and collectively, and as these various forces are presented, we will find the various phases of [[Reincarnation|re-incarnation]], its fundamentals, its application to that of the various conditions as present themselves to man, in the developing of the mental abilities of man to understand conditions as are seen in the world to-day - for, as we see presented here an individual well in years, yet just presenting the first phases of a renewed LIFE, and then we see the same phases as are termed by INDIVIDUALS know to the ENTITY, and changed again into realities by the presentation of various forms of an intelligent force, acting dependently, yet independent in their reaction, see? meaning as in this:  That, as each force in the [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] of the elements begin as in this - in creation, see - now we are beginning from first principles, see?  from the first principles, we find these are separated into integral parts.  For instance, as we take that as the most elemental conditions - water - separated into two elements, yet each time these elements are combined in certai forms the same common principle is presented.  Now, we have had, then, the reincarnation of an elemental principle into that as is materialized before the MATERIAL forces in earth's plane into the form of an elemental condition.  Again we find through this same elemental condition - water - we begin with the first forms of life.  First those of the nature without body, without the elemental forces of even propagation. Again these grow into that form of able to move and begin then to multiply, by the division, forming then the first cell forces.  In cell forces, then, we have taken on the first principle of the beginning of that as is seen in the evolution of [[Ideal|an ideal]] in that brain force or center.  Hence we find reincarnation, resuscitation, and that of the re-inhibition [re-inhabitation?], becoming a portion of those very conditions as the first principle in same, or the very first experience in same, to the body mind of [900], presented in this.

8. We are through for the present.
