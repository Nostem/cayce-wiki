# TEXT OF READING 1952-1 F 72

**Reading [[1952-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/10/1939  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 8420

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1952], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [2091].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 to 4:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Yes, we have the body, [1952].

4. Weakness and fatigue, lack of the vital forces necessary for keeping a better equal balance, as we find prevent the better functionings for the body.

5. These are the conditions from the nature of the age and indigent activities through the system; that is, the lack of the proper circulatory forces, lack of the proper activities through the system of the circulatory forces.

6. As we find, we would add to the body those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the use of the Violet Ray (hand machine, bulb applicator), each evening when the body is prepared for retiring.  This should be applied along the cerebrospinal system, from the head downward, and extend same over the lower limbs, so that there will be the rest to those portions also.  In the beginning, use this for about fifteen minutes. Within a week or ten days increase it to twenty minutes. This should be kept then for at least four to five weeks, resting from same a week, and then begun again.

7. Following the Violet Ray treatment each evening the body should be massaged with an equal combination of Olive Oil and Peanut Oil.  Massage all the body will absorb of this, along the spine and ESPECIALLY around the neck and head, and around the lower limbs and across the small of the back.  Massage gently, but all the body will absorb.

8. Then, following the oil rub, we would use the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|Vibrator]]; the sponge applicator in the limbs, the cup applicator along the spine.  This part of the treatment - the [[Electric Vibrator|Vibrator]] - should be for about twenty to thirty minutes, - so that the body is completely relaxed.

9. We would take Codiron with the meals; one tablet each meal that is eaten, see?

10. And we would keep very near to the diets that have been suggested here for the body.

11. A real strengthening diet will be the use of the [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]], taken two, three, four times a day, but VERY small quantity at the time.

12. Do these; for as we find these offer for the body in the present the better resuscitating forces, and improvements for the general physical condition.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

Codiron is manufactured by The Lawrence Laboratories, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

At your convenience we would appreciate a history of your case, so that we may have something with which to compare our information.  This is valuable for our files.)

> been getting worse.
>

> Thanks again - Miss [1952]
