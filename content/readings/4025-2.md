# TEXT OF READING 4025-2 M 39

**Reading [[4025-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/26/1944  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 11715

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the sister-in-law, [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., through [845].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

At home, 
Time of Reading                                   ... Ave., 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.  As we find, there are changes and, as has been indicated, the spiritual attitude of those who would help the body has been indicated, the spiritual attitude of those who would help the body has been and is the greater contributing factor.

3. The weaknesses apparently at times are some improved, more than the incoordination between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems, and the irrational periods would indicate that there is still definite incoordination.

4. We would find that in the present we would keep those periods which have been indicated in the seeking of this spiritual help.

5. We would have the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] to relax all centers of the body gently, at least twice a week, and each day when the body is prepared for rest of evening, do use the Radio-Active Appliance for an hour and let this be the particular period when the body and those who are closest with the body will have a searching of [[Soul|the soul]] of self for helpful influences, and these will bring bettered forces.

6. Do continue to add the vitamins B-1 and E as they are necessary in the supplementary activities for the body.  The E may be used in the wheat germ oil pellets, or they may use the wheat germ upon the cereal dry or cooked, a heaping teaspoonful would be the quantity used at least once each day.  The B-1 we would take One-A-Day brand or the B complex. These would be the helpful forces for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is condition of the body and what causes it?
(A)  As has just been indicated and given, incoordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous system brought on by a nervous breakdown, see?

9. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to [845] (one for sister-in-law) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing "   for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] (We would refer you to:  Dr. Emil Smith, 322 No. Charles St., Baltimore, Md.)

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of Radio-Active Appliance.)
