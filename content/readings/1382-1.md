# TEXT OF READING 1382-1 M 45

**Reading [[1382-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/5/1937  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 7097

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1382], through his neighbor, Miss [5313] - new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, these are very serious conditions with this body, and there may only be palliatives.

3. For the advanced condition of the disturbing forces is such as to involve the vital organs; through not only the infectious forces but the breaking down fast in the bodily activities.

4. And the internal loss of blood, with the inability of the system to handle same - or for the coagulation to take place, makes it all the more serious in that the assimilations or digestive forces have gone back upon the body as it were.

5. As we find, there can be then only those things given that would keep the body quiet, those that would make for the better activities - and, to be sure - under the directions of the physician.

6. These properties may be helpful in causing a quieting in the stomach, and in enabling the body to assimilate somewhat; also in giving some relief for the body.  We find that the life may be prolonged, if the agonies of its existence are lessened.

7. Add these ingredients IN THE ORDER NAMED:

Cinnamon Water..........................1 ounce,
Limewater...............................1 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium.......10 minims,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium......40 minims.

This combination a teaspoonful taken in about a tablespoonful of water an hour apart until four or five doses are taken, and then resting from same for several hours, should quiet the body, enabling it to retain foods.

8. Give the body only liquids or semi-liquids in the beginning: [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]] (made from the lean beef, no fats nor fatty portions in same), Liver Extract, Blancmange, Arrowroot or the like.  Not soups - these carry too much greases. Vegetable juices may also be taken a little later, if those properties as indicated settle the stomach.

9. To take the Animated Ash will be helpful also to reduce those temperatures and to quiet the condition through the intestinal system.  A quarter grain may be taken twice a day.  Empty the powder on tongue and swallow; do not take in capsule.

10. Three to five minutes after taking each dose of the Ash, use the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray (not the Carbon Light but the Mercury Quartz or the Cooper-Hewett (?) [Note given E.C.F. 5/8/73 by Don Button:  Cooper-Hewett is (or was) the Company name of a Mercury Quartz type Ultra Violet Lamp] Light) over the lower portion of the spinal system - but at least thirty-eight to forty inches from the body.  This will help produce coagulation if assimilation is set up.

11. These as we find offer the better conditions for the body, [1382].

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What is condition of his right hip?
(A)  This, as has been indicated, is bleeding somewhat internally - which makes a hard proposition.

14. (Q)  What is the cause back of it?
(A)  Cancer!

15. (Q)  What is condition of left side and bowels?
(A)  As has been indicated.

16. Do these things, as we find for the better.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [5313] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
