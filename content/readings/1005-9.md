# TEXT OF READING 1005-9 M 22

**Reading [[1005-9]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/19/1931  Sex: M  Age: 22  ReadingID: 6069

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [1005].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [1005], Mrs. Levy, Dr. Crews, L. B. Cayce and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  The general conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find improved.  There is still some congestion seen more from the effects of impactions, that tends to return when there is still a state of irritation in the caecum region.

2. We would be mindful of the diet; not foolishly so, but sensibly so.

3. We would also use more intestinal antiseptics with those intestinal food values, as in the oils - or those that produce more alkalin in intestinal reaction, and keep more of the irrigations that will cleanse same.  Then we will find a normalcy in this direction coming for the body.  Do that. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should the oil packs be continued?
(A)  When necessary.

5. (Q)  Is coal oil beneficial or detrimental to the body?
(A)  Helpful in some respects, harmful in others.  Harmful as to irritating that already irritated.  Better an intestinal antiseptic, or the coal oil taken as for a bacilli destroyer in system.

6. (Q)  Any further suggestions for this body?
(A)  Do that for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] to become corrected in this body.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
