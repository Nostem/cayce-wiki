# TEXT OF READING 900-129 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-129]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/25/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5332

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-126] taken on September 21, 1925, on the [[Dreams|dreams]] had by [900] on September 16, 1925, especially the last question asked in that reading and the given answer, and you will answer the following questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the [[Dreams|dream]] and the interpretation and lessons as were given to [900].  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  According to this, the operation of [900]'s [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] in relation to [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]] is subject to Spiritual Law and is unchangeable, except as the physical [900] lessens or increases the ability of the physical [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] from bringing to his conscious mind aid or so called information from the cosmic mind or cosmic force of mind, but the operation of mind itself is an unchangeable spiritual law. The physical mind may use information gained from its own sense impressions, or impressions gained through another faculty from mind of the cosmos, (all the operation of the Spirit Force) to make laws by which the man (Spirit Force phenomenized) shall live.  These laws, these conditions in the physical are subject to change as this:  H20 = water, a phenomenon.  Water frozen into ice is another phenomenon. The One lump of ice may change into many lumps.  Some of the lumps may melt owing to change of environment - others may not owing to remaining in some cold environment.  All the lumps are phenomena of water - i.e. water changed into another condition, into ice, and the whole business but a phenomena of 2 gases, H20, and even these gasses but a phenomena of atomic force, which force is the phenomenon of the One Spirit Force, a first dimension phenomenon or first dimension change of the Spirit Force into that which our sense minds cognize as the atom - gas - water - ice.  The Spirit Law governing this first dimension manifestation is fixed always the same - Man may change the form but never the dimension.  Is that so?
(A)  That's so.

4. (Q)  Now, to apply this law or two-sided law, [900] saw Pan Pet sell down from 67 to 49 or 50.  The means by which this was presented to him was an operation of the Spirit Force of Mind in 3rd and 4th dimensions - the result of a 4th dimension [[Visions|vision]] by [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] being brought to [900]'s physical consciousness by physical subconscious.  In other words, the laws governing the spiritual [[Visions|vision]] of the subconscious are One and fixed, and laws governing [[Visions|vision]] of physical mind are also One and fixed, but physical mind may be brought close to subconscious vision, or gain information received from [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]] by subconscious faculty, by the action of physical subconscious in bringing the result of subconscious vision to conscious mind.  Thus, and in this manner, when senses are subjugated, the physical subconscious has a chance to give to the conscious mind that which the subconscious or [[Soul|soul]] mind gains from a 4th dimension vision.  So much for Spiritual Law.  Now the physical mind may change that which it receives from either conditions - sensuous or otherwise to suit itself - its own conscience, but its action in doing so is a phenomenized action of Spirit Force.  Thus the conscious mind's action in changing value of the price of a stock is the conscious mind using, for its own benefit, the phenomenized One Spirit Force.  The change is physical and subject to physical Will, Desire or Law,
(A)  (Interrupting)  Should be rather WILLS instead of WILL, see?

5. (Q)  (Continuing)  but the operation of [[Free Will|the Will]], Desire or Law to cause that change and the change itself is caused by the operation of the Spirit Force in phenomenized form.  Is this all true?
(A)  Changes as suggested, Wills meaning [[Free Will|the will]] or the spirit of those that manipulate such stocks, bonds or issues of such, the Spirit of that taking its place with the changes as occur, for as is seen in that as given as the illustration of the phenomenized condition presented to [900] in that of Pan Pet B at 50 or 49, the Spirit of the change that took place in the action of the cones controlling the stocks, see?  The actions of [900] in the cosmic forces brought to the conscious mind of [900] that condition, see?  The change presented by that same Spirit Force by those who prevented same from reaching that point as set.  Hence the use of WILLS in the sense as given, see?  Hence in this same manner may there be seen from time to time the changes, and as has been given, again we come to that as pointed out in the study of this law.  Consider or use as the parallel for study in the same manner as is used the illustration of change in H20, in its various forms it may be an expansion in heat, it may be an expansion in cold, and normal in the normal state, see? Then all shall be studied in that light as given, see?

6. (Q)  Then Pan Pet action is changed according to Physical mind's Will - such is physical Law.  The action of Pan Pet and the action of the Physical Will and Mind to cause that change is caused by the action of the One and always the same Spirit Force - is that so?
(A)  As given.

7. (Q)  Then this is the lesson intended to be given to the mind of [900]?
(A)  This is the lesson as is given to the mind of [900].

8. (Q)  Then from physical standpoint, how could the physical mind of [900] have known that 60-61 represented the physical change that would be the low change in the value of Pan Pet stock?  The physical mind thought somewheres near 49 or 50 would be the low - how could [900]'s mind perceive the change to 60-61?
(A)  By being able, through the action of the cosmic forces, to keep, as it were, in touch from time to time the various forces as manifest in same.  These have been given in other lessons, see?  That is, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] keeps self in that way and manner in which the information that will keep [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that way which leads to the best, for "the silver and gold is mine, and the cattle upon a thousand hills."

9. (Q)  Right now [900] is long Southern R.R. stock and bonds. The latter are now selling at about 79 - the gen 4's of 1956 are.  As given, they ought to go to 99 or 100.  Again [900] is long the common stock for all issues of Southern R.R. will go up in value as given.  How will [900] know when the right change comes in the value of Southern R.R. bonds and common stock to dispose or sell these for all of his accounts? Changes may come which may cause Southern R.R. bonds to go to higher or lower prices or values than 99 or 100.
(A)  This, as we see and as just given, and as has been given heretofore, in the manner in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] keeps itself and in the presentations of the changes as come from time to time are presented to the body, the mind, in such an manner as the entity will be able to discern, to know, wherein this is the advance or decrease, for again as is given, "My Spirit beareth witness with Thy Spirit, whether ye be the Sons of God or not," see?  As the Spirit Forces bear the witness, then, one law with another, remembering all law is built upon the spirit force of that law, and the changes as may come are of a physical force, yet has its spirit directing force, whether of good or bad, depending upon the force impelling same, yet he that keepeth the way shall be guided, guarded and directed in the way they should go, for truth IS truth, see?  and will ever present itself in that way and manner to guild and direct in the correct channels, see? Then ye shall know by and through the same directing channel. Study this phase of that given.  When [[Law of Moses|the law]] was given to Isiah [[[Isaiah|Isaiah]].  See Mr. [900]'s [[Dreams|dream]] on 9/22/25 in re reading [[Isaiah|Isaiah]], interpreted under 900-141, Par. 3-A, on 10/13/25], at the overturning of Ahab, the change came in the heart of Ahab.  When [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of Nineveh was given, it came in the hearts of the peoples, see?  Study the effect each had upon those who had given as messengers of the living God [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] to each.  Apply same to self, for in the way that goeth to do right, the ever directing hand leads on.

10. (Q)  How will [900] know when to sell these securities for the greater good of all?
(A)  In the way as will be shown to the body, when keeping itself aright, see?

11. We are through for the present.
