# TEXT OF READING 5671-10 F 34

**Reading [[5671-10]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14092

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5671].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5671], Drs. Lydic and Parker.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [5671].  This we have had before. In many respects we find the body improved from those conditions as have existed in the system.  While there are still evidences of the disturbance, and at times it becomes irritating, tormenting to the body - still, will the body but take the time to allow the system to adjust itself, without CONSTANTLY producing the over-irritation, then we may expect the conditions to be eradicated from the system.

2. The tendency of the sympathetic nerves' reaction still, as has been given, needs attention in the lumbar and sacral region; as does for the local applications for the irritations in the system.

3. We would keep much of that as has been given for the corrective measures, and be more consistent with that as is applied for the system.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  How can we obtain pure Castile to make up ointment as given?
(A)  Order it, from Colgate's!

5. (Q)  Should medicine given in first reading be re-filled?
(A)  Yes, this may be re-filled, and will be helpful to the body, will the body REST sufficient to take it!  We wouldn't, with the constant irritation being kept, take it.

6. (Q)  How often should [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations be given now?
(A)  Once each week would be sufficient.  This prolapsus still continues to produce some irritation.  This will aid in relieving this condition in system, by the stimulation of those muscular forces and nerve centers through the lumbar and sacral, and by the continuation of ridding the system of those mucus producing processes in the colon itself will aid in RELEASING those toxins as produce to the whole of the sympathetic system that irritation.

7. (Q)  Are the sitz baths being given correctly?
(A)  These beneficial - these are being given very good, when given.

8. (Q)  Would you advise vaginal suppositories for relief of pelvic inflammation?
(A)  These, we find, would be very good - but preferable, too, that these be those that act more with the secretions in the system, or that as may be made from the compounds that are used in such conditions, or in soft tissue, and active with the soft tissue.  This originally comes from the fish oil, but RECTIFIED - see?

9. (Q)  Should patient remain in hospital until complete cure is effected?
(A)  Would be more to the ideas, as has been given, and it will be very much quicker were this carried out in that manner; for, when the conditions begin to improve, and changes - groups - crowds - bring irritation mentally, physically, these are bound to go back somewhat!

10. (Q)  Should the rectified fish oil be given on tampon?
(A)  Be very good.

11. (Q)  Where can it be obtained?
(A)  Eimer & Amend, or from [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Chemical Company - this, mono-hydrated, would be very good in such cases.

12. That is all the questions regarding this body.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self
