# TEXT OF READING 779-28 M 56

**Reading [[779-28]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/8/1944  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 4799

This Psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]. Va., this 8th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [779], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, this is the same place, ... Street, [779].

3. We have the body here; this we have had before.  There are changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had it here.  As we find about the only condition the body has to be warned of here is keeping better eliminations and the colon clean.

4. With the use of the elevator as has been suggested for this body, we would have at least once a month or once every two months, a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], and occasionally the adjustments by a [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]] or an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] or neuropath. Either of these would be well for this body, but let the the adjustments follow with the nerves of the central nervous system especially where they are more in association with the nerves of the sympathetic system; through the areas of the lumbar and sacral, 9th dorsal, brachial plexus and the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th cervicals.

5. In the diet take rather the cleansing foods.  Include often raw vegetables such as watercress, lettuce, celery, carrots, chard and all of those that may be eaten raw; mustard greens would be well.  These cut up very fine and prepared often with gelatin would be well for this body.

6. The general conditions of the liver, heart, kidneys, as well as the central nerve and blood supply of the body reflexly at times are disturbed, but if eliminations are kept good through the manner as indicated, we will find much better conditions.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Any organic or cancerous or [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance?
(A)  Only sympathetic.  Do as has been outlined for this body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
