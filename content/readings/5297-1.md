# TEXT OF READING 5297-1 M 82

**Reading [[5297-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/3/1944  Sex: M  Age: 82  ReadingID: 13433

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by self through wife, Mrs. [5394], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [5297].

3. As we find, those applications which are being made in the present for the eyes and for the general debility, we would not change these in the present.

4. Only we would add that the Radio-Active Appliance be used to give the body better equalization throughout the system, aiding in stimulating the application made for the benefit of the circulation, for the absorption to help what is being done, and to gain more rest for the body without the use of sedatives in the body.

5. These, as we find, will contribute the most to the helpful influences in the experience of the body in the present.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What is the cause of the hard nosebleeds?  What can be done to prevent them?
(A)  Do as has just been indicated, which will stimulate the circulation to a more uniform activity without making for the loss of blood throughout the lymph circulation in soft tissue of head, throat and neck.

8. (Q)  Is everything possible being done for his eyes?
(A)  As just indicated, only those which will keep the better balance make for strengthening of these.  Use the Radio-Active Appliance one hour each day, or twice a day a half an hour each time.  This should be prepared, though, at least twenty minutes before it is attached to the body. Right wrist, left ankle, left wrist, right ankle.  Be sure to attach the same plate first each time, you see.  Then the other to the opposite extremity, keeping these plates or anodes very clean.  Polish before attached and when taking off.  These should be firm but not so as to stop circulation. Using adhesive or cotton cloth which may bind these to the ankle or wrist is the better way to maintain contact.

These will bring best conditions for the body.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining the theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.)
