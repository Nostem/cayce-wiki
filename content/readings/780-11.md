# TEXT OF READING 780-11 F 53

**Reading [[780-11]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/28/1942  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 4810

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self through husband, Mr. [779], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [780]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, many changes have gradually taken place.  Some are of more specific, more sudden nature; yet in the main we find conditions very good.

4. We would be very mindful, however, of the blood pressure, that this does not assume those proportions or conditions as to so strangulate the aorta artery as to become chronic or to produce such muscular forces as to produce a neuralgial condition from cold about the heart or liver.

5. These arise, as we find, more from those tendencies of a fullness in the colon area.  Hence the diet is very important, to keep down the calories or the fat-producing foods, and to keep the colon cleansed.

6. This we would do most by the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, or the use of such as Tyrrell's elevator, or such as may be used by self.

7. But we would occasionally have such adjustments and treatments as to keep an equalization throughout the areas in the cerebro-spinal system, as to keep a normal or equal balance.  This once a week for some five or six weeks, rest a period of a month or five weeks, and then have another series.  This will enable the muscular forces to throw off poisons, where bruises and injuries have caused accumulations or congested areas.

8. And with the regular [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, at least every ten days, we will keep down the poisons and those tendencies for the heaviness in the circulation.

9. These as we find are the precautions and measures to be taken for this body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What is condition of heart and blood pressure?
(A)  As indicated, there are those tendencies for irregularity of the pressure, and it is a little above the normal now, of course, but those precautions in the diet, as well as those measures taken as indicated, should bring bettered conditions.

12. (Q)  What about eyes?  Do glasses need changing?
(A)  We would have changes made in glasses AFTER there have been at least four or five treatments.  Then they would be nearer in keeping and it may not even be necessary for changes as yet.

13. (Q)  Are there any ill effects now, or that might arise later, because of being struck by auto Dec. 20, 1941?
(A)  As indicated, there are congested areas and bruises. And the manipulations, as well as keeping a better balance in the circulation, will aid in removing any of those tendencies for these to cause greater disturbance.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
