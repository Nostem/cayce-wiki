# TEXT OF READING 480-23 F 23

**Reading [[480-23]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/18/1935  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 3502

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Warshawsky home, 3046 Webb Avenue, Detroit, Michigan, this 18th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [480], through Associate Membership of her husband in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [480], Mr. [619], Dr. [[John (Apostle)|John]] R. Thompson, and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
5:10 to 5:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Detroit, Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [480]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are generally and steadily on the improve.  While there are still some conditions that are to be corrected through the system, the manner in which these will respond depends upon those adherences to those activities in relationships to the disturbances in those reflexes from the centers along the cerebrospinal system - as indicated.

3. While there are those acute conditions through congestion from cold or from draft, as indicated in the muscular forces in the upper cervical areas, with a stimulation of those centers from which there is the radial activity of the muscular forces as well as the nerve supply, the blood flow should soon make for a proper reaction and eliminate the tendencies for the drosses to produce those tensions in the muscular forces there.

4. In the general conditions through the system, we would keep those that have been indicated.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What causes, and what should be done for, the pain in the arm?
(A)  Release those tensions and pressures in the radial activity from the brachial centers, that will allow the flow of the circulatory forces through those portions of the body itself.

This arises from a slowed circulation at times, due to those tendencies for the pressures or the flow of the circulation to become slowed through the superficial circulation.

7. (Q)  What causes, and what should be done for, the pain in the right side?
(A)  Here we have a reflex from old disturbances than have arisen at times in the body.

The keeping of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces through the manipulations, through the lumbar and lower portion of the dorsal area, with an increasing occasionally of the circulation through the eliminations - or the alimentary canal, should relieve this disturbance.

8. (Q)  What causes the falling out of the hair, and what should be done to prevent it?
(A)  This is a lack of an activity through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that are secreting from the system the elements necessary to make for activity in those portions of the thyroids that affect the circulation to those portions of the system.

The diets affect this principally, though those portions where this is indicated would be stimulated by a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] - which may be had with properties that aid the scalp circulation; such as a small quantity of the crude Oil.  Rub this into the scalp once or twice a month; this should be sufficient to renew the cells that produce this - if there is the stimulation through the gastric flow in the digestive forces and the stimulation through the general distribution of the circulatory forces of the body.

Each time after the crude oil [[Massage Therapy|massage]], when the oil is rubbed in, cleanse same with a twenty percent (20%) solution of alcohol - water added; grain alcohol, not those that are cut. This should be sufficient to make for a growth that is most helpful, and prevent the falling out.

9. (Q)  Are [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments as now given correct?
(A)  These are very good, else there would have been given changes in the character of manipulations.

As indicated in the present, there will be the necessity of those stimulations from the brachial center - for the drainages through the circulation in the arm and, more specifically, from acute conditions in the upper dorsal and the cervical areas.  These, as we find, though, in accordance with other manipulations that have been given, will care for this condition - and be the correct applications.

10. (Q)  How often?
(A)  Once to twice a week.  Or should there arise any disturbances from the lack of eliminations from the upper portion of the centers that control the stomach's elimination and the intestinal activity through the solar plexus area, then these should be the more often.

Do these, though, as we find, as indicated - and we will find the continued improvement through the whole system of this body, [480].

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
