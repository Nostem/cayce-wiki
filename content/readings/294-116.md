# TEXT OF READING 294-116 M 51

**Reading [[294-116]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/19/1928  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 2075

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of October, 1928, immediately following [[Dreams|dream]] interpretation rdg. 900-412, without a suggestion being given to obtain it.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(No Suggestion - Information was volunteered)

1. EC:  Elimination.  Suggestion.  There needs be the antiseptic for the condition existent in nasal cavities, through the antrum.  And use an inhalant for same.  Do that.

[GC gave him the suggestion for increasing the eliminations.]
