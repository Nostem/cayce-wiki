# TEXT OF READING 5285-1 M 25

**Reading [[5285-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/27/1944  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 13421

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5285], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, the tendencies here are for the accumulations of drosses through the hindrances which have been experienced by an accident, or a pressure as produced upon the areas in the sacral and ileac plexus.

3. These have brought about those tendencies for these segmentations, so that the obstructions in circulation bring to the head accumulations, and thus the violent character of headaches experienced at times.

4. These, as we find, should be dissipated and, unless they are, may grow to become constitutional or turn towards what is sometimes called migraine headaches.

5. These are not true migraine, rather are they the thickening of tissue which causes the pressure over the eyes, from the particular area indicated in the lumbar, and where the brush end of the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic nerve reflex, from the 3rd cervical, branch into the area.

6. We would first have two or three low - not higher than three amperes - of the short-wave or radio-active-wave of electrical treatments.  These should be over the areas of the lumbar and sacral.

7. Then follow these with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, though give about two or three of the electrical treatments to one of the adjustments or stimulations for increasing the normal balance of circulation throughout the rest of the system.

8. These, as we find, followed through consistently, persistently may rid these disturbances, as well as producing conditions where improvements will be in the general status of this body, [5285].

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Any specific treatment for the eyes?
(A)  The short-wave and the adjustments which are to be made will aid.  The adjustments will include the upper dorsals and throughout the cervical areas, including the nerves and energies which are to be stimulated or relaxed about the head and neck, see?

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] who may also give the short-wave treatments - We would refer you to Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, 105 No. Van Ness Ave., [[California|Los Angeles]], Cal., unless you have another preference.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

> me no relief."
> 
> R2. 10/30/44 Letter:  "I still have had no relief from the pain
> in my head.  I have had twice the amount of treatments
> suggested by EC's reading."
> 
> R3. 11/6/44 Report requested by GD as to exact manner in which
> he followed the treatments and by whom:  "It is quite likely
> that the treatments have not been properly given.  This
> information is very important to our research work, through
> which we hope to help others."  [Due to EC's illness [5285]
> was not given a second physical reading appointment.]
> 
> R4. 8/31/49 Questionnaire sent [5285] by GD.
> 
> R5. 9/8/49 Report from [5285] that he followed the treatments
> for 3 months without results; he considered the reading a
> "failure".  He still did not give us details as to how he
> followed the treatment, by whom given, etc.
