# TEXT OF READING 949-7 M 23

**Reading [[949-7]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/15/1937  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 5833

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 15th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [949], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [949]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are very good in many respects; yet there are disturbances the removal of which in the present would prevent greater distresses arising in the system later; as well as aiding the body in keeping or maintaining a better equilibrium and growth in EVERY way in the present.

3. There are those inclinations for a toxic condition to arise through the digestive system.  This prevents the better or normal eliminations at all times through the perspiratory and respiratory system.  And through this variation it acts upon the system in producing a sensation through portions of the extremities, especially causing an unpleasant sensation in the cuticle as well as in the muscular forces of the body.

4. As we would find then, in the present:

5. We would have the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, every other day at first, for a period of three to four treatments, and then have them once a week or three times a month.  These should be preferably the Fume or the Pine Oil Steams and Baths, and then the thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and rubdown with the oils that would make for a stimulation to the superficial circulation.

6. At the same time, or during such periods, have at least one high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] to free the system of the poisons through the alimentary canal that have arisen from sudden changes in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] and the surroundings, accumulations of excesses in one manner or another.

7. And as we find, these - added with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that should be kept occasionally of the Radio-Active Appliance, and with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments occasionally - should keep the body physically fit.

8. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], when given, should be with special reference to the circulation of the vagus centers, those to the sympathetic and cerebrospinal as they coordinate with the activities of the sensory forces; that is, eyes, ears, nasal passages, throat and the like. Or more specific from the upper dorsal, or from the 4th and 5th dorsal to the 1st cervical; occasionally making coordinations of all these with the locomotories through the lumbar and sacral plexus.

9. Do these.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is this trouble serious with my arms, as far as the piano goes?
(A)  No; if these exercises, the baths, the rubs are given as indicated.  The oil rubs, of course, should be with the combinations of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Tincture of Myrrh.  These will strengthen the muscular forces.  After such rubs, of course, have an alcohol rubdown.

12. (Q)  Could such treatments be taken at home, or would you advise going to a regular hydrotherapist?
(A)  We would take them from Reilly's.

13. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Keep these at this time, as we find the best for this body, [949].

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
