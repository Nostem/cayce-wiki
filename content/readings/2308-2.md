# TEXT OF READING 2308-2 F 6

**Reading [[2308-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 6  ReadingID: 9078

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2175], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this is a developing body.

2. As we find, acute conditions exist as well as those more of a general nature, that need care and attention.

3. There has been a tendency for the lack of proper eliminations.  This, with the drying of the lymph flow through the colon, from the conditions of a general nature that exist in the cerebrospinal system, caused a clogging.

4. And then, with those activities from the food values taken, the acute condition was caused.  Thus the pain through the colon and especially in the left side, in that portion of the transverse and descending colon.

5. Some inflammation still exists.

6. Thus, we would begin first - while keeping the body quiet - to apply warm, upon a warm flannel cloth, an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]].  The activity of this is needed.  Change this rather often during the day.

7. Begin with small or broken doses of Castoria, that to which an extra amount of Syrup of Senna is added.  Pour out a teaspoonful from a regular size bottle of the compound and add a teaspoonful of Syrup of Senna.  Shake this thoroughly when it is mixed and each time before the dose is given, which would be about fifteen to twenty drops every half an hour - until there is the thorough elimination through the alimentary canal.

8. Should there be recurrent pains, as this begins to react upon the system, give an enema to start the flow from the alimentary canal.

9. These conditions that exist in the system arise from external pressures that have been produced.  There is in the upper cervical area the jamming of two segments.  This tends to deflect the flow of energies in the activity of the sympathetic and cerebrospinal nerve energies to supply to the alimentary canal; especially as related to the area in the upper portion of jejunum, and in the area of the lacteal ducts - just below the liver.

10. This causes quick pulsation, over-nervousness.

11. We would correct these osteopathically, for the corrections will prevent those conditions that may arise from the deflections in the brachial center, - as to arms, as well as to the side of face and eyelids, that are sympathetically governed by the impulses from these areas.

12. To be sure, coordinate the REST of the cerebrospinal system, especially from the 9th dorsal down, with those corrections made in the upper dorsal and cervical.

13. At least six to eight such adjustments should be made; making these at least twice each week.

14. Do not begin these until there is a cleansing of the alimentary canal, in the manner indicated.

15. Give only liquids and semi-liquids through this period of cleansing.

16. Keep the body quiet.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Is this a result of her fall two years ago from a second story window?
(A)  Pressure outside, - this contributed to same.  Do these things indicated, if we would bring the better conditions for this body.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] for Dr. Richardson.)
