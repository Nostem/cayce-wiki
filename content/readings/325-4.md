# TEXT OF READING 325-4 F 52

**Reading [[325-4]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/5/1924  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 2442

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by her son - Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M.                                         ..., Ky.

1. (Q)  LS:  What caused the hemorrhage that this body had?
(A)  EC:  Produced by the breaking of tissue under strain in the nasal cavity and in the upper portion of the throat.

2. (Q)  Where did it come from?
(A)  From the tissue as involved in the circulation accorded the system from the sensory organism.  Then we find this being both from the physical and mental condition of the body, and in this condition is the show of the reaction as taking place in the body from age and condition of the system, as has been given.  Had we had the properties as given in the system when this was given here before, this might have been prevented, unless the mental worries would have capitulated same.

3. (Q)  Is this an organic condition?
(A)  Functional.

4. (Q)  Is the medicine as prescribed now being taken into the system?
(A)  Not as given.  Neither will it be in accord with other properties as being given.  This then must of the individual necessity be determined, and follow only one or the other.

5. (Q)  What should be done for this body at the present time?
(A)  The properties as given, the rest and the other outline of [[Vibration|vibrations]] as has been given for the system.  [See 325-3.]

6. (Q)  How long will it take, with the treatment as given, to bring the body to its normal state?
(A)  Depending from what condition is judged as being normal with the present conditions being existent in the system.  To the normal state for the body itself will be in from one to two weeks.  To the normal condition as judged from a normal [[Physical Body|physical body]], with all conditions and the prelude to same considered, both from the biological and pathological conditions of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], from four to fourteen months.

7. (Q)  What should the body be careful of?
(A)  Physical strain, either from mental or physical exercises.

8. (Q)  What should the body eat?
(A)  Those properties as have been outlined for the body.  Nothing of high stimulations, yet at the present time stimulus of a character that is of the meteoric [mediocre?  medicoric?] that should be accorded until the system adjusts itself to conditions.

9. (Q)  What special electrical machine should be used and when?
(A)  Violet ray, as has been outlined, using a very light [[Vibration|vibration]], yet one that will give the accord to the system over the plexuses, as given.

10. (Q)  Was the prescription of medical properties properly filled?
(A)  Depending on which one is concerned.

11. (Q)  The one as given in the last reading.
(A)  Yes.

12. (Q)  What kind of water should the body drink?
(A)  That carrying elm.  Not too much lime, rather that as found in the elm.

13. (Q)  How much should the body drink?
(A)  The more water the body would drink the better.

14. (Q)  Should the violet ray treatments be given now or wait?
(A)  Should be given each day now.

15. (Q)  What are the changes in the body since the last reading was given?
(A)  There have been many changes both ways.  The correllating of the pressure as was given in the system in both that of the condition as exists in the system through the action of the sensory system, and of the mental and its attributes toward the physical in its direct connection with the action of the sensories in this correlation brought about the overflow of the circulation in these portions of the system, for with the connection as lies with the nerve center that traverses the whole system and is especially affected in the menstrual periods, with that of the perineurium nerves, and the center of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve, these have brought the conditions and the congestion that broke in the throat and nostrils.

Do as we have given, if we would bring the best.  The body must act of its own volition.
