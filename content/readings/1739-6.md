# TEXT OF READING 1739-6 M 36

**Reading [[1739-6]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/8/1930  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 7960

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1739] and wife, and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1739] - this we have had before.  Very good are the conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  There should be those clarifications in the blood supply.  See that with the food values, or with those properties taken, there is a clarification of the blood stream - especially as related to the capillary circulation. Quantities of the [[Sassafras|sassafras]] bark, or tea - or as medicinal properties - or sulphur and [[Baking Soda|soda]] or cream of tartar - THESE will clarify the better.  Keep the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and mental forces active and well balanced.  Keep the manipulations as will keep a normal balance, but not over-active as to same as to OVERDO the conditions for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How long should body remain at [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]?
(A)  That should depend more upon the body, than upon what would be given.  As long as it is felt that it's needed!

3. (Q)  Where is the best place for the body to live permanently after treatment has been given?
(A)  Where he chooses!  That that will make the most pleasant surroundings, that will make for the activities as is determined by the body within itself as that it should endeavor TO carry on!

4. (Q)  In what endeavor should the body spend his efforts which will be best suited for the body when he is back to normal?
(A)  Let's get back to normal first!

5. (Q)  How can body become thoroughly self-reliant?
(A)  Become reliant upon self and that which you would try to make self!  As the body would do to others, do so to them - self, see?

6. (Q)  Should the body be guided by suggestions made by relatives and friends?  or follow his own ideas in spite of everything?
(A)  If that towards ideas are in keeping with the [[Ideal|IDEALS]], follow those.  Have [[Ideal|IDEALS]], not IDEAS!  Ideas may be as thoughts, made criminal or [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]].  Be sure [[Ideal|the IDEAL]] is proper.  Follow that irrespective of outside influence.  Know self is right, and then go straight ahead.  So live each and every day that you may look any man in the face and tell him to go to hell!

7. (Q)  Is the body now ready to leave hospital?
(A)  If it had been, we wouldn't have given that the treatments should be carried on for the time being!

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
