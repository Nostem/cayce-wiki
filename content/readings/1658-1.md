# TEXT OF READING 1658-1 M 36 (Professor of Zoology, Quaker)

**Reading [[1658-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/15/1938  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 7761

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach Va]]., this 15th day of August, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1658], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1646].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time - with special reference to lung condition. Also you will give the cause of the existing conditions, with suggestions for help and relief for this body.  You will answer the questions that may be asked, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mr. [1658].

3. Now as we find, while conditions are very disturbing - and there is little resistance - if there is the ability to begin soon enough for resistance to be builded, by and through the applications of influences which we will give - there may be help, and the possible resuscitation for quite an active experience yet for this body, now.

4. The lung forces, of course, we find are VERY much honeycombed.  There is little to build upon - or the abilities of the system to be reoxidized, or the blood supply for the body.

5. If there is the ability, though, to keep these poisons from reacting upon the liver and the kidneys, or the poisons reverting to same, we may as we find be able to build a resistance that will be most helpful.

6. We would then, in the beginning, begin with these:

7. Prepare an [[Charred Oak Keg|oak keg]], charred inside - about a two gallon keg, from which there may be inhaled the fumes from Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].  Put an opening in same, and another small opening. In the larger opening put a tube, or cane, through which there may be inhaled the gases from the Brandy.  In the two gallon container put at least a gallon of Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]]. Best that the openings be put in the END, and the bung closed tight.  Remove the peg from the small opening when there is the attempt to inhale.  Do not inhale too deeply in the beginning.  Only the fumes from the Brandy are to be taken, to be sure, and this two or three times each day.  Begin with only one or two whiffs, when inhaling.  Keep the keg where it will cause evaporation, and the gas is that alone to be inhaled.

8. Also we would take Calcidin as a reaction to be had from the calcium as well as the [[Iodine|iodine]] in same.  So, dissolve at least three grains in an ounce of distilled water and keep this warm - not hot, not boiling but just warm, see, and covered. And during the day take sips from same, about every three hours taking just a sip - or not more than half a teaspoonful; taking the whole quantity in two or three days, see?

9. If there is indication of hemorrhage, take - under the direction of a physician* - about once or twice a week, if there is the spitting of blood or the indication of hemorrhage - three minims of Compound Tincture of Benzoin dissolved with - or dissolve in same one-sixtieth of a grain of Codeine.  Dissolve one-sixtieth of a grain of Codeine in three minims of Compound Tincture of Benzoin, see?

10. We would keep the diets of strengthening foods.  Prepare [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].  Take lean meat - beef - fresh, see?  dice in about half inch squares.  Put in a glass jar - at least a pound of same - no fat whatsoever - no water in the jar.  Have the top on the jar but not real tight.  Put the jar in a container of cold water, with a cloth in the bottom to prevent jar from cracking.  Allow to boil for about two and a half to three hours.  Strain off the juice.  Take this in sips - half a teaspoonful about every thirty minutes, or until the appetite is satisfied.  This may be kept up so long as it agrees with the body - and it will agree if the fat is not allowed to be in same.  It may be kept cool and heated as desired or needed, but do not keep the same quantity - even in the ice

* [Note:  Partial paragraph left out of Reading:

"If there is the indication of hemorrhage, take - under the direction of a physician - a compound prepared in this manner:

Oil of Eucalyptus..................1 minim,
Oil of Turp......................1/2 minim,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.......1 minim.

("No, leave this off - we would take it rather in this form: Dissolve one-sixtieth grain of Codeine in three minims of Compound Tincture of Benzoin, and take this about once or twice a week if there is spitting of blood or the indication of hemorrhage.  Leave off the Eucalyptus and the other ingredients as just indicated, see; for the stomach won't carry this, but it will carry the Compound Tincture of Benzoin with the Codeine in same.")] 

box - OVER two days, but make fresh, see?

11. Also we would use [[Calcios|Calcios]], a small quantity - about the size of a quarter, about a quarter of an inch thick - or as much as may be taken on the end of a case knife - spread on a Graham or Whole Wheat Cracker or wafer.  This would be taken with the meal, once or twice daily, depending upon the ability of assimilation.

12. Keep quiet.

13. Keep cool.

14. And bathe off the body with a little grain alcohol; not wood alcohol nor other characters but GRAIN alcohol in the water when the body is sponged off.  This will also strengthen the body.

15. Do these for three weeks, as we find; and then we would give further instructions.

16. Ready for questions - Mr. [1658]

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Mr. [1646]
"    " Ass'n file

> hence be a living demonstration of the power of God to
> overcome evil.  I am always glad to hear from thee but
> suggest thee be wise in conserving all thy strength for the
> battle.  Please feel free to write to me as much and as
> often as thee desires.
> 
> Faithfully thine, [1646]

> 
> 
> Government of Puerto Rico
> UNIVERSITY OF PUERTO RICO
> College of Agriculture and Mechanic Arts
> Mayaguez, P.R.
> 
> Box ..., ..., Mass.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> Your good letter of the 16th arrived yesterday.  To be frank,
> the reading raised in me principally a fear that if the
> instructions were carried out they would just prolong my days
> of torture in this world.  And when I say torture I mean, to
> some extent, physical torture, but to a much greater extent
> mental and [[Soul|soul]] torture, for ever since my third attack of
> pneumonia (last Oct.) which brought on this T.B., just one
> torture after another (mostly only very indirectly connected
> with the illness) have been brought upon me.  I would have
> ended this physical existence long ago if it had not occurred
> to me that maybe all this was either some sort of a
> punishment of God or a test of His to try me out.  I may yet
> find that necessary, as now in some of the more recent
> tortures other dear ones are beginning to be involved as
> well.  As long as they concern me alone I could grin and
> bear, but I can't stand it to see my dear ones suffer on my
> account (relatives and adopted family).
> 
> You perhaps realized in the reading something of the length
> of this illness.  I have not been well since 1922.  Then I
> got pernicious malaria, which caused most diverse symptoms
> and was most difficult to cure.  Then I just got over that
> when in Jan., 1925 I came down with my 2nd attack of
> pneumonia (double pneumonia, accompanied by pleurisy with
> effusions, which left adhesions).  Ever since then I have run
> fevers, with some mysterious, apparently fungus infection, in
> the chest, and probably incipient t.b. as well.  But all that
> kept getting gradually better, and I was able to do more and
> more work (though always very subject to violent grippy colds
> and influenza), until in October, 1937 I came down with a
> violent attack of influenza, which developed into pneumonia
> the 2nd day, and the t.b. developed after that, although not
> particularly active until late April, when it suddenly flared
> into the open and raced ahead in two weeks as much as it
> usually does in many months.  Since June my decline has been
> quite slow, due to the great inherent resistance to disease
> which our family (and particularly I) possess.  I have had
> some 7 or 8 illnesses which would have killed almost anyone
> else, and scores of more ordinary ones.  (Once I lost 5
> quarts of blood in hemorrhage from tonsil operation, in 1929
> after chest trouble had started).  But since this last attack
> of pneumonia I have felt I could never be well again.  I
> don't believe I want to be cured unless it can be really
> cured, as really I am tired, very tired, of these illnesses
> and other tortures, and it would seem pretty good to go [to]
> the place He has prepared for us.  It must be a wonderful
> place.
> 
> Nevertheless, I am following your instructions for awhile,
> believing that they MAY offer a real cure.  But as yet, I
> have not been able to put them fully into effect, as we have
> not been able despite exhaustive search in Worcester [?] and
> Boston, to obtain the Keg yet.  [...], a Puerto Rican boy,
> the one of my adopted family who is with me now, and the only
> person here in whom I can have complete confidence, due to
> the old-fashioned ideas and non-understanding characters of
> my own relatives, has spent days in its quest, and has
> finally been promised one of slightly larger than 2-gallon
> size for Monday.  Perhaps with increased contents it will be
> all right.  Possibly until then the sips of pure cane rum
> prescribed by my Boston specialist will partly take it's
> place, but I intend to do all you say as soon as I can.
> Also, the [[Calcios|Calcios]] has not arrived from Long Island yet.  In
> case of hemorrhage (which I have not had so far) in case
> my doctor doesn't want to cooperate, I could make up
> your prescriptions myself, as I always have quite a bit of
> codeine on hand, prescribed in pure form by the Dr. for
> reducing cough paroxysms, and I believe the other ingredients
> would not be difficult to obtain.
> 
> Well, all I want is for His will to be done, and I can't help
> but feeling that out of all this mess it will.
> 
> Please pardon for troubling with all these details.
> 
> With best wishes,
> 
> Sincerely yours, [1658]

> your having given me so much of your time and for Hugh's

> B2. 8/12/38 [1658]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> I am enclosing blank for Associate Membership and asking for
> physical reading, at [1646]'s insistance [Mr. [1646] was Mr.
> [1658]'s Quaker-Spiritualist friend].  I have made the
> decision to ask for this after some hesitation, as I must
> admit I have always been much of a skeptic in these psychic
> powers and cures, not that they are possible (because with
> God all things are possible), but that they actually do take
> place.  But [1646]'s great faith has increased mine, and
> possibly now I have sufficient to have the reading be of
> use.  I certainly believe strongly it is the power of God to
> produce all cures and do all things when it is His will.  I
> would be happy to pass on to the greater and better Life if
> it is His will, but I would also be very happy if He cared
> to let me serve Him longer in this world, and as I feel I
> have important work to do here it is worth making the
> attempt; though the doctors don't expect me to live over a
> few days or weeks, and I am panting like an exhausted race
> horse to get the necessary air from the pieces of lungs that
> still function.
> 
> I will be very appreciative, if thru Him, you are able to do
> anything.
> 
> Sincerely, [1658]
> 
> B3. 8/15/38 Extract from Mr. [1658]'s letter to Mr. [1646]:
> 
> 
> "I have not been well for 17 years.  Before 1925 I suffered
> terribly from pernicious malaria.  That disappeared, and then
> that year I had double pneumonia and pleurisy with adhesions
> (at Ithaca, N.Y.).  That left me with a fungus infection of
> the chest which has always kept me on the verge of being an
> invalid, and made me very susceptible to influenza, etc.
> Then in Oct. 1937 I came down with another attack of
> pneumonia.  I was not expected to scrape thru, but did,
> thanks to the loving administrations of my adopted family.
> But it left me with this trouble, at first not very active,
> but becoming violently so about the latter part of April.  I
> tell thee all this before receiving the Reading, to see how
> any of it may check with anything in the Reading, in case any
> of these points are mentioned.
> 
> "After all that I have been through I would really be
> overwhelmed with joy if [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] should summon me to the
> better life and deliver me from these manifold troubles, but
> I don't want to be a coward, and if there is more work for me
> to do in this world I want to do it, and in case I could be
> relieved of some of these troubles I would go ahead really
> joyously with it.  I am [[Prayer|praying]] that some indication may
> appear to show God's will in the matter.  Maybe the results
> of the Reading will help."
