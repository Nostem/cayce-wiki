# TEXT OF READING 5317-1 F 69

**Reading [[5317-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1944  Sex: F  Age: 69  ReadingID: 13456

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Miss [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. [3211].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5317].

3. As we find, there are disturbances.  These, the basis, are poor eliminations.  These come from the crystallization of acids through the system, causing contractions and pressures in muscular and tendon forces and in the extremities arthritic reactions.  These, then, are the disorders.

4. We would begin first, for the body, to take internally for at least thirty days a teaspoonful of Eno Salts each morning before any meal is taken.

5. When this has been taken for about two weeks, we would then begin with an oil [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine.  This should be given at least twice each week.  Use equal portions of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the spine, as well as from the finger tips to the body, along the arms and from the toes to the body, along the sciatic centers, as well as in foot, ankle, knee and along the limbs where there are the sciatic nerve and muscular forces.

6. This we would do each time the application is made of the short-wave electrical treatments, and follow with these massages.  The short-wave should not be given stronger than three amperes nor longer than ten minutes at a time.  Follow these with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], keeping up for at least thirty days the small broken doses of Eno Salts.

7. Then begin with Eno Salts only about once a week, but take small doses of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] daily, a half to three-quarters of a teaspoonful.  Take this twice or three times each day, keeping up the massage at least once a week or once in ten days with the treatments, and we should eliminate these disturbances from this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
