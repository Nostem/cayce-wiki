# TEXT OF READING 4523-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4523-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/29/1911  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12401

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce this 29th day of November, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Jno. C. King, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:30 A. M.                                 ..., [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have him here.  Yes, there is a good deal the matter here with this body.  We have trouble over the face, over the trunk of the body and the limbs - the whole business, the whole organism.

2. (Q)  Tell us what it is.
(A)  The first condition we had in the body here from times back was produced from the trouble of pains in the side that produced the conditions we had in the liver.  Then the action that has been produced on the body itself from the exterior portions has brought about other conditions that we have through the system here.  The inactivity of these parts of the body then producing the abnormal condition between that of the circulation through the hepatic and the lymphatic circulation in the system, to the lungs, face and other portions of the body, brought to the abnormal portions first produced in the system.

3. (Q)  What was the beginning of this trouble?
(A)  The condition here in the side.

4. (Q)  What is that condition?
(A)  The condition is there now; the condition from the first beginning of it here, from times back, first produced the condition that we had here from inflammation carried into the system from this pain or adhesion formed in the side here.  This carried into the system here through this through the absorbtion of the system to the eliminating powers and carried to the parts until the set up a seat of trouble here through the liver, and through the action of this to the other organs, and this then carried into the system has produced the inactivity of the unison between the circulatory system, that is, the hepatics and lymphatics in the distributing of the red and white, or eliminating principles of the used forces, or the excess as thrown out from the liver from the inactivity, produced the condition where these are carried back and forth in the system as shown in the body.

5. (Q)  Where is the pain located at present?
(A)  In the right hand side of the jaw and along in the body here from the 4th to 9th dorsal, at the left side here.

6. (Q)  How do the symptoms show themselves?
(A)  By the condition of the blood; in its bloated condition and the abrasion of the skin here, you see, at times over the body; by the tightness of feeling of deadness to the upper portion of the body to the right hand side, and the soreness to the head or base of the brain; ligthness to the trunk portion of the body, heaviness at the time he ought to go to sleep.

7. (Q)  What treatment would you prescribe to relieve these conditions?
(A)  By removal of the condition in the system that of active principles to these parts.  In the removal of these of taking into the system those parts which remove through that of a dross to the system in the proper elimination of powers in itself, as we will find through these of bromides of [[Baking Soda|soda]], with those of sulfate through the intestinal tract, with the electrical forces applied to the spine, or along the spine to remove the nerve forces, and produce a unison between the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic nerve system.
