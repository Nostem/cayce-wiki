# TEXT OF READING 1147-1 F 52

**Reading [[1147-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/15/1936  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 6447

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 15th day of April, 1936, in accordance with request made by Mr. [1113], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Nathan Sachs and daughter, and [1147]''s son-in-law.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1147].

2. Now, as we find, conditions that surround the body and the conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body in the present DO NOT allow for these changes, those alterations in the administering to the better welfare of the body in the present; other than that which IS being administered in the present.

3. Then, as we find, it would be better that those conditions, those circumstances that arise in the physical reactions, be carried on in the manner that has been outlined for this body.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is wrong there?
(A)  As given and intimated, those conditions are understood by those administering.  To alter or to change these in the present would be harmful rather than for helpful or hopeful conditions.

6. (Q)  Is there any cure for the body?
(A)  Those administrations that are being made are the more helpful; not curing - these are only helpful and only may maintain those conditions about the body for the best in the present.

7. (Q)  What has caused this condition?
(A)  These have arisen from the general activities and the infectious forces that have arisen in the general activities of the body, from those sources as come from without to those periods of activity in the development for this body.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to [...] [her son-in-law]
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. GD's note:  Mrs. [1147]'s son-in-law, who was present, said
> she had arthritis, couldn't get about, the doctor considered
> it incurable.  He couldn't understand why if we had had a
> successful treatment for another arthritis case [951], no
> details were given for Mrs. [1147].  This comment was made
> following the reading and not preceding it.  [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]]
> later talked with the son-in-law who explained that he had
> talked with Mrs. [1147]'s doctor before the reading was given
> and that he had been very antagonistic and uncooperative.
> [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] felt this was the reason for the reading being so
> brief and non-committal.
