# TEXT OF READING 1010-3 F 56

**Reading [[1010-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/17/1928  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 6090

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1928, in accordance with request made through Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes.  In many ways we find the conditions show improvements, yet apparently there are other conditions arising - and that have arisen in the body - that cause undue alarm, especially in the application of the manipulations with the system in the condition from medicinal properties given so that other elemental conditions become involved; such as the filling up, or the inability of the body to swallow properly, or a fullness in the throat, larynx - and the body feels a burning sensation produced then from the lack of assimilation in the system from properties given, see?  Then as the condition has been reduced or relieved in the eliminating, and as assimilation is being overcrowded, then there is seen - unless these are made to balance - these would produce a reaction of more to be eliminated than the system would be able to handle without undue stress put on the body.  Hence we will find that by cutting down on medicinal properties as administered, and only that of the massage, the manipulation and especially of light rays applied to the cerebro-spinal system, these will produce a better assimilation by the gentle massage or neuropathic massage downward on the spine following such application of lights.  Keep those properties for massage as has been given.

3. Let the diet be that only as is easily assimilated, but strengthening.  When stimulants are given (and these may be in the form of any alcoholic beverage that is of the mild enough nature to produce the proper digestion within the body), Spirits Frumenti with egg would be well for the body, but BURN the Spirits Frumenti before mixing with the egg and milk, see?  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What causes dryness and thickness at root of tongue and what should be done for it?
(A)  This may be reduced by the manipulation or by the application of cold at the throat or at the back of neck, though keep massage following same.  We are through for the present.

> wouldn't be here, now, if it hadn't been for your readings.
> 
> "I knew nothing about neuropathical massages but as she did,
> I followed her advice and have given her 3 every day since

> little each day now and think she will be able to stay up in
> another week.
>
