# TEXT OF READING 900-421 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-421]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/12/1929  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5624

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of January, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Thos. B. House, and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], ..., N.Y. City, and his anxiety concerning a note executed by Kramer, due February 8, 1929.  You will give [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] any information regarding this that will be of assistance to him.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before - as well as the anxiety concerning those conditions as respecting [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of body with Kramer. Information has been given concerning this, and of the desire Kramer to settle same - which will, as we find, be taken care of in the manner as has been designated.  As for the papers, look to that one HANDLING same - or who has ATTEMPTED to handle same - for these are in HIS possession.  We are through.
