# TEXT OF READING 808-12 F 32

**Reading [[808-12]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/28/1940  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 4874

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [808], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are still those tendencies, those inclinations for the upsetting in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, from the lack of coordination in the impulses in the sensory system and the organs of same; and the fuzziness in the blood supply, as might be said produced by the poor [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reactions.

4. If those suggestions that have been indicated would be followed along those lines outlined, we would have the better results.

5. To be sure, the activities from the proposed injections, - while they are rather caustic in their nature and reaction, - would prove helpful.

6. It would be well to begin with the smaller dose; one cc. administered often enough, and watching the effects until the system is being reacted upon by same, - and then judge as to the quantity and the periods it would be well to keep same, until there IS that reaction from the blocking, or the caustic reaction as SHOULD be the effect upon the genital activities, from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the pelvis as well as the other portions of the body itself.

7. We would do that, then, in that way and manner.

8. And as for the periods of rest, a little exercise will be necessary; but within ten days the body should be able to return to regular activities which have been indicated.

9. Then keep the precautions and be in the open, and have the activities which have been indicated.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Should these injections be given together with the former instructions through this channel?
(A)  Former instructions as to diet and as to activities.

12. (Q)  Have had sore throat which refuses to clear up.  What should I do to eliminate this condition entirely?
(A)  This as we find is a part of the same reaction, you see; and the caustic reaction of the injections should be helpful; but a good thorough relaxation, as from a good masseuse or masseur, would be better, - with the general rubs for the superficial circulation.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
