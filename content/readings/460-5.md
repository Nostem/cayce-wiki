# TEXT OF READING 460-5 F 25

**Reading [[460-5]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/27/1933  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 3359

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. DeVera Kaplan, 20 Ridgeway Ave., West Orange, N.J., this 27th day of November, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - through her mother, Mrs. [325], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Fannie [[David Kahn|Kahn]]; DeVera, Jane and Sol Kaplan.]

Time of Reading 
8:35 to 8:40 A. M. Eastern standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind.  Questions as to Physical 
condition, associations and connections.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body; this we have had before.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Did the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] cause the constant menstrual dripping?
(A)  No.  The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] will be HELPFUL to any of the eliminations and the functionings of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], if taken properly!

3. (Q)  What causes the breaking out at times on back and face?
(A)  Improper coordination in the distribution of eliminations in system.  This may be corrected by taking massages and sweats, and corrections or adjustments along the spinal system.

4. (Q)  Why wasn't the condition corrected from following the treatments outlined in the last reading?
(A)  Because they weren't carried on through!

5. (Q)  What should be done for her better health now?
(A)  Just as given.  These we would take sometimes twice a month, sometimes once a month.  Have the adjustments of the whole cerebrospinal system, so that all the sistrolic [systolic?] movements of the muscular forces with the eliminating forces of the system COORDINATE.

6. (Q)  Was the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] taken correctly?
(A)  Read just what we have said a few minutes ago!

7. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
