# TEXT OF READING 187-2 M 20 MONTHS

**Reading [[187-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/10/1926  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 698

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 10th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [199].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement and cure of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, this we have had before. [See 199-1 on his mother before his birth, and Life Reading 187-1.]

3. The physical conditions in this body are very good at the present time, except of the over acid as exists throughout the system and the excess of eliminations as being shown through the capillary circulation by this acid in system, and the effect as same is producing on the eliminations of the body, especially in capillary circulation.

4. Well that the diet be looked after closely through the warm season, using, when possible, cool (not cold) enemas, to relieve the condition in the colon.  Also dusting of the body over the abrasions, where heat shows on the body, with Stearate of Zinc powders, see?

5. Chalk, Magnesia, or Petrolagar, may be well to give the body in small quantities, keeping the system, or the stomach, sweet with small doses of any of those conditions that bring the normal digestion in system - Castoria, or small quantities of a combination of Senna and Yellow Saffron Tea, and small quantities of elm water.  All of these would be good for the body, through the various stages of the condition as existent.  Each would tend to reduce also the acid in the system.

6. The diet consisting, chiefly, of those of orange juice, fruit juices (not too acid, see?), of cereal, as of dry, and the liquid from any vegetables that have been well, WELL cooked - these would be good for the body.

7. These will bring normal conditions and keep the body in its regular developing stage; looking carefully after the condition as exists by the formation of teeth - so that necessary for the Chalk, as at times, in system, see? Magnesia will be well, as given.

8. We are through for the present.
