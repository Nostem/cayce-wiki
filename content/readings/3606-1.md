# TEXT OF READING 3606-1 F 20

**Reading [[3606-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/28/1944  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 11103

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Indiana.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - the body prays.

3. We have the body here, [3606].

4. As we find, there are disturbances that are gradually becoming more and more aggravating to the physical and mental activities of the body.  These are very definite as to their sources.

5. In times back, just before puberty, the body had an accident on skates and the end of the spine - the coccyx, 1st segment - was injured.  From this, as there came the menstrual periods, disturbances arose and reflexes to the medulla oblongata have been uncontrolled.

6. These, then, are not in the form of spasms but the loss of control of the reflexes of the body.  To be sure, these occur the more often closer to the menstrual periods; thus in a manner they are associated or connected with same.

7. We would take those measures to have this segment replaced - don't remove it, but have it placed in its proper position, and correct those lesions that have been formed through the areas of the lower sacrals and through the lumbar areas, also breaking up the tendencies for those lesions in the lacteal duct area.

8. Have the corrections made osteopathically in the coccyx, sacral and lumbar areas, coordinating the upper dorsals and cervicals with same.

9. At the same period, begin using a tonic made from the Passion Flower.  Put a gallon (by measure) of the leaf, the fruit and the vine of the Passion Flower in an enamel container, with an enamel or glass top - not aluminum nor any other metal. Add two gallons of distilled water.  Reduce by slow, not heavy or fast boiling, until reduced, when strained to one quart.  Then add sufficient grain alcohol as a preservative, which by volume would be six ounces.

10. Take a teaspoonful of this each day until the whole quantity is taken.

11. Do these and we will relieve this body entirely.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Will this tend to ward off the attacks?
(A)  As indicated, it should remove the causes, not only ward them off, but do away with them.

14. (Q)  Would you suggest a physician to administer these treatments?
(A)  Any good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

15. (Q)  Is it safe for me to have children while in this condition?
(A)  This would have nothing to do with the affliction for children by this body, but until the conditions are changed, it wouldn't be very well or safe, for the body or the child.

16. (Q)  Is it indicated how long it might take for me to be cured, if these treatments are followed?
(A)  Whether it's a day or a year, do something about it! And prepare self to use the opportunities offered by not being disturbed in body and mind by the condition.

17. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Loose-leaf book in Library for indexing

(1/28/44 GD's note:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. T. R. Wirsing, 1001 Third St., S.W., Roanoke, Va., who can prepare the compound for you if you are unable to obtain same locally.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/22/43 Mother's letter to EC:  "In the Coronet for
> September, I read an article concerning your work, and I
> write to ascertain whether or not you can give a reading
> without the party in question being present.
> 
> "This is my problem:
> 
> "My daughter, now twenty years of age and married one year,
> has been afflicted with epilepsy, her first seizure occuring
> when she was thirteen years of age.  She has been taking the
> usual course of treatment which the average doctor gives,
> namely phenobarbital and bromides.  She has been taking this
> treatment for about five or six years.  The child became
> moody, attempted suicide on several occasions, was depressed,
> would tell of incidents that never occurred.  Recently, she
> had occasion to see a doctor because of an eye infection,
> and the doctor immediately asked her what she was taking, and
> thought she had come for 'dope'.  He talked with her after
> being assured that that was not what she came for, and
> gave her treatment for her eye, also asked her to stop
> the treatment she had been taking, as it was impairing her
> health, also her eyesight.  She promised him faithfully that
> she would not take the treatment and to this date she has not
> taken any medicine, and she says she feels like a different
> person, she looks better, and in fact is more like she used
> to be.  The doctor told her that 'of course you will jerk and
> have seizures,' but he seemed to think they would wear off.
> The child does jerk, but she hasn't been home long enough
> I imagine to have a seizure, as they usually appear at her
> menstrual period.  I am training myself not to think of her
> affliction, but think of her as being perfect.  Her husband
> has been sent to the West coast, and she returned home, (of
> course he is in the service) for the time being.
> 
> "If there is anything you can do for her or be of help to
> her, I shall be forever grateful to you and [[Lord's Prayer|our Father]], for
> your work certainly is a gift from Him, and I shall also be
> happy to remunerate you for your kindness."
> Sincerely,  Mrs. [...]
> 
> B2. 11/30/43 Mother's letter to EC:  "I herewith enclose check
> for $20.00, together with application for Psychic Reading.
> 
> "I have carefully read the literature you sent me some weeks
> ago, also 'A Series of case Studies of Psychic Information
> on Epilepsy', prepared by your Association.  I take note
> that one case was given a reading and helped, altho was not
> present.  I delayed writing you on receipt of the literature
> in the hope that I could make the trip to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] and
> bring my daughter with me, but as matters stand today, and
> the over crowded trains, my daughter suggested that I write
> for a reading to be given in her absence.
> 
> "As I wrote you sometime ago, my daughter is, as the doctor
> diagnosed it afflicted with epilepsy, and the last month
> has had gall bladder attacks, altho she is only twenty years
> of age.  May I ask that she be given a Reading as soon as
> possible, or an 'emergency reading', as she does not want
> to lose any more time at her work.
> 
> "Please may I ask to hear from you at your very earliest
> convenience as to when she may have a reading.  She is in
> full sympathy with this work and is desirous of receiving
> help.
> 
> "Thanking you for all that you may do for my daughter, and
> may God bless your work, I am, yours"  Sincerely, Mrs. [...]
> 
> B3. 1/18/44 Mother's letter to EC:  "I herewith enclose
> 'Appointment slip' received in yours of recent date, which
> I trust is properly filled in, together with questions my
> daughter [3606] would like answered in the reading.
> 
> "In this connection I would like to say that I don't like to
> term her affliction epilepsy, as some doctors have said it
> wasn't that, and others said it was.  However, I do want to
> say, and perhaps it will give you a better idea of the case,
> that even on her best days her arms will jerk or shake, and
> her feet also will jerk if she is laying down, and if she is
> standing at times her knees seem to give way and sometimes
> she will fall to the floor, and at those times, if she is
> talking, her conversation is cut off.
> 
> "Now this all happens in just an instant and then she goes
> on as tho nothing happened, but yesterday morning she was so
> jerky that she couldn't go to her position.  At the present
> time she is not under a doctor's care except for the gall
> bladder trouble which she experienced last month.
> 
> "I have written this in detail, thinking perhaps it may have
> some bearing on the reading.
> 
> "Then at her menstrual periods her trouble is more apparent,
> she may have seizures which last a few minutes, her whole
> body jerking at the time, and saliva flowing from her
> mouth, recently she has had as many as [[Trinity|three in one]] day.
> 
> "We will keep the hour with you as you suggest.
> 
> "Inasmuch as you say 'sign full name', I had her sign her
> name [3606] in place of Mrs. [...].
> 
> "Trusting in God that you may be the channel thru which my
> daughter may be healed, I am,"  Sincerely,  Mrs. [...]
> 
> B4. Questions submitted:  "Will I be cured and how long will it
> be until I am cured?  Is there any certain doctor I should
> go to for treatment and where is he to be found?  Why is the
> trouble more apparent at the menstrual period than at any
> other time, and what should be done at that time to ward
> off the attacks?  If I should have children would they be
> afflicted as I am and is it safe for me to have children?
> Does the marital relationship bring on attacks?"
