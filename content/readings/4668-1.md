# TEXT OF READING 4668-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4668-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1917 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/2/1917  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12608

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Selma, Ala., this 2nd day of May, 1917, in accordance with telegraphic request made by relatives (?), Mr. & Mrs. [...] via [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] (?).

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                      [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. EC:  In times before some trouble with intestines body [4668].  Conditions brought about by treatment produced through action of liver and action of gall ducts.  Reflexes brought back to the action in the circulation through hepatics that carries back into system.  Those produced should be carried off by secretions of the liver, hence nervous condition at times, in turn set up condition we have had in pelvis before, along lumbars acting through sympathetics, produces condition at times to head, produces these pains, temple, eyes and fore part of head.  To remove these conditions, the body should be taken to a higher climate, give to body more oxygen through air that will produce to blood pressure and cells renewed cellular forces to rebuild.  Treat regularly by exercise and manipulation by hand.  Eat such things as easily digested without aid of gall ducts, meats without fatty parts such as wild game, also milk, eggs, cereal, fruits.  Edgar Cayce

[Note:  Above reading text was transcribed on a Postal Telegraph - Cable Company form, and was sent as a telegram night letter collect.]
