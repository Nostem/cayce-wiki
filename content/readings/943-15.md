# TEXT OF READING 943-15 M 40

**Reading [[943-15]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/18/1931  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 5815

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [943].

[Edger Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Room ..., 
Time of Reading                         Hotel Victoria, 
6:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       7th Ave. at 51st St., 
[[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [943] - this we have had before. Now, we find there are some changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same.  We find that there is the tendency for the forces as produced irritation to continue in that of gas forming in the system, and hence the uneasiness and soreness as is felt.  There is NOT inflammation in the appendix itself, rather that of an intestinal inflammation. Were those properties taken as given, with the non-acid or more alkalin diet - so that there would be a cleansing OF that that has PRODUCED the inflammation, this would be all that would be necessary.  Should there continue the inflammation, and the appendix itself BECOME inflamed, operation - but it ISN'T that condition in the present.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What causes pains in stomach and intestines?
(A)  Didn't you just see what was said?  Gas forming from the condition in the intestine, produced by over-acid condition - see?

4. (Q)  Has he been having the proper diet?
(A)  Would there be inflammation if it was cleansed?  or with the proper diet would there have been PRODUCING acid, or gases?

5. (Q)  Please give proper diet.
(A)  We have given this!  Do that, as we have given!  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n

> to see him through this and try to be a good soldier, but

> hardly know what I'm about.
>

> parties to try to see what he can do to raise some money.
