# TEXT OF READING 2491-3 F 70

**Reading [[2491-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/4/1941  Sex: F  Age: 70  ReadingID: 9374

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2491], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2491], sister and niece.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are some improvements.  Though these in the present may appear to be very slight, the conditions are of such natures that it requires patience and persistence.

4. We would keep all of those things that have been suggested.

5. In the present we would add medicinal properties to be taken. Make two solutions, in this manner, and keep separate until the dosage is to be taken:

6. Add 2 grains Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to 1 ounce of Distilled Water. This would be one solution.

7. Add one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of Distilled Water.  This would be the other solution.

8. About twice each week, preferably in the evening, put 1 drop of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] and 2 drops of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution in half a glass of water and take.

9. Then the rest we would keep consistently, persistently; and DO the things indicated - that there may come better conditions for the body.

10. The Gold will tend to react upon the activity of the eliminations.  Thus this will necessitate that there be used the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] occasionally; using a colon tube. When such is done, use a level teaspoonful of table salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] dissolved thoroughly to each quart of water, and have the water body-temperature.  In the last quart and a half of rinse water put a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

11. Do these, and we will keep in line for bettering the conditions for this body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  After second treatment from second re-fill of the Appliance, there was a yellow discoloration on the body.  Was this normal, or am I getting too much of the mineral?
(A)  This is normal.  It is why, now, the mineral is to be given internally also, see?

14. (Q)  Is it helping me as it should?
(A)  It WILL help as it should.

15. (Q)  What is cause and relief for swelling and stiffness of joints of my hands?
(A)  Poisons and the poor circulation.  The rubbing of the body, and especially the extremities from the ends TOWARD the body with the Peanut Oil - or the combination of Peanut Oil and Olive Oil - will be beneficial.

16. (Q)  Why do I have difficulty in swallowing?  Please give relief.
(A)  This should be materially aided as conditions are helped.  This is a nervous reaction.

17. (Q)  Why do I still have the acid and bitter taste in mouth?
(A)  The inability of the system to eliminate the quantities of poisons and accumulations that have caused the paralysis agitan, as well as the pressure upon the nerve forces of the body.  It will require time, and the precautions not to do or eat those things that produce MORE.

18. (Q)  What can be done to restore my equilibrium, so that I can walk straight?
(A)  Just the constant use of all those things as have been indicated.

19. (Q)  For the past two weeks the agitan (paralysis) in my hands has been worse.  Why?
(A)  This is the beginning, as it were, of the breaking up; and naturally there is greater irritation as the resistances are broken before the body gets its equilibrium.

20. (Q)  Is there any goitre trouble?
(A)  No goitre trouble.  There IS trouble with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, but the internal activity of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] as indicated here should aid, as well as the rubs.

21. (Q)  Recently the nausea has returned.  Why is this?
(A)  Continue to use consistently all of those things indicated and it should be eliminated.

22. (Q)  Has Dr. Hudgins been giving the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments correctly?
(A)  These are very good.  But we would keep more of these in the present, as least; and keep up the better eliminations.

23. (Q)  Why do I not feel better at this time?
(A)  Because the body is responding in such a way as to be feeling worse.

24. (Q)  Why are my knees still so stiff?
(A)  The POISONS, as we have indicated, that have settled in the nerve ends and in the joints and the sinews.  These must be a gradual change.

25. (Q)  Why do I feel so chilly in the early forenoon, and shake worse?
(A)  This is the body attempting to attain, by the mental activities, the normal equilibrium of impulse through the system; and it is a natural consequence of conditions as they change in the body.

26. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Keep to the being purposeful and using the abilities to help others.

27. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
