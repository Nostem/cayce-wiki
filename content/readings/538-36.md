# TEXT OF READING 538-36 F 54

**Reading [[538-36]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/4/1934  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 3772

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 4th day of June, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [538]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions in some respects are progressing very satisfactorily for improvements.  While there has not been the breaking loose of the lesions in the area of the 4th and 5th rib in the lung, the general resistances have been builded considerably, and there is more resistance in the body.

3. We would keep much of that which has been outlined, though we would - after the next two or three manipulations - REST a while, then change these when the next series would be begun, which should be in two to three weeks from the time the period of rest is begun.

4. In the activities, be mindful that cold or congestion does not enter in.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  How may these lesions be broken up?
(A)  As indicated, it would be better that changes be made after the period of rest; and then it may be given as to the manner in which the resistances - that are gradually being built up - may be used as the measures for overcoming these conditions.  See?

This may be changed, to be sure, but - as we find - it would be deeper manipulations, not so much over the area itself but to BUILD those portions where the body REPLENISHES itself, see?

7. (Q)  Is the blood building up?
(A)  This has been given; the blood is being built up - the general resistance, that's the blood, you see.  There's no resistance except in the blood supply, that only that is assimilated and supplied there; and if you haven't it in your blood you are in a poor fix!

8. (Q)  Should all the other treatments be continued?
(A)  As given, all the other treatments continued.

9. (Q)  Please give the exact time of the physical birth of this body on Feb. 22, 1880.  [See 538-30, Par. 13-A indicating [[Soul|soul]] birth was 3-4 P.M.]
(A)  Yes.  7:58 in the morning, Central Standard Time; not Eastern time nor exact sun time.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
