# TEXT OF READING 5067-2 M 52 (Policeman, Chief of

**Reading [[5067-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/5/1944  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 13190

Identification, Quaker) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Delaware.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5067]; this we have had before.

3. With those changes wrought in the body, there are opportunities; while these may only be prolonging, we would continue with the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] with the green glass.

4. We would also, as there are those hindrances, use the radio-short-wave.  Begin to use it only about twice a week.  Do have a general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]], eliminating treatment following same.  Because the multiple short wave is not available or in accord, use the radio-short-wave and give then not a stimulating treatment, but a relaxing and eliminating treatment.

5. As the currents of the body are changed by this radio-short wave, then let the manipulations be such that the drosses, the eliminating centers, lungs, heart, liver, kidneys, respiratory system, perspiratory system, do eliminate poisons.  And let nature, with the life giving forces by a spiritual attitude, bring help.

6. We are through with this reading.  [Note:  In most cases when EC reached the end of a reading he would say:  "Ready for questions".  In this case, however, he did not do so.]

Two copies to Wife (one for Dr. George) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 6/6/44 Dr. George's letter:  "Mr. [5067] I am afraid, is
> about to expire.  His kidneys have entirely collapsed, his
> heart action has become weak, and I believe that within a few
> hours he will go into a coma and die.  I am very sorry, but I
> want you to know that Mrs. [5057] and I are very grateful for
> all your interest and help."
> 
> R2. 7/27/44 Wife's letter:  "As soon as it is available, I
> would appreciate receiving 2 copies of the minutes of the
> Congress held recently.  I found the meetings intensely
> interesting, and stimulating, and my visit there was so
> enjoyable that I plan to spend the remaining week of my
> vacation in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], from July 29 to August 6.  I
> hope you will be able to give me a few minutes, so that I may
> prevail upon you to read my [[Aura|aura]]...  Also, I would like to
> browse in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]]'s library, and perhaps present to
> it some of my husband's books, if they are acceptable."
> 
> R3. 8/8/44 IMG's letter:  "After my telephone conversation
> with you when I said that Mr. Cayce would be out of town,
> we have been expecting you to visit us and browse in our
> library.  Since we have not heard from you, I am taking the
> liberty of answering your letter of July 27.  The Congress
> Booklet has not been completed...  We are always most
> grateful for contributions of books to the library...
> We hope that Mr. Cayce will be back soon..."
> 
> R4. 10/46 Ordered various publications of A.R.E.
> 
> R5. 3/22/50 Questionnaire sent to Dr. George.
> 
> R6. 5/7/50 Dr George's reply to questionnaire:  "Chief
> complaint:  Weakness.  Physician's Provisional and Final
> Diagnosis:  Paresis.  Duration of Treatment:  One month.
> I felt that absolutely nothing could be done before Mr.
> Cayce gave the reading.  However, Mr. Cayce's diagnosis
> was correct.  I do believe that the physical condition was
> from 'self-indulgences and infections from general blood
> disorder'".
> 
> R7. 8/25/52 Comment by GD:  "On 1/19/46, a luncheon was held
> at the Town Hall Club, [[New York City|New York City]], at which there were
> present about 30 guests, consisting of active members,
> officers and trustees of ARE.  The [[Purpose|purpose]] of this gathering
> was to discuss plans for the future program of ARE.
> 
> "Dr. George gave a talk on that occasion regarding the
> importance of Physical Readings.  To illustrate his point,
> he cited the particulars concerning this case.  He emphasized
> the following fact:  Mr. [5067], although in an advanced
> stage of paresis, was nevertheless relieved from intense
> suffering by the application of treatments recommended by
> EC.  Dr. George's conclusion was based upon his personal
> observations in treating this patient."
> 
> R8. 8/26/52 No record of verbal or written contact on this
> case since 5/7/50.
