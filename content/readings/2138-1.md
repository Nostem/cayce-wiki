# TEXT OF READING 2138-1 F 59 (Registered Nurse, Protestant)

**Reading [[2138-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/1/1931  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 8797

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1931, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mrs. [2138].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2138] and Son [2137], Mrs. F. M. Freeman, two children, and L. B. Cayce.]

Born February 5, 1872, in Greenville, Darke County, [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, now known as [2138], and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and manifested in this present entity.

2. In entering, we find astrologically those influences in Aries, Jupiter, Mercury, Venus and Uranus.  In this relation, as has been builded in the present, as well as in the experiences in the earth's plane, have been altered much by the application of will's influence as respecting [[Astrology|astrological]] influence, and in the present experience we will find that those influences in Jupiter, Mercury, Uranus, will in the next few years bring those of more a satisfaction in the mental, the material and the spiritual influences, than have been experienced in the past of the present experience; for, as is seen and known by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present, there has been those periods when not as REGRETS, as the body finds in the present understanding, rather as that that has oft - or at periods SEEMED to have been an unwise decision, yet these have been SOME NECESSARY experiences for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to HAVE arrived at that understanding as IS experienced BY the entity in the present.  These, as we find, will - with the understanding, with the knowledge and experience - make for those that, will the entity in the next few years but hold to that as has been gained as the knowledge and understanding, bring more of not only a satisfaction of seeing the labors prosper in material ways, but bring more of that of experiencing the broader [[Visions|vision]], the greater capacity for aid to others, the greater capacity for the ability to give insight, knowledge, counsel, aid, understanding to OTHERS in that field as the body mentally, materially, gains ITS OWN concept, workable as it becomes in the present experience. These, as we find, should take very DEFINITE changes in the middle of the present season, coming October - these will find as outlet and see the beginning of those of WHOLESOME knowledge, wholesome understandings.  Do not allow petty differences, or those of a difference in knowledge or understanding deter the entity from applying THAT that HAS been and IS the life AND hope OF the body in this present period.

3. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], and the effect these have had and do have upon the present entity:

4. In the one before this we find in this present surrounding, in the present land, under different environs, and during those periods when there were those persecutions in that now known as New [[England|England]] and about that portion of the country now known as Massachusetts.  The entity then among those peoples who where misunderstood for the concepts as were gathered BY those of the entity's group as concerning the activities of unseen forces IN the material plane.  The entity then in the name Marie Jughan, being of those of the group who came westward from the Norwegian land, and of those peoples of that group that were considered as the leaders of this that was not in whole accord with the religious rites [rights] of the period.  The entity suffered persecution in the period, but gained through the steadfastness of the understanding as was gained; losing only in the answering back when railed upon by those who would laugh at the entity's physical position, ESPECIALLY those days in the stocks as it rained.  Hence as is felt in the present, that oft of the daring to do, to be, that as is felt is right, IRRESPECTIVE of what others may think, with a TENDENCY - sometimes too oft - to give to others that are unappreciative of that PRIVILEGE they are being granted by the knowledge or the manifestation of that being told or shown.  In this should the entity, even in the present, take a second thought.  As also the experience brings to that is of an [[Intuition|intuitive]] finding, yet oft will the entity find that for SELF the second thought, the second sight, the second determination, is the better.  While in many the first impression lasts, to THIS entity WEIGHT well both sides of EVERY question.

5. In the one then before this we find in that land known as the promise land, and yet the entity again a sojourner from a northern and western country.  The entity then a worker in linens, and an embroiderer of no mean estate, coming in the latter period of the experience under the influence of the teacher as became the LEADER, of the chief of the SPEAKING exponents of the new set, or sect.  The entity gaining through this experience, for - as was said of the follower of [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]], "so in that graciousness of thine grandmother Eunice".  The entity Eunice through this experience, the name being changed from that of the combined - or the [[Greece|Grecian]] and the Hebrew name also, Amon-Dekar.  In this experience those truths, though they find a different meaning to the entity from many, find an answer within that is oft not explainable by the entity, and the holding to those tenets will bring TO the entity that of the greatest faith, the greatest satisfaction, the greatest understanding; for though there be those things that would attempt to make one afraid that are of the earthly making, and those things that would bring those bindings from material things, in HIM - as taught by [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] - IS the light, and there IS no variableness in His understanding.  When one once gains that consciousness of that presence as was gained by the entity [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]], nothing in the heavens above, in the seas beneath, neither far or near, can shake or change THAT understanding; for He IS the light, and the way man AND woman may approach [[Throne of God|the THRONE]] of [[Grace|Grace]].  In the present this, as we find, brings many of those of the love of the household, yet the longing for ministering to those that are needy in any condition or position; especially love for brocades or raised figures in cloth, and for that that makes for beauty in women's apparel.

6. In the one then before this, we find in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, and during those periods when there were dissensions in the land.  The entity then among those of the natives that were associated with the peoples in the TEMPLE worship; later associated with those in the establishing of the [[Healing|HEALING]] forces to the peoples in many lands.  THE entity very decided, as it has been in the present at times, sometimes to self's own weal and sometimes to self's own woe - as in the present.  In the re-establishing of the priest and those that understood and that made for a spreading of that attained and gained by consecration of individuality, personality, and selves to [[Ideal|AN ideal]], the entity rose then to one of power and position in the arts of applying those remedies as were gained during that period.  In the name Awa-Lairon.  In the present, again we find there are those inclinations INNATELY as make for rebellions within self at conditions; yet with the understanding as has gradually come to the entity even in the present experience, again much as that as experienced in that period has come to make for the little leaven that leaveneth the whole.  Not in might, not in power - saith the Lord, but in the LITTLE things day by day.  Just being KIND to a brother brings greater understanding than to he that taketh a whole city.  Greater is the knowledge OF the truth gained by just being a friend to the friendless, than he that ruleth over many peoples unwisely.  In THESE may the entity excel in the present.  Ever, then, as has been, westward will the entity take its way - in the latter portion of its [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in each experience has been - will a knowledge, an understanding, and the satisfaction of a LIFE, an EXPERIENCE, an attaining of a better understanding come to the entity. Be thou faithful unto those that are in the formative years of THEIR experience, even as was said, "To thine grandmother go for counsel."  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Was [2137] even my child before?
(A)  There's been many [2137]'s in you life!

8. (Q)  The present son, [2137].
(A)  Grandson.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
