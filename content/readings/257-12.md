# TEXT OF READING 257-12 M 32

**Reading [[257-12]]** · Series: 250-261 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/18/1926  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 1284

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding the physical and mental conditions, and business relations of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find the general physical conditions of the body are very good, as the mental equilibrium of the body is very good.  Those conditions as have been given for the body, as in reference to the teeth, and that condition as regarding same, should be attended to, for we find this needs attention.  We would remove same, for we will find this gradually becoming longer and causing much trouble, with the accumulation of poison or pus, and its dissemination in the system will prove detrimental, especially to the digestion of the body, which is easily upset by the way and manner the body treats self, as respects to same, see?

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Examine entire body and advise about diet and exercise.
(A)  We have given it.  Follow it out!  Keep what we have given!  We would not change it, if you would do it right!

6. (Q)  Is the present treatment given hair [for baldness] satisfactory?
(A)  Very good.  Keep it up.

7. (Q)  Why is there so much dandruff?
(A)  Use Listerine and there won't be so much!

8. (Q)  What causes scalp to itch?
(A)  The irritation, as is produced by the accumulations in the system.  Digestion bad - and nerves kept on edge, see?

9. (Q)  How often should the Listerine be used?
(A)  Twice a week.

10. (Q)  Should the body travel for new business this month?
(A)  April and May.

11. (Q)  What is the best plan for the body to follow for best business for the next 60 days?
(A)  After the next 15, we would travel for some, see?

12. (Q)  Is the body progressing as it should?
(A)  The body progressing very good.  Should be content - not satisfied with it.

13. (Q)  What would make him more successful?
(A)  Get married.  Take on more responsibilities.  He will find them in that!  [See 257-10 Life Rdg. Reports.]

14. (Q)  Any further information that will be of assistance to this body, either physically, mentally, or regarding the business relations?
(A)  There may be much said or given.  These, as have been asked, very good, and there has been fully OUTLINED those conditions NECESSARY for the body to make the success for self, mentally, spiritually, morally, and financially. Then why insist?  FOLLOW those, and INSIST on keeping self in that path.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
