# TEXT OF READING 900-177 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-177]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/22/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5380

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and the lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], the [[Visions|visions]], as we see that come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and more and more in that way and manner as has been given would come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].  Hearken!  We are ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Saturday Night, Dec. 19, while reading James' Religious Experiences.  Voice:  "How is it?  Finish your supper, kid."
(A)  This, as we see, the awakening in the conscious from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, near to that as is known in the [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces as cares for the elementals in physical for the sustaining of all force as manifest in the individual, for, as is seen, all force as is manifest in a material world may be manifested through a living organism in the human body, and as this warning, then:  That [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], as it feeds the mental and the physical, these must be kept in that full [[Oneness|oneness]] in [[Purpose|purpose]] as entity sets for self.

4. (Q)  Voice:  "But, you have no proof!"
(A)  Again the forces in [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] bringing to the entity those physical concepts of that being (at this time) carried through the mental expansion of the mind, and the proofs, as are called in this instance, being that as of the experience of the conscious forces in the mind of entity toward the concepts of that in the physical [[Visions|vision]] at time, for more and more we find the body conscious reasoning with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] of entity.

5. (Q)  Voice:  "They had you lost."
(A)  In the [[Visions|vision]] as is being attempted to be shut from the mental forces, the hearkening of the subconscious but calls the entity to that position as is set in minds of some towards entity, see?  and, as we see in the next, that answer comes in self as to the position of entity in same, for as is seen in the next, the answer in "They had you lost" but in HIM is the Light, and the Light is the Light of the World.

6. (Q)  Voice:  "I gave it to you [900]"
(A)  That the entity might know that unto It is committed that truths, as is being magnified in the entity, see?

7. (Q)  Voice:  "On Tuesday" - seemed to refer to Atlantic Gulf."
(A)  Then, as has been given, from day to day, buy and sell, or GET OUT, as has been given, from the various positions as have been occupied in this particular stock, and TUESDAY, giving this day as to clear from same, see?  until after the holidays, see?

8. (Q)  Should we wait until Tuesday to sell any more A.G.?
(A)  Clear from same on Tuesday.  Then neither buy and sell, but be CLEAR.  Sell that that is now long, buy in that as is now short.  Be clear of stock.

9. (Q)  Tuesday, Dec. 22nd?
(A)  Tuesday, Dec. 22nd.

10. (Q)  Voice:  "That I gave his child a dress for $100 no doubt."
(A)  That is, the child of the brain of entity would bring that as a physical action of the labors spent, see?  That is, as these pieces, these preparations, these truths as are compiled in form are presented to the various peoples, magazines - Atlantic Monthly - Delineator - Woman's Home Companion - Monitor - through any of these channels these may be disposed of and bring much satisfaction, much pleasure, and much condemnation from many; yet these truths will find their way into the hearts of many, bringing joy and peace and happiness to them.

11. (Q)  Saw [139], my mother-in-law.  Voice:  "Hasn't had a change yet."
(A)  This referring to the operation of the forces as may be exercised through that of the value of same to physical applications to a [[Physical Body|physical body]] in distress, see?

12. (Q)  Voice:  "$100 - Write!"
(A)  This refers to the same as has been given, see?  and this applies to the different ones, or magazines or papers, as has been given, and when same is written, is edited, copied, present same, then, to the magazines until accepted by same, see?  In time we would find all EAGER for such articles, see?

13. (Q)  Does this indicate $100 will be paid for the article I am writing and that I should get it done and send in to the Atlantic monthly?
(A)  As given, see.

14. (Q)  That article I have just completed - is it in shape to send in, or its revision which I am now undertaking, will it be in more acceptable shape to the editors of Atlantic Monthly?
(A)  When edited, acceptable, see?  Send to them.  Should this meet with certain of these editors, a rejection slip would return with same.  Should this meet with certain others, an acceptance check would return, see?  As to which of these it will meet, we don't see here.  Send to them.  If not acceptable, send to others, see?

15. (Q)  Sunday Morning, Dec. 20.  First seemed to see numbers 128 - 80 - 90.
(A)  This, as we see, referring to stocks in a future date, in that as has been given to same, see?  First, Southern to 128 - Atlantic Gulf and West Indies 80 - 90, see?  Not for present.

16. (Q)  No definite emblematical expression that I can recall - of late it has been this way - but rather impressions upon my consciousness that I seem to translate into thoughts upon awakening.  This one I translate as follows:  Every force you see in creation as outside of yourself is a power of creation contained within your own faculties.
(A)  Correct.  Just as given, see?  Expressed in different words.

17. We are through for the present.
