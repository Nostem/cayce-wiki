# TEXT OF READING 522-2 F 43

**Reading [[522-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/24/1930  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 3667

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [522].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [552].]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [522].  This we have had before. The general conditions, as we find, show improvements from that as we have last had here.  There needs be set up more of the drainages in the system as respecting eliminations, even to the extent that through the alimentary canal - and especially through those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that have most to do with the assimilations for the system, and the blood replenishing and rebuilding - that we make for not TOO much strain, but rather that of an ENLIVENMENT of the fluids of system, throwing out drosses THROUGH the alimentary canal. These should be with those of the ALKALIN PRODUCING in the system, see?

2. These, as we find, may be aided materially in that as may be found with those of an alteration between - that is, one day one would be taken, the next day another would be taken; so that the system does not adjust itself, with the changes as are being carried on by the manipulations - as must be given to release the pressures and remove those tendencies of pus producing conditions in the system, as is seen in throat, as is seen in portions of the system where the distresses have existed, where there have been tendencies of inflammation through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and those of the shoulder, those of the side - these are congestions.  The manipulation aids those in throwing off these.  These properties will aid the system in a general manner, by their differentiation in activities in the system, of producing a nearer normal balance in conjunction with same.  That is, those of Milk of Magnesia, Milk of Bismuth, and those of the Upjohn's phenolphthalein, or Phenolax - these may be altered, see?  in their usage with the rest of the conditions.

3. In the application of the sinusoidal, these we would make from the 4th lumbar to the 3rd and 2nd center in the upper dorsal, see?

4. In the conditions as come from the discharges of forces in system, as an excess, this increase in the eliminations through other channels - and with the applications of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, and with those of the manipulations - should set the system into a normal balance.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is condition of throat and tonsils?  Any special treatment for same?
(A)  Special treatment has just been given for all of the system as relating to those conditions where pus shows in the system, THROUGH the inability of that portion to throw off drosses, see?  Hence the changes as made in the system through the alteration of the CHARACTER of the ELIMINANT, see?  Pus SHOWS in the tonsil, but - as WE find - this may be set up as drainage, and not as operative at present.

6. (Q)  What causes the pain and burning sensation in right breast?
(A)  This as the inflammation, as we have given, from the strain, the bruise, in the side of the body - as has been given, and CONGESTION following same.  Straightening the system as regarding the eliminations, and removing the ACIDS from the system, and those tendencies to produce those toxic forces, should bring the normal force to the body.

Be mindful that the diet is kept - not of meats, not too much of any TUBEROUS nature, see?  Greens, those of the pods may be taken - as peas or beans, or lentils - those of celery, tomatoes, those of salsify, or of ANY of those that are as a tendency for the ALKALIN PRODUCING in the system.  Fruits, cereals, conditions of that kind.  These are well for the body.

7. (Q)  Should the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] be used now?
(A)  This may be used when there is not the ability to get the sun's rays, but with the sun's rays, or sand and sun, these are MORE beneficial than those of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]].

8. (Q)  Are sand packs beneficial?
(A)  Be beneficial, if they are not taken for too great a period of time.  Every other day should be sufficient for these, ten to fifteen minutes for such treatment.  Wet the body, but cover in dry sand.

9. That is all the questions regarding this body.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Lydic
