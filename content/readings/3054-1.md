# TEXT OF READING 3054-1 F 49

**Reading [[3054-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/22/1943  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10388

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [3178], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]], & Miss [2898].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  In giving an interpretation of the conditions as we find here, many things are to be taken into consideration. For, all may appear sometime to have the interruptions in the physical well being.

3. In this body we find no exception.  There are disturbing forces, but we find that - unless there develops a great deal more of temperature or inflammation - there need not necessarily be the resorting to operative measures.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find with this body, [3054]:

5. In giving the sources of the disturbing forces, and as to what may be done about them, we find in the blood supply - with the retractions that are caused by the manner in which it reflexes to not only natural sources but the unusual abilities in the mental as well as spiritual outlook of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - there are certain indications - and at times contacts may be made in the associations here.

6. As we find, the basic disturbance is the alimentary canal. This has not produced any form of dissention, nor should it, in the minds that would minister to the better well being of the body.

7. In the lymph circulation, through alimentary canal, there has come accumulations in the pockets along portions of the jejunum.  And this has at times produced a tendency for regurgitation, so that there is that full, uncomfortable feeling; with the formations of gas through the intestinal tract, as well as in the duodenum and the organs of assimilation.

8. These arose first by inflammation from cold, or sometime ago intestinal flu; and then something of a disturbance in the pancreas as related to the circulation of lymph through the area - because of the activities of these upon food particles.  This was the beginning of the condition.

9. This, combined with a subluxation that exists in the 5th and 6th dorsal, tends to produce a slowing of impulse through the area.  These have affected the deeper or central circulation, and these find reactions in a sympathetic system upon the activity of kidneys, and the slowing of the heart at times in its reaction.

10. These cause more anxiety, and a nervous headache may ensue.

11. As we find, in bringing the better conditions, - we would first begin taking internally a few drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] daily - in most of the water.  However, because of the disturbance to the taste, don't cut off the amount of water taken.  Put about three drops of the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in each glassful of water taken.

12. Each evening, for some five or six evenings, apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the area of the lower portion of the liver and across the abdominal area, and extend these down to the caecum on the right side.  Use two to three thicknesses of cotton cloth or toweling well saturated in the Glyco-Thymoline.  These we would keep on for about an hour. Cover the Packs with oilcloth or something of the kind to prevent soiling or wetting the bed linens.

13. Do this for five days.

14. THEN have - not before - first a good [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], scientifically given, to cleanse the whole of the colon area.

15. Begin then and have about ten [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments (one every other day), specifically to relax the body; stimulating only in the area below the 6th dorsal, and this done more by the activity of those centers as related between the cerebrospinal system and the conjunction in the plexus with the sympathetic nerve system.  Do relax the lymph centers, in chest, and especially in the areas along either side of the diaphragm area - this especially in the liver and in the spleen; these from the external, these lymph centers, see?

16. As to the diet, - until the alimentary canal has been purified, - we would not have too much of solid foods, and yet not so much liquid or juices as not to carry a stimulation.  Use rather gruels of all kinds, - as the combinations of whole grain cereals, as oatmeal and all such, and both the dry and cooked cereals.  Use citrus fruit juices (but at opposite times of the day from the cereals), also eggs and vegetable soups.  Not too much meat in the soups, but let them be seasoned rather with butter.

17. And if the body will do these, we should find bettered conditions.

18. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments we would have by Waters.

19. The Packs may be given at home.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  Is there a tumor?
(A)  The lymph pockets are of the nature that they may be called tumors, yes - but with these applications, these should be made to drain themselves and thus cleanse the system of those tendencies for the accumulations, so that the system should be aided in adjusting these disturbances.  The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments will relieve those tendencies that have been produced upon the whole central nervous system, as well as the circulatory system, and relieve the tension on the heart, liver, lungs and kidneys.

Do that.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for osteopath, Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, Copy to Ass'n file  1707 Columbia Rd., Washington D.C., when you are ready for these treatments - Dr. Waters has had experience with Mr. Cayce's readings.)

(If you would like to go over the Reading with someone of our membership in your vicinity who has had personal experience with Mr. Cayce's work, we would refer you to Mr. and Mrs. Louis B. Poss, 212 Woodland Terrace, Jefferson Park, [[Alexandria|Alexandria]], Va.  They would be glad to discuss the Reading with you and help you get started with the treatment, or help you to understand anything that is not clear.

At your convenience we'd appreciate a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading.)

> R1. GD's note:  Many pauses and hesitations in this Rdg.,
> especially at the beginning - seemed difficult to locate the
> body, then hard to get into the physical difficulty - seemed
> interruptions by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] from other side, etc.

> R2. 7/20/43 [3054]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce
> 
> I shall now report on the treatments, which I just completed
> last night.  It seems to me that I feel almost as well as
> when I started the treatments, I feel better underneath, if I
> can make my self understood, but I have just as much gas and
> the stomach is still distended, and I am weak of course.
> Before I took the treatments I was working and since I have
> been in bed most of the time.
> 
> The doctor, however, who gave me the adjustments said to tell
> you that I was better.
> 
> Well, I took the five packs as advised, really took six,
> because we were not sure that the first one was given right.
> Before they were finished, and they made me as sick as a fox,
> I had a very satisfactory bowel movement, one which made me
> not worry in spite of the fact that the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] which
> followed produced nothing but gas.  The nurse who gave it to
> me was greatly disturbed, and so was Miss Waters.  They both
> thought I have a stricture, or a pressure of some sort.
> 
> After I went to the nurse, (Miss Waters recommended her, I
> never saw her before) I went to Miss Waters.  She gave me a
> complete examination, and read your letter.  She said anyone
> could give the adjustments as they were simple.  Right here I
> want to say that Miss Waters is a most marvelous person - she
> is one person who has real sympathy and understanding.  It is
> easy to see why "The Forces" would pick her out and use her
> to help in your work.  There was a time in my life when I
> would have thought that such sympathy as I found in her had
> something to do with me - that set me out in some way, but
> now I know that I have had the privilege of meeting a person
> of great and universal understanding.
> 
> I was so sick that my husband talked with her after the
> treatment, and by that time she had talked to the nurse, -
> she suggested X-Rays, that I use the packs every hour, that I
> take ten glasses of water and salt, and I don't know what
> all.  She was so disturbed about the failure of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]],
> (I couldn't make her see that the movement I'd had covered
> that - I think it did).  Miss Waters could not take me at
> night, and I have been going to a [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] doctor for
> some time, who understands me thoroughly, so I went to him
> for the treatments, which he gave as faithfully as he could.
> I did none of the things she suggested.  He thinks that the
> diagnosis you gave is correct, however he has told me for
> some time that I have a large pancreatic cyst, full of water,
> which presses on things.  He says he does not see how it will
> drain, but I think if that is it, that it has drained some.
> What I suffer from most, is the gas, and the fact that my
> stomach is so large.
> 
> You asked for a history of my present condition.  Here it is.
> I have been sick most of my life, I mean never well, but I
> have a lot of vitality, and always "come back".  When I was
> young I had malaria for a long while, after which I was a fat
> youngster, then when I grew older I was thin, very thin.  In
> 1922 I had my tonsils out and from then on took on a great
> deal of fat, which my doctor has always told me was not real
> flesh, but an accumulation in the lymph, which should have
> been thrown off, and would be some time.  The treatments he
> gave me were toward that end, and to correct a kidney and
> ovarian condition.  I have now lost about forty pounds.
> 
> Last Sept., my husband and I took a trip, to Boston, stopping
> at several cities on the way.  Had a grand time.  We flew
> home, I am afraid of "high places" but I apparently had no
> reaction to the flight, I just felt I was in a box, (they
> kept the curtains drawn) and hoped we'd get home.  The night
> before we left I was awakened by a scream, which for some
> unknown reason scared me stiff.  I tell you this, for I
> personally think it had some part of my illness.  Then I got
> home I was tired, but I pitched right in to do some things
> that should have been done, but by the next night I was very
> ill, and went to bed and stayed there for I don't know how
> long.  I had my doctor, (after trying an MD, who didn't know
> very much about me) and he said I had almost complete
> paralysis of the diaphragm, the solar plexus and he was
> baffled as to what had happened to me, and still can't figure
> out.  In some way, he agrees with me that I had some sort of
> "psychic shock".  Now your "writing", speaks of
> regurgitation, I did all of that - I was deathly sick.
> Finally I got better, and went back to work, and dragged on
> till I wrote you.  Never well, but able to navigate.
> 
> It is all very peculiar.  Personally I think the diagnosis,
> you have given may be correct.  Oh yes, I went to a
> diagnostician, and he wanted me operated on for some tumor,
> (my doctor says I have no tumor - except the ovarian
> condition which I've had for so long, and which he has under
> control).
> 
> Now, I should like a check-up, as soon as possible, and would
> like to ask the following questions:
> 
> 1. Can this stomach condition be cured?
> 2. Do I have a pancreatic cyst?
> 3. What did you mean by this sentence in my Reading, "In
> giving the sources of the disturbing forces, and as to what
> may be done about them, we find it in the blood supply...with
> the retractions that are caused by the manner in which it
> reflexes, to not only natural sources, but the unusual
> abilities  in the mental as well as spiritual outlook of the
> entity...thee are certain indications...and at times contacts
> may be made in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] here."  [3054-1, Par. 5]  What
> effect do these "abilities" have on this particular illness?
> 
> I have talked with Mrs. Poss.  She is most charming and
> believes that if I follow the instructions I can get well.  I
> believe so myself.
> 
> When I was first sick I thought I had ptomaine poisoning, and
> my doctor said it always seemed to him like some thing of the
> sort.
