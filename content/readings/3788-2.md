# TEXT OF READING 3788-2 F 22

**Reading [[3788-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/8/1926  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 11356

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 8th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [3788].  EC volunteered this information (without suggestion being given to obtain it), immediately following a second Physical Rdg. for Mr. [4212] and after the suggestion was given for a check physical for Mr. [304], but in locating Mr. [304] this information was volunteered for Miss [3788]. (Miss [3788] and Mr. [304] lived in the same town.)

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ky.

1. EC:  For this condition here, [3788], we would order these properties from Five Point Drug Store, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]]. They will be able to obtain same from there.  [GD's note: Maypop Bitters was no longer available to purchase, so EC gave the formula.  See detailed formula given 11/10/30 under 543-5.]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/9/26 EC's ltr.:  "I am in receipt of your of the 5th.  I
> find, upon investigation, that I was in error in telling you
> who was the manufacturer of this Maypop.  I find [[John (Apostle)|John]] B.
> Daniels, Atlanta, Ga., is the manufacturer, or you may obtain
> same from the Five Points Drug Store in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala.
> 
> "Hoping this will prevent any further delay in your getting
> the proper ingredients, I am"  Sincerely, Edgar Cayce
> [EC:GD]
> 
> [GD's note:  I doubt that above short volunteered rdg.
> [3788-2] was sent to Miss [3788]; probably just EC's letter
> giving her the information.]
> 
> R2. 4/19/26 Mr. [304]'s ltr.:  "I haven't talked to Miss [3788]'s
> father, but just from what his brother said and the way he
> talked about what had been done, I have my doubts about his
> carrying out the treatment.  You may imagine what
> encouragement he received from Sargent [Andrew Sargent, D.C.]
> when he went to him and told him what he had and what he
> wanted, and really I doubt now whether he got the adjustments
> from him.  Of course I don't know, but doubt it...  I really
> and truly doubt his getting any encouragement from Sargent
> and believe he just laid down on the proposition."
> 
> R3. 7/60 Dr. Walter N. Pahnke's notes on Case [3788] in making a
> survey of 96 cases of epilepsy from the Edgar Cayce records:
> 
> "This case was classified in group 2.  The only history given
> was that the condition was 'something like epilepsy.'  There
> was no follow-up."
