# TEXT OF READING 304-34 M 81

**Reading [[304-34]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/10/1935  Sex: M  Age: 81  ReadingID: 2287

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [304], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [304], Boyd and Burlynn Davis.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before. Conditions in general, as we find, are NOT so good.  There are the indications of the poor circulation in the eliminating systems, and the effect is indicated not only in the manner in which the hernia makes for IRRITATING the body but in the abrasions or eruptions on portions of the body.

2. As we find, it would be well that the eruptions be bathed LOCALLY with a very weak solution of the antiseptic that has been indicated for same; [Bichloride of Mercury - see 304-27] then the use of the Resinol as an application - and the body keep quiet.  BEST to keep the feet up level or above the head in resting, see?

3. Make for the better or greater activities through the eliminations, taking the antiseptics with the Milk of Magnesia.  This to be alternated, of course, with the Liver Regulator.  The antiseptic that would be taken would be the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in small quantities, taken once or twice a day.

4. Taking these precautions, being mindful of the diet, should eliminate these conditions.

5. Ready for questions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
