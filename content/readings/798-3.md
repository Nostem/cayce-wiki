# TEXT OF READING 798-3 F 49

**Reading [[798-3]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/12/1935  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 4845

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of August, 1935, in accordance with request made by the cousin - Mr. [261], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

[Near Utica, 
Time of Reading                                [[New York City|New York]]] 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  (In making the location, "Near Cooperstown!")  [GD's note:  He may have mentioned this since Mr. [261] had been born in Cooperstown - see 261-5.]

2. Yes, we have the body, [798]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there has been a great deal of change for the better in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. While there may be much to be desired, the general reactions are such that - as WE find - if there will be kept up much of the same line or character of applications, conditions will continue to improve.

5. We would, then, recharge the Appliance; or take it apart, cleansing same and putting new solutions or new charges, tightening all the connections; and continue to use this in the manner as we have indicated for same.

6. While the changes in the present do not permit of this, soon there will be the abilities to use this when it is prepared; you see.

7. We find that the irritations are continuing, yet they are not so severe.  And we would continue with the rubs in the same form, over those areas of the locomotory centers - upper and lower.

8. Keep near unto the same character of the diets.  Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [261]
"    " Ass'n file

> My dear Mr. Cayce-
> 
> I am sorry to have delayed answering your letter of January
> Tenth.  I expected to do so every day, but after the
> extremely busy and executive life that I led for many years,
> when everything had to be done on time, I find, since my
> illness that there is a luxury to PROCRASTINATION which is
> more of a temptation than I am always able to resist.  The
> absolute physical and mental weariness which after my long
> years culminated in this disabling illness was such a burden
> that even after two years I find I still have to push myself
> to accomplish even the simplest sort of thing.  My mind is
> much more active to plan than my physical strength is able
> to carry out, altho I am surprised when I suddenly realize
> the steady increase in both physical and mental strength
> which becomes perceptible when I find myself worrying or
> thinking easily and naturally in a way very impossible for
> the past two years.  Dr. Hale has given me exceptionally good
> care and advice and I have been wonderfully blessed in the
> loving attention of my family and nurse.  As you said in your
> letter it is difficult, even with the best of intentions
> to give the fullest cooperation in these cases.  But I have
> tried to have a regular routine, as much as is possible as
> we are a energetic family.  I have kept quite accurately to
> the diet suggested by Mr. Cayce, particularly as regards
> fresh vegetables, salads and fruits.  The battery I believe
> has been very worth while and at the times when I have not
> used it for a few days, I have missed it.  Whether this was
> a reality or imagination I can't say, for there is no
> perceptible sensation while it is in use but it seems to
> me that it has a very decided effect on the circulation
> and condition of my hand and foot.  The rubbing compound
> I have used quite regularly and thoroughly enjoy.  Once the
> family became accustomed to the odor of the mixture of
> gasoline and oils, there were fewer sniffs when it was being
> used and personally I rather like it.  I have just begun on
> my third half-gallon bottle.  It is surprising to think that
> my body has absorbed over a gallon of that compound.
> 
> I have been most interested in the way you are trying to make
> a definite test of Mr. Cayce's powers and to get as much of
> a scientific basis for the remarkable results he has had.
> The diagnosis of my present condition and, in the Life
> Reading.  The description of characteristics probably known
> only to myself [See 798-4], were most accurate and
> convincing.  I should be glad to have you use my case in the
> record without the name, of course, if you think it would be
> helpful.
> 
> As for another reading, I think it would be very useful just
> now.  As my cousin Mr. [261] has arranged for all the
> previous readings.  I am uncertain just what there is to do.
> If you will let me know I should be glad to try.  Assuring
> you of my interest and with thanks for which Mr. Cayce has
> done to help me, I am
> 
> Very sincerely yours, [798]
