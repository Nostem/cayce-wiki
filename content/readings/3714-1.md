# TEXT OF READING 3714-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3714-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/27/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11220

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [...], who met EC and [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] years ago.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.              ..., Maryland.

1. EC:  In these mental conditions as we find, bringing the strains on this body, these we find will be necessary to use those of the physical conditions to overcome that as has been produced in a physical manner in the body.  This, then, should be brought under the care and attention of one who gives deep manipulations, and remove the pressure and strain as is produced on the 1st and 2nd cervical, producing depressions in and about that of the vital nerve passing through the system, that comes in touch with the functionings of the pneumogastric and of the organs in pelvis.  Without the removing of this pressure, we find these conditions will gradually grow to that wherein the body would become violent in nature and in action, and necessary of confinement, for this condition is an exaggeration of the mental unbalancing through strain produced by the condition of the mental through the action of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pituitary]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]].  The sooner these would be taken care of, the better for same.

2. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the relief of this body?
(A)  These should be sent at once to [...] [his wife].  As this:  Seek the advice and counsel of an expert [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]. Full instructions in mail.

> Dear Mr. Cayce:

> I received your telegram on Saturday, and the letter this

> moods last sometimes for six weeks...and sometimes they are
> gone in half that time.  They seem to grow more violent...but
> there isn't a thing I can do, but to try to be gentle and
> patient.
> 
> I am thinking a great deal about you, these days.  I am
> positive that I must do something about this house...for I
> cannot keep on doing without any money...  Mr. [3714] has the
> most impossible mental attitude about the entire thing.  The
> farm, and house...and everything is mine, bought years before
> I knew Mr. [3714] but he insists everything is his.  He keeps
> every cent that comes in, in his bank account...checking
> it out - and buying the supplies...which is alright from
> his stand point.  But - I haven't a cent.
> 
> I am trying to find a way to sell the place to a corporation,
> and to continue to run it...of course on a salary.  I want
> to build a 20 room "rest house" for men only.  At a flat rate
> of ten dollars a day, this would pay well.  The Justices of
> the Supreme Court, the members of the Cabinet, the biggest
> Senators and Members, and the many prominent Professional men
> of Washington, would keep it filled and - it would do a lot
> of good.  The new Southern Maryland Highway comes within half
> a mile of this place...which means all the year round, good
> roads.  I have lately found a most wonderful woman, Dr.
> Brosenne.  She was first a trained nurse, then - studied
> Chiropractor method.  Then, she added her own idea of
> "cupping"...and in this way, she reached deep circulation.
> There is a white sulphur spring on this place, and I can see
> how wonderful it would be to have besides the rest house,
> a "Cure House"...using her to give the treatments.  If Your
> Institute could be here, too...I am thinking a great deal of
> good could be done.
> 
> It would take a great deal of money...but I can see how it
> would pay well, too.  I have an Estate, house of 24 rooms,
> and a business with a reputation second to none.  It ought
> to be possible to capitalize this business...of course
> incorporating it...and by developing the many sides to this
> business, enough money would be earned to pay the interest
> and much more...on the amount of the capitalization.
> 
> Let me hear what you think about it.
> 
> I want to thank you for your kindness to me.  I shall receive
> some money in a few days for a hanging which I sold...and
> then I will send you something to cover my share of your
> overhead.  I am in deep waters, these days...but I KNOW God's
> Will must be done...  I am very certain that I shall be led
> to do whatever is the Thing.
> 
> Again thanking you, with deep appreciation for your
> friendliness, I am...always faithfully your friend,
> Mrs. [...]
