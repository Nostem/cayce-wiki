# TEXT OF READING 365-4 F 38

**Reading [[365-4]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/27/1935  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 2832

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of February, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [365], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions that have caused and still cause disturbances with the better physical functioning of the body, [365].

2. Now, as we find, conditions are improved from that as we have had here before.  While there is not the full coordination in the physical functioning of the body, and this at times brings distresses to the body in those reactions in the physical functionings, to those portions of the extremities, portions of the functioning organs that are disturbed at these times, the applications that have been suggested have kept the body in a much nearer and a much more normal reaction.

3. As we would find, we would continue the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments through the manipulations, rather in a spasmodic sort of way or manner.  That is, we would have two or three adjustments and general manipulations for two or three weeks, and then rest or leave off for two or three weeks, and then have them again - in the present.

4. But we would add to these a tonic for the body, and, as we will see, while the combinations of these are many, in a manner, they will function with the digestive forces of the system, with the corrective measures necessary in the activities through the eliminating and assimilating system. And if there will be the more adherence to an alkalin- reacting diet, and a body and blood and nerve building, we will find the conditions better and greatly improved in the very near future.

5. Combine then, these ingredients for a tonic, in this manner: To 1 gallon of rain or distilled water add:

Wild Cherry Bark.................2 ounces,
Sarsaparilla Root................2 ounces,
White Oak Bark.................1/2 ounce,
Prickly Ash Bark...............1/2 ounce,
Yellow Dock Root...............1/2 ounce,
Burdock Root...................1/2 ounce,
Elder Flower.....................3 ounces,
Buchu Leaves...................1/4 ounce,
Mandrake Root...................10 grains.

Reduce this by slow boiling to 1 quart.  Strain and while warm add 3 ounces of pure grain alcohol with 2 drams of Balsam of Tolu cut in same.  The dose would be 1/2 teaspoonful 4 times each day.  This would be kept until the whole of the quantity is taken.  Take it before the meals and just before retiring in the evening.  Do this.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is my body in better working condition, especially the liver and stomach?  How is my digestion?
(A)  It is in some better working condition.  The disturbances that arise from the formations of gas and the tendencies for the constipation to disturb the body will be materially aided with the addition of these herbs in the combination as indicated.  And, of course, these will be distributed and these tendencies changed by the manipulations.

8. (Q)  How is my digestion?
(A)  At times very much better and at others not so well. These conditions arise from the disturbances in the liver and gall duct area.

9. (Q)  What can I do to prevent the teeth from wearing down?
(A)  Use more of an alkalin-reacting diet; as quantities of orange juice with a little lemon in same, as four parts orange juice to one part lemon; grapefruit, raw vegetables, potato peels, [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] taken occasionally, these will materially aid in adding those elements necessary for the correcting of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] with the system that make for the proper secretions to the body for the building of enamel, and for the activity - of course - of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]].

10. (Q)  Would Kal or Vegetrate, rich in calcium and phosphorous, be beneficial in this condition?
(A)  As we find, the [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] with the iron would be the more helpful; this taken about three to four times each week.

11. (Q)  Will the enamel form again on the teeth?
(A)  Provided the correct diet and correct elements for the system are kept.

12. (Q)  Do the headaches and bilious attacks come from incorrect eating and what can I do to prevent these?
(A)  Keep more in the alkalin reactions and the eliminations, even though it becomes necessary at times to use the high enemas.  This will be the better way to keep away the headaches and reduce those tendencies for the accumulations of too much acidity in the system.

13. (Q)  What can I do to gain in vitality?  Would a [[Ultraviolet Light|sun lamp]] help?
(A)  Sun light or the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] rays are helpful or beneficial.  As we find, the sun baths would be much preferable to a [[Ultraviolet Light|sun lamp]] in this particular season or time. These are the better violet ray at this particular time, especially with the properties as indicated for the gastric juices of the stomach itself, that will make for better assimilations.

14. (Q)  Does my body eliminate as it should?
(A)  If it did we wouldn't have these recurrences of the liver disturbance or the gall duct and the tendency for constipation to form in the system!

15. (Q)  Is my blood as it should be?  Have I lost the anemic condition?
(A)  Some better in these directions, but - as we will find - these properties as indicated in the tonic will be correcting in this direction.

16. (Q)  Can I drink a glass of wine or beer occasionally?
(A)  Wine is a food if taken with brown or black bread, or whole wheat or rye bread.  But do not take beer for this particular body.  A glass of wine a day is helpful; that is, not too big a glass.

17. (Q)  Am I able to eat egg yolk because of my liver condition?
(A)  The body may eat egg yolk; but NOT the white.

18. (Q)  Does my body need any special food which it is not getting?
(A)  As indicated by those necessary elements of calcium, the chlorines, the forms that make for the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], as indicated by the character of the diet.  And not too much of meats at any time for the body, and when meats are taken let these be either of liver, pigs' feet, tripe or lamb, or fish, or shell fish, in moderation.

19. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these, as we find, will be the better for this body, [365].

20. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File (Enclosed was a letter to Druggist for preparing herb tonic.

The [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] - be sure to specify WITH iron - may be obtained at most any drug store; it is put out by Parke-Davis & Co.

If everything is not clear, let us know.  GD)
