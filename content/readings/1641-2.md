# TEXT OF READING 1641-2 M 29

**Reading [[1641-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/17/1944  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 7730

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1641], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenographers. Mr. [1641].]

USS ..., 
Time of Reading                                % FPO, 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A.M. Eastern War Time. San Fran., Ca.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; and answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; [1641].

3. As we find, conditions are very good in most respects through this body in the physical.  There needs to be, as we find, some precautions taken as to conditions which are reflected in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, which have to do with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system and the circulation between the sensory system and the secondary or sympathetic and cerebrospinal system.  Organically, then, we find the conditions of this body good.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body; [1641] we are speaking of, present in this room:

5. In the blood supply we find indicated in the manner of the circulation through some portions of the body, as in nasal passages, throat, and the head, those tendencies for clogging in the superficial circulation and thus irritations at times are the result.  These cause little hindrances in the activity, as reflexes for the eyes, for the throat and voice, in the ears at time; though there are no organic disturbances as we find in the present.

6. In the nerve forces of the body it is indicated from the manner of reflexes to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces which are related to digestion, or related to general eliminations as related to the sensory organisms, that these have been under stress and strain.

7. With activities in the open and a regular exercise, and then the keeping in accord as may be indicated for the sensory system, we may keep a well balanced condition through this body.

8. These, as we find, in the sympathetic forces are from the emotions of the body, not an organic disorder, and it would be well - the body should get married.  It would be better for the general physical reflexes for the body in the activities and the responsibility as this brings for the body, mind and the reactions in same.

9. As to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] or those that produced these, as indicated these are just tendencies.

10. Then we would have prepared for this body this character of inhalant, which will work with the activities of the organs for purifying those areas through which sensory influences are at times clogged.  Thus we would prepare as this:  Take at least a six ounce large-mouth bottle.  In this we would prepare two vents.  These are not to enter the solution.  [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|The vents]] should be so that they may be corked when not in use. Then add these in the solution, put them in the order named:

Grain alcohol (at least 90% proof)...........4 ounces,
Oil of Eucalyptus...........................20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp........................5 minims,
Rectified Creosote...........................2 minims,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin................15 minims,
Tolu in Solution............................20 minims.

When this is to be used, and use it on arising of a morning and before retiring of evening, shake the solution together. Then inhale deep through the nostril, either side of same, about twice.  Then in the throat once.  These will act as purifiers and also will assist the body-functionings as each of these have their effect upon the activity of the organs themselves.  The lungs, the activities of the eucalyptus and the activity from the Tolu are to clarify or purify the lung tissue itself.  The activity of the [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] is to act upon kidneys and liver for the assisting in tuning or toning the activity with organs for better eliminations through the system, while the other ingredients as they act upon the mucous membranes give better actions through the lymph circulation in the body-forces.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Please comment on the following:-  [[Glands (Esoteric)|Thyroid]] disturbance.
(A)  This is merely a portion of the glandular forces which will be purified through the activity of that which has been indicated to be absorbed in the action of the body-forces.

14. (Q)  The abdominal pain and discomfort?
(A)  As there are those purifications through the circulation by the activities of that given upon the blood stream, these should gradually disappear.

It would be well for the body to take a regular exercise for the shoulder and head and neck.  These, though, are outside but don't take it just occasionally.  Of a morning three or four movements of the body, as:  Rise on toes (this is before dressing) and gradually raise the arms high above the head at the same time, and then, as on the toes, the circulation of the head and neck.  These will aid abdominal muscles, aid the abdominal activity, as well as the whole of the respiratory system.  Do this about three times of morning when first arising, of evening just before retiring.  You'll sleep better, you'll feel better.

15. (Q)  Intestinal disorder?
(A)  These are included in the abdominal area.

16. (Q)  Excessive sweating in throat area.
(A)  All of these are from this glandular activity and it has been indicated in those things to be done.

17. (Q)  What foods should be avoided?
(A)  It is rather the combination of foods; it is those which should be stressed that we would give.  Stress leafy vegetables for this body and fruits of every nature.  Not too much meats at any time, especially during hot weather.  These should be provided most when there is the greater excesses of physical labor and not mental labor.  But keeping those stressed in the body as given will be much preferably.

18. (Q)  Is tobacco harmful for this body?
(A)  This used in moderation would be well, for it supplies some poisons necessary to make better, or that which will make better, digestion.  Excess use would be bad for the body, for it would block the throat and nasal conditions, but we will find that moderation is not bad.

Then the mental attitude.  Remember, these are part of the whole of the mental and spiritual and material results. Depend upon the spiritual attitude [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] keeps.  Do that and we'll make bettered conditions.  Be creative, never negative in the thoughts.

19. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for Indexing
