# TEXT OF READING 5441-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[5441-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/12/1928  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13608

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by her brother, Mr. [4666], through Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are those conditions that bring distress to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These have to do with the inroads that are made upon the vital forces of the system through the effect of bacilli that causes wasting away of tissue.  Hence is tubercular in its nature.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them with this body. First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this shows the effect of this condition on the system in the manner in which the various functionings of the system is disturbed by the inability of the blood stream to at all times furnish, against the condition existent, that necessary for the full resuscitation of the body-physical.  There are accentuations in physical functioning, as indicated in various portions of the system, by the very character OF the condition.  This is another effect of the blood condition, in the manner in which it separates itself, or is separated by the plasm cell in the various cycles of functioning of organs as related to the blood stream; as is seen in reproductive forces as would be supplied in body; as seen in some portions of the mental abilities of the body, and the over-active forces of the brain itself in and through this existent condition.

4. THE NERVE SYSTEM, naturally, through the way and manner the body has had administered for the condition, is lax in its activity, and in some respects is near subnormal in its activity.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, these, as see, show the effect of the condition existent, either to the detriment of or to the accentuation of the functioning of same.

6. Conditions in lungs, bronchials, throat, and especially that seen in some portions of body where the separations have been thrown out in the system; attempting to segregate those conditions as bring destructive forces - THESE show greater effect of the condition in the body.

7. To meet the needs of the conditions at this time:  These, as we find, taken even at the present state, may be segregated sufficiently that the body would find much in recuperative forces, and much good may be administered by the body for self and others.

8. First we would prepare as this for the body:  Use those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be created in the system by the use of the Radio-Active Appliance for the system.  This will so stimulate the blood supply as to bring about better coagulation and more resistance, and more vitality be assimilated from those administrations as would be given for the body in the food values and in the activities of the system.  This first.  And we would use that as would be found in the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]].

9. We would use as an inhalant for the respiratory system, to give the reaction of same, that as would be found in this: To 2 quarts of [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] (which would be put in at least twice the size container - charred - inside a keg), we would add:

Eucalyptol..............40 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...10 minims,
Rectified Creosote.......5 minims.

This would only be kept where the heat as about the container would form the gas in chamber above same.  At least twice each day the gas as forms above this would be inhaled deep into the lungs, see?  This, as we find, will make more activity for the kidneys, as WELL as supplying that which will segregate that necessary to be thrown off in sputum.  In the action of this we will find restoring qualities, also in the action of the brandy on the tissue itself as carried in, in a gaseous form.

10. As for the foods, necessary that as much of the oils and fats be taken as system will assimilate.  [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod-liver oil]], or liver of ANY character, provided same is fresh, and preferable (when it is prepared) to have it still with the animal heat in same.  In milk and in nuts there will also be found those properties as will bring bettered conditions for the body.

11. Well, too, that the body rest until there is sufficient strength to begin with exercises as would keep that as will be added to the system in vital forces distributed properly in the system, and do not become burdensome or over-active in directions that would PREVENT the restoration of vitality for the body.

12. After this has been kept up for ninety days, then we would give further instructions for this body.  We are through for the present.
