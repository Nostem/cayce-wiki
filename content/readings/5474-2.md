# TEXT OF READING 5474-2 F 19

**Reading [[5474-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/24/1930  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 13684

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by her father, Mr. [4788], and her Aunt Mrs. [310].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [310].]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Tennessee. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find many changes are seen in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that as we have had before, while those conditions or causes - or disturbances as we have had or given - show little change in the vitality, or in the repressions as have existed, and still do exist in the system.  While the system has responded to those applications made, these have been rather as those of the effects in system; not the causes in system; as have been removed.

2. We would find that, were changes made - so that those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the physical functioning of the body, as is related to the organs of pelvis, as indicated - and the eliminations as come through the functioning of organs in same through those periods, as has been indicated - this taken rather into consideration, and as the basis from which the effects are to be created in the system, to bring about a normal coordination - and the manipulative measures used AS the basis of same, with only those of the NECESSARY local applications as to CLARIFY the conditions for the body, and the general health as related to the digestive forces and of the nerve forces of the body accentuated in the rebuilding and replenishing, we would bring about the nearer normal forces for this body.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Has she chosen best subjects for special work? regarding school?
(A)  These are very good.  Depends upon the application in the directions as are chosen.

4. (Q)  Has she selected the proper school for this?
(A)  That depends more upon self than upon school.

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
