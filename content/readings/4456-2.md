# TEXT OF READING 4456-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4456-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/18/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12317

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 18th day of March, 1925, in accordance with wire request made by self - Mr. [4456].

[Edgar Cayce, Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                            Norton Hotel, 
3:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                    Detroit, Michigan.

1. LS:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief for this body.

2. EC:  Now, the general physical conditions in this body are somewhat improved from these as we have had before.  There are still abnormal conditions with the body.  Much of this is produced or kept in that manner (better it would be to say) by the body's inattention to self and care of diet, and of things taken into the system that produce distresses to the body in its functioning.  The greater distress being through the improper eliminations, produced by system being overtaxed.  That is, through the digestive tract and through the eliminations, especially in the kidneys, and these as from over-stimulus to the hepatic circulation and the system attempting to eliminate and to create the equilibrium necessary for the functioning of the system brings distress to these portions of the system.  These, as we find, will be assisted most by the care and attention to the diet and to the eliminations in the intestinal tract, keeping that in system in the form of laxatives that will produce the proper eliminations through the lower portion of system.  Not [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] the system with stimulants that make digestion the harder, and overtaxes kidneys and liver in its functioning, by taking such properties in the system and keeping the diet coarse and of the nature producing these eliminations, adding to this every day the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be found in this:

3. To one gallon of rain water, add:

Plain Charcoal.................1/2 pound,
Copper Sulphate..............1 1/2 pound,
Sulphuric Acid C.P...........1 1/4 ounces,
Common Zinc......................6 drams.

4. This solution as we find will charge itself.  The container (which should be glass) should be placed in a large container, about which there would be placed quantities of ice, keeping solution very cold.  The poles as would be used in this one should be copper, the other nickel.  To these poles should be attached insulated small copper wires. To the end of the wires on the copper end a small copper plate or band.  On the nickel pole the small nickel plate or band.  When attached to the system, one would be attached to the wrist and the other to the ankle.  Copper to wrist. Nickel to ankle.  These would be alternated from right and left side.

5. These we find will bring the best [[Vibration|vibrations]] to this body, [4456], keeping the system, as we have given; with the proper care and attention we will add much to this body.

6. We are through.
