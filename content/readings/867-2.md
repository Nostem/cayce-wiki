# TEXT OF READING 867-2 F 31

**Reading [[867-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/7/1939  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5133

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of September, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [867], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. [815].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [867]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while there are some changes - some that have been towards improvements - there are others that are accentuations or extenuations of those disturbances which have been indicated in the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, - especially towards those influences which, as we have indicated, have produced the abnormal condition in or to and for the lower extremities.

4. These are effects in a portion of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pituitary]] system, as well as a part of the adrenal [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].

5. As we have indicated, these may be the better or the MORE influenced by the electrification of the impulses by the use of the Radio-Active Appliance; used about the body, - that is, the more often make the first attachment to the right wrist one day, the left wrist the next day, and the last attachment to the opposite ankle, rather than circulating the body with the attachments as ordinarily outlined, see?  Be sure the same plate, or the plate coming from the same anode, is always attached first, - that is, the one marked as the positive anode.  Use the Appliance for one hour each day, but don't use it one day and then change the hour or use it some other time of day at the next.  Be consistent and be persistent, and we will bring results.

6. Then the diets that have been indicated, and the Fume Baths, with the exercise that has been outlined, as we find would bring the better conditions, - and better health in many other disturbing ways that have at one time or another arisen in the system.

7. Also be consistent with the exercise, not doing it one day and leaving off the next, but be CONSISTENT!  With the exercise, - we would sit on the floor, raining the limbs (lower limbs) without the use of the hands, see?  Also brace the feet against the wall, rise upon the hands, and then circle the body, - that is, the circular motion for the hips and the torso of the body.  This will tend to aid in the corrections produced by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, as well as the corrective [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces to the glandular system.

8. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections would be in the lumbar and sacral, - these would be most beneficial.  However, these treatments - rather than being close together in series - would be a real correction in the lumbar axis and the coccyx end of the spine, and through the sacral, but far apart, - that is, if one a month were taken and properly given, it would be better than a series - for THIS particular body, - if the other conditions as indicated are adhered to.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Any disturbance of heart, functional or organic?
(A)  None save as incidental to the disturbed glandular force as affects the pituitary, especially.

11. (Q)  How can extreme chronic nervousness be eradicated?
(A)  By the removal of the pressures as indicated, and the use of the Appliance.

12. (Q)  Would [[New York City|New York]], [[California|California]], [[Florida|Florida]], etc., prove most beneficial location for body?
(A)  As we find, in the very extreme seasons [[Florida|Florida]] (that is, extreme cold), and the greater portion of the time in [[New York City|New York]].

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self through Mr. [815].

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Ave., New York City.)
