# TEXT OF READING 3980-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[3980-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/29/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11660

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [3980].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:50 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, telling whether the treatments have been, and are being, given as fully and completely as should be, and whether there are any other treatments that should be given the body at this time.  You will also answer any questions relative to this body that I may ask.

2. EC:  Now we find in many respects the physical conditions in this body are better than when we had them before.

3. The treatments as have been accorded the body, and are being given, in many ways are in the right manner, though not as fully as should be.

4. The violet ray, we find, has not been given properly at all times.  Not as much of the [[Vibration|vibration]], neither has the applicator been projected sufficiently far in the vagina to reach those portions of the system that should receive the [[Vibration|vibration]], for the womb and tubes must be rejuvenated by this [[Vibration|vibration]].

5. We find also that the injections (endoderm) [endocrine?] have not been of sufficient number.  The three are proper at and to the present time.

6. The stupes [sitz?] have not been taken regularly, neither has the water been of sufficient high temperature, and these had preferably be taken in enamel ware, that we have the correct vibrations for the system and not changing the temperature and the ozones and fumes as thrown off from the properties placed in water, and with these conditions we would at this time, before the other injections are taken, correct chiropractically or osteopathically, preferably chiropractically, if these are done properly, the condition in the lower lumbar and sacral region and coccyx.

7. This, when given at least twice each week, for five to six treatments, will change the body's vibrations sufficient so that the injections will have the direct influence upon the genitive organism.

8. The body should not be so much on its feet, or so active while these are being taken.  Rest more.  Sleep more. These must be accorded the system that the rebuilding in the system, and the recuperation as is given through the treatments accorded, will find their incentives to coordinate with the body throughout.

9. Do that, and we will find we will bring this body to that condition as desired.

10. (Q)  What causes the trouble with the kidneys?
(A)  The standing on the feet, reducing the circulation to the lower portion of body and producing pressure through the lower part of the hepatic circulation, which brings strains on kidneys.  Hence the reason for not so much on feet, or lifting, and that the adjustments necessary in the lumbar and sacral region be given to stimulate circulation properly, and not produce strain on kidneys.
