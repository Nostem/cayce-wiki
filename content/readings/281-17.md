# TEXT OF READING 281-17

**Reading [[281-17]]** · Series: [[281 — The Glad Helpers - Healing Prayer Group|281]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/24/1933  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1800

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of May, 1933, in accordance with request made by the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Florence and Edith Edmonds, Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:30 P. M.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers]], members of which are present in this room.  You will give at this time advice and counsel regarding their work and answer the questions which will be asked concerning any phase of it.

2. EC:  We have the group and the individuals that are gathered here, designating themselves as the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers]].

3. In the manifestations that are the experiences of those that labor, or who give themselves in service through the activities of such a group, this then would come as the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] at this particular time:

4. Find in self that desire, that love for service that He gave in the ministrations and manifestations of His power in the earth, concerning those things and conditions that prevented individuals from being whole indeed.  And as He sought to comfort them in their various phases of experience, in their varied forms of development, so would ye do well to manifest without thought of self, rather that His name may be glorified in the earth; remembering ever that He gave, is "Blessed he whose errors are covered by kindness done to a faltering brother," and "Inasmuch as ye do it unto one of these, my little ones, ye do it unto me."

5. Be ye then faithful, every one, keeping that counsel, keeping that period of [[Prayer|prayer]], meeting them that seek in thought and with them present before Him to whom all power is given, that ye may all indeed be whole every whit.

6.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Please give five affirmations for patients to be used by them for meditations.
(A)  CREATE IN ME, O FATHER, THAT PEACE, THAT HARMONY, THAT LOVE, THAT WILL BRING THE COMPREHENSION OF THY LOVE, THY MERCY, THY [[Grace|GRACE]], IN ME, IN MY BEING, JUST NOW.

The second, AS I SEEK, WILT THOU HARKEN, O FATHER OF MERCY, AND IN THE PROMISES THAT WERE GIVEN BY AND THROUGH [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]] IN THE EARTH, MAKE ME WHOLE EVERY WHIT - NOW.

The third, AS I COME TO THEE, O FATHER, SEEKING TO KNOW THY LOVE, THY [[Grace|GRACE]], THY POWER, MAY I SEE THIS LOVE, THIS POWER, THIS MERCY THOU HAST PROMISED THROUGH THY SON, IN ME - JUST NOW.

The fourth, [[Lord's Prayer|OUR FATHER]], IN THY MERCY HELP ME TO SO LIVE, TO SO KEEP MY INNER SELF IN ACCORD WITH THY LAW, THY LOVE, AS TO MERIT THESE BEING MANIFESTED IN MY LIFE, MY BODY - JUST NOW.

The fifth, [[Lord's Prayer|OUR FATHER]], OUR GOD, IN THY MANIFESTATIONS OF LOVE, REMEMBER ME - JUST NOW.

8. (Q)  As I call the name of each individual on our [[Prayer|prayer]] list, you will please give an individual message. First, [294]:
(A)  Keep thy face to the light, my son, that those who behold thy going in, thine coming out, may be strengthened by the brightness even of thy countenance; may it show, through the thoughts that are kept there, that thy presence is seeking His presence day by day.

Keep the body, keep the mind, in accord with those promises, that in and through thee, in thy love manifested in the earth, may come those glories, that those peoples may talk, may speak, may hear, as face to face with him that may bring peace, harmony, glory, and be the forerunner of His return.

9. (Q)  [539] and family:
(A)  Be patient, be kind.  Be gentle in thy ministerings day by day; for though there may come those periods when the burden seems heavy, and the light fades in the life, yet he that is faithful unto the end shall wear the crown.

Keep the fires of love burning in thy hearts day by day, for the love of God is manifested in the earth through those that are just kind one to another.

10. (Q)  James [[295]'s brother]:
(A)  Listen more often to the voice within, Kick not against those things that seem to hinder, for whom the Lord loveth He chasteneth and purgeth every one.

Be thou, then, faithful in the little things of life and He will crown thine efforts with contentment, with joy, with life, with love.

11. (Q)  [585]:
(A)  Keep thy paths straight.  Be not unmindful that the many seek with thee that thou shouldst have an understanding heart, that the glory of the Father may be manifest in thee.

Be ye patient, be ye gentle, be ye kind.  Speak gently one to another.  Know thy redeemer liveth, and calls that "If ye love me, keep my commandments."  They are not grievous nor hard to bear when thou understandest that the love of God is sufficient to those that wholly trust in Him.

12. (Q)  [2155]:
(A)  This would be well to give as the [[Meditation|meditation]], as there is the administering of the magnetic influences that we have given should be administered to the body:

LET THY INNER SELF BECOME MORE AND MORE AWARE OF THE PEACE, THE HARMONY, THE COORDINATION, THE COOPERATION OF GOD'S LOVE, GOD'S PEACE, GOD'S HARMONY, IN THY PHYSICAL AND THY MENTAL BEING - NOW!

13. (Q)  SARAH [[993]'s sister]:
(A)  Let the peace that comes from knowing, by [[Meditation|meditating]] on His love, be thy portion - just now.

14. (Q)  [146]:
(A)  This should be given with those periods of [[Meditation|meditation]] and the magnetic powers that go from body to body, as the [[Superconscious|superconscious]] and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces possess or direct the material or [[Physical Body|physical forces]] through the activity of the spiritual reactions in body:  [See 146-8.]

THE PEACE AND THE HARMONY THAT COMES FROM THE FATHER OF LIGHT, AND LOVE, THAT IS OURS THROUGH THE PROMISES OF [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]] IN MAN, IS POSSESSING THY BEING - NOW - AND MAKING THEE WHOLE, AS HE WOULD HAVE THEE - NOW!

IN HIS NAME, IN HIS POWER, BE THOU WHOLE!

15. (Q)  ROSEHILL SCHOOL:
(A)  Let the peace that comes from the knowledge and the power of the Father, through the Son, manifested in the spirit of mercy, justice and love, be shed abroad on all that aid in making the experience of all more worth while in the undertaking here - just now.

16. (Q)  MRS. A. M. POPE:
(A)  Keep the mind and body singing the glories of the Father, through the Son in the earth, just now.  [See 146-8.]

Keep the ways open for His voice, His presence, to be manifested more in the love that ye show through the ministerings day by day to the needs of His children that have erred and - through thee - may see the light now.

17. (Q)  [300]:
(A)  It would be well that this be given with those periods of suggestion, in administering the power or emanations from body to body in periods of meditation or [[Prayer|prayer]]: LET THE POWERS THAT MAY MANIFEST FROM THE HOLDING OF THE [[Christ Consciousness|CONSCIOUSNESS OF THE CHRIST]] LOVE IN THE EARTH NOW, QUICKEN THE SPIRIT WITHIN THEE AND MAKE THEE WHOLE - NOW.

18. (Q)  [326]:
(A)  In Thy mercy, in Thy power, make manifest Thy blessings on me now.  As Thy glory, as Thy peace comes to me, may I be conscious of its action upon and within me - now.

19. (Q)  [409]:
(A)  Let the glory of the love, that has been and is being manifested in thee now, become the guiding influence in thy conversation, in thoughts, in deeds, day by day, that ye may grow in [[Grace|the grace]] of Him that has brought to thee - NOW - that understanding in His name!

20. (Q)  [422]:
(A)  Let the spirit of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] be near thee now.  Let His HAND guide thee day by day.  Feel HIS presence about thee at all times.  Be not afraid in His love, that thy body, thy mind, may be a channel of blessings to someone - now!

21. (Q)  ASS'N. FOR RESEARCH AND ENLIGHTENMENT:
(A)  May we, as members of an organization that has its [[Ideal|ideals]] in being of a service to its fellow man - through the grace of God that is given to all that would seek through such a way to be a channel of blessing, cast not this opportunity aside as it comes day by day; but may I, as one of His children, so live that OTHERS may glorify Him - now! [See copied also under Work Rdg. 254-65, and used in 6/33 [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] Bulletin.]

22. We are through.

---

> R1. 6/18/33 GD's talk at 2nd Annual Congress - [Originally

> 
> THE [[Purpose|PURPOSE]] AND WORK OF THE [[Glad Helpers|GLAD HELPERS]]
> By [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Second Annual Congress,
> June 18, 1933.
> 
> The [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers]] is a [[Prayer|prayer]] group within the Norfolk Study
> Group No. 1, that is open to those who seek through it.  I
> think the [[Purpose|purpose]] is very well signified by the name - Glad
> Helpers.  We ARE [[Glad Helpers|glad helpers]].  It is our [[Purpose|purpose]] to help
> individuals, physically and mentally, by attempting through
> [[Meditation|meditation]] to awaken the divine within each.  We have been
> told through a reading by Mr. Cayce that the spiritual is
> the life, the mental is the builder, and the physical is
> the result.  Therefore, no [[Healing|healing]] of any nature, mental
> or physical, can take place without an awakening of the
> inner or spiritual self.
> 
> We have three periods of [[Meditation|meditation]] each day, and we ask
> all to join us who have the desire to aid in bringing peace,
> harmony, light and immortality to others, through the raising
> of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Consciousness within and projecting it to
> others.  We believe that [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], has shown the
> perfect Way, He has laid the foundation, He has reached
> perfection; but we must raise ourselves and overcome the
> carnal forces even as He, in order to reach the perfection
> towards which we should all be striving.  We believe He
> taught that every inharmony, every suffering, is a result
> of disobedience to divine laws; not a punishment direct from
> God, but a natural result of our own acts.  Earthly parents
> might try very hard to impress upon their child that it must
> not do a certain thing or it will suffer thereby.  But if the
> child goes ahead anyway, then it must suffer the
> consequences.  The parents can help, but the child must learn
> the lesson.  Jesus, The Christ, through having overcome, has
> made the way easy.  He has blazed the trail.  We only have to
> tune in to His Consciousness to be free from all earthly
> woes.  No matter how straight and narrow the path, we can hew
> to it with His help.
> 
> So, it is our purpose to try, through [[Meditation|meditation]] - and also
> through our thoughts, words and acts day by day - to break
> down those barriers which have been builded between the
> material and spiritual, and project into those vacant places
> the love, peace and understanding that may cause perfect
> harmony.
> 
> The higher we are able to raise the Christ Consciousness
> within us, the more we are able to project it outward and
> direct it to certain individuals who are seeking.  If those
> individuals are in attune at the time, they catch it
> immediately.  If not, there may be a delay in their receiving
> it, but the good is not lost.  It goes out into the ether,
> and adds to the constructive influences that are building
> towards perfect harmony and peace.
> 
> The following illustration may explain just what takes place
> in [[Healing|healing]].  As a reading has said, [See 281-8, Par. 31-A,
> 32-A] it is not the ONLY way that individuals find
> attunement, but it IS a good illustration:
> 
> The bodies whom we hold in meditation are as musical
> instruments, some very finely attuned and others almost
> entirely lacking in harmony.  The desire on the part of
> the one seeking aid creates the attunement of the instrument
> so that the player ([[Entity (Cayce Term)|the Entity]]) may begin.  The letters
> in each individual's name may represent the notes of music
> that must be learned by the player.  We (the Glad Helpers)
> represent the teachers of music.  When the teachers and
> the players are able to cooperate in bringing the greatest
> harmony from the notes given them, then the Master Musician
> of the music of the spheres, (the Christ) becomes the
> orchestra leader, and perfect harmony reigns throughout.
> [Used in 8/33 [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] Bulletin.]
>
