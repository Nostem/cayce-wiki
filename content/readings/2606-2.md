# TEXT OF READING 2606-2 M 3

**Reading [[2606-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/31/1942  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 9646

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2606]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are real and definite improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  Still, there is much yet to be desired, and there is the natural inclination or desire of hurry, or to see better improvements in the ability of speech as well as the response in other directions.

4. But rather as we find, for at least another six months, make haste slowly; that is, keep very persistent and consistent with those applications that have been indicated for this body.  Do not hurry through ANY of the applications.  Be as patient and as persistent in the daily things to be done as if the whole existence depended on same; for it does!

5. Thus we will find, being patient, being prayerful, being consistent in the applications, the better forces for this body.

6. We would not as yet make changes, save as the natural developments for season and time, as to diet, etc.

7. Be sure that good eliminations are kept, and that there is a good balanced diet for normal developments.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] being used in the right way?
(A)  Being used in the right way; only we would disconnect when not in use, and keep the plates or connections very clean; cleansing with the emery paper or steel wool when it is taken off and again before making the attachments. Make the connections of the Appliance about ten to twenty minutes before attaching to the body.  Make the changes for recharge regularly.

10. (Q)  Is the massage being given correctly?
(A)  This is very good.  Be very prayerful as this is made.

11. (Q)  Is his diet satisfactory?
(A)  As has been indicated.

12. (Q)  How much improvement is indicated in his mentality?
(A)  About ten percent.

13. (Q)  What can be done to help him walk more steadily?
(A)  Just the persistence and being consistent in all those things given.

Do that, for another six months, see?

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
