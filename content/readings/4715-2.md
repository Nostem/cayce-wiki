# TEXT OF READING 4715-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4715-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/24/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12677

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of December, 1932, in accordance with request made through Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will describe the specific conditions with this body, explaining just how the treatments suggested in previous reading may be helpful.  You will answer questions.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As has been give, the CONDITIONS are as described; and there is the necessity - would there be aid brought - that the FIRST things be done first for the body, as outlined.

4. That the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] are involved, and cause the brain infection, is evidence by the properties given for cutting off - through the oxygen that may be released, and for the specifics for producing [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] elimination that absorbs FROM, rather than the overflow TO the brain, which if effected through the circulation.

5. Give that which we have outlined, if we would aid in REDUCING the condition.

6. (Q)  How long should this treatment be followed before having a check reading?
(A)  Sufficient to effect changes in the condition.

7. We are through for the present.

Wired to [277] Copy to [277] Special Delivery
"    " Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]
"    " File
