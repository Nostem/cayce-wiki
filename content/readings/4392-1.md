# TEXT OF READING 4392-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4392-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/14/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12232

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno. Mrs. [4392].]

Time of Reading 
12:35 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we have some abnormal conditions in this body.  They have to do with the elimination in the system, and the effect as this produces on the circulation and the nerve forces, especially, of the sympathetic nerve system.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body here, Mrs. [4392], we are speaking of, present in this room: First, in the blood supply in the body, we have that subnormal force as shown in the circulation, that being produced by elimination as shown through the system where the blood should be cleansed from impurities as is shown in used forces in the system, so we have some functional trouble in portions of the body at times produced by the dross being left in nerve and ligament centers, this then produces the strain on these portions, the numbers of blood cell force, in red being below normal, the white above normal, see.  In the lymphatic forces we find secretions at times being attempted to be created above organ excess in portions of the system.

3. In the nerve forces we find through the pelvic region some distress to the centers there and sympathetically, this is carried over the system.  The [[Vibration|vibration]] being below the normal for the perfect elimination there as in other portions of the body, though it is shifted about, as it were, from time to time, see.  This produces at times some strain to the nerves, reflexly, to the head, and then we have in this reaction, the low circulatory forces and produces the poor capillary elimination in the system.  This then becomes the submerged condition in the body, see, and acts sympathetically and reflexly over the system.

4. In the functioning of the organs themselves we find most of these very good, only as the poor circulation at times, or submerged circulation produces in the functioning organs the distress rather than diseased condition, see.  This as we find through the digestive tract.

5. With the [[Vibration|vibration]] increased in the lumbar and sacral region we will find that the increased circulation through the hepatics will produce the stimulation to the lymphatics in the capillary circulation and relieve the conditions to this body, see.

6. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, is there any other treatment for this body?
(A)  Do this if we would relieve this condition in this body, see.  The [[Vibration|vibration]] would be either those to the centers in the sacral and lumbar region or with medicinal properties that will give the vibration for relief.  With the vibration to the centers we did not have to overcome the dross as produced by medicinal properties in their reaction to the pneumogastric forces, see, so the vibration of the force necessary in the fourth lumbar and in the sacral from the lower lumbar to the end, see, would produce the correct vibration for the body to find relief, see.

7. (Q)  What must be used to produce these vibrations to the body, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  The vibration to these portions of the body necessary to increase the incentive of nerve energy and stimulus of blood supply, which is taken care of through the system, see.

8. (Q)  Do you mean vibration given by violet ray or something like that?
(A)  We mean vibration to the centers, not of applied force, but the vibration set in the system.  Not of material, mechanical or applied vibration, but that as set in the system of vibration, that of the deep massage or shock or vibratory force, see.

9. (Q)  What is the condition of this body's eyes, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Through the reflex forces as applied to the circulation being deep-seated and in removing dross from the circulation, see.  With the correct vibration in the body here in the lumbar and sacral, you see, we increase the vibration and circulation in all portions of the body, see.  With used forces in the body there becomes dross from energy used, and this must be replaced by new cellular force or else we produce to the nerve energy, the slacking of vital forces as here, you see.

10. (Q)  Will this body ever be able to remove her glasses?
(A)  If she makes up her mind to, and with the correct vibrations, she might.

11. (Q)  What is the condition of her skin?
(A)  Poor circulation and bad elimination through the skin force, or the circulation to the skin produces within this between the cuticle, you see, here, the forces attempting to be eliminated this produces then the dross element trying to be eradicated by the increased forces as supplied through the over-amount, as the system creates, of destructive forces in the body.

12. (Q)  What causes the joints of her fingers to be distorted?
(A)  The body attempting to eliminate, as we have given, see. When we get an excess about the ligaments here, the body is trying to eliminate the used forces, see, and the hands are used more than other portions of the body, and the excess not being carried away, produces the distortion about the joints, you see.  The same vibrations will relieve the condition here, and comes from the lumbar and sacral region.

13. (Q)  How will these vibrations be applied?
(A)  With the deep vibration to the centers in the lower lumbar and sacral region as would be applied by deep manipulation or vibration as given here, you see, by the jar, by the vibratory reaction as produced by deep manipulation from vibratory forces as applied in this manner, you see.

14. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, do you mean vibration by percussion, as you saw demonstrated in Dr. Palmer's office yesterday? [H. E. Palmer, M.D.?]
(A)  Percussion, that is very good.  That will be produced either by the percussion or by the deep manipulation, or that, that sets these centers in the vibration and gives the extra flow of blood to this portion of the body and stimulates throughout.  Percussion will be alright.

15. (Q)  Do you mean deep manipulations by an [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]]?
(A)  If we had, we would have said it.  Deep manipulation that produces vibration.  [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] only stimulates the circulation.  Do as we have given if we would correct these conditions.  We are through.
