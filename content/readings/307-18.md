# TEXT OF READING 307-18 F 62

**Reading [[307-18]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/8/1940  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 2326

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of October, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [307], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [307].

3. The disturbances in the present, as we find, have been caused by cold or congestion in combination with the acute conditions from the toxic forces, as combined with the general conditions of weakness, lack of vitality, lack of blood supply, as we have indicated.

4. With the eliminations somewhat aided, there has been shown improvements in some directions.

5. In the present we would apply a Musterole over the chest, and also in the back between the shoulders.  This would be not so hot as to blister, but as to heat the body.  This is more to the right side, of course, than to the left, in the present.

6. We would keep the body quiet.

7. Keep mostly on liquids, especially plenty of orange, lemon and grape fruit juices.  Best if the fruits themselves of these are taken, rather than just the juice.  There is needed not only the bulk of the pulp, but the influence arising from the system's attempt to digest the pulp of these.

8. Oatmeal, whole wheat, [[Beef Juice|beef juice]]; any stimulant that the body can assimilate - these natures are the better for the body.

9. We would use the Appliance, but have both anodes correct. One should be copper, the other nickel.

10. These as we find, with the gentle massage and the use of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], should aid in strengthening and in building up the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What should be taken to aid eliminations?
(A)  Other than the course of the juices and fruits and the characters indicated, we find that those of the senna base are better for this body; such as Syrup of Figs, Boal's Rolls, or the like.

13. (Q)  What should be done for sore mouth?
(A)  Keep it rinsed with Lavoris two to three times a day.

14. (Q)  Have the treatments as suggested been properly taken?
(A)  So far as they have been administered or taken, these have been very well.  These as indicated here are the acute conditions, as combined with the general condition.

15. Do those things indicated for this body.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
