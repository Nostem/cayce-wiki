# TEXT OF READING 2084-15 F 15

**Reading [[2084-15]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/6/1943  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 8708

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [2841], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2084]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions have progressed very well with this body.  There may be expected at times the repression or congestion in the ovarian areas, especially when there is cold in the body or excess poisons at the Periods. These are the sources of the pains inside.

4. At such times apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs, using hot salt as the source of heat for such, and these will be found to relieve the disturbances.

5. Afterwards have the gentle [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxation, especially stimulating the emunctory patches to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in those areas just above the pubic center that set up drainages to the ovarian areas.  A gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over these areas, then, will stimulate a better flow as well as stimulating these patches for activity of the emunctories in the area at [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach.  These gentle treatments would be from the front, not from the cerebrospinal system; these are rather to the sympathetic and emunctory and lymph circulation.

6. Do that and we will find, with keeping close to the general suggestions given, we will keep better conditions for this body.

7. Through the periods of the winter or cold weather, keep the vitamins A and D especially for this body, with the occasional use of B-1.

8. For this particular body, we will find that the A and D in the One-A-Day brand, also the B-1 complex would be well; only don't take the B-1 every day - about twice a week should be sufficient.  Take the One-A-Day, A and D every day, from the 15th of December, through the 15th of March. This will be well for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Any changes in her diet other than these vitamins?
(A)  No great changes in diet, except don't get too much fats for the body.  Don't have too much starches.  Vegetables, brown bread, rice bread, rice cakes, and such foods as have been indicated are better for the body.  When fowl is taken choose rather the very small bony pieces rather than what might appear to be the more delicate.  Take those that are not supposed to be [[Free Will|choice]] pieces - as the wimblebit, the back, the neck, the feet.  All of these are much preferable and have much more vital energies for body building.  Fish should be taken occasionally, sea foods especially.

11. (Q)  Any further suggestions for treatment or attitude?
(A)  Keep those that have been outlined.  These will be well for this body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
