# TEXT OF READING 225-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[225-1]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/11/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 1050

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by the mother of [225].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]].]

11:15 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions and these are of the nature that is hidden in many respects, for they have to do with physical conditions and with mental aberrations in the physical.  Thence the mental, [[Soul|the soul]], the spiritual, enter into the conditions in this body, [225], we are speaking of.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the system very good, yet from the conditions produced in the body by the improper functioning of the organs through conditions, this becomes distraught in other elements and incentives as carried in circulation.  Hence the surging up of the conditions in the mental, that brings the hallucinations and the demented action in physical functioning of organs in the body, through blood stream, yet blood itself is very good in the elements of same.  [Insanity?]

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find the centers, especially in the solar plexus, frontal and back, involved in the condition, producing the high super-sensitive nature and the condition that is brought through nerve system, both sympathetic and cerebrospinal; in the cerebrospinal affecting the functioning of the organs; in sympathetic affecting the functioning of the mental forces in the body.  In the lower lumbar plexus also we find this reaction, and the adrenal [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] becomes involved in the condition.  Hence the strain in body through the functioning of the pelvic organs and the super-sensitive conditions regarding all the functioning of the creative system in the body.

5. IN THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find most in very good condition, yet the body's functioning through these conditions as produced in the system brings many deflections in the functioning.

6. THEN TO BRING THE BETTER CONDITIONS, for this body, we would subjugate the physical through that reaction of mind over mind, and while in such conditions give those suggestions that will reach the inner portion of the body and awaken the innate forces in the system.  At the same time we would manipulate the organs of the pelvis and stimulate the first cervical, until we get a reaction through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] and the adrenal glands; that is, to the head, and the same as shown in the reproduction organs.  With this, we will bring the better conditions to the body.  [[[Osteopathy|Osteopathy]]?]

7. Do not use narcotics to subdue the mind but rather use those subjugations of these conditions through the action of mental over mental, or mind over mind, and awaken the body from within.

8. Do that, [225].  We are through for the present.
