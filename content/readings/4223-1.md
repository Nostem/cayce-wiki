# TEXT OF READING 4223-1 F TEENS

**Reading [[4223-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/5/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11987

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of July, 1923, in accordance with request made by Dr. J. H. Farrand, D.C.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M.                                  [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  You see this body is in the developing stage.  Now we find that some of the conditions as produced in the body at the present time have been caused by the predisposition or by the condition as existed in the [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] from which the body's [[Physical Body|physical forces]] began.  In the coordination of the nerve through the sensory organism is the lack of the vital forces necessary to raise the body to its highest efficiency.  Throughout the body we find the organs as organs of the physical functioning rather correctly.  The nerve supply, by the lack of coordination between that of cerebrospinal forces and the sensory organism in the body that makes connection direct with those to make the body discern those forces that lay dormant with the adjustment as is being made, we should give to this body those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that will give the coordinating forces throughout the whole system.

2. We would then apply to this body, those forces as found in this:  To one gallon and a half (1 1/2) of rain water, add one (1) pound of Heavy Sea Salt, one (1) pound Copper Sulfate, one (1) ounce of Sulfuric Acid, C.P., three (3) drams of Zinc, that as found in tin, you see, or zinc, galvanized iron or zinc, see.  There would be two heavy poles, copper, one on each side of glass container, in solution.  To each of these would be attached a small wire, copper wires preferred; on each end of wires would be attached a silver plate, very thin plate or tin plated would be very well.  One of these would be attached to the body just above the umbilicus or navel, the other to the third cervical.  Each day this should be done for at least one hour, preferably in the evenings.  Every third day, the one attached to the cervical may be changed to the vocal chords or ear.

3. Do that, and we will find we will not only assist general conditions, but the [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to produce vocal and auditory forces will be created in the system to its best force.  [4223] Columbus, Ohio.  We are through.
