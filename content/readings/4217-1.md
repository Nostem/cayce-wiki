# TEXT OF READING 4217-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[4217-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/12/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11974

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 12th day of October, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Bertha Rosenberg, Steno. Mr. [4219] [[4217]''s father].]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with this body and they may be corrected even to a greater degree than the normal developing will bring the body when the physical is so controlled as to give the greater balance through both physical and mental and [[Soul|soul]] forces with the developing body.  The condition produced is lack of coordination through the system produced more by un-ease, from condition existing in conception, than from conditions existing in the physical at present time.

2. The conditions have to do with the nerve control throughout this system and involve all of the nerve systems within the body itself.  Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, of [4217].

3. First, in the circulation we find rather the lack of quantity than quality of blood force throughout, for the lack of coordination through the ability of those organs to function normally, creating the new life [[Soul|soul]] force within [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself, see.  In the nerve system, as we find in this body the lack of un-ease with the developing body as produced within the whole nerve system and centers in this body, the sacral, the lower dorsal and upper dorsal, involving lower cervical centers.  These affect the body in the distributing of the force throughout the trunk portion of the body itself, and prevent the normal developing forces of the nerve system in their proper force.  Hence we find that there are those portions of the body affecting various portions of the nerve systems developed beyond the normal for the body itself and its present stage of developing.  The effect of these on the organs of the system are various from time to time according to the cycle of the developing of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as is shown in the condition of digestion, of circulation, of the respiratory functioning of both heart and lung, of liver and the lower portions of the pelvic organs in their functioning also.

4. To give the proper balance through the body with the dietetic forces as is given, we would add those of [[Vibration|vibration]] as would be produced from this:

5. To one gallon of rain water add:

Charcoal Plain..............1/2 pound,
Copper Sulfate................1 pound,
Sulfuric Acid CP..........1 1/4 ounces,
Common Zinc...................9 drams.

6. This solution we see will charge itself.  One pole positive used in this solution should be of copper, the other of nickel or silver.  The negative, see, and positive.  To these poles would be attached small copper wires, the end of each wire attached to a small silver plate, both wires, which each evening would be attached to this body, - one evening at the upper portion of sacral region, the next at the solar plexus center, 10th dorsal vertebra, the next the frontal portion of the body, to be fastened in place when in use by a cord or tape.  Used for thirty minutes each evening.  Do that.  This will be kept up until at least six solutions have been used. Best this be kept in glass container and somewhat larger than the quantity of water taken, else it creeps over the sides, see.  We are through.
