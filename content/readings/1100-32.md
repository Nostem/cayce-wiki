# TEXT OF READING 1100-32 F 45

**Reading [[1100-32]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/23/1941  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 6319

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1100], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time. ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1100].

3. As we find, physical conditions in the main are very good.

4. While there are those tendencies for the formation of strep, that has before been indicated, in the present this arises more from changes that are taking place through the general forces of the body; and the unusual temperature is more the nerve exhaustion from these changes.

5. If there is kept good eliminations, there is little to be alarmed about.  But keep these in a bettered condition; and stimulate same for activity not only by good relaxing adjustments osteopathically but with a mineral laxative in the present.  The unbalancing becomes superalkalized conditions in the immediate.

6. These we would do; using something like Eno Salt - taken for a few days, or Sal Hepatica taken occasionally.

7. Then be mindful of the diet and the general activities. These we find as conditions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
