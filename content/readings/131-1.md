# TEXT OF READING 131-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[131-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1910 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/2/1910  Sex: F  ReadingID: 297

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce this 2nd day of November, 1910.

[Edgar Cayce; LBC (?), Conductor; Katherine Faxon (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. (Q)  LBC (?):  You are now asleep and will be able to tell us what we want to know.  You have before you Mrs. [131], who is in the building right now.  She is before you now.
(A)  EC:  Mrs. [131].  We have had her before.

2. (Q)  Well, I want you to go over her carefully, examine her thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with her, if anything?
(A)  Good deal the matter.  Good deal of change from what we had before in the system.

3. (Q)  Tell us what the troubles are now.  Well, what do you find?
(A)  Well, I find all along here, we take from down the spine, from the head down the spine and we find the trouble, most of it, you see, - the seats of the trouble.  All along the nerve centers come in contact with the solar plexus nerve center here and with the cerebrospinal nervous system, enlargement of the nerve centers, of the sympathetic coming in contact with the solar nervous center and same here from the cardiac plexus coming in contact with the sympathetic and cerebrospinal at the eighth dorsal and at the tenth and eleventh dorsal we have a tie-up of the nerve force, you see. Then here is an enlargement of the tissue around the nerve system, just above the solar plexus comes in contact with the sympathetic cerebrospinal and produces to the whole system (externally), to the capillary circulation, TO THE WHOLE SYSTEM, a filling up of these tissues and produces an extra amount of tissue thrown out to the capillary a fullness, a watering of the tissue itself and not coming back into the body itself which should be thrown off through the blood.

The digestion here in the system carries out to the body to re-build the tissue in the blood and then we gather to the nerve force a supply, the supply here is all used up and the waste should be carried out from the digestion or expansion through the lung or through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] carried through the respiration, produces a checking of this matter.  Through the whole system, we have from the reflex action of the kidneys and in the liver itself, refusing to carry out from it. There is no organic trouble.

In turn as the same effect on the white and gray matter of the nerve system produces the same effect on the spleenic action, on the nerve, on the brain and produces a sick, droopy, achy feeling all through the whole  system.  The feet hurt, all through the limbs we have the same feeling that is the reaction from this effect; produces in the nervous system an action on the white and gray matter by this tie-up at the dorsal and lumbar all along the whole system.

4. (Q)  What treatment would you suggest to remove these conditions?
(A)  Give electricity.

5. (Q)  How would you give that?
(A)  All along where the places are affected.  Along the spine.  Electricity is life; life is electricity, you see. Force or the power here in the atom in itself, which is the body drawn up, we have the electric force on the body will if given into the nervous system produce a reaction on the nerves themselves; that within, nature then produces the effect that we need to take out from the system the waste. Stimulate the body along the digestive organs, supply the force that has been burned up which should have aided the stomach in its digestion.  The stomach refuses to take in the proper nourishment because it has been choked up.  Ease it down; the whole system strained produces this whole effect as if it was water, too much water, but it isn't water.  It is this matter being thrown in the system not being allowed to be carried off or expelled from the system.

6. (Q)  Any trouble about the heart?
(A)  We have it here from the same action that we have here from the plexus that governs the heart muscles, produces by reflex action just at times.  No trouble with the heart, no organic trouble in the system at all.

7. (Q)  Any medicine you would prescribe to give in the stomach?
(A)  Take something to carry off the waste matter in the system not to choke the intestinal tract.  Carry it off as we lower it into the intestinal tract or get it from the kidney or from the liver.  This choking up here is all on account of the matter gathering in the system here and electricity along the spine will produce this, make it good, you see, get it out.  The inflammation produced in the system here from these troubles will have to be carried out, you see.

8. (Q)  Any trouble about the bladder?
(A)  Good deal of inflammation along there you see, but no organic trouble.
