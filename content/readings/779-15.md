# TEXT OF READING 779-15 M 38 (Marble Cutter, Protestant)

**Reading [[779-15]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/18/1926  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 4786

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [779].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [779], ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]], also the information as has been given this body in Life and business readings concerning his associations.  You will also have before you his family, home life, the circumstances and conditions surrounding same.  You will tell how he is progressing, and you will answer the questions as I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, here, and those relations as have been made and as exist with family and business, and that information as has been given as respecting same.

3. Now, we find, while the conditions have arisen that are, or have been, unsatisfactory with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], know that there are those conditions that have been made in the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that will prove to be the greater advantage, will [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [if the entity will] take same in that manner as to use same for the better conditions in the life; for in the new associations as will be made, the entity will find that the experience will prove beneficial.  Though the entity may feel that this condition has put burdens on the entity that are hard to understand, or to even make plain with the information as has been given, yet that as given was correct, with the abilities of the entity and that which should have been carried out - yet to meet the needs of conditions at the present, the entity will find that there are at least three propositions that will be open for same.  Take that that gives the most opportunity for the salesmanship, as WELL as the abilities of entity in the manual work as the engraver, see?  Be rather the specialty man.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  It has been given the entity should extend the salesmanship endeavor in 1926 and 1927 farther East, for the greater success, financially and mentally for self and family.  You will please tell us when this condition will come about, and what the entity should do in respect to same at the present time.

(A)  Make those connections as has been given in that which comes near and toward the farther east - for, as the changes are necessitated through the circumstances and conditions as have arisen, these then will bring about, and have brought about changes in the way and manner, and in the line as has been given, see?

5. (Q)  Has the entity's work been satisfactory to the company? If not, why not?
(A)  The entity's work satisfactory.  The dissatisfaction to the firm of changes brought conditions that were not satisfactory with the company.  Work satisfactory - the amount not as satisfactory as might have been.

6. (Q)  What would the Forces advise as the proper course for this body to pursue, under the present conditions and circumstances?
(A)  As that as has been given.  Take that offer as will facilitate the conditions of the entity's abilities as salesman, as workman, and at the same time gives the most time in the home, see?  for through these channels this will work out to the best for the entity - for experience is often the dear teacher, yet experience is the lesson through which each and everyone gains that ability to APPLY knowledge or information gained.

7. We are through for the present.
