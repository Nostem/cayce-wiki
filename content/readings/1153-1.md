# TEXT OF READING 1153-1 F 5 (Hebrew)

**Reading [[1153-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/16/1936  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 6498

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 16th day of April, 1936, in accordance with request made by the parents through a relative and friend, Mr. Albert Weiner, new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Messrs. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] and Nathan S. Sachs.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Albert Weiner, Nathan Sachs, Wm. Goldman, [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], and Drs. Lucien Warner and [[Moses|Moses]] Benmosche (psychologist and physician).]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, those conditions that surround the body.

2. In giving that as we find might be helpful in these conditions, many varied surroundings should be taken into consideration.

3. While these conditions apparently in themselves are both psychological or psychopathic AND pathological, the causes, the reasons and the treatments for, and the effects upon ALL should be considered; when too oft these are looked upon rather as being a hardship, a cross for those about the body, with little opportunity for the physical or mental development of such a body.

4. Then, as to the pathological conditions that exist - and how these may even under their existent conditions be made to become more effective in their reactions, or to give the mind, [[Soul|the soul]] of the body-entity itself, an opportunity for expression or manifestation in this material experience:

5. We find that there is little proper coordination, and that soon must develop from this incoordination the inability for the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] to become either self-sustaining or entirely dependent upon the cares, the attentions of others continually.

6. Then, these become APPARENT problems in the experience of those about the body.

7. As we find, these in the physical or from the pathological standpoint may be aided to that of a helpfulness; as to producing the perfect coordinating mental with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body may depend a great deal upon the desire, the ability of those that have become [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which this [[Soul|soul]] has FOUND expression.

8. So attune themselves in a cooperative unison, in a consecrated, in a spiritual manner, for there being given AGAIN - not of their physical bodies but of their mental, their spiritual selves - the expression of the creative, divine forces.

9. Thus there may be a helpful assistance through the material or physical appliances for producing the proper coordinating forces through the body.

10. As the body-physical IS of energies or electronic forces in itself, there are varied centers throughout the system of a [[Physical Body|physical body]] from which, through which, energies are created by the proper coordinant electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces for not only the sustaining but for the creating of those influences that make for the propagation of the own fluids and forces from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], the centers of organized activity in a body.

11. Then, those influences from the pathological or material standpoint added to the body in such measures as to give IMPORT to each of the centers along the whole of the system for their PROPER coordinative forces, we may bring help and aid to this body.

12. This COMBINED with the efforts of a life lived, not in word but in act, in truth - by those influences from which the physical sprang - we may bring aid that would be of a helpful influence in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], this [[Soul|soul]].

13. Then, with those forces of the low electrical applications carrying elements that act upon the centers of the body through their [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces - not into the system other than in [[Vibration|vibratory]] ways, there may be brought an aidful experience for the body.

14. We would use, then, the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying an influence of the low vibrations of Gold in such weakened proportions as to be assimilated, or a minim of the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] solution to an ounce of distilled water; alternated with the iodides or [[Iodine|iodine]] in soluble solution such that the vibration of same may also be carried into the body.  These elements given in such a manner, alternately, each day, would be found to be helpful in such experiences.

15. (Q)  How should the attachments be made?
(A)  On the one day when the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] is used, the attachments would be:  The smaller copper anode would be attached first, to the brachial center.  The larger nickel anode, passing through the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]], would be attached last, over the lacteal and umbilicus center.

On the next day when using the [[Iodine|iodine]] solution (or the single strength of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]), the attachments would be:  The smaller copper anode would be attached first, to the axis of the lumbar.  The larger nickel anode, passing through the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], would be attached last, in the lacteal duct area.

Not more than twenty minutes each day, in the beginning.

A gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with oil after same ([[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]), over the whole of the cerebrospinal system, would be helpful.

But remember, these are only half a portion.

The other half is in the sincere [[Meditation|meditation]], [[Prayer|prayer]], and LIVING on the part of those that would aid.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. Albert Weiner
"    " Ass'n file

(4/16/36 GD's note:  See letter [which was enclosed] with directions to be sent to the H.H.R. Co., when ordering the Appliance; also article explaining the theory of such an appliance.

Please let us keep in touch with you; and if everything is not clear, let us know.)
