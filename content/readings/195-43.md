# TEXT OF READING 195-43 M 44 (Realtor, Mfgr. of Auto

**Reading [[195-43]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/19/1927  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 756

Accessories) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va.  Beach, Va., this 19th day of July, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195], Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Dr. [5618].]

Time of Reading 
12:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you, present in my hand, article written by [195], complete with exception of some copies of Life Rdgs. in a body.  You will give suggestions for betterment of introduction and make any other corrections, and you will also on page 8 enlarge on statement at bottom of page.  It is given that these changes were occasioned by the relative position of the earth with the other spheres in the solar system [195-29?].  On page 9 you will make any suggestions and will dictate a more clear syllogism and give a more logical conclusion relative to [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]].  You will speak distinctly at a normal rate of conversation.

2. EC:  Yes, we have that as has been written, and this, as we find, is very good, and rather in keeping with that suggestion as has been given as regarding the preparation of such an article.

3. As to those conditions relative to disturbance in the earth's surface, and changes as have occurred from time to time, as stated, these are very good, and that is needed to bring discussion, so that other articles and other data may be gathered from these same sources.

4. To state the full and complete change in the one would be to defeat a portion of that as is desired for the work of one who would take up this phase of the phenomena as may be presented.  For this, as is seen, will be proven, that, as the scientific change has been found by the observation into the terrestrial forces and into those places and conditions about the earth's plane, there is missing some one of the earth's companions or planets, and the combustion or destruction of same caused much changes, see?

5. As to that and the change in the end, or as to the syllogism to be drawn - that as is set forth as regarding [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] should be asked rather as questions, than as statements, and let individuals draw conclusions.  And in THIS manner there will be the call, the necessity for many other articles concerning the data as may be gained through these sources.

6. So, then, these are well, as stated.  Present them and watch the fur fly.

7. Either present these through syndicated articles to be published in various periodicals, or through magazines such as the Ladies' Home Journal, Woman's Home Companion, Delineator, or ANY of those magazines or periodicals that carry such as these.  And there will be seen, as has been given, the necessity of many more such articles, and these first premises as set forth will be established in the fifth article.  These will bring much moneys.  These will bring much discussion.  These will bring many in favor, and many in disfavor - but what is needed most of mankind is to THINK!  This will make one think.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through for the present.
