# TEXT OF READING 2633-1 F (AGE ?)

**Reading [[2633-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/5/1941  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9678

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of December, 1941, in accordance with [phone] request made by Mr. [2413], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

New Rochelle 
Time of Reading                            Hospital, 
10:45 to 10:55 A.M. Eastern Standard Time. New Rochelle, N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now, as we find, the conditions, the circumstances that surround this body, [2633], are such that there should not be any interference in the administrations which have been and are being made.

4. We would only suggest that those close to and responsible for the body should apply themselves, their lives, their activities, prayerfully, sincerely, that there be accomplished those [[Purpose|purposes]] of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], God, with this body.

5. These are the administrations we would give here.

6. Do not interfere, then, other than that as is understood and being applied - but [[Prayer|prayer]], sincerity AND application of [[Prayer|prayer]] in the lives of those about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [2413] - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file

(At your convenience please let us have a history of the case to the time of obtaining the Reading.  Also fill out the regular application form for our files.)

Wire:  No interference suggested  Only sincerity prayerful application in lives close to body.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 12/10/41 [2413]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Enclosed please find my check for $20.00 for a reading on
> [2633] and want to say that I am very much disappointed in
> the reading as you mention nothing but "[[Prayer|prayer]]".
> 
> I would appreciate it very much if you would please be good
> enough to explain the reading that would give the cause, and
> also suggestions for help and relief, and there was nothing
> mentioned about this.
> 
> With best regards, I am
> 
> Very truly yours, [2413]
> 
> R2. 12/16/41 EC's letter to [2413]:
> 
> 
> Thank you for yours of the 10th with enclosures.  Do not
> wonder at your request for an explanation of the information
> Miss [2633] - yet hardly know just how to explain.  Possibly
> if I tell you of a few experiences have had only in the last
> week of similar cases you may be able to draw a conclusion of
> your own.  Evidently, Miss [2633] was not aware that the
> reading was to be given.  Then, too, her being in the
> Hospital, the information was not given to interfere with the
> application being made, thus suggested that as would help
> under any condition.
> 
> But to explain - few days ago a lady from Washington D.C.
> phoned me asking for a reading for her sister [2631].  She
> too was in a hospital, I told her would give same if she
> would talk with her sister about it telling her was doing
> this and have her consent for same.  The information was
> given, sent to her.  Yesterday received word from her sister
> like this "information received, gave it to the nurse asking
> her to do that suggested - she said will have to have consent
> of the Dr., but he refused, but Sister asked for what had
> been said, and has been growing steadily better since talked
> with her, now Dr. says he will do what was suggested as
> there must be something in the attitude of the lady."
> 
> Another Mother from same town phoned for her daughter [2634]
> told her as the other, please have the consent of the
> daughter, but said she is unconscious.  I told her to tell
> her any way - prayerfully, she did, in three days the fever
> had left, she is still on the improve as she says this
> morning.
> 
> There is needs then there be some activity, oral or mental,
> on the part of the person wishing help.  Am not trying to
> build an Alibi, Mr. [2413], not at all, but in one case might
> be coincidental could hardly be so two days running.
> 
> Do hope Miss [2633] is better and all is well.  There is
> answer to [[Prayer|prayer]], am sure you feel that.
> 
> Thank you and with all good wishes, Edgar Cayce
> 
> R3. Subsequently Mr. [2413] wired for emergency reading, on
> someone else, but EC did not give it.
