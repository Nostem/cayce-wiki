# TEXT OF READING 5473-3 M 50

**Reading [[5473-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/14/1924  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 13676

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by wife.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M.                                         ..., Ala.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Isn't feeling very good at the present time.  Has had some conditions that are not at all satisfactory.

2. Now, we find the conditions in this body not as good as when we had them last, you see.  It (the condition) is nearer as that we had at first, though there is not as much of the formation in the duodenum and stomach as there was at first. More tissue now becoming involved, and the lower end or pyloric orifice being inflamed, the exact position of the seat of trouble being rather that just to the left and little lower than the pyloric orifice proper.  Inflammation through duodenum, pancreas, liver and spleen affected somewhat, causing distress in the whole system, and the condition needs to be corrected, and will not be reached by medicinal properties, only to ease conditions.  The properties as medicinal properties should be that of a mild tea, with equal quantities of sage and Yellow Saffron, one to twenty parts. Also well if the water that is drunk carries the properties of elm bark.  This, we see, will empty the stomach, and at the same time act as a sedative and soothing to the parts that are suffering from this condition.

3. We would give then the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as were given the body before, only we would change the poles in this manner:  A plate would be attached to each wire, and one applied to the front portion of body, just below where the misery or distress [is] in the abdomen, or just above the umbilicus, and the other alternated from wrist to ankle.  That is, one evening on ankle, the next evening on the wrist, but opposite sides of body.

4. Do that.  We will find we will assist and bring back the normal [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body.

5. The diet should be that that carries much nutriment, yet easily digested in the system.  Whole wheat in gruel, eggs (not well cooked, but fresh) and the cereals that carry much of milk and such properties.  Junket, arrow root and such foods are best for the system.

6. Do that.

7. (Q)  Give the cause and remedy for the rash.
(A)  From the condition as produced in the system, you see, here, this carries in the circulation as this affects the liver and the spleen and pancreas, and not giving the full assimilation in system.  The body exhumes the poisons and toxins through the capillary circulation producing this. Bathe the parts (as we see the changes) with a solution twenty percent boracic acid wash, with tincture of myrrh ten percent, once each day.

8. (Q)  Should anything be done about almost constant nasal secretions and not being able so much of the time to breathe through nose?
(A)  This produced we see by the pressure on the hypogastric center, and affects direct the front facial muscles, and produces the lymphatic secretions, enlarging the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the portion in nasal cavity.

With the reduction of pressure the body will find relief from this condition.  Then, when this is relieved, relieve the pressure in the fifth dorsal and third and fourth cervical. Chiropractically or osteopathically either.

9. (Q)  The body has been troubled in the past by a condition which the Doctors termed as Hay Fever.  Can you give a preventative measure or remedy when trouble is not being had now?
(A)  Remove the pressure from the hypogastric, and that in the cervical and dorsal, as we have given.
