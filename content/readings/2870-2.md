# TEXT OF READING 2870-2 F 12

**Reading [[2870-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/28/1943  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 10098

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2870], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is she in condition to go to school?
(A)  If there would be administered a regular series of treatments osteopathically, we find that very soon she should be in school.

There needs to be those administrations osteopathically given that will aid [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in meeting those things that are a part of the experience of every woman; in the eliminations - or the Periods.

As indicated heretofore, there have been those subluxations in the lumbar, sacral, and cervical area that need correction.  These corrected, when those periods are beginning their activities, we should find real relief for the body.  There is the general tendency for the formation of gas through the alimentary canal.  This, too, will be materially aided if drainages are set up by the character of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] stimulation in the lumbar and sacral and lower dorsal areas.

5. (Q)  When will menstruation start?
(A)  It should now in the next six months - within that period.

6. (Q)  Will that clear up any of the present symptoms?
(A)  Depends upon whether the conditions of the body have been set in order or not.

7. (Q)  Should the courses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] be continued?
(A)  [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] should be given periodically.  Take one drop each morning in half a glass of water, before any meal is taken, for about five days in succession.  Repeat this about every two weeks.  Don't take it closer together, but don't skip any.

8. (Q)  Also nightly rubs?
(A)  These should be most helpful.

9. (Q)  Why is the back of neck and head so sensitive?  Can this be fully corrected?
(A)  From those subluxations in lower portions of spine. When these are corrected, and one portion coordinated with the other, we will find much bettered conditions for this body.

10. (Q)  Did the appendix operation just after the last Reading, before starting treatments, have anything to do with present condition?
(A)  Much to do with present condition.  For, the sources or causes of disturbance in the appendix area have not been removed.  Hence those subluxations in the lower lumbar and sacral, as well as the dorsal and cervical should be corrected.

11. (Q)  Is it possible for her to be normal?
(A)  Very possible.

12. (Q)  Why are her fingernails so red?
(A)  The manner of the circulation.  This is from pressures along the centers where the extremities - arms and limbs - show stresses from those areas.

13. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mother - (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file
