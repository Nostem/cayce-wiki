# TEXT OF READING 4110-2 F CHILD

**Reading [[4110-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/12/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11810

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:55 P. M. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had it before.  It isn't any different.  We haven't done anything yet.

2. (Q)  What makes this body so very slow in doing things that is in dressing herself?
(A)  We have given these conditions.  The condition of the nerve system is such that voluntary forces become involuntary in certain conditions, as we have just given.  This only shows the condition and how it manifests itself.

3. (Q)  Why does this body continuously pull out her eyelashes and eyebrows unconsciously?
(A)  Just the condition we have given.

4. (Q)  Does this body have [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] trouble?
(A)  Only as we have given.  We have given it.  We are through.
