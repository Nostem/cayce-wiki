# TEXT OF READING 5603-4 M 13

**Reading [[5603-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/23/1930  Sex: M  Age: 13  ReadingID: 13933

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Room ..., 
Time of Reading                           Cavalier Hotel, 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [5603], this we have had before. In a general manner we find the physical conditions are very satisfactory.  While there is not the whole adherence to the diet, nor the consistency in the activities of the body with that being attempted to be accomplished; yet - will these conditions as have been outline be adhered to - we would find a nominal, normal development for the body, providing there are not outside conditions that do not show or manifest at present.

2. We would keep, for the equalizing of the coordinations in the system, those of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as have been outlined, and keep same in a CONSISTENT manner; not as just something to be gotten through with, but as for a [[Purpose|purpose]] - and that [[Purpose|purpose]] may be accomplished, if such is done in such a manner.  Just to tie it on, to get through with it, and say it's done - doesn't mean much!

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What substitute could be used for battery this summer, as ice is so hard to obtain while at camp?
(A)  Don't use a substitute!  No substitutes are as good as that given original!  Just get the ice!

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
