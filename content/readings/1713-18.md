# TEXT OF READING 1713-18 F 31

**Reading [[1713-18]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/5/1929  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 7900

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of September, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1713].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Pa. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [1713].  This we have had before.  Now, we find in many respects the body still shows some improvements.  While there are at times the accentuation of old disturbances, these are rather of the nature as may be called neurotic, or nerve reflexes - and the body should keep rather close to those suggestions as has been made regarding manipulations, osteopathically, and of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] electrically that are necessary to keep the accord within the muscular, the nerve forces, and the general physical functioning of the system.  The ideas often that such a ride, such a walk, such a condition as may be suggested would be harmful or detrimental for the best interest of the body, are more reflex through the nerves' forces than of purely physical weaknesses.  These, then, need to be careful - that there are the proper diets, the proper reactions given for corrections, and then KNOW - within self - these will come about, will the body keep itself MENTALLY in the proper attitude.  Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
