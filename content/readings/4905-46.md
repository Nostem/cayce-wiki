# TEXT OF READING 4905-46

**Reading [[4905-46]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/27/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12971

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                Bosque County, 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[Texas|Texas]].

1. HLC:  You will tell us the conditions existing with the operations at this time.

2. EC:  (Interrupting)  Now, we have [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], the operations here.  These we have had before.  The conditions, the delays as are being and have been caused are the natural consequences of conditions surrounding such operations.  The inability to make further progress in the last few days due to these conditions.  As the operations begin now in the evening, we will find there will be sufficient hole made to encounter that formation in which the gas will be of sufficient pressure to break through into the hole, bringing with same the oil in large quantities.  This should occur in the Sunday morning turn - 30th - 29th to 30th of the PRESENT month.  Be in that same attitude as has been given, for these, as we see, will succeed - see?

3. We are through for the present.
