# TEXT OF READING 5532-2 F 17

**Reading [[5532-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/14/1932  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 13794

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office in Pinewood, on Lake Drive, Va. Beach, Va., this 14th day of March, 1932, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [437].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                   Apt. 14-E, 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5532], and those conditions and circumstances as surround the body.  This we have had before.

2. Now, as we find in the present, while there are physical conditions to be met, and these are apparently of such a nature that little could be - or would be - accomplished in correcting same, yet - as given - are the spiritual and the physical relationships that exist in a living active organism taken into proper consideration, we would find these may be aided materially in the activities of the body that even has the scar tissue affecting the movements of a portion of the body, even in the brain cells themselves.

3. As has been given, this will require - from the physical viewpoint - patience, persistence, on the part of the body and those that would administer those things, those outside elements of the physical, and that there may be added those incentives for the creation of - first - absorption of those affectations, and then the gradual growth of same, that there may be more response and reflex action in that portion of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] so affected; and there will be the necessity of the spiritual activity upon the applying of the treatments for the physical and the reactions in same, as well as upon the part of the body, to that consciousness that may see - in an imaginative first, in a [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] second, in a CONSCIOUSNESS third - the physical conditions taking place in the body.

4. As has been given, those things that would apply to the advent into the physical experience in the present would be of interest AND aid to the body, AS this developing and awakening takes place.

5. Then, in the PHYSICAL activities in the present, first we would apply the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the wet battery appliance that carries Gold in the system.  These would be applied, when the application is made, each day for twenty to thirty minutes.  The anode through which the Gold is being carried into the system would be applied to the 1st and 2nd cervical center, or those impulses as are carried from the trunk portion of the body to the brain cells themselves and the circulation of the greater portion through the medulla oblongata, or the central brain impulse; while the other would be attached to the right SIDE of the body first (keep this rotation in order, see?)  (Attach this with the Gold FIRST, see?), the right ankle plexus; the next treatment the forearm - right side - plexus; the next to the lower lumbar or 4th lumbar plexus; the next to the brachial center or 3rd dorsal plexus.  Then begin at the ankle, then the wrist or forearm plexus, then to the lumbar, then to those of the brachial center.  These, as given, each day. These would be given in this manner first for the twenty-eight days, which would be the practical life of the [[Vibration|vibrations]].  Let the battery when not in use be disconnected from the anodes or leads from container, see?  Keep the anode that are attached to the body bright, by being each time rubbed or polished.  Also keep the lead that passes through the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Chloride]] solution CLEANSED, and we would use at least four OUNCES of the solution from same to pass through. Make the battery of extra heavy charge; that is, to the gallon and a half of the distilled or rain water add two ounces of the Copper Sulphate, see?  one and a fourth ounces of the Acid C.P., and ten drams of the common zinc.  Do not use the charcoal in the this PARTICULAR battery, at THIS particular time.  This should last (the charge) for twenty eight days, beginning with the treatments as outlined.

6. In the mental applications there should be those things carried in the mental mind of the body of one making the application.  This as an affirmation, or [[Prayer|prayer]], or [[Meditation|meditation]], during the whole period:

THERE IS BEING CARRIED INTO MY [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL BODY]] THAT WHICH WILL AID IN ENLIVENING TISSUE, THAT I MAY EXERCISE THE FACULTIES OF MY BODY THE BETTER.  THIS BEING CARRIED THROUGH [[Creative Forces|CREATIVE FORCES]] AS ARE FROM GOD'S STOREHOUSE.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What percent solution of Gold, or is it made in ordinary manner?
(A)  Fifteen grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to ten ounces distilled water.  That proportion.  At twenty-eight days we would give further suggestions.

When there is the desire from the body that there be the spiritual understanding of its advent into the material plane, these may be given.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n
"    " Mr. [437]
