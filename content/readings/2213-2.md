# TEXT OF READING 2213-2 M 16

**Reading [[2213-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/28/1926  Sex: M  Age: 16  ReadingID: 8930

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 28th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by the mother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
12:30 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the information given in a life reading on [2213], July 9, 1926, 7:15 P.M., and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information here.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What direction from [[New York City|New York]] is he?
(A) The information as has been given as respecting the movements of the individual are as those conditions as have been builded in the environs and [[Free Will|the will]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  As to the direction - he left towards the East - at present far East.

5. (Q)  From what port did he sail?
(A)  Gloucester.

6. (Q)  On what kind of a ship?
(A)  Doesn't matter.

7. We are through for the present.
