## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 12/27/23 Mr. Armstrong's ltr. to EC:
> 
> The City National Bank of Selma (Letterhead)
> Selma, Ala.
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> 2411 East Fifth Street,
> [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> Remembering that two or three years ago we asked for some
> information from you [4964-1 a Business Rdg. - no copy
> available] in reference to a shortage of $250.00 claimed by a
> certain bank depositor of ours, and that your help in this
> matter was useful to us, we are writing to you again to ask
> for some further assistance.
> 
> There has been occurring in our bank at intervals from three
> to six months, a shortage over our Receiving Teller's desk,
> running from $50.00 to $100.00.  (I think at least three or
> four of these shortages have occurred.)
> 
> They occur usually after a holiday, when there is a rush at
> the windows, a good deal of currency coming into the bank.
> 
> We believe that one person is effecting this shortage, and
> that he has a plan or system of putting it over.
> 
> The latest shortage ($100.00) occurred yesterday, through Mr.
> [...]'s window [12/26/23].
> 
> We are writing to ask if you cannot endeavor to assist us in
> locating the origin of this trouble.
> 
> Yours very truly, H. C. Armstrong, President
