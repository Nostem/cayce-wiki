## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/20 [1921?] H. Frank Williams letter to [[David Kahn|David Kahn]]:
> 
> UNIVERSAL OIL & PRODUCING CO.
> Fourth and First National Bank Building
> Nashville, Tenn.
> 
> Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]
> 2154 McAlpin Bldg.,
> [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.
> 
> Dear Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]],
> 
> While you and Mr. Cayce were in Nashville Mr. Cayce gave me
> two readings - one of which was on some oil property in this
> state.  I was partly familiar with this oil territory
> and at first it seemed that Mr. Cayce was reading my mind; he
> was describing it so accurately, although I knew he had never
> seen the property.  He was describing a gate through which I
> had to pass as being broken down; also a ravine just beyond
> that and a small spring branch.  I knew nothing of these
> latter things.
> 
> Since you left Nashville I have been up to see the territory
> and found the gate was not down.  On inquiring from the owner
> I found the gate was down the day the reading was given and
> was reparied the same evening.  I found the ravine and dips
> just as Mr. Cayce described and am now making financial
> arrangements to develop this property.  I have utmost
> confidence in you and Mr. Cayce and believe this will be one
> of the greatest oil territories in this section.  Knowing
> that Mr. Cayce was entirely ignorant of the surroundings of
> this place, it seemed wonderful to me to find every detail,
> without exception, just as he described it.
> 
> So far I have found the phosphate reading [3777-3 - See
> [[Arizona|Phoenix]] Phosphate Co. letter 12/13/20.] just as Mr. Cayce
> said an I am expecting great things of this.
> 
> With best wishes, I am
> 
> Very truly yours, [signed] H. Frank Williams
> 
> STATE OF TENNESSEE
> COUNTY OF DAVIDSON
> 
> Personally appeared before me, T. E. Brandon, a Notary Public
> in and for the State and County, H. Frank Williams, and
> acknowledged the above to be his signature, and that the
> statements above set out are true to the best of his
> knowledge, information and belief.
> 
> Witness my hand and official seal, at Nashville, Tenn., this
> 22nd day of January, 1921.  [signed] T. E. Brandon  N.P.
