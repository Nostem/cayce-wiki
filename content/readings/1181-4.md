# TEXT OF READING 1181-4 F 62

**Reading [[1181-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/26/1924  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 6603

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of April 1924, in accordance with request made by her son, [5086], via daughter, Mrs. [1690].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Geo. S. Klingensmith.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                         ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find there is a great deal of difference in the body at the present time than when we had this before.  While the conditions at present are not as bad or as serious as when we had it before, there is  much of the same condition producing that which is the cause of the existing conditions.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body at the present time, Mrs. [1181], we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this below normal, with little reserve forces or energy in the system being stored by the organs through which the blood supply passes.  The system then we find in an anemitized condition without being anemic. The blood supply being deficient refuses then to circulate in its normal condition, giving distress to the organs that are especially acted upon by the circulation through elimination. That is, the heart's action, the functioning of the liver, the functioning through all hepatic circulation is deficient and defective.  The blood supply itself being below normal we have one deficient in red blood and in white blood supply, though their balance is near normal with existing conditions.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find there are many centers that are involved in the condition, and those lesions that have been formed about centers and plexuses are still existent in the system, and those properties that have been given and taken into the system to release, as it were, or to stimulate the functioning of organs as affected by these lesions have only, as it were, run their course without the system being able to create sufficient of those properties to meet the needs of the condition, and to again ward off those ravages as are made on the vital supply in the system.  Hence these centers, these lesions, must be removed if we would give the incentive to the system to function near its normal conditions.  These centers we find, especially in the 7th and 8th dorsal, 12th dorsal, 9th dorsal and in the 3rd and 4th lumbar, these affecting many others and many of the other plexuses, both in the anterior and interior portions being affected by these conditions.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS, we find that many are involved through these conditions, especially that in the digestion and in the functioning of elimination through the emunctory forces.  These will be and may be corrected, with the correction of the conditions that produce same.

6. Then to bring the relief necessary to this body, we would by the manipulation, chiropractically given if applied with the science of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces also give the better relief than either separate.  Then, also to the exterior portion and to assist the circulation, both in blood supply and in the nerve system, apply the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] forces over those portions that give distress, especially in the diaphragm and in the abdominal region.  This we find will bring relief to this body.  These adjustments and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] should be taken at least every second or third day.  Second to begin with, or they will have to of necessity be made light at first, gradually increasing, and the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] taken each evening for five to ten minutes.

7. Do that.
