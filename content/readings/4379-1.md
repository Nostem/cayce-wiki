# TEXT OF READING 4379-1 F 52

**Reading [[4379-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/19/1925  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 12212

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of June 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [4379].

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]]; Conductor, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                 ..., Georgia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions in this body, the seat, cause or beginning of the trouble dating back many years, and have to do with conditions as produced much by self, through conditions as the body knows of.  The condition as produced in the body at the present in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] being those of incoordination, especially as is in the nerve system, the locomotories, and the inability of the coordination with the sympathetic system and the cerebro-spinal, for many of the actions of the involuntary become voluntary and involuntary of the voluntary.

3. In the condition then, these are those to be met, and the rest of the system is subjugated much to this condition. This being then the chief condition to be met, and these may be assisted somewhat through care, persistence and through following out the conditions as will develop by the use or application of these properties to the body.  First, we would every other day apply, from the 2nd cervical to the base of the spine, mud (stiff mud) from the creek near this home. The black loam, with as little of the gravel and sand as possible.  Let his remain on body until the next morning. This will be found to ease and rest the body, after the body becomes to that condition where this is not burdensome - from second to third application.  Next morning after use, bathe the portion of the body thoroughly, massaging into the portion of the spine so covered that property of oil and Life Everlasting, equal parts, massaged into the whole length of the spine.  Make also a battery [[Vibration|vibration]], as will be found in this:

4. To one and a half gallons of rain water, add:

Heavy Sea Salt...........1 pound,
Copper Sulphate......1 1/2 pounds,
Sulphuric Acid, C.P......1 ounce,
Plain Zinc, or Common Zinc..........6 drams.

5. Place in this also an opaque container containing one pound Tincture [[Iodine|Iodine]].  This then should be charged with high voltage, low amperage, for at least one hour.  Using then as poles the copper in one, the nickel in the other.  To these poles attach small copper wires, with thin copper plates on the end of each wire.  Attach to the body on days the mud is not used.  The larger plate, from the nickel pole, to the body just above the umbilicus.  The other plate to the neck, near or at the 1st and 2nd cervical.  Attach these with tapes.  Let this remain on body for at least forty to sixty minutes.

6. Take internally these properties:  to 10 ounces of distilled water, add 15 grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], with 10 grains Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]].

7. Shake solution well before taking, which would be (the dosage) three to five drops in half a glass of water twice each day.

8. When these applications have been made to the body consistently and persistently for thirty days, we will find a change coming to the body.  Then we would give from here further instructions.

9. We are through for the present.
