# TEXT OF READING 136-43 F 21

**Reading [[136-43]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/2/1926  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 350

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by her husband, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same, and you will answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find there is little change, as far as the physical conditions are concerned, from that as we have had here before.

4. That correction as is necessary for the correction of those conditions throughout the system have not been carried out as yet.  Hence there is that arising which brings some conditions in the mental forces of the body of worry, and added to that which may be the contraction in same through physical conditions might bring detrimental effects; yet, were these carried out in the way and manner as has been given, we would find the better conditions for the body - for, as has been seen, with those corrections as would be for the pelvis condition, this correction would bring about that which would so correct the condition, and the organs in the pelvis, that the normal condition throughout pregnancy might bring the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for the body.  With the existent conditions, and the suppressions in same, the corrections as were given, of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, would be that to correct the condition.  With this standing - that is, the physical effects and the suppressions as at present added to same would be used this:

5. To 1/2 pint of Spirits Frumenti we would add Essence of Silk Weed 1 ounce.  The dose of this would be (when shaken together) teaspoonful twice each day.  This, as we find, with corrections, would bring on those conditions as of the normal force, and the suppression would be overcome - but the corrections must be made, for the better conditions of the body.  We would follow those out.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is the body pregnant now?
(A)  Those suppressions, rather, as of those conditions, though the body is in that position where this might come about; yet would not be well under the existent conditions, for with the condition as is seen in the position of womb itself, this would not be well.  Hence those corrections, those treatments, AS SHOULD HAVE BEEN TAKEN for the condition, as has been given, and with the twist, the turn, those conditions existing, these may be brought back in place in a normal way and manner, see?  for, with this condition existent - that is, were pregnancy to begin under the present existing condition, we would find suffering, and scarcely that as is necessary for the strength of the organs so involved to carry out their normal functioning, UNDER the existent condition, see?  Hence that injunction as has been given - MAKE THOSE CORRECTIONS, as would be necessary for the organs of this portion of the body to function in keeping with the other [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the system, and the mental attributes of the body towards such conditions. Osteopathically, make the corrections!  Medicinally, those properties as given.  Do that.

7. We are through with this reading.
