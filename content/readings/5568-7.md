# TEXT OF READING 5568-7 M 6

**Reading [[5568-7]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/1/1930  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 13879

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 1st day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by mother and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, Mrs. Wigal and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                         [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before, [5568].  In many ways conditions have improved in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  There is a tendency towards being overactive with the general building up of the system, and the necessity of suppling, as it were, more of the blood supply TO the system.  This, as we find, may be best aided or added to by those elements as will act as developments for the system, as flesh building, blood building, nerve and muscular forces.  These we may find in those properties as will act WITH the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system, and in the use of same there should be those precautions in the giving of same, that these do not form for the system too GREAT an amount before there is the distribution of same through the system. This, as we find may be in any of those that will aid in the digestion and in adding to those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] and of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the digestive system, especially as related to those in the digesting of fats, see?

2. In manipulations, let these be those that will carry more of the oils - and of that that will stimulate externally those of the capillary circulation, and as these are absorbed in system we will find - with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are kept - this will materially aid the body.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What substitute may be used for the wet battery when body goes home?
(A)  There will be no substitute, but there may be given those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of that in the Radio-active Appliance as carries with same [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  This will aid, but after the rest of one to six weeks, or eight weeks, the body SHOULD have more manipulations, and more of the battery [[Vibration|vibrations]].

4. (Q)  How should Radio-active Appliance be applied?
(A)  Apply same to those of the 2nd cervical and to the umbilicus.  The positive or first attachment to the cervical, to be sure, and that of the larger - or that through which the medicinal properties or vibrations act - to the umbilicus last.

5. (Q)  How often should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given now?
(A)  Once a week will be sufficient, if the changes are made at the end of six to eight weeks.

6. (Q)  Any further suggestions for treatments at home?
(A)  Those of the properties as will add to the building of the system, is the only change.  These must add for flesh, blood, muscle and sinew building, see?  and may be aided most through those of that for the thyroids, and for those of the digestive glands.

7. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Association office
"    " Research Department
