# TEXT OF READING 2877-1 F 6 WEEKS

**Reading [[2877-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/7/1943  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10108

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [2341], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through his sister, Miss [1747].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In undertone - "Yes - where the Dillmans lived.")

3. We have the body here.

4. As we find, there are disturbing conditions preventing the better physical functioning.

5. These, we find, are partially from torpidity of the liver, partially from cold and partially from a subluxation, or a deflection in the flow of energies - even in this developing body - to the liver; and thus lack of proper coordination in the gastric flows in the stomach itself.

6. While those conditions about the mouth and throat, and even on the face, are serious, it is much better that these be outside than internally.  For, this would soon lead to a form of colitis; and unless it is kept in a better state it might become very serious.

7. We would begin with small doses of Fletcher's Castoria; about five drops every half hour until there is good activity from same.

8. After this has cleansed, then begin giving [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]; one to two drops in a teaspoonful of water at least three times a day.  Keep this up until the odor of same may be detected in the stool.

9. If it becomes necessary, repeat the series, or repeat the course of the Castoria - after resting a few days, and followed by the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - two drops in a teaspoonful of water, twice to three times a day.

10. At least twice each day, regularly, massage the spine thoroughly with Cocoa Butter.  Do give these rubs.

11. Do put over the liver and the caecum area a flannel cloth that has been saturated in an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (liquefied), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  This we would keep on the body, pinned to the clothing, and change daily - of course - but do keep this on, until these conditions have cleared up.

12. Do not change so much the formula for the feeding, but do add more water - and make it a little bit weaker.

13. Do these, and we will clear these conditions for this body.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Should any local application be used on face or mouth?
(A)  Carbolated Vaseline would be the better on the exterior portions.  The [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], of course, as indicated to be given, will purify the mouth and throat.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Father - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Dillmans
