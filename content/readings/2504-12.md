# TEXT OF READING 2504-12 M 64

**Reading [[2504-12]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/23/1930  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9425

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions are not ALTOGETHER satisfactory with the eliminations of this system, for the administration of those conditions as to prevent pains are not being eliminated properly from the system.  There should be somewhat of an increase in the eliminations as through the alimentary canal. General conditions as related to those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system, where there has been trouble - through the prostate, through the bladder, the kidneys, in their functioning - these show somewhat of an improvement.  There are better conditions through this region, but there should be more eliminations as related to the alimentary canal, and this will prevent this tendency of the capillary circulation to become full, and we will prevent much of that condition wherein the kidneys becoming involved in the condition, through that as has been warned of in the beginning here, when we had these changes from those injections in the system, as affected the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system, causing first those retensions.  In these ways we would find the conditions would be much improved, as would the battery charges - when properly made connections.  These connections are not in their proper form, as they have been given heretofore.  Give these in a better contact, cleansing same before making the contacts, and their contacts being in perfect accord.  These will make for a better balancing through the system, and this would be best given when the body is resting SITTING up, rather than lying down.

2. (Q)  Should the prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] be massaged?
(A)  They should be massaged occasionally.  Once or twice each week, as we find at PRESENT, would be sufficient.  Watch for the eliminations most, especially those as related to the alimentary canal and hepatic circulation.  These are the things that we need to be mindful of.

3. (Q)  What would be -
(A)  Any of those things that work in conjunction WITH that as has been given as sedentary forces in the system.  These haven't been well balanced, nor proper precautions taken in giving same.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (2 copies)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
