# TEXT OF READING 3584-1 M 2

**Reading [[3584-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/24/1944  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 11076

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

At the home of his 
Time of Reading                           parents, just off 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.    highway 41, 1 mile 
South of ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here - for this will prove to be just that, unless there are definite measures taken to make changes - and these will not change.

3. As we find, there are [[Karma|karmic]] conditions of the body and also the parents.  Yet there are physical disturbances that come from conditions which existed during the period of gestation, and these can be interpreted within themselves if they will analyze it.

4. These pressures in the brain cells, from the portion of the central [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] through which and from which the life processes begin, caused at the time of presentation, pressures in the head and neck.

5. The days in the present may be materially lengthened, but the response will depend upon whether or not those responsible wish to carry through with this or to let processes of nature or time disintegrate this body from [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or [[Soul|soul]].

6. There may be somewhat of a response, and this may be better accomplished (should there be the desire to keep the life here) by the direct application of the low electrical forces that may be had from the Abrahms Machine or this low energy to the body, which would carry the life-forces.  Massages should be given along with this.

7. These applications, as we find, might be given in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]] as by Lydic or others in the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] hospital, there.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is it possible for him to become normal?
(A)  These questions have just been answered.  As we find, the life may be prolonged - but normalcy - unless there's a great change in the conditions, no normalcy in this experience.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

> Remember I have your literature stating you have 4500
> readings ahead - two a day how long will it take to give
> them?
> 
> They aren't interested in joining your organization it's the
> readings.
> 
> Sincerely yours, Mr. & Mrs. [...]
> 
> R2. 3/8/44 GD's ltr. to [3584]'s parents:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. & Mrs. [...]
> 
> Due to Mr. Cayce's illness, I am attempting to answer your
> letter of February 29th.
> 
> The word ([[Karma|karma]]) IS in Webster's Collegiate Dictionary (fifth
> edition).  However, I do not agree with you that a word is
> not of God if it is not in the dictionary.  Before man
> discovered electricity, the word electric was not in the
> dictionary, but it was in God's universe.  Man had not yet
> become aware of it.
> 
> According to Webster's dictionary (since you seem to accept
> it as quite an authority), [[Karma|karma]] is "the whole ethical
> consequence of one's acts considered as fixing one's lot in
> the future existence; hence, loosely, [[Destiny|destiny]], fate."
> 
> Your letter is the greatest proof to me that the Reading is
> correct.  Indeed God does work in mysterious ways His wonders
> to perform.  I believe that if you will accept it, this
> affliction of your child is God's way of giving you an
> opportunity to turn within and seek guidance from Him to help
> you understand why this condition has come upon you and what
> lesson you are to gain from it.  My thought is that you will
> not accomplish this by finding fault with others because they
> cannot miraculously cure your child, but by looking for the
> fault within your own heart and [[Purpose|purpose]].  "Great peace have
> those who love [[Law of Moses|the law]], and nothing shall offend them."  In
> other words, all affliction, all suffering has a [[Purpose|purpose]]
> behind it that is governed by God's immutable laws.  The only
> answer is to become attuned with God's [[Purpose|purpose]] for our lives.
> I think that is what Mr. Cayce's Reading has to say to you.
> Whether or not you accept this truth and attempt to apply it
> is up to you.
> 
> For Forty-three years thousands of individuals have found Mr.
> Cayce's Readings helpful.  He has never made any claims for
> himself.  He has never promised anyone that they would be
> healed.  Always his attitude has been "Because of the good
> that has come to me and mine, I will try when people ask me."
> 
> Those of us who have studied his work through the years feel
> that the information actually needed for the individual is
> given, but sometimes individuals do not want the truth - they
> prefer telling God HOW He should go about to perform a
> [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]] for them; not realizing that the [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]] is to be the
> transformation within themselves.
> 
> Perhaps you do not thank me for writing you in this manner,
> but I feel impelled to give you the interpretation of the
> Reading as I see it.
> 
> Sincerely, [signed] [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Secretary to Mr. Cayce
> GD/jf
