# TEXT OF READING 3181-2 M 40

**Reading [[3181-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/9/1944  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 10562

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                               At home of 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M.                   his father, 
Taken 3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have had this body before.  As we find, there are improvements but there is the lack of consistency in the use of the Appliance that there has been indicated for the body.

3. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments have been very well but there should be more consistence in the preparing of the Appliance for its attachment in its proper relation of one area to the other.  Keep the attachment plates very clean, keeping the Appliance charged properly.  Thus the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments will make for better responses and there will be a great deal of strengthening to the resistances that will be builded in the body.

4. Also be mindful as to the diet and the activity.  There should be as much activity in the open as practical for the body.  Not that there should be activities in the cold or that would cause too great a strain on the body, but keep in the open, in the sunshine as much as practical.

5. Thus we will keep the improvement and bring about the helpfulness that may be given the body through these applications.

6. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments should be continued, especially as the adjustments are made in accord with the Appliance treatments as here indicated, so that there is the relaxing and stimulation for the ganglia controlling the locomotories. These treatments should be given at least once a week, but the Appliance should be given daily.  Do give a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] daily, following the period of using the Appliance.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Are there any special foods which would be beneficial?
(A)  Those carrying the larger quantities of Vitamin B. Whole grain cereals and a great deal of citrus fruits, but don't take these at the same meal - rather alternate these. Not too much of fats.  Plenty of fish - preferably sea foods. Fowl and lamb should be taken.  None of the foods should be fried.

Do these and we will make better conditions.

Copy to Sister
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

> 
> [[Chicago|Chicago]] 37, Illinois
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> I received a copy of your reading which you made on April 9
> for my brother [3181].
> 
> I know you are correct in saying we had not been consistent
> in following through with treatments.  The schedule and type
> of treatment which you have indicated almost requires having
> the patient in a sanitarium.  Such a sanitarium as we should
> desire is much beyond our financial possibilities and also
> it's difficult to find one that would cooperate on your
> readings.
> 
> My father is 76 and his sister who is his housekeeper is 60
> years old.  These two are taking care of my brother on a two
> acre place near ..., Mich. which is just a crossroad and
> there is no [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] there.  Also the diet is difficult
> because the patient will not cooperate.  He is a very heavy
> smoker and a big eater and chooses meat and bread and rejects
> fruits and vegetables.  The only way it is possible for these
> two old people to carry on is to go along with the patient
> rather than to dictate too much.  During the winter months I
> arranged to have him here in [[Chicago|Chicago]] near Dr. Sampson who
> treated him twice a week but our [[Wet Cell|wet cell]] suffered some
> breakage so we were out of it for a time even after it
> arrived which was late.  All in all I am a bit discouraged
> and scarcely know how to plan.  Do you know of a small
> sanitarium any where that would follow your reading
> consistently where we might take my brother.  The one great
> advantage in his present location is of course that he can be
> out of doors and has a garden if he can hold himself to the
> [[Book of Job|job]] of tending it.
> 
> I appreciate your interest and help and certainly hope we can
> arrive at a more satisfactory arrangement for treatments.
> 
> Sincerely yours, [...]
> 
> R2. 5/1/44 EC's letter to Sister:
> 
> 
> Dear Mrs. [...]
> 
> Have yours of the 28th and note what you have to say about
> the reading for your brother, let's hope the sincerity of the
> attempted treatments by the folks at home will make up for a
> lot more scientific means, recall the story of the little boy
> in "The Harvester" and how he treated the little lame girl.
> Such will be the case here.  Please, then, encourage them and
> don't let them become discouraged, for his sake as well as
> theirs.
> 
> Wish could suggest a place for your brother but do not, do
> try to encourage him to follow through, go give the work a
> fair try, for six months or a year, and believe he will see
> help coming.  Yes, being there this spring and summer may be
> the very thing for him, let's try looking at it like that, at
> least.  If it will all become a labor of love am sure it will
> be good for each one of them.  Do let me hear now and then,
> and if we can help in any way, know am glad to try.
> 
> With every good wish,
> 
> Sincerely, [signed] Edgar Cayce
