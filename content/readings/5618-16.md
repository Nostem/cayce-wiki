## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/25/29 EC gave reading 5618-16 in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], but
> unfortunately it did not get taken down or transcribed.  No
> copy is in A.R.E. files.
> 
> R2. 9/26/29 Wife's letter:  "Sure was glad to see you yesterday
> and think your visit did Dr. [5618] a lot of good...  Dr.
> Lydic said Dr. [5618] is better this morning.  He gave him a
> more thorough treatment yesterday and this morning.  Dr.
> [5618] said before the reading his treatments were good but
> not long enough, didn't relax him and that was what the
> reading said.  Sure wish I had that reading down."
> 
> R3. 9/28/29 Mr. [195]'s letter:  "I have just returned from the
> hospital and find Dr. [5618] not resting well and Mrs. [760]
> considerably worried at the apparent setback.  The liver
> won't work and Dr. [5618] was resting so poorly today that
> Mrs. [760] didn't put on the fomentations as suggested by the
> reading.  In my talk with her I intimated that if Dr. [5618]
> would stand for it that it would be advisable to apply the
> [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] to liver this evening.  She seemed to be so upset
> that I said I would talk to Dr. Lydic about holding a
> consultation in the morning if she thought best."
> 
> R4. 9/30/29 Mr. [900]'s letter:  "I had bad news from [195] over
> the telephone this morning regarding Dr. [5618].  [195]
> seemed doubtful that the Doctor will pull through.  They did
> not seem to be applying the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs consistently,
> and I told [195] how this should be done, as we had this
> experience with [140].  [195] also said that Dr. [5618]'s
> psychology is against him, as he feels his own pulse, and
> seems to believe the end is near.  This, in itself, works
> against his improvement, and seems to have been a detrimental
> factor ever since that doctor [?] treated him in ..., Va. and
> started him mentally on the down path."
> 
> R5. 9/30/29 Dr. Lydic's letter:  "I wish to advise that I am
> carrying out your instructions to the best of my ability
> regarding Dr. [5618].  We had some little trouble getting the
> light treatments but I have taken my burner down for use and
> that is all adjusted now.  It is a bit difficult to take care
> of the diet since Dr. [5618] can't retain the foods that
> would help him or else he just doesn't care for them.
> 
> "As a whole his condition in general is better.  The liver is
> more active, the edema is better and the heart is not
> laboring as it had been but he is still a very sick man and
> the outlook is not very favorable.  I have suggested a
> consultation but I shall be glad to follow any suggestions
> you may have."
> 
> R6. 9/30/29 See 5618-17.
