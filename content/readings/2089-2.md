# TEXT OF READING 2089-2 F 44

**Reading [[2089-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/4/1940  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 8726

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of March, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother-in-law, Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Methodist Hosp., 
Time of Reading                             Rm. 235, 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 29th & Hope Sts., 
[[California|Los Angeles]], Cal.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find in the present, conditions are such that if there is the proper preparation for the body a few days beforehand, operative measures may remove the lymph cyst, - which from its formation has begun to gather those forces from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity of the system, and thus necessitates removal; else it would cause a greater disturbance and faster growth.

3. The preparation would be for the better coagulation, and there should NOT be the operation until the test of the blood shows sufficient of the element to cause proper coagulation.

4. Then, with care and attention afterward, we should have much better conditions - after a few months - for the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is the growth of a sarcoma nature?
(A)  As has been indicated, it is a lymph cyst, - with a center that might become anything!  It is not sarcoma in its nature, as we find, in the present.

7. (Q)  What treatment should be followed after she gets home?
(A)  Just the rest, and all forms of creative influences of suggestive nature, as well as a low electrical force which would aid in building up nerve and blood pressure, - as from the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that would carry [[Healing|healing]] properties in same, - [[Camphor|Camphor]] and also Gold.  But in this case the Gold would be half the strength of that ordinarily used.  One solution would be used one day, the other the next, you see; but we haven't gotten to that yet.  When we get to that period, we will then give the changes necessary.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother-in-law - Special Delivery, Airmail
"    " Ass'n file
