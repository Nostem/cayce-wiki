# TEXT OF READING 2084-12 F 14

**Reading [[2084-12]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/10/1942  Sex: F  Age: 14  ReadingID: 8705

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [2841], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2084], this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are very good considering all of the factors which have existed.

4. And as we find in the present, there only needs to be kept those precautions as to the diet; and the occasional - twice or three times a month - having of those [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections which enable the body to keep those activities so that the kidneys stay clear.

5. Be mindful as to the diet.

6. Have plenty of exercise, though not of too severe a nature.

7. These as we find, kept in accord, should keep the body very good.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  In what way has her condition improved?
(A)  There is not as much trouble with the Periods, nor is there as much inflammation indicated in the kidney area.

10. (Q)  Is she to have any treatment other than the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  And the diets that have been indicated.  We would keep rather close to these, keeping down too much of the avoirdupois or the fat.

11. (Q)  What about [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?
(A)  This should be taken occasionally, but only for a few days at the time; about three to four days a month should be sufficient; unless the body is able to have a vacation near the seashore, when the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] should be left off during such a period.

12. (Q)  How about the Watermelon Seed Tea?
(A)  This should be taken about four or five days in succession during a month, but have a regular period each month to do this, see?

13. (Q)  Should any change in diet be effected?
(A)  Just be mindful about not too much of sugars, and do keep the raw vegetables, AND the artichoke when this is practical.

14. (Q)  Is Badminton injurious for her?
(A)  Rather helpful, if not overdone.

15. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  These we would do.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file

(Extra copy for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].  As you perhaps know, Dr. Dobbins is going into the army and has recommended Dr. George N. Coulter to take his place, 18 East 41st St., [[New York City|New York City]]. See Dr. Dobbins' letter to Mr. Cayce, 7/9/42 [2084-12, Par. R1].)
