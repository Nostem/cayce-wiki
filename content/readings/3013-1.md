# TEXT OF READING 3013-1 M 52

**Reading [[3013-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/23/1943  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 10323

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3013], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3013].

3. As we find, conditions are rather serious.

4. These disturbances have been approached from the wrong sources, we find; yet in the present there may be some relief - though it will require a long period.

5. The circulation disturbed between lungs, heart, liver and kidneys, causes the poisons to be distributed through the system in such ways that it is hard for their eliminations through their normal channels.

6. While there may not be a great deal of change in the present from the administrations, and from those cares and attentions the body has been receiving since this recent attack has so upset the circulation as to cause these disturbances through the limbs, - we find that there would be better response in the administrations given to keep the circulation nearer to the normal IF there would be a stimulating treatment osteopathically administered, that which would be only to allay, or to set a better circulation through those channels that would cause better eliminations.

7. For, in the 6th dorsal, the 3rd and 4th dorsal, and in the 3rd cervical, we find DEFINITE deflections of the circulation by the impulses from - or those coordinating between - central nervous system and the superficial circulation.  And this is what has brought about those deflections in heart, liver, lungs, and now the greater distress or pressures on kidneys.

8. So, we would keep the administrations being made, and have - about every other day, or at least twice a week, - the GENTLE [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments in those particular areas indicated; coordinating the rest of what little change is given.

9. Gradually this should build back to better conditions.

10. Later - much later - there may be the possibility of making definite corrections in the 6th dorsal, in the 3rd cervical, and the other areas where these deflections occur; and we should gradually be able to leave off much of that stimulation which keeps the superficial and deeper circulation incoordinant.

11. To be sure, it is necessary to keep the alimentary canal eliminations good.

12. These are the conditions as we find that may be helpful, if they are administered GENTLY; not stimulating, but relaxing to the body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Any suggestions regarding diet?
(A)  Keep away from sweets, and from too heavy foods - as ANY sort of meats.  Liquids and semi-liquids rather than too much heavy food.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra copy [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].  Dr. A. R. Tucker and Dr. T. T. Spence [Spence, Talmage T., D.O.], both in the Masonic Temple Bldg., Raleigh, have had experience with following Mr. Cayce's Readings.  Let us know which of these you choose.)
