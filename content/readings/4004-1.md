# TEXT OF READING 4004-1 M 39

**Reading [[4004-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/29/1944  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 11693

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the Sister, Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 and 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  L.A., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those disturbances that have been and are a part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experience.  As we find, it has been rather an experience which others must meet, in that there has not been better attention given or more precautions taken respecting this body.

3. In times back there was an injury to the area between the last of the dorsals and the lumbar area.  A pressure exists from a subluxation.  With care this may still be reduced, but in times back could have been easily corrected if precautions had been taken - or at least it could have been much more easily eliminated.

4. From these pressures there are those causes for a mental breakdown, so that great imaginations are produced at times and at others there is a sullenness that becomes aggravating to others and equally as depressing to the body-forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself.

5. We find that if there would be precautions taken easily by a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], this subluxation might be reduced or corrected.  We would have these treatments twice to three times a week in the beginning until at least twenty-three or twenty-four such adjustments have been made.  For it will be found that at times this will become quite aggravating and there will be required the attention of a special attendant.

6. Also about once a week we would apply the short wave therapy to coordinate the centers where sympathetic and cerebrospinal coordinate throughout the whole cerebrospinal system.

7. Thus we may eventually bring near normal conditions for the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do give the body the opportunity of these adjustments and we may bring the opportunity for a normal, complete expression of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s self, which has been denied - either by negligence or lack of interest.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Sister (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 5/12/51 Sister's reply to Questionnaire:
> 
> Dear [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]]
> 
> I was never able to give my brother the treatment that
> was suggested.  He is in a private sanitarium, under their
> Doctor's care.  We would have to take him home and have a
> private Doctor treat him.  He is much better than he was at
> the time I wrote you altho, time and the fact that he likes
> it there very much seems to have helped him a great deal.  I
> still believe there is some kind of pressure or that a great
> disappoint[ment] he had when he was younger was the cause of
> his trouble.  They called his trouble dementia praecox, a
> disease usually caused by worry and disappointment or
> resentment.  I do know that for the past two years, he has
> seemed contented and happy.  He works in the kitchen and
> every one out there likes him.  Two years ago I had a big
> disappointment myself.  I feel that if I had given up, I
> would have become ill like he did, but I went to work and
> tried to attend church every Sunday.  I couldn't give up
> because I have a child to raise.  My mental attitude is much
> better as I know now, my disappoint[ment] was small, compared
> to what others have had, and illness others had had to fear.
> Sometimes I think retribution caught up with me, and if I
> think less of myself and more of others, I will have a better
> future than I have had a past.  I think one must live
> completely for others to be able to be happy & contented,
> which is a hard plan to follow.  I doubt if I can ever
> accomplish it.
> 
> Thank you for writing.
> 
> Sincerely,  [...]
