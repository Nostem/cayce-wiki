# TEXT OF READING 1950-1 F 34 (Sales Manager Retail Furniture,

**Reading [[1950-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/7/1939  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 8418

Hebrew) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1950], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born December 7, 1904, in [[England|London]], [[England|England]]. 
Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, and that to which it may attain, and how; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from present - " - '36 - changes in the environs - '26 - a change again - '24 - " etc. on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity called [1950].

4. In giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these interpretations are chosen with the desire that the experience may be a helpful one for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present.

5. Thus, applying those suggestions for the correction of inclinations and tendencies, and magnifying the virtues for the greater awakening, the entity may through the experience become more and more aware of its [[Purpose|purposes]] for entering the material plane; and for the application of self in such a way and manner as to become more content and to live a more helpful, a more useful experience.

6. Please comprehend and understand that this experience is not for a selfish motive, and if the desires are turned into those things that are for self-indulgence or self-aggrandizement, these - with the inclinations - will bring a very disturbed, a very unsuccessful life.

7. For, as would be termed by the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, this was a very unlucky day on which to be born.  But, as we have indicated for others - and would give for this entity, such inclinations only indicate tendencies; and there is ever [[Free Will|the will]], the [[Free Will|choice]] in self.

8. Then, first study to show thyself approved unto God.  Let thy yeas be yea, thy nays be nay.  Look more to a spiritual awakening, a spiritual understanding.

9. This does not make one a recluse, but rather - if same is applied in a conscientious and consistent manner in the experience and the dealings with the fellow man, it will bring of itself - as has been the promise ever - contentment and greater blessings materially, mentally, socially, financially.

10. But if these are forgotten, then the inclinations to be self-indulgent and to glory in accomplishments are the result.  Because of the natural high [[Ideal|ideals]], let not those influences of a social nor of an economic nature turn thee aside.

11. As to the urges from the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]], - we find Venus, Jupiter, Uranus being the greater influences or forces; and these are independent one of another oft in their influence.

12. Hence there are periods when there is the desire to withdraw from a general social surrounding.  There are other periods when the activities or urges from Venus, as a combination with Uranus, increase the desire for greater social accomplishments, greater economic accomplishments.

13. All of these are well, provided - as has been indicated - these are not allowed to become that sought, rather than the same being a result OF a conscientious life lived for the good works of every nature.

14. There are the abilities in searching out, - for as the entity has been an explorer in the experience before this, there is the natural inclination for the desires to give, to know, to be acquainted with, to have a large cortege' of friends, to have a wide acquaintance and a [[Free Will|choice]] to make with those who have accomplished or may accomplish various activities in every field, - not only in the one in which the entity is engaged but in the political, the economic, the social, in art, in letters, in all of those things.  These are natural attractions for the entity.

15. These are very well, but know what thou desirest to be, in every walk of life.

16. Wood and things made of wood will always be a channel or a manner or way for activities of the entity to succeed.  And these should be, as we find, a part of the entity's experience.  In anything that has to do with the things or articles that are manufactured from wood, or in the manufacturing of same might there also be those activities in which accomplishments of a material nature may be had.

17. In the Uranian we find the extremes, as indicated in the associations with people and in the being alone, as to social order, as to political affiliations, as to group activity and of every nature.

18. For as indicated from the Jupiterian influences, the entity will deal with masses; individual activity with same, but the effect upon crowds or the mass rather than the individual activity.

19. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane:

20. Before this we find the entity was in those activities as indicated, where the explorations of varied lands were the labors sought for the entity, - and in the northern part of South [[Africa|Africa]] - Rhodesia, were the activities in which the entity materially and physically accomplished many things.

21. But there was a lonesomeness, a lack of sociability, that has made and does make for a longing within the experience of the entity.

22. Also the acquaintance with and the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the Uranian force make the entity itself VERY psychic.  These are well if they are used for HELPFUL forces.

23. The abilities to read or to feel the emotions of others is well, but to use same for self's own indulgence, for self's own gain without consideration of others whom ye may be able to interpret so easily, is sin!

24. In those activities then the entity gained.  Yet there are urges latent in the present that MUST have a development in one direction or another, from that experience.

25. The name then was Rodman Carr.

26. Before that we find the entity was in the land of promise when there were those activities with the returning from the [[Persia|Persian]] land.

27. There we find the entity was among the priests with Zerubbabel, in the name Eljah.  In the experience the entity gained, the entity lost.  When the activities were such as to make for the greater developments for the whole peoples, well.  When there was the use of the office as well as the social affiliations turned into self-indulgence, these brought periods of debasement, - not only of self but of the office itself.

28. And remember, these are but experiences that must be met in thine own self.  For an individual through varied experiences in the earth is constantly meeting himself.

29. Those abilities as an interior decorator, those abilities to choose hangings for draperies or the like, are innate from those experiences, - as are the mental urges to DOUBT - because of self's own abilities to analyze others and their emotions.  This does not indicate, because the emotions of an individual may be analyzed, that the individual's real [[Purpose|purpose]] or desire is as open a book, - even to one so gifted as self in the interpretations of the emotions of others.

30. Use there abilities, then, as well as the indications given, for a greater spiritual awakening, that the material and mental [[Purpose|purposes]] may arise from the results accomplished within self and self's own development, THROUGH the aid to others.

31. For, know, ever:  What ye are speaks so loud, seldom is there heard what ye say.

32. Also, know this:  The manner in which ye treat thy fellow man (and this means woman also) is thy concept of thy God.  For as ye do it unto the least, ye do it unto thy Maker.

33. Then, beware of selfishness in thy dealings with others.

34. Before that we we find the entity was among those peoples who journeyed from [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land to [[Promised Land|the Promised Land]].

35. The entity was among those who were of the sons and associates of Korah, but not of the Levites who were destroyed.  Being young in years during the experience, the entity learned a lesson, and became among those who joined with the Gershonites as the keepers of moneys or [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of same, until there was the division of the lands in [[Promised Land|the Promised Land]].

36. Hence we find the entity in the present is one that may be a very good statistician, or datastician, especially as to things pertaining to the working of or working with things made of wood, - as varnish or finish, of form, of shape, of moulds, of things that would have to do with ornaments for same.

37. For as one of the Gershonites, though of course - as indicated - OF the kinsmen of Korah - the entity gained throughout that experience; and was close to the elders as they established their activities in [[Promised Land|the Promised Land]].

38. Hence there is the great desire for knowledge pertaining to those who are in high places, - the innate urge to be TRUE to all influences, and yet - as it were - seeing the shady tradings of those about the entity, the upper hand as taken because of weaknesses of some character or nature in the associates or friends or the activities with others; but be not tempted by same.

39. The name then was Ajlon.

40. Before that we find the entity was in the land now called [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], during those periods of the banishment of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] in the Abyssinian land - of which the entity was a native, becoming very closely associated with those who were the guards or armor bearers or companions of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] in [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]].

41. Thus, the entity was among those who returned with [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] to the activities when there were the classifyings and the developments of individuals for a greater service; the entity being among those who were the wood carvers, making for the beautifying not only of the inner portion of the dwellings but also of the burial places, when there were the carvings not only of wood but the fitting of same with metals, - as gold, silver, copper and brass.  All of these were a part of the entity's experience.

42. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, and that to which it may attain, and how:

43. First, as indicated, there is no position too high for the entity to desire to attain.  There is nothing to hinder the entity from attaining same, in any field of endeavor that it may desire, except the practising of selfishness or the forgetting of its relationship to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

44. There has been an unusual experience for the entity, there may be within the present the meeting of same - and thy name be called blessed among men, - if ye will but apply thy abilities in whatever the field chosen.  There has been indicated those fields through which greater contentment may come.  But know thy [[Ideal|ideal]], and what ye believe; and then act in thy relationships to each and every individual as if it were with thy Maker.

45. Ready for questions.

46. (Q)  How may I solve, or what should I do about, my domestic problem?
(A)  As ye would that it be done to thee if conditions were reversed, if circumstances were reversed, then live in that manner.  For remember, "As ye would that others should do to you, do ye even so to them" is the beginning of wisdom.

47. (Q)  Is it possible to know about my position, its security and possibilities for the future?
(A)  As ye apply thyself in the manners and ways indicated, ye may go FAR, far in the chose field of activity.

Do that.

48. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
