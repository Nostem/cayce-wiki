# TEXT OF READING 4741-1 F 41

**Reading [[4741-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/12/1927  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 12710

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4741] via Mrs. [4691].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now, we find there are abnormal conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  These date back to conditions existent in times back and an improper treatment of the body when there existed conditions having to do with the generatory system and the discharges from same.  Irritation first begun, then that of ulcerations, and when caustic or cauterized conditions used, these accentuated the condition; yet it is not wholly separated into a malignant condition as yet, and with care, attention, and the proper eliminations kept in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, we see that much help may be brought to the body toward correcting or at least alleviating the suffering and bringing a much better condition to the physical functioning of the body.

4. In this manner, then, we find the general physical system of Mrs. [4741]:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this shows the nature and the character of the condition, minus that of the malignant nature, for those bacilli as are discharged are of the different nature from that where wholly malignant troubles have set in; for we find that malignant wholly is an organism that has set up a growth within itself independent of the system, other than as a parasite toward same, drawing from, or absorbing, CONSUMING the organs or portion of system to which it becomes attached or attaches itself.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this shows much stress and strain, both through the cerebro-spinal and the sympathetic system. The dread of the condition and the outcome brings as much suffering as the condition itself.

7. IN THE PHYSICAL FUNCTIONING, most of the organs, as we find, are in very good condition, save where the system shows reaction toward that existent in the pelvic organs, or the condition in vagina and the womb itself.  These show the ulcerations and effect of lacerations.  The application of [[Radium|Radium]] has been beneficial, yet this in itself produced conditions that are not so well at present, for the plethora condition existent from same.

8. In THIS manner would we apply forces for the body at the present, until we may gain a better reaction through rest of body and mind toward the physical condition.

9. Use under the direction of physician (for it may not be applied in any other way and manner) that of [[Radium|RADIUM]] WATER as the internal forces for the system, see?

10. Use the electrical driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] to relax the system all along the cerebro-spinal system, using the sponge appliance over the lower portion of body; that is, across the lumbar and sacral region.

11. Use the warm douche with CREOLIN at least once or twice each week, OFTENER when irritation shows.

12. Take at least TEN DOSES of the [[Radium|Radium]] Water and the treatments as go with same each day; that is those forces - though the Radium Water will be changed by the physician to meet the needs of the histology and of the conditions in the system.  After the ten doses have been taken - that is, they may not be taken each day, or they may be taken each day - but after ten doses we would give further suggestions for this body.  Dr. Tally may give this.

13. We are through for the present.
