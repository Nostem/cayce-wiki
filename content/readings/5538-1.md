# TEXT OF READING 5538-1 F 36

**Reading [[5538-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/28/1930  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 13805

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5538].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       Baltimore, Maryland. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the conditions and the body here, Mrs. [5538].  Now, we find the conditions, while their reactions at the present are more mental than apparently otherwise, this condition - as WE find - mostly the fault of one that attended the body in childbirth, and the conditions as were left at the time.

2. In applying that for the physical benefit of the body, and for the mental conditions, we would find that - were there applied for a few days, that of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] with that of Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]]; 10 grains Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to 4 ounces distilled water, 10 grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to 10 ounces distilled water.  Give the dose about 3 times each day, early of morning, in the afternoon, and at night; 3 drops of each in half glass of water, see?

3. Then, when this - in the course of 5 to 10 days - has set up the changes as will come through the functioning of change in system, we would then make those alterations through the pelvic organs, through the manipulations osteopathically applied - that will RELIEVE this pressure and gradually allow the mental forces to rebuild in the system.  Keep the medicinal properties when the treatments begin, but NOT so often.

4. Keep the eliminations open by the giving of oil, and of those conditions that will keep the acid low in system.

5. We would find there would come bettered conditions for the body.  While these pressures cause distress, hallucinations, and disorders throughout; yet, will the body-mental - as it is being treated osteopathically - be given that suggestion as to that which will be builded in the system, the body-physical and mental may be able to hold of self and build up.  Do that.  Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/10/30 Letter in answer to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] Questionnaire:
> "...Dear friends - oh dear you are still interested in my
> case.  No, I am not better.  No, I never will be.  Oh God,
> why can't I at least die in my sleep?  Why can't I be
> relieved of my suffering?  Have I committed such terrible
> crimes to be punished like this?  I can't follow those
> directions.  I can't drink any oils because it makes me sick.
> I have continual spells of diarrhea and continual mucus
> flowing all the time.  My pain through my right side and
> bladder is most unbearable, always so terribly inflammed,
> continual pressure of the bladder and burning pains through
> my head.  Oh please, dear friend, let Dr. Cayce pray for me
> to have an easy death.  I can't stand it any more..."
> 
> R2. 8/18/30 Letter from [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] urged her to get the
> treatments carried out as suggested in the reading and that
> she then write to Mr. Cayce for a check reading.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/10/30 Letter:  "...This morning as usual I looked over the
> Morning paper [Baltimore Sun] and I have discovered, as it
> seems to me, a [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]] of a cure.  Dear Sir, I have been
> looking for a [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]] for myself as there does not seem to be
> any doctor to be able to diagnose my case and help me.  It
> seems much too late but this write-up about the Dr. Cayce and
> you [Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]]] gave me some hope again.  Help me,
> dear friend, if there is only a possibility.  I am a woman 36
> years old and have been married 17 years; have two boys, one
> 16 and the other 14, a good man who has been working for the
> doctors all these years and I am getting worse all the time.
> I am in a very nervous condition now; I am afraid that I will
> yet be placed in an insane asylum.  I still have a few
> hundred dollars for fee for the doctors.  My health does not
> allow me to travel.  Is it possible to be helped.  Can such
> [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]] happen?  Has God doomed me to a life of such
> terrible misery?  Pray, Help me, dear sir.  May I hope for
> something in this world yet?..."
> 
> B2. 4/23/30 EC's letter:  "...I have yours of recent date, also
> yours addressed to Mr. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]].  I am sending data
> respecting work of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], the information necessary
> to become a member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], which entitles you to
> the privileges of same - including information through these
> sources.
> 
> "As I am sure you will gather from this data, it is not
> possible for us to tell you how much good we may be able to
> do - until we have had the information.  I do not imply from
> this that we are attempting to induce you to become a member
> in order that we may have the little entrance fee - for we
> are just as willing to try and be of help to those who are
> not able to contribute even the membership fee, as we are to
> try to help those who contribute hundreds and thousands.  It
> is to be of service that [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] was organized.  It
> is necessary that an individual be able to have that
> something within self that will find an answer in the
> information, or - if you choose to put it that way - it is
> necessary for one to have faith in the Divine that is within
> self, and not in us, but in self's ability to contact the
> divine from within.
> 
> "After you have studied this over, if you care to make
> application, we will be mighty glad to hear from you, and
> sincerely trust to be of service..."
