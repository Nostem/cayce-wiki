# TEXT OF READING 1743-1 M 28 (Astrologer, Chiropractic

**Reading [[1743-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/28/1938  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 7969

Student, Religious Preference, "Searcher for Truth") This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of October, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1743], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1472] and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born March 23, 1910, in [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]], N.J. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P.M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the relationship of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [1743].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, it is well - owing to the very nature of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - that some consideration be given as to the source of the information; and as to what is meant by much of the phrasing that is chosen here in giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]].

4. By records we mean those activities, mental and physical, in the planes of consciousness; that make their impression upon time and space much as light makes its impression upon the same elements or forces or conditions that are called time and space.

5. As this entity is among the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]], we find that the natural tend or trend of the entity is to be scientific, - as all of those [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] who are to make themselves felt in the affairs of the earth during their activity, some in one direction, some in another.  All are more or less inclined to be termed, in the parlance of the day, scientific in their very natures.

6. Hence those thoughts, characters or natures of activity in which there may be an outlet for the expressions of such in the material experience in the present will offer the opportunities for the greater possibilities of development for the entity in the present experience.

7. [Here EC had a coughing spell and said, "We are through for the present."]

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/27/38 Mrs. [1472]'s letter:  "...Now for [1743].  This is a
> very different story.  [1743] is a young astrologist, 28 yrs.
> of age.  Since he was 16 he has been a profound student of
> [[Astrology|astrology]], and seems to have the gift of [[Healing|spiritual healing]] by
> "[[Healing|laying on of hands]]".  His life is consecrated to service,
> and he would be a great light unto the world, I am sure, if
> he had a little more guidance.  This guidance H. L. and I
> feel he can get through a reading.  He has no money except
> what he earns through writing [[Astrology|astrological]] charts for people.
> He charges but $10; for these, and seldom does more than 1 a
> week and has his mother and himself to take care of out of
> this.  They own a home over in N.J. but have no other
> assets.  [1743] is a beautiful spiritual boy.  He gives a
> spiritual interpretation to all his [[Astrology|astrological]] work, often
> laying aside a chart he is writing to go to sleep for
> guidance.  When he awakens it is with knowledge and
> information necessary to the work.  I want you to know him
> well, for I feel you will be a great help to him, and will
> love him also.  He did a horoscope for [1151] which is one
> of the most magnificent documents I ever had the privilege to
> read.  [1151] is very much interested in [1743] and will, I
> am sure greatly approve a reading for him, although I have
> not had an opportunity to mention the matter to [1151], as I
> have not seen him for months.  [1743] can arrange this time
> for a reading for any afternoon..."
> 
> B2. 10/12/38 Mrs. [1472]'s letter:  "...[1743] came over from his
> home in ..., N.J. this afternoon to have a talk with me in
> preparation for his reading.  I am enclosing the questions he
> wants to ask and his application for the reading - which
> H. L. signed...  I am sure he will be writing you of his
> great appreciation of the reading.  He is certainly set
> apart for some great work, I feel.  He has no interest
> whatever in girls, or romance, or marriage, and would seem
> to have renounced personal ambition altogether.  I believe
> the reading he is to have will throw much light on his path
> and help guide his young footsteps.  I offered [1743] the
> use of my apartment for his reading and will appreciate it
> if you will write him directly regarding the date of his
> appointment.  As he has chiropractic classes in the
> mornings it will have to be some afternoon other than a Sat.
> or Sunday.  His address is ..., N.J..."
