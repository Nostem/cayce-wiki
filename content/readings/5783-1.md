## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. GD's note:  Mr. [5783] obtained a reading from Edgar Cayce,
> according to reports from written accounts of results
> obtained by citizens of [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.  There is no record
> of a personal reading by him, but he did refer several other
> people for readings during the years that I was secretary to
> Edgar Cayce (1923-45).  His whole family - wife and several
> children - obtained readings from Mr. Cayce which are on
> file, but we do not have one on record for him.
> 
> Perhaps he was reporting on results in his family and the one
> who wrote the record that he was among those in Hoptown who
> obtained readings just assumed that he received them for
> himself.  I make a note of this, with a Case number for him,
> just in case a validation turns up later.  I can no longer
> ask him or his wife as they have long since passed on, also
> his older children - who possibly were born long after 1911
> or thereabout anyway.
