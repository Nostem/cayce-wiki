# TEXT OF READING 5125-1 F 30 (Secretary, Baptist)

**Reading [[5125-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1944  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 13258

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5125], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the office force of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5125], [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Wirsing, Willa Irons and Harmon Bro.]

Born October 28, 1913, in Bokhoma, Oklahoma. 
Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.     [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces, giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  A new [[Vibration|vibration]]!  Very good.  Quite an interesting fact about people born here [Bokhoma, Okla.?].  One out of ten thousand would ever commit suicide.  Something in the soil; get it between your toes and you'll never commit suicide.

3. Yes, we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [5125].

4. You should return for all of these, for the experiences in the earth throughout have been interesting; quite varied in their effect upon [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present, for they reach from being the miner's wife to being a queen on [[Throne of God|the throne]]. Hence these give to the entity those abilities not only in the applications in the earth; for while the entity oft is material-minded, throughout the experiences in the earth, there have been periods when the spiritual aspects were taken into consideration.

5. Few individual entities understand the variations in sects and schisms, "isms" and cults, as the entity; and yet the entity asks no questions about any!  For it has had something to do with preserving or forwarding the activities of many. So these have become an acceptance; rather than tolerance, acceptance of that individual search for some way to gratify that longing within each [[Soul|soul]] going beyond the moment.

6. The entity is an individual, individual in itself; that is, its personality and its individuality are nearer one and the same than you will find in one out of a million; and this is well.

7. This is an individual entity inclined to talk too much at times, and yet one who can keep a secret - and has many she has never told anyone, not even her husband!

8. As to the urges latent from [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]], we find that these are merely influences accepted by the entity; for to the entity it is what an individual IS that counts, not what an entity professes.  Little use does the entity have for those who are two-faced or something today and something else tomorrow, or one thing in one crowd and something else in another.

9. The entity is democratic in its [[Ideal|ideals]], and thus the entity is good for what ails most of the world - lack of tolerance and patience, the looking after your own and not judging the other fellow.  She will never attempt to steal another man, nor will she make a promise and break it.

10. These are parts of the individuality and personality of the entity.

11. To give that which would be as a criticism or as counsel: Just don't talk TOO much!  This is well when you know what you are talking about.  Most of the time you do, but oft you talk to try to draw out others, in such a way that you make them untrue to themselves.  You learn something, yes, but sometimes it's not well to know too much about those you think a great deal of.

12. As to activities and experiences in the earth, we find that these, combined with the [[Astrology|astrological]] urges, give the personality and individuality of the entity; and you won't find another in a million just like it!

13. The [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth vary a great deal.  Many have been the experiences of the entity in the earth.  Hence you can't tell the entity very much about the littleness or the bigness of those who are big or those who are little, in their dealings with their fellow men.  The experience has been of the mental-[[Soul|soul]].  Not that [[Soul|the soul]] is divided, but as the mind.  You have a physical mind that is aware of the physical attributes of an earthly body.  You have a soul mind that is aware of the longings of the soul in its search for its Maker.

14. So in the soul mind is that which weighs, as it were, the benefits as well as sorrows, also that the entity has gained by soul experience.  And this entity doesn't trust in the stars - so no one need try to read her [[Astrology|horoscope]], it means nothing to the entity!

15. As to the activities in the earth, we find:

16. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity, not of the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], but of nativity - when the entity first became conscious of the activities in the earth.

17. For the entity was among those who had come from portions of [[Alabama|Alabama]] and settled in what is now Arkansas (or Ar-Kansas as then called); and the entity was among those who panned for gold and found diamonds.

18. But the entity learned from those experiences that which would be well for the body.  While it should never wear a great deal of jewelry - it doesn't befit the universality of mind of the entity, nor does the [[Vibration|vibration]] of jewelry (cheap or good) mean much to the entity, but - wear the moonstone close to your body, or on your body.  It will give strength, and it will keep that which is nearest to you closer to you; not as an omen but as a part of your mental and spiritual consciousness.  Have same as a chain, or upon a chain, about your neck; not as an ornament, but rather as a helpmeet, as an urge, as a [[Vibration|vibration]] that will be most helpful - as it was in the experience in that land as Margaret Fitzhugh.

19. The entity gained through the experience; gained in those abilities that it learned to put things where they belonged, people as well as things, by word as well as by look.  The entity learned to be, as it were, a fit companion for what- ever befell those of its associates or its companions through the experience; and the present companion was the entity's last [[Free Will|choice]] and the one you made the good with, for ye "dug well" in those experiences, in that area.

20. And in the southern hills of the Ozark, in what is now Arkansas, ye may still hear of Robert Fitzhugh.

21. Before that the entity was in the English land during those periods when questionings arose between Church and State.

22. And there the entity for itself settled that question for good:  the church is within yourself and not in any pope nor preacher, nor in any building but in self!  For thy body is indeed [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God, and [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] becomes a personal companion in mind and in body; dependent upon the personality and individuality of the entity as it makes practical application of the tenets and truths that are expressed.  This the entity learned, as [[Grace|Grace]] Callahann (hann, too!).  In the experience the entity learned the lessons of tolerance, and to not discuss family problems nor church problems with those whom she thought a great deal of. And she doesn't yet, for she learned her lesson well.  She learned, or has the ability, to help those who sorrow; or, as the entity learned, to "weep with those who weep, and rejoice with those who do rejoice."

23. As to the abilities in the present from those experiences, these are well; though the entity should learn, and as warned, ye talked too much!  Don't talk too much again!  in the wrong place!

24. Before that the entity was in the "city in the hills and the plains," being among those of the household of Uhjltd; among the children of Uhjltd in the experience.

25. Thus the entity grew under the tenets that were put into those activities in and among those peoples in the "city of the hills and the plains."

26. Hence the great outdoors has an appeal for the entity, as also those who ride horses well; as do those who bedeck themselves with unusual costumes.  The entity still likes that activity.  This is latent and the entity only gives expression to it, but the entity feels better with an individual wearing a costume (or such) than otherwise; though she wants to go places when such are in the activities!

27. The entity [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] learned to care for those in distress.  Hence the entity is one who invites confidences, and it is these that become the secrets of the entity - and they are safe for those who have confided in the entity.  But if you don't put it as in confidence, she may tell it!

28. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land during those periods when there were the activities in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice, as well as in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful.

29. There the entity was a daughter of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] and was set in those activities that brought the entity to [[Throne of God|the throne]] as a queen in [[Persia|Persia]], or in those portions of [[Persia|Persia]] that had been the dwelling place of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]].

30. Hence those things [[Persia|Persian]]:  much color, things having to do with rule (and let rule), and customs in various places.

31. The entity was a good queen, as well as a good mother, through those experiences.

32. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

33. Set thine [[Ideal|ideals]] where ye will or may, ye may attain them. For as has been indicated, ye have earned - for ye have practiced peace first within self, and the ability to make peace with others - unless you talk too much!  And these will ever stand the entity in good stead, to attain, to gain whatever may be the desire or the [[Purpose|purpose]].  So long as they are kept in that way of "live and let live", and of helping the other fellow, ye will keep those experiences inviolate - and there will be harmony in this experience, unless you talk too much!

34. Ready for questions.

35. (Q)  When, where and how have I been associated in the past with my husband, and how can we use our influence in the present to help other married couples be as happy as we are?
(A)  As given, in the experience just before this were the greater material activities.  Hence in and about things that have to do with the product of the soil may ye work together, in and with such, to keep the better interests in each; and things that have to do with mining or with prospecting - don't be afraid of them!  You'll gain when everyone else would lose, each of you!

And in those experiences in the "city in the hills and plains," ye were again companions.  There, too, ye set about to help others.

When ye were the queen in Persia, as a daughter of [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] Priest [[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]], the present companion was thy son.

36. (Q)  With Edgar Cayce, and in what way am I best fitted to help him in his work now?
(A)  As indicated, ye were his daughter twice.  As to the way in which ye may help, choose thou - you can do it, whatever you choose!

37. (Q)  With my mother-in-law?
(A)  In the English land ye didn't gee very well, until you came to the better interpretation and learned not to talk too much.

38. (Q)  With my office associates:  Miss [2589]?
(A)  In [[Egypt|Egypt]] were the closer associations, in the experiences through that period.

39. (Q)  Mrs. [1523]?
(A)  In Egypt.

40. (Q)  Mrs. [4046]?
(A)  These associations had best be seen by comparison.  Ye knew of one another more than knowing one another personally; though ye talked about such other scandalously!

41. (Q)  Miss [288]?
(A)  Close associations in the "city in the hills and plains".  Ye knew of the entity and didn't think well of it, in Egypt.

42. (Q)  Mrs. [5070] and Mr. [3188]?
(A)  These, you will find, you may parallel also.  Ye were associates, there were associations as of a distance - you both talked too much!

43. (Q)  Mrs. [538]?
(A)  In Egypt as well as in Persia.  The closer relationship was in the Persian experience, as helpers one to the other.

As has been given, let each of these experiences in the earth be magnified.  For in each there were those activities that might be helpful to the entity, in that way of being instructive to others.

44. We are through.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. IMG's note:  The [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] Employment Agency sent
> Mrs. [5125] to [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] as a stenographer to help with the
> flood of correspondence which resulted from the September
> 1943 "Coronet" article.  Her husband was in the Navy,
> stationed in the vicinity.  During her employment here,
> Mrs. [5125]'s duties consisted of not only answering first
> inquiry letters, but taking dictation from Mr. Cayce.  Almost
> instantaneously, there was a strong affinity between them.
> As a result of that mutual interest, Mr. Cayce volunteered to
> give Mrs. [5125] a reading without receiving payment for it,
> which she graciously accepted.  The relationship between her
> and [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] was also harmonious.  At that time, Mrs.
> [5125] had been employed here for a full month.
