# TEXT OF READING 2504-16 M 64

**Reading [[2504-16]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/23/1930  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9429

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made the by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, and Miss Painter.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2504], this we have had before.  In some respects we find the general forces of the body still show some improvements.  Some IMPAIRMENTS as to the eliminations, especially as to the left side - through the non-activity of the sweat [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - or those as have been set up BY the respiratory system through the baths.  These should be added to, and those oils as have been given - in the form of the alcoholic and oil - will make for a better food value to the system.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Give cause and treatment for increasing stiffness in shoulders and arms.
(A)  It's just been given, and what to do to limber it up!

3. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments now?
(A)  At least every other day.

4. (Q)  Should bladder be irrigated again?
(A)  Whenever there is an over activity of the system as respecting same, well to irrigate same.

5. (Q)  What type of baths?
(A)  Preferably those of the witchhazel fume baths.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
