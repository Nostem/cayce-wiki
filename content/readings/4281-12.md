# TEXT OF READING 4281-12 F 7

**Reading [[4281-12]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/24/1926  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 12072

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by Dr. A. C. Layne.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Georgia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same, and you will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find there have some unsatisfactory conditions arisen in the physical condition of the body, yet in many ways and manners we find the body gradually on the improve, and the vitality and strength of the resistance in the system gradually on the improve; while the great care and attention as has and is being given the body, yet we find that these conditions at times break through, as is were - that is, those conditions that bring distresses to various portions of the system.

3. That condition as is seen in the functioning of the lymphatic circulation, which has caused some distress through the bronchials, nasal cavities, and through that of the thyroids and the portions of the throat proper, shows how that the system becomes easily excited or exaggerated by an increased circulation.  The temperature as is produced at such times comes from the inability of the system to eliminate properly.

4. We would reduce, by the manipulation, the temperature; also using lukewarm or cool enemas.  This, as we find, will remove much of the condition that is excited by this undue circulation, see?  and we would keep many of those conditions for the system as have been given.

5. In the application of the Coco-Quinine at the present time, this we would rather [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the system than taking internally.  That would be massaged in the lymphatic circulation - arm pits, elbows, abdomen, thighs, with cocoa butter - Coco-Quinine and cocoa butter massaged together into this portion will give strength, and will also reduce the circulation, you see, through the capillaries.  The lymphatics taking this up, this will act directly on this circulation, see?  Be careful that the feet are kept dry, especially.  Not over heated, yet allowed to take sufficient exercise that we have a good full capillary circulation, see? Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is there any trace of tuberculosis in her blood or system anywhere?
(A)  No.

7. (Q)  Are her tonsils involved in any way?
(A)  All the portions of the throat, and all the conditions in the lymphatic circulation, as we see, are involved; not to the extent that these have been derogatory to the system at the present time, yet these should be looked after, see? Hence the conditions as we have given for the blood supply and for the stimulation of the circulation in its proper channel of elimination.

8. (Q)  Should the tonsils be removed?
(A)  No.

9. (Q)  What causes the earaches?
(A)  This same effect as is produced in the throat and bronchials, in the lymphatic circulation, affects directly the Eustachian Tube.  This gives the earache.

10. (Q)  Should she take milk from cows tested for tuberculosis or diphtheria?
(A)  Those that are inoculated the body should not take milk from.  Those that may be tested would be a different condition.  Those that are inoculated give an undue effect to one that is easily swayed by even suggestions through the milk, see?  All milk, as has been given, should be heated and boiled, or sterilized.

11. (Q)  Has the body any malaria in the blood now?
(A)  Little.  This is shown through the liver and the poor elimination, or an unbalanced elimination.

12. (Q)  Does she need salt or [[Baking Soda|soda]] baths now?
(A)  Rather that as outlined.  Of course, [[Baking Soda|soda]] baths may be given when the body is bathed off from that of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of quinine and butter.

13. (Q)  Should she have any codliver oil now?
(A)  This is always good to build the system, yet do not OVER stimulate the system with drugs, or ANY nostrums that give an unbalanced condition for the system.  Rather be controlled by the diet and by the activities of the system, see?

14. (Q)  How soon can she have sweet milk on her cereals now?
(A)  When there is no trace of the malaria in the blood.

15. (Q)  Any special diet for the body?
(A)  None than that as has been given.  Follow that.
