# TEXT OF READING 2501-4 F 20

**Reading [[2501-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/3/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 9403

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic, Parker and Hayes, Miss Norris, Messrs. Benstock, Snyder, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  In the suppressions as have caused the incoordination, much of the condition is being aided.  Some PARTICULAR attention to the local condition existent in the pelvic organs should now be given consideration, and we will find that - with corrections, and with the proper precautions taken with the body, physically and mentally - the mental reactions will be nearer normal. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are all the treatments given properly?
(A)  They're very good.  Not enough control at times kept over the mental activities.

3. (Q)  Would you advise rest in bed for certain periods of the day?
(A)  When these corrections are being made, would be VERY well; in fact, be better.

4. (Q)  Would it be advisable to let her visit home over a week end?
(A)  At least let's make these corrections and changes first. That will take two weeks.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
