# TEXT OF READING 3520-1 F 25

**Reading [[3520-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/15/1943  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 11004

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3520], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., North Carolina.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  AS we find here, there are acute conditions. Little help may be brought here unless, through operative measures, there can be the removal of the growth of a bony or grisly nature that presses upon the organs of the body and the nerve forces of the body, as to upset the activities of the liver - and will soon upset the coordination between the liver and the kidneys.

3. These have reached too great a stress, or too much of a cartilaginous or bony structure, and these are gradually destroying the activities of the body.

4. Then, we would take the body to such as Hopkins or the like, or Temple [Johns-Hopkins, Baltimore, Md., Temple University Hospital, Philadelphia, Pa.], and let these conditions be followed out and, through first determining the position and the character of the growth.

5. It is not malignant, but it should be removed.  While this may cause partial paralysis for a time from the very position or place, we find that this would be the better means for obtaining help.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Where is this growth in the body?
(A)  Where would it be, if it presses on the kidneys?

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 11/27/43 [3520]'s letter:
> 
> ..., N.C.
> 
> Dear Sirs:
> 
> I read the article concerning Mr. Cayce's work in the
> "Coronet" magazine while I was in a hospital during the
> latter part of August & the first part of September.
> 
> I had given up hope of finding out what was wrong with my
> back, until I happened to read that article, and it has
> given me new hope.
> 
> My trouble started 2 years ago July.  I had a cold first
> - then my back started to hurt so I went to a Dr. at home,
> (... Hts., [[Ohio|Ohio]]) he said I had an inflammation in my kidney,
> he treated me for awhile and then advised me to go to the
> hospital for observation. - I had lost 10 pds.  I was at the
> hospital 5 days, - they X-rayed my kidneys and they said they
> could not find anything wrong with them, - but they did find
> a bone which had grown to my pelvis, from my 5th vertebrae.
> The Dr. said if I could gain weight it would help me.
> 
> Last March, I had the inflammation again, - I went to a local
> Dr. here (N.C.) - he prescribed sulfa thiazol tablets, and
> later a tonic.  I gained weight and wasn't bothered much with
> my back until June. - I went back to the same Dr. I had gone
> to in March, - and he advised an X-ray, which I had, & he
> said I had a kidney stone.
> 
> My husband & I both felt we should get the opinion of another
> Dr. - My husband is in the service, he inquired about medical
> care, as he felt they would give me a thorough examination.
> I was sent by them to a hospital for examination & they
> advised me to stay for 6 days for observation. - At the
> end of 6 days they wanted to take more X-rays of me, so I
> remained there 15 days before being discharged.
> 
> There, they took 3 X-rays of my kidneys, gave me a
> cystoscopic treatment, urinal tests, blood tests,
> fluoroscopic examination, X-rayed my teeth, and
> took 8 other X-rays of my stomach, etc.
> 
> The kidney specialist said the X-rays did not show any stone,
> or even where one would have had been, & there was nothing
> wrong except a slight inflammation in a kidney.  The other
> X-rays did not show anything, the Dr. said everything was
> normal, - but they noticed that bone again that I have
> mentioned in the first part of this letter.  I had again lost
> 10 pds (before I entered the hospital,) and when I asked the
> Dr. what caused it he said he couldn't honestly tell me what
> it was.
> 
> I haven't wholly recovered from this attack, my back-ache is
> severe, and now I am starting to get the inflammation again,
> pus in my kidneys.
> 
> Mr. Cayce, I honestly don't know what to do anymore, won't
> you PLEASE give me my Reading soon as possible.
> 
> Please notify me of the date you can give me my reading.
> 
> On page 11 of, "Edgar Cayce, His Life Work" it says, "case
> studies are available in pamphlet form for those desiring to
> know something of the results obtained in following Readings
> given within the last few years."  Will you please send me
> these pamphlets?
> 
> These are the questions I would like asked at the Reading:
> 
> What is causing my illness, the back-aches & inflammation (&
> pus)?
> Would it be advisable for me to have a child?  (While I have
> this trouble.)
> I usually have a bad taste in my mouth which is generally
> worse in the mornings, it stays with me thru the day.  I can
> say I have had it for some years.  Can you tell me what is
> causing this?  (The Dr. at home could not diagnose it.)
> 
> Please write me your suggestions.
> 
> Thank you VERY VERY much,
> 
> Very Sincerely,  Mrs. [3520], ..., N.C.
>
