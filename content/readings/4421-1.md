# TEXT OF READING 4421-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4421-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/1/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12270

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 1st day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M.                                   [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala.

1. EC:  Yes we have the body here.  Now we find that the trouble that is caused in the ear, or the discharge that shows there at times is produced by the nerve strain on the portion of the system that has to do with the circulation toward the upper portion of the body and causes too much secretions to be thrown off and not absorbed and eliminated through its proper channels.

2. To relieve this condition would be to adjust the impinioned nerves in such a manner as to relieve this condition.  This we find at the first and second cervical, and the first, and the third and forth dorsal.  These should be adjusted by Chiropractic manipulation for the permanent cure.  For the local condition, to prevent the trouble, we would locally apply with a dropper two drops of St. Jacob's Oil, warmed, in each ear, every second evening, and place cotton in the orifice to prevent it, the oil, from losing or wasting during the evening, see, then cleanse with a mild antiseptic, such as would be found in [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], warmed of course, the next morning, see.

3. Do this for from two to three weeks and the body will find this will remove this condition and prevent the wasting away of the organs of the ear, and prevent the drum from becoming involved or from decay setting in, see.  Do that, and we will correct this condition.
