# TEXT OF READING 2790-3 F 24

**Reading [[2790-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/18/1924  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9960

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 18th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [2790].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find there are many conditions in this body at the present time a great deal better than when we had them before.  There are at the present time some specific conditions, where organs in their functioning have been put in distress by the conditions as taken in the system, and these cause distress at the present time.

2. There are other conditions of a different nature than that we had when we had the body before that have arisen in the system.  These have much to do with the nerve system, and the strain both physically and mentally that has been produced upon it, and the manifestations of this nerve condition in the physical is more than the condition that is of the local nature at the present time, and adds to this condition.

3. We would for the condition in the body at the present time, for the betterment of the body, take often, until the system has been purged well of these conditions, the broken doses of Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] with that of [[Camphor|Camphor]] and of the Syrup of Sarsaparilla, the dose being half a teaspoonful in half a glass of water (warm water).  Two drops of [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] with four drops of Syrup of Sarsaparilla, taken every two hours until system is well purged.  This will give distress to much of the system, but the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] should rest through all of these.

4. When the system has been cleared, and the body has rested for five days, take this:

Gum [[Camphor|Camphor]].............20 grains,
Sulphate of Morphia....1/2 grain,
Muriated Ammonia........30 grains.

5. Mix well and make into twenty pellets (not capsules).  These are to be taken one each evening until the whole quantity is taken.  Keep the pellets in cool, dry place, else they will dissolve or run together.

6. And for the conditions in the system, it would be well that each evening for five to ten minutes the Violet Ray would be applied to the whole system, especially to thyroids and above the solar plexus centers.

7. Do this.  We will find we will bring the body to the normal forces.

8. (Q)  Does the body have goitre?
(A)  It is the condition we are giving here for the Violet Ray, in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] region.  This is [not an] enlarged [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] as yet, but may turn into such if the system is allowed to secrete those particles that will draw and settle in this portion of the body.

Do as we have given if we would give the best to this system.
