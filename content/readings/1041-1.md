# TEXT OF READING 1041-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[1041-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/30/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6156

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 30th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by Mrs. Geo. Baldwin, through Active Membership of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Lucille, S. [[King David|David]] and Richard [[David Kahn|Kahn]].]

Time of Reading 
5:35 to 5:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Penna. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, conditions are very serious.  There is the lack of the resistances in the system through the inability of the blood supply to maintain an equilibrium, through the throwing off of poison accumulations by the excess of the inroads of bacilli activity within the body itself.  So, this makes for complications.

2. As we find, there is a possibility of a reviving under those applications that are being given - for the producing of a stimulation in the heart's activity; yet the excesses of the toxic forces or poisons are gaining upon the system.

3. And there is little likelihood of a balance being created, unless more oxygen may be had in the blood.  And we do not find it apparent that there can be sufficient stimulation without being detrimental to other portions of the system.

4. We would apply, then, those things that are being used; making for as much ease for the body as possible.

5. We would make it EASIER for the body, not merely physically but by the mental attitudes of those with and about the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What caused this condition?
(A)  As indicated, bacilli activity in internal organs.

8. (Q)  What solace can be given to this body mentally, in her present state?
(A)  Rather the LOOSENING of the bonds of material associations, that there may be the easiness of transition.

9. (Q)  Who can do that, around her?
(A)  Those that are the closer to the body, in the activities about the body.

10. (Q)  Do you mean that she should really be prepared for this parting of the body?
(A)  Rather that those who would hold the body under these existent conditions would LOOSE their desires for maintaining of these sufferings.

Keep the body, then, easy.  The applications, as we find, should make a response in the heart's activity.  Yet too great a resistance is being builded against the body responding, by the lack of the eliminations or absorptions.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. Geo. Baldwin, Phoned also - to Greenwich Country
"    " Ass'n file                     Club, Greenwich, Conn.
