# TEXT OF READING 3822-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[3822-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/5/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11426

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by those present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
P. M. [Exact time unknown]

1. GC:  In a reading given May 4, 1925, on [3822], a prescription was suggested and given in this reading for this body.  I will read this prescription which you will correct.

2. EC:  Yes, we have this here.  This should read:

3. To one gallon rain water, add 6 ounces green ragweed or Ambrosia Weed.  Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain while warm.  Add:  Sugar, alcohol and the other ingredients, as given.
