# TEXT OF READING 325-55 F 62

**Reading [[325-55]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/24/1935  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 2493

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [325], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, the conditions are very much in the manner as indicated.  It is necessary for the removal of at least ONE of the teeth, as we see, that - as has been indicated - has the pus at the root of same.  We do not find that it would be necessary to remove others in the present. Should the condition continue, why it would become necessary. But if the eliminations are kept somewhat above normal, and there is kept more of an alkalinity in the system, with the use of the properties in Ipsab in the present, we find that conditions should be on the improve.  If the Ipsab may not be used in the full strength at present, then dilute it; add one teaspoonful of water to two teaspoonsful of the Ipsab, for this particular body, and massage the gums thoroughly.  It will not only relieve these conditions but be helpful for the whole general system.

2. In the main conditions are much as has been indicated, and we would through these periods - when there is not a great deal of activity outside be more consistent with the use of the lamp.

3. Be very careful about overexercising in a draft or exposure, but be sure that there is plenty of CHANGE of air in the room.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  In regard to my teeth, should I follow Dr.? [R. Lieberthal or Dr. S. Apisdorf]
(A)  (Interrupting)  As indicated, there is ONE to be removed.  One wants to take five, and one wants to take one. Follow the one that wants ONE.

6. (Q)  Is it necessary to put a removable bridge in mouth?
(A)  Unless there is to be taken more than the one, it is not necessary.  With the one it would be necessary to use something for that indicated, see?

7. (Q)  Does gold in mouth help to cause bitterness?
(A)  It does!  No teeth should ever be filled with heavy metals, as gold.  Much preferable would be the compositions that make themselves one with the properties themselves; much preferable.

8. (Q)  Why can't I sleep?
(A)  If you will use the Radio-Active Appliance, you can sleep!

9. (Q)  Does the Ash upset my stomach at times?
(A)  Only when there is too much grease, or too much of those properties that upset WITH such, or cause it to float too much - prevents assimilation.  At such times, take a small quantity of apricot brandy or a little wine; it will be much better.  We would do these in the present.  We are through with this reading.
