# TEXT OF READING 849-9 M 24 (Writer, Catholic)

**Reading [[849-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/3/1931  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 5002

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1931, in accordance with request made by Mr. [849].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [849], [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], L. B. Cayce and Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and the enquiring minds of Edgar Cayce and [849], present in this room, and the literary works of these two individuals, both those written individually and those written in collaboration; also the propagation of this work through the literary efforts of these two [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]].  You will give criticism of the works already executed, advice and guidance on those contemplated, and suggestions for definite channels through which these works may find their way to sympathetic and receptive publishers.  You will answer any and all questions concerning same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind - Edgar Cayce, [849], present in this room.  These we have had before.  Some of the efforts towards the literary field we have had before.

3. In considering these conditions, many are the minds and conditions of peoples and places to be considered.  First, as is asked - as for criticism respecting each, this again would DEPEND upon that from what ANGLE, or to what PEOPLES this would be disseminated, as to the criticism.

4. In that as has been attempted by Edgar Cayce - in the one there is the need of simplification; in the preparing of that as has been given as discourses, or talks, needs more of the POSITIVE efforts.  [See series of Edgar Cayce's lectures published during the year 1931 in The [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] News. Some of these were printed later in booklet, WHAT I BELIEVE.]

5. In that as has been prepared by [849], needs more of that as CLARIFIES same from that of satirism, that CLARIFIES same from satirism, and of more definite line and distinction as to the aims, [[Purpose|purposes]] AND [[Ideal|ideals]].

6. In collaboration of the efforts of these, would there be in the beginnings those of preparation of articles on varied subjects as these affect the individual from their various approaches to the information themselves, or itself, this clarified through the information CHANNELS, would make for matter as would be ACCEPTABLE by any of the STANDARD magazines in the present, and these may be approached either THROUGH Hearst's interest, or they may be approached through Crowell [Merele Crowell?] and THOSE interests - as of Osage; or they may be approached through any of the various ATTEMPTED magazines on kindred subjects, but of these BEWARE! unless there is a perfect UNDERSTANDING of where, how, and WHEN same is obtained.  Also through those channels of that that is termed those of the magazine sections of many of the syndicated papers may these, properly written - Now, as to the approach:

7. That of a layman in the ORDINARY walk of life, with his mediocre surroundings, and seeking for physical aid - or as the description of a case, see?  but DO NOT approach same too much from the approach of criticism to any sect, individual, or group of people!

8. Also the approach of one with a finished college education, seeking that which will answer for the various activities as has been encountered by the body through its experience in college and university life; the breaking up of ideas, theories, long cherished in their various ways and manners.

9. Then the approach from that of the rather serious minded individual in the position of that of the scientific, or sub scientific position of a questionable profession by MANY of another in KINDRED or similar professions that disagree with that individual.

10. Then upon the approach of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] SEASONED professor, or scientific research individual, that has not found an answer to many of the questions, either in the church or OUT of same, as respecting same.

11. This, as will be seen by [849], will be the experiencing in the mental self of THAT as has been given, that the self must find WITHIN self the answer IN the work, or that manifested that gives credence to self's OWN understanding.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Who can best collaborate with Edgar Cayce on the complete story of his life and work, and what literary form should it take - autobiography or biography?
(A)  Biography.  It has been given who should collaborate with anything that they would have anything to do with! Would you question these?

14. (Q)  Considering present conditions and circumstances, where can [849] find work at his time which will enable him to be of service to himself and to others through the expression and development of his literary talents?
(A)  Should he seek - should he desire to seek to WRITE such a COURSE or series or articles as has just been outlined, commence wherever he chooses!  Collaborate them with the Work itself and it'll sell 'em!  but he'll have to write 'em right!  Can't write his own self - write that he experiences! or experience that that he makes such individual experience, and if he can't find the answer within himself - then rather dig ditches than write on any such subject!

15. (Q)  Where should [849] send his novel "Bunkum," for the most sympathetic and advantageous publishing?
(A)  Simon & Shuster!

16. (Q)  Where should [849] send his book of poetry, "Forever [[Dreams|Dreaming]]," for the most sympathetic and advantageous publishing?
(A)  This, as we would find, would be the better proposition or manner in which to publish this, and to receive the BEST and the most advantageous results than from the publishing of same:  Seek out, through the correspondence, those who would subscribe to autographed copies of the first addition, and collect for same and pay for the first publishing.  THEN the comments as will be received BY the various press, various reports from press, will be the BEGINNING of a success - but for mercy's sake don't let it go to your head!  for, should this occur, the END will be much worse than the present feelings in many directions.

17. (Q)  Should [849] approach Simon & Shuster through Krell, or send it direct.?
(A)  As WE would find, while Krell would be well - but there's too much strings tied with it!  There are other channels through which this may be sent to Simon & Shuster better than through Krell, or through direct.  As we would find, first approach through some of those who have - or THINK they have - those of literary talents that are FINANCIALLY able to publish their own when so desired, as through the Music Club, or as through [[Woman of Revelation|the Woman]]'s Club of [[New York City|New York]], or some of those associated with same.  Then, through their introduction to one Ray Long, approach it.  Now, this is going both through those of the Hearst interest - in fact, the highest one, yet one word from Long, "This is pretty good"  would mean much more than Krell with a thousand lines added to it!

18. (Q)  Should [849] go to N.Y. personally to do this?
(A)  He should, and within the next few weeks, or as SOON as one whom he may approach this individual is in their home.

19. (Q)  Who is this individual referred to?
(A)  Mrs. Danzig.  [Helen W. Danzig - Mrs. Jerome J. Danzig]

20. (Q)  What subject matter, and in what forms of expression - lectures, articles, "Through the Subliminal," the autobiography or biography - should [849] and Edgar Cayce begin to collaborate on at the present time?
(A)  Now, there's been outlined, as has been seen - if studied over properly - a DEVELOPMENT for both of these "poor simps" to study, as to how to get the proper insight as to how such MAY be written - see?  In applying the articles, why not make something as you go along with it, too?  and then by the constant rejections that may be had in the first, constant corrections as may be made - for do not think the Forces will not allow these to be rejected, and GAIN the view of the material and secular-minded individuals in their various positions!  but a more BEAUTIFUL LAY-OUT for approach to the highest authorities as exist in the present doesn't exist, if they'll work at it!  yet if they don't want to do it, don't know how to do it, nor what to do, and don't try - and are already discouraged - who can help 'em!  God can't! unless you help yourself!

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [849]

> it seemed foolish to buy furniture when we are not sure how

> a period of two years it is a considerable saving and the
> advantages of having our own place outweigh the slight
> disadvantage incurred in the beginning.
> 
> I have been too busy for my own temperament of late, but that
> is a matter which will settle itself, for I feel already a
> shifting in my nervous make-up which will acclimate me to the
> routine.  The banquet season has begun, which is [[Fallen Angels|the devil]] on
> young reporters.  It means we spend night after night eating
> chicken a la king and listening to boring speeches.  The main
> annoyance is the late hours.  Most of us pray for over-nights
> - or morning assignments, which allow us to quit
> automatically at six p.m.  For some time I was in Norwalk and
> Stamford on the Collins murder case.  I don't like police
> work anyhow, and the complete isolation up there was an added
> burden.
> 
> I have specially kept in mind your advice not getting
> hard-boiled.  I'm afraid that will be impossible now - though
> it would have been natural several years ago.  I have come to
> have too intimate a [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] of the spiritual side of
> humanity, and however discouraging and disillusioning some of
> the things seem that I contact it is easy to remember the
> real basis of all greatness and degradation, and strangely
> enough it does not touch me except to make me more determined
> and faithful in my [[Prayer|prayer]] and idealism.
> 
> One portion of my work is delightful - the religious side
> which I attend to on Sunday.  It keeps me in church most of
> the day and in a spiritual atmosphere all the day.  I also
> cover whatever dedications or other ceremonies are in order
> during the week.
> 
> I am now of the conviction that all our training and hoping
> and waiting and [[Prayer|praying]] is not preparation in a physical or
> intellectual way for the high post of service we wish to
> occupy, but a training for the choosing by higher forces.
> Perhaps it will seem to many of my friends in Connecticut (as
> they have expressed) that I am being forced through a
> needless and lengthy term of training and suffering, not to
> mention the ignominy of working under some unvisionary and
> hard-boiled man.  They consider my abilities deserve more of
> a break than they are getting.  On the other hand I know
> myself that I am only on the very outermost rim of that
> spiritual development which will make me worthy of [[Free Will|choice]] for
> high place - and I shall probably never write anything
> worthwhile until I attain to the center of the feeling which
> now and then wells up within me and gives me the conviction
> that I am on the right path.
> 
> I may have things published before long, but I myself contend
> that I will be thirty-five before I do anything which will
> deserve the attention of posterity.  Meanwhile it is not half
> so hard to go along without recognition or reward - as it
> used to be - and to the eternal glory of your own friendship

> R14. 9/27/32 Ltr. from [849]:
> 
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> I hardly know how to begin a letter that must, by reason of
> the condition of its source, be somewhat confused.  In all of
> my short life I have never been so inarticulate, and never
> have I had more to say.  But when it goes off, my friend, I
> fear it will blow me all over the earth, and make a
> detonation that will tumble several turrets in heaven and set
> not a few crowns askew with the jar.  Something, whether it
> be diabetes, [[Fallen Angels|the devil]] or the deity, is happening inside of
> me.
> 
> This is the third week of my return from vacation, and it is
> the nearest I have come to being explicit.  It seemed that
> every day I stayed home I felt worse, for my nerves were only
> slowly untightening.  I had to keep going, so that after
> revising a short story I took over the column in the local
> newspaper and did it for a friend who was on vacation.  When
> time came to return I was at the ebb, and arriving in the
> city in that state was pretty close to suicide for one of my
> temperament.
> 
> For the first two days I covered the Eighth Avenue subway,
> which was preparing to open.  The dust in the new passage did
> something to my sinus I know (I rode on the trains before the
> tube was opened, just when they were cleaning up) for
> afterward I seemed to have a dry catarrh rather than a wet
> catarrh - and believe me I'll take the moist variety anytime,
> the other is torture.  Then the regular transit expert came
> back and I did some interviews, for which I received by-lines
> and praise.  The night the subway opened I did a feature on
> the first ride, in the manner of a dramatic review, which
> pleased everyone immensely.  For a week thereafter I rode on
> a crest of splendid stories, but I worked unmitigatingly and
> without a single moment's respite.  I brought up at the end
> like a horse that has run its heart out and begins to quiver
> before dying.
> 
> I've been that way for a week now, never quite knowing
> whether my kidneys were going to collapse or my stomach,
> whether my hands would shake themselves off or my head
> tighten so that it would burst.  Every now and then I felt
> that, unless something happened in a hurry, I would fall on
> my face and be picked up in little pieces.
> 
> I am inclined to credit it all to nerves, and to believe that
> a tonic and perhaps an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] will set things right.  But
> there is something else which is aiding the trouble, a sort
> of nigger in the woodpile which only a few people like you
> can understand.
> 
> In my letter to Hugh Lynn under this date I explained that my
> life reading pointed these two years (25th and 26th years) as
> the period of my crisis in the affairs of Venus, and as the
> years during which my life went awry in the last appearance.
> Miss Adams predicts them as the years of change, the
> beginning of a successful writing career, and the years when
> I shouldn't marry and should beware of a dark-haired lady
> (that doesn't give me much of a lead, as I've never cared for
> blonds).  Many times as I trot about the city I run into
> amateur psychics and fortune-tellers, and for amusement I let
> them read cards and palms for me.  All of them seem to hit
> the same line.  Travel is imminent (over water) and success.
> There is always a dark lady mixed up and a red-haired man who
> is the agent of my travel.  The years of change are always
> indicated.  One palmist became angry when I refused a further
> fee for a more detailed reading and said I was not making the
> most of my artistic abilities.  I laughed at her, but I
> thought about it for several reasons.
> 
> Please don't think I place stock in these amateurs and
> fakirs.  I merely cite them as a curious phenomena, and I
> realize that most of them have a slight leaning toward such
> things, and that I am an easy person to contact from that
> angle.
> 
> Anyhow, the period of change is obvious to me - so obvious
> that I am sure it has a lot to do with my physical condition,
> perhaps is all of it.
> 
> I am only emerging from adolescence, and I have so many
> curious strains and influences in my life that the change is
> a painful procedure.  In early years we never feel the result
> of anything we do right away - it is later they begin to take
> effect.  I have so many things that weigh against me -
> supersensitiveness that treasures hurts in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]],
> Celtic mysticism and melancholia, Catholicism, the influence
> of my contact with you and your work, the southern influence
> of [..., Va.], and now New York.  My [[Soul|soul]] has been a
> battleground for a long time, and there have been experiences
> we won't mention that haven't helped the result any.  The
> debacle is therefore all the more bloody and the fruits, if I
> live through it, will be all the more rich.  I think it is a
> inherent decency and recognition of the truth that is
> twisting me so badly in an effort to get rid of the poisons
> that I planted there with my own foolishness.
> 
> Perhaps you can feel what I mean - my expression of it is
> terrible.  I am still strangled economically under the same
> $100 a month salary, and I still work so hard that play is
> for me a matter of bed, long baths that soothe a little, and
> light reading.  Now, when I can look to easier things after
> election, I feel a tremendous power surging in me, and a
> great desire to write - in song and in story as my reading
> says.  But what I don't know.  I only knew a great power is
> developing, is welling up in me, is destroying my vitality in
> its effort to get loose.  When it does, as I said, I feel it
> will be tremendous - and good.
> 
> I have come almost to the top of the hill of understanding -
> that is why the final effort is so painful, and I know that I
> have a power of expression ready to let go when I get there -
> and never before in my life have I been able to say this with
> certainty - it will be good, and it will be beautiful.
> 
> If you read Hugh Lynn's letter you may find some of these
> incoherences explained - and vice versa.  I can't anymore
> tell what I'm going to say, or whether it will be what I want
> to say, than a baby learning to talk.
> 
> I wonder if you will have time, someday when you are doing
> that sort of thing, to give me a check reading?  I am at home
> in the morning always, and I only want to know what in the
> world ails my nerves and my stomach, why the sides of my
> forehead ache and feel tight, why my catarrh seems dry
> instead of wet, and why I am certain at times I shall fly to
> pieces like a wheel that has reached its point of fatigue.
> For the rest - my work and its future, I think the best thing
> to do is wait.  To make a slightly bawdy but very true
> simile, I feel like a mother with child - and I am sure it
> will be nothing less than a president.
> 
> There are many years ahead of us, but the next few will tell
> a swift, terrible, and glorious tale.  The world is on the
> toboggan slide - and when it hits the bottom someone must be
> there to pick it up and drag it back up again.  I have a
> feeling we'll be a few of the pullers.  I've never had more
> faith in you, in God, and in myself.  What I said to Hugh
> Lynn goes for you and for me - and for all the family and
> friends of the work -
> 
> Sometimes the dice come seven.  [849]
