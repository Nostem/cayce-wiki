# TEXT OF READING 1676-3 F 28

**Reading [[1676-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/19/1939  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 7792

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1676], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions submitted as I ask them.

2. EC:  As we find, there still continues to be some improvement with the general condition of this body.

3. As there is the lack of energies in the nerve forces of the body, we find that NOW the application of electrical forces - with the rubs, with the packs - would be the most beneficial. Apply these not too strongly but as from the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]], Bulb Applicator.  This would be especially to the cerebrospinal system, gradually coming over the nerves of the lower extremities.  We would do this for the present, about one and one-half minutes every other evening before retiring.

4. The rest we would keep as has been indicated.

5. It is not necessary now to have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments more than twice a month, if these are given GENERALLY when they are given, see?

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note at the time of the reading:  If you do not have electricity, a [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] may be obtained that is generated by a battery.)
