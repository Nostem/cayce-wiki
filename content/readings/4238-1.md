# TEXT OF READING 4238-1 M 53 (Osteopath)

**Reading [[4238-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/11/1923  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 12004

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 11th day of September, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:45 A. M.                                        ..., Ga.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find the conditions in this body quite different from those when we had it before.  Now these are the conditions as we find them at this time.

2. First, in the blood supply, with the condition of the blood and poor elimination through the proper channels of the hepatic circulation, we find there are poisons attempting to be eliminated through capillaries.  Hence, the abrasions we have in the cuticle itself, the upper portion of the body, hands, arms and the body.

3. Also with this the centers in the nerve system are affected, and become as we have in the brachial plexus center, over stimulated, until co-ordination or control becomes through this same pressure from non-elimination [non-activity?] distressing to the center itself - see?  so that when voluntary action is attempted to be expressed it becomes involuntary forces from the sympathetics in their actions, this affect coming from the juncture here of the sympathetic cerebro-spinal sensory trunk, pneumogastric and perineurial nerves - see?  This, in the eliminating forces, gives this condition in the system.  This, also, as we see, produces the condition in the nerve system, this being a part of the nerve system disturbance.

4. In the functioning of organs we still have the condition in the liver and the intestinal tract, as we have had before, this being the seat of the trouble in times back, or that of a catarrhal conditions of the gastric centers, and producing the effect through the liver duodenum and intestinal tract over-charging the system, and not allowing elimination proper through those organs themselves.

5. To give then the relief to this body would be to relax osteopathically the centers from the twelfth dorsal to the neck, and to give the body, when in the relaxed condition, voltage from Violet Ray or electric forces along these same centers, and over the lymphatic centers of the extremities, the upper ones, and the portion of the body above the diaphragm - see?

6. Attend also to the condition in the mouth itself.  Poisons accumulate there.  Do that.

7. We are through.
