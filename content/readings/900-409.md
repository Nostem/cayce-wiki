# TEXT OF READING 900-409 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-409]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/6/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5612

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Dr. and Mrs. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], the [[Visions|visions]] as are experienced by the body, are to be applied in the way and manner as has been indicated, and same should be applied, would the better manifestations and understandings come to the body.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  Recently.  I was looking under cover of bed, being directed to do so by Schwartz, my employee.  I saw a skunk that changed into a man that looked like Louis N. Hammerling.
(A)  This, as would be indicated in that seen, that the body as changed into the skunk has had that in the actions of the life that has caused, and does cause, others to feel and speak unkindly, and are warned against the activities of the body.  As is also indicated in that there are, and were, physical defects with the body that, through the associations, may be made beneficial.  Not that the body Hammerling, as seen, is NOT to be watched and studied also. Know many of those conditions that have caused unsavory comment as respecting the body, how the activities of same have brought about various experiences in the life of the body, or as how the body Hammerling has applied that as has been obtained by same, and take a lesson for self from that understanding as is gained by same.  As is seen also respecting the associates and ones who are handling some of the business for the body Hammerling.  THIS body watch more closely than either of the two.

3. (Q)  Is Samuel Genis the one referred to?
(A)  Samuel Genis one referred to.  THAT individual be wary of, yet much may be learned by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], by the body [900], that would be beneficial to self, from these two individuals. Do not trust self's interests as financially too far into the keeping of either of these, until their motives and principles are understood better by self ([900]).

4. (Q)  Oct. 3rd.  Experience in which Gary spoke to me, referring to bankers & money rates:  "Did you expect them to do it for less?"  Then saw Sears-Roebuck.
(A)  This, as has been indicated, and as is given the body from time to time, that understanding of the concept gained by the forces, that would give better understanding in application of self as respecting activities, whether individual stocks, or whether groups, even as bankers or money raters.

5. (Q)  Last night, while reading, Gary spoke to me.  "Why did you not wait for me to tell you?"
(A)  This that calling, or reprimand, that the body would heed, would bring better understanding.  Has it not been given that as to the activities in various things the body would be directed by those of a higher, farther seeing force. Why not heed?  As is referred to here, respecting the unstable situation the body finds self, not knowing whether to carry over for the period during the change, or just what the action would or should be.  Then heed, and when same is given, act - and there will be much surety in the understanding as is gained at that time.

6. (Q)  Something referring to Genis.
(A)  That is, as the body would follow advice by one that is questioned in the conscious mind, does it make the activities of self as respecting those of the higher forces compatible with same.  Answer from within.

7. (Q)  Does this mean I should have waited to sell Universal Pipe?
(A)  Until it passes, or reaches - which it will - forty-one, as has been seen - while the selling had been at a much lower level, see?  for there will be given, as has already been outlined for the body, those activities that the body should should indulge in for the better understanding of all associations.

8. (Q)  Saw number 41.  Does this refer to Pressed Steel or Universal Pipe, or both?
(A)  Rather to both.  Still advances in these, though there will be many slide offs, but still advances to near this price.  Yet there will be given as respecting WHAT the activities will be regarding these and others.  As the body has been warned, then heed.  We are through.
