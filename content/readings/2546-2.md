# TEXT OF READING 2546-2 M 45

**Reading [[2546-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/5/1942  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 9543

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2546] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2546], this we have had before.

3. As we find, there is a great deal of improvement in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  However, the chemical reactions in the system - and the natural NERVE reactions from same - tend to continue to attempt to revert to old disturbances.  As has been indicated, when the body was overtired it had those relapses to the former disturbances.

4. We would attempt to follow more closely those suggestions that have been made.  To be sure, it may be somewhat troublesome to obtain the whole milk for the Acigest, but this is the better form for this to be taken, and it will assimilate with the body gastric flow BETTER than in other forms that it might be administered.

5. Thus, we would given, make the attempts when it is at all practical to adhere to this daily.

6. Continue with the [[Calcios|Calcios]] at times also.  This may be taken about three days a week now, if this is preferable.

7. And at least once a week have a good [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxing treatment.

8. These should bring continues helpful forces for this body.

9. Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Any suggestions as to holding down my weight, so that I won't get too heavy?
(A)  Keeping way from starches will keep down the weight. Also taking [[Grape Juice|Grape Juice]] about thirty minutes before each meal and before retiring at night, - two ounce of [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] with an ounce of plain water (not carbonated, not distilled, but plain tap water and preferably [[Grape Juice|Welch's Grape Juice]]), - this will keep down the sugars and the fats for the body.

12. (Q)  Was the one attack I have had since taking the treatments caused by being overtired?
(A)  As has been indicated.

Do as we have outlined.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
