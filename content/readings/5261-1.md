# TEXT OF READING 5261-1 F 31

**Reading [[5261-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1944  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 13397

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5261], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  As we find, there are disturbances of a complex nature.  There are those tendencies for the position of the stomach to cause fermentation which, in turn, causes a great deal of disturbance in the eliminations of the body.  And it is from the toxic conditions produced by this fermentation and conditions through the colon that the severe headaches are caused as well as the sour stomach, an acidity through the mouth and throat, and such great anxiety at times to the mental processes; that is, the organs of the sensory system give a great deal of trouble by the pressures produced in body through the areas.

3. We would have, first, at least two or three of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, which would be tending towards lifting the stomach, the alimentary canal organs, to their proper position; and then using a brace of an elastic or just gauze wrapped about the body to keep these in position after such treatments osteopathically are given.

4. In taking the intestinal antiseptics such as a few drops of Lavoris or [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the water would be helpful; that is, taken each day, not during the night, but during the day, and the adjustments so as to keep these in their position, we will bring much aid and help for the body.

5. For throat and teeth we would massage the gums with Ipsab as a purifier for the teeth.

6. And for poor eliminations that cause the excess of conditions through the feet, ankles, these we would bathe in Ray's Ointment [See 5261-1, Par. R6] and we would relieve the tendencies for infectious forces produced by this acidity in the system.

7. Also cleanse the colon with enemas, after the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] water is taken for some two or three days.  Do not attempt to give the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] for self, but have them regularly so that all the mucous in colon being eliminated may, in two or three of such adjustments and such eliminations set up, eradicate the sources of the distress through the body.

8. Do that.  These [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments first, then [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], second.  As these are begun do begin with the change in the diet, to that which is easily assimilated would be the best. Those even with the least reaction may bring all digested food and the chyle from the stomach.

9. Keeping, then, the eliminations better and the respiratory and perspiratory system, we should bring much better conditions for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What should be done for athlete's foot?
(A)  As has been indicated, use Ray's Ointment.

12. (Q)  To keep hair from graying or falling out?
(A)  Don't worry about the hair, just keep it nice and smooth.  Take care of it more often with either Drene hair tonic or Olive Oil Hair Tonic, followed with the shampooing with crude oil and tar soap.  These, while making irritation at first will maintain the better conditions for the body.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two Copies to self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] of your [[Free Will|choice]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(GD's note with the reading:  Please let us have the name of the osteopath you choose.)
