# TEXT OF READING 4353-3 F 20 (Actress)

**Reading [[4353-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/12/1924  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 12182

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by self, through Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                            ... 79th Street, 
11:40 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over any physical ailments in this body very briefly and give cure.  Then you will go over the mental and spiritual of this body in great detail, telling how this body may develop her mental faculties to attain her best and fullest development in this earth's sphere, giving any faults in the mental outlook or inclinations of this body, and tell us in detail what the body may do now in a practical way to help her attain success in her career as an actress and bring material gain to herself.  You will also answer any questions pertaining to this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  We will find the physical conditions better in the body at the present time.  We need, however, to carry out more thoroughly the suggestions as given in that of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] especially, for with the strain as is placed on the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] the body needs the relaxing for the muscular tissue throughout the body, as has been given, that the whole physical may function the better.  In this we would give follow close to those conditions as have been outlined for the physical health.

3. In the mental and spiritual, we find the body well developed in every way.  There is in the mental forces those conditions that have brought strain in the mental activity through that of distress or disappointment.  In this the body should exercise the mental and the physical to the manner that will give the best to the work in hand, for this will be found to be both remunerative and give the advancement necessary to make the income from such labors more than sufficient for the needs, and will lead to the success of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  While the strain on the body, and the temperament in mental, are such as to work hardships on the mental and spiritual, the relaxing through physical, as outlined, will give much to developing in mental and spiritual forces.

4. Then, to increase, we would give that the body mentally, physically, spiritually, should carry on and work toward the end as will come with the production through which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] labors.  This will be found to be a wonderful success for many moons.  Then the change that comes with this will bring the possibilities to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to develop in the career as an actress.  The spiritual forces need only be given the incentive to act through the mental and the physical.  Hence the necessity in this to keep the physical and the mental fit, that through such work the body may develop.

5. There are many channels through which these labors and endeavors will lead or open for the entity.  We would give to follow closely in the one being enacted at present, with whole heart, [[Soul|soul]], mind and body, for the numerical development comes, a does that of the remunerative elements.

6. (Q)  If this body, [4353], fitted for the work she is now doing?  If not, what work, to bring her material gain, would she be better suited for?
(A)  She, [4353], is especially fitted for the work she is developing toward, and the present is the means of development to make the success the body desires.

Do that.

7. (Q)  If fitted for her present work, how, or what, should she do to secure greater success, or a better part in her present production?
(A)  Give self wholly to the work in hand, keeping the mental and the physical fit for the spiritual development that may come through giving one's best to that in hand, for in the present we find the entity developing toward the better parts that will come with the remunerative elements as developments progress.

8. (Q)  If not fitted for her present work, how may she secure success in the work she is best suited for?
(A)  As given, labor in the direction as given.

9. (Q)  Give us advice, counsel, and information that will help encourage and be of practical assistance to the body, [4353], in her daily work and development on this earth's plane.
(A)  As given, the body needs keep the correct incentive in its work.  The body physically, mentally, spiritually, is peculiarly, particularly, sufficiently, evidently, fitted for the work as is before the body at present.

Then, enter in same with the whole heart, [[Soul|soul]] and body, knowing that the laborer is worthy of his hire and that success will crown the efforts when all is kept in the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]], and all must correlate one with the other.  Not in the effect of eye-service but as giving to those that behold in the entity's labors that in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] exemplified in a manner and way that brings the better thought to the public and to the minds in spirit and in truth.

Follow in that way and we will come to the success that is desired in the body, remembering what the price must be paid for a success and the price is of self in mind, in spirit, in body.

10. We are through.
