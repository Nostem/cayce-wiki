# TEXT OF READING 1100-4 F 33

**Reading [[1100-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/12/1928  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 6291

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Estella Smith and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Pa. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1100] - this we have had before.  Now, there are many changes for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that as we have had before.  Some for improvement.  Some in their changing bring those conditions which are unsatisfactory.  While not wholly an abnormal, to the extent of being diseased, but rather as dis-ease for the body, troubling same - but the body is passing through a period of change [See 1100-4, Par. R1] in it's mental application of spiritual and mental truths for the body. Physically, we find conditions rather those of a nervous digestion, or nervous indigestion, as would be called. This producing irritation to the extent of restlessness in its reaction not only from nominal sleep, but the body finds self easily irritated at OTHER periods.  This more (physically) a form of acid as is being produced in the system through unbalancing of conditions as are thrown off from the system itself by non-eliminations in a proper manner.  Correct those conditions in the lower dorsal and lumbar region by one to two good thorough manipulations, osteopathically given; taking about half of an ordinary bottle of agar and the oil, or Petrolagar - the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] - and we will find the conditions disappearing.  Those troubles as caused throughout the rest of the system are irritations from the same condition.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What further is necessary to clear the scalp condition?
(A)  Keep the eliminations more in order and lose acid in system, and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to the hair - of the scalp - and applying the white vaseline occasionally, thoroughly rubbed in, and at least once each week cleanse same with the diluted alcohol, and we will find these conditions will clear themselves.  Principally the acid must be removed from the system.

3. (Q)  Why is my tongue constantly coated?
(A)  The acid condition existent in the digestive system. This producing regurgitation, forming that of the signal of distress through alimentary canal.  Have the corrections made osteopathically, take those properties as given, and we will find the system clearing itself in five to ten days. Do that.

4. (Q)  What causes restlessness at night and what should be done for it?
(A)  Just as has been given.

5. That is all the questions.

6. Do that as has been given, [1100].

7. We are through with this reading.
