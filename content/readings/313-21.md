# TEXT OF READING 313-21 F 54 (?)

**Reading [[313-21]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/29/1939  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 2384

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of December, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through husband, Mr. [228], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Here we have acute conditions arising from a congestion through the colon area.

4. And with the application of so much heat there has been brought on those irritations through the activity of the organs of the pelvis; producing a strain upon the general system.

5. Thus a spasmodic condition or reaction has been caused through the womb, as well as the ovarian area also.

6. As we find, first, in the present:

7. We would apply a combination of three parts Laudanum to one part Aconite over the areas where there is pain; that is, the right ovarian area, the ascending colon area and portions of the transverse colon, especially on the right side.  Paint the area with a small brush, camels' hair preferably.  Then apply hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  Wring at least three or four thicknesses of flannel out of the warm Oil, - not so hot as to produce too great an amount of heat through the area, but warm.  Apply such a Pack about every hour, - taking it off, re-painting, and then applying again, until a little bit easy; that is, until there is not such acute pain.

8. Then take a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], using the colon tube. To each quart of water, body-temperature, put a heaping teaspoonful of table salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] - well dissolved.  This would be better taken with the body upon hands (or elbows) and knees, see?  and gradually, as it is given, work the abdominal area from the caecum side ACROSS the body; gently, but sufficient of this so we may remove the impactions there.

9. If on the morrow, or the next day, there is a continuing or a return of temperature, the body should then go to hospitalization for greater consideration of these conditions; but these applications as indicated should reduce the conditions, so that it may be handled by themselves.

10. Do that - IMMEDIATELY!

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail, Special - Wired also [See below]
"    " Ass'n file

USING SMALL CAMELS' HAIR BRUSH PAINT ENTIRE AREA OF PAIN ESPECIALLY RIGHT SIDE  WITH COMBINATION THREE PARTS LAUDANUM ONE PART ACONITE THEN APPLY FOUR THICKNESSES FLANNEL WRUNG OUT OF WARM [[Castor Oil|CASTOR OIL]]  REPEAT THIS PROCEDURE EVERY HOUR UNTIL ACUTE PAIN RELIEVED THEN TAKE HIGH [[Colon Hydrotherapy|COLONIC IRRIGATION]] USING COLON TUBE AND DISSOLVING HEAPING TEASPOONFUL TABLE SALT AND LEVEL TEASPOONFUL [[Baking Soda|BAKING SODA]] IN EACH QUART BODY TEMPERATURE WATER GIVE THIS [[Colon Hydrotherapy|COLONIC]] WITH BODY UPON ELBOWS AND KNEES MEANWHILE GRADUALLY  GENTLY MASSAGING ABDOMEN FROM RIGHT SIDE ACROSS BODY  IF TEMPERATURE CONTINUES TOMORROW OR NEXT DAY HAVE HOSPITALIZATION.
