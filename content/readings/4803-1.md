# TEXT OF READING 4803-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[4803-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/21/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12786

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by Miss Ernestine Ruprecht, via his grandmother.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. 
[GD's note:  No copy of this reading can be found.  My memory 
is that it was very short, indicating that [[Soul|the soul]] was 
withdrawn from the body but lingering near.  Constructive 
suggestions were given for treating the body, physically and 
prayerfully, in order to reorient [[Soul|the soul]] with the body.]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/23/27 EC's letter to Ernestine Ruprecht:
> 
> Dear Miss Ruprecht:-
> 
> Your wire respecting the reading for Billy [4803] came before
> we had either of your letters - but, as you already know,
> the reading was taken as we had the name and address.  I
> certainly am in hopes that the suggestions given were carried
> out, and that you have long since gotten wonderful results
> from same.
> 
> We have had quite a number of cases very much similar to that
> described in the reading, and following very much of the same
> outline as you indicate in your letter, and there certainly
> have been wonderful results gotten where the suggestions have
> been followed out.  I am quite anxious to know the results
> of this case, and shall appreciate your letting me hear from
> you.
> 
> We sent the information to those addresses you gave us, and
> are enclosing you a couple of the copies of the testimonial
> pamphlets.  We shall be glad to send you any information or
> any data we may have on hand, and we assure you of our desire
> to be of help whenever possible.
> 
> Hoping to have been of service, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
> 
> R2. 10/4/27 Ernestine Ruprecht's letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Elm Cot,
> Sharon, Mass.
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> I have indeed failed in not expressing sooner my gratitude
> for your very good and prompt response to my telegram in
> regard to Billy [4803].
> 
> It helped considerably for it made the grandmother (Billy's
> guardian) consent to [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment as you advised.
> 
> Her (Dr. Marjorie Johnson's) diagnosis agreed with yours
> and also established the fact that Billy had several lesions
> in his neck, and a very strong toxic breath.  Dr. Johnson
> is also treating the 1st and 2nd cervical and gradually
> working to the 3rd and 4th cervicals.
> 
> Also Billy was put on a six day fast to overcome the toxic
> poisoning, with only orange juice, tomato juice and plenty of
> water to drink, and daily enemas.  This, to-gether with two
> treatments, seemed to help him considerably and showed a
> better state of MIND and body on the 5th day of fasting.  We
> are having [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments twice a week (having had
> three so far).
> 
> We are inclined to think that there was some poisoning, as of
> poison mushrooms, as the convulsions he had were not entirely
> of an epileptic nature and his recovery was so slow.
> 
> He had been in the habit of eating anything he can get
> outdoors, bark, bits of twig, leaves, and even ate the husks
> of sunflower seeds, also chewed up bits of a wooden boat,
> wooden blocks and a harmonica.  What is it that induces
> this growing hunger?
> 
> It may also interest you to know about the following.  He had
> been very apathetic and indifferent to his surroundings, no
> toys or anything ever holding his attention for more than
> five minutes.  We were quite worried about it and yesterday
> morning we received the following writings thru Miss Chany
> and the results obtained were so instantaneous - his mind
> responded immediately and we held his attention for 1-1/4
> hours with his "Meccano" - a very beloved construction
> mechanism.  Also he made several constructive statements
> during the remainder of the day.
> 
> The writings I will give only in part as there is quite a bit
> - an explanation too of his [[Karma|karmic]] ties and need to overcome
> through these very difficulties which he has incurred thru
> past unintegrity.  I would give them all to you if you would
> wish them.  The following is quoted:  "He can be cured for
> time in relative can overcome many an evilness.  Demand that
> his [[Soul|soul]] come and inhabit his body, imagine it there, demand
> it there.  The human [[Soul|soul]] seeks to evade the responsibility
> of the body upon this earth placed.  Never, never shake him
> as that in the past has weakened near to severance the chord
> that holds [[Soul|the soul]] within the body.  If that chord that
> holds the soul within the body be broken the body will live
> on but soullessly.  The integrity with him is that the soul
> be made to inhabit, to dwell within the body.  He seeks at
> times to bring about this severance thru harming the body
> with auto-intoxication.  The struggle you witnessed was but
> the body seeking to throw off the soul.  Make him do things
> - set tasks definite.  This drifting aimlessly is wrong as
> it allows the soul to drift off into sensuous space.  He
> will either be a soulless one - not idiotic, but vague to the
> extreme or else if the soul be coordinated with the body he
> has every opportunity to be a rather flashing personality and
> to achieve along one line.  First get him coordinated thru
> the line of least resistance.  Take the thing that most
> interests him and gradually from fifteen minutes up to
> four hours a day make him concentrate and coordinate."
> 
> We have been told that the mother (now passed on) was
> continually shaking the child in irritation at his
> stubbornnesses - his eating of the twigs, etc., would
> corroborate the matter of auto-intoxication - also - we find
> it VERY difficult to make him have proper eliminations and
> have had to give him enemas for two weeks now - in spite of
> careful [[Free Will|choice]] of laxative foods in the past few days since
> his fast we have not been able to get a natural elimination.
> You can see how well these things bear out the writings.
> 
> Thanking you again for your quick and helpful response, and
> trusting that my explanations will not bore you - I am
> 
> Sincerely yours, Ernestine Ruprecht
> 
> P.S. Dr. Johnson is working out a diet for Billy.
> 
> R3. 10/12/27 EC's letter to Miss Ruprecht:-
> 
> 
> Dear Miss Ruprecht:
> 
> Many thanks for yours of the 4th.  I certainly am in hopes
> that Billy is responding wonderfully to the treatments by
> Dr. Johnson.  I feel sure from what you say that if the
> suggestions are carried out very sincerely, the results will
> be most satisfying.  I thank you very much for writing me
> respecting Miss Chany's prognosis of the condition.  This

> that Billy responds to the treatment, for this is my great
> desire - to use whatever forces that may be manifested
> through me toward the help of others and especially towards
> the young - for on the young depends the hope of the world.
> 
> Again thanking you, and hoping you will let us hear from you
> whenever you have the opportunity, with all good wishes to
> you both, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD

> in charge of someone we do not know."
