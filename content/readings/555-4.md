# TEXT OF READING 555-4 M 36

**Reading [[555-4]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1934  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 3909

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1934, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [540], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Esther Wynne, Helen Ellington, Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                  ... Avenue, 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body; this we have had before.  Some indiscretions, and the lack of the proper eliminations being coordinated; or the stopping off too soon of eliminations; these tend to make for irritations.

2. We would prepare about six large onions, you see, steamed preferably in Patapar paper.  Squeeze out the juice and sweeten just a bit.  Begin with small doses of same taken about every two hours apart.  This will change the condition for the stomach, change the condition in the respiratory system.

3. Dissolve about five grains of Calcidin in one ounce of water, DISTILLED water.  Keep it warm.  Keep it covered.  Begin with three to five drops every fifteen minutes, this evening, until the body goes to sleep; until we can get the [[Iodine|iodine]] reaction from same to the lungs, you see.

4. We would keep the bowels open, preferably with enemas.

5. Let the diet be only fruit juices, principally lemons and grapefruit.  A little orange juice may be taken, but mix it rather with the lemon.  And the other periods of diet would consist of vegetable juices.

6. And you ought to be alright in another day!

7. (Q)  What properties should be taken three to five drops at a time?
(A)  We have just given it!  The Calcidin!  Made into solution, you see.  We want the [[Iodine|iodine]] reaction in the lung, with the onion juices.

Do this.

8. (Q)  What were the indiscretions?
(A)  Eating!

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
