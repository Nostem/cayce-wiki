# TEXT OF READING 1042-3 F 58

**Reading [[1042-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/22/1936  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 6159

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 22nd day of April, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1042], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [1042].]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1042]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while conditions are very good in many respects, there are still those conditions that have been indicated; the correction of which in the present would make for a much nearer and better normal functioning throughout the system.

3. Those disturbances that have been indicated in the assimilating forces of the body, that produce - as it were - the activity in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, in the functioning of the organs in their relationships to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body. These becoming more and more disturbed are producing along the cerebrospinal system, especially in those areas or about those ganglia from which these portions of the system take their impulses in their activity, more disturbing conditions.

4. Hence these find their distresses or give a reflex to dullness in the body; headaches of an indeterminate nature, but making for a dullness that will gradually increase until there are other disturbing factors - unless eliminated.

5. As we find in the present, the use of the Radio-Active Appliance, with those combinations of the massage and the baths that make for a stimulation to the superficial circulation, and then those corrections at the time of the massage, will add to a sufficient [[Vibration|vibratory]] influence for the body as to bring a nearer normal reaction.

6. Use the Appliance each day, or whenever the body feels the tiredness or the lack of sufficient pep or the activities for the resting of the body will also be made more normal by its use.

7. Do these and, as we find, we will bring the bettered conditions for this body, [1042].

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What causes pains along spine?
(A)  There are those reactions in the assimilating, or forces that make for the activities through the blood supply, in the lymph circulation and the nerve forces as to make the ganglia have that reaction.

This is a reaction rather than the cause, or the pains in those areas between the shoulders and to the head, and even to the 9th dorsal, arise from these disturbances as indicated.

Hence these corrections, and the equalization of the impulse through the use of the low form of [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as set up, will bring equalization.

Do these for this body, and we will make for a much bettered condition.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
