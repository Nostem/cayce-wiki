# TEXT OF READING 4471-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4471-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/31/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12335

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 31st day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4471].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. LS:  You will answer the following questions:  What causes the ringing in the ears?  Is the treatment the body takes correct?  Can this body be restored to its normal condition?

2. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in this body are improved from that when we had this before.  While we have conditions in the system that have become at times, and for a period of time, seemingly accentuated rather than assisted; that in the system producing the condition is being gradually corrected, and when the system adjusts itself, and the eliminations are set properly in the system by the alleviation of pressure caused in portions of system, the body will become in the normal forces.

3. The condition in the tonsils and the pressure on those centers that produce the disturbance to the sensory system and to the auditory nerve force, specifically, will, with the incentives and adjustments of eliminations, be overcome.

4. The condition in the teeth or antrum, and directed or the distress coming through these needs of local treatments.  The center or that producing the cause coming from that as given. In this only accentuating the condition by the condition left in circulation by non-elimination in the proper channels.

5. Do as we have given, consistently, persistently, and we will find we will bring the correct equilibrium for and to this body, [4471].

6. The condition in the ear or auditory forces should be treated by removing of the stress and strain as will show in the circulation, nerve center and nerve incentive by that as given.

7. Do that.
