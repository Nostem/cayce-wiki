# TEXT OF READING 3799-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3799-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1911 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/26/1911  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11373

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 26th day of July, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Alice Anderrsee, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 P. M.

1. LBC:  You have before you [3799] go over her thoroughly, examine her carefully, and tell us what is the matter with her, if anything.

2. EC:  Yes, we have her here.  We have in this body two conditions existing in the organs of the body, which may be classed as one, because they act under the same head, that is the liver and kidney.  The pain is more from the liver.  The action in the system is shown by the eliminating properties of the kidneys themselves.  The reverse forces, or the forces thrown out by that of the sympathetic system, through the body, shows accumulation in system, and in the lungs, along the lower part, at the connection of the diaphragm.  We have the circulation of the blood, thrown into contact with the digestion of the stomach itself, - produces a filling up, or accumulation, as shown here, instead of being carried through blood as it should be and carried through the system, - causes an excess of matter here.  The acid is allowed to remain in the system, not being eliminated through the liver. We have at times headaches, which is produced from the condition here of the liver and kidneys, sometimes from one, and at times from the other.  The action on the gastric juices of the stomach and of the secretive powers of the liver, act toward eliminating the pains from the nervous system, i.e., the headaches.

3. (Q)  What other conditions do you see?
(A)  We have an abnormal condition of the whole body.

4. (Q)  What treatment would you prescribe to relieve conditions?
(A)  That of manipulation of the muscular forces along the spine, that act in conjunction with these two organs, of the eliminating properties of both, and the eliminating properties of the liver that assist those of the secreting organs themselves.  Especially along the spine, in the region of the 8th and 9th dorsal, 2nd and 3rd lumbar, 2nd and 3rd cervical, and the whole muscular system.

Keep plenty of water in the system.  We haven't the nature of the forming of matter in the system through this condition of the organs, but, if allowed to stand, with this overtaxed condition of the liver and kidneys, we will have to depend on what is taken into the system to assist the blood itself.
