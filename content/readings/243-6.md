# TEXT OF READING 243-6 F 48 (Milliner, Protestant)

**Reading [[243-6]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/3/1927  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1090

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by Miss [243].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Morton Blumenthal|Morton Blumenthal]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [243] and Mr. [304].]

Time of Reading 
5:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ky.

1. MB:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [243], present in this room, and the business associations and relations of this body.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those associations and those relations as pertaining to business.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What is the best move for this body at the present time?
(A)  Keep that as is already set before the body as the correct means at the present.  There will be seen that in the change that will come about with the last portion of the present season, that VISTAS will open for the body that have not as yet even shown the possibilities of same.

4. (Q)  She should remain with the present position until these additional or new offers are made?
(A)  Should remain with the present position until these new conditions open up - which, as is seen, a portion has been expressed, as it were, in a direction - yet, as given, new conditions will arise which will open greater and better opportunities.  Although there will be some disappointments, some sadnesses, as will come in the life; yet we find through these channels MUCH will be gained by this body, if the body will keep in that path as it has set for itself to keep.  Do that.

5. (Q)  Are there any associations in a business way that might be advantageous for her in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]?
(A)  These - as is seen - will open for this body, and these will come about in the latter portion of the season as is coming on.  This [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should keep the eye open for. As has been given, "Watch as well as pray."

6. (Q)  Any other advice that the Father would give to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]?
(A)  Keep that way as is right in the sight of that one [deceased mother, [3776]] that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] gave much to, for in that is the greatest satisfaction, in body, mind, and spirit.

7. We are through.
