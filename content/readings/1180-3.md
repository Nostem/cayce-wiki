# TEXT OF READING 1180-3 M 32 (Oil Well Driller)

**Reading [[1180-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/26/1924  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 6593

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of August, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [1180].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.           Wichita Co., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will now have before you the lands in the forty-acre tract of the J. W. Robertson sub-division #5, lease known as Turner Sheagogg lease, now owned by the Port Chester Oil Company.  This land is located in Wichita County, [[Texas|Texas]].  You will give particular attention to the East twenty acre end of this tract.  Tell us if any production of oil would be found here, and where, giving the depth and amount of production. Also the twenty acres North of the West twenty acre end of this tract.  Would we find oil here?  What quantity of oil and where would it be found?  Would it be better to operate this twenty acre tract, or to accept the proposition now made on same?

2. EC:  Yes, we have these lands here and conditions relative to same.  We have had these lands before.

3. We find in the East end that production may be obtained, and that the quantity and quality would be satisfactory in many respects.  However, the proposition that is offered at the present time would be more satisfactory than operating one's self; that is, from the viewpoint and position of the Port Chester Oil Company at the present time.

4. The lands to the North of West end may be made to produce. This will be found at the four corners almost of the twenty acre tract.  In this we find the lay of the surface indicates the conditions.  This would be found at six hundred and fifty (650) feet, and the production in the North and East well or location would be the better one.  This would produce, we see, some ninety (90) barrels of oil per day.

5. In the other sections where locations might be made would be found in the South and East end of this tract, or that nearly as an offset to another well that has been drilled that proved unsatisfactory, but at the hundred and fifty-three (153) feet from this location, North by West, we would make the location; that is, in the South and East end of this tract, but the proposition offered by other parties to the Port Chester Oil Company would be satisfactory, and more so, under existing conditions, than the operation themselves.

6. We are through.
