# TEXT OF READING 2514-10 F 24

**Reading [[2514-10]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/4/1942  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9455

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of December, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.                     ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2514]; this we have had before.

3. While there is still much to be desired in improvements, we find that there are decided improvements in many ways.

4. Though there are periods when it appears that the disturbance is catching hold again, by the breaking of tissue, much has been accomplished.

5. We would continue the use of the Appliance, but we would keep this charged regularly; and keep connections in order.  Some of these need attention.  Clean the poles as well as the anodes, and the anodes especially, before and after using same.  Clean the poles thoroughly with the sandpaper or emery paper when recharges are made.

6. The oil packs we would keep occasionally, especially if there is the indication of incrustations.  Use the ointment on the areas indicated.  The heavy covering with this, over broken tissue, will be better.

7. Continue the oil rubs in the manner suggested.

8. These, and the appetites keep in the lines indicated.

9. The change that we find would be the most beneficial now would be the use at least once a week, in the beginning, of the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Light.  This should be the Mercury Quartz Light, and at least thirty-eight inches from the body, but inject a green light (green plate glass) between the Ultra Violet and the body - about fourteen inches from the body. This should be given principally over the back of the limbs and the knees, and the lower part of the torso - that is, across the hips, about the central portion.  In the beginning give this about a minute and a half, but have the light working, to be sure, at the time the body is put under same.

10. These as we find should prevent further incrustations, and aid the circulation in eliminating the disturbing or infectious forces themselves.

11. Keep the eliminations well.

12. Do that.

13. The stand lamp may be used, or what is called the bed lamp. But use the Mercury, not the Carbon Light.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Why can't I go to sleep, and when finally asleep why can't I stay asleep?
(A)  This is the anxiety in the sympathetic nervous system.  As indicated, use a mild sedative.  But do not use same without the [[Baking Soda|soda]].  And, as we find, the use of the electrical forces should materially aid in this disturbance.

16. (Q)  What causes these drawn muscles or whatever they are across the top of my legs?  On the days they are tight, it is all I can do to walk.
(A)  All of these things are taken into consideration, as to what is to be done for same.  The drawing is the infection in the superficial circulation, drawing the lower portion of the epidermis - or the lower skin.  And this is why the use of the light would be beneficial in the present, to allay.  Do not use it without the green light, see?

17. (Q)  Do my wisdom teeth cause any of my trouble?
(A)  To be sure, they are contributory to the general nervous upset.  If it is practical for the body to have these attended to, it would be well.

Do those things we have indicated here, and we should find continued improvement.

Keep the Ichthyol liniment or ointment, even when there is the use of the light.  This will not disturb, but drive in. It may produce itching, a little irritation after same, but the oil - especially the peanut oil - will aid in allaying, if this becomes aggravating to the body.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
