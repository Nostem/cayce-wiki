# TEXT OF READING 4673-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4673-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/6/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12615

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of July, 1926, in accordance with telegraphic request made by Mr. R. M. Taylor.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find that this system has become so uneven in its equilibrium as for conditions as regarding eliminations of used forces to become acute, and then these conditions affect various portions of the body in specific manners. However, not always the same portion of the bod, for at times we find the extremities in distress.  At others it affects the kidneys, and again the throat, and the sensory system. Hence the condition in the last spell (though the body is some better at the present time) affects, as we see, the digestive system.

3. Now, this condition affects the system through the blood and through the nerve system, for we find there are impingements in the 10th, 11th and 12th dorsal.  This in such a manner that in cold or congestion this becomes acute, affecting then directly that portion of the system that is in its cycle of functioning.  Hence the various conditions as become affected in the system.  The treatments for same have been of a varied nature.  Hence the condition has come to that station where the general diagnosis is of complications, when the seat of the trouble is rather the complications, and results are the natural effects of the existent condition, though much that has been accorded the body from time to time has brought on different conditions in the functioning of various portions of the body, as is seen in the kidneys, the liver, and the bronchials.  Especially has this affected the condition in the nasal cavity, where there exists a form of a catarrhal condition that, with the over amount of these impurities of toxins not eliminated, this becomes then affected in a toxic way and form.  Same occurs in the emunctories of the kidneys. Hence the distresses as are caused from time to time.

4. To rid or clear this system from these conditions, we would first have those conditions in the dorsal region corrected by the adjustments, either chiropractic or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] - preferably the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], for the kneading and relaxing and tauting both of different portions of the trunk portion of body needs this treatment and massage, that the adjusting will be more effective in the system.  At least twelve to thirteen (12 to 13) such adjustments and treatments should be given.  Every other day these should be given.

5. When this has been completed, then there should be applied to the body those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from that as known as the In-Mo-Ray treatments, or the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance.  Such will create the proper metabolism and the proper [[Vibration|vibration]] in system, that the whole may work as a unit in this vibration created by system in rebuilding.  Necessary that many more of the applications of the Radio-Active [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] than the In-Mo-Ray, for one works from the point of the equalizations - that is, the Radio-Active forces, while the other works from that of nerve and blood cleansing, yet both giving the same results in the end on this particular body.  Do that then, keeping the system in proper rebuilding through the diet, which should consist principally of fruits and vegetables, yet little of the very acid, as apples or lemons, yet any and all others would do good for the body.

6. We are through for the present.
