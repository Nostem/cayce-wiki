# TEXT OF READING 213-1 M ADULT (Leather Importer, Hebrew)

**Reading [[213-1]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/3/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1034

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 290 Riverside Drive, Apt. 15D, [[New York City|New York City]], this 3rd day of December, 1927, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [213].

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Bella B. Sidelman, Steno. Mr. [213]; Mrs. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].]

Time of Reading 
4:50 P. M.                                    [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Body & enquiring mind; questions all phases)

1. EC:  We have the body here and those conditions as surround same in the mental and financial as respecting business associations.  In the physical condition, [213], we find the body very good in many respects.  Rather would the body be warned concerning the developments of conditions that are existent which if not corrected would eventually bring about conditions hard to cope with.  These have particularly to do with the katabolism and metabolism of the system, or those forces as are being used and not sufficient rebuilding to replenish all those conditions, wherein the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as relate to the mental part being so overused that these conditions hinder the elimination and assimilation of the system.  These then leave those conditions in the blood, in the liver, in the kidneys and those forces in [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]' supply that make for harder reaction in the body.  Be not unmindful of these conditions, for these left may bring about that condition in either or any of these portions of body so acted upon by the functioning of these organs as for this to become a specific or an organic condition.  While this does not exist in the present, yet we find signs in the blood itself, in its [[Vibration|vibratory]] force, in the heart's action, in the kidney, in the forces of the ducts that supply the blood from assimilation in the system.

2. To meet the needs and to correct these conditions, we would use this as an active force for the body.  Use oxygen certine [Occy-Crystine?] as the addition to those properties of the drinking water and we will find this condition will bring as renewed activities to the whole functioning organism.  The percentage used, four ounces to two or three gallons of water, and use this as the drinking water altogether.  In that water in other places will not be taken but keep this as the water for the body.  Do that.

3. As respecting those of the mental forces of the body, these are good.  At times will appear those forces as related to self and those about self that bring rather consternation, rather the troublesome forces of not knowing just to what, to whom, to turn for advice, self feeling lost, as it were, in the force of action.  These are that condition, in the makeup of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], harking to those promptings of the call that is innate within entity's being.  Turn to that within self that makes self more secure to those [[Creative Forces|creative energies]] that may be made manifest through the spiritual life of the body.  For each entity is the reflection of that God [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] worships, and blessed is he whose God is the Lord of hosts.  [1 Sam. 1:11; Ps. 40:4]

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What about the future development of the business?
(A)  This in the natural consequences of forces as are and as have been set in motion from time to time shows the expansion to be coming even greater than had been hoped for from some directions, but at the present and for several, several seasons those conditions as have to do with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s business and those things surrounding same are particularly in the way of being 'the thing', as slang would say.  Hence these developments will be more and more.  Do not be afraid to keep that already laid out nor do not be afraid to expand even farther.  Well that much that may be obtained through the [[South America|South American]], Latin countries, also be considered as well as those of the Ukraine and Himalayas, for all of these will add to that already laid out.

6. (Q)  Will the body go to Europe?
(A)  Should.

7. (Q)  When should he go?
(A)  These as we find will come for the next season's layout; at the present time these would appear in the early spring.  These must be added by using own common sense and consideration.  The body has plenty of that.

8. (Q)  Should the body incorporate his business?
(A)  Such business, as may be expanded, well to ever be incorporated when considered from that standpoint.  This, however, should be a matter of the body's own interest.  For the developments are of the nature that the expansion in this line would tax the mental and financial status, yet ofttimes there is the necessity of considering long before such steps are taken for with the gathering together of those to conduct such undertakings as this under corporate conditions many new added troubles will come.  The body does not take trouble any too well.  These added may bring much more trouble for these expansions under this would reach far.  Do not let the expansion get beyond the control of the body's own activities.  Do not wade into deep water before the shore is seen.  Do not trust others that the body would not trust self in the same manner.  Hence, consider well before such corporate conditions, yet the expansions, the amount of business, the amount of accumulations from same may be tripled, may be fourfold, may be even sixtyfold in the next seven years.  [See 7/24/34 Reports.]

9. (Q)  Will the body find himself in a lawsuit?
(A)  The body may find himself in any way.  Best to find himself from within rather than in a law-suit.

10. (Q)  Has he an enemy through the business?
(A)  Who has accomplished anything without enemies?

11. (Q)  Will the body be benefited by his trip to Europe?
(A)  With applying those things as given would be beneficial.  Those uneasinesses, those little conditions wherein the dizziness and the harrowing effects come from same are the effects of that unstabled condition as has been given.

12. We are through.
