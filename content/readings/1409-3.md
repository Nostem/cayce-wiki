# TEXT OF READING 1409-3 F 60

**Reading [[1409-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/23/1937  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 7148

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1937, in accordance with request made by the family, through Miss [307], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 to 10:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, the heart's activity, the circulation - while the heart's activity is rapid, the circulation is poor, the temperature continues to come and go.

3. We will find that the better conditions will be to keep just as quiet as possible, using the stimulations now and then of the burned whiskey (Rye Whiskey).  Burn about a tablespoonful in a saucer, and when the light goes out and it is cooled sufficient, drink just about this quantity in small sips - two to three times a day or whenever the body arouses sufficiently.

4. If there is the continuation of the ability for the circulation to hold out, it will be necessary to produce some eliminations by enemas; these cool, not cold but cool.

5. In the diets - just a little of the Beef Juices - these are the more strengthening.

6. Aspirin would tend to make for some sweating, provided the body is kept in such a manner as to not have more of the rigors from perspiration.  Five to ten grains - not oftener than two hours, however.

7. And sponging off with a little [[Baking Soda|soda]] water will be the better.

8. Of course, the alcohol rubs - not rubbed, but the grain alcohol applied across the throat, the head, down the spine - will give some stimulation as well as retard the temperature.

9. Do these.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Should the fruit juices be continued?
(A)  When they can.

Keep up the spiritual guidance for all.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [307] - Special Delivery - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
