# TEXT OF READING 5671-12 F 34

**Reading [[5671-12]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/24/1930  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14094

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker, nurse, Mrs. [5671], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                         [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Much better are the physical conditions as are being manifested through this body, and the body has even made up in its mind now in this direction.  Now KEEP it in that way and manner!  and when these are ESTABLISHED - that is, the mental activities of the body WITH the eliminations in the system set up, we will find the body will be NEARER normal than it has been in many - many - many - moons.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Cannot get pure Castile.  Where else could it be obtained, or should substitute be used?  If so, what?
(A)  Pure oil of butterfat may be substituted for same. Greasiest grease there is!

3. (Q)  Should all former treatments be continued?
(A)  When the substitute is used, CARE should be taken, of course, in the compounding of same - and it will be found necessary that some cocoa butter be used with same, about a third.  This will produce the better activities through the rest of the treatments - that is, WITHOUT those of the actions as cause irritation through the rectal muscles.  The REST of them should be used.  Not TOO much physical activity, but sufficient to keep the NORMALCY.  In three to four weeks we should find mighty near a normal condition, will these activities be adhered to - and DON'T worry, because other things will be alright.

4. (Q)  How long should battery be used at each time?
(A)  About forty to sixty minutes.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] Two extra copies to self
