# TEXT OF READING 3653-1 F 28 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[3653-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/7/1944  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 11155

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3653], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Born June 18, 1915, in Penrod, [[Kentucky|Kentucky]]. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  What an unusual record - and one of those who might be termed as physically the mothers of the world!   For [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity was]] one of those in [[Mount Ararat|the ark]].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, we find that there is much from which to choose.  But these we choose with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] to be the means of help to the entity in better fulfilling those [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it has entered the earth's plane in varied periods of its experience.

4. For the entity has appeared when there were new revelations to be given.  And again it appears when there are new revelations to be made.

5. May the entity so conduct its mind, its body and its [[Purpose|purposes]], then, as to be a channel through which such messages may come that are needed for the awakenings in the minds of men as to the necessity for returning to the search for their relationship with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or God.

6. For as has been given from the beginning, [[Flood (Noachian)|the deluge]] was not a myth (as many would have you believe) but a period when man had so belittled himself with the cares of the world, with the deceitfulness of his own knowledge and power, as to require that there be a return to his dependence wholly - physically and mentally - upon the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

7. Will this entity see such again occur in the earth?  Will it be among those who may be given those directions as to how, where, the elect may be preserved for the replenishing again of the earth?

8. Remember, not by water - for it is the mother of life in the earth - but rather by the elements, fire.

9. Then, in giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, these we choose; minimizing the faults and magnifying the virtues of this entity.  May the entity also ever use such policies in its dealings with others.  For with what judgment ye mete to others it is measured to thee again.  In the manner, then, ye treat thy fellow man, ye are treating thy neighbor.

10. In giving the urges latent and manifested, here is an entity wherein we find the urges from the moon, the earth and the solar signs.  Thus the entity is controlled by the emotions of self as well as the activities in others.

11. As to whether or not the entity will be among those in the earth when the changes again come, will depend upon the entity's preparation of self.  Remember as to how it was stated in regard to those chosen for such channels, in what to many was the beginning of man's advent into the earth: "They were perfect in their day and generation."  As individual entities they had chosen virtue in every form in relationship to things, conditions and personalities in the earth.

12. In the entity's temperament from the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, we find Mars, Mercury, Venus and Jupiter indicating the manner in which the entity's activities are to be in the present.

13. Then, abilities exist.  And it is up to the entity to begin with the first things first - spirituality, mental preparation, physical application.  For as indicated, much must come from the experience if there would be the living with self, as it were, in this [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; else we will find the entity becoming so disturbed as to reach that condition or phase of consciousness best expressed in "Well, I don't care."

14. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, not all will be given; yet these indicate a portion of the entity's abilities.  We would here for this entity reverse, as it were, the order and begin with the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the first and show the variations according to the manner in which the individual has applied opportunities in conformance to its [[Ideal|ideals]] in relationship to spiritual things.  And this means spirituality, not spiritualism!

15. In the first we find that the entity appeared in the land called [[Lemuria|Mu]].  There the entity was among those who gave expression to the activities pertaining to the consideration of others as equals, as to individual rights also.  Thus there was an unfoldment in mental and spiritual things.

16. Then the entity gained, in the name Oru.

17. Next we find that the entity entered in those periods when the littleness of man had come up as a stench before [[Throne of God|the throne]] of [[Grace|mercy and grace]], or the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

18. The entity was among the peoples not of the lineage of [[Mount Ararat|Noah]], but of those later known as the Hittites.  Then the entity gave expressions to that same experience that was materially manifested in [[Lemuria|Mu]], and the entity - as the wife of a son of [[Mount Ararat|Noah]] - became among the eight [[Soul|souls]] in the ark, in the preparation and in the endurance thereof, and in those experiences gained in the activities through the earth's influence.

19. The name then was Maran.  From that [[Incarnation|sojourn]] there is much experienced in the present in the entity's liking of the history of the early ages of man, of those experiences that are the promptings for the [[Soul|souls]] and minds of individuals, rather than the material things.

20. At times the entity is berated by others for being rather in that mind of "Don't care" than in that of a practical, everyday life.

21. Hence the entity is a "sensitive."  Read the Scripture pertaining to the days of Noah, [[Law of Moses|the law]] proclaimed by Enoch (found mostly in Jude and in the early chapters of [[Genesis|Genesis]]). Here is one entity who may write, not automatic but rather inspirational writing, shutting itself away and attuning itself by very distant music, and especially bells.  For, under such an inspiration - not as a means of doing other than attuning the inner self - the entity may write inspirationally of things pertaining to mental and spiritual aspects of individuals grasping for attunement to the divine.

22. After that the entity appeared in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] during those periods of the Judges in Israel, and in that period of what is oft spoken of as Jepthah's rash vow.

23. The entity was a close friend of Jepthah's daughter offered as a human sacrifice to that overzealousness of an individual entity, who used his powers for material gains and strength and yet accorded those judgments of truth; causing this entity, Judyth, to become disturbed, distraught, and to lose much of that which had been the impelling force.

24. Hence the making of rash vows brings individual activities that yet disturb the entity, as to why individuals vow this or that and then, because they change, the entity can never exactly connect them with truth or sincerity.  These bring disturbances for the entity.  Yet the entity itself gained.

25. Following that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] when there were those changes wrought by the entity called the [[Prophets|prophet]] Mohammed.

26. The entity then was among those individuals who were caught, as it were, in circumstance, and the entity in its activity, in that portion of the land, again brought a balance in the manners of presentation.

27. The name then was Shusan.

28. Following that the entity was in the land of the present nativity, during those periods when there was the warring of one against the other, when brother was pitted against brother.

29. The entity was among those of its present land of nativity who were associated with those who were considered as rebellious against the government.

30. For the entity was among those peoples of the land of its present nativity who were associated with those who were rebels, and the entity itself was rather a rebel in its purpose as to the needs for servants for labors.

31. The name then was Jane Polk.

32. As to the abilities of the entity in the present:

33. The periods of change that have been wrought in the earth are, as it were, a part of the entity's experience.  And in applying self to truth, to light, to justice as may be found in those tenets and truths of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], the entity may seek to glorify that purpose, that ideal.

34. As a writer the entity may succeed.  As a teacher it may become well known.  As a student the entity may go far, in still supplying to those who seek, a way, a means of expression.

35. Ready for questions.

36. (Q)  Can my song become a success if published?
(A)  It will have to be altered as to there becoming a purpose for the song, and not an emotion of a group or individual.

37. (Q)  Who would be the best one to publish it?
(A)  This depends upon the change.  But this may go through regular channels.  This would necessitate [[Ideal|ideals]] being set for it, and first that it be set to a type of music that would be in keeping with the purpose set for the song.

It would be well, as we find, to have this considered by one who sets songs to music, or who prepares them as in the Hour of Charm.

38. (Q)  Should I write other songs?
(A)  These are well, but you may write things greater than songs.  [[Psalms|Psalms]] and songs, yes - sayings and songs would be well.

39. (Q)  How can I best help my family financially?
(A)  This will depend upon what the needs are.  There is much more important help to the family than financial.  If this is put first and foremost, ye may lose the way.  If [[Ideal|the ideal]] is set first, the finances will follow.

40. (Q)  Any further advice to help my present needs?
(A)  Begin at the beginning.  Learn the way of love.  "For the earth is the Lord's, and the fulness thereof."

41. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
