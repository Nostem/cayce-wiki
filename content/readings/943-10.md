# TEXT OF READING 943-10 M 37 (Promoter, Protestant)

**Reading [[943-10]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/16/1929  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 5810

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of June, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [943].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor and Steno (GD away on visit to Ala.). Mr. [943], Dr. H. A. Moore and HLC (?).]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [943], present in this room who has available five thousand dollars for oil drilling [[Purpose|purposes]].  He desires information as to best shallow oil field to spend this money - giving exact location to drill in one of the following fields:  [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], Kansas, [[Texas|Texas]] or Mexico.  Profits to be used to develop [[Florida|Florida]] oil leases.

2. Yes we have the body and enquiring mind of [943], this we have had before.  Now regarding the conditions, each named territory has its advantage and disadvantage - we would give, however, all things considered the [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] field in that district about Irvine, [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].  When leases are made more specific locations may be given.  This will be the better grade and the easier to turn into ready capital on account of the Penn. grading of production.  Then get in touch with C. S. Ringle and use that rig and experience.  He again enters the [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] here.

3. (Q)  What depth will the production be found?
(A)  From 380' to 520'.

4. (Q)  What amount of production will be obtained here?
(A)  From 50 to 300 barrels per day.

5. (Q)  What will be the cost of drilling each well?
(A)  $900.00 to $1800.00.

6. (Q)  Where will C. S. Ringle be found?
(A)  Irvine, Kentucky or Willard, [[Ohio|Ohio]].

> were definite indications of gas but that it was impossible
