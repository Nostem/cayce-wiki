# TEXT OF READING 900-165 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-165]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/27/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5368

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. HLC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind, of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding any of these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900]. This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] as we see come more and more closer to that co-relation of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] in the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and as the spirit forces manifest more nominally, the understanding to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] comes clearer and clearer.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Night of Nov. 25, 1925, while reading "Religious Experiences" by William James.  The Voice:  "Just a minute - 41."  It seemed to refer to Pan Pet B Stock."
(A)  In this we find, as that being read gives the experiences in the manner of the spiritual insight to the manifestation of force as seen in phenomenized form in the material world, this then as the spirit or force in numbers, and as is seen and felt by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as referring to a specific stock, the action in same being in the spirit of the five (5), which means then an advance, or breaking of that many points; as we see it here at present, meaning the falling away to the 68 - 67, see?

4. (Q)  Does 41 refer to stock price, or is it the numerical system referring to Pan Pet B?
(A)  As given.  Numerical system, and the action of same as given, see?

5. (Q)  I am still short 200 shares of the stock.
(A)  (Interrupting)  Then when it reaches 68, sell it, see?

6. (Q)  "My name, [900], was called, and 'Don't!'  I missed the rest."
(A)  This, as we see, more and more of that co-relation of [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] experiences being manifest to the physical consciousness, or the consciousness of the entity, [900], expanding to that extent wherein, in this condition as the as the entity attains when the Voice is heard, the consciousness of [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] becoming as a consciousness of physical being.  As is seen here, "Don't let those truths as being obtained give the entity the undue balance in the control of self, physically, mentally, morally or financially."  Keeping, then, the even [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Purpose|purpose]] in each and every manner, as has been outlined for this entity, for as is seen, and and as is understood by the entity in the expanded study of phenomenized forces in the physical plane, these same forces show how that the One Force is that manifested, and the use to which same is applied in man becomes the characteristic, or the good or evil to which same attains, see?

7. (Q)  Morning of Nov. 26, 1925.  "Atlantic Gulf & West Indies. I saw the stock price and I also saw the outline of the fate of the fate of price, and then the Voice:  'The price of this stock is the Spirit, and the price movement is the Spirit Action.'"
(A)  In this we see further the exemplifications of those truths as are being given, and as have just been given to this entity, and as the entity sees in this, this action of this particular stock, that in which this stock has followed the general trend of the market in the last few days, yea weeks, the general trend of which will, as we see under the existent conditions as will affect this in the near future, find the advance from the present of 57 to 80 - 90 - dollar, see?  for this we will find will be a high or a active stock in the next few weeks, advancing then near the 15th of December to a high point, see?  for as the spirit of same is seen and understood by entity, the action of seeing same in motion will in part, as is seen and understood by entity, be a portion of the work of same, of [900], as to reports, as to conditions, as those things as pertain to the action of same, see?

8. (Q)  In reference to the first, on Nov. 25, when will Pan Pet B Stock reach the mark as given?
(A)  Tuesday - 30th - oneth - see?  first day of December. [Tuesday, December 1, 1925?]  We are through with this reading.

[HLC then gave EC the suggestion for an oil well rdg. in [4905] series.]
