# TEXT OF READING 3352-1 M 45 (Banker, Married, No Children)

**Reading [[3352-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/5/1943  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 10786

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3352], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [3299].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3352].  (He's keeping the time pretty well.)

3. Now as we find, much might be said regarding the conditions existent here.  To be sure, these are after effects of indiscretions of a nature in the body-[[Physical Body|physical forces]], and then those applications to produce a better circulation and a better elimination.  Though now working under quite abnormal conditions.  These are in the present almost normal for this [[Physical Body|physical body]] of [3352].

4. The sources or causes of these have not been entirely eradicated.  Thus there has not been gained full control of the body-forces through the body.

5. We find that these arose as a result of what might be called occupational disturbances; not enough in the sun, not enough of hard work.  Plenty of brain work, but the body is supposed to coordinate the spiritual, mental and physical.  He who does not give recreation a place in his life, and the proper tone to each phase, - well, he just fools self and will some day - as in this body in the present - be paying the price. There must be a certain amount of recreation.  There must be certain amounts of rest.  These are physical, mental and spiritual necessities.  Didn't God make man to sleep at least a third of his life?  Then consider!  This is what [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] meant when He said, "Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow."  Do they grow all the while, bloom all the while, or look mighty messy and dirty at times?  It is well for people, individuals, as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], to get their hands dirty in the dirt at times, and not be the white-collared man all the while!  These are natural sources.  From whence was man made?  Don't be afraid to get a little dirt on you once in a while.  You know you must eat a certain amount of dirt, else you'll never get well balanced.  For this is that from which all conditions arise.  For of dust man is made, and to dust he returns.  Because he doesn't look dirty once in a while is no sign he isn't dirty in mind, if not in body, if not in spirit.  For these are one, ever one.

6. Here we have had conditions where there is the neglect of eliminations.  Neglect in taking sufficient exercise in the open.  Then we had a plethoric condition existing in a portion of the colon and a prolapsus in other portions, so as to overtax and burden the heart and the area between the heart, the liver, lungs and kidneys.  These have brought about disorders and disturbances.

7. To meet the needs of the conditions for this body:

8. Systematically, take at regular intervals the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments including [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, and [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments especially as related to a condition existing in the 3rd and 4th dorsal 5th and 6th dorsal and in the 3rd cervical.

9. Take these regularly for about six weeks, and then two or three months take another series for a week or two weeks, and we will find a great deal of difference.

10. But take time to add something to your mind mentally and spiritually.  And take time to play a while with others. There are children growing.  Have you added anything constructive to any child's life?  You'll not be in heaven if you're not leaning on the arm of someone you have helped. You have little hope of getting there unless you do help someone else.

11. Do that and live a normal life, and you'll live a heap longer.  Be worth a heap more than the position you occupy. For it is not what you do but what you really are that counts.  This shines through - what you really are - much more than what you say.  You can say No, or you can say Yes, but do you ask God to show partiality?   Do You show partiality to others?

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> playing volley ball in gymnasiums in the winter.  Every year
> he goes up into the wilds of northern Minnesota fishing and
> portaging canoes and rowing and living in the open.  This
> past summer he had a large victory garden and grubbed and
> hoed and weeded in it in all his spare time.  This does not
> sound like a man who is afraid of getting dirty or who does
> not have any recreation.
> 
> Reading:  Here we have conditions where there is neglect of
> elimination...systematically take at regular intervals the
> [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments including [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations and and
> [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.
> 
> Answer:  Mr. [3352] has progressive arthritis of the spine.
> He has been to the Mayo clinic, the Billings clinic and in
> the hands of numerous specialists in this field without
> getting any relief.  That is why he was anxious to know what
> Mr. Cayce might reveal.  He has taken all kinds of therapy
> treatments, [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations and [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments
> for years.  However, [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments would be useless
> now because the vertebra have disintegrated and grown
> together in a solid mass.
> 
> Reading:  Take time to add something to your mind mentally
> and spiritually.  Take time to play a while with others.
> There are children growing.  Have you added anything
> constructive to any child's life.
> 
> Answer:  Mr. [3352] is quite a student, always reading and
> studying things of a economical and social nature and much
> interested in the betterment of governments and the world in
> general.  He is deeply involved in every civic affair of our
> village.  He has headed the Welfare Board for years, the Baby
> Clinic, the Library Board, the Community Chest, the Service
> Men's Committee of the Church Federation.  He has been
> instrumental in most of the good work done for the youth of
> our village.  He is a hard worker, and conscientious and it
> is because of his willingness to serve that every time there
> is civic work to be done it's a case of "Let [3352] do it"
> and he does, giving of himself until he is physically worn
> out.
> 
> He has no children of his own but for years he has desired to
> adopt two children.  He wanted to take a boy and a girl, not
> cute babies, but half grown youngsters who have been knocked
> around.  He wanted to watch their young minds unfold and to
> be instrumental in directing them in the right direction.
> This never came about because his wife is a nervous
> temperamental type and wouldn't shoulder the burden.
> 
> That is all there was in this reading.  We thought the
> reading would be for ONE HOUR, as I presumed from previous
> arrangements made and the literature on [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] read,
> but this reading covered but 15 MINUTES.
> 
> Although a list of questions was asked for and sent to you,
> most of them were not asked or answered.  I asked about his
> steady loss of weight; his deafness, his lack of a sense of
> smell.  These questions were not answered.  I asked that Mr.
> [3352] and the room he was in during the reading be
> described, as I understand Mr. Cayce has done in other
> readings.  I was particularly anxious to have this
> information and wrote Mr. Cayce specially about it, as it
> would naturally remove any trace of skepticism on Mr.
> [3352]'s part if this had been revealed.  It was not.
> 
> I had expected that without advising you beforehand, Mr.
> Cayce would know that Mr. [3352] has arthritis of the spine,
> and how badly, but there was no mention of arthritis in the
> reading save for a brief reference to certain "dorsals".
> 
> The whole reading summed up does not seem to fit Mr. [3352]
> either physically or in character.
> 
> Unfortunately (from reading [[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]] and other
> literature since received from [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]]) I told Mr.
> [3352] that Mr. Cayce would go over his body from head to
> foot and give him a complete analysis of his entire physical
> being.  I was embarrassed to give Mr. [3352] the reading when
> it came and revealed so little.
> 
> Sincerely yours, [3299]

> Dear Mr. Cayce,

> It is a real pleasure to have an excuse to write to you
> again.  I have wanted to write ever since I received your

> any bad connections when the day of the reading comes.
> 
> There are two things I want to talk about and I hope I am not
> taking up too much of your valuable time - I realize how busy
> you are these days.
> 
> The man I work for, the President of our bank [3352], is
> quite a student.  A mental type you might say and a fine
> person.  However, he is in very poor physical condition with
> a trouble that numerous doctors have been unable to help.
> Lately he has been unaccountably losing weight which is an
> added worry.  While he has not read [[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]], he has
> read the literature you sent me - Life and Work of Edgar
> Cayce etc. and has been very much impressed.  This morning,
> feeling quite miserable with his old trouble, he asked me if
> I thought you would give him a physical reading.  I told him
> I was afraid it would be some months before you could get to
> him and he said "if I keep on losing weight as fast as I have
> been I probably won't be here by the time the reading comes".
> However, he wrote out his check and I have filled out his
> application and we both will appreciate it if you will give
> him an appointment as soon as you can.  I have a feeling in
> my heart that you will find the cause of his trouble and what
> a wonderful thing that will be.  In sending him his
> appointment date, please write your letter to me.  Your
> letters will mean much more to me than to him and I will keep
> track of the date for him and see that he is in the right
> place at the right time and in the right frame of mind.
>

>

>

> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

> but I am listing all of the questions we hope to have

> you can manage this if you are booked up, unless there will
> be cancellations and such that will give you an open hour.  I
> shall certainly appreciate it if it happens that way as my
> conscience won't bother me so much for not giving him my
> time.  That first question asking you to describe him and the
> room he is in, is an important one to me.  He has not read
> [[Thomas Sugrue|Sugrue]]'s book.  He only knows of you what I have told him.
> And while he is a student and reads and thinks a lot about
> God and life and its meanings, as we all do, still he is a
> man of the world - a banker, and so a correct answer to this
> question would wash away any skepticism he may possibly feel.
> 
> Many of these questions will probably already be answered
> during the reading but I am listing all of the questions we
> have in mind just in case some of them are not covered in the
> reading:

> 1. Please describe Mr. [3352] and the pace in which you find
> him.
> 2. Given an analysis of his existing physical condition and
> diagnosis of his ailments.
> 3. What was the original cause of this condition?
> 4. Is the cause still present in the body and is it still the
> source of his trouble?
> 5. If so, can you suggest a treatment to remove it.
> 6. What are his future prospects with the disease?  Has it
> run its course and will not get any worse, or is he likely to
> have further acute attacks?
> 7. The disease has already left its mark which cannot be
> eradicated, but is there any treatment, or way of living,
> that would improve his condition and make him more
> comfortable and less likely to have another acute attack?
> 8. What is the cause of his recent steady loss of weight?
> 9. Will you please examine his ears and tell what is causing
> deafness and if there is anything he can do to improve his
> hearing.
> 10. Mr. [3352] says he has never had a sense of smell - what
> causes this and can any treatment be suggested?
