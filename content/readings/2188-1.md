# TEXT OF READING 2188-1 F 34

**Reading [[2188-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/7/1940  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 8896

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2188], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mrs. [623].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

[[Florida|Miami]] Valley Hosp., 
Time of Reading                          Magnolia Street, 
3:45 to 3:50 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. Old Bldg, 2nd floor, 
Ward C, Room 217, 
Bed 4, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions have gradually grown more serious. Of course, there cannot be interference with that being administered, or that will be the outcome as to the decision of those in charge of this condition.

4. But as we find, the nausea is being produced from lacerations in the stomach itself, - from superacidity, which has arisen from the combination of copper and other poisoning, as from aluminum.

5. The applications we would suggest would depend upon what is the general decision of those now in charge.

6. Consequently, it would be better to wait until the body is released from the hospital; that there may be given then - through this channel - the helpful suggestions for administrations.

7. Of course, the rest - and the washing of the stomach - will be helpful.

8. In the present, then, we would not interfere.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Was this poisoning caused from foods cooked in aluminum or copper utensils?
(A)  It was caused from pieces of copper being accidentally cooked in aluminum vessels with food.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [623], Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file

Wired also:  Information Air Special Advises Waiting Until Released From Hospital.
