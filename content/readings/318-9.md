# TEXT OF READING 318-9 M 17

**Reading [[318-9]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/1/1943  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 2414

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [318], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Viola Bech.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What has been causing the pains in the upper right hand side of my chest?
(A)  The natural consequence of the change in the developing body, as combined with excess in the amount of fats taken when these have not been assimilated, or have not been working well with that already assimilated in the circulation through the lungs.

Nothing to be alarmed about, unless it should be through contraction of cold or flu or something of the kind, that would attack the lung area at the time.  Just beware of taking too much fats.

And do take six drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin in half a glass of water each day after the heavier meal of the day, whether it is breakfast, lunch or dinner.  Take it only after the heavier meal, not after all of them.

5. (Q)  Have I ever broken a rib on the right side of my chest?
(A)  Not in this experience.

6. (Q)  What causes my eyesight to go blank when I read or look down for a long time?
(A)  Lack of vital energy in the body.  Lack of the proper assimilation of the foods for replenishing the vital energies to the body, as indicated by the lack of certain vitamins in the body - as will be given, or as we will suggest to be given.

7. (Q)  Do I need glasses?  and would I have to wear them all the time?
(A)  Not necessarily, if there are the corrections of the general physical conditions.

8. (Q)  What kind of treatment could I take to cure my face of pimples and blackheads?
(A)  The keeping up or the creating of a better elimination.

For this body, taken twice each day the combination of vitamins found in Yellow Label Yeast.  [Fleischmann's Yellow Label Yeast]  Stir this in milk and take of morning.  In the evening take it stirred in tomato juice.  Dissolve it first, of course, or break it up with a fork and then add a little of the juice - the milk or tomato juice, and then fill the glass - but not too big a glass; that is, not a full tumbler.

9. (Q)  What causes little brown skin blotches on my chest and abdomen?
(A)  Poor circulation.

Do the things given.

10. (Q)  How can I gain weight?
(A)  Do the things outlined.

11. (Q)  In what condition is my blood pressure?
(A)  Near normal.

12. (Q)  Do I have any kind of trouble or defect that might cause me to be deferred from the army?  How can I correct it?
(A)  Depends upon what is the application for a general service or for some particular branch.  No defect that cannot be corrected by building up the health in the manner indicated; using after the heaviest meal each day the six drops of Pepsin, and twice each day the Yeast.  Do this not for just one day and skip the next day, but do it EACH day for at least thirty days - and then you'll commence filling out!

13. (Q)  Is there any special diet I should follow?
(A)  Eat plenty of fish and chicken, and when you eat it chew the bony pieces of the chicken - especially the neck and feet.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
