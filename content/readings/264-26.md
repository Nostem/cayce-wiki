# TEXT OF READING 264-26 F 42

**Reading [[264-26]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/19/1932  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 1557

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 19th day of November, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, H. L. and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [264] - this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, the conditions are progressing satisfactorily.  There is the greater desire on the part of the body mentally and physically to meet the situations as they arise.  There is greater ability in the physical to meet the conditions as they progress.

3. There needs be kept not an over-stimulation, but sufficient stimulation that there be the continuation of eliminations at the present, with the strength physically to meet these conditions without there being too great a strain on the vitality causing the reactions to the organs that are naturally under stress with the lessening of the pulsations by the strain upon the system in elimination.

4. We would keep those activities in the body that have been outlined, the use of those instruments for the clearing and clarifying of the system of the drosses that have broken loose in system; not by a strain, but by the natural flow from such an activity.

5. Keep those percussions, those movements, those minor adjustments, stimulating those organs that assist in the purging of and cleansing of the organs through this period that there may be the less of those forces to be absorbed by the system and eliminated through other channels, or finding lodgement in some weakened portion cause distresses there.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  In what part of body is growth located at present?
(A)  In the vagina.

8. (Q)  Will it be dissolved or absorbed so that it can pass normally from the body?
(A)  It will be dissolved sufficient that it may pass.  We would NOT have it absorbed wholly, for this would produce those conditions for the body that have just been given; hence the necessity of the injection of those instruments that will aid in the flow.

9. (Q)  What kind of instruments should be used?
(A)  Those that dilate the vagina sufficiently that this may pass.

10. (Q)  Will it require a medical doctor, or can Dr. Brenner [Edw. P. Brenner, D.C.] do it?
(A)  With the proper instruments used that have been indicated, we find he may do it; though, for his OWN precautionary measures it would be well to have the ASSISTANCE of medical man, or one of that school.

11. (Q)  Who would you suggest?
(A)  One in sympathy with the work the man is doing!  Not select one that would ready to find fault with what he is doing, to be sure!

12. (Q)  Will this pass in the next few days?
(A)  It should.

13. (Q)  How soon should they use the instruments?
(A)  They should have already been used!

14. (Q)  Give condition of the growth at present, and the uterus.
(A)  This is in that state of EMPTYING itself.  Stimulation to produce coagulation is to hinder it from passing in its nominal way.  That portion which has broken loose MUST be taken or eliminated from the system, else it would become such that must either produce infection or start other irritations that would be detrimental to the system; hence the use of that to keep the eliminations going for the present, WITHOUT producing irritation.

In the uterus there are those tendencies for the EMPTYING and the breaking loose of the growth, by the activity of the system RIDDING same from system - see?  The more that is taken or relieved at present, the less must be the absorption or diffusing of same throughout the system.

15. (Q)  Is pessary inserted correctly?
(A)  Very good at present; needs some dilation.

16. (Q)  Should any changes be made in any of the treatments?
(A)  None other than keeping the proper stimulation for the heart's action, as with wines, champagnes, gin or the like - this with sugar, cinnamon and the like, that makes for a stimuli to the organs of the pelvis that produce the eliminations.  Stimulate by the treatments and percussions those centers that assist in producing greater drainages for the body - see?

17. (Q)  How often should douche be taken?
(A)  Whenever necessary for the removal of those portions that are being thrown off; so that the body is kept cleansed from any possible condition of infection.

18. (Q)  What measures should be taken to aid in COMPLETE elimination of growth?
(A)  We have just been giving this over and over, and over and over.  Look at the condition in a sensible sense, as to what is attempting to be accomplished and what is taking place at the time in the body!  Then work together, mentally, physically, materially, for the carrying on of this in a manner as to bring the normal conditions for the body.  Do not lose this opportunity at this time.

19. (Q)  Any other or special advice for the body?
(A)  Patience, persistence, consistence; these in their definite terms for all conditions, physically, mentally and spiritually.

20. We are through with this reading.
