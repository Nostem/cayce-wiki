# TEXT OF READING 2457-5 F 40

**Reading [[2457-5]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1932  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 9324

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 8th day of January, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2457], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., and an Official Representative.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  While the conditions as exist in the present are giving a great deal of anxiety, will there be the proper precautions and care taken, these may come about - that when necessary, and the period for the eliminations and ejections that come by the natural means in such conditions, this should NOT be so severe.

2. In ALL, better that the whole physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]] keep attuned with that as is known in self that makes for the more perfect knowledge of the SPIRITUAL life.  Keep in close touch with OTHERS, for safety's sake, at all times.  Keep off of feet so much, else the swelling of the lower limbs - and the weakness in the abdominal regions - will make for pressures in the kidneys and bladder, as to cause too much irritation.

3. Be mindful that the eliminations through alimentary canal are kept at least a bit above normal, that there are not too many irritations in the system for the producing of acid, or for those of the producing of too great an activity upon the kidneys.

4. Well that there be the increase in amount of calcium and of BLOOD and nerve BUILDING tissue, but DO NOT INCLUDE those that are of the bulbous nature - for they will only tend to make for an increase in the tendency for plethora in the pelvic regions.

5. We would keep those low [[Vibration|vibrations]] of electrically driven motors, or vibrators, for the easing of those pains in back and lower limbs.

6. Keep the ash, as given; when there are those distresses or the nausea - these may be allayed best with small quantities of lime water, cinnamon water and bromide of potash - NOT [[Iodine|iodide]] in this, for this body.

7. In those conditions when there is dizziness, or a tendency for the head to ROTATE - from the pressures - well that ice be put at the base of the brain, and that the feet be put in hot water - see?

8. (Q)  What is the cause of this condition at present?
(A)  The natural, as has been given, of conditions in such natures of conditions.  Do these, and we will find that in the proper time, proper periods, the deliveries will be made, and the necessity for those removals of the larger portions of those plethora conditions and formations will come with same - or be taken care of at the same time.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
