# TEXT OF READING 470-24 M 49

**Reading [[470-24]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/28/1939  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 3451

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through wife, Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Michigan.

1. GC: You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body. [470].

3. Those inclinations for that tightness between the heart's activity and the liver are from the lack of the better or proper eliminations.

4. The poisons that accumulate in the system tend to make for the pressures upon the nerve system, especially in the brachial plexus.

5. Hence through the shoulder and the shortness of breath, - these make for that little dizziness and uneasiness that comes to the body.

6. As we find, it would be well to have about two high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations within thirty days; about fifteen days apart, you see; until there is sufficient of that tendency removed that causes - though not a great deal, but there is the tendency for mucous in portion of the colon as well as some disturbance in the area about the caecum that tends to produce this disturbance upon the system as indicated.

7. We would take another round, - that is, for at least a month, - of the occasional use of the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]]; to keep a better attunement or tone between the upper and lower hepatic circulation.  For the inflammatory forces as indicated will tend to become disturbing to the activity of the pancreas, spleen, and portions of the liver.

8. And occasionally we would have the full, deep manipulations. It is not necessary that these be given three to four to five times straight along, but rather an occasional one, - once a week or once in ten days, or farther apart, depending upon the reactions.  These given following the Turkish bath would be MOST helpful.

9. Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
