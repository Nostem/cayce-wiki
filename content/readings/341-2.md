# TEXT OF READING 341-2 M 16

**Reading [[341-2]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/26/1924  Sex: M  Age: 16  ReadingID: 2641

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [341].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [341].]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M. Central Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, the conditions throughout the whole system are better in many respects than when we had them here before. [Copy of an earlier health rdg. is not in E.C.F. files.]

2. At the present time, we find there are abnormal conditions that may be corrected in the physical.  They have to do with the nerve system, and the elimination and assimilation as affecting portions in the body as directed or following the branches of the nerve system.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body at the present time, [341], we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this very good.  There are conditions in the muscles and tissue that have become strained by usage that calls for more supply of vitim [vitamin?] and vitality than the system supplies at once, yet the condition of the blood supply is that with the usage becoming more localized, and by the effect being produced in the system, this will prove of benefit to portions of the body, yet may prove detrimental unless other conditions are kept in accord with same.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find there are centers and vertebrae that should be adjusted, else the strain will be on those portions of the system that have to do with the forces of the sensory system, especially those of the eyes.  The taxation to muscular forces will become detrimental to this portion of the body.

6. To reach the seat of the trouble, remove first the cause. This, we find is the impinionment [impingement?] of the nerve branch in the second cervical and third dorsal.

7. To give the supplying nerve forces that supply to blood force, both in nutriment and in eliminating used forces, give first the release to these nerve centers, through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] or [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] adjustments, and be sure they are done properly.

8. For the condition as exists in the focal plane of [[Visions|vision]], as affected by conditions produced in times back, [flashlight powder explosion in eyes at 6 yrs.?] and at present aggravated by the impinionment, [impingement] we would, with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or adjustments, use specific exercise morning and evening to strengthen the muscles and tissue about the head and neck; and use the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] [hand machine, eye applicator?] over the eye proper.  With this [[Vibration|vibration]] can be created the sufficient of the blood supply in nutriment and eliminating the waste or used forces until the adjustments can be made proper and correct in the system, using the adjustments first.  This will only be necessary to be made three times, and then the exercise and [[Vibration|vibration]] will remove the refuse and strengthen the tissue to the eyes proper.

9. In the digestive system, and for the building in the system of those conditions necessary to meet the needs for the physical, both in physical and mental, to reach the development, it is necessary, with the conditions as at present exist, to meet these with sufficient of the vitim [vitamin] and of protein forces to create sufficient heat, and to give the supply of vitim [vitamin] to the blood through the hemoglobin constituent.  More iron and vegetable forces.  Meats, only those of sufficient quantity to meet the needs of system, never using hog meat of any character. Small quantities of beef, fowl and fish especially.

10. Do these, and we will find we will bring the best to this system.

11. As to the mental and spiritual forces, we find these are only in their moulding, and may, by the spiritual insight, be led and kept in that straight and narrow way.  Keep the physical fit, that the development of mental, [[Soul|soul]] and spirit may manifest in and through this physical, that others may know that this body has been with [[Jesus Christ|the Master]].  [See Life Rdg. 341-1.]
