# TEXT OF READING 1790-1 M 7

**Reading [[1790-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1939  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 8091

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [1790], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. Philip P. Nover.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

The Woods School, 
Time of Reading                            Wildwood Bldg., 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Langhorne, Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body; go over it carefully, examine it thoroughly; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1790].

3. Now as we find, there are disturbing conditions, and they arise from incoordination; as produced from the lack of that WITHIN the system during the period of pregnancy to carry the full flow of coordination between the lymph and the activity of the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system.

4. Hence in those areas about the assimilating system, without great precautions, we have that breaking of the connection between the active forces of IMPULSE and the ABILITY to carry same out in physical reactions.

5. Thus we have retardments to the development of the coordination in the impulse, and the nerve forces for carrying same out.

6. However, other than the disturbances with the eliminations, we find that the body develops physically.

7. As we find, with CONSISTENCY, there may be help brought; but it will require patience AND persistence in the application of those influences and forces as may bring a better activity in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the nerve IMPULSES of the body.

8. There must be precautions as to the diet, and the correcting of those subluxations which exist in the lower dorsal, through the lumbar and sacral area.

9. WE would advise a shadowgraph or X-Ray to indicate just THAT portion in the lumbar and sacral that needs correcting.

10. First we would apply the heavy [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs for at least three days, each time before the corrections are attempted. Apply these heavy [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs an hour each day, over the liver and the caecum area, all along the right side; that we may break up those tendencies that cause this incoordination there.

11. Then after each third day of the Oil Packs, leave off the Packs the next day - the fourth day, you see - and have an adjustment made as indicated, in the lower dorsal, through the lumbar and sacral area (having the shadowgraph or X-Ray made to indicate just that portion needing correction, see?). Do not have the Oil Packs on THAT day, you see.

12. Then take the Packs again for three days; and on the fourth day ANOTHER adjustment and a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

13. Again the Oil Packs, and then ANOTHER adjustment on the fourth day.

14. After THAT series, begin with the low form of electrical forces where Gold is carried to the system.  The connections would be to the 9th dorsal plexus with the small copper plate, and to the umbilicus and lacteal duct plexus with the large nickel plate through which the Gold [[Vibration|vibration]] passes (the solution being in the proportion of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of distilled water).  Keep this attached for thirty minutes each day, for a period of twenty-eight days.

15. When the Appliance is begun, we would then have the adjustment once a week - not through the upper portion of the cerebrospinal system, but those areas below the 9th dorsal area - all adjustments, see?  That would be once a week for four weeks.

16. Then begin again with the Oil Packs three days, and an adjustment; three days again, and an adjustment; three days, and an adjustment.

17. Then begin with another recharge of the Appliance for thirty days, having the adjustment once each week as indicated.

18. After THAT series we would give further instructions.

19. Ready for questions.

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
