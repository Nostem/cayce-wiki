# TEXT OF READING 2714-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2714-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/28/1942  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9835

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self through Mrs. [1553]''s daughter, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, with this body [2714], while the outward conditions cause the greater distress, the effects arise from an infection - that is latent within the system. It gives an expression which is indicated in the ulcered conditions, that respond little to the local applications.

4. We find that three measures are indicated as being needed for corrections; first, those that will aid in purifying the system, in the blood stream, eradicating those infections; and then those applications that will aid the body in producing the elements or activities as to heal AND eradicate the disturbing influences.

5. Begin first with taking [[Mullein|Mullein Tea]], made in the proportions of two ounces of boiling water poured over a tablespoonful of the dried or crumbled leaf [[Mullein|Mullein]] and allowed to steep as regular tea.  Take this much each day.

6. As soon as the body is able to obtain the green [[Mullein|Mullein]] (which may be procured from the South, - any good wholesale crude drug company - as in S. B. Penick & Company, Asheville, North Carolina), - bruise two or three leaves in hot water and apply - without squeezing out the water - directly over the affected area and around same, as a stupe or [[Poultice|poultice]], see?

7. In cleansing the area use warm [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an antiseptic; for this has the [[Healing|healing]] oils as well as being an [[Alkaline Diet|alkalizing]] antiseptic.  Do not use hot, but warm - and warm same in the container, you see, by pouring hot water over the bottle.  Use full strength.  Heat same in bottle or container, by turning the warm water over it, and use at least AFTER each of the Mullein Stupes has been applied (and it is best that these be applied when the body is not to be on its feet, you see).  Use the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] before and after applying the Stupes, see?  This will be even better for this particular condition than the poisonous substances that have been used.

8. Only use the elastic, above or below the affected areas, when the body is on its feet.  Remove the elastic when the body rests.

9. Add plenty of those food values in the diet that carry the excess quantities of B-1 vitamins.

10. Also, at least every other day, relax that area in the sacral and ileac plexus that distresses the circulation to the affected areas.

11. Do that and we will bring the bettered conditions for this body.

12. Do ALL suggested.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Is the Quartz [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] radiation (concentrated) indicated at this time?
(A)  A little later this will be more beneficial than at present.  If this is applied in the present to those areas FROM WHICH the body obtains impulses for circulation through the limb, it will be more effective than directly to the affected area, - as through the sacral and lumbar area, see, in the present.

15. (Q)  Should the Mullein Stupes be applied directly to the body or between gauze?
(A)  They may be applied directly OR between gauze, depending on the severity of the ulcered area from time to time.  Only the green Mullein would be applied in the stupes, though the dried Mullein may be used in the tea.  Of course, the green may also be used for the tea, though it doesn't require so much - about half the quantity - but do not let it boil; just steep as tea, by pouring hot water over same.  Let it stand for fifteen or twenty minutes, see?

16. We are through for the present.

Two copies to self through Mrs. [...] (one for Dr. Wm. D. Taylor) [D.C., N.D.] Copy to Ass'n file

(Please fill out the application [which was enclosed] and return for our files.)
