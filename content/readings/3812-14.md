# TEXT OF READING 3812-14

**Reading [[3812-14]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11404

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1931, in accordance with request made by those present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], A. C. Preston, Gray Salter, Thomas House and [[Thomas Sugrue|Tom Sugrue]].]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] and enquiring minds of A. C. Preston, Gray Salter and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], present in this room, and the treasure located near Kelly's Ford, Culpepper Co., Va., and the conditions surrounding same in the excavation made by these.  We prayerfully seek advice and guidance at this time as to how to proceed to recover this treasure with which we seek to carry forward the work planned.  You will also answer any questions which we may ask regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies, the enquiring minds, A. C. Preston, Gray Salter, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], the operations and excavations as made by these in seeking the treasure here.

3. As we find, these are intact - save as of changes that have come through the various changes in time, space, and conditions as make for same.

4. In that direction where the cairn (charring?) or piled stones and logs, as are indicated by that in the western portion of the excavation, this would be the way, the manner, to locate or to gain same, carrying on in the manner as has been given in precautions as respecting the operations, with the same prayerful consideration of that purposed in the hearts must be fulfilled as a promise to the Giver of all good and perfect gifts.  Carry on, making those checks with that given, with the instrument, and uncover it - that's all! Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is the direction and the distance of the treasure from the marker placed in the west end of the excavation by Gray Salter on Thursday afternoon June 4th?
(A)  (interrupting after first few words)  This has just been given!  The distance?  Find it!

6. (Q)  Please interpret the experience of Gray Salter had early this morning, June 8, 1931, in which he seemed to be digging under the ledge in the southern part of the excavation?
(A)  (Interrupting)  This is only a portion of the experience PARTIALLY had by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; that is, the experience is partial, and partially is of the mental attempting to coordinate with those references as has been given may be attained or gained by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in making those interpretations of experiences, see?  These are as but shadows of that that may be gained by self, see?

7. (Q)  Are there any Forces trying to prevent recovery of this treasure?  If so, why?  and how may we meet these objections?
(A)  This has been given, that there are those to be dealt with, and the manner in which these are to be dealt with has also been given, as to how they should carry on as concerning same.  Do that!

8. (Q)  Are there any influences in the community that would prevent these recovering the treasure?
(A)  We find there are some in the southern portion, or - that is - south from this location, who have been question- ing, and who have been meddlers.  As to whether their abilities would extend to where they might prevent same or not, we do not see so - unless they could influence others.

9. (Q)  Are there any precautions that we should take at this time?
(A)  Just as has been given, those precautions as regarding the operations should be the greater precautions to take.

10. (Q)  Any other advice and guidance?
(A)  Keep on with the operations until they are satisfied within each that these do not check, or that this may not be carried on - but it is here!  where the cairn (charring?) was, where the ashes are, and the logs between same - see? This should not require more than three to four feet's operations to enter the cache itself.  The cairn, of course, was for the marker - the ashes, as indicated - and the entrance must be below and on the side - the edge of the logs here.  Check with the instruments, check with information. We are through.

Copy to Hugh Lynn Cayce
