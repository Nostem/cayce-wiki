# TEXT OF READING 4331-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4331-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/18/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12153

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 18th day of April, 1923, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4331].

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno. Others (?)]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M.                                    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we have here the body of [4331], of ..., [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala.  The body, we find has been suffering with the condition through the stomach and lower digestive forces on account of the lacerations and the cell force showing and carrying some inflammation.  The properties as have been taken, we find, have been of benefit to the body, yet they have not brought the perfect condition on account of the active forces and elements in the system - the body changing about with the different classes, kinds of water, and the diet used.  To make the better connections and [[Vibration|vibrations]] in this body, we would again use just those properties as we have given [in 4331-1] and add that [[Vibration|vibration]] as found in the violet ray, from the cardiac end of the stomach to below the liver on the right side.  Do this each evening from three to five minutes, taking the medicinal properties just as we have given heretofore - simply adding these [[Vibration|vibrations]] and keeping as near to the diet as has been outlined for this body, [4331], see?
