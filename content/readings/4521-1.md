# TEXT OF READING 4521-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4521-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/21/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12399

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by her brother.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find this body in the physical is very good in the functioning of most, or of the organs that have to do with the purely [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the system.  There is in the nerve system, and the effect that is brought to the physical, and in the manifestations through same that brings distress to self and to those about the body at times.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body.

3. There is in the structural portions that which must be corrected to bring the normal development to the body, and to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in its reaction through the physical.

4. Blood supply very good.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find in the sacral plexus and from the coccyx end that abrasion and that pressure in the nerve system causing distress to the nerves that brings distress to the system through the reaction as this brings to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve, and to the nerves that govern the refractory reaction in the mental that brings the abnormal development in the mental and in the body toward that development.  In the pressure as created we find as here:  In the coccyx end and to the ilium and sacral plexus, bringing pressure on the nerves of the false pelvis, which pressing on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve brings then the whole reaction to those centers as brought in the system in the junction with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve's reaction in the organs through which it passes, and the center through which it becomes entwined, as we have in the solar plexus, brachial plexus and at the first dorsal and first cervical plexus, bringing these contractions, bringing these depressions, bringing these forces that find its manifestation in the action of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and the inability to control those voluntary centers as depressed through these.  Not that the brain does not expand; not that the development does not come, but abnormal development through many of these reactions.

6. In the physical and in the organs of the physical, we find development very good.  Not altogether normal for their reactions in their functioning brings at times the distress in the system.

7. Then to give the correction for these, we would through that of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, directed from an internal pressure that would be brought on these portions to bring into linement those portions at the end of the coccyx and to relieve the pressure as has been brought by this strain on the centers throughout the whole nerve system, both cerebro-spinal and sympathetic.

8. Do that.  We will find that within four to six months we will bring about a different condition in this body.  It may be treated from the internal through the orifice itself, you see, or in the anus and to the exterior portions also, so we have a posterior and an anterior treatment for the body, [4521].

9. (Q)  How often would be best to have these treatments given?
(A)  That would be accorded by the one giving the treatments, depending on how far and how much the body is able to take these [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be accorded through the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]. Depending on the individuals themselves, and as to how the personality of the individual complies to the personality of the body being treated.  (This will not be a pleasant treatment you see.)

10. (Q)  Have you anything further to say?
(A)  Do that.  This happened, you see, from an accident when the child was young.
