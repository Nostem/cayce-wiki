# TEXT OF READING 4112-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4112-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/11/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11812

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office, of Dr. [4112], in [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y., this 11th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [4112].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. & Mrs. Edwin [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]], Mrs. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]], Sr., [[Morton Blumenthal|Morton Blumenthal]], Miss Miriam Miller, Mrs. Blanche Kaufman, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Dr. and Mrs. [4112] and others.]

Time of Reading 
9:30 P. M. N.Y. Time.                    [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find the body very good throughout in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, yet there are some conditions in physical that may be so directed that the body throughout many function to a better development of the body in this earth plane.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4112] we are speaking of present in this room.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find very good throughout.  The distribution in the system in the cellular rebuilding forces are very good.  Coagulation and assimilation being carried out very good.  In the eliminating centers in portions of the body, that the circulation should eliminate in direct channels, are disturbed somewhat.  In the elementals of the blood supply, we find the hemoglobin showing the effect of the poisons as not eliminated in its functioning in portions of the system, as we shall see, in portions of the nerve's supply from the circulation.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in cerebro-spinal nerve supply, this very good, except in the left portion of the center of the portion about the distribution of the locomotary of this side.  This shows, from the condition that existed in the body in times back, that the distribution was disturbed, and the elimination as produced from this disturbance has not been brought to the equalization in system.  Hence the sympathetic in that portion of the body shows its reaction, especially in the lymphatic circulation, to the extent that the forces of the system respond in the negative sense at times, especially through that of the emunctory force in the the kidney in left side.  In the distribution, then, of lymphatic forces, the eliminations are disturbed.  In the sympathetic, with the sensory organism, we find these show the effect upon the whole body, more in the mental attitude of body than the physical conditions, yet find their reflection in the physical reaction to other minds.  Then,

6. THE ORGANS OF THE SYSTEM, brain forces very good.  One that would, under the correlation of those mentalities already acclaimed in the body, do well to manifest same more.

7. In the organs of sensory system, the lymphatic condition, the condition in emunctories, show reflection in the sensory system.

8. Lungs, bronchials, good.

9. The digestive organs very good.

10. Heart's action normal, though at times abnormal, being above rather than subnormal.

11. In hepatics, the eliminating forces in these show the effect in kidneys rather than in other portions.

12. In the digestion, other than in the stomach proper, the effect is shown at times of the elimination disturbance. Kidneys show effect of the emunctory condition.

13. Then, to bring the better conditions to the body, keep those applications for the body that would produce the nearer normal eliminations throughout the system, whether through that of interior application or through heat and water, applied through mental forces to the body; that is, choose for itself.

14. In the mental attributes, keep close to those that would bring the better development in the present earth's plane.

15. Do that.

16. We are through for the present.
