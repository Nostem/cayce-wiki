# TEXT OF READING 227-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[227-2]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/7/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1054

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [227].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have him here.  We have had this before, you see.

2. Now, we find the body physically is improved in many ways from that we had before, especially through the blood supply and the functioning of the pulmonaries, with both internal and external circulation.  The abnormal condition in the muscular and ligament forces, in the upper dorsal, lower cervical and lumbar regions, has not become the condition through the functioning of the organs affected by these, for elimination through the deep manipulation [osteopathically?] of the organs affected by these conditions has not been carried out as fully as should be, nor have they taken the incentive proper as they should.  Keep these up, however, until we give the correct incentives to the organs affected by these conditions, in the sacral and lower lumbars, upper dorsal and lower cervicals.  Keep those medicinal properties [Calcidin tablets], for they do not hinder the manipulation that the body needs.  They change the circulation, so far as it is concerned, through the hepatics and through the lungs proper, but the condition of elimination should be carried on through the eliminating and emunctories of the organs affected by these congestions, or lesions.

3. Do this, as we have given.  We will bring the better condition, and the normal forces, for the body, [227].
