# TEXT OF READING 266-2 F 57

**Reading [[266-2]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/8/1933  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 1608

This psychic reading given by Edger Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 8th day of February, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [266], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Oklahoma. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, Mrs. [266]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, there are a great many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and in specific conditions that disturb the body.

3. As we find, those conditions that have been applied for the body - while removing in part those affected organs and tissue that had become involved - have not eliminated the CAUSES entirely of the conditions in the system.  And there is the continuation of the effects being had upon the various portions of the body, by the attempt of those germs in the blood stream and in tissue to set themselves, as it were, in an at-variance to the better physical functioning of the body.  Then, these make for the continued separations of elements that are attempted to be assimilated in the system, and cause those excruciating pains through the pelvis, through the stomach, through those PORTIONS of the body where assimilation would take place.

4. With consistence and persistence, we may find that there may be those applications made for the body wherein there would be a greater resistance established; and a great deal more of the pain and destructive forces retarded, at least, if not entirely removed and eventually eliminated from the system.

5. There needs to be those forces added to the body that will work with the elements created by the assimilations in the body, and that will be more of the constructive nature than destructive to tissue; that would of itself; WERE it allowed to be supplied with the proper elements, make for a better coordination of the body.

6. Then, these would be the applications that we would give for the body:

7. Begin with [[Castor Oil|castor oil]] rubs (rather than packs).  Take a very small quantity on the fingers and massage into the abdomen, lower portion of stomach proper, and across the lumbar and lower dorsal area.  This would require patience and persistence on the part of those making the applications, for it would require at least two tablespoonsful of the oil and at least twenty to thirty to forty minutes to do it; and should be done at least every other day.  Massage into the body all that the body WILL assimilate, by gentle massaging.

8. Following such a treatment it would be well that the sinusoidal (of the very LOWEST [[Vibration|vibration]]) be passed through the body, with one anode on the 4th AND 5th dorsal plexus and the other over the lacteal [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and area.  This will of itself carry portions of the oil THROUGH that portion of the system, and become effective in the assimilations that will be made in the body towards the elements carried to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the functioning organs of the body.

9. We would also have prepared and use the wet electrical appliance, creating a low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]]; but in the solution there should be carried - through a CHARGING of same - the suspension of [[Iodine|iodine]] in a container, so that - when the applications of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the body are given - there will be the effective or ELECTRIFIED forces of the solution itself carried into the system, that will be destructive to tissue that has become involved by activity of outside influences - as well as aiding the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body to produce, WITH those vibrations created in the electrical forces carrying the oil (the sinusoidal), a better activity; reducing the destructive influences and aiding the system to be EASIER, also to bring RECUPERATIVE forces for the body. This low electrical vibration that has been charged, and carrying the [[Iodine|iodine]] should be given for ten to fifteen minutes EACH day.  The first anode should be attached to the 3rd and 4th cervical.  Then the opposite anode, or that one coming from the nickle post or pole, would be attached to the umbilicii center.

10. To be sure, it will be necessary for the diet to be such that the blood and nerve supply is replenished in the system. Mild forms of the alcohol content, as in brandies or light wines, or malts, may be taken in moderation, to assist the circulation through the digestive system and to aid in better assimilations through the gastric forces of the digestive system.  The foods must be those that are easily digested, and that make for replenishing and rebuilding.

11. The activities, naturally, are very much curtailed.  These treatments will assist in bringing about a better activity for this body, [266].

12. Do that.

13. Ready for questions.

Copy to Self
"    " File
