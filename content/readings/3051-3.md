# TEXT OF READING 3051-3 F 45

**Reading [[3051-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/25/1943  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 10376

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3051], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3051]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are improvements in many ways in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  The applications that have been made through the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments have been well, and in accord with that given.

4. There are doubts and fears (that have been submerged in physical expression) finding manifestation in the physical being in self, and these must be met as they are indicated in the body.  But do not become impatient with self nor the lack of those materializations at once of those hopes in the body.  For ye grow in [[Grace|grace]], in knowledge and in understanding.  Where it has taken years to produce a fear, a doubt, an activity that begins to find manifestation in the twitching of a muscle, in the expansion of a vein, in the frustrations in the body forces, - be patient, be quiet within; and we will find those administrations that have been made - and that may be made - will aid thee in growing in the right directions.

5. For ye are made body, mind, [[Soul|soul]].  They each have their part in thy [[Oneness|oneness]], or they are one; as Father, Son, and [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] is one.  Yet each may function independently of the other - as the Son manifested in the earth could have, by [[Free Will|choice]], NOT gone the whole way.

6. As ye put thy trust, thy faith in Him, so in thy body, thy mind, thy [[Soul|soul]], ye may not go astray save by [[Free Will|choice]].  Keeping in the faith and in the way called straight, ye will come to a completeness, to a [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Purpose|purpose]] in Him.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Are treatments being given correctly, if so why weren't results not attained; explain fully.  Reilly asks if adjustment is necessary?
(A)  Had these not been necessary they would not have been indicated.  They ARE necessary.  Have these made osteopathically, not in the same places as the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments; that is go to an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], see?

9. (Q)  Is that part of colon which hemorrhages ulcerated and how can normal elimination be established so that this condition may heal permanently, is this possible or would an operation help or will cathartics have to be resorted to for life?
(A)  As indicated these fears and doubts become localized. In this particular instance, to correct these conditions, we would use a compound prepared in this manner.  This is merely added and is not to do away with any of those things suggested for the body.

To four ounces of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin add - in the order named:

A Fusion of Wild Ginseng....................1 ounce,
(It would be made by putting one dram of the Ginseng, ground or mashed, in two ounces of distilled water and reducing by simmering to one ounce. Strain and add to the Elixir.)
A Fusion of Wild Ginger (made in the same manner)....................1/2 ounce.

Shake the solution before it is to be taken.  Take one teaspoonful each day for five days.  Then leave off for five days.  Then take again, and again.  And by the third series you should have taken the whole quantity and should find the condition corrected - provided the other treatments have been carried forward as indicated.

10. (Q)  Why are veins in legs and feet becoming prominent, especially in left calf, what treatment follow?
(A)  This is not a great change.  This is part imagination. But these should disappear if the corrections are made osteopathically as indicated, in the lumbar and sacral and at the head of the sciatic centers.  Take the bicycle exercise also  - this will be effective for the body.

11. (Q)  What steps taken to restore usefulness of left thumb. Was tendon sewed, explain?
(A)  The massage of this daily with Sweet Oil or [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]] will now prove the most helpful.  Of course, this is where a tendon has been not only pinched but has become in part atrophied.  This will be aided, not entirely restored, but may be used again.

Keep using it; not as one that you can't get along without; but every day or two before retiring, massage it yourself for five to six minutes with the oil  - just what it will absorb.  Extend this to the wrist and to the elbow and this will aid.

12. (Q)  Gums and SORE BRIDGE not responded, why?
(A)  If you'll keep up the treatments and the things suggested, they will.

13. (Q)  Give report on teeth and how keep them for life?
(A)  Better go to the dentist to have much of this done! For this particular body, these may be kept by the use of Ipsab occasionally as a massage for the gums, and then taking care of those activities that are to supply sufficient calcium to the body at the periods when cycles change. Cycles change for the teeth during the second year of each cycle.  During that year take at least three to four series of [[Calcios|Calcios]] or its equivalent to supply calcium to the system, and it will aid not only the teeth but all the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].

14. (Q)  Why did I have migraine attack on the 13th after so many treatments?
(A)  These were a part of the experience.  These should or will come until the body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] are one in [[Purpose|purpose]].

15. (Q)  Pain and tiredness in the eyes?
(A)  Overtaxation.  Taking the head and neck exercises regularly will relieve this condition.

16. (Q)  Will canichrome tablets I take restore color of hair?
(A)  NO!  If the juice of potato peelings is taken regularly (two or three times a week) it will keep the hair nearer to its normal color than all other forms of chemical preparations.

17. (Q)  How eliminate dryness to hair and skin?
(A)  Just as given, when there is a perfect condition in the scalp, in the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], by - keeping a correct balance, the oil, the dryness, the color will be normal for the body.  Don't try to make something abnormal for self that should be this or that condition.

18. (Q)  What preparations help keep firm muscles in face and neck?
(A)  The Black and White are the better preparations for keeping muscles, or the whole body, or the face, in the proper condition.  These would be used after a thorough cleansing with any good toilet soap, preferably that prepared with Olive Oil, or Coconut Oil rather than other characters of fats.

19. (Q)  Explain meaning of my seal and why I have it, how benefit from this?
(A)  We haven't the seal, in this.

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file 13-Q & A to Miss Goodall for indexing

(If you do not already have an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in sympathy, we would refer you to Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 East East St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

Directions for preparing potato peeling juice:  Stir the peelings of three or four potatoes in a little water and drink the juice as a cocktail before the meal about three times a week.)
