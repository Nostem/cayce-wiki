# TEXT OF READING 2003-1 F ADULT (P.O. Clerk, Catholic)

**Reading [[2003-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/16/1939  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8519

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of September, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2003], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Miss [380].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [2003], who at this time seeks mental and spiritual guidance, especially as relates to her husband, [...], who was last seen September 10, 1938, at the Veterans Administration Facility, at Gulfport, Miss. and also regarding just how she may be of the greatest help to her sons, [1261] & [1442].  You will give her counsel at this time and answer the questions which she has submitted.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [2003].

3. As respecting relationships, associations or activities regarding the husband, - these are at a standstill for the present.

4. So, leave these as they are.

5. Relative to the son, or sons, - these as we find, from information that has been indicated, are in part working in those directions.

6. As to [1261] - that circumstances have arisen, or apparently lack of connections for the carrying on in some of those directions, - let those activities in the finishing be in [[Law of Moses|the law]] as related to international relationships.  These will be the more advantageous to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in those activities as will come about.  Then there may be the completion of same in those fields of activity as we have indicated.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Just what should be my attitude regarding my husband? Would it be well to push a search for him?  Just how may I help him most?
(A)  As has been indicated, leave as is.

9. (Q)  Just how may I be of the greatest help to my son [1261]? Give as many practical suggestions as possible.

(A)  Study the information as given as to the character and characteristics of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and follow those suggestions as to how the entity might acquit himself respecting the weaknesses in the character; using such knowledge, thus, to aid the entity to gain the better control of self.

10. (Q)  What causes the apparent antagonism between [1261] and me and how may I clear this up?
(A)  The feeling of resentments as arise; and if there is the studying of the characteristics as we have outlined, these will be seen.

Do not be so pessimistic.  Rather be in the optimistic manner as respecting those desires and [[Purpose|purposes]] of the entity.

11. (Q)  Just how may I be of the greatest help to my son [1442]? Give as many practical suggestions as possible?
(A)  If you leave [1442] alone he'll take care of himself!

12. (Q)  As advised in a Life Reading, [1442]'s work seems to lie in agriculture in connection with redistribution of supplies. Would it be best for me to advise him to undertake work at Georgetown where he has a a half scholarship and may take a social science course or go to Maryland University where he may begin at once with agriculture but without the scholarship help?
(A)  Georgetown will be the more practical.

13. (Q)  Any other advice that will be of help regarding the sons at this time?
(A)  As has been indicated in the experiences of the sons, - study their characteristics, and adjust self to be as a help in each gaining the best from the suggestions made for improvement in same.

Analyze self, and DO NOT be selfish with either.

Let those influences that are prompting thee in thy spiritual life be the guide, ever.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Miss [380]
"    " Ass'n file
