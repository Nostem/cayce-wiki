# TEXT OF READING 2229-1 M 47

**Reading [[2229-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/23/1924  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 8950

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23rd day of September, 1924, in accordance with request made by self, [2229].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Jack Stone and Mr. [2229].]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will give the cause of the existing condition, also the treatment for the cure and relief of the body.

2. EC:  Now we find there are conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in this body that prevent the giving of the best elements and forces and abilities of this body.  Some are conditions that to the body at present have become normal.  Others may be corrected and the body physically assisted to give the better development in the physical, and to add to its own development in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s force in present earth plane.

3. Those conditions that may be corrected have to do with eliminations and assimilations in the body.  These also give the distress to portions of the body in the functioning of organs in the system.

4. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [2229], we are speaking of, present in this room.

5. First:

6. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find a high hepatic circulation from conditions existing in and through the digestion and intestinal tract, yet a low supply of the red blood forces and a lack of the vitality necessary to store energy.  Hence the strain that comes on portions of the body under different conditions.  The red blood being deficient, and the white being near normal.  The constituents of the red blood being below normal, especially in the hemoglobin of this supply. This is shown more especially in the lungs and in the kidneys in their functioning.  The heart force is near normal, though below normal in the pressure in blood supply, and a slow pulsation, though not abnormal for the body.

7. IN THE NERVE SYSTEMS, in this we find much of the trouble that is in the body.  There has been in times back an accident to portions of the body that has brought to the system a condition in nerve centers that produce distress in the system.  Better at present than it has been in times back.  This produces in the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus both disturbing forces.  This affects both the digestion and the condition of the system in assimilating the properties from foods to give the proper rebuilding forces in the body.  This we find affects directly the condition in the duodenum and in the pancreas, so that the action of food values in system is not corrected in either the duodenum digestion, nor in the lacteal [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]].  Too much of the forces that should be eliminated becomes the assimilation of the blood forces.  In the center then from which this trouble and disturbance radiates is from the 9th to the 12th dorsal plexuses, repression then particularly through the solar plexus center itself.  Hence the hypogastric center, both in nerve and in the blood radiation, becomes affected under and through this condition.  In the action then of the sympathetic nerve system, which radiates through its branches from the solar plexus center, this gives the condition in extremities, where at times only we have the lack of blood flow to extremities, the system attempting to adjust itself under the strain.

8. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, brain forces very good.  The action of the subliminal forces in this body above the ordinary.  Hence the ability of the body to control its mental proclivities even under stress or strain of physical conditions existing.  That is, the body has the ability to force the mental action even under the stress of physical pain.  Hence has been termed in days back as one of iron will and nerve.

9. In the organs of the sensory system, these are very good, though under the strain of the sympathetic condition existing between the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus in the center about the 5th dorsal segment.  The condition there affects the functioning in blood flow to the sensory organs. Hence the strain at times to voice, to the sight, the hearing, to the whole sense of feeling.  This is only at times, for the body through the mental forces controls much.

10. Throat, lungs, larynx and bronchials, some local conditions in throat and mouth that need attention.  These are surrogated by circulation, but should be attended to through their proper channels, to prevent absorption of poisons in system.

11. Lungs good organically.  In the centers from which the radiation of the secondary cardiac plexus gives a depression, spasmodically, in blood flow in lower portion of lung.

12. Digestion under the strain of those conditions as given.

13. In the liver the hepatic, high hepatic circulation is produced by condition in the hypogastric centers, shows how this condition affects the liver direct, though not organically, for both excretory and secretive system is worked above normal, though the system does not assimilate properly.  The reflex condition from hypogastric plexus and from the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the system through the condition in pancreas overtaxes the kidneys in their elimination and the emunctories all are engorged, as is the spleen.

14. Then to bring the better conditions and to correct that portion that may be corrected, we would give these for this body.  First, correct either osteopathically or chiropractically those conditions existing in the 9th to the 12th dorsal, and those in the 5th dorsal, cervicals and dorsal, and give those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be accorded to the body from wearing the applicator of the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator. [[[Radium|Radium]] Applicator Company, [[California|Los Angeles]], Calif.]  Do that, and we will find we will bring the better forces to this body, [2229].
