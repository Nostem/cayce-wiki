# TEXT OF READING 900-451 M 34 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-451]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/29/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5654

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind.  Tell what effect the appeal to 
President Hoover made by the Independent Radio Corp. will 
have on Radio Corp. of America and associated holdings).

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  Also those conditions as respecting those claims as have been and will be filed as respecting the Radio Corporation of America and associated holdings.  These, as we find, with those of other speculative conditions as regarding effect such investigation would produce upon many holdings and many combines, will have the depressing effect upon the market quotations; yet these, after a small period of time, would necessarily go again to new high peaks, for these combinations WILL, eventually, take place.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
