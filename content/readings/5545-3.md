# TEXT OF READING 5545-3 M 41

**Reading [[5545-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/3/1930  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 13820

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Levy, Drs. Lydic, Parker and Hayes, Miss Morris, L. B. Cayce, and Mr. [5545].]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5545].  This we have had before.  Now, in the general way and manner, we find conditions are still on the improve.  There are evidences, however, of some retractions that have caused some uneasiness.  This is as much from the system adjusting itself to the new conditions as apparent, as anything else - especially in those regions where scar tissue has had, and does have, to do with the changes being wrought.

2. Keep, then, those conditions for the present, that have been given for this body, and we will find these will continue to be on the improve.  Do not attempt to overeat, or to change too radically from those conditions as have been given for the diet for the body.  While changes may be made, but not too radical changes as yet.

3. Keep the saffron and the elm still active, but do not allow the elm to become rancid for the system.  Would be WELL, in this, to ALTER this a bit and use the olive oil as a base for the food's activities; that is, then, taking same just before the meals are taken.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is body in condition to return home?
(A)  Not just yet.  Wait at least ten days.

5. (Q)  How is pain in groin?
(A)  This, as has been made reference to, that of system adjusting itself to conditions as produced from the reactions in system from scar tissue.  In the activities this may be best aided.  Not too severe, but in the open - and some activities, though they must NOT be strained in same!

6. (Q)  Would you advise [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]]?
(A)  [[Colon Hydrotherapy|Colonic irrigation]] would be well.  This had best be, however, in two or three DAYS from PRESENT, than at the PRESENT.  Let's have some oil first, food values in the intestinal system, so that the activities through the system as related to the mucus, or the fluids in system, are more active; so there will not be irritation from the irrigation.

7. (Q)  Will business suffer if body remains at hospital longer?
(A)  In this there are, of course, MANY conditions to be considered.  First, the body should consider this:  Will the BODY suffer most by returning, and the business eventually - by the body not being capable to handle same?  Just be consistent with that as is being taken for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and KNOW that when the body is more physically fit the business may be EASILY brought into proper relations.

8. That is all the questions regarding this body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to be sent to wife
