# TEXT OF READING 4782-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4782-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/29/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12763

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [...] [[4782]''s husband].]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of this body.  You will answer any questions relative to this body that I may ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are changes in the physical conditions in this body from those as we have had here before.  The general conditions are on the improve.  The specific conditions in portions of body have become accentuated at times.  There are those conditions that still need the correction that should bring about the relief to the body, especially those in the left lower side, where, with the corrections as are made, and being made in the lower dorsal and in the sacral, bring about that inflammation in the false pelvis and in the organs in the pelvis that bring distress to portions of the body.  This would be better accomplished in and under the present conditions by the placement of ligaments and tissue in the dorsal and lumbar regions, and by binding same with strip or adhesive, and for the condition in the pelvis, use those of the local application that will remove that strain and pressure as is caused on the lower left side.  These would bring about the removal of the strain in the false pelvis and remove that pressure as causes the distress to this portion of body. Keep with this those manipulations that will give the body the rebuilding, through the proper eliminations being carried on in the system, not allowing the toxins as would form, from the distresses as brought on by the nerve condition produced, and taxation being left in system, to cause those of lesions in any portion.  The distresses as are brought are in portion retractions and are the lack of keeping up those conditions wherein the medicated forces as were used as that of the cleansing solutions were kept from and these brought on those conditions in this portion of body.  In the general structure in the general conditions, we find the body in better condition that as we had before.

3. (Q)  What is the cause and cure of the soreness, cramping and pain in left side of abdomen, about two inches from lower part of hip bone in pelvic region?  Has come at intervals for over three years and gets body down in bed at times.
(A)  This, as we have given.  We find this is produced by the condition wherein the ligaments and tissue have caused the strain through the form of lesion as produced there.  This brings, then, the distress to those portions in the false pelvis, that bring the distress to the body.  Hence the local applications as would be.  These would be of those that would give the cleansing solutions to the body, taking away those discharges as come from the organs in the pelvis, which have in times back been helped by packs, you see, in same.  Now, remove those conditions, and holding these in place with those bands about the portion of body, until the ligaments, the tissue, are re-united in their proper form.  This we find in the hip joint proper, in the lower portion of the sacral, lower dorsal and lumbar region.  Tape is better than casts or bands about body, and as much rest and off of the feet as possible, until these conditions have brought about their proper connections, using occasionally those of the cleansing solutions for the system.  Well, too, at times were the violet ray used in these portions of body.  This strengthens the nerve tissue.

Do that.  We will bring these conditions to the body.  Be necessary to keep these bands about the body from three to three and a half weeks.  Hold the abdomen up.  Hold the organs of the pelvis, as it were, in place, removing the pressure in the left side, on the Fallopian Tubes, you see.

4. (Q)  Would it be best for this body to be taken to the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] sanitarium at Delaware, [[Ohio|Ohio]]?
(A)  Be best that the body have the rest, whether at sanitarium or at home, so the conditions are carried out properly.  We will get the local attention in the hospital better than in home.  The pains about the body, as we have; or the whole portions of abdomen and of structural portions of body in that position wherein the strain is removed from the system, and until the ligaments and tissue can gain their normal elasticity and their normal hold, as it were, on the portions of body causing the pressure.

5. (Q)  Are there any obstructions in the tubes from the kidneys?
(A)  No obstructions in the tubes.  Pressure on tubes cause the distress.  Hence the condition as would be found by the proper adjustments of the ligaments and tissue and held in place, for the strain on the drain from kidney through the bladder, with that inflammation produced by the inflammation caused in false pelvis, on Fallopian Tube on the left side, brings the greater distress and the desire for passage of urine through the exciting of the nerve tissue in the lower portion of the hypogastric plexus.

6. We are through for the present.
