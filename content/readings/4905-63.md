# TEXT OF READING 4905-63

**Reading [[4905-63]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/26/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12988

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give the conditions of operations at the present time, and how the operators may go about to insure the success of these operations, telling us, if possible, just what time this may be expected to be made a producing well.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations here.  These we have had before.  The conditions surrounding the operations are gradually straightening themselves out.  The preparations for the drilling into the production, and to the lower shales and producing sand, are progressing now in a more satisfactory manner.  The setting of that as necessary to prevent casing crumpling, and to cut off the obstructions in cavings, and to insure the success of operations, should be completed now in three and a half days.  Then, the drilling in will require about a week to ten days.  Then we will find that between the fifteenth and thirtieth of September should make this a producing well, with sufficient production to produce, with acreage and all, fifteen hundred for each dollar already invested.  The operations will be successful.  The patience will be rewarded - for much has been given, much will be received.  Ready for questions.  (After pause)  The casing is swinging clear, and while some cavings occur with each dumping of the cement, this is decreasing, and the stability of conditions increasing.  Follow those suggestions as to time and manner of dumping or pumping same.

3. We are through.

> to see, it may yet bend to the more [[Psychic Development|psychic development]] of

> ills.

> But enough of this now.  My mind has undergone radical

> health.
> 
> As ever, Your friend  [signed] M. B. Wyrick

> Dear Mr. Wyrick:-
> 
> I received yours of the 31st and of the 6th.  Should have

> collapse.  For a week or more I haven't been able to do
> anything - not even for myself, but I'm feeling a whole lot
> better now - seem to have gotten myself in hand, and I am in
> hopes that everything is going to work out alright.
> 
> I certainly do hope that you are going to have the
> opportunity to get down this way sometime in the near future,
> so that we can talk things over.  There is a whole lot I'd
> like to talk to you about - a whole lot of things that
> sometimes worry me, about the work, and about the way to
> handle it - so many different phases of it seem to present
> themselves, and I know that you have given all of it a great
> deal of study.  We are having a demonstration of it, in a
> way, that to our own minds will verify a great many phases of
> it, and it is with our most precious possession - our boy
> [HLC].  I think he has studied the work more than anyone
> under ordinary circumstances, and while he's very young, and
> he is entering into his college work and in same he may be
> taught or trained to think along different lines, still this
> seems to have already mapped out certain conditions that I
> know he will be on the lookout for, and for the last two or
> three years it seems to have checked so thoroughly that I
> know that it is bound to influence his life a very great
> deal.  Naturally, his mother and I are rather anxious, and
> it's about these conditions, as well as the rest of the
> things, that I'd like to talk to you about.  If the
> information that we have gotten is true, it is the most
> natural thing in the world for us to desire to counsel with
> you, and if it is true again, no one could counsel us quite
> as well as you!  So you see how we feel.  I don't mean, of
> course, in any way except that exchange of ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]],
> that really is the foundation of all buildings, whether of
> the material earthly structures, or of our bodies whose minds
> sway individuals, groups, classes, nations - just as
> Mussolini's is at present.  So you know I'm anxious to see
> you.
> 
> Well, I am glad to know that your stay at the well seemed to
> indicate that there were still yet some prospects.  I'm
> wondering what the people at Clerburne told you.  Would you
> mind telling me?
> 
> I should have gotten this reading to you before this, and
> would have, but as I told you, I have been unable to do
> anything until the last day or two, and of course the first
> thing I wanted to do was to get myself straight.
> 
> Let me hear from you whenever you have the opportunity.  You
> know we are always more than anxious to hear from you.
> 
> With kindest personal regards from all,

> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
