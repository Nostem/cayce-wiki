# TEXT OF READING 5536-3 F 35

**Reading [[5536-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/3/1929  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 13801

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by her uncle, Mr. [...], through Mr. [5540].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. Thomas B. Brown.]

Time of Reading                                 ... Street, 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Indiana.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, and advise if any improvements or changes are now noted in her physical condition; also if present treatments are being given properly, how long they should be continued, and what changes should be made in treatments now given, with any further suggestions for the improvement and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [5536] - this we have had before.

3. Now, we find there are changes taking place in the physical functioning of the body, and these are being administered in a way and manner that will bring bettered conditions for the body.  There needs be the general distribution of used forces through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] as may cause better eliminations through the administration of the manipulations osteopathically given.  NOT that these are not being administered PROPERLY, but as the gradual changes come about by the change in the physical replenishing, through the assimilation as takes place in system and through the reviving of resistive forces through the application of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as are set up in the system through the application of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-Violet]] Ray, will bring changes that will NECESSITATE changes in the administering of the manipulations in such a way and manner as to prevent any portion of the system becoming overclogged through non-eliminations.  For, with the conditions as have been brought about in the UNUSED forces, becoming then resuscitated through the enlivening of nerve energy and of the vital forces in system, these will necessarily be changed as conditions then improve, and as they bring about change, then the change IN the manipulations would be as such as to create the BETTER COORDINATION throughout the system, as portions of the body - and as there are loosened centers that bring about reviving of forces, see?  that the manipulations carry FROM the system to the various channels of their PROPER elimination, that there be the proper coordinating and resuscitating forces administered for the replenishing of the various portions of body as have been under stress and strain.

4. Well that occasionally there be kept properties of those forces as has been given for cleansing of the alimentary canal, to PREVENT the reaction of used forces in system; that is, of the elm water and of the tea - as prevents fermentation, when PROPERLY given, and prevents the activity of forces in the body that would produce in the mucous producing forces of the intestinal system those that would bring the improper reaction through nerve energies, by CLEANSING the system in a proper manner.

5. Do that.  Keep these up until there is seen the proper reactions taking place.  THEN there may be given other necessary changes that may arise, PROVIDED the manipulations and the administration of the light rays for the body are carried properly.  Then, sufficient of these MUST be given to bring resuscitation to those portions of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] that have so long remained dormant.  Ready for questions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
