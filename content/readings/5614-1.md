# TEXT OF READING 5614-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[5614-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/25/1929  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13956

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by his father - through [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, the disturbances of the conditions with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, [5614], are caused by the unbalancing of the eliminations in the system, and with the re-occurrence of conditions as produced from such, there is brought about easy affection, or infections in the system - as indicated in this body.

2. To meet the needs then of the conditions, we would get at the basis of the condition first by giving that which will alleviate the conditions where infections show - by the use of the antiseptics as will prevent the spreading of same, and first bathing off - after the antiseptic solution, see? - with a carbolized antiseptic, see?  Two antiseptics, see? Then using those properties of Unguentine as a balm for same.  These are local.

3. Then we would give that which will create the proper [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the stimulating of eliminations for the body.  This would be in each dose, and at least four such doses should be taken - and one day between each dose. Preferably these should be taken in the evening:

Podophyllum.............1/4 grain,
Cascara Sagrada.........1/4 grain,
Senna...................1/4 grain,
Leptandrin..............1/4 grain.

These would be given in capsule.

4. We would also give at least five [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments for the conditions as are existent in the 6th, 7th and 8th dorsal, also the 12th dorsal and the 2nd and 3rd lumbar. These manipulations will aid the system in adjustments and in adjusting itself for the proper eliminations.  These should be taken days when the medicinal properties are not taken. Do that, [5614].  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]
