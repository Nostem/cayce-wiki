# TEXT OF READING 1886-1 F 58

**Reading [[1886-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1939  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 8312

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the physician - Dr. Frank Dobbins, through Associate Membership of Mrs. [1886] in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there is a complication of disturbances, which involve the incoordination between the activity of the superficial and deeper circulation, with ossification in the extremities; so that, as these continue, and the tissue in structural body becomes involved, this will cause separation of body and mind and [[Soul|soul]].

4. As to the manners or means of preventing this condition, as we find:

5. We would apply hot stupes of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; not [[Iodine|iodine]] - for while these basically are the same, their reactions under heat AND wet heat are entirely different.  Not sufficient, of course, to blister, but sufficient length of time to relax and allow some circulation through the affected portions on the body and limbs and locomotories.  As combined with the activity of the pulmonary circulation, this will aid same.

6. Then there needs be better eliminations.

7. These as we find offer relief and an extenuation of the activities, if these applications would be made; combined, of course, with the assisting of the circulatory system through the relaxing of those areas about not only the cardiac reactions but the setting up of better activities through the lumbar and the abdominal areas.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What proportions of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] for the stupes?
(A)  A tablespoon in a quart of water.

11. (Q)  Where applied?
(A)  As indicated, on affected and stiffened or stiffening areas.  These applications would be to the lumbar extremities, pulmonary extremities, - that is, from the pulmonary center to the extremities, and the arms and head, see?  all of these areas where there is the greater affection and infection between those circulatory forces.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. Dobbins
"    " Ass'n file

([[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is distributed by Schieffelin & Co., N.Y.

At your convenience we would appreciate a history of this case, so that we may have something with which to compare our information.)
