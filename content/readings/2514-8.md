# TEXT OF READING 2514-8 F 24

**Reading [[2514-8]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9453

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2514].

2. As we find, in some respects conditions show improvements. In others there are those areas in which there tends to be a spreading, or great irritation arising from the disturbance in the body.

3. We find that there has not been sufficient emphasis upon, or precautions taken in regard to, keeping those solutions in the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] in their regular order and in their correct strength.

4. While these changes indicated in the present are the better, we would keep the proper strength of each solution; keeping the plates very clean, and keeping the Appliance charged well.  This will make for greater improvement.

5. In those areas under knees and on hips, where the sores are formed, do keep the Icthyol Ointment.  Keep the Packs on the areas about same.

6. To soften the areas where there is the hardness at times, apply for at least three to four hours the warm packs (not too hot) of a saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], changing often during the period.  When the packs become hardened, - that is, by crystallization, - remove and then moisten again, or change to another pack over the areas.

7. Keep the rest of those activities that have been indicated, applying the other ointments and massages which have been outlined; keeping close to the diets.

8. Keep in the open, but not too much in the sun, when it is practical.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Why have I not had a menstrual flow since March?
(A)  As has been indicated for the body, there are natural causes.  Do not be overanxious about these conditions as yet.

11. (Q)  Why the recent soreness in stomach, or from waist down; also aching in side and back?
(A)  As has been indicated, the lack of the proper [[Vibration|vibrations]] being in the system to ward off or retard those disturbances as given, see?

Keep the solutions that are charged to the body in their proper strength.  Don't try to use them too long.  These should be changed every fifteen days, - the Gold, the [[Camphor|Camphor]], as well as the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], see?

Do these and as we find we will keep better conditions for the body.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(8/3/42 GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs.)
