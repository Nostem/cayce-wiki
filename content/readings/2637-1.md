# TEXT OF READING 2637-1 F 56

**Reading [[2637-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/16/1941  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 9687

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 16th day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made by the friend - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. ..., New Hampshire.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - here is the body.

3. Now as we find, conditions are such that the body must meet those conditions manifest in same.

4. The condition, as has been determined, is of that nature that sets up a system in itself; thus is in the present so advanced as to be causing disintegration.

5. So, little or nothing from the material angle may be administered in the present.  It IS malignant.  It HAS entered vital tissue.  It may NOT be changed from present environ advantageously.

6. That being administered we would keep.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  This it has - latent; may scarcely be reached.  All who would may aid in the passing - or transition - that must come soon.

9. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mrs. [1158] Copy to Ass'n file
