# TEXT OF READING 243-27 F 57

**Reading [[243-27]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/19/1937  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 1111

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of October, 1937, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [340], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Helen Ellington.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M.                              ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [243].

2. Conditions are not so good.

3. As we find, with the anxieties, the disturbances that have arisen mentally and physically, the activities of the body have become rebellious as it were to the very INFLUENCES of the activity of the deeper organs themselves.

4. Hence the congestions that have arisen.

5. As we would find, though, in the present, such manipulations and treatments that would come with Shackleford would be the better.  These as we find offer in the present the conditions that may make for the better activities.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [340]
"    " Ass'n file

WIRED

MANIPULATIVE TREATMENTS BY SHACKLEFORD WOULD BE BEST UNDER PRESENT CONDITIONS.  [James R. Shackleford, D.O.]
