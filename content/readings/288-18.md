# TEXT OF READING 288-18 F 22

**Reading [[288-18]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/17/1927  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 1909

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [288].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                  ... Street, 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give a physical, mental and spiritual reading for this body, with suggestions for the improvement of any or all of these conditions.  You will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before, you see.

3. Now, we find the general physical conditions of the body very good.  There is the lack of proper eliminations, especially with the condition as exists in the tonsil region, and with this condition it becomes, then, NECESSARY that elimination THROUGHOUT the body be kept RATHER at an excess, than allowing to become clogged at times.

4. With these corrections - that is, with salines taken to relieve this condition from time to time, and by the keeping up of those exercises, or those applications that keep the eliminations, the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] will be brought nearer to their normal condition.

5. Same condition exists in the eyes - for with that depression as is caused by the overflow, or the backing up of poisons to these portions of the body - that is, the neck, and throughout the sensory system, these become super-sensitive, as it were, and suffer in consequence.  With the eliminations set, these will be found to be improved.

6. Use the BiSoDol as the eliminator, for this, as we see, is an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] with an acid resection.  Teaspoonful taken about every second or third day, or WHENEVER necessary, see?  Do that.

7. As for the mental and the spiritual forces, these are good. These are active in their proper way and manner.  No suggestions for improvement - for these should be made from within self.  Do that.

8. (Q)  Is there any trouble with the teeth?
(A)  We do not find trouble with these.  Some in the gums, where there is at times irritation as is created by the poor eliminations.

9. (Q)  What is the [[Purpose|purpose]] of the tonsils?
(A)  Act as drosses, or the elimination of drosses for system.  We are through.
