# TEXT OF READING 720-2 F 56

**Reading [[720-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/10/1935  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 4554

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Ellsworth Garrison, Myrtle Demaio, Helen Ellington, Ruth LeNoir, Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, [720].  ("Yes - same place - part of the body - ".) [See 720-2, Par. B2.]

2. Now, we find conditions are somewhat bettered here than when we had them here before.

3. We would add now the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs across the abdomen, and the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] saturated solution packs across the LOWER abdomen and across the small of the back; these will relieve those pains that come at times.

4. The [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs we would use about three times a week; across the lower portion of the liver area, across the pancreas and the spleen or across the lower portion of the stomach and the upper bowel, and especially to the caecum area on the right side.

5. In using the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] packs, use these across the lower portion of the kidney and the false pelvis area, across the lumbar and upper portion of the sacral and across the LOWER portion of the abdomen on the LEFT ovary area.  These would be applied about once or twice a week, whenever there is the heaviness in these portions.

6. We find also that the use of the enemas to relieve the poisons will be helpful.  These we would take (the enemas) at least one every other week; and even oftener if there is disturbance in this respect.  Use an antiseptic solution in at least a portion of the injections.

7. The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine will be most helpful in quieting and making for the ability to rest.

8. KEEP the battery appliance for the body as indicated.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Would you advise taking any laxative for eliminations?
(A)  Not necessary if the enemas are given, and if the oil packs are kept, and the diets are kept of a laxative nature - which would be much better than medications internally taken.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file

(Directions for [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] and [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs were enclosed.)
