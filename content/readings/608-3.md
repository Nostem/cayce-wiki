# TEXT OF READING 608-3 F 20 MONTHS

**Reading [[608-3]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/23/1928  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 4204

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [635].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving the cause of same, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same, and will answer the questions regarding this that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before.

3. Now, we find the physical conditions somewhat changed from those as we have had here before.  There have been those conditions of an acute nature, showing there are poisons in the system, produced by taking some conditions that were in such nature as to produce an acute condition in the region of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that change the chyle or digested portions in the system through assimilations for the body.  These in turn showing their resistance to accept same, upset the gastric juices of the stomach; yet the conditions to be met are the eliminating of these poisons as were assimilated, and as will continue to produce some temperature until these are eliminated from the body, for the body in its developing stage is susceptible to those elements that besiege the blood supply in its tentative forces for the building system.

4. Now the conditions are improved from those which have existed.  Hence this also must be taken into consideration - but we would begin first (even with the cleansed system, in a manner) with broken doses of Castoria, after having flushed the system with two small doses of common [[Baking Soda|baking soda]], see? that is, this [[Baking Soda|soda]] in the system - about a quarter to less than a quarter teaspoonful in three quarters glass of water. This will produce an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] condition in the system so that when the properties in Castoria are taken they produce a different effect on the body from that of entering an acid or a poison-ridden system.  Keep this up - that is, the broken doses of Castoria - for at least day and a half, see?  When these have acted thoroughly on the system, then requite [requiet?] the system by the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the whole of the spinal system - use cocoa butter as a carrier and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] this especially into the groin and abdomen of the body.

5. For the diet:  Do not give any water that is not first boiled, see?  Do not give any milk unless it is first heated - not necessarily boiled, but heated, see?  Give then those properties in dried milk gruels, especially oatmeal - let this be strained.  [[Beef Juice|Beef juice]] and Graham crackers and bread.  Lentils, beans, or conditions of that nature - and plenty of the juice of tomatoes, and canned tomatoes - not of the fresh - those that are canned without Benzoate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]]; such as may be found in the Bull Brand, or in those of the [[California|California]] fruit company brands, as of Del Monte brand.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  How far apart should the two doses of soda be taken?
(A)  Three to six hours.

8. (Q)  What quantity at a time of the Castoria and how far apart?
(A)  Less than half a teaspoonful an hour apart for the first six doses.  Then may be given two to three hours apart, continuing for thirty-six hours.

The rubs should be given every evening when the body is ready to rest.

The water, the milk - in its precaution - is to meet those conditions as are seen produced in the assimilating system of the body.  Hence when the conditions are NORMAL again, this not necessarily kept up.

9. (Q)  How much of the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] can be taken at the time?
(A)  Not more than tablespoonful.  This may be given three to four times a day.

10. (Q)  Would it be best to dilute this a little?
(A)  Certainly!  but of the juice a tablespoonful.  May be diluted to one-third or less.  Depend upon the body.

11. We are through with this reading.
