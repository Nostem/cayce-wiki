# TEXT OF READING 536-3 M 11

**Reading [[536-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/7/1939  Sex: M  Age: 11  ReadingID: 3734

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [910], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [404], Mr. [462], and Mrs. [413]]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Now as we find, there have not been those corrections made in the cerebrospinal system as has been suggested.

3. Hence we have something of a disturbance that will need more immediate attention.

4. Through the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces which have been set up, there is the tendency for the tipping of the stomach, as well as for an excess of drainage in portions of the intestinal tract.

5. As we find, under such as Hudgins, we would correct the subluxations in the sacral, the 4th lumbar, and relax those areas in the lower portions of the 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 5th dorsal; stimulating the upper portions of the 5th, 4th, 3rd cervical.

6. Let the body rest during those periods of the first three or four days, as these first administrations are given; which would be given preferably every day, until three are given, - they may then be given twice a week, until there is sufficient to have not only adjusted the subluxations but to have quieted the nerve forces through the system.

7. Such stimulation, for the first two or three treatments, will tend to make for some more nausea.  Hence the system should be gently cleansed with the taking of those properties the basis of which is the activity of Senna; or Castoria AND Syrup of Figs combined, or alternated.

8. We find that the giving of Codiron will tend to stimulate the diet; as well as plenty of oatmeal given of mornings, - this especially made from the cut oats rather than the rolled oats, and cooked for a LONG period.

9. The dosage of the Codiron in the beginning, for this body, would be two tablets or pellets; one in the morning and one in the noon or afternoon.  AFTER these have been taken for four or fivedays, then take two of morning and two in the afternoon.

10. Have plenty of cream or milk given at all times.

11. Chicken broth and rice would also be well for the body during the first ten days or two weeks.

12. Then we should find by that period sufficient of strength and vitality gained to be in the normal reactions.

13. Do these as we find for the better conditions for this body, [536].

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Did Dr. Frischkorn carry out the Reading correctly?
(A)  Do as we have given.

16. (Q)  Has he any kind of growth in his intestines?
(A)  The condition may produce one, unless - as we have indicated - there is the immediate attention to those portions of the system!

17. (Q)  Does he have streptococcus?
(A)  NO!

18. (Q)  Was he given the right medicines?  If so, would it be of benefit to continue?
(A)  DO AS WE HAVE GIVEN!

19. We are through with THIS Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(Directions for doctor were enclosed - [see 536-3, Par. R1].)
