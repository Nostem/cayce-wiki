# TEXT OF READING 1932-2 M 31

**Reading [[1932-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/2/1934  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 8383

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [778], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, Esther Wynne, Helen Storey and Helen Ellington.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       Atlanta, Ga.

1. GC:  you will have before you the information given through these sources for [1932], in his Life Reading of Dec. 21, 1932, in connection with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] who is now living in ..., Ga., and his life's work.  You will answer the questions which he has submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and the information that has been given respecting the developments and the associations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in meeting those conditions that become - and may become - as experiences in the development, mentally, materially and spiritually, in that entity known as [1932].

3. As to those suggestions that have been made through these sources, there has not been - possibly owing to the lack of the UNDERSTANDING - the correct applications in connections with the [[France|French]] manufacturers of odors [Mr. [274]'s firm, perhaps?].

4. As the entity has prepared, and is preparing self for that line of study, thought and application in relation to [[Law of Moses|the law]] that governs the relationships of individuals and groups and associations and organizations and countries and organizations between such, then these connections would have been - and yet, as we find, may be made - a source of a development that would become the more advantageous associations.

5. To be sure, within the environs of the city near to the dwelling place of the body, there are those of the nature or acting in the capacity as representing the [[France|French]] country in its associations with that particular portion of the vicinity and the connections in that country.

6. Through such a channel there may come the contacts that may be necessary for the entity, even in that line.  And we would take the examination in December for same, and make the connections for those things that would be related to the relations of those of that country, represented by those in that particular line of endeavor.  And these connections would make for the channel through which the associations of the body, in its connections with the firms, make possible the dealing with such conditions.

7. For it would be necessary that these be carried to, or through, better or more perfected organization of law firms than of the self alone.  Yet the individual connection may be made through the representatives in that particular vicinity, by the entity itself.  In a manner, as it may be called in common or very unpopular parlance, take it cold turkey - and you'll find you may make the connection and association through the very activities that may be brought about in connections with the whole relationships of the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Was the proposition which came to him the one referred to in the reading; that is, that he be Georgia agent for a [[France|French]] doctor's medical discovery?
(A)  No.  Rather in connection, as has been indicated, that the entity may be rather [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] or connection through which the firms may do business; or the representative of those firms doing business through those associations and connections in that particular line as indicated, see?  Go then, as indicated, to those that are in the Consulate Service, that act in the capacity as the representatives in the city of the French legations, or the French associations, connections, for that particular type of product indicated.  And this may be seen, in connection, through the same channels in which the body is so closely associated.

10. (Q)  How should he go about contacting the proper parties?
(A)  It's just been given!  Do that!

Then, when the effort has been made in these directions, we will find that there will be set in motion those things that will bring about that necessary to be done.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file

[EC's first words on awaking from above reading:  "Didn't you get any reading?"]
