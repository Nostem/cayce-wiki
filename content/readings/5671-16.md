# TEXT OF READING 5671-16 F 34

**Reading [[5671-16]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14098

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5671].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, the conditions as cause the uneasiness are more from those of a mental worry, producing a reaction through the nerve forces of the system, than of PHYSICAL conditions; though the physical reactions are apparent in the condition as is produced in the system, following out much of that as has been given.

2. We would, then, have - for the period - those of the changes in the manipulations, being mindful of the diet and of the irrigations to ELIMINATE the poisons.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What is making the itch so much worse?
(A)  Just as given, this is reflex in the system from mental conditions, poison accumulations.  Hence the irrigations.

4. (Q)  Please give treatment for relief immediately.
(A)  Irrigation.

5. (Q)  Has the diet been followed correctly?
(A)  As has been given, be mindful more of the diet, for these have been allowed to slip CONSIDERABLE.  Too much potash has been in the system, and this NATURALLY comes for the reaction.

6. (Q)  Can the body give her baby anything for the whooping cough?
(A)  That as has been outlined for whooping cough.  Can give it anything, but this would be helpful - as has been outlined for same through these same suggestions.  Use occasionally a few drops of the Syrup of Squill - three to five drops may be given two or three times a day.  When the cough is severe, give five to ten drops of KEROSENE - in sugar or plain. MASSAGE the body thoroughly - especially through the upper dorsal region - with equal parts of olive oil and tincture of myrrh.  Heat the oil and add the myrrh.  THIS will relieve the condition.  When the nausea comes, if there is given the juice of onions - not with that that is boiled in water, but heated and the juice pressed out - this is the best thing for pneumonia that may be given!

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
