# TEXT OF READING 5208-1 F 30 (?) (Artist, Oil Painter)

**Reading [[5208-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/15/1944  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 13344

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5208], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Wash.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, there are serious disturbances where there has been the injury to the spine, there are pressures, though not of such natures that these may not be bridged if that attitude is held which has sustained the body so long.  For these are worth while.

4. We would use the low electrical treatments of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], and we find the body would get help.  To be sure, the nature of these is the low form of electrical forces, and these will work with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body; and where there are hindrances, as here, we will, with the massage, bridge these much.

5. We would carry in the Appliance the [[Iodine|Iodine]], but let this be charged in the Appliance.  Use, then, one of two pounds of the Copper Sulphate and three ounces of the Tincture of [[Iodine|Iodine]] suspended in it, but use this as the Radio-Active. One plate, the copper pole, use a copper plate; the other that which is the nickel pole, use a nickel plate.  Both of these would be made small.  The copper plate would be attached first to the right wrist, the nickel plate to the left ankle.  These should be on wrist and ankle where the pulse is felt the stronger, see?  These should be charged three amperes for thirty minutes on the low or slow charge, in charging the Appliance.  Do not put Charcoal in the solution.

6. It will be necessary to recharge these and to keep plates very clean.  Polish them with emery or sandpaper when, or before, putting on and when taking off.

7. These [[Vibration|vibrations]] would be used thirty minutes each day, with the massage, and we should bring definite improvements to the body.

8. After the use of the Appliance, massage the body with an equal portion of Olive Oil and Peanut Oil.  This is to be used on the spine; not too close, of course, to where there has been the pressures, but gradually closer, and then on the limbs and the feet.  These should be massaged away from the body.  Then, when massaging the spine itself, massage downward, see, in a circular motion, and we will make better conditions.

9. The diets have been very good, but keep those foods which are nerve and body-building.  Plenty of raw vegetables, as well as the cooked.  These should be kept very well balanced, but use at least two above the ground to one below the ground. Fish, fowl and lamb, but never fried foods, if these are to be taken at all.  In regard to these, to be sure, it depends upon the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] respecting these.  They each carry those elements for body-building.

10. Ready for Questions.

11. (Q)  What should be used for massage?
(A)  Olive oil and Peanut oil.

12. (Q)  What is the condition of my nerves and spine?
(A)  As has just been indicated, these are pressures, but not sufficient that these may not be bridged.

13. (Q)  Does this cause the paralysis?
(A)  This, of course, causes paralysis.

14. (Q)  What is condition of kidneys?
(A)  As from the pressure in the areas, these do not cause undue stress, though there are times, of course, when there are those reactions from the pancreas that cause disturbance. That is why fats should be eliminated from the diet, see?

15. (Q)  What is causing present threat of temperature?
(A)  More anxiety than other conditions.

16. (Q)  Should I go to Marion Frenche's school for paralysis correctment this summer and fall?
(A)  Not necessarily, if there is a good masseuse to be had each day.  This treatment could be given even by those about the body, if these will be consistently done.  The massage would be given in a circular motion.  Don't hurry through with it, but go down the spine, on either side of the spine, not so much on the spine itself.

17. (Q)  Would E. L. S. be a good one for giving the massage?
(A)  Very good one.

18. (Q)  From a physical standpoint, is it advisable for me to marry?
(A)  After this treatment is used for at least nine months to a year - why not?

19. (Q)  Did H. O. U. help me?
(A)  At a time, but we wouldn't advise renewing this.

20. We are through with this reading.

Copy to self (extra copy for Mr. Godfrey - for preparing the Appliance to be used in this individual case - also article explaining theory of this Appliance)
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
