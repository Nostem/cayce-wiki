# TEXT OF READING 2299-7 M 1

**Reading [[2299-7]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/10/1941  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 9051

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:50 to 4:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. There is still a great deal of congestion in throat and head.

4. We would use closer the fumes of the croup cup as indicated.

5. About two hours apart we would give internally one drop of Compound Tincture of Benzoin in a little water or honey, until the cough is alleviated - or until the phlegm in head and throat has cleared.

6. Keeping up all the rest of the applications suggested, we find that these offer the better conditions for the body.

7. Keep massaging with the [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]] compound; back of the head, along with spine, across the face and nose, you see.

8. Be sure to keep up the intestinal antiseptic (the Glyco- Thymoline) for the drainage to the stomach and bowels.  Of course, this will be hard upon this particular system owing to the tenderness through same.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother - Wired also as follows:
"    " Ass'n

USE CROUP CUP FUMES CLOSER GIVE EVERY TWO HOURS ONE DROP COMPOUND TINCTURE BENZOIN IN LITTLE HONEY CONTINUE OTHER APPLICATIONS.
