# TEXT OF READING 1541-11 F 62 (Housewife, Baptist, Flower

**Reading [[1541-11]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/31/1941  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 7494

Business, [Grade School Teacher Before Marriage]) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of January, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self, Mrs. [1541], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1541], Miss [1548] and Mrs. [1523].]

Born December 13, 1878, in Spencer County, [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], 36 mi. 
from [[Kentucky|Louisville]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '34 - changes - in the mental - '27 - anxieties - '12 - changes - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called Mrs. [1541].

4. In choosing that to be given from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, there are varied experiences or interpretations that might be indicated.  These are chosen with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling it to understand and interpret in self much of the [[Dreams|dreams]] or [[Visions|visions]], and many of the things that have come to pass in the material manifestations that have been questions - that have brought sorrow and gladness; anxieties and assuredness.

5. For, all are a part of the material experience.  For each [[Soul|soul]] enters material manifestations not by chance but purposefully; that the entity may indeed be a manifestation of that [[Free Will|choice]] or ideal concerning spiritual laws, and the advent of Him who IS the way, the truth, the light.

6. Not that this interpretation would intend to intimate any dogma or tenets, but that which IS [[Ideal|the ideal]] should be stressed; not as to a tenet but as a LIVING thing - known and interpreted in the life, in the associations with others, as such.

7. As to urges latent and manifested, - in materiality things ARE in relationships with others because of activities and choices of individuals.  So thine own experience, thine own sphere of consciousness is stressed because of that thou hast chosen to do.  For, as He gave, "I came not to take away [[Law of Moses|the law]], but to fulfill the same."

8. So in self, as He gave, thou shalt give an account unto thy God for the deeds done in the body.  What body?  That material, mental or [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]].  All are one and are subject to their OWN urges.  This has been the experience of the entity, as it has analyzed same in the material and mental life of self and those reliant upon the entity for directing the [[Free Will|choice]] through formative years; as well as what the entity has done ABOUT the glorifying rather than the justifying, by and through faith in Him who is the way of life.

9. These urges are given that should be interpreted in the experience and expression:

10. Minimize the faults.

11. As ye would be forgiven, forgive others.

12. Magnify the virtues, for the Lord thy God is a jealous God. So in thy own experience a soft word turneth away wrath, grievous words stirreth up anger.

13. The sowing of the seed of the spirit bringeth peace.  The sowing of the seed of strife bringeth contention.

14. The urges latent and manifested from the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in other realms express themselves, then, in the [[Dreams|dream]], the [[Visions|vision]], the hopes, the fears.  Those manifested from the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in materiality express themselves in relationships, in awareness, in consciousness, in the relationships through temporal things.  All partake of the unison of truth in so far as they are CONSCIOUSLY a part of the entity.

15. Astrologically, we find Mercury, Mars, Venus and Jupiter as the ruling forces.

16. Hence the high mental abilities; the abilities to direct, to teach, to magnify the experiences of others by precept and by example.

17. In Venus we see the love of nature, the love of the beautiful - spiritually manifested; the appreciation of home and its ties, as well as its troubles.

18. We find in Jupiter the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] to which the entity has gradually grown in its expression in the material plane.  Though it may often find material expression in hopes, in faith, these are growths.  For, ye grow in [[Grace|grace]] and in knowledge, and in understanding.

19. Thus that which has been IS, because of the application of truth in relationships to others.

20. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], - all may not be here given.  For, as has been expressed in the [[Astrology|astrological]] interpretation, growth, development, advancement is a part of the entity's experience; and much that has been - through some periods in this [[Incarnation|sojourn]] - the entity has haply grown beyond, though it may find expression in those with whom the entity may in the present be associated.

21. These are a part of the experience, and the influences that give expressions in the emotions of the body:

22. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity, and the present sojourn; during those periods of turmoil when there were the activities in Dearborn.

23. There the entity was engaged rather in that activity which would to the entity today be quite questionable, yet in those periods the entity brought helpful influences to many; those activities in which many of those disappointed, many of those that sought rather the broad than the narrow way, were set again in the way of material and spiritual hopes.

24. For, the entity then was the keeper of the Inn in which most of those stayed who were a part of the dance hall activities.

25. With the periods of turmoils, and the necessity for the evacuation of the fort, the entity with associates and companions crossed into those areas now a part of the land of its present nativity.

26. Then later, with the turmoils arising in the central portion of the dark and bloody land, the entity settled near to what is now Williamsburg and Yorktown.

27. These brought periods of advancement and development for the entity, not only because of its return to nature and the activities in and about same, but because of the associations and the helps the entity made for the settlers during those periods of activity.

28. Then the entity was known as [[Grace|Grace]] Winslow.  The entity gained, lost and gained through those experiences.

29. In the present many of those associations in family, in friends and neighbors, have become a part of the entity's experience.  In many of these the entity has found help; in many of these the entity has found turmoil and wonderment, disappointments and yet blessings.

30. These are a part of the entity's experience, and yet in the present such has been the greater part of the material associations.

31. Just as from now there may come the greater appreciation of those experiences of the entity in a sojourn before that, in what is a part of the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] - and that land questioning in the present.

32. For, the entity was among those of the group called the holy women, or those that saw, that knew many of the associations with the disciples, the apostles, and the helpful, direct influence of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] himself.

33. For, the entity then was the mother of [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]]'s wife, once healed by [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] himself.

34. Those periods brought to the entity the closer associations through the latter periods of the activity.  For, being healed as it were, physically, by [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] himself, the entity's sojourn in the earth was long and varied, and would make a book in itself.

35. The entity's ability to write in the present should be manifested, especially in the stories as concerning the life of the Master as might be read by children.  For, these the entity would be able to do; as it brought through those experiences the channel through which the early church attracted others; as many lived and drew upon those truths as illustrated by the entity.

36. During that experience the entity had TWO activities; the growing of plants, especially adaptable for the [[Healing|healing]] of the body, and flowers; and also the ministering to the sick.  Not as a nurse, - rather as a comforter.  For the entity at periods acted in the capacity as a hired mourner, as was the custom of that period.

37. The name then was Esdrela.

38. In the present, as indicated, - by the attempts and the applying of self in those directions of writing of those things pertaining to the life and teachings of the Master, as to the manner of physical ministration of teachings to the young, there may come much joy, much hope, much pleasure, - yea, much material gain might come to the entity.  [See 1541-11, Par. R12.]

39. Before that that entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when there were the attempts to spread abroad the activities, when there had been the unison of activities by the choices of those of many lands to bring peace.

40. For, there is no expression of the divine save where peace reigns within the individual, and where that as a united effort may be the part of the experience of a city, a country, a nation.  For, all were of one mind, that the glory of the Lord God might be manifested, in all phases of human experience through those periods of activity.

41. It was following the return of the Priest, the unifying of the teachings from [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] as well as from Said, [[Og (Land)|Og]] and On. All of these the entity understood.

42. Hence the ability in the present as the teacher, the instructor, has been and is a part of the experience. For, there the entity was among those who aided in preparing those who were to be emissaries and teachers throughout the the various lands to which they were sent.

43. The name then was It-El-Al.

44. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

45. Many fields of service and activity have been opened, have been completed in the entity's experience.

46. That as it fulfilled in its activities in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] and in Laodicea, and throughout portions even of [[Greece|Greece]] - as it journeyed through the periods of activity, now opens itself for practical application - in the abilities latent that have NOT been manifested in this experience.

47. Not of self may it be accomplished, but through trusting and finding in self the answer that "I must be about my Father's business."

48. Ready for questions.

49. (Q)  Why do I feel such an abhorrence of the Catholic religion, and how may I overcome this?  [See 1541-11, Par. R3.]
(A)  This arises from those periods of the controversy which arose between the followers of Cephas and the Paulites; that made for those changes wrought in the foundation of that first or early group.

Overcome same in giving expression in those manners in which the young will weigh well in their own lives the teachings of the Master, without condemning any - as He, yet requiring that all be sowers in the life journey.

50. (Q)  With the aid of my daughter [1548] would it be advisable to enlarge my flower business?
(A)  Be very well to enlarge, but enlarge more your abilities in writing!  There will be more joy, less hardship, more pleasure, and a LOT more money!

51. (Q)  Would it be advisable to sell our present home site and build a smaller place?  If so, when and through what channels?
(A)  The regular channels.  For, in the next year or year and a half property will be valued much more highly than it will be for many years afterward!  [GD's note:  It became a boom area during war years '42 - '43.]

52. (Q)  How have I been associated in the past with my present husband, [2051] and how may I best aid him to meet our material needs?
(A)  Associations in the experience before this in Dearborn, as well as in the journeys through the varied lands and the settlings in this.  Ye upheld him through the experience, though he was not thy husband - until the later settlings in the land of the present nativity.  These will be the experiences through which mental, spiritual and material aid and help may be given.  In the material aid, - this may be best had by writing.

53. (Q)  What past association has brought about the differences between my daughter [1523] and myself, as to our respective opinions and activities?
(A)  Parallel the experience of these in the various periods and we will find reason sufficient.

54. (Q)  What was the name of the Inn in the Dearborn experience? [Question submitted by [1523].]
(A)  Do you want this, or more of the other experiences?

55. (Q)  What have been my past associations with the following, and how may I best aid them in the present:  My daughter, [1548].
(A)  These may best be found by paralleling the various experiences.  The closer association was in the [[Palestine|Palestine]] land.  For, THERE we find there were the needs for not only the strengthening in the spiritual imports but as to the [[Purpose|purposes]] for same.  Hence more help, more strength may be given in the mental and spiritual than in material or other manners.  For, the entity listens but keeps her own opinions!

56. (Q)  My son, [2460].
(A)  These had best be found by paralleling.

57. (Q)  My son, [2385].
(A)  These will be found to have been in the experience before this, as well as in others.

58. (Q)  My son, [4930].
(A)  Most in the Dearborn experience.

59. (Q)  What was my past association with my father, [...] deceased; and why do I feel he was so much ahead of his time?
(A)  Closely associated; for he was the younger brother of [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] and Andrew.

60. (Q)  Also my [little] daughter [...], whose short experience with the family brought so much love, and left our lives so empty upon leaving?
(A)  The daughter in the Palestine experience, who suffered martyrdom there.

61. We are through for the present.

> & fell at Jesus' feet & said, "Depart from me, for I am a
> sinful man!"  For he was astonished & all that were with Him,
> seeing such a large catch of fish.
> 
> James & John, sons of Zebedee, who were partners of Simon
> were also surprised at the large catch of fishes.  Jesus
> told them not to fear for He would make them fishers of
> men from now on.  As soon as they brot their fishing boats
> to land, they left them & straight way followed Jesus &
> became His disciples.
> 
> Afterwards Jesus saw Levi, the son of Alphaeus sitting at
> the receipt of customs for he was a tax collector & Jesus
> said to Levi, "Follow me!" & he followed Him.
> 
> Now Levi made a great feast in his own house & invited his
> friends & there were many publicans at this feast & Jesus
> & His disciples were there.  The Scribes and the Pharisees
> murmured against the disciples & asked "Why sitteth your
> Master with publicans & sinners?"  Jesus told them the
> people who were well or whole didn't need a physician, but
> those who were sick.  Jesus also told them He came to call
> the sinners & not the righteous to repentence.
> 
> JESUS HEALS THE WITHERED HAND
> 
> Jesus again went into the synagogue on the Sabbath day &
> there was a man there with a withered hand & the people
> watched Jesus to see if He would heal him on the Sabbath
> day.  Jesus told the man to "Stand forth!"  When Jesus
> asked the people "If it was lawful to [do] good on the
> Sabbath day or to do evil, to save life or to kill," but
> no one answered & Jesus was grieved at the hardness of
> their hearts, & said to the man, "Stretch forth thine
> hand!" & the man obeyed & his hand was made well.  Then
> the Pharisees were filled with madness & went out & communed
> with the Herodians how they might destroy Jesus.  [Mark 3,
> Luke 6]
> 
> So Jesus withdrew himself to the sea & a great multitude
> followed when they heard what Jesus had done.  So Jesus
> asked His disciples that a small ship wait near them for
> fear the crowds might become too great & press so great
> that He could not speak to the multitude; for they pressed
> upon Him because so many wanted to touch him & be healed.
> Many unclean spirits fell down before Him & said, "Thou art
> the Son of God!" & they were healed, but Jesus charged them
> than they should not tell who He was.  Jesus went into the
> mountain & prayed all night & when He came down, He called
> those out of the crowd - those whom He wanted to be his
> disciples.  He chose 12 that [He] might send forth to
> preach & He gave them power to heal sickness & to cast out
> devils.
> 
> Now SIMON, he surnamed [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]]; JAMES & JOHN, sons of Zebedee
> surnamed Boanerges which is "sons of thunder."  ANDREW &
> PHILLIP & BARTHOLOMEW, THOMAS & JAMES, son of Alphaeus;
> [[Judas|JUDAS]] - son of James, THADDEUS & SIMON, the Canaanite &
> [[Judas|Judas Iscariot]] which [[Judas Betrayal|betrayed]] Him.
> 
> Then they went into a house & the multitude crowded around,
> so they couldn't even eat bread & when His friends heard
> of Him, they went out & lay hold for they said "He is beside
> himself" & the scribes said, "He is casting out devils,
> by the Prince of Devils."  Jesus asked them "How could Satan
> cast out Satan & a kingdom divided against itself cannot
> stand?"
> 
> While Jesus was discussing this, His mother & brethren came
> calling for Him & when they told him of it, He answered,
> "Who is my mother or my brethren?"  He looked around Him
> & said to them "Whosoever shall do [[Free Will|the will]] of God, the
> same is My brother & My sister & My mother."
> 
> [[Sermon on the Mount|SERMON ON THE MOUNT]]
> 
> Jesus' fame went out into the land & great multitudes
> followed Him, & when He saw the multitudes, He went
> into the mountains & sat down.  His disciples came to
> Him and He taught them the important things of life &
> the things for which they would be blessed for.  Those who
> were poor in spirit or humble in spirit would inherit the
> kingdom of heaven & those who were sad would be comforted;
> & those who were meek would inherit the earth or those who
> sought to be good & know the things that are right would be
> filled with righteousness.  Jesus told them that those who
> were merciful to others would also receive mercy & those who
> are pure in heart that is have pure thots & pure motives
> shall see God.
> 
> Those who were peacemakers would be called children of God
> & if they were persecuted for doing right of Jesus' sake,
> they would be blessed - the kingdom of God would be theirs
> & if men said evil things about them falsely for Jesus'
> sake, they must be happy and rejoice because their reward
> would be very great - for the prophets before them were
> persecuted.
> 
> Jesus told them they were the salt of the earth & thru them
> the world would be brot to Jesus' way of living - they were
> also the light of the world & must not put their light under
> a bushel.  That is, carry it where the light would not be
> seen, but live their lives so those who saw their good works
> would glorify God in heaven.
> 
> Jesus told them He had come to fulfill [[Law of Moses|the law]] & not destroy
> it & those who would break His laws or commandments would be
> called the least in the kingdom & whosoever shall do & teach
> them, the same shall be called great in the kingdom of
> heaven.
> 
> Jesus also told His disciples that unless their goodness
> was greater than that of the scribes & Pharisees, they
> should not enter the kingdom of heaven.
> 
> Jesus taught them many things.  One, was that they must
> not kill nor be angry with their brother, not to follow
> the old law which said "an eye for an eye & a tooth for
> a tooth."  But [to] love their enemies & do good & pray
> for those who hate you.
> 
> Jesus also told them not to pray on the street corners where
> they could be seen by passers by, but pray in secret & God
> would reward them openly.  Then Jesus told them an example
> of how to pray by giving them [[Lord's Prayer|the Lord's Prayer]].  Jesus told
> them "not to lay up treasures on earth where they could be
> so easily lost or destroyed, but lay up treasures in heaven
> living a good life & no moth or rust would destroy it."
> 
> Jesus too told His disciples, not to worry about what they
> were to eat or drink or what they were to wear for if they
> would just look at the birds; they do not sow or reap, but
> the heavenly Father feeds them & cares for them & knows if
> one falls to the ground.
> 
> He also told them "to look at the lilies in the field, they
> do not toil or spin, but [[Solomon|Solomon]] in all his glory was not
> dressed like one of these and if God did this for the birds
> & flowers, how much more would He care for His children who
> are made in his likeness?  Their heavenly Father knows just
> what they need."
> 
> Jesus told His disciples not to judge one another for they
> would also be judged in the same manner that they passed
> judgement on others; that they should watch their own
> faults & not look for faults in others.  Also, every good
> there brings forth good fruit & those trees which bear bad
> fruit will be destroyed.  [Matt. 7]
> 
> Jesus told His disciples of a wise man who built his house
> on a rock & how the winds blew & rains came & fell on this
> house, but because it had a solid foundation the house stood
> in this storm [and] was not blown down.  But, He also told
> of a foolish man who built his house on sand & when the storm
> came & the winds blew & the rains came - this house fell
> because the foundation was soft & shifting - great was the
> fall of this house.
> 
> When Jesus had finished telling this story, the people were
> astonished & said "He taught us as one having authority &
> not as the scribes."
> 
> JESUS & HIS [[Ministry of Jesus|MINISTRY]] OF [[Healing|HEALING]]
> 
> When Jesus came down from the mountain where he was teaching,
> a great multitude followed Him & a leper came to Him & said
> "Lord, if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean or well" &
> Jesus was very sorry for the leper & put forth His hand &
> immediately the leper was cleansed.  Jesus told him to "tell
> no one," but to go & tell the Priest & offer a gift that
> [[Moses|Moses]] commanded for a testimony unto them.  [Matt. 8]
> 
> Jesus also healed the centurion's servant.  The centurion
> came to Jesus & told him how sick his servant was of palsy,
> Jesus told him [that] He would come & heal him.  But the
> centurion said, "I am not worthy for you to come under my
> roof, but just speak the word only & my servant shall be
> healed."  Jesus marvelled at his faith & said "I have not
> found so great a faith, not even in Israel!"  He told the
> centurion to go his way, that as he believed it would be
> done unto him & that very hour his servant was healed.
> 
> On the Sabbath, Jesus taught in the synagogue & afterwards
> at [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]]'s home where his wife's mother lay sick of a great
> fever & Jesus touched her hand & the fever left her & she
> arose from her bed & ministered unto them.  That day when
> evening came, the people brot many who were sick & also
> those possessed of the devil & Jesus healed all that were
> sick.
> 
> One of the scribes came to Jesus & told him he would follow
> Him where ever He went & Jesus said to him, "The foxes have
> holes & the birds of the air have nests, but the Son of man
> has no place to lay his head."  Jesus wanted him to know
> that following Him would not be easy & He must be willing
> to endure hardships if he was His follower.  [Matt. 8]
> 
> HEALING THE DEMONICS OF GADARENES
> 
> Later Jesus entered into a ship & His disciples with Him.
> And Jesus said, "Let us go over to the other side," [and]
> as they sailed, a great storm came up & their ship filled
> with water & they were in great danger.  Jesus being very
> tired had gone to sleep on a pillow in the back of the ship
> & the disciples came to Him & said, "Master, carest thou not
> that we perish?"  Jesus arose & rebuked the wind & said to
> the sea, "Peace!  Be still!"  The wind ceased & there was
> a great calm & Jesus asked His disciples why were they
> afraid, where was their faith?  And they were afraid &
> wondered & said "What manner of man is this?  Even the
> winds & water obey Him!"  [Mark 4, Luke 8]
> 
> Then the ship reached the other side & they were in the land
> of the Gadarenes.  In this land, a great many swine were kept
> which was not approved by the [[Law of Moses|Mosaic Law]].
> 
> In this land were 2 men possessed with an evil spirit & when
> they saw Jesus, they asked Him to go away & not bother them.
> They recognized Jesus and called Him Son of God & begged Him
> not to torment them for Jesus commanded the evil spirit to
> come out of these men.
> 
> These men were very strong & no one had been able to bind
> them for they broke even the chains that were put upon them.
> Now these evil spirits were called Legion for there were
> many of them & they asked Jesus to let them go into the
> swine, many of which were feeding nearby on the mountainside.
> Jesus suffered the evil spirits to come out of the men & go
> into the swine & they ran violently down a steep place into
> the lake & were drowned in the lake.  The men who took care
> of the swine ran into the city & told everything that had
> happened to these men who were possessed of the devils.
> 
> Then all the whole city came out to meet Jesus.  When they
> saw Him they begged Him to leave their coasts for they were
> not pleased that Jesus interfered with their business &
> caused this large herd of about 2000 swine to be destroyed
> & they saw these men who had been possessed with devils
> clothed & in their right mind & they were afraid.  Then
> Jesus went into the ship & returned back again.  [Mark 6,
> Luke 8]  Now this man who had been healed asked Jesus that
> He might go with Him, but Jesus would not allow this, but
> said "Go home & tell your friends what great things the Lord
> had done for you" & the happy man departed & began to publish
> in Decapolis how great things had been done for him and all
> men began to marvel.  When Jesus returned, the people were
> waiting & received him gladly.
> 
> When Jesus & His disciples were passing thru a cornfield on
> the Sabbath day, they were hungry & pulled the corn & ate
> it.  The Pharisees saw them & found fault with Jesus & His
> disciples & said "they do things that are unlawful" because
> the Pharisees were very strict about keeping the Sabbath.
> Jesus asked the Pharisees if they didn't remember how David
> & those with him entered the House of God & ate the shew
> bread which only the priests were supposed to eat.
> [Matt. 12]
> 
> RAISING OF JARIUS' DAUGHTER & [[Woman of Revelation|THE WOMAN]] WHO HAD BEEN
> ILL 12 YEARS
> 
> Jarius was ruler of the synagogue & he came to Jesus & fell
> down before Him begging Him to come to his house for his
> daughter of 12 yrs was very ill & lay dying.  Now Jesus
> started to Jarius' house but a great throng gathered around
> Him & hindered Him.  In this crowd of people a woman who
> had been ill for 12 yrs & had spent all her living on
> physician's & none could heal her; came close enough to Jesus
> to touch the border of His garment & immediately she was
> well.  Jesus knew at once that healing had gone out from
> Him & asked "Who touched Me?"  Peter & those with Peter
> said, "Master, the multitude have pressed you.  Why ask
> 'who touched me?'"  Jesus said, "Somebody touched me for
> I perceive virtue has gone out from Me!"  When [[Woman of Revelation|the woman]]
> saw that Jesus knew, she came to Jesus trembling & fell down
> before Jesus.  [She] told Him & all people why she touched
> Him & how she was healed and Jesus said to her, "Daughter,
> be of good comfort, thy faith hath made thee whole - go in
> peace."  While Jesus was yet speaking, there came a man from
> the ruler of the synagogue saying, "Thy daughter is dead, do
> not trouble Jesus," but when Jesus heard the message to the
> ruler, He answered him and said, "Fear not.  Believe only &
> she shall be made whole."  When Jesus came to the house, He
> only allowed Peter, James, & John & the father & mother of
> the maiden to go with Him.
> 
> All wept & wailed but Jesus said, "Weep not, she is not dead
> but sleepeth."  They laughed & made fun of Him for they knew
> she was dead.
> 
> Jesus put them all out & took her by the hand & called to her
> & said, "Maid arise!"  Her spirit came again and she arose
> & Jesus told them to give her meat & her parents were
> astonished, but he charged them to tell no one what was done.
> [Mark 5]

> JESUS HEALS THE CENTURION'S SERVANT

> After Jesus had given a charge to His disciples, He went

> dear to his master was very ill & when he heard of Jesus,
> he sent to Him the elders of the Jews begging him to come
> & heal his servant.  When they came to Jesus then told Him
> how worthy this centurion was, for he loved his nation &
> had built us a synagogue.
> 
> When Jesus came near to the Centurion's house, the centurion
> came out & [said], "Lord, do not trouble thyself to enter
> my house, for I'm not worthy that thou should enter under
> my roof; neither did I feel I was worthy to come unto thee.

> man set under authority - having under me, soldiers & I
> say unto one, 'Come' & he comes; another 'Go', & he goeth."
> Jesus marvelled at him & turned him about & said unto
> the people that followed him, "I say unto you, I have not
> found so great a faith in all Israel."
