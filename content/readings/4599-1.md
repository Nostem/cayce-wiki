## Text

Date: 1/18/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12501

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at Wellmore Apts., 77 & Broadway, [[New York City|New York City]], [[New York City|New York]], at the home of Mrs. Bamberger, on this 18th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mrs. Bamberger, via Mr. Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]].

[Edgar Cayce; Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce, Dr. Krauss, Mrs. Bamberger, Mr. and Mrs. Koff, and others.]

Time of Reading 
12:05 A. M. [[New York City|New York]] Time.               New York City, N.Y.

1. MHB:  You will give a complete physical diagnosis of this case, giving any abnormal conditions and cure for these conditions.  You will answer questions relating to this.

2. EC:  Now we find the physical conditions in this body in that stage of development in the physical world, and is one passing through the physical plane for its development in and through this plane.

3. In the physical conditions in this body, we find one losing in the resistive forces in the physical, and taking on that condition that will bring the change, or the disintegration in the physical, for we find as these:

4. In the structural portions in body, that element necessary in its development beyond those conditions in which the natural forces in its transition had to do with that development, for with the arising of this condition, as we see at present, we find these resistance are losing their effect in this physical plane.

5. Give that care and attention to these conditions necessary for the alleviation of that portion that give to the physical the rest in the body - physical.

6. Then we find that the transition will bring to those about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] that necessary for those conditions to bind then to that plane in the realms beyond.

7. As to the alleviation, give those in that form as has been applied in the system.  Do that.

8. (Q)  What are the physical conditions of the organs at the present time?
(A)  Those of the conditions that bring to each and every organ in the torso of system an exaggerated condition in their functioning, bringing to the portions of the air forces in body the destructive forces, or the transition in their elements.

9. (Q)  There is then no cure for these conditions?
(A)  In its present state, the condition reaches that wherein the elements of the physical enter not into the curative forces in physical.

10. (Q)  How is the heart, and how is the cerebro-spinal system?
(A)  The heart's action, or pulsation, at the present time is 91.  The temperature 102 1/4.  The cerebro-spinal system, affections in the small portion of the spine, or that in the lumbar region shows the effect in structural conditions. While in the 1st cervical, the pressure raising the temperature.

11. (Q)  How is the brain?
(A)  With the temperature, and with the injections in the system, this becomes dormant in its co-relation with physical portions of body.

12. (Q)  How are the body's ears?
(A)  This, again, we find the effects of the conditions in structural portions, and in the cerebro-spinal affect through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]].
