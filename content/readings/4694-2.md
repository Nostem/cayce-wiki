# TEXT OF READING 4694-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4694-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/26/1911  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12645

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 26th day of April, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; E. D. N. [E. D. Noe?], Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno. Mr. [4694].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EDN:  You have before you Mr. [4694] who is in this room with you.  You will be able to see him.  Go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly and tell us how he is getting along, and if there should be any change in his treatment.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had him here before.  The condition is improved from what we have had before in the body, especially in the elimination in the circulation, which was in the particles or parts wherein the body was deficient.  We have here more of the red blood, more of resistive force in the body, in eliminating the used or waste matter from the body. The digestion is better.  The weariness or heaviness to the feet and lower extremities is not so severe.  We are taking more oxygen to the body than we have had before.

3. (Q)  Do you see anything further?
(A)  You see, we have his condition here now, as compared with what we had before.  First the difference in the circulation.  That is, of the blood, in the quantity of the red blood corpuscles in the body, and of the resistive force, or the gray matter in the nerve supply to the organism of the system; to the texture or the tone to that of the mind in itself over the matter in the body, through that of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] self in the body.  The action of the organs themselves, through those of the digestion, is better.  The organism of the nerve supply of the force, kept as it is, though, will produce a tautness or irritation to those of the lumbars, which we have here.

To keep the improvement in the condition in the body, as we have it at present, for bettering the condition, keep plenty of water into the system at all times.  Not too cold; and not too much of electrical force applied to the spine, else we will produce this predominant irritation to the lumbars, or that of the celiac ganglia.  Into the digestive organs, or to the stomach more of peptone and iron to act on the digestion, and the body will improve as it goes along.  Keep plenty of fresh air.  The body is improving but it needs time.  Keep it up.

4. (Q)  Would you not suggest any other treatment other than that which he has been getting?
(A)  Do this, here.
