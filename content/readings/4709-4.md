# TEXT OF READING 4709-4 M 45

**Reading [[4709-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/18/1924  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 12668

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 18th day of June, 1924, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [4709].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                   ..., Arkansas.

1. EC:  Now, we find there are other contradictory conditions existing in this body at the present time.  Condition is rather extraordinary.  From biological standpoint would be rather interesting to biologists.  Not satisfactory entirely to the individual, yet the body realizes and feels there is a great deal of difference in the body and that there is the response to the active principles as have been taken in the system, but the psychological effect of same in system has only been of indeterminate nature.  This we see manifest in the physical conditions at the present time.  While those conditions as have been given for this body to carry out, that the whole system may receive that incentive and that [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to create the equalization in the whole body, and an equilibrium necessary to produce the eliminating and assimilating forces to become normally balanced to each other, yet the whole system as at present become contradictory to itself, unless other conditions pathologically are affected in the system, with the properties now to be taken.  Hence, we would change somewhat the conditions to be accorded the system.  With the taking of the medicinal properties, which should be done following those already taken, we would give each morning a [[Vibration|vibration]], electrically driven, over the whole system, with the body lying prone, and especially would we apply this for the dorsal regions and across the sacral region.  That is, in giving the [[Vibration|vibration]] lateral to the cerebro-spinal, in dorsal region, across the body in sacral region, and rubbing the whole spine with cold water and irritating afterwards until the circulation is brought to the surface, taking then the medicinal properties as have been outlined.

2. Do that.  We will bring the best for this body, [4709].
