# TEXT OF READING 900-27 M 29

**Reading [[900-27]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/25/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5230

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.             [[New York City|New York City]], [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body [900], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the early morning of January 25, 1925, at his Apartment ..., [[New York City|New York City]].  You will give this in outline, and the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, and give it in such a manner as to be understandable to the mind of [900].

2. EC:  Yes, we have the conditions relating to same, and we find there was more than one presentation of conditions for this mind and entity at this time.

3. In the first, we find that of the condition wherein there was counsel given by someone close in business associations with the body, and this we find is the presentation of conditions that should be acted upon through that channel, for these are the conditions presented that have to do with the physical conditions for the body, [900], and should be acted upon in that manner.

4. In the second and third we have projections as of conditions that are to arise that will bring message and instruction to the body from across many waters.  These are as of those relative conditions as set in motion by thoughts of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], and the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, seeing those in action, brings about conditions that will be the representation of things seen at present in this state in which these are viewed from.  This [is] only then to be tabulated and acted upon when the time is correct.  Do not force an issue in such matters.  Rather view with intelligent understanding the gradual unfolding of what is meant by "Thoughts are Deeds, and may become [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]] or crimes."

5. Then, we would give as this, as instructions for [900].

6. In [[Visions|visions]] of partner and stocks, act at the conclusion of such conference.  Rest by letter.

7. We are through for the present.
