# TEXT OF READING 90-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[90-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/24/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 207

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [90].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Arizona|Arizona]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body Here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are some changes in the physical conditions of the body from that as we have had here before. Some show a little improvement.  Others appear the accentuation of those conditions as have been given.  The change in the climatic conditions has not proven as beneficial yet as might be hoped for.  This, however, may not be expected when the conditions as regarding the nerve system and the heart's action are taken into consideration.

4. We would use then - with those properties as have been given, and under the existent conditions - we would change to an inhalant, in this proportion and manner, for the asthmatic and spasmodic, and for the heart's condition, for the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from all of those will bring better results.

5. To 6 ounces pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol..............30 minims,
Benzosol.................5 minims,
Canadian Balsam..........5 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp....5 minims,
Tincture of Tolu (in solution)........35 minims,
Heroin (dissolved)....1/60 grain.

6. Use this as an inhalant when the spasmodic conditions appear, and this may be used a little at a time almost constantly - that is, until the body is perfectly relaxed - for this, as we see, acts as the relaxor, and is an antiseptic, and also a [[Healing|healing]] to the lung, the larynx, and those portions as are affected.

7. We would also have those corrections made in the cerebro-spinal system, osteopathically.  These would bring better conditions for the body.

8. The climatic conditions will assist under this stress.  Do not stop that of those properties to be taken internally, for these aid materially to the change in the blood supply - the Calcidin, you see.

9. (Q)  Did the body act wisely in changing climate?
(A)  If it will remain with the conditions, yes.

10. We are through for the present.
