# TEXT OF READING 4709-5 M 45

**Reading [[4709-5]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/29/1924  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 12669

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of September, 1924, in accordance with request made by the self, Mr. [4709].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:20 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   ..., Ark.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for the improvement of same.  You will also answer any questions relative to this body that I may ask.

2. EC:  We find there are some changes in the body since last we had it here.  Some have been for better, but the most have been for the worse condition in the body.  The conditions as we find them at the present time, this condition produced in the lower portion of the stomach, and in the duodenum, there producing the fermentation of foods, and the reaction in the gastric centers, producing gas and the pressure on all of the centers, and especially on that of the hypogastric plexus, near the umbilicus, to the right and above.  This we find is caused by the lacerations and ulcerations as produced in the system by this taxation and by adhesives as were given in times back.  These have not been entirely removed from the system.  Some have been assisted.  Others we find are not so good, for the sediments as taken in the system to produce the relaxing by the stimulation of the mucus forming condition in the body only produces the greater distress in the descending colon, for with the high hepatic circulation as produced by the raising of the circulation, hence producing the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the mucus forming [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the intestinal tract, only cause the system to become drained of the normal condition of mucus producing conditions in the intestinal tract.  Then the reaction in the colon produces the dryness in the ascending colon, and with the lesions as produced there from external conditions this only becomes aggravated.

3. Then to give the body the best and the greater relief to this whole system, we would, leaving off other conditions, adhere to this condition.  Take no water in the system save that carrying properties of elm and lithia.  Take no foods save those that carry the incentive for the proper producing condition in the system.  Namely these:

4. Whole wheat, pressed, cleansed and pressed, and this formed into a well prepared gruel, with the milk as would be used, with this only beet sugar, and sufficient milk to make such palatable to the body.  Do not take large quantities of this, but take it more often.  Graham crackers may be used, Junket may be used.  This we find will also assist the conditions in the liver's action in the body.  Also, at all times, wear the applicator of the [[Radium|radium]] applicator, but across this portion of the body, where the distress is caused; that is, the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus.

5. For the condition in the rectum, where the distress is caused, prepare a solution as an ointment of these properties:

6. To 3 ounces of butterfat, rendered you see fresh, no salt in same, only the oil of same, add 15 grains of Burton's Snuff, 1 minim [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], 2 minims Eucalyptol.  Mix well together.  Anoint the parts with the injector as would be used in a tube, would be inserted in rectum, causing these properties to be put high in the lower portion of the colon, using high enemas of oil, and use the high grade of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] for the rectum, that we may cleanse the whole colon, using these high enemas at least twice each week.  Do this, with also the stimulation at least once each week of the deep manipulation, osteopathically, over the whole system, and work this, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment, in conjunction with the properties applied; that is, stimulate the excretory system from the cerebro-spinal centers supplying the conditions in the body.  Do that, [4709].

7. (Q)  What causes the body to become depressed at times?
(A)  The pressure that is produced on the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus cuts off the blood supply through the sympathetic nerve system, directly cutting off the recuperative forces of the cell producing properties between brain and spleen.  Hence the depression, melancholia and insomnia, all produced form this pressure.

Follow these, and we will find the body bettered.

The applicator may be obtained from [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator Company, [[California|Los Angeles]], [[California|California]].
