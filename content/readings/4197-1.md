# TEXT OF READING 4197-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4197-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11944

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1927, in accordance with request made by self, through Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now, we find there are those conditions in the physical functioning of the body that cause distress.  While there are MANY conditions concerning the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] that are very good, yet these troublesome conditions - would they be allowed to remain - will bring about those constitutional effects that would be hard, and yet harder to remove from the system.

4. These conditions have to do with specific effects produced in the system, and in THIS condition effects - as well as that causing same - must be dealt with; for, as we see, this is of an undermining nature, and the effects are causing specific conditions or effects in physical functioning - as well as the cause itself being of a subtle nature.

5. These are effects, and these as conditions, then, as we see in the body:

6. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this shows the effect of those conditions which PRODUCE this condition, and is of that subtle nature - and the effect as is produced in this is as of a detrimental condition both to the heart's action and to the assimilating conditions and functioning of the organism.

7. THE NERVE SYSTEM, too, becomes involved in the condition, and the body becomes irritable, restless, and does not find that any application, or any properties, as yet taken, makes an EFFECTIVE condition - but still the condition remains, or - as it were - simply changes or shifts from one portion of the system being affected by same to the other.

8. The subtle condition consists in the effect as is produced in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body, especially those of the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the central portion of the system, as well as in the extremities and the effects produced on same.  Hence we find the thyroids, the condition about the appendix region, those in the generative system, are all involved in the condition.

9. To meet the needs of these effects, to meet the needs of the correction of the condition, THESE would be applied, as WE find, for this body:

10. First we would take these properties into the system:

11. Prepare [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] ten (10) grains, to eight (8) ounces distilled water.  Then we would prepare ten (10) grains BROMIDE of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to give (5) ounces of distilled water.

12. The dose would be, beginning with two (2) drops of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] and five (5) drops of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution, added together in half a glass of water, taken twice each day.

13. We would also apply those [[Vibration|vibrations]] along the whole cerebro-spinal system as are found in the Violet Ray.  These should be prepared and should be given each day as the body is ready to retire, see?

14. The changes as will be effective in these conditions will be for the better assimilation, the better elimination, the change in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and their effect in the system, to the throat, to the nostrils, to the digestive system, to the kidneys, and the mesenteric system.

15. After these have been taken for two (2) weeks, consistently and persistently - [[Baking Soda|Soda]] and Gold taken every day, the Violet Ray taken every evening - then we would leave these off, see, for five (5) days.  Then we would begin as these for medicinal properties, for the correction of the balance necessary in the blood and nerve supply - taking this:

Tincture Valerian.......................2 ounces,
Elixir Calisaya.........................1 ounce,
Tincture Stillingia...................1/2 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium......1/2 ounce,
Tincture of Capsici.....................3 minims,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]........................5 minims.

16. All of this in a carrier, see?  Peptotol - to make eight (8) ounces.

17. Shake solution well together, and the dose would be half a teaspoonful in water three times each day.

18. Refrain from meats, save fish or fowl.  Eat vegetables that grow ABOVE the ground, and not below.

19. Follow these instructions and directions until the full amount of the last medicinal properties are taken, and we will find a general system rebuilding, and the condition corrected.

20. Do that.

21. We are through for the present.
