# TEXT OF READING 275-7 F 17

**Reading [[275-7]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/7/1930  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 1711

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [275] and mother [255], sister [276], brother [488] and sister [457], and Dr. Wigal.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [275] - this we have had before.  Now, we find conditions are still on the improve with the body.  There is little of the covering, and of that portion in bone, not strengthening - and in a COMPARATIVELY short time, without there be other alterations, this should be in that position where there needn't be fear of further inroads in same.  We would keep much of that in the manner as has been outlined for the body.

2. We would find that, WITH the ash - this will combine most in the system as a resistance by the releasing of oxygen in the system, with that of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]], than with the Infra red.  While the red NECESSARY, the variations in the ultra violet's [[Vibration|VIBRATION]] - even though it be with the exterior, rather than interior activity - is better in the blood, and the releasing of same.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is swimming good for body?
(A)  GOOD for body.

4. (Q)  Will body grow any more?
(A)  Body will grow more.

5. (Q)  Should stupes still be given twice a day?
(A)  Until we change it!  We haven't changed it yet, have we?

6. (Q)  Is the rest of body in good condition?
(A)  VERY good.

7. (Q)  Can the mother be of any help to the body?
(A)  Mother can always be of help to the body.

8. (Q)  In which way?
(A)  The dependence as is of an offspring for those of the mental building forces within, are ALWAYS an addition to that which builds for that within the body.  Mental always the builder, whether it be for that as becomes physical in its reaction, or that as becomes mental in its expansion.

9. (Q)  When will the body be ready to go home?
(A)  In two to four weeks, with the CONTINUATIONS of the improvements - unless there be other conditions arising.

10. (Q)  What will be the growth?  How much would she grow?
(A)  From inch to inch and a half in height.

11. That is all the questions regarding this body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Wigal (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self to send home
