# TEXT OF READING 900-283 M 31

**Reading [[900-283]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/26/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5486

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:15 P. M. Noon - Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Saturday afternoon, Nov. 20, 1926.  I beheld a white draped figure in the room - only for a flash of a second - but I experienced its presence as such.  I felt the figure to be that of [Mrs. [139] - his deceased mother in law] - this I knew, for I nearly cried out her name as I beheld the figure.
(A)  This, as we see, again that presentation to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of the fulfillment of that body conscious mind study, correlated with the mental activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], combined with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] action of the spiritual entity of the body bringing to the physical consciousness the actuality taking form of the spiritual presence of that as may be transposed by a spiritual entity in the material plane, and the lesson is rather that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may study such phenomena, such conditions, as to how these may benefit rather than be used by others, or that such an entity's force in the Universal plane, as correlated with the material, may gain in its assistance to others.  Study same, then, in the light of the manner of the presentation, for, as is seen, it is the combination of the forces as are manifest in body conscious, in mental conscious, in [[Superconscious|super-conscious]], or the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of the entity correlated or in attunement with those forces as may be projected into the physical force or plane.

4. We are through with this reading.
