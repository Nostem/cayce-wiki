# TEXT OF READING 139-13 F ADULT

**Reading [[139-13]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/13/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 536

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of February, 1926, in accordance with phone request made by her son-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body which I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before. Now, we find that the vital forces in the physical of the body are still on the wane, and that to awaken the spiritual forces to that of the ability to overcome this physical condition would bring to the body those of the rejuvenating of the mental forces in physical, which are being kept subdued through the action of those properties affecting directly the locomotary of the sensory system.  Then, we will find that within forty-eight hours that disintegration will take place, unless there is some awakening through the conscious moments of the body.

3. We are through.
