# TEXT OF READING 5198-1 F 38 (Secretary)

**Reading [[5198-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/31/1944  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 13334

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5198], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 and 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at present; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, she's coming in now from the toilet.

3. Yes, we have the body here, [5198].

4. As we find, there has become, through the general activity, a deficiency in the hormones in the blood (which are of the male hormones) and this has caused in the cellular forces of the body those tendencies for accumulation in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] areas.

5. The lymph accumulation in small nobules makes pressure, as through the abdomen, as through the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and portions of the breast, as also through a rash or a swelling under the skin that causes irritation in portions of the body.

6. As we would find here, there should be, under the direction of such a one as Dr. Babcock of Temple University Hospital, Philadelphia, Penna, an injection once or twice of these hormones and then with the pellets of the hormones that he may give, we should find these general conditions disappearing.

7. Do that.

8. Don't put this off until cellular force breaks in some portion of the body or through some part of the alimentary canal.  For these are forming and then gradually disappear, but these are the lackings, and once or twice of these injections, and then the vital energies for the vitamins as will be administered, will bring better reactions and prevent serious conditions later for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  How can I get rid of bad breath?
(A)  By making for better conditions in eliminations.  Take [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an intestinal antiseptic.  Two, three times a day.  Put six drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the water. This is a throwing off into the lung, into the body-forces, poisons from this changing in cellular activity, or through the body, of lymph forces that become fecal.

11. (Q)  Is the matter of diet important for me?
(A)  These will be outlined by Babcock.  These should include a great deal of foods rich in vitamins; as raw foods and prepared with gelatin often.

12. (Q)  Is there any tendency toward arthritis?
(A)  It can be something much worse.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Babcock) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

> R1. 12/28/44 Letter:  "Today I wish to give in detail a report on
> the results obtained from following the recommendations of my
> Physical Reading [5198-1]:
> 
> "It is difficult to describe the feeling of elation and
> gratefulness I had when I received this most accurate
> diagnosis of my condition.  My main trouble at the time was
> the great sensitiveness in my breasts, with occasional small
> hard lumps in that area; a large rash on my right elbow,
> which would disappear at times for a few days only to
> reappear constantly; and slight arthritic pains in elbows and
> knees.  The reading suggesting my getting in touch with Dr.
> Babcock of Temple Hospital, Phila., Pa., I did so, and
> arranged for an appointment on June 22.  While I could not
> get a definite statement from Dr. Babcock, he seemed to agree
> with the findings of the reading and called my attention to
> the fact that there probably was a direct relation between
> the breast pains and the monthly period.  On subsequent
> observation, I found this to be correct, that is, the pain
> decreased or disappeared during the period and reappeared
> thereafter.  Dr. Babcock gave me one hormone injection,
> prescribed pills to be taken daily after dinner, and asked me
> to report to him by letter after 2 or 3 weeks.  After about 3
> weeks, on July 11, I could report to him as follows:
> 
> At the moment, the sensitive areas in my breasts have
> entirely disappeared.  The 'vague' pains of arthritis in
> my elbows, too, are almost entirely gone.  The big rash,
> which appeared so frequently on the arm inside of my
> right elbow, has not shown itself since a few days
> before my visit to your office on June 22.  I kept a
> daily record of my 'bodily disorders' since that date
> and am enclosing a copy of it herewith, which will give
> you a more detailed [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]].  You will note that a few
> days before the monthly period, the sensitiveness in my
> breasts increased considerably, reaching a very painful
> height the day before and the first day of the period.
> The most peculiar thing I noted was the appearance
> of the brownish-reddish spots on my body, which now
> seem gradually to fade...  (copy of the daily record
> mentioned above is enclosed* herewith for your files).
> 
> Dr. Babcock replied that the report 'is gratifying to me as
> it must be to you.  Evidently the hormonal deficiency in your
> body has been supplied, and as a result you are in what we
> might term 'hormonal balance' at the present time.  I would
> suggest that after the 15th of the month you discontinue all
> treatment, to determine if your metabolism has not now become
> sufficiently normal so that it may be not necessary for you
> to use additional medication.'  Since that time, improvement
> has been steadily more noticeable.  Although the breast pains
> appeared again, they have decreased from month to month, and
> during the month of October I was ENTIRELY free from them,
> although the following month there was a slight reappearance.
> 
> I think that perhaps a 2nd injection will clear up that
> condition completely and Dr. Babcock has offered to supply
> the formula so that my local physician can administer the
> same.
> 
> Not since June 1944, NOT ONCE, has the big, ugly rash
> appeared on my elbow - it seems like a [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]] - and the
> arthritic pains in knees and elbows are gone, too.  I cannot
> express my gratitude for the opportunity to have had this
> Reading.  It was an experience good not only for the body,
> but also great for the mind.  The recommendation to use
> [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an intestinal antiseptic and a help to
> get rid of bad breath is excellent.  I found it most helpful,
> indeed.  If you would like to have any further information,
> please let me know."
