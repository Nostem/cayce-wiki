# TEXT OF READING 2153-12 F 14

**Reading [[2153-12]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/28/1942  Sex: F  Age: 14  ReadingID: 8833

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [2345], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., West Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2153].

3. As we find, the conditions show changes - and in many respects these are very much improved at times more than others.

4. There is a reverting to those disturbing forces of every nature that have been indicated through the body, or arising from those conditions that have been indicated.

5. In the present there is the forming rather of an irritation to the coordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous systems.  The allaying of same brings about, of course, helpful forces in some respects.

6. And so long as those attitudes are kept that are necessary in the relationships to those about or responsible for the body, there are better conditions.  When there is any resentment, or emotional or material upset, there is an irregularity to the tendencies - and irritations arise.

7. These find a reflection in the activities in the body as indicated.

8. We would keep most all of those things that have been suggested.

9. Periodically, or regularly, we would have a series of the corrections osteopathically; especially as related to breaking up those adhesions and those tendencies for incoordinations in the body.  The adhesions are especially in those areas of the lacteal duct and the caecum, and those areas along the right side; while the tensions are in the lower cervicals and the upper dorsals.

10. We would now have the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], carrying only [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] - vibratorially - commercial strength.  Make the attachments as follows:

11. The larger nickel plate, through which the [[Camphor|Camphor]] passes vibratorially, would be attached at the lacteal duct and the umbilical center - or, for this body, about three fingers from the navel center to the right, and two fingers up.  The small copper plate would be attached at the lumbar axis.

12. Make these attachments thirty minutes each day, as the body rests.

13. Change the [[Camphor|Camphor]] solution every fifteen days.  Renew the charge for the Appliance every thirty days.

14. Keep the attachment plates clean or polished, before and after using.

15. As for the acute conditions in the knee, or the limb here, - we would massage same with salt dampened with Pure Apple Vinegar.  If the body wants to be in motion, just massage this into the area.  But when the body is resting, this mixture may be put on a cloth or gauze and bound on - not too tight - for an hour, twice or three times a day.

16. We would keep the activities physically and mentally as has been indicated; staying close to the diets outlined, if we would bring the bettered conditions for the body.

17. The use of the allaying drug we would keep, but do not take so much as to interfere with those other applications that are indicated.

18. Do these things, as we find, and we will bring the better influences for this body.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [2345]
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note 7/28/42:  See extra sheet [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., who will prepare the Appliance for you.)
