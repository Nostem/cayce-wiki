# TEXT OF READING 2567-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2567-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/5/1941  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9576

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the step-daughter-in-law, Mrs. [808], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Washington 
Time of Reading                                 Sanitarium & 
10:50 to 11:11 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Hosp., Takoma 
Park, D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2567].

3. As we find, while conditions are rather serious, and in the present not a great deal of activity is possible, - with the care and the corrective applications for helpful influences to meet the depleted energies in the body, we may bring a much better condition for this body.

4. These are the conditions as we find with this body, [2567] we are speaking of:

5. For some time there has been an unbalancing of the chemical forces of the body, owing to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance.

6. With the strains of a mental nature, there has been brought about a deflection of energies so that the conditions in the structural portions are lacking in sufficient energies for their activity.

7. Hence the inability for the locomotion, the weakness that exists throughout, and the lack of the proper assimilation of foods in the body.

8. These are the conditions as we find them with this body.

9. This, of course, has depleted the blood supply.  With the nerve forces being depleted, it has made for slow mental reactions to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]]; as the body becomes easily tired, and there is the suffering in the locomotory centers - as in the hips, the knees, the arms - all of these.  It is not of an arthritic nature but rather a laxness of nerves in the body - though is of the acute nature.

10. In making administrations, then:

11. Keep the body quiet.

12. Keep whatever foods that are high in the vitamin forces, or blood and nerve and body building; that carry excesses of Vitamins A, D, B-1 and G - all of these; as the juices of liver, tripe, pig feet (if these are prepared properly), raw carrot juice, all forms of such natures.

13. And especially give [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], often, in minute quantities.

14. Each morning when there is the sponge bath, let this be rather warm, and follow same with a thorough rub with Peanut Oil - all the body will absorb; especially over the abdomen and small of the back, and over the joints.

15. Sponge off afterward and apply a little little rub alcohol. This will rest the body, and also be stimulating.

16. Also prepare the B Battery, this low form, to carry the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] as a [[Vibration|vibratory]] force for the body; that is, on the positive side have a connection that passes through a [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water - but use at least three to four ounces of the solution), with a nickel plate to be attached to the body.  On the minus side have a connection with a copper plate to be attached to the body.  These may be connected to the wrist and ankle, on opposite sides of the body, or to the knee and the elbow at times (the opposite sides of the body), which are those areas where the pulsation is closer to the surface - so that the [[Vibration|vibratory]] force of the properties themselves may be carried into the system.  Make the attachments each day, preferably of evening, for at least thirty minutes.  Change or renew the Gold Solution every fifteen days.

17. Do that.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Is a [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] affected in the stomach?
(A)  The [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system throughout is affected.

20. (Q)  What can be done for hip joints in order to get use of the spine?
(A)  As indicated, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over same the Peanut Oil; and supply those energies needed through the diet and the Gold vibratorially.

Do these things.

21. (Q)  What is cause of knees aching so badly and what can be done to ease this condition?
(A)  Do as has been indicated.  All of these conditions are covered in that indicated.

These as we find offer the better opportunities for the body, and will bring results if they are consistently applied.

22. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mrs. [808] Copy to Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

The B Battery may be secured from a garage or hardware store.  Perhaps [2784] could prepare those connections, or have it done.  Or, if you prefer, you can buy one that is prepared nicely - that is, in appearance, on the order of a radio, from:  Photobell Corp., Atten. Mr. A. Edelman, 116 Nassau St., [[New York City|New York City]].

However, if you do this, you will have to specify that they make the particular attachments as indicated.  For this Appliance is ordinarily made plain, without any attachment passing through medicinal properties.

P.S.  If you could get [[Edgar Evans Cayce|Edgar Evans Cayce]] one evening, he could probably prepare the B Battery for you - provided you had the materials ready for him to work with.  GD.)
