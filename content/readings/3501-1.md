# TEXT OF READING 3501-1 M 51

**Reading [[3501-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/30/1943  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 10984

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3501], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3501].

3. As we find, there are conditions that disturb the better physical functioning of the body.

4. These have to do with the effects of unbalanced conditions in the body-functionings and in the chemical forces of the body, also the poor eliminations - the effects upon the organs in their reaction.

5. Then, these are conditions as we find them with this body, [3501] we are speaking of:

6. The blood supply in itself indicates the character of the toxic forces, and a plethoric condition in the transverse colon; while the manner of circulation indicates the effect has upon the heart, kidneys and lungs at times.

7. The change in the environs, where there may be a great deal of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] rays from the sun, is well for the body.

8. Yet the disturbances caused in the body-functions will require that the poisons be removed from the system, else we will have reactions in the muscular forces or a tendency toward congestion in the liver that will produce the effect either upon the gall bladder or upon the kidneys - producing sedimentary conditions.

9. Thus we would begin having first the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, including about six high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations - about ten days to two weeks apart.

10. Before each of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] we would have a three-day series of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver and gall duct area.  Apply these for an hour each day for three days in succession. Then take internally two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].  When this has acted thoroughly, then have the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]]. Wait then for about a week and have another series of the Packs, and so on.

11. Occasionally have the gentle [[Massage Therapy|Swedish massage]], rather than attempting to correct or make adjustments in the cerebrospinal system.  To be sure, the rotations in neck and shoulders would be well.

12. Do these and we will get rid of those tendencies towards rheumatic and neuritic reactions in body.

13. Keep quiet, not too active but oft in the sunshine and out of doors.

14. Not too heavy foods in the diet, but plenty of fruits, vegetables, citrus fruits and the like.

15. Do these and we will make for better conditions of the body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)
