# TEXT OF READING 166-1 M 63

**Reading [[166-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/23/1929  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 669

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by self, through his daughter - Mrs. [1381].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [166].  Now, we find there are disturbing conditions with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  While these conditions at present do not cause disturbance sufficient to distress the body, other than for the body to be at dis-ease at times, allowed to run on may bring most ANY character of disturbance, dependent upon what cycle of functioning the poisons or the distresses, or the congestion, would attack.

2. These, then, are the conditions, as we find them, with this body, [166], we are speaking of:

3. THE BLOOD SUPPLY, low in vitality, disturbed by the lack of proper eliminations, and through the activity of the system there is carried back into the bloodstream much - at times - of the poisons that should be strained or drained from the system.  This is evidenced in more than one direction.  At times the body seems to easily take cold; phlegm easily arises in throat; pains at other times across the abdomen; pains in the small of the back; tingling sensations at times - when the body has rested - in the extremities.  These are the effects of the poor elimination, as related to the bloodstream being clarified.  Hence low vitality.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this, also, rather under the strain of the deflection of nerve energy through various centers, by distresses caused in the functioning of the organs, especially through the liver, the hepatic circulation, the kidneys, and the distresses general.  These at times show their effect sympathetically in the nerve system, as is seen in the headaches - the soreness about the eyes - irritation in the eyelids themselves - at times pimples or sores, of a minor nature, in mouth - throat irritation.  The conditions are reflex.  The seat or the cause of these distresses, as WE find, is from toxics, or toxic poisons as come from the distress in the ascending colon, and especially in that portion of the system where the ascending and transverse colon shows its operations.

5. Then, to meet the needs of the conditions - we would REST at least for ten days - two weeks.

6. First we would have, after two days rest, the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]], or the irrigation of the colon, and at the end of two weeks again - then, these should be sufficient for this portion of the treatment.

7. Then, we would stimulate the nerve centers along the whole of the cerebrospinal system, using as medicinal properties those of Petrolagar with the Pepsin - lactated pepsin - in each dose taken.  These should not be OVER stimuli to the system, but sufficient to keep the system cleansed.

8. We would also be careful of the diet.  Let these be not too much of sugars, nor too much of meats - though these may be taken in moderation.  PRINCIPALLY those that will give more vitality to the BLOOD supply, or ironizing the blood - or, well would it be that THIS be taken as a stimuli to the system - these need not be taken more than ONE capsule each day, or it may be one every other day, dependent upon the effects of the reaction in the system.  Do not attempt to keep the labors while irrigation or the colon is being cleansed - too much strain on the system.

9. The stimuli would be:

[[Camphor|Camphor]] Gum.................30 grains,
Muriated Iron...............30 grains,
Sulphate Morphia...........1/2 grain.

Mix thoroughly together and put in thirty-five or thirty-six pellets or capsules.  It will be necessary to keep these in a cool or cold place, if put in capsules OR pellets, for they will disintegrate.  Take one each day, or one every other day, dependent upon the condition.  Do that.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
