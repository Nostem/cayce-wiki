# TEXT OF READING 900-56 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-56]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/7/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5259

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A.M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you, as I read same, the manuscript as prepared by [900], on Psychic Phenomena [Based on [3744] series].  You will make such corrections in same as will be in keeping with the truth of such phenomena as viewed by the Forces of Edgar Cayce while in the Psychic State.  I will read a whole page of this, and when I stop you will make the corrections as outlined, and when you have finished with that page you will so state.  (Reading Page 19)  [GD's note: The manuscript was returned to Mr. [900].]

2. EC:  No corrections to be made as here.

3. (Q)  Page 20.
(A)  Instead of "gain that power" acquired that power. The difference in these being, one is as of meted out, the other is as of merited.

4. (Q)  Page 21.
(A)  No corrections, save this might be added, or may not be added.  The gift or the gained psychic force may be abused, the same as any of the senses of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], and through abuse of same may bring destructive forces to the individual in the physical and in the spiritual life.

5. (Q)  Page 22.
(A)  We have no corrections to make with this portion.

6. (Q)  Page 23.
(A)  (Interrupting)  We are conscious of the action of psychic and spiritual forces in the same manner we are conscious of the action of electrical forces in any manner in the physical world, or as we see, "The wind bloweth where it listeth.  Thou heareth the sound thereof though know of from whence it cometh nor whence it goeth."  The electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] of man's direction we may reach the action, yet no one fathoms the force, the direction of the applied force.  All becoming under one law, and are of [[Law of Moses|the law]] pertaining to psychic force and psychic [[Vibration|vibration]] as are manifested in the material world.  We recognize the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in electrical forces, and such are weighed, measured, and in those of radio forces we have electrical vibration magnifying the vibrations as are set forth in same.  The same we have in spirit or psychic or mind vibration.  These are magnified by the action on those in attune with that vibration.  (Reading)

Correct.  No corrections other than given.

7. (Q)  Page 24.
(A)  No corrections.  May be some expanding as regards the conditions of the birth into the spirit plane and to gain the spiritual consciousness as is reached in that plane and the bringing of the spiritual consciousness, as reaching that dimension as is set forth in the first chapters, first portion in this.

8. (Q)  Page 25.
(A)  No corrections.

9. We are through for the present.
