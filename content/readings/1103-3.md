# TEXT OF READING 1103-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[1103-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/30/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6339

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of March, 1928, in accordance with request made by Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, telling us if the treatments are being accorded the body properly; giving suggestions for further relief or improvement of this body.  You will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before.

3. We find the conditions easier at the present time.  While there has not been the perfect coordination established in the system through those conditions as being applied for the reaction of the liver and the kidneys, to take the blood away from the head and from the pressure as is caused above there - that is, above the diaphragm, or toward the head - the condition general is easier.  More of the manipulations to adjust this, and the keeping up of those stimulations of the oil applications over the liver area, would - as we see at present (unless other conditions arise not seen at the time) - bring about the nearer, more normal conditions for this body.  Ready for questions.  (After pause)  Not too severe with the manipulation, but that that gives a more thorough relaxing for the liver and hepatic circulation, that we may keep the blood away from the head, or from those centers as produce pressure ABOVE the diaphragm, see?  secondary cardiac plexus - cardiac plexus - liver plexus - those below the 9th dorsal.

4. We are through for the present.
