# TEXT OF READING 1320-1 F 40

**Reading [[1320-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/15/1937  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 6987

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of January, 1937, in accordance with request made by the aunt - Mrs. [1319], through sponsorship of Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, [1320]; we have the body here.

2. As we find, there are disturbing conditions.  These have to do with accumulations that have become a part of the circulatory system as related to irritations where tissue has become involved through the pelvic organs and the alimentary canal.

3. These as we find become rather specific but produce many disturbing forces through the activities of the system, in the incapacitating of the body.

4. And then combined with anxieties, conditions in the general welfare as related to diets and the like, these bring the distresses for the body.

5. As we would find, in ministering for the needs of these:

6. Not necessarily is there the need for the operative measures; though if these conditions continue and there is not the setting up of that as would enable the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the body - as related to the eliminating systems - to DISSEMINATE the disturbance, operative forces would become necessary.

7. As we would find, we would use the deep therapy - as the sinusoidal that would make for an activity first (not too great) as to the dilating and functioning of the gall duct area, and then - after some several applications in this direction for its setting up - eliminate through the use of the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] those poisons as would be the natural overflow from the system by these applications.

8. First the sinusoidal treatments would be about twice a week; and give at least three to four of such treatments.

9. Then set up the eliminations through the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], at least three to four days apart - and at least two of these to cleanse the system, giving the time for rest between same.

10. Then (after the sinusoidal treatments and the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations have been given as indicated), we would begin with the application for the dilation and the activity of those organs as related to the pelvic system.  Here we would find the necessity then of using the Elliot Machine applicators for the eliminations of those tendencies for adherences in the pelvic organs.

11. These as we find should be administered in the order first of the activities to the digestive or assimilating system through the activity of the liver, and then the heat as would be applied through the use of the Elliot applicators for the pelvic disturbances.  Begin with these treatments at least every day, or every other day WE find these would be required.

12. If these do not relieve, after at least ten to twenty of such treatments, then we would find it would be necessary for operative measures; but if there are the applications made as indicated we find these would bring the better and the near normal conditions for this body.

13. Ready for questions, [1320].

14. (Q)  What advice regarding diet?
(A)  We would abstain from too great a quantity of starches or highly seasoned foods, or a diet that carries great quantities of grease of any nature - whether oils or butterfats.

15. (Q)  What produced this condition?
(A)  As has been indicated, it came from adhering of tissue first in the system through the pelvic organs.

16. (Q)  What should I advise my husband to do to make a living?
(A)  Applying self in those trades, those channels in which there has been the greater amount of applications of self, offers [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which the greater opportunities may come.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [1319]
"    " Ass'n file
