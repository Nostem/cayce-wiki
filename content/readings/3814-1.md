# TEXT OF READING 3814-1 F 79

**Reading [[3814-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/5/1926  Sex: F  Age: 79  ReadingID: 11410

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Mrs. [...], via Mr. Frank E. Mohr.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Indiana.

1. GC:  Now you will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are many conditions that need correcting, while the cause of the existent conditions is an accumulation of various sundry conditions, these brought on by many changes as have and do take place in the body laboring under stresses as have been produced in the system.

3. These, then, are conditions as we find them - many of these varying, many of these changing, as we find, but this as the general outline of conditions:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this too full at times, and apparently carrying too heavy supply, as it were, of those properties that should be eliminated, until we find where tissue becomes plethora in its effect, and conditions are produced or brought about that separate themselves, or cause conditions to arise as swellings in portions of the body.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find the general seat or cause of conditions, for with subluxations as have been created, and through [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of conditions through those of lesions and adhesions as have been formed in various portions of the system, this brought the first general condition, as we see, that causes distresses in the body.  These forming, then, as we find, in the lumbar and in the organs of the pelvis. These conditions in the change here as came about for the body, through the natural effects and with these disturbances in these portions of the body causing the lesions, this, then, produced subluxations in the lower dorsal and in the lumbar region.  Disturbances, then, to the mesenteric system, from which this has received, as we see, the nerve incentive, and the whole digestive system becomes involved, see?  Then, the natural outgrowth, without these corrections, have been the variations as have existed from time to time, until in the present condition we find many variations of this same condition, with the effect of all, as combined, on the mental forces of the individual.

6. To overcome, or to meet the needs, then, of these conditions, would be as this:

7. We would have three, as it were, separate and distinct forms, manners or ways of applying beneficial influences to this body.  As well as the diet that should be built up, these other properties should be added in their regular form and in their regular manner.

8. First, we would begin - not too heavy at first, but with those of the manipulation, osteopathically given, to correct the conditions in the dorsal and lumbar region, giving the GENERAL manipulation at least once each week, or each two weeks, for the whole alignment, as it were, or to keep the whole system in accord with the various changes produced by the different corrections being made, see?  Well that before each one of these are given that hot packs - wet - but not sufficiently wet that the water would run off on the body - as hot as can be stood by the body, be applied to the whole cerebro-spinal system.  Then the treatments given following same.  Necessary, then, that the body rest after such.

9. Taking internally, during this whole time, these properties:

10. First we would prepare as the carrier, this:

11. To 16 ounces of distilled water, add 8 ounces Wild Cherry Bark.  Reduce this by simmering to half the quantity. Strain.  Then add 6 ounces of sugar.  Then reduce, by the steaming or boiling, to 6 ounces.  See?  Fluid ounces.  To this we would add:

Tincture of Stillingia................1/4 ounce,
Fluid Extract Burdock Root............1/4 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya.......................1/2 ounce,
Syrup of Sarsaparilla.................1/2 ounce,
[[Iodine|Iodine]] (Bromide)........................3 minims,
10% Solution of Bromide of Potassium...15 minims,
10% Solution of [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium....20 minims,
Tincture of Capsici.....................1 minim,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]........................4 minims.

Shake the solution well to-gether before each dose is taken. The dose would be half a teaspoonful 3 times each day - early morning, noon, and just before retiring.

12. After at least 13 adjustments, osteopathically, have been taken, and these medicinal properties being taken all the time, we would begin with that of the ultra [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]], or Alpine or Rino Ray.  This would be applied to the body in the variation as is found that the body responds to.  This would, preferably, be applied to the front and back portions of the body, see?  for a few minutes every third day.

13. These, we find, will bring better conditions for this body. Do that, [3814].

14. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments be made?
(A)  At least every other day.  At least 52 such adjustments should be made.

15. We are through for the present.
