# TEXT OF READING 568-4 M 28

**Reading [[568-4]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/9/1943  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 4005

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through the mother, Mrs. [379], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [379], Mrs. [413] and Mrs. [1079].]

Navy Yard, 
Time of Reading                              Bldg. 262, or 
3:25 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.         under big crane, 
..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - here he is between the two.  We have the body here, [568]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are some changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

4. But if there is kept that proper elimination or activity, with this body, of the liver, AND that activity in the elimination of kidneys, we will find conditions as related to the general circulation - and those tendencies for the toxic forces through body - will be kept in nearer normal balance.

5. Occasionally, - say once a week or the like, - we would take a CRC tablet or pellet.  This is calomel, rhubarb and colocynth; a combination of minerals and vegetables that will be preferable to keep the balance for his body.

6. About once a month, - or at times it may be necessary twice a month, - we would take a lithia tablet in a glass of water. Make this sort of a habit or a regular routine, to take - say on the fifteenth of the month - in the evening, a glass of water with a lithia tablet in same.

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q) What is causing the disturbing conditions in the present?
(A)  As just indicated, the unbalanced condition between the liver and the kidney circulation; thus the hindrance, because of the poisons settling in portions of body.

10. (Q)  Has the liver condition improved any since last reading?
(A)  It has improved in some respects, but there are still those corrections needed - by the taking of those properties that are to work upon the activities of the system; to stir the activities and to aid in the chemical reactions necessary - from these properties in the body.

11. (Q)  What causes soreness in body, especially in lower abdomen and chest?
(A)  This is the effect of pressure upon the tissues of the body from those poisons or accumulations in the lymph, and these from plethoric conditions that produce pressures upon nerves through these areas, see?  It is the liver, the kidney and the heart and lung circulation.  Thus at times it will be as in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach, at others close around the heart, at others upon the right side high up, at others down across the lower portion of the abdomen, at others in the lower portions across the back.

12. (Q)  What can be done to cure the cough?
(A)  Get the activity of the liver.  This is arising from the circulation to the bronchi and larynx.  Eliminating the poisons should eliminate the cough.

13. (Q)  Is condition between eyes sinus trouble and what should be done for it?
(A)  This is more or less from a sinus trouble, but the basis of this - mostly - is the lack of proper drainages through alimentary canal.  With the taking of the properties indicated regularly, some six to eight to ten weeks, we will have a different reaction through the body.

14. (Q)  Please give diet best for this body, tonic and vitamins.
(A)  As we find, a well balanced diet is better for this body.  Not altogether starches nor altogether proteins, but a diet that is very well balanced.  It is necessary that there be not too much sweets, but not too much roughages either at times.  For instance, while pop corn is good, is isn't so good for the body; though pop corn cakes are excellent for the body.  Oat meal and some cereals are good, but corn flakes are not good for the body.  Citrus fruits of any character are good for the body, but if taken with starches at any time they are not good for the body.  The meats should consist of fish, fowl and lamb; not any hog meat ever for the body.  Vegetables of the leafy nature are preferable; not too much fats with same, just sufficient to season or to make palatable for the body.

Do use those things indicated, and we will find better conditions for this body.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
