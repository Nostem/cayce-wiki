# TEXT OF READING 1255-1 F 18

**Reading [[1255-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/24/1936  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 6836

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the parents, through Mr. [478], sponsored by Study Group #3 of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1255].

2. As we find, little may be done to aid save as giving relief. While the conditions in the advanced stages of disintegration in bone structure and in body-building have come from lack of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] influences, of their secretions, and these become infected through body-forces and of body-[[Vibration|vibration]], the greater aid as we find would be in giving a quarter grain of the Animated Ash followed with the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray; this given five minutes after the Ash has been given dry and washed down with a little water.

3. Also we would use Edelman's ionizer, but use same with a lead that passes from the minus anode through the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]], making the anodes or leads with the larger plate used over the umbilicus and the smaller plate at the 3rd and 4th dorsal plexus.  Use this for thirty minutes about twelve hours difference between that and the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]].

4. Necessary, to be sure, to use antiseptics about the body as well as narcotics for relief of body.

5. In the diet keep from those of bulbular vegetables and from rare meats.  Beef juices, liver and liver extracts, the leafy vegetables, light wines - all of these we find would be the better, and offer the means as we find of staying though not [[Healing|healing]], but palliatives and staying of the inroads in the present.

6. These as we find, if there are responses, may be altered or changed.

7. Do that for the present.

8. (Q)  Where should the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] be applied and for how long?
(A)  Over the back of the lung area, for it is to carry into the blood more carbon and may be extended at times over the structural portions especially as the pelvis, larger portions of the lower limbs, but principally over the RIB area from the back.

The body should sit up when taking the ionizer.

The taking of the ultra-violet would be for a minute or a minute and a half.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [478]
"    " Ass'n file
