# TEXT OF READING 739-1 F 30 (?)

**Reading [[739-1]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/19/1934  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 4582

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th Street, Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 19th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the husband, Mr. [...] through Mr. A. O. Englander, new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [739]''s husband and Alfred O. Englander.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
3:15 to 3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body called [739].

2. Now, as we find, there are definite conditions that disturb the better physical functioning of this body.  In the present there is rather a complication of disorders, and the effects are at times taken for causes of the disturbances; yet in its incipiency or in its basis the condition is the effect of a disturbance in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body - and at times acts upon those reflexes in the whole mental attitudes of the body.

3. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [739].

4. The BLOOD SUPPLY we find carries in same the effects of disturbances through the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], making for disorders in the eliminations; thus providing [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which the activities in the eliminating systems become crossed, so that we have poor eliminations through some portions while there is an excess in the activities through the perspiratory or the outer portion of the body - as in a splotch, and the activities to the system in its general manner.

5. In these effects we find also the lack of the clarification or the sufficient oxygen to purify the whole blood stream from the lack of those vital forces in the system to create the proper resistances through this unbalanced condition that we find in this portion of the system with this body.

6. As to the NERVOUS SYSTEM, here we find those incoordinations between the mental reaction and the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body; produced by the incoordinations in the body itself.

7. The ORGANS of the body show their disturbances through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]' activity, through the functioning of the organs in the lung supply or blood activity, and in the heart's action as to its abnormality; also in the digestive system through its inability for the proper assimilations - through these effects upon the system.

8. As we find, then, in meeting the needs of these conditions, these may be aided in making for a much nearer normalcy. For in those portions where there has been the effect of the applications for the system, that has produced the condensation in the lymph circulation in its accumulations and making for pressures, these will necessitate being taken care of as to the effects; but as for those things that would make for basic changes it would be necessary for those stimulations to the body that would produce for the glands' activity that which would bring to the body not only sustaining strength but revivify those activities in the organs, such as to make for same the associated connections between the coordinating forces of the body and bring a much bettered condition.

9. Hence we find these would be the applications in the present. Then, as the changes are brought about, we would add those things necessary for that which has been accomplished - or the change necessary to create the proper balance:

10. First we would add, then, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the active forces of the glands themselves, that will act with the assimilating forces of the body as to create a nearer normalcy in the assimilating system; and then as changes come about for the corrective forces, making the changes necessary to meet those needs.

11. We would add, then, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the low form of the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] in the Wet Battery that carries with same both the Gold in solution and the Nitrate of Silver solution; that these activities may make for such changes in the body as to bring about that where there CAN be the aids in the body here.

12. First we would make the application of the Gold each day for periods of three to five days.  The smaller copper anode would be attached first, in the 9th dorsal plexus area; while the larger nickel anode passing through the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] (one grain to one ounce of distilled water) would be attached last, to the umbilicii and lacteal duct area.

13. After each three to five day period, rest or leave off the applications for one to two days.

14. Then begin with the Nitrate of Silver solution, as we have indicated through these sources for its activity - a ten percent solution - solvent in alcohol.  Make the attachment of the smaller copper anode first, in the brachial plexus area (1st and 2nd dorsal); while the larger nickel anode passing through the Nitrate of Silver solution would be attached in the 4th lumbar area; that we may overcome the tendencies for the accumulations of poisons that have hindered the body in its activity through the locomotions and through the portions of the body where these distresses or pressures have been made.

15. After the Nitrate of Silver applications have been made for a period of three to five days, then we would have the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] for a like period; [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations that would coordinate all those centers along the cerebro-spinal system as to make for the activity.

16. Then repeat the whole procedure, in the manner outlined, for thirty days - and then we would give those changes necessary for this body.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Would an improvement in the glandular condition improve the reproductory system?
(A)  A stimulation to these would be a portion of the glands themselves.  Hence the application of the Silver solution to the area in the lumbar that affects the organs of the pelvis themselves, see?  While the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] is active through the blood supply for the changing of those activities in the general strength of the body.  These would, then, be helpful.

19. (Q)  Does the patient's so called migraine headaches come from this faulty glandular condition?
(A)  They come rather from the EFFECT of the glandular trouble upon the eliminating system.

We have in a normal body the assimilating and eliminating system as provided for through the organs of eliminations; as through the alimentary canal, the breath, the kidney activities and the functionings of the organs of same and in the female through the action of the menstrual periods, that make for these variations in the activities of the system.

When these become disturbed they produce, then, a pressure upon the nerve forces such as to produce headaches - which are as an indication of disturbance in the system, see?

If we will follow these conditions as outlined, using the low electrical vibration, we will find many of these disturbances that are effects in the present will disappear; and with the changes that would come about in the activities we will produce a much nearer NORMAL body.

First - three to five days with the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]]; letting each application to the body be only fifteen to twenty minutes in the beginning.

Then three days rest from the applications.

Then have the three to five day period of the Nitrate of Silver solution; fifteen to twenty minutes at each application in the beginning.

These solutions would be electrically given to the body, you see; not any internal medicines.

Then have the like period of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations; once each day for three to five days.

Then repeat.

And after thirty days we would give further instructions.

20. (Q)  What diet would you recommend for the body?
(A)  A normal, well-balanced diet; more an alkalin than acid reacting diet.

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to A. O. Englander
"    " Ass'n file
