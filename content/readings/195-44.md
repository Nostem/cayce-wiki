# TEXT OF READING 195-44 M 44 (Realtor, Mfgr. of Auto

**Reading [[195-44]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/31/1927  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 757

Accessories) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of August, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. LBC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [195], of ... St., ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding the physical condition of this body, also regarding his business relations and associations.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [195].  This we have had here before.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Are lesions disappearing in 2nd dorsal and 4th and 5th cervical [which have been the cause of my partial deafness]?
(A)  These conditions are improved, but the lesions have not as yet disappeared.  More heat applied just before the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and manipulations are given would assist this condition materially.

4. (Q)  Are conditions in ears clearing up?
(A)  These also show improvement, but until there can be the perfect coordination in the branches of the nerve and blood supply to the auditory forces, this cannot - and will not - clear entirely; though, as we find, these are on the improve, and show a much more healthy or normal reaction than as we have had before.

5. (Q)  Any other recommendations for the body?
(A)  Keep those things for the body as have been given.  We would apply the Radio-Active Appliance occasionally - every night or every other night, or whenever there is a heaviness felt by the body, this will give the better [[Vibration|vibration]] for the body, and with full corrections of adhesions and lesions, as have been seen and as have been given concerning in the dorsal and cervical region, and along the outline of the Eustachian Tube, these will assist in bringing about the normal forces of the body.  The [[Vibration|vibratory]] force as is set by that of the Radio-Active force is to equalize and cause perfect coordination in the body.

6. (Q)  Regarding business, in connection with Ralph DeWeese, William M. Bready and W. R. Brookins, will the purchase of 1/2 stock of Crystal Laundry and Rug Cleaning Co., 229 Clinton St., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], be profitable?
(A)  Be profitable, and while some policies as concerning the conducting of the business should be changed somewhat - especially that as to how the returns from work called for and delivered is made - the general business and policy has been and is good, and this will prove a profitable business.

7. (Q)  Will it more than pay for itself out of the earnings?
(A)  It will more than pay for itself out of its earnings. Good business!

8. (Q)  Will the purchase of minority stock interest in Seitz-Brudenbach Hardware Co. stores at 527 Salem and 2017 Salem Ave. - 1928 N. Main St & Hoover Ave., in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], Ohio, help them weather the storm - will they pull out and afterwards be able to show a big profit?
(A)  This would be questionable - for there are many conditions to be considered as regarding the manner and way these businesses - in their various channels - have been and are handled.  While some are good, some are excellent, some conditions as are seen are not so well.  WE would give that this MIGHT be accomplished, yet we do not find that this would be ADVISABLE, unless one desired - through either friendship or through that of changing many of the policies of the concern - to take hold of same and expect same to weather the EXISTENT conditions.

9. (Q)  Are there any further suggestions concerning this business?
(A)  This is a profitable business, and this business enjoys - in these various places - a good clientele - but some of those conditions surrounding and associated with same are not so well.

10. We are through for the present.
