# TEXT OF READING 728-2 F 14

**Reading [[728-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/4/1935  Sex: F  Age: 14  ReadingID: 4564

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [728], through Associate Membership of her mother, Mrs. [...], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and H. L. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Long Island, N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [728].

2. Now, as we find, the conditions are an improvement from that as we have had here before.

3. While there are still those conditions that may be improved, as we find, it might be said that there has been the start in the proper direction, making for the going to the seat or the cause of the troubles that hinder a perfect coordination and reaction in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. True, there are the continued reactions at times with the spasmodic conditions, yet - as is seen - the NATURE of these and that refraction which occurs between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system is not as severe; though the turn or the attack is apparently somewhat more severe at times than others.  But the breaking up or the turning of the reactions in the system is being accomplished in the right direction.

5. We would continue with the oil packs in the caecum, in the liver area, as we have indicated; using the application to relieve these strains, for the adhesions have NOT been broken up.  While they have loosened in some areas of the caecum and in the lacteal duct area, this very change makes for the seemingly more violent reaction - but of a lesser duration. However, the stoppages - or the tendencies for a lack of the proper flow of the lymph through the colon and caecum area (or constipation) - make for the more violent or the more SEVERE reactions.

6. To be sure, we would not use the packs during the menstrual periods; although we WOULD during such periods make for deeper manipulations in the cervical and also in the dorsal (lower dorsal) and lumbar area - either with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].

7. In the matter of the diet, keep those things that are nerve and blood building for the body, especially; those that are the more alkalin in their nature.

8. We would be mindful also to keep away from the tendencies for using - at any time - the hypnotics or the bromides.

9. As an eliminant we would use the Fletcher's Castoria in small doses.  Even if it is necessary to use this two or three times a week - if there are the vegetables eaten, and the fruits, juices of fruits, that make for more and more tendency towards the normal eliminations - it will be of greater help to the body.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Why do no attacks occur when there is fever in the body?
(A)  This should be an indication to those who would study such conditions that the information coming through this channel knows what it's talking about!  For the fever makes for an increase in the flow of the lymph through the body. Hence all those areas where there are the adhesions and those portions that are attempting to be broken up, these then contract with the cold or the less high temperature in the body, see?  As these are gradually broken up, we will find there will be less and less of the attacks.

Don't add to that which has been outlined heretofore - and here; but DO all of these CONSISTENTLY, persistently, and we will get results!

13. (Q)  Should she apply the packs when the body has a fever?
(A)  When the body has a fever it is well to apply packs.

14. (Q)  What is to be done to enable the body to fall asleep at night?
(A)  The use of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] should make for the relaxing sufficiently for the body to fall to sleep. Use this over the cerebrospinal system, or around the back of the head, the neck, across the shoulders, even down to the lower portion of the body, as has been indicated.

15. (Q)  How many ounces of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] should the body take every day?
(A)  From two to three and a half ounces is sufficient. This should not be taken all at once, you see.  For a tablespoonful of the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], made properly, carries the same quantity of nourishment that you would have in half a pound of beef!  What would be a half a pound of beef at once in the body?  Take, then, a teaspoonful at the time - this would be much better, and take it every few hours.

16. (Q)  Should the Aconite and Laudanum be continued before the packs?
(A)  It has been given; it should.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
