# TEXT OF READING 1467-11 M 36

**Reading [[1467-11]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/25/1941  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 7285

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1467], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1467] and Mrs. [263].]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1467], present in this room, together with his present work, his [[Physical Body|physical body]], his sister [1540], in Williamsburg Sanitarium, with the present conditions and environments surrounding each, the making of the Electric Appliances and his mental and spiritual development.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1467]; together with the physical, the [[Mental Body|mental body]], the associations, relationships, and activities in relationships to influences for others.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is the condition of my [[Physical Body|physical body]]?
(A)  In the main conditions physically, as we find them, are very good.  To be sure, there are some disturbances or distresses that at times cause anxiety.  Or, the body responding to or rebelling against overactivity gives off warnings or indications of distress.

These are conditions as we find them that are disturbing factors in the present:

As for those conditions in scalp that at times cause some anxiety, - at least every ten days [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with any good, plain soap and water.  Then shampoo with any good shampoo. That called the Quinine Shampoo, as we find, is better for this body - to prevent falling hair.  But if there is the thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with any good soap, that will CLEANSE, this is preferable to most of those solutions or preparations so highly recommended.  The shampoo as indicated is the better here.  Do this about every ten days, and we should have little or no trouble with falling hair.

Those conditions in the gums and mouth need the occasional use of those properties found in Ipsab.  DO [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the gums with an equal combination of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] and Salt, at least every other day.  This will be found to be most helpful.  Have some local attention very soon to some of the teeth.

Those conditions through the lungs, throat, larynx, will of themselves keep very good, if there are the general precautions and not overtaxing, not overirritating nor the allowing of the body to become overacid.

The digestive forces as related to the heart's activity are very good.

The manner of correcting the strain in the pelvis is being gradually aided by the correct proportions for feet and ankles.  The present shoe is VERY good.  We would keep this same type.

The condition produced in the opposite side of the pelvis is from the general strain that has existed, - this in the area about the caecum or appendicial area.  When tired, we would massage these areas very thoroughly with Peanut Oil. [See [1467]'s report on 1/3/47, 1467-11, Par. R4.]

Occasionally take internally, for this body, an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] AND Peanut Oil, - the same proportions.  Don't take too much at a time, but sufficient to keep the strains in the ileum plexus and the pelvic area from becoming irritated by tiring out, and this causing adhesions.

The adjustments that are given for relaxing the body are very well done.  Just don't hurry through with same so much at times, and relax those main centers - that is, the area from the 1st cervical to the 5th and 6th cervical, through the area of the 3rd and 4th dorsal, and especially relax THOROUGHLY from the 12th dorsal to the lower portion of the sacrals, - not by trying to strain so much, but relaxing the area.

These we would keep, - not too [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] in the diet, not too acid, but a well balanced diet.

Of mornings have citrus fruits oft, with hot cakes, eggs, bacon or the like, or alter to cereals - both dry and cooked; but do not take these the same day that the citrus fruits are taken.

Noons, - if practical, either soups, green vegetables or the like.

Evenings, - plenty of cooked vegetables, more of the leafy than the pod or tuberous - these are preferable.  Fish, fowl and lamb are preferable for meats, but occasionally a good, thick steak - but cook it WELL.  Very seldom have hog meat, though liver, the pigs' feet, the ear, any of those portions that are the digestive foods, that are palatable for the body, are very good - if prepared properly.

Do these and we will keep the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] fit.

5. (Q)  Are my glasses properly fitted?
(A)  These are very good.

6. (Q)  Do any of my teeth need extracting?
(A)  As indicated, rather the local attention; not extracted, unless there is the breaking through in such ways that these are unalterable.

7. (Q)  Please designate which of my teeth need dental attention?
(A)  This should be designated rather by the one making such corrections, see?

8. (Q)  Are the minerals properly balanced in my body or do I need to take some?
(A)  As indicated, with the outlines of the diets given we should keep minerals very well balanced.

9. (Q)  Would it be well at this time to take my sister [1540] from the State Institution at Williamsburg, Va., or does she need to have more discipline at the institution?
(A)  If it is practical for the environ that has been indicated that would be well for the body, very well.  If this is impractical at this time, do not change then as yet.

10. (Q)  I have been advised from this source that I would to have an advancement in position, may I expect it from the ... Grocery Co., or from some other company?  When will this advancement come?
(A)  As we find, the body may expect - with the present activities of the body - an advancement soon.  This may be in a month, it may be in three months, it may be in six months, - that of advancement with the present company associations.

We WOULD - through the offices of those that have been associated with the body - start those activities for consideration of back or overtime pay.  DO NOT let it come as from self, but through associates or old associates who have the same kind of claim with the organization.  If this is handled in that manner it may bring relief, or the abilities to do some of the material things desired, and would advance the body's association with the company.

If it brings crosses between the officials and the body, it might bring disturbing conditions.  But handled properly, it will be alright.  Be diplomatic, but be very positive.

11. (Q)  Would it be advisable for ... Grocery Co. to be reminded of it by [...], a former employee now employed in the Norfolk Navy Yard, through a lawyer reporting it to the Wage and Hour Board?
(A)  This will be a very good outlet, provided there is NOT mention of the body [1467].

12. (Q)  Is there any advice for me at this time in regard as to making the Electric Appliances?
(A)  This as we find will be given if there are any changes. Use those instructions that have been indicated for the building of these.  And as changes are desired, the body will be warned of same in the advice for the individuals who need same.  In the present these are very good.

13. (Q)  Should the plates on the Radio-Active Appliance be both nickel until otherwise specified in a specific reading?
(A)  As indicated, both nickel unless specifically indicated for special conditions otherwise.

14. (Q)  Please give me some spiritual advice.  I would appreciate an affirmation.
(A)  Do not at ANY time in thy associations with others do that thy conscience condemns thee for; that is:  Let not self, nor self's [[Purpose|purpose]], be ahead of thy [[Purpose|purpose]] with thy Lord.  For, as ye have heard, as ye do unto the least of thy associates ye do unto thy Lord.

Let thy [[Prayer|prayer]] oft be, though in thine own words:

HERE AM I, LORD, SEEKING TO BE A CHANNEL OF HELP AND BLESSINGS TO OTHERS.  USE THOU ME IN THE WAY AND MANNER THOU SEEST FIT.  I ACKNOWLEDGE MY WEAKNESSES, BUT I LOOK FOR THE PROMISED STRENGTH IN THEE TO KEEP ME IN THE WAY I SHOULD GO.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(10/25/41 GD's notes:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Dr. Hudgins.  The Genuine Black and White beauty preparations include a Quinine Shampoo.  Meredith's, Va. Beach, has a supply of these on hand.)
