# TEXT OF READING 2996-1 F 29

**Reading [[2996-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/12/1943  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 10297

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through Mrs. [3006], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [2946].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

St. Luke's Hospital, 
Time of Reading                          6th floor, 
3:35 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.     [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, it would not be of a helpful influence to interfere with those administrations that have been begun in the present.

4. While there might be disagreement with some, and agreements with some others, we find that suggestions through this channel cannot be of a helpful force in the present.

5. But there should be greater precautions taken as to the quantity of the drug that is being administered that deals with infection.  For, the quantities given are producing infection in other forces and manners in the body, rather than reducing same.

6. When there have been changes from the present environs, - should there not be too great a strain put upon the central nervous system, - we may then give helpful suggestions.

7. But we are through for the present with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [3006]
"    " Ass'n file
