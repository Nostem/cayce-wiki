# TEXT OF READING 4061-1 M 21 MONTHS

**Reading [[4061-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/15/1944  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 11756

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]], via Mrs. A. F. MacArthur.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) [4061]''s mother and friend.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4061].

3. There are disturbances preventing the normal development of this body but we find that there may be brought better conditions, if there will be taken the proper precautions, though it will require persistency and consistency.

4. These, then, are the conditions existent and that are to be met:

5. From temperature, congestion and those activities in the throat, bronchi and lungs, there was caused a suppression in the coordination of the circulation between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous systems.  This especially in the areas of the brachial center has caused a deflection that prevents the normal flow of circulation between impulses and the deeper circulation to the optic forces.  Also in the lumbar axis the suppressions have caused the inability as yet for controlling the locomotory centers.  As we find, both of the locomotory centers are affected, though the brachial center shows the effect more to the sensory organism or especially to that portion controlling the optics and the nerves of the optics. While there is not the taking away entirely of the [[Visions|vision]], there are those incoordinations preventing the proper activity in these directions.

6. For the swelling produced along the optic center, do not operate - else there will be formed across the area, scars that may not be eliminated.  While there might be some help, scars would be formed that would continue to hinder full development.

7. The general activities in other portions of the body are very good, or will be with the applications for strengthening nerve tissue and for supplying from that assimilated the necessary influences for the reproduction of activities through portions of the body.

8. For as indicated ever, there is gradually a replenishing of every portion of the body, if the proper balance is kept in the system so that the eliminations and assimilations are such that each portion of the system may reproduce itself.

9. These we would add for the body here:

10. We would begin with the use of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], though for this particular body we would use it a little stronger than the regular charge.

11. Use, then, two pounds of the Copper Sulphate and the other ingredients in proportion.  This is static electricity, the lowest form.  Through this we would carry into the system vibratorially those properties that will supply through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system and through the eliminations the activities to rebuild nerve energies.

12. The Appliance would be used each day for thirty minutes, one day using [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Solution (in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water) the next day using [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], commercial strength.  These are to add to the purifying of the system and to replenish the energies in the nerve reflexes themselves.

13. Each day the large nickel plate, (through which the solution passes vibratorially) would be attached at the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus or two fingers (the width of the mother's fingers) from the navel center to the right and three fingers up from that point.

14. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] the small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal center.

15. The next day when using the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution, the small copper plate would be attached at the plexus between the shoulders - the 2nd, and 3rd dorsal center.

16. Continue alternating the attachments in this manner.

17. Be sure to keep the attachment plates clean.  Polish them with the emery paper each time when removing from the body and each time before attaching.  Do disconnect from the solutions when not in use.  Do connect the Appliance twenty minutes before the attachments are to be made to the body for their activity in the system.

18. Each day when removing the Appliance give the body a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with cocoa butter.  This should be begun at the base of the brain and extend up over the mastoid areas to [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] and under the point of the chin, and the centers just between the eyes and over the eyes; so that the nerves to the brain forces in their reflex action will be carrying the energies that are created by the daily uses of the Appliance.  Then begin at the 1st cervical, massaging gently, more on each side of the spine than on the spinal column itself, along the sympathetic nerve areas, and at the 3rd cervical, 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal, 9th dorsal and throughout the lumbar-sacral areas.  These areas particularly should be stressed in giving the thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with the cocoa butter.  This should not be hurried through with but should be given gently and thoroughly.  Give constructive suggestions to the body especially during the period when the Appliance is being used and when the massages are being given, for the body will tend to go to sleep during these periods.

19. Do these, keeping the normal diet, and in sixty to ninety days there should be found more quiet, a great deal of improvement; and then there may be given those changes for corrections that may bring the nearer to normal conditions of this body - if there are no operations.  If the operation is necessary, from the growths this may be later performed, but as we find if these applications are kept in the manner indicated, it will not be necessary.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  Is there anything else to be done for the locomotion or towards strengthening the legs?
(A)  Do give the massages in the lumbar-sacral area, and let it extend over the limbs.  This, will, through these applications indicated, bring locomotion to the body.  Use the cocoa butter in all of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], at least during the first ninety days.

22. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

If you are in doubt as to the places of attachment for the Appliance, we would suggest that you get in touch with Dr. Marion Dick, 5041 Spruce St., Philadelphia, an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] who has handled several cases for us with remarkable results. She will no doubt be quite interested in observing [4061]'s progress.)
