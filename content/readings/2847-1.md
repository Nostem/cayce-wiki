# TEXT OF READING 2847-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2847-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/11/1942  Sex: F  ReadingID: 10066

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the niece - [2958], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [623].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Military Hospital, 
Time of Reading                           Room 9, Ward 9, 
3:35 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.      Third Floor, 
[[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now as we find, while conditions have been and are still rather serious, in the circumstance and the conditions that exist, it would not be well to interfere with the administrations that are being made.

4. For, we find that there is the comprehending of the circumstance, and conditions will - with those careful attention - move for improvements in the body forces.

5. When there has been sufficient recuperation for the body to be removed from the present environs, and there is the desire for further instructions, these would be the correct times to give suggestions through this channel.

6. Keep the attitude of helpfulness, hopefulness; knowing from and of Whom help comes.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [2958] - Air Mail - Special
"    " Ass'n file

(Let us know when she is home and we will make an appointment to give a Ck. Physical Rdg., if desired, without further cost.)

> R1. 11/13/42 EC's ltr. to Miss [2958]:
> 
> Dear Miss [2958]
> 
> Yours of the 9th received - and as you know we attempted the
> information for your Aunt [2847].  Feel from the information,
> she must have shown some improvement and as most always, they
> will not be interference by the reading when one is in a
> hospital - unless it is very urgent.  When your Aunt is
> better and out of the hospital, will be glad to try and
> help if she so desires.  Am afraid too, your Aunt was not
> aware of the information being taken was she?
> 
> Do hope she will soon be better, thank you for your interest
> and trust we may be of a real help.
> 
> Sincerely [signed] Edgar Cayce
> 
> Sending under separate cover a booklet - this may explain
> the work some what in line with what you have heard.
> 
> R2. 2/1/43 Niece, Miss [2958]'s letter:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I regret the delay in answering your letter relative to your
> reading for my aunt, Mrs. [2847] at the [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Military Home,
> [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], November 11, 1942.
> 
> Immediately upon receipt of your reading I made arrangements
> for a close and understanding friend of my aunt to be there
> at the time you specified; however, I subsequently learned
> that on that particular afternoon my aunt had a very severe
> vomiting spell and hemorrhages.
> 
> When I went to [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] the 13th of November I found her
> condition much worse and she passed on the morning of
> November 17, which was a shock to me as I felt somehow
> - even though her condition was serious - that she would
> recover.
> 
> I read your report to Mrs. [623].  She said it was so
> different from the one you made for her daughter.  I can't
> tell from your report whether you realized at the time that
> her case was hopeless.
> 
> I am enclosing a money order for $10 and hope to send the
> remaining $10 to you February 15.  Am sorry about the delay
> but I had some other financial matters that had to be taken
> care of.
> 
> Very truly yours,
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> R3. 2/4/43 EC's ltr. to Miss [2958]:
> 
> 
> Dear Miss [2958]
> 
> Have yours of the 1st, with enclosure, thank you.  Had heard
> from Mrs. [623] of the death of your Aunt, was indeed sorry
> to hear of this, but felt from the first it was almost
> hopeless.  Am sure that is the principal difference Mrs.

> 
> No, the information is not a cure all, at all; far from it.

> 
> Do not send more Miss [2958], unless you wish to.  But hope
> you may find something of interest in the data as may be
> sent you from time to time for the next year, unless you
> had rather it not be sent.
> 
> Thank you and with every good wish
> 
> Sincerely [signed] Edgar Cayce
