# TEXT OF READING 341-35 M 24

**Reading [[341-35]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/24/1931  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 2674

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of November, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [341].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [341], Sarah Hesson, Sarah E. McPherson, Anne Penn and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  In some respects we find conditions are very good in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Those tendencies for the body to burn the candle, as it were, at both ends, and fight down the resistive forces, must necessarily bring its results in the physical activities of the body, both as to its mental abilities and physical resistance, and as to the physical organisms of the system that are under strain through overtaxing the system in any way and manner.

2. We would use more discretion.  We would act in a way that would be in keeping with that that is really DESIRED, for the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

3. Keep the system cleansed properly.  These, we will find, will aid in bringing about nearer normal forces for the body.

4. More of the manipulations would be well, taken occasionally, once a week or three times a month, until the adjustments are made in the nerve forces of the body as respecting the sensory organism, from those conditions as have existed and have been corrected in the larger part in  the cervical and cerebro-spinal system.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What about the eyes at present?  What is the condition?
(A)  As has been given!  Well, they are not as good as they have been!  Read just what has been given - this may be seen!

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
