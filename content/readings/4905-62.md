# TEXT OF READING 4905-62

**Reading [[4905-62]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12987

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:20 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will tell us the conditions of operations at the present time, and give information as to how they may proceed to make a success of these operations.  You will also answer any questions I may ask you regarding these operations.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find the way and manner in which the success of these operations may be brought about would be in this manner: In the setting of the casing to shut off the cavings (which continue in part), and to prevent the water giving trouble, and also the casing and pump as is seen in the hole.

4. The pump, as we find, is to the side and almost straight. The casing piece being to the upper strata somewhat - that is, from the pump base, see?  and Northeast, Southwest, in the hole, lying crosswise, see?

5. In the manner, then, would this be prepared - lowering casing to within the reasonable distance of the bottom, see? suspending same with the spider, and running tool or sinker to know that same is clear.  There will be about a hundred and forty-five (145) cubit feet of the cement to be set so as to prevent troubles from the water, and from the casing and pump causing trouble, when drilled through.  When casing is set, or suspended, swab hole sufficient to remove water. Then pour cement in (by dumping) and allow to stand for thirty to thirty-six (30 to 36) hours.  Then drill, for there will be less than thirty-one (31) feet to make from the bottom of the hole for production to be reached, making then, as is seen, about a hundred and three (103) feet to be drilled after casing is set, and cement in same, and ready for the drilling.  Do that.  Ready for questions.  This, as we see, would insure the success of the operations.  Little, if any, trouble will be encountered by the casing and pump as is in hole, should this mode be followed, depending, of course, upon how dumping of cement and certis (?) acts upon the casing in hole as same is dumped, but, as to the PRESENT condition, and as to the condition as we would see from pulling off water would bring, there should be no trouble with this.  Just prepare as given - drill in.

6. (Q)  How big in diameter, in depth, is -
(A)  (Interrupting)  Why do we have diameter and depth?  Know the square foot!  This we have given, see?  Know that to be covered!

7. GC:  That is all the questions.

8. EC:  Divide the number of square feet by 3.116 (?), and we will find the number in the diameter.  Multiply this by the half of the square root of this, and we will have the depth.

9. GC:  Of the caved section below the 5900 feet?

10. EC:  There has been given the amount of space to be covered! DO IT!

11. We are through.

> My dear Mr. Wyrick:-
> 
> Yours of the 28th received.  I am certainly sorry to know
> they're having so much trouble with [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], but we all, I
> am sure, anticipated as much when we found the tools wouldn't
> go through the casing.  I'm sure this is growing very heavy
> and burdensome, but I am in hopes from this as was gotten
> to-day that if we can carry this part of it out, we'll have
> no further trouble.
> 
> Be sure and let me hear from you about this, and what they
> all think of it at [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]].  You know we are mighty anxious
> and would like to be of help whenever it is possible.
> 
> Mr. Wyrick, our people from [[New York City|New York]] were down here the other
> day, and we are considering the feasibility of opening an
> institute here at once.  What would you think of it?  Our
> plan was to organize it in a benevolent corporation, put a
> physician in charge, start on a small way, perhaps rent a
> house that could be fitted up with six or eight rooms to take
> care of any that would come in - start, of course, first with
> the regular physician, nurse and orderly - then the physician
> could call in whoever he saw fit to use under the existing
> circumstances.  This, as we figure, ought to be almost
> self-sustaining from the start.  It will require a whole lot
> of work on the part of the physician, and perhaps without a
> great deal of pay for the first year, except "board and keep"
> and a good lot of prospects.  We believe we have the right
> sort of fellow interested with us.  He is a man from [[Kentucky|Kentucky]]
> - Dr. House, who has had a good deal of experience in
> institutional work - is a graduate M.D. and [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].  We
> We could also prepare, under his supervision, all of the
> prescriptions that are given for people that were not in the
> institution.  We feel like that, with the number of readings
> we are getting, we ought to have at least one or two every
> month, that would come to the institution.  That ought to
> keep us fairly well going - in a small way, of course.  Now,
> in such an organization, we have had the attorney looking
> into the legal phase of it for the last week.  They haven't
> rendered their opinion as yet, but they tell us they think it
> will be alright.  Of course, the only phase is what would be
> my official connection with such an institution.  Such an
> institute, then, of course, is under the supervision of
> trustees [Work:EC:Trustees:ANI] or managers, and I will want
> as many of those my own close personal friends as possible,
> and of course we expect you to be one of those trustees,
> or managers.  We want at least nine in all, I think.  You
> have told us to always count on you as being one of these.
> This does not carry any financial obligation, but would
> necessitate your meeting with the Board at least once or
> twice each year.  Would like for you to tell me, frankly,
> just what you think of the idea.
> 
> We are keeping several weeks ahead with appointments.  We are
> not getting as many contributions as we would like to have,
> or as we could USE.  I believe that, having the institution,
> would change this somewhat, or do you think it would?
> 
> We also have this group from [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].  I believe you
> met Mr. Mohr in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], or at least Mr. Mohr met some people
> in [[Florida|Florida]] (supposedly very wealthy people) and they have him
> here making reports to them every day or two.  [See [5741]
> series.  LANDS:FLA:Dade Co.]  They are also trying to get, of
> course, some information as respecting some oil properties in
> [[Florida|Florida]].  So far they seem to be checking alright.  I don't
> know what the outcome of this will be, but I am in hopes will
> be beneficial to all concerned.
> 
> As for the reading that you asked for you - we will get
> these this week.  This bunch from [[New York City|New York]] and the ones from
> [[Florida|Florida]] being here, this has kept us on the jump for the
> last two weeks, and we have appointments for well up into
> September already.  But I'll get these this week, and I
> hope they can do some good.
> 
> Well, I certainly hope that [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] reading will prove
> interesting, and with kindest personal regards from all,
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD

> of closer relations with the Infinite forces controlling
> all [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] physical and spiritual.  Your work will be the
> only asset of such an institution and you may not hope to
> demonstrate in more volume than at present and there will be
> much more expense to be taken care of out of your earnings.
> An institution with medical professional attendants will
> be no more attractive than are the thousands of such
> institutions now operating throughout the country.  My
> thought of an institute suitable for you, one that will
> demonstrate more fully to the populace the psychic work most
> peculiar to your talent must be one in which an endowment
> must care for the expense of operation at least for a time.
> And you must be assisted by a staff of people with talents
> like or similar to your self.  Then and not until such an
> arrangement can be perfected will an institute be a success,
> until such an arrangement can be made.  It is best you
> work alone.  Too much association with professional people
> discussing the readings you give, giving opinions from the
> conscious mind to you in the conscious state detract from
> your demonstration.  The readings will become less valuable
> to the [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] for whom given - less clear and correct -
> your tendency is to inject into the reading suggestions given
> the conscious mind.  Pardon me for speaking thus plainly and
> critically.  I have your best interest at heart and am most
> sympathetic with the work.  Do not mix your talent with
> professional work for yours is from the storehouse of
> knowledge from whence all conscious thoughts come.  Honest
> effort continued as you are going will eventually reveal to
> mankind the "lost word".  Would love to talk to you on this
> subject but cannot get away just now.  Let me know what you
> decide to do.
> 
> Very best wishes to yourself, family & [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]],
> M. B. Wyrick

> My dear Mr. Wyrick:-
> 
> I want to thank you for yours of the 15th, and I certainly
> do wish that I could talk with you concerning the project
> in mind, and the manner and way of handling same.  Honestly,
> I had never gotten just the angle as you present, though
> I have known that such a thing would be very possible, was
> the physician in charge of the dictatorial nature.  My whole
> thought and idea has been to be able to serve those better
> who ask for the readings.  It was not an idea to have the
> hospital a money making proposition at all.  If we were able
> to even meet expenses, we would feel that we would be doing
> wonderfully well, and while the institution would be builded
> around the work I am able to do, still it would separate and
> apart from the regular readings as we give at the present
> time - but there are so many (and the number is increasing)
> that are asking if there is not a place where they could have
> the suggestions as are given in the readings carried out to
> the letter by a regular physician, and we know this is not
> possible in any institution in the country at the present
> time.  Just whether, now, there would be enough of these to
> make this support itself, is the question.  The doctor and I
> figure that it would, for, where we have twelve to fourteen
> readings a week, at least one or two would consider the
> institution, if we had one, and unless the doctor could cure
> them mighty fast, at that rate it wouldn't take very long to
> have a pretty good house full.  I'm sure you are correct in
> that it should be an endowed institution, and I'm sure it's
> going to be necessary for the expenses of same to be made up
> by outside individuals, but we have reasons to believe that
> there are a few individuals already interested who will
> make contributions to this fund from time to time.  Then
> you see how necessary it is that I know the managers and the
> distributors of such funds.  Perhaps I am getting a little
> fast, and should wait for some other development, but so many
> are begging for a place where they can have these suggestions
> carried out, to the letter, and I don't know how this can be
> done without the hospital.  I believe if you knew the man we
> want to put in charge, you would know there was nothing save
> the desire to be of service in his make-up.  I think I sent
> you a copy of the Life Reading we had on him.  There must
> have been a time when you two had considerable words, but I
> believe you could agree now, without question.  It isn't that
> I can serve more, that I want the institution, but that I can
> serve better.  We could tell mighty soon just how well this
> would serve, if we had an institution.  I'm sure this man
> has had experience sufficient in his own life, and with each
> member of his family, to put him in that position wherein
> he does not question, but ACTS!  He has also had experiences
> with others, and, as he says, more and more he is convinced
> that if he could only have a place where this could be
> followed without question, the results would soon speak
> VOLUMES for themselves.
> 
> We have had the report from the attorney.  With same we can
> certainly understand, now, why the readings have said that
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] was the place from which to operate, for
> it seems to have been enacted to fit the situation in its
> entirety.
> 
> My idea was (and I don't know whether it will succeed or not,

> the readings for in the last twenty-five years, asking them
> to establish a fund sufficient to begin this work.  I believe
> I'll get it if I ask for it, and start with this, we would
> be in a position to be endowed.  Do you think this is a

> this angle, and see if you can't get down here real soon, and

>

> Hoping you can get by to see us in the next few weeks, and
> with kindest personal regards from all,
