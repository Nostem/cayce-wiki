# TEXT OF READING 4281-4 F 5

**Reading [[4281-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/9/1924  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 12064

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [4280].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; A. M. Banning, Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce, Lucille [[David Kahn|Kahn]] (?).]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                              ..., Ga.

1. LS:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will also answer all questions I will ask you relative to the condition and treatment of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this body before.  You see.  Now, we find the body in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] better than when we had it here before.  There are conditions that need correction, especially those that have to do with the digestive system.  The change in the milk diet not been good for the body.  This milk as used should be heated before given the body, whether in cereal or as diet.  The whole wheat as used for the body is very good.  The apples as given the body should be roasted and not so much of the raw fruit given.  Better that the potatoes as given be roasted in jacket or boiled in the jacket.  See?  Dry.  And that those properties of citrus fruit by the change between orange and grapefruit.  This may be given with very small quantity of sugar added to the juice, provided the sugar used is beet sugar.  The Bulgarian milk as given is very good, yet should be seasoned for this body at the present time.  Meats, the darker portion of fowl may be given the body, and the juice of meat; that is, of beef may be given.  This diet, we find, will reduce the quantity of the acid as forms in the duodenum and in the colon.  The regulation of whole wheat regulating the condition in lower digestion and in the colon proper. Let that given as [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body include those of [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] as well as the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] [[Vibration|vibration]].  Do this, and we will bring a better condition for this body.

3. (Q)  How often and for what period of time should the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] be applied?
(A)  For three minutes every other evening before retiring.

4. (Q)  At what portion of the body should the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] be applied?
(A)  Principally at the solar plexus center, or the 10th and 11th dorsal center.

5. (Q)  At what intervals should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  Every third day.

6. (Q)  How should the Bulgarian buttermilk be seasoned?
(A)  Salt and pepper.

7. (Q)  Should the body take any of the Holstein sweet milk?
(A)  As given, very good.  That is, heated before used for this body.

8. (Q)  Should the body take any very ripe bananas?
(A)  No.

9. (Q)  What causes the body to grind or grit her teeth while sleeping?
(A)  Contraction of the action of the nerve system between the hypogastric and pneumogastric circulation, affecting the reflexional and salivary [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]].

10. (Q)  Would the climate at ..., Tennessee, be agreeable to this body?
(A)  Very good.

11. (Q)  What vegetables should be given this body?
(A)  Those as have been outlined for this body, heretofore. Give these in the way as has been given.

12. This is all of the questions.

13. We are through for the present.
