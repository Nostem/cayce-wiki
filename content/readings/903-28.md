# TEXT OF READING 903-28 F 36

**Reading [[903-28]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/28/1938  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 5701

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we have the impressions of the body here.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is the body in proper condition for the operation?
(A)  As we find, if there are the precautions that have been indicated - as to the care during and AFTER such - very well.

4. (Q)  Are the organs in position?
(A)  Very well.

5. (Q)  Any further advice regarding this body?
(A)  Very well.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [257]
"    " Ass'n file

[Wired also]:  [903] VERY WELL IF PROPER PRECAUTIONS DURING AND AFTER OPERATION.
