# TEXT OF READING 5671-3 F 34

**Reading [[5671-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/2/1929  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14085

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5671].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Berger, Hansen and Parker, Mrs. Hopp, Mrs. [5671], Mrs. Ringle, Mrs. Murray, and Miss Dozier and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5671].  This we have had before. Changes are seen in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that as we have had here before.  Most of these are for the betterment.  The eliminations set up properly, with the relaxing of the muscular forces as have been begun, and the corrections in the cerebro-spinal centers as have been given SHOULD be, we will find the response in the proper way respecting the conditions as have and do exist in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Any special diet?
(A)  That as is alkalin in its reaction, though the blood building materials should be well balanced with same.

3. (Q)  How often irrigations?
(A)  At least every week, until the whole of the colon is cleansed.

4. (Q)  Why does body feel worse?
(A)  Retraction.

5. (Q)  Should body have rectum attended to?
(A)  It's been in part!  Why stop there?

6. (Q)  What causes trouble in rectum at present?
(A)  As given, the prolapsus as comes from the upper muscles in the lower portion of the colon.

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Berger
"    " Dr. Parker
