# TEXT OF READING 378-23 M 56

**Reading [[378-23]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/24/1934  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2905

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by Miss [[275], daughter], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  In appreciation of the help that has been given [[Soul|the soul]] of [378], through this channel, we ask that it be continued - and urge him to have faith in his desire for his home.

2. EC:  Yes.  Come!  Why dost thou rebel against those impulses that arise?  Why dost thou continue in subjugating the better self, in making for those effects that will weaken those desires?

3. Let rather this as He gave fill thine self, thine mind, thine body, to the desire to put same into action:

4. "I will arise and go, trusting in the Father and His promises that though I wander far afield, though I have erred in word and act, I will accept that forgiveness and make the start to correct in myself in such a manner that I may claim those promises mine now."

5. The way seems hard, and self will feel as a pressure - yet He will make thine heart glad, wilt thou but come.

6. Yield not to self, but rather to that desire that is growing in thee to be with and among those that love thee best.

7. COME!  He will not forsake thee, and thou wilt not walk alone!  He will guide thee, and protect thee!

8. Be not overcome, but overcome with the good that He will do in thee.

9. We are through.

10. (Then, in such an undertone, just could hear it, "His cough is bad.")

[When EC awoke he put his hand to his head, saying he felt like there was something on it.]

Copy to [275]
"    " Ass'n file
