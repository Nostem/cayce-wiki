# TEXT OF READING 900-60 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-60]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5263

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Street, 
1:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [900], his mind, and the [[Dreams|dream]] he had on the night of April 11th, or early morning hours of April 12th, 1925, on board the train from New Orleans, La., to [[New York City|New York City]].  You will interpret same in a manner understandable to the mind of [900], giving the lesson to be gained and give that which he may do to use that information given in the lesson or [[Dreams|dream]], which was as follows:  (Reading)  [GD's note:  I can find no copy of the [[Dreams|dream]].]

2. EC:  Yes, we have the mental forces of the body, [900], and the dream had on the 11th and 12th of April, 1925.  We find these separate and different.  One, the first, regarding the [[Visions|vision]] or impression of information that bank failed in [[Chicago|Chicago]], trouble in [[France|France]], sell steel, this as we find is again that of the impressions as are acted upon the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces reacting when the conscious forces are laid aside, bringing then impressions, yet the same impressions weighted with material conditions.  As will be found, there will be from existent conditions in [[Chicago|Chicago]], and from information regarding conditions in [[France|France]], will cause on 15th and soon after the change in the valuations, or quotations in valuations of steel.  This will cause something of a flurry, be the reaction from such conditions.

3. As to the lesson that should be gained from this is, the individual should be on the watch for the conditions to arise, and in his own mind, as determined in dream, whether he shall use information to protect self in manipulation of stocks and bonds held in such commodities.

4. In one pertaining to the [[Visions|vision]] of the home and former conditions, that of the retracing of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces to the conscious mind, seeking out those channels wherein with the remembrance of such relation there comes those satisfactions in many ways and manners.  [[Soul|The soul]] forces in [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] mental action finding the resuscitation of elements necessary in the life of the individual, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

5. The lessons should be then that of not the secular things, not material things; those that bring to the mental those that may be lived, cherished, nourished, brought to remembrance with a satisfaction from time to time.

6. We have finished with this.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 4/14/25 At 4:25 P.M. see 900-61, a Business Reading.
> 
> R2. 4/21/25 "On 4/15/25 steel had a flurry as you said and rose
> in price to 117-1/2.  I had sold previously at a lower price
> and was beginning to wonder, but had the faith in my [[Visions|vision]]
> that it was best and sure enough steel has since sold down to
> 115-1/2.  It looks - the whole market looks lower.  The
> trouble in [[Chicago|Chicago]] is and was the smash in the price of
> grain, and whether anyone there has become financially
> embarrassed by this remains to be seen.  As to [[France|France]], they
> are surely having financial trouble and news from Europe
> grows daily worse.  Altho I know all this I have not of my
> own volition, for my account, sold a single share of stock
> short.  Whether my [[Dreams|dream]] gave me the right to or not I am
> not sure, and I surely wish I knew, but the past few weeks
> have taught me much and what with that lesson fresh in my
> mind I refuse to do it.  Furthermore, I hold my love of God
> and Father too dear, and my faith too high, to bargain it
> away for money.  I have come to realize there are more
> valuable and finer things in life to be attained than great
> wealth, so I'll do the best I can, knowing that I shall
> always be taken care of.  The [[Visions|vision]] regarding steel came
> after my resolution to follow this policy."
