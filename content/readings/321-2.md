# TEXT OF READING 321-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[321-2]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/24/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 2421

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Ernest W. Zentgraf, 400 St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]''s Ave., Stapleton, Staten Island, [[New York City|New York]], this 24th day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [321], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [321].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [321], present in this room. Associations with this body we have had before.

2. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find conditions rather that the body should take warning concerning, than any great disturbances that exist in the present.  Rather those that are having such influences in the general activities of the functioning system as to disturb, and thus producing that which will cause greater distresses - unless corrected in its incipiency.

3. These, then, are conditions as we find physically with this body, [321], present in this room:

4. The BLOOD SUPPLY we find above the normal, produced - as will be seen - from pressures or conditions that exist primarily from a nerve center in the cerebro-spinal system, but causing in same rather a slowed pulsation, with an aggravation in the activities of same as related to the hepatics and to the pulmonaries.

5. Hence, as may be indicated from the activities in the ORGANS themselves, we would find this in the 9th and 10th dorsal area; making then for a reaction to the centers to the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal system, that produces in the body-balance that of an uneven reaction.

6. Hence, while the strength, the vitality of the body as yet is not impaired, the mental or imaginative - and the body as related to its activities through these - become involved, producing in this the inability for the normal rest or equilibrium to gain its usual influence in the functioning of the system.  Hence we have at times a headache, a repression, or bearing down across the lower portion of the body - in the hips and the lumbar area; with periods when the foods and the reactions of same do not seem to taste just right or to act with the system as they usually did.

7. As to the NERVE FORCES, these make for those reactions that have been indicated; also making for those periods when there is the fullness and the heaviness in the stomach after eating, that makes for periods of when arising from rest or sleep without the rest being normal; insomnia, and more tired at times when arising than when lying down.

8. As to the activity of the ORGANS THEMSELVES, as we find, these are very good in the main.  There is too great an activity in those portions that produce for the circulation through the upper portion of the body, which tends for the accumulations in the throat - from the activities of the lymph circulation.  This is a spasmodic condition, but if it continues must increase the flows of the blood supply to the heart's activities.

9. Lungs, bronchi, are clear; yet these fullnesses show in the larynx - as indicated, also making for the conditions in the heart's activity.

10. Digestive forces in system, as indicated - while the disturbances have not reached that point where the activities are such as to make for inactivity, yet these disturbing influences continue in the body.

11. In meeting, then, as we would find, the forces that will aid the greater in making for corrections and bringing about the better influences in the body-activities, we would first have those conditions in the lumbar and the sacral area and in the cervical area coordinated with the subluxations in the 8th and 9th dorsal area.

12. We would also use those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance, that will bring for the body the equalization of the circulation in the body.

13. These, combined with the corrections in the system, being mindful of the diet as to not overtax the system, will overcome these disorders and bring to the body the near normal reactions.

14. Ready for questions.

15. We would give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active (plain) in the extremities, opposite portions of the body, each evening when ready for rest - or if when overtired during the day, if this is applied for only a few minutes there will be felt the reaction; and the ability to rest, to overcome all those tendencies for the nervous reactions from those disturbing factors, will come about.  Use it from thirty minutes to an hour each day.

16. There should be at least eight to ten such corrections made.

17. (Q)  Outline a diet for the body?
(A)  The body is a very good eater!  We would outline rather that there be kept a normal balance in the diet, with a tendency more for alkalins than too much acids.

18. (Q)  Will the treatment suggested here completely relieve the trouble during sleep or lack of sleep?
(A)  It will completely relieve all of those conditions that disturb the body from being unable to rest normally.  For, it will not only relax the body but, as indicated, will overcome those tendencies for the nerve regurgitation - and for those pressures in the heart's activity, and the pressure in system.

19. (Q)  Has the navel trouble anything to do with this disturbance?
(A)  Only sympathetically.  As we correct these conditions as indicated, from the 8th and 9th dorsal area, we will make a much easier and a much better reaction for the whole system.  And this will not give a great deal of trouble.

20. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body at this time?
(A)  If we will make these, or have these corrections made in the body as indicated, and use the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as indicated in the Radio-Active Appliance, we will bring for the body a near normal reaction.

21. (Q)  Should the corrections be osteopathically or chiropractically given?
(A)  As we would find, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] would be much the better; for these as indicated from that given MAKE a coordination between the lumbar and the cervical WITH the subluxations in the 8th and 9th dorsal area.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Alkalin diet list was enclosed.  Also was told about Frank P. Dobbins, D.O., getting Radio-Active Appliance from Mr. [315].)
