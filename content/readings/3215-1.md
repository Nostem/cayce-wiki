# TEXT OF READING 3215-1 F 62 (Chiropractor)

**Reading [[3215-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/14/1943  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 10612

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [3215], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3215].

3. As we find, there are conditions that may be assisted in the present toward correction, as to prevent a great deal of disturbance in the body later, for there are those unbalanced conditions chemically that are gradually causing a "letting down" of the vital energies in the centers that coordinate with the impulses of the [[Mental Body|mental body]], and the activities of the central nervous system.

4. Thus the tiredness that is experiences by the body often, a stiffness in portions of the body activities, other than that experienced by the natural hindrances that are a part of the locomotion of the body.

5. These are shown specifically in the centers of the 9th dorsal area and in the 3rd cervical area.  These as we find, in the blood supply, bring about irregularity at times, slowing up of circulation through the lower portion of the body, and a quick pulsation at times through the lungs and the torso area; while at other times, as these are subsiding; we find those tendencies towards a fullness in the head, the throat, even in the chest at times.

6. In making corrections for these, as we find, - we would use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] in the same manner as the Radio-Active.  These [[Vibration|vibrations]] would bring for the body a stimuli that would be most beneficial.

7. Other forms of electricity overcharge at times and prove to be effective for a short period, but these in this form - in the low cellular force that builds to central portions of the body - will be most helpful.  This should be applied only when the body is tired and for a period of thirty to sixty minutes.  This may be done twice a day for thirty minutes, or once a day for sixty minutes, but not more than twice a day.

8. Following each application of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], give the body a thorough massage - in the form of a full Swedish massage.  This should include a stimulating of the areas especially in the 9th dorsal and throughout the cervical area.

9. These do.

10. And add to the body the vitamins B-1 complex.

11. Do these and we will make for much better conditions for this body, [3215].

12. Ready for questions.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  Also see article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of this Appliance, and a sheet of directions [which were enclosed] pertaining to manner of application of the Radio-Active Appliance.)

> you is paid for if not otherwise contracted.  In December I

> covering it up with a linen to keep clean.  According to your
> design - of which you find a copy here - I simply did not
> dare to set the thing in operation because to me it was
> confusing.  The wires seemed to be in the solution not the
> poles.  Some friends could not help me either.  So in my need
> I called on famous and very kind old Dr. Starr White [George
> Starr White, M.D.] who in all his life has done things like
> that and his office is full of copper wires etc.  He looked
> the appliance over and explained everything to me very
> nicely.  He said that he made such an apparatus more than 30
> years ago and enjoyed the whole matter.  But he said also,
> that it was about the crudest thing he ever saw for the price
> I paid for.  Well then there was sickness around and other
> things and when I came about to put my credentials into the
> water, I found everything soaking wet in the box.  The paper
> with which the jar was surrounded and on which it stood,
> simply looked as if someone had manipulated the box in a
> violent way, yet there was none but me who touched it.  I
> was dumbfounded, YET I PUT THE BLAME ON ME.  This time, when
> I had taken all the wet paper out, I put a News Paper, 4 time
> folded on the bottom and put the jar back for observation;
> and to-day - having not touched the box at all - I discovered
> again to my great surprise that the paper was again soaking
> wet and the box also and smelling moldy.  For the second
> time I had to dry the box before the fire.  Now there is
> not the slightest evidence of a crack on the jar and yet
> the water has been diminished to about 1 Gallon.  Now think
> if that had happened to the corrosive solution! - What could
> it be that such an amount could penetrate the pores of a jar?
> I think it was a good thing that I hesitated in order to find
> that out.  I shall send a copy of this letter to Mr. Godfrey
> of whom I have the impression to be a fine and absolute
> honest man.
> 
> I hope, dear Mr. Cayce, that I do not give you the impression
> as if I complained about unreal things, but I have to tell
> you the truth just as it happened.  What is to be done?  I
> could not stand such a disorder around me, one cannot fool

> 
> I hope you and the family - which became very large now - in
