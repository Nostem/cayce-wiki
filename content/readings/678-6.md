# TEXT OF READING 678-6 F 58

**Reading [[678-6]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/12/1943  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 4466

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [678], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.      Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are acute conditions from an OVERRELAXING of the sciatic muscles and nerves.  And these produce a lock or catch in the sciatic centers, and the feet draw or contract - as it does under the knees and along the lower portion, or the under side of the limbs.

4. In the present we would apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the lumbar and sacral area, AND heat.  Use two or three thicknesses of cotton cloth, or towelling, soaked in the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  When this area is relaxed quite a bit, then apply the same around the feet, see?

5. Don't be too active.

6. As soon as there is some relaxing by the heat AND the applications, DO have an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment.  This will possibly be in the evening.

7. There needs to be more of the treatments for aiding and bringing about a better condition in the feet.  The arches have given away, and there should be supports worn there all the while.

8. Do take a good cathartic, as the Rochelle's Salts, or Sal Hepatica, or the like - sufficient to relax and to flush the alimentary canal.

9. Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air - Special - Phoned also (2 copies, 1 for
"    " Ass'n file             Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]])
