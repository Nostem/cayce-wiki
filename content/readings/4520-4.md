# TEXT OF READING 4520-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[4520-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/20/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12398

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by her brother, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4520]''s brother.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are some changes, some improvements, some accentuated conditions in the body from that as we have had here before.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them at the present time.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, in this we find some change from that as we have had before.  The tendency toward the reduction in the red blood supply, with the light giving the tendency to overtax the system, especially as is shown in lymphatic circulation in the lower portion of body, or from that plexus in the sacral and lower lumbar region.  The numbers then in red blood lower than as had before, with the white blood count above that.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find improved.  This we find shows how the equalization in the nerve system, through manipulation and the application of [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] or light that assists the body.  The light, however, hardly of sufficient amperage or quality that carries the straining or restrain- ing condition through the blood stream.  This we find would assist more in eliminations and prevent the assimilation of those fluids turning to capillary circulation, causing the expansion of the capillaries in the extremities, as is still shown in the lower limbs at times.  In the light we should use more of the ultra violet rays that gives the blood the effect as is seen by ozone action in the lungs and in the effect as produced through the whole system.  Hence, the body would be better were it able to inhale more ozone or oxygen in the entire system, for we need the clarification and the change in the attempt, as it were, of increase in white blood forces through lymphatics, but turned rather in their regular channels of elimination.

In the lower lumbar and sacral we should have deeper manipulation to prevent this turning of the lymphatics to lower portion of body, and stimulating the solar plexus center, both in the 10th and 11th dorsal centers and in the plexus of the hypogastric and pneumogastric center, in the right side of body, near the Peyer's Patches.  This we find would stimulate the whole mesenteric system, causing the eliminations and the lack of assimilation through lymphatic circulation, stimulating the hepatics to their functioning and producing the nearer elimination.  Hence an equalizing in circulation, blood supply and equalizing in pressure in nerve distribution throughout the whole system.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find all of these improved.  With the nearer equalization in the nerve pressure and blood supply, we find the mental forces reacting to suggestion.  The greater influence will be received through the mental suggestion as outlined, through the treatments.  That is, keep those suggestions while the manipulation is given the body, of the ability of the physical through the mental to eliminate through proper channels.  Do this, with these changes as given, and we will find we will gradually build these conditions better for this body throughout, [4520].

7. (Q)  Is there any tubercular infection?
(A)  No tubercular infection, other than that being cared for in system.

8. (Q)  What is the blood count?
(A)  As given, the red blood below normal at present, with the white blood a little above normal at present. In numbers, nearly a million short in red.

9. (Q)  What causes the night sweats?
(A)  In the whole system of a human individual, we find as this:  There are cycles of functioning of organs of the body. The natural tendency then of system is upper circulation, or plain activity during the day, and of body building through trunk circulation during evening.  These are natural cycle conditions.  Then, the lack of the equalized circulation produces the over stimulation to mental forces, of the blood attempting to form this cycle operation going, as it were, against nerve plexuses, with the improper incentives, create the too full capillary circulation.  Hence the inclination towards respiration increased, or perspiration increased in sleeps.

Then this as given, in the change in the lights; that is, more of the ultra violet, less actinic force in the system, we will create the nearer circulation with the correct incentive.  The deeper manipulation in the lower lumbar and upper sacral, with the stimulation in the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus, will reduce these conditions.

10. (Q)  What causes the swollen feet?
(A)  This same condition, as given, of too full capillary circulation, produced by pressure in lower lumbar and upper sacral.

11. (Q)  What will reduce this swelling?
(A)  Equalized circulation through manipulation, and the whole condition changed, as it were, through these conditions as given.

12. (Q)  How is her mental condition?
(A)  This, as we see, and given, shows the improvement in the general condition in body, and may be further improved by the suggestions as should be given constantly as the manipulations are given the body, for as the body thinks, so the reaction must be found in a motor reaction in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  This, then, must be made of sufficient impression to reach, as it were, the inner self, so the body thinks itself, for we have good days, we have bad days - a reaction.

13. (Q)  Would the Morse wave machine help?
(A)  Better that of the Alpine or of the Rotogen ray [Roentgen-rays], for in this we have more of the mercury crystal light and less actinic force, and more ultra violet light in the body.

14. (Q)  Is the correct [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment being given with the lights?
(A)  Very good.  Only the changes as are given.  Stimulate the secretions of the hepatics, in liver and kidneys, which we will find by stimulating plexus of the hypogastric and pneumogastric where conjunction is made in right side and in solar plexus reaction, and deeper manipulation in the lower lumbar and upper sacral region, for we relieve tension by the deeper manipulation or the relaxing, and by the relaxing and causing the reaction in hepatics we reduce the tendency toward the blood to turn in the channel of destructive forces and increase the number in red blood supply necessary in the body.

15. Do that.

16. We are through for the present.
