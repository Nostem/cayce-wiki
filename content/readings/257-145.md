# TEXT OF READING 257-145 M 41 (Sales Mgr., Hebrew)

**Reading [[257-145]]** · Series: 250-261 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/12/1935  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 1417

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Artic Crescent [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(You will advise him regarding settlement and new sale of 
[[France|Paris]] Medicine Co.)

1. EC:  Yes.  Yes.  We have the enquiring mind of [257].  On counseling as regarding those conditions respecting this, we would advise - SERIOUSLY - that the advice and counsel of Reid be considered paramount in the issues that NATURALLY arise under the existent conditions.

2. To be sure, that agreement that has been made with Reid's associates and the officers and officials of the [[France|Paris]] Medicine must all be considered; but with the advice and counsel of REID IRRESPECTIVE - because these will disagree from the others - FOLLOW that.

3. Do not JEOPARDIZE the claim already had, BY indicating or releasing from same that to make a sales.  For all of these must be cared for.

4. We are through with this reading.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

Day Letter wired to Self % Hotel Statler, St. Louis, Mo., as follows:

Seriously advise considering Reid's counsel paramount. Naturally associates and officers must be considered, but follow Reid's advice irrespective, for it will disagree from the others.  Do not jeopardize claim already had by releasing from same to make a sale.  For all must be cared for.
