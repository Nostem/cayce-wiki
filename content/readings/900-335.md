# TEXT OF READING 900-335 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-335]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/18/1927  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5538

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Of recent date.  Voice:  "The pleasure I get from that baby!"  Exactly so.  I do get a great deal of pleasure from him.  What meant by this then?
(A)  More and more there is seen that - were the body, [900], in the way and manner of applying that which comes to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through the intuitiveness of the body conscious, or the [[Mental Body|mental body]] and the [[Physical Body|physical body]], the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] body correlating the truths, lessons - the application of same will mean more and more to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the way of development.

Here we find these conditions existent:  Study these, my man, from this viewpoint:

The conscious mind studying the various phases of development of the child, the baby, and with this there comes - when there is the least consciousness of some condition arising, or the POSSIBILITY of condition arising that might not be entirely satisfactory - the expression then comes as from from within OUT, that of the actions of the good, the joy, the peace, the understanding that comes to the body with the study of this development as is seen in the child.

Study from the viewpoint, keeping feet firm and mind aright, discriminating between the action of the mental mind or [[Mental Body|mental body]], the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or [[Soul|soul]] body, and that to which the two developing.  Remembering that this lesson may be gained in truth:  God the Spirit, and seeks spirit to worship same.  The Son the manifestation of that Spirit in the earth's plane and, as given of old, the answer from within through [[Soul|the soul]] - that spirit beareth witness with that spirit - that ye be the sons of God, or not.  For, as was given - "I go away, that I may prepare a place, that where I am there ye may be also.  If I go NOT away, [[Holy Spirit|the COMFORTER]] will not come" - Did my spirit not bear witness with His Spirit, it would not be possible then for the God within thine self to bear witness with thyself that ye may be brought to the [[Oneness|oneness]] with the Father.  See?  This construction, this understanding then, may be gained also in that as seen respecting the son [142], in this study of the baby life of the developing [[Soul|soul]], or the relationship - as it were - again of the son with the father, the soldier with the soldier, the action of the financier [142] with the theologian [900].

4. (Q)  Morning of August 15th.  I was in some sort of business establishment, seemingly Block, Maloney's new office.  It was a spacious place, but that which interested me were the examinations I had to take in the morning.  It seemed that I had to go up to college during the following morning.  It seemed that I had to go up to college during the following morning and take exams in chemistry, history, philosophy and english, etc.  I drove [137] up to Boston and showed him the old narrow English streets.  "Note how old and narrow they are," I said.  Then back in the office - the largest room of all - and said, "This is your room, isn't it Mr. Burnett?" He said yes.  I was pointing it out to Edwin, showing him that the blinds were drawn and room darkened now, but would be lighted later.  Then I noted an office with a large brass plate.
(A)  In this here (we will interrupt here, for this should be given in this way and manner to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]), it has often been given study thyself and study those interpretations that thyself may obtain.  This - as is seen - in an emblematical way and manner, and each figure as is shown may be drawn by the entity - and with the application of that as was given concerning the son, the boy, the baby, and that as is shown as to what is meant by [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s interpretation of "I go away", and the entity applying same in the changes that each position, each street, each light, each plate, each study, each exam, each figure represents - study thyself.  Then, understandest thou not in toto, ask and ye shall receive.

5. We are through.
