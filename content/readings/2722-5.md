Date: 7/18/1942  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9849

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the husband - [4959], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there is not the response that should have been with the application of those things suggested.

4. It would be better, then, if there would be had not only a nerve specialist but an excellent nerve gynecologist, for an interview respecting these conditions.

5. For, those pressures that continue to arise from the attempts of the body to adjust itself are working greater hardships on the body, as well as producing anxieties about the body.

6. Then, we would make those connections either with a specialist in [[Ohio|Cleveland]] or in Rochester, - making those associations, see?  either through Crile in [[Ohio|Cleveland]], or Mayo's.

7. (Q)  Would the removal of her female organs correct the condition?
(A)  If this is properly done, and there is the ability to adjust the body in its mental hopes and its determinations for its activities, this would bring about better conditions. It will require time, and some period of recuperation - but these are the more hopeful measures for this body.

8. (Q)  Is there anything she could rub over her neck and shoulders and back of head when the pain becomes so intense?
(A)  This as we find arises from those pressures deep within the body.  As has been indicated, there is not the response from the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxation given to the centers along the spine.  That is the reason why it would be necessary for the services of the nerve specialist, - Crile, or Mayo's.

9. (Q)  With the menopause over, would there still continue the jerking and pulling of head?
(A)  Not if the nerves are adjusted.  But you see, with the removal of the organs, this would discontinue just as soon as there were the adjustments made.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Husband - Air - Special
"    " Ass'n file

(Dr. George Crile, Euclid Ave. at 93rd St., [[Ohio|Cleveland]], Ohio; or Mayo's, Rochester, Minn.)

> that my friend, is only one of hundreds have experienced in
> the last 15 years.  No, no, no, not proof to any one but
> mighty meaningful to me.
> 
> Jesus told us the proof of any thing was in the "Fruit
> thereof".  The letter sent you a few days ago, is an answer
> to our boy's belief and practice of the information.  No, it
> may not be infallible - am sure it is misunderstood oft.  It
> is not me, [4959] that I know, for of myself can do nothing -
> only as the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] may work in or through me.  It is
> always the spirit of truth - there is, of course, the whole
> question - so long as I have Christ as [[Ideal|the Ideal]], am not
> afraid.  When self or self-interest - am afraid and it has
> failed.
> 
> Not so long ago, [4959], had this experience [GD's note:
> See this [[Dreams|dream]] also under 294-196, 12/12/42].  These am
> telling you not to convince you of any thing - only they
> are what have convinced me.  I do not ask any one to accept
> my experience.  Jesus promised to "come and abide with us"
> conditionally.  Did He mean it, or was it written badly.
> I believe it, and believe He meant what he said.  Had a
> [[Visions|vision]] - [[Dreams|dream]] or whatever one wishes to call such - I
> believe the [[Visions|visions]] had by the seekers after God of old,
> and Believe He is the same to day as He was then.  But any
> way, some one knocked at my door - I was alone in the room
> reading.  when I answered they asked "Would I go with them
> to a meeting that evening?"  At first, I made the excuse
> of not going out of evening, not having finished my S.S.
> lesson for the next day.  They insisted, I finally went
> out.  Another figure whom I did not recognize joined us;
> we walked toward the Ocean at its edge; we walked into the
> sky.  We came to a large tent where there seemed to be a
> meeting of some sort.  We approached, the one walking with
> me pulled back the flap of tent, then I knew with whom I
> had been walking:  Mr. Moody and Sam Jones.  The light was
> unusual, very weird, felt afraid - but was assured all was
> well by those with me.  Walked into this large place, people
> seemed to be gathering, hordes of them - all [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] be
> unreal to me.  Then a low rumbling noise from the front of
> the place, with lightening flashes.  I asked what that was.
> No form or figure could be seen.  A Cloud, or mass, - was
> told it was the All Mighty God that would speak to man.
> Then a voice asked "Who would warn my people?"  Again and
> again, it was asked, then I boldly asked "Could I do so?
> Would if was ready or acceptable."  Then out from the throng
> came the Master Jesus; He Blessed me and said "I will go
> with you all the way."  Unreal - self-delusionary, am sure
> you will say.  But [4959], it has given me a strength, a
> meekness and I hope a kindness that did not have before.
> No, this is not to be made a religion - a schism or a cult,
> never!  These are only assurances for myself, not to be told
> to bring other than the assurance that God is mindful of the
> children of men.
> 
> I hope you understand me, [4959].  I may be all wrong in
> even repeating these experiences, and know they are dangerous
> used in an unseeming manner - but God forbid, that I should
> boast of them in any sense!  I realize I am possibly
> entering the testing period of my life - many of us can stand
> adversity and seek Christ and His help.  Few can stand
> popularity and seek Him in same.  Pray for me that I keep
> in the straight and narrow.  One gets afraid at times, only
> HIS assurance can and will sustain.
> 
> Hope haven't wearied you.  How many personal experiences have
> had, as with the child, many.  But they, too, I have accepted
> as answer to [[Prayer|prayer]].  If I am fooling my self, they answer
> to me.  There is in such a life as is indicated in my son's
> answer to an offer of an easy way.
> 
> Write me again, [4959], tell me just how foolish I am.  Don't
> wash your hands of me, for I seek only the Christ way.  I
> have read "Oh yes - Jesus we know and Paul we know, but who
> are you?"  Hadn't the Master spoken to me, well I am sure.
> 
> Thank you - hope haven't bored you too much.  Do hope
> Mrs. [2722] is better.  Hope you have found just the right
> one to give her treatments.
> 
> With every good wishes and May His Blessings be with you,
> 
> [signed] Edgar Cayce
