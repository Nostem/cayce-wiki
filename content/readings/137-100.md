# TEXT OF READING 137-100 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-100]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/19/1927  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 490

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 19th day of December, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [137], through brother, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give to you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this [[Dreams|dream]], as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had here before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]] come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through those channels as has been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and may be used or applied in the daily life in the manners as has been given, for the better understanding of self and its relations with individuals and conditions and affairs in the material world.

4. Ready for dream.

5. (Q)  Of recent date.  [5768], with me on floor of exchange and now seriously ill, was about to board a train to take a journey.  His mother died, but he seemed reconciled as he said his mother was 80 years old anyway.
(A)  This is as an indication of the condition as it is to come into the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and of that one as is seen, and as the life - [137] - studies these presented truths to self, the entity, the inner being, should apply those lessons as may be gained from same, and these then are as warnings - but would the entity heed that as has been given, then more may be given.  Would the entity reject or leave aside those truths and lessons already gained, how will he apply those greater lessons as may be brought into the life, into the mind, or into the developing self?  Apply those already gained.  Keep that thou hast in hand, and apply the greater lessons to thine self.

6. We are through with the reading.
