# TEXT OF READING 4260-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4260-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/16/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12027

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, Room 1006, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 16th day of October, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stallings, Brazelton & Hale, Court Reporter, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.                                       ..., Penna.

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep.  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep and all of the organs of your body will function properly; you will hear me and answer all of my questions.  You have before you the body of [4260], ... Ave., ..., Pennsylvania.

Now, Mr. Cayce, we want you to go over this body carefully, and tell us all about her physical and mental condition, [4260], ..., Pa.

2. EC:  [4260], we have the body here.

FEM:  He is finding her; he will tell you all about her now.

3. EC:  Now this body is very good.

4. The abnormal conditions we find have to do with the replenishing the blood supplying forces that function to certain organs of the body - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

5. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body here, [4260] we are speaking of.

6. The blood supply in the body we find there is a lack in the hemoglobin of the component parts that take up the oxygen as is used in the system itself, so that there is gradually, little by little, left within the body some of the effect of this condition we have produced within some of the ganglias, where blood supply furnishes to the lymphatic circulation the charges that produce that over-taxed forces in the body - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

7. Till we produce in the action of the nerve, with mental forces, the conditions as expressed, to the capillary and to the emunctory circulation - see?
FEM:  Yes, sir.

8. The effect of this is shown through the upper portion of the lungs, and the action of the blood forces to the larynx, and this portion of the body, being effected the most, or showing the effect in the system of these conditions - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

9. The action of this force in the digestive tract give the expression to the circulation through this portion of the body the effect as we have as produced through the lower intestinal tract at the present time, the condition that exists through the ascending colon, the reflex condition as expressed through the pelvic organs; though these are not organic conditions, as shown through that part of the body, you see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

10. All are reached - all are brought about by the gradual disintegration of the rebuilding cellular force, as produced within the body itself.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

11. The effect to the nervous system is to produce at times overtaxed conditions, that is, the body becomes easily tired from certain mental strain as well as from physical strain, and one would act direct as much as the other effect on the body - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

12. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, what is the condition of this body's mental forces?
(A)  Mr. Cayce, what is the condition of this body's mental forces.  Through the action of the cerebro-spinal forces the direction of the mental forces is carried, just as we have the spiritual and mental forces act, with, through and by the physical condition in the body, we have the action of this body here of the mental forces, as being directed to the action of the physical - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

So we have the mental forces within this body as being directed through the action of spiritual rather than of physical.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

Physical, mental and spiritual forces are combined in one in the action over physical matter as expressed on this plane; so the action in mental force and balance here is used or directed in this individual by the spiritual forces - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

13. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, are there any treatments that you could recommend to be of assistance to this body?
(A)  Mr. Cayce, is there any treatment that you can recommend that would be of assistance to this body?

We have the conditions here, we will give what to do for the body.

14. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, are you studying the conditions of this body so that you can give proper treatment; is that why you are delaying?  Mr. Cayce, we are waiting for the treatment that you shall recommend for [4260], ...,  Pa.
(A)  Yes, this party has trouble with her teeth, too, and sometimes trouble over the whole portion of her body.  This bacilli that effects this should be corrected, from the other forces as would be applied to the system.

15. (Q)  What should be done with the teeth, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  There is a combination here:  Tooth-ache bark [Prickly Ash Bark] made into a very strong solution, enough of common powdered salt added to make a very thin paste, and that be applied to the gums, you see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

With the hand, only once in three or four days - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

16. (Q)  Then how often would this treatment be needed?
(A)  How often would this treatment be needed.  Twice, and then skip from five to six months before giving it again. That has very much of an irritant to it - the whole body, this condition has existed there.

For the nerve forces to produce the equilibrium between the cerebrospinal sympathetic forces and those of the sensory organism, where we have lesions along the spine here, notably at the 4th cervical and the 5th and 6th dorsal and the 1st and 2nd lumbar; we would relax these by neuropathic forces till we have the proper coordination between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal nerves, by the relaxing of the these muscular forces and of these ganglia, as we find in these places, to cause the proper flow of the circulation through these portions of the body, and by going through the mental forces to reach the spiritual which acts over the physical, as in this body.  Those suggestive powers to reach the reaction of the desires of the body and to produce through heart forces and stimulation of the solar plexus circulation as reached by suggestive force that which would bring a better temperamental condition within this body here - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

[4260], this is how and manner that we would reach the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] within this body.

The direct action as exists in this body here, we find the action of the mental and spiritual forces governing or using the forces of the physical - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

With the physical finding the lack of certain component factors in the blood supplying element of the hemoglobin in the body, till we have produced lesions across and along the spine, as we have given here.  These affect functioning organs - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

About the body - yet are not organic conditions, yet produced within the mental and physical status of this body the force as we find apply to the emunctory and nervous system. To reach this, we would relax, as we have given here the conditions along the spine by neuropathic force - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

Massage?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

And suggestions as would be reached through the mind or mental force itself, the health and vigor and vitality to reach through the pneumogastric or through the solar plexus circulation as is found in the individual - we would do that, [4260].

17. (Q)  Are there any medicinal properties that should be given to relieve this body?
(A)  Are there any medical properties that should be given to relieve this body.  Medicine is poison to this body.

18. (Q)  What about this body's diet.
(A)  What about this body's diet?  That will be governed by suggestive force, as used or applied to the body, rather than received direct from the individual, or by the study of forces to apply to the body - see?
FEM:  Yes, sir.

19. (Q)  Would this body be able to work at this time if she so desired?  Mr. Cayce, what media - what medium of mental suggestion would you advise for this body?
(A)  Mr. Cayce, what media, what medium of mental suggestion would you advise for this body.  Either that of direct mental or suggestive therapeutics, or that of Unity forces, as used or directed for the application of mental, spiritual and [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

20. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, would Christian Science assist this body?
(A)  Mr. Cayce, would Christian Science assist this body? We would advise the other, as being better; it is higher development of the same forces.

21. (Q)  What are those forces?
(A)  What are those forces.  Spiritual elements that enter into with mental action of the force.

22. (Q)  Is there anything further that you have to suggest to help relieve this body of its physical and mental ailments?
(A)  Mr. Cayce, is there anything further you have to suggest to relieve this body of its physical and mental ailments. Just the conditions as we have given, which makes of the spiritual force to be acted upon, and through which the body must receive the beneficial effects to both physical and mental forces, the relaxation of the conditions along the spine, and the treatment after this is done to the direct mental forces to act over the whole system, through both mental, physical and spiritual elements of the individual, and as received through the direction of the teacher or minister of the Unity force as applied to physical treatment of the body.  We are through.

23. Now, Mr, Cayce, you will forget all about this reading you have given; you are feeling all right and all of the organs of your body are functioning properly; you are feeling good.  Now, wake up in two minutes feeling fine and alright.
