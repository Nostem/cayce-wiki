# TEXT OF READING 3667-1 F 43 (Registered Nurse)

**Reading [[3667-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/17/1944  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 11169

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3667], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.  Contact:  [[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Maryland.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3667].

3. Now as we find, there are many disturbing conditions which have prevented the better physical functioning of this body. While there are apparently certain conditions blocked off in the system, all of these have to do with the general physical conditions of the body.

4. This then is an analysis of the disturbing conditions as we find with this body:

5. First, in the blood supply, this we find abnormal at times, owing to the general conditions of the circulation between heart, lungs, liver and kidneys.  Thus, we find that some of the functionings of these organs are at variance to the better conditions of the body.  These are the attempts of this central or deeper circulation through the organs to eliminate poisons from the system.  These cause for the body at times a disorder with kidneys and bladder, and the liver is not always setting up as much drainage as it should. Neither is there sufficient flow of the bile duct activity. And this causes certain types of food, especially any greases, to be disagreeable or to disagree with the body at some stage of its digestion.

6. In the nerve forces of the body, we find that the sensory is blocked, in a manner, or the auditory forces especially are disturbed.  These, then, cause the hearing to be deficient. Naturally, then, the eyes, the taste, the feeling through those portions as to the coordinant effect or the sensitiveness of the body, all suffer at or under varied conditions.

7. We find that these have reached those stages where, although there may be little definite change, there can be help to the auditory forces and in relieving those tensions - which also would aid in the restoration of better [[Visions|vision]], more uniformity in taste and in smell, and in all of the olfactory nerves, there will be attained a better reaction through a better lymph flow in soft tissue of head, neck and throat. For the nerves through those areas that connect with the central nervous system are disturbed functionally, as indicated from the upper dorsal and throughout the cervical areas.  Hence better associations should be established through these areas and also the eustachian tubes should be cleansed.  Not by tubes being injected or by these being blown out, but rather by a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with stimulation by electrical forces that may be directed to specific or distinct areas that will cause the circulation to be increased through these areas.  We refer to the heat wave or short wave electrical treatments for this particular area - 4th dorsal upward to the 1st cervical and around the face, the mastoid, [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], over the eye, the point of chin, even close to the ear in the frontal portion, or where the muscular forces are attached to the eustachian tube itself. These would be beneficial.

8. The nerves of the sympathetic system, then, are not in accord with the central nervous system or the organs of the sensory system.  Thus there are periods under the stress, when the body easily becomes aggravated or reacts to slights or the overactivity from others as to their self-importance or of such natures.  These cause the centers and incoordination to become more affected.  For through the lumbar and lower dorsal area, as associated with the disturbances in the eliminating from kidneys and the activity between liver and kidneys, we find that there becomes a pouring of poisons into the general flow of blood and the lymph activity through the abdominal areas.  Thus those conditions that reflect to the feet, toes, the bursa of the feet, the frontal portion, the heel, and afterwards the limbs ache.

9. All of these, of course, might be said to be symptoms.  But these are the sources from which these arise.  The disturbances in the central nerves and blood supply, or the organs - heart, lungs, liver, kidneys - these are the sources.

10. In making for assistance or in adding to that which would be helpful for this body:

11. First, there is needed a period of rest, when there would be good, thorough eliminations set up; when the gall duct area, the kidney area, the activity of the central blood and nerve circulation would be brought to such activity that during such a period of rest there may be natural recuperative forces for the body.

12. Following this, there would be electrical treatments for the eustachian tubes, the sinus condition, the mastoid area, the nerves of the throat, neck and head.

13. Thus, we will find that we may bring much better conditions for this body.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What part of the country would the best place be for me to take this rest?
(A)  As we find, [[Florida|Florida]] would be preferable; in such an area as near Clearwater or Lakeland, with the hospital or [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of activities there in drugless [[Healing|healing]].

16. (Q)  What can I take for my chronic state of constipation?
(A)  As indicated, all of the eliminations should be done rather by the diet, the water treatments, the oil applications and colon cleansings.

17. (Q)  Please suggest a doctor to carry out instructions.
(A)  You'll find a good one for such in Lakeland.

18. (Q)  The dreadful condition of the mucous membrane of my nose, mouth, throat, etc. does not heal - will you please advise me how to cope with this?
(A)  As we find, until there is a purifying of the eliminating system through the alimentary canal, this condition will not heal properly.  For we must change the circulation so that there are [[Healing|healing]] forces brought and not those that will be contaminated with the poison that should have been eliminated through alimentary canal.

19. (Q)  Is my deafness progressive?
(A)  At present it is.  As indicated, there are measures to be taken and we have outlined as to how these may bring material aid.

20. (Q)  What particular climate would it be best for me to live?
(A)  Where it is not too damp, and not too high an altitude.

21. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail Special
"    " Ass'n file

([[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]]'s address is 11 Glendale St., Clearwater Beach, [[Florida|Florida]].  He would no doubt know of this drugless institution, if you do not.  A Dr. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] E. Cold, 6212 [[Florida|Florida]] Ave., Tampa, Fla., has had experience with the readings and uses electrical treatments but this may not be the type you need at all.)
