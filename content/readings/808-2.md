# TEXT OF READING 808-2 F 27

**Reading [[808-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/22/1935  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 4864

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1867 Kalorama Road, Washington, D.C., this 22nd day of February, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [808], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [808], Mr. [2784], Miss [681] and Mrs. [839].]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will tell her if the diathermy treatments by Dr. Bolkhardt have been given as the reading suggested, after which you will answer any other questions that may be asked.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [808]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are on the improve in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Not only in the proper incentives and impulses being created in the centers of the system themselves but the general mental attitude and the outlook are on the improve.

4. Those administrations of the diathermy, as given thus far, are correct.  These should be, as indicated, from the brachial center, solar plexus center and lumbar center, so that these pass through the organs as indicated that need their toning with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the system necessary to create the proper balance in the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Please state just which centers should be -
(A)  (Interrupting)  Just been stated!  Brachial center, solar plexus center, lumbar center.  2nd, 3rd dorsal; 8th, 9th, 10th dorsal; 4th lumbar.  Lumbar center, solar plexus center, brachial center.

7. (Q)  Should the treatments be given as light treatments or should they be increased to heavier treatments as they go along?
(A)  As we find, for the better conditions for this body, as indicated, not too heavy a treatment.  The light treatment. The impulses are the better than that which would force through the activities in the system that are tense, to become reactive in the pulsations as created by the electrical forces.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Boklhardt) Copy to Ass'n file
