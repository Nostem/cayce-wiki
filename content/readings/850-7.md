# TEXT OF READING 850-7 F ADULT

**Reading [[850-7]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/2/1942  Sex: F  ReadingID: 5076

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [850], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [850]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while conditions are not entirely satisfactory, in some respects they are bettered.

4. What the body needs the most is a rest period from labors as well as from peoples and conditions; where it might remain in the open, read, think, bathe - and this preferably in spring or fresh water; and stay in the open for at least a week to two weeks.

5. Keep up the [[Healing|healing]] forces as indicated for clearing of the poisons from the system, - or the inhalations.

6. These we would do, - with plenty of food; plenty of chicken, plenty of liver, plenty of beans, plenty of squash and such.

7. These would be the better manners for the body, and we might save the body from a great deal of trouble later on.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  How often should [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] be added in the charred keg?
(A)  It would be well sometimes to cleanse the keg.  It should be added whenever there is not enough to give plenty of fumes off from same.  Keep this corked a little bit tighter, else you are losing a great deal of that you should be inhaling.

10. (Q)  Are the fumes from this particular keg correct?
(A)  If kept properly - might be made so, yes.

11. (Q)  What should I do for the weak spells I have?
(A)  As indicated, the best thing to do is to take a rest!

12. (Q)  Also sore throat?
(A)  Best to take a rest!  and use of morning a gargle with Listerine, and of evening with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

13. (Q)  Should I take Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]] again?
(A)  This depends upon whether or not you are to be in the open and rest!  If you are, take it!  If not, better leave it off for the time being, until you do take a rest - and then during that period we would take it.

14. (Q)  What shall I do for a bump on my finger, which looks like a blood clot from a pin prick?
(A)  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] it with [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] two or three times a day.

15. (Q)  For the condition something like neuritis in my hand?
(A)  You better take the rest, and keep your eliminations well!

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Bill for $10 was enclosed.)
