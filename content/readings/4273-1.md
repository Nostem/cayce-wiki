# TEXT OF READING 4273-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4273-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/2/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12041

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 2nd day of November, 1923, in accordance with request by daughter - Miss [4689].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M.                                    ..., [[Colorado|Colorado]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find, while this body is very good in many respects, there are some abnormal conditions that may be corrected in this system.  There are conditions that may be assisted to give the better [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the body, and in giving these, assist the body to receive that incentive that will bring about better physical condition within the system.  Those affected have to do with the elimination and assimilation in the system and the effect this, with the mental upon the physical, has produced within the system itself.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body. First, in the blood supply of the system, this we find very good as a whole, only reflecting that condition of the poor assimilation and elimination from various organs in the blood's circulation through the body.  Hence, we find in some portions of the body where the tissue and ligament by the distribution or distributing forces in extremities have become exaggerated and left circulation in place until some of the ligaments have become affected thereby, - such as we see in the hips at times, or better directly in the locotive [locomotive?] centers, see, this affecting along the sciatic nerve forces, in the brachial forces or to the extremities, see.  In the nerve centers of the body, with this contraction, we find some of the gangulae and gangulins [ganglia?]; in other portions become directly in the position as to receive the greater force by this action of contraction through the nerve by the circulation passing along these centers, see.  Such as we have in the sacral and ilium plexus and that in the secondary cardiac plexuses and in that of the plexus governing the action of the sensory forces in the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal, where all nerves enter the brain.  Hence the dull headache at times such as this body suffers, see.

3. In the organs themselves, we find as this.  The brain center itself very good, one differing much pathologically from normal forces.  This is when seen in its manifestations through its conversation with others, by the use of the forces or knowledge as stored there.

4. In the throat, larynx and bronchials, we find this show of the circulation, especially in that of the ducts that supply the circulation to the nutriment, to carry on this portion of the body, so the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] suffer.  In the lungs proper, very good.  With the digestion the seat of some of the troubles producing this, though toxins play the most important part as we shall see, as to conditions that were at one time produced in the intestinal tract.  Liver not as active as it should be.  Hepatic circulation high.  Pancreas, gall duct, spleen, in their relative forces very good.  In their active forces, singly, over-active at times, under at others, depending upon the secretions in the system.  In the intestinal tract, through the pyhes [peyer's?] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] region, these we see have lost their effectiveness, hence the condition as produced through the system and the toxins absorbed that have become in the circulation that element that gives to the mucous secreted and the lymphatic forces that which gives the force to the circulation to cause the distress in extremities, - that is the circulation to the heart, not in its active force, as that FROM the heart, see. Hence the condition produced.  The liver and kidney in their circulation producing the high state existing in the hepatics.  Kidneys not secreting effectively through the emunctories therein, yet as the organs themselves caused distress to the body on account of the reflex with the nerves of the lovotives [locomotives?] and of the branch or bush end of the cerebro-spinal end of the nerve cord itself, many reaching into this region, you see.

5. To give the balance of force in the body to equalize this circulation would be to give that which would change the [[Vibration|vibration]] and equalize the assimilation in the body.  Take then this into the system:

6. Plain phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]] 1/2 teaspoon in 1/2 glass of water, with 9 drops of syrup of sarsaparilla, with one drop of white oil of pine, or oil of white pine.

7. This would be taken each day in the morning before eating, for five days, then rest five days, then take again for five days.  While this is taken, and for the [[Purpose|purpose]] to give the rest to the body, give the [[Vibration|vibration]] as would be found from this:

8. Two pieces of chilled steel 1/2 inch square and 4 inches long, bound together with a non-conductor between, see, small copper wire, insulated or covered, if so desired, which would be better, attached to each bar.  To the other end of the wire, a small nickle or copper plate would be attached, so that when in use these may be attached, one to the wrist, the other to the ankle on the opposite side of the body. When ready for use, put in very cold or ice water, see.  Let remain for thirty to sixty minutes each time.  We will find we will correct conditions for this body and give relief desired by the body.

9. Do that.  We are through.
