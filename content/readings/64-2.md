# TEXT OF READING 64-2 F 23

**Reading [[64-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/11/1930  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 156

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March 1930, in accordance with request made by Dr. Lydic.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [64].  This we have had before. In some respects we find the body not so good as when we last had same.  The exercise, and the changes wrought in the physical functioning, has caused more distress in the mesenteric and in the hepatic circulation, and the pressure as produced there causes an over activity to the functioning of the organs in elimination; especially in the kidneys and the bladder.  These, we find, would be relieved by the application of the packs of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] (hot), and with a few drops - three to five drops - of [[Turpentine|turpentine]] in the water that the cloths are wrung out from.  Saturated solution, see? This would be applied especially of evenings, or oftener if necessary.

2. Also we will find, throughout the lumbar region there will be necessity - with this relaxation - to give that manipulation that will produce stimulation to the lower portion of the dorsal region, as well as stimulating the muscular forces through those of the locomotary centers, that the whole portion of the mesenteric system may be relieved of the stress as is produced on same.

3. Will be well to keep the body COMPARATIVELY quiet, though there should be sufficient of the exercise that there is the proper reaction through the activity of locomotion, see? that is, that the physical activities should be rather those taken medicinally, that is under strain, than those taken - well, just moving about, see?

4. In the diet, we would keep nearer that as is outlined for same.  These will be very good, and we will find the body will improve under these conditions.

5. When the pains are relieved through the lumbar, then we would apply occasionally those of the VIOLET ray.  The Infra RED should be given at least every second day, see?  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is there a gastritis or colitis condition?
(A)  Colitis.  This the form of the conditions moving out from the stomach, and throughout the upper intestines, see?

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. Watch the body carefully, that the eliminations through the colon are proper, and if necessary use oil enema.  These will relieve - for we do not wish to produce the strengthening of those tissues that in scar tissue form strangulations in portion of the system, see?  The upper part of the colon, where the ascending - see?  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
