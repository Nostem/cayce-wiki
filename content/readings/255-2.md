# TEXT OF READING 255-2 F 48

**Reading [[255-2]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/23/1930  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1256

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Mrs. [255], and others.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [255].  In many ways we find the body continuing to be bettered.  The general distribution of those portions in the drosses as should be eliminated, is not wholly BALANCED as yet; consequently, there comes at times those distresses from portions of the system; one time here, another there, from the system attempting to balance itself; for, as is seen and well demonstrated in the general physical condition here, there is as much necessity in there being the proper coordination in elimination, as in elimination being proper in the system - for, were the alimentary canal attempting to eliminate that as must necessarily be thrown off the respiratory system, or from the kidneys, we MUST have drosses that occupy the wrong positions in the system, see?  These are conditions as most disturb the body at present.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What treatment would you advise for gums?
(A)  Those as will cleanse first those of the ACCUMULATIONS near the gum proper, and then the application of those properties as may be found in Ipsab, rubbed THOROUGHLY and LOCALLY applied with the tweezer - these will be effective with same.

3. (Q)  Would you advise any change in treatment from former outline?
(A)  We would NOT advise change; for, as is given, these only need the better coordination in eliminations, and the treatments as have been outlined is BRINGING this about; though distresses occur at times, these are NATURAL retractions as come from changes being effected in the general physical conditions; for the age, the repressions, the distresses of long standing, the conditions as from impulses from nerve centers that carry for both the internal and the capillary circulation, must be taken into consideration.  Time, as called - time is factor, then, in same.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self
