# TEXT OF READING 1521-4 F 2 1/2

**Reading [[1521-4]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/13/1940  Sex: F  Age: 3  ReadingID: 7419

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of June, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1102], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Helen Godfrey and Myrtle Demaio.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1521]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, rather the acute conditions have arisen, produced from cold and congestion.

4. From the poisons in the system we find that a congestion has been produced through the liver area, allowing a form of toxemia that is close akin to malaria; though the temperature is an effect arising from intestinal worms.

5. This causes the temperature to vary; the loss of appetite; the tendency to be rather fretful and peevish, to pick or pull at the nose, and to proclaim that various parts of the body at different times cause disturbance.

6. In the present, we would first begin with giving almost a whole bottle of Fletcher's Castoria, but in very small doses; not quite half a teaspoonful about every thirty minutes, until the bowels move thoroughly from same.

7. Then leave off, and the next day give half a Bromo-Laxative Quinine tablet; and then in the evening give the other half.

8. After two days, then give another round of the Castoria.

9. Then, dissolve three grains of Calcidin in about half a cup of water and give little sips of this about ten drops every three hours, for two days; of course, not when the body is asleep, - that is, don't arouse the body for such.

10. This as we find, then, will cleanse the system.

11. And if necessary after this give the third round of the Castoria, see?

12. We should rid the body in this manner of not only the causes of this congestion but also relieve from the circulation - by the addition of these properties in the system - the poisons in the blood supply, purifying the system.

13. Thus we will find a return of the appetite and of the general well-being of the body.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Is there a condition of the heart which should be corrected?
(A)  This is rather of the liver, NOT of the heart; though the congestion in the liver makes the quick pulsation and the irregularity there.

16. (Q)  Should the doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] be continued?
(A)  We would leave these off in the present; for the Calcidin carries sufficient of the [[Iodine|iodine]] reacting principle - if those properties are given in the manner indicated; and here we have also the calcium and the lime effect, as well as aid through the stimulation - when the Bromo-Quinine has acted upon the system - to relieve the congestion.  This is the combination, you see, of salts of quinine and dover's powders with same.

17. (Q)  Should Neo-Cultol be given as suggested by Dr. Moser?
(A)  If this is desired, alright; but these things as we have indicated would be preferable, for they are better than a coal tar preparation.

18. (Q)  What precautions and safeguards should be taken with regard to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s health during the coming year?
(A)  As indicated, - purify the system so that the digestive activities may be very much improved, and that not only the blood supply may be purified but the organs through which the circulatory forces are active, and the digestive system cleansed.

19. (Q)  What about the diet during this period of treatment?
(A)  This is not so important.  Fruit juices, vegetable juices, would be the principal diet, with as much milk as can be conveniently given without a great deal of trouble. The appetite or desire for food will not change so much until there has been a purifying of the system from these disturbing conditions in the body.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother, Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
