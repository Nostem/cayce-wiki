# TEXT OF READING 1703-3 F 52 (High School Teacher,

**Reading [[1703-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/8/1939  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 7850

Theosophist) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1703], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born February 2, 1887, in Waterloo, Iowa. 
Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from present - " - '33 - some very hard years of understanding - '28 - yes - another change - '21 - years of great expectation - 1900 - what a largeness of the whole experience! - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, Miss [1703].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these are chosen with the desire to make this experience a helpful one for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; that in the application of those tenets which have been and are a part of the entity, there may come the greater awakening to the relationships between the entity and the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].  And, applying them in the present experience, there may be an enlargement of the [[Visions|vision]] of the entity to so live that the [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Purpose|purpose]] for which the entity enters this experience may be magnified in its dealings with its fellowman.

5. The virtues, the faults are given, that there may be a better comprehending of the necessary forces and influences to bring an at-onement of itself with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

6. These we find latent and manifested in the experience, - but irrespective of what the entity has in the present done about urges:

7. One versatile in many phases of material activity, owing to the application of self through material [[Incarnation|sojourns]].

8. One inclined to be - or oft leaves the impression of being very hard or cold and dispassionate; yet innately the entity seeks for expression to bring love into the experience. Yet the entity so expresses self by and through its self-assertiveness as to oft leave those COLD who would seek or who feel drawn to the entity, and wondering as to what IS the impelling forces innate and manifested in the activities of this entity.

9. One especially gifted in the ability to write; and as to literary abilities.

10. And the entity may in the present write what might be called a "best seller."  Necessarily this should be both of fact AND fiction; fact told in a fictional manner; truth clothed in the message that may arouse that which IS innately sought within the inner self, - love for the [[Universal Consciousness|UNIVERSAL consciousness]] of individuals or groups.

11. As to the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, - these in the present have become of little import in the experience, or have become so contradictory - by this intent, or this application of self-assertiveness - as to become of little meaning.

12. But Venus, - beauty, artistic temperament, that especially as to the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] of love and its motivative forces becomes a part of the entity.  It may or may not be applied in this present experience.

13. For, as each entity, each [[Soul|soul]] will find in its journey through materiality, it is [[Free Will|the will]] in self to do this or that, or to attempt to force that which is the desire or [[Purpose|purpose]] in self; and thus it may build that as may become a barrier to even that innately sought, or it may bring the awareness of the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] of love.

14. Love in its broader sense is the motivative influence, then, as to that which is creative.  For love is giving, and the spirit of same is NEVER demanding.

15. Then, so long as that which is manifested is the pronouncement of self of the own self, love is kept in the background of the experience of the entity.

16. In Jupiter we find that innately there is the need of associations that may bring an awareness to great crowds, or organizations, or groups.  And the abilities as an instructor or director come from those influences.

17. The tendencies and inclinations to store for a day of leanness (in ability) arise also from those conditions or phases of urging in the innate self, - as well as from the Mercurian.

18. The Uranian influences become the extremes, - as has been and is the experience of the entity through this particular or immediate [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

19. These are periods when decisions are often made.  Hence the entity to many becomes a SEVERE extremist, - when, as indicated in the spirit of love, it is universal and gives and takes; is patient, is kind, is forbearing, shows brotherly love.

20. For it is only in such that one becomes, in materiality, aware of the closeness of relationship to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or God.

21. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - these we find quite varied in their experience - as they are judged or would be judged by a historical fact or condition.  But as we find, these are the ones that make for the innate urges in the present, - though not all, of course, are given here.  For each cycle is as a grade in the experience of an entity or [[Soul|soul]], and as application is made does the material experience become an urge in the present influence or force.

22. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], in the early days of the activity of the white men IN this land.

23. The entity was among those who were born in the early settlements around what is now [[Ohio|Toledo]], and journeyed to what is now known as [[Chicago|Chicago]] - or Fort Dearborn, - in the name Amy Merkle.

24. In the experience the entity gained much through its study and application of those tenets of the natives of that land.

25. Hence the entity was not understood by its own peoples, - neither WHOLLY understood by those whose customs and habits the entity adopted.  But nature and all of its phases of experience became the entity's teacher, as it were.  And the abilities to apply natural and nature's sources for the healings to the body, the diversities of employment of the mental and physical beings, were impressed upon many, - even during the turmoils and strifes through the experience.

26. In the present we find that of positiveness as to nature's ways, and the outdoors, - these become as it were a part of the entity.

27. The ability to describe such in the writing may be a channel through which the entity may find an outlet that may give expression of the longing, - for love, for companionship in the experience; and thus bring to the minds or consciousnesses of many HELPFUL influences in their experience; and also for the entity bring material security in the present.

28. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], during those periods when there was the interpreting and the attempt to write or correlate those teachings of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], as well as of those who were in such close companionship to Him.

29. The entity was close to the younger of the disciples, [[John (Apostle)|John]]; and it was to the entity that one of his last letters was addressed, - to the lady.  For to that entity, [[John (Apostle)|John]], who should come again soon into material experience, seeks a means, a plane of expression.

30. Through those periods the entity gained in attempting to give to the various groups in the many divisions of the church, as it might be called; though not considered such in that period.

31. The name then was Cleopus.

32. From the activities in that sojourn much might be gained, if there would be the turning to the inner self for recognition of that which is the impelling force that motivated the activities of [[John (Apostle)|John]] through that experience - of which the entity caught a glimpse oft, - and yet was hardened in the experience through the disappointments not only of those in authority in the various groups or congregations but through the intent in self to impress others with the feeling of superiority owing to its close affiliation with one known and spoken of as the Beloved.

33. Understand, this is not the John, author of that called the Gospel, but rather the Epistles, the letters, - the elderly man then, - the last of those in his period PERSONALLY in association with [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] of [[Nazareth|Nazareth]].

34. Study those letters, then, for in them ye may find much that may give thee an insight into that ye have sought so often through this experience.  For know, as he gave to thee in those periods of counsel, in those periods of direction, there is NO NAME given under heaven whereby men may be saved but He, the begotten of the Father, - even [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], the Christ!

35. Before that we find the entity was in the Promised Land when there was the return from the land of the Persians, - and with Zerubbabel the entity came into [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]], - saw the desolation, and the raising up of [[Purpose|purpose]] in the hearts and minds of individuals through the experience; and saw and hear the interpreting of much of [[Law of Moses|the law]] as was discovered among the ruins of the temple.

36. Hence the entity through the experience became one well-grounded, materially, in ritual and rote for a purpose of purifying body and mind, as same was designated or dedicated for any individual activity for many.

37. The entity gained, the entity lost through that experience, - as Adonajahem.  For as the entity gave and let others give also, it gained.  As it attempted to impel or to prevent those not so endowed with the broader [[Visions|vision]] from their class or environ, or by the heritage of the environs in a strange land, the entity lost - as it hindered others.

38. Yet in the present, if there will be the application in respect to the warnings that may be given to others for such activity, much may be met in the attempts to give instructions to others THROUGH the writing.

39. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when there were the turmoils materially between the King and the Priest, and when the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] came into [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land during the return of the Priest and the reestablishing and rehabilitation of the land.

40. The entity was among those first born of the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, - hence grew under those instructions and activities of Ajax, Ajalon, Ra-Ta and Araaraart, for the activities in the temple service - where all forms of the animal were rejected in relationships to sacrifice or to service in any manner.

41. Thus we find the innate urges in the entity at present, - the desires to be free from those things pertaining to that as brought so much destructive forces by the mingling of purposes of man - as in spirit - with the material and animal forces in the experience.

42. The name then was Asmyan.

43. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, then, - that to which it may attain, and how:

44. Study to show thyself approved unto God, a workman not ashamed, but rightly divining the words of truth, and keeping self - in thine own consciousness - unspotted from the world.

45. Find not fault with thy neighbor, nor thy friend, but rather by example than by precept LIVE that thou art in such a manner that when ye HAVE found the truth ye may STRENGTHEN thy brethren.

46. Ready for questions.

47. (Q)  What was the exact time of my birth at Waterloo, Iowa?
(A)  The record as has been given thee is well - in the afternoon.

48. (Q)  What is the reason for having so few near relatives?
(A)  [[Free Will|Choice]] of thy forefathers.

49. (Q)  What wrong living in a [[Reincarnation|past life]] brought ill health now? How can I live, now, to make amends, and live long and well?
(A)  This is more thine OWN imagination than because of past experiences.  But as ye apply thyself in the present to the tasks, the duties, the opportunities, these may be better unfolded to thee within.

For it is not who shall descend from heaven, or come from over the sea to bring a message, but lo, it is within thine own consciousness, and thine own mental nature.  For THERE the promise is that He will meet thee, to bless thee!

And only those who have expected and do expect, and wonder at His mercies, attain the full understanding.

50. (Q)  Is there a psychic, mystic, or occult reason for bad eyes?
(A)  Rather a psychic, - from that of race consciousness.

51. (Q)  Did some [[Karma|karmic]] tie lead me to go to Egypt and into the activity following?  If so, what?  Will that activity yet bear results?  If so, what?
(A)  As has been indicated.

As to results, - depends upon the application of SELF.  It is within thy abilities to use.  Apply same.

52. (Q)  Did I contact the people or teachings in the book sent to Mr. Cayce, sealed, at the time they were taught?
(A)  As indicated.

53. (Q)  Have I ever had any direct, first-hand experiences as to the facts of the life and death of Jesus, the Christ?
(A)  As indicated.

54. (Q)  Which person, or group, today, has the nearest to the correct teachings of Jesus?
(A)  This would depend upon who is the judge.  The teaching, the [[Christ Consciousness|consciousness of the Christ]]-life is a PERSONAL thing. And hence they that are aware of His abiding presence are the nearer.

Who?  They that walk with Him and do not, CANNOT, WILL not, force themselves on others - even as He!

55. (Q)  What spiritual teacher should I follow?
(A)  John.

56. (Q)  Or should I continue to glean here, there, and everywhere?
(A)  JOHN!

57. (Q)  Is there a physical reason, such as congested [[Kundalini|Kundalini]], curved spine, or loosely connected [[Spiritual Body|etheric body]], or any [[Karma|karmic]] reason, for my not hearing The Voice of the Silence or getting SELF-Realization?
(A)  Only within self.  For He has promised.  He will speak if ye tune in.

58. (Q)  How can I overcome it?
(A)  In thine own consciousness.  Walk with, talk with, Him.

59. (Q)  What was the significance of writing to music?  Is it of importance personally, publicly, or both?
(A)  As has been indicated, it may be the channel or the means through which that as may be the greater work of the entity here, now, may be done.

60. (Q)  Why do I feel so frustrated with life these past years?
(A)  Lack of the peace and calmness within.

61. We are through for the present.  [See 1703-3 Par. B2, for questions submitted but not asked.]

Copy to Self
