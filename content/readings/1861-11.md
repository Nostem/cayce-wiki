# TEXT OF READING 1861-11 M 35

**Reading [[1861-11]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/30/1942  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 8262

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1861], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1861]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are still improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  While there has not as yet been much change in the [[Visions|vision]], since the second improvement indicated by the test of the [[Visions|vision]], we find that this is - as to abilities - being improved.

4. To be sure, there have been some conflicting emotions in the body as respecting sources or agencies of improvements.

5. This would be well for the body then, to analyze, in its own self:

6. There is the body, the mind, the spirit, [[Soul|the soul]].  As to whether that spirit is in unison with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]] (no matter what name it may be called) depends upon the activity of same through the mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]].

7. As we have indicated for the body, all [[Healing|healing]] - of every nature - MUST come from the spiritual.  It is the attuning of same to the divine within self that brings [[Healing|healing]] forces. Thus, as was given, it is well - with the mechanical applications - that the body raise that awareness, that spiritually energizing force, consciously within self, in [[Meditation|deep meditation]]; provided there IS the consciousness of what it, the influence, is to do, and this not IN its activity of a selfish nature.  It may be personal, yes, - but individual more than personal.

8. Thus this will aid in bringing greater relief in the [[Visions|vision]], as well as the interpreting of love, peace and harmony in the body and mind and [[Soul|soul]].

9. We would continue occasionally, at least once a month, the GENERAL [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and the specific treatments to head nerves and the eye, - especially from the upper dorsal to the optic centers.  It would be better that there be one treatment CORRECTLY given than half a dozen partially or wrongly given! Thus, we would insist that there be kept the present applications with Dr. Richardson.

10. Keep the carrot juices as a portion of the diet often.  These in the physical sense will aid.

11. As to the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] application, - there has been indicated a specific time.  This has been overstepped at times, with the idea that if a little would do good more would do more good; while at times more does harm rather than good!  It is as an overtaxation even to a strong muscular force may weaken, may even deter the best activity.  Do not overstrain, but keep the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] - and not more than the minute and a half.  Two to three minutes is worse than none being given!

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  How long should the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] be given?
(A)  This is different, of course.  Give this until the body is relaxed.  Whether it is ten minutes or fifteen minutes or what not, give it until there is felt the perfect relaxation. This is to relax the body.

14. (Q)  Give color this body might [[Meditation|meditate]] upon beneficially for self [[Healing|healing]].
(A)  The white light of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], if the body, in itself, would find help.  It isn't the color, it isn't the [[Vibration|vibration]], - it is rather the awareness of entering into the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]], the power of health, the power of love.

Do that.

15. (Q)  What are the reactions of the Kundaline forces, - physically, mentally, spiritually?
(A)  We might write five or six books upon this!  Just which one is desired to be known?  There are twelve centers acted upon, each in a different manner, and from the varying sources from which these [[Vibration|vibrations]] are raised in and through these centers - and for what [[Purpose|purposes]].  How many characteristics and desires does the body have?  Figure those and multiply it by about fifteen, and you'll have just how much variation there may be in such activities in the body! How many dispositions have you seen in the body?  These are all activities of the kundaline forces acting upon some reactory force in the centers of the body.  These are well to be controlled, or maintained, but purposely - not for selfish motives; to be sure, individual, but creative.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
