# TEXT OF READING 3242-2 F 53

**Reading [[3242-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/23/1944  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 10647

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3242], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.         [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3242].

3. As we find, many changes have come about.  The congestion through the organs of the pelvis is proving very aggravating, and a portion of the discharge there comes from inflammation, aggravation - or causes such.

4. Twice a week, regularly, we would use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douches; body-temperature water, half water and half commercial strength [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

5. Once a week we would use an [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche, with a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in a quart of body-temperature water.  Use only a Fountain Syringe.

6. Occasionally, once a week or the like, have a massage osteopathically administered.

7. Do these and we will make better conditions.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What has caused the passing of small amount of blood from vagina?
(A)  This, as indicated, has been because of congestion - from the use of astringents that have not been well for the body.

10. (Q)  What is condition of urethra?
(A)  As just indicated, there is inflammation.

11. (Q)  What has caused the nasal catarrhal condition, with severe sinus headaches occasionally?
(A)  This is from the poor circulation.  Thus massage, as indicated, that should be for the whole body and especially in the areas of the upper dorsals and cervicals, and in that area from the 9th dorsal to end of the spine.  Take these treatments once a week for five to six weeks, then leave off a week or so, and then commence all over again.

Use an inhalant to purify the sinus and the antrum and clarify these conditions, so that the activity of the massage may be effective in removing strain on the general nervous system.

In a 6 ounce container or bottle, either having a double vent or a screw cap so that the fumes may be inhaled from the bottle, add - in the order named:

Grain Alcohol.....................4 ounces,
Oil of Eucalyptus................20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp.............5 minims,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.....20 minims.

Shake the solution, remove the cork or the double vent stoppers, and inhale the fumes deep into the nostril, especially early of mornings and in the evenings.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
