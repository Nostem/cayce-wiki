# TEXT OF READING 2467-2 M 37 (Leather Goods, Protestant)

**Reading [[2467-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/30/1929  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 9339

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2467].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Pa.

1. LBC:  Body and enquiring mind, and those associations and relations as respecting the business.  You will answer the questions which we will ask you regarding these associations, also regarding the life reading given for this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, [2467], and those associations and business relations as respecting this body.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  May I have something more tangible to recall the occurrence between September 20th, 1925 and January 22, 1926, referred to in my life reading?
(A)  Use his mind - if he has any!

4. (Q)  What suggestions for furthering sales?
(A)  WE haven't anything to sell!  We have the body and those relationships would be different.  As for selling - we might sell anything!  HE can sell most anything!  What does he wish to sell?

5. (Q)  Where may costs or expenses be reduced?
(A)  Be reduced on what?  These are very poorly put together. The intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] may be correct, but the tangibility of that being sought - no WONDER he can't remember the 25th of November!  Has he tried very hard?  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
