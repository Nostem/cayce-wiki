# TEXT OF READING 1534-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[1534-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/2/1938  Sex: M  ReadingID: 7466

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the brother - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Post-Grad. Hosp., 
Time of Reading                             20th St. & 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 2nd Ave., 
[[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  As we find, there has not been the compliance with those suggestions as indicated.

2. These as we find would have, as indicated, offered the greater opportunity for the allaying and the removing of the causes of those conditions that have become and are more active as growths upon the organs and the activity of same through the system.

3. While the high voltage or the X-Ray may allay, we find that it will inflame the conditions.

4. As we find, the injections as indicated [Atropine] - and the LOW form of the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]], or the reverse coil of same, would offer the greater possibilities for help.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  How are the kidneys?
(A)  These are more involved owing to the greater disturbance in the liver, in the attempt to keep an equal balance.  These become now more and more involved.

Do these, then, as suggested.

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to [...] [Brother] - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

4/2/38 Wire:  Suggestions first indicated have not been followed.  These, the injections and the low reverse coil electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] would offer greater possibilities for help.
