# TEXT OF READING 3140-1 F 1

**Reading [[3140-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/25/1943  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10508

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother, [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Betty Corson.)]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - as we find, the body here - there are the needs for all those applications that may be made; yet, as we find, if it will just be accepted, this condition is a part of the development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

3. For this is a prenatal condition, and there should not be self-condemnation on the part of the parents.  Yet here is where those who study the betterment of the race should make the greater application for helpful environs for the birth of [[Soul|souls]] that would enter and the education and the conduct of the prospective parents; that it may be that preparation and attraction for [[Soul|souls]] in keeping with the lives and the experiences, the [[Purpose|purposes]] and aims, of the individual so making the preparation.

4. Here we find the deeper circulation, or the walls of the arterial circulation, so disturbed as to cause leakage in the very pressure and the activity of the heart itself, in its adjustment to material manifestation.  Thus the hemorrhage that caused the reflexes of the body to react in spasmodic contractions, that have caused the lack of the usage of the body in the reactions or reflexes for this body.

5. While there may be developments, and while there may be helpful influences, so that there may be the ability to use self, and parts of the locomotories may be helped, we find that this will require long, tedious, applications.  For, the ability of the reflexes to produce coordination between the physical being and the mental being will be the greater problems to solve, as the body physically develops - if the physical does develop; as well as the ability of the [[Mental Body|mental body]] to remain in control constantly of the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

6. Thus, from some, there might be the impressions that such should not be allowed to develop and become ever as a charge to others.  Yet, these, too, are necessary in the experience of man as he approaches the applications of the tenets of [[Creative Forces|creative energies]], or truth in his experience.  For, to the measure that an individual entity applies itself for helpful physical, mental or spiritual unfoldment, so does that body do to its Maker.

7. For this individual body, make these applications:

8. Along the base of the brain, at the edge of the medulla oblongata, extending even to the second and third cervical, apply daily for an hour the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] packs.  Use three thicknesses of cotton cloth well saturated in the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Let these remain on for an hour.

9. Then, when removed [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with a combination of oils that are structural building to tissue, through the absorption to arise where there has been opening of the cellular forces in the applications made.  Combine in this manner.

10. To 4 ounces of [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil (or Nujol or Usoline), add in the order named:

Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root.......1/2 ounce,
Oil of Cedar Root...........1/2 ounce,
Peanut Oil..................1/2 ounce,
Lanolin (liquefied).........1/4 ounce.

Shake this well together, for they will tend to separate, owing to the various properties; but shake together when ready to use and pour a small quantity into an open saucer. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this particularly in the area from the base of the brain, or first cervical to the third, fourth and fifth cervical.

11. After this has been kept up for about three months, then begin using the [[Wet Cell|Wet-Cell]] Appliance that carries to the body the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium, in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of Distilled Water.

12. Make the Appliance regular strength.  Use this daily for about six months, changing the charging solution every thirty days and renewing the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] every fifteen days.

13. Make the attachments for thirty minutes each day as follows:

14. The larger plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially to the body, should be attached at the umbilical center or the lacteal duct plexus; that is, on this body, two fingers from the navel center to the right and one finger up from that point.  The small copper plate would be attached at the ninth dorsal center, or from the area from which the plexus of the cerebrospinal and sympathetic aid in coordinating the controlling forces not only through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] from above the kidneys, and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]], but the connections between the liver, the kidneys, the lungs, and the heart.  On these connect in this solar plexus there.

15. Each day when the appliance is removed [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body all over with the oils that have been used for the months before.

16. Keep a normal diet.

17. These offer the better opportunity for the body in its unfoldment, in its development, for maintaining and gaining control of the connections between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous system; and for the reducing of those pressures.  For it will take away those pressures made upon the brain by the hemorrhages caused in the arterial circulation, that came along the nerve centers to the brain reflexes.

18. Do that.

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  Well - the age - this will have to be close to the normal diet for the age or the developing here of this body.

21. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be mailed to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., so he can make up the Appliance according to specifications in your individual case.  Also see article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of this appliance.)
