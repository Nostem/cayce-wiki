# TEXT OF READING 4090-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4090-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1921 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/21/1921  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11786

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Hotel McAlpin, in [[New York City|New York City]], this 21st day of March, 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; G. K. Nicodemus, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  This body has been in here, but is not here at the present time.  We will have him here in a minute.

2. Now the conditions that are disturbing to this body are abnormal, [4090] we are speaking of, see?  and have to do with improper elimination is abnormal, you see, so that the used forces or poisons in the system find their way through absorption to the lymphatic circulation and affect the muscular portions of the body so that they become taut in their movements, and especially do the limbs feel the effect of this tautness; and the nerve centers cry out with the pain or exertion to this portion of the body, see?  This has been produced by the poison that has been distributed in the system through the generative organism of this body; it being absorbed in the system, striking through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and tissues to the lymphatics become taken up by these portions more than is shown in the blood, but this is the condition as we will find in the elimination through the kidneys, that they themselves show by the discharge, or urine, that an improper amount of poisons are being eliminated as they are taken through the respiratory organs, and are affecting the lymph and their centers over the system.

3. The blood supply is fairly normal in view of these conditions.  The nerve forces is depleted by these conditions as we see in the muscles and lymph.

4. To relieve these conditions in this body we would take in to the system this:  Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]], one half teaspoonful in a glass of water in which, after it is stirred up, there is put 5 drops syrup of sarsaparilla, this to be taken every day; and every 5th day there should be added to this same glass 2 drops of 10% solution [[Iodine|iodide]] of potassium.  Now these properties act directly on the blood, especially that that has to do with the eliminations process between the digestive tract and the capillary circulation, or that coming out from the trunk portion of the body.  [[Iodine|Iodide]] acts on the generative organs, producing suppression to these organs, so that the elimination is through the proper channels.  The body is better now than it has been.  Take regular exercise with this and we will eliminate these conditions from the body.

5. (Q)  How long will this take?
(A)  In fifteen days.  In ten days this body would show a change.

6. (Q)  Should this body remain in Fort Worth, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  This is as good a climate as anywhere for this body. Produce proper elimination and there will be no more of this condition.

7. (Q)  Has this body any ability in prompting or placing stock, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Yes, he has ability.

8. (Q)  What state would it be best for this body to work in, he has friends in many states, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  That decision he must make within himself.  The proposition of placing stock or selling, in conjunction with using mind over mind, he must first use his own mind to create what he expects to do, then this body and others work up to those expectations.

9. (Q)  What is the extent of his ability in carrying out his plans and ideas in selling, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  He depends a great deal on others, he would be more capable if he would train himself more.

10. (Q)  How shall this body overcome other difficulties he has has, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Difficulties are given to be overcome.  What is not of his own making, forget.  Those of his own making, study them and try correct them.

11. (Q)  What are the weak points of this body, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Indecision and selfishness, these are his weak points.

12. (Q)  Will financial conditions soon begin to improve?
(A)  With labor, yes; without labor, no.

13. (Q)  Does this body affect the forces favorably?
(A)  Unless it changes it would not, but it is capable of changing.  The change should first be worked on the [[Physical Body|physical body]], they can be changed and they should be; there is destruction in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] for itself unless the forces are changed for the better.

14. We are through.
