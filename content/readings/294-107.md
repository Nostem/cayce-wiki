# TEXT OF READING 294-107 M 51

**Reading [[294-107]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/7/1928  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 2066

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of April, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the night of the 2nd or early morning of 4/3/28.  You will recall this [[Dreams|dream]] or [[Visions|vision]] for this body and give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, EC - this we have had before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we have given, are interpretations or [[Visions|visions]], or the activity of the forces seen from the mental endeavors of a body.

4. This, as seen, is a [[Visions|vision]].  And while much is experienced, much is to be given again in the experience to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], that there may come the opportunity, as it were, to act upon that application of will in the direction that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], as a whole, may be builded toward the proper direction.

5. The vision is seen.  There were the peoples who were in the way of some giving consent, some objecting, to the banishment of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] from the office of the officiating priest, or the officiating minister and teacher of the tenets of the relationship of man and the creative energy as related to the daily activities of each individual, and these were being set in rules and in orders, see?  [[[Egypt|Egypt]]:Ra-Ta Period] [See Life rdgs. 294-8 & 294-19.]

6. The scene is where there are divisions of the people, as some cry for the deliverance, some for the banishment, and all are seen in the streets and in the way, as the ruler and his advisors have the people (as then known as scourgers) to drive before them those with the priest who were being banished from the working of the temple, or house which has been builded and prepared, see?

7. The lesson that is seen in this portion of the vision is that, while there come conditions in the life  - through the activities of some of those present of those present in that period - bringing disappointments, bringing fear, bringing railings, bringing conditions that are as of the same character (though presented in a different manner, they mean the same thing!) - there should never be malice, nor any element that would hinder the entity from development in the proper channel.

8. We are through.
