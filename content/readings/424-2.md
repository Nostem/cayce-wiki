# TEXT OF READING 424-2 F ADULT (60's?)

**Reading [[424-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 3193

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Ernest W. Zentgraf, 400 St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]''s Ave., Stapleton, Staten Island, N. Y., this 29th day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [424], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [424].  This we have had before.  In some respects conditions are improved; in others not so good.  There are evidences that a continuation of the use of those influences that make for the deadening of nerve reactions has grown rather than lessened.

2. As we will find, if there will be used the Radio-Active forces for the producing of rest for the body, these will be more effective.  [As recommended in 424-1, Par. 26-A for insomnia.]

3. Then, we would change somewhat those things that may be used instead, but if these are used with the hypnotics it will produce nausea.  Prepare this:

4. To 4 ounces of compound simple syrup, add:

Oil of Eucalyptol..................14 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...............5 minims,
Tincture of Benzoin................20 minims,
Essence of Wild Ginger.............20 minims,
Sufficient alcohol as a preservative...............1 1/2 ounces.

5. This may be taken once or twice each day.  Preferably take this, half a teaspoonful, in place of those tablets.

6. As for the general system, where there has been the creating for the flushing of the system, use the high enemas the more often for cleansing the colon of those poisons and accumulations that have made for the disorders and distresses in the extremities and through portions of the body, making pressures on the head.

7. Discarding now the tonic, [given in 424-1] we would use preferably this:

8. To 4 ounces of Cherry Pectoral, or Wild Cherry - Syrup of Wild Cherry, as a carrier, add:

Compound Syrup of Sarsaparilla....1/4 ounce,
Tincture of Stillingia............1/4 ounce,
Essence of Indian Turnip............3 minims,
Syrup of Rhubarb..................1/2 ounce,
Alcohol.........................1 1/2 ounces.

9. The dose will be teaspoonful three or four times each day.

10. Be very mindful of the diet, that there are not meats nor white breads taken.  Keep a well balanced diet, and especially green vegetables - some should be included in every meal, even for breakfast when you eat fresh or raw fruits - raw vegetables, see?

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should the massage be continued?
(A)  It should.  We didn't leave that out, did we?

13. (Q)  Do we understand correctly that all sedatives being used should be discontinued?
(A)  Be discontinued, and use the compound as given in its place; for this is not habit-forming, and is constructive to the system, where poisons have made for disorders in the system.

14. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body?
(A)  Follow these in the order, in the line, in the way given.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
