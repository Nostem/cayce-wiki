# TEXT OF READING 760-4 F 50 (Housewife, Practical Nurse,

## Text

Date: 7/15/1925  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 4684

Art Teacher, Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [760].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born March 2, 1875, in ..., Ky. 
Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                      ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the Universe, and the Universal Forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life.  Also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and to that which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those relations with the Universe and Universal Forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present earth's plane, with those influences that bring about these urges, these conditions in the entity, with the influence of the [[Astrology|astrological]] forces, or planes through which the entity passed in reaching this.

3. In the entering then, we find many conditions as are apparent and exhibited in the present earth plane from those conditions as come from existent conditions in others.  The entering then, as we find, as these:

4. Under the influence of Pisces, with Jupiter, Neptune, Mars and Venus.  Then we have those of these urges, as latent and as exhibited, in the present earth plane:

5. One we find of the highest ennobling influences in the life of others, rather than of self.  One that gives a great deal more influence of good in the lives of others than is felt within self's influence.  One who is termed by many as being the [[Incarnation|incarnation]] of all ennobling in the lives of those whom the entity contacts, yet bringing to self and to self's own relation many conditions that are only an accentuation of the good and ennobling influences brought to others.

6. One whom we find many of the mystic influence, unaccountable even to self, of the influence had over others.

7. One we find who is and shows capacity in any line of endeavor, especially in that of any pertaining to the artistic ability, and the entity would and could have made a success (as world knows success) in any branch, either as teacher or as commercial artiste.

8. One we find who gives much of self in every way toward assisting others.  This coming then, as we find, from the ennobling influence as is found in Jupiterian forces.

9. One that we find, seeming, though giving much to others, rather draws for self those conditions that to individual is unsatisfactory.

10. One that will lend much to the development of the foundation of truth as is found in mystic forces, especially pertaining to occult, in the assistance lent in same to the physical assistance to individuals.  One who would have made a wonderful nurse.  One whose abilities to sympathize with anyone, under any condition, reaches far into that field of the attraction for others.

11. As to [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], these we find have much to do with the various and varied conditions as are manifest and exhibited in the present life.

12. In the one, then, before this, we find in that of the Queen who was beheaded, in that of Marie Theresa, or Marie Antoinette, as known in the historic forces, and in that we find both development and the retarding, for the beginning of the force as exercised in that plane brought much good and much bad to many people, and all must be met in the earth's plane, for save through that do we reach that force as is found in why [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] entered the earth's plane.

13. In the one before this, we find in the [[Greece|Grecian]] forces, during the days when the rule of the peoples were being turned toward the expansion of the then known rule in the world, or that portion of same.  Then in the name of Airdarel, and the companion, in the household, of then Xenophon [[2903]? [2903] her brother at present. In that we find the entity developed, and the influence as we see in the present of the ability to advise and to gain the hearing, as it were, when others fail.  In the abilities of the entity at that time, much may be discovered yet as pertaining to these, for the writings of same are preserved in some of the destroyed cities in that place.

14. In the one before this, we find in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] forces, when the greater laws were being given, and during the reign of the second of that rule, wherein the greater civilization of earth's people were being exhibited and the first temples being built.  [[[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] Period]  The entity then in that of Usisso, and in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] worship, for the entity then the daughter of the first King of that rule, a brother being the ruler [341] who gave the greater rule to the peoples.  The entity then giving much to the people, and the desire to preserve relics, old and new, of the desire to preserve the best for the worship of every nature, whether in family, friendship, or in the higher oracles, giving of the best; yet ever afar in some manners from a show of ecclesiastical worship.  The urges also show in the artistic abilities, and the ability to design of every character, anew, as it were, from others, for much of [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] decorations in that day were of the mind of this entity.  The desire also to seek out such, see?

15. In the one before this, we find in the [[Peru|Peruvian]] country, when the Ohlms [?] [Aymaras - See 4/29/77 GD's note in 4292-1 Reports re prehistoric Aymaran Indians of Peru.] were in the rule, and the entity then in that of Dolrhales, and the entity gave much to the degradations of the people who rebelled against to the rule, and this brought the condemnations to the individual, for this entity then sought to destroy that being built in the minds of the people towards service to the highest then as was given.

16. As to the abilities in the present plane, these, as given, would succeed in a two-fold way and manner.  The greatest will come through the home and the building of the friendships in and through relation with the elements of the occult and mystic forces coming from Neptune, with Mars, though these bring much troubles at times.

17. Then, in using all, give this as account unto self:  In every age, in every clime, is the attempt to manifest the God force as is manifest in each individual, and the giving of self as the channel of experience to that element that will give the better manifestation of same to His people, is the highest service in all.  Then, study to show self approved unto Him, and the coming of the Lord will be sure and certain within the confines of the individual self, for as same is manifested through self, same becomes acceptable unto Him, for as we are seen and known of men, as the way is the manifestation of Him, of Him we are known.

18. We are through for the present.
