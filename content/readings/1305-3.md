# TEXT OF READING 1305-3 M 46

**Reading [[1305-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/28/1936  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 6938

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of December, 1936, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [1337], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:20 to 12:25 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, there has not been the adherence to those suggestions indicated.

3. And all too soon there is seen that the leakages are causing and may produce SUCH a clot as to bring greater distress.

4. Why, oh why will - !!!

5. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [1337] - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

[Wire]:  Mrs. [1337]  Condition is from leakages. Suggestions have not been followed. Collect.  Ass'n for R. & E., Inc.

## Reports

> 
> FH10 20 5 EXTRA=DETROIT MICH DEC 30 1203P
> EDGAR CAYCE=
> 
> LETTER RECEIVED IS THERE STILL HOPE IF ORIGINAL TREATMENTS
> WERE STARTED AT ONCE WIRE COLLECT=
> 
> MRS [1337] 1237P
> 
> R2. EC's wire:  Not unless you feel impelled by spiritual forces
> rather than material.  Collect  Edgar Cayce
> 
> R3. 1/5/37 EC's ltr. to Mrs. [1337]:
> 
> 
> Dear Mrs. [1337]-
> 
> Have been really concerned about your brother.  Know just how
> hard it is to get the cooperation of the Dr's in many places,
> and at times it appears they had rather just let a body die
> rather than even admit there might be help in the suggestion.
> And know just how you and Mrs. [his wife] must have felt,
> when you knew the Dr. would just throw you overboard were you
> to consider even changing Dr's but do hope there has been
> some change for the better, for realize from what we had Mr.
> [1305] was in a very bad way.
> 
> May [[Grace|the grace]] of a merciful Father be with you through the
> hours [[Trial of Jesus|trial]], and may the very best come to your brother.
> 
> Know we are glad to try, but realize we oft have a losing
> game from the angle of gaining cooperation, in some
> directions.
> 
> Hoping for the best.
> 
> Sincerely,  Edgar Cayce
> 
> R4. 1/8/37 [1337]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> Had intended writing to you sooner, but we have been having a
> very distressing time.  There has been the usual difficulty
> of lack of cooperation, but the morning the second reading
> came, Mr. [1305] had a serious set back, and the following
> week another very serious set back.  He had to be removed to
> the Hospital on January 2nd, and these last few days have
> shown some gain.  We are hoping that as soon as it is
> possible to bring him home, and just before doing so, will
> ask for another reading, so as to know what method to follow.
> 
> My sister finally got a doctor to cooperate in connection
> with Dr. Thompson, but before they were able to start
> treatment, the set backs occurred.
> 
> Am enclosing money for last two readings.
> 
> Yours truly,  [1337]
> 
> R5. 1/18/37 [1337]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> Received your letter of the 14th, and although I must admit,
> I have had a very pessimistic feeling, owing to the fact that
> conditions interfered before they used the suggestions, your
> letter gave me renewed confidence that all may not be lost
> yet.  The folks have asked for another reading, and these are
> the facts.  They were making plans to start the treatments,
> and had already dismissed the Dr. on the case (because of
> course, he would not cooperate).  Mr. [1305] suffered a
> severe relapse, which caused him to become unconscious.  He
> was brought to the Hospital in that condition, because it was
> necessary, to give him intravenous injections to supply
> necessary & sufficient body nourishment & fluids.  While he
> [1305] regained consciousness shortly after coming to
> Hospital, he is still (over 2 weeks now) unable to make any
> sounds or swallow anything but liquid foods.  He is now
> receiving one intravenous feeding daily.
> 
> R6. 1/23/37 See 1305-4.
