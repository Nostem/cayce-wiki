# TEXT OF READING 3353-1 F 53

**Reading [[3353-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/5/1943  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 10787

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3353], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the impressions here - they were pretty good once.

3. Yes, we have those conditions that are problems and hindrances in the general physical and mental application of the abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], [3353].

4. As we find, there are definite disturbances that exist in the balance in the pelvic center.  This is indicated in the fourth lumbar and the lumbar axis.  Here there was a strain, yes a bump that jarred the end of the coccyx bone.  This, with this tendency for [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to become weakened, has set up a general pain through the sciatic and bursa of the feet - as in the frontal portion and in the heel, also the arch is tending to make for a very poor control of the ball of the feet in walking, standing.

5. First, as we find, there should be a correcting osteopathically of those subluxations that exist in the coccyx and in the lumbar and sacral axis, and then through those periods of correction massage the feet and limbs - especially from the knees down - with an equal combination of Olive Oil and Tincture of Myrrh.  Heat the oil to add the Myrrh.  Massage this into the areas every evening and every morning, all that the feet and knees will absorb.  That this gives rather a good tan needn't disturb the body for you should keep 'em covered anyway.

6. But in adding to the body the proper chemical forces, do have the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to carry Gold to the body - to do away with the weakening of the muscular and nerve tension in the body.  There would not be required too long a use of this, if done properly, but it will contribute much to prevention of disturbance later and to correcting conditions in the present.

7. The large plate, through which the Solution passes vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus or - on this body - three fingers from the navel center to the right and three fingers up from that point. (Very pretty body!)

8. The small copper plate would be attached to the fourth lumbar.

9. In the massages, be careful with those proper corrections at the end of the spine, in the coccyx area and in the fourth lumbar.  It is very well to coordinate the rest of the body with same, 9th dorsal, 1st and 2nd cervical, 3rd and 4th cervical, 3rd and 4th dorsal, but the corrections more in lumbar, sacral and coccyx.  But the massages would be given daily.

10. Have at least two charges of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] (one grain to each ounce of distilled water) and regular strength charging Solution for the Appliance.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should any local application be used to relieve the painful feet?
(A)  This is a pretty good local application, if you rub them twice a day with the oils you'll have a good [[Book of Job|job]].

13. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] - Please let us know the name and address of the one you choose. Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va. Also article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of this Appliance.)
