# TEXT OF READING 243-12 F 52

**Reading [[243-12]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/10/1932  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 1096

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this l0th day of February, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [243], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [243] - this we have had before. In the disorders that are apparent in the present from the disturbances of the eliminations of the body, and the adjustments of the organs of the system in the ways of that as comes about in the natural forces of the system, we will find that we would apply physically a half solution of a mild opiate, as of laudanum, thinned with water (not alcohol), and spread over those portions of the body - the lower portion of the lumbar, over those portions of the false pelvic area, from that of the cerebro-spinal centers - see?  lumbar, portion of the sacral, and the lower part of the dorsal area. Then apply the saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] packs.  This will relieve the pain and produce activities that will make for an EASING of the disorders in the system.  During such periods there should not be too much activity of body upon the feet.

2. Be mindful of the diet and of the general elimination as respecting the alimentary canal, that there are LESS of the alkalins during such periods.  Not TOO much of the acids, to be sure, but MORE of an acid - for these will coordinate better with the eliminations that are established in such a way and manner.

3. The whole body will respond with those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be set from the plain electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], from the heels to the hips, from the central portion of the body toward the finger tips.  See?  In that manner should it be applied.  These given two or three times a week will make for an equalization of the circulation FROM the extremities TO the head, and - as is already indicated - the flow of blood supply in opposite direction is very good, but it doesn't get back as fast - see?  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Why does the soreness under left shoulder continue?
(A)  The disruption or disorder in the circulatory system, and a tendency of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] (that come from the mammary supply of those forces from the system to the organs) to clog in carrying away refuse forces.  At such periods, or at times, these are worse than others - but with these applications, with these cares taken, we will find an adjustment, and better application for the eliminations and assimilations of body.

5. (Q)  What can be done to overcome nervousness?
(A)  Release the pressure on the nerves' reactions, as we have outlined for these to be given!  Not have run FROM the upper portion to the central portion of the body, but begin at the back portion of feet and limbs and go TOWARDS the central portion - see? to the central part of body.  Then from the central part of body continue UPWARD to the neck, or the finger tips, over the shoulder, to the head, and over the whole body.

6. (Q)  What causes the sort of freezing or tingling sensation, particularly in limbs and through intestines?
(A)  Poor circulation!  This has been described.

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
