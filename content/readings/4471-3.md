# TEXT OF READING 4471-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[4471-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12336

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4471].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find the body in a better condition than when we had this before.  While those conditions to be corrected through that of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces in the system have not been corrected, still we have a better assimilation and elimination in the system than as before.

2. In the [[Vibration|vibrations]] accorded the system, these have been very good, save where, with the change in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the system, when with that of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces to renew the connection in the muscular system, that relaxes and contracts the muscles in the cerebrospinal system, this accorded then to the system produces the tingling, itching sensation over the system.

3. We would, to give the best to this body, carry out, as we have given, those suggestions for the treatment accorded the system, in the way and the manner as given for the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, to relax and contract centers in the cerebro-spinal system that function with the functioning organs of the system proper, as we have in the upper dorsal and in the upper lumbar region, and especially that in the 9th, 10th and 11th dorsal.

4. Do that in the manner as given, if we would bring the better to this body, and this will give the correction when taken as given.  Not before then.

> 
> It looks to me as thought the readings were all wrong - don't
> you think so?
> 
> Yours truly, [4471]
