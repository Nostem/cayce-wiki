# TEXT OF READING 5397-1 F 68

**Reading [[5397-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/21/1944  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 13538

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5397], new Associate member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], [[Grace|Grace]] Premo, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, as we find, conditions are far advanced.  If there is to be help, it must be with patience and with prayerful application of all of those tenets and truths which bring a consciousness of at least trying to use the energies which are given to self for the betterment of others.

2. Here, we find the locomotories, especially, have been and are in such states of tautness as to prevent their use.  Also portions of the spine are involved.

3. These measures will be painful, but if they will be followed through, we will find relief.  The attitude, though, must be kept ever constructive, creative, in its relationships, and not condemnation of things or individuals.

4. First, begin to take small quantities of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] - one drop in half a glass of water - every day for five days.  On the sixth day, take five drops; and that same day, though at least three hours after taking this, have an [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath.  This should be at least twenty pounds of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] to forty gallons of water; and while the body necessarily will have to be placed in same, there should be the massage given the limbs (especially in the locomotories through the hips and small portion of the back; the knees; the feet; the elbows; arms; shoulders) through the whole period.  Have this just about as warm as the body can well stand, without causing too great a distress.  Keep an ice pack on the head during the time the body is in the bath.

5. When taken from the bath, massage thoroughly, all the Peanut Oil into the body it will absorb - the spine and the limbs, especially.

6. Rest for at least five days from these, and then begin again, and for five days take one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water before any meal is taken of a morning; and again on the sixth day, take five drops; and again the bath in the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], at least as heavy as that given at first.  Then have the oil massage.

7. Following these, begin with the low electrical treatments, as with the radionics, as from the radio-short-wave.  Keep these for at least a month, and then the third of the Baths should be prepared, and this (the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]) taken for five days, and on the sixth day, the larger dose, and the massage throughout the period in the Bath.

8. The diets through these periods should be more of the raw vegetables, especially watercress and carrots; lettuce and celery.  The juices of these should be taken quite often throughout the day.  Not too much quantities, but just sufficient to prevent the body from finding it abhorrent or intolerable to take so much juices.  Have easily digested foods.

9. Massage over the abdomen; and keep better eliminations. These will bring helpful forces for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  How may I get my bowels to be active as should be?
(A)  Only through taking that which is easily assimilated, and, as has been indicated, massage occasionally with the acid - as grape acid, see?  that is, Tartaric Acid.  This will cause movement of the bowels.  But not too long - not too much!  It is best to apply the Tartaric Acid in a cloth or gauze over the abdomen, this just patted on.  Cover, and then allow to remain for ten to fifteen minutes.  Then remove, and gently massage the bowel with Peanut Oil.  These will cause movement.  If necessary, repeat and let it remain a little longer, but don't get them so they will be started and then not be able to stop.  Beef juices taken in the diet will give strength; also liver and cereals, especially combinations of barley and wheat (as in Maltex).  Wheat germ oil would be well for the body, or this may be taken on the cereal in the form of the wheat germ.

12. (Q)  What should be done for little place on nose?
(A)  Don't irritate this too much!

13. (Q)  How may I get rid of fear of closed places?
(A)  The attitude must be kept as to that of helpfulness in the spiritual forces of the body - within and without.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the electrotherapist who gives you the short-wave radionic treatment when you are ready for it - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(8/21/44 GD's note:  Atomidine is distributed by The Atomidine Co., Atten:  R. S. Bisey, 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y., and sells for $1 a bottle.)
