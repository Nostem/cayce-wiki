# TEXT OF READING 1045-11 F 38

**Reading [[1045-11]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/23/1936  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 6173

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 23rd day of April, 1936, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Nathan Sachs, Wm. Berg, [[Paul (Apostle)|Saul]] Silverman and wife.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 to 5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1045].

2. In some respects conditions appear to be not so good.  As we find, unless there are greater precautions taken in the diet and in the manner of the exercise, there will again occur a hemorrhage; FROM those conditions that have set about in the lung area or the affected portions.

3. But when this does occur, or if there will be taken more of the Animated Ash WITH the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-violet]] ray, these will heal; for it will loosen sufficient of the oxygen.

4. And continue more often, not too long at a time, the use of the inhalations; that they may HEAL those areas in the lung that make for such distresses from the breaking of tissue, in the throat and in the lung.

5. The throat, of course, is more irritated in the present. Hence we find that the Ash here - an eighth of a grain morning and evening, followed by the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] for a minute - would make for a helpful condition.

6. The massage should be general and, as indicated, keep the liver and the kidneys active; for with the drosses, or the natural excess of the quantity of toxic forces that are to be thrown out, do not let these become more and more involved.

7. Stimulate, then, in the massage, at the 9th dorsal and the 4th lumbar, gently - but in a rotary nature, and in those specific centers.  The lower portion of the 9th dorsal, the whole of the 4th lumbar or the lumbar axis.  This will rest the body, it will also prevent the PRESSURE FROM the tendency for the dilation from the coughing of the stomach to press upon the diaphragm.

8. These will be the helpful conditions in the present.

9. Do that.

10. Twice a day (this may be wired) the Ash, one-eighth of a grain; the light, [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]], one minute, following the giving of the Ash - three to five minutes afterward. Stimulate the 9th dorsal, the 4th lumbar, GENTLY, with the massage.  Use the inhalations of the brandy for the searing or for the cleansing of those conditions in throat and lung.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What should be done for the extreme weakness?
(A)  Massaging with pure grain alcohol will give strength as well as keep down temperature.  This should be massaged over the abdomen, even to the lower portion of the lung area itself, and across the spine - from the lumbar area to about the 4th dorsal area.

13. (Q)  Regarding the diet, is there any food that should be eliminated from the present diet?
(A)  Keep that as has been indicated; but be very mindful we do not include sweets.  THIS has been included, which has been harmful.

14. (Q)  Anything else to do for the coughing?
(A)  Just that as indicated.  This may produce - by this coughing - and by this irritation - a hemorrhage.  But this should not cause too great an anxiety, so long as it can be controlled.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Husband - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [1100]

[4/23/36 GD's note]:  Wired as follows:  EIGHTH GRAIN ASH MORNING, EVENING.  FOLLOW FIVE MINUTES LATER WITH ULTRA-VIOLET ONE MINUTE.  STIMULATE MASSAGE NINTH DORSAL FOURTH LUMBAR GENTLY ROTARY MOTION.  BRANDY INHALATIONS. GRAIN ALCOHOL RUBS OVER ABDOMEN, LOWER LUNG AREA AND SPINE FROM LUMBAR TO FOURTH DORSAL FOR WEAKNESS AND TEMPERATURE. WATCH DIET.  NO SWEETS.  FULL INFORMATION AIR MAIL.
