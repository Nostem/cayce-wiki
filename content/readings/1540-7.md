# TEXT OF READING 1540-7 F 47

**Reading [[1540-7]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/16/1944  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 7483

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made the by the self - Miss [1540], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; Jeanette Fitch, Steno. Miss [1540].]

Present in 
Time of Reading                                   room. 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1540]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here and the greater portion is for bettered conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  There are those tendencies for restlessness and anxiety.  These naturally come from those tensions in the general nervous system.

4. If there would be the occasional relaxing of the body through [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments; a very light sweat, not too much, but more of the [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]], then the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to make for better eliminations and stimulation to the activity of the respiratory, perspiratory system, we would find we would make for better conditions for the body.

5. The use of the Radio-Active Appliance also in the body and for the body is most beneficial, for this keeps a better coordinant activity in the general reflexes of the nerve forces.  And this is what the body has need of more than most anything else.  If these will be kept along in order we should have better conditions for this continuously.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What causes the nervous cracking sensation in head?
(A)  As has been indicated, these arise from the incoordination of a too great tension, and is why there has been indicated the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] rather than taking of properties for nerve relief, as sedative or any form in that nature.

8. (Q)  Did I injure my back from my fall?
(A)  Strained the muscular forces, but not an injury other than this strain.  This is one of the things that, when the body eats, it tends for slow digestion or there is set up reactions which apparently prevents foods from digesting very quickly and thus causes sleeplessness, as well as a poor circulation in the extremities.  But the light sweats with the addition of properties in the circulation which work directly through absorption to the emunctory and the outer edges of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] it should bring quick relief for the body.

9. (Q)  Should I continue to use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] if the Radio-Active is used?
(A)  We would continue to use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]], for this is nerve force building.  The Radio-Active is to keep an equal balance in the upper and lower circulation and to prevent too much increase in the avoirdupois for the body.  Use [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] as has been indicated, see?

10. (Q)  Am I overweight?
(A)  As has been indicated, use the Appliance for making for equal balance in the system and we would find this would be reducing to the body.  Less starches in the diet and we should make nearer to normal reaction.

11. (Q)  How could I bring about a more harmonious unity in my home in regard to my father and his wife, or would it be best at this time for me to leave their home?
(A)  There must be first the quiet or harmony in one's own self if one would find harmony with [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] with others.  As we find, if we would have an alternative, not to remain away from home all the while, but portions of the time, it would be well.

12. (Q)  Am I doing the work that is best fitted for me at this time, or should I seek work elsewhere; if so, where is best?
(A)  This is very good.  We would keep these for the present. For there'll be changes soon enough and this will necessitate changes in the activities which the body was identified with.

13. (Q)  Is the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] treatment, such as that I took at the Beverly Health Studio [Mrs. Sarah Jane Beverly, Ph.T.], helpful to me?
(A)  Yes - yes we find this is helpful.  We would use it!

14. (Q)  Please give me any mental and spiritual advice needed.
(A)  Keep that correct attitude of being helpful for others, as ye have been in these directions in the main.  Keep them so.  Study, as it were, to show self approved unto the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] and not condemning self nor others.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
