# TEXT OF READING 137-31 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-31]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/13/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 421

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made through his brother, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137], with the [[Dreams|dreams]], the [[Visions|visions]] as come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] from time to time.  These, as we see, are those correlations through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of physical actions in material world, weighed with cosmic experiences in the spiritual world, and give those lessons through which the body-mind, the body-physical, the body-spiritual, may develop in the material world - see?  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Wednesday evening, November 11, 1925.  "Someone (probably myself) was looking up to corner of a tent of some kind and the person (probably me) seemed to be ordering God."
(A)  As is seen in this emblematical form, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] gains through this the understanding wherein the spiritual forces in the physical being may so magnify self's interests as to come under that direct influence of those spiritual laws. And as the tents represent to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] the insecure dwelling, and the looking up and ordering to the High and Holy, that which the entity, through the [[Prayer|prayer]] of the righteous many may be saved, and as the ordering is then to self, to so direct life in self as to be in that closer accord, closer touch, that the orders from the Highest may be through that channel from which entity has seen much development, see?  Well that in this connection entity read Jude, especially 10th, 11th and 12th verses.

4. (Q)  "Saw the outline of a man's face."
(A)  Just that [[Visions|vision]] of the correlating of conditions that may come to entity, with the closer relations with those truths as are being gained by entity, and through same will come power, force, and those great truths that bring peace and happiness to the inmost being of entity.  Gain those [[Visions|visions]], for through same will come those lessons for this entity.

5. (Q)  Night of November 11, or morning of Thursday, November

6. (Q)  Does this indicate I should go to Dr. Kraus for treatments?
(A)  No.  That as given.

7. (Q)  "[[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Gas & Electric selling between 130-132."
(A)  This, as we see, those conditions as seen again before such happens, and as entity gains more and more of the truths in the laws pertaining to such, more and more will entity be able to apply same.  This, then, is the lesson, as an experience, that entity may gain the concept of the truths being shown entity in this phenomenized form, and that phenomena in this manner to this entity may be the same as that in the practical or to physical REAL world or life, see?

8. (Q)  Should I buy or sell P. G. E. at these prices?
(A)  Rather that as given, than that entity should act upon same, other than as the reasoning from self's own standpoint, with the general physical condition existent, see?  A lesson rather than a direction, save as entity gains same through entity's concept of conditions pertaining to that individual stock.

9. (Q)  "Dreamed of Sidney Rhodes, who just returned from Europe."
(A)  Again that presentation to this entity, that through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of one just returned from many waters, there will come to this entity, and those close associated, much concerning those conditions from across many waters, see? for as the entity, as seen, represents in the physical life, so may those lessons, those truths gained through such represent to the entity in material life.

10. That is all the dreams.

11. There are several we have had here yet, you see.  We are through for the present.

12.  "Saw myself going to Dr. Kraus.  [Eugene R. Kraus, D.O.] It was early in the morning, before breakfast."
(A)  In this, again we see those truths as have been given, as to that necessary for entity in the physical to gain the more perfect coordination throughout the [[Physical Body|physical body]], and as the entity through the mental gains this vision, these then are the lessons:  Follow those instructions closely, persistently and consistently, as have been given for the physical condition of the body, see?  Do that.
