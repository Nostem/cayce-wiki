# TEXT OF READING 2267-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[2267-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/8/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 8997

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions are much bettered in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  There should be, however - even at the present time, and with the changes that have been wrought - this consideration given by the body, that the applications and the conditions as are being brought about are RIDDING the system of the reinfection, and that those conditions as to where the prolapsus - or the tendency to overlap - or as, instead of a falling down a tendency to move OVER, see?  to engulf, engorge - the condition has been relieved somewhat, but the STRENGTHENING of the muscular forces as to keep same in place has NOT been fully accomplished, and THIS SHOULD be accomplished before too great an activity is undertaken again.  While necessary that the strength of the body, the vitality of the whole physical organism, be tested - as it were - that the equilibrium and the balance of the whole body is in shape, but we would, for the time being, keep most of that, or all of that - practically - that has been given, until this is near in shape.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are all treatments given properly?
(A)  These are very good.

3. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments after he returns home?
(A)  At least twice each week.

4. That is all the questions.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
