# TEXT OF READING 1676-4 F 30

**Reading [[1676-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/4/1941  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 7793

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1676], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there have been improvements; then lapses, and then changes as have come about.

4. In the present, - at least once a month we would have a general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment to keep the centers in the cerebrospinal system in the correct proportion one to another.

5. To be sure, most of such operators would require or desire treatments more often; once or twice or three times a week, but we find that once a month would be very helpful IF the rubs are given at home in this manner:

6. Once each day have a spinal and limb rub with Peanut Oil.

7. Use the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] carrying the Gold; this plate carrying the solution would be attached over the lacteal duct, while the small copper plate would be attached to the lumbar and sacral - or lumbar-sacral axis.  Attach this for thirty minutes each day.  The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of Distilled Water - one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], you see, to each ounce of Distilled Water - and use at least four to six ounces, dependent upon the container used.

8. Then keep the diet and the activities much in the manner that has been indicated.

9. And these as we find in the present will continue to improve and bring bettered conditions for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  I am considering marriage, and would like to know if I should become a mother would it be injurious to me or the child?
(A)  Would not be advisable for either.

This may be possible later, but DO keep up the treatments. If there is the desire for the wedded life, very well; but do keep up the treatments, and work towards the desire for motherhood; which with that expectancy, as well as the consistency in taking the treatments, should bring better conditions for the body.  But be improved in health before such is consummated - that is, motherhood.

12. (Q)  Can you tell me whether my condition is inherited or caused from the fall I received?  I was in perfectly normal health until the fall of 1934.
(A)  It is partially a condition innate.  Thus, as indicated, it is better that this be nearer corrected before the actuality of attempting motherhood; for self as well as for offspring.

13. (Q)  Miss Marian Williamson, Director of the Crippled Children Commission, [[Kentucky|Louisville]], Ky., has asked if I would be interested in an examination from that hospital.  Can you advise me as to whether this examination will do any good?
(A)  It is what one does ABOUT the condition and not the examination that does good!

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self (two copies; one for Dr. Martha Beard, [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.)
"    " Ass'n file
