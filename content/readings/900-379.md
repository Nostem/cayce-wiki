# TEXT OF READING 900-379 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-379]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/6/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5582

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of April, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.          [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.

2. [[Dreams|Dreams]] are the experiences of those conditions in the mental activities of the body as related to life in its [[Oneness|oneness]]. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Night of April 5th.  Heard conversation - didn't know who it was, but heard someone say "On Monday right at the opening - hit it hard."  Didn't know whether it meant to sell out, or to buy, or just what it had reference to. Then saw Meinhard building a fire under a table.
(A)  In these two experiences we find the variations of the activity of the mental forces of the body.

In the first there is seen that [[Telepathy|telepathic]] force of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] listening, as it were, to those of the forces that would put down the market on the first opening in the Monday trading.

In the next there is seen the emblematical conditions of that destructive force as would be made by an individual respecting the efforts of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  In the last, as has been given for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], act in that way and manner that the outcome of same would be as if fire builded under table would not be harmful, without coming in contact with the table itself (which is not seen in the [[Visions|vision]]).  Hence emblematical of how the entity should act in self.

In the first there is seen that the activity will begin in the opening of pressure to reduce those conditions, or those prices as attained; yet there will be the tendency to react to even yet higher levels; yet well were the advance, or advantage taken in the decline and in the advance.  Act as will be given the entity respecting same on the evening prior to the opening.  Do that.

4. (Q)  Wednesday morning, March 28th, while telephoning Cayce on [4665] Motor corporate set-up, about 8:30 A.M.  As I was talking to Cayce about this I beheld a definite white figure right next to me, so close I could feel its pressure.  It was the latter that turned my attention to it, and seeing it I jumped with fright and it disappeared.
(A)  Here we have that activity of the consciousness as guides or directs those forces as seen in the body of [4666] in its activities respecting that which the entity was interested in, concerning that individual appearance - and studied in the manner, the INDIVIDUALITY of same may be known by that already given.

5. We are through.
