# TEXT OF READING 4827-1 M 45

**Reading [[4827-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/14/1931  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 12818

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of January, 1931, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [...] - through Dr. W. H. [[Wesley Ketchum|Ketchum]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., [[California|California]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4827].  Now, we find as to the disturbing factors in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, these are as much from the distortions as produced in nerve reaction - from incoordination in the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal system - as from PHYSICAL disorders themselves; for the physical disorders are more the EFFECTS than affectation in the system.  While these, we find, are pressures - or lack of coordinations - these conditions, or these incoordinations, as are also in turn an affect of disturbances in the system.  Not from brain lesions; neither from (at present) a softening of the tissue itself; neither from INJURY, direct - but rather from an affectation of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system that have to do with the creation and operation of stamina in the system.  This produced by conditions existent in the system from taking in the body those quantities, or a qualified or qualitated condition for conditions from exterior, and the effects that these have produced upon the body through those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], especially, that make for the coordination in sympathetic and cerebro-spinal - especially in the region of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]], the thyroid reaction, and the glands as TO the system in its progenerative or generative forces, as gentitation.

2. In the effects as created, in keeping the body quiet - the administration for the sedentary effects to the body have gradually lost their effect, so that the body - while not in that state as of drivel from the system, may become so unless there is a change in the character of such an active force in the system - that is, the nerve forces - and unless there may be accorded that which may bring a better coordination in the system, so that the muscular forces, in the sinew and tissue itself may not become inflammed, and an increased lymph and capillary circulation so bring such reactions for the body. The blood supply must, sooner or later, become attacked by same, and while we will find, the reactions to the greater extent would be of a negative nature, yet there will be periods when positive reactions are also seen - especially if taken in the early portion of the day.

3. In those reactions to the general system, as of digestion and the other portions of body - we will find that these must be kept more in accord with the administrations that are given for beneficial effects to the body, whether those applications of an external nature, internal, or medicated conditions.

4. Then, in meeting the needs in the present - as related to applications - we will find that the sedative effects may be given in a manner that will bring a better and easier reaction for the whole body.  These we would prepare in capsule form and give in this manner, this in each capsule - and this one dose:

Oil of Eucalyptus..........................1 minim,
Rectified Creosote.......................1/2 minim,
Rectified Oil of Turp......................1 minim,
Codeine, preferably in the tablet.......1/20 grain.

This would be given not oftener than one each day, unless a reaction is necessary - when a more powerful nature may be applied.

5. We would also begin with those injections as of a typhoid serum.  Give a whole course, yet in MINIMUM dosage.  Then, in three to eight weeks, repeat such a course.

6. We would also use those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the system that may be found in the sinusoidal ray, of the short wave length - or that as makes for a REVERSE coil activity; the applications or anodes, or pads, being connected at the plexus where the brachial center emanates, and in the 4th lumbar.  These we would not give oftener than twice each week.  After the third week we may apply them in the 2nd cervical center (the first anode or plate), and in the umbilicus center (the other plate).  In either application, do not let [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] be of longer duration than one and a half to two minutes.

7. It would be well also that the electrical driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], or rotator, be applied along the cerebro-spinal system.  This we would apply from the 1st cervical to the end of the spine.

8. In the diet - keep more to the citrous fruits, nuts, and other fruits, bewaring of apples (unless of the jenneting variety), and NO bananas.  Pears, grapes, grapefruit, peaches, plums, apricots, dates, figs - ALL such may be taken, but those as given (apples and bananas) should be eliminated from the diet.

9. Keep the mental surroundings for the body in an atmosphere of cheerful helpfulness; not as centralized on DO this, or DON'T do that - but let's TOGETHER do this, that, or let's TOGETHER don't do this or that.  See?  a guiding force.  We will see a response and the nearer coordinative forces in this body of [4827].

10. We would give further instructions in six to nine weeks.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

> knew it.  Since then I have labored incessantly to cure him
> but to no avail.  He has as fine a physique as a man can have
> and has never dissipated in any way except gambling but he
> has hurt himself mentally & seriously through his gambling
> and now has not [[Free Will|the will]] power to quit.  I am at my wits end
> and am also well nigh worn out myself, trying to match and
> care for him and keep our business in good shape.  We have a
> bake-shop in [[California|San Francisco]], a tea-room & bake-shop here in
> Burlingame and are putting a bake-shop in [[California|Los Angeles]].  My
> husband is a quaker and well educated man - a brilliant mind,
> a fine musical ear and exquisite, artistic taste.  He could
> have gone far in music & art had he been trained along those
> lines - has a fine singing voice.  He is throwing away his
> own life against his will and I seem powerless to help.  I
> feel sure his generative life has suffered severely for lack
> of proper mating?  My own sex life has gradually died and I
> have little left to offer him in that respect.  He is a
> strong healthy man by nature and needs proper mating.  I have
> pondered these things from every angle and do not know which
> way to turn.  Perhaps I should take him out of business -
> away from all money constructions or perhaps I should give
> him his freedom that he might have a chance to work out his
> own salvation.  If you have medical connected with your
> institute who could treat him and keep him under
> surveillance perhaps I should bring him there for treatment.
> Perhaps I should place him in a sanitorium under other care
> than my own.  I am writing frankly to you hoping and [[Prayer|praying]]
> you may be able to help me save him from himself.  I am
> enclosing ten dollars for another reading and hope you will
> be good enough to help me right away and forgive my
> negligence in writing as it has been almost impossible to
> find a time away from him to write and I did not desire it
> advisable to tell him as yet about the reading.  Hoping I may
> hear from you very soon and thanking you for your kindness.
>

> Mrs. [...]
