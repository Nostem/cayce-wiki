# TEXT OF READING 1215-2 M 17

**Reading [[1215-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/18/1936  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 6750

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of December, 1936, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1348], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mass. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those surrounding conditions with the body.  And many there be that might gain a lesson most worth while in their experience if they would, if they could, but see and know and experience the love that is beyond compare - as is shown in the lives, the care and the attention of those about this body.

2. These as we find may grow, may go into, a greater and greater awakening; to that knowledge and experience that all health, all life, is as a full consciousness of the individual entity and [[Soul|soul]] being aware of the presence of Divine forces and influences in its experience.

3. Not a great deal of change in the physical or material aspects of the conditions in the present, yet there is a quieting, a coordinant condition in the activities of the mental with the physical reactions through the very use of the Radio-Active influences in the body.

4. These precautions we would give, and these as a helpful influence:

5. Do not attach the Appliance until it has been in the water, or the ice, for at least ten to twenty minutes; and do not allow the ice or water to come closer than an inch and a half of the top of the Appliance.  Keep all very clean, by rubbing off with a cloth (dry) and polishing the plates or anodes with emery paper each time before applied to the body.

6. We would find that it would be well to use the periods during which the Appliance is attached as periods of [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]].

7. As the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are subjugated to the mental forces. For the [[Thought Forms|Mind is the Builder]], whether of that we term as the upper, the lower or the middle strata.  And if these are in accord with that unison as created by this mechanical aid for attunement of a perfect circulation that radiates by the very [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the body through this creating of the current that forms for coordination, such study as of the 30th chapter of Deuteronomy would be well.  Especially those portions of same as to the admonition to each individual, that the divinity that is sought finds the greater expression and is in the own self that attunement may be had.  And also those as may be found in the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th chapters of St. [[John (Apostle)|John]]; how He - that is the Way - has given His promises.

8. And know that they are not just to those who were present, those words and that message given, but to each [[Soul|soul]] that seeks to know His Face.  For He IS Life!  He is Strength. And [[Healing|Healing]] is in His Presence!  And no ill that may hinder can be in those hearts and minds that realize His Presence is near, even as is given there - that ye ask in that Name!

9. That there has been some confusion in the combinations of the oils that were intended to work with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as set up by those influences from the Radio-Active Appliance, is indicated by the disturbances created with the use of same and the irritations as arose.

10. These were not prepared properly.  But for the present, and to bring about that as we find may make for the more PERFECT coordination under the existent conditions, let's substitute - or let's use in their stead these:

11. Use equal portions of Tincture of Myrrh and Pure [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]. Heat the Oil to add the Myrrh.  Keep this combination then as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over those portions of the cerebrospinal system, and all of the structural portions; that we may bring to all of the activities a stimulation of help as arises from the activity of the influences of these properties to the body.

12. Do these, and then as we find in no great distant date there may be the considering of the orthopedic surgeon making braces that there may be the greater activity for the body; eventually so much greater outlook upon all the conditions as will arise in the experience of the body.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
