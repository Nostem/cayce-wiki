# TEXT OF READING 294-25 M 48

**Reading [[294-25]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1984

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Suggestion to answer questions on EC)

1. EC:  Keep those conditions for those abnormal forces manifesting in the body [hemorrhoids] until all are made to function in normal manner.

2. (Q)  What causes the dizziness?
(A)  Unequalized circulation, same as that which produces the bearing down to the [[Physical Body|physical body]], which prevents the equalizing of blood to flow to head.  When the system becomes equalized this will pass away.

3. (Q)  Are there any suggestions for this body?
(A)  Not at present.
