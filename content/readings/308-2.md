# TEXT OF READING 308-2 F 10

**Reading [[308-2]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/29/1934  Sex: F  Age: 10  ReadingID: 2331

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of August, 1934, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [585], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time    ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those impressions of the body here, [308]; these we have had before.

2. In the main we find conditions show much improvement from that we have had before.  Still there is incoordination in the assimilating and digestive forces, and this irritates the sympathetic nerve supply.

3. As we would find in the present, the better benefits would accrue to the body from following an altered outline - as follows:

4. First we would have the thorough eliminations through the alimentary canal; which may be the better effected through taking three to five doses of Simmons Liver Regulator - a teaspoonful night and morning for two days and a half.

5. After this has begun to act, then we would use small doses of Fletcher's Castoria; which will stir the liver and its activity, the secretions throughout the alimentary canal, to a much better response, and eliminate much of that which has caused and does cause the irritation to the nerve system.

6. We find too, that for the alignments in the cerebrospinal system, it would be well for the body to have a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] at least two or three times each week, when it is ready for retiring in the evening.  At such periods it would be well for the body to be massaged along the cerebrospinal system, along the limbs and even to the extremities, with a compound prepared in this proportion, though it may be dou- bled or havled (this AFTER the thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]], you see, should be rubbed in - what the body will absorb):

7. To 4 ounces of [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil, Usoline or Nujol, add:

Witchhazel...................2 ounces,
Tincture of Benzoin..........1 ounce,
Rectified Oil of Turp......1/2 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]............10 minims.

8. These will tend to separate, and necessitate that they be shaken together thoroughly before a small quantity would be poured into a saucer or open container for using to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] from the base of the brain toward the end of the spine; massaging the limbs and the feet also with same.  Also it would be well for the body to be massaged across the abdomen and the lower portion of the stomach proper, and especially over the liver and caecum area.

9. Do this, being mindful of the diet - which, after this elimination, should be as follows:

10. Mornings - either fresh fruits or melon or dry cereal, or the citrus fruitjuices, but do not mix ANY of these together.  With the fruit may be taken a little crisp bacon and a coddled or poached egg, but no fried eggs.

11. Noons - rather the green, fresh, raw vegetables, even when there are the activities in school let the lunch be rather of the lettuce and tomato sandwich, but as much of the green, fresh, raw vegetables as may be eaten.  Such as lettuce, celery, carrots, tomatoes, peppers, beans, radishes, radish tops, turnips, turnip tops, and ESPECIALLY onions - preferably, though, the onions would be eaten before retiring at night.  This, as we find, will overcome those tendencies for the activities of the kidneys, and adjust same better.

12. Evenings - either liver (as meats), fish, tripe, pigs' feet and ears, souse or the like, and green vegetables that are well-cooked.  And each evening let a portion of the meal be of BOILED onions, that are boiled in their OWN juices; not in water.  Those eaten before retiring preferably should be raw; not large quantities, but small quantities, for these carry a vitamin and the elements necessary for the dilation of not only the pulmonary activity but for the juices - gastric juices of the stomach and assimilating forces of the body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Has she any worms in her system now?
(A)  Not as we find.  Only pinworms, or small.  We will eradicate these with onions.  They'll scare 'em away!

15. (Q)  Is her appendix alright?
(A)  Appendix alright.  The soreness in the area is produced rather from those pressures in the 7th and 8th dorsal, which will be overcome in the corrected eliminations through such measures as indicated, and then the diet and the massages.

16. (Q)  What is the condition of her tonsils?
(A)  Natural condition from a poor or stagnated circulation.

17. (Q)  Anything wrong with her kidneys now?
(A)  Nothing wrong with the kidneys; only pressures, as indicated, from that in the appendicial area - as indicated, 7th and 8th dorsal.

18. (Q)  Will following this treatment prevent bed wetting?
(A)  This may be accomplished best by the suggestion in this direction as the body is almost asleep, by the one who makes the application of the massage and rubs.  POSITIVE suggestion!  Not that she  WON'T do, but that she WILL do this or that, see?  that when the desire is for the activity the body will arouse and attend to same!

19. (Q)  Was she injured in any way when she was knocked unconscious on the pavement early part of summer?
(A)  As indicated in the dorsal areas, which will be overcome by strengthening through the rubs - and the diet.

20. (Q)  What should be done about foul breath?
(A)  This is being applied.

21. (Q)  Could the mother give the massages?
(A)  The mother could give the massages and, as indicated, should be the one to give the suggestion.

22. (Q)  Any other advice regarding this body?
(A)  We would follow these rather closely.  First CLEANSE the system, you see; then after the system is cleansed begin with the rubs and the diet.  These should correct the conditions.

23. (Q)  Can anything be done to make her hair curl again like it did when she was a baby?
(A)  The diet, and those external applications may be made. These are natural conditions.  Each body should rather prefer to be natural than using any make-up; for when individuals do this it bespeaks of their dislike of that the Maker made them!

24. We are through with this reading.

Copy to the Mother
"    " Ass'n file
