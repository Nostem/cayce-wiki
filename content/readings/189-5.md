# TEXT OF READING 189-5 F 43

**Reading [[189-5]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/11/1938  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 704

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [189], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  As we find, there are real improvements in the general conditions of the body.

2. To be sure, owing to the stirring up of poisons there are times when there is a reverting to many of the old disturbances; as through the soft tissue of face, throat; some disturbance through the eliminations and the digestive system.

3. But if there will be kept a CONSISTENT application of those things which will bring about the results as outlined, then we should find the conditions continuing to be on the improve.

4. To be sure, these should be taken periodically; that is, don't just begin and continue all the way through but be CONSISTENT with it.  Take them for periods of three weeks, then rest a week or ten days; then take another series.

5. But do not be unmindful that the diet should be adhered to as indicated, and as may be indicated by the manipulator; also the cabinet sweats.

6. Do these and, as we find, we will bring the better conditions.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Are my present glasses right for my eyes?
(A)  These will necessitate changing, as changes are brought about.  It will be found that there will be an increase in one lens and a decrease in the other.  This should be done, of course, by one versed in such.

9. (Q)  Is there an obstruction or growth around the liver, colon, kidneys or anywhere in the digestive or eliminant tract?
(A)  Only the natural consequence of the conditions that have arisen from poisons that have affected same.  NO growth, as we find.

10. (Q)  What is causing slight defect in hearing?
(A)  The stirring up of poisons and their flowing through the system, causing pressures.  This needs then that there be stimulating, when the manipulations are given, through the ducts especially that lead to the auditory centers, see?

11. (Q)  What causes the headaches?  How can this be cured?
(A)  Just as has been indicated.

12. (Q)  Please outline a complete diet for the suggested three meals a day.
(A)  This should be rather outlined by the one MAKING the adjustments, as we have just given.

13. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Be consistent with the applications and we will find help.

When the headaches come, [[Alkaline Diet|alkalize]] the system; this will be found to be the more helpful.  Use such as Sal Hepatica as an eliminant, as well as an alkalizer.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
