# TEXT OF READING 325-2 F 51

**Reading [[325-2]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/29/1923  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 2440

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of June, 1923, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [325].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:20 P. M.                                        ..., Miss.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  The conditions are some better in this body than when we had them before, still some show of the conditions as exist through the digestive tract.  Follow with those conditions, as we have given, for the correction of this condition in this system; taking these medicinal properties as we have given and with the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] forces to the body. Do that.

2. (Q)  Why does this body's heart beat so fast?
(A)  Only abnormal in its beat when the [[Vibration|vibration]] was raised above the normal by the over-amount of [[Vibration|vibration]] given through the electric forces.  Only use those as we have given, not the current so strong, see, and use [[Violet Ray|violet ray]].

3. (Q)  Why does this body have smothering spells?
(A)  Reaction from the pneumogastrics.  Take these properties in the system, as we have given, to overcome this condition.

4. (Q)  Why does this body spit up blood?
(A)  From the stomach.

5. (Q)  Why is this body's mouth so bitter?
(A)  Gastric juices as rise from the stomach itself.

6. (Q)  Should this body have her medicine refilled?
(A)  Just given it.

7. (Q)  Is this body's right lung affected?
(A)  No.

8. (Q)  Why does this body's right breast hurt?
(A)  Reaction from pneumogastric conditions - only reflex pains.

9. (Q)  Have you anything else to say regarding this body?
(A)  Do as we have given.  The [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] must be of those in accord with the body and must be from the violet ray.  Not the full current, but that, that only gives the Violet Ray effect in the system, not over three minutes to the whole portion of the spinal column.

10. We are through.
