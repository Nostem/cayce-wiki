## Text

Date: 4/1/1944  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 11737

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4044], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. 
Taken 11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern War Time.    Ashland, Pa.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Ashland - what a quaint place!

3. Yes, we have the body here.  These, as we find, are progressive conditions, but these are the effects more of the lack of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity, produced in the main by an injury to the pelvic area, or on the side of the buttocks.  This has made the condition acute.

4. So we would find, in making the application for correction of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance, the lack of chemical balance should first be corrected by the correcting of the lesions formed through the pelvic area, especially in the ileum plexus and through the lumbar and even into the sacral and the 9th dorsal.

5. This might be done best by a naturopath, one who wold not only make some minor adjustments but stimulate the centers that are released by the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

6. Rather than the powder ordinarily used by the naturopath, use this combination of oils:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].............2 ounces,
Peanut Oil............2 ounces,
Lanolin (melted)....1/4 ounce.

7. Shake these thoroughly and use in giving the massages or adjustments.  Give these practically every day, or at least five to six times during a week and have at least eighteen to twenty-four such treatments.

8. Then begin to use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying gold in same; in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water, using at least three ounces.

9. The Appliance would be attached each day for thirty minutes, the larger plate through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center - which would be the width of one finger from the naval center to the right and three fingers up from that point.

10. The small copper plate would be attached to the lumbar axis.

11. Each day following the Appliance treatment, give the body the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with the oils; particularly over the limbs, the feet, the bursa of the feet, the muscular forces of the lower limbs and through the muscular forces of the ends of the nerves at knee, upper and lower portions, and also across the abdomen.

12. In the diet keep to those foods that are strengthening.  Add the combinations of the B complex vitamins; as in the B-1, or those prepared by Miles Laboratories - One-A-Day B complex.

13. Leave off the Vaiavi and use the B complex, when you begin with the Appliance.

14. In the diet have a great deal of raw vegetables; keeping well balanced.  Have a great deal of sea foods.  Fresh water fish is not as well for the body, but the deep sea - Forty Fathom fish are well, also shell fish in their season.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Should I discontinue all the treatments and the things that I am doing at present?
(A)  We would, if there would be gained the better conditions.

17. (Q)  Is it harmful to try to do small duties around the house?
(A)  Not harmful.  Keep in the open as much as possible.  Not so much in the direct rays of sun, but in the sun at times.

18. (Q)  Is it helpful to use a hot water bottle on the lower part of my back when I retire?
(A)  When the hot water is used, put on a [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Pack.  This will ease the body much better and keep a balance through those areas.  Yes, use the hot water bottle but do not use it over three to four thicknesses of cloth saturated with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

19. (Q)  What causes the night sweats which I suffer from very frequently?
(A)  Strain on the pelvic organs and the pelvic structural portions, that need correction first, you see.

20. (Q)  What causes pus to form in the urine after sexual intercourse?
(A)  These are from the same conditions existent as indicated.

21. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the naturopath) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(If you do not already know of a good naturopath, we would suggest that you get Dr. Thomas T. Lake, 264 S. 16th St., Philadelphia, Pa., who will be able to direct you to someone in your vicinity.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance [See 1800-24 Reports].)
