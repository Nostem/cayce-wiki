# TEXT OF READING 5144-1 F 53

**Reading [[5144-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/25/1944  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 13277

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5144], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in "Coronet" magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5144].

3. As we find, these are rather serious disturbances with this body.  For as we find, unless there is great care taken in the administrations for the body, the applications for some portions of the disturbance may be very hard upon other portions of the body.

4. As we find, the main disturbance arises from ankylosis, or the deposit of too great amounts of calcium in portions of the structural body; hands, wrists, elbows, shoulders, feet and toes, ankles and knees, all of these show the effect of this character of disturbance.

5. These, as we find, may be gradually eliminated, yet there will be the necessity of careful diet and of real precautions taken, else too severe reactions would become very hard on the heart; circulation between the heart, lungs and liver, especially.

6. Also there has long been a prolapsus in the Eustachian tube. This is as the basis, for this accumulation at the joints arises from an unbalanced condition, weakening the resistance in the lymph and emunctory circulation through extremities, through all portions of the body where there are those areas where great lymph pockets are gathered in the regular functioning of the body.

7. As we find, these will require a great deal of patience, a great deal of persistence, a great deal of patience on the part of the body itself, and being very persistent and consistent with administrations.

8. We would find that using daily [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salt]] Packs would be very helpful.  Take a hand and an arm on the same side of the body, wrap this in hot [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salt]] water; this made almost a saturated solution.  And this is to be followed by a rub, and rub toward the body at all times.

9. There should be begun at once regular periods of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, which will aid in keeping the system rid of that which is a part of the necessary activity to set up better eliminations of the poisons from the body.

10. These packs would be, then, applied to each arm, each limb. As these are aided, then the massage would be given daily with Peanut Oil.

11. Vegetables raw, not too much, but sufficient each day to give those tendencies for better alkalinity through the body.

12. It would be well if there were general massages administered almost every other day; at least have them Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays.  These we would give as a thorough massage all over the body following the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salt]] Packs.

13. As these conditions are gradually aided, this will work towards also an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgery.  Had this been administered some fifteen to sixteen years ago, we might have had quite a different story for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  But begin now, first by, setting up better drainages, breaking up this tendency for joints to become static and for swelling in the centers through the extremities of the body, and gradually within the next six months to twelve months working towards betterment for those conditions in ears or Eustachian tube.  While this may not restore the hearing entirely, we will have a 20 to 50 per cent gain in the abilities of hearing, if these administrations are made regularly.  These should be at least twice each week.

14. The Packs and the rubs should be given over the areas that are inflamed and that show the inability of proper motion in the extremities.

15. Do that.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Should all upper teeth be removed?
(A)  These, as we find, while not altogether a source of the trouble, are so much contributory to same that the body will be much bettered if these were removed.

18. (Q)  What causes spasmodic pain around heart?
(A)  The disturbed circulation.

19. (Q)  I constantly hear music in the air, has this anything to do with my physical condition?
(A)  This is the expression of [[Psychic Development|psychic abilities]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

20. (Q)  Would it be beneficial to remove residence to a warmer climate, such as [[California|California]]?
(A)  Such a climate as [[Arizona|Arizona]] or [[New Mexico|New Mexico]] would be well.

21. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgeon, when you are ready to start these treatments - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Epsom Salts Packs.

At your convenience you might like to get in touch with one of our members in Detroit:  Mrs. Sydney Krause, 5470 W. Outer Drive.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 11/1/43 Letter:  "...'The Coronet' article so impressed
> me that I am writing in the hope you can give me a
> reading...  I am deeply interested in psychic matters and
> have had many manifestations of its authenticity.  At the
> moment I am in need of help..."
> 
> B2. 1/10/44 Letter:  "...On the last day of 1943, I finally
> found a copy of '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]'.  I sat up all night
> reading it, and since, have read and re-read it.  It held me
> spellbound.  It partly answers so many things I have vainly
> searched for since the passing of my twin sister in 1902, and
> my husband in 1934.  So you see, I am not curious, but deeply
> and sincerely interested.  I have been deaf since the age
> of 19.  I am told the nerve is not destroyed, that it is a
> prolapsed condition.  Atmospheric changes cause fluctuations
> in my ability to hear.  Wherever there is a [[Vibration|vibration]] I hear
> perfectly, in street cars, trains, elevators, airplanes,
> rooms filled with talking people, etc.  Last summer a swollen
> condition was noticeable in my hands and fingers.  This has
> steadily grown worse, so much so, that I cannot close my
> hands, and they pain constantly, sometimes stabbing pains and
> at times as if needles were sticking into them.  Even cutting
> a loaf of not-too-soft bread causes excruciating pain.  There
> are hard white incrustations on all the joints, so that they
> are not flexible; as a matter of fact, all the joints in
> my body creak and crack, even my vertebrae, when I turn
> my head...  My questions are:  (1) What is this swollen
> condition in my hands, principally fore and middle fingers
> (swollen almost twice their former size)?  (2) Is there fluid
> in the tissues and will this destroy them...?  (3) Can the
> hard white incrustations on my finger joints be cured or
> removed in any way, so the joints will be flexible again?
> (4) Have I an excessive amount of poison in my system that
> causes this condition, or is it faulty assimilation, nerves,
> inactive or over-active [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], or a breaking down of a vital
> organ?  (5) Do my teeth contribute to this trouble?  (6) What
> is wrong with my hearing, and can it be improved in any way?
> (lately I have noticed it is worse - for 13 years, eight
> hours a day, I operated a dictaphone which I am told, pushed
> my eardrums in).  I have had very little occasion to call a
> doctor in my life, and at present, we do not have a family
> doctor.  Shortly before this condition came about in my hands
> last summer, our good doctor developed cancer and went away
> to be with his only son, also a doctor.  I have not consulted
> a medical man about my health, because so many of our
> well-known physicians have been called into service.  If
> there is any way of helping myself after my reading, I
> shall do all I can to faithfully follow out instructions.
> I simply must use my hands!...  I should like also to tell
> you of a psychic experience I had when my husband was on an
> automobile trip and was injured.
> 
> "He was down in W. Va., and I was in Mich., but he came to
> me and I saw blood just gushing out of his forehead where
> the injury was...  I have abiding faith that my reading will
> bring relief...  later I hope to have a Life Reading and a
> Mental and Spiritual Reading also..."
> 
> B3. 5/17/44 Letter:  "...I will be at Mich.  Perhaps the case
> history part does not apply to me, since this is my first
> reading.  However, so that everything will be intact, perhaps
> I had better give this information:  Painful swollen joints
> both hands; middle and forefingers flesh and joints both
> badly swollen; stiff, cannot close hands; all middle and
> forefingers are becoming mis-shapen; ankle bones, shoulder
> joints, neck, hip and vertebrae crack when moved; spasmodic
> pain around heart."
