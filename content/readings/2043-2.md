# TEXT OF READING 2043-2 M 54

**Reading [[2043-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/20/1939  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 8595

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of December, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2043], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Conditions are not so well.  There is still so much of the poisons, as we find, attempting to be carried through the system, - from those conditions as we have indicated, of the inflammation through the alimentary canal, as well as the accumulations that cause the greater pressures upon locomotories in those areas indicated.

3. As we find, the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Baths would be the better manner to eliminate these poisons.  These we would take just sufficiently often so that it does not overtire or overweaken the body, - that is, about twice a week; provided there is a general rubdown afterward with pure grain alcohol, - not rub alcohol nor denatured, but grain alcohol - for the body may gain strength from the food values in grain alcohol, by the absorption and by the perspiratory and respiratory reactions.

4. Thus we will find the better conditions.

5. Also, for the excruciating pain, we would be more consistent in the efforts to gain the better reaction from the Cobra Venom, - rather than overgiving, or undergiving.  Follow the general reactions, and don't give it each day, but every second or third day.

6. These offer, as we find, the better conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Would it be possible to outline more in detail the proper diet, keeping in mind that within the last 3 years I have had 2 very severe internal hemorrhages from a duodenal ulcer, - in addition having a gastric ulcer?
(A)  As we find, we would have plenty of fruit juices, - orange, grapefruit, lemons; especially mixing a little lemon with the orange juice.  At other periods, but not at the same meal with the citrus fruit, nor even on the same day, we would have the whole wheat cereal, or the combinations of wheat and rye, or steel cut oats, or all such.  Have plenty of beef juices, - not the meat itself; and plenty of liver and liver extract.  This especially should be ground, and all the juices retained in same when it is prepared for the body. Whole wheat breads, Graham crackers or Graham bread or the like, - all of these are the better for the body.

9. (Q)  How can I obtain better relaxation, especially for rest at night?
(A)  As we have indicated, if there is the persistent effort to gain the better reaction from the Venom, better relaxation may be gained.

These offer the better outlines, as indicated.

We would do that.

10. We are through for this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
