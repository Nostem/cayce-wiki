# TEXT OF READING 1194-2 M 55

**Reading [[1194-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/25/1936  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 6662

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of July, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - through Active Membership of his daughter, Miss [421], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1194]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while conditions are very much improved, there is the necessity - if we would not have a recurrent condition, and if we would aid in eliminating the causes of those disturbances in the circulation and relieve those pressures on the heart - of being very close and mindful about the diet.

3. DO NOT have a quantity of meats, especially with quantities of starch; as potatoes or the like.

4. Well that at least once a week, oftener if necessary, there be used the high enemas to evacuate the colon throughout, to prevent this tendency of these pressures causing those disturbances in the circulation, in the heart's activity, and a recurrent condition in the appendiceal area.

5. Keep the rubs occasionally along the spine; in addition to the corrections that are well to be had chiropractically or osteopathically once a week or the like.  But the rubs would be in addition, for the heart's activity.  Commence as a circular motion, with equal parts [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], Tincture of Myrrh and [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil.  Heat the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] to add the others.  Commence just between the shoulders, circular motion across the spine, up to the head and neck, down to about the waistline; and extend occasionally even to the soles of the feet; that is, along the back portion of the leg, circular motion, and then long rubs - circular motion and then long rubs over the whole of these areas, to keep the circulation to the extremities; the same in the arms, hands, and clear to the fingertips.  This will remove much of those disturbances; such as the numbness, heaviness in the limbs, dizziness, and disturbances for the whole body.

6. Do these.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Are the strychnine tablets good for the body?
(A)  At times they are necessary.  If the rubs are adhered to, the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], the diets and adjustments occasionally, we should gradually be able to eliminate these by degrees and eventually eliminate them entirely.

9. (Q)  What makes the condition on left side and just below short rib and top of hip bone?
(A)  This is an accumulation from an excess superficial circulation.  These rubs, these eliminations should gradually make for absorbing of same.

10. (Q)  Is blood condition better?
(A)  Blood condition and circulatory forces are some better. But we need to keep in a nearer normal force that there may be the proper eliminations and proper adjustments in same.

11. (Q)  How is the appendix?
(A)  This is better.

12. (Q)  Why the continued constipation?
(A)  Long standing of diet, long standing of the character of exercises and the lack of the proper attention.

Take the care of these through the manners indicated.

13. (Q)  Is heart, liver and colon condition better?
(A)  Better, but they need to be - as indicated - cared for, that these do not have recurrent conditions.

14. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
