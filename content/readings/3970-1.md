# TEXT OF READING 3970-1 M 74

**Reading [[3970-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/12/1927  Sex: M  Age: 74  ReadingID: 11621

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12 day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [3970].

[Edgar Cayce; Mrs Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  We have the body here.

3. Now, we find the inabilities to bring about the perfect coordination throughout the body is produced from many causes; the first being that of too much of those properties in the system carrying mercury, until we have that character of reaction in the nerve centers, and especially in the locomotion in such centers.

4. To bring the nearer normal conditions for this body will require a great deal of very strict care and attention, and while there are being many things administered for the welfare of the physical conditions of the body, in a manner, yet many of these are not meant well.  These are bad.

5. But to bring the better forces would be to change from the environment entirely, and be under the care and attention of those that WOULD administer discretely and directly to the upbuilding of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  Until these changes, we would only give as this:

6. We would apply to the body those properties as would be found in the Radio-Active Appliance, carrying only those properties in the negative anode, as is found in [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], administering same through the heavy plate in and about the umbilicus, and this should be applied for at least thirty (30) minutes three (3) times each day, see?  morning, noon, and evening, see?  with sufficient of the ice kept in the temperature that will give off the highest [[Vibration|vibration]], see? that is, not too much water, but more ice, and only in the wooden or crock container - not a metal, see?  and do not let the anodes come in contact with each other.

7. We would follow these.

8. As medicinal properties, those of this combination:

Tincture of Valerian..................1 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya.......................1 ounce,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium 10% solution.....30 minims,
Bromide of Potassium 10% solution....10 minims,
Tincture of Capsici...................2 minims,
and sufficient simple syrup to make eight (8) ounces.

9. The dose of this would be half a teaspoonful in water twice each day.  If the body inclines to become nervous or irritable, the dose may be repeated more often, see?  but never closer than two to three hours, see?

10. The diet should be kept nearer to those that produce the better assimilation.

11. Follow these directions, though, for thirty-six (36) days. Then we would give the changes as have come about in the surroundings, and that as necessary for corrections for better condition in the body of [3970].

12. (Q)  Should that which is being done for the body be left off?
(A)  Leave off ALL, and do these as we have given.

13. We are through.
