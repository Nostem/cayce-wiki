# TEXT OF READING 2779-2 M 12

**Reading [[2779-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/13/1943  Sex: M  Age: 12  ReadingID: 9942

This psychic reading given at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Dr. [2772], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Road, 
3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.                  ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2779], born 3/10/31, in NYC, and the information given in his Life Reading through this channel [2779-1] on 7/7/42.  You will give a Ck. Life Reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], with information, advice and guidance that will be helpful in bringing about a well-rounded development and the best expression of his abilities.  You will then answer the questions he submits, as I ask them, concerning his present unfoldment and his associations from previous [[Incarnation|incarnations]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information that has been indicated and the interpretation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s experiences in the earth.

3. To fulfil that as to the better [[Purpose|purposes]], some application must be made - if there would be helpful influences obtained through these channels.  Only the awakening in some portions, but no application as yet has been made as respecting the necessary preparations or studies the body should make.  And these must be begun.

4. Then, within a year, two years, three years, there may be given that which would be helpful.  This as applied in the present might be only confusing.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. [2772], the father
"    " Ass'n file
