# TEXT OF READING 1561-21 M 61

**Reading [[1561-21]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/1/1942  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 7580

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of September, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. Geo. L. Marquess, thru Mrs. [2174], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:40 to 12:50 Noon - Eastern War Time.      Kilgore, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the enquiring mind of Geo. L. Marquess of Kilgore, [[Texas|Texas]], and his prospective operations on the Carmerson Oil Corp. holdings, or vicinity.  Please give special consideration to area adjacent to the Starnes property, East, embracing 500 acres good land, identical surface exposure, cap rock continuation.  Should Marquess take adjacent acreage or property?

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there is to the east of the properties indicated as a good location, a high hill.  This has a great deal of the same outcroppings as seen farther west.  Any portion of this would be well, for it is a continuation of the same.

4. But toward the creek that lies north and northeast from same there is a greater depth, or the rising of the land and a pinching out even to the upper sands.  But that which lies to the east and to the south would be good, or a part of the old holdings of L. Starnes.

5. Yes, we would obtain these acreages.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

Wired Day Letter as follows:  TO EAST OF PROPERTIES INDICATED AS GOOD LOCATION THERE IS HIGH HILL WHICH HAS MUCH SAME OUTCROPPINGS AS FARTHER WEST AND ANY PART OF WHICH WOULD BE WELL FOR IT IS CONTINUATION OF SAME  BUT TOWARD CREEK LYING NORTH AND NORTHEAST IS GREATER DEPTH OR RISING LAND AND PINCHING OUT EVEN TO UPPER SANDS  BUT THAT LYING EAST AND SOUTH WOULD BE GOOD THAT IS PART OF L. STARNES OLD HOLDINGS YES  WE WOULD OBTAIN THESE ACREAGES.

---
