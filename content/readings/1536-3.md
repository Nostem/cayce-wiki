# TEXT OF READING 1536-3 M 29

**Reading [[1536-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/11/1938  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 7470

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1536], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

... Apt., 
Time of Reading                            Clearwater Beach, 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  [[Florida|Florida]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, [1536].  We have the body here; this we have had before.

2. Now as we find, while there continues to be those reactions and the spasmodic attack of the asthmatic reaction, there are improvements.

3. As we find in the present, we would have the character and the nature of the applications as first administered; not merely for the correcting of the alignment of the segments, but the manipulations of the nature where the ganglia - as they emerge from the centers along the cerebrospinal system, especially in the areas of the 4th dorsal to the 1st cervical - are aided in their activity for the impulse and flow; and especially, as has been indicated, to the vagus center.

4. Also, with such a character of mechanical manipulation, we would use regularly - when there is a spasmodic reaction - the one grain tablets of Calcidin.  Or, dissolve three to five grains in three to five ounces of water and let this be sipped every two or three hours - say half to a teaspoonful at the time, so that we have the reaction from the Calcidin - or the calcium, and an [[Iodine|iodine]] reaction.

5. Then, with the precautions as to the character of exercise, we should find the body continuing on the improve.

6. If it is then more plausible for the body that there be the return to the home surroundings FOR these activities, these would be in the PRESENT in keeping with the anxieties as well as other general physical conditions.

7. We would then give that it would be well for the body to return; but have those adjustments and the mechanical applications in the manners as indicated.

8. These as we find are in keeping with the better influences for the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  The coughing attacks are very violent, particularly at night -
(A)  (Interrupting)  As indicated, at such periods more of the Calcidin should be taken - until there is the reaction from both the calcium and the [[Iodine|iodine]], see?

And we will find a better reaction, NOW, in a little higher altitude.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
