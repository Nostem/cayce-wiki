# TEXT OF READING 2096-2 F 22

**Reading [[2096-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/18/1931  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 8742

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of May, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [2096].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround same, [2096].  This we have had before.  In many respects we find conditions have been on the improve.  While there is the tendency for the body to immediately take on weight when the diet is neglected, or too much sugars or too much starches are added to the system, we will find that were the [[Vibration|vibrations]] added in the proper ratio or manner from the Radio-active Appliance (the plain), the assimilated forces will be better distributed in the system and the [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]] as are set up will be nearer EQUALIZED, and that conditions will then CONTINUE to be in a nearer NORMAL way and manner; for THESE [[Vibration|vibrations]] NOW, with the changes as have come about in system, will act with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] of the system that tend to make for too much of the avoirdupois of body.

2. In the distributing of the used forces as of elimination, would be well that - as was outlined - there be taken those forces as will INCREASE eliminations, and ESPECIALLY that that will affect those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of assimilation, or in the lacteals direct.  These, we find in the present it would be well that each morning - before the breaking of fast - a level teaspoonful of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom salts]] be taken for ten days straight running; then skip at least ten days, then repeat again.

3. With the application of the Radio-Active Appliance, these we would alternate from the opposite sides - rather than being opposite.  When attached to the right wrist, attach to the right ankle.  When attached to the left wrist, attach to the left ankle.  See?

4. In the rest, we would keep that as WAS outlined in the matter of diet.  The general physical conditions are VERY good. Do not take internally properties that are to act upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], for this will prove detrimental in other directions, other than that as has been outlined here.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How often and how long at a period should the Radio-Active Appliance be used?
(A)  At least thirty minutes each day.  This may be increased to sixty minutes when this has been taken for fifteen to eighteen days.

6. (Q)  Have weight-reducing treatments been followed out correctly?
(A)  In part, but when the body has taken starches or sweets there's a tendency to increase.

7. (Q)  What is normal weight for this body?
(A)  This has been given.  Should be about one-forty.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
