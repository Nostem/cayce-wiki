# TEXT OF READING 5137-1 F 24 (Dance Teacher, Religious

**Reading [[5137-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/20/1944  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 13270

Preference:  "Metaphysics") This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self thru mother, Mrs. [5138], Associate Member of the Ass''n for research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5137].

3. As we find, there are disturbances which cause incoordination in the body-functionings.  These as we find are forming accumulations, which has caused sediments in the kidney area.  We find that these may become very serious unless quiet and precautions are taken, and there must be certain activities until there are the eliminations of these disorders.  And there are certain conditions and activities in which the body must not engage during these periods.

4. As we find, from those subluxations that exist between the 7th and 8th dorsal, there has been a hindrance in the amount of nerve energies going to activities between the liver and kidneys.  There are those disorders in the gall duct area, the reactions cause heavier flow of activity to be performed by the kidneys in eliminating poisons; that is, lacking in the necessary flow produced accumulations.

5. These should be tested by x-ray, but if these applications are made we will find we can eliminate this disorder.

6. About twice each week, Tuesdays and Fridays, when the body is ready to retire, put about 5 drops of [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] in one quart of warm, little better than lukewarm water, wring a rag or cloth out in same, and place this over the kidney area and let this remain on the back for at least one hour, then remove.  Do this for about three times.  Then begin (not before) with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments with special reference to the dorsal, from the 9th dorsal up to the 1st cervical.  And while these are being made, during at least twelve to fourteen of such adjustments, do keep having twice a week the stupes of [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] over the area.  If it causes anxiety in the bladder, do put the same type of [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] stupes over the lower portion of the abdomen, to the pubic center, across this whole area, but especially right in the center to the pubic center.  These can be kept on or off in regular periods, or irregular periods, depending upon the pain.

7. With the corrections, do have at least one scientifically given [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

8. For we should increase the eliminations by using senna pods or senna tea.  Put two or three pods in a cup and pour boiling water over same, let stand for thirty minutes, strain and drink.  This do once or twice a week.

9. Thus we will bring better conditions for the body.  Do not take during these treatments any hard drinks or soft drinks where carbonated water is used.  A little later on when there has been lessening of pain, do use Coca Cola but with plain water, not carbonated water.

10. Do this and we will bring better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is the diet alright?
(A)  Diet is very good.

13. (Q)  Should any of the vitamins and treatments now being taken be continued?
(A)  These should be continued, after the treatments suggested.

14. We are through with this reading.

2 copies to Self thru mother (one for Dr. [1397]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
