# TEXT OF READING 4888-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4888-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/7/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12903

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of July, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno. Miss [3797] (?).]

Time of Reading 
4:50 P. M.                                Pittsburgh, Pa.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find, while this body is very good throughout, there are some abnormal conditions that may be assisted in the physical by the correction of conditions to comply with the [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to produce the better elements for the physical.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them:  First, in the blood supply, while the body is very good in this, number and quantity and quality, there is the constant strain on the circulation produced by some weakened forces in the heart action in the body, this being caused by nerve strain rather than by organic condition in the system.

3. In the nerve system we find with the action over the system of the forces as applied to the body through the pneumogastric force that the centers at the lower and upper dorsal region are contracted to such an extent as to loosen the tension, as it were, upon the forces as governs the heart action in the body, and with the condition in the pyloric end of the stomach itself, this reflexly affects the pneumogastric center, else we have that in the system from this that produces the strain to the nerve, one working in conjunction with the portions affected in the body.

4. In the functioning of the organs themselves the body is very good throughout.  That then gives into the system the resistive forces in the organs to meet the needs as is necessary for the conditions as they arise.  Only with the relaxed nerve tension from pneumogastric forces need the body, at the present, be wary of, for with the time and the inclination and predisposition towards the weakness in the pyloric, the body must then give of its best forces to meet the demands upon the physical in the present plane.

5. To give then the better force to this to meet the resistance in the system we would only take those elements that do not become contradictory to the [[Vibration|vibration]] in the body, but which will overcome the weakened forces and relieve the body entirely from the inertia from these forces at times upon the physical in the body.  This then as we see is for the body to develop the forces that will overcome this condition.  This would be reached through the exercise for the trunk portion of body, especially, the emanation from this exercise gives the strength necessary to bring this change in the system. The circular and stooping exercise with the mental force deflected, you see, that is, when the body takes the exercise.  Do that.

6. (Q)  Should this body take any medicinal properties?
(A)  Only these as we have given except when there becomes acute attacks or conditions in the pneumogastric and the pyloric end, but only use at present, that of the exercise. Be persistent and sincere in the suggestions to the body as the exercises are taken.  Medicinal properties would only hinder those of adjustments not necessary except as the body with the exercise of the trunk or torso gives of its best.

7. (Q)  Should this body have a special diet?
(A)  He is dieting at present a plenty, you see.

8. (Q)  Will the newspaper campaign prove good that this body is thinking of going into?
(A)  The idea itself is very good and would be a success in the financial returns.  Beware of the present connections, however in regard to such work.

9. (Q)  What towns will prove best for this advertising?
(A)  This can be made the success in any one where there is a newspaper and sufficient territory to demand.

10. (Q)  Can this be carried out successfully if the connections with it are watched carefully?
(A)  Can, with the idea for a success and may be carried with any town and city if directed correctly.  Beware of individual that presents same at present.  Do as we have given if we would follow closely the best for this body.

11. (Q)  Where is the body at the present time, 5:50 P.M.?
(A)  He is here at this address, you see, right at present. Just came in.

12. (Q)  Where did you first locate him?
(A)  At the street here, Union.
