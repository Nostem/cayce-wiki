# TEXT OF READING 512-2 M 6

**Reading [[512-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/9/1934  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 3641

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of February, 1934, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 to 12:10 Noon Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  We have the body here, [512].  This we have had before.

2. Many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body since last we had same.  Most of these are for the betterments.

3. When there is kept ALL of those suggestions in the application of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]], as indicated, better and better should be the reactions.  And the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] should be kept full charge, see; that is, very soon the properties that charge the battery should be renewed.

4. But after such treatments a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] should be given all along the cerebro-spinal system.  Begin at the base of the brain, and over each segment - from the 1st dorsal to about the 9th dorsal - use the right hand (of the mother who is to apply the [[Massage Therapy|massage]]) in a rotary motion (with the middle finger) as from left to right, as the coffee mill grinds, see?  Go over each segment gently, firmly, moving TOWARDS the 9th dorsal FROM the 1st cervical to the 9th dorsal, see? Then begin at the end of the spine, or coccyx area, and move in the opposite direction, from right towards left - just opposite from the grinding of the mill - move towards the central portion, or the 9th dorsal, very gently but very firmly over each segment.  Make each treatment, the whole of same, for ten to fifteen minutes; not just as rote, but SEEING as it were - that there is being carried into the system the necessary [[Vibration|vibrations]] to create proper coordination from each ganglion that is stimulated by such manipulation, see?  with the points of the fingers, and it should be done directly on the body.  Have the body lying, preferably, prone - as the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] is being applied.  Hence it should be applied, of course, with the right hand, middle finger, preferably, see?

5. Then, with the body lying prone, begin with the umbilicii center, on each side, with both hands, and gently raise the body, pressing around the area of the diaphragm, see?

6. And keep the eliminations open, especially after each of these treatments.  See that there is a perfect MOVEMENT of the alimentary canal.  This with the treatment in the middle. Commence from each side of the umbilicii, or navel, and RAISE the body gently, just a little motion of the hands as it is brought to the 9th dorsal area; for there we bring it, you see, both from the top and from the lower end of the spine.

7. Then, lying the body on the back, see, place the right hand over the 9th dorsal area and the left hand over that just under the chin and over the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  Not a movement of the hand, now, but just the gentleness to keep the body quiet.

8. These manipulations, these applications of the magnetic influences in the body of the mother making the application, will quiet the body to sleep, gently, and make the general coordinations throughout the system as the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] are supplying renewed energy, as the mental activities are carried on.  And during such periods, in this [[Meditation|meditation]], give from the heart to the body these suggestions - to self and to the body:

9. MAY THERE BE BROUGHT INTO PHYSICAL MANIFESTATIONS THOSE THINGS AND ELEMENTS AS NECESSARY FOR THE MAKING OF PERFECT COORDINATION IN THIS BODY, AS GOD, THE FATHER, THROUGH THE LOVE AS SHOWN TO MAN IN THE GIVING OF [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]] SPIRIT INTO THE WORLD, CREATE THAT NECESSARY FOR THE BEST NEEDS OF THIS BODY.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [959]

> operated on for those spells, and he jerks his eye bad
> sometimes and trembles all over.  I never have used the
> [[Massage Therapy|massage]] your last rdg. called for.  It says to use the
> [[Massage Therapy|massage]] after I use the battery.  He goes to sleep as soon as
> the battery gets on him.  So I am writing to ask you if the
> [[Massage Therapy|massage]] could be used before the battery treatment?  and I am
> going to ask your advice on something and I feel sure that
> you will know exactly how to advise me.

> "Do you think that little [512] should be sent to a school
> such as Mrs. Pope's (Mrs. Anne M. Pope, Rosehill School,
> Chester Heights, Penna.) for training, etc.?  and if he were
> to go to any school, could the teacher there in charge do the
> massage with the same results you mentioned, that they were
> to come through the mother?  Not that we are able by far to
> send him away, or want to give him up, but we want to do what

> for we feel that knowing his condition you would know best.
> 
> "I hope very soon to send you some money on your past rdgs.
> I will worry over it until you have it all, for you have been
> so good and kind."
> 
> R4. 8/4/34 EC's letter:  I have just received yours of the 1st.
> We realize what a hardship the last few years have brought to
> most everyone, and what a struggle it is to meet the bare
> necessities of life; especially with a family and a child
> that is requiring a great deal of attention and considerable
> amount of expense.
> 
> But our [[Purpose|purposes]] are just as sincere, I believe, as anyone's.
> Of course, we do not want to make a burden, wherever it is
> possible to lend whatever power or influence may come through
> us for the assistance of individuals; yet it is upon just
> this alone that we depend for our livelihood.  It isn't as if
> we had some other source of income.  There are seven in this
> office and organization whose whole sustenance depends upon
> the contributions that may be made by those for whom we have
> given our time and service.  [EC; GC; HLC; EEC; LBC; GD and
> MD.]
> 
> And I feel very sure that you, as soon as your condition
> permits, will be as ready and as willing to contribute that
> you can towards the carrying on of the work that has tried to
> be of a service and a help to others.  We appreciate your
> feelings in the matter, and hope you will understand our
> position.  It is those who contribute very small sums that we
> rely upon; not upon any great benefactor, for we have tried
> such things.  There might be those induced to contribute
> large sums, but they require with same that their own
> personal desires be considered first and foremost; while it
> is for the spiritual, physical and mental upbuild for our
> fellow man that we desire to give whatever help we may
> through this work.
> 
> I'm glad you have gotten all the things together again, and
> hope that very soon you will be able to give the entire
> treatment to little [512].  I'm sure it is just as necessary
> to see that he has the massage as it is that he gets the
> battery; and I'm sure you will find he will overcome a great
> deal of this trouble.  Too, I'm sure you can give the massage
> before the battery just as well as afterward; that is, if, as
> you say, the battery affects him in such a way as to make him
> go right on to sleep.  The idea was that the portions of the
> system be relaxed, that the Gold in the body through the
> battery might be more effective throughout; but I'm sure the
> change will be alright.
> 
> As for the training and schooling, I do not know of any place
> that would be better than with Mrs. Pope.  However, as he is
> only four years old, [See 2/23/35 letter saying the child
> will be 8 yrs. old on 5/26/35.  Until then we had not known
> his age.]  I do not think this would be necessary
> yet; and by the time he is seven with all these treatments
> followed by you at home (and no one else could do them the
> same as a mother) - he should be able to better take
> advantage of any mental training that might be given; and
> that shouldn't be too late.  When the physical condition is
> all awry, you don't want to overtax him by crowding the
> mental.  He has plenty of time yet to learn.  First, we must
> get his [[Physical Body|physical body]] in shape so that it can contain and
> carry out mental training.  With these treatments given
> persistently and consistently, including the suggestions as
> the body goes to sleep at night, I feel that he is going to
> gradually develop into a normal, healthy boy.  Then the
> mental training will be easy; while it is only a secondary
> thing at present.  You are fortunate in being able to start
> with correcting the condition while he is so young; otherwise
> it would take much longer.
> 
> Thank you for your letter.  I trust you will let us hear from
> you, and that you will understand just what our situation is
> and the attitude we hold.
>

> living:  "I know you will think me very foolish for writing
> for your advice about little [512], especially when I haven't
> been able to do as you told me to do with him.  It is rather
> hard to clothe the other children for school and feed all of
> them, along with other expenses, when work has been dull, but

> hospital and school combined, at St. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]]'s, West Va.  It has
> a wonderful reputation.  The man that proposed the building
> of it is a friend of ours, a big American Legion man, and our
> neighbor, a Mrs. [...] has a part in it.  So there is no
> doubt in the world that [512] can be admitted, but that
> doesn't mean everything.  So without going into more details
> and taking up your time, I shall do as you advise regardless
> of what others wish or think.  I am willing to do as you
> advise, whether it is to keep him at home or to put him in
> this school, which they claim is wonderful, until Christmas."
> 
> R6. 2/26/35 EC's letter:  I'm just in receipt of yours of the
> 23rd and note what you have to say about [512] and about the
> improvement that has come; also the possibilities of your
> being able to put him in the school.  I appreciate very much
> the confidence that you have in asking me to make this
> decision for you.  I do not know that I have really a right

> applications in a consistent manner, considering all
> circumstances it might be advisable for you to make the
> change; provided you are able to get the people in charge to
> apply the treatments.  I know in some schools this is a
> plausible thing, especially if the individual has personal
> attention; in some others it is an unreasonable thing to ask,
> owing to the necessity of the children being treated as a
> group rather than individually.  However, from what I have
> heard thus far from other sources respecting this institute
> in West Virginia, it would seem that you could get some
> personal attention.  If this can be arranged, I would say by
> all means do that.  It would make it better, no doubt, for
> the other children at home, giving you more time; and perhaps
> would be better for [512] too.
> 
> I hope this will work out for the best for everyone
> concerned.  I assure you that we would be glad to cooperate
> in any way whereby we may be of the greatest service.
> 
> With kind regards, and hoping to hear from you again, I am
> Sincerely, EC:GD
> 
> R7. 3/3/35 Mother's letter:  "I rec'd your letter Fri. and thank
> you very much for your advice.  The doctor was here yesterday
> making a statement of little [512]'s condition and age, etc.,
> to send in to the hospital I mentioned, and I learned through
> him that they would not use the battery but do their own
> experimenting and treating the child.  Although [512] isn't

> to [512].  I would give him over in a minute's time to her,
> for she works under your instructions faithfully.  Well, to
> my wishes and opinion, she is [[Woman of Revelation|the woman]] we ought to have him
> with.  I wrote her 3 wks. ago and enclosed a stamped envelope
> but have gotten no reply...
> 
> "If Mrs. [959] can afford the expense for her [146], surely
> it can't be so dreadful, but I will leave all this to your
> better judgement, and only wish that it were possible.  If it
> isn't, why I don't know just what to do, as [512] needs
> training badly.  We have grates in our house and he has
> burned everything up that we have, nearly.  He burned a new
> coat up this morning.  It is pretty bad on all of us; still,
> I hate to give him over to that hospital for new experiments
> and maybe useless results.  It is a wonderful place and good
> specialists, but the better, etc., is what I wanted used, in
> fact what should be used.  If you feel like you have no right
> or reasons to write Mrs. Pope, it is perfectly alright, Mr.
> Cayce, but I had hoped to try it before we take any further
> steps."
> 
> R8. 3/8/35 EC's letter:  I received yours of the 3rd, and note
> what you have to say.  I do not blame you for your feelings

> know they are not receiving that which might be their
> privilege to have.  Yet in this particular case, I wonder if
> it wouldn't be possible for you to persuade whoever has
> charge of [512] to at least give the battery treatments a
> try.  I assure you I'll be more than glad to write to Mrs.
> Pope, and I know that she has been in sympathy with our
> undertakings.  And I'm writing her today, and I'm asking her
> to communicate directly with you as well as letting me hear
> from her.  I hope that whatever comes will be for the very
> best.
> 
> With kind regards and best wishes, I am Sincerely, EC:GD
