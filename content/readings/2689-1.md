# TEXT OF READING 2689-1 M 79

**Reading [[2689-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/23/1942  Sex: M  Age: 79  ReadingID: 9792

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Dr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [2185].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

General Public Hosp., 
Time of Reading                         St. [[John (Apostle)|John]], New 
10:35 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.  Brunswick, [[Canada|Canada]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, while conditions are rather serious, they need not necessarily prove too much to respond, if these applications or administrations are made.

4. There are old disturbances, as we find, which arise from a condition in the liver; a hardening in one lobe and an inactivity of the general flow of the secretions from same.

5. Thus a condition is produced in the duodenum that prevents the natural the natural flow of the gastric juices and the refuse or digested forces for the activity through the jejunum and alimentary canal.

6. This closes, almost, the valve or the connecting forces here. [See 2689-4 Reports.]  It produces the inability of retaining foods, the inability of assimilation, and the natural consequence of those conditions through the colon also.

7. Then, we would wire this:

8. Apply [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, at least three thicknesses of flannel saturated in [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], as warm as may be applied over liver, gall duct area, covering the lower portion of the stomach, applying the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from an electric pad, for an hour.

9. Do this night and morning.

10. Stimulate to activity, osteopathically, the area from which the lower portion of the duodenum AND the upper jejunum receive their impulse, - that from the 2nd and 3rd dorsal to at least the 6th, - this as a relaxing for activity.

11. Give, at least every other day, a [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douche, - one ounce of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to ten ounces of body- temperature water - these proportions.

12. Do this, until there is relief.  Then we would give further instructions.

13. The foods should be those of the easily assimilated or pre-digested nature.

14. Ready for questions.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Dr. [...] - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file

Wired as follows:  SATURATE THREE THICKNESSES FLANNEL IN HOT [[Castor Oil|CASTOR OIL]]  APPLY WITH ELECTRIC PAD OVER LIVER  GALL DUCT LOWER STOMACH  FOR ONE HOUR NIGHT AND MORNING  STIMULATE TO ACTIVITY  BY RELAXATION OSTEOPATHICALLY AREA FROM WHICH LOWER DUODENUM AND UPPER JEJUNUM RECEIVE IMPULSE SECOND AND THIRD AT LEAST TO SIXTH DORSAL  GIVE EVERY OTHER DAY A [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCO-THYMOLINE]] DOUCHE  PROPORTIONS ONE OUNCE TO TEN OUNCES BODY TEMPERATURE WATER  USE ONLY PREDIGESTED FOODS  FULL INFORMATION AIR SPECIAL.
