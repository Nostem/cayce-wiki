# TEXT OF READING 4778-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4778-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/22/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12756

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 22nd day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:30 P. M.                                       ..., Ala.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now the abnormal conditions as we find in this body have to do with organic conditions, and the effect that is produced on the system through the function of the organs so affected, and the effect produced on the rest of the system.

2. Now these are conditions as we find - the abnormal conditions that have to do with the functioning of the organs through the emunctories and those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and functioning organs that have to do with the eliminating conditions from the system through all of that portion of the system affected by those conditions, either in that of blood forces or of the organs of elimination, affected directly or indirectly, as we have within the system that bacillus that produces within itself, it the bacilli, becoming a factor to be dealt with, not being as yet a malignant condition, but one that has growth within and propagates itself.  The effect at the present is to the functioning of the kidneys in elimination, and the bladder in its irritant to the portions affected, that, by trying to be eliminated.  The [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the ilium plexus being affected reflexly, the conditions show those effects in the blood as thrown or drained through the system.

3. To give the relief to the body to bring the perfect elimination, we would cleanse with pure water the bladder itself, that is, wash it out, you see, by the injection to the bladder to wash or cleanse it and prevent the action of the bacilli on the system through those channels, then take into the system that which will give the proper incentives to the functioning of the kidneys and the organs involved in the condition, taking this:

The same properties as is found in Vinol.  Every fifth day until the quantity is taken, we would take this:

Tincture of Valerian..........4 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium..........20 grains,
Elixir Calisaya...............3 ounces.

4. Simple syrup sufficient to make eleven ounces.  The dose would be teaspoonful, see.

5. The injections to cleanse the bladder would be taken every seven days until the relief is given to the pressure on the bladder and inflammation does not show again.
