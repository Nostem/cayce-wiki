# TEXT OF READING 2392-2 M 57

**Reading [[2392-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/25/1941  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 9214

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2392], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Ky.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2392]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while there are improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, there is much yet to be desired - if the body would be rid of those infectious forces that, as we have indicated, may cause a great deal of disturbance to the muscular and nerve tendons of the body.

4. While there has been a stimulation to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces to purify the system, we find that at times there are still those infectious forces that affect the urethra.  Hence unless there is the purifying of same, or the destroying of the germ force, there will be a greater affectation to the bladder as well as the prostate.

5. If and when there are signs of the inflammation, we would use a weakened solution of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] as a penis injection with a syringe, see?  The proportions would be about half a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to half a pint of body-temperature water, to be used as an injection through the penis.  Do not use these injections oftener than every other day, and not then unless necessary.

6. Also we would have the general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, with the sweats and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] that goes with same.  We would take these sweats and [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments at least once every ten days, until four or five have been taken.

7. Then be very careful of the diets and of those things that may cause the irritations to the general system.

8. Refrain from any hog meat, unless it is crisp breakfast bacon.  But keep plenty of good, well balanced foods.

9. Refrain from any alcoholic beverages, or even drinks with a large percent of carbonated waters.

10. As a stimulation to better body-building, and to keep down the irritations, we would take ADIRON tablets as a tonic; two tablets each day, one with the morning meal and one with the evening meal.

11. Just occasionally, now, we would take the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; not too large quantities, but taken as has been indicated heretofore. Take two, three to five drops in half a glass of water each morning for a week to two weeks, then leave off, and then take again.

12. Keep the eliminations well throughout the period of following the suggestions indicated, for correcting and eliminating the causes of the disturbances.

13. The [[Gold Chloride|Gold and Soda]] Solutions should only be continued AFTER the better eliminations have been set up.

14. Do these, if we would keep the better conditions for this body.

15. Ready for questions.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
