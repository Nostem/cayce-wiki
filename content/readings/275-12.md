# TEXT OF READING 275-12 F 17

**Reading [[275-12]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/21/1930  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 1716

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of October, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [275].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           ... Avenue, 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [275].  Some chalkiness shows in the structural forces in bone supply.  Something is lacking or changed in the diet, and the manner of ministration of the properties from within.

2. In other respects we find the body still on the improve.  We would either LESSEN the distance, or lengthen the period of the infra application, see?  While this strikes through, but does not make - of itself - for chalkiness or brittleness in structural portion of bone tissue.

3. In the matter of the diet, study well that as has been given, and as is NECESSARY to keep an equilibrium.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What vitamin does the Life Everlasting Tea contain?
(A)  Three.

5. (Q)  Are all treatments being given correctly?
(A)  We have just made a change.  Let's change it!  Then it would be better!

6. (Q)  Is oil of [[Sassafras|sassafras]] needed in oil rub?
(A)  Needed.

7. (Q)  How do you clarify oil with water to correct dandruff?
(A)  Used with those of grain alcohol one to twenty, or one to forty in clarifying hair sufficient.  Half a teaspoonful to gallon of water would be sufficient, in cleansing oil from hair.  Best to use the VASELINE after the shampoo and THEN the alcohol in water.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department Two copies to Dr. Lydic
