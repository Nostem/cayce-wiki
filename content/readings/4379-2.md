# TEXT OF READING 4379-2 F 52

**Reading [[4379-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/19/1926  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 12213

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [4379].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Georgia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, those conditions as have been given for the system, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be from that of the battery formation for the body, has not been applied in the system. Hence, we find that the condition has, in some respects, become more aggravated than has been in some times back. Easier at the present time.

4. Then, we would apply to this body those [[Vibration|vibrations]], as is found in the Radio-Active Applicator, using the negative from the central portion of body, with the Iron, see?  Hence the lead anode, or connection.  This, we find, will assist and will bring better conditions for this body.

5. Do that at the present time.  This should be kept on the body, when ready to retire, for at least two hours, see?

6. We are through.
