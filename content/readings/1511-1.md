# TEXT OF READING 1511-1 F 28

**Reading [[1511-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/5/1938  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 7395

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of January, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1511], through her mother, Mrs. [...] [and sister, [1510]], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1511], Miss [1510] and their mother.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1511], present in this room.

2. Now, as we find, there are some disturbing conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body the correction of which would not only alleviate disturbances that automatically arise at times in periods but relieve other disturbing conditions that are a portion of same also.

3. Thus we would have a near to normal physical reacting body, giving the opportunity for the greater mental and physical expression through same.

4. These then are disturbances as we find them with this body, Mrs. [1511] we are speaking of, present in this room:

5. First, in the blood supply we find - owing to the nerve pressures and disturbances - there's the inclination towards a character of anemia; more in the nature of that in which there is the lack of the proper hormones in the blood stream for the creating of the balance between the red and white blood supply, or the lymph and the leucocyte supply of the blood forces themselves.

6. This is produced as we find primarily by some adhesions and pressures that exist in the lumbar and sacral areas; and with the pressure upon the nerve system, with the effect as produced to the reactions between the sympathetic or vegetative nerve system and the cerebrospinal we find also a sympathetic condition through the soft tissue and nasal passages and through the throat - those inclinations for the lack of the proper oxidization through the blood supply in the lung areas.

7. Not that there are organic disturbances, but these as we find arise as sympathetic conditions through those portions of the body as well as from the high nervous tension from those conditions that have been indicated which arise from adhesions and pressures IN the lumbar and sacral areas.

8. As to the activity of these upon the nervous system, and the activities upon the organs of the bodily functionings themselves, we find:

9. These, as we have indicated, produce pressures and deflections in such measures as to affect - through the nerve system and systems - the assimilating forces, the digestive influences, the activities to the organs in the sensory forces; all of these come under stress through the reaction of these disturbances in the system.

10. Then in meeting the needs of the conditions:

11. While it is well that constructive activity physically, mentally, spiritually be exercised at all times in the reactions to the conditions in and around the bodily forces, we find that when there are those PHYSICAL reactions that cause a disturbance of the specific natures that exist, then mechanical applications may relieve these when these may NOT be done through mere suggestions themselves.

12. Then, as we find, through manipulations osteopathically administered - through the sacral and the lumbar area; correcting, of course, after the first four to five of such adjustments made, those disturbances in the coccyx end of the spine as well as coordinating the centers through the upper dorsal and cervical - we will relieve the disturbances for this body and bring about a near to normal condition.

13. With the pressures existent there will gradually come more and more of the tilting of the stomach.

14. These are then the activities necessary in the present. We would give some ten to fifteen such treatments, then a rest period from same, and then we find only a few more being necessary - if they are given properly; one or two occasionally might be necessary.

15. But do these for this body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Will I have another baby, and when?
(A)  For the carrying forward of same it will be necessary for corrections to be made, before it will be possible for it to be carried to the full extent or time.  When these corrections ARE made, then this may be brought about for full conception - when desired.

18. (Q)  Any special [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]?
(A)  One that would be in sympathy, or that would carry out those suggestions for the proper adjustments and corrections through the system.

To be sure, we want to coordinate the dorsal and upper cervicals for those conditions in the lung, as well as in throat and head; but these should become SECONDARY or the latter portion of the adjustments - making proper corrections in the lower portion first as given.

Do that.

19. (Q)  Do I need any local treatment for my nose or head?
(A)  If there are the corrections, this should NOT be necessary - if these are given properly.  There WILL be, of course, some local treatment - but this will be AFTER the adjustments are made for the CORRECTING of the flow of the circulation, the removal of the inclinations for the accumulations and for the growths there; but these should be secondary and NOT the first primary cause.

20. (Q)  Any suggestions regarding diet or building up the system?
(A)  The body is a very good dietician!  if it would follow same, though it doesn't always follow!  But keep that normal balance of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] and acid, or eighty percent alkalin reaction to twenty percent acid reaction in the foods.

The body may keep a well balanced diet in this direction and keep normal in that respect.

21. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Do these as we find, and we will bring the better normal and near to normal conditions for this body.

Keep the mental activity constructive in EVERY manner, to make for more OPTIMISM throughout - to have the better physical force for the better physical and mental reaction in same.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
