# TEXT OF READING 2514-3 F 23

**Reading [[2514-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/21/1941  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 9448

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 21st day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2514]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there is continued improvement in the general as well as specific conditions of the body.  Not that the sources of the disturbances to the body have been wholly eradicated, but they are being held in check; and there is resistance against the disturbance being builded in the system.

4. Thus we find the abilities of the body for the effective activity from the applications made for the varied disturbances, that naturally arise from such conditions and the effects from the affected areas.

5. We would keep the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, regularly.  Have regular periods for them.  Have them each day for periods now of five to ten days, and then leave off a period of three, four to five days, see, - this especially during the menstrual flow. Let that always be a period of rest from the Oil Packs. The way these are applied in the present is very good; just so there are not streaks through the flannel that are not thoroughly saturated with Oil, so that a maximum amount of the Oil may be absorbed each time with the application of the heat to keep the absorption.  It is much easier to put these on warm, as is now being done, and then apply the heat and not raise to so much as to cause a great deal of perspiration.  But keep same warm so that the body does absorb the Oil.

6. Keep up the application of the Ichthyol ointment for the abrasions.  We would keep a gauze, and then an oil gauze over this, so as not to allow the spread of infection to other portions of the body.

7. We would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body with the combination of oils, as indicated; especially the tendons from the lower portion of the knee, under the knee, to the heel and to the bursa of the feet.  This will aid in allowing these muscles and tendons to relax.  Also [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the area of the sciatic center, the back, and the inside area of the limbs.

8. Keep the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over all areas where the skin is affected, or the spreading of the disturbance to any portion of the body; for these conditions are on the improve.

9. The Appliance, - keep the balance; that is, keep the charges changed regularly.  Do see that these are measured correctly, with the proper amounts of each chemical.  Do have the proper amount of zinc as well as the acids, as well as the copper sulphate.  These should be kept properly.  Do have them with rain water or distilled water when prepared.  Don't attempt to use tap water, ever; it does not give the same charge nor the same reaction.

10. Do change each of the solutions, - the Gold, the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], the [[Camphor|Camphor]], - every fifteen treatments of that particular solution; not every fifteen days, for you see these come in rotation, or should.  And be sure the plates are kept clean.

11. Do these; and keep as much of the exercise as practical; not overtiring the body but keeping out in the open when the weather permits.

12. Any exercise also among others, with groups, is well for the body.  As there is the desire for more companionship, use it - the companionship - for creative influences; a living thankfulness to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] for the OPPORTUNITIES for a service in this world.  For, as ye do unto others, ye do unto thy Maker.

13. Hold no grudges.  Find no fault with others.  DO show appreciation for all efforts, not only for self but appreciation for the OPPORTUNITY to speak to and with others as to how they should appreciate THEIR opportunities among their associates in WHATEVER walk of life it may be.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What causes the passing of water 2 or 3 times daily during the menstrual period?
(A)  This is part of the affectation through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.  We find that this may be materially aided if there is the massaging, during that period, the areas above the kidneys (or the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]]) with Cocoa Butter.

16. (Q)  What is hurting in lower back, just about the waistline?
(A)  Pressures, as indicated, that come from the general breaking up of the disturbance, by the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] changes coming because of the use of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the Appliance - with the use of those properties indicated.

Hence the necessity for keeping the Appliance fully charged at all times.

Know there will be periods of anxiety, good days and bad days, but keep [[Ideal|the ideal]], keep the [[Purpose|purpose]], and keep the applications consistently.  Don't overtax self in conversation or activity, but DO keep mind and body busy also.

17. (Q)  Will white places gradually go away?
(A)  Gradually go away, as there will be a little change later in the character of oils; for we will use more of the Peanut Oil, but this should come a little bit later.

18. (Q)  Will it be harmful to my condition to get a permanent wave for my hair?
(A)  If this is taken carefully and slowly, it will be rather inclined to be helpful; for it will give the body opportunity for associations and to look forward to things, to do things. These will be helpful influences if rightfully used, harmful if wrongly used.  We would have such.

19. (Q)  Is there anything I can do about the stiffness in my knees, - the skin seemingly tight and drawn, so the knees won't bend as they should?
(A)  As indicated, the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  This is particularly from the under side of the knees, through the calf of the leg and to the knee, and the bursa of the feet; then to the area from the knee to the hip, and especially on the sciatic nerve - the under side and the inside of the limbs, see?

20. Do these, and we should keep on the improve.   Keep the attitudes mentally.  Keep the diet regularly.

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
