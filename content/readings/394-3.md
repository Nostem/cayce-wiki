# TEXT OF READING 394-3 F 54

**Reading [[394-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/26/1933  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 3001

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. DeVera [[David Kahn|Kahn]] Kaplan, at 20 Ridgeway Ave., West Orange, N.J., this 26th day of November, 1933, in accordance with request made by the family, through [391], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miriam B. Frey, Lois Bothem, Dr. Cornelia J. Browne, Fannie [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Devera Kaplan, Jane and Sol Kaplan, Rabbi Marius Ranson and his mother-in-law Mrs. Morris; also Mrs. Steinberg and Mrs. Flanzer.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [394]; this we have had before.

2. Many conditions show an improvement in the physical.

3. As to the mental balance, the ability to coordinate between the activities of the imaginative system and the cerebro-spinal responses there has not been the advancement as much as in the general physical conditions.

4. Then, unless we may keep a better and more proper coordination between the activities of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] and the [[Mental Body|mental body]] - that, as there are the improvements in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] there may be the coordinating one with another - the last estate may be worse than the first.

5. We would, then, change somewhat the application of the influences that are to be given through the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the low electrical formations, that may enable the activities of that which is carried on through the nerve impulses of the system, as revived by the activities of this low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] carrying the emanations of the minerals that have been indicated.  Leave off the silver activity [via [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?] but continue with the gold [[Vibration|vibration]], also with the [[Camphor|camphor]] that it may make for the [[Healing|healing]] ohm necessary in the activity of the pulsations through both the veins and the arterial activity.

6. And, we would make those ADJUSTMENTS that are necessary now in the upper dorsal and, more specifically, in the upper cervical areas, where there is the crossing of the cardiac and sympathetics in their reaction to head and muscular forces; which will relieve the tension to the body.

7. Doing these, we will find, we will make for better conditions here for this body.

8. Do that.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  How often should such adjustments or treatments be given?
(A)  At least every other day; for, the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as the body is sponged each morning would also be well.  But these ADJUSTMENTS should be made.

10. (Q)  May these adjustments be done chiropractically?
(A)  They may be done chiropractically.  It would be very well were these done by those that understand just how such may be made helpful with the activities that are given.

Do not give so much bromides!

11. (Q)  Are there any special directions as to re-charging battery?
(A)  Make the solutions!  Keep the anodes clean, and especially those that pass through the medicinal properties.

12. (Q)  Have the treatments been given properly so far?
(A)  At times very consistently; at others mere matter of rote; at others they have been really more harmful than good, for the connections and activities were not kept normal.

13. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body at this time?
(A)  Do as we have outlined, for the better reactions.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file
