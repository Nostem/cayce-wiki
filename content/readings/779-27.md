# TEXT OF READING 779-27 M 54

**Reading [[779-27]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/28/1942  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 4798

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [779], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, [779]; we have the body here - this we have had before.

3. As we find, in many respects conditions are very fair. There are ever those tendencies through the area of the caecum, as well as in the whole colon, for the accumulations that cause heaviness AND a tendency for such accumulations in pockets of the colon to cause areas in the covering - or the area of the folds - to open.  This produces in this area and in the scrotum those pressures at times in the prostates, causing heaviness, uneasiness, languidness, or a feeling of a little bit of nausea at times.

4. Here, too, we find that a most excellent cleansing would be well through the use of the Tyrrell's elevator; the cleansing of the colon, you see.  Use either a colon tube, then, or the elevator methods, to purify or cleanse the colon, - at least every ten days.

5. This purifying, and then keeping a well balanced diet and a balance in the activities, should bring about a much bettered condition.

6. For the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], put a heaping teaspoonful of table salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to each quart and a half of water injected, and use at least a gallon or gallon and a half at the time - but have the water body temperature.  Not necessary that the container should be this large, but fill the container the second or third time, to have such a quantity.

7. In the last water put a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the quart of water; this body temperature also.

8. These will prevent a drain, or the weakening of the system, and will help to remove the poisons and keep the colon clear; removing these tendencies from the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Any organic or [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] condition?
(A)  Only sympathetic conditions in the prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], showing some tendencies for irritation by what may be termed the protuberances or pressures in colon causing pressure in the scrotum area, see?

11. (Q)  How is heart and blood pressure?
(A)  This is very good considering the age and the activities of the body.  These [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations will help in keeping the body clearer.

12. (Q)  Why the swelling and tightness of fingers at times?
(A)  Pressures from colon to the areas in the locomotories of the body.  The cleansing of the colon will aid in taking away this condition.

Do that.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
