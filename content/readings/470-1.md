# TEXT OF READING 470-1 M 34

**Reading [[470-1]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/5/1923  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 3428

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by self - [470].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M.                                        ..., Utah.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now in giving the conditions in this body that have to do with the physical and with the ability of the physical to propagate that of the physical that would have the full entity in that propagation, it will be then necessary to give the physical, mental and spiritual forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], [470] we are speaking of.

2. First, in the physical, as the physical, we find the body well balanced, well developed throughout the whole system. There are some minor disturbances within the system at the present time.  They have to do with the tissue that creates the excretions in portions of the system; especially is this in reference to that of the lymphatic circulation as affects at the present time the nasal and the throat, see.

3. In the blood supply in the system, the body is very good. The balance between the red and white, normal in normal conditions; at the present time an oversecretion in the white blood supply of the body.  In the nerve system, we find the body very good, well balanced in the whole system, abnormal in that of the highest [[Vibration|vibration]] in the system, in that of coersive ability [cohesive? coitus?] in genital organs, which gives the inability of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] through those organs to contain self at the time, see.  In the action then of the mental, spiritual and [[Soul|soul]] forces that enter into the creation of this cell where propagation with the fertilization from the female species may be propagated, with the inability of containing this, loses then the force as is given through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, and it is better with the condition as has existed in physical that none of this was propagated.  This may be, with the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]] created in the system, overcome and that of the species be propagated, for within the generation we have come to that point where with [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit must be propagated from within the mental of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], see.

4. In the [[Physical Body|physical body]], the organs we find normal in their functioning, save those at the present time of the nasal cavity, the throat and larynx giving some trouble, - some foreign forces causing condition in tonsils at present time.

These needs be removed, - not the tonsils, the condition at this time, - if we would give the best to this body's future development in itself or in the propagation of its species.

5. In the lungs, heart, digestion, very good.  The liver shows the effect of congestion in the mucous forming tissue and ligaments of body, or where this is formed.  In the hepatic circulation, very good, and normal for conditions in the body.  In the liver, the reaction through hepatics to kidneys give the excess of secretions as is being eliminated through the system at the present time.

6. To give the best to this body from the physical, remove those conditions in throat at the present time by the wash or gargle of equal parts of salt, [[Baking Soda|soda]] ([[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]]) and salaritus [saleratus], with twice the quantity of water, see.  Use this twice each day in throat and snuffle into the nostrils, of course warm for such applications.

7. For the condition in the physical that would produce the propagation, we would take those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that will give the organs of gentation [genitation - generation] the power of retaining that seemen [semen] necessary to become fertile instead of sterile by their lack of becoming in touch with the physical connection between [[Soul|soul]] and spirit force of the individual or where that cord [Eccl.12:6] is not cut between life and the soul and spirit world, see. Life, from the physical, we are speaking of.

8. Take then into the system, this:

To 8 oz. of distilled water at [add?] 10 grains of [[Gold Chloride|chloride of gold]] with 8 grains of [[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]].

9. The dose of this would be 15 drops in half glass of water, twice each day, and use vibrations electrically driven across the sacrol [sacral] and lumbar regions each evening.  Within the second moon the body will become fertile.  Do that.  We are through.
