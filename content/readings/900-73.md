# TEXT OF READING 900-73 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-73]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/22/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5276

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-68] as given for [900], May 9, 1925, especially the paragraph which reads, "[[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] is being drawn out from those conditions wherein the cry may be raised, can any good thing come from the street?  Can any force give spiritual understanding that seeks to make money in such channels?"  You will explain this in a manner understandable to the mind of [900].  You will also give the interpretation of the [[Dreams|dream]] which this body [900] had, in which he saw some young men and women surrounding him and addressing him as Master.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the reading here as had before.  This paragraph is as reference to conditions that arise it the individual's life, with the study of those conditions as has been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and in giving same to the populace this cry would be raised, "Could anything good come out of the Street", by peoples of that plane of thinking; that is, these are the class, character of people who would raise such a cry, people to whom it may be said of, they see, or having eyes they see, and yet do not perceive; having ears they hear, yet do not understand, and by another class would be raised that same as given.  Those of a one-sided or a self-centered, conservative individuals, lacking in full spiritual understanding.  Because of the fact that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] attains that condition, position, wherein the information as obtained may be used in the Street to gain financial ends, the cry would be raised by these narrow, self-centered individuals, "Can any spiritual understanding come from one whom would gain monies in such manner?"  The inability of the entities themselves, who would raise such a cry, to co-relate [correlate] the relations as bear between.  These given as warnings to the entity, that such things may be said, yet there has been given to this body, this mind, this individual, this entity, the possibilities of bringing to the world those thoughts, those elements of truth, wherein many will see the light and draw nigh unto Him, the Giver of all good and perfect gifts, and "He who having put his hand to the plow and turneth back is worse than the infidel".  In the day when thou hast put on the robes of Him who would approach [[Throne of God|the throne]], as is given in the illustration of those chosen to sit at the feast of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], "When thou comest with the comely garments, then thou are acceptable unto Him, the Chosen of the Peoples and chosen as a Light unto many who sit in darkness".

As to the [[Dreams|dream]] as had on May 12th, called Master, this as we see, only a portion of that brought back to the consciousness.  As we see, this represents the broad expanse of a seaside and the dais raised, as it were, by the peoples for the one learned to speak unto those gathered about, even though in the beach costume.  As they gathered at the feet of the entity, some asked, "Master may we not hear the words thou hast to give?"  This then merely that co-relation [correlation] of conditions that in a form do appear, and may be literally given.  Be able to give unto him who would ask spiritual gifts.

3. We are through for the present.
