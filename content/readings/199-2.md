# TEXT OF READING 199-2 F 17

**Reading [[199-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/2/1925  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 1008

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 2nd day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by her sister, Miss [288].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  Now you have before you the body of [199] who is located at ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].  You will give a complete physical, mental and spiritual reading on this body, with suggestions for the improvement or correction of any or all of these.  You will also answer any questions I might ask you relative to this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now we find there are many changes in the physical conditions in body since we had this before.  Also in the mental attributes to the physical.

4. The delivery of the body from the body has taken place, as has been given, and the conditions that surround this condition were not all satisfactory, though in the physical conditions at present the body is in very good conditions, considering those through which the body has passed.

5. The lacerations as produced in and about the pubes and puba show the inability of those attending under the existing conditions.

6. The condition in the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] at present shows the lack of the diet, and of the inflammation as produced in the system through the strain the body endured.  With the conditions, necessary that care be given the child through the conditions of the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].

7. In the general condition in physical, only necessary that the body have care and attention.  The care needs be with the eliminations, especially, for the system, especially that of the lower portion of the hypogastrics, and the system needs have that care that we have no undue toxins return in system to cause disturbance, and be careful of getting on feet too soon.

8. In the spiritual, we find the physical and mental so bring the conditions that at the present time there is little to be given for the perfect in mental, [[Soul|soul]] or spiritual environment.  Beware of allowing the mental to be taxed in the manner as to bring contention or disagreements about any conditions arising in or about the body, for this would bring in the physical the reaction through the assimilation in [[Physical Body|physical body]], bringing detrimental conditions for self and those depending upon same.

9. Do that.

10. (Q)  It was given in a reading on this body on June 13, 1924, that her child would be a girl.  On December 21, 1924, at 2:30 P.M. the child was born a boy.  You will give us the reason for this mistake in the reading.  [See child's readings 187-1 and 187-2.]
(A)  It was not the mistake in the reading, for it was given the child will NOT be a girl.  [On waking, EC shook his head at this answer, considering that it was not an answer at all.]  [See 281 series on [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] later, in re how a sex change often takes place.]

11. (Q)  What should the diet be for this body?
(A)  Those of broths that give strength and vitality, especially to the rebuilding forces in blood supply.  Let the vegetable forces be of fruits, with not too much acid, and the form of vegetable as of celery and such nature; celery, lettuce, tomatos, and such.  Strong beef broth, chicken broth, or wild game.  No pork.  Crisp bacon in small quantities may be given at times.  Plenty of eggs, and little stimulants.

12. (Q)  Should any special treatment be given the body at this time?
(A)  The massage will be well for the body at all times, to produce perfect assimilation; especially in the lower limbs, and gently about the locomotaries, so we do not disturb the conditions in the centers where lesions are, yet we want to give circulation of such a nature as to prevent lesions that would cause trouble in after time.

13. (Q)  Should any special treatment be given for the lacerations, as given, in the body?
(A)  None other than being accorded at present.

14. That is all of the questions.

15. We are through for the present.
