# TEXT OF READING 1112-4 M 62

**Reading [[1112-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/13/1936  Sex: M  Age: 62  ReadingID: 6360

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of June, 1936, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [1010], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1112]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are in the present acute.  There arising from the toxic forces that have been indicated in the system the effects from cold, phlegm, congestion and the slowing of the internal circulation, these have produced that formation through the colon particularly of mucus that produces pain or griping; and inactivity and at other times overactivity through the alimentary canal, upsetting the digestion, making for a quick pulsation, tendency towards a little temperature; general achey feeling, cold and hot flushes, and the like.

3. Added to the general manipulative forces for the relaxing of the body, we find it would be well at this time to have high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] - about once and then the next day not quite so much, then about three days later not so much.  But have the water just the temperature of the body; not hot, not cold, with [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt.  To each quart of water put a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and a heaping teaspoonful of salt. This as we find will remove a great deal of the mucus and relieve these pressures through the area.  In the last or rinse water it would be well that [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] be used; a tablespoonful to half a gallon of water.  This should be preferably a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] or a Fountain, so that there is not too great a pressure.

4. Take Vinol as it is put up, but take only a teaspoonful twice a day.  And once each day, preferably the evening dose, take six drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin in same.  This will act upon the kidneys, the liver, and upon the general circulation, cleansing the blood supply, and with the general manipulation as should accompany same should bring about better strength.

5. Be mindful that in the diet fat meats are not taken.  Fowl, fish or lamb, and the greater foods should be vegetables. Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Wife - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
