# TEXT OF READING 140-19 F 25 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[140-19]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/19/1927  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 555

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 19th day of December, 1927, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [140] - this we have had before.

2. Again we find there is presented - through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of self - lessons or [[Visions|visions]] of conditions as respecting others, and the manner in which others handle situations, that would be to self untenable in the present state of mind or attitude towards action of one individual toward another.  And though self sees conditions as of one stabbing another in back, through the action, and that these conditions appear as experiences or realities rather than as [[Dreams|dreams]], yet - would [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] study those things, those truths as have been and are being given in this - there is seen that the thought, the intent, the purport as is builded in self is as one stabbing own self in the back; and that while the truths are unattractive to self, yet the manner in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] seen is handling the one who has committed a physical offense, through this condition, through this very act, there is being builded - as has been given - that condition in which the body, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] seen, is BUILDING for the one that has brought offense, rather than CONDEMNING same and building destructive forces within the heart and [[Soul|soul]] of others.

3. Then look to thine own self, in the manner and way that self acts towards those who would bring to self the better things of life - rather than condemning in them those things that are as truths; for the life is to be lived, and the life IS action in the justice to self and mercy to those who would err.

4. We are through with this reading.
