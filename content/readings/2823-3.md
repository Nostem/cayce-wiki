# TEXT OF READING 2823-3 F 34 (Stenographer)

**Reading [[2823-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1944  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 10018

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2823], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2823], Mrs. [2390], Mrs. [2175], Mrs. [3257], and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the life existence in the earth plane of [2823], born December 10, 1909, in [[California|Los Angeles]], Calif., and the earthly existence of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] as It-Ao in [[Egypt|Egypt]].  You will give a biographical life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that day and plane of earthly existence, from entrance - and how - into the earth's plane, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s departure; giving the developing or retarding points in such an existence.  You will answer the questions the entity submits, as I ask them, concerning the problems of the present life and the associations arising from that period.

2. EC:  Yes, we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] again here of that entity now called [2823].

3. In interpreting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of the entity through the experiences in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] [[Incarnation|sojourn]], many varied things and conditions are to be taken into consideration - if such an experience is to be made helpful or applicable in the present.

4. For each entity in the earth is what it is because of what it has been!  And each moment is dependent upon another moment. So a [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth, as indicated, is as a lesson in the school of life and experience.  Just as it may be illustrated in that each entity, each [[Soul|soul]]-entity, is as a corpuscle in the body of God - if such an entity has applied itself in such a manner as to be a helpful force and not a rebellious force.

5. There have been other experiences of the entity, yet this was among those that are the more far-reaching in earth expressions in the present, causing the closer associations that would be a natural consequence.  For like begets like. Like attracts like - or the natural needs for such contacts that may give the entity the opportunity, the privilege of applying such truths or lessons in given experiences in a material [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

6. Something of the activities would be necessary to be given, as to the background of the entity's experiences in that particular period.

7. As indicated, there were those particular periods when there were the political and religious activities in the land (that is, during the entity's sojourn there), when there was the greater expansion.

8. The entity came into the earth, though, during those periods of turmoil, when there has been the banishment of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] and when there had been those activities that had caused political and religious turmoils in the land.

9. But with the return of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]], with the setting up of those various activities indicated through [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice and [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful, the entity was among those chosen because of its abilities for the close associations with the various groups, to pass through those experiences not only for preparation but for the training of others in such activities.

10. The entity was among those groups who had come with the King, and was not of the Natives; though in its developments the entity was known as a Native because of its close associations with some of those through that experience.

11. Then the entity's activities were those that it may, or must, learn again - or as it has already experienced, as then - that of patience.

12. Many of the individuals who were associated with [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] in the various offices, however, were tempted or taught - as it were - by the misinterpretation of [[Law of Moses|the law]] by the Priest himself.  For there was the attempt to placate [[Law of Moses|the law]] of evolution.  While those activities brought within themselves conditions that greatly improved the situations, they left in the mental self the applying of spiritual law to attain material desires - without the mental concepts being in accord.

13. A good lesson here for everyone to learn who would know the way of the Lord!  Be patient, be just, be kind, be longsuffering, show brotherly love - and then don't worry about what's going to happen!  but be sure you do these! When you get to the place where you would worry (this is for the entity), stop and pray!  For why worry, when you can pray?  For God is not mocked, and He remembers thee in thy sincerity in thy [[Purpose|purpose]].

14. The entity then gained throughout those periods, both in the activities in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice - when there was the elimination of those associations and experiences as to conditions in body - and in the Temple Beautiful.

15. Those physical conditions find themselves expressed in the material body-conditions in the present at times, yet in nature - in nature, as has been indicated for the body - there may be attained in the physical being the purifying, rather than by the fires of nature or of created elements by man.

16. The entity's application in the experience had to do with [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] pertaining to ways and means, or factors towards the preparing of individuals for their activities in the earth - those who were to be set in various channels of activity.  These were a part of the entity's experience through those activities in the Temple Beautiful.

17. Hence as a datastician, statistician, as a teacher, as a practitioner in those things pertaining to detail as well as principles, the entity may find its greater application of those experiences attained through that sojourn in the earth.

18. The entity grew in power, in might, in body, in mind, in [[Purpose|purpose]] through the experience.  And in the application of self in those directions in the present the entity may make attainment of influences that may draw others to, as well as be an assurance to self of, the greater interpretations of the universal laws - which is love.

19. Then, keep those tenets there applied.  Apply them in the daily experience at present.

20. And in activities do assist in those records that may be a helpful influence to those who would seek to know how body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] may be accorded the influences to meet those very things that the Priest would have the entity pass over [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

21. For body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] are one, even as Father, Son and [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] are one - and in the same ratio and relationship.

22. These, then, are the basic principles that the entity may attain in presenting ways, manners and means in which others, too, may learn their relationships to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] as daily manifested in life itself.

23. Ready for questions.

24. (Q)  How can I increase my awareness of the universal God Force, to enable me to become more of a blessing to my associates?
(A)  As just indicated.  In that attuning of body, mind and soul to those onenesses of [[Purpose|purpose]], as they each give expression of those influences manifested in the Godhead.

25. (Q)  Please give instructions on [[Meditation|meditation]].

(A)  For this body - not for everybody - odors would have much to do with the ability of the entity to [[Meditation|meditate]].  For, the entity in the experiences through the Temple of Sacrifice became greatly attuned through the sense of smell, for the activities were upon the olfactory nerves and muscles of the body itself.  For there the protuberances were taken away.

As to the manner of [[Meditation|meditation]], then:  Begin with that which is oriental in its nature - oriental incense.  Let the mind become, as it were, attuned to such by the humming, producing those sounds of o-o-o-ah-ah-umm-o-o-o; not as to become monotonous, but "feel" the essence of the incense through the body-forces in its motion of body.  This will open the [[Kundalini|kundalini forces]] of the body.  Then direct same to be a blessing to others.  These arise from the creative center of the body itself, and as they go through the various centers direct same; else they may become greater disturbing than helpful.  Surround self ever with that purpose, "Not my will, O God, but Thine be done, ever," and the entity will gain [[Visions|vision]], perception and - most of all - judgment."

26. (Q)  Should I change my [[Book of Job|job]]?
(A)  Whenever there are those experiences wherein the entity can become more closely associated with the records as in [[Egypt|Egypt]], get to them - commence!

27. (Q)  Should I connect with [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment, Inc?
(A)  If that's the channel that answers to the entity, good beginning!

28. (Q)  With what church would it be well for me to seek affiliation at this time?
(A)  The better is the church within self, not by name or place.  For the church is the living Christ.  Make association first with that, and whether it is in this, that or the other name, Christ ye serve and not a church!

29. (Q)  What have been the past relationships, and for what purpose was I brought into present contact with the following:  [2390]?
(A)  Closely associated in Egypt, as helpers in those purposes carried on by the entity now called [2390].

30. (Q)  [2175]?
(A)  Close in association in activities of both Temples in Egypt.

31. (Q)  T. J. O'Connor?
(A)  Knowledge of, but differences arose, in the Egyptian experience.

32. (Q)  [3257]?
(A)  These associations can be found better by paralleling, for they were more than once.  Parallel the periods when their activities crossed one another.

33. (Q)  My son, [2869]?
(A)  That has been indicated, regarding the experiences in Egypt when the relationships were close to the the same as in the present.

34. (Q)  My husband, [5163]?
(A)  Not in Egypt, but a knowledge of one another in Egypt - as they were in different experiences during that period. Closely associated in other sojourns.  Ye may become a complement one to the other, or you can make good scrappers!

35. (Q)  What attitude will be most helpful in directing my son toward right thinking?
(A)  What has been indicated?  Judgment, patience, love, gentleness, kindness.  These are [[Law of Moses|the law]] of love.  These may carry weight where other things, yea where everything else fails.

36. (Q)  What counsel can be given to improve or correct my attitude about my home and my work environment?
(A)  Look within self and see those differences as indicated. Correct them in mind and ye will correct them in purpose and in reality of physical being.

37. (Q)  Any other advice or guidance?
(A)  Know in Whom ye believe and in what ye believe.  Know the author of thy every activity, as to its purpose and [[Ideal|ideal]].  For the law of the Lord is perfect, it coverteth the soul.  And the soul is of [[First Cause|the First Cause]].

38. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
