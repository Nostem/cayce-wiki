# TEXT OF READING 2237-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2237-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1921 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/21/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8962

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 21st day of June, 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  We have the body here.  Now you see the condition we find in this body here, that they are abnormal and have to do with the illuminating powers of various parts of the system, having to do with the nervous system in their connection with the blood supply.  Some of the organs affected are sympathetic with the nerve force.

2. The condition we find is this:  We find the blood force good, if not forced by improper circulation, or if not forced through the wrong channels.  The hemoglobin, the red and white blood are balanced.  The flow of blood is not at all times abnormal yet at times it is.  The nervous condition between the cerebral [cerebro] spinal and nervous system in second, third and fourth cervical is impaired.  Condition existing in body when a child brought about a condition in throat which attacked organs in throat and head and trying to throw off these become taut.  They became impaired by lesion in head which caused imperfect circulation.  The drum of the ear became impaired, also the eyes were affected.

3. Remove condition, relieve strain on this portion of the system by treating through mouth through [words missing], also first, second, third and fourth cervical, the relaxation of system through [words missing] and taking away by absorption through the blood, by heat, manipulation and [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] force.  Will take four to five months to restore the hearing.  Treatments, should be given every other day. Every four days hot applications, every third day antrum and thyroids and tonsils.  You can use any hot applications that are practical.

4. Do as we say here and this body will be entirely relieved.
