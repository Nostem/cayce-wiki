# TEXT OF READING 3810-6 M 61

**Reading [[3810-6]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/4/1924  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 11389

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of June, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [3810].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M.                                    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. LS:  Do the planets have an effect upon the physical conditions of this body at the present time?  What should be done to overcome the effect of the planets upon the physical condition, if any?

2. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in this body somewhat different from those when we have had them before.  The physical conditions of the body, as a whole, are very good, and better than when we had them before; yet the specific conditions in the nerves leading to the eye proper, and the condition of the nerve and its action as is caused in the expression of manifestation of [[Visions|vision]], in one is better, in the other not so good.  Still we have the lack of the circulation being equalized so as to replenish the needed forces as have been set by the conditions in the body.  We need more of the [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to direct the used forces to be eliminated from the system, and for the system to give more of the blood supply to the body, especially to the optic nerve proper, as we have at present the scar, and circulation, and encircling condition around the optic nerve has gradually cut off the supply of nutriment to this as to prevent the proper circulation, and causing scars and atrophied condition of the nerve proper.

3. With the properties as have been taken in system, with the applications as have been given to the body, with those of all conditions we have yet lacked the sufficient to create the equilibrium between the circulation and to overcome the condition, though equalization has been in part; yet not over balanced sufficient to supply the needs of demands of the body to bring the perfect [[Visions|vision]] again to the body.

4. With the adjustments taken of the cervical regions, taken chiropractically, we would apply the ray ([[Violet Ray|violet ray]]), with the eye applicator, direct for two minutes.

5. Do this each evening.  The secretions as will be produced by this condition, remove by the application of a solution of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] antiseptic, that there may be no choking of the ducts that lead to and from the eliminations in the body.

This we will find will assist the body and gain strength, especially for the eye that has not as yet become so involved in the condition.

6. As to the effect of the [[Planetary Influences|planetary]] conditions, these we see have the effect in times back, some three years ago, and the condition at present is the outgrowth of that condition rather than of the present making.  Hence these may be and might have been overcome by direction of will as applied to the attributes of the individual I AM, as in this, as in all individuals, the application of that portion changes or directs the relativity of all forces as directed to the individual.  [See 3810-6, Par. R1.]
