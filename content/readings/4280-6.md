# TEXT OF READING 4280-6 F 38

**Reading [[4280-6]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/9/1926  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 12057

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [4280]. Involuntary.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ga.

1. EC:  Now, for those conditions as surround the [[4280] family] in their position, well that the advantage be taken of the opportunities offered in [[Florida|Jacksonville]], for this will open the vista of the conditions in the life that will prove beneficial to all.

2. As for the health of the younger [4281], this should not be changed until the fall, for this would prove detrimental, were a change to be made at the PRESENT in those treatments being accorded to same.

3. While the rubs are being applied the body, the feet be kept dry - not wade in water.

4. While the adjustments are being made, not too much use of the tricycle, for this tends to undo that being accorded in the dorsal and lumbar region.  Some exercise of this nature well and good, but not too severe.

5. The water - well that that other than is supplied through the regular channels be used.

6. We are through for the present.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/3/26 Letter:  "...Mr. [4283] and I have a few direct
> questions which we are very anxious to have you tell us
> about.  It is this way, and before we make a move I want
> your advice.
> 
> "Mr. [4283] has a fine opening offered him in [[Florida|Jacksonville]],
> [[Florida|Florida]], and I want you to see if it would be best for all of
> us to live there, the year 'round, winter and summer.  Would
> it be well for [4281] to live in the climate of [[Florida|Jacksonville]],
> Florida, the year 'round?  Would it be wise to take her
> there now, or wait until September or October?  Or should
> she remain in ..., Georgia?  So many people are afraid of
> contracting malaria in Florida in the summer months.  Would
> there be any danger there of malaria for Mr. [4283] in
> summer, or for me [4280]?  Would distilled water be as
> beneficial for little [4281] as the boiled water?  (The water
> supply for drinking in Jacksonville isn't very good.)  You
> can secure White Sulphur Springs water there.  Please see if
> it would benefit her in any way.  Should [4281] be allowed to
> wade in the water while we are using the Coco-Quinine drugs?
> And should she go barefooted while using the drugs?  [4281]
> has a tricycle, and Dr. Layne feels as if it isn't very good
> for her to ride it now, on account of the lesions in her
> spine.  She gets so much pleasure out of riding it.  Please
> see if it is in any way injurious for her to ride the
> tricycle!
> 
> "[4281] is improving now under Dr. Layne's treatment, hence
> all these questions about moving her!
> 
> "We are going to sell our place in ... very soon, and I hope
> to be able at that time to send you an offering.
> 
> "We are hoping that the mild winters in Florida would benefit
> our baby [4281].
> 
> "Please let me hear from you by the 10th or 12th of June, as
> Mr. [4283] will have to be there not later than the 14th..."
> 
> B2. 6/9/26 EC gave the sixth reading - 4280-6; which was
> volunteered following a first physical for Mrs. [4620] in
> Illinois, without a suggestion being given to obtain it.
> 
> B3. GD's note:  This reading - 4280-6 - is noted in 4281-13
> report and 4281-13 Background, since most of it pertains
> to the child [4281]'s condition.
