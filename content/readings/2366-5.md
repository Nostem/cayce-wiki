# TEXT OF READING 2366-5 M 46

**Reading [[2366-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1941  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 9163

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2366], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:40 to 12:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, while apparently there are conditions that might cause some anxiety, we find that very soon there should be much improved conditions if there will be the application of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] [[Massage Therapy|massage]] such as to set up drainages nominally from the system to the alimentary canal.

4. To be sure, if there is such stimulation that the disturbance is picked up by the circulation, this may become more aggravated.

5. Yet, as we have indicated, this method is the manner in which better conditions can be brought for the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What has caused the difficulties in walking?
(A)  Stirring up the conditions that cause pressures upon locomotories.

8. (Q)  Is this due to [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  Due to [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, as just indicated. If there will be given the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] so as to set up better drainages, and NOT stimulate circulation to head, it will be beneficial.

9. (Q)  How should these massages be given?
(A)  As just indicated, - one who understands osteopathy should understand how to set up drainage and NOT stimulation.

10. (Q)  Any other advice for patient, considering all the circumstances?
(A)  Carry on with these; being patient, being persistent, as has been indicated.  Undertake the exercises each day. And we will find that these will work to the advantage and to the betterment of the body.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self "   " Ass'n file
