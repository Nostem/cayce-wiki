# TEXT OF READING 137-36 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-36]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/3/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 426

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 3rd day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind here.  This we have had before, you see.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] and [[Visions|visions]] as come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] from time to time are as those interpretations as may be gained through the forces of the psychic developments of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and the phenomenized conditions as are presented in the physical plane of same are the truths in the way of experiences to the mental forces of the body-mind, when taken as such.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Wednesday night, December 2, or Thursday morning, December 3, 1925.  "Saw the number 144.  Seemed to refer to P C G."
(A)  This, as we see, correlated with that information as already given regarding this special or specific stock in its action on the market.  Then, when this reaches the price 44 - that is 144, see?  close the contracts, see?  and this we find will occur in 9 days - 4 - 8 - 9 - see?  Then the selling off and the up again in the 15th - 18 - see?

4. (Q)  Does this mean that we should sell at this price, instead of 156, as has been given, or does it mean that there is another indication that the stock is going up."
(A)  The indication the stock is going up, see?  and sell at, or close contracts, see, at 144 - breaking off two to three points from same, see? - three - five - four - THREE points from same, then going to 56 - 58, see?

5. (Q)  "Saw National Dairy Products.  It was strong and active."
(A)  This, as we see, when correlated with that as has already been seen, in the buying of stocks in this commodity from Herbert Son, will see that the stronger indications are for the advance in this stock, see?  While, as is seen in the buying of stock from Herbert Son, this indicates the stability of the commodity, and the advanced price as will be seen in same - advancing four - six - TEN POINTS - then SELL, see?  from this price as is bought at, at the present time, or that being held for long, see?  which is above the price at the present.

6. (Q)  Correlating this with other dreams on this stock, what should be done with the stock that is long?
(A)  Hold for the higher price, as given, see?

7. (Q)  "While shaving I cut my face and a rash broke out."
(A)  The showing to the body of the necessity of that in the blood to prevent the auto-inoculation of self through the condition of blood.  Apply, then, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have been given for same, see, with the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] carrying the iron, see?

8. (Q)  Night of November 24, or morning of November 25, 1925. "A dream relative to morals."
(A)  This rather the after effect of physical conditions as existed, and the repulsion of same in the body-physical presented the condition in the mental forces through [[Visions|vision]]. Rather to be studied personally than as a lesson, other than gained by self.

9. (Q)  What significance has this to me?
(A)  As given.

10. (Q)  "I seemed to be buying 500 shares of National Dairy Products from Herbert Son at the price of $87.  It seemed to be $87, but there is a possibility that it may h ave been $77, although $87 remains as the stronger impression."
(A)  This, as we see, has been correlated with those conditions as have been given in same.  The 10 - the rise (that is the 10 points) above that at which the stock, National Dairy Products, is held, see?  The stability as is seen, as given, through that same force.

11. (Q)  Was the price $87 or $77?
(A)  $87.

12. (Q)  Night of November 25, or morning of November 26.  "A headless man in uniform of a sailor was walking in an erect manner with either a gun or a cane in his hand."
(A)  Seeing things.  This, as we see, the developing of those forces in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] wherein the physical projection of spiritual elements come to the entity in certain stages of the condition of the body, physically, mentally, see?  and as this is seen, one in arms or one in uniform, or one in the action of defense or in duty, see - this, then, to the entity the lesson:  Do not lose the head too much in duty as seen, to accomplish the greater lessons as may be learned from [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of ideas as pertain to things more spiritual.

13. (Q)  "A man approached many, including myself, in what seemed to be a hotel lobby.  When he first approached us he had the appearance of a detective, but as he drew near I had the feeling that he was [[Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]."
(A)  In this there is seen, in the correlation of these conditions as passing through that in groups, in the individual, in the mass even, there, in the form of man, as man to man, may come to the entity these spirit forces as would guide and mean as much to the individual as the life, as the living, as the example, as the ensample of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]'s coming means to the whole world; these forces as come in man may mean as much to this entity, when applied spiritually.

14. (Q)  "There was something I felt related to the [[Visions|vision]] I previously had had regarding an arrow shot from a bow, and which a reading interpreted was an indication of a message to come to me of powerful intent and [[Purpose|purpose]], but just what that something was in this particular vision I cannot now remember.  Recall the vision to me, interpret and give me the lesson.
(A)  As is seen first in the vision of the arrow shot into the air, going high and strong, and as the interpretation, the lesson, was that to the entity there would be presented those visions, or those truths coming as the greatest truth, or greatest [[Purpose|purpose]] in the life, these as we see here are presented in these two as seen, headless soldier with cane or defense weapon, [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walking into the group.

15. We are through for the present
