# TEXT OF READING 4561-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4561-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/19/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12448

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of June, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4561].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:35 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions that may be corrected.  We have abnormal conditions that to this body have become normal conditions.  The conditions that may be corrected have to do with the digestive tract and the eliminations as are affected by the disturbances created in digestion, and the conditions have to do with the nerve system and produce distresses in many portions of the body reflexly.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body (He doesn't hear very well, you see).  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find there is carried at the present time some of the toxins as are carried in the blood by the conditions being produced in the system of toxation and this toxin affects the system in many portions of the body; the extremities, the joints, the portions of the system where congestion set in, either from drafts or cold, or produced by pressure in the lung forces and in the pulmonaries of the lungs proper and produces pressure at times, with spasmodic conditions with same.

4. In the division of bloods, we find the white blood above normal, the red blood below normal.  System attempting to create an equilibrium through quick coagulation, yet this in itself affects the eliminating in emunctories.  Then, we find the liver becomes clogged, kidneys secrete improperly and vice versa, for when one pole in the system and the liver and kidneys, or poles in hepatic circulation, when one becomes overcharged it is at the expense of the functioning in system of the opposite pole.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find these conditions affect many of the plexuses of the nerve proper, and in the ends of nerves in tissue we find this affects the respiratory system of the condition that has to do with eliminations through the capillary circulation.  In the plexuses of portions of the system, noticeably, do we find the plexus that are involved; those of the second lumbar, sixth, seventh dorsal, first, second cervical.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, with the change in conditions from normal in times back we had an increased circulation, with a mishap, as it were, in the system (accident) that produced in the auditory forces the prevention of an equilibrium in used and of distribution of forces to produce the physical action in auditory forces. This brought about the condition in the ear, the drumming at times past or at some times we have the condition of this forms into the nature that gives off secretions from same.

7. Throat, lungs, bronchials show the effect of conditions in blood supply, and with congestion of same at times gives some trouble, as is reached also through spasmodic conditions in lungs proper.

8. In the lower end of the stomach do we find the seat of these troubles as are distributed in the system through circulation, through sympathetic system, through the action of mental over the physical in body.  Here we find in the pyloric end the distress signs are given in the system. Both in the lower portion or reached through hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus and their branches both in blood and in nerve supply to the system.

9. The organs of digestion are involved at times, though not often of the specific nature, as was shown in the system in times back.

10. Hepatic circulation high.

11. Kidneys show excess of secretions.  Trouble experienced at times in bladder.

12. Liver engorged.  (Present conditions we are giving)

13. R E L I E F, then to reach the seat of the trouble and to bring the equilibrium to the body, we would first cleanse the body by drinking very large quantities of water.  Drink a very great deal until the system is flushed and the kidneys react or respond to the condition.  Then prepare and take into the system these properties:

14. To one gallon of rain water, add:

Garden Sage, dried.............6 ounces.

Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain and add 4 ounces of Beet Sugar dissolved in 1 ounce of very hot water; 15 grains of Ambergris dissolved in 1 ounce of grain alcohol.

15. Then add also 4 ounces of grain alcohol with 6 drams of Juniper Berry Juice, with 10 minims Oil of Peppermint, and 1/2 dram Cinnamon in stick form or Cinnamon Bark.

16. The dose of this will be (shake well before taking.  That is, the medicinal properties) 2 teaspoonsful half an hour before each meal.  Take the whole quantity.

17. After this has been taken, the exercises then for the whole body, and each and every organ exercised by a consistent and persistent exercise.  Of mornings for the upper portion of the body above the trunk.  Of evenings for the lower portion of body.

18. Take these for at least five minutes morning and evening, to create the equilibrium necessary in distribution of circulation, [4561], ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].
