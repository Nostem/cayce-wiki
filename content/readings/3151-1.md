# TEXT OF READING 3151-1 F 23

**Reading [[3151-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/11/1943  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 10520

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the Mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] LaClair.)]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3151].

3. As we find, here is a [[Karma|karmic]] condition.  Not that this may not be met in a great measure in the present experience, but first, this body, this mind, this [[Soul|soul]] must learn patience with self and with others.  The condition is the lack of supplying to the body-forces the abilities for the assimilating in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the central nervous system that energy sufficient to carry on the proper amount of energies needed in the nerve plasm for control of the locomotory nerves, tendons and plexus.  Thus the inability of locomotion.  These we would do, then, first:

4. Find within the self, relationship to [[Creative Forces|creative forces]], - call it God, call it hope, call it the Savior, call it the self, that which is eternal, though.  Seek and know thy relationship to THAT.

5. Then begin with the use of those influences that are in the earth, where those forces have been misused in other experiences, now must be paid back.  That which became the wastrel in the use of self-indulgence must be the application in the present to build those influences which are lacking in the elements in the chemical forces of the body itself.

6. Thus we will begin with the use of the low electrical Appliance carrying Gold.  Begin with the regular strength charge.  The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] should be in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water.  Use at least five ounces.  Hence, the 15 grain ampule would make at least three charges of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] for the Appliance.  Change the Gold Solution every fifteen days. Recharge the Appliance every thirty days.

7. The attachment should be made to the body as follows:

8. The large plate through which the Gold Solution passes vibratorially to the body should be attached at the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus.  This on this body will be four fingers from the navel center to the right and two fingers up from that point.  The small copper plate would be attached to the lower part of the ninth dorsal plexus.  Let these attachments be made for one hour each day.

9. Immediately after removing the Appliance, give the body a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine, especially from the area between the shoulders downward, or down to the lumbar area.  Then begin at the feet, massaging the soles of the feet and the bursa of the feet, going upward to the lumbar area.  Use this combination of oils for the [[Massage Therapy|massage]]:

Nujol, as the base.................6 ounces,
Peanut Oil.........................2 ounces,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root...................1/2 ounce,
Oil of Pine Needles................1 ounce.

10. These will tend to separate, but shake them together, pouring a small quantity in a saucer, dipping tips of fingers in same and massaging, not rubbing with the hand or the palm of the hand, but with the fingertips, along each vertebra, along each portion of the nerves at the head of the long bone of the limbs, under the knee, the calf of the leg, the ankle, the heel, the sole of the - foot; though as indicated we would begin at the feet and go upward - when massaging the lower part of the body.

11. Do keep the eliminations well.

12. Include often in the diet seafoods and plenty of watercress, celery, and lettuce, and occasionally carrots grated with celery and lettuce.  Beets cooked, but grated with the celery and lettuce.

13. Do these and at the end of six months we will give further instructions.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Is this multiple sclerosis as the doctors say?
(A)  Just as indicated.  Do as given and in six months we would give further instructions for this body.  Begin at the beginning.  Until there is found within self the hope, don't begin with the mechanical applications.

16. We are through with the reading.

Copy to Mother

"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
