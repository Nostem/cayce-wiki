# TEXT OF READING 2221-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2221-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/24/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8938

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 24th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by her sister.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of the body.

2. EC:  Now we find there are many abnormal conditions for this body.  Some have become normal that are abnormal to other bodies.  Some conditions may be corrected that have to do with the functioning of the system as is hindered by some existing conditions in the body.

3. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [2221] we are speaking of.

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find an aenemitized condition.  Not whole anemia, yet from the conditions as produced in the system, especially through the mesenteric [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and the effect as this produces upon the hypogastric plexus and the pneumogastric nerve supply, these affect the circulation to the extent as to produce this incentive towards anemia, as well as the lack of supply from the system being deflected by structural conditions in the system, and this produces much of the condition producing pressure in the circulation, in nerve supply, which directly affects tissue and muscular forces in the body, as is shown in extremities.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find there are lesions along the spine, produced by [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the system by congestion and temperature as was produced in the body in times back.  Also with the pressure as caused, we find distress, dis-ease, brought to structural portions in body.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find many under the strain of these conditions, especially as those that affected through the trunk portion of the body, and the suppression to the functioning of the organs in the trunk portion of the body, prevent the proper circulation, the proper distribution, the proper assimilation, throughout the body.

7. These may all be assisted.

8. We would find this would take the characteristics of the deep massage over the whole system, especially in cerebro-spinal centers, beginning from the 2nd dorsal to the end of the spine, using those heat applications that assist in relaxing the muscular tissue, that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the massage may bring circulation, and at the same time relaxing those blood producing organ centers in such a way and manner through same treatments, that the body will build in this portion; taking care that the system is supplied with those properties in food value that gives the rebuilding to blood through the system.  Let those carry, especially, properties of iron and the food values that dilate the system in its course through the system.  Fruits, vegetables.  Little meats, save that of mutton, or of goat meat.  These carry the necessary properties.  This should all be roasted, not with much fat. Lean portions, carrying the juices of the meats proper.

9. In the [[Vibration|vibration]] as would be accorded, with heat applied, using those properties of bathing the body - saturated solution of equal parts of [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] and Salt, saturated solution; then applying the electrical radiated heat to the body to drive this into the system.

10. Then treat the body in the places as given.  Follow these. We will bring the better conditions to this body.

11. We are through for the present.
