# TEXT OF READING 3487-1 M 67

**Reading [[3487-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/4/1944  Sex: M  Age: 67  ReadingID: 10969

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3487], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - he's asleep!  We have the body here, [3487].

3. Many of the conditions are to be considered in giving that which will be helpful for the body.

4. If there would be the real help brought, there must be some effort on the part of the body to control self.  It is very well that the body has been able to control self to have its own way, but it should control itself to cooperate with others.  There's a vast difference in the activity of the mental self "to have my own way" about a thing and to have that which may be cooperative with others.

5. And this should be the basis, first.

6. Then there should be the effort on the part of the body through the following of a teacher, or teachings that would indicte a great lesson in cooperation, and in an individual entity or [[Soul|soul]] giving that which is of itself constructive in the environs and in cooperating with others.

7. Because you can't always have your own way, no use to set up an atittude!  And this not only applies to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] but it might be universally given to man!  For [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord is perfect and converts [[Soul|the soul]], and that's the part to work with - in the earth and among thy brethren.  Remember, the Lord gave [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] "When thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren."

8. In the physical conditions, yes, there are definite disturbances - as indicated in throat and in the nerves and muscles of the head and neck.  And these are partially the result of injury to the jawbone from the extraction of a tooth.  And this has been used by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as a means, that has builded much of having its own way.

9. These should be corrected if there will be applied an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] for the throat and around the jaw, even down to the area of the thorax and the throat. Do this daily.  When the body is ready to retire, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] this mixture into the areas, all that will be absorbed; then sponge off and let the body rest.  Keep this up.

10. Then change the attitude, and we will have much better conditions for this body of [3487].

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What is the general condition of his health?
(A)  Change the mental attitude and the general health also will be much improved.

Keep eliminations well and we will keep the body in attune and in accord with that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], as an entity, [[Purpose|purposes]] to do.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
