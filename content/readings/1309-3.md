# TEXT OF READING 1309-3 F 56

**Reading [[1309-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/4/1940  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 6950

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1309], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is [[Grape Juice|Welch's Grape Juice]] preferable to Premier unsweetened?  I like Premier much the best.
(A)  If that is the more desirable, use same.  The Welch's, however, has more of the elements in same that aid in the reduction of the carbohydrates in the system, - and thus tends to supply the food values in a way that is in keeping with that which has been indicated as the [[Purpose|purpose]] for taking same.

But if the other is more desirable, and the appetite is such that the Premier is desired, then use same.

5. (Q)  Is Vitamin B and also E good for deafness?  If so, should I take one or both?
(A)  Vitamin B is not in the direction of a helpfulness. E is.  Take E.

6. (Q)  Should I continue to take head and neck exercise after treatments stop?
(A)  By all means!  For, this will tend to keep the circulation, and the improvements that are gradually being noted, in the right direction.

To be sure, with the first - even possibly the second of the finger surgery treatments - there will apparently be caused an upsetting, with more of a roaring, and more disturbing conditions; but gradually this as we find will become more and more normal, and - as it were - the body become more and more used to the changes being wrought.

7. (Q)  Would you advise me to take another series of treatments in late Fall from Dr. Richardson in [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]]?
(A)  This may be best indicated after there has been carried out those so far suggested; and dependent, of course, upon the response to same, see?

8. (Q)  After finishing all treatments given in Reading, would you advise further treatment from Dr. Schoelles?
(A)  This, too, will depend upon the response.  This can be best given in the early Fall, or in September or early October.

9. (Q)  Can you tell me anything about my right eye?  Was ulcer on eye caused by iron rust?  Will condition clear up?
(A)  As we find, this is a form of infection; and it should clear - with a very mild antiseptic, and the general treatments aided by the head and neck exercises - with the general treatments to the body and the head.

10. (Q)  Had I better leave needle work alone for a while?
(A)  Had better leave needle work alone until there are better conditions, and until there is not the causing of pain or watering of the eyes.

11. (Q)  Is it advisable to go to northern Michigan this summer?
(A)  This depends, of course, upon whether it interferes with the general welfare physically and otherwise.  It depends, of course, upon the attitude of the body.  If desired, as we find it would be very well.

12. (Q)  Shall I use the Radio-Active Appliance thru the summer?
(A)  We would use same through the summer.

13. (Q)  Have you any further suggestions for the summer?
(A)  These should be kept in line with the suggestions which have been indicated through the series of applications for general improvement.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
