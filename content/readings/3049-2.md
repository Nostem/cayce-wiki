# TEXT OF READING 3049-2 F 12

**Reading [[3049-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/1/1944  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 10371

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - there are improvements as we find with this body, and there should not be impatience.  For this necessarily must be a "growing out" of disorders in the body, and of incoordinations in the centers that reflect to the auditory forces - yes, to all organs of the sensory system.

3. The inability for activity of the vocal chords, the inability of [[Visions|vision]] at times and of speech, are all a part of that which is gradually being met.

4. We would continue with those Applications that have been suggested.  Be consistent, be persistent.  Most of all be prayerful.  Don't doubt within self, if the applications are made consistently in line with the suggestions indicated.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Have we been following your instructions properly?
(A)  You can answer best within self.  You have not, altogether.  Part of them have been applied fairly well physically; mentally not so good.

7. (Q)  Is she taking the proper [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] medication?
(A)  This is very good, so far as it will act, but most of this - unless there are corrections made so that the organs of assimilation are improved - won't come by absorption, from taking it just as it has been doing.

8. (Q)  How can we get proper lenses for her?
(A)  By fitting them.

9. (Q)  Is her hip out of place?
(A)  No.

10. (Q)  Is there any chronic trouble in her throat?
(A)  No.

11. (Q)  How far is her unclear [[Visions|vision]] affecting her mental processes and coordination?
(A)  This hasn't the effect.  It is the incoordination affecting [[Visions|vision]], as well as the activities of all the organs of the sensory system.  There should be better stimulation in the 1st, 2nd, 3rd dorsal, 3rd cervical, on up to the areas behind the head or in the mastoid area and the bridge and nasal passages.

12. (Q)  Is she overcoming her innate fear?  How can we help her in that regard?
(A)  In part, but this has to be grown out of.  Do as given.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
