# TEXT OF READING 106-3 F 48

**Reading [[106-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/9/1925  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 239

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born October 20, 1876, at Wabash, Indiana. 
Time of Reading 
12:15 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], a reading giving the effects of the solar system on the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present earth plane, naming the planet, or position, from which [[Soul|the soul]] took its last flight.  You will give the vocation in life for which this body is best adapted, or may adapt itself, giving also the high points of the life in former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], giving the name and place of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth plane, with characteristics as brought forward in its personality in the present life.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body as here, with those conditions co-relating with the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] as lived in earth plane, and the effect of the development upon the body, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

3. In this, we find the conditions as regards the entity, without [[Free Will|the will]]'s influence, as HAS been exhibited, or as MIGHT have been exhibited, or as MAY be exhibited in the earth's manifestation yet to come.

4. In the present, we find, then, one that has been considered as an exceptional one in all conditions pertaining, especially, to those of the mental and of the financial, co-related with the ennobling influences in either, for we find the entity took its flight in the present sphere from that of Mars.  Hence the influences as come in the present plane are ever with that resistance, as it were, in the elementals of the entity; elementals meaning attributes of mental, with the ennobling influence of Jupiter and of Mercury, and of Neptune, with adverse forces at times with Venus, and at times good influences from Saturn, and at times very bad.  Hence we find many conditions in the [[Reincarnation|past life]], as shown in the earth's plane, contradictory one with the other; yet were these studied wholly, we find the greater influence is yielded with [[Free Will|the will]], and that the entity has that of the combative nature in the present earth's plane.  One, then, who finds both good and bad in conditions that have to do with the elements of love conditions in an earthly sphere, both in the entity's own life, as lived, and in those about the entity.  Too, we find those adverse, both of the mental and of the spiritual elements in the entity's mental forces; that is, there have been many combative conditions in the conditions surrounding the entity, as regarding conditions of mental forces of individuals closely associated with the entity.  Ever has there been the greater ennobling forces and influences of Jupiter's forces in the present earth's plane. These have been, and may be, made those successful means to combat the conditions arising in any of these elements.

5. In the entity, then, we find one considered by many as being eccentric.

6. One as is given to very strong likes and dislikes.  One that goes far to defend one in whom this entity places much confidence, regardless of what others may say.

7. One that has been of help and counsel to many, pertaining to those conditions in the affairs of others that have to do with mental conditions, love conditions, financial conditions.  One that is given to great scope of [[Visions|vision]], especially in these conditions as described.

8. One that may develop much in the present earth's plane, through [[Free Will|the will]], and the study of past, present and future conditions, as respecting the ennobling influences in mental, and in financial forces, that WILL surround the entity in the present earth's plane.

9. One given to be of many days in present earth's plane, through the enforcing elements in Jupiter and in Mercury, with Mars as the ruling effect of entity's [[Destiny|destiny]].

10. In the conditions, then, as set, irrespective of that, that may be made with the will, in the next return we would find one in these conditions that have to do with one of power, and of rule over many peoples, yet in the same sex as at present.

11. In those conditions as we find before in the earth's plane, we find as this:

12. Before this present, we find in the days when the rule of the peoples were in the hands of the [[France|French]], under the Louis the 14th.  This entity then in the household of an orderly in this Monarch's house, and in the position knowing much of conditions existing in that time, yet over the condition, or knowledge, forced to be kept submerged.

13. In the days before this, we find in the time when the chosen people were in [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]], and this entity then of that people, and in the land where the preparations was made for return of the chosen to the Promise Land, and this entity had much to do with these preparations, and connected with those who rebuilded the City.

14. In the appearance before, in the days of the rule in the land that gave the encamping place for the chosen people, and just before their entrance into this land, this entity saw the preparation for that entrance.

15. In the present, we find these conditions exhibited in the present personality:

16. That of the desire to express self, under any condition.

17. That in the second, the beginning of many conditions, yet all must be well founded.

18. In the next, the ability to express self in a manner that will not be misunderstood, whether correct or incorrect in surmises.

19. Then, the good that may be gained through these:

20. Keep the self well in hand, through the will, making it one with Him, the Giver of all good and perfect Gifts.

21. We are through for the present.
