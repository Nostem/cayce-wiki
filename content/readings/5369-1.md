# TEXT OF READING 5369-1 M 63

**Reading [[5369-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/17/1944  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 13509

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5369], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Coronet article.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, while there is much that's very good about the body:  [5369], there are tendencies of which the body should take warning.  As we find, there has been a slowing up in the activity of the central nerve and blood supply as related to the organs of same, and thus we find inclinations and tendencies which, unless greater precautions are taken, may bring severe conditions to the body.  These affect the heart, liver, and kidneys.  These arise from this incoordination which has existed for some time between sympathetic and cerebrospinal nervous system.  These find expression in a subluxation which exists in the 6th, 7th dorsal and in the lumbar axis, or in the 4th dorsal areas and throughout the axis, in the pelvic centers there.

3. These cause irregularity in the heart's activity.  These also cause upsettings in the digestive system when there is the feeling as of not digesting properly.  A slowing up at times for the eliminations.  Disturbances to the kidneys in which there are those upsetting disorders.

4. As we would find, first we would cleanse the colon.  For with these changes which have been coming about, gradually there is the slowing of the circulation and eliminations from colon.  These we would purify by the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, given professionally and preferably with the combination of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]], also including a good massage. And yet there should be definite corrections of those conditions existent in the dorsal and in the lumbar and sacral but coordinating the cervicals and the rest of the conditions with these corrections which should be made.

5. If ye will do these we will relieve these tensions and make for bettered conditions with this body.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the physiotherapist who is to give the treatments - Please let us have name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, 105 N. Van Ness Ave., [[California|Los Angeles]], has had experience with following the readings.)
