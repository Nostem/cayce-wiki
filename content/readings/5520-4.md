# TEXT OF READING 5520-4 M 21 MONTHS

**Reading [[5520-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/19/1928  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 13772

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of November, 1928, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [4280].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ga. 
(By questions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [5520] - this we have had before.  Conditions existent are better than that as we have had before.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What caused him to have the bloody diarrhea?
(A)  Improper eliminations produced by the discharge in the system of refuse forces in an over or excess quantity, and the system responding, attempting to correct itself; throwing, then, the blood in an extra amount or quantity through this portion of system, causing such inflammation as to produce an excess of the mucus producing portion of intestines and caecum.

3. (Q)  What is best to lubricate his bowels and cause them to move?  Should he take Squibb's Liquid Petrolatum?  What can be added to his diet to make his bowels less firm?
(A)  The Squibb's may be taken.  The better would be to use Usoline in small quantities in conjunction with [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] - pure [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], just what the system will assimilate. Beginning with a few drops once to twice each day.  Every second to third day give half teaspoonful of the Usoline. These will be better than with any of the agar preparations, for with a developing body, those that are nearer in conformity to a developing system are better than that which will add to the system those properties that cause the necessity of a continued use of same, see?  With the diet, also, this may be altered or changed considerable, as will be seen from the other questions you have here.

4. (Q)  What causes him to cry whenever his kidneys want to act, and what will relieve this pain?
(A)  The proper manipulation of those centers and ganglia as control same will be the best stimuli for this condition, rather than that of drug or even of any OTHER; for this needs the proper stimuli, which will be found from the 9th, 10th and 12th dorsal, and the 3rd and 4th lumbar ganglia.

5. (Q)  Is that what makes him cry out in his sleep?
(A)  This rather from the former experiences.

6. (Q)  Will he have to be [[Circumcision|circumcised]] now or later?
(A)  BETTER that it be done at present.

7. (Q)  What change in his diet?  Should he take whole milk with or without cream?  How much water added to 1 quart of milk? Is 1 quart of milk per day too much for him?
(A)  The whole milk - that of the cream and milk MIXED, and then one-fifth of water, will be the better for the body - and the one quart is the proper amount now for the body. Five parts milk, one part of water, see?

8. (Q)  Does he need "Casec" in his milk now?
(A)  Not with the whole milk, as given.

9. (Q)  Should some of the starch be taken off all his cereal?
(A)  No.

10. (Q)  Should he eat brown rice with husk on it, or should it be run through a sieve?
(A)  Run through a sieve.

11. (Q)  How much gruel per day?
(A)  Three to six ounces.

12. (Q)  Should he eat cracked wheat or Wheatena?
(A)  Wheatena.

13. (Q)  Whole wheat bread?
(A)  Whole wheat bread.

14. (Q)  Can he take one Graham cracker per day?
(A)  One, or one and a HALF a day.

15. (Q)  Should he eat mashed beans, peas, potatoes and spinach? or just the broth off of these?
(A)  The broth off of these, and a portion occasionally of the bean, lentil, or spinach.  Spinach excellent for the body.

16. (Q)  Should he take cooked figs or raisins?
(A)  Cooked figs or raisins occasionally are well for the body.  Both carry those properties of the iron, also of the bitumen, that are excellent for the developing of the body. Also will aid in the digestive forces for the system, causing that laxness as is necessary.  Also there may be added that occasionally of the prune.

17. (Q)  What causes him to hiccough so much?
(A)  The development of the body.  A natural inclination with growing conditions in a body.

18. (Q)  Would the climate of ..., Ga., be beneficial?
(A)  Not necessarily so.  We are through with this reading.
