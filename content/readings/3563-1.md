# TEXT OF READING 3563-1 F 42 (Clerk)

**Reading [[3563-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/15/1944  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 11049

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3563], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M.                   In room ..., 
Taken 3:30 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Iowa.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3563].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions which prevent normal reactions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  From impairments in segments in the cerebrospinal system the supplies of activity have been cut off through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system as related to locomotion.  Thus there has become such characterizations in the eliminations that the muscular and sinew forces in locomotion have become crystallized and hardened; causing an arthritic reaction of the inflammatory nature in the beginning, and then a tendency - in the present - towards atrophy.  These cause a great deal of excruciating pain if there is the attempt of locomotion through the body.

4. We find that these conditions may be materially aided.  As to how far the corrections may go will depend upon the consistency and persistence of the body as it makes administrations for correcting the disturbance.

5. Thus the beginning must be in the mental attitude of the body-mind.  There must be kept a creative, constructive attitude, and not the condemning or faultfinding of others - which has been a portion of the [[Karma|karmic]] forces being met in self.

6. In the administrations we would begin with these:

7. Use [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs over the various areas affected, one at a time - one receiving the application one day, another the next day, and so on; the hips, the knees, the feet, the arms and the hands, so that all portions of the afflicted areas receive the administration - including those segments which in the beginning caused the disturbance.  Each day these segments should receive the applications, and also oil massages - from the last lumbar to the 9th dorsal.  Apply here the saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], and afterwards massage with this combination of oils, combining these in the order named:

Olive Oil, as the base.........2 1/2 ounces,
Peanut Oil.........................1 ounce,
Oil of Pine Needles............1 3/4 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied)..............1/2 ounces,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root...................1/4 ounces,
Oil of Mustard.....................5 drops.

8. These should be thoroughly mixed, and even then they will tend to separate.  Each time when ready to use, shake thoroughly and pour a small quantity in an open saucer.  Do not pour back into the bottle any that may not be used, or in which the hands have been put.  Massage those areas over which the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs have been applied.

9. The Packs should be hot, not so as to burn the body but as warm as the body can well stand and let these remain on for thirty-seven minutes.  Immediately after removing the Packs, wipe off the areas and massage the oil in.

10. Also begin to use daily the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] for thirty minutes carrying alternately [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], the Gold one day, the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] the next day.

11. When using the Gold, in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water (and use three ounces), attach the copper plate to the 9th dorsal center - or between the 8th and 9th dorsal center.

12. The next day, when using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], in the proportions of one ounce commercial strength Atomidine in two ounces of distilled water, the copper plate would be attached at the 4th lumbar center.

13. Each day the large nickel plate would be attached to the lacteal and umbilical duct center, or on this body the width of four fingers from the navel center to the right and three fingers up from that point.

14. The diet each day should include some raw vegetables, or one meal each day should consist chiefly of such as celery, lettuce, carrots, onions, mustard greens, and all of those that may be eaten, plenty of water cress especially.  These may be prepared often with gelatin.

15. Do drink plenty of water - six to eight glasses each day.

16. If we will do these, being consistent and persistent, we will have help for this body.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  What will stop sleepless nights?
(A)  These massages and the rest will stop sleepless nights.

19. (Q)  What will reduce occasional severe pain in rectum?
(A)  The correction in the 4th lumbar area will correct this.

20. (Q)  What causes the feeling as if a motor ran in my stomach?
(A)  The applications of the Gold and Atomidine, through the Appliance, will correct these disturbances.

21. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these things as indicated, during these periods making corrections osteopathically of the coccyx end of the spine and in the last of the dorsal centers.

22. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/14/43 [3563]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> ..., Mo.
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Sir:
> 
> Your telegram in which you state you can be of no help at the
> moment was rec'd yesterday with regrets.  When can I hope for
> a reading?
> 
> I bought the book "[[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]]" by [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]] at ...
> here in ... and the clerk who sold it to me informed me that
> your readings were all taken for the next two years.
> 
> The book "A [[Search for God Study Groups|Search for God]]" has been sent for and I hope it
> arrives soon as well as the 25 cent booklet offered by the
> Association with the paper on vocational guidance.
> 
> I am not a young person for I will be 42 next month - 29th.
> I was in the East for 8 years but was forced to return to ...
> last January - seemingly a nervous breakdown.  My mother
> passed on in July leaving me bereft of all affection & love.
> My father and I were never very close and my brother is
> abusive at times.  My brother has threatened to have me
> adjudged incompetent and placed in a state institution.  I
> tried so hard to have a life of my own in the East.
> 
> If you could help me soon I would be so grateful.
> 
> Sincerely, (Miss) [3563]
> 
> B2. 9/25/43 [3563]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> ..., Mo.
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Sir:
> 
> Your letter of the 20th was rec'd yesterday.  Twelve months
> seems such a long time but I have enclosed my application
> with check.
> 
> My address may not be the same a few months from now for I
> don't know what changes may take place with me.
> 
> I believe my nervousness stems from frustration and
> loneliness.  My life seems to have no pattern - just a blank.
> If I could find my right work & place - some place to build
> for the future.  I tried to hold the position I had held for
> six years but there was so much antagonism I seemed to break
> under it.  Miss [3563]
> 
> B3. 1/4/44 [3563]'s letter:  ...I am feeling much better and have
> gained weight but I still continue to have many sleepless
> nights and a feeling at times as if a motor ran in my
> stomach.  These two conditions are very annoying and I would
> certainly like to know how to be rid of them if possible.
> 
> There is also a hurting in the region of the rectum.  Not
> within the last month but it has awakened me at night from a
> sound sleep and would last for a half hour or forty-five
> minutes and then stop and not occur again for several days or
> weeks.
> 
> I want to be a well balanced person, to be healthy both in
> mind and body.  Please help me if you can.
> 
> Sincerely (Miss) [3563]
