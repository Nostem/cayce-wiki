# TEXT OF READING 294-65 M 48

**Reading [[294-65]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/12/1926  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2024

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of EC, and the enquiring mind, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be learned from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, here.

3. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  Friday Morning, 2/5/26.  "[[Dreams|Dream]] regarding having the Institute.  We were preparing for a big dinner.  All the people came in.  [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]] causing [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]] to happen, etc."
(A)  In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that, through the requests to be made by those individuals entering for the banquet, there are to be offered studies to each one seen respecting their physical condition.  This is seen by the words of each as they are seated, at being tired, others lots of fun.

Then it is seen that through the lack or through the over quantity, or through the inconsistency of diet, each of these who requests these READINGS, has this as the cause of the conditions to be met.

As seen in the calling down of conditions that appear as [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]]:  that through those suggestions the greater strength and faith will be created in the minds of all. For, as they are become applicable in the lives of each individual, and as the truths are studied and seen in each of these, and in the various changes as respecting same, there is to be seen many changes, many conditions, that will appear and become as [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]], as the changes come about.

5. We are through.
