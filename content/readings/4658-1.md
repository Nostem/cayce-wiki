# TEXT OF READING 4658-1 F 45

**Reading [[4658-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/7/1927  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 12582

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [...], via Mr. Archie Adlman.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are some changes with the physical conditions of this body from that as we had here before. These are more the effects of the improper coordination in the system even than that as we had here before.  The elements as taken for relief in the body, with the properties that were to produce the proper BUILDING or [[Vibration|vibration]] for the system, do not coordinate in the vitality of the body.  Hence we have one [[Vibration|vibration]] warring, as it were, with another. Either take one and leave the other, or take the one as has been administered and keep in that way as has been produced or brought about in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body by that continuation of those properties as bring these varying conditions in the system.  Not that that prepared would not BRING the better conditions wherein other elements applied would correct the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], but taking those that do not coordinate WITH these (for one is vegetable, the other mineral, and these do not associate one with another) conditions were brought about that were as unpleasant to the body.

4. Would the body reduce each dose there would be found a different result, until those properties as were given for the system might be taken in a way and manner as to bring about the variation in the system so that there might be applied those conditions that would bring the better, more normal [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for the system; that is, with the activity of the body.  We have given those conditions as necessary to lay, as it were, the correct portions and the correction of portions of this system for the better condition.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What caused the weakness in the body after taking part of the prescription?
(A)  Just as given, the incoordination of one element with another.  Reduce the doses of each, for this is as a stimulant for the system, and would build the [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body in such a way and manner as better conditions might be brought about within the physical forces of the body. These may not be taken to-gether, for, as was given, do that, that other conditions might be administered to the system in a way and manner as would bring better forces for the body.

6. We are through for the present.

> R1. 11/8/27 EC's letter to [4658]'s son:
> 
> My dear Mr. [...]
> 
> Replying to yours of the 29th, enclosed you will find what
> was given in the reading as respecting the conditions
> surrounding your mother and taking of those things
> prescribed.  I do not know (not knowing any more about the
> conditions than I do, or just how you and your mother feel
> respecting the work) whether I know how to advise you or not,
> but I am of the opinion, if you will study both of these
> readings very carefully you will be able to arrive at that
> conclusion which will be the best thing to do.  I believe, if
> she will go at the suggestions in the proper way and manner,
> she will be able to gain a great deal from them - but, if it
> is not obtainable in your own mind, and in her mind, why
> then I don't think it would be the best thing to go on with
> it - for only in so far as an individual is able to make the
> suggestions given compatible in their own minds and hearts,
> will these ever be of any benefit.  I have been hoping to be
> in [[New York City|New York]] for several weeks.  I possibly may be there by or
> before the 1st, and when I do come, I would like to see you
> and talk the matter over with you, for of course I want to
> see my good friend Mr. Adlman, also at that time.  But I am
> certainly hoping and trusting that you will be able to get
> something out of these that will make them really worth while
> for your mother's condition.
> 
> Thanking you for your letter, and hoping we may be of
> service, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
