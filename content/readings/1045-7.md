# TEXT OF READING 1045-7 F 38

**Reading [[1045-7]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/21/1936  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 6169

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of February, 1936, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., also by the sister-in-law, Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:50 to 4:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, there are improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

3. We would not crowd, neither would we change a great deal the applications that have been suggested.

4. It will be necessary, as we find, to alter the diets from time to time.

5. Then, instead of so much of the fruit juices - that tend to react so easily upon the system - we would use rather the ground liver, or goose liver, or a mixture of pigs' liver with calves' liver (not beef liver but calf liver).

6. Any of these would be helpful, but NOT in large quantities. The [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], as indicated, would be well.  Also the Yogurt, which may be had from the Kellogg laboratories.

7. The [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance will be found to quiet and ease the body.

8. The inhalations from the fumes of the brandy in the charred container will STRENGTHEN and give more vitality.

9. Keep the body quiet, and we should build much for this body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self and Mrs. [1100]
"    " Ass'n file
