# TEXT OF READING 5111-1 F 35 (Social Studies Teacher,

**Reading [[5111-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/15/1944  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 13244

Methodist) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5111], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]] and article in "Coronet".

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time. ..., Wyoming.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, just stirring.  We have the body here [5111].

3. As we find, there are disorders which are gradually growing more and more disturbing to the body.  For the lesions in the areas of the 6th and 7th dorsals make hindrances, more and more, in the activity of the lacteal duct, in the lower portion or in the left side and lower portion of the jejunum. As these cause more and more lesions to form, or adhesions, greater and greater become the headaches and nausea and the weakness that comes with this, causing greater and greater pressures upon both the sympathetic nervous system and (is beginning to make pressures upon) the organs of elimination, the kidneys and bladder.

4. As we find, we would use these alternately:  that is, one day use [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs over the abdomen, sufficiently large to cover the area of the lower portion of the liver, and extend to the caecum and to the lower portion of the left side also. Make the Packs out of three or four thicknesses of flannel and saturate with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], and when this is applied over the portions indicated, use the electric pad.  Have a good size electric pad, not just a small one.  Keep this on for at least one hour.

5. The next day apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], not [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], and use cotton cloths instead of the flannel.  Apply over the same areas as the Castor Oil Packs.

6. After this has been given for an hour, do have a gentle massage.  Sponge off first with tepid [[Baking Soda|soda]] water.  Use about a teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] in a quart of tepid water and sponge off the area covered with the oil.  Then massage with cocoa butter.  This massage should be gentle but deep.

7. After these have been taken, two Castor Oil Packs and two [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs, have a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] scientifically given.

8. Rest from these for two weeks, then repeat, and also have another [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

9. Though this has been long-standing, the second series should eliminate the sources.  This may reoccur, but one of the series should eliminate sources.  After each of these Packs, if it is necessary, give the massage to the body.

10. It would be well during the second series of packs to have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations.  These should not be adjustments, but relaxing treatments, from the 1st cervical to the end of the coccyx, using the extremities as levers in the exercise of the abdominal areas, stretching the arms up, pulling the limbs and holding up and twisting out.

11. These will aid.

12. Do that.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]], when ready for these treatments - Let us have his name and address.) Copy for Ass'n file
"    "  indexing

(Please see instructions [which were enclosed] for Castor Oil and [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 12/17/09 or 1908, "not sure", in Kansas; single.
> 
> B2. 9/10/43 Letter:  "I read the article about you in 'Coronet'
> for this month, and couldn't resist writing to you...  For 15
> years I have had severe headaches which doctors diagnose as
> migraine.  They said there is no cure for them, so I struggle
> from day to day with the pain.  Gynergin, hypodermically
> taken, stops them until another return, but I can take only
> so much of the drug, or gynergin poisoning will result.  At
> the end of every school day, I'm wretched with one or I awake
> in the morning with one.  Since I am a teacher, the headaches
> make me increasingly nervous and depressed.  Sometimes I
> think I can't go on.  Yet I have to, because my parents
> need my financial aid..."
> 
> B3. 1/3/44 Letter:  "...These headaches have increased until I am
> frantic with them, and the drug I have to take to stop them
> is dangerous if too much is taken...  Won't you please help
> me?...  I've prayed and prayed about these headaches and I
> try to live right...but because they are so much worse, I
> know you can help me..."
> 
> B4. 1/23/44 Letter:  "...My need is urgent, both from a physical
> standpoint and mental...  I am simply holding down my [[Book of Job|job]] and
> hanging on because I must.  These headaches have plagued me
> for years.  I have been to Mayo's and to all parts of the
> country.  Most doctors have diagnosed them as migraine and
> said they did not know the cause and could recommend no cure.
> Now, however, they are occurring every day, usually waking
> me from sleep at night and they are so severe that only
> hypodermics can stop them, and I am so weary when I arise
> it is torture to go on through.  Also my digestive system
> is upset, pain has appeared in my right side, and I can do
> nothing but grit my teeth and pray to get through each day.
> The last doctor suggests gall bladder trouble which may
> cause an operation.  Mr. Cayce, I have my parents to support
> partially.  I must work; I'm steadily going into debt from
> doctor bills.  I cannot read, go to a movie, go to any
> entertainment without bringing on one of these headaches.  I
> weep at the slightest provocation, and my nerves are jumpy
> from weariness and pain.  I can't wait long to be helped,
> because I am afraid of going to pieces completely...  I want
> to enjoy life and everything in it, but it's an effort just
> to stay alive...  Perhaps my [[Prayer|prayers]] will be answered through
> you..."
> 
> B5. 3/44 Letter:  "For 13 years I have had terrific headaches
> which aspirin or any common headache remedy would not
> alleviate.  Each year they have grown steadily worse in
> intensity, and more frequent, until now I have one every
> day, sometimes only 18 or 20 hours apart.  Gynergin, taken
> hypodermically, is the only drug that will stop them.  The
> pain is intense, forming over either eye, causing the back of
> the neck to stiffen.  Recently they have been accompanied by
> severe leg-ache.  They bother me most frequently at night,
> waking me from sleep, although they will come at any time of
> the day, as well.  All these years I have tried everything,
> been to Mayo's, and all doctors said they're migraine and
> can't be cured because they don't know the cause.  For this
> reading, I shall stay at home..."
> 
> B6. 5/7/44 Friend's letter:  "Since [5111] is ill, I am writing
> this letter for her.  Friday she started Histamine treatments
> for her headaches, and they have made her very ill.  The
> following are the questions she would like asked for her
> reading May 15:  (1) What is the cause and remedy for these
> headaches?  (2) What is the cause and remedy for her painful
> menstrual periods?  (3) What is the cause and remedy for any
> other difficulty she may have?..."
