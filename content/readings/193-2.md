# TEXT OF READING 193-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[193-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/13/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 712

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Perlin, Mrs. Johnson, and others.]

2nd house north of 
Time of Reading                         Coast Guard Station, 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Cape Henry, Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. In some respects, but mighty little, the body responds to those applications.  As we find, little - save keeping easy - may be done, save keeping up the ELIMINATIONS as far as is possible.  Give those of the sedatives, or hypnotics, as will keep the body easy, and keep up eliminations, draining the system; that is, the alimentary canal, when necessary; keeping down the temperature by the rubs, and would be well that the enemas be cool, and oil given after same - that is, as an enema - the white oil.

2. These, as we find, will give the greater ease for the body. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What nourishment should be given?
(A)  As has been outlined.

We will find that the pains, or the bloating, will be eased most by equal parts of limewater and cinnamon water.  The dose would be a teaspoonful of each in half a glass of water, see?  This may be given whenever there is any distending. This will ease, as we find, most.  Those of camomile tea, or of yellow saffron and of elm, will aid - but will ferment very easily.  We wouldn't advise them at the present time.

4. (Q)  What can be done to relieve the nervousness at night, when body can't sleep?
(A)  Only be by increasing the quantity of the hypnotic, or sedative.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
