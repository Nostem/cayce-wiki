# TEXT OF READING 187-1 M 5 MONTHS (Christian, Protestant

**Reading [[187-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/16/1925  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 697

Background) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 16th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [199].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Born December 21, 1924, 2:30 P.M., in Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
Time of Reading 
12:15 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                 ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the Universal Forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life.  Also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here.  This we have had before, you see, and those relations with the universal forces as may be and are manifested in this present earth's plane.

3. In the position as is taken in the present plane, we find this comes from Jupiter, with Uranus and Mars, with Saturn and Neptune in the adverse conditions, bringing then to this entity one that will be and is strong of body, and will find, with the proper training, the normal development towards those conditions of the magnanimous, and of those ennobling influences as come in Jupiter; yet with those diverse influences in Uranus will bring very eccentric, very good or very bad, very high tempered, or one that will need the necessity then of being trained in that proper manner, which will give the resources of the urges as are exhibited in the body the opportunity of their development.  For with the adverse influence in Mars, that comes with Saturn's conjunction with same, we would bring, under the adverse conditions, when we would have the extremities of the Uranian influence, a force very detrimental to self and others. Hence, as has been given, the necessity, the responsibility, the manner that rests in the hands of those who would guide, guard and direct the trend of the thought of the developing individual.

4. With the greater influence, we find these will bring many of the ennobling and assisting influences in the earth plane.

5. One whose abilities will lie in that of the student along the line of monies and of psychological study, either that as the trained mathematician or banker, or as that of the student along psychological and astronomical studies.  These may be, however, reversed by the conditions as exhibited and as has been given.

6. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] that we find, these come as the urge to the individuals:

7. In the one before this we find in the days when the strife was between the peoples in the present land, the entity then being among those who fought in that rebellion.  In the name then we find of Artemus Davees [?].  In this we find the urge as will be in that of the thought as is exercised in self, being strong-minded, very centered in thought and action. Hence the necessity as will be of the training of the direction of the mind and of the developing in same.

8. In the one before this we find in the days of the development of the country into that of the republic forces in [[France|France]], when the rebellions were in the land.  The entity then in that of Vasi Borgenes [?].  The entity then the one that assisted in the capture of those forts about which the soldiers of the ones defending the royalty became the ones necessary to overcome or overthrow these conditions.  The entity from this we find gains that dislike for those who would be over-bearing, or those who would be presumptuous in their action.  Hence the necessity of those training and giving the correction for the development of the entity of always knowing and showing the reason, and reasoning with the individual in its development.

9. In the one before this we find in the days of the rebellions in the plains country, when this had become settled.  The entity then was in that of the Viva [?] who became the heralder of the new religious thought as was given to that people then in Arabian forces.  This we find became the developing point of the entity.  Hence in this present urge we will find the desire of the study of such, of the religious thought and study as pertaining to psychological and astronomical conditions.  Yet for the same reason, by not being directed properly, may be turned into adverse channels.

10. In the one before this we find in the days of the [[Atlantis|Atlantis]], when the peoples were in that of the higher state of civilization.  The entity then was the teacher in the psychological thought and study, especially as that of the transmission of thought through ether.  Hence the urge as will be seen in this entity in the studying of those as has been given.

11. Then, as to what this entity may develop, along those lines as has been given, and well that these be followed and directed in that manner as to bring the best to the entity, ever knowing that the way of the transgressor is hard, and that knowledge gives the understanding, when given in the manner that may be applied in the individual's life.

12. We are through for the present.
