# TEXT OF READING 4905-56

**Reading [[4905-56]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/16/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12981

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give us any information concerning same that will assist in making this the success as has often been given, tell us whether it will be necessary to run a string of casing, that they may finish this lime, and any other information that will insure the success of these operations.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], the operations here.  Now, we find, while the cavings have been bad, and the necessity of drilling up portions of same has caused delays, we do not find it would be necessary to set casing for the completion of this operation, or until production is shown in the operation.  THOUGH, the string of casing SET, this would insure the quicker completion of same, for this would of necessity only be run, and then this would prevent further troubles; would also enable the operations to pass the obstructions that have hindered the operations, see?  yet we find that the cavings have been cleaned or cleared now to where the operation may be carried on in a more satisfactory manner, without much trouble from further cavings, and that as soon as cemented, this may be then drilled through without further trouble.  The running of casing, which may be done, would insure the operations being completed SOONER, and no TROUBLE encountered, or ensuing, when the gas and oil BEGINS, which will, of course, bring more cavings in the operations, under the existent conditions, see?

3. Then, this must be decided through the way that the principals may decide to operate, see?  though, were the string of casing, or tools, used, would show that the tools would run easy, level - 6,000 feet - $6000 - $8000 - about $7000 to set the string of casing - though may be used again, and would insure the success of the operations, see?

4. We find the oil will be found here, only with a few more feet through the LOWER PORTION of where hole HAS BEEN OPENED.  See, there are 30 feet of caving at present.

5. We are through for the present.

---

> to send them out, and hope we can put them where that they
> will do us a great deal of good.  We are keeping fairly busy,
> though don't have a great deal of return from the work, so
> far.  Many make us promises, both I'm sure we are getting
> some results, from what they say of same from time to time.
> Some, of course, I know do the very best they can; others
> I feel could be stimulated by a little more activity on our
> part.
> 
> I'm glad to say we are all fairly well here.  In fact I
> believe the whole family now are in better health than they
> have been for some time.  We certainly find ourselves in
> the midst of a big activity - in real estate and building.
> It promises to be a very live place here for the coming
> season, and I believe, from the buildings that are under
> construction, this is more of a permanent operation than
> thought to be possible here for a good long while.  It would
> seem apparent that the readings were correct, when they said
> this was the place.  I'm sure, if I had been able to invest a
> few hundred dollars here, when it first told me to come here,
> it would have made sufficient to have done most anything
> with now.  One man who invested five hundred dollars in 1914
> closed out PART of his holdings for two hundred and thirty
> thousand dollars!  And still has a piece of property they say
> is worth about nine hundred dollars a foot, and the WHOLE
> thing cost him five hundred dollars in 1914!
> 
> I certainly am in hopes that the suggestions here for
> yourself will prove effective and that right away.  Be sure
> and let us hear from you, that we may check up on this, even
> though it's every week or two, for we want to get you good
> and well.  With kindest personal regards from all,
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD

> R5. 3/22/26 Robert Collier's letter to Mr. Wyrick:
> 
> 
> Mr. M. B. Wyrick
> % W. U. Telg. Co.,
> [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.
> 
> Dear Mr. Wyrick:-
> 
> Thank you for your good letter of the 18th with check
> enclosed in full payment for a set of "The [[Book of Life|Book of Life]]"
> to Mr. Edgar Cayce.  The books have gone forward today.
> 
> I read his little booklet with a great deal of interest, for
> I can readily believe the possibilities of such work as he is
> doing.  His experience reminds me of a similar character in
> one of Richard Harding Davis' stories.  This character was
> able to use his [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] in much the same way, as
> long as he was doing it for others.  But the moment he did
> it for any selfish [[Purpose|purpose]], he lost the power.
> 
> Now for your own problem, Mr. Wyrick.  Have you ever cut your
> finger?  Who was it coagulated the blood, stopped the gash,
> wove new skin?  Who was it called upon the little phagocytes
> to come and kill the septic germs?  Not your conscious mind,
> certainly.  Most people don't even know there are "any such
> animals".  Their conscious minds don't know the first thing
> about [[Healing|healing]].  Whence comes the information?  Whence the
> directing genius?  Where but from the same intelligence that
> keeps your heart and lungs on the [[Book of Job|job]] while you sleep, that
> regulates your liver and your kidneys, that attends to all
> the functions of your body.
> 
> The intelligence is your [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]].  With the proper
> cooperation on your part, your [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] will attend
> to these duties indefinitely, keeping your every organ
> perfect, your every function regular as clock work.
> 
> But it is exceedingly amenable to suggestion.  Worry about
> sickness or contagion, hold before it the thought that you
> are getting old, or that some organ is becoming feeble,
> and it will be perfectly agreeable to bringing about the
> condition you suggest.  But convince it that there is no
> danger from contagion, hold before it the thought of health
> and strength, and it will be just as prompt in manifesting
> them.
> 
> So what you must realize is this:  Before anything can be
> made, there has to be a model for it in Mind.  Before a house
> can be built, there has to be a plan, a blueprint from which
> to build.
> 
> Before you were created [[Universal Consciousness|Universal Mind]] held in thought the
> model on which you were made.  That Model was perfect then.
> It is perfect now.  The only idea of you that Universal Mind
> knows is a perfect model, where every organism is formed
> along perfect lines.
> 
> Your body is changing every moment, Mr. Wyrick.  Every cell,
> every organism, is constantly being rebuilt.  Why remodel
> along the old imperfect lines?  Why not build along the lines
> held in the thought of Universal Mind.  You CAN do it.  But
> the essence of it is in words of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]:  "Whatsoever
> you desire when you pray.  BELIEVE that ye Receive it, and
> ye SHALL HAVE it."
> 
> Mind you, He didn't say "believe you are GOING to get it."
> He said - "believe that you RECEIVE it."  Know that the
> R E A L you - the image of you held in subconscious mind - is
> PERFECT.  Believe that you H A V E this perfect image.  On
> the day that you can carry this sincere conviction to your
> subconscious mind - on THAT day you will be healed.
> 
> I am enclosing herewith a copy of a booklet that will, I
> believe, help you.
> 
> Cordially yours,  [signed]  Robert Collier
