# TEXT OF READING 2604-2 F 12

**Reading [[2604-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/10/1942  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 9643

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. The mother and Marion White.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, while conditions have improved a great deal, there is much yet to be desired.  And, as we find, it may be accomplished - in the body's gaining nearer to normal equilibrium and activity.

4. There is the tendency for weakness and a clogging in the circulation.  This is as related to nerve as well as blood supply, which is indicated in the superficial as well as deeper circulation.

5. We would continue with the use of those applications that have been indicated.

6. But we would add the vitamins (concentrated) in tablet form, for at least four to five days each week; these to be A, B-1 and D.

7. Also we would have, at least twice a week, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments to allay or to cause the GENERAL reactions; specifically in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal areas and through the whole of the lumbar and sacral areas.

8. These, we will find, will bring better conditions for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Would the routine and discipline in a hospital be to her advantage in hastening her recovery?  If so, which one would be best?
(A)  As we find, if those administrations are made as we have outlined it would be better outside than inside a routine. For, the body itself in its reactions is free and NOT routine.

11. (Q)  Will there be a toe drag in the left foot?
(A)  If that's what is desired, if that's what is being sought, there will be!  If there is being sought, if there is being expected a COMPLETE RECOVERY, it will come - if there will be done that as indicated!

12. (Q)  Is it best that her mother or someone else take care of her?
(A)  That should be better decided by the body itself.

13. (Q)  Is the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] properly made up?
(A)  Properly made up.

14. (Q)  Is it being used to best advantage?
(A)  Being used to best advantage.

15. (Q)  It should be continued in the same manner?
(A)  Continued in same manner.

16. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Keep that same constructive attitude; not only being good but good FOR something.

Let each thankfulness be made toward the applying of that received in aid to others.

Let the [[Prayer|prayer]] oft be:

"IN THE JOY OF THE LORD, IN THE JOY OF BEING HELPFUL TO OTHERS, MAY I SING THE PRAISE OF [[Jesus Christ|THE MASTER]]."

17. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Richardson) Copy to Ass'n file
