# TEXT OF READING 4092-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4092-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/10/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11789

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 10th day of October, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stallings, Brazelton & Hale, Court Reporter, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep.  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep and all of the organs of your body will function properly; you will hear me and answer all of my questions.

You have before you the body of Mrs. [4092], of ..., near ..., Virginia.

Now, Mr. Cayce, tell us the physical and mental ailments that you find within this body; and also the treatment for same, should you find ailments that need treatment.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

FEM:  That is good luck.

3. EC:  In the home at the present.  Now the abnormal conditions within this body - Mrs. [4092] we are speaking of here - have to do with the functioning of organs, and some organic conditions existing in the body at the present time.

4. Now these are conditions as we find within this body at the present time; through the circulation, the blood supplying forces we find show the condition existing within the body where the rebuilding of cellular units of physical force are created within the body itself, - see?

FEM:  I see.

5. The units of cells to replenish life-building forces show this through the action of the arterial forces as well as the conditions through the veins themselves, giving the active force to all physical matter, as within this body here, showing the condition, the time, the age, - you see.

FEM:  I see.

6. The quality, the number of red blood corpuscles carrying the rebuilding force, or the hemoglobin within itself, produces the force that gives the life to the force within the body; in this condition here the numbers being deficient at present - you see.

FEM:  I see.

7. The numbers of the leukocytes being above the normal for the condition existing throughout the rest of the body, and the age and condition taken into consideration.  The nerve-giving forces within the body show through the action of the sensory organism the deficiency in the action of the organs as are functioned through the action of part of the sensory organism - see.

FEM:  Yes sir, I see.

8. The circulation, that is, blood supply, lymphatic forces as are carried by the circulation necessary for the sensory organism to function properly, show the effect of the age, of the differences in [[Vibration|vibration]] that has been produced within the body and brought about the condition that exists that should not be or is the abnormal forces in the sensory system at present.  The body in time back has been high-strung, nervous forces, - see.

FEM:  I see.

9. Kept at a high tension without reference to either mental force, that is, extravagant use of mental force of self, - physical and mental, - see.

FEM:  I see.

10. This brought then to the forces the condition existing through this part of the body here, - see.

FEM:  Yes sir.

11. We have in the nervous forces and in the nerve ganglia at present lesions formed at the second dorsal, the second and third cervical that affect the flow of circulation lymphatically to the head and neck and to those portions of the body that show their deficiency in their action and in their response to the cerebro-spinal forces as giving back to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] the [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to produce the functioning of the organ themselves, or the auditory forces, you see, here.

FEM:  Yes sir.

12. Are affected by the condition existing in the physical force, see -

FEM:  Yes sir.

13. Of the body; the functioning of the organs of the body here, the brain forces, above normal in its action in the functioning of the effect as used to produce or create within the brain force itself, - see.

FEM:  Yes sir.

14. The action of the organs of the throat, of the lungs, the effect is only shown through these, more in the action of the arterial forces and along [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of the arterial forces rather than the functional force of the organs themselves, - see.

FEM:  Yes sir.

15. The climatic conditions having more to do with this condition in the body, - see -

FEM:  Yes sir.

16. - than other forces.  Through the functioning of the other organs of the body, as given through the digestive forces or through the functioning of the other organs themselves, with the conditions existing, we find no abnormality save the effect at present which has nothing to do with the condition expected to be corrected within the body at present of some congestion produced by an overtaxation of the digestive organs themselves, - see?  This occurred some two days ago.  Make note of this.

FEM:  Yes sir.

17. To give assistance and relief to the organs that are hindered, that is, through the ear forces themselves, by the action of the voice or [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the ear drum so that it will produce in the system that which will bring the same vibration necessary to bring the body to its normal force, - see.

FEM:  Yes sir.

18. First, relief by relaxation, mechanically, the congested forces at the second dorsal, at the second and third cervical; then give to the body the vibrations from electrical forces as prepared in this manner and form:

19. To 2 gallons of rain water we would add:

Willow Charcoal..............2 pounds,
Copper Sulfate.............1/2 pound,
Sulfuric Acid C.P............1 ounce,
Zinc.........................6 drams.

This of course should be placed in a glass jar, rather than an earthen jar, - see.

FEM:  I see.

20. The poles, to which would be attached fine electrical wires, we would charge this from the battery of 120 volts, 30 cycles, 28 amperes for thirty minutes; then the poles would be changed, one being of iron, the other of silver, they both attached to one plate and attached to this body of Mrs. [4092] that we were speaking of, at the second cervical for the space of ten minutes each evening, - see.

FEM:  I see.

21. This done for three cycles of vibratory force, to the auditory nerves, and we will find we will relieve the condition existing in this portion of the body to a great extent - not wholly, for we have some effects to the conditions in the ear itself that will not be overcome entirely; but this will relieve and give great relief to many of the other forces within the body.  Do that.  We are through.

22. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, you have told us that the climate probably not best for her.  What climate would you recommend?  [No answer]  Mr. Cayce, are you going to answer my question?
(A)  [No answer]

23. Mr. Cayce, now you forget all about this reading that you were giving.  You were feeling all right, and all of the organs of your body functioned properly; you were feeling good.  Now wake up in two minutes just feeling fine and all right.
