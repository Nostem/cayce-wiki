# TEXT OF READING 1340-1 M 18

**Reading [[1340-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/14/1937  Sex: M  Age: 18  ReadingID: 7012

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of February, 1937, in accordance with phone request made by Miss [681], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., sponsoring new Active Membership for Miss Sarah Townshend.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Burt Davis.]

Room 934, Univ. 
Time of Reading                              Hosp., Lombard & 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Greene Sts., 
Baltimore, Md. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions are very serious.

3. While there is the disturbance in keeping the circulation and the coagulation in same going, to interfere in the present would as we find rather jeopardize conditions.

4. The necessity for meeting the condition producing the immediate disturbances, then, is to keep the heart's activities going and to keep coagulation from forming in such matters as to hinder the flow through the whole of the eliminating and assimilating system.

5. And to interfere with that being accomplished would not be well.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What is the nature of the disease?  Is this understood?
(A)  In the immediate it is that as has been indicated.  As to the causes, these have little to do with the conditions we are to meet right now.

8. (Q)  Any advice regarding same?
(A)  As has been indicated.  Keep the heart action going but do not hinder the circulation from proper distribution by the character of administrations.  We are through for the present.

WESTERN UNION - Feb. 14, 1937 - 11:30 A.M.:  EMERGENCY UNDERSTOOD  TO INTERFERE WITH PRESENT ADMINISTRATIONS WOULD JEOPARDIZE CONDITIONS.

Collect   Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.
