# TEXT OF READING 4760-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4760-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1910 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1910  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12733

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 11th day of December, 1910.

[Edgar Cayce; Al C. Layne (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.

1. ACL:  You are now asleep and will be able to tell us what we want to know.  You have before you Miss [4760], ..., [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.

2. EC:  Miss [4760].

3. ACL:  Of ..., Hopkinsville, Ky.

4. EC:  Miss [4760] ..., Hopkinsville, Ky.

5. ACL:  Yes, she is before you now, you will see her.

6. EC:  Yes, we have her here.

7. ACL:  Now I want you to go over her carefully and examine her thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with her, if anything.

8. EC:  Yes, we have a good many troubles and a good many imaginary troubles.

9. ACL:  Tell us what it is.  Well, what do you find?

10. EC:  Let me get it all together.

11. ACL:  Alright.

12. EC:  Well, take first here the circulation of the blood in Miss [4760] in the system, in the whole body here.  The seat of the power in the blood given out here from the heart, circulated through the whole system, then the heart here, you see, into the lungs, back to the heart and on out to the system; it is not clarified enough, does not become red enough from the air that the body receives from here into the lungs and leaves particles at times; goes out through the whole system these conditions; we have these conditions along the cells and out to the capillaries and to the smaller ends of the system - the respiratory system, to the skin itself, of the outside part of the body is not as thorough as it should be here; it does not throw off enough; it leaves too much to be thrown off from the air, too much work for the kidneys is what we have here.

13. We have here the hepatic circulation reduced from the same condition, an overcharge of the blood in these cells until we take up so much of the white blood, or white blood corpuscles, or leukocytes, in the system (nature's warriors); takes up too much of what should be expelled from the system; what we have that is abnormal - not good for the body.

14. The blood is a rebuilding force of the body; what we take in goes into the system, used for the digestion - rebuilding of the system in itself.  This rebuilds the whole body, but as we have these properties left in it has reduced the hepatic circulation through the intestinal tract, kidneys and liver. Where the blood is used twice, we have reduced these conditions or the power to reinforce the nerve supply until we have reduced the condition in the nerve forces until we have left impression that she is sick all the time; we have not enough to carry out the force, the nerve properties of the body - of the system itself.  This is from the seat of the nerve at the brain and throughout the sympathetics and the lymphatics.

15. We have in the lymphatics both nerve force and the circulation out of rebuilding of the forces here; it has left the body in the condition we have from the lymphatic circulation and nerve forces itself, both, from the lymphatic and all branches of the sympathetics get into the system all over the body, especially, from the lymphatics into the lungs and into the intestinal tract and liver, where we have most of this, until we produce a nerve force and until we have lesions along the spine, until we have left a matter in the nerve forces and supply itself - the body of the nerve, - until we fail to carry the impression as received from the condition in the system.

16. The trouble has been through the pelvis and intestinal tract until we have reduced the condition from the whole body, - the muscular force, the nerve force, the pelvis, the lower end of the body - the trunk - has reduced these conditions until we have a neuesthenic [neurasthenic?] condition of the nerve and blood supply to the pelvis you see, from the condition in the hepatics and nerve and blood supply; this has reduced these conditions until it can at times and then at times it cannot.

17. The lack of nerve force and quantities of matter left there, that should be thrown off, has reduced the condition of the kidneys, in the pelvis just above, which carries out the secretion from the system to the condition where it cannot carry all the forces or supply to be carried out of the system.

18. The condition, you see, here in the pelvis, of the organs or lobes in the pelvis, which secrete the generatory organism, secrete the matter you see, not of the forces to be thrown out to the system, as a force that should have been expelled from the forces above, until it has produced the pains across the pelvis and abdomen, from the sympathetics, from the forces or non-supply to the forces, to the sympathetics to expel from it has left the condition we have.

19. The condition of the kidney themselves produced an inflammation until we have some showing, at the time, in the quantity and quality of the secretion of the kidney; we have it in the substance taken out from it, showing the condition of these organs themselves.  We have an inner coat of the kidneys (the kidneys are made up of an outside coat and an inner coat or section of cells and forces to it), expelling from it these particles or acids that should be thrown out from the system and not carried back to the system, through the respiratory organs, which are weak.

20. The condition into the opposite pole or into the liver has been produced and we have a larging of the lobes from the lack of secretion thrown out from it until we have produced then the capillary of this (the lymphatics in the capillaries) this enlarging or lack or torpidity in themselves to act on the organism of it, not torpidity of the organism but organism of the action.  We have the liver as an organ and we have an action of the liver as an action from the organ itself.

21. We have a condition here produced from the lack of juices of the stomach until, at times, it produces nausea at the upper end.  We find at the fourth and fifth dorsal a soreness; it hurts from the frontal part - upper end of the stomach.  In the stomach we find this from lack of power from lack of nutriment thrown back; we find the body here weak from lack of forces; we find the whole system, as it were, living on itself for days and days and then it is better.  The whole system or whole organism lives on what is thrown out to these, then becomes better - rests from what it has had before in it.

22. (Q)  What causes the intermitting heart?  Any organic trouble?
(A)  The blood you see here into the forces, we have that through the circulation produced from that.  We have the heart here is the organ that forces the blood through the system.  The blood enters into the heart, is thrown to the lung and comes back to the heart and thrown out to the whole system; a continual amount of matter throws out or returned back to the heart has been distributed through the system and taken up by the lymphatics to be taken out through the pores of the skin, left to carry back until we have produced to the cardiac plexus the condition, at times, shown through the condition of the heart, in the organism of the heart, either cell - the right or left ventricle - to supply nerve force that governs these organisms, loses the condition to exist. Take any condition from the opposite body (opposite of the body), we have there the limbs in this same body the condition exists until we move the hands up here - like we have it on the body - it lies until it becomes hurt, pains; remove it.  Now we have that same condition - the blood to the cardiac plexus or nerve forces, governing the heart produces the same condition from lying in one state until we produce the condition we have here in the heart.

23. (Q)  What treatment would you suggest to relieve these conditions?
(A)  Treatment?

24. (Q)  Yes.
(A)  Treat one system we work hardships on the other like we have had in the system all along.  Treat it for the pelvis or organism of the pelvis - kidney - produce the condition in the stomach and when we produce the condition in the stomach we have sickness, nauseating and more trouble with the heart.

25. (Q)  What would you suggest to relieve these conditions?
(A)  We have it here.

26. (Q)  Alright.
(A)  Take first here along the pelvis, along under the lower lumbars in the pelvis, from the exterior here in the system, of this system here of Miss [4760], until we produce a moisture to the exterior of the system by heat, with anything to produce heat and moisture and a gathering of these forces from the system; it weakens then the condition of the rest of the organism; then an opposite from this will produce into the stomach here a stimulation to the nerve and blood supply or force the circulation faster until we produce a healthier condition of the system.

Then we will have to rebuild the system as we need it, from one organism to another; watch this and produce these conditions again to reduce them.  We produce a kind of irritant, you see, to the condition in the pelvis along down here, to take away this inflammation as we had rebuilt the organism through the stomach here.  Through the pelvis water - hot water - to bring a respiration here; create more oxygen into the lungs, get more into it if we have to get it from outside.  Clarify this blood more until it feels better - not so weak and drowsy.  Impress the mind of the body.  The mind is through the cranium, through the sensory system - the feeling, hearing, seeing.  The body will improve after removing these conditions.  It mends, then don't feel so well; gets up and then gets down.

27. (Q)  What medicine would you suggest to give in the stomach?
(A)  Something to stimulate here.  We will have this when we reduce these conditions along the pelvis, we reduce then the circulation here, you see, through the hepatics, then we give into the stomach something that affects the nerve (I don't mean deaden the nerves, but quiets the nerves), something to increase the circulation - get the blood out from the heart - make it stronger out to the capillary and add that stimulation to the whole system; something to rebuild the whole system here.  Not something to produce excitement to the nerve force here through the cardiac plexus or solar plexus or it will make, if we do excite it, make the stomach sick, quicken the heart, scare the body when it is not hurt.

28. (Q)  What particular medicine would you suggest to reach that trouble and not cause these bad effects?
(A)  Do not get it.  Do not see the medicine.  It is here, though.

29. (Q)  Well, see what it is.
(A)  It creates to this system, to the circulation and getting the blood away from the heart (the hepatics do not get anything for it without producing to the nerve supply, to the nerve force here, a letting loose or quickening of the pulsation); it makes the pulsation too strong, bound to do something or we would not get it out.

30. (Q)  Must we do anything else?
(A)  Not there.  Give something into the stomach; reduce this soreness; get more air into the lungs.  If we do not, it will stir up a cold and congestion in the lungs and further trouble in the stomach.  KEEP THE MIND STRONG ON IT and get the circulation out, more away and the body can and will improve.
