# TEXT OF READING 849-56 M 33

**Reading [[849-56]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/24/1941  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5049

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of January, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [849], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Margaret Wilkins and Ruby Ellington.]

Time of Reading 
4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Va.

1. HLC:  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What is causing, and what should be done for, the severe muscular pains.
(A)  Take the exercises, as has been indicated!

4. (Q)  Is there any other suggestion for the body at this time?
(A)  We would do that as last outlined; it hasn't been done as yet!

5. (Q)  Any suggestions regarding diet for the body at this time?
(A)  Rather the suggestions would be to keep the eliminations in proper order.  For, the assimilations are fair; but the eliminations are not so well, and poisons accumulate from drosses in the system.

Use more of the enemas with the salines in same; and the sweats would be very well for the body in the present.

6. (Q)  What kind of sweats?
(A)  In hot water, with the salts in same!

7. (Q)  How often should they be taken?
(A)  Until we remove the muscular forces and the exercises are begun!

8. We are through with with Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Wife
