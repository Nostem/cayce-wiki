# TEXT OF READING 281-61

**Reading [[281-61]]** · Series: [[281 — The Glad Helpers - Healing Prayer Group|281]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/13/1942  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1844

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of May, 1942, in accordance with request made by those present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Frances Y. Morrow, Florence Edmonds, Helen Ellington, Esther Wynne, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Hannah Miller, Ruth LeNoir, Mae Verhoeven.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers Prayer Group]], members of which are present in this room; who seek a personal message as to the much work each must do, as a group and as individuals.  You will give this personal message as each name is called.  Then you will give a set of affirmations to be sent to those on the [[Prayer|prayer]] list:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the work of the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers Prayer Group]], - as a group and as individuals.

3. There should be more consistency and more unified effort on the part of each, as to time, as to sincerity of [[Purpose|purpose]]; not as individuals personally but as in keeping with BEING [[Glad Helpers|glad helpers]] for others.

4. (Q)  [379]:
(A)  Keep the faith, that ye may walk closer with Him day by day.  For, in the manner ye measure to thy fellow man, so must it be measured to thee again.

5. (Q)  [413]:
(A)  Let the light of the countenance of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Spirit guide thee, that ye walk not alone.  Let those things, those conditions as may annoy or aggravate be put aside in service to others.

6. (Q)  [993]:
(A)  In joy, in patience, run the race that is set before thee; knowing He is faithful and keeps His promise to those who are faithful in [[Purpose|purpose]], in aim, in desire, and will not withhold anything good from the righteous.

7. (Q)  [294]:
(A)  Let that mind be in thee as was in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], who went about doing good, and not to the glory of self but making himself as naught that He might serve others.

8. (Q)  [288]:
(A)  The way, the [[Purpose|purpose]], the aims and desires should ever be set in being of a purposeful helpfulness to others; that His joy IN THEE may be full.

9. (Q)  [307]:
(A)  The joy of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom.  Let thy understanding be in Him as the light, as the joy of the world.  For the world is His.  Forget it not.

10. (Q)  [404]:
(A)  Let fear, let worry, be lost in thy service to others for His name's sake.  Let thy heart be lifted up, that ye may hear that "Well done" from Him, who is mindful of those who seek His face.

11. (Q)  [585]:
(A)  Let that as would disturb or disappoint become rather the means, the ways of being of a greater service to others. Let thy heart, thy mind, be lifted up; that ye may hear, that ye may know the JOY of service in the Lord.

12. (Q)  [341]:
(A)  Let that as may disturb be taken to Him, who HEARS [[Prayer|prayer]] and has promised to answer those who pray in faith, NOTHING doubted.

13. (Q)  [1523]:
(A)  Let the heart be glad in the Lord; not in self nor in that as may be accomplished in self, but rather that ye may walk circumspect, in all good conscience, and praise to [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].

14. (Q)  [538]:
(A)  Keep the faith, knowing that He is mindful of thee. For He loveth those who seek to be channels of blessings to others.

15. (Q)  Any particular work we should do in preparation for the Congress?
(A)  That which has been indicated; each being more faithful, more consistent, more DESIROUS to be of help to others.

16. We have others on the list!  (Pause - while everyone tried to think of whose name to call next.)

17. Each has a list, and those upon the current list.  THIS is your own inconsistency, - ye have self before others!  [See 281-65, Par. R1.]

18. We are through for the present.
