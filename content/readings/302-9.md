# TEXT OF READING 302-9 F 38

**Reading [[302-9]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/11/1929  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 2210

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [302].  Also by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Levy, Mrs. Jacobs, Drs. Berger, Hansen and Parker, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                              [...] Brothers, 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We don't find it there at present - but we will find it - here - [EC mumbled over the address - GD couldn't get it clearly.]

2. Yes, [302].  This body we have had before.  In some respects physical conditions are bettered.  In others there are the evidences of inroads being made by the conditions of which the body has been warned, and unless there are more precautions taken respecting the conservation of the vitality, there must sooner or later - and not in a great distance - come the breaking up of cell and cell tissue to such an extent as to become detrimental.  More rest, better eliminations - not merely through alimentary canal, but COORDINATION of eliminations, and more of the vitality in assimilations in the system are needed.  Such vital energy may be aided - not without the use of those as have been given - but by VARYING the character of the lights for the body.  The [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] changed to the Infra Red, as well as the green and blue.  Cobalt blue, and nile green.  Changing these through the prism reflection.  Well also were the body to use the carbon ash in quarter grain dosage EVERY day.  Do that.  Ready for questions.  Alternating [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]], or the light treatments - cutting same into near half time as has BEEN used in some, and give same EVERY day - see? Alternating the color.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mr. [900]

(GD's note to Miss [302]:  Obtain carbon ash from F. F. Bradley, 2626 So. Dearborn St., [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.)

> have been under the weather, and I sincerely hope ere this
> reaches you, you will be your old good self once more.
> 
> As for me, to be very honest with you, after following your
> last reading to the most minute detail, I am happy to report
> bonafide and absolute improvement.  I realize now, if I had
> followed your first and second [302-1 & 302-2?] reading,
> which I received from you a couple of years ago, that I would
> have been able to do away with quite a little of the
> suffering I did during the early part of 1929.  [302-8?]  But
> then we must all pay for experience, and I paid for mine by
> not listening to you sooner.  Rest assured, however, that it
> shan't happen again, and when you tell me to do a thing I am
> going to live up to it to the best of my ability and follow
> out all instructions."
>

> Mrs. [136].  Apparently he saw Miss [302] and she requested a

> exact location four to five today."  She replied by wire:
> "Have business appointment four to five.  Will go directly
> home ... Ave., there from five-thirty until tomorrow morning
> business time."  [No rdg. was given, and no further

>

> Sometime I do hope to be given the opportunity to sit alone
