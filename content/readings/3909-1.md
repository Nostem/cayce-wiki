# TEXT OF READING 3909-1 F 61

**Reading [[3909-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/28/1944  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 11542

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3909], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., via [1690].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

At home on the 
Time of Reading                              old place, about 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M.                2 miles from 
Taken 11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time. ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find there are disturbing conditions.  These will gradually give a greater distress unless measures are taken, which should have been taken long ago.

4. We find that there has been an injury in the lumbar-sacral area which has caused unbalancing of the hip position, that gradually allows one limb to become moved in its socket - and it is shortening the limb.

5. We find that this should be corrected.  It may require that there be a period of rest, a correction, and even a weight tied to the limb to keep it in position.  This would be the better manner as we find, for correction.

6. This we would have done in Nashville by an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in accord or one who will consider the suggestions made here and who will make the corrections found to be necessary as to the position.

7. It is better that this be done when there has been some preparation made, and should require the body keeping off the feet for at least four to five weeks, with these corrections.

8. Do that if we would make for better conditions.

9. The rest and the adjustments will also aid in better assimilation through the whole body-forces, for the natural stress and strain upon the nerve system has also upset the hypogastric and pneumogastric centers.  These will be relaxed with the general treatments that would be made osteopathically.

10. Do that for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is there anyone in Nashville you would recommend to make these corrections?
(A)  One of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] profession such as we have indicated.

13. (Q)  What was the nature of the injury received?
(A)  There was a slipping that caused the gradual pulling of the muscular forces at the head of the femur.

14. (Q)  Is the vitamin preparation I am taking benefiting my hip condition?
(A)  Not benefiting, it is rather making more of a pulling out of place than correcting.  There has to be a correcting of the position.

15. (Q)  Why is the pain so much more severe at night than in the day?
(A)  Because of the equilibrium attempting to keep the balance after having been on the feet.  Thus, as indicated, the body should keep off the feet while these corrections are being made.

16. (Q)  Is sitting down as beneficial as lying down?
(A)  Not as beneficial as just indicated.

Do as outline.

17. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Unless you know of another more preferable, we would refer you to:  Dr. J. R. Shackleford, Belle View Drive.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
