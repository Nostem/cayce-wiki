# TEXT OF READING 2950-1 F 30 (Actress)

**Reading [[2950-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/2/1943  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 10216

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2950], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [2598].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born October 25, 1912, in [[Canada|Montreal]], Quebec, [[Canada|Canada]]. 
Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '41 - expectancies, frustrations - '35 preparations - '22 - not so well - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes - we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2950].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2950] - there is much to be interpreted.  But these phases of same we choose with the desire that this information may be used by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as a helpful experience; enabling the entity to better understand the [[Purpose|purposes]] and the urges that are latent in the experience of the entity.

5. Thus may the entity be enabled to better apply self as to fulfil that for which it chose to enter this experience.

6. We find the entity an [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] and a Uranian.  And these indicate that the entity will in this experience either become one well known, and excel in many ways, or it may become the complete failure.

7. But never lose the confidence in self or self's abilities. For it appears that those extremes will be in the experience of the entity.  But hold fast to thy faith in the DIVINE, and know that as ye walk in the light of Him who is the way, the truth and the light, ye will find peace - and that peace which comes from being as a channel of help for others.

8. Taking the latent urges arising from consciousnesses other than the material, and combined with the material - or as may be called a counterpart or a composite of each, we find:

9. One who finds it very hard to work FOR anyone else.  Then, learn - in self - to work WITH others.  This is the greater problem of the entity, and one it must meet in self - by keeping its [[Ideal|ideals]] spiritually as well as mentally, but coordinate and collaborate with others.

10. It is not always easy, but will be for the unfoldment of the entity.

11. In the experience the entity should become associated with the manager or the director of some financial undertaking. For, at the handling - or in the handling of finances, it may be said that the entity may become a past master.  And in the control, and learning to control self in its work with others, the entity may through such become materially independent.  But know that it is only lent thee of the Lord. And do not become self-sufficient in any of the choices of thy activities in the earth.

12. As to urges indicated from [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in consciousness or realms other than the earth, - we find Uranus the greater factor.  For, the influence there of the extremes becomes so oft crosswise with the other urges.

13. The high mental abilities, and the love of books, the love of power, the love of the limelight, come from those influences of Uranus as combined with Mercury; while as indicated in Venus with Uranus, the entity could trim a hat almost as well as a professional if it chose!

14. Hence there is indicated the decided technique in whatever the entity chooses to do, as well as so oft considered by others as being a most too self-efficient and self-sufficient individual.

15. These are not all the talents or abilities, either!  For, in Mercury, Jupiter and Uranus, there are those abilities as a portrayer of life, or incidents, or as a screen or stage artiste.  There the entity may find latent and manifested elements and influences.  The greater activity of these, if applied in the experience, will be found in other lands, as will be seen from the overshadowing of experiences of the entity, - when these once again were the part of the entity's material experience in the earth.

16. Then, these are not conflicting urges or abilities but they are the extremes of those things the entity chooses to do. But choose whatever ye will, ye will do it well - if ye choose to do so.

17. But in the end, do have the home and the companion associated in either an insurance or a real estate or a banking concern.

18. As to [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - these, as may be suspected from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] urges, are quite varied also.

19. For, as in the experience before this, the entity was in the land of the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], rather than nativity; during those early periods when there became those consciousnesses of the activities that led - later - to that freedom of speech and of activity among the fair sex.

20. The entity in those early periods, of those who became outspoken for such, was an adherent; and yet was decidedly squashed by those about it.

21. Hence the innate feeling of "hard to get along with others," or to work for others.  And, as given, learn to work WITH others.

22. For, the eventual activity of the entity, as Emily Hurst, was in that field of taking care of those preparations or foundations for ladies' hats.  The entity has a feeling for same, and yet has some peculiar creations about herself often - in this line, but has definite feelings about same.

23. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] the entity spiritually gained, mentally was oft in turmoil, materially became discouraged; and almost reached mentally a "don't care" attitude.

24. It will become in the present, then, the more necessary that the entity analyze self, and as to its dependence and independence.  For, one CAN depend on the entity as to what it says it will do!  for it will, in spite of others, what others may say, if the entity gives its promise to do a thing!

25. In thy activity, then, keep that same character of activity; for it will build to that which will bring peace, - yea, happiness, even in this experience.

26. Before that the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] and [[Greece|Grecian]] land, when there were the choosings of individuals that would bring those activities from the Grecians to the Romans as pertaining to the stage (from which, of course, the screen grew), or the dramatizing of speech, of activities, of emotions.

27. The entity then was in the opposite sex, but brought a power and an influence from the Grecians to that activity in the [[Rome|Roman]] authorities.  Thus, as is expressed and manifested in the present, - good of figure, good consideration of stature and of person, and the care of same.

28. In the experience the entity gained.  And thus with those freedoms that have been latently and are now manifestedly in the environ also of the entity, there is the seeking for expression of that ability in such activities.  It is well that such be given an opportunity for manifestation.  For, while it may have many ideas that will be in a manner revolutionary, they will find response in some measures that will be good for the activity as a whole.  But just as the entity came from [[Greece|Greece]] to [[Rome|Rome]], so should the entity in the present go from its present environ to that land where it may have the advantage of the English accent, the English personality and individuality.  And in same other associations may be brought about.  While it may lead the entity, eventually, away from its chosen profession, it will be in those fields where it may find the greater experience and happiness - in helping others, and its companion, to attain to a material and mental, even a spiritual security - if the experience of the spiritual, as in the basic [[Purpose|purpose]] of the entity, is held first and foremost.

29. The name then was Echoras.

30. Before that the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] or Arabian land, when there were those activities in the "city in the hills," where there was the combining of the efforts of many, even after the attempts of the [[Greece|Grecian]] influence to change.

31. The entity was of the Persians, and thus the oriental, those things pertaining to [[Persia|Persian]] colors, have their influence.

32. The entity was an aid in those activities when there was the establishing of the hospitalization activities.  Hence tenderhearted and true, deep down, - though cold at times, or apparently so, but is cultivated and not innately felt by the entity.

33. But abilities as an actress, as a poser, as an artiste in every sense, may be a part of the experience, arising from those activities of the entity then - as Persus, a beautiful maiden in that [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

34. In the application, USE the abilities attained; do not abuse them, or become too self-sufficient.

35. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, when there were those periods of reconstruction and choices made by individuals for definite services, or - as might be broadly termed - vocational guidance.

36. The entity was among those who assisted in putting individuals "through their paces," as might be termed, to determine those factors, latent and manifested in individuals as to their activities - especially in the fair sex.

37. Thus the entity is a fair judge of human nature, especially of others of its own ilk or sex.  This is often resented by others.  For, when those are met who were a part of the experience then, they clash again - as has been experienced by the entity time and again.  Yet the abilities to control self, control self's emotions, control self's wants and desires, arise from that experience of the entity in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] period, - as Tpi-ere.

38. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, - these are quite varied.

39. But know what ye believe.  Analyze thyself, first, and thy hopes, desires, [[Purpose|purposes]].  Know as to whether these are in keeping with the [[Ideal|ideals]] thou hast chosen, with which or by which to conduct both thy spiritual and mental life.  And keep that faith.

40. Keep in those directions which will lead to a closer walk with Him.

41. Thy hopes may be attained to in material things, if ye learn to work with others.

42. Ready for questions.

43. (Q)  Am I in the right profession?
(A)  For now, the right profession; but do go to [[England|England]] - and USE thy abilities in the entertaining, during the present period of activity.  And there ye will meet one who must be either a financier in banking, insurance or in trust organizations; and take CONTROL, or be the power behind [[Throne of God|the throne]].

44. (Q)  Shall I, or can I, become a star in films while I am still young enough to take the roles I want to?
(A)  This the entity might do, BUT the bigger field is the home when the present disturbances are past.

45. (Q)  Should I take the offer made me to go to [[England|England]]?
(A)  As indicated, this would lead to those associations - if the entity will take advantage of same - which would build for the best conditions.

46. (Q)  Am I wrong in hoping to combine my material desire for fame, wealth and power, with my strong wish to serve others in the way in which I feel I can do it best?
(A)  Not thy will but His.  Know indeed that thy body is the temple of the living God.  Give the credit to Him.  Use thy abilities to the glory and honor of Him, and that will be to the honor of self.

47. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
