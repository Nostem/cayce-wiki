# TEXT OF READING 5664-3 F 27

**Reading [[5664-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1930  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 14071

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5664], Dr. Lydic, Mrs. Jahngen, Mrs. Ornstein, Miss Hodges and Miss Kite.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - Mrs. [5664].  These conditions we have had before.  In some respects we find the conditions are on the improve.  While we find there are periods when there is not the response through the sensory system to the auditory nerves, these are of more the psychopathic condition than of purely physical - yet the physical condition in the body, especially those centers from which the sensory system - and especially the auditory centers - receive their incentive, or the coordinating between sympathetic and cerebro-spinal from the 2nd dorsal plexus to the 1st cervical - these strengthened, muscular, and in nerve reaction, will continue to improve the general physical conditions and aid specifically the disorders as occur from same.  The massaging of the Eustachian Tube, the reaction through the activity of the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]], will aid in bringing resuscitation to the centers in a stabilized way and manner.  We would also use the Radio- Active Appliance, using same with those of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the center in the dorsal plexus region, as to produce coordination throughout the sensory nerve; applying same to the center - or 9th dorsal center - and to the extremities - first wrist, then ankle - alternating same.  These will prove effective and helpful in stabilizing the conditions.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How long should the Radio-Active be given?
(A)  Should be given at least once each day for thirty to sixty minutes duration, and should be kept up until there is a better or a greater equalization.  This will also assist the body in stabilizing the avoirdupois.

3. (Q)  How and where would you use [[Electric Vibrator|electrical vibrator]]?
(A)  From the 2nd dorsal to the 1st cervical, using same on each side of the cerebro-spinal column itself.

4. (Q)  Should medicine prescribed in first reading be taken?
(A)  Be taken for the time being, that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as set up from same may aid in the nerve equalization from ASSIMILATIONS in the system.

5. (Q)  Is same prepared properly?
(A)  Prepared properly.

6. (Q)  How long before hearing is restored permanently?
(A)  This depends upon the response of [[Physical Body|the body physical]] and mental to the applications.  REMEMBER, this is given as psychopathic as well as physical.  Then, the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]] must be acted upon.

7. (Q)  If body should become pregnant, would it be detrimental to hearing again?
(A)  No.

8. (Q)  How often should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  BETTER if a light treatment given each day, though this may be OVER done, but a light treatment for the equalization and a thorough deep treatment going all OVER the cerebro-spinal system about twice each week.

9. That is all the questions regarding this body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mrs. [5664] to send home
