# TEXT OF READING 758-19 M 8

**Reading [[758-19]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/23/1930  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 4636

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and others.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [758].  This we have had before.  The conditions as a whole are very satisfactory in the restoration of the proper coordinations and equilibrium being established in the system.  There should be the proper precautions taken in the various characters of [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are set up; also in the manipulations, as has been once pointed out, that there be the proper coordination created in the lumbar and cervical regions.  To overtax one is to produce distresses in the other; for, as in those centers that radiate with the sympathetic and cerebrospinal, that have been so long out of balance in the lumbar and lower dorsal region, they produce the pressure and the unbalancing of the impulses through the upper portion, and to the brain centers themselves proper; so when these are relaxed, let's keep their proper distribution of energy through the manipulative measures as are used; for, as has been oft pointed out, the massage in THIS particular condition, OSTEOPATHICALLY given, is much better than adjustments.  That is why the regular, or the properly GIVEN [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments make for the better coordination, and allow a normal [[Physical Body|physical body]] to be produced, where pressures or improper impulses, and improper distribution of lymph and blood circulation have been hindered in system.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are all other treatments given properly?
(A)  As has been given, be cautious about those of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, see?  That is, the battery appliances, that they are kept in accord with that as necessary for the pro- ducing of the proper [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the impulses of the system, for nerve forces, nerve ganglia, are as that which produces the impulse for the activity of centers in functioning organs, as well as brain cells themselves; for with the brain forces active, each center, each ganglia, from which impulse comes, MUST coordinate.  That's why [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] is very good, and better than most treatments.

3. That is all the questions regarding this body.

4. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Parents
