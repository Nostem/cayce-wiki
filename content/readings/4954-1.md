## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/21 [4954]'s affidavit:
> 
> THE STATE OF [[Texas|TEXAS]]  COUNTY OF JOHNSON
> 
> This is to certify that I have personally known Mr. Edgar
> Cayce for some few years, and desire to testify as to what
> I consider his marvelous powers.
> 
> I am, at the present time, one of the local surgeons for the
> Gulf [[Colorado|Colorado]] & [[New Mexico|Santa Fe]] Railroad Company and have been a
> practicing physician and surgeon in the city of Cleburne,
> [[Texas|Texas]], for the past twenty five years.  [Also trustee of
> Sam Davis Oil Co. - see [3777] series.]
> 
> My wife [4954]'s physical condition for the last few
> years not being of a satisfactory nature to me, and having
> knowledge of Mr. Cayce's powers, I decided to get him to
> give me a reading on her condition.  He did so, giving me an
> absolutely correct diagnosis of her condition and prescribed
> a most logical treatment for her which is effecting an
> absolute cure of the patient's ailment.
> 
> I will further say that the said Edgar Cayce, prior to the
> reading, had no knowledge of my wife's condition, had never
> seen or met her, nor has he, to this day, personally come in
> contact with her.
> 
> Witness my hands this the 22nd day of January, 1921.
> 
> [signed] D. Strickland, M.D.
> 
> Sworn and subscribed to before me this the 22nd day of
> January 1921.  [signed and sealed] M. P. Allard  Notary
> Public, Johnson County, [[Texas|Texas]].
> 
> R2. GD's note:  4/29 Dr. Strickland's death notice appeared
> listing his wife as a survivor.
