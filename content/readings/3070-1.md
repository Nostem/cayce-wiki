# TEXT OF READING 3070-1 F 18

**Reading [[3070-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/4/1943  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 10410

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 4th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3070].

3. Now as we find, conditions of this nature are so far advanced that only that may be indicated which may be helpful in prolonging the experience.

4. These have to do with the lymph and emunctory circulation, and consequently have much to do with the general circulation - or a form of anemia.

5. Then, this draws upon the whole blood supply, in supplying that which will produce lymph flow through the body.  Thus all areas become involved where there is a great deal of mucous or lymph, - as in soft tissue through alimentary canal, through throat, through head, nasal passages, even through the lungs themselves - when the conditions have advanced to the present state.

6. We would stimulate by [[Massage Therapy|neuropathic massage]] the bursa of the lymph centers along the frontal portion of body, from just below the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] area to the areas about the pubic center; also the centers along the spinal area - from the base of the brain to the end of the spine, or in the coccyx area.  If these are gently stimulated to activity, we find that it will aid in prolonging those abilities of the body to function under the stress in which this condition is activative.

7. Also we would supply nutriments in the body that will aid in the addition of this stimuli to the body; as the slippery elm bark, the gum of elm, that will aid the elements being produced in the joints by the lymph.

8. Add two pinches of the slippery elm (Powdered Elm Bark) to a glass of water, stir and keep cool.  Take this as the water through the day - one glass of this, see?

9. Also each day we would take half a teaspoonful of Knox Gelatine stirred in two ounces of water, drinking this before it begins to jell, of course.  This, with any form of Jello, or the component factors of gelatine, will aid in stimulating the flow and secretions to those areas where there is the lack of these elements in the body-forces themselves.

10. But do find out, seek out, neuropathically, those ganglia or bursa or centers as indicated - on either side in the frontal portion of the body, as well as along the spinal area.

11. Do these, if we would bring the better conditions for this body of [3070].

12. Ready for questions.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Father (one of these for Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], if you wish him to help interpret or locate these things suggested) Copy to Ass'n file

(At your convenience we'd appreciate a history of this case to the time of obtaining the reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.)
