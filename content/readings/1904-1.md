# TEXT OF READING 1904-1 F 52

**Reading [[1904-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/1/1939  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 8338

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of June, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1904], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. Sydney Este.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[Colorado|Colorado]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, there ARE disturbing conditions which prevent the better normal physical functioning.  While these in the present are rather aggravating at times, they have not reached those proportions in disturbance that they WILL unless something is done about same.

3. The disturbing forces as we find arise from, primarily the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system; caused by an unbalancing in the chemical reactions in the system and not sufficient of iodides or too MUCH of others that cause a reaction upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - that is beginning, as indicated in the pressures upon the nerve system - to cause a disturbance in the locomotion of the body at times.

4. Naturally, the blood supply has also become unbalanced, so that there is insufficient of skin foods or vitamins in same as assimilated through these [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbances to produce the proper superficial or capillary circulation.

5. These are hindrances by impulses as well as the necessary influences to be taken in the system that would cause a cleansing, and the allowing of the body to become more normal IN those reactions from the glandular forces, as the applications would be made for the purifying and the cleansing of the blood forces as related to the glandular secretions, the abilities of the assimilating system, - through the digestive forces and the distribution of that assimilated to aid in adding the vital energies and necessary forces to stimulate a better capillary circulation, as well as removing those pressures from the impulse centers that will aid in this distribution as the cleansing takes place.

6. As we find, then, in the present we would begin first with taking small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  For THIS body we would take it in this manner:  For the first seven days, take one minim (drop) in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken.  Then leave same off five days.  Then begin taking TWO minims each morning for seven days.  Then leave off five days.  Then at the third series take THREE minims in half a glass of water (not more than this) each morning for the seven days.  Leave off three to five days.

7. THEN begin with an adjustment, osteopathically administered, to relieve those tensions which exist in the lumbar, sacral and coccyx area, - as well as in the upper dorsal and through the cervical area.  But remove in the beginning those pressures in the lumbar and the dorsal and the sacral, BEFORE there is the attempt to other than make COORDINATING activities through the upper areas that also need these adjustments and stimulation for the better eliminations.

8. Take these treatments about twice a week, until at least sixteen adjustments have been made.

9. At the beginning of the second series of taking the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (in the manner indicated), we would have a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] to cleanse the colon, to assist in removing those poisons from the system that tend to aid in producing the pressures through the colon and caecum area that cause disturbances in the body as to the irritation, as well as pressures in the area along the limbs, the sciatics, and especially under the knee and those portions of the lower extremities.

10. After the first [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]], if it is necessary that there be a further cleansing, owing to the pressures as may be felt through the caecum or abdominal area, take another high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] about ten days or two weeks after the first.

11. For the abrasions on neck, use the tetrachloride as combined in the Ointment known as D.D.D. Prescription.  This would be applied about twice each day, evenings when ready to retire, mornings before dressing or after the bathing.

12. Then as to the diet:  Add to the diet the Irish potato PEEL, but not the pulp a great deal.  It would be better if the nice potatoes are cleansed, peeled and only the PEELINGS cooked and eaten!  Throw the other part away, or give it to the chickens, or distribute it in some other manner besides eating it!

13. Especially we would have in the diet all forms of artichoke, - the American, the bulbular, as well as occasionally the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]].  Not so often either of these, but about once or twice a week, - this preferably boiled (the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]]) as you would boil an Irish potato, but the WHOLE of this would be eaten, you see.  When it is boiled, boil it in Patapar Paper, and the juice which comes from same would be saved to be mashed with the pulp of the artichoke; and it should be mealy or crumbly just as the roasted or baked potato, see?

14. Also we would add [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] to the diet WHEN the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations are begun, but NOT during the periods that the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is being taken.  Take the [[Calcios|Calcios]], about the quantity as would be thinly spread on a wafer, - not a saltine but rather that which is of the plain nature, - this about once each day; this to be taken during the whole period the adjustments osteopathically are being made.

15. Leave off red meat of any kind.  At least one meal each day should consist principally of raw vegetables.

16. After the sixteen [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, we would give further instructions for this body.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Is the pain in leg sciatica?
(A)  Rather as indicated, it is NOT true sciatica; rather a combination of pressures or conditions existent in the alimentary canal as well as the glandular forces that are contributory to, or are the BASIS of those conditions which produce disturbance in the limbs.

19. (Q)  What causes the migraine headaches - chronic?
(A)  These come from that same character of disturbance. After the beginning of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations we should find these gradually disappearing, entirely.

20. (Q)  What is cause of baldness, with no eyelashes or eyebrows?
(A)  The glandular forces, which we are to purify and then stimulate by the addition of food values that will aid in supplying those nutriments to the system to supply activities to cause growth, or activity of those organs as will produce same, - portion of the thyroid.

21. Do as we have indicated, following all of these in the manner outlined for the body, and after the sixteenth osteopathic treatment we would give further instructions.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self

> York gave me your name and address...
> 
> "For the past several years I have had much pain in my leg

> latter, however, is getting better; but I should like to know
> how to help my general condition and to free myself from the
> sciatica - if it is that..."
>

> sciatica, about which I wrote you, and the very disfiguring
> and continuous breaking out on the back of my neck, both of
> three years or more duration, I have been a chronic sufferer
> from migraine headaches - and am practically bald, with no
> eyelashes or eyebrows.  This started with a partial alopecia
> in 1920 and became complete about nine years ago.
> 
> "These are all minor ailments and I am almost ashamed to ask
> help when so many need it for more serious things; but I lead
> a very active life and the almost constant pain in my leg
> causes excessive fatigue which causes an unhappy condition

> headaches and baldness - only because I thought there might
> be some underlying cause for all - nerves or infection..."
