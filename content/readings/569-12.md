# TEXT OF READING 569-12 F 46

**Reading [[569-12]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/23/1927  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 4020

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [569].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same, and you will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Yes, we have had it here.

3. Now, we find there are evidences in the physical conditions of the body, that show the lack of the balanced eliminations is causing the rising of disturbing conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and some accentuations are reaching those proportions where it will be necessary to resort, or to follow close until these are set again in an excess of eliminations, so that the body may keep equally balanced throughout.

4. As is seen in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] trouble indicated in the breast, this again giving cause to some distress, and the greater trouble being that of those extenuations of the connecting tissue (nerve) between those and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the region of the genitory system, in and about the false pelvis.  [She died 10/43 after surgery to remove cancerous tumor from Fallopian tube.]  These show that the discharges from the used forces, or exercising of the organs (that is, discharged by the exercise, see?), are not eliminated from the system. Hence cause the accumulation of those refuses or drosses that produce the pressure in the form of lumps, globules, or lobes forming - and, as given, were these to become accentuated or allowed to centralize to such an extent (as were these to become in themselves of the nature of the formation known as growths developing in self), these would prove such a drain on the system as to be very - very - detrimental, and sapping vitality soon.

5. To meet the needs of these conditions we would, then, keep more of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] Petrolagar, and add again more of the tea from the Ambrosia weed, [as in 569-9] and the addition of the [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] and alcohol in same would be sufficient to enable the blood [[Vibration|vibration]] to meet the needs, were the stimulation by those forces in the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] increased to that [[Vibration|vibration]] in the heavier pad that gives the greater emanation.  Do that.  Ready for questions.  Keep off of the feet, for this in its activity naturally produces depression in the pelvic region, as is indicated by the attempt in the body to lift with the arms, thus brining the torso and the organs of the torso in closer conjunction by natural leveration to the structural portions of the body.  This only necessary while the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are being increased. With these taken internally in ways given, and the higher vibration these should be set in motion in ten to fifteen days, see?

6. Keep the eliminations occasionally - that is, in cleansing system [with enemas] - but never use the waters WITHOUT the antiseptic in same, for THIS is irritating to the body, see? Use the Glyco - at least the TABLESPOONFUL to the gallon of water, see?

7. The diet, too, kept in the way and manner as given.

8. (Q)  In what quantity should the [[Sassafras|Sassafras tea]] and alcohol be added to the tea as has been taken?
(A)  That that would equal that as being taken, see?  in the [[Sassafras|sassafras]].  Sufficient of the alcohol to preserve the tea, see?  These proportions have been given.  Follow those.

9. (Q)  How much Petrolagar should the body take each day?
(A)  Two tablespoonsful.  One morning, one evening - one before meal is taken, one just before retiring.

10. (Q)  Should the body drink the sassafras tea, other than as given in the prescription?
(A)  Take it either way - preferably would be for the tea, for in the combination these would be more beneficial - for one makes the other taste better, more palatable, and the vibrations of both in system at once are more beneficial. Ambrosia weed, or ragweed, these proportions have been set.

11. (Q)  Is the trouble in the breast near coming to a head?
(A)  Not as yet.  Any exterior application, such as Iodex or [[Iodine|Iodine]], or any softening salve - as Cuticura or soap and rosewater - these are good though do not press too hard when rubbing over same, see?

Never lift anything heavy.  Do not sweep too much.

12. We are through for the present.
