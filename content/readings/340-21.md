# TEXT OF READING 340-21 F 45

**Reading [[340-21]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/25/1933  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 2613

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 25th day of January, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [340], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [340], Mr. [348], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [340]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, the general conditions are in a much better action and reaction at present than when last we had same here.

3. There should be those properties taken that will add to the strength and the vitality of the system.  Not in such quantities as to upset the whole of the digestive system, but adding to the system in such ways and manners that which will be for the strengthening; and then the activities should be such as to use up the energies in action of the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]], so that there may be the resuscitating reaction through the system.

4. As outlined, the diet has been and is very good.

5. We would use the Willow Charcoal Tablets occasionally to prevent gas; preferably those prepared with the honey - which are made by the Battle Creek Sanitarium.

6. Also we would occasionally use a few drops of Pepsin in a tablespoonful of Milk of Bismuth.  This may be taken just before the meal, or just after the meal.

7. Then, the general activities of the system should keep for an increase in the resistance that will be built up.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is it best for this body to wear a belt support at this time?
(A)  Best.

10. (Q)  How long will it be before it is wise to eat solid food?
(A)  Right now!  While you are hungry!

11. (Q)  Would Caroid Bile Tablets be advisable to take?
(A)  When necessary.

12. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Follow these outlines that have been given.

13. (Q)  Who might this body approach to best dispose of household goods?
(A)  We have the body, not the goods!

14. That is all the questions.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
