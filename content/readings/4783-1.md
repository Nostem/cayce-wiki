# TEXT OF READING 4783-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4783-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1921 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/24/1921  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12764

This Physical Reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 24th day of October, 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here [In Dr. Taylor's office over Simmon's Drug Store, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].].  Now we find there are abnormal conditions in this body that affect the system throughout, both through the functioning of the organs and the organs themselves, as acted on the [[Physical Body|physical body]] here, [4783].  Now these are conditions as we find:  first, in the circulation of the supplying blood flow of the body we find it is impoverished by contamination by bacilli that is being carried.  The blood supply is affected throughout by taking too much of form of lactics in the system to produce coagulation of the blood itself.  The number of the white corpuscles are more than the red, not in actual figures but as proportioned themselves.  The nerve forces in this system here, [4783], non-resistive, easily broken, both through spiral forces and sympathetic system, through the action of the solar-plexus and through the action of pneumogastrics overtaxed.  We find organic conditions existing in the system through broken cellular forces existing in toto, you see. The seat of the trouble beginning through the stomach a long time ago when first there was taken into the system the lactic foods, the contraction of the wall itself beginning with which being acted on by the gastric juices, as they were itself, produced the lacerations, which have become embedded in the wall itself or of [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of the digestive tract.

2. This body can be assisted but cannot be entirely remedied. There are things that can be given, which will produce alleviation of pain itself, that which will be taken into the system the things that will heal.  By using elm water carrying with it small quantities of yellow Saffron or by taking into the system, water carrying [[Radium|radium]], which will tend to centralize or relieve pains through the system of the body or to alleviate relations of this portion of the stomach of the body.  None of it will cure it entirely.

3. (Q)  What form should this elm water be taken in?
(A)  Be rather in form of tea to be prepared by the Yellow Saffron taken whenever water is taken into the system.  The [[Radium|radium]] is water, you see.  The [[Radium|radium]] water should be used twice [a] day.

4. (Q)  Where is location of these abnormal conditions?
(A)  In lower end of orifice of stomach itself.

5. (Q)  What is origin of this abnormal condition?
(A)  Through the blood supply force of itself or through other forces.  Taken into the body, it became localized by broken cellular forces.

6. (Q)  What is symptom of this trouble?
(A)  Not enough distribution of forces in the system to supply the blood supply in the body.

7. (Q)  Has there ever been any hemorrhage in the stomach?
(A)  There has, in times past, been more than one.

Do this and it will give more relief to the body of lasting conditions, and it will prolong life of the body.
