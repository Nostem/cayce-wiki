# TEXT OF READING 204-2 M 35 (Psychology Professor)

**Reading [[204-2]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/5/1929  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 1022

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [204].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                             ..., University, 
11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of Dr. [204], of ... University, ... Virginia.  You will advise this body as to the general aspect and the advisability of his relinquishing his present position without more definite assurance for the future of making a campaign for the candidancy of the Governorship of Virginia, affiliating with the anti-Smith and Republican Party for same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [204], with those conditions as confront and surround [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as respecting affiliation, association, and conditions in general.  Much might be said in this respect of the various contacts and experiences that would, of a natural consequence, come to the body, the mind, through such an affiliation.  This, as we find, would be a step in the proper direction for the broader, wider view for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and would bring those elements in the experience that would be an ADVANCEMENT for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

3. As to the possibilities of the election to such an office, this would depend a great deal upon many changes that may arise in the interim; but WE would give, it would be WELL for the body to make such connections.  Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.
