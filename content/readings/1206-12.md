# TEXT OF READING 1206-12 F 15

**Reading [[1206-12]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/19/1941  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 6711

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of April, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1206].

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What suggestions for eating to build better blood and more vitality?
(A)  Not that these would be all, but we would have these as part of the daily diet:

Mornings - either citrus fruits with rice cakes and syrup or honey afterwards; or cereals - whole wheat, cracked wheat, cut oats or the like, with the yolk of an egg; and some cereal drink.

Noons - whole combination salads; some days fruit, other days vegetables; and broths with barley in same - these, of course, altered from time to time.

Evenings - either fowl, fish or lamb; combined with the greater quantity of vegetables grown above the ground, rather than under the ground.

Being mindful that these are a part of the daily diet, the body should build to a very, very good condition.  For, as we find, little is lacking save in keeping the proper attitude towards conditions and appetites.

5. (Q)  Are Unicap Vitamin Capsules advisable?
(A)  As we find, here we would preferably use A and D tablets, or the Vitamins as of One A Day Tablets, and one a day is all that is needed to be taken.

6. (Q)  Is liver advisable?
(A)  Occasionally; about once or twice a week.

7. (Q)  What foods should body avoid?
(A)  As just indicated, let those foods outlined be a part of the diet.  Then, these being taken, the body will be avoiding most of those that are not best.

8. (Q)  What suggestions for summer?  How and where would it be best for her to spend the summer?
(A)  Let that be rather the [[Free Will|choice]] of the body for good recreational activities, and most of that outside.

9. (Q)  What other suggestions?
(A)  Keep constructive attitudes, and we should bring better forces for this body.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [1158] - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file
