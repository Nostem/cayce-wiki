# TEXT OF READING 3027-2 F 34 (Stenographer)

**Reading [[3027-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/28/1943  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 10341

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3027], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.  Contact:  [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born September 15, 1908, in Mons En Bardeuil-Le Lille (Nord) 
[[France|France]]. 
Time of Reading 
10:40 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back to birth date - "Another [[Atlantis|Atlantean]]!")

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3027].

4. In giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, these we choose from same with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this information may be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling the entity to apply tenets that we would give as [[Ideal|ideals]] in the present experience; that the entity may better fulfil those [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

5. No entity enters a material [[Incarnation|sojourn]] by chance, but from those realms of consciousness in which it has dwelt during the interims between earthly [[Incarnation|sojourns]], the entity chooses that environ through which it may make manifest those corrections - or those choices it has made and does make in its real or in its inner self.

6. Thus we find this entity entering as an [[Atlantis|Atlantean]], or from an [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] urge (in one of its [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]]), as many Atlanteans are in the realm of activity with which the entity may come in contact in various ways or manners.

7. Thus, as in most entities, this experience will be either one in which the entity will succeed or make a complete failure.  Whether this is mental, material or spiritual depends upon the choices made by the entity.  Not that one may not excel in all, yet - as given of old - many are called but few respond.  There is ever before an individual entity life and death, good and evil.  The entity is given the ability to choose.

8. That the entity has great abilities is indicated throughout the sojourns in the earth.  That these may be changed a great deal is the privilege, is the opportunity for every [[Soul|soul]]-entity.

9. That this entity WILL succeed is and will be evidenced by the applications the entity has made in past experiences.

10. As for urges latent and manifested, these are not in keeping with what is commonly called [[Astrology|astrology]], or [[Astrology|astrological]] urges; but these are rather from the greater relationship the entity bears with this present [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] - indicated in the signs, the seasons, in the earth's solar system.

11. For, it is through this system that the entity is still working its way towards infinity, towards its consciousness of the infinite forces as may manifest in the experience of each [[Soul|soul]], - in spirit, in mind, in body.

12. Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Uranus are the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects.

13. High mental abilities, as in Mercury - a reasoning mind, as Mercury.

14. Jupiter - as a [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]].  Hence seeing, being, travel - all of these, as well as a feeling for its kinship, its relationship with a universe.

15. Venus - beauty, song, music - and yet detailed activities are a part of the entity's consciousness, as will be seen also by material sojourns.

16. Uranus - those extremes, not only material but the mental activity.  And to this entity there may cove unusual experiences, in the mental, in the material, in the spiritual.  Things, conditions, where others may not ever even experience.  These will be seen not only from the application the entity has made in its earthly sojourns but the Uranian interest in the wisdom of the ancients, as sometimes called; the ability of the entity to become aware, in the physical, of a [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]].  Few may attain this.

17. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - not all of these will be given in the present.  For the entity should seek some of these, that are given, to know the better relationships with individuals, especially.  For, the entity has had an unusual experience in the earth.

18. Not that the entity has fallen far from the abilities, - for, as given, in whatever field of service or activity the entity chooses in the present, it MAY succeed - whether spiritual, mental or material.  It may succeed in all, or it may - as most Uranians - be a complete failure; but because of self-aggrandizement or self-indulgence, should such be the experience.

19. Before this the entity was in the land of the present sojourn, during those early days of activities, especially when there were the journeyings into the land with the soldiery that aided in the freedom finding of this American land.

20. The entity then was in the opposite sex, to be sure, and among those very close to LaFayette - the entity being a general, not in name but in abilities, and those associations with many in authority in political places brought favors not only to LaFayette's efforts but also to General Washington's.

21. These activities enable the entity in the present to carry weight when many another will fail, whatever the attempts may be in a political sense, whether socially or in politics alone.  But remember, think not more highly of self than ye ought to think.  And he or she that is the greatest among the fellows, among the associates, is the one who may serve the better.

22. In that attitude, in that approach, should the entity apply its abilities in these directions in the present experience. For, as may be indicated, while there is the desire for home and home life, we find that those experiences as Jean Juahn Destuyvelent made for those activities that have prevented the entity in the present from making associations of a conjugal nature.

23. Thus, even though the entity were wed, there will be very little chance of there being offspring - UNLESS changes are made in [[Purpose|purposes]] and [[Ideal|ideals]].  Do not be disappointed in this. Not that this is impossible, but it would require almost a special dispensation from on high, through seeking for an activity that would bring ONLY glory to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

24. Ye stand much in the same position, spiritually and mentally, as did Hannah.  Remember her, and what came forth.

25. Before that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], during those periods when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the earth.

26. The entity was among the women of Bethsaida that heard Him gladly.  Yet persecutions, in those days when there were the activities following [[Crucifixion of Jesus|the Crucifixion]], brought the entity's choosing rather to flee from the environs.  But attempting to hold to its activity, then in the name Jeseuha.

27. The entity then became the wife of one that became a martyr, when there was the establishing of the first of the deacons in the church at [[Jerusalem|Jerusalem]].  With that persecution the entity fled.  Coming in contact, in the latter part of the life, with the problems of [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]], [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] and [[Lucius|Lucius]], and Luke, in Laodicea, the entity there gained much.

28. The children of the entity [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] came to be the leaders in that church, when [[Lucius|Lucius]] left the activities there.

29. Hence we find an interest not only in [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s teachings, but the interest in the intrigues of political and social natures; the abilities to ferret out or detect the little, undermining things others would do, are a part of the entity's consciousness or awareness.

30. If those associated with the entity want to prevent the entity from knowing all about them, they had better keep their mouth shut!  For the entity can piece, bit by bit, a little here, a little there, to know almost the thoughts of others.

31. The interest in, and the abilities in spiritual things may be aroused from that experience.  The ability as a writer may be indicated from those experiences there.  These the entity should know much more about.

32. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, when there were those activities in which the Priest had set up the Temple of Sacrifice, and later the Temple Beautiful.

33. The entity then was associated with the Priest, and came near destroying most of those activities in some directions - because of the [[Ideal|ideals]] and purposes of the entity.

34. When there were those activities in the Temple Beautiful, and the preparation of others for active service in other lands, the entity joined wholeheartedly.

35. Here again the entity might do well, to put together its activities and experiences.  For not only did the entity become an emissary to one of those lands wherein there was much dissension, but had the ability to unite the efforts - until these were turned to self-indulgence by the companion, who is an associate of the entity in the present.

36. The name then was El-It-San.

37. Before that the entity was in the Atlantean land, during those periods just before the second upheaval.

38. There the entity was among the [[Sons of the Law of One|children of the Law of One]], and attempting to embrace - in the services and the activities - those "things", or the evolutionary individuals in the land by the decisions of the [[Sons of Belial|sons of Belial]].

39. The abilities as an executive, as a politician, as a detective, as a writer, come from those applications of mental tenets and truths through that experience, - as Eschuchu.

40. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

41. First, know thyself and thy ideal, - spiritually, mentally, materially.

42. Then study to show thyself approved unto that ideal, and know ye chose wisely in Laodicea.  Know that He is the way, has ever been the way; just as ye rejected Him in the Atlantean experiences, through the voices as were heard, and yet ye may again arouse this to that activity.  But wait ye on the Lord, show thyself approved, a workman not ashamed.

43. Ready for questions.

44. (Q)  Has any progress been made in the last two or three incarnations?
(A)  There is progress whether ye are going forward or backward!  The thing is to move!

45. (Q)  What progress or retrogression have I made during this present life?
(A)  No one may be the judge of that ye have chosen.  Do RIGHT!

46. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
