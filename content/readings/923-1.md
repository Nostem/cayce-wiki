# TEXT OF READING 923-1 F 49

**Reading [[923-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/24/1926  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 5762

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions with this body.  Many of the conditions are a complication of conditions that have arisen in the body from time to time, and the system attempting to adjust itself has become rather unbalanced in the distribution of assimilated forces in the system, produced by weakening of other conditions and the system becoming so set by the taking on of forces as become accumulative in the system by not being eliminated in their proper channels.  Hence we find the system becoming unbalanced in this way and manner as to cause the certain portions of the body, as are seen in the structural portions, especially those about which there are the soft bone structure, to increase in size and in form and, as will be seen, unless this is stopped, or the balanced created, will continue to increase until all of the structural portions - where ligament and tissue and serum [sinew], as between joint and bone - will gradually increase until there would be [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of the structural ability of the bone portion of the body.

4. Now, the properties as have been given the system have been of assistance in decreasing this accumulation, or this separation in the lymph and blood stream of assimilations that causes this condition, but the strengthening of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, that would create a better balance, has been neglected.

5. Now, the causes of these conditions are of numerous natures. The histology of the condition shows as this:  First we have a suppression of the normal functioning of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system as produce the creative or generative elements in system.  Then we have, added to this, that increase in the system of those elements, as known as of the germ nature, producing congestion in the system, creating destructive elements that destroy the replenishing or rebuilding in system.  Then we had added to the system an increase in the numbers of the cells necessary to destroy this condition - that is, "flu" - cold, see?  and congestion.  Then this excess, added to the suppression, set up in the various portions - first in antrum, then in the soft bony structure - these congested conditions as produced this condition as is seen, see?  This may be termed or called the enlarging, or a plethora created in soft structural portions of system by drosses - an oversupply of rebuilding of the unproper sort left in system, through the poor eliminations as were caused by suppression and by the congestion in system.  Temperature produced portions of it.  Water as was taken and assimilated produced portions of it.  The lack of the proper material for normal structural building, see?

6. Then, to overcome these conditions, we would still keep those properties that have been given the system, as injections, to decrease the amount of the structural portions of building in the body - through the serum, see?

7. We would also add, both internally and vibratorially, those properties as will MATERIALLY CHANGE THE ACTION OF THE [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] IN THE SYSTEM AS CREATE THE GENERATIVE SYSTEM, and will add also, THROUGH the [[Vibration|vibratory]] force in same and internally, that as will produce a normality in elimination, and in the creating of that as will bring about the equilibrium, and the collaboration and coordination of rebuilding in system, and eliminating from system.  Taking, then, in these proportions and properties:

8. To 15 ounces of distilled water, add 15 grains of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], with 20 grains of Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]], see?  The dose would be 3 to 5 drops of this solution in half a glass of water twice each day, see?

9. Then we would take those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be supplied through the Radio-Active Appliance of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] (15 grains), dissolved in 10 ounces of distilled water, and the anode of the positive attached to the wrist and the negative - through which the [[Vibration|vibration]] from gold - attached to the umbilicus, see?  and the next day attach the positive, or the FIRST anode to the ankle - opposite sides of the body each time, see?  but ever the last one attached, or large circular plate attached to the umbilicus, see?  This should be attached for at least an hour each day.

10. Let the diet be those properties that carry those of easy digestion, and from which drosses may be easily eliminated. Vegetables rather than meat.  Mineral rather than of flesh, see?  or of too much of the rebuilding in that direction.

11. Do this for at least thirty (30) days.  Then we would give the changes as necessary.

12. The operations in this condition would not prove effective. The elimination and the equalization of the system must be established, and the NORMAL forces brought about, see? Unless this would prove effective, then it might be necessary to give injections direct to cerebro-spinal system.  Use these as have been given, however, first.  With the proper serum given, as has been in the past, that has acted somewhat with this condition, with the addition of that which adds to that of the gland forces in system, especially to the generatory system, or generative system, we will find this will show improvement.

13. The nausea as may be created in the first two or three days - this will be overcome by using any soothing condition for the stomach, or a mild emit.

14. Do that, for the present.

15. We are through for the present.
