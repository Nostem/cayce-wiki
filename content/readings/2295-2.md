# TEXT OF READING 2295-2 M 70

**Reading [[2295-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/9/1940  Sex: M  Age: 70  ReadingID: 9040

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of July, 1940, in accordance with request made by the daughter-in-law, Mrs. [1237], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 to 12:05 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find here, only partially have the treatments been applied as we have suggested.

4. With the stirring up and the increasing of the circulation, by the excess pressures created, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations are necessary to set up DRAINAGES from the system.  Unless these are done, that already administered is only half and will be more harmful than beneficial!

5. As to the administrations of the vitamins necessary by injections, - this must eventually be done through medical administrations; and as to who should do this and the manner will depend upon the [[Free Will|choice]] of the individual.

6. But DRAINAGES must be set up, through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, else this condition will grow more and more severe.

7. DO as given.

8. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [1237]
"    " Ass'n file
