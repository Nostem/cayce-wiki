# TEXT OF READING 3054-2 F 49 (Government Employee)

**Reading [[3054-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/9/1943  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10389

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3054], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find the body is on the improve.  There are still those disturbances from a distended condition in colon, and those tendencies for the formations of gas in the upper portion of the alimentary canal.

3. We would keep those applications suggested rather consistently, even though with the improvements there is a general better activity of the body.  This is even more reason that these be kept in regular order; that there may be established better coordination in body building throughout the body.

4. Do that.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is there something I can take to aid my digestion?
(A)  After each meal, and especially the heavy meal of the day take a half teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth and seven drops (using a medicine dropper) of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin stirred together in half a glass of water.

Each morning as this is begun for at least five days at a period, take a level teaspoonful of Eno Salts in a glass of water before any meal is taken; so that all of these poisons will be cleared out, by keeping down the tendency for opulency or swelling or gas.  This will aid in keeping the channel clean and yet not prevent proper assimilation, and will give greater strength.  Do these for the present and keep up those general suggestions indicted.  We should bring better conditions for this body, [3054].

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> 2. Do I have a pancreatic cyst?

> do these "abilities have on this particular illness?"
>

> sent one other which was answered.
> 
> I suppose it is too late now to have them answered.  I can
> by inference gather that I do not have a "cyst" or if I do it
> can be cured.  And I have my own ideas as to the meaning of
> the 3rd question as I have a feeling that such things may
> have a bearing.  It seems rather an unusual thing to put
> in a "physical reading".  I thought if I brought it to your
> attention again there might be something that could be done.
