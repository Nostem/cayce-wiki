# TEXT OF READING 2671-2 M 54

**Reading [[2671-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/26/1926  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 9756

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. [2671] through Mr. [287].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will have before you Mrs. [3828] who left [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]], June 30, noon, 1926, was at Ft. Pierce, Florida, July 1, about 9:30 A.M.  She was last seen driving in a [[Abraham Lincoln|Lincoln]] Fleetwood car, via Lake City, Fla., Atlanta, Ga., Middlesboro, Ky., [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], O., Lima, O., Detroit, Mich., Port Huron and to Hamilton, [[Lake Ontario|Ontario]], and now near Hamilton - at Mount Hope, about 10 miles South.  Her father, sister and little brother are supposed to be with her.  Also Ed [...] chauffeur or driver.  You will tell us the conditions at the present time and if the conduct was proper on this trip.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and that time when the body left Miami, Florida, and the present state and conditions.

3. In the conditions at the present time there are many that are of the nature that are best known to the body itself, and those conditions that transpired in the trip were in their proper order, proper conduct, under the existing circumstances under which this body left; for while the conduct not exemplary in the eyes of some, what right was there for the questions put?

4. The conditions as exist at the present are not altogether satisfactory with the body, but are in proper way and manner, and proper conduct being kept.

5. These are the conditions as we find.  These are the conditions as at present.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is meant by "there are other conditions?"
(A)  That which in the mind of the body caused this sudden departure.  Those have to do with others.  We were asked about THIS individual.

7. We are through, then, for the present.
