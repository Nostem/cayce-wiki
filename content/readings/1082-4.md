# TEXT OF READING 1082-4 F 42

**Reading [[1082-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1938  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 6249

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1082], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., appointment verification by [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] in wire of 5/13/38.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       N.Y. City. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, as there was not the adherence to those suggestions that were indicated for this body heretofore, we would have the physician's suggestions carried out in detail - if there would be brought the better forces for this body.

3. It is not well that any interference be made, or any suggestions otherwise be given under the existent conditions; from the mental attitudes and the conditions; for the betterments may come through that which has been or may be indicated by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s physician.

4. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> just as it was for her.  Do hope she gets along alright.  It
> is not because we are not anxious about her, for would like
> if possible to be of real help, but where we can't, we can't.
> Would like to know how she comes along, and will be glad to
> try at any time for her."
