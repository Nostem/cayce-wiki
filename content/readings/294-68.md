# TEXT OF READING 294-68 M 49

**Reading [[294-68]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/17/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2027

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC, present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the two dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and the enquiring mind, EC, here.

3. Without the actions physically, of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to gain those lessons and apply them in the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], there needs be few given through these channels that would assist, unless these become more applicable and more applied in the life than they have been through those that have been given.

4. Then, without this, take those heeds of these that have been given through those lessons, and gain thereby, that the greater forces may be given through these channels.

5. We are through.
