# TEXT OF READING 3548-1 M 52 (Insurance Broker)

**Reading [[3548-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/14/1944  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 11033

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 14th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3548], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  Now you will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [3548], born August 4th, 1891, in Utica, [[New York City|New York]]; and you will give a Mental and Spiritual Reading with advice and counsel as to how he may develop his [[Psychic Development|psychic abilities]] to better help others.

2. EC:  Yes.  In analyzing the body-mind, the [[Purpose|purposes]] and [[Ideal|ideals]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], a great deal might be said for and against [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s application of the [[Psychic Development|psychic abilities]] that are latent and manifested through the entity.

3. In a manner the entity does not need anyone to tell him what to do with these abilities.  There might be warnings indicated as to the acceptance of sources of information, also as to the inclination of the entity to think more highly of that which is innate in self than there is true justification for.

4. Rather would this be given as counsel for this entity:

5. The entity will find itself body, mind and [[Soul|soul]].  There are in the three-dimensional applications of influence outside of self, Father, Son and [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]].  Then, of course, there is the mental-body, as well as [[Soul|the soul]]-body.  These are not visible to the normal eye and yet this body may discern about others not merely their own mental bodies but that such a body-mind uses in its application of physical, mental and material truths.

6. Then, in the study of self there is the recognition that there are forces outside of self, there are forces and influences within self.  The true God-forces meet within, not without self.  For when there are altars builded outside, which individuals approach for the interpretation of law, whether it be physical, mental or spiritual, these are temptations.  It is concerning such that warnings were given to the peoples.  Though the entity or others may say, "Oh, that's the old Jewish conception of it," but be ye Jew, Gentile, [[Greece|Greek]], Parthenian or what, [[Law of Moses|the law]] is one - as God is one.  And the first command is "Thou shalt have no other gods before me."

7. Then those influences without - know of whom they give evidence.  It is true that ye have been warned, "Try ye the spirits."  But this is also that that is questioned.

8. Rather then as we would give, so live in thine own life, in thine own application of the divine law, that there may be no question within self.  And if ye live with the divine law, who may question.  To be sure, there may be questions by some who have interpreted [[Law of Moses|the law]] to their own understanding or their own undoing.  Yet there is ever the answer "My spirit beareth witness with thy spirit as to whether ye be the child of God or not."

9. These, then, should ever be.  But there is produced by same humbleness - not as has been in some instances in the entity's self - not the lording of others.  For as He gave, who was, who is, who ever will be the manifestation of the Spirit - which is true psychic force - he that will be the greatest will be the servant of all.  Not necessarily being as a doormat, but ever willing, ever helpful - but to the glory of God, to the honor of self.  Not to the glory of self in any manner or way, but in humbleness preferring one another.

10. These are the laws.

11. Then we would set, as for the entity, this:

12. Begin first and analyze thyself, thy [[Purpose|purposes]].  Know that thy body, thy mind, thy [[Soul|soul]] are one.  Thy body is [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]]. Treat it as such - [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of God.  Don't abuse it, don't misuse it, but - in mind and in spirit, as well as in physical outlook - keep it as a place worthy of thy Father-God, and thy Savior, [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], meeting with thee in that body, that mind, that soul.

13. Then, know in what ye believe - and know who is the author of same.  His [[Purpose|purpose]] has been "If ye love me, ye will keep my commandments and if ye keep my commandments I and the Father will come and abide with thee."

14. Then, are ye looking for some other?  These should be questions.  Remember, those who had familiar spirits were classified as wizards.  They are classified with the liars, the adulterers, and those of that class - which as ye know is not in keeping with patience, love, kindness, brotherly love and the like.

15. Then be consistent in thy choices of that which will be thy source, thy understanding.  For if the Lord thy God be with thee, who may be against thee?

16. Ready for questions.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
