# TEXT OF READING 1206-15 F 17

**Reading [[1206-15]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/29/1942  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 6714

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of December, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                          The Waldorf-Astoria 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.   Hotel, [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind of [1206]; this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Please spell name of liquid I am to take, giving dosage and place to be purchased.
(A)  Z-y-r-o-n-e.  Should be purchased from any first class pharmacist.  Take the dosage as indicated on the package.

5. (Q)  What causes scale on scalp and how correct?
(A)  This is merely the slowing of the circulation, as indicated, and with the exercises as well as the general diet outlined it should be cleared.

6. (Q)  What explanation for right breast being larger than left, and how remedy?
(A)  This is a nominal condition that exists in most individuals.  If there is the desire for increasing the lesser, massage the lower portion with Cocoa Butter.  If there is the desire to decrease the other, massage with Cocoa Butter adding the water from alum in same.  Do this each evening.

7. (Q)  Is operation on bone in nose advisable?
(A)  This, of course, would rectify some disturbances, but if there is the proper correction of same in face and neck, this should not be necessary.  This must be the [[Free Will|choice]] of the body.

8. (Q)  Is it necessary or desirable to have teeth straightened; if so, how soon should I begin?
(A)  If they are to be straightened, the sooner begun the better.  These, of course, do indicate some irregularity, but not sufficient to cause any disfigurement.  But if there is the desire that they be more uniform, the sooner such is begun the better.

9. (Q)  How may I avoid the skin eruptions?
(A)  Keep good eliminations and use such cosmetics as do not become too great astringents.  The best for this particular skin would be such as Pond's, or the regular Black and White.

In the diet beware of too much of fats of any nature, or sweets, and do keep good eliminations.

10. (Q)  What type of school or college should I attend next fall, for best all-round physical, mental and spiritual development?
(A)  That type as has been indicated [See 1206-3], and this as we find is well in the neighborhood or environs of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] - in its nominal activities.

11. (Q)  Do you refer to Sarah Lawrence?
(A)  Is there any better?

12. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Keep the spiritual [[Purpose|purposes]] first and foremost.  For if these principles be correct, the other forces as they manifest will be in keeping with that as will bring the greater opportunities for a well-rounded purposeful life.

Keep ever before the self that of a very GOOD understanding, not merely being good, not merely being attractive, but be good FOR - and attractive TO - a [[Purpose|purpose]].

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
