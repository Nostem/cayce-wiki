# TEXT OF READING 900-418 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-418]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/2/1929  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5621

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of January, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., Apt. ... N.Y. City, and his contemplated trip to [[California|California]] with his family on January 19th, on the Virginia.  You will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before, also the contemplated trip as has been seen.  We DO NOT find, in so far as the individuals concerned, that conditions have changed in ANY manner that should or would prevent the beneficial conditions coming from such a trip.  While, as has been given, there would be some inconveniences experienced by some of the individuals - especially as to the baby [142] - YET, this would soon - and EASILY - be overcome, and the sun, the air, the changed conditions, the surroundings, would be such as to bring for each individual bettered conditions in many ways.  There are, of course, outside influences that must be considered, YET - as we find at present - these will so clarify themselves that it would be WELL for this to be considered and for this trip to be taken at this time.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Will it be detrimental from the standpoint of physical health to any one of us to make this trip?
(A)  Just as has been given, it would be beneficial.

4. (Q)  Is there any more danger of getting the "flu" in Coronado Beach than there is in N.Y. City?
(A)  Not as much - this season.

5. (Q)  Taking everything into consideration, would there be any detrimental condition brought about by virtue of our taking this vacation - that is, myself with the family?
(A)  Just as has been outlined.  Do that.  We are through for the present.
