# TEXT OF READING 3127-2 F 27

**Reading [[3127-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 10492

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [3127], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 and 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions show a great improvement; and many of these changes that have come about would apparently be just as much at variance to that ordinarily found in reactions in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  But these are according to the temperament of the physical being - the expectancy of the body, the using or taking advantage of circumstances.

4. For if each [[Soul|soul]] would learn not to make itself anxious, it would be able to control the mental, and when controlling the mental we control the environs of the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  How do you find my present asthmatic condition?
(A)  As indicated, very good.  To be sure, the temperature as brought about has aided in relieving some of the tension, when the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation caused those areas where there were adhesions or poor circulation to be relaxed by temperature so that the circulation could improve.

7. Keep up all of the suggested applications.

8. (Q)  Has just the congestion disappeared or have also the muscles causing the asthmatic spasms been improved?
(A)  As just indicated, we have taken advantage of a situation.  Keep it up.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for Indexing
