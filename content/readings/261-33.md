# TEXT OF READING 261-33 M 51

**Reading [[261-33]]** · Series: 250-261 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/29/1938  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 942

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of August, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [261], Active member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with special reference to rash or breaking out on body; giving suggestions for corrective measures, and answering the questions that may be asked.

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body, [261].

3. As we find, the specific or acute conditions arising as a rash are the effects of contact with poison ivy.

4. We would for this particular disturbance in the present:

5. Bathe off the affected parts with a weak solution of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; the proportions being about a teaspoonful to half a glass of water.  Sponge off same with a tuft of cotton.

6. Then, after sponging off with the tuft of cotton dipped in the weak [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] solution, we would apply - full strength - the tetrachloride solution, or that found in the combination of the Prescription D.D.D., in the solution, see?  Apply this two or three times a day - just patted on with a tuft of cotton.

7. For four or five days it would be well to take internally two drops of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water, before the morning meal; two drops of the Atomidine full or commercial strength, see, in half a glass of water.

8. Then, be precautious as to the diet - in the general manner as we have indicated for the body.

9. These as we find should bring the better forces in the present.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
