# TEXT OF READING 136-74 F 23

**Reading [[136-74]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/24/1927  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 381

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Mattie Coggin, Miss Beth Graves, and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same, and you will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body here - we have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are still those conditions which cause distress in the eliminations through the system.  Those properties as were given for the cleansing of the blood supply have not been taken in the proper way and manner, for these would cleanse this condition from the system, but the taking of only one to two doses, then using the properties that were as the rectifier for the organs disturbed, did not cleanse these drosses from the blood stream, nor adding to the system that that is as fuel - for such conditions will not allow the proper cleansing.

4. We would apply - as a local antiseptic for the condition existent - those of Yellow Oxide Ointment, and this may be alternated with Gray's Ointment, which will relieve the local condition - but to cleanse the condition from the system, do not take oils or fats, nor candies, or sugars in ANY form until the system is cleared of these conditions. Using those properties as given - the sulphur, the cream of tartar, and salts - see?  These in small quantities, taken properly as given, will cleanse the condition, see?  Keep the antiseptics about those abrasions and those conditions as has been outlined.  These will clear the system, taken in the proper way and manner.  Take same until the irritation disappears, see?  mixed in equal proportions, and half teaspoonful taken as dose either dry or may be taken in water - PREFERABLY taken dry, and plenty of water kept in system.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How many doses should be taken each day?
(A)  One to two, dependent upon how same reacts with system, and THIS depends upon the diet taken by the body.  ONE should be sufficient each day.

6. (Q)  Is there any danger at the present time of blood poisoning?
(A)  Not if these antiseptics are kept as given, see?  Well that an antiseptic WASH be used occasionally to prevent any infection.  Keep abrasions covered with cloth, so that there may be no rubbing or any bruising of flesh about abrasions.

7. (Q)  What should the diet be?
(A)  Those properties as have been given for same.  No meats, no oils, no butterfats, no sugars or candies.

8. That is all the questions.

9. Do these as we have given, and we will find this will clear from these conditions, for these are the outcome of those conditions of which the body has been warned - but it will not be well for THIS dross to remain in system, for this is detrimental to the better physical condition of the body.

10. We are through with this reading.
