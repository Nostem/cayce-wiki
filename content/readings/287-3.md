# TEXT OF READING 287-3 M 61

**Reading [[287-3]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/4/1924  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 1874

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [287].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. and Mrs. Purdy, George Klingensmith and Mr. [287].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find this body is greatly improved in many respects from that when we had it here before.  There are still some conditions that may be assisted and corrected in the system.  They have to do with those of the nerve system. Also with that condition in the esophagus itself and in the cardiac end of stomach proper.

2. Now, to give the best for this body in the correction of the nerve system, and to produce and to keep elimination properly in the system, we would take regularly the [[Vibration|vibrations]], not from the direct applicator of the violet ray, but from the electrode; that the system through its cerebrospinal system would partake of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] which will give to each and every center, and to each organ in the eliminating system, and supply the emunctories sufficient nutriment and incentive to function properly.  This will also assist the centers in the sensory system, and give to the system that of the [[Vibration|vibration]] that will assist the ear in gaining through the auditory vibration sufficient of the incentive to function through proper and normal.  Take this vibration for eight minutes each evening, just before retiring.

3. For the condition in the esophagus, and the upper portion of stomach, we would continue to take the properties as given, but in regular way and manner.

4. Still take some of the tea as given - Saffron.  In this proportion and manner, take that:  To two drams of the bark, add sixteen ounces of rain or distilled water.  Let this steep for thirty minutes, not boil.  Pour off, strain.  To take, make this half the quantity with water.  The dose would be two and a half ounces.  This would be taken once each day.

5. Olive oil only when necessary for the elimination in the intestinal tract.  The vibrations are sufficient for the organs.

6. Also take the head and neck exercise to keep elimination proper in the system.

7. Do that.  We will bring the best to the body.

8. Rest as much as is possible from both worry of the mental forces and of the physical.

9. (Q)  What is affecting memory of the body?
(A)  The vibration that has to do with the sensory system and the sympathetic nerve system.  The mental or memory forces may be trained by a constant usage of same in work taken just before going to sleep.  That is, set the mental forces to retain a certain amount of reading matter, and sleep as it were.  Then repeat that when awakened.  Gain it through sympathetic and through the mental forces acting in the sensory organism.

This is only begun after we have had, you see, (that which produces it) those vibrations in the system that create in the sensory system those obstructions.  (This has been in times back.)  We may bring these back through this training, as we have given, which is nothing more nor less than mental gymnastics to the mental mind.

Do that.

10. (Q)  How far back did this occur?
(A)  1915.  Do as we have given, if we would bring the best to this body.
