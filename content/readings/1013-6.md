# TEXT OF READING 1013-6 F 47

**Reading [[1013-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/20/1942  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 6117

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1942, in accordance with phone request made by the self - Mrs. [1013], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in the present, from strains, anxieties, poisons in the system, there is a general congestion throughout the system; affecting heart, liver and kidneys.

4. These cause lack of proper eliminations in any of these, working a hardship, causing undue activity on the part of the heart and a very slow circulation through the extremities, coldness in kidneys and some inflammation through the lobes or the circulation of the liver.

5. Unless ALL of this is done, don't do any of it!

6. [1/28/83 GD's note:  Today Ingram Pharmacy, Va. Beach, Va., filled this prescription [1013-6] for an [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] member and mailed it to her.]  Have prepared three capsules, each containing the following:

Podophyllin........................1/4 grain,
Leptandrin...........................1 grain,
Sanguinaria..........................1 grain.

7. Mix these ingredients thoroughly.

8. Take one of these each day for three days.

9. And then take a good [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]]; followed the next day - that is, the second day after the third dose has been taken, you see - with at least two heaping teaspoonsful of a good eliminant, - either Rochelle Salts or Sal Hepatica.

10. Keep quiet during the time these are being taken; and if you're not going to do all of these in the manner outlined, don't do any of them!  For there WILL be a reaction from the poisons, owing to those other conditions which have existed, unless these poisons are eliminated and better conditions are established in the circulation between heart, liver and kidneys, and of course lungs.

11. Do these, though, and then gradually build up by RESTING; not running around or working, but resting at least five to six days.

12. During the period take mostly whole cereals and fruits, and fresh raw vegetables; a little meat after the third or fourth day may be added, but not great quantities of same.

13. Do that.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Have I taken sufficient of the green light treatments on back of my neck?
(A)  Do not worry about this now!  Later there may be the necessity for taking these, but if taken in the present - unless some of this is eliminated - we will have a recurrent condition in the neck.

16. (Q)  Do I have a touch of tropical fever?
(A)  You have a touch of most everything that you may well name, that has to do with overtaxing the system and causing congestion and poisons in the system!  The tropical climate is partially responsible, but it is not tropical fever.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
