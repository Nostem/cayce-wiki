# TEXT OF READING 4685-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[4685-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/24/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12632

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 24th day of April, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M.                                    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find there are a great many changes in this body at present from that we had before.  The greater part have been toward assisting the body to find adjustment for self and has been in many, more than success.  The body as yet we find shows some distress in the (UREBELIAN FOCI) [cerebellum?, medulla oblongata?] of the nervous forces and the action on the circulation to reduce the high blood pressure and so leave the mind and cycle force to build out from its debilitated functional organic trouble, see.

2. With the leaving off of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body should, each day, take the form of exercise to equalize the circulation throughout the system, not violent, either of mental or physical exercise, but some of each character, that the equalization of nerve energy spent in rebuilding the body shall be equally distributed according to the suffering of the parts affected.  There may be expected some relapse condition from time to time.  Overcome these with the nursing of the body in that he must show dependability, and he must physically and mentally be capable of showing that the trust is not misplaced, and he is not wanting individually.

3. The body must have the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the electrodes as given though there is not the perceptible shock to the individual from this.  It works to the equalizing of the whole condition.  With the diet depends the greater part of the developing forces with the exercise and of the equalizer, see.  The diet - use this:

4. Alternate of days in this line, see, - use discretion and common sense, see, for mornings, gruel, either hot or cold such as found in whole wheat or puffed wheat or corn or rice with little sugar and cream or good milk.  Alternate this of days with fruits that carry acids, see, and those that are good eliminators such as will soon be found here in berries and the citrus or grape fruits, see.  Little or no meats save fowl or wild game or fish.  The outdoor life with plenty of company is good for this body.  We would not change the climatic condition, for the body at the present time, see, for the surroundings are better if plenty of congenial company is kept about for this body.  Do that as we have given.

5. (Q)  Would the [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] adjustments do for this body?
(A)  Only if they are followed after the line of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation, as adjustments are not needed for this body.  It is those that produce perfect elimination and assimilation and carry the conditions necessary to make these conditions possible in this body, [4685] we are speaking of. We are through.
