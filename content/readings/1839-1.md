# TEXT OF READING 1839-1 M 39 (Rabbi)

**Reading [[1839-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/7/1939  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 8215

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through wife - Mrs. [1033], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Hannah and [[Mount Ararat|Noah]] Miller, and Helen Ellington.]

Mt. Sinai Hosp., 
Time of Reading                             Rm. A-2, Private 
11:25 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. Pavilion, between 
99th St. & Fifth 
Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there HAS been rather the acute condition. While this is somewhat on the improve, we find that imprudence or too great a stimulation in some directions electrically might bring on a recurrence; or the lack of the prompt carrying on of eliminations of the disturbances which have existed, or too great an activity might bring on a hemorrhage - by too fast an attempt of the stone to be carried either by the natural impulse or increased impulse through the area between the bladder and through the penis.

4. Here as we find, there would be - at home - the better applications as follows:

5. Apply [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes across the pubic area.  These would be applied about every hour for a whole day, or for twelve hours, see?  changing them about every hour.

6. It would be well for there to be taken a mild tea made from crushed or ground watermelon seed.  The proportions would be about a teaspoonful of the ground seed to a pint of water, allowed to steep as ordinary tea.  About two ounces of this would be taken every four hours, you see - until there is the relaxation to the system.

7. The [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes would be applied over the pubic area, in front, you see, as indicated.

8. Also we would apply [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] Stupes across the kidney area, or just below the kidney area, - that is, across the lower portion of the back or the lumbar area, just below the edge of the 4th lumbar of the lower portion of the kidneys.  This will tend to rest the body.  A tablespoonful of [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] would be put in a quart of hot or warm water, you see, and then cloths wrung out of same - preferably flannel cloth - and applied over the area.

9. Do not get on the feet too soon.  No stirring about until there is almost normal reaction from all of the eliminating forces of the body.

10. Keep up that which will tend to make the perspiratory system react normally, but don't keep the body in too close an area, - have plenty of fresh air.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What should be done to relieve hiccoughs which he has had for 6 days?
(A)  Let this be done by suggestion, through such as Kuhn.

14. (Q)  Should he be taken home today?
(A)  If there are great precautions taken that there is not too much activity on the part of the BODY.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Wife - Special Delivery - Phoned also
"    " Mr. [257]
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note:  I am sending a copy to [257] as he will be able to help you get started on the treatments correctly.

Get Watermelon Seed from SEED STORE.

Dried [[Mullein|Mullein Leaves]] may be purchased from any crude drug place.  You might try Eimer & Amend.  You make the [[Poultice|Poultice]] by steaming the leaves with hot water and applying directly to the body between thin gauze.

[257] or [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] can put you in touch with Dr. Kuhn [Leslie Kuhn, M.D.?] I think he is associated with Columbia, and was on the radio one night with [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]].

Please let us hear how he responds.

We have had another case similar to this - in North Carolina. He has gotten along MARVELOUSLY through following the reading suggestions, which were similar to these here given.  I'm sure he would be glad to tell you his experience, if you care to write him:  Mr. [843] ..., N.C.)

> R1. 10/7/39 Letter from Rabbi [1839] himself to EC, Institute of
> Psychic Research, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> It is not lack of appreciation of your fine service and your
> unfailing co-operation, but the press of activity occasioned
> by world events and the unhappiness of my people in other
> countries, which is responsible for my negligence in not
> writing before to thank you for the reading last March.  I
> contacted the doctor mentioned in that reading, followed his
> suggestion that I use certain powers of my own to summon the
> necessary resistance to my continuing hiccoughs and, after 48
> hours, managed to stop the spell.  Later a hernia developed
> or recurred as a result of this incessant hiccoughing but,
> after nine days, be setting up a certain image in my mind
> which pictured me tying together the vagus nerve, I did
> manage to control the spasms and to end them altogether.
> Some other time I will write you more in detail.  Suffice it
> to add that I made the experience the subject of a [[Sermons of Jesus|sermon]] on
> the use of faith in [[Healing|healing]] and indicated your role in my own
> recovery.
> 
> R2. He requested Physical Reading for wife [1033]'s cousin - See
> 2022-1.
>

> bladder stone with success:

> "1. Kidney stones (1) diagnosed July 16
> 
> 2. Sent for Circulating File July 17
> 
> 3. File received July 28 after 4 additional attacks (mild).
> 
> 4. First [[Turpentine|turpentine]] pack applied July 30 (4 P.M.).  Had 1 oz.
> Watermelon tea.  Went into labor 10 P.M. July 30.  Continuous
> to 7 A.M. July 31 at which stone passed into bladder.  Caught
> stone 6:50 P.M. July 31, carried to doctor 8/1/73.  Many
> thanks.  Stomach burned from [[Turpentine|turpentine]] but will be O.K.
> couple of days!  W. E. Callahan."
> 
> R4. 8/10/76 Report from [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] member:  Using the CF on Kidney

> me your Circulating File on Kidney Stones.  I am sorry for
> keeping it so long but firstly we had difficulty deciphering
> the best treatment for my husband, and secondly, we wanted to
> wait until we saw some improvement.
> 
> "At the time when we received the file, my husband had a
> continuous dull pain in his right kidney area and sometimes
> in the groin.  This dull ache began after an extremely bad
> attack which was diagnosed as a kidney stone by our doctor
> and a radiologist.
> 
> "He got his attack on July 10.  On July 31st he began
> drinking one ounce of Watermelon seed tea each day.  He also
> started applying turpentine packs every evening.  By August
> 4th the constant pain was gone.  In its place was occasional
> pain.  By August 8th, when he stopped the turpentine packs,
> he no longer had any pain, occasional or otherwise.

> "Whether the stone passed intact and he didn't catch it, or
