# TEXT OF READING 763-2 M 45

**Reading [[763-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/30/1935  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 4715

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 30th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [763], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                            ... Building, 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Detroit, Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [763].

2. Now, we find conditions very much better in many ways.  While there is still experienced some discomfort and trouble from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in those areas where the conditions have caused distress heretofore, the general conditions have improved. But this local condition has at times made and does at times make for distress.

3. We would use, as we find, the Elliot application of heat for this particular condition; and we would find that this would bring, with those other applications that have been made for the body, almost an entire eradication of this disturbance.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations been correctly given?
(A)  These have been very good; and so far as they have been able to go have removed much of that condition; hence have made the whole general condition improve so that there would not be the requirement of more than six to eight treatments of the heat through the Elliot machine - as applied to those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] through the rectum - for the correcting of this condition.

6. (Q)  Should any more [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given, and how often?
(A)  These would be given until there is an equal balance created in the system as these other treatments would be applied.

7. (Q)  Have the serum injections aided in the improvement?
(A)  They are not so good.

8. (Q)  Should these be discontinued?
(A)  We would discontinue same, and use the HEAT applications as indicated from the Elliot machine that will eradicate same from the system rather than putting others into the system to destroy an irritation.

9. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body?
(A)  Do these for the present.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
