# TEXT OF READING 654-6 F ADULT

**Reading [[654-6]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/12/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4375

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [654], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Miss. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Those applications as we find that are being made very good for this body.  There should be a little bit more precaution taken as to the diet and as to the regular activities of the body.  There should be more activity in the open, and not too great a strain of cold or heat - especially at those periods when the irritation and those changes are coming about in the eliminations of the body.

2. The use of the vaginal applicator or the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] would be most helpful for this body, and would stimulate those activities of all the organs of the pelvis so as to rectify the discharge and tendency for the irritations that arise there.  After such applications it would be well that there be a douche taken, preferably of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]; a tablespoonful to a quart of water, using the spiral douche. These will relieve these pressures.

3. For those conditions in the colon itself, those tendencies for the hemorrhoids, we find it would be well for there to be the activity of body bending forward as standing on feet and touching the floor bending forward - keeping the knees as stiff as possible.  This exercise, raising the hands far above the head, will help in these directions.

4. The use of enemas, or of the high enemas, using the colon tube, would be helpful also.

5. The use of less and less of meats, that constipate and make for blood irritation, will be helpful.

6. Do this.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> Box ...,

> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I am ashamed of the long delay in writing to you and also
> in paying my dues to [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] but I will tell you a
> few things I've been through then perhaps you will see why

> I'll start with last summer.  I was getting along fine after
> taking the last treatment I rec'd from you and had almost
> stopped menstruating for good when I fell in a hole in the
> back yard which had been covered up and I didn't know was
> there.  I dropped down in the ground 4 1/2 feet.  I suffered
> agony for three weeks, was in bed and the Dr. kept me doped
> up to relieve the pain.  I finally got better but have been
> menstruating or rather flooding twice and some times three
> times a month since.
> 
> When I had streptococcic throat, then a black widow spider
> bit me and I nearly died, my leg turned black up to my knee
> and I suffered terribly and was awfully sick too, then I got
> poisoned, had the flu and nearly had pneumonia, then got shot
> accidently in the arm and hip, then was sick for weeks with
> my throat.  It was terribly ulcerated and it turned black,
> and they operated on me, took my tonsils out, was in the
> hospital two weeks and was at death's door.  Then I had the
> flu again and had ptomaine poison again.
> 
> Was just getting better and feeling my self again when
> [[...] Husband] had a stroke and nearly died, was in the
> hospital eleven days and here in bed for weeks with special
> nurses day and night all that time.  I nursed him the first
> week and had to lift him so much and lost so much sleep and
> the worry etc. I collapsed physically and mentally, was in
> bed about two months.
> 
> Also last summer Mr. [[2656] stepfather] died and Mother
> [979] was ill and came here.  I had to look after her, and
> [653] too had streptococcic throat and came very near dying.
> 
> So you see I have been at deaths door a doz. times and with
> all the other's illnesses and the worry I just haven't even
> been able to write.  I think of you often and how good you
> and [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] have been to me and our letters and the
> literature I've rec'd I always keep and read over and over
> and they help me so much.  It has been awfully hard to come
> back up hill after so much suffering but I keep trying.
> 
> I was so glad to receive the little book of your life and
> have read it many times.  I appreciate your sending it and it
> has helped me a great deal, also your last after.
> 
> I do hope I haven't lost my membership in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]].
> 
> You will see that we have been to a great expense with all
> the hospital bills etc.
> 
> It's been just this way for several years just one thing then
> another.
> 
> The Drs. don't think [[...] Husband] will live long.  He
> doesn't look or act like the same person had his mind doesn't
> seem just right and he can't see well at all even with
> glasses.  Even if he lives he won't e able to work much
> longer I'm afraid.  So you see things don't look very bright
> one way or the other.
> 
> I am so weak and nervous.  The Dr. says I'm doing fine after
> having been so ill and having been through such a strain but
> it seems to me I'm awfully slow about regaining my strength.
> 
> I would like you to give me a reading as soon as you can and
> if you will let me know when I will send a check.  Any time

>

>
