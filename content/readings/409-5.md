# TEXT OF READING 409-5 F 19

**Reading [[409-5]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/21/1929  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 3048

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Levy, Mrs. Jacobs, Mrs. [[Rome|Rome]], Drs. Berger and Hansen, Mrs. Kaufman, Max Sidelman, L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [409].  Here we would only change the character of the exercises of the body, in that the body may use self with supports, crutch, or prop, to get the DOWNWARD motion of the lower portion of body - with as little strain on the muscles of the side, or the intercostal muscle, so that irritation is not produced in same; for these have direct connection with the disturbance as first caused distress with this body, or the marrow or bone - see?

2. We would, then, use the crutch, or crutches. We would keep the manipulations, the pulling.  After such exercise, occasionally it would be well to pull the limb, and WEIGHT it.  Not for too great a period, as to cause too much stress or strain, but well that this be done occasionally.

3. (Q)  Will hip in time be normal?
(A)  As near normal as was given at first. For, as is seen here, this where the ooze, or fracture and misplacement - these may be brought to their proper position, but the LENGTHENING is only the condition to be disconcerting.  This, however, will be so that the body may walk and use same, without support, prop, or aid.

4. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
