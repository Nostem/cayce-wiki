# TEXT OF READING 5473-2 M 49

**Reading [[5473-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/19/1922  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 13675

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 19th day of June, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had it before. Now, these are the conditions in this body, [5473], of whom we are speaking; there is considerable difference since we have had it here before.  While the seat of the trouble has not been entirely removed, yet, there is a breaking up or remove in part, as shown by its action in the system and in the condition which is being brought about in it.  This, of course, is shown in the action in the system, especially in the duodenum.  The secretions and excretions from the liver are not in their proper state as yet.  The condition in the stomach, which was the seat of the trouble, is breaking up and is better.  The entire system is better.  The action of the nerve forces is better.  The reflex conditions do not tire or affect the rest of the system as before.  Now, these are the conditions that we find in the body of [5473], of whom we are speaking.

2. First, in the circulation we find in almost a normal condition, yet carrying some of the bacilli as shown by the conditions existing in the larger secretive organs of the system, especially in the liver, and the taking of toxins from this into the system.  They are shown by the warriors in the blood in their attempt to carry out and destroy this in the system.  The body has no temperature.  The action of the nerve forces throughout the entire system is very good. There are shown some conditions existing about the ganglia near the fifth to the third cervicals that are affecting in part some of the sensory organs which it will be well to correct.  The functioning of the organs themselves is very good.  While the effect of the reflex conditions as existing here at present affect the throat and larynx and cause a desire to clear the throat, the condition is reflex only and there is nothing in the organs themselves that causes the trouble.  This is caused by an over supply from the nerve forces but is not to be bothered with, as we have given.  The lung forces are good.  The digestive tract has improved. There is not enough secretion and excretion carried off from the liver for the proper action throughout the intestinal tract and this causes the condition existing in the lower end of the stomach and produces a hardening or congestion of a part of the intestinal tract; it also causes trouble, especially in the ascending colon.

3. To relieve this body, we would still continue to take this as we have given before - [[Vibration|vibrations]] as from the battery. Recharge the batteries.  Apply now to the ninth dorsal vertebrae in its usage, making the application every third day.  Then between this, on the opposite third day, take this into the system:

Fluid Extract of Cascara Sagrada.......1 ounce,
Tincture of Phodophillin...............1 ounce,
Syrup Rhubarb..........................2 ounces,
Simple Syrup.........................1/2 ounce.

The dose would be one teaspoonful, taken every third day. Do that.  We are through.

4. (Q)  Should the batteries be recharged?
(A)  Yes, recharge the batteries as before.
