# TEXT OF READING 3222-1 M 22

**Reading [[3222-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/18/1943  Sex: M  Age: 22  ReadingID: 10621

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3222], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. The conditions here are not very satisfactory.  But as we find, with the precautions, with care, and [[Prayer|prayer]], and expectancy, these can be eliminated - and conditions can become near to normal.

4. While this will require, of course, care of the body for many years, we find that by the second cycle - or by the time the body is twenty-nine it will be entirely well or there will be no use - dependent upon the attitude and [[Purpose|purpose]] of the body and how consistent it may be in the use of those things that may be suggested here.

5. That there has been active tubercle through the lungs has been indicated not only by the administrations that have been made but by the actual conditions that exist in the upper portion of one lung and in the central and lower portion of the other.

6. The general health has varied as to resistance, but the applications of those injections we do not find beneficial. True, it relieves pressure, but it is only a temporary and not a curative, not even a [[Healing|healing]] influence at all.

7. There needs to be the attempt to let nature respond, through the most natural means.  We find the lack of assimilation and low resistance, so that the gastric flow in the stomach have become deficient.  The blood supply itself is lacking in elements that assist in making for structural portions, that is sinew and bone when there is that call on same from digestion to supply the means for the normal change.  Hence calcium, is lacking in the body.  This we find not only in the saliva but in the gastric flow of the stomach, as well as in the urine and stool.

8. These, then, are to be met.

9. In making preparations for corrections, then:

10. First, keep in the open - wherever the body may be - as much as possible.  To be sure, it would be better if the body were in those areas where there are the pines; such as portions of Carolina, or [[Colorado|Colorado]] or areas along the sea coast of the Atlantic where sea and pines are close together.  These are the natural environs, for the balm of each of theses adds elements to body energies.

11. Prepare a [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]], with an opening so that [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] may be put in same and the fumes inhaled.  Use only double distilled [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]].  This is not to be taken but is to be kept where it will evaporate (that is in a warm place).  In a gallon and a half or two gallon keg put half a gallon of the brandy.  The holes in the top of the keg or on neck over it should be kept tightly corked.  Keep the keg close to the stove or heater where it will not be too hot but where natural evaporation would take place.  About twice each day, or twice during the day and once during the evening, inhale the fumes from the brandy into the lungs and throat. This acts as an antiseptic also as a [[Healing|healing]] influence to the lung areas themselves.  Use only the [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] not any other brandy.

12. Also take internally calcium in the form of [[Calcios|Calcios]].  This would be taken one week every day, and the next week every other day.  We do not wish to have too much, but all the body will assimilate.  Spread this thinly on a whole wheat cracker and eat it, preferably at the noon meal; just the one cracker spread with this and eaten - one week each day, the next week every other day.

13. Each day also take internally half a teaspoonful of Acigest in raw milk, preferably at the evening meal.

14. Do take daily two teaspoonsful of [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]], one in the morning - or between breakfast and the noon meal, and one in the afternoon just after the body has rested and sleep for at least a few minutes.  Let the body be a minute or two minutes in sipping the teaspoonful.

15. Do have occasionally - this means once or twice a week Yogurt as an intestinal antiseptic.  This is to create bacilli that may clear the colon area.  This should be obtained from certain groups of individuals that prepare certain food properties, (such as Battle Creek Sanitarium Products). However, if the Yogurt cannot be obtained, then use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] internally as an antiseptic for the intestinal system - two to three drops in a half a glass of water two to three times a week.

16. At least every ten days have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], using [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the rinse water at such periods - a tablespoonful to each quart of body temperature water that is injected.

17. Do not get the feet wet nor sit in drafts.

18. Do not be out of doors without something on the head or without something around the neck.  But keep in the open.

19. Do not have a great deal of night air directly on the body, though do not sleep in a room where there is no ventilation. The ventilation should be from the top and bottom, not merely bottom in any room.

20. Do these and we may bring those influences that will bring conditions for normal forces with this body.

21. Ready for questions.

22. (Q)  Why am I nauseated at sight of food, and how overcome it?  (Doctors here have not been able to recommend anything to remedy this condition.)
(A)  As indicated, it is the lack of the proper digestive fluids.  After the Acigest has been taken, and the alimentary canal has been cleansed, we will see that the body will want food.  So, it may be a few days before you can begin taking much foods.

Do eat liver, fish and fowl, and do have all the leafy vegetables the body can absorb - as foods are changed.  Not too much of the pod or tuberous vegetables, though carrots, yams, (sweet potatoes) and the oyster plant (salsify) should be taken.  These are very good.

23. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
