# TEXT OF READING 3259-2 M 12

**Reading [[3259-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/1/1944  Sex: M  Age: 12  ReadingID: 10668

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by Mother, [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3259]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are real improvements.  To be sure, the body as yet is slow in making responses, as has been indicated; though these are partially [[Karma|karmic]] and there needs to be consistency and persistency, a great deal of teaching the body to pray, and for those responsible to teach it and practice it themselves.  Thus we will bring about, not only improvements, but a revolution so far as some respects are concerned in the activities of the body.

4. Do this then.

5. For regular periods, let there be so much study of man's relationship to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or the reading of the Book, the Bible, and the promises especially made in that book.

6. Let the body hunt for same, as well as telling him where and how to look, and what is a promise, and that all promises are provisional, and that there may be application of this in the body-forces itself.

7. Keep up all the treatments, adding suggestions in their proper order and place.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  How long should he continue lessons with his present teacher?
(A)  These we would continue until through with this activity of the body, or through this period in which there are helps obtained from the teacher.  But it is well that the mother apply much also.

10. (Q)  Is there any other type of training?
(A)  Not just at this period.  These, you see, will soon enter the second cycle and these will, through those periods, show a greater response by the body.

11. (Q)  Will he attain normal development, and if so, how long will this take?
(A)  Will tomorrow come, or Christmas?  But here these depend a great deal upon sincerity, the desire and the consistency of keeping in touch with [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]].

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
