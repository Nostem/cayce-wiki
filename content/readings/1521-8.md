# TEXT OF READING 1521-8 F 6

**Reading [[1521-8]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/27/1944  Sex: F  Age: 6  ReadingID: 7423

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [849], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Fla.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, [1521].  We have the body here; this we have had before.

3. There are many changes in the general physical conditions of the body since last we had it here.  As we find, we would not in the present environ give more of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  This will make an excess and cause too rapid a heart beat, though there is no indication of the heart being affected.

4. We would have adjustments in the upper dorsal or throughout the cervical area and the upper dorsal area.

5. There needs to be no operation for the tonsils as yet, unless there are more poisons indicated in the system.  If these should occur, then we would operate for the disturbance in the areas.  These, we find, would be well to carry out.

6. Activities of the body in dancing and music should be a part of the developing periods for the body.

7. Do that.

8. These activities will bring better stimulation to the body.

9. Do have rubs occasionally with Peanut Oil, along the spine from the 1st cervical to the end of the spine.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is her mental and spiritual development satisfactory?
(A)  Very satisfactory.  The activities of the suggested nature will make for changes, but keep those periods of the study of those things of spiritual nature.

12. (Q)  Any other precautions necessary now to the child's health and well-being?
(A)  Here, catechism is well for this body.  These are very good and the care is very well.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Father (one for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
