# TEXT OF READING 3904-1 F 21

**Reading [[3904-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/20/1944  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 11537

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3904], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  1075 Park Avenue, Apt. ... very unusual in some of these halls, isn't it - what funny paintings!

3. Yes - we have the body here, [3904].

4. As we find, there are conditions causing disturbances with this body.  These are because of improper coordination of the activities of the inner and outer [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] force as related to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]].  This allows for deficiencies in certain chemical forces, especially as related to the epidermis, or the activities in the toes and the fingers and the hair. These are distressing disturbances to the body.

5. There is that which has caused much of the disturbance - a long-standing subluxation existent in the 3rd and 4th lumbar centers, which prevents the perfect circulation through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the thyroid area.

6. We would make corrections osteopathically in that specific area, coordinating the 3rd cervical and the lumbar axis with same.

7. We would also begin taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] internally as a purifier for the glands and to stimulate better thyroid activity. This may change the heart's regularity for the time but if it is properly administered and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections are made properly, we will find changes wrought in the activities in the epidermis and as associated or related to the hair.

8. For the scalp we would prepare a close-fitting cap - oil cap to be used once a week or left on over night, when the scalp would be massaged with pure hog lard.  Not that which has been mixed with vegetable matter but the pure pork or hog lard.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this in at night.  Sleep with it in the hair and scalp.  Using the cap as protection.  In the morning have a thorough shampoo with [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] shampoo, massaging the scalp afterward with white vaseline cut with a little alcohol solution - just sufficient to cleanse same; about a drop of grain alcohol to an ounce of water - just enough to change the activity of same.

9. Do eat more of sea foods, more carrots, and - while certain times will have to be chosen for such - do eat onions and garlic.

10. Do these and we will bring better conditions for the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - We would refer you to:  Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 E. 41st St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

([[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is distributed by The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Company, Attention:  Mr. Harold S. Bisey, 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y.  It sells for $1.00 a bottle.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 12/16/43 [3904]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> I had just been home a few days after leaving my husband who
> is on his way overseas, when my mother told me the story of
> your good works in the behalf of humanity.  We decided that I
> should ask your advice on a matter that is compared with
> many of the problems you deal with will seem insignificant,
> yet to me it is very important.
> 
> I have very weak hair and a decided tendency to baldness.
> My mother has worried excessively over this for years, and
> recently it has affected me increasingly.  The reason is that
> I'm joyously married to the finest, most idealistic,
> intelligent boy imaginable, and want to be as worthy and
> attractive a wife for him as possible.
> 
> The thought of baldness within the next few years (I'm
> twenty-one) is a very depressing one for a girl.  It disturbs
> me not only for my husband's and my sake, but for our
> children-to-be who may inherit the tendency.
> 
> I would appreciate your help so very much yet realize that
> this may seem a minor problem.  If you are willing to
> consider this could you please let me know how much reading
> costs so that I can send a check.
> 
> I'd like to wish you continued success in you work which has
> made happy the lives of so many.
