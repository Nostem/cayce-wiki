# TEXT OF READING 3015-1 M 30

**Reading [[3015-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/24/1943  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 10326

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through his sisters, Mrs. [...], through Associate Membership in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3015].

3. As we find, conditions are advanced so that little of curative forces may be given at this time.

4. We find that the greater help might come from the use of the cobra venom to ease the pain, and this may only be administered under the direction of a physician.

5. Then after this has taken effect, by a use of this for a week, - if the body responds at all, - we find that the bee venom treatment would be well for that character of neuritis, or arthritis AND neuritis, that exists.

6. But we see little response from this, save as to ease the pain of the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is the cause of this condition?  What has brought it about?
(A)  It has been long coming on, or has existed for some time.  The development of the conditions is from the lack of the body's ability to eliminate properly.  This is partly [[Karma|karmic]], partly psychopathic, and partly of a purely pathological nature.

These, as we find, if there are the abilities to obtain cooperation, may bring the greater responses to the body.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [...]
"    " Ass'n file

> old.  His illness first began to show itself 6 yrs. ago.  For
> a long time doctors did not seem to know what it was, but
> when he was forced to his bed 2 years ago, and after spending
> many months at 2 hospitals, the diagnosis was given as a
> severe form of arthritis.  Every method of cure known to
> medicine has thus far been unavailing.  He grows worse and
> his pain increases.  By now, there is no part of him, from
> head to toe, that is free of pain; such pain as it is
> impossible to describe, or even to believe, unless you have
> seen it.  From a tall, perfectly healthy and very athletic
> young man, he has become a mere skeleton of 90 lbs. - 90 lbs.
> of agony.  Oh, please, please, Mr. Cayce, with tears I beg
> you, take this case to your heart.  Please give it your
> attention at the earliest possible time, for every moment of
> delay is another moment of agony for my poor brother.  His
> name is [3015] and he lives with my sister, Mrs. [...], ...,
> Conn.  (His wife left him.)  God bless you, Mr. Cayce, and
> grant you may discover some help for my brother.  Perhaps the
> doctors' diagnosis is incorrect, or the proper treatment has
> been overlooked.  You may have it in your power to uncover
> the mystery of my brother's illness."
> 
> B2. 4/26/43 Letter from other sister, [...]:  "My sister, Mrs.
> [...], wrote you about our brother [3015] who has been an

> a rdg.  But please, Mr. C., may I make a plea to you?  My
