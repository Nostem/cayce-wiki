# TEXT OF READING 207-1 F 29

**Reading [[207-1]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1920 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/10/1920  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 1026

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], Ala., this 10th day of July, 1920, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [207] and her husband.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce (?), Conductor; (?) Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                          ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  The conditions in the body are not as good as when we had it before.  She is still suffering with pain in the side and head and along the spine.  The limbs are still giving trouble, and there is a tired feeling, and an overstimulus in the lower intestinal tract; at times this is the reverse, which is the reflex action as we have in the pelvis, as we have given before.  If you will do as we have given before, though there are some things we will have to change, we will do this to the body.

2. Use manipulation or [[Vibration|vibration]] along the lumbars at the first, second, third, and fourth lumbar, across each side, towards the front, down to the head of the locomotor nerve axis, along the thigh from each hip to the head of the thigh; and from the first lumbar to the sixth and seventh dorsal.  By doing this we would cause stimulation to the solar plexus in the lower end of the axis.  When we are treating the body in this way by [[Vibration|vibration]] or relaxation through the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment, we would give into the system those properties as we find in this:

Yellow Root..................1 ounce,
Burdock Root.................1 ounce,
Snake Root...................1 ounce.

3. All put together in one quart of water and allowed to reduce to one pint by simmering, not boiling, over the fire.  Strain and add four ounces of cane sugar, or three grains of saccharine.  This in turn is added to one and one half ounces of cordial.  We would have the same effect if put in Wine of Cardui, which is practically the same.  Use these in this way and with this treatment, and the body of Mrs. [207] will be brought to a normal condition by relieving the strain of the muscles of the pelvic organs, caused by childbirth.  By doing these things the body will come to a normal condition.
