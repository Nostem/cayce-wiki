# TEXT OF READING 5624-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[5624-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/25/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13989

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [...], via Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

St. Luke's Hospital, 
Time of Reading                          5535 Belmar Ave., 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       St. Louis, Mo. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now we find, as associated with this body, [5624], and those conditions as have surrounded same, there are those of the mental affliction, as well as those of the exterior forces as have to do with the acts of the body and the concussion as was caused through the act, or fall, and that these combined have to do with the existent conditions.

2. While time, attention and care, and close watching, may bring about those of the recuperative forces of the body (provided there are kept the surroundings that will make for the building in the consciousness the changes necessary for resuscitation and recuperation) these might be materially aided were changes sufficient in the manner and way in which this was handled to bring about bettered conditions and a more active force with, for, by, and through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the system.  With the change of surroundings, and these surroundings made such that there were those conditions that are more pleasant, or in keeping with those things that would bring developments for the body mentally, physically adding those applications as would be found in the Radio-Active Appliance with the carrying in the system of those properties in Gold, and alternated the next day with those of [[Camphor|Camphor]], we would build that as will find a more ready response for the replenishing and the creating of [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are necessary in the system for the better resuscitation of life, of usefulness, of mental reaction, of capabilities in the physical for the use of the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]].

3. We would, then, make those changes - and with these surroundings would be well that there be those about the body continually that PHYSICALLY, materially, care for the body's reactions to become nearer normal, or through those that, through the spiritual insight of such forces may aid and abet in bringing the proper reactions for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]]. Either those as of the nurse that would be well grounded either in that of science, or known as Christian Science, or of direct thought, or of Unity.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What caused the condition to happen?  and when?
(A)  When there were those reactions that produced the over nerve strain as to cause a snapping of the cords that keep the equilibrium through the system.

5. (Q)  Why don't the X Rays show the cause of the pain?
(A)  For it's not of that nature that is other than of tissue and of nerve plexus involved in same, of which the body complains, and the X Ray of the head and of the lower portion OF the coccyx will show a different story, if made perfectly flat.

6. (Q)  How can we locate the trouble, so the doctor can treat same?
(A)  Do as we have given.  We have located it, here.

7. (Q)  Can the body be brought back to normal condition?
(A)  It can, through the exercising of those conditions in the ways and manners as have been outlined!

8. (Q)  Where is the best place to get a complete treatment that will bring the body back to normal health?
(A)  Kansas City, Mo.

9. (Q)  What diet should the body have?
(A)  Those in keeping with the desires and needs of the individual for sustenance, rather than of any special diet that might be outlined.  The body will choose, as it is acted upon mentally and physically.

10. (Q)  Do the present doctors understand the condition?
(A)  No.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mr. [257]
"    " Mr. Thos. E. Brown, 139 W. 2nd St., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]] (Who manufacturers the Radio-Active Appliance)

> R1. 4/9/35 Mr. [257] requested another reading for Mrs. [5624]
> who had become dissatisfied because of the depression and had
> divorced her husband and remarried a doctor "who has been ill
> - flat on his back - his office closed in ..., Calif.  She
> [5624] took down with cancer of the breast and then the boy
> [son of her first marriage] with infantile - and this poor
> fellow [first husband and father of the boy] bears all the
> burdens...  She [5624] is a lovely person - you gave her a
> reading some years ago..."
> 
> R2. 4/10/35 EC wrote Mr. [257] that he would have to get a signed
> application from Mrs. [5624] herself, for herself and son,
> before he would give the readings:  "It can't possibly do
> anything but make confusion - the desire must be on the part
> of the individual needing the reading.  Even for Mr. [...]
> [the first husband] to get it, under the circumstances, would
> do very little or no good.  Mrs. [5624] would have to ask for
> her son and herself."
> 
> R3. 4/21/35 Mr. [257]'s letter:  "I've written the [5624]'s and I
> agree with you too."
