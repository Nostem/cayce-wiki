# TEXT OF READING 288-42 F 31

**Reading [[288-42]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/8/1936  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 1933

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Bernice B. Brooke.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [288]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are very good.

3. There should be more UNIFORMITY of eliminations and assimilations and rest.  And we would find that these would make for a nearer normal balance.

4. For those irritations as appear in the cuticle, bathe same with a weakened solution of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].

5. And TAKE an alkalin emit.  And we would find these will be cleansed from the system.

6. (Q)  What alkalin emit would you suggest?
(A)  Whatever is chosen by the body.  Any of these; whether a diet - of orange diet, apple diet, citrus fruits of a combination, or whether it is of the mineral.  The vegetable, as we find, is preferable.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
