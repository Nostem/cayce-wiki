# TEXT OF READING 1069-4 F 65

**Reading [[1069-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/22/1936  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 6216

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of January, 1936, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Estella Smith and Jack Tobin, Jr.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1069].

2. In many respects the body shows some improvements.  Still there are those disturbing conditions that cause anxiety; not only to the body but those administering for the body, and these anxieties, of course, become communicated to the body - thus making for disparaging conditions at times.

3. These are from the lack of the abilities of the eliminating systems to carry off the larger quantities and the conditions that become as drosses for the system, and the despair according to those administering the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations that these become irritating to the colon itself - or are too much to be handled.

4. As we find, we would begin first with a massage gently, from [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach across the lower portion of the duodenum to the entrance to the jejunum, crisscross the whole of the abdomen to the caecum and across the ascending, transverse and descending colon area, gently, about twice each day, with all the olive oil the body will absorb. This would be massaged gently into the system.

5. Begin also with small doses of Agarol [Agoral?], about a teaspoonful three times a day; but be SURE that before twenty-four hours have passed the enemas are given to produce an elimination, allowing a peristaltic and a lymphatic activity of the system through those properties that would be absorbed and assimilated.

6. For the rest of the system keep up those suggestions that have been indicated.

7. Ready for questions.

8. This Agarol, you see, is a combination of Agar and Russian Oil, and a combination of Caroid in same.

9. (Q)  How long should the massage be kept up?
(A)  Until there has been massaged all that the body will absorb, each day.  Massage gently and, of course, in the direction of the eliminations; but begin at the upper portion - at the end of the cardiac portion of the stomach, across the pylorus, down and through the duodenum, across the entrance into the lacteals, through the upper portion of the jejunum, crisscrossing the body down to the ileum and then across to the caecum, then up and across the ascending, transverse and descending colon.  Do this gently, but massage ALL the oil the body will absorb, and any surplus - of course should be sponged off.

10. (Q)  Should more than one enema be given in twenty-four hours?
(A)  One enema in the twenty-four hours, but do not allow forty-eight hours without a passage from the bowels.  And, as we find, as there is the softening - as the body absorbs these properties of the Agarol and the olive oil - this should make for better corrections, better assimilations and better activities throughout.

And none of these, of course, become habit-forming.

Do that.

11. We are through with THIS reading.

Copy to [Son] - Wired also, as follows
"    " Ass'n file

TWICE DAILY MASSAGE GENTLY ALL OLIVE OIL BODY WILL ABSORB FROM PIT OF STOMACH ACROSS LOWER DUODENUM TO JEJUNUM CRISSCROSS ABDOMEN TO CAECUM AND ACROSS ASCENDING TRANSVERSE AND DESCENDING COLON  BEGIN GIVING TEASPOONFUL AGAROL THREE TIMES DAILY  GIVE ENEMA EVERY TWENTY-FOUR HOURS UNLESS NORMAL ELIMINATIONS OTHERWISE.
