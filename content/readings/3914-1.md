# TEXT OF READING 3914-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3914-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/22/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11548

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3914], via Mrs. Harry McWilliams.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those physical conditions with this body that produce disturbances in the physical functioning of the body.  This particularly so through the lower dorsal and lumbar region, bringing distresses through pressure as is created in those regions where lesions are formed in that region of adhesions in the body.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find them:

5. In the pelvic region, in this is the seat of the greater distress, especially in the left side where there were torn portions of the uterus, and in the return to the position in the false pelvis these adhered, or adhesions occurred, through improper coagulation.  These, producing pressure on the organs of the pelvis, produce the lesion in the lumbar and lower dorsal.  Through this disturbance we find many disorders are brought about in the physical functioning of the body, especially when the body is on feet, and near the period of normal menstruation this plethora condition produced brings distresses through the side, in loins, and in the mesenteric system.  Through such irritation, the nervous system becoming overtaxed, produces headaches, inability to locomote, as it were, or to bring about the normal functioning of any portion of the system requiring the strength or vitality of the system.

6. Unless these conditions are removed, and the system brought nearer to the normal condition, the improper functioning and irritation will continue until there is produced such adhesions and such lesions as to bring about those conditions that would form those of the nature becoming in a manner and form of running sores, terminating either in forms of those pus sacks that would necessarily mean removal by operation, or becoming malignant in form such as to take away the whole vitality of the body.

7. Then, to meet the needs of the conditions at the present time, and to bring about normal conditions, we would find that those manipulations as would be NEUROPATHICALLY given gradually break up the lesions and adhesions, and by the care of the person - in taking douches of small amount of Creolin in warm water in vagina (about teaspoonful to half a gallon of water, or that proportion), as to cleanse any secretions from same that are thrown off when such lesions and adhesions are broken up - we will gradually bring the body back to the normal condition, and overcome these existent forces in this body.

8. When the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the neuropath and the adjustments are made, it would be well were these rubbed in also:

9. Equal parts of Tincture of Myrrh and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] - heating the oil, then adding myrrh to same.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this in the lower portion of the abdomen and groin, and across the lumbar and sacral region.

10. When pain or misery of these natures arise from these conditions, the application of very warm packs of salt will be found very beneficial - more effective if those parts that give pain are kept bathed in pure apple vinegar when packs are applied, or rag or cloth wrung out (not entirely dry, but wrung out) of the solution and laid next to the skin - then packs applied.  This will bring relief.

11. For the conditions as arise from inflammation in intestinal system, and for the benefit of the appetite to gain strength through these portions of the system, well that the lower intestine or colon be kept cleansed, even though enemas are used.  Rather use the enema than too much of purgatives, though mild salines would be effective and beneficial; such as would be found in those of sodas as are especially prepared for such conditions, see?

12. Necessary that at least two periods - of menstruation periods - be covered by the treatments, and of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the myrrh and [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] - these being given once to three times each week - the massage neuropathically every day.

13. Do this, if we would bring the better conditions and rid the system of those existent conditions that would bring detrimental forces for the body.

14. We are through for the present.
