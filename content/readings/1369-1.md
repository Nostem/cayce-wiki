# TEXT OF READING 1369-1 F 75

**Reading [[1369-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/11/1937  Sex: F  Age: 75  ReadingID: 7061

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1369], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1151] and Mrs. [1158].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1369].

2. Now as we find, the conditions that are disturbing in the body have been of long standing; and have produced - and are - a complication, though there are specific conditions that are indicated in the manner of the affectation to the locomotory system, to the general nervous reaction - in the effect to the activity of the system in its relationships to movements in general, and those specific conditions that are as an accumulation.

3. However, as we find, in the greater part these have been very well cared for; yet the basic forces from which the disturbances arose in the ability of the system to build up resistances have not received as full activity as we find may be accorded to same.

4. As to whether these may be entirely eradicated from the system or not depends upon the manner of the reaction to the body.  Had these applications as we will suggest been made some time ago, there might be an elimination entirely of the disturbing conditions.  However, as we find, these may be materially aided, and given in this experience the opportunities for a great deal more ease and for a great deal of activity on the part of the mental and the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

5. In the circulation there are the effects of this incoordination between the nervous forces of the body and the abilities of the system to produce the natural effect through the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.  For these began when there were the changes in the body, or some fifteen years ago began the basic forces of the disturbances.

6. These may be materially aided, as may be the activities to the body's function itself.

7. We would add then rather these; not, to be sure, having these take the place of any others but rather have these added:

8. First, we would add the low electrical forces from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying properties that may make for the proper [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the body, the re-ionizing, the revitalizing, the assistance to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system; also the assistance to the functionings of the organs of the hepatic circulation as related to assimilations and the distributions and the eliminations of the system may be materially aided by these.

9. The [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] would carry [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] one day, Nitrate of Silver the next day, and [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] the next day - given on successive days, in the order named.  Do not use the same connections with each of the solutions.  These should be taken out of the solutions after they have been used, and only put in - cleansed and put in - about fifteen minutes before they are to be applied to the body.

10. The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] would be used half strength.  Add only the pure water in same, though; distilled water.  Half commercial strength [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and half distilled water.  Change this solution every twenty days.

11. The ten percent (10%) Nitrate of Silver solution would be changed every fifteen days, when a new solution would be put in.

12. The [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] solution would be in the proportion of two (2 grains) to an ounce of distilled water, and should be changed every twenty-eight days.

13. Now, as for the attachments of same to the body:

14. The first day, when using the Atomidine:  The small anode would be the first attached, to the 2nd and 3rd dorsal plexus center; while the larger plate - through which the Atomidine solution passes - would be attached last, to the umbilicus center - or tho the lacteal duct and umbilicus center, which is just below the gall duct area, or four fingers from the navel center towards the right side.

15. The second day, when using the 10% Nitrate of Silver solution:  The small anode would be attached first, to the 2nd cervical center, while the larger plate - passing through the Silver solution - would be attached last, to the 4th lumbar.

16. The third day, when using the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] solution: The small plate would be attached first, to the 9th dorsal center, while the larger plate - passing through the Gold solution - would be attached last, to the gall duct and liver area, a little bit higher than the Atomidine application.

17. Alternate the applications in this manner, but each day the Appliance would be used for thirty minutes.

18. As we find, these will aid in purifying, cleansing, and making for the ability of the system to assimilate the diets that have been and are given as suggestive forces for the body.  These (the diets) we would not change.

19. Use the Appliance each afternoon for thirty minutes, preferably between three and four o'clock.

20. After each application of the Appliance we would massage the body gently with Pure Olive Oil and Tincture of Myrrh.  Heat the Oil to add the Myrrh - equal portions.  This massage given thoroughly over practically all of the body as we find would be the more helpful, except - of course - on abrasion places and in the organs that make for eliminations or activities.  Massage gently what the body absorbs.  Take thirty to thirty-five minutes to give the massage.  Let this extend over the limbs, the hands, the arms, the neck, the spine, the abdomen and across the lower portion of the chest. All of these as we find will make for the more helpful conditions of this body, [1369].

21. Ready for questions.

22. (Q)  How long must instructions be followed to obtain the desired result?
(A)  By the second or third change of the solutions, or the second or third charge of the Appliance - which should be recharged every twenty-eight days, you see - we will see effectiveness to the general condition.

23. (Q)  Can my condition be cured or only arrested?
(A)  As has been indicated, this depends upon the general reaction to the system.

Let the mental attitude be as for a curative, or as for a palliative, that what is attained is to be used in a useful, helpful, spiritual service to others; and we will find a great deal of difference in the outlook upon the general condition existent.

24. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [1158]
