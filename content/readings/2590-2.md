# TEXT OF READING 2590-2 F 37

**Reading [[2590-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/27/1941  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 9611

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2590], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [2590] and Lelia Robertson.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2590].

3. As we find, while there have been some disturbances set up - through changes of [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the body, these are temporary; provided all of the applications suggested are carried out.

4. We would follow very close to all that has been suggested; keeping in the attitude of purposefully applying those influences as may bring the better conditions for this body.

5. That we would do.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  The intestinal spasms are worse.  What should I do about them?
(A)  Just keep the massages and the rubs as indicated for all portions of the body.

If there is the continuation, then take internally half a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth and ten drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin stirred together in half a glass of water. But when this is taken, it should be followed in eight to ten hours with a good laxative; preferably of a vegetable nature - or of the senna base.

8. (Q)  Would it be as effective to use the Appliance one part of the day and the massage another?
(A)  If this is more desirable, use it in that manner; though it is NOT as effective.

9. (Q)  Or would following this procedure of treatment at another time of the day rather than evening be just as effective?
(A)  Evening is preferable.  If the desire is to do it at other times, do so - but don't expect to get the whole results and do the way the body desires to do!

10. (Q)  Would something else be suggested for better eliminations?
(A)  A compound with a vegetable base would be the better. Follow such a cleansing, of course, with a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

11. (Q)  Is there any better way that I could meet the home conditions, in the care of my invalid mother, without producing such a strain on me?
(A)  These we must meet in the associations with those who would work or pray with the body.

12. (Q)  Should I leave off any of the things I am doing?
(A)  Do not leave off.

13. (Q)  Am I following the treatments accurately as suggested?
(A)  This can be better answered in self.  There have been periods when portions of same have not been carried out as suggested, but circumstances and conditions have altered. Rather meet these in the best way and manner that is practical.

14. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Be purposeful in all that is done.  Know that there is much work to be done.  Prepare self for it.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(10/27/41 GD's note:  Senna base laxatives - some of them: Senna Pod Tea, made from Senna Pods - obtained at Peoples Drug Store.  Fletcher's Castoria, Syrup of Figs, Etc.)
