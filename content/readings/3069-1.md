# TEXT OF READING 3069-1 M 2 DAYS

**Reading [[3069-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/1/1943  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10409

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 1st day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [487], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] Dillman and I. [[King David|David]] Wollermann.]

Born June 29, 1943, 8:20 P. M., in the Sarah Leigh Hospital, 
[[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Virginia. 
Time of Reading 
3:20 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity; that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now designated as [3069].

3. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these in the present would be directed rather to those responsible for the care and the training of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

4. We will find, if there are the nominal or normal developments, unusual characteristics demonstrating themselves.  These may be altered a great deal by those directions that will be a part of the experience of this entity.

5. One that nominally will be waiting - and just waiting - for something to happen; and yet with an inner [[Visions|vision]], as will be manifested in a great imagination - as it may be termed at times by others.  While this should be tempered and trained, do not prevent the entity from giving expression to its experiences of a psychic nature.

6. For, the entity is indeed an [[Atlantis|Atlantean]].  And, as just indicated, there may be those suppressions or those unfoldments that may make or cause the use of those tenets and truths that were a part of the experience of many through those periods, that may bring the unusual usage of spiritual and mental laws in the experiences of those with whom the entity may come in contact.

7. As to warnings in the early development:

8. Be mindful of things pertaining to accidents to the extremities of the body.  [See 3069-1, Par. R1.]

9. Be warned as to the digestion of this body, and be mindful, in its early stages.

10. There needs be little fear of the children's diseases, as commonly termed, for this body.  For here we find as near a pure blood as may be indicated in material experiences.

11. Thus the needs for opportunities for the proper spiritual and mental unfoldments for the body.

12. Without proper precautions as to sufficient of the structural forces, we may find certain [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] lacking development - but these are not in the spiritual and mental associations. Hence unless these structural forces are supplied, the stature may be low.  [See 3069- 1, Par. R2.]

13. In the unfoldments we will find [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] of unusual natures manifesting in the experience.  Not only may the body learn, or teach, much pertaining to [[Levitation (Atlantean)|levitation]], but also there are the abilities to translate much that has not been interpreted in the experiences of but few thus far in the material plane.

14. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and their effect upon this entity, and those with whom he may come in contact, - few and far apart have been the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity in the earth's plane. For, as we find:

15. Before this the entity was in the Norse land, when Charles was the king of Norway, Sweden and the Norse lands.

16. The entity was among the counselors to that mad king; though the entity then, as Nordien, was active in keeping the relationships, and in establishing greater unions with, what is now the English land and the [[France|French]] land, against the Huns and the mountain folk.

17. Through those experiences the entity was a diplomat, but established much that later had to do with those activities of art and literature in those lands.

18. In the present, then, we will find those tendencies to seek strange lands, especially oriental, and those things that deal with mystical forces will be a part of the entity's experience.

19. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, in close relationships to its present earthly parents; being their offspring at that time.

20. Thus in the present experiences there will be doubts and fears, those tendencies for allowing the temperament and temper to get the upper hand.  For the entity will have plenty of temper, even though at times it will appear moody and waiting - and patient; but waiting for its own time to use its own self in its own way and manner!

21. The entity in that period contributed to those activities which had to do with the mechanical devices that dealt with the combinations of metals as well as electrical forces.

22. Thus we will find that the entity in the present may be interested in, or directed towards, many varied phases of experiences in regard to the usages of things and conditions for the welfare of group - or man as a whole.

23. Before that the entity was in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, when there were those activities that brought about the second period of breaking up of the land.

24. The entity was among the [[Sons of the Law of One|children of the Law of One]]; being one pitted against the children, or the sons, of [[Sons of Belial|Belial]].

25. Thus we will find in the present experiences and those unfoldments in the early development of the entity, descriptions that will be quite fanciful - but will be a harkening of the entity to those tenets and truths which were a part of the experience there.

26. It would be well, then, in the sixth year, or between the sixth and the seventh year, that there be sought directions for those who would direct the tempering of the activities of this entity in this experience.

27. Be warned as to those things indicated - digestion, and accidents to the extremities.

28. Ready for questions.

29. (Q)  Approximately through what periods should these warnings be especially noted?
(A)  In the digestion, the first fourteen to eighteen months. In the extremes, or the accidents, the second to the fifth year.  [See 3069-1, Par. R1.]

30. (Q)  Would you advise having a physical reading for the body; if so, how soon - or when?
(A)  Whenever it is needed.  It is very well in the present, and as indicated - few have as pure blood.

31. We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file
