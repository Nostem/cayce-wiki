# TEXT OF READING 3410-1 F 20

**Reading [[3410-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/19/1943  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 10866

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother Mrs. [...] new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., via Coronet article & Mr. Felix Frazer.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[California|California]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, there are disturbing conditions.  Part of these are pathological, part are psychopathic.

3. There has been the opening of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|lyden]] (Leydig) [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] and thus a disturbance through [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.  Possession at times is the result.

4. There is also an impaction on the left side upon wisdom tooth.  The combination produces a distress throughout the nervous system, loss of control of itself at times.

5. As we find, we would remove the tooth.

6. Under the direction of a physician, we would give for some ten days the combination of bromides - or Bromidia.

7. Then begin the use of the low short wave electrical treatments; followed by a massage.  There should not be required more than ten or twelve of these, when we should find condition becoming nearer normal.  Such a massage following the shortwave treatment would best be given by a masseuse or a neuropath.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Should any oils be used in the massage?
(A)  Better use powder here.

Do these and we should find better conditions for this body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
