# TEXT OF READING 2746-3 M 31 (Naval Officer)

**Reading [[2746-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/1/1944  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 9884

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2746], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born October 9, 1912, in Warm Springs, Georgia. 
Time of Reading 
11:45 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the life existence in the earth plane of [2746], born October 9, 1912, in Warm Springs, Ga., and the earthly existence of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] as Tek-El-La, in [[Egypt|Egypt]].  You will give the biographical, physical, mental and spiritual life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that day and plane of earthly existence, its [[Ideal|ideals]] and [[Purpose|purposes]] from entrance and how, to departure; giving the development and [[Purpose|purposes]] or retarding points in that existence and the influences from same in the present; stressing those periods that represent the present era, and that which will enable [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to better fulfill the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this [[Incarnation|sojourn]], giving in detail that type of class or work engaged in; giving the associations with his present wife [2454], then known as Eu-Eul-Lo, his present son [2542] then known as Ax-Ten-Tor, also any information that may be of historical value.  You will answer the questions he submits, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2746] as Tek-El-La in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience.

3. In giving a biographical sketch it is well that much of that termed history be reviewed, as to the happenings of the time. For the entity was rather an active individual in the capacity of advisor, or sometimes watcher between the King and the Priest and the activities pertaining to banishment and the return, and then the closer associations with the Priest in the capacity as an emissary - with activities among other groups.

4. The entity, in the period of anxieties between the King and the counselors, and the propaganda, brought about by certain members of the King's council, was then close in the activities of the King, not as a counselor, but more in the capacity as a secretary, an emissary or a go-between as a secretary between the King, the Priest and many of those of the council.

5. The entity was among those peoples who had entered [[Egypt|Egypt]], being among the young men near the age of the King; thus an associate, a companion.  So, when the Young king was put into authority the entity was as a personal advisor rather than in the capacity of a counselor of the people; rather a personal counselor, a personal secretary, a personal friend and acted in the capacity of sometimes preparing some of the propaganda devised by some of the counselors against the Priest.

6. But the conditions devised by the council or some of the counselors for the lowering of the activities or the general conditions of the Priest were to the entity most amusing. For the companionship between the dancer and the King had been such that the entity had warned the King, as a personal counselor, about he interest of the dancer in the activities of the Priest.  Thus these brought rather the form of, "Well, I told you so," and "What did you expect, when you allowed yourself to be controlled by such an one with such a power over the opposite sex?"  These were rather as the railings of the entity.  Thus the entity acted as a watcher or as a counselor to the King, watching the activities of the Priest and the dancer.

7. When there were the periods of banishment, when this became a fact, the entity chose, asked permission to see, to know, to be among those who would see, where, how the care was given to the Priest and the associate or companion in their banishment.  After the period of such years when there was the decision that there would be the recalling of the Priest, the entity was sent as one of those to acquaint the Priest and the companion personally, from the King, as to their return to those activities in the Egyptian land.

8. With the return and the rejuvenation of the Priest, the interest of the entity was aroused in the Priest's determination to prepare individuals or groups for activities as emissaries.

9. Thus the entity sought permission of the King to become one of those in the service of the Temple Beautiful, or in the school or college that prepared the groups or individuals to become emissaries to other lands.

10. The entity chose the Carpathian and the [[Gobi Desert|Gobi]] land, as those lands where the entity as an emissary could be better acquainted with the sources from which not only the King but the Priest had received their training; and the entity prepared itself to establish better relationships with the peoples in the [[Gobi Desert|Gobi]] land.

11. Thus the entity and those who were in authority in the [[Gobi Desert|Gobi]] land found much in common.  It would be well that such relationships come about in the present, it would be well for these to meet and to be conscious of the position of each.

12. The activities of the entity throughout that period were in relation to the friendships that in the present make the entity a leader, a counselor with those of other groups.  As the entity applies self in the present, and as we have indicated, while there is a great deal of travel upon the sea, there must be the preparations for the entity's activities upon the land - if there would be the greater activities of the entity's abilities in supplying to groups that which may be beneficial.

13. It would be well if the entity would work in its activities, on the land through the associations or connections that might be established with or through that one who was the ruler in the Gobi land when the entity was emissary there.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Do you refer to [877]?
(A)  The ruler there, in the present known as [877].

16. (Q)  What was my association then with my present wife [2454]?
(A)  An acquaintance and one from whom much instruction was received in the activities in the Temple.

17. (Q)  My son [2542]?
(A)  An associate and a companion, as has been indicated.

18. (Q)  Clarify the statement given concerning that period as to my ability to meet or to apply self in relationship to others.
(A)  As an instructor, as a teacher, as an officer or in a director of groups.

19. (Q)  What was the nature of my physical form - color of skin?
(A)  Almost copper-colored and in heighth, 5 ft. 11-1/2 inches; weighing about 170 pounds.

20. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one to be sent to Mr. [877]) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
