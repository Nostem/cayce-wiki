# TEXT OF READING 4648-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4648-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/28/1911  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12570

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 28th day of February, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno. Mr. [4648].]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M.

1. LBC:  You have before you, [4648], who is in this room with you.  Go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly and tell us what his condition is now.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had him here before.  His condition is much bettered from what we have had it before.  We have it cleared through the blood, the straining particles of the blood, or the retarding process into the stomach, digestive, has passed into the new blood through the lung here, entered the circulation and has produced a different status through the whole system.

3. While we have some signs, or ligaments, - enlarged nerve, that is the walls are enlarged, you see, along at the ends here at the 5th and 6th dorsal, still we have the condition to the supply to the blood force to the nerve force proper is here in a state of rest, from the sympathetic and cerebrospinal and cardiac plexus, interlateral nerves coming between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal, supplying force to both of these at the same time, through the circulation and nerve supply to these, and the condition has been improved there, by the condition that has existed in here, or from things taken in here to retard or strain the blood.

4. It is true, we have a different status here in the blood from the conditions which have existed in the body by the eruption here in the skin.

5. As we have the condition in the body now, it needs only to exercise itself more here, thoroughly.  Keep assimilation in the stomach through things taken into it.  Plenty of salt into the food of everything that is taken into it.  Something that will assimilate to the system.

6. (Q)  Any other treatment you would prescribe?
(A)  This body is well.  It don't need any more, except keeping the forces from returning here, you see.
