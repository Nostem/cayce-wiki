# TEXT OF READING 2233-2 F 37

**Reading [[2233-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/30/1925  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 8956

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 30th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [2233].

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [2233].]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.  You will answer any questions relative to this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find the body somewhat different in its functioning and in the incentive through the functioning of the system than that as we had before.  The general conditions in the system are improved.  The tissue in the eliminating centers, and from those portions where the toxins and poisons have been taken in the functioning of the system, this we find on the improve.  That is, through the region of the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|Glands]] or Patches.

4. In the blood stream, these bacilli as have shown in the stream itself are gradually diminishing.  The effect of this toxin in the system, in the effect as is produced in the nerve system, is gradually on the improve.  The conditions only need be kept in the way and manner that will bring about the perfect eliminations in the system, through their proper channels, preventing the blood supply from taking on and attempting to eliminate through capillary circulation those portions in its entirety that should be eliminated through dross portions of the body.

5. We would still take those properties as have been given for the system.  With this we renew the tissue and the system in the manner in which the lymph and the blood supply are able to destroy and rebuild cellular tissue in blood stream.  This gives new life, or renew life to tissue in the nerve system, especially sympathetic, and adds to the resistance of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in body.

6. Well that those portions about which this toxin gives distress that there be used saturated solutions of salts or of [[Baking Soda|soda]].  Salts once, [[Baking Soda|soda]] next, when the pain becomes severe.  Adding, or driving in with the hot packs (moist) of heated sea salt.  This we find will assist the system in the reduction of the conditions and carry the properties through their proper channels.

7. We would do that, and keep the conditions for the body, with the diets, as has been given, and we will bring the normal conditions for this body.

8. (Q)  What is the actual condition of the knee joints?
(A)  In this region we find the tendrons [tendons] show how that the toxins in their action in lymphatic circulation cause the distresses to the action in tendrons [tendons], producing then the soreness, at times the swelling and pain as is produced through.  The actual condition then, drosses in system gather in tendrons [tendons], there producing the distressing effects to [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

9. We are through.
