# TEXT OF READING 254-79

**Reading [[254-79]]** · Series: 250-261 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/28/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1217

This psychic reading given vouluntarily [without suggestion to obtain it], by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of September, 1934, came at end of 673-1 for a 25 year old woman with a brain tumor (inoperable and incurable as per doctors).

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           Arctic Crescent, 
4:20 P. M.                                [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

1. EC:  How much suffering there is!  And how happy those should be that have been called to a purposefulness in relieving suffering physically, and most of all in bringing and in giving hope to those who find life's pathway in the material world beset with shadows and doubts and false hopes.

2. Give ye thanks for THY own calling; for whom He has loved He has predestined - and ye are in thine own light when ye allow the foibles of material things to hinder in thy application of that THOU KNOWEST to do.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/28/34 MD's note:  EC was a long time waking after above
> volunteered message [254-79], snored slightly as in normal
> sleep.  When waking he asked, "What's the matter?"  GC
> answered, "Nothing - did you have a [[Dreams|dream]]?"  He said,
> "Uh-huh," then told this [[Dreams|dream]]:
> 
> "I kept seeing things.  Went into places and saw things.
> Went into one place that was very beautiful.  Somebody took
> my feet up in her lap.  Then I went to another place where
> there was beautiful music.  Then I saw another place where
> somebody was being buried, and I saw another place - Oh, I
> don't know - there were so many - "
> 
> R2. 7/17/61 GD's note:  We interpreted this [volunteered reading
> 254-79] to be a message of encouragement to the staff,
> especially to EC and GC who were concerned over finances and
> inability to keep up monthly payments on their home.
> Interestingly enough, when Baldwin Brothers had the property
> re-surveyed with the idea of refinancing it, discovery was
> made that the house was built half on the lot next door, not
> even owned by the loan company.  This worked out beautifully
> for the Cayce family, because it took the loan company almost
> a year to get everything straightened out [to quietly buy the
> lot on which the other half of the house rested, etc.] and
> during the interim no payments needed to be made.  [See
> 9/13/34 reading 538-38 which advised that re-financing be
> sought.]
