# TEXT OF READING 5664-5 F 27

**Reading [[5664-5]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1930  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 14073

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5664], Dr. Lydic, Mrs. Jahngen and Nancy, Mrs. Kaufman, Mrs. House, and [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, these conditions are coming along NICELY.  The body should be PERFECTLY satisfied with the reactions as are being set up.  Keep that as will keep a reduction in the fat producing in the system, and we will find the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as related to the auditories will respond.  Keep this up.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Why do ears ache at times?
(A)  Retraction.  That's a very good sign.  Don't be afraid of that.

3. (Q)  Should there be any change in diet?
(A)  Just so they don't give anything that's fattening!

4. (Q)  How does the reducing of the avoirdupois affect the hearing?
(A)  Make it more sensitive to all reactions in the system.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
