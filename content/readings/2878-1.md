# TEXT OF READING 2878-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2878-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1943  Sex: F  ReadingID: 10110

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the sister - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [1703].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Bed #2, Room 290, 
Time of Reading                         [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] Hospital, 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.  Detroit, Mich.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, while the body is responding somewhat, there is still much to be desired.

4. We find that the acute conditions arise from an obstruction or a telescoping in a portion of the upper part of jejunum, and this is a complication with a liver and kidney circulation.

5. There may be the prevention of operative measures, as we find, if there will be the wet heat applied along the spine before there is the attempt to relax and stimulate the circulation through these areas.  This, as we find, may prevent the necessity of operative measures.  Without the keeping of this area in better circulation, operative measures will be required.

6. These are the suggestions, then:

7. In the area from the 2nd and 3rd to the 9th dorsal, apply wet heat for at least twenty minutes before the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations and adjustments are made.  With such adjustments, to be sure, coordinate the cervical and the lumbar circulation with the corrections made in the dorsal area.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What produced this telescoping in the jejunum?
(A)  As indicated, a disturbance in liver-kidney circulation.

10. (Q)  Why did she improve once?
(A)  Because the correct applications were made!  But, as just given, it will be necessary to keep these in the directions indicated.  And these may be done the more easily in the manner suggested.

Do that.

11. We are through for the present.

Two copies to sister - Air Mail, Special Delivery (one for Copy to Ass'n file                     [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] in charge)

Wired as follows:  OBSTRUCTION OR TELESCOPING IN UPPER JEJUNUM COMPLICATED WITH LIVER KIDNEY CIRCULATION  PREVENT OPERATION BY APPLYING WET HEAT WHOLE DORSAL AREA FOR TWENTY MINUTES BEFORE RELAXING AND STIMULATING CIRCULATION OSTEOPATHICALLY THROUGH THESE AREAS  COORDINATE CERVICAL LUMBAR CIRCULATION WITH CORRECTIONS MADE IN DORSALS  FULL REPORT AIR SPECIAL.
