# TEXT OF READING 1274-16

**Reading [[1274-16]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/4/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6881

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of April, 1928.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. 
[Sixteenth reading given on Broad Creek Channel treasure 
Monroe County, [[Florida|Florida]].  On 2/24/32 reading was loaned to 
Mr. [943] by HLC and not returned.]

---

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 5/7/28 Mr. [943]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> [[Florida|Miami]], Fla.
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I was at Broad Creek Channel during Saturday and Sunday and
> made another thorough search for the treasure and if it is
> within the forty square feet it must be down under a layer of
> hard rock as I have covered the entire forty square feet in
> detail either by excavation or probing down to the solid rock
> and the reading says that it is from six to twelve inches
> under the mangrove roots.  I cannot say as to just to what
> I think at the present time, but, I still believe in the
> information given by the forces.  I do not understand unless
> the correct information is not being given for some reason.
> All the people that I have interested in helping defray part
> of the expense are through and say that they think that I am
> crazy and this has been the result from Mr. [...] to the last
> party that helped me in the project, so if there is some way
> or some question that good [could?] be asked the forces with
> reference to the correct information and with reference to
> the exact location I would appreciate you giving another
> reading and asking the following question if you think it
> proper:
> 
> You have before you the buried treasure at Broad Creek
> channel and all the information that has been given regarding
> it and the inquiring mind of [943] who has attempted to
> follow the instructions given regarding the location of the
> Treasure and particularly the information regarding the
> location within the forty square feet, without success.  Will
> the forces correlate the information given so that a specific
> location will be given as understandable to the mind of
> [943].  The mind of [943] does not question the information
> given but seeks to find the treasure so that its contents may
> do some good to mankind.  Time and money and particular money
> for the expense of trying to locate the treasure are limited
> and the mind of [943] seeks once more information concerning
> the location of the treasure.
> 
> And in addition the the above, Mr. Cayce, please ask the
> following questions:
> 
> Give size of chest
> Depth buried under mangrove roots
> When was treasure buried
> Number of feet from broad Channel
> Has chest been probed through by searchers.
> 
> Please give a reading this week if you can Mr. Cayce as my
> time will not be available soon to devote this work as I must
> find work to do of some kind.
> 
> If you can give a reading on what I should do and what I
> should follow in order to carry out the [[Ideal|ideals]] and plans
> which we have in mind.
> 
> With kindest personal regards,
> 
> Sincerely yours, [943]
> 
> R2. 5/7/28 Mr. Thomas' ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> A. H. Thomas, Incorporated
> Real Estate - Investments
> 149 East Flagler St., P.O. Box 1693
> [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]]
> 
> Mr. Edward Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,-
> 
> Please pardon me, but what I am to say is of the most vital
> importance to me, and you too, and others concerned, but this
> part Mr. Cayce in between you and I, that is if you so desire
> it, and it may be for the best to treat it so, and as for me
> it is absolutely confidential, and will be kept so, and go
> with me to my grave if necessary.
> 
> Now, Mr. Cayce, I am interested in the buried treasure near
> Broad Creek Channel, the same as you have given various
> readings, for Mr. [943], and for which I have financed of
> late, being the one who sent Mr. [943] to see you, and sent
> him the fifty dollars by wire for you when he was there, and
> I have financed now to date five expeditions in search of
> this treasure, and it was I who did locate the block of wood,
> which in a later reading you have used as the S.E. starting
> point for us when we cleared off the forty feet and made
> other searches.
> 
> So much for all this, and I am glad and happy to know I could
> do it, and God in Heaven knows I had every good [[Purpose|purpose]] and
> thought in view by doing it, which was to help us all out of
> the financial difficulties, and for you to further your good
> cause and work which I understand you are doing, and may God
> Bless you in this.
> 
> But Mr. Cayce, is there something wrong with some one or any
> one of us?  if so I want to know it, and how and what to do
> to correct it, that the work may be carried to success, for
> I believe the treasure is there, and it has come to me in a
> [[Visions|vision]] so clearly that I can see it as it lies there, and
> will make a sketch on back of this to show you as it appears
> to me, and I might add that I stopped to have a talk with a
> reader of minds I would call it, but do not know, any way
> they told me, and drew a sketch almost identical with my
> [[Visions|vision]], but went farther to say the treasure was six feet
> deep, and covered with stones, and that there were nine men
> in the party who hid treasure, and they were all killed to
> the last one being an indian, and he would not betray the
> secret, and he too was disposed of, but we went down again,
> and with probes we struck some rock at six feet, and on
> through this the probe could be forced to nine feet, striking
> solid rock as we find at about four feet elsewhere within the
> forty feet.
> 
> Now Dear Mr. Cayce, is there anything you can do further
> to help on this, and if there is anything wrong with me at
> heart, please tell me so, go into a reading if you will, and
> tell me this, and if it is some one else tell me so, and tell
> me what to do, and I will do it, and I will never betray any
> one or anything, but work it out, and do only the right thing
> about it & with all concerned.  We have worked hard &
> faithful & it has cost me several hundred dollars in cash
> & I really need it now, as things are so very bad here in
> every way.  Please pardon me for writing you, but I feel it
> my duty to you, also myself & others.
> 
> Very kindly, A. H. Thomas
>

> above letter depicting and explaining [[Visions|vision]] of treasure's
> location re 1274-16.]
