# TEXT OF READING 4666-3 M 35 (Inventor)

**Reading [[4666-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/20/1928  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 12604

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [4666] - this we have had before.  [[Dreams|Dreams]] and [[Visions|visions]], as has been given, for this body, come in varied manners and are the correlation of experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] with those various cosmic forces, and the information comes respecting varied conditions as are in the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  Last night or this morning.  One spoke to me saying, "Tell [900] and [195] to sell Sears-Roebuck short for 50 point decline and then buy ten thousand shares for a 100 point advance.  The profit will be 750 thousand dollars. It will be a very quick turn."
(A)  Here we have presented just those conditions as have been given.  While there is to be some changes, as has been seen, respecting this particular stock, and there may be profits gained by the turns as are to occur, but we do NOT find such radical changes.  Rather as has been given, well were this stock bought at the decline, put away and forgotten, for the increases as will come will be much more satisfactory than attempting to carry such a stock both ways. As to changes, as has been given, there is to be declines in themarket.  As to the particular stock, with changes, there will be much in this, but there will be hedging as well.

3. (Q)  When can this decline in this particular stock be expected?
(A)  With the general break as is to soon occur, as has been given.  Well, when this has reached the nominal point, or low point, that there be the advantage taken of such.

4. (Q)  Any other information [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] cares to give?
(A)  That as has been given would be well for the body to consider well, for - as is seen and given - that upon which the entity allows self to become so attuned to as to receive those impressions or experiences of ones in the cosmic forces, so may the body-consciousness become aware of the application of that given.  Keep, then, rather in that field in which the consciousness is attuned nominally.

5. We are through for the present.
