# TEXT OF READING 4893-3 F 55

**Reading [[4893-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/13/1927  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 12910

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by Mrs. C. W. Dillman and Mrs. Cooney.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                          ... Avenue, 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. LBC:  You will go over this body and tell us the physical condition at this time.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. The physical conditions, as we see, have gradually grown to be more exacting, or more distress, more tautness, more of the gradual hardening of the muscular tissue, more of the drawing and contracting throughout the body, or the extremities, and now directed towards the body.

4. We find while there have been times when an allay or an ally to the condition has brought some relief, yet never have those conditions been carried out, or carried forward in the way and manner as was given for the rejuvenation of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system that would have overcome or met the needs of the body.  While the condition may be allayed at the present time we do not find that it could be, or would be now, removed entirely from the body - but the requirements as will be necessary for the overcoming of these conditions will be [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]], and it will be necessary for persistency on the part of those who would apply these conditions for the body, for these must of necessity change from time to time.  These are the conditions as we find them.

5. (Q)  What treatment would you give for the present condition, now?
(A)  We would apply those properties of [[Radium|radium]] water, as medicinal internal properties.

We would use as [[Massage Therapy|massage]] for the cerebro-spinal system, especially in the extremity radiating centers, that of the Balsam of Sulphur.  This to be used at least twice each week.

The [[Radium|radium]] water would be given under the direction of a physician.

Do that for TWENTY-EIGHT DAYS.  Then we will change that as necessary for the betterment of the conditions for the body, Mrs. [4893].
