# TEXT OF READING 900-155 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-155]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/1/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5358

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-154] as given Saturday Morning, Oct. 31, 1925, in relation to the price on L & N.  Also the position occupied by [900] and [137] of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and you will tell us what is the proper course for them to pursue in relation to same at the present time, giving this full and in detail, that we may understand same, and you will also have in this relation their position with Southern Railroad, Arnold and Constable, and you will answer questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given and those positions as are occupied by [900] and [137] at the present time in respect to same.  The conditions as existed in that as regarding the price as given on L & N, as to which the price would go on Saturday, as seen, this did not occur in fact, as the conditions that go to make up that intent and spirit of same is [was] changed in the way of its manifestation in that force as manifested through those in control of such.  Hence the change, then, rather begun on the up grade than the down, for we find those in the outside gave the larger buying orders on this particular stock, running then to four - three - thousand, see?  This then increased rather than diminished, this intent as was set by those in control of same, guided, directed, by that law as has been given as for changes in the manifestations of a force as is set in motion by man, that goes to make up the whole Spirit Force, for while we see the price of this particular stock (L & N now we are speaking of) will decline to the figure as has been given, this in its present position is put at a distance, for the larger holders to unload, as it were, those shares held by same, so with the combine, and the merger as will come, these then will be in the nature of the condition, see?  that is, at that time will the prices soar the lowest, see?

3. As the position that is now occupied by those holding L & N short, when the price which we find will recede again to near the 26 (126) mark, then cover and buy for the upper grade, for we will find this will now increase, see, rather than diminish, for the present conditions, for the Spirit of the Force of that is turned, see?  Then the position is to cover and buy for the up turn of this market, see?

4. In that of Southern, we still find that will advance to 122 mark, when the greater decline to the 15 - 14 - will again appear, see?  Then gradually going in the next five weeks to the higher marks.

5. Let it then be given this as a residue, or resume, of the conditions:

6. As have been given in the relation to the quotations as given regarding L & N Stocks, when the first was given [900-131?] (information meant, see?) the greater changes will appear in this stock being the higher than any of the others at this time, in fluctuations, see?  and the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] of many was to bring the decrease to the low measure, as given. Then the change set in and the motion was started, and the Spirit of the Force has changed in the manner as was given. Then, the test has come to the one that is set in that position as the judge of manifested forces being brought before [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and all phases then of same are to be studied; as has been given, now in the test of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s study in those connected with such forces, for all are given in that way in which every manner of force may be seen.  Then study well and be able to correlate same with conditions that arise from time to time, for as has been given, many of the laws as pertain to different phases of a force are being presented, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] must gather that necessary to be able to present and meet all obstacles as they come from time to time, for as is seen:  First test the money, the next honor, the next the position, the next the fame, the next that occupied in the social and religious favor, and that condition then should be studied, felt, know, experienced, that each phase in its [[Oneness|oneness]] must be known.

7. As to that which the bodies should do in relation to those stocks now held, then that as given.  In the near future, in the days when that stock (L & N) is nearer the 126, cover for the higher price.

8. In the condition then regarding that of Southern, hold.  In that of Arnold-Constable, this we find again that in which there has come much of the contestable ground, and the up price now in this is apparent.  Then cover by buying long, see?  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  It was given that L & N would well at 126 on Saturday. Why this error?
(A)  That as given, these forces as set in motion, has brought those conditions to pass that make that change, see?

10. (Q)  Is it that the information has not been understood, or has the information been in error regarding the action of L & N?  Please co-relate all information regarding L & N that has been given in readings and tell us what is the trouble, and what should be done regarding same at the present time.
(A)  In this, we find this has been given.  The information has NOT been understood, the information has [been] and is still correct.  The conditions have changed, yet all conditions are one condition, see?  and now same must be met as that as has been given, in the way and manner as given.

11. That is all the questions.

[11/1/25, 11:15 A.M. Immediately following GC's statement that "That is all the questions" EC volunteered rdg. 294-47 on himself indicating that much more spirituality was needed in his own life before the information coming through him would be clearly understood, or given in such a manner as to be perfectly clear.]
