# TEXT OF READING 1554-5 F 47

**Reading [[1554-5]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/25/1938  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 7547

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by Madame [1554], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mme. [1554].]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Calif. 
(Regarding the Oil Well at Oplin Field, Callahan County, 
[[Texas|Texas]] - 8,000 acres, 40 miles from Abilene:  Will the South 
well now being drilled come in and produce?)

1. EC:  Provided the salt water is not allowed to interfere - that is a part of the warnings in this particular territory.

2. As to the manner of protection, with the manner of the drilling that is carried on here:  There must be casings set when there ARE the signs of the first show of the Oil, see?

3. This should be a most satisfactory development, opening a large territory.  However, it extends farther to the southernmost portions - more than the east or west portions.

4. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Extra copy of the ques. and ans. for friend
"    " Ass'n file
