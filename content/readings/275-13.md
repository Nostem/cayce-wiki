# TEXT OF READING 275-13 F 17

**Reading [[275-13]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/1/1930  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 1717

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [275].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [275].  Now, we find conditions are somewhat bettered in many ways than when we last had same.  While there is an uneasiness at times indicated or felt by the body, in a general manner, and in that as a whole, the body continues to show improvements.

2. In the taking of the ash, this should NOT be taken when there is not to be soon given - or while in system the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, and WHEN taken this should be taken in water and NOT in a lump - as to cause too much of the calling together, as it were, of the VITALITIES, through the creation of the oxygen loosed as a whole better disseminated throughout the system - and it will act more in accord with that being attempted.

3. The tendency of the chalkiness, or brittleness that is apparent, is from those forces as make for the bony structural forces not being enlivened from the marrow itself, or the lack of that vitality as comes from sufficient sunlight.  Hence that of both the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] and infra red light to the system.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Are treatments being given correctly?
(A)  These are very good.

5. (Q)  How much longer should body take carbon ash?
(A)  This is reduced now to the dose only when the light is to be applied.  Hence will be of a LONGER period, but not so MUCH at a time taken, see?

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
