# TEXT OF READING 2792-2 F 43

**Reading [[2792-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/20/1942  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9965

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs, [2792], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2792]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are improved; though there are periods when there is apparently even more of the weakness and the disturbance with the head.

4. This is lack of vital energies and the body's overactivity with this lack of energy.

5. We would continue with the adjustment at least for six to ten more.

6. But begin now and take internally Abbott's B-1 Complex capsules or tablets, one each day, and three each day of the Parke-Davis Haliver Oil capsules.

7. These as we find, now, will have the reaction of quieting the body, renewing the energies, and within a week to ten days there should be material help.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Have I any internal disturbance?
(A)  As we find, the internal disturbances are the effects of activities disturbed in the functioning of organs.

10. (Q)  Would one sick and nauseous menstrual period indicate anything?
(A)  This only indicates that there is the readjusting of the activities of the organs and their functionings.

11. (Q)  Has menopause any direct bearing upon my condition?
(A)  The weakness is a part of the attempt of the system to adjust itself under those changes coming about.

12. (Q)  Why did I used to get slight, short dizzy spells long prior to 4 years ago when my present condition became acute?
(A)  This is a part of the disturbance between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal circulation.  Thus these effects upon the body were as of incoordination, or dizziness, the inability to coordinate.

Hence the needs of the adjustments, that have been VERY well done, and should be continued for at least another three to four weeks.

13. (Q)  How can I correct too much gas in stomach?
(A)  This as we find will be materially aided with the strengthening of the body-forces.  For the gas we should use enemas to help eliminate same.

14. (Q)  Do the tiny white pinpoint spots on my tonsils drain into my system?
(A)  We do not find that these are drainage points; rather the effect of the accumulation of lymph flow at such points.

15. (Q)  Approximately how long will it take for the menopause?
(A)  This depends upon the response of the body.  It usually requires at least twelve months - or nine months.

16. (Q)  What should I do to tide it over?
(A)  Do the things that have been indicated here, as we find.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Coulter) Copy to Ass'n file
