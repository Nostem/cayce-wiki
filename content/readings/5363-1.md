# TEXT OF READING 5363-1 M 62

**Reading [[5363-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/25/1944  Sex: M  Age: 62  ReadingID: 13503

This Psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5363], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body:  [5363].

3. As we find, there are disturbances and these have been of long standing.  As we find, the greater trouble remains or consists in the conditions where there are lesions around the capsule of the kidneys themselves.  These, at times, cause a great distress and these, while they have not upset the activities of the kidneys, owing to the nature of the adhesions, these have come originally from an injury, this in many years back when there was a lack or a strain over the area of the 8th and 9th dorsal.  These are lateral lesions.

4. These, to be sure, have at times affected the circulation and these have tended to make irregular the heart's activity, at others they have affected the circulation through the lungs.

5. In making for helpful forces there should be first the applications for easing the pain.  We would first combine Laudanum and aconite, one portion of laudanum to nine portions of aconite.  Paint the areas with this and then apply, not hot, but warm salt, heavy salt heated, so as to produce or to dry these properties in same.  Repeat this about twice a week for six weeks.

6. Then have the short-wave treatments, and these we would have about once a week.

7. Then set up drainage through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations. These would bring the greater relief.  These may be necessary for some time, owing to the long length of time, but these will set up eliminations so as to ease and relieve and eventually remove those disturbances there.  Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is causing the headaches which occur?
(A)  These are the sources of the headaches.

10. (Q)  Aching in back of neck?
(A)  All of those which reflex to the sympathetic nervous system.  For as the body finds when it gets on its feet usually it wears off.  This is because of equalizing the circulation.

After we have had about two or three courses of this treatments, it would be well that there be used the Radio-Active Appliance to equalize the circulation.  Don't begin with this but first have at least three series of the treatments and about four weeks apart.  Then use the Radio-Active Appliance as an equalizer for nerve energies and blood supply.

11. (Q)  Is the smoking of a pack of cigarettes per day, which I have been doing for years, injurious to my eyesight, hearing, or in any other way to my health?
(A)  This has not been other than just at times a contributory cause.

12. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Know that all [[Healing|healing]] and that of constructive thinking lies within the realm of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s own consciousness itself.  It is that which is applied within self that brings helpful forces to the body conditions, constructively.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.

If you do not have a preference we'd suggest that you get in touch with Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, 105 N. Van Ness Ave., [[California|Los Angeles]], Calif., who has had experience with following Mr. Cayce's readings.)

## Background

> B1. 7/2/44 [5363]'s letter:  "For the past 44 years, or since I
> was 18, I have had a deep, dull, constant aching in or near
> the 5th lumbar or the sacroiliac.  I have been to medical
> doctors, osteopaths and have had many chiropractic
> adjustments and I have never received any material benefit...
> I have read and re-read many times the book, A Search For
> God, and I am trying very hard to get myself in attunement
> with God."  Questions submitted:  1. What can be done to
> relieve aching in small of back, near the lumbar or
> sacroiliac, which is my principal trouble?  2. What is
> causing and what should be done for the headaches which occur
> at 4 or 5 o'clock in the morning, for the past few years,
> gradually disappearing when I have been up 2 or 3 hours?
> 3. Constant aching in back of neck?  This has been occurring
> for the past 3 years, and a doctor told me it is a nervous
> fatigue.  4. Is the smoking a pack of cigarettes per day,

> hearing, or in any other way to my health?  5. Any spiritual
