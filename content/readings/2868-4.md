# TEXT OF READING 2868-4 M 52

**Reading [[2868-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/8/1943  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 10095

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2868], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through his cousin, Miss [307].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  We have the impressions of the body here.  These conditions we have had before.

3. As has been indicated, this has been long standing.  There will be required patience, persistence.

4. If there has been the completing of corrections, wait at least two weeks and then do have others; that will enable the body to equalize the circulation through head and neck AND the chest portion of the body.

5. If there is no indication of mucous from the intestines, wait a longer period between [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]].  But as we find, SOURCES of this are still existent, and are indicated.  But, to be sure, too much of these would not be well, - that is, too much CAN be taken.  But if they are given as suggested, with those forms of the clearing or purifying of the alimentary canal, these will not be straining on the body.

6. Do take now, at least twice a day, five drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - in water.

7. Do use heat about the head when the body has been in the cold, in the evening when it attempts to rest.

8. Do use a spray of half and half of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and Distilled Water, for nasal passages and throat.

9. And we should find better conditions gradually coming about.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is head trouble hay fever?
(A)  It is more nerves.  As indicated, when there is relaxation by heat, or warmth, these do not disturb.  When there is cold, or cold weather and the body in same, it does disturb.

12. (Q)  Will I get rid of this trouble?
(A)  Will tomorrow come for the body?  Such conditions depend upon the application of self, physically, mentally, spiritually,

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
