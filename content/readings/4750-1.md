# TEXT OF READING 4750-1 F 69

**Reading [[4750-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/15/1931  Sex: F  Age: 69  ReadingID: 12723

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of May, 1931, in accordance with request made by the daughter through Mrs. [959].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

In her room at home, 
Time of Reading                         ... Street, 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., W. Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4750].  Now, we find there are disturbances and disorders in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  In this particular body, as we find, there is an unbalancing of that as is assimilated for the replenishing in the blood supply, through the condition produced by poor eliminations in the system.  This has caused the excess of plasm as forms in the system to produce a plethora or tumorous conditions.  These have not become, as we find, in any nature as that as ADHERES or becomes GROWING within itself; rather from the FLUIDS as gather in that portion of the system, and once created begins to add more and more to the same - see?  We find there are conditions as of a tendency towards so much constipation, that those poisons from the system calling on the forces of the body to create that NECESSARY force to produce coagulation and to throw out, has produced too much of that plasm as makes for gluco [glucose], or the portions in system or blood supply that make for the increasing of the system in a way and manner as to bring, not only in one portion but in many portions, that excess in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], in the fluids in the system, and causes these dilations, these tumors, these excesses.  By bruising any portion, these set up.  These have not become a malignant nature.  They may become so, unless separated - or unless conditions are brought about where we can create nearer an equilibrium in the system.  To this we would, then, add:

2. Take each day one-eighth grain of the carbon or animated ash. Each day we would, not over those places where the conditions are affecting the system - but rather from the solar plexus center, see?  apply the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray thirty to sixty minutes AFTER this ash has been taken.  This we would keep up for at least eighteen days.  Then we may give whether this has been SEPARATED sufficiently or not for the removal of same, or whether it may be removed by absorption.  That will depend upon the conditions that have arisen.

3. We would also take those in the diet as of this manner:

4. Do not mix citrous fruit juices and those of any of the cereals.  These may be altered from time to time, but let a PORTION of same - that is, the morning meal - from time to time consist of a great deal of prunes or prune juices, figs or fig juices, or those of the pie plant or the like.

5. Occasionally we would take a dose of a combination (which is prepared) of calomel, colocynth and rhubarb.  This will LOOSEN the whole system.  Now, do not over STRAIN the body in these eliminations.

6. The noon meals should consist principally of green vegetables.  With these there may be taken any of the oil dressings, or of that that makes same palatable.  A great many of these vegetables, or as many as may be combined.

7. Of evenings have the vegetables well cooked.  The juices of meats may then be taken.  Not too much pastry, pies or the like, though any of the fruits that are in SEASON may be taken in moderation.

8. We would also find that packs of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] over the liver area will aid in assisting the eliminations.  These we would take - large packs as of whole towels, see?  Dip them in saturated solution of as hot [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] water as can be well applied to the body.  When these have cooled, or caked, then change same again.  Do this at least every second or third day, until we have a FULL elimination - see?  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Should the light be given after each dose of ash or twice a week?
(A)  After each dose, just as given.  EACH day these are to be taken - the ash EACH day, and the light given thirty to sixty minutes after the dose, but do not give the light longer than two to three minutes in the beginning and at least thirty-eight to forty inches from the body.

10. (Q)  What can be done for the neuritis in the 3rd and 4th fingers?
(A)  When we start the eliminations we will find these will be relieved.

11. (Q)  What can be done for the nausea?
(A)  Let's get the system cleansed out, and the nausea will be overcome.  This is overloading or backing up of the bile ducts, see?  and those forces from the assimilative system.

12. (Q)  What can be done for burning feet?
(A)  Change the circulation.  This is a portion of the general condition.

13. (Q)  The itching scalp?
(A)  This is poor circulation externally.  When we have changed the inner circulation, or the eliminations are set up, we will find better conditions - as we find.  When the eighteen days have been carried out, then we would give further information - unless conditions have so set themselves that eliminations may be seen being carried on.

14. (Q)  How often should the calomel preparation be taken?
(A)  Once or twice a week, depending upon the eliminations from alimentary canal.

15. (Q)  What dosage of this?
(A)  These are already prepared in pellets - calomel, colocynth and rhubarb.  One pellet as a dose, see?

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [959]
