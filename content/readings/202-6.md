# TEXT OF READING 202-6 M 45

**Reading [[202-6]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/24/1930  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 1018

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [202].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker, nurse, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Florida|Florida]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we have the body here - this we have had before. Many are the changes that have taken place with the physical functioning of this body, from that when we last had this here.  The body would be much bettered would it be under the supervision, or where those things as may be GIVEN FOR corrective measures of the condition in the blood pressure as is ASSUMED, rather than always active by the body - may be carried out, rather than the body attempting to do same in the present surroundings, or in many another - for the foods are, necessarily, to be followed closely; also the manipulations; also those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that may equalize same, through the application of the Radio-Active Appliance; and these the body would NOT follow unless in those surroundings. Do THAT, then!  Come to the [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]!  We are through.

Copy to Self - Also wired
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
