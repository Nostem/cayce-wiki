# TEXT OF READING 632-7 F 12

**Reading [[632-7]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/16/1934  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 4293

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Hirn home, 6844 Nansen St., Forest Hills, L.I., N.Y., this 16th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [668], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [632], Mrs. [669], and Mr. [668].]

Time of Reading 
5:30 to 5:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [632], this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, conditions are continuing on the improve. While there are still those conditions that we have indicated must be warned concerning, the reactions that have come are the effect of the NORMAL reaction.  And if there will be kept those conditions that we have outlined, consistently, persistently, meeting the needs or emergencies that arise in a normal way and manner, we will bring the normal conditions for this body.

3. With the existent disturbances in the bladder, or clitoris area, as we find, a HELPFUL condition would be to take small quantities of Syrup of Squill - we will say in the morning; ten to twenty drops - and in the evening five to six drops of Sweet Spirits of Nitre.  These will be not only helpful in RELIEVING the flow of the tissue but will make for the prevention of further disorders in this direction.  But should such arise, MEET it NOMINALLY, see?

4. We would keep, then, what has been outlined.

5. As has been indicated, we would find that - when the manipulations are given - the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] for two to three minutes over the area of the 9th dorsal to the 4th lumbar would be beneficial.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What was the cause of the existing condition today?
(A)  As indicated, a NATURAL result of the system's attempting to eliminate; or a clogging from the lack of the proper lymph circulation, see?  Hence those properties we have indicated; not given together but separately, see?  Also the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ULTRA-violet]] lights.

8. (Q)  Would you advise an X-Ray for the benefit of the [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]]?
(A)  This will not be HARMFUL; not necessarily helpful.  For if these treatments are carried on, the body will change every two to three days!  Because the body is on the improve! Keep it that way!

Be very mindful of the diet that HAS been indicated.  Keep ALL of those properties that have been outlined for the body.

9. (Q)  What is the cause of, and what should be done for, the cough?
(A)  The cough is the natural reaction from the poor circulation, and the tissue as we have indicated - from there being a poor lymph circulation.  The properties of the brandy with honey - but there should not be too MUCH honey in same, but so the use of these may be active upon the gastric flow or the lymph circulation; and this will gradually be overcome.

10. (Q)  Are there any foods being taken in the present diet that the body should not have?
(A)  The body is not eating at the present time!  There has been outlined that which should be well for the diet for the body.  Those foods that are BLOOD and NERVE and muscle building for the body.  Not too much sweets, not too much of starches.  To be sure, these should be well-balanced - but too much of these - sweets tend to irritate the kidneys, that have been under stress through the conditions existent in the system.  And while not wholly taboo, these should NOT be taken at ANY time in excess.  There never should be - in any one day, in the present conditions - more than an ounce of sweet or sugar during a day; and this includes the sweets from the fruits.  But the fruit sweets, or natural sweets, or vegetable sweets, are the better for the body.

11. (Q)  How often should the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray be taken?
(A)  When the manipulations are given, as indicated; it should become a part of those treatments.

12. (Q)  Should the Ash be taken before the ultra-violet treatment?
(A)  This will be more effectual, you see; then the plain [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] may be left off - for the time being.

13. (Q)  Any further suggestion for the body at this time?
(A)  Do these.  Be consistent; be patient.  And know that - if there would be the better conditions for self, and for the correction of those conditions - one MUST adhere to those things given!

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(11/16/34 GD's note:  Enclosed was a letter with further suggestions to Dr. Robertson.)
