# TEXT OF READING 294-49 M 48

**Reading [[294-49]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/16/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2008

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by self.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [569].]

Time of Reading 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC, ..., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, EC, here.

3. These [[Visions|visions]] which are presented in [[Dreams|dreams]] are those correlations of body-mind with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces in action regarding physical thought and study of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s actions, as related to those conditions in which thought, intent and [[Purpose|purpose]], with the urges of body, are correlated with same, see?

4. As is seen in the [[Visions|vision]], or [[Dreams|dream]], wherein entity passing through gate and attempting to prevent hogs, pigs, from coming through at time of passage, and one chasing through, entity then attempting to drive same back and not understanding just how to prevent others from coming through when this one is attempted to be driven back, the flying up on the fence of the peacock, and the strutting and hollowing of same, this the lesson and the interpretation of same: There is to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] no more confidence to be placed in those who would hog the whole conditions as pertain to the work attempted, than in those who make a great deal of cry about such, yet accomplish nothing - see?

5. In the one again as seen, wherein there is the discussion between two people regarding what is meant in the information given regarding the prices on stocks, and the disagreement between them respecting the information:  In this there is seen that both are incorrect, that the information as given refers to the time same is given and not two days afterward acting in the same capacity.

6. In others we find, these as related to numbers, give the change that occurs at the given time, see?

7. We are through with this reading.
