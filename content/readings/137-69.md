# TEXT OF READING 137-69 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-69]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/5/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 459

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Sunday morning, April 4, 1926.  I woke up with a decided impression that the stock market prices went down, very severely, another panic, and that the prices went below the old low prices.  Prices were so emphasized that I got up and looked in the newspaper to see if it had actually happened.
(A)  In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] many of those various conditions which have been carried in the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and are now, as we see, correlated through, with, [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] condition of that which may happen as respecting certain conditions that that of rumors may bring a climax in condition on the Exchange Floor.  With same there would come a few hours of a panic, see?  That would send prices very, very, low.  This, then, is as a warning, that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may be careful of these conditions that would arise, should this condition occur.  As to WHEN, between now and the 12th, see?

4. (Q)  Does this mean that this will actually happen?
(A)  Under certain stresses of conditions, as we see.

5. (Q)  What is the cause of this happening?
(A)  As given.

6. (Q)  As given, will this be the last deflation before the steady upward climb?
(A)  Last deflation before the steady upward climb - that is, last deflation of any import.

7. We are through.
