# TEXT OF READING 3982-5 M 34

**Reading [[3982-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/11/1925  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 11668

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by - an involuntary reading, immediately following 4905-33 (oil reading on Bosque County, Tex.)

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.           Winslow, Arkansas.

1. EC:  Now, we have the propecting location here, as Riley Hurst is seeking for, near Boston Mountain, in the Southeastern portion of Washington County, Arkansas.  The location as has been staked out at present is in the area of the treasure, and with spreading out of same a little to the South and East, he should be able to locate same.

2. (Q)  How much should he spread out?
(A)  Two feet.

3. (Q)  Anything further regarding this?
(A)  This information may be useful, unless they spread farther as they work in this.
