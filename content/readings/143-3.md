# TEXT OF READING 143-3 F 3

**Reading [[143-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/10/1926  Sex: F  Age: 3  ReadingID: 583

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by her father, Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              Deal, N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

2. Now, we find there are those disturbances in the system as are produced by the eliminations becoming choked with toxins of that nature as produce congestion [cold - flu?] between the capillary and lymphatic circulation.  That is, we have the capillary circulation of blood proper and lymph circulation.  In these drosses are the effects of those conditions as are produced by that that is separated in system through these contaminating effects of this nature of poison in the system.  Hence the repressions as are produced on the nerve plexus, especially that of the ileum plexus - that's about the kidneys and about this portion of the body where the eliminations are produced through these portions of the body - and of the hypogastric plexus.  Hence the nausea, the headache, the tired, fretful condition, the flushed condition, with some temperature as HAS shown - nothing to be scared of, no condition arises that would not be easily met - though this may produce that elimination or rash [roseola?] in the system.

3. In that of meeting these conditions, the simpler form to meet these would be by using broken doses of Castoria, taking at least four or five - a few drops - taken every few hours, see?  until the eliminations are set up thoroughly throughout the intestinal system.

4. After these begin, see (the eliminations), [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or rub thoroughly a small quantity - piece about the size of a good sized pea or bean - along the whole cerebro-spinal system of Cocoa Butter and Bromo-Quinine.  Just [[Massage Therapy|massage]] it in system along the cerebro-spinal system, rather than taking internally, see?  for the system is developing, the body is in very good condition throughout, save this little congestion that has set up here in the condition.  We would do that, see?
Would be necessary that at least two to three of these rubs be given.

5. Keep the body quiet - that is, out of drafts while this is being used or given.

6. Give mild stimulants if so desired of Yellow Saffron Tea, and no heavy foods - that is, meats.  Gruels, oatmeal - any of the cereals.  No apples, but any other fruits, COOKED apples may be used, see?

7. Keep the body quiet for two to three to four days, see?  Do that.

8. (Q)  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] with Cocoa Butter and Quinine in combination?
(A)  Cocoa Butter and Quinine in combination.  That carries both, see?  The Cocoa Butter as the carrier, so that it penetrates and caries into the nerve centers and to the circulation, as the congestion shows in capillary and the tissue circulation, or that of the fluids in action in capillary circulation, see?  and lymphatic.

9. (Q)  What caused this trouble?
(A)  Contraction in diet, see?  and the meeting of the germ of this that causes congestion in this circulation. Breathed in.  [Cold germ, influenza as per P.M. Rdg. 143-4.]
