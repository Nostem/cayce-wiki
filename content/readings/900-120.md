# TEXT OF READING 900-120 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-120]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/1/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5323

This psychic reading was given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] this 1st day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; HLC (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind of [900], with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as we find, come to this body.  These as we see, are as those lessons, as may be given for the understanding of that which the body seeks to gain by the correlation of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces with the mental attributes to the body-mind; then giving same to the consciousness in such an manner as to make the lessons of same the practical application of that being studied by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Friday morning, August 28, 1925, at home in Deal.  I was riding on a train and saw [136] riding in seat next to window; was trying to measure something with a croquet mallet.  And I said it can't be measured that way.
(A)  This, as we see, is in an emblematical way the interpretation of the forces as present themselves to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] for the study of force, and of that especially pertaining to those universal forces being of a one measurement; for, as seen, the relation to the individual with entity, the position of same on moving train, the measurement of conditions as relate between the two, with the movement as of motion by the croquet, or that of pleasure or enjoyment, or of worldly forces - the relationships this as can be seen, cannot be measured by the manifestation of conditions rather than those producing same.

4. (Q)  Sat at the bottom of a hill with Moe Wallack.  The hill above was lined with ridges and little clovers grew in each of the ridges.  I said to Moe Wallack, "It was just a year ago that it happened."  "No," he replied, "not that long ago."  "Yes it is," I insisted.  "It was just one year ago."
(A)  This, as we see, is an explanation to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of its own development in a specific time, as of a conscious or third dimension mind taking in, in the emblematical form presented, all phases of the development of the entity.  The hill presented bringing that of the advancement to the mind; those of the ridges, the various stages of that development, this showing some farther advanced than others.  The clover presented, representing the abilities of the full development growing and giving that to the entity of the full, well-rounded life of the study; the questioning same, being that of the doubt, as the entity seen, to the physical mind of [900] the insistence of the entity of time in which this has occurred, being that of the warning in that all must be of a [[Oneness|Oneness]] in its development, whether of the body-physical, or of the spiritual forces; for Knowledge is the power when used or guided in the correct manner, the abilities of the entity [900] to recognize the growth, being that of power even in self.  Then be faithful to all those forces and abilities of the physical, spiritual; and make the life [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-rounded, full life and an ensample such as is found in HIM.

5. We are through.
