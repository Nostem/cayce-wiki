# TEXT OF READING 136-28 F 21

**Reading [[136-28]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/5/1926  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 335

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.

2. EC:  Now, the physical conditions as are existent in the body are accentuated at this time, and to co-relate these existent conditions with that information as has been given the body, we will find that these are the conditions of which the body has been warned often, in many ways, and that the manner in which this may be best assisted at the present time, added with that rest that is necessary for the physical welfare, that spiritual [[Last Supper|communion]] with the two responsible for that condition taking place in the body, and as has been given, through same gain the more perfect manner in which this spirit movement is to manifest through the physical, and through that same bring to bear on the physical receptacle of the body that force necessary to carry out that desire in the inmost being of EACH - co-relate one with another, for only in such a channel may this be brought to that termination that is in keeping with conditions existent in the mind, the heart, [[Soul|the soul]] of each.  The physical action can only be rest, should they gain that, see?  Then, the whole action lies within the heart, [[Soul|the soul]], the spiritual force of each.

3. We are through with this reading.
