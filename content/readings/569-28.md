# TEXT OF READING 569-28 F 62

**Reading [[569-28]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1943  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 4036

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of the [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 14th day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [569], Associate Member of the [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [569]; this we have had before.

2. Conditions are grave - and serious.

3. As we find, only the administration of narcotics, or hypnotics, is indicated; and we would increase especially the hypnotic - such as Luminal or the like; but, to be sure, under the direction of the physician.

4. These have come as indicated, that might have proved better if there could have been the use of the [[[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]]] light.

5. There might be had some aid in relief if there would be prepared in the present a very strong magnet, this encased in metal - as [[Germany|German]] Silver and this applied over the area in the abdomen where there are those great pains, and especially when there is the desire for urination, or the action of the bladder and kidneys.  This applied for a little space at a time, not too long, will aid in relief.  It will dry the accumulations, it will delay activity of those inroads being made by the accumulations.

6. Do that.

7. (Q)  Can this instrument be obtained anywhere, or will it have to be made?
(A)  Have to be made.  There's no such made at present. [See 569-28, Par. R5 and R6.]

8. (Q)  What diet is best in the present?
(A)  Whatever will agree best with the body, and whatever it desires give it.

9. (Q)  Would a little elm in the water be helpful?
(A)  Stop the burning in the stomach; it will aid too, of course, the activity of the kidneys.

10. (Q)  Any further suggestions for help?
(A)  These as we find offer the greater help, or relief - not curative, in the present.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to her aunt, Mrs. [760] - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
