# TEXT OF READING 313-18 F 53

**Reading [[313-18]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/20/1939  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 2381

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [313], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the conditions have not become excessive. In a great many ways the body is much improved from those conditions as we have had at some times heretofore.

4. There are still those inclinations for a disturbing through the pelvic organs, because of the weaknesses which have been indicated there, and the tendency for the disturbing discharge.

5. We would use the weak solution of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], in those proportions as have been given, as a douche.

6. We would find that for the body in the present the use of Codiron tablets, two taken especially at the evening meal, would be most beneficial in making for better resistances for the body.

7. Also we find that the use of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] at least three times each week, just before retiring, would relax the body.  This should be given along the spine, with the sponge applicator, over the lower portions; and the suction or cup applicator for the head and neck and to the middle portion of the body.  This should be on either side and down the spinal column.  This will not only make for a stimulating of the circulation but will relax the body sufficient to gain better recuperative forces with the rest.

8. Do these and as we find we will keep better conditions for the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What is cause of pain in left side for past ten days?
(A)  This as we find is the tendency for an engorgement in the transverse and descending colon.  If there is the use of enemas, these will relieve such tensions, - as will also the [[Vibration|vibrations]] aid as we have just indicated.

11. (Q)  Is there an ulcer in stomach?
(A)  NO.  This as we find comes from the inclination for regurgitation by the slowing of the eliminations and the lack of the stomach emptying itself.  With the [[Vibration|vibrations]] given as indicated, and especially with the food values (that is, the combinations given as a tonic - in the Codiron), these will aid in eliminating these disturbances, - though precautions should be taken that no great quantities of grease or greasy foods are taken.

12. (Q)  Are present pains from digestion?
(A)  As indicated, a tendency rather in the colon for the dilation, or engorgement, or plethora condition; and this tends to slow up digestion, with the lack of the proper eliminations.

Do these things as we have indicated.

13. (Q)  Should Zilatone be taken?
(A)  As we find, this may become necessary, but first take two or three of the high enemas to cleanse the colon before attempting to stimulate the secretions of the upper hepatic circulation for overactivity.

14. (Q)  Is goat's milk beneficial?
(A)  Goat's milk would be very good for this body.

15. (Q)  Should violet ray be continued, and how?
(A)  Violet ray occasionally is helpful, but as we find - at present the [[Electric Vibrator|electrical vibrator]] would be more beneficial than the violet ray.  While the violet ray is helpful, - it should be used preferably after there has been a better cleansing of the system.  Then use it about once a week - following the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerves, down either side of the cerebrospinal system, and then the muscular forces over shoulders and neck, and the sacral and lumbar, and especially the sciatic centers.

16. (Q)  Name foods for diet.
(A)  As we have indicated, there should be plenty of body-building foods.  As we find, cereals or fruit juices of mornings.  Occasionally the yolk of an egg, wheat cakes and syrup, - but not all of these at one meal, of course. In the noon meal have the greater portion consisting of the raw, green vegetables.  In the evenings, let the meats be either fish, fowl or lamb, with generous portions of vegetables that are well cooked - most of these being those that grow above the ground.

17. Do these and we will find we will make the better forces for this body.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
