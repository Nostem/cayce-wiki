# TEXT OF READING 900-178 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-178]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/27/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5381

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by [900], himself.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the morning of December 27, 1925.  You will recall these [[Dreams|dreams]], giving the interpretation and lesson of each, and will answer any questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, here, [900]. This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] we have on this, the 27th, morning of, are varied and different in their precept and in the lesson as is to be gained from each.

3. In this wherein is seen the box and the weeping as those gather about same, that is the presentation to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of the conditions as are existent in the life and lives of each and everyone, how each are hemmed about by the cares of the world, in the way and manner that they become the first and foremost in thought and mind and in action, for truly, as has been said, "From the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh."  This, as we see, does not refer to conditions that may become existent.  Rather as to conditions that the personal experience brings to the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] force for the confirmation of the lessons as are being gained by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], see?  Ready for questions.

4. No questions.

5. Then, question first:  We do not find, then, that this refers to a condition as may be seen as regarding the mother-in-law, for the daughter is seen weeping.  No.  As given, this refers rather to that experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], for through trials and tribulations are the greater lessons in spiritual truths gained.  Second:  Then do we find that this in any way intimates that there would come the dissolution of this body at this time.  No.  We are through with this reading.
