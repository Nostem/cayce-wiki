# TEXT OF READING 1069-9 F 65

**Reading [[1069-9]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/10/1936  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 6221

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1936, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Conditions, as we find, are not so good as when last we even had it (the body) here.

3. There are inroads of destructive forces; that have been allayed or assuaged by some of the applications, yet we find that which may bring relief in any great extent only becomes the more detrimental to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. That to bring the most relief to the body, that would be of a constructive nature, would be the keeping of the Calcidin - warm - sipped rather often, to keep rather in line with the [[Iodine|iodine]] relaxations; also the pressures osteopathically given, as we have indicated.

5. To be sure, gentle massages over the abdominal area - beginning from the lower portion of the esophagus and following the whole alimentary canal, as indicated - would be allaying and helpful; also would relive somewhat this stress.

6. These, as we find, would be the more constructive measures.

7. To be sure, injections in the form of the Adrenalin or the stimuli either the Strychnine of the volatile forces that may be given hypodermically would allay.  But these would shorten the [[Oneness|oneness]] of the physical and the life forces.

8. We would, then, give those things as indicated; the gentle massages with the oil (olive oil) along this area; those pressures in the 3rd and 4th dorsal; 2nd, 3rd and 4th cervical pressures - gentle pressures with a rotary motion to relax.

9. Keep those others, as for the diet and the general applications.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is there any immediate danger?
(A)  Depends upon the manner in which the condition or the body is handled, as to the immediate danger.

As we have indicated, there is danger - of course; for these continue, as we find in the present, to become destructive forces.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Son
"    " Ass'n file

Day letter as follows:

CALCIDIN SIPS OFTEN.  [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] PRESSURES AS INDICATED. GENTLE OLIVE OIL MASSAGE - PRESSURES THIRD - FOURTH DORSAL, SECOND - THIRD - FOURTH CERVICAL, ROTARY MOTION TO RELAX. CONTINUE DIET AND GENERAL APPLICATIONS.  HYPODERMICS WOULD RELIEVE MOST BUT BE MORE DANGEROUS.  SO NATURAL METHODS ARE BEST FOR AS LONG AS POSSIBLE.
