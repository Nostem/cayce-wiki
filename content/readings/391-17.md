# TEXT OF READING 391-17 M 25

**Reading [[391-17]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/11/1936  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 2994

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of July 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [391], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:10 to 5:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - [391].  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while the body is under a great deal of stress and STRAIN, [financial - marital, etc. - see 1208-3 Background] and the anxiety as is produced by the disturbing conditions that arise from the conditions that have been indicated, we find that unless the body keeps away from those of intoxicants, those of beer, those of wine, those of ANY alcohol, these will CONTINUE to arise until entirely eliminated from the system.

3. Use the injections of a weakened solution of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  This if it is persisted in will be effective.  The proportions would be as ONE to five, see?  and if this is used consistently, and the activity is such as not to cause too great a strain, and if there is the refraining from the intoxicants, we will find help.

4. Coca-Cola, limeades and such are WELL for the body.  These will act WITH the kidneys IN the eliminations.

5. Unless these precautions are taken, this will become deeper and deeper-seated.  And unless PRECAUTIOUS may produce such an irritation in the bladder, or such an irritation in the urethra, as to become malignant.

6. Better take precautions NOW!

7. Do as has been given.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What can be done for the lack of appetite for food?
(A)  Keep those things as has been indicated, just given. REFRAIN from the use of those things that may supply foods and the appetite for food as a normal thing!

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
