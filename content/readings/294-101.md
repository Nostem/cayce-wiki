# TEXT OF READING 294-101 M 50

**Reading [[294-101]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/19/1927  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 2060

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by self.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [369], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC, present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, EC.  This we have had before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come through those various channels we have outlined for the body, and should be applied in the physical activities of the body in the way and manner as has been given.

4. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

5. (Q)  Night of Tuesday, 10/18, or morning of Wed. 10/19/27. Dream regarding [369] holding up some men, and being applauded for her bravery - then one of the men caught her and used her as a protection so no one could shoot him.  I got a gun and shot the man through his foot - the bullet ran through his body and came out his hand, making him drop the gun.
(A)  In this we find conditions are as but experiences of the [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] in the earth's plane.  [America.  See 369-3 Life Rdg. 11/3/27 saying she was Bainbridge's companion in escape from fort.]

And as these conditions are given, there is seen there enacted in this manner those conditions in the experience in the earth's plane of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as seen.

And the lesson is that, as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may act as protector, teacher, guider, director in ways for the physical, mental, spiritual protection of others, this he should apply in the action seen.

While it is seen that those elements in which the protection is applied are in the form and manner of modern warfare, or modern defense, yet this represents in this way and manner the modern defense of individual activity, thought or [[Purpose|purpose]].  Use, apply same, then, in the manner as given towards those as seen in [[Visions|vision]], see?

6. We are through with this reading.
