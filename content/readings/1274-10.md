> 
> Mr. [2671]
> Miami, Florida
> My dear Mr. [2671]-
> 
> Yours of March 30th came just after I had mailed the last
> reading given on Broad Creek, where the suggestion was made
> that if Mr. [...] [your son] and Mr. [943] would correlate
> their ideas, and take all the information as had been given
> and cut out any questions that might have hindered, and then
> work out the directions as were given, and follow those
> explicitly, they would be able to locate the treasure - and
> I had been hoping that I would hear something from you all

> following out the suggestions in your letter of the 30th.
> So that's why you haven't heard from me before this!
> 
> Yes, I have thought about our trip to Florida, and about
> meeting each and every one of you, and I've thought many
> times about various conversations and circumstances that
> arose during my stay there.  Sometimes when I think of it,
> Mr. [2671], I don't wonder that you don't feel more juberous
> [dubious] about it than possibly you do - for, seemingly,
> there was so much of it that was a failure, thus far, that
> you could hardly think anything else - and I'm honest to
> say I don't understand - but I know, from all our years of
> experience, there's something wrong somewhere!  Whether it's
> in the information, or in how the information was tried to
> be applied, I don't know!
> 
> Yes, I've thought quite often of our ride that night - and
> I didn't see you any more to tell you good-bye.  I wonder if
> you have ever had the other end of that [[Dreams|dream]], of if you
> have seen that one you told me about work out.  Of course,
> I know, from the information that was given you last fall,
> there are a lot of changes to come about - and we never did
> have a chance to see whether we could get further information
> concerning what that was that would happen to you in '28, I
> believe it was.  But, I am in hopes that we WILL have the
> opportunity, if you still desire that we see what we can get.
> 
> Now, as regarding the information that was given respecting
> [996] [[Bimini|Bimini]] - that seemed quite wonderful to me.  You, from
> having studied the situation, no doubt know whether any of
> it is plausible or reasonable or not - while I, in my little
> mind, don't.  I've never had occasion to THINK along such
> lines - never read of anything of the kind - don't know even
> the possibilities of such a development - yet I believe, from
> other information that has been given that HAS worked out,
> this would work out, provided the individual uses same in a
> correct way and manner.  I know this:  It isn't anything to
> be toyed with.  I don't know whether I ever told you of this
> circumstance or not - I hate to think of it even sometimes.
> Possibly it was only a coincidence.  Anyway, here is the
> tale, and it is a true one -
> Some years ago (as you remember, possibly, I told you) I had
> a contract with a Mr. Noe [A. C. Noe, [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.].  He
> was to do certain things and I was to do certain things, and
> whatever profit was made out of it was to be divided equally.
> In other words, he was to establish an office, furnish the
> necessary means, etc., for the work to be conducted - certain
> fees were to be charged for the work, and so on.  Before I
> would enter into the contract, I had several men - who were
> supposed to be honorable, upright, good business men -
> attorneys, lawyers, doctors - to hear one of the readings,
> question it themselves, and tell me whether I had a right
> to make such a contract or not.  The contract was drawn.
> The judges of the court - both county and circuit - told me
> it was alright, legal, and everything.  Six or eight months
> after this was drawn, the circuit judge offered to pay
> Mr. Noe several thousand dollars for his contract.  Several
> months later, when I felt Mr. Noe had abused the privileges
> that had been given him, my father being a part to the
> contract brought suit to try to make BOTH of us carry out
> our contract.  The case was tried before this judge, who had
> given me a written opinion that the contract was legal, and
> who had later offered to buy the contract from Noe, and he
> ruled from his bench the contract was illegal, and dismissed
> the case.  Something within me at the time made me rise
> and ask the judge if I might speak without being ruled as
> contempt of court.  He said I might speak.  That same
> something said, "For the lie you have this day enacted, the
> worms of your body will eat you up while you are yet alive!"
> In less than two years this was true, and he lived two years
> in this agony.  Possibly it was only a coincidence -
> possibly it was playing with fire.  Such may BE the reason
> that we have not been able to follow out, or to follow to a
> SUCCESSFUL END, information as has been given us concerning
> these two treasures.
> 
> You know it has made me wonder a whole lot since I came back
> - it was a MIGHTY pleasant trip in a lot of ways - it was
> MIGHTY fine to meet all of you men!  Everybody took special
> pains to make things pleasant for each and every one of us
> - everybody went to a whole lot of expense - and yet -
> possibly we were all worse off in the end than if it hadn't
> been.  I know we all were - from the financial way, if we
> look at it from that end.  I know you all were, from a
> financial way, if you look at it that way.  But whether we
> are to judge things like that in that way or not I don't know
> whether I know or not!  And I've tried mighty hard to find
> out what's the trouble - and the nearest thing I've gotten
> is what was given us in this last reading.  We are not in
> accord one with another!
> 
> Now, I'd like for you to write me what they did about this,
> if anything.  If you haven't done anything - if you would
> like it, I'd like to go ahead and take the reading, just as
> you have suggested here.  I don't know whether we will get
> answers to questions that are to prove conditions that
> possibly do not relate to the treasure, and only relate to
> the actions of individuals at the present time, or not, but
> I'll be mighty glad to try.  Let me know about it.
> 
> A few days after I returned from Miami, I was called to
> [[New York City|New York]].  I wondered - while I was there - if Mrs. [4498]
> was still there.  I thought once I'd wire you and ask you
> - I would have liked to see her again, and to have met your
> daughter there in school - but I thought possibly, even if
> she was there, they were busy, and maybe they wouldn't want
> to be bothered with anyone else just coming in.  So I didn't.
> But I had rather an interesting experience while there.
> Something that seemed to answer, or possibly it made a big
> question mark, from my worry of how things had turned out
> in Miami.  I was there for the week end - from Friday until
> Monday afternoon.  On Saturday I was invited to a dinner at
> the Pennsylvania Hotel.  There were two hundred and fifty
> or three hundred people - guests - at the dinner.  I did not
> know, until that afternoon, that I was even expected to go
> - and only the president of this club knew that I was
> expected to come, but I found there twenty-three people,
> for whom I had given physical readings, and each one CLAIMED
> to have received personal benefits from same!  Or I should
> say, possibly, twenty-three people for whom I had given
> readings, or some member of their family - and you know
> there was not a single one of these people that I had ever
> seen before (in my normal mind), nor not a one that had ever
> seen me before - though some of them I had given readings
> some fifteen years ago, and I had lived there in [[New York City|New York]]
> for six or seven months at one time and they had never been
> in to see me!  Nor had I ever hunted a one of them up!  Quite
> a remarkable experience, wasn't it?  Unusual - out of the
> ordinary!  But certainly one that would make a fellow think.
> If these people HADN'T received benefits, they wouldn't have
> any reason to stand up there before all the rest of those
> people and tell them they did!  I wasn't anybody to make a
> fuss over!  And I certainly had never tried to make a fuss
> over them!  But, evidently, there is something IN the
> information these people got, that they were able to apply!
> Why can't we do it in this?  What's wrong?  Is it the
> character of the information - the [[Purpose|purpose]] of the information
> - the character of the individuals - or the [[Purpose|purpose]] of the
> individuals?  It's a hard thing to reason, isn't it?  Maybe
> there ISN'T any reason - BUT - if you could just see the
> letters we get every day, and see the effect the information
> does have on the hearts and lives of people, you know it is
> worth while or they are the darnest liars there are in this
> country!  And isn't that it brings any great thing for me
> - it WASN'T!  Not in this world's goods - but the thing that
> is worth while, that it makes husbands say they are better
> husbands, and fathers, and sons and daughters that they are
> better sons and daughters - treat their own families better
> - treat their own neighbors' families better - and LIVE
> better!  and they've got more hope for the life to come!
> But I don't want to run on with a whole lot of this, for
> I know you are tired now.
> 
> Please remember us all to Mrs. [4498], Miss [4501] and
> Junior.  Let us hear from you, and with kindest personal
> regards to you, I am
> 
> Sincerely, [signed] Edgar Cayce [EC:GD]
