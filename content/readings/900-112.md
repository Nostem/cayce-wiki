# TEXT OF READING 900-112 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-112]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/17/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5315

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 17th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [900], of ... Road, Deal, N.J., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretations and lessons to be learned from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and the enquiring mind of this body.  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] we find, as given, are the interpretation of [[Soul|the soul]]'s development in the physical and spiritual elements viewing same, with emblematical forces that give the lesson to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]. These are given, as we see, that each may profit by same. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Morning of August 14, 1925, at home in Deal.  "Saw the huge concrete bridge the C.R.R. of N.J. is building, especially the wonderful $10,000,000.00 foundation."
(A)  This we find is giving to the mental forces of the body mind those illustrations of the stupendous work the body physically and mentally capable of coping with and the necessity of a firm and fixed foundation for same, would the best be accomplished by self and the work contemplated, no matter the character or kind, see?  Hence as the instructions and lessons, that each and every contemplated venture by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should be founded such as this viewed, that same may be carried through with impunity and with the truth as is necessary, for as is seen in this, as referred to in others, as bridge means way, foundation of the way must be right.

4. (Q)  Words that seemed in relation to some corporation, or association:  "[137], [900], and [106]."
(A)  This showing that the full cooperation is necessary in the minds, in the actions of each concerned, that the cooperation of each is necessary for the better development, the better preparations for every character of work, see?
