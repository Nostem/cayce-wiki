# TEXT OF READING 1202-2 F 25

**Reading [[1202-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/25/1927  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 6692

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [1202], via Mrs. [310].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body at the present time from that as we have had here before.  Many of these have been for the improvement of the physical condition, and the body very good at the present time, though there are conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] that were these corrected we would find the more perfect functioning of the organism of the body, and the nearer normal equilibrium kept in the circulation and the eliminations.

4. In the cerebro-spinal nerve centers we find there are indications of lesions that form in the various cycles of functioning of the body.  These, then, are the effects more of the nerve tension under which the body labors at times, and with this increased condition there is brought about obstruction in the nerve system and in the nerve centers that give to the functioning system from same improper incentives, as it were, as is seen in these centers, though in making the corrections these would be necessarily aligned all the way along the whole cerebro-spinal system.  Those at present causing disturbance are those in the cervical and upper dorsal region.  These, as we see in cervical, from the 5th to the 3rd, bring improper reaction, or nerve and blood supply in its giving and taking, as it might be expressed, or in the nerve energy and in the blood supply in eliminating the used forces from same, so that the sensory system becomes impaired, and the condition as is seen in eyes and facial nerves at times are the effects of this condition.  As is seen in the lower portion of cervicals, and in the upper dorsal, those extraordinary effects as created in the nerves of the upper portion of face, in nasal cavities, and in the bronchials, these are effects, then, that are caused by this improper nerve energy, or deflection in its action in the system of [1202].

5. Then, to correct these conditions we would, through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation, have these lesions ELIMINATED, by manipulation and correction of the energy, or loosening of those tautnesses produced in the whole cerebro-spinal nerve system, especially those in the cervical and upper dorsal, and those in the sacral.  This, as we see, will create in the system that as is necessary for the better elimination and better assimilations for this body.  At least twenty-six (26) such adjustments and treatments should be taken, see?  Take one every other day, or twice each week.  Anyway, at least this NUMBER will be necessary to make the full correction for the body.

6. No internal medicine will reach the condition, for, as we see, the whole sensory system, and the whole supplying of the mucus and tissue in the upper portion of body, will be relieved by these adjustments being made, and by the correction of these lesions as form, and as are formed at present, in the cervical and in the lower portion of cervical and upper dorsal.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What causes the abnormal condition pertaining to menstruation?
(A)  By the correction of the condition existent in the sacral region, and by the normal elimination and assimilation, these corrections will be made, see?  The lack of nerve energy being equally distributed in the system, see? Well, too, after these corrections are made, that the body wear that of the [[Radium|Radium Pad]], see?

8. (Q)  Which one?
(A)  The regular.  This should be worn across the solar plexus center, see?  for this will clarify blood and ELECTRIFY the nerve energy, by the change in [[Vibration|vibration]] as is created in body.  Do not wear pad until after corrections are made, see?

9. (Q)  What causes hay fever condition?
(A)  The inactivity, or the unequalized condition in the nerve energy and in the elimination and assimilation system, for with the lesion as forms in the dorsal region, the nerves of the face, nose, and bronchials, are super sensitive to those conditions as are of the atmospheric condition.  With the correction of these conditions in the cerebro-spinal system, and with the renewed [[Vibration|vibration]] as will be set by the [[Radium|Radium Pad]], we will find this condition will disappear, and there will be no necessity of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] changing climatic conditions for the correction of same, for the condition will be corrected that PRODUCES same, and the body will be near normal.

Do that.

10. (Q)  Does this same condition cause the trouble with the neck, also eyes?
(A)  Gives the trouble with the neck, also eyes, just as given.

11. That is all the questions.

12. We are through with this reading.
