# TEXT OF READING 1553-6 F 71

**Reading [[1553-6]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/19/1938  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 7521

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of August, 1938, in accordance with request made by the son-in-law, Mr. [1561], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the present physical condition of this body, with suggestions for further corrective measures; and special reference to throat condition, constipation, and paralysis of leg; answering the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find with this body, [1553] - in some respects there are improvements in the general conditions.  In others there is apparently still the lack of coordination through the general activity of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. If it is practical - and it may be made the more so by the general suggestions made to the body in the periods of the suggestive therapeutics that are being given the body occasionally - we would add these:

5. Use the Russian White Oil as an assistant in eliminations.

6. Have a GENERAL massage using an equal combination of Olive Oil AND Compound Tincture of Benzoin, over those portions of the limb and along the spine.  This will not only aid in quieting the body but the absorption of these properties will stimulate the better circulatory forces, with the suggestive forces as to the activity of the flow of circulation in the system - and thus quieting the nerves, stimulating the ganglia.  And especially make this stimulation, through the massage, in the areas of the upper dorsal and across the lumbar and sacral, as well as the limbs - or those centers from which impulses for nerve and blood supply are received for circulatory forces to the limbs.

7. These as we find, with the general conditions that have been indicated, are the better for this body in the present.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What causes the throat condition?
(A)  This as we find is still an impediment through the lack of the proper [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as may be received from the coordination of the sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems; and an inclination for the body-mind to "cloister" as it were, influences.  Thus the need for the suggestive therapeutics and the low ELECTRICAL [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body.

11. (Q)  Is it possible that this body is possessed by an unclean or [[Demons|evil spirit]] which causes peculiar crying and expression of rage at times?
(A)  As has been indicated, there is the inclination for the inner self to GATHER the influences of same.  Not a case of complete possession, but ENTERTAINING of such influences at times.

Hence the electrical forces will aid, with the suggestions, in eliminating these IMPRESSIONS - or POSSESSIONS of the mental attitudes.

Do these, and keep these active - ALL - for the system.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [1561] - Special Delivery
"    "  Ass'n file
