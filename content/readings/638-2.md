# TEXT OF READING 638-2 F 72

**Reading [[638-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/11/1934  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 4331

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of September, 1934, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [638]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, there are bettered conditions in some respects; yet the anxiety in many portions over the system from the lack of the stimuli that have been altered in the system produces the nervousness - and the anxiety on the part of the whole body.

3. As we find, there has been a too sudden change from the circulatory stimulant to a more normal reaction.  We would alter or change these in this manner:  Rather than giving the Gold in the system we would give it through the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Radio-Active Appliance, thus making the stimulation to the system but making it in a manner such as not to produce the strain from the lack of the heart stimuli.

4. We would use, then, the stimulus - that is, the digital and the strychnine - in rather restricted doses; not so much.

5. And we would continue with the massage.

6. As the changes come about there should be the gradual decrease in the amount of stimulus.

7. The Radio-Active Appliance would be attached to the torso of the body, to each extremity - in a circle about the body; first the plain anode to the right wrist, and the anode carrying the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] we would attach to that portion of the abdomen OVER the lacteal duct or to the right, and two to four inches - this being a larger plate - will cover. At the next treatment attach the plain anode first to the opposite side of the body, or the left ankle, while the anode carrying the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] would be attached to the same spot.  We would alternate the attachments in this manner.  The treatments would be given two or three times a week for thirty to forty to fifty minutes at a time.

8. The same doses of the digital as indicated heretofore.  The strychnine would be rather an eightieth grain, and reduced in number.

9. We will bring quiet and help to the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  How should the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] be prepared?
(A)  One grain to one ounce of distilled water, or fifteen grains to fifteen ounces of water - in that proportion. Do not use the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] with same; only the Gold solution in the anode or lead going to the trunk portion of body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Sister
"    " Ass'n file

> restless, nothing I can do seems to help much, although she
> sleeps some better than in the past.  I am using the
> appliance as directed."

> R6. 12/31/34 "My Sister is much better than when I first wrote
> you and I am sure the appliance is doing good work.  I think
> I have solved the massage problem.  I have a roller that I
> use - it is rubber balls on a wire cable, attached at each
> end to a handle.  As I roll it over her body each rubber
> ball revolves.  I use it as you directed for the massage.
> At first I didn't notice any quieting effect, but for the
> last week or more it has given fine results.  I am greatly
> encouraged...  Do you think my sister will ever recover?
> She seems in good physical health, but at times get very
> nervous over anything, small or large, and never mind what
> one tells her she declares she has been told that before...
> Much of the time she seems perfectly normal, so much so
> that one thinks at last she is over the trouble, then without
> any apparent cause back she goes, but she no longer gets
> frantic as she used to...  I am so grateful for what you
> have done for her - she is so much better than a year ago...
> Do you think if she had her teeth out it would help?"
>

> 
> 
> "Your letter of recent date I have just received, and am
> very glad to know that you feel your sister is better.
> I think you have solved the problem regarding the massage,
> and if this works out - as it apparently is doing - it should
> be just the thing.
> 
> "The results from the Radio-Active Appliances have been and
> are marvellous, even to us, Mrs. [...].  Of course, as yet
> we are somewhat at a loss for words when we try to tell
> people what they are supposed to do - these appliances; but
> there is more and more research work being done on them all
> the time, with some most unusual and remarkable reports.
> 
> "I wish I knew just how to answer you about your sister's
> condition.  Of course, at best, I should say that only
> palliative help could come - when we consider her age, and
> the ordinary span of life; yet from the experiences we have
> had time and again with the use of the appliances, it seems
> they give strength and vitality in such a measure that we
> hardly know what to expect.  As an illustration:  Some years
> ago a physician had a reading for his mother in [[Texas|Texas]], who
> was then about ninety-two years old, [actually 75 yrs.] very
> delicate, and complained a great deal of getting very cold.
> She would pile in bed with a lot of cover.  In her active
> life she had been a practical nurse.  But in the reading
> one of the Radio-Active Appliances was suggested to equalize
> her circulation.  She has now passed the century mark.
> [Actually 87 yrs. - GD's note:  This is an example of EC's
> exaggeration at times the story would have been just as
> effective if telling Mrs. [247]'s exact age.]  Quite recently
> I heard from her son-in-law, with whom she is staying.  He
> tells me that Mother [247] is still as spry as a cricket;
> gets about wherever she wants to go, back and forth between
> the towns there in the State, visiting her children - but is
> never to be found on a visit without her little appliance,
> which she carries along with her and - as he says - swears
> by it as she does her Bible.  So, I hardly know what to tell
> you.  Of course, if she recovers her memory at all it is
> going to be somewhat slow.  It wouldn't be well to irritate
> her, but to recall things might be a very good way to find
> the reactions building up in such a way that she might be
> entirely rid of the trouble - and be able to go along for
> some years yet.
> 
> "As to her teeth, I would suggest that you have a thorough
> examination made first - to see whether they should come
> out, and if it could be done without a great deal of worry
> and discomfort.  That would be better; because, as you very
> well know, the readings are usually quite practical but
> at times are different from what may be ordinarily termed
> practical by some physicians.  But I would advise that the
> examination be made.  Should at any time any condition arise
> about which you would like to ask me questions, please don't
> hesitate doing so.
> 
> "I certainly hope you all spent a very pleasant Christmas
> and that the New Year holds many joys and pleasures for you
> and yours."
