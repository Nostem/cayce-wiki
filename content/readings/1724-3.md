# TEXT OF READING 1724-3 M 40

**Reading [[1724-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/19/1939  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 7920

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1724], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, while the reactions that cause almost an extra flow of the blood, or hemorrhage, is apparently very discouraging to the body, this arises from too great a quantity of the electrical forces over certain portions of the impulse system.

4. We would leave these off, now, - that is, the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.

5. But we would have the deeper [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] adjustments with special reference to the 9th dorsal and those all the way through the cervical and upper dorsal; coordinating the lumbar and sacral axis and ileum plexus with same.

6. Be mindful that the diet is kept very close to those lines that have been indicated.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Who would be suggested now to give the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments? (Q)  Such as Dobbins.

9. (Q)  What are the developments since my last Reading?
(A)  As indicated.  Some have not been so good.  Hence the change that is indicated, with special reference to those areas.  For there has been set up sufficient of the coordination between superficial and deep circulation.  Now there needs to be a balance created, you see, between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system, through the nerve impulses and through the ganglia along the cerebrospinal system.

Still be mindful of the diet, and when necessary use those properties that cause a better flow from the pylorus to the lower portion, or the better assimilation in the digestive forces.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(1/19/39 GD's note of:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Dr. Dobbins.)
