# TEXT OF READING 459-14 F 45 (Widow, Hebrew)

**Reading [[459-14]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/22/1942  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 3354

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of May, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [459], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [459], ... Rd., ..., N.J., [GD's note:  At 4:45 P.M. a wire was rec'd from Mrs. [459], sent at 2:56 P.M. saying she would be at "... Ave., ..., N.Y. until 3:45 and ... St., ..., N.Y. until six."  Evidently EC gave the rdg. from her '[[Vibration|vibrations]]' in her N.J. home.] who seeks information, advice and guidance as to her health, her mental and material affairs in relation to self and children.  You will answer the questions she submits, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind, [459]; those conditions respecting the physical, mental and material welfare of self and children.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What is the present physical condition of my body? [See 959-11 on 1/17/41.]
(A)  Very good in most respects.  Precautions must be taken as to those changes being wrought in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  [Menopause?]  But the gradual adjustments are very good.

4. (Q)  Any special treatment I should have, or that I should follow at this time?
(A)  Only those precautions for such periods.

5. (Q)  What should the summer program be for [1856], [2444] and myself?
(A)  This, as we find, would depend upon the later developments that are to be expected.  If there is the [[Free Will|choice]] of self as respecting connection or association with a camp, keep [2444] with the body.  If there is the decision of self to become engaged in the activities as may be further carried on in association with brother [257] in the furniture, keep [2444] with the body.

If there are the activities practical, with the aid of [1856], with the service to be rendered, these would be better for the body than other associations or connections.

6. (Q)  Where should they attend university?
(A)  This should be the regular outcome that is prescribed in the courses that are being taken.

7. (Q)  Mrs. [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] Gorfinkle, owner of Camp Birch Crest and Cedar Crest camp in Maine, would like for us to associate with her.  [See 459-2 on 9/6/30 mentioning same connection.] A former Rdg. said Camp at certain periods.  Would you advise this?
(A)  This will be a very slow process or activity at this special season.  But as for a rest period, this might be a very good connection; not any amount of moneys made from same, to amount to anything.

8. (Q)  Would it be advisable for [1856] to work at Jewish Theological Seminary at N.Y. or Stephen Wise Institute, N.Y.?
(A)  The Theological Seminary.

9. (Q)  The following rabbis have designated an interest in [1856].  I would like to associate myself in the future with the temple that he is so [[Destiny|destined]] to assist.  Please advise me which one, and in what capacity:  Rabbi Rosenblum, Temple Israel, [[New York City|N.Y.C]].  [Rabbi Wm. F. Rosenblum.  See 459-14, Par. R1.]
(A)  (Interrupting)  This as we find would be the most advisable, and those associations and activities here would be the more satisfactory than ANY or either of the others. [Other Rabbis mentioned in [459]'s question, not allowed to be named in Rdg:  Rabbi Goldenson, Temple Emanuel, [[New York City|N.Y.C]].; Rabbi Samuel H. Goldenson; Rabbi Scharkman, New Rochelle, [[New York City|N.Y.C]]; Rabbi Marius Ranson, Temple Sharey Tefilo, East Orange, N.J.]  While there might be a great deal of sentiment in association with Rabbi Ranson and the work there, we find that with Temple Israel, with Rosenblum, would be the better of ANY of those.  As to the type, this would depend upon the manner or the development; but that as to the young people's work and its connection with the universal affairs to which there should be the lending of self, and building of self in those directions would be the better or greater channel for activities.

10. (Q)  Should I make connections with firms and carry out Home Furnishings or Ammunition at ..., West Va., with my brother [257]?
(A)  This for the time being, or for the next eighteen months, should be the better association; for later there would be those connections in the same associations that would be in the decorative - or in the furniture and of same.

11. (Q)  Any suggestions for good and welfare in the assistance of humanity by us three?  [[459], [1856] & [2444]]
(A)  In following those lines that have been indicated, either as to those associations with camp or in the activity of either of the children through the periods, and of this special season keep the [17 yr.] daughter [2444] with the body in WHATEVER service or activity is determined.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
