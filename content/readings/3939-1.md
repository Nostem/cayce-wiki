# TEXT OF READING 3939-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3939-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/8/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11579

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 8th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [3939].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [3939].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are conditions in this body that need correction.  They have to do with the elimination in the system, and the effect of this condition upon the functioning of organs as exercised through their cycles of functioning in the various stages in the system.  The effect coming to the used forces not eliminated as well as the functioning of the organs attempting to equalize the condition.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3939] we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this below the normal in quantity, and at times, though not always, below the normal in the supplying of the vitality necessary for the stored energy.  Hence, with this condition and that of centers affected, we find the depression to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], the worn or tired effect that comes to extremities and body, and the worn or tired effect even at times to the functioning of the sensory system.  Not of brain, but of sensory reaction. Eyes tired, hearing dull, speech below and the manifestations of sensation lost.  In the constituents or elements of blood force, this is that which is producing the lack of vitality, and hinders organs in their functioning at times, as we see in the liver and the upper portion of intestinal digestion. Lungs at times give through the diaphragm region or lower portion the expression of lack of carbon for system, and depression rather than distress in the organ as functioning.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find there are centers that are under distress from the blood supply and lack of nutriment.  Hence this relaxing in the perineurial center itself, or instead of inflammation created at centers, or the sheath, it becomes so relaxed as to give the expression as manifested in extremities and the sensory and portions of body.  This we find especially in the secondary centers of the hypogastric, and of the solar plexus, with some reflexes to the pneumogastric center.  Hence the food as taken in system often causes distress, and little of same seems to agree, yet not the indigestion as would occur from the elements of digestion not functioning properly.  Rather the lack nerve energy to carry the functioning of digestion to the intestinal system.  Hence in the pyloric, duodenum and in the upper end of the intestinal tract foods appear to remain overtime, as it were, often.  This give distress rather than being a condition that would produce trouble, unless allowed to remain too long in system.  The system is handled very well at times.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find as this:

6. Brain forces well balanced.

7. Sensory organism functioning with the condition as outlined, without specific condition save occasionally.

8. Lungs, larynx, bronchials:

9. Lungs show effect of blood supply and the effect as produced in the system.  No organic force.

10. Heart action very good.

11. Liver, spleen, pancreas:

12. Liver dormant in action.  Hence deep circulation.  Hepatics high.  Depression to the pancreas at such reaction.  Kidneys taxed at times, especially with the relaxing of the nerve system through the hypogastric.

13. Organs of the pelvis show the nerve relaxed condition rather than tension.

14. R E L I E F, to give then the relief to this body, we would take those properties in the system that would be the stimulation for the conditions in the blood supply and to assist equalizing of circulation through normal channels.

15. Take, then, this into the system:

Gum [[Camphor|Camphor]]................20 grains,
Sulfate of Morphia........1/2 grain,
Muriated Ammonia...........30 grains.

Mix well to-gether, and make into twenty pellets.  One would be taken each evening, and use the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over the whole system.  Not only over the centers in the cerebro-spinal system, but over frontal portion of body. Especially those centers that have to do with elimination from the organs that assist in blood distribution.

16. Do that.

17. We will find we will soon bring the normal conditions for this body.
