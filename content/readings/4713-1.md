# TEXT OF READING 4713-1 M 8 MONTHS

**Reading [[4713-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/30/1925  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 12674

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 30th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by his mother [583], through his father [953].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born August 13, 1924, about 7:00 P. M., Lutheran Memorial 
Hospital, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois. 
Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Daylight Savings Time.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life.  Also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those universal forces as have to do with the earth's influence and those conditions surrounding same.

3. In the present entrance we find the entity takes its position from Mercury and with Venus and Mars.  Hence we find one with these inclinations, and many that must be warned of, should the development be in the manner as would be for development of the entity from the spiritual phase in the present earth's plane:

4. One with the high temper, yet tempered by the better influences in Jupiter, but often those of an unreasonable nature.  Hence the care, the attention that must be given in the development of the mental forces in the present earth's plane.  We find those influences will be in the direction of mathematical calculations, especially as pertaining to electrical energy, and would be well for every condition to be surrounded the entity in its mental development towards conditions pertaining to such elements.  Then we would find the wonderful mental development of the entity in the present earth's plane.

5. One that will have the inclinations of the sickness in early childhood and young manhood of fevers and of conditions pertaining to the digestive system.  Hence the care and attention that should be given in regards to these [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

6. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]]:

7. In the one before this we find in the present sphere and among those who first entered the western shores of present country in seeking the treasure of the earth's storehouse. In the name then of James Johnson.  In the vicinity of what is now Eldora Pas, [[California|California]].  In this experience in the earth's plane we will find these urges gradually developing in the present entity:

8. One given to proclaim truth, yet in such a manner as may be understood in many ways.

9. In the one before this we find in the English rule of George the 1st in [[England|England]].  Then in the name of Earl Warwick and in the House of Commons.  History will be found of this individual, as we will see.

10. In the urges then as will be in the present entity:  That of the domineering spirit of self's development pertaining to knowledge.  Hence the training that will be necessary in earthly development in the earth's plane of unselfishness.

11. In the one before this we find in the far southern country, when the rule in then the Ohums were given over to the rule of the developing race.  The entity then in that of Objudhi and was the armor bearer, or the scribe to the last ruler of the Ohums [Aymaras], who were an unwarlike people subdued by the peoples coming in from the place of many waters [[[Atlantis|Atlantis]]?], in the south country from there.

12. In the urge as will be found in the present entity:  That of the ability to correlate data in a manner that will be at fingertips, as it were, in memory and mathematical form.

13. In the one before this, in now the far eastern countries of the Mongoloids, when the peoples were becoming united as groups to guard against the invasion of beast and man.  The entity then was in that of Hiliho and the ruler of the people as first set in that fair valley.  The urge in the present will be in the direction of that development in aerial craft as pertaining to electrical unit and electrical force.

14. Then, that to which the entity may attain in present earth's plane, and how:  With the correct guiding through the moulding age, the entity will take on those urges of the electrical forces and application of same in a mathematical manner beyond that as has been undertaken heretofore. Keeping all in that manner of service to man and Creator, for without that incentive, though the world be gained, nothing if it lose its own identity, or [[Soul|soul]].

15. We are through.
