# TEXT OF READING 184-2 M 40

**Reading [[184-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/29/1924  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 692

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195] and Mr. G. S. Klingensmith.]

Time of Reading 
11:2O A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Calif. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find conditions are much improved in this body in the general way from that as we had here before.  While the corrections in the structural portion, and especially in the coccyx, have not been wholly corrected in a manner that there is not the return of some of the conditions produced by this condition, yet there are other conditions in the body at the time that are causing distress to portions of the functioning system.

2. Now, these are conditions to be corrected at the present time.  We find the heat was not applied in the correct manner and in the times as given for the corrections at the time the adjustments and the massage were to be given to the body. This has been assisted, however, a great deal.  We would correct this condition in the end of the spine.  We would apply the heat in this manner.  Take a towel or cloth (heavy) as turkish cloth.  Dip in warm water (not overly hot but warm water).  Fold and apply to this portion of spine.  Then apply [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] of the electric reflector that will heat the exterior portions and also make the heat through the applied cloth very much more intense, and with this relaxing it will be found that adjustments can easily be made, and that with the body resting until the system has again reached its equilibrium in temperature the assistance to the body will be of a great deal more benefit.  Also for the condition that is existing in the hepatic circulation, and the distress that the kidneys and lower intestinal tract is being put to, will be reached by the adjustments properly made in the lower dorsals, 12th, 11th, 10th and the 1st lumbars, with the gentle massage of the diaphragm region from frontal portions.

3. Medicinal properties should not be taken in the system at the present time, save salines may be taken for the intestinal tract.  Do not overeat of meats.

4. Do this.  Follow this consistently, persistently.  We will bring the perfect equilibrium in this body.

5. (Q)  How often should the chiropractic treatments be given?
(A)  Every third day.

6. (Q)  How many of these chiropractic treatments should be given?
(A)  Until the body is perfectly normal.  Depends on the attitude of the one treated and the one treating as to how well and efficient the work is done.  Should be within ten to eighteen treatments.

7. (Q)  Have you anything further to say regarding this body?
(A)  Do as we have given for the best.
