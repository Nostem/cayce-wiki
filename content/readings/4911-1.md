# TEXT OF READING 4911-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[4911-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/11/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13020

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [4880].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Rosenberg.]

Time of Reading 
2:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find while this body is very good at the present time, for it is in the developing state; yet there are those conditions in the physical that are beginning to manifest from existing conditions, and those that have preceded in the system that unless they are corrected will bring destructive elements to the body, and a constitutional condition that would become a chronic condition with the functioning of organs, yet if corrected at the present time will relieve the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the body, for they are specific conditions and have to do with the structural portion of the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4911] we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, at the present we find this very good. There, however, is carried in the system that affects the blood supply that condition as produced by the functioning of the kidneys and of the lower intestinal tract.  This, we see, produced by a condition in the body.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, very good, though the latent or inner nerves of certain centers show how the impinionment of those nerves affect the functioning of the organs of the body. In those centers of the 8th and 9th dorsal do we find these conditions.  In the effect that this then produces upon the system is the condition produced in the pancreas and duodenum, with the hypogastric center that affects the conditions proper, and brings the improper functioning and elimination in the kidneys themselves.  Hence this condition will produce the action of the these portions of the organism, and render them helpless in a manner in their proper functioning, unless the condition is corrected.  We would find also, in the later years, it would affect the lower portion of the right limb, for the pressure comes to the right side, and the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the locomotaries that affect the locomotion in the lower plexus, or that of the ilium plexus proper.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, we find these very good at the present.  The digestive tract shows the inroads as have been made in the system, and the condition has become accentuated in the intestinal tract by the pressure as we find in the dorsal region.

6. R E L I E F:  Then to bring the normal for this body would be to remove those lesions we find in the region of the 7th, 8th, 9th and 10th dorsal by [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation, taking into the system those properties that will produce counter irritation through functioning of the kidneys and of the pancreas, as we would find in this:

7. Every tenth day take with the water that is taken in the system, or with a glass of water as would be given at that time:

Sweet Spirits of Nitre.....1 minim,
Oil of Juniper.............1 minim.

8. They will affect and produce an irritation that must be produced, that the stimulation to these portions of the organs may make the correct adjustment.

9. Do that.  We will find we will bring the correct developing throughout of the nerve system, blood supply and the functioning of the organs of digestion.
