# TEXT OF READING 618-2 F 60

**Reading [[618-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/2/1934  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 4227

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of October, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [618], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Some conditions are very good; some are not so well.

2. As we find, there is a tendency for the exercising of the mental forces of the body in such measures and such ways as to make for disconcerting activity in the assimilations of the body.  Hence we have the tendencies for the formation of gases in the body, indicated by the times when there is the apparent MOVEMENT of the intestinal system, or the roaring, growling, grumbling throughout the system.  This is also indicated by the tautness in the lower portion of the descending colon, by the tautness in the sphincter muscles.

3. If there will be used a DEEP [[Vibration|vibration]] in the 9th dorsal and in the sacral area, of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], this PARTICULAR condition may be overcome.

4. As to the digestive area, be more mindful that the activities of the body-mind are quiet and easy.  Do not eat when excited.  Do not eat because it is just the time to eat.  Eat when the appetite calls for it, and in most cases - as well as for this body - WHAT the appetite calls for; but do not gormandize at any time.  TEND towards those foods that are of the alkalin reacting nature, rather then acids.  Make one meal entirely of raw vegetables.  Even if you have to include meats in it, eat it raw!  But don't eat meats, or not great quantities of them.

5. We will find the body will overcome these conditions, if we will follow those suggestions.

6. And keep occasionally a dose of Upjohn's Citrocarbonate and Milk of Magnesia; not both on the same day, no, but once or twice a week take each of these, or one or the other of these.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What causes breaking out under arm?
(A)  The toxic forces that return to the circulation, as indicated.

9. (Q)  Is it time to have my glasses changed?
(A)  If the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are kept a little bit longer, we will find by the 18th to 20th of the present month it will be time to change the glasses.

10. (Q)  Any further advice for my better welfare?
(A)  The body is on the improve.  Don't be overanxious, but keep quiet, even keel.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
