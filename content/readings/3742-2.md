# TEXT OF READING 3742-2 F 33

**Reading [[3742-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1930  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 11251

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Boyd Davis and Bryant Speed.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  The physical applications for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as become distorted in the body, as has been effective in the system, are on the improve - and we only need the quiet, the rest, the awakening of the ASSURANCE within self of the ABILITIES to carry on as desired.  Then, just KEEP it in that manner, and at the present keep that as has been given.  Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
