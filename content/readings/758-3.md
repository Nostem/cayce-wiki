# TEXT OF READING 758-3 M 7

**Reading [[758-3]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/16/1929  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 4620

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of September, 1929, in accordance with request made by his parents, Mr. [417] and Mrs. [1192].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. and Mrs. Morris [[Rome|Rome]], Dr. Berger, Mrs. [1192], Mr. Geo. S. Shaw, and others.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
12:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [758].  This we have had before. Now, we find conditions are much bettered, for with the changes as have and are coming about with the pressure being relieved from the lower portion of the dorsal and lumbar region, we may expect the better coordination through the system.  There may be expected periods when there, apparently, will be retractions - but, with the general physical condition improved, and with the pressure removed, we will find the better coordinating forces operating throughout this system.

2. CARE should be taken in those adjustments as will be necessary IN the lumbar and upper sacral and lower dorsal, but keep that manner in which these are BEING corrected, and we will find these will respond.

3. As for those conditions as are found in the upper dorsal and the cervical, as have to do with the sensory connection with the sympathetic and cerebrospinal, DO NOT relax these so much as to allow the OVER activity THROUGH these.  Rather produce that tautness in the manipulations, so that - as the [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]] are changed from those low forms of the electrical forces to and from the body - these will bring the proper reactions as the coordination takes place in the nerve system.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is body responding to treatment?
(A)  As just given.

5. (Q)  Are treatments being given as ordered?
(A)  As just given.

6. (Q)  When can we expect decided change spoken of in first reading?
(A)  As soon as there are the proper coordinating forces, for it must be gradual - but will show much greater improvement as soon as the pressure on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], as has been given, is removed.

7. (Q)  How soon can we expect body to overcome excitable manner?
(A)  In the next cycle of change, fully.

8. (Q)  Should the treatment be given stronger to the cervical and brachial plexus?
(A)  As has just been given!

9. (Q)  Should firm discipline be used?
(A)  Strong discipline, but in a LOVING manner.

10. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Do these first - then we may give more.  We are through with this reading for the present.

Two copies to Mrs. [1192] Copy to Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
