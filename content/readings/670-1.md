# TEXT OF READING 670-1 M 19 (Protestant)

**Reading [[670-1]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/24/1934  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 4434

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of September, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father, Mr. [333], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [670]''s parents; Minnie, Irene and George Hirn; Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Born May 28, 1915, about 8:00 A. M., [[Canada|Toronto]] General 
Hospital, [[Canada|Toronto]], [[Canada|Canada]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those relations with the universe and universal forces, that are latent and manifested in the personalities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as [670].

2. In entering the present experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], and in giving that which may be helpful in the present experience, well that the [[Astrology|astrological]] as well as the innate influences in the experiences of the mental and [[Soul|soul]] development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] be considered in their proper relationships.

3. First, in entering, we find the entity coming under the influences from the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]]; not because the planets were in a given position but the indwellings of the associations of the entity-[[Soul|soul]] in those environs having an influence, as:

4. Neptune - making for the interests of the entity in those things having to do with the out of doors, with the activities of animated objects or beings, or animals; and in the mysterious nature, as the reading, the stories, the influences that make for an activity in the experience that must deal with something in the mystical or mystic life of individuals.  These are as innate influences, and this lends also to the abilities in the entity - if the applications are made in self in the material sense - to make or to become interested in things dealing with the nature of inventions; such as traps, catches, things dealing with the activities out of doors, peculiar character of springs, locks, fastenings, that deal with the mysterious activities or workings in wood, that would deal with anything having to do with the mysterious influences or that are as a mystery in the experience of one.

5. Hence we find that certain types of puzzles are of an interest to the entity; something of the mysterious nature, as in cryptograms, anagrams, would find an interest for the entity - and these may be used in the material world as experiences that may be made into applicable or practical things for an advancement in the material things of the entity's experience.

6. Also in Mars, as related to the activities under Saturn, the influences are such that LITTLE things often change the whole activity.  An unkind word, a cross word here or there often to the entity means much more than a good bawling out respecting some activity in which the entity feels or knows there have been errors or that conditions might have been changed for the betterment.  Yet these influences make for the same changing as to what are the desires, wishes or activities in which the entity may engage itself; tending to become - with those influences that have been seen - rather the dreamer, the visioning of things and planning what may be made activative in the influence or experience of the entity in itself.  These are as influences that may be made for weal or woe.  For, if such an entity or [[Soul|soul]] were to find itself without those influences that must ever be as [[Ideal|an ideal]] to which the balance may be drawn as to its relationships with others, as to its changes in its experiences or environs or activities, these would make for detrimental conditions - unless safe and sure in those things that bespeak not only of the moral but of the mental and spiritual life as well.

7. These things, then, would be as warnings to the entity and to those who would advise or counsel with the entity; not merely from fear, not merely from being in the right in this direction or nature, but as from that which bespeaks from the conscience's sake, those things in which the judgements or activities of the entity may engage - either in the mental, material or spiritual - ever must be governed by those things of a spiritual source or nature.  For, as will ever be seen in any influence, the spirit is willing but the flesh is weak.  The environs and hereditary influences that may be said to be from the purely material sense would never answer in the activities for this entity, whether in relationship to material or spiritual things, as an answer for that to which it would bring itself in relationships with or without these conditions.  For the basis in this entity's experience must be that which is well-grounded in self from first taking counsel within self and knowing from what basis, from what standard the judgements of the activities are to be judged or governed; and these will make for the general assurance within the experiences that the outcome of whatever activity in which the entity may engage in the material sense will bring for the experience that which is worth while.  For, it is not all of life alone to live nor all of death to die, to those that have visioned - and do [[Visions|vision]] - that [[Purpose|purpose]] for which the experience of an entity is engaged in its passage through any particular experience in the earth.

8. The influences also from Jupiter, we find, will come shortly in the experience of the entity; and - as we will find, with the directing of self in those activities that may be outlined in the experience - in the latter portion of the coming month, when Jupiter is as a ruling influence in the activities of those born under those signs and environs in which the entity finds itself, these will become more of the benevolent nature in the experience of such an entity.  And then these are not only as an influence for good, but used properly may bring the associations in the broader activities of a material life that will make for successes in the EXPERIENCES - if these are grounded in those truths of spiritual life that have been indicated in the experience of this entity, now known as [670].

9. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], then, in the earth, and their influence upon the entity in the present experience:

10. Before this we find the entity was in those lands of the present nativity, rather than of birth, but about those same lands where there were many changes being made during the periods when oppressions came from the disputations between the various lands regarding the ownership, and as to whom and to which Crown there should be the payment of taxes or dues as to their relationships in carrying on the governmentations.

11. The entity then, in the name Robert Enezel, was near the upper portion of Lake Champlain, and in the activities there as the miller.  Yes, it still may be found in those portions of same - in the waters that enter into this expanse - where Enezel with the others defended this land, where they had chosen to set their own possessions; a miller, an actor in reclaiming the land from the forest - the making of the relationships with the dwellers in the land during that period.

12. With the coming of the disputations the entity joined with the active forces against the English peoples, but with the [[France|French]] - and these made for periods and days of turmoils and strife, and yet these joined in to make for the periods of a soul development; for when one in a material or physical life expends self in service for others, SOUL development, spiritual enlightenment, must be the result of same - for it is according to the judgements that have been set, "As ye do it unto the least of these, my little ones, ye do it unto me."  And it has been given as to Who, in answer to "When saw we thee in prison and visited thee, when saw we thee ahungered and gave thee drink?"  These were the activities of the entity, and through the experience brought much to the soul development of the entity.

13. In the present from the experience we find varied inclinations in the activities of this entity.  Kind, gentle-hearted; yet often very stern, often very hardheaded in determining factors in the experiences of the activities of the entity; these harkened from those things innately builded within the experience of the soul.

14. Before that we find the entity was in that land now known as the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], during those periods when there were those followings for those that had named the Name of Him that walked in the land of promise.

15. The entity was among those of the soldiery that were sent from the [[Rome|Roman]] land to keep not only the peace but to make for the collecting of the customs in that land; joined in with the soldiery that made for the guard about those of the land when there were the rebellions that arose just previous to the arrest of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] and the Savior of men.

16. Then the entity heard much of that as went on during that period of activity.  This brought conviction in the inner self, yet fear of those things that would be said about one for embracing the cause that apparently was lost with [[Crucifixion of Jesus|the crucifixion]] of the leader caused the entity to keep same within self - and only with those periods of persecution after the entity had returned to the [[Rome|Roman]] land were there activative forces in the actions or the experiences of the entity, and then this brought to the entity troublesome days and periods; for the entity befriending the ones that had embraced the Cause lost in the physical and material things because of its kindly act.  This has made in the experience of the entity in the present those things where it has been rather uphill to even talk with the entity concerning such experiences in the life, yet INNATELY and within self judgements have been passed - and oft does the entity counsel with self without giving the material expression concerning same.

17. In the name then Philose, the entity PHYSICALLY lost; AGAIN spiritually, soully, gained - for those experiences brought turmoil in the material life yet satisfaction in the inner self.

18. Before that we find the entity was in that land now known as the [[Persia|Persian]] or Arabian, during those periods when there was the building of the city in the plains and hills where many gathered for their activities, in the ways between the northern and southern lands, the ways between the eastern and [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] lands.

19. The entity then was among the people from the [[Greece|Grecian]] land who came into that land for aid in the physical things; given then to those that made for the developments of the period in the out of door exercise for the developments of the body in relationships to the activities of individuals.  In the experience in the [[Persia|Persian]] land much of the mental and spiritual forces were added in the experience of the entity during that [[Incarnation|sojourn]] or experience, for the BODY developed physically, and material things, material wealth, material welfare that had been the principal of the entity's activities and [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; yet broken in body through the activities in that particular sojourn gained again the position in those activities.  Then, when there were rebellions in the land, and the sojourn of the entity's own people, the entity - Exerexten brought for the peoples of the land aid and help; for the DEFENSE of the city and the saving of those portions depended upon the activities of the entity [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

20. Then, the entity gained in the material, and also in the mental and physical experiences during that sojourn.

21. Before that we find the entity was in that land now known as the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]], during the periods when there were the turmoils after the first outburst - or the separation of the isles and the land.

22. The entity was among the people that were with the [[Sons of Belial|sons of Belial]], that warred with the sons of the One influence - or the forces that are known as the relationship of man with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] that may be manifest in the earth.  Yet with [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]], there was the gaining activity because of the influence in the experience that in the present would be termed GOOD.  When those divisions again came, the entity was among those that attempted to leave the land for the sojourning in other lands; bringing eventually those activities in what is now known as the Central American land.

23. In the experience the entity became in the latter days or sojourn, in those active forces in what may be termed the Incal land, the second in command; becoming the priest in the latter portion of its sojourn.

24. As to the abilities, then, and that to which the entity may attain in the present:

25. From the experiences of the entity through its earthly sojourn, and those influences that brought to the entity activative forces for soul or material development, it may be seen that when the promptings of the self's activity in the mental or [[Physical Body|physical body]] are from the spiritual sources, peace and harmony come with the experience of a soul.  When these arise from the self-aggrandizement of self's own interests, they bring turmoil and strife not only within the mental but the material experiences of an entity.

26. Then, first find self - and the promptings of the inner self. What are the motivative influences in the experience?  And enjoin self in those things that may deal with the constructive force in the creating of new experiences for man, in the activative forces of those things that become creative in the material plane these will offer an outlet for the abilities of the entity that will bring material-mental success to the experience of the entity.  Or join with those who may be in that field of activity of the scientific research into other fields, where there are the development for the searching for lost or hidden civilizations or activities.  This will bring the experience to the entity, through the abilities, for the creating of aids and helps to man's experience - in its active forces from without - which will bring understanding in many of the fields of activity of the entity.

27. There are those expeditions being formed; as from the Pennsylvania University, even from some of the other universities in the northwest, or the Smithsonian Institute, to which the entity may join by the latter portion of the coming month - or by those regular activities when such begin in February of the coming year, when these may bring activative forces that will be in keeping with that to which the entity may attain in the present.  But let ever this be as the guide for the things that may bring the greater help in ANY experience:

28. Study to show thyself approved unto thy Maker that meets thee from within, avoiding the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of evil, keeping self unspotted from the world; for THESE bespeak of those that are the children of light.

29. Ready for questions.

30. (Q)  For what vocation in life is the entity best fitted?
(A)  An inventor, or an aid in scientific research in fields of activity that may seek for lost civilizations.

31. (Q)  What further training is necessary for this?
(A)  In the one, the application of the ideas of the entity in a more practical way and manner.  In the other, the joining with others in research, that necessary for not only the geographical but the scientific research in the various fields - whether in the East or in the Far East, or whether in the central portion of the own land, these make for their own developments, or the TRAININGS in same, through whatever field in which there is the activity.

32. (Q)  How may it be obtained - this training?
(A)  By making application to or through those channels that seek those who may aid in this particular field of endeavor.

33. (Q)  What use should he make of the natural gift in drawing?
(A)  It will be necessary to draw for these things, should this be chosen in this field of activity - and be well!

34. (Q)  How may this gift be further developed and expressed?
(A)  As seen from the experiences and the inclinations, to the entity it must be forced as a necessity for its own better expression of self.

35. (Q)  Should he specialize in any one phase of outdoor activity?  If so, which?
(A)  As we would find, it would be better in the field of research.

36. (Q)  What steps should he take in the coming month?
(A)  Make the connections, associations and applications to be among or a portion of the parties in such research.

37. (Q)  In what capacity?
(A)  As an aide.

There are researchers in Montana, in [[Arizona|Arizona]], in [[Yucatan|Yucatan]]; there are researchers in Honduras, in Asia, in [[Egypt|Egypt]], in the Chaldean country - all over!  All of these expeditions go from - MOST of them are financed from - the SURROUNDINGS of the entity.  Contact these various ones.  The abilities and the activities of the entity in those fields may be far-reaching.

Become associated with Andrews; become associated with the University - with any of these, whichever field is chosen!

Do that.

38. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
