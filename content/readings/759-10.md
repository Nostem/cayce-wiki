# TEXT OF READING 759-10 M 5

**Reading [[759-10]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/6/1934  Sex: M  Age: 5  ReadingID: 4667

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 6th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [257], Mrs. [903], Bertha and [[King David|David]] Levy.  Also Eva Levy.]

Time of Reading 
1:00 to 1:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Those conditions we find here are the effects of much that we have indicated before as being present in the body, through the poisons that make for the effect upon the blood supply, that produces the temperature which arises from the infection in the system from the tonsil condition.

2. We would take 'em out!

3. (Q)  When?
(A)  When the temperature is somewhat lower.

4. (Q)  Is this a safe time of the year to have the tonsils taken out?
(A)  (Mumbling in undertone, "February 15, 1927 - November 6, 1934 - ")  By the 10th or 15th of the present month.

5. (Q)  What would be the proper way to reduce the temperature now?
(A)  By the applications that have been indicated heretofore for the relief, through drainages that may be set up in the system, and by the activity of the perspiratory and respiratory system.

6. (Q)  What will eliminate the warts from the hand and body?
(A)  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] them with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]].

7. (Q)  How often?
(A)  Every evening.

8. (Q)  When should we see results from this treatment for the warts?
(A)  In two to three weeks.

9. (Q)  What is the cause of the warts?
(A)  It is the accumulation of cellular forces attempting to act themselves.  Or, as we see, every atom of the body is as a whole universe or an element in itself.  It either coordinates or it makes for disruptive forces by its activity being expelled from the system through the activity of the eliminating system; and as it accumulates it gathers those things about it and is not absorbed.  Hence we have them as moles or warts.

10. (Q)  What causes the unusual perspiration of the feet?
(A)  The circulation being disturbed, of course, especially through the hepatics, produced by poisons or the attempt of the body to throw off same.  This being cut off in the lower portions of the system makes for the accumulations there.

Do these, as we find for the body [759], would be the best in the present.

11. (Q)  How should the proper drainage for the system be set up?
(A)  Use those powders that have been given for same.

12. (Q)  What diet does the body need?
(A)  Those things that are easily assimilated, and that are NON-acid (which means no meats, sugars and starches) are the better for the diet.

13. (Q)  Does that mean the use of no sweets or sugars?
(A)  Very little.

14. (Q)  Is what form should the sweets be taken?
(A)  In fruits or vegetables.  Not that NO sugars should be included, but let it be rather in the form of custards, blancmange, arrowroot, junket or such, that are the natural sweets and are better than those from cane sugar.

15. (Q)  Should meat be included in the daily diet?
(A)  This would depend upon whether we are speaking of the individual conditions in the present or of general conditions.  In general conditions, we must know that there is a GROWING body; that there is necessarily the usage of the body in its activity, and these energies must be supplied - else we make for a drain upon the whole of the nerve and blood supply of the body.  Hence meat should be a portion; not the GREATER portion - each day; not necessarily every single meal, but each day.

16. (Q)  What causes the sound of regurgitation in the throat occasionally?
(A)  The attempt of the lymph supply to overcome to meet the needs of the unusual conditions in the throat, or the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the throat, as in tonsils.

17. (Q)  To what do you refer as the powders for the proper eliminations?
(A)  Those that have been made for the use of same for this body!

18. (Q)  Were these given in previous readings?
(A)  They were given in previous conditions that were similar to the present!

19. (Q)  Will you describe these powders; tell us what they are?
(A)  We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file
