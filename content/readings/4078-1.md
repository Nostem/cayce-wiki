# TEXT OF READING 4078-1 M 40

**Reading [[4078-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/10/1944  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 11774

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Capt. [4078], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                             Bachelor Officers 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M.                 Quarters, 
Taken 4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Fla.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find there are disturbances.  These arose first from congestion through the soft tissue of face and throat. Gradually these have grown into a catarrhal condition.

3. This has caused a prolapsus to the Eustachian tube.

4. We would prepare the body for [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgery, through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments and [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  This will require at least treatments three times a week for three to four weeks to prepare the body for the finger surgery, and this will require some little time - to keep these from adhering again.

5. Thus the ability of hearing will be increased at least thirty to forty percent from the condition in the present.  This will also set up better circulation throughout the whole body.

6. Do increase the eliminations through these periods, though it may be necessary at times to use high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations for the body.

7. These do, if we would make the better conditions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Why is it that some times when I yawn I can hear better momentarily?
(A)  Because it opens the Eustachian tube.  It is the prolapsus in the Eustachian tube that runs from the ear to the mouth.  If you open your mouth you hear better.  If you're on a train you hear better.  You hear better in an elevator if it is running up and down.

10. (Q)  Can the hearing increase sufficient to get oversea duty?
(A)  As indicated it will be increased thirty to forty percent.  You'll get over, but it depends on where you are sent as to whether this will be to the advantage or disadvantage.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self (two copies - one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgeon - Let us have the name of the one you choose.)
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note at the time of the reading:  Among the members of the American Osteopathic Association we find listed in West Palm Beach, Fla.:  Dr. Howard K. Brinklow, 1009-11 Harvey Bldg., and Dr. C. E. Dove, Guaranty Bldg.  If neither of these is an osteopathic surgeon, they can refer you to someone in that field.)
