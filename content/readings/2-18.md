# TEXT OF READING 2-18 M 49

**Reading [[2-18]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/14/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 19

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of October, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Wigal and Wallin, Miss Painter and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:5O P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2] - this we have had before. In the general physical functioning, we find conditions on the improve.  Not much change in the refractory nerves of the eye proper.  These, however, show strength in the forces of the system, and with the keeping up of the GENERAL health - and with the stimuli as comes from the usage of those applications to the eye PROPER - we will find these WILL be on the improve.

2. We would use occasionally - at least once or twice each week (but do not use the Degnen's lens on such days) the double eye applicator of the violet ray over the eye proper.  Use same for one and a half to two and a half minutes at a time. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How long before body will be able to see, under present treatment and conditions?
(A)  With changes, and with the continued use of those as OUTLINED, we will find improvement will be seen in the next fortnight.

4. (Q)  What causes burning and numbness of legs?
(A)  This produced most by inactivity of the body with the mental forces.  When action or exercise is taken, this is much lessened.

5. (Q)  Should all present treatment be continued?
(A)  All present treatment be continued, save as on Tuesdays and Thursdays when there is the use of the double eye applicator for the eyes INSTEAD of the lens on those days. Do that.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Wigal Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self to send home
