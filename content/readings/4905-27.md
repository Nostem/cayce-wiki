# TEXT OF READING 4905-27

**Reading [[4905-27]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/11/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12952

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mrs. Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] E. Evans ([[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]]''s mother).]

Time of Reading 
3:00 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.

1. GC:  You have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on the land of Madison Byron Wyrick in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].  You will give the conditions of operations at the present time, giving any suggestions that will assist the operators in bringing in this well.  Also telling of any conditions of which they should be warned.  You will answer any questions relative to this I may ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], the operations here.  We find there has been trouble with the operations, for the cavings and the tools have been giving troubles.  They have finally succeeded in getting these straight and have again begun the drilling in of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]].  We will find that within the next forty-eight (48) hours there will be good information from the operations.  Patience, care, attention, and we will find successful termination of these efforts, for we will encounter the production.

3. (Q)  At what depth are they drilling at present?
(A)  Fifty-three fifty-six (5356).

4. (Q)  Is there anything special of which they should be warned?
(A)  Most has passed at present.  These are very good conditions that are beginning at present.
