# TEXT OF READING 522-6 F 48

**Reading [[522-6]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/21/1935  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 3671

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1867 Kalorama Road, Washington, D. C., this 21st day of February, 1935, in accordance with request made by the son, Mr. [309], for his mother, Mrs. [522], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Lillian B. Whitmore and J. Howard Lawler.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - [522].  As we find, those conditions that disturb and bring about distresses are from accumulations of sedimentary forces produced by the character of administrations that have been given the body from time to time.  These produce a congesting condition in the functioning of the eliminating system, as do any drugs that make for an affecting of the nerve reactions in the circulatory forces of the body.

2. There are accumulations in the liver and gall duct area especially, and the poisons from the system generally from toxic forces have contributed to the condition.  And if these continue to contribute, unless there are the measures used either to empty the gall duct or to cause the solving, they will necessarily require operative forces to remove same. This, as we find, may be done by the gall duct pump, or they may be solved by the application of heat as from oil - such as the oil packs over the area, for sufficient time to make for the enlarging by relaxing of the duct area itself; making through absorption that necessary, as with a purgative such as from a quantity of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] internally - hot; with the properties that would make for the engorging or the expanding of that particular area with the system's activity. Thus we may EMPTY the sediments that form there.  These are only as sediments, or gravel, as yet; but they produce an irritation and prevent the natural secretions and activities for the digestive system, causing excruciating pain and making for those conditions that are very detrimental to the body.

3. In either of these ways, or that which would be chosen, we would empty same; which would be PREFERABLE to operative measures in the present, as we find.

4. Then, if the hot oil method is chosen, we would begin with the hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs and keep them constantly on the body for three days - or in periods of twenty-four hours, as hot as they can be kept on the body, over the liver and gall duct area.

5. At the end of the three days, begin taking the hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] internally; two to three tablespoonsful, twice or three times.

6. (Q)  Is the neuritis caused by a dead tooth?
(A)  As has been indicated, this was caused from a condition in the teeth, as well as a general condition in the gall duct area.  And, naturally, the condition or the change in the activities - as indicated has its bearing upon the nervous forces in the body itself.

7. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  These we would do for these conditions; either the drainages by the pump, as indicated, or the Castor Oil externally and internally - which will clear the system the more thoroughly; and keep away from the use of all the "ols" ["oils"?] that have been given!

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Directions were enclosed for preparing Castor Oil packs. But apply as indicated in reading.)
