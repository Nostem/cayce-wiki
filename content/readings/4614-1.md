# TEXT OF READING 4614-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4614-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/27/1911  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12524

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 27th day of March, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno. Mr. [4614].]

Time of Reading 
12:00 P. M.

1. LBC:  You have before you Mr. [4614], who is in this room. Go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly, and tell us what is the matter with him.

2. EC:  You see, we have here in this body, of [4614] that which affects not only the [[Physical Body|physical body]], that is, statue [stature], and make-up of the whole organism of the body, but muscular fibers, nerve, circulation, and blood properties of the body, or that which affects the organism, of organs in the body, in themselves.  We have in this system, as we have in most bodies, organs involved in the body which affect the circulation, or the blood, which is the main attribute to the [[Physical Body|physical body]], or that which keeps life in the whole system, or body, in itself.  The seat of the trouble, as we have it here now, is through the organ of the liver and its circulation, or its properties to the circulation of the blood in the body.  The liver secretes and excretes both, taking from the blood the used up red blood corpuscles, nerve ends, matter that is rejected, or refused forces, used up by the muscular force, eliminating them from the body, secreting from the digestion proper, that which carries on digestion through the lower intestines here.  We have first had into the system, taken in those particles which fasten or effect themselves first to the circulation in the body proper. They, thrown out, or carried through the circulation, show themselves first in the organ of the liver, secreting, or taking up these excretions from the blood, not being destroyed or taken out by these cells, it has brought about the condition we have in the body here, of a lack of blood cells in the body in themselves, or in the blood, to carry on the rebuilding of the whole organism of the body.  These, then, being carried back in the system, we have the carrying of the poison back into the system, producing to the whole organism the condition we have existing in the system.  They show themselves in the capillary circulation, in the lymph, in the body excreted to the exterior part of the body.  Shows itself then through the forces they exert into the system. Coming then to an extra amount of the matters close to the body, we have the condition along the ends of the bones here, producing then a lack of matter to carry on the forces of rebuilding of the body.  Not being excreted or eliminated from the system by the proper channels, they show themselves to the surface of the body.

3. (Q)  What other conditions do you find?
(A)  As these forces are carried into the body by the circulation, through the whole body, then we have the other organs which secrete, or go to make up the ends of the blood proper of the body, become involved through these.  These, then, in turn, work on to those organs whereby they are controlled or do control in the system.  We have first the liver, which is the secreting and excreting organ of the system, by which is purified most of the blood in the body. While we have the same forces exerted through the spleenic action and force on the system, producing at this time, these particles, or this condition in the spleen in itself, of the eliminating of the white blood corpuscles, or producing extra quality, and quantity of these lobes themselves.  The action of the spleen is on to the forces of the blood, or its controlling properties to the rebuilding forces to the nerve cells or matters into the brain.  As these forces are acted on to these, the organism of the body of [4614] we are talking about, is strong, within the matter of mind, through the sensory organism of the system, or brain proper.  The action on these forces, produce then, an attribute condition of the lower end of the spine, close to the side, near the liver.  We have the same condition here, of the cells, of the lobes of the liver in themselves, - the same condition we have from these here.  The action of these two forces on the blood force in the body, produce, or keep affected, the condition we have in the body now.  It has not always been like it is now.  It has been worse than it is now.  The condition in the system of these two organs, involve the whole circulation of the body, of the blood.  The eliminating of the quality of these from the system, these used ends that should be eliminated from, would be through the capillary circulation, to the respiratory organs, the lungs, out through the breath, also through the kidneys, by which most of the dross of the body should be eliminated, through these. The over-taxed condition of the liver in itself, has produced the condition to back up in [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]], eliminating through the intestinal tract here, not carried out from these at all, until we produce along the sides of the channel, no blood, or an anemic condition of the intestinal nerves or circulation carried through these or the upper end, left side, and the hepatic circulation becomes forced.  The blood is carried through the hepatic circulation, faster than through the other organs of the body, normally.  Here, we have the circulation carried through the liver faster then, and as we go on to these contacts, which has action on the nerve force, the cardiac plexus, producing an over-stimulation of these forces will produce then to the lungs in themselves proper, an opening of the cells or expansion or quickness of the breath at times, and fill the lungs too full, until enough of the carbon is carried into the system to help the forces from burning out of the organs of the body in itself.

To eliminate these properties from the system, from the body, that have their forces on the whole other organism of the system, - to eliminate these from the system and improve this body, bringing it to a normal condition, take into the system, that which will produce a unison of the distributing of the used or waste matter of the system, or that which would produce a condition in the system of excitement or aggravated condition to the kidneys in themselves proper, - not to produce that which will produce inflammation, but a stimulation to act freely.  Take plenty of water into the system at all times, so it will not over-excite the secretions of these proper.  Don't want the water hot, nor don't want it cold.  Want it moderate.  Keep it all times, in the system, as much as possible.  Then, we will stimulate these forces along the spine here, at the solar plexus, helia [iliac?] plexus, cardiac plexus, especially, or all along the spine to the end, whereby is force or stimulate the secreting properties of the liver and spleen, with electrical force.  Not that which we would have by current force, and produce a static, but the reverse force to the body, or that reverse force of the current of electricity.  The electrical force proper, or the straight current as we would have, produces reaction to the nerve ends or stimulating force through the lymphatics, while we have the reverse force, which stimulates them to act, or assist nature in resisting the white blood to enter into the force where the red is used.  The white blood is the resistance in the body; the red blood is the rebuilding or the richness of the body, whereby we rebuild the wasted cells, or keep the body alive.  Remove this condition as we have along here, at all times, and the body will improve.  It has improved here, from what it has been.

4. (Q)  Any other treatment you would prescribe?
(A)  Keep these forces in the body; plenty of water in the system and stimulation of the electric force along the lumbars, until we excite or aggravate the kidneys to act, to eliminate that from the system whereby it is secreted or thrown out by the liver, to keep out these forces, and we will have secretions or properties of the body will become of a normal condition.  Electrical force and water in the system.  The diet of the body will have to depend on the wants of the system.  Let that be well masticated, that is taken into the system.  If the electrical force becomes nauseating to the stomach, take pepsin and iron into the stomach.  Don't do it unless it becomes nauseating to the stomach by the treatment of the liver in itself.
