# TEXT OF READING 808-14 F 33

**Reading [[808-14]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/22/1941  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 4876

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [808], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Va.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [808]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while there may be much to be desired in the general physical conditions or reactions, we find that the body is very much improved from that as we have had here before.

4. At times, to be sure, there is some relapse or some reaction in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces towards the disturbing conditions; but in the main we find these are much improved.

5. With some of the changes here indicated, we would leave off the injections at present.  By the latter part of April they may be begun again for a period of some two weeks; then left off again, and then another shorter period taken again. After that they should not be necessary.

6. While there has been some effect of cold and congestion, we find the reaction in the leucocytes of the system is such as to supply sufficient in the present to overcome the congestive forces.  Hence there is the need rather for a tonic, or a toning of the body forces, as related to that which will assist in the assimilating activities through the digestive forces.  We refer to the activities in the salivary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces through the pyloric portion of the stomach and through the duodenum, and the activity to the gastric flow from gall duct, and a stimuli for spleen and pancreas.

7. Such a tonic would be found in the taking of ADIRON tablets; one tablet at each meal for a period of three weeks; then leave off for a week; then take again.

8. Also with these, twice each week, Tuesdays and Thursdays, take a teaspoonful of Grove's Tasteless Chill Tonic.  It isn't tasteless, but it's called tasteless!

9. These as they combine with the system, and with the general activities kept as to exercises in the open and precautions as to the diet, should be that necessary for producing a better and a near to normal balance.

10. Once or twice a month have a good, full [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment.  This, too, will assist in bringing better activity to the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is the nerve dead in lower left jaw tooth with large gold filling?  If so, is there anything I can do to preserve it or will it have to come out?
(A)  As we find, the nerve is dead.  It supplies, then, a source of infection.  There should have been a little more precaution taken when it was prepared for its present filling, and that portion that is now reacting to the forming of pus might have been avoided.

As we find in the present, there is little to be done.  Or the better thing to do would be to remove same.

13. (Q)  Does this body need extra calcium for teeth and bones, and if so will Abbot's Dicalcium Phosphate wafers prescribed by Dr. Lansdale [George LeRoy Lansdale, D.D.S.] supply the lack?
(A)  As we find, if the ADIRON vitamins are taken, as combined with those properties for clarifying the system, there will be a better assimilation of calcium by the foods taken than there would be supplied by the use of the Abbot wafers.

Then in the diet there should be supplied more often those elements needed, - as in fish, raw carrots and the like.

Thus we should have sufficient calcium for the body functionings.

The Adiron carries iron AND a calcium reacting influence, see?

14. (Q)  Should I continue taking the sodium chloride and sodium citrates in water every day as I have been?
(A)  We would take these on alternate days, now, rather than each day.  Take every other day, see?

15. (Q)  Is my blood in good condition or should I continue taking Valentine's Liver extract?
(A)  As we find, we would take those properties indicated, rather than taking more of the extract.  For, the diets - now - with the changes being wrought, and with those periods of supplying the added influence or "booster" through the Adiron, should bring better conditions.

16. (Q)  Is there a vitamin deficiency or am I taking too many vitamins?
(A)  There will be just the right quantity taken with the amount of Adiron indicated, see?

17. (Q)  Any other suggestions at this time?
(A)  These for the present we would do.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(ADIRON is manufactured and distributed by the Lawrence Laboratories, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.  It sells for $1 a bottle of 60 tablets, $3 a bottle of 250 tablets.  According to the Readings, it is the best balanced vitamin tablet on the market.)
