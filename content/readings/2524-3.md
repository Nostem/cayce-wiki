# TEXT OF READING 2524-3 M 41 (Engineer, Christian Scientist)

**Reading [[2524-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/10/1941  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 9488

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2524], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2524] born February 22, 1900 in Freudenstadt, [[Germany|Germany]].  You will give a mental and spiritual Reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] at this time, expanding upon his mental and spiritual abilities, with information, advice and guidance as to how he may make practical application of these abilities for the development of self and others.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [2524]; the information which has been indicated from the experiences of the entity as it made application of the opportunities that presented themselves through the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth's plane.

3. In giving advice or counsel, there are some definite and immutable laws that the entity should consider; not only in its daily life and dealings with others, but that which is to be met by self; knowing that the answer to the manner of application must come within self.

4. For, [[Law of Moses|the law]] is:  "My spirit beareth witness with thy spirit - saith the Lord - as to whether ye be the [[Sons of God|children of God]] or not."

5. Again, "Who IS my brother, my mother, my sister?  saith the way, the truth and the light.  He that doeth [[Free Will|the will]] of the Father, the same is my mother, my brother, my sister."

6. "With what measure ye mete it is measured to thee again."

7. "Know the Lord thy God is One."

8. Then, what meaneth these laws?  They are not merely statements or ideas, but are immutable, unchangeable, eternal.  And these are the manners in which these tenets, these truths are active and retroactive in the experience of an individual entity:

9. "Inasmuch as ye do it unto the least of these, thy brethren, so ye do it unto me, saith the Lord of hosts."

10. What, then, ye may ask, IS [[Free Will|the will]] of the Father here, now, under the varying circumstances that ye confront in the present?  with thy problems that are thine heritage through the use thou hast made, and are making in thy dealings with thy fellow man day by day.

11. To do good, to eschew evil, to love mercy and judgement, saith the [[Prophets|prophet]], is the whole duty of man, - as related to his activities in the earth.

12. And what saith the Son, [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]?

13. "To love the Lord with all thy heart, thy mind, thy body, and thy neighbor as thyself!"

14. This is the whole will of the Father to His children.  The rest of that recorded in Holy Writ - as may be said by man in his relationships, in meeting the problems every day, every experience - is merely the attempt to explain, to analyze, to justify or to meet that saying, that truth; which must, which will become a consciousness, an awareness in the experience of those who seek to do His will in the earth.

15. What is His will?  That ye love one another, and ACT in that manner.

16. What, then, are THY problems?  What manner would ye use in meeting, in becoming that channel which He has designed for thee?

17. Know, as He hath given, that whatsoever place any [[Soul|soul]], every [[Soul|soul]] occupies in its awareness in the material world, is by [[Grace|the grace]] and the mercy of a loving heavenly Father.  For He, as [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] has said, has not willed that any [[Soul|soul]] should perish, but has with every temptation prepared a way for the INDIVIDUAL to meet that problem.  And His promise IS, has been (for He changeth not):

18. "Though ye may be afar off, if ye call I will hear - and answer speedily."  For it is as has been proclaimed by him of whom it was said, "a man after God's own conscience":

19. "Though I take the wings of the morning and fly to the utmost parts of the earth, Thou art there, O Lord!  Though I descend into the depths of hell, Thou art there!  Though I enter into the thrones of [[Grace|mercy and grace]] and justice and peace, Thou art there."

20. THUS does the individual come to the awareness that "Lo, I am with thee always, even unto the end" is spoken as a personal thing.  It means thee!  It means that He IS mindful of thee.

21. Then ye say, "SURELY!  But how may I make some practical in my daily life, in my relationships in business, in my social life, and in my manner of worship?"

22. All are one, my brother.  All are one; even as the Father, the Son and the [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] are one - in [[Purpose|purpose]]; but each to its own phase of experience, of manifestation; even as thy body, thy mind and thy soul are one.

23. The application, then, is not to justify - but for all thy body, ALL thy mind, all thy soul to GLORIFY Him among, amid, WITH thy fellow man!

24. The answers, then, are within thyself.  For thy body is indeed [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God.  There He has promised to meet thee, in the [[Holy of Holies|holy of holies]] - thy [[Purpose|purpose]], thy will.

25. Will ye make it then to be at-one with His [[Purpose|purpose]]?  Let thy [[Prayer|prayer]] ever be:

26. "LORD, HAVE THINE OWN WAY WITH ME!  USE ME IN THE WAY AND MANNER THOU SEEST, O GOD, THAT I MAY BE THE GREATER CHANNEL OF BLESSINGS TO OTHERS, TO GLORIFY THEE - HERE AND NOW!"

27. THAT is the attitude, my friend, that will bring thee closer and yet closer to the knowledge, the understanding and - yea - with same the wisdom to apply that thou knowest.

28. He asketh not that ye apply that ye know not of, but desires that every soul "Seek and ye shall find, knock and it will be opened unto thee."

29. These are not merely sayings, beautiful to hear yet impractical in daily life!  Have ye not observed and found that these are in keeping with [[Law of Moses|the law]]?  Like begets like. What ye sow ye reap.  The Lord is one.  To love mercy and to eschew evil is the duty of man.  "If ye love me, ye will keep my commandments."

30. Then, take that thou hast in hand, - that which has been given thee as a helpful experience upon which ye may draw thine own conclusions.

31. Yea, in [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]] with Him, choose - now - thy way, thy course among thy fellow men.

32. Minimize the faults, in others - as ye would have them minimized in thee.  Forgive, if ye would be forgiven. Show mercy if ye would have mercy shown.

33. These are simple in speech, yet so hard oft in application - because we judge one another.

34. Yet He came to His own, His own received Him not.  He KNEW the power within and yet gave Himself a ransom for others.

35. He IS the way, the truth, the light.  They that climb up some other way attempt to belittle themselves.  For the Lord thy God is One!

36. Analyze thy life's experiences, - see thy shortcomings, see thy virtues.  Minimize those faults, magnify and glorify thy virtues.

37. These be the ways, the manners in which each soul, - yea, thy soul, - may know that He indeed standeth at the door of thy heart, of thy conscience, and will enter if ye invite Him.

38. For the promise has been, "If I go away the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] will come, and will BRING life and that more abundant."

39. Then, magnify the seeds of the spirit in thy life.  Love longsuffering, brotherly kindness, patience.  These are the fruits, these are the seed of the spirit.  Sow them in the lives of others, as ye meet them day by day; and the fruit of these will bear judgement before [[Throne of God|the throne]] of [[Grace|grace]].

40. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
