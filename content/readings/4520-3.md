# TEXT OF READING 4520-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[4520-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/27/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12397

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by her brother, [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [...], [4520]''s brother.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will answer the following questions, as I ask them, regarding this body.  You will then give the physical conditions of the body at the present time and any conditions that need to be given at this time, giving suggestions for the improvement and relief of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, the conditions are such as there may be much done for the improvement of same.  Some conditions are better than when we had them.  Some are not so good, for we have good and bad days, or spells, as it were, and the change in the conditions, much at present is of the psychological nature.  Ready for questions at present.

3. (Q)  What is the quality of the blood?
(A)  The quality of the blood we find very good.  The quantity subnormal.  In the quality we find, with the subnormal condition being considered, this very good.  The constituents or elements of the blood itself are below the normal on account of the condition in circulation, as is produced especially to the lower portion of body.  The inability of the action through the lower portion of body, as is produced by the conditions as exist in the lower lumbar and sacral regions as hinder the nerve supply and the circulation, for, as we see, in lymphatic circulation as produced to the lower portion of the body through over-sympathetic conditions reaching even to the stage of a psychological effect as produced in [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  We find the expansion of the lymphatic circulation is shown in extremities, giving then the inability to the reaction. Hence this added to conditions in blood supply brings a subnormal amount, with the exaggerated condition in quality, though very good for the condition as exists in the system.

We would, to aid to the conditions, to help bring the normal forces for this in the blood supply, we would apply [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] of the Alpine Light, or the ultra violet rays to the lower portion of the spine and the limbs, that we may clarify through the capillary circulation the blood stream, in much the same manner as blood is clarified through the exhalation and inhalation through lungs, so this would be done from exterior forces.  Hence, when the system functions, the blood stream then, in the increased force of blood supply, would give the better eliminations in the system.

We would keep the better and deeper manipulations.  These have been carried only in part, have only been given in part. We have not used the packs as were suggested in the manner as given.

To add medicinal properties in this would assist in some ways, which would be given as this:

Make this in solution in simple syrup - four ounces of same. Add:

Eucalyptol...................24 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp.........5 minims,
Canadian Balsam...............5 minims,
Benzosol.....................10 minims.

The dose of this would be (and must be shaken well together before giving) 5 to 10 drops in water twice each day.  This will assist in the blood supply and in preventing the increase of the white blood forces and build the red blood supply.  Better that the body be outside as much as possible.

Then, add these rays, or treatments, to that being done, with medicinal properties.  We will bring better results to this body.  Do that.

4. (Q)  Will she be able to walk?
(A)  With restored confidence in self and in its ability. The physical conditions have reached such condition that the body, psychologically, has lost the control of self when using voluntary movement to the muscular forces in lower portion.  Yes, the body would be able to walk.  There may be added afterward with other conditions to produce this incentive for the confidence in self.

5. (Q)  What causes the swollen feet?
(A)  Poor circulation in the lower portion of body, produced by pressure in the lower portion of sacral and lumbar region. Increased amount of lymphatic circulation.

6. (Q)  Should there be any other change in the treatment?
(A)  None other at present.  There may be some later, when the conditions in blood supply and the activity, as this medicine, with the increased sifting, as it were, or stimulus in blood stream in lower portion of the body, stimulated circulation, we may find use for suggestion.  This would come later.

7. (Q)  Where is the trouble that causes her not to be able to walk?
(A)  Lack of confidence in self, plus the condition existing in the lumbar and sacral plexuses.

8. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the improvement of this body?
(A)  Do these, as was given.  Giving medicinal properties twice each day, giving the manipulations, little deeper. Apply more heat, which may be used now with [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]].

9. (Q)  How often may [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] be given?
(A)  At least every other day, for fifteen to twenty minutes. Distance from the body but the body exposed.  Temperate same to the temperature and temperament of the body; that is, by the distance.  See, there will be times when the body can take more than at others.  Do not produce too much sunburn by this, so do not bring too close or too long at first, gradually increase as the conditions arise necessary for same.

10. We are through for the present.
