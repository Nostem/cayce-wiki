# TEXT OF READING 1594-5 F 2

**Reading [[1594-5]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/29/1938  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 7646

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the parents - Mr. [1693] and wife, through Associate Membership in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Parents.]

At home of 
Time of Reading                                   her aunt, 
5:50 to 6:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there is a continuing of improvements in nearly ALL ways.

3. While there are some portions of the body - in the structural reactions - not responding as fast as others, these as we find - through some little changes that as we find would be necessary - should soon, after a little, bring about a much better coordination.

4. In the present there should be about once each week a gentle, thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment made along the cerebrospinal system.  To be sure, it would be the desire on the part of one administering such to make same much oftener; but as we find it is not necessary oftener, but at least once each week - until we find, in six weeks, we would give other instructions.

5. Owing to the weakening of the cartilaginous forces, or between the segments, there is an inclination for these not to assume their normal positions - as the better assimilation is being brought about.  Hence once each week we would have the gentle but thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment, for six weeks; then we would give further advice.

6. We would keep EVERY evening the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] that has been indicated, gently along the whole of the cerebrospinal system, with the equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], Tincture of Myrrh and Compound Tincture of Benzoin.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] same gently but positively, all the body will absorb, of an evening.

7. Also it would not be amiss to give the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the abdomen (though it would necessarily be done more gently) and especially on those portions close to the groin on either side; that is, in the caecum area as well as the descending colon area.

8. Such massage, gently given, will not do other than STIMULATE, and will not disarrange any of that as would be corrected by the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

9. We would keep the Packs occasionally.  These may be gradually gotten farther apart, but there MUST be the setting up of conditions - or these kept in such a manner that the broken tissue that allowed for congestion or accumulations close in the brain forces may be absorbed - as it is gradually being taken away.  Hence it must be kept being eliminated.

10. Do not overfeed, but keep much of those foods that are body and blood building for the body.  That may include, now, WEAKENED [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].  This would be made regularly and then about HALF the quantity; that is, put half water and half [[Beef Juice|beef juice]]; but be sure that the water that is put with same has been BOILED!

11. These as we find, with the other GENERAL condition, would be those for this body for the next six weeks.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Should the Appliance with the Gold be continued?
(A)  This should be continued, most positively!

14. (Q)  In the same manner?
(A)  In the same manner.

15. (Q)  Is she responding to the Gold treatment as she should?
(A)  She is responding, but as has been indicated - there is such a weakening in the segments that it is necessary for there to be, about once a week, an adjustment made along the whole of the cerebrospinal system.

16. (Q)  What has caused apparent dullness for the last week?
(A)  Pressures on the brachial centers, which is through the vagus center to the reactions to brain.

17. (Q)  Should the enemas be continued?
(A)  ALL of the conditions now must be met as they arise. If it becomes necessary, use them!  Do not let conditions or accumulations of any sort arise as poisons from lack of eliminations.  However, do not use them so often that there may not be the proper peristaltic movement AS conditions are improved through the body.

18. (Q)  With following these suggestions, now, may it be given how long it will take for her to be able to sit alone?
(A)  Whether it's today, tomorrow or next year, much will have to do with the faith and persistence.

19. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these as indicated for the next six weeks.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file
