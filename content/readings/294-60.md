# TEXT OF READING 294-60 M 48

**Reading [[294-60]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/12/1926  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2019

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC, of ..., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of this body, EC, with the [[Dreams|dreams]] that have come to the body from time to time.  The applications of [[Dreams|dreams]], of [[Visions|visions]], are in that way and manner given as to the physical application of same.

3. Ready for dream.

4. (Q)  Tuesday Morning, 1/12/26.  Dreamed I had yet another dream.  I dreamed I had dreamed they had stopped work on [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], that we learned from someone who was not associated with the operations.  In conversation with the family, to whom I was telling the dream, we wondered why Mr. W. [M. B. Wyrick] had not informed us of this condition.  We decided to take a reading on it and see.  In the reading it said, "Correlate this condition with dream had more than two years ago, when [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] was first begun, where you went into a hole or well, bodily, to see if they were ever going to get any oil.  Then, with the interpretation of dream, in which you gave 'The time will come when they will despair of completing these operations,' and you will find, though even they were to close this up, though they were to put a board over it, [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] will produce.  [See [4905] series; oil lands in Bosque Co., [[Texas|Texas]], especially 4905-52.]
(A)  There is seen then in this, that which should give to the body-physical mind that assurance that no matter what may be the conditions apparent in these operations, THIS HOLE, THIS WELL, HERE AS SEEN, will produce and be a paying proposition, see?

Then, as in connection with this, there should be given those assurances to those operators, who are having at this time that trouble with the operations, and the changes that are coming about in regard to same.  Tell each and every individual who has strained himself to see the final outcome of these operations, to strain yet, as it were, just the bit more necessary for the completion of these.  And there will be found sufficient of the monies to finish these operations. For though there has been a tie-up for some time, it is seen that operations are progressing yet, and that these WILL PRODUCE, see?

5. We are through.
