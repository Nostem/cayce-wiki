# TEXT OF READING 3973-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3973-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1917 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/21/1917  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11624

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 21st day of July, 1917.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:55 to 7:20 P. M.

1. EC:  In this body we have here we find many conditions existing through the functioning of the different organs of the body, there is only one cause - that is, the seat of the trouble came from one cause.  In the 4th lumbar we find broken cellular forces or a condition which has produced abrasion internally or enlargement whereby the nerves supplying to the brain causes distorted ideas to this part of the brain.  This does not remain all the time.  At times aberrations existing in the body from one force, others there is a reaction from the brain, both carrying forces to the action in other parts of the body.  There is over activity of genitory organs of the body, the pelvis or lumbars, coming in connect with the brush end of the system, produced first broken cellular forces, first hallucinations.  We find the body is overtaxed with nerve force, hence anemiatized blood condition enlarged spleen, used cellular forces being destroyed.  Red blood is extinct in the spleen - there is an enlarged spleen - over functioned.  The used quantities of red blood produced anemiatized condition, there is not excess of white or leukocytes.  Over taxed nerve forces in the pelvis, remove this and we will have a normal body.  Should be removed by absorption.  To absorb from the system the improperties - increase the amount of the destructible forces in the system to this particular part, produce more phosphorus which is life or cellular force in the make up of the genitory system.  Treat this body first by manipulation by electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] force the whole length of the spine beginning from the 3rd or 4th cervical to the end of the spine by [[Vibration|vibratory]] movement or manipulation with the instrument we have here to relax all of the spine.  Then take into the system, sweet spirits of nitre; oil of juniper, and phosphates of [[Baking Soda|soda]] as in sodium phosphates.  Dose, sweet spirits nitre, 5 minims; oil of juniper, 3 minims; sodium phosphates, 4 grains; 3 times a day before meals.  Electrical treatment should be twice a day, morning and evening for one week.  Diet should be buttermilk, no meats, but cereals, grain, bread - those of proteids [proteins].  Do this and the body will slowly come back to normal, should be in 6 to 8 months.  In 4 months the body will realize that the treatment is doing it good and will accept it more readily.
