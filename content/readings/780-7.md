# TEXT OF READING 780-7 F 42

**Reading [[780-7]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/30/1931  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 4806

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of July, 1931, in accordance with request made by her husband - Mr. [779].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [780] - this we have had before.  The conditions have become so acute, it has been necessary for immediate activity.  When the conditions have been allayed somewhat under the applications as have been administered, we would give as these for the correction of those disorders, and for the relieving of that as has been PRODUCING the distention and disorder in the caecum region:

2. We would take the green ragweed 6 ounces, in 16 ounces distilled water.  Reduce this by slow boiling to 1/2 the quantity.  Strain while warm.  Take 1 ounce wild ginseng in 6 ounces of distilled water.  Reduce this by slow boiling to 2 ounces.  Then add the 2 solutions together, with 1 ounce simple syrup and 3 ounces of grain alcohol - 85% to 95%. Shake solution together when the dose is taken, and the dose would be teaspoonful 3 to 4 times a day,until there is the entire relieving of the pressure in the alimentary canal. Also we will find that this will change the difference in those conditions as have disturbed the blood supply.

3. For the NAUSEA in the present, we would use those of equal parts limewater, cinnamon water, adding to same - to the ounce of each - 10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium 20 minims, 10% solution Bromide of Potassium 10 minims.  The dose would be a teaspoonful in a little water every thirty to forty minutes until the stomach is settled, and fever is allayed. Ready for questions.

4. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
