# TEXT OF READING 1698-2 F 62

**Reading [[1698-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/2/1939  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 7841

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Now as we find, there are indications of some improvements.  While they have not as yet advanced to such measures that there is a great deal better use of the extremities, we find that there is not as much pain and that the crystallizing, or the static force in the muscles and tendons is being somewhat softened and, in some directions, even dissolved.

3. The continued improvement then, as we find, would necessitate that there be kept those applications in their entirety, and in the ORDER as they have been outlined for the body.

4. These applications should not be made in the manner of something just to be gotten through with; but as the varied forms of applications are made, know that there is being accomplished in the physical functionings of the system that which, if done consistently and persistently, will not only relieve but will gradually and eventually bring to the body such relief, such help, as to make for a much nearer normal reaction throughout the system.

5. Then, all of those things that have been indicated should be continued.

6. Do not attempt to use the Solutions vibratorially in the same container, but each in its OWN container; and after each usage REMOVE the lead FROM the Solution, and keep the Solution covered in such a manner that there is not the deterioration through the action of the air on the Solution itself.

7. Recharge the Appliance every twenty-eight to thirty days, - that is, when it is is to be used.  Hence in this case it would be recharged at the beginning of the thirty-day round of this particular portion of the applications, see?  and the Gold and Silver would be recharged, - that is, the Solution, - every fifteen to sixteen days; which would mean TWICE for this body during the thirty-day round of using the Appliance in the regular order as it comes with the other series of treatments, see?

8. Do this, and keep up all the applications indicated.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Please describe the effect of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] in this case.
(A)  The Gold, which is carried into the system vibratorially through the Appliance, is to act upon the elements in the nerve system so as to renew the energies in same.  The Silver is to enable the activity of the eliminating system to so eradicate the effects of the suppression of activity in the muscular and tendon forces as to allow same to be eliminated from the body.

If there are the rubs and the application of other properties carried on as we have indicated, this enables all of these to work together, see - consistently, prayerfully, persistently they should all be done.

11. (Q)  Is it indicated that she will receive enough benefit to compensate her for all this time and money spent?
(A)  What IS time OR money, compared to the awakening of [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] within the mental and physical self?

This then would depend upon who is judging same.  As for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], it has ALREADY MORE than done such.

12. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do those things indicated, consistently, persistently - if we will bring help.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mr. [...] Husband
"    " Ass'n file
