# TEXT OF READING 4625-1 M 4

**Reading [[4625-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/13/1926  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 12537

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by his mother, [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading                          University Hospital, 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       Ann Harbor, Mich.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find, while those conditions as are being accorded the body are beneficial in many ways and manners, they are not entirely reaching the cause of existent conditions. Hence there will remain that undeveloped portion that is not receiving the full equalization in correction of conditions in this body.

4. The cause, as we see, of the unbalanced condition, produced by the normal development of physical conditions in the body against a physical defect in structural portions of system, and also by the prenatal inhibitions in the development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as the whole - the physical condition existent in that of a pressure being produced on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] and its nerve supply as the thread through the system.  Hence the enlarged condition existent in [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] proper, and that produced by the presentation of the embryo at the time of birth, and the pressure produced in the 1st and 2nd cervical, producing then an unequalized condition to the metabolism of the body, and this producing this spasmodic condition in the system - the prenatal effect being that of the inductive forces as are shown in the pineal thread in this prenatal condition.  This being weakened, and pressure caused in the cervical, gives an unbalanced condition to the whole normal development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and of the responsive forces of same to the brain.  Hence there showing, by the close examination of the body, this unalignment in 1st, 2nd, cervical; also in the lower portion of the cerebro-spinal center, just below the ilium plexus.

5. Then, for the full correction of the conditions, we would, through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, correct those conditions in the cerebro-spinal system, at the cervical centers, and in the coccyx, and the plexus about the sacral.  Then apply, while these are being adjusted, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from that of the Abram's [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], or those from the Radio-Active appliance, using the negative at the umbilicus, with the positive at the change from the right wrist to the left ankle - that is, alternating those connections, using same [[Vibration|vibration]] at least three HOURS each day, making adjustments with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, applying at the same time those magnetic forces from the electrical forces that may be created by the palm of the hand.

6. The diets as have been outlined are excellent, as also those that produce elimination.

7. These conditions, we find, should in three to six months eliminate the conditions producing this abnormality, and the depressions in system be brought to normal forces.

8. Do that.

9. We are through.
