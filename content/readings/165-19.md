# TEXT OF READING 165-19 M 53

**Reading [[165-19]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/25/1930  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 661

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Health Room 
Time of Reading                            Equipment, Inc., 
12:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you Health Room Equipment, Incorporated, Suite 306, ... Street, [[New York City|New York City]], and the work that has been done by this corporation so far.  You will tell us how this may be made successful, if it is to be continued, and answer such questions as we may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those associated with, and the work of the Health Room Equipment, Incorporated.  This we have had before - the work as has been done in respect the application of the information given respecting the preventative conditions, rather than that of the equipment.  As has been given, this department should be made successful, or put on such basis as that it may be made successful before undertaking developments for equipments for Health Rooms.  The position, or the conditions as have surrounded same, have been at a disadvantage, in that there were not sufficient room nor equipment, nor manner of handling same.  Under the PRESENT surroundings, we do not find that this may be made as successful as would be desired for same.  Scarcely, were the whole time taken, would the work as may be done, UNDER the present surroundings, be made to be a FINANCIAL success; yet, in these surroundings, were there the proper methods - as WE would find - given to the developments, these would be made to PROVE the conditions as BEING in accord with that as has been given respecting same.  The methods as we would give, would be to interest those that are interested in health movements - especially in preventative measures, and especially where suggestion is the greater part of that used in the application OF those things given for the development OF the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]], or for preventative measures; also the regular profession.  Those of the VARIOUS professions, writers, athletes - those of the profession - these, and through these channels - this, as WE find, may be made to DEMONSTRATE the PLAUSIBILITY, the FEASIBILITY, of the undertaking.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  If we continue Health Room developments, as suggested by readings last year, to what curative device or substance shall our efforts be directed?
(A)  As we have given, we have not found it changed - that there were two characters; those that were to be of the superficial nature, or those as relate to conditions as have to do with skin disorders, nervous disturbances, and such. These characters needing the superficial; while those of the structural or the BONE portions would need that of the DEEPER [[Vibration|vibrations]], and a different element in the application of things to be taken as curative measures for individuals. These may be done through the lights.  Those of the reflection, or those of a reflected character may be used for those of the superficial measures; while those of a different character should be used for the deep.  Develop ONE at a time!  though experimentations in the various character of filament, of reflectors of the various natures, as necessary to produce the greater penetrating powers.  Those that, with the application of the various forms of properties, WITH the application of electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] - this, and these natures, SHOULD be those that should be developed first.  The EXPERIMENTATIONS may be made on more than one.  The DEVELOPMENTS should be confined FIRST to either the superficial nature - the EASIER one made, or of the deeper [[Vibration|vibrations]] - the harder, and one needing more experimentation.

4. (Q)  Shall we continue such developments with the idea of marketing the results for a profit?
(A)  They should be.  These should be developed first, then presented to those through whom such marketing is made most possible, or they may be made as a portion of equipments in various institutions where such curative measures are carried on.

5. (Q)  How can [3952] function best in the near future?
(A)  Dependent upon how deep into the development the Equipment Corporation expects, or intends, or DOES go. [3952], as given, is good in the experimentation of such equipment.

6. (Q)  Shall experimental work be done in [[New York City|New York]]?
(A)  In New York.

7. (Q)  An affiliation with Dr. Stearns of N.Y.C. was suggested but his services are not available.
(A)  Then, someone that is interested in the developments of that as being attempted.  There are MANY.  Hunt 'em up!

8. (Q)  Will violet or [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] rays, reflected from a surface coated with aluminum paint or other compounds, be changed in therapeutic value?
(A)  Be changed in therapeutic value, but there be FEW conditions that such would be beneficial in.  That, that would be treated with such treatment, and to which the radiation OF those of the mercurian effect as obtained in the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] - but the added, that of the [[Radium|RADIUM]] - or uranium forces would; ultra-marine colors will also vibrate WITH such.

9. (Q)  Should impinging rays be in the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] or in the X-ray field for changing therapeutic characteristics?
(A)  In the X-ray.

10. (Q)  Give general outline and advice for procedure?
(A)  This has been given.  Just do that.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to [137]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician Three extra copies for [137] to distribute as seen fit
