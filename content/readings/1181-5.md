# TEXT OF READING 1181-5 F 62

**Reading [[1181-5]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/9/1924  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 6604

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of June, 1924, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Mrs. [1690].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer; Conductor, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]]; Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                          ..., Ky.

1. EC:  Now, while the body is not feeling as good at the present time as it has for some days back, yet the general condition throughout the whole system is very much improved.

2. The condition as arises at the present time is produced by the toxins as absorbed in the system from the condition in intestinal tract and from the lack of the digestion, and from the condition produced in the system by foods allowing to be in the system so long without digestion taking place.  This produces the pressure, the uncomfortable condition through the lower portion of stomach and intestines.

3. The system needs only to keep those conditions as have been outlined for same.  At the present time and under the conditions as exist at the present time, the body needs to have high injections of Usoline or of those oils necessary to produce elimination through the intestinal tract.  Keep those conditions up.  Beware of overloading the stomach, using those foods as outlined, and pre-digested and cereal, with the formation that gives the system that glueten [gluten] and protein necessary to rebuild in the blood supply and give nourishment and nutriment to the organs that need the replenishing at this time, with the same conditions kept in the system, making the corrections necessary in the spinal column, where ligaments and nerve systems have been impinioned.

4. Do that.  We will bring the best for this body.
