# TEXT OF READING 3776-11 F 70

**Reading [[3776-11]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/3/1926  Sex: F  Age: 70  ReadingID: 11324

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Miss [243].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.  You will answer any questions I ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before. Now, the vitality and resistive forces in system have reached a very low ebb and the circulation, with the undue functioning of the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexuses, causes the distresses in the nerve system, combined with those complications - (in undertone) Mother!  Mother! Mother!

3. The stimulation then to assist the system under the present strain would be, give often small doses of burnt Spirits Frumenti, using the warm and hot packs on the abdomen, rubbing, massaging into the spine, all along the lumbar to the cervical region, with equal parts of pure olive oil, with Tincture of Myrrh, see?

4. The inhalations should be given.  That as found in:

Pure grain alcohol................4 ounces, with
Eucalyptol.......................20 minims,
Oil of Turp.......................2 minims,
Canadian Balsam...................2 minims,
Tincture, fluid extract, Tolu....30 minims,
Creosote..........................2 minims.

5. Shake solution and inhale.  This will clarify, see, and assist in breathing.  Do that.  We are through for the present.

1/3/26 Wire:  FOR STIMULATION GIVE OFTEN SMALL DOSES OF BURNT SPIRITS FRUMENTI STOP USE THE PACKS ON THE ABDOMEN STOP MASSAGE INTO THE SPINE FROM CERVICAL TO LUMBARS EQUAL PARTS OLIVE OIL AND TINCTURE OF MYRRH STOP  USE AN INHALANT GRAIN ALCOHOL FOUR OUNCES  EUCALYPTOL TWENTY MINIMS  OIL OF TURP TWO MINIMS CANADIAN BALSAM TWO MINIMS  FLUID EXTRACT TOLU THIRTY MINIMS  CREOSOTE TWO MINIMS STOP  INHALE OFTEN AS NECESSARY.  EDGAR CAYCE
