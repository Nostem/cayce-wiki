# TEXT OF READING 4488-2 M 23

**Reading [[4488-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/23/1932  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 12357

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 23rd day of November, 1932, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [315], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and H. L. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, this we have had before.  Now, as we find, while there are at times those conditions and reactions in the physical force of the body that apparently the body grows weaker, rather than gaining strength or vitality, would there be the more consistent use of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance WITH the benefits derived from the manipulations or adjustments given by the chiropractic applications, these would give much more satisfactory returns and that relief that is given for the MOMENT by the adjustments would be of longer duration, giving the opportunity for a better reaction.  As given, had these adjustments been made, or these impulses been quickened more during the developing stage of the conditions, body mentally and structurally, these might have been overcome entirely. Now that they have become constitutional, necessarily these are rather slow in their reaction, and are they kept more in accord with those outlines more satisfactory results may be expected.  Not over ten to twenty minutes of the application of the Radio-Active, with those solutions being carried through the anodes connected to the body, so the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are as outlined; these following the adjustments will make for better reaction.  Well to test these, that the variation that is ordinarily seen in the pulsation be watched carefully DURING those periods when the applications of the Radio-Active are made, and the regularity that is seen during the application of the Radio-Active Appliance, see?

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Have they been applying the appliance correctly, and in the right places?
(A)  Had these been in the consistent manner as had been outlined, there would not be the suggestion that these be given more consistently and the effects or benefits given more closely!  We would follow these more closely, as given.

Use not only, as outlined, the solution of the stimuli in the appliance but the odor of the foxglove blossom should be in the room, or in the air breathed by the body occasionally. This will be helpful.

4. We are through for the present.

Copy to [315]
"    " File
