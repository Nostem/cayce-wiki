# TEXT OF READING 2299-2 M 8 MONTHS

**Reading [[2299-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/16/1940  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 9046

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of July, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, while there is much improvement in some directions, there is still a good deal of phlegm and congestion and soreness throughout the intestinal tract.

4. DO NOT overcrowd the body with food!  Let the natural sources and resources be corrected first.

5. Before each feeding we would give the body three to four drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in a teaspoonful of water.  Keep this up until the odor of same may be detected in the stool.

6. At least once a day we would gently [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the abdomen and bowels with an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]].  Heat the [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] and then add an equal amount of the other ingredients to the liquid. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this gently, preferably of evenings, - all that the body will absorb.

7. Keep Carnation Milk as the basis of the diet.  The present formula is very good; it needs only to be altered as necessary for the agreement to the weakened digestive forces.

8. Until the body is near to normal from the inflammation, use just the Milk and add with same - as feedings - the strained oatmeal; this to be taken with milk of the same character or formula; and add with each feeding - either in the bottle or in the oatmeal - about half a teaspoonful of Lime Water. Preferably use the cut oats, not rolled oats.

9. Orange juice WEAKENED may be given once a day.  Do not give same at the same feedings with the oatmeal!  This should be tested for the quantity and as to how often given.

10. Then, as the other foods are added of the various characters, we would use preferably Gerber's foods - all of them; but add these gradually, as the body improves; not all at once.

11. Also a little later, as the other foods are gradually added, we would add the [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]], but preferably in the form of White's [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] Tablets.  Crush one tablet and mix with whatever food is given once each day, - not in every feeding. This should only be prepared as it is given the body; do not give the body food that is left over in which this has stood. Prepare fresh each time.

12. These as we find should bring better conditions for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
