# TEXT OF READING 147-32 F 53

**Reading [[147-32]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/23/1927  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 771

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 23rd day of July, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will check up on the physical condition of this body, and see if any change might be made in the treatment she is following.  Has the [[Radium|Radium Pad]] lost its usefulness?

2. EC:  We have the body and those conditions as surround same in the present.  At present we find some little change for the better.  In general we find the physical conditions are often very discouraging in this body.  For the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as have been set in the physical being, and the radiations from same, often are combative one with another, as has been given.

3. The change we would give for the physical well-being would be that the [[Radium|Radium Pad]], that has been applied by the body, should be recharged, see?  It should be returned for a recharge.  And the Radio-ACTIVE Appliance forces for the body should be changed, in that these should have better CONNECTIONS.  Return each, then, for readjustment, and for the betterment of the physical well-being.  These we find will radiate - both - good for the body.

4. Keep in the attitude as near as possible to not be overcome by the trials of the physical being.  Rather give self to that of doing what is best in thine own scope, applying every effort toward the betterment of others, and of self toward the physical, mental and spiritual well-being.  Do that, see?

5. We are through with this reading.
