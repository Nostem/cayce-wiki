# TEXT OF READING 3012-1 F 51

**Reading [[3012-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/20/1943  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 10322

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3012], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. Arthur H. Newman.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Connecticut.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3012].

3. As we find, there are disturbances that may be aided and corrected in the present with a consistent application of those influences which may create a balance in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, that are in the present upsetting the coordination in the eliminating system.

4. For these, the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, are in the present disturbed; especially as related to the digestive system and the eliminations in the system.

5. Thus there are those accumulations around the joints, or an excess of calcium deposits in the body.

6. These give distresses to the extremities, especially the hands, the feet, the knees.

7. We would suggest these applications:

8. Take internally one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water, each morning before breakfast, for ten days.

9. During that ten-day period do not take ANY carbonated waters or drinks, nor any malt, nor vinous nor spirit liquors of any kind.

10. After the ten days, begin with [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  Each should include a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] (which is preferable to the Dry Heat Cabinet), using Witchhazel.

11. Following the [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] have the hot and cold needle showers; then a thorough rubdown with a combination of two parts Peanut Oil to one part of Pine Needle Oil.

12. Do this each week for at least six weeks.  Then leave off and begin with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (which would not be taken during the period of the hydrotherapy, see); this time taking one drop each day for only a five day period, leaving off five days, then taking five days again, and so on.

13. During the period of the hydrotherapy there should be at least one good [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], but this should come toward the latter part of the treatments, see?

14. In the diet, - meats should be banned.  Not any great quantity of high seasoning in any foods, especially salt.

15. Vegetables such as beets and beet tops, carrots, peas, should be taken fairly often.

16. Refrain from greases or fried foods.

17. Do these, and we should find bettered conditions for this body.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  How may the peculiar exhaustion be overcome?  Also light sleeping?
(A)  The exhaustion is from the pressure upon the nervous system.  This is affecting the pulsation, of course, but it is not a heart affection - rather a sympathetic condition. Thus the Fume Bath is preferable to the dry heat, as to produce the perspiration and tone the body for better eliminations.

The resting of the body by eliminating of the poisons will make for better sleep.

20. (Q)  How can I clear up the phlebitis?
(A)  This is a part of the circulation.  With the pressures removed as the massage is given, and with the hydrotherapy and the character of treatments indicated, there will be corrections made in the sacral and lumbar areas of the body.

Do as we have given and we will have bettered conditions for this body.

21. (Q)  Since menstruation ceased last September, why is there a tendency to superfluous hair in such an unnatural place as the shoulder blades?
(A)  The lack of proper circulation and the disturbances in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, as given.

Do as outlined, and we should find bettered conditions for this body.

22. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Newman) Copy to Ass'n file

([[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is manufactured by The Atomidine Co., 9 South 8th Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y., and sells for $1 a bottle.

Dr. H. J. Reilly, Physiotherapist, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., is familiar with the type of hydrotherapy suggested for you.  If not convenient for you to go to N.Y. for these treatments, perhaps he - or Dr. Newman - can suggest someone in your vicinity.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 3/23/43 Letter:  "Your work seems to be just what Dr.
> Alexander Cannon hoped might be brought to humanity, as
> described in his book SLEEPING THROUGH SPACE.  Since you
> are helpful to those of us who cannot come to you, I hope so
> much that you may redirect my life and put me on a basis of
> self-improvement.
> 
> "I write at the suggestion of our personal physician, Dr.
> Arthur H. Newman [D.O.], ..., N.Y.  He has known of your
> gift and is much interested...
> 
> "This information might be helpful:
> 
> "A peculiar exhaustion and light sleeping are my chief
> problems.  It seems as tho someone else will have to breathe
> for me, and I have never honestly been equal to anything that
> I have had to do.  Yet I am fortunately free from all the
> allergies, sinus trouble, colds, etc., from which my healthy
> and vigorous friends suffer so constantly.  Childhood
> diseases were mumps, chicken pox and scarlet fever.  When
> our son was born phlebitis followed a difficult delivery
> in 1920, and bursitis suddenly appeared in 1939.  In the
> light of metaphysics they must have been expressions of the
> [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]], as I had never heard of such illnesses.
> My own interpretation is that they could be a rebellion
> against too much 'management,' as I have always lived with
> dominant people, and have not been encouraged by parents or
> my husband to freely express myself.  Frankly, I do not go
> foreword into new experiences very easily, as I am 'fond of
> the familiar' and do not crave variety as restless natures
> seem to."
> 
> B2. 4/14/43 Letter:  "I should like to clear up a tendency to
> arthritis, in the left hip, where phlebitis occurred after
> the birth of our son, and to bursitis.  Calcium seems
> to want to go where it is not supposed to be.  My 'hard
> thots' must be misplaced if I am causing the condition
> metaphysically.  I should like to clear up the phlebitis,
> there is a tendency to swell when I am on my feet too much,
> and several veins are swollen and blue all the time.  Doctors
> refuse to shrink such veins, as they are different from
> varicose.  The whole left leg discolors at the slightest
> bruise.  Since menstruation ceased last September there is
> a tendency to superfluous hair in such an unnatural place
> as the shoulder blades (I should much rather be sprouting
> wings!).  There seems to be a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] relationship as yet
> unsolved by physicians.
