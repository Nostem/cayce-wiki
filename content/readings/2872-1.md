# TEXT OF READING 2872-1 F 22

**Reading [[2872-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/30/1942  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 10100

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of December, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - [2872], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. [619] via phone.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2872].

3. As we find, while there are many conditions very good, there are disturbances that - unless some corrections are made - may eventually bring great disturbances to the body.

4. We find that these have to do primarily with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body, and the effect being produced upon the chemical reactions in system; and this effect upon the organs through the body, especially pelvis, AND the activity as related to the muscles and tendons.

5. These sources arise from a congestion that has made for a portion of overactivity in the thyroids, and another activity in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]], and an incoordination between the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in assimilation between the liver and the kidneys.

6. Hence the varied character of disturbances that are thrown into the blood stream; the disturbances that arise at the periods, and those conditions in the tendons and muscular forces as well as those in the superficial circulation.

7. These, as we find, are the conditions with this body, [2872].

8. Naturally these conditions leave, or produce, areas that are reflected in centers from which the varied portions of the organs receive impulse in the coordination between the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic nerve forces of the body.

9. These we find specifically indicated here, in the present, in the 3rd and 4rd cervical, 2nd and 3rd dorsal, and through the lumbar areas.  These now are the result of other disturbances, and thus the character of lesions that are formed there are varied in their reaction to impulses to the organs themselves.

10. Then, in making applications for helpful forces, - we would begin first with Tonicine as a tonic; this to be taken about a teaspoonful each day.  This would be as an active principle upon the thyroids, as well as the glands in assimilation.

11. The mechanical application osteopathically given should be the stimulation that will affect, with these cleansings, through the lumbar-sacral as well as in the area from the 3rd and 4th dorsal to the first cervicals.

12. In these applications, - have these rather in series; two to three weeks at a time, then rest from them for a period of a week, and then take them two or three weeks again.  Repeat this procedure several times.

13. And these, with those properties indicated, should bring better forces for this body, [2872].

14. Keep in the open as an exercise.

15. Keep the eliminations well with the vegetable forces rather than minerals.

16. Ready for questions.

17. In the application of these, - keep the mental attitude of being constructive in thinking; being in that attitude of using the influences and the abilities for helpful forces for others.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Assoc. blank sent.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]]; let us have the name and address of the one you choose.

At your convenience, please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.)
