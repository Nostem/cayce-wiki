# TEXT OF READING 137-11 M 26 (Stockbroker)

**Reading [[137-11]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/1/1925  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 401

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  Now you have before you [137], and the impressions that he received on December 29, 1924, between 5:30 and 6:30 P.M., O'clock.  You will give the correct interpretations of these, or the lessons or instructions he is to understand from them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the impressions here.  In these, we find, first the heights to which the manifestations may lead the individual, and the impressions as others may obtain from such heights, given in the emblematical way, as others have been given.

3. Following these, we find the way, the how, the why, of such attainment, and as to the manner in accomplishing the good in the many various fields.

4. Well, as has been given, that the individual entity, [137], prepare this as data and make the individual interpretation, through the conscious forces, that same may be made an integral portion of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], for with this manner and way do we reach the attainments of the whyfore of such manifestations, and in such way this may be made manifest in a material world.

5. Do that.

6. We are through for the present.
