# TEXT OF READING 294-124 M 53

**Reading [[294-124]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/28/1930  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 2083

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Suggestion given at end of reading, before waking him up)

1. EC:  Too much toxic poisons from active forces of indigestive forces within the system.  Irrigations, and those of sodas, and those of the lactics as will sweeten the stomach juices - THESE should be that for the bettered conditions in the system.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
