# TEXT OF READING 294-24 M 48

**Reading [[294-24]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1983

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., this 14th day of April, 1925, without suggestion being given to obtain it.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:10 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(No suggestion - information was volunteered)

1. EC:  In the [[Physical Body|physical body]] there are conditions (physical) that need attention from the physical or material manner that will bring better functioning in the system.  With properties as have been taken in body [for purgation] we find the relaxation too great in portion of the system, until the muscular conditions controlling the orifice of expulsion in the anus itself brings the folds to such conditions as to cause inflammation in this portion of body.

2. We would apply this property, which we find would be a specific for such conditions:  [Hemorrhoids] To the oil as would be found from 1/4 pound of butterfat (fresh) add:

Pulverized Tobacco or Snuff.....1 dram, with
Mentholatum, or oil of same.....2 minims, with
Tincture [[Iodine|Iodine]].................1 drop or minim.

3. This to be used as salve or lotion for such conditions or abrasions, internal or external.  Apply that to the body.  We will bring better relief and relations to the whole [[Physical Body|physical body]], with the action of the physical when in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or subnormal conditions.

4. We are through.

[GD's note:  This was one of the early descriptions of the ointment for hemorrhoids which later was termed TIM.]
