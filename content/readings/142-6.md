# TEXT OF READING 142-6 M 20 MONTHS

**Reading [[142-6]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/5/1928  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 579

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of December, 1928, in accordance with request made by his parents, Mr. [900] and Mrs. [136].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. R. B. Atkinson.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [142].  This we have had before. We find the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are very good.  While not ABSOLUTELY normal, yet the disturbances as have existed with the digestion, without too MUCH attention, will gradually adjust themselves if there will be given sufficient of the exercise, light and heat, as is nominal for the developing body.  There should NOT be uneasiness as respecting the conditions.  The body in its development easily becomes used to, or spoiled to, conditions that make for fretfulness; yet were the nominal or normal things carried out as has been suggested for the body, we find there should be no need of other than the normal and nominal development.  As for the diets - in the change as has been suggested, as given for same - these should not be given too OFTEN; that is, don't CONTINUE to feed every few minutes. But the body following, in a general way, the outlines as has been given, there will be seen the normal, nominal development.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is he getting sufficient amount of food?
(A)  Rather over abundant - most of the time has been.  Best that a body's digestive system REST when disturbances are there, than overloading same to create MORE poisons, that create MORE disturbances, that cause MORE anxiety!

3. (Q)  How should the milk be given and when?
(A)  Regular, nominal way, and as has been outlined - most of same comes with the cereals, or after the food, as has been outlined for same.

4. (Q)  Should he be vaccinated now?
(A)  As soon as the conditions have been set near nominal, for the conditions as are contemplated - this is necessary - but we would certainly use [[Iodine|iodine]] soon after such vaccination.  Tincture of [[Iodine|iodine]] rubbed over same.

5. (Q)  Does the body need any more Castoria at the present time, in broken doses?
(A)  WELL, until the system gets NOMINALLY set, see?  As long as there is sourness or belching.  This will be necessary to change from time to time, or resort to this as the cleanser.  This will be the acting of the best with the body under the present conditions - Castoria.  Of course enemas may be used to cleanse the lower system.  This is dependent upon the action or the secretions as thrown off from the system.  Watch the activity of the kidney and of the digestive system.  According to the way the digestion takes place, alternate or change the foods - especially the amount of milks.  The milk, as has been given, has been the disagreeing element - on account of OVER or EXCESS feeding.

6. (Q)  Is there anything wrong with the kind of milk that is now being used?
(A)  Rather in the manner that has been outlined; that is, half milk and half cream, rather than all of either, see?

7. (Q)  Any further suggestions regarding this body?
(A)  Follow these for the present, as we find them.

8. We are through for the present.
