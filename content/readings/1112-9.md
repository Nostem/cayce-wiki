# TEXT OF READING 1112-9 M 68

**Reading [[1112-9]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/7/1943  Sex: M  Age: 68  ReadingID: 6365

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [1010], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find the body is some little better.  The acute conditions in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] force of neck, though, are coordinating or correlated with the activities of the kidneys and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]] - the areas just above the kidneys and the bladder.

3. We would apply over the kidney area a thin layer of coarse salt (or table salt) with a few drops of [[Turpentine|turpentine]] put in same.  Don't put too much [[Turpentine|turpentine]] - only three to five drops, not more than that to the amount of salt needed for the whole pack.  Then apply low heat.

4. On the throat apply three to four thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], full strength, normal temperature - not heat applied.

5. Also gargle the throat with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

6. Change the diet somewhat.  Eat plenty of raw carrots, plenty of beets and turnips.  These will aid the body in the regular diet and will make for much better conditions.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Have I Nephritis?
(A)  Not yet, but this may come from same if there is greater irritation continually arising from the kidneys. These things indicated are to set up drainages.

It may be necessary for the body to drink coca-cola, (but use plain water, not carbonated) or to put a little lithia tablet in the drinking water - after using these packs.

9. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Keep the attitude of hopefulness and helpfulness.

Do massage the body, especially in the area from the 3rd cervical downward.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file
