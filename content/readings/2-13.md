# TEXT OF READING 2-13 M 49

**Reading [[2-13]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/5/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 14

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 193O, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Berger.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2], - this we have had before. In many respects we find the general physical conditions are on the improve.  The changes as are effected by the conditions brought about in eliminations, are gradually removing much of the pressure as seen in the system, and especially as effective through the nerve system in its coordination with the cerebro-spinal, sympathetic, and coordination of the sensory forces.  We would keep much of that as is being accomplished in the system, and we will find conditions will continue to be on the improve.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  If treatments are persisted in, will eyesight eventually clear up?
(A)  We would find that it would.

3. (Q)  Would it be detrimental for body to go home for week or ten days?
(A)  For not a longer time than that.  For a week, eight days would be better.

4. (Q)  What causes nausea when exposed to sun and sand?
(A)  The alterations in the assimilating and eliminating system.  Not detrimental, though - of course - the body CAN take too MUCH sun.  Must remember that it has been such a long period that there was so little of the sun's rays effective on the body.

5. (Q)  How long at a time should the body stay in the sun?
(A)  For THIS body, thirty to sixty minutes would be sufficient.  The exercises in the open would be BETTER for the body EARLY mornings, and late in the afternoon - but not in night so much.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mr. [2]'s wife
