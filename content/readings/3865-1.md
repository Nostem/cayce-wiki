This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of July, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3865].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M.                                        ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with this body.  We find there are some conditions in the abnormal way that have been of a long standing, until the system has adjusted itself to some of the conditions, yet they have not as yet become normal forces.  We find other conditions that keep, or are a general or a constitutional condition in the system, produced by conditions arising of a long standing and of the condition as is developed in the body day by day.

2. Now, the conditions to be met, or to be corrected, have to do especially with the eliminations and assimilation, and with the organs as involved through this inability of this condition to be brought to normal forces in the body.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3865], we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the body not anaemic, not wholly depending or being a blood condition, yet the blood is more or less involved in the conditions as manifested in the system from time to time.  Not so much that the conditions are produced through the blood supply but that the blood supply, with the existing condition, becomes involved in its functioning, thereby causing at times distress to portions of the system by the lack of assimilated forces being given in the system to function normal.  Others by the carrying in the system of those properties that in the functioning organs bring distress to those organs.  Such conditions we find affecting the system especially in the digestive tract.

5. We have had in times back conditions that were much more aggravated and aggravating to the system than at the present time.  In this we see the blood supply becomes involved in the distribution of forces or eliminating forces in distribution of the rebuilding forces in body.  Hence conditions that arise in the cuticle, those in the throat, the larynx, the bronchials at times, the condition in the outer portions of the body, the system attempting to eliminate and to find the equalization, affecting then the blood supply at such times.  At others we find the system being overcharged with these and it affecting the organs direct.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find there are lesions that have and do produce much of these conditions.  In the second lumbar, coming from the pelvis condition and the overtaxing of nerve forces that have been changed in the system from times back to the present condition, then again at the solar plexus center and again at the secondary cardiac center, which becomes involved in the condition as shown in the lungs itself proper.

7. In the nerve forces then, the cerebrospinal directly, the sympathetic indirectly, making all reflexes in the system to the detriment of the body, or the body has become one under the stress and strain of the nerve system, until the mental with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] become to portions the direct.

8. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find all under the stress and strain of these forces, though we find the body at times feels very good.  At others never just clear of pain.

9. In the sensory system some specific conditions, though have not to do with the general or the constitutional condition to be met.

10. Lungs, the effect of the condition as shown through the secondary cardiac plexus forces and a nerve reaction as vibrates in these.

11. Digestive tract much under the influence of those conditions as shown in the hypogastric, pneumogastric, centers, especially when the hypogastric becomes involved, as the kidneys and the bladder show the effect as how these, with the digestive forces, lack the proper elimination, and the system attempting to adjust and bring the normal forces to the body functions improperly through all the reflexes of the hypogastric center, both in nerve and blood supply, producing a cold hepatic circulation.  The liver becomes clogged, as it were, with the secretions as should be thrown off in the system.  All portions of the intestinal tract become then involved, with the tendency then to the system to prevent the system from eliminating in its proper channels.

12. R E L I E F - Then, to bring the normal forces to this body will take those of specific nature and of perseverance, and of those elements as have to do with the exercising of the mental forces in the body to coordinate with the physical of the body to bring the equalization of all elemental forces in the system, taking those properties that will bring the correct incentives for the condition existing, and building through the mental forces of the body that inherent [[Healing|healing]] that must ever be found in the individual, exercising the I AM in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

13. Taking then this:  This would be one dose.  We would take these in capsules form, one each day for at least five to six days.

Podophyllin.................1/2 grain,
Cascara Sagrada.............1/2 grain,
Leptandrin....................1 grain,
Licorice Compound...........1/2 grain.

14. Mix these properties well together, put in capsules and take the quantity as given for each dose for five days.

15. Rest from this and keep the body then cleansed well with water internally and externally.

16. After the fifth day of taking plenty of water, begin with the adjustments (mechanically) necessary to bring the normal functioning of the organs and system, as has been outlined, in the region of the lumbar and dorsal, especially.

17. With this then begin the study of those forces that are found in Unity.

18. When the system has been prepared we may find the responding [[Vibration|vibration]] in this element to bring the normal forces of the physical to this body, [3865].

19. (Q)  How should these adjustments be made?
(A)  Either by [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] or chiropractic forces.  Be made every other day for at least eight treatments, but not until after the medicinal properties and the correction through plain water, in and out of the system, has been taken.

Do this, and we will bring the normal conditions for this body.
