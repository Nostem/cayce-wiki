# TEXT OF READING 5562-9 F 12

**Reading [[5562-9]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1930  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 13858

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5563] and [5562], Dr. Lydic, Mrs. H. Goldberg, Mrs. Kaufman, and [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].]

Time of Reading                         [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5562] - this we have had before.  As we find, the general physical conditions are on the improve.  There is little change as we would make from that as is being applied for the proper corrections and bringing about the proper expressions of the self in the proper directions.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What is condition in nose and throat as mentioned in first reading?
(A)  This is on the improve; for with the setting up of the proper drainages in the system, THIS [is] RELIEVING the condition.  This would be WELL, that occasionally there were used local antiseptics for the soft tissue.

3. (Q)  Should there be any change in diet?
(A)  We would not change the diet at the present.

4. (Q)  Would it be advisable to prescribe colloidal oenentha?
(A)  Not at the present, we would not.  While the activity of same in the system, especially as relating to the manner in which it would affect the assimilations - these at present, as we find, are - with the change in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up, and in the changes as will be through the activity of those properties given in the proper way and manner - that is, of the Maypop - THESE will give that necessary change in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] here.

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
