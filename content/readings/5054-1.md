# TEXT OF READING 5054-1 M 43 (Bank-Clerk Bookkeeper, Catholic)

**Reading [[5054-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/20/1944  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 13175

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5044], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., La.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5054].

3. As we find, the disturbances have been of rather long standing, and they affect the organs of the sensory system through inflammation and adhesions that have arisen from the use of properties that seared soft tissue in the nasal passages, throat and vocal cords.

4. These we find, are the sources of the trouble.

5. The present applications being made are beneficial.  We do not find that they will restore to normal the affected areas, but they will contribute to the general welfare of the body.

6. We would use a spray of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], just as near normal strength as can be held by the body; at first using 1/2 distilled water and 1/2 [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], as a spray for the nasal and antrum and those portions of the face.

7. Of evenings we would use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the soft tissue of face.  Do not apply heat to the area, though heat should be applied in other ways a little bit later.

8. We would use these regularly, the spray and the packs, preferably of evening as the body rests.  Use the packs over the antrum and those portions of the bridge of the nose and cheek.

9. Also we would begin with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments; not merely corrections, though the chiropractor can give the massage if there would be the time taken to give it correctly - about three times a week.  Have manipulations as well as corrections in the areas from the 4th dorsal to the 1st cervical, and of course, not bruising but gently massaging the body, the neck, head, shoulders, face, antrum, the mastoid area as well as chin, forehead, cheek, all those areas - so there is the breaking up and the eliminating of destroyed tissue.  If the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are taken instead of the chiropractic, then twice a week should be sufficient, if given properly; but these treatments can be carried on by the chiropractor if following these direction.

10. At least twice a week we would also have the short wave electrical treatments to relieve the inflammation, to aid in stimulating so that the tissues that has been injured would be gradually replaced, replenished, rebuilded, and not leave the scars in same - as will be accomplished by the proper manipulations as well as corrections in the manner outlined.

11. When giving the massage, use just enough Pine Oil to make the massaging easier - this is the preferable oil to use.

12. Do use that necessary to keep eliminations a little better. Use vegetable compounds as laxatives and eliminants.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra copy [which was enclosed] for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] or chiropractor who is to follow these directions.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyco-Thymoline Packs.)

> It was very hard for him to get it up, and at times I had to
> work my finger in his mouth and pull it out.
> 
> I am very discouraged with the treatment, as it is 4 months,
> and there is no good change in him.  The doctor says the
> excess mucous and saliva is due to paralysis of the throat.
> He has started him on a new powder for his muscles called
> Blycocoll.  As my husband has been unable to eat, he advised

>

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 6/14/1900 in Louisiana; married; no children.
> 
> B2. 12/30/43 Wife's letter:  "I have just finished reading 'There
> Is A River'.  God has certainly given you an amazing power to
> help those in trouble and we hope and pray He will guide you
> to help my husband...  His case is similar to your own at the
> time when you lost your voice.  He has for several years been
> treated for sinus, and about October 1942, our friends and
> relatives noticed they were unable to understand him clearly
> due to his inability to form syllables in words.  He also
> has trouble in swallowing, legs are becoming very weak, tires
> easily, poor appetite, hands are weak, particularly the thumb
> and index finger, and at times his shoulders, ribs, and neck
> have a slight twitching.  One doctor claims drippings from
> the sinus has caused this condition; another, weak muscles in
> the vocal cords; another, a partially paralyzed nerve in the
> palate, and still another claims vitamin deficiency.  He has
> had several blood tests, metabolism and spinal tests which
> have all proven negative as to infection in his body.
> 
> Mr. Cayce, my reading the book has given us both hope and
> courage, and only wish that I had heard of your wonderful
> work sooner, as I feel by this time my husband would be well
> and strong and able to speak without straining...  An early
> answer will be deeply appreciated as we see no improvement by
> taking vitamins and tonics."
> 
> B3. 1/44 Wife's letter:  "Any interest shown for an early reading
> would be so deeply appreciated as my husband's condition
> seems to be growing worse.  His legs are getting so weak."
> 
> B4. 2/9/44 Case history and questions submitted by wife:  "I
> am 43 years old and for several years have been treated for
> sinus.  About October 1942, I noticed my inability to form
> syllables in words; very little control of my tongue; trouble
> to masticate food; cannot prevent from biting cheek and lower
> lip when chewing; trouble in swallowing food and liquids;
> great quantity of saliva collects in the mouth; muscles in
> face and lips very weak; body always seems tense and find
> difficulty in relaxing; knees have become weak; tire easily;
> poor appetite; hands are weak particularly the thumb and
> index finger and at times my shoulders, ribs, arms, and neck
> have a light twitching.  One doctor claims drippings from
> the sinus has caused this condition; another, weak muscles in
> the vocal cords; another, a partially paralyzed nerve in the
> palate, and still another claims a vitamin deficiency.  I
> have had several blood tests, metabolism, Wasserman and
> spinal tests - all have proved negative as to infection in my
> body.  Also had my appendix removed August 1943, but that did
> not relieve my condition.
> 
> In October 1943, I heard that chiropractor manipulations have
> helped many, so I decided to see one.  I am going 3 times a
> week for 4 months, and do not seem to show any improvement.
> I still have 2 more months' treatment, as it is a 6 months
> treatment.  So now, Mr. Cayce, I come to you for help.  Will
> you please ask these questions at the reading:  (1) System of
> treatment?  (2) If treatment can be given in ..., La., will
> you recommend the doctor?  (3) How long after treatment has
> started should I see some improvement?  (4) What precautions
> to follow so no recurrence?  (5) What activity in past has
> caused this condition, and how long has it been coming on
> before I noticed it?"
