# TEXT OF READING 2884-2 F 11

**Reading [[2884-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/31/1931  Sex: F  Age: 11  ReadingID: 10121

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Hotel Victoria, Room #2135, [[New York City|New York City]], this 31st day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by her mother - Mrs. [5423].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Bertha [[Rome|Rome]], Julia Glickstein, Charlotte and Clara Palmedo.]

Time of Reading 
12:40 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2884] - this we have had before.  Many are the changes that have occurred in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] since last we had same here.  It might WELL be said, "I told you so."

2. In the conditions as are existent in the present, and the needs - or that necessary is, as is seen, to create for the system those forces as will supply more blood, richer blood, and that that will function with the system in meeting the GROWING needs of the body - see?  Let's analyze that for a moment:  The necessity of more blood is evident, by the anemic condition as exists.  The anemia is of the nature that not only is the system, the body, using that assimilated for growing, for replenishing, but DOING so at the expense of some of the VITALS of the system!  as in the lungs proper, as in the digestive system proper.  These organs working under the STRAIN of such, then, need that vitality as may be had by plenty of OXYGEN, plenty of rich blood CARRYING oxygen in the system to replenish the system!  Not in a way and manner as to UNBALANCE the body, but keeping same coordinated!  Should ACUTE conditions arise by an unbalancing, it will be necessary for those of transfusions, but - as we find - will the body be kept in the open, plenty of sunshine; not in excess, as to take FROM the system, but as to aid those vital forces necessary - as comes from light and heat of sun, as from no OTHER source.

3. Take internally the animated ash, that will CARRY INTO the system that of OXYGEN!  and let the food values carry ALL of those things necessary for replenishing the system.  These have been well outlined to the body often through other sources, but keep the vitality going.

4. We will find that massage, stimulating the capillary circulation, will aid materially also.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What treatment would be necessary to bring about normal condition of left leg?
(A)  These of the massage, that assist a depleted circulation in carrying to the portion of the body those of the vital forces necessary for proper development; that which by stimulation carries the vital forces of the blood supply to and from any portion of the organism will develop normally.

6. (Q)  What causes redness of eyes?
(A)  The STRAIN as continues on the system.

7. (Q)  What should be done to clear skin condition?
(A)  Keeping an even balance in that as is assimilated in the system, making coordinations - see - throughout the eliminating and assimilating system, by the food values, by the diets, by the activities that are taken by the body in the SUNSHINE!

8. (Q)  What foods are best for the body?
(A)  Those that will build blood and nerve for the system, and - as given - these have been outlined.

9. (Q)  Why does her skin show worse signs and irritations starting about June of every year, and lasting in a very bad stage, until September?
(A)  The activities of the respiratory system through those periods, where PERSPIRATION - see - brings poisons to the system, makes irritation.  Then, when the circulation is changed by deeper conditions, see?  Just as will be found, will the body exercise in the sun, the rash - or the irritation - at FIRST would appear to be more IRRITATED - see?  but with the continuation of exercising, and proper food values, with the ash taken - Do not take the ash unless the sun baths are taken, or light baths, see, for that will make a tendency to make accumulations in portions of the system without proper distribution.

10. (Q)  Can anything be done before this season of the year to prevent such a bad condition?
(A)  Do as we have outlined!

11. (Q)  Is the time of her development near?  If so, how soon?
(A)  That will depend upon how MUCH of the vital forces are acted upon in the system; that is, the blood supply, the eliminations, how they are kept, to make DEVELOPMENTS of the ORGANS as MAKE for the changes in a developing body!

12. GC:  That is all the questions.

13. EC:  (Continuing)  For, are these kept impoverished - see - as the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] call for development, and - as outlined from the first - as the body is growing and calling ON the whole system to supply the demands, these MUST be supplied!

14. (Q)  How much ash should be taken and how often?
(A)  Quarter grain each day, if the sun baths are taken each day.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
