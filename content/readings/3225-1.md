# TEXT OF READING 3225-1 F 51

**Reading [[3225-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/20/1943  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 10626

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3225], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended through her physician Dr. A. E. Clark.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3225].

3. As we find, there has not been a perfect coordination established in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system as related to the activities of the eliminations of the body - or pertaining to the activities of the organs of the pelvis.

4. This produces those periods when through exhaustion arises from the least mental or physical exertion; and these react upon sympathetic nervous system - those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reactions especially in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] and the adrenals.

5. We would relax the body osteopathically, rather than by stimulation; supplying those elements - that is, vitamins B-1 and its complexes, and sufficient thyroid.  These would aid in bringing a normal balance, and in a little while make for a better equilibrium, better ability to sleep and rest; making for a decreasing of the pains in the head and through portions of the body.

6. Do that.

7. These necessarily should be under the supervision of a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], or one in sympathy with such treatments.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Are there any organic causes for her state of depression?
(A)  We have indicated the sources of these disturbances, and the corrections to be made osteopathically - which will be found needed most in the lumbar and sacral areas; coordinating the upper dorsal and through the cervical with the corrections in the lumbar and sacral.

10. (Q)  What steps should be taken to clear up her anxiety about the status of her two children?  done about it?
(A)  We haven't the two children in this body or with the body at the present.  This can only come from within self and its trust in the divine, that is a part or the basis of associations with every individual.

11. (Q)  Is a change indicated for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]?
(A)  Depends upon what is done about the general conditions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - through Dr. Clark
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
