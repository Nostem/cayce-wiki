Date: 1/3/1935  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 3701

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [531], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                             ... St., 
11:00 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. Philadelphia, Pa. 
(Body and enquiring mind, who seeks information, advice and 
guidance, as to his better physical, mental and material 
welfare.  Questions.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind, [531].

2. In the body-forces [See 531-2], from these mental attitudes, we find that - owing to the disturbances in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - small quantities of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] would be the most helpful, rather than potassiums.  The potassiums, as we find, are not as helpful as the iodines.  And in this form and manner (as in the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]), the dosage would be one minim in a glass of water before breakfast and before retiring at night; one minim twice each day, you see, for periods of four or five days at a time, then leave off for an equal period, then take again.

3. Also we find it would be most beneficial to the general health to have the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and corrections from the thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, once or twice a week - or two or three times each month.

4. As to the material relationships, we have indicated for this body that the fruits of the earth and their preparations for the fellow man would be [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which the greater advancements may be had in this present experience.

5. As we find, there are being made those associations with the organization whereby the body - or [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - may become more and more the GENERAL representative of the organization in a wider territory than that indicated in the present. While this would necessitate an expansion of the body's activities, it would bring those returns that will be - from the remunerative standpoint and from the monetary point of view - more satisfactory.

6. Also we find that the larger or more exclusive clubs may be used, where the fruits of the vine may be disposed of - that are of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s or body's own associations.  Not as a GENERAL way, you see, but EXCLUSIVE orders.  Such as the Racquet Club, the Boot and Spur Club, or the more exclusive of such natures.  These would be [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] that would be made helpful.

7. As well as an association with or distributing of the particular brand of the vine or tree - the preservings of one in the full South, that may be helpful in making greater associations for the body, see?

8. Also the Tea Biscuit; not as commodities to interfere with the particular activity - but those that would become exclusive for the more EXCLUSIVE organizations through which the body would act.

9. These two associations (for the preserves and the tea biscuits) we would make; which, as we find, would be with two ladies - widows.  Merritt - Pharr.  [Mrs. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] B. Merritt, Montgomery, Ala.  Mrs. King Pharr, Catherine, Ala.]

10. (Q)  Define attribute of health and give guidance.
(A)  As indicated, there is the lack of proper activity or impulse for activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in system - and the using of the energies through this lack of activity.  And it has been indicated as to what to do.

11. (Q)  Could I land the big [[Book of Job|job]] with the Biscuit people?  Is such an association possible?
(A)  To become, as indicated, a greater field representative - or to include a much larger territory; requiring the associations of an organization, in a measure, of the body.

12. (Q)  Or must I continue in my own business?
(A)  This has been given.  [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|The channels]] as outlined, we find, would work for most satisfactory arrangements.

13. (Q)  Please suggest what to do or not to do regarding Biscuit account.
(A)  As indicated, carry on.

14. (Q)  How can I go about getting other products and agencies in order to increase my income during this year?
(A)  By making associations with the two agencies indicated, specifically:  Preserves from Pharr; Tea Biscuits from Merritt.  These with the liqueurs in the specific clubs mentioned, or those TYPES of organizations - in [[New York City|New York]], Boston, Philadelphia, Newark; in all of these EXCLUSIVE clubs.

15. (Q)  With what definite countries should I deal, and with which not deal?
(A)  As indicated, those which the body represents - or the Latin-American, or the Spanish or Cuban and American.

It would be very well for the body to consider, at this specific time, the representing of those very same properties that are to be taken internally for self - that is, the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; the distributing of same from America for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s OWN countries - or in Cuba, Domingo, Porta Rico, the Isthmus, and the portions of these countries with which the entity would be associated, see?

16. (Q)  Regarding my wife and baby, [1228] where shall I go to get insurance that will protect them when I am gone - what type of insurance shall this be?
(A)  With the Mutual of Philadelphia.  [Penn Mutual Life Ins. Co.?]  And it should be the type that makes a specific amount for certain periods, or for ten to fifteen years, and then a specific amount for the rest of the life.

17. (Q)  What are my outlooks or promises for 1935?
(A)  In these directions as indicated, most hopeful.

18. (Q)  Are my connections well and good now, and my plans in accord with psychic laws or suggestions.
(A)  We have outlined the plans.  The connections here may be used for the furthering of these, as we find, and be in accord with all of the laws - as related to the mental, the material and, naturally, the physical developments.

Do those.

19. (Q)  Do my associates appreciate my services and the results obtained?
(A)  If they didn't, there wouldn't be the possibility of expanding the activities!  This should answer for itself!

20. (Q)  In trying to develop a psychic power, am I to feel that "the mind" dictates to the "[[Soul|soul]]-spirit" or does the "spirit" or "emotion" dictate to the mind?
(A)  This depends upon the approach the individual makes to same.  The MIND is both of [[Soul|the soul]] and of the material things.  When one enters into the [[Holy of Holies|holy of holies]], or within self, with the surrounding of self with the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] - according to the promises made by Him who is to be propitiation for the activities and the associations with divine love in the material manifestations, then the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of TRUTH]] directs as the MIND HOLDS same to that channel, see? To clarify this, let's give an example:  A body-mind, through its own self, raises in the mental power that which is for the satisfying of the desires of the flesh, that may find their manifestations in power, in fame, in fortune, in the place of this, that or the other seen before men.  This is applying the mind in the EARTHLY realm, see?  Then there may be the applying of the mind, HOLDING to that attitude of "Lord, use me as THOU seest fit," you see; seeking only for the material manifestations which may HOLD that in perfect accord with what may be manifested through physical or material activities of the individual.  Then the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] are from those meetings within, in not the aggrandizement of self or self's attributes towards the material of the physical standards, but towards that which is to be the activity of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] in Him.

21. We are through for the present.

> 
> 
> Now, don't answer this letter, because after all, you are
> too good, to take your time and that of your steno, and among
> hundred others, or among your duties, etc., write me long,
> interesting letters.  I read your letter and like to read
> what you say, your arguments, your expressions, and
> impulsively I take the typewriter begin to hit the keys, and
> here I am, again, banging, banging to you.  So, as it is read
> this when you have time - don't reply, it is not necessary.
> For that matter in some temperamental moment I could chat and
> chat with you - then be quiet, and all of a sudden burst in
> with a question.  It seems, I am learning to know myself - a
> task, and a slow process.  When I know myself, I will be
> turning myself inside out and the outside in and it will be
> again the constant bugaboo of [[Alpha and Omega|beginning and end]], and the
> circle of life, with beginning and with end, yet without
> beginning and without end.
> 
> As I write, it seems, some idea, some [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] of that MAN
> which I see when I am under ether (and I have experienced
> this twice) comes to me and wants to either assist me, or
> just watches me.  I guess, it must be a mental impression, of
> that man that appears powerful to me, as a very intelligent
> being that wants to be with me.  You once said this was my
> [[Angels|angel]] - it may be my hidden guidance, that I am seeking - a
> light, a something or may be a nothing - yet I continue
> impulsively to express my ideas, what I experience,
> consciously, and I don't know what it is.  A puzzle eh? - but
> yet as you say you can know your own experiences and perhaps
> not be able to express it, that's why I have faith in what
> you do because your [[Ideal|ideals]] are in unison with mine.
> 
> At that, I may say - well, what's the use?  Yet if I could
> find an experience, if I could see what you see, and create a
> power, for once or twice to experience a rarity based on the
> examples you say must be followed in "meditation" then I
> would feel better - perhaps it would bring a change in my
> being, in my life, because, I would have seen.  I need not
> the fear, and I want not fear, but I want light, some sublime
> experience, something that will guide me and tell me - here
> is the way, and that I may say - Eureka, I found it!
> 
> To night again I am reading meditation and if you have
> anything around there that will help me be LIKE YOU ARE
> or will help us commune, during my sleep or in some way or
> manner, that will bring me to a realization, gee whiskers,
> I would feel like a king!  Then, something tell me, well -
> may be it is not your time, may be God doesn't want you to
> experience, but then why does it impulse me to write and
> seek?  There seems to be a fight of some kind.  And I say
> fight, because in your letter you make me ask you this -
> How do you know Cayce, that the soul doesn't die?  If it
> doesn't die, why then doesn't express itself in some way or
> manner to enable me - MAN - to know that it is a reality, to
> believe?  Washington crossed the Delaware - history says so
> and men attest to this, so I believe he did, but at that I
> didn't see it, and all I know that as one of men physical
> actions or movements, some other man will convince me and
> prove it to me, and I will say yes, he crossed the Delaware,
> yet something in back of me, will say "you liar".  See the
> confusion here?  You understand me, don't you?
> 
> I cannot be convinced by faith, because faith may be an
> attribute of fear, and faith may make me believe that red is
> blue and blue is red, yet the director of my being goes ahead
> and injects into my conscious mind, such evil as to make me
> doubt my faith?  Why?  If the director, if the creator, if
> the unknown being is intelligent why doesn't he make me be
> capable of reasoning with myself, or why does he use me as
> his tool or his play boy.  Well, may be he has a reason - but
> gee whiskers, is it fair?
> That example of the seed, is okay with me - it seems life is
> an unknown beginning, and an undetermined end, yet we
> experience life, we know, we are living, and then I wonder
> how happy is the baby in the crib - does he know he is
> living, or is he just acting upon some impulse which like
> electricity doesn't know what it is, itself, yet it is
> directed by some being, and that being, will have to
> invariably be directed by another being and the other
> being by another and on, and on, and on, and on.
> 
> We experience life as I say, and we don't know what happened
> before we were born, nor what happened after we disintegrated
> - why such confusion, why are we martyrs of the directorship
> of that guiding being, nature, God, the supreme artifice, the
> deity?
> 
> And if I am not reasoning or if I am puzzled, why don't I see
> something while I am asking, while I am searching - may be I
> have to keep searching, and when death comes - then I may say
> to myself, within myself - I see, I found it, but then it
> will be too late, I wouldn't be among man, in the world, and
> I will be in the puzzling unknown...
> 
> That's why I should like to be able to do rare things, along
> the line you are doing, things that will enable me to see
> more, to be better, to help others, help myself and to be
> a being among my fellowmen, for their benefit and for my
> satisfaction.  Why not?  If my ego drives me to seek, to
> find, to look for what I don't know, then it is guided by
> God, and he knows best.
> 
> That's that.  [531]
> 
> P.S.  You don't know yourself how you do certain things,
> but somehow you experience some things - in your [[Dreams|dreams]]
> or trances - you come back, wake up and have accomplished
> something, and something that is of VALUE something that
> makes you be more and faithful, so my friend if you
> experience those things and I am a seeker of light, of
> knowledge, if I want to dig into [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of the
> unknown, if I want to solve the puzzle, do you know of some
> way that I can come to that realization, and say - I did
> something - yet I don't know how I did it.  Then having done
> it once, PROVES that I can do it again and again and again.
> 
> P.S.  No. 2  Another thought.  There is always an interval
> in things, a time, a moment, the millionth part of a second,
> when things change - when we change from unknown being into
> life, when we change from life to death - there is a
> continuity and there has to be a time, within which time,
> that changes go into effect - sort of an in-between interval,
> short as it may be.  Sometimes I wonder, if it is that
> "inbetween" interval GOD?  So quick, so rapid, that he
> doesn't permit men to know.  Last time I was under gas, some
> few months ago, the doctor took me to the hospital at around
> 11 o'clock.  They had to open two boils in my leg.  I went
> under the ether, and began to [[Dreams|dream]] with this man, who asked
> me to count, or rather he wanted to show me something.  All I
> know is that when I got up, the first thing I said:  "Oh, God
> that is impossible."  I was speaking to the man, to that
> which Called God, to that supreme being or perhaps to an
> imaginary impression of my mind, dormant at that time and
> affected by ether, yet I had a delightful experience, trying
> to count with God, 1929, 1928, 1930, 1931, 1932, 1933, fast,
> faster, until I came to 1934, and then I woke up - what
> happened between the time I ended counting and the short time
> when I woke up?
> 
> There was an interval, an obscure moment, the short moment
> of the winking of an eye but if that was a continuity, it was
> to me an unknown, a darkness, an undefined thing - yet I am
> thankful to God, that I returned, to know that I was living,
> that I didn't continue in a [[Dreams|dream]] which may have ended (or
> may have continued - this is problematic) even if I had
> disintegrated, and never had returned to earth to write you
> this type of letters and ask you these questions, that may
> make you think I am not all there, eh?

> R8. 1/23/35 EC's letter:

> Dear [531]:  I can't help but say something about yours of
> the 17th.  While you asked me not to answer your letter,
> still some of the things you speak of I feel I might able,
> in saying a word, to get you straight.  It is true, you
> cannot wholly profit by my experience or anyone else's
> experience, but it is true the knowledge of such experiences
> that, we may say, brings the awareness of such things being
> possible.  Oftentimes, one may try to have an experience and
> by the very trying prevent it from coming about.  Do you
> understand me?  Very much in the same manner as trying to
> work an example in math.  One will try and try, and then when
> they have quit trying, all of a sudden something happens and
> it is easy to work out.  Or, as another illustration, one
> will try to recall a name or an address of some individual
> whom they know very well they should be able to remember,
> and they will finally quit trying, - and it is there.
> 
> The real activity, then, of an individual, is to keep
> themselves at all times as near as possible in attune with
> the Infinite, which means just doing and being those things
> that God has indicated to us in the attitude that we should
> remember in reference to our fellow man; for, until we see
> the God Spirit in our fellow man - even in those we feel
> dislike us, we will never be well enough in accord to receive
> a great deal of instructions from Him.
> 
> I am sure (I may have told you this before) you will not find
> anything that will work better than the information or data
> on meditation than the reading of the 14th, 15th, 16th and
> 17th chapters of St. [[John (Apostle)|John]], and as you read them, don't look
> on them as being history, nor as a mere phenomena; rather, as
> you experience the truth as is conveyed there is within your
> own self, hear the voice of the Master speaking.  Know that
> it isn't just to His followers that were about Him that he is
> speaking, but that He is speaking to [531] - now!
> 
> Now, as to FAITH - we have a real lesson, I think, on faith,
> [Lesson IV, [[Search for God Study Groups|SFG]] I] and I am supposed to try to teach a class
> tonight something about the lesson, "Faith".  Faith is an
> attribute, you know, of the soul.  We don't have faith in
> individuals.  We have CONFIDENCE.  Confidence, of course,
> may be an out-growth of knowledge.  "Faith," (not merely
> blind faith), as the writer has said, "is the substance of
> things hoped for, the evidence of things unseen."  Confidence
> may beget faith, or confidence may be broken down, for it is
> only confidence when fear enters in.  We may doubt our own
> selves, or we may doubt our fellow man, and that is only our
> lack of confidence; but faith is of those things that we
> cannot see, yet we act as if they exist.  To illustrate, we
> cannot see electricity; we know by experience that such
> things exist.  Consequently, we prepare our houses, we put
> the wires in, we push the switch and the light comes on from
> some unseen power.  The force or power that is electricity
> is the same force or power we call God, and there is ever
> such a current - either constructive or destructive,

> meaning that we are trying to give.
