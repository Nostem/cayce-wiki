# TEXT OF READING 4358-3 F 36

**Reading [[4358-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/25/1926  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 12190

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of May, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4358].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Mo.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are improvements in the body since last we had same here.  The general condition of the system is bettered.  The eliminations are somewhat on the improve, and the ability of locomotion and the general condition of ataxia over the system is improved from that as we have had here before.  The increased circulation, produced by the application of the Balsam of Sulphur, shows the effect as has been produced in emunctory centers.  This last, we find, was not prepared so well as that before.  Hence the condition as is produced, in the form of a rash, by its use; yet, were this still applied in the same way and manner as first given, we will find this will still assist.

4. That condition about the head, neck, shoulders and face, would be greatly improved by BINDING about the centers (after a thorough massage with olive oil, massaging this well into the skin between the shoulders, along the neck, side of jaw) a hot application of saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], for the sooner this portion of the body is loosened sufficient that the mouth may be opened enough to take out these teeth that need to be removed, the less irritation will be produced in the system from this source.

5. The condition along the lower portion of the spine and the back will also be assisted by rubbing or massaging into same, first the olive oil, then this sponged off good with a weak solution of the [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]]; then applying and rubbing into same that of a STRONG solution of Life Everlasting and Hydrastis - equal parts, see?  making into a solution, see?  and massage into this portion of the body; keeping up those medicinal properties as have been given for the eliminations, see? Take at least one more quantity, full quantity, of these medicinal properties, keeping up the other conditions; keeping the diet.  More vegetables, and seasonable vegetables, and NO MEATS; for we will bring better conditions for this body, will these be kept up in the way as given.

6. We are through with this for the present.

> them carried out just as near as it is possible for you to
> do, that you are going to find relief, even greater than you
> have ever anticipated.  I assure you we would like to hear
> from you from time to time.  I am sorry that there has been
> such a delay in getting this reading, but I was called to see
> my mother, and a week away from my engagements made them pile
> up on me; but, we will try to attend to these just as fast
> as it is possible, and I assure you it is our desire to be of
> help.  We are limited in the number of readings we can get in
> one day; consequently, it takes us some little time, once in
> a while, to catch up with the appointments.  I am very much
> in hopes that this finds you feeling a whole lot better and
> that you will have a good report to make real soon.
> 
> Hoping to be of service, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
