# TEXT OF READING 144-1 F 54

**Reading [[144-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/30/1929  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 591

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by her sister- in-law, Mrs. [106], on 10/7/29.

[Mr. Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion - GC held the request of 10/7/29 in her 
hand)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [144].  Now, we find, many are the complications with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, and these are complications both in the functioning of organs and in organic disturbances.  These have to do with the depletion of the strength and vitality as related to the functionings, yet the forces in life continue to operate through these [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and were the care and attention given, much might be accomplished yet for this physical functioning body.

2. The blood supply shows the vital forces, as have to do with little that is assimilated.  While the body is under stress when digestion takes place, assimilation is much bettered. While the organs work under stress, yet the light - or the life - holds out.

3. Now, to meet the needs at the immediate time, or for that as we find that would aid - we would give, or prepare this, and this as the medicinal properties for the system:

4. We would take 16 ounces distilled water.  To this we would add:

Wild Cherry Bark.............3 ounces,
Sarsaparilla Root............1 ounce,
Wild Ginger................1/2 ounce,
Indian Turnip..............1/4 ounce,
Ginseng (wild)...............1 ounce,
Prickly Ash Bark...........1/2 ounce,
Buchu Leaves.................1 dram,
Mandrake Root..............1/2 dram.

5. Reduce this by simmering, not boiling, to 1/2 the quantity. Strain, and while warm add 2 ounces of grain alcohol, with 3 drams Balsam of Tolu cut in it.  Shake solution together before the dose is taken, and we would take half a teaspoonful every four hours.  We would not awaken the body to give the medicines, but when the body IS awake, give it.

6. We would also apply these of the COLORED lights for the body. The infra red, we would say, on a Monday.  The nile green on Wednesday, and the ultra violet on Saturday.  See?  These should be applied for not LONGER than TWO minutes in the beginning.

7. Do that.  Then, at the end of ten days, when the whole is begun, we would give further suggestions for the changes as may come from this.  Will the body respond, much bettered conditions may be brought to this body of [144].

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [106]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
