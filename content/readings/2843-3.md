# TEXT OF READING 2843-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[2843-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/3/1942  Sex: F  ReadingID: 10058

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Miss [630], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had in part before.

3. Now, as we find in the present, there is rather the complication of disturbances that prevent the better normal functioning through the body.

4. In making corrections, we find that many varied conditions are to be taken into consideration.

5. And, if the body would gain the better results, it should keep rather quiet through the greater part of the period of preparation for making corrections, at least - or for the first period of the applications that will be indicated for eliminating the sources of disturbances.

6. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [2843] we are speaking of:

7. First, the blood supply indicates quite an anemic condition; as a great deal of the resistance is builded against those disturbances that require a great deal of activity through the white blood and the leucocyte.

8. Hence the low blood pressure, and the quickening - or pulsations becoming rapid; dizziness, and tendencies for the body to easily lose its equilibrium.

9. More of these effects may be felt at times through the first three to four weeks of these treatments.  Thus that injunction that the body should tend to keep more quiet than active.

10. As to the injuries which have existed at various times through the body, we find that while there are not broken bones, or fractures, there are strains produced through areas, - as through the side and chest, and through the limbs, knees and ankles.

11. And with the lack of proper quantities in the lymph, and the lack of red blood supply for energies, there is the tendency to form greater quantities of scar tissue.

12. Thus the stiffness, and the lack of proper elasticity to body-forces and body-functionings.

13. Also there are those conditions in the lymph where there are accumulations, as the condition of the eyes.  While these show some betterment, with the proper set-up of - or for eliminations - we should find still greater improvement.

14. We would also use the Irish Potato [[Poultice|Poultice]] for an hour each day, at least five days each week.  Scrape the Irish potato (old potato) and apply with a bandage for one hour.  Each time after removing the bandage, cleanse the eyes with a weakened solution of Boracic Acid, the strength as would be applied even for a baby's eyes.

15. Afterwards use, for a minute, the Double Eye applicator of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]]; not more than a minute; using after same a warm saline solution as a wash for the eyes, see?

16. This should be done an hour each day, five days out of each week, for at least three weeks.

17. Also each day, or five days each week for at least three weeks, - apply, over the area where there is the hurting in the side, and close to the rib, an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] into the body what will be absorbed.  Heat the [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] to add the others, in the order named. Then, after this is massaged into the body, apply the heavy flannel saturated with the same solution, see?  Then apply an electric pad.

18. This may be done on limbs, knees and also ankles; though on the ankles and knees we would bind same on with a heavy hot salt [[Poultice|poultice]], rather than the flannel, though putting a flannel around same.  Heat the heavy salt, you see.

19. Also we would prepare a compound in this manner:

Rochelle Salts.............2 level tablespoonsful,
Cream of Tartar............2 level tablespoonsful,
Sulphur....................2 level tablespoonsful.

Mix these thoroughly together, VERY thoroughly.  And each morning before any meal is taken, for three weeks, take at least a level teaspoonful of this mixture, - either in water, dry, or combined with syrup or honey - however it may be more palatable for the body.

20. In the matter of the diet, for the strength of the body, use vegetable juices; not raw juices but cooked combinations; beans, lentils, peas, cabbage and the like; a little meat, but not large quantities of same.  Chicken soups with barley, with rice; varying these.  Fish and fish soups, - all of these take in varied quantities according to the appetite of the body.

21. Include in the morning meals egg, principally the yolk of same, and breakfast bacon at times, with rice or buckwheat cakes at times.

22. Do take at least three teaspoonsful of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] daily; not all at once, but about a teaspoonful at the time, and take two to three minutes in swallowing this amount.  This may be made in a small quantity, a pound of beef at the time, but take only the juice - seasoned to the taste - for strength.

23. Do these, and be consistent with them, for at least three weeks; not just three periods of five days each, but keep up all of these for a full three weeks.

24. Then we would give further instructions.

25. Do have each day, after the bath, a Peanut Oil rub along the spine, and along each limb; gently, to be sure, over the painful areas in the ribs, the knees, ankle and feet, but DO rub them with the oil.

26. These as we find should bring better conditions for this body, [2843].

27. Ready for questions.

28. (Q)  What is the matter with her throat?
(A)  This comes from a poor circulation, a drying of the mucous membranes through the area.  The better eliminations set up, with the character of foods indicated and the massages, should soon clear this condition; as well as the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] treatments for the eyes will aid this condition also.

Do ALL indicated here, and be persistent.  Do not be overactive.  But keep in a better mental attitude, of not only being good but good FOR something, - in counsel, in advice, in association.  Speak well of self and better of others.

29. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
