# TEXT OF READING 4844-2 M 15

**Reading [[4844-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/16/1924  Sex: M  Age: 15  ReadingID: 12842

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 16th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., N.C.

1. GC:  Now you have before you the body of [4844] who is at the Dept. Phipps Clinic 3, occupation shop, [[John (Apostle)|John]] Hopkins Hospital of Baltimore, Md.  You will give a physical, mental and spiritual reading on [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], with suggestions for the improvement of any or all of these.

2. EC:  In the physical we find the body much improved from that as we had here before.  There still is that lack of perfect coordination reached through the sensory organism and the conscious mental forces.  Hence, with the suppression at times of the conscious by pressure in nerve coordination, or lack of same, in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] that centers about the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve and [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], with this suppression, the coordination of the sensory [[Vibration|vibration]] brings the hallucinations, the indeterminate reaction in mental proclivities of the body, causing over action of [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and the pains that come after such reaction through brain proper.

3. In the mental forces the body very well balanced, capable of being so trained through the subjugation of conscious forces as to overcome this condition, also to give much [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], subliminal, reaction that would be worthy of study, and that could be trained properly to be of benefit and help to many.

4. In the spiritual, this we find well defined in normal conditions.  In the abnormal, supersensitive reaction to other conditions and other impressions as carried with [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces.

5. Then, for the improvement in the physical, keep constantly those [[Vibration|vibrations]] in system of the tea from Maypop, as has been given, for this constantly gives that incentive in plasmatic cell for brain and nerve development; giving the treatments of subjugation of the consciousness in the mesmeric or hypnotic stage and through such give those suggestions to the body as would bring perfect coordination of all nerve systems in the body.  Keeping that ever present in the body in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces:  "The body well, strong, nothing can bring unbalanced forces.  The body will coordinate."  Such suggestions given to the body, and we will find the body will respond to such treatments.  These may be given by any honest, sincere individual, who understands the control of the mind in such conditions.

6. Do that, and we will bring the body of [4844] to its normal development physically, the mental conditions far above the ordinary, and the spiritual manifestations that would be beneficial to self and others.

7. We are through for the present.
