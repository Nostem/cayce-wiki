# TEXT OF READING 2501-7 F 20

**Reading [[2501-7]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 9406

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [413] and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2501] - this we have had before. Conditions are on the improve with the physical and mental forces of the body and the correlation of same.  This the condition as is to be most mindful of, and especially centralization of endeavor in the manipulations as given, since the greater portion of that condition existent in the pelvic and the lumbar regions are nearer aligned.

2. Then, let the principal interest be given those centers where the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic are coordinant with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the plexus of the 3rd to 4th cervical, in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal, in the 9th and 10th dorsal plexus, and in the 4th lumbar.  In this manipulation, not so deep as to produce too much reactory force in the nerve, but more as that as stimulative to the nervous systems.  Well were, following this manipulation, the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] used, and preferably the sponge applicator should be used.  No, this not with this machine - but can be gotten!  In the application, this, as is seen, makes that quick [[Vibration|vibratory]] motion as becomes active especially with the sympathetic system, and - as has been given - this has much to do with the changes, or the lunar conditions, see?

3. Keep the rest as has been outlined, especially in the positive actions of the body.  Well that due consideration be given to the environment of the mental activities; that is, as to WHAT is read, WHAT is said, TO whom, and WHERE.  These not as suppressions, these not as hindrances.  Rather as guiding and as COUNSELLING WITH that being created in the mental activities of the body.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Where should [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] be used?
(A)  From the 1st cervical to the end of the spine, down each side of the cerebro-spinal - or in that region which lies, as it were, BETWEEN the SYMPATHETIC and the cerebro-spinal; cerebro-spinal being interlocked through the vertebrae, while the SYMPATHETIC is of a deep MUSCULAR central nerve, see?

5. (Q)  Explain reference to [[Revelation (Book)|The Revelation]] in connection with this body, as given in previous reading.
(A)  In [[Revelation (Book)|The Revelation]] there is given that illustration of how the [[Mental Body|mental body]] is raised from the various degrees of consciousness, and - as is given here - their ACTIVITY are through the correlated centers of an anatomical body, for they are represented by the refuse as comes from the 4th lumbar, emptying into the lower portion of the system.  While the consciousness as comes through the system in sympathetic system is raised to the inner court, or in the holy mount, through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] - that coordinates with sympathetic forces - see?

6. (Q)  Why is it taking more time to effect a cure than was promised in first reading?
(A)  The manner in which the body responds to the applications that have been made.

7. (Q)  Would it be wise for parents to provide new environment to take body to when leaving hospital?
(A)  Would be well.

8. (Q)  Where would be best?
(A)  Where there is the environment that makes for the proper coordination of that desired to be builded in the mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Where is such a place?  In ANY environment that bespeaks not of that as is called sect, cism or ism, but where the activities of individuals are at least SINCERE in the efforts to bring for others more than is in their present experience.

9. (Q)  Are all treatments given properly?
(A)  These are very good.  Only the changes as we have outlined, which - with the study of that as has been given - will bring to consciousness of those applying the various forces, that as necessary to give those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the upbuilding of the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]], [2501].

10. (Q)  Where can he [the doctor] study on [[Revelation (Book)|The Revelation]]?
(A)  Comparing, ANYONE, will they study that given in the Book and compare same to the anatomical conditions of a [[Physical Body|physical body]], will LEARN the [[Spiritual Body|SPIRITUAL body]], the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]] - NOT metaphysics either!

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving
"    " Mother     hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
