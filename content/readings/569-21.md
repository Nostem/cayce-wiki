# TEXT OF READING 569-21 F 53

**Reading [[569-21]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/4/1934  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 4029

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self, through her cousin, Mrs. [538], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [569]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, we have very serious conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body in the present.  While these may be eliminated, it will require the proper precautions and the persistency, without creating too great an amount of irritation.

3. There have been very good reactions in the system in many ways.  There have been the more recently the neglecting respecting the eliminations, and with the high state of the nervous system - through the sympathetic system - has tended to make for accumulations throughout the body of toxic forces.

4. And while there have been accumulations in various portions of the system (as in the mouth or the teeth), these now being a contributory cause are not [[First Cause|the first cause]].  Yet it WILL be necessary that these be removed, after there has been so much poison and accumulation as to make all the nerves and roots become dead - or defective, not dead from the sense of their feeling - and after the sources of the accumulations of poisons in pockets are in separations, until they have made for a disorder that will be hard to combat.

5. But to remove the teeth at present, without the ability of the blood supply to properly coagulate in or after such necessary operative measures by the removal, would make such an infection that would become rather of the nature as to be very destructive to the body; not wholly malignant but the effect of same.

6. Hence, as we find, it will be necessary to stimulate the system to such an activity as to create a balance so that these may be GRADUALLY removed, you see; and then these conditions may be overcome.

7. First we would begin not by very stringent but rather very CONSISTENT measures to replenish and build up the system so as to be able to meet the conditions in same.  And yet we have so little resistance upon which to build!

8. We would begin first to take all the Olive Oil the body WILL assimilate; very small doses taken often.

9. At least every day we would also take a quarter grain of Animated Ash.

10. And at least twice a week we would have the diathermy treatment; not too strong, but sufficient to arouse the nerve centers through the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] as to stimulate the eliminations.

11. After the first week, we would then begin with first the AL-CAROID taken after meals; a quarter teaspoonful after meals, so that it may be digested easily, you see.

12. And every day, for at least a week, take one to two tablets of the CAROID Bile Salts tablets.

13. After ten days or two weeks we would begin taking OUT the teeth, those that are in the worse condition, but not too many at a time; and be sure that there is the recuperation from same after each one is extracted.  Wash or CLEANSE each such place from which one is removed with laudanum and aconite, or such an antiseptic that will PREVENT infectious forces spreading in the body.

14. The diet should be not too much meat, but some strength from beef juices and vegetable juices concentrated should be used.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Should all of the teeth be removed?
(A)  Eventually, as just given; but not AT ONCE!  The body would not stand for it, until there is BUILDED sufficient so that coagulation will take place.

17. (Q)  What special diathermy treatments should be used?
(A)  The special diathermy is just diathermy!

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
