# TEXT OF READING 176-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[176-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/13/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 681

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by her husband.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [176].  Now, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find there are those conditions that are becoming abnormal for the body.  The conditions have to do with the eliminations and as same have affected and do affect, specifically, organs of the body.  In the action of eliminations there has become that condition wherein the blood supply, becoming so overcharged with drosses that too much is left in system, to the extent where it has allowed bacilli to become active in the organs and tissue of the system, as is seen especially in the lungs themselves.  This effect is also seen in various portions of the system. Especially are the liver and kidneys involved in the condition; yet, as the conditions exist, taken in time, and with the proper administrations, under the proper conditions, directions and surroundings, this may not only be allayed, but may be ELIMINATED or eradicated to such an extent and degree that the system may respond to NORMAL reactions for the body; yet, allowed to become such that there will be the continued spreading of the condition - especially where the eliminations in other portions of the system, as is seen in the liver and kidneys, become so involved, there CANNOT be but one end - that, that the pulmonaries, breaking down under the conditions, and the other portions of body becoming so involved as to give the inactive forces as towards resuscitation, the opportunity to function the greater. Inactive, here, meaning the drosses to overlap or overcharge, and little vitality could be builded in the system.

2. To meet the needs, then, of the conditions at the present time, first we would remove the body from the present conditions and surroundings.  Better were the body entirely in the open, or housed WELL - other than for plenty of the atmosphere and of the change in altitude, such as would be at or about Asheville, N.C.; and we would then give the body PLENTY of rest - lie in bed, but with plenty of air about the body; for at least six to ten weeks.  During the time, give the body all of the milk warm from the cow that it can take.  Taking at least twice each day a tablespoonful of codliver oil; using at times, when necessary, enemas, to keep and set up eliminations through the intestinal system. All of the orange juice, corn bread, beans, lentils, spinach, and such, that the body can take.  The JUICES only of beef, as meats, should be taken - none of the flesh itself, but the juices of same.

3. For the conditions in throat, bronchials and lungs, use that of pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] - at least a half a gallon in a gallon charred container (wood), with sufficient of the space above the liquid to form gas.  Keeping this well corked, but so prepared that the body may inhale the gases as come from this in evaporation, inhaling sufficient of this of evenings and mornings to feel the effects of same through the whole system.

4. Well, after the body begins with this, and is able to be up, to wear the Double X [[Radium|Radium pad]].  This will assist in eliminating the effects of the conditions from the system. At least a dozen oranges each day should be a portion of the diet.  Do that, and we will bring the better normal conditions for this body.  The changes in the positions of the body will necessitate the changing of the surroundings, for the HOME should be made near Asheville.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Would the electric blanket called Magnecoil, made by The Magnecoil Co., Inc., of Salt Lake, Utah, be well to use?
(A)  This as is given [Double X [[Radium|Radium Pad]]] will be more effective, for the body needs the outside, and the active forces in the [[Radium|Radium]] Active Appliance are more effective in such conditions, see?  While the blanket is very good in many conditions, but in this PARTICULAR condition that of a more local condition is needed to be set up.  Hence the stimuli would be about the solar plexus, allowing the blood supply - from the Radium Active Appliance - to be through the liver and the kidneys, so that the establishing of the equilibrium of eliminations would be set up properly.  The diet, the inhalation - which is both an antiseptic and a DESTRUCTIVE force for tubercular bacilli in the lung.

6. We are through for the present.

> what I desire.  I am writing Atlanta jobber and will also
