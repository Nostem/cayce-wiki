# TEXT OF READING 3011-3 M 68 (M.D., Metaphysician)

**Reading [[3011-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/29/1943  Sex: M  Age: 68  ReadingID: 10321

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [3011], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:40 to 4:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.        Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [3011], who seeks a Mental & Spiritual Reading, with information, advice and guidance as to his life and work.  You will answer the questions he submits, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here.

3. They look for signs.  Know rather, as has been given, signs are only given to those that seek only in [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]]' name.  Through messengers, yes.  As ye have seen, as ye experience in the present, the great white light in the center of the table here is a force or power ye give to those influences about thee.

4. But let this be sent rather by [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], than calling for same from and through other channels.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Will I be able CONSCIOUSLY to communicate with Masters of the Brotherhood in this [[Incarnation|incarnation]]?  If so, is there anything I can do to develop that ability?
(A)  If the self is purified of every selfish motive, and if the seeking is that such be given that it may be administered to warn His people.  But if it is only for the gratifying of self, no.

Possible then, and probable, if the self will sanctify thyself in [[Purpose|purpose]], in body, in mind.

7. (Q)  Is it likely that I will meet any of the brothers in the flesh, as Mr. Cayce has done, in this [[Incarnation|incarnation]]?  If so, where and when?
(A)  Ye may meet many.  For, oft doth man entertain [[Angels|angels]] unawares.

8. (Q)  What can I do further to become more serviceable to the Brotherhood and my fellow men?
(A)  Purifying, dedicating, consecrating self and [[Purpose|purpose]] in [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] - the one Master - Him.

9. (Q)  We have been told that [[Second Coming|the Second Coming]] of the Master in the flesh would not take place until 1985.  Is this approximately correct?  Can't we expect to see Him sooner?
(A)  How doth He interpret, as to the day or the hour?  No man knoweth save the Father.

Live ye then, each [[Soul|soul]], as though ye expected him today. Then ye shall see Him as He is, when ye live such a life.

10. (Q)  Can you give us any suggestions as to our lecture work when my body is sufficiently regenerated for that phase of our work?
(A)  As ye receive, so give out.  And in giving, remember the Lord is the way and the truth and the light.  They that climb up some other deceive themselves.

11. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
