# TEXT OF READING 900-80 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-80]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/11/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5283

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:50 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                ..., West Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-79] as given on the [[Dreams|dreams]] of [900], on Tuesday, June 9, 1925.  You will answer the following questions as regarding these [[Dreams|dreams]]: Is the entry on books as referred to in reading one that has already been made, or may be neglected next week?  Would it be safe to take 42nd Street Ferry to Deal?

2. EC:  The entry over which discussion may arise, with troubles arising, on ones that may be made next week, so they refer to ones having been made and an error of the same or similar nature.

3. The 42nd Street Ferry will be the better route to take.
