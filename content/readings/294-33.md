# TEXT OF READING 294-33 M 48

**Reading [[294-33]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/3/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1992

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], O., this 3rd day of July, 1925, in accordance with regular request by GC in beginning suggestion for someone else''s rdg. for further advice for keeping EC fit.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Physical condition very good.  Needed more of the physical eliminations throughout the body, that the body may be free of toxins as form from congestion, or of feces remaining too long in the ascending and transverse colon.

2. Then these as suggestions:

3. The body will so adjust the circulation as to produce the necessary eliminations in system for the best physical and mental condition of the body.  Then in a perfect, normal, nominal manner, with the desire to keep the mental in the proper frame of activity for the best for the health of the individuals about and of the individual itself - [you will wake up.]

4. Give that in a strong, positive manner.

5. Do that.
