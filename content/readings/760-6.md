# TEXT OF READING 760-6 F 51

**Reading [[760-6]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/11/1926  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 4686

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [760].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [760] and husband.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 Noon Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same, and you will answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find that many of the conditions are improved in the physical functioning of the body from those as last we had here.  While there are still those conditions that may be brought nearer to the normal condition, the general condition, the general resistance, the general building in the system, is bettered.  While we find there are those conditions that have become accentuated in the system, by over heat, and by strain in the body, as to cause the reaction in system of functioning organs that were as dormant in manner, yet this, with the general recuperation in the general organism, shows the improvement in system, and the abilities of the organs in system to respond to the normal conditions.  This, as we see, is that that produces the achey, painy, tired, dull feeling as is produced over the system.

4. The condition as exists in the coccyx and in the rectum, these may be approached from the local condition, and through adjustments and manipulation for the strained portions as are seen from old conditions that existed in pelvis region, may be overcome.  For that as is seen in the rectal region, there should be used that ointment [Tim for hemorrhoids] as has been given for such conditions, WITH the adjustments and the manipulation in sacral, with the application of the Violet Ray - will overcome these conditions.

5. The stomach and intestinal system shows much improvement, yet there should be kept more of those properties as have been taken with same, to bring about the NORMAL eliminations in the body.

6. The high enemas to remove the conditions in the ascending colon, see?  in the ASCENDING.  Necessarily, then, that as much of the water be taken in such as is possible, else this portion is not reached, see?

7. Do these, and we will find we will bring the better conditions for the body, for, as given, the body shows improvement throughout.

8. The diet should be - never heavy for the system.  Those properties that are easily assimilated, especially when those properties are being taken to produce normal elimination in intestinal system and in the liver.  The liver has the tendency towards that of dormancy.  With the constant [[Vibration|vibration]] of the Violet Ray over the lower portion of the spine, this creates that [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to set in motion, not only those for the pelvic organs, but also for the eliminating centers, through that connection of the radiation of the [[Vibration|vibration]] through those plexus and centers as come from the pelvic organs and the sacral region to those of the functioning of the liver and its organs connecting with same.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What kind of ointment should be used?
(A)  That as has been given for rectal use, see?

10. That is all the questions.

11. We are through with this reading.
