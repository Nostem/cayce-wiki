# TEXT OF READING 1612-4 F 57

**Reading [[1612-4]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 7680

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1612], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, Miss [1612]; this we have had before.

3. Still, as has been indicated for the body, there has not been the consistent application of the suggestions made for the corrections through the body.

4. The disturbance in the eliminations, the tendency for the lack of removing the poisons and toxic forces from the body is bringing about the effects of pressures in the nerves of the locomotories.

5. This condition does not necessarily require a change of climate, though there would be better conditions for the body where the climatic conditions are more uniform - and where there is a drier climate (though this is supposed to be). But do follow that outline which has been indicated for the body and we will get better results.

6. Do continue with the use of the [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] diet.  Try this in series of taking two ounces of the Welch [[Grape Juice|Grape Juice]] with one ounce of plain water (not carbonated) four times each day, half an hour before each meal and at bedtime.  While this will reduce the activities through the appetite, it will supply sufficient sugars without adding avoirdupois.

7. Do have the massages at regular intervals.

8. Do be mindful of starches in the diet.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What climate would be more beneficial than the present one?
(A)  The southern portion of [[New Mexico|New Mexico]] or the central portion of [[Arizona|Arizona]].

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
