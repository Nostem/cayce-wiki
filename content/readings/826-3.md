# TEXT OF READING 826-3 M 33

**Reading [[826-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/17/1935  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 4932

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1851 Columbia Road, Washington, D.C., this 17th day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [826], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [826] and Mrs. [811].]

Time of Reading 
5:20 to 5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [826]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there are considerable improvements and there only needs to be those precautions taken as regarding the general mien and health of the body; making for the diets and the activities of the body mentally and physically to keep same in the constructive way and manner.  These, as we find, have been outlined; and when it becomes occasionally necessary for reverting to those activities, use same.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should I continue to take [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], and if so in what quantities?
(A)  The better manner to test for the needs of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] for the system is to test the spittle and the urine for the acidity.  And when these show same, it would be well to use the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], see?  It is true that these change, as will be seen, according to the manner in which the drinks are taken. This does not necessarily mean outside things that are taken occasionally, but carbonated waters or the various characters of waters, or the food values that are taken.  These show a great improvement since taking the Atomidine; and when taken do not take large doses.  One to two minims twice a day would be much preferable for the body (this body in particular) than taking large doses at any time.  One to two doses taken when these conditions occur will make for the clearing of the disorder.

5. (Q)  The prescription for inhalation increased my nasal discharge and thus hindered my breathing.  Was it beneficial as taken?
(A)  This was not compounded properly.  The order of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] was altered, as to the properties combined. While it has brought beneficial results in some directions, it has not brought the results it should.  We would put these ingredients together in the order named, and they will NOT cause the disorder; for the combination in that manner is an antiseptic and an astringent and works WITH the mucous membranes of the soft tissue of the head and nasal passages and the throat.

6. (Q)  When this inhalant has been correctly filled, how often should I use it?
(A)  Once or twice a day, or whenever there is a filling up or fullness in the head and throat.

7. (Q)  Has the Radio-Active Appliance proved beneficial, and how long should its use be continued?
(A)  The Radio-Active Appliance is good for ANYONE, and especially for those that tire or need an equalizing of the circulation; which is necessary for anyone that uses the brain a great deal - or that is unactive on the feet as much as is sufficient to keep the proper circulation.  Best to use same occasionally, for - as has been given, and as it has been - it will be found to be most beneficial to the body.

8. (Q)  Should the body avoid entirely the use of meats like beef and pork?
(A)  The use of pork avoid entirely.  Beef may be taken, provided it is roasted or broiled; NOT rare, or especially not that ground or soggy in any way, for this is detrimental. Beef juices are always helpful.  Broiled beef regularly or well cooked is good occasionally.  Mutton, lamb, fish, fowl, are much preferable for most bodies.

9. (Q)  Is there any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  As has been indicated, the body is very good - it is in very good shape, see?  It needs to keep the mental attitudes as respecting its general conditions that arise.  When there are any little disturbances use those things that have been indicated.  These will be found to be helpful.  Only take the properties of the Atomidine for a small portion of time, or two to three days at the time and then rest.  And keep the mind active and the body GROWING in the understandings of the mental forces, the spiritual application of same in material forces.

And WRITE!

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
