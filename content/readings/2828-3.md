# TEXT OF READING 2828-3 M 40

**Reading [[2828-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/3/1943  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 10031

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2828], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Am I responding to the prescribed treatment given in first Rdg.?
(A)  At times; and until there is that doubt allowed to come into the mind, the body shows resistance building.  For, of course - unless that as indicated may be added - this is more of the mental and the activity of that assimilated in the body.

4. (Q)  Do my teeth have any direct effect upon my present trouble?
(A)  Those that are showing disturbance have some effect. They are rather the combination, not what might be said to be causing same, but they contribute their part to same.

5. (Q)  What treatment do you suggest for condition of eyes?
(A)  Head and neck movements.

6. (Q)  What causes occasional relapses?
(A)  The inability of the body to hold to that ideal as indicated, and to which it must subscribe, not only in its mental self but in its reactions and in its associations or applications of same to others, and to conditions and environs.

7. (Q)  Is it safe for me to drive my car?
(A)  There are times when it would be perfectly safe.  At others, it would not.  Unless circumstances demand it, as we find it would not be well.

8. (Q)  Will Life Reading be beneficial?
(A)  That would depend upon the application of same and that [[Purpose|purpose]] for which it was sought.

9. (Q)  What hobby would you suggest?
(A)  Anything that would have to do with detail, - as that of stamps or photographs, or the making of things with the hands.

10. (Q)  What kind of a [[Book of Job|job]] will suit me best?
(A)  Depending upon the application of self, or the desire of the body in its relation to those things.  As we find, that as would have to do with the making or building of things - just so it does not require too strenuous labor.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
