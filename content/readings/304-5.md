# TEXT OF READING 304-5 M 70 (Retired Farmer, Insurance Man,

**Reading [[304-5]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/24/1924  Sex: M  Age: 70  ReadingID: 2258

Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 24th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [304].

[Edgar Cayce, Linden Shroyer; Conductor, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]]; Steno. Geo. S. Klingensmith.]

Born July 3, 1853, 
Christian County, Ky. 
Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                         ..., Ky.

1. LS:  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading, giving the effect of the planets upon the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of this individual or entity.  You will give the exact time of birth.  You will give the name of the planet from which [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] took its flight.  You will give the vocation in life for which this body is most adapted.  You will give the previous [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] on this plane, and the personalities exhibited in the present life as brought forward from each of these former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].  You will answer the following question: Is the body fitted for an organizer or as an organizer? [GD's note:  He was trying to perfect an organization to preserve the supremacy of the white race.]

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and the record as has been made and as may be made with [[Free Will|the will]] as exercised, and the condition irrespective of [[Free Will|the will]]'s influence or effect as has been created.  We have conditions that might have been, that are and that may be.  Do not get the three mixed or crossed [[Purpose|purposes]] of either.

3. As to the time of birth, we find the completion of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and the beginning of a record in the earth plane at the present time was at four-twenty-eight, central time.  [A.M. or P.M.?]

4. The influences as we have in this present entity, irrespective of [[Free Will|the will]], then, we may give that in the developing points in the plane where the will, with the [[Astrology|astrological]] conditions have altered the present plane's existence.  For, as in this entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] upon this present earth's plane we have in this entity one that may be taken from the earth's plane of understanding at the present time as an ensample of conditions as may be wrought with will exercised in the right and in the wrong manner, and the influences of [[Astrology|astrological]] conditions as exercised in the earth's plane.

5. Now, as to the astrological conditions, we find this is the influence:

6. This entity took its flight from the planet Venus, and is under the influence of Mercury, Mars and Jupiter, in the order given, and with adverse conditions from Saturn and of Neptune, with the better forces in the Sun with Uranus, though only finding or exercising those influences with the will's force, as shall be seen.  Hence we have one with these influences.

7. With the zodiacal signs and influences in that of Cancer, the influences of the Mars and Uranus forces have brought many sudden changes to structural conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical body]]. These have been at times beneficial to development, again to the detriment - because of the lack of the exercising of will in the correct or direct manner, and the selfish forces in that of desire being the factor or appetite to be met with, and these have brought many conditions that must again be met and overcome in earth's plane.  [GD's note:  Mr. [304] had been a drunkard for many years during first half of his life; his wife and children had suffered for it.]

8. As to the influences then, we find these conditions:

9. One that has had many bodily physical influences exercised in the life.  [GD's note:  His children told me that nearly every bone in his body had been broken at one time or another.]

10. One that overcomes all of the forces from the material world, for with Venus forces giving the ultra influence in the sign with Uranus experiences brings the overcoming of much when will is exercised to the degree that the answering of the spirit and [[Soul|soul]] forces with the call of the inner man comes to the I AM.

11. In the influence of Mercury with Venus in Cancer brings the higher mental attributes to the physical, and with the will might and may be exercised to the degree to make the entity the leader among many classes and over the masses of men, yet as an individual standing with the minority.  In the element of Venus has brought and does bring many cares in the field of affection, and has brought many of the earthly ills and the material cares and worry.  Exercise will that these may be used as development and not meted against that [[Karma|karma]] that is made in each entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth's plane.

12. This we find then one that would be well in any vocation where the classes and masses are the ruling factor rather than of individual effort in production alone.  In the elements or forces that have to do with Mercury's influence in earth plane especially.  In the force then of that as a producer in field or stream or wood, or in that as the producer for that which when developed in the mental forces of the classes and masses will find fruitage, and will bring forth many manifold increases.

13. In the forces then as shown in this entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] here, as weighed with the will, we find these:

14. There has been much made in the sojourn here to be met and overcome.  There has been development in the mental and the ultra forces, for in the present these we find the influence of those in Cancer's constellation with Uranus in its present position with earth's sphere, that those in Venus that do not allow the love or the element of the carnal to enter in the life may bring forth fruit worthy of acceptance before Him who giveth all things.

15. In the will's influence then, let those that would, beware of these conditions, else in the bodily forces of the physical will be created that which will be the destructive element in earth plane.

16. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of this entity, we find that before this, this entity was in the court of [[England|England]], in that of Alfred, called the Great [[848?] & [900]], and those influences as exercised in the present sphere are seen in the understanding of [[Law of Moses|the law]] as written in any given State, and in the next [[Reincarnation|re-incarnation]] we will find this entity a lawyer.

17. As to the forces as given before this, we find in the reign of the Gauls, when the coast of Gaul was overcome, which is now [[France|France]], and in this we find the entity lost its life in the hold of the trade ships that then crossed the Great Sea at the hands of those in the rule of Hannibal [247-183 B.C.], and in this present sphere and individuality is exhibited through the personality the inherent desire for the rule of the white race, and in this we find the greater strength of this present sphere's development may be advanced in service to self and others through this work.  [295-9. Par. 19-A indicated an incarnation of [304] in [[Egypt|Egypt]] during Ra-Ta period.]

18. In the one before this, we find in the land of Alta [[[Atlantis|Atlantis]]], when the country was in the first developing, and the entity's main force and development was in that of subduing the animal life in both man and beast, and in this we find that all animal life dread this present entity, though with love this entity may subdue.
