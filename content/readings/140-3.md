# TEXT OF READING 140-3 F 22 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[140-3]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/10/1924  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 539

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 10th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [140].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born September 23, 1902, 
in [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y. 
Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                 [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading, for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]; a reading giving the effects of the solar systems on the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present earth plane, naming the planet, or position, from which [[Soul|the soul]] took its last flight.  You will give the vocation in life for which this body is most adapted, or may adapt itself, giving also the high points of the life in former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], giving the name and place of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth plane, with the characteristics brought forward in the personality in the present life.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the conditions, the impressions as given, here, with respect to will as thus far exhibited and without reference to [[Free Will|the will]] as is to be manifested in the present earth's plane.

3. In the [[Astrology|horoscope]] as would be given for the present entity, we find [[Soul|the soul]]'s forces took its position in earth plane from the influences direct of Jupiter and of Venus, and Mercury's influences, with those afflictions in Moon and Saturn, with the better influence in Uranus and in Arcturus.

4. The effects then as manifested in the body, without [[Free Will|the will]]'s influences, are as these, for we find the birth comes in that position when the earth's change comes near the cusps.  Hence one who has many conflicting emotions, yet with the [[Purpose|purpose]] of the Jupiter influence, or ennobling in the extreme.

5. One whose greater distresses will come in the early part of the life.  One that should be warned of accidents near the 21st of December in present year, when the afflictions are in the square of the influences that would be toward detrimental conditions, and again in the early part of April, 1925.  The first from accidents, the second from fire, for the Mars' forces are at the cross with the other influence. These, in their afflictions, might bring detrimental conditions.  Be warned that the body be careful, especially of fires in April, of car or train accident in December.

6. As to the influences, we find one given to be what would be termed in earth's plane "a perfect love" in every way.

7. One given to the higher ennobling influences on self, yet bringing many questions from others.

8. One given to be in the influence of affections through the greater portion of the life, especially in the wedded life, yet one that should be dominated, or controlled, by other influences, especially those under the Uranian influence.

9. One that will find the necessity of using will's force in keeping from those positions wherein there would be evil spoken of the good the body would do.

10. One that is given, especially, to be the student of the higher principles in well doing, or in ecclesiastical thought, yet often mis-judged in intent and [[Purpose|purpose]].

11. One that will, in the middle and latter portion of life in present plane, make the good ennobling, loving, home for those gathered about the body.  One that should ever keep the ennobling and the elements that lead to such in the thought and intent, and in will, for this brings much of the world's goods and therewith places great responsibilities in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s hands.

12. In that of other [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]]:

13. We find just before this in the Russian forces, as in the first rule in now Ukraine.  In this we find the entity then in same sex and in the rule as was exercised by the peoples of the chosen that sought to give relief to others of the same faith.

14. In the one before this, we find in that of the wife of the guard in the rebuilding in the walls of [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]], and in the present we find these again in the same position as at that time, and the necessity in the present that the rule of the one in the head of household shall be the one to submit in many ways to.  In this the greater development came to the entity's forces.

15. In the one before this in the days in Poseida when the laws were given in that land, and the entity then was in the courts of the people as taken from other lands and brought to this, and became the one that gave much of the licentious [[Intuition|intuition]] to the peoples in the land to which the entity was brought.  [See husband's rdg. 137-126, Par. 12-A in re [140]'s influences from [[Atlantis|Atlantis]].]

16. In the personalities then as exhibited in the present, we find those that give the influence of others not understanding the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]].

17. Again in the household, where [[Free Will|the will]]'s influence must be manifested to those about the entity at present.

18. In the next the position that brings this world's goods to the dictation of the entity, and again will's forces must be manifested.

19. Use, then, these as given and develop in that sphere that will bring entity farther along to the goal.

20. We are through for the present.
