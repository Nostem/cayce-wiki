# TEXT OF READING 1713-2 F 25

**Reading [[1713-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/30/1923  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 7884

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 30th day of August, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. LBC:  Now your body is assuming its normal forces and will be able to give such information as is desired of it at the present time.  Your body physically will be perfectly normal and will give that information now.  You will hear just me and will answer all of my questions.  Now you have before you the body of Miss [1713], ..., Pa.  You will go over this body carefully and tell me the condition you find.  Diagnose the case thoroughly and give cure relief for this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, we have had it before you see.

3. LBC:  Now go over the body carefully now and tell the condition now.

4. We find the conditions generally throughout the whole system are on the improve.  There are some local conditions that are apparently still giving distress to portions of the system. Especially do we find this in the sacrum region where the lesion, as we have given, shows the nerve tension as produced in the false pelvic cavity, giving the distress to this portion of the body, producing the strain on the system when the body stands for any length of time or when any undue [[Vibration|vibration]] is created in this portion of the body by exercise not in accord with that as has been outlined for this body and system.  We need only to keep those medicinal properties as has been outlined for this body and the exercise of the specific values that will strengthen the organs as has been given.  Be more regular and specific with the exercise that is to be taken of mornings and evening with the body lying prone.  We find the condition producing inflammation at times to the throat and larynx has been improved by the exercises and the cleansing of the blood through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] as outlined for this body.  Keep more of the stooping exercise for the body that we may overcome this condition by the strengthening of the muscles of the abdominal region, see.

LBC:  Yes.

5. EC:  Miss [1713], ..., Pa.

6. (Q)  ...?  [word(s) missed?]
(A)  If it hadn't been we would have told you.

7. (Q)  Are the lesions disappearing?
(A)  They have been assisted as outlined so that the condition is being removed by strengthening of the muscles to replace the lesions caused in the body.

8. (Q)  Is the blood pressure normal?
(A)  Nearly so except at times.

9. (Q)  Is the stomach still contracted?
(A)  To some extent, keep this up.

10. (Q)  Why does the lower part of the back still hurt?
(A)  The condition we have described.

11. (Q)  Why is it still tender around the abdominal and bowel region?
(A)  Follow what we have given.  It will correct condition.

12. We are through.
