# TEXT OF READING 287-8 M 66

**Reading [[287-8]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/24/1930  Sex: M  Age: 66  ReadingID: 1879

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [287].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker, nurse, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes - [287] - this body we have had before.  Now, the changes as have been augmented by the system's reaction in the purely medicated forces, has not WHOLLY been good.

2. There needs to be some attention given to the teeth, for puses come from same in system.

3. Those conditions as have been distressing in throat, especially in the lower end of the esophagus and in the stomach proper, and the heart's action, show distress. These, we would find, will be better by the MANIPULATIONS given [osteopathically], and by the stimuli FOR the heart's action in that of mild or minute doses of Digital, or Digitalis.  It's not ORGANIC in its nature!  Hence the manipulations and corrections as may be made osteopathically, especially in the cervical and upper dorsal, would prove most beneficial.  We would keep up, however, for the stomach [for the white lead colic effects?] - so that the eliminations may be made properly from the whole of the intestinal system - those of the olive oil and of the saffron.

4. The manipulations we would give at least twice each week.

5. The Digital would be in small quantity under the supervision of a physician.  This may be reduced as corrections are made.

6. The teeth should be attended to, so that the system is not filled with these poisons of pus accumulations, which is NATURALLY hard on heart's action.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

> all my teeth and as result had three upper front teeth

> 
> "I am following your instructions and do hope to get out
> of here soon, and will keep you posted."

> R2. 3/24/30 He wrote:
> 
> 
> "I have your letter of March 15th and beg to advise that
> I am gaining a little every day, but I still cannot walk more
> than about two squares until I have that heart palpitation,
> but not nearly so bad.
> 
> "I have followed your instructions except I have not taken
> the digitalis.  Dr. Cooperrider says that he would not
> recommend digitalis, but since you do he thinks it would be
> all right.  Dr. [[John (Apostle)|John]] Thomas and Dr. Fisher of this city would
> not prescribe the use of digitalis at this time.
> 
> "However, if I do not improve faster soon, I will get the
> digitalis myself and take about 5 drops.  Would you think
> this would be about right and how often.
> 
> "As I have written you before, I just feel fine when I don't
> move about.  My only trouble is that I cannot walk, and of
> course I could not work at anything that requires much
> exertion.
> 
> "I wish you would let me know by return mail about how much
> digitalis I should use.  Your doctors there could probably
> answer that for you or advise you to that effect.  I am sure
> I need the treatment as you have suggested in order to get
> the relief."
> 
> R3. 4/19/30 EC's letter:
> 
> 
> My dear Mr. [287]:-
> 
> "Please do not construe my silence as negligence or
> unthoughtedness, but I have been very, very busy - had to be
> away about the time your letter arrived - was in [[New York City|New York]] on
> a little business regarding the further developments going on
> here with the institution - came home to find my father very
> ill with pneumonia.  He hasn't allowed me to be away from him
> for about ten days or two weeks.  I'm glad to say, however,
> that he is better now, and we are hoping on the road to his
> normal health.  The last few days we moved him to the
> hospital.  During his more serious illness he was here at my
> home, and we had two nurses with him day and night, also the
> Doctor was with him most of the time.  So, you know just how
> it has kept me tied down.
> 
> "Certainly am in hopes that you are continuing on the
> improve.  I'm of the opinion that, as the information was
> suggested, it would be the proper thing for you to do -
> possibly you have already done so before now.  Know that I'm
> anxious to be of help if I can, and should conditions not be
> as satisfactory, just as soon as I'm able I'll be glad to do
> whatever I can.  I haven't been able to do anything for about
> two weeks myself, so I'm pretty well run down.  I want to get
> away for a little rest in the next few days.  Don't know just
> what the chances will be.  Haven't been able to do any work
> though, for myself or for anyone else.
> 
> "Let me hear from you.  Please remember me to all the folks.
> With kindest personal regards, I am"
> 
> Sincerely,
> 
> EC:GD

> R4. 5/2/30 Letter:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> "Received your letter a few days ago.  Am sorry to hear that
> you are not feeling well.  I presume you are just
> overworking yourself.
> 
> "With reference to the digitalis, I waited four or five days
> after writing you the last letter - I then started in taking
> five drops three times a day.  I have not taken but ten drops
> in the last week and am holding up fine.  I can now walk
> without any distress at all.  I will wait a week or ten days,
> and if I continue to hold up without taking digitalis, I will
> write you a letter worthwhile.
> 
> "Hope this will find you in good health again, and I beg to
> remain,"
> 
> Yours truly, [287]
> 
> R5. 7/11/30 He wrote:
> 
> 
> "It has been sometime since I have written you, and will
> advise you as to my general condition at this time.  Glad to
> say that I am feeling real good and can walk and get about
> without my heart breaking down, but I still have to take 15
> drops of digitalis a day.
> 
> "However, since I have heard from you, I have been very much
> worse and hardly able to leave my home.  My folks insisted
> upon seeing a Doctor here, a Dr. Fisher.  He at once gave me
> digitalis capsules.  He thought that I might take too much of
> the liquid.  He also took my blood pressure and said that it
> was back to 200 - had been down to 150 three weeks previous.
> He said the capsules each contained about 15 drops.  He also
> gave me medicine to reduce the blood pressure.  I got worse
> every day until I stopped taking his medicines and went back
> to your digitalis, or in the liquid form, also took the olive
> oil and saffron tea, and am glad to say that I am feeling
> fine and can walk now without hurting me, but cannot as yet
> stand heavy exercise.
> 
> "I also have gone back to taking the Turkish bathes, and I am
> going to stay by your treatment, and eventually I feel that I
> will be back in good health."
> 
> R6. 8/12/30 "I believe now that I am in perfect health again.
> However, since last Oct. I have not been able to do anything
> and, as I have written you before, could not even walk up my
> stairs without stopping and resting two or three times.  I
> could chop wood now if necessary, and all thanks and praise
> to Edgar Cayce."
> 
> R7. 10/29/30 "My last letter to you stated that I was feeling
> real well again, and able to look after my work.  However, I
> only lasted about one week.  I had gone over to Indiana, but
> had to come back and seemingly was about as bad as I had been
> at any previous time.
> 
> "At the time, I learned that you were away on your vacation.
> Mrs. [Mr. [2]'s wife] advised me.
> 
> "Dr. Cooperrider said that he thought that I should consult
> a specialist on heart trouble and he recommended Dr. Barnes.
> I did go to see Dr. Barnes and he made a thorough
> investigation, and finally said that I was in perfect
> condition except the Coronary Artery was impaired.  That was
> causing my trouble.  He took a [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] of my chest, and in
> that way he determined the trouble.  He has spent one year in
> Europe recently studying troubles of the heart, etc.
> 
> "He advised sweats, one a week, just as you have done except
> you did not say how many I should take weekly.  Also gave me
> one remedy to help this trouble.  He says my heart is all
> right and that I have no trouble except this artery.
> 
> "You did not mention this artery, but you did prescribe about
> the same treatment, especially the sweats.  Now, I am
> wondering whether this Doctor is right about the artery
> trouble.  This has been about a year now since I first had
> the attack and the sweats have always relieved me, but in
> about a week I would go back again.  However, I have been
> gaining a little all the time, and if I take a Turkish Bath
> I can walk anywhere for four or five days, than I can hardly
> get up stairs again.
> 
> "I just wonder if I should take a Turkish Bath every day for
> awhile as I did when I had the eye trouble.  I believe you
> will remember that I at that time had taken a sweat for
> almost every day for two years.  [See 287-1 Reports.]
> 
> "When you have time, I would be glad to have you tell me
> whether or not it is that artery that is causing my trouble.
> While you did not mention this in your reading, it might just
> be possible that the treatment that you prescribed was for
> that [[Purpose|purpose]], and I believe it was for the reason that I am
> always better when I take sweats, but you did not say how
> often I should take them, hence I only resorted to the sweats
> two or three times a month.  Otherwise I feel just fine, and
> today I can walk anywhere without trouble.  I took my last
> sweat on last Saturday.  It seems to be holding up a little
> better than it did some time ago."
>

>

> 
> "This will be just a short letter.
> 
> "Hoping to hear from you soon."
> 
> Yours truly, [287]
