# TEXT OF READING 3159-1 M 38

**Reading [[3159-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/18/1943  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 10531

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the sister - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., thru Dr. [3211].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Mt. Sinai Hospital, 
Time of Reading                           Fifth Ave. near 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.    100th St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are very serious with this body, [3159]; though there is a little change for the better.

4. There needs to be the administration of an intestinal antiseptic of an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] nature; that there be no further after effects of the weakening, or of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] or centers of the lymph circulation through same, in producing greater disturbance.

5. The infection arises from the duodenum, and conditions dealing with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in that area.

6. Also we would massage locomotory centers between the shoulders, from base of the head down, from the lower portion of spine to central portion of spine upward, with a weak grain alcohol solution.  This will aid, now, in keeping down the temperature, and will supply food for the emunctories and the lymph [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] circulation, or lymph patch circulation, and prevent stiffness later.

7. Keep the rest of those applications being made recently.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Sister - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file

Wired also, as follows:

INFECTION ARISES FROM DUODENUM AND GLANDS IN THAT AREA ADMINISTER INTESTINAL ANTISEPTIC [[Alkaline Diet|ALKALINE]] NATURE  MASSAGE FROM BASE OF HEAD DOWN AND FROM END OF SPINE UPWARD TO CENTRAL SPINE WITH WEAK GRAIN ALCOHOL TO REDUCE TEMPERATURE AND PREVENT STIFFNESS LATER  CONTINUE MORE RECENT APPLICATIONS.  FULL REPORT AIR SPECIAL.
