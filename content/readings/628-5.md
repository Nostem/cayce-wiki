# TEXT OF READING 628-5 F 10

**Reading [[628-5]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/8/1938  Sex: F  Age: 10  ReadingID: 4272

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of October, 1938, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [261], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         L.I., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [628].

3. As we find, conditions in the general manner are very good. There needs to be only those precautions as to the GENERAL welfare and health of the body; in those directions of the inclination for the mucous membranes of the throat and nasal passages to be affected.

4. We would prepare an inhalant in this form, for this body:

5. Use at least a six or eight ounce large mouth, dark colored bottle; with a cork through which two openings may enter, one through which the inhalation is to be made, the other to allow for the passing of air into same.  These must be kept tightly corked when not in use, and PREFERABLY in a dark place.

6. In such a bottle, put - in the order named:

Pure Grain Alcohol................4 ounces,
Oil of Eucalyptus................30 minims,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.....20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp.............5 minims,
Rectified Creosote................3 minims,
Tolu in Solution.................20 minims.

Shake the solution well each time before using, which would be at night just before retiring and in the morning before the body has broken the fast - or upon arising.  Shake the solution, then inhale into either passage of the nose - deep into the throat, one, two, three times.  Or if there is an inclination for the sneezing, or cold or the like, use this also as an inhalant to the throat and lungs.  It will clarify and make for better conditions generally for the body.

7. We would be mindful, of course, as to the diet and exercises for the body.

8. Well that precautions be taken YET about the feet and ankles.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Would further treatments be advisable, or are her ankles now growing properly?
(A)  Just so the proper care is taken, or the proper shape of shoe used or kept.  This should be done.  Otherwise as we find the condition is very well adjusted in the present.

11. (Q)  What type of shoe should be used?
(A)  That which has already been used in the adjustment of the disturbance.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file

(A separate sheet of directions was enclosed for preparing Inhalant.)
