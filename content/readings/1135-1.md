# TEXT OF READING 1135-1 M 35 (Ph.D., Psychologist, Gentile,

**Reading [[1135-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/23/1936  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 6424

Religious preference not listed) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of March, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [1135], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. [1135] and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Born September 9, 1900, in Irvington, [[New York City|New York]]. 
Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, that are associated with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as or called [1135].

2. In entering the present experience, from these we would find there are definite [[Purpose|purposes]] or desires on the part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] for those accomplishments of that which will be seen indicated in the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] through the earth's plane.

3. Then, these make for definite [[Purpose|purposes]], definite lines of endeavor or activity in the present experience.

4. As for those that become as [[Astrology|astrological]] influences, from the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in those environs about the earth, we find these from the [[Astrology|astrological]] viewpoint alone would be little in accord with that which is purposed or indicated in the activities of the entity in making manifest those [[Purpose|purposes]] in the earth's plane in the present.

5. For while the entity's [[Incarnation|sojourns]] before THIS entrance into the earth's plane are indicated in ONE environ, the activities of the entity are - as we find - from these:

6. The influences from Jupiter are indicated in that activity which would deal with large groups of peoples in many varied spheres of activity.  While the relationships would be individual, the effects or the activities that are in keeping WITH the purpose have to do with groups or masses rather than with things; but rather conditions in the experiences of many.

7. From Mercury we find the mental expression, and an analytical nature in its relationships with the mental influences and forces dealing with the activity in such relationships.

8. The Venus and Uranian influences make for the exceptional relationships in the activities that deal with the MATERIAL relationships of individuals; and in same the entity becomes as an extremist in some of its ideas.

9. As to the relationships these bear, then, with that which will be, should be, or may be the activity of the entity in the present experience or sojourn, we find:

10. One whose activities will be with individual action or relation, yet the effects will be upon the many, or the masses, or groups, those dealing with mental forces rather than the material relationships.  Those in the material relationships become rather of the EXCEPTIONAL nature, or rather those that will be AT TIMES apparently at variance one to another.

11. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, and those that have their influences in the activities of the entity in the present, we find:

12. Before this the entity was in those lands of the present nativity or sojourn, during the period just following what is known as the American Revolution.

13. The entity's activities were among the soldiery, and the soldiery of the [[England|British]] or the English activity - acting in the American land in what would be termed the Intelligence Service.  Not as a spy was the entity, but rather among those who mapped and laid out the plans for the campaigns by Howe, Clinton, and those in charge of those portions of the service in that particular portion of the land.

14. However, the entity REMAINED in the American soil AFTER those activities.  Not as one dead, but as one alive in the very services of making for the cooperations then BETWEEN the peoples of the entity's native land and those of the land of adoption.

15. Then in the name Warren, the entity gained through the experiences of those relationships established.

16. Yet from same in the present we find those definite INNATE urges as respecting varied trends of thought, either of a political nature or of that termed a patriotic influence of the country or peoples to which an individual or a group may be of service in a broader sense.

17. Hence in the present we will find the diplomatic relationships, the exchange of ideas and plans of the various nations or groups or leaders in such a relationship becoming of an INTEREST to the entity in the dealings with ITS OWN relationships to others.

18. Before that, then, we find the entity was active in those periods of the Crusades, when the peoples especially of the English land led those who - as it were - took upon themselves those defenses of an idea, rather than of the deeper [[Ideal|ideal]].

19. The entity then was as a soldier, or among those who made the journeys to that now known as the [[Palestine|Holy Land]]; and among those who became quite different in their aspect or view of the Mohammedan religion, or the followers of that faith, when there had been those periods of the activities among those peoples in showing THEIR (what ye term in the present as) brotherly love rather than the destructive forces.

20. Aside:  What a variation this is to the Moslem or Islam expression in the present!

21. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] we find the entity gained and lost; gained in purposes as set in self, but lost in that self was led rather than being in the attitude or position of purposefulness in self for [[Ideal|an ideal]].

22. Hence we find in the present those variations from that termed the moral or the religious or the philosophical standpoint, in the basis of the entity's activities as to the findings of those relationships.  Yet the study of that which prompts each individual, as well as that which makes for its activity upon groups or masses, becomes of special interest in the activities of the entity in the present.

23. The name then was Belduen.

24. Before that we find the entity was in the land now known as the Arabian or the [[Persia|Persian]], during those periods when there were those activities in the 'city in the hills and the plains.'

25. The entity then was among the [[Persia|Persian]] peoples who were under the activities of those upon whom the leader of the nomads made war, and was in the capacity then as the keeper of those things that made for the classifying of the activities of those ABOUT the court or the rule.

26. And when there became those activities in the 'city of the hills and plains' to such an extent that they influenced the activities of many lands, the entity then joined in those activities to DETERMINE from what basis they arose.

27. Hence the entity became among the leaders in the 'city in the hills and plains.'

28. There we find the entity gained and lost.  Lost when the activities were turned into those fields of self-indulgence, self-aggrandizement; yet the abilities of the entity in the reckonings through the stars, the movements of the earth during those experiences, are STILL a portion of those records in the PERSIANS' experiences of that period.

29. Then, in the name Iradell, the entity was a close associate of Edress [?] [Edssi?].

30. Before that we find the period which makes for the greater activities in the present experience.

31. It was during the egress of those peoples from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land (now known) who settled and became active in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land.

32. There [in [[Egypt|Egypt]]] the entity made for the greater progress in its development from the mental or [[Soul|soul]] experiences, in that particular period.  For with the return of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]], with the gatherings of the various understandings or teachings, the entity then made a study of such; and classified and interpreted same for the many, so that APPLICATION of same might be made in the experiences of those in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land as well as in the Indian, the Mongoloid or Mongolian or [[Gobi Desert|Gobi]], the Carpathian, and the [[Og (Land)|Og]], and those lands across the seas in those experiences.

33. Then the entity, in the name Ax-Tenuel, was a close friend of [487] Ax-Tell - that made for those developments in the chemical and electrical appliances in the experience.

34. The ENTITY, in its activities then, applied self in such a manner as to make for those growths that have been indicated in the various fields of service to which the application was made of man's relationship to man, man's relationship to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] in those experiences.

35. Hence the schools, the varied arts, the varied temples of activity, the various influences arose from the service as led by the individual in applying those tenets of [[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] and the leaders from Sin (?) and from On (?).

36. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, then, and that to which it may attain, and how:

37. As may be indicated from the activities of the entity throughout those periods of sojourn in the earth, when there is the material application of those influences or forces that arise from the mental and spiritual influence in an entity's activity, first KNOW in what thou believest; making sure that it (that thou believest) is founded in that which responds to that something from within that is creative in itself.

38. Then, making same practical in the experience of thyself, thy neighbor, press on to the mark that is set by self and in thy [[Ideal|ideal]].

39. For the way is open; the time is well.

40. Ready for questions.

41. (Q)  To what specific field of endeavor should I devote my efforts for the greatest possible development during this life?
(A)  As indicated, in that which makes for the greatest advancement in man establishing his relationship with his fellow man and with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] that work in and through man; even as the entity did as the activities are shown in the Egyptian experience.

42. (Q)  Would you advise that I continue my work relative to music?
(A)  As an example of the unison and rhythm of life, yes.  As that which may supply oft that which bridges space or time, well.

43. (Q)  What do you advise regarding my work in writing fiction?
(A)  If this is based upon the FINDINGS in the mental and spiritual, well - for THESE would make for an awakening to the many.

44. (Q)  What has been my relation or relations with my wife [1136] in past incarnations and just how can I develop this relationship in the present to mean the most for us both?
(A)  In two experiences, as we find, there have been the relationships.  Once at variance, as in the period just previous - when the greater disturbances arose ever between the activities respecting the promptings of those for whom the entity acted as the agent, or as an intelligent.  The other period of relationships was in the [[Persia|Persian]] and the Arabian experience; when they were joined in an activity that made for BOTH developing spiritually.

Hence in the applying in the present, let each know that from the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] - that is present in each - may come the promptings that will bind each in a union of service for that for which each stands in the material world.  [GD's note:  We later learned that he had divorced Mrs. [1136] and married someone else.]

45. (Q)  What would be the best approach for me in studying the psychic work of Edgar Cayce?
(A)  We have ONLY those in relationships to others.  We wouldn't approach it from here.

46. (Q)  What specifically would be indicated for my best work in the present?
(A)  The relationships of individuals to individuals, and the relationships of individuals to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].  Or, that which may be termed as the study of the [[Soul|SOULS]] of men.

47. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
