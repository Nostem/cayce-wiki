# TEXT OF READING 5227-1 M 10 (Presbyterian)

**Reading [[5227-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/20/1944  Sex: M  Age: 10  ReadingID: 13363

This psychic reading was given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the uncle - [1641], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born April 22, 1933, in Henderson, Michigan. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now called [5227].

3. Here we find an individual with talent, provided it is used in directions for [[Creative Forces|creative energies]].

4. As we find, there are two outlets.  While in some of the associations there may be questions, we find that it will depend upon the manner in which the individual applies itself.  We may, by the training, spoil an excellent actor and have a poor minister, or a poor worker at other work; or we may have an excellent minister who would be a dramatic evangelical minister - or an evangelist.

5. For, as we find, there are these talents of the dramatic art; of speaking, of dramatic activity.  These are evidenced in the personality as well as the individuality of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

6. Thus the training, the education, the activities that are given the body in its unfolding and developing life would be well to be along those lines, particularly.

7. Other activities will be nil, compared to whichever one of these fields of service the entity may choose to another.

8. As to the instructions, directions and warnings, these would be that the health be particularly guarded, especially as to the respiratory system.

9. Instructions should be given, then, in English, languages, things pertaining to the normal business rather than any great stressing on mathematical problems - just sufficient for a normal activity.  But Biblical history, English, American history; general as well as ancient history, should be stressed in the activity, in the education, in the teaching for and with the entity.  These, as we find, need stressing.

10. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity in the earth, - these mean so very little in the immediate; yet later may mean much.

11. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity, in the name then Johnie Foxye.  The entity made overtures for that activity in the theatre, the stage, in dramatic activities, and was closely associated with Booth who brought some of the most unusual dramatic activities to this particular land.

12. Before that the entity was in the period when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] was in the earth, being among the children who were with the crowds at the feeding of the five thousand.

13. The entity was among those who witnessed those happenings. Thus there is seen the ability for the entity to easily pray, to see, to feel, to experience the presence of duty, of patience; those virtues which most every individual needs to be thoroughly acquainted with and able to apply in the experience of self.

14. There we find the entity was among those who were teachers and ministers when there was the spreading of the gospel following the activities on the [[Pentecost|day of Pentecost]], though the entity was of those groups who came from both the teachings of the Samaritan as well as the orthodox Jewish relations.

15. The entity gained, in the name Jason; and it was in Jason's house that some of the activities took place which brought about questions; yet the abilities of the entity in its speaking, in its acting, brought great strength to others. And it may in this experience bring those understandings which will draw peoples to the knowledge of the relationships of man to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or God.

16. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, among those who were taught in the temple Beautiful for the administrations to other groups.  There the entity was active in presenting the tenets, the truths, and especially those as related to home building.

17. The entity was among those who held to the tenets which had been proclaimed or pronounced by the Priest; of a universal rather than of an individual nature, yet taught individuality for the presentation of the tenets or truths in other lands.

18. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

19. The stage or [[Ministry of Jesus|the ministry]], as indicated, should be the line of activity to follow; as a dramatic artist or as an evangelist - rather than any set [[Ministry of Jesus|ministry]] in any one particular field.

20. Do these, and give the opportunities for the expression in either of these fields of service.

21. Ready for questions.

22. (Q)  What school would be suggested?
(A)  That which, as indicated, teaches those things such as history and English, or wherever these may be stressed.

23. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Uncle, [1641] "   for Indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/39 GD's note:  While visiting EC's home, [1641] told of
> his family background:  how his father a retired minister,
> had adopted [5227] as a baby following an automobile-train
> accident, in which [5227] and his mother (now in an insane
> asylum) were the sole survivors.  Killed in the wreck were
> [5227]'s father, an older brother, and his grandmother
> ([1641]'s stepmother).  The shock was too much for [5227]'s
> mother, and she was committed to the institution in 1934.
> 
> B2. 6/15/40 GD's note:  Death of [5227]'s grandfather (his
> constant companion) took place, and he was adopted by [1641]
> and left in the care of his uncle ([1641]'s younger brother
> [1330]) while [1641] was in the Navy.  Later, [1330] also had
> to enter the service, but his young wife (newly-wed) took
> [5227] to live with her.
> 
> B3. 8/12/43 Uncle [1641]'s letter:  "According to a birth
> certificate here at ..., Va., my little nephew [5227] was
> born in ..., Mich. on 4/22/33.  His father [...] was born in
> [[Ohio|Ohio]].  His mother, (my sister) was born in Ind.  At the time
> when the Life Rdg., is executed, he will likely be in care of
> [...] my brother [[1330]'s wife] in Va.  That is his present
> address.  I am hoping it will be as spoken to him personally,
> and helpful with regard to his origins, experiences, talents
> and personal [[Purpose|purpose]] in this appearance."
> 
> B4. 1/11/44 Uncle [1641]'s letter:  "...My nephew made his
> school honor roll and had a wonderful holiday..."
