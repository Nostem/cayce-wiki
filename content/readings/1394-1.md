# TEXT OF READING 1394-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[1394-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/3/1937  Sex: M  ReadingID: 7121

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1937, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...] new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., rec. by Mrs. [1056]''s Mother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               Burnham Hosp., 
11:40 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Champaign, Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find in the present, conditions are such that any change in administrations in the immediate would be rather detrimental.

3. Rather, as is the experience of those who are anxious regarding the life, the mind, the body of their loved one, submit this to those that would join in supplication, in [[Prayer|prayer]] for same; that what is in keeping with [[Free Will|the will]] of the Divine may be accomplished in the present in the body.

4. For all [[Healing|healing]] must be of the Divine - that is aroused to activity even in materiality or matter, for that which is of the constructive force.

5. Then let those know, who are so anxious, that others care - and are joining in with them in this plea for mercy, for health, for that which may be in the wisdom of the Divine to accomplish in the present in these conditions with [1394].

6. This is that as may be accomplished as we find; strengthening those who wait, giving courage to those who doubt, bringing in the consciousness that as WILL aid.

7. When these conditions have come to the turning - that must come - know that this, under such a stress, is the better way in the present.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Any advice to those administering to the body?
(A)  Just as given.  Hold fast to that which is good, and keep the faith in the Divine.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mr. [Father] - Special Delivery - Wired also
"    " Secretary of the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers]] of the Ass'n - Phoned
"    " Ass'n file                                   also

WESTERN UNION - Day Letter charged to Edgar Cayce's phone 259, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]:

To change administrations immediately would be detrimental. All [[Healing|healing]] comes from the Divine.  Our [[Prayer|prayer]] group joins with you in plea for mercy, health, that which is in the wisdom of Divine to accomplish.  Hold fast to that which is good.  Keep faith in the Divine.  We are [[Prayer|praying]] with you.

Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

(Phoned Miss Edith Edmonds, Norfolk, Secretary of the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers]] (prayer group of the Ass'n), submitting name of [1394] for prayer daily by members of group.)
