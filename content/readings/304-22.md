# TEXT OF READING 304-22 M 77

**Reading [[304-22]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1930  Sex: M  Age: 77  ReadingID: 2275

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [304] and W. L. Evans.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [304].  This we have had before.

2. Much bettered are the conditions in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, than when we had them here before.  The drainages as have been set up in the system, the rebuilding - in the manner in which the blood supply has responded - are very good.  The conditions, as we find, are much improved. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What is cause and treatment of bloody expectorant?
(A)  As long as these are being eliminated from the system, and they are breaking the cells from the conditions as have been existent, they may be expected to be bloody and to show a good deal of mucus.  They would be much better eliminated, than to remain to fester within.

In the treatments as set up for eliminations, these may be aided by the [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] manipulations as go for the stimulation of the circulation to the pulmonaries, and an equalization of the circulation from lungs to heart, and from heart to liver, or liver to heart.  These, as we find, may be given once or twice each week, and without cold or congestion, continue to improve.

4. (Q)  Any other advice for the benefit of this body?
(A)  These, if they will keep, will be good.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
