# TEXT OF READING 257-245 M 49

**Reading [[257-245]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/30/1942  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 887

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mae Verhoeven, Burlynn Davis, Ruth Gimbert, Amanda Bowman and Norma Bailey.]

In his office 
Time of Reading                                at ... Ave., 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.         [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  We have his impressions!

3. Those disturbances as we find that have been aggravating to the body (Yes, he's coming in now), - these would disappear if there would be the consistency in cleansing the body tissues, the body issues.

4. While these disturbances are very aggravating, think what they would be if they were expressed in the alimentary canal - that has given the body so much trouble!

5. Then, be consistent with the diet.  Be consistent in the taking of sufficient periods of rest.  Be consistent in taking regularly the eliminations that are natural to the keeping of bettered conditions in the body.

6. And have the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]], - that is, when these are taken with the rubs, do have a good deal of the hot and cold applications.

7. When the portions of the cuticle or the rash or irritation under the skin become over-aggravating, use the D.D.D. Prescription, - preferably in the caecum, for this body.

8. These, with precautions as to diets, will allay, will remove the disturbances.

9. There are bettered conditions, - but you break over too easily, oft - and these will bring better conditions if the suggestions are adhered to.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Was the [[Baking Soda|soda]] enema and bath at Reilly's correct?
(A)  This is not harmful, - it is helpful, but more of the hot and cold water is preferable.

12. (Q)  Am I getting enough [[Iodine|iodine]] through foods I am eating?
(A)  When the regularity is kept in the foods at the periods indicated, sufficient.  This hasn't been at all times.  But there is improvement in the [[Iodine|iodine]] content in the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].

13. (Q)  Should [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] be again added?
(A)  If the body desires to use this rather than following the diet, do so - but take small quantities.  However, the diet is preferable.

14. (Q)  Any special attention needed to kidneys or liver?
(A)  As indicated, keep good eliminations by altering the diet and by the exercise, by the rest and by recuperative measures, yes.

15. (Q)  Has the rest and the planning of my day bettered my condition?
(A)  When that has been applied, yes!  When it has not been applied, then - To will to do a thing and then not do it is just too bad!  It's sin!

16. (Q)  Should [[Massage Therapy|massage]] again be used while itching persists, or just the bubble [[Baking Soda|soda]] bath and lamps?
(A)  The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] is well, and the application as indicated will allay the itching.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery, Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
