# TEXT OF READING 328-1 M 59 (Ex-Catholic Priest, now a

**Reading [[328-1]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/5/1933  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 2531

Spiritualist [[Healing|Healer]]) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of May, 1933, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, H. L. Cayce and L. B. Cayce.]

Born May 2, 1874, Wenigerath, [[Germany|Germany]]. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes (this wasn't all the name given at first), we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, that are latent and manifested in the personality of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], now [328].

2. In entering this present experience, from the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspect we would find, as compared to the experiences and that present in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s manifested abilities in the present, not a great deal that would be true in form to that ordinarily termed [[Astrology|astrological]] views or readings.

3. Yet, some of those generalities that may be applied to those born in this phase of the earth's position in the universe would apply well in the abilities of the entity, in the mentality of the entity, in the tenacity of the entity, and the positiveness of the entity.

4. These tendencies, though, are more from the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourn]], or the environs in the [[Planetary Influences|planetary]] experience of the entity, as we shall see.

5. Then, as we have from an entity-[[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the environ of Jupiter, the entity is under those influences in its broadness of [[Visions|vision]], broadness of experience; yet hemmed about in this particular experience by those influences from Saturn's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] that make for tests, and clarifying of much that the entity has had in experience, that makes for the changing in positions or experiences of the entity; as well as those influences from the environs of Venus making for the adaptability, and - while positive, yet - PLEASING activity as related to associations in groups, in classes, in masses; as well as those in Neptune, that makes for the mystic influence which, bordered with fervor in earth's experience through sojourns, makes for the particular interest in the mental and the materializing influences of the force of spirit and its activity in the material spheres of one's development.

6. These then, as we have given, appear rather as the basic conditions or forces in the personality of the entity, while the individuality from the sojourn will be from those activities - or that which it has done ABOUT its associating, contacting, or efforts respecting that which has been, or is, or was, or may be maintained as the criterion of judgements, or the [[Ideal|ideals]] set up for the mental being, or mental consciousnesses of the entity (this including, to be sure, the physical consciousness, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconsciousness]], the superconsciousness of the entity).

7. As to the application of self in the present experience, and that innate and manifested in trait or character of the entity in the present, we find:

8. One whose mien and manner is such that invites confidence from others.

9. One whose activities in the present makes for the correlating of mental activities with rote, and with rule of order, both in moral and mental, and the social life of self and others.

10. One to whom those things that are manifestations of that called the arts, or that appeals to the imaginative forces or senses of individuals or groups, are of special interest.

11. Hence the ability to visualize in verse or song, the abilities to judge the activities of others in the fields of art; or its kindred subjects are abilities of the entity, through not only material and secular but the innate experiences of the entity in its sojourn, or its development through the eons of experiences.

12. Those influences that pertain to the inner mental mind are as the field of exploration of the entity; that is, to judge, to delve into the feelings or activities of others, in their mental selves, is as the reading or studying of any particular line or field of endeavor would be to many another individual.

13. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, then, and those that influence the entity in the present, we find:

14. In the one before this the entity was during those periods when there were those sojournings from the land of nativity to the colonizations in then called a new world, or during those periods especially in fifteen and fifty-eight (1558) when the Bergersols came to this present land.

15. The entity then was among those who established in this new land the understandings of the rule as related to the material and spiritual relationships.

16. And the entity may be termed to have been the real power in making, and declaring for making, the land "home for the free" from persecution of those tenets as related to religious thought; while those in opposition (and whom the entity must contact in the present experience, for the better development) were those that made for this land to become the land to which many a one in shame, in persecution, was banished.

17. Hence the two factions that arose in the entity's experience during that sojourn; yet the entity gained, for many were they that found in the thought as taught, as material manifestations of a land where, the dictates of the conscious might rule rather than by might or main.

18. Hence the particular interests in the present, of the entity's activity, in the communication with those that may of themselves seek for aid, or who may aid seekers in the realms of higher mental thought that seek through [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] or communication with such channels for their activity.

19. Also with this experience (that is aided possibly more from a sojourn) there comes the ability to heal, through the activity of mental application in contact with bodies in attune.

20. In the one before this we find the entity was in that land, and that experience, when there were those being persecuted for their thought in the developments of tenets, truths, lessons, that were given by the followers of the [[Nazareth|Nazarene]], the Son of man - [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] - the Savior.

21. The entity then was among the [[Rome|Roman]] soldiery, under Aurelius, and in the name then Jhan.

22. The entity gained, and lost, and gained through the experience.  For, the entity - through that of material obligation and duty - persecuted those that adhered to those tenets and truths; yet, coming under the influences, there was the change - when there appeared, as it were, the [[Visions|vision]] - when [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] came to many that sought to have the freedom of [[Free Will|choice]].

23. Then the entity joined with those that adhered to these tenets.  While suffering in body, the entity gained in the mental [[Visions|visions]].

24. And from this experience in the present comes also the abilities to reach deep into the mental forces with those whom the entity contacts in [[Meditation|meditation]], or who - WITH the entity - seek for those quietnesses from within in [[Meditation|meditation]].

25. This, used - and not abused, may make for abilities in the present to mean much to many that are seeking to know the way.

26. Turned into self's own aggrandizing of material gains, makes for hardships and those of detrimental conditions in the physical being of the body.

27. In the one before this we find the entity was in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], then Aar (?), in those periods when there was the gathering of many from other lands, that there might be sent the truths that were being proclaimed by those peoples after the return of [[Ra Ta|the priest]] and the building up of those tenets and truths that CLEANSED man of the impediments of the animal influences in the physical manifestations of the body.

28. The entity then was in the name Apht-elder.

29. The entity came into this land from those sojourns of that now called the land of the rising sun, or Shinto land, or now known as [[Japan|Japan]].

30. Then the entity joined with those of the priests who had given much to those peoples, and for the whole experience wrought much in this land, aiding in the distributing of those tenets that built for the return of the considering man's relationship to creation, man's relationship to his fellow man.

31. These are the truths, the tenets, with which EVERY entity, EVERY [[Soul|soul]], may gain the greater contentment in a material or earth manifestation; those studies, those lessons.

32. In the one before this we find the entity was in that called the [[Lemuria|Lemurian]] land, during those periods when the people were leaving - through the conditions that were arising in that land.

33. The entity then was among those that first came to that portion of the present sojourn that would now be called [[Ohio|Ohio]], or western Pennsylvania.

34. For, the entity then set up in this new land the first of the temples from the [[Lemuria|Lemurian]] land; that has since been termed the Mound Builders' land.  Not the present as seen, but that from which same arose.

35. In the experience the entity gained, and in the present may be seen the tendency towards rote, ritual, and subjugation to powers that be, within and without the experience of every entity.

36. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, and that to which it may attain, and how:

37. Through the application of self to those abilities in [[Healing|healing]], those abilities in guiding aright without thought of self-aggrandizing, may the entity give those lessons, those tenets, those counsels in man's relationship to his Maker, man's relationship to man, that will bring for contentment, harmony and peace in this experience.  And bring for the entity the continued development towards [[Ideal|the ideal]].

38. Lose not [[Ideal|an ideal]] in heavenly things; rather than maintaining the ideal in rite, rote or rule, for material achievements.

39. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255] (2 copies)
"    " Ass'n file
