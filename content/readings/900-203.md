# TEXT OF READING 900-203 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-203]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/2/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5406

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. T. B. Brown.]

Time of Reading 
2:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement and relief of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are some changes in the conditions in this body since last we had same here.  There are two causes of the condition becoming exaggerated again in this body. First, those manipulations as have been given for the system have not been applied in the manner as given.  The other, the body has taxed self too much, mentally and physically, for the system to gain its equilibrium throughout the digestive system.  Not so much sweets or pastries.  More of vegetables and fruits should be taken for the body.

4. Then, to bring the better condition for the body at the present time, first we would change from these treatments to one that would give them in the way and manner as has been outlined, and give treatment immediately.

5. Then, we would take at least two doses - one this evening, another to-morrow evening - of Simmons' Liver Regulator, see?  and let the body rest; drinking plenty of water during the time, keeping the system FULL, see?

6. Rest mentally and physically.  With the treatments, we will find this will overcome these conditions and bring about the normal forces for the body, [900].

7. (Q)  Who would give the treatments correctly, and who should he change to?
(A)  Kraus, Green, or Barnewell (?).  [Eugene R. Kraus, D.O., Charles S. Green, D.O., Chas. F. Bandel, D.O.?]

8. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the best conditions of the body?
(A)  Do that, as has been given, for the best conditions of the body.  We are through with this reading at present.
