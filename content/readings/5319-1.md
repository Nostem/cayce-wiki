# TEXT OF READING 5319-1 F 55

**Reading [[5319-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/7/1944  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 13458

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5319], new Associate member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Coronet and [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Mrs. [5319].]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5319].

3. As we find, there are conditions which are preventing the better physical functioning.  These conditions are gradually beginning to take toll, as it were, of the body.  For there are complications arising from the disorders in the pelvic area and those which have to do with the reflex from these to the structural portion of the body.

4. Segregations are beginning to take place and, unless these can be dissipated, must eventually bring the necessity of operative measures, and yet these would not be the preferable in the present.  For these would bring a distribution of the poisons through the system at the present time.

5. These, as we find, then, are the conditions with this body, [5319] we are speaking of present in this room:

6. The blood supply indicates the character of the disorders, the pelvic conditions, the disturbance in the structural portions of the body, the ache, the pains.

7. In the beginning we would first use regularly, at least for a half an hour each day over the groin and hip area, [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.  Keep these on while the body rests, and do stop to rest at least a half an hour each day.  Apply this across those areas where there is the pain in the groin and over the hip area.

8. Then follow this with a gentle massage along the area of the lower portion of the spine and over the right limb so that the disturbances, as they are disintegrated by the absorption of the conditions, properties which would disintegrate inflammation, may be eliminated from the body.

9. Increase the eliminations by the use first of a senna base eliminant and then the next time with the mineral salts eliminant, or we would use senna leaves.  Take the senna leaves each evening that these are to be taken, and about twice a week we would take the senna pod.  Put about three pods in a teacup.  Pour boiling water over this and let it stand for thirty minutes and then cool and drink.  The mineral salts when these are used, about once or twice a week, take Sal Hepatica a heaping teaspoonful, not other than to keep the eliminations bettered.

10. Do add more strengthening foods to the body-forces in the diets which carry E vitamins as well as A and B or the B complex or B combinations, niacin and iron.  For we must enrich the blood from the deteriorations that are beginning to indicate how a form of anemia is affecting the resistances in the body.

11. When these have been done for at least a week, two weeks, begin with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, with the use of the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] which will aid in dissipating accumulations or tumorous natures in the pelvic area.  Have these done by one who carries both of these in his activities, such as Ober [Vincent H. Ober, D.O.].  These, we will find, if these will react, will prevent serious disorders later.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What kind of electrical treatment?
(A)  The short-wave, or here it may be the short-wave at one time and the sinusoidal the next, which is a form of short-wave but this should be for the hip and then the lower pelvic areas.

14. (Q)  What is causing pain in right side?
(A)  These are from the conditions that we have just been giving.  This is accumulation in the area in the lower pelvis.

15. (Q)  Does that cause hip condition also?
(A)  This is the cause of the condition of the hip.

16. (Q)  What can be done for the left kidney?
(A)  All of these will be a part of the general conditions, see?  These first, though, do set up the [[Alkaline Diet|alkalizing]] of body and the eliminations of poisons before we begin with the building up or breaking up of the disturbances and building the body back to bettered conditions.

Do follow the diets in foods which are rich in necessary minerals for replenishing blood supply.  The E vitamin, the wheat germ with the cereals or wheat germ oil taken in the capsule at least once a week or twice during the week.  Best that it be varied, one week once a week, the next week twice, next week three times, but have regular periods for it.  The B-1 should be rich in the foods which are taken.  We would find this from all foods which are yellow in color, not as greens that would turn yellow, but as the yellow variety of squash, carrots, wax beans, peaches, all of these are well. Though, there are other foods, to be sure, as liver, beef juices, fish, all of these are well to be portions of the diet.

These treatments would be taken for a week, two weeks, as has been indicted; though the diets we would keep up, when there is begun the other treatments.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Vincent H. Ober, Bankers Trust Bldg., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
