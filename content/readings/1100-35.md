# TEXT OF READING 1100-35 F 47

**Reading [[1100-35]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/27/1943  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 6322

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1100], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   Hotel ..., 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Utah.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1100].

3. As we find, the body has not responded wholly to those suggestions indicated for the body; owing to the variation in the pressure and the quickening or irregularity produced by the pressure upon the whole of the nerve and blood circulation.

4. The acute conditions as we find through the body in the present would best respond with the application of stupes or packs made with salt and vinegar, - pure apple vinegar; this not merely rubbed into the areas where there are the acute conditions, stitches or pains in the side, but making a pack and applying the electric pad over same.

5. These, as we find, would be the better manner to relieve these acute conditions.

6. And we would keep the quiet and the equalizing in the gentle treatments of the circulation and the pressures that are naturally produced in various centers along the spine, in the attempt of the body to adjust itself.

7. Keep the eliminations rather regular by the use of the varied forms not only in the diet but in the vegetable compounds as well occasionally, until the body is thoroughly cleansed, and also now and then the salts or mineral laxatives also.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  How long at a time should the packs be applied?
(A)  Thirty minutes to an hour.  And then when these are taken off, bathe off the body, preferably with a little weak [[Baking Soda|soda]] water.  This will be effective, and still the rub alcohol or grain alcohol is preferable over the area just after bathing with the [[Baking Soda|soda]] water, - for this closes the pores and prevents congestion.  These will not be hard on the circulation and will relieve the tendency for accumulation of toxic forces through the body.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

HEAT TABLE SALT SATURATE WITH APPLE VINEGAR  APPLY IN PACKS OVER PAINFUL AREA USING ELECTRIC PAD  KEEP QUIET  CONTINUE ELIMINATIONS  FULL REPORT AIR MAIL.
