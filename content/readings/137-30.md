# TEXT OF READING 137-30 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-30]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/12/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 420

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 12th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] ..., of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding any of these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, here. This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, more and more give the body-mind and the physical understanding to the body of the manifestation of spiritual laws, through cosmic forces, in the material world.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Monday night, November 9, 1925, at home.  "I was eating, and imagined I had difficulty in physical awakening - that is, I did not realize I was asleep - I was in one of my fainting spells."
(A)  In this we see there is brought to the physical mind, in this emblematical manner, those physical conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] that have been given the body, and in this there is seen just how same operates on the [[Physical Body|physical body]]. With the overtaxing of the vitality and strength of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], the whole force of the nerve system collapses, or enters in that state in which the recuperation of the assimilation forces in system may gain strength from the vitality of the rebuilding forces in the body.  Hence the necessity of those Radio Applicator [[Vibration|vibrations]], as was given, in the forces as seen with the combination of the iron, see?  for this active [[Vibration|vibration]] in the system brings that vital force necessary to the blood supply in that state when recuperating forces are on the build, see?  and the system should, then, be warned with the diet.  Not that at the present time the body is taking any foods that do not agree particularly, but that NEVER, under strain, when very tired, very excited, very mad, should the body take foods in the system, see?  And never take any food that the body finds is not agreeing with same (as the warning for the milk was given).

Well that a tonic or mild stimulant be given the body.  That is, such as pure spirits frumenti in toddy, or milk and egg, once each day.  No MORE, no LESS, see?  Whole egg, with four to six (4 to 6) teaspoonfuls of the spirits frumenti, see? Milk and egg, see, and the diet then will be, of necessity, increased, see?  Then, using those Radio-Active forces as given, see.  The constitution of the body would be added to by taking, half quantity as prescribed on bottle, Wyeth's Iron Beef & Wine, see? as regular as given, but half the quantity of the spirits frumenti is taken.  Do that, and we need not be afraid of the re-occurring of these spells, if the Radio-Active forces are applied, with the Iron in same, see?

4. (Q)  Does this indicate the coming of a fainting spell at a particular time, that I might be forewarned of?
(A)  As given, if these as given are taken, needn't fear the re-occurrence of one, see - with the same forces as are seen for correction of taking food in the system, or circumstances concerning same, see?  Using that care and attention as is both consistent and persistent.

5. (Q)  Tuesday, November 10.  "Saw two automobiles, peoples in or near machines arguing about which gas the better.  One set claimed one gas better, the other set other gas better."
(A)  This, as we see, referring then to stocks and of that of the [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Electric Gas. see?  and as the varying conditions have arisen, and as there are being set many of those combinations as concerning same in the day's (today) trading, when there is the profit for the individual, take same.  Clear out of all TODAY, as much as possible without losses, see?

As is seen in that of Southern, see, the two prices have been given, see 15 1/2 - 21 1/2.  Take the 15 1/2 - be satisfied - get out, see?  Clear out of all of these before another break begins with these, see?  Same as seen in Dairy Products, same as is seen Arnold Constable, see?  Clear from all TODAY as near as possible, see, without the losses, for we should have in each of these commodities, as are seen in the trading today, for as has been given, there would be the turning back today - THIS day, see, to those more perfect understandings between [137], [900], and their correlations of conditions as respect to self, mentally, morally, financially.  Then do it!

6. (Q)  November 11, Wednesday morning, at home.  "Saw the figures 130 1/4."
(A)  These, as we see, refer to the various stocks and conditions, see?  As is seen, this is relative to L & N and its descent as will begin with many of the others, see?  The action then as is to be taken in reference to this is, watch that condition, and as it changes in its action, then on the morrow be ready to follow same, see? for we will find with this recuperation, again the descent coming soon.

7. (Q)  "Saw Bangor & [Aroostook]* Herzotg [?]."
(A)  This, as we see, the correlation of those conditions as have been previously given for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to act upon.  Then act in that way as given, see, at that time, selling and buying as the conditions arise, yet as given, clear from all as near as possible THIS day.

8. We are through.
