# TEXT OF READING 53-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[53-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/14/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 141

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of October, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Wigal and Wallin, Miss Painter, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [53], - this we have had before.  There are changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, as is indicated by the better use of the limbs, of the changes in the eliminations and in the distribution of that as is assimilated.  In the releasing of the pressures as have been apparent in the lumbar, the sacral, and especially the coccyx, we will find that nerve energy to extremities will be more active in its activities in the physical functioning of the body.

2. It would be well that there be added the saturated solutions or packs of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], and that the stimulation, after the manipulative measures in lumbar and sacral - be through the applying of the violet ray, rather than the ultra violet.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is condition of genital-urinary system?
(A)  Inflammation as has existed shows some improvements there, but the local application for the conditions existent will be the most effective.  As for the GENERAL reaction in same, these will be aided most through those applications made in lumbar and sacral.

5. (Q)  How long before improvement will be noticed in legs?
(A)  In eight to twelve days.

6. (Q)  Could body obtain treatment at home?
(A)  Not so well, as yet.  Later, there may be.

7. (Q)  Any suggestion as to change in treatment?
(A)  As given.

8. That is all the questions regarding this body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Wigal Copy to Ass'n Office
"    " Research Dept.
"    " Self to send home
