# TEXT OF READING 1010-7 F 64

**Reading [[1010-7]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/6/1935  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 6094

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1010], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

12:15 to 12:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1010].  Some bettered are the influences and forces of the body.  The stimulations that have come to the body through the activities of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the electrical forces, the carrying of those influences into the body, have been helpful.

2. But there has been the increase, to be sure, in the circulation from the heart to the liver WITHOUT the congestion or the inflammation in the gall duct area being carried out through the system.

3. Do not OVERSTIMULATE the heart's activity, but it would be well to use the enemas, the oil packs, and even to take QUANTITIES of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] internally, until there IS an activity.

4. This, of course, will be weakening for the time.  But if there will be the stimulation through the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], that may be given through the neuropathic adjustments AND [[Massage Therapy|massage]], along the upper dorsal and through the cervical area, this will bring the better conditions.

5. We would, then, use high enemas until there is there near NORMAL pulsation.  This doesn't mean to use them continually, to be sure; but use the colon irrigation, see?  and following such we would use an intestinal antiseptic, as the Glyco- Thymoline or the Alkalin Patrolagar, stirred in the last water, that there may be no irritations produced by the quantities of refuse forces - or the activity of the gall duct area in its elimination through the alimentary canal.

6. These enemas or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be used one possibly every day, possibly every two to three days, UNTIL the pulsation or the heart beat is near normal, see?

7. Take the Oil Packs again, then a quantity of the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] through the system, until we get these conditions allayed.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
