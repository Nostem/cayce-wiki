# TEXT OF READING 4800-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4800-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/20/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12783

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of December 1923, in accordance with request made by relative [sister?, aunt?], a physician.

[Edgar Cayce, Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                c/o Aigburth Manor Sanatorium, 
11:25 A. M.                    Towson, Maryland.

1. EC:  Now we find in this body in the physical and the mental and the spiritual forces are to be dealt with in giving the physical conditions, and through these mental, [[Soul|soul]] and spiritual forces must be the applications that would enact or bring the physical equilibrium throughout this [[Physical Body|physical body]]. These physical conditions may be corrected when properly handled, yet the mental suffers and must suffer to reach the physical betterment for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body.

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, for the blood supply, while the system has been well balanced with existing conditions and sedatives for the system to create the forces of nerve action in the system, the strain begins to show especially in the hemoglobin of the blood supply, and this kept in this manner would bring the destructive forces throughout the whole system.

4. THE NERVE SYSTEM, in the nerve system, that is reached especially through the sympathetic, do we find the seat of the trouble, where the entrance of the sympathetic with cerebro-spinal and [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerves enter the brain, and through the medulla oblongata we find the impression as carried through does not coordinate with the impressions received sympathetically through the sensory system.  Hence, the mental of the body must be submerged that the forces in the nerve system may be forced to carry circulation through brain in proper form to create the correct equilibrium and correlation in sensory organism in brain proper to create the correct impressions to the body proper.  In this we find this received this condition by two; yea, three specific conditions happening in the system.  Under outside forces impressions were received.  In [[Physical Body|physical forces]] the action was to repress the cell force as entering in the brain forces proper through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve in the condition when the shock to the nervous system presented improper impressions to this brain, or sensitive forces in the body.  Hence, we have a physical reaction from the sympathetic, cerebro-spinal and the correlating of impressions as carried by the gray forces of nerve tissue proper.

5. TO THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, we will find the body gradually losing the control of correlating movements of the physical, yet without the organic condition being present; rather that of sympathetic conditions existing in the action of muscles governing those organs.  Hence the inability to eat unless it is exercised through some other force, the inability to find those properties that will act direct on nerve centers, even to their reaction as would be found in a normal [[Physical Body|physical body]].

6. R E L I E F - To give then the balance of force, and to prevent the cell in brain force forming a globule, or impression, that would become destructive to brain forces, we would through that of mesmeric forces (not hypnotism) mesmeric forces sub-ordinate the mental forces of this body. While in this condition, with electrical forces as may be applied to the body from the body of another, as heat or friction created, give that application at the first cervical, with those suggestions to create the balance and to correlate all elements in the system, with between times (see this should not be done too often) that of the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], either through neuropathic or the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, to keep the correct impressions to the NORMAL physical, mental forces of the body, [4800].

7. Do that.
