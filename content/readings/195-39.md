# TEXT OF READING 195-39 M 43 (Realtor, Mfgr. of Auto

**Reading [[195-39]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/17/1927  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 752

Accessories) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] E. Evans.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [195], of ... St., ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], and the business investments of this body.  You will answer the questions concerning same as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [195].  It would be well for the body to use that inhalant as has been outlined for others for the condition which exists with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body at the present time.  This will assist the body in overcoming these conditions, clarifying the throat, removing the trouble, and assisting the body in gaining its normal equilibrium, with the other treatments being made for the body.  See?  [Inhalant 538-18?]

3. Ready for questions  [See previous Physical rdg. 195-34.]

4. (Q)  Relative to investment in May Oats, has any change taken place whereby high will not be around Jan. 25th?  If change has taken place, when will high quotation be, and what price?
(A)  As seen by the conditions which have become existent, while the price in same has advanced to some extent, the continual falling off of same has gone even below that as has been given would be as the low point, but we will see a constant, gradual rising of same until it reaches near the 63 to 64, see?  around 25th to the 3rd, see?  We will have the rebound to this - an advance.

5. (Q)  Concerning 20 shares [[Ohio|Cincinnati]] Suburban Common, would it be advantageous to withhold this stock from the pool now being formed to take up at $27.50 per share, or hold for higher price?  How high a price?
(A)  Best price will be gained through the pool.

6. (Q)  How high a price?
(A)  $27.50.

7. (Q)  Relative to interest in the settlement of the Gray estate suit in Evansville, Ind., is the compromise offer going to be made?  When?  When will settlement be forthcoming to heirs?
(A)  Compromise offer going to be made, and best to accept, for outside of same there will be little that will be as a return for same.  Compromise offer will be made.  Needn't to accept the first, but some actions between the two may be taken before the next is offered.  Then accept that, for that would be the best, see?  That will be before the next court convenes there.

8. (Q)  When will this be?
(A)  Not until after the 31st of May.

9. (Q)  Will [[182] [195]] Mfg. Co. be successful in a large way in putting Brakenu (Brake Renew) on the market?  The Lantenschlager preparation for automobile brake linings and for factory belts?  Or would it be a better policy for us to stay out of this?
(A)  There will be a great many complications, if the [[182] [195]] Mfg. Co. would undertake to make or

10. GC:  [Interrupting]  You will speak more slowly.

11. We are through with this.
