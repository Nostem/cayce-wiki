# TEXT OF READING 4431-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4431-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/30/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12281

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 30th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4431].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:50 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will give the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of the body.

2. EC:  Now we find there are some abnormal conditions for this body.  While the body is very good throughout, there are those conditions that produce some abnormal conditions in the system.  These have to do with specific conditions in the distribution of used forces and of supply to organs as they function supplying the nutriment to the plasmatic cell force in the functioning system.

3. Now these are conditions as we find them in the body.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this in quantity and in elements very good.  The pressure, however, above normal.  While this creates a condition that is disturbing in the heart's action, yet not an organic condition of the heart, for, as we will see, produced from a specific condition.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in the plexus about the 4th and 5th dorsal, or 3rd to 6th dorsal, 4th and 5th proper, we find there an impinged nerve plexus, that the branches of plexus do not distribute to the body the nutriment necessary. Hence the condition created in secondary cardiac reaction, which gives pressure in blood supply as this reacts with pneumogastric nerve and hypogastric sympathetically, creating in digestion a pressure, creating high pressure in blood supply, creating an abnormal heart action, creating reflexes to lungs, throat; also disturbing the plexus in 2nd dorsal sufficient to hinder the auditory nerves, deflecting the refused forces, or used forces.  Inaction of the auditory plexus and centers.  Hence the disturbances in the ears, the roaring, thumping, that we have at times.  Through the sympathetic system does this react in auditory forces more than direct.

6. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, in brain forces very good.  One given to be well balanced in all matters pertaining to the mental and spiritual development, for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s development in earth plane has been a very remarkable one.

7. Auditory forces, and all of sensory organism, under the strain as given.

8. The throat and bronchials reflex condition from blood pressure.

9. Lungs show the effect of this disturbance in cardiac, secondary cardiac plexus.

10. Digestive tract only as the reflexes of this subrogated condition in the upper dorsal.

11. The condition as produced in kidneys and in bladder coming partly from reflexes, partly from strain to the body in times back in the lower lumbar plexuses.  Unequal distribution also assisting.

12. Then to bring the normal condition to the body would be through deep manipulation, osteopathically, correct the conditions in the upper dorsal, and keep with the mental forces these developing of the effect of the mental over the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Not taking medicinal properties in the system, but equalizing the circulation, stimulating excretory system and adjusting the subrogated condition in dorsal and in lumbar.

13. Do that.  We will bring the normal conditions for this body.

14. Be persistent, consistent, in all things.
