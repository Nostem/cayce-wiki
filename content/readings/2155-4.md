[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Illinois. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2155] - this we have had before.

2. Now, while the body-physical at times apparently reverts to as serious reaction as at any time, we find that these attacks are not as severe nor as long lasting.

3. There is much better condition in the reactions when near normalcy of the physical functioning does occur, or during those periods; there is much more of the mental's response to the coordinating impulses in the physical.

4. While there are being kept those same combinations and applications that have been given for these corrections, we will find this improvement will continue.

5. While at times those [[Vibration|vibrations]] received through the magnetic reactions apparently have little or no effect, these are changing materially those reactions in the centers from which assimilations are made, and the clarification of the system respecting the type of element in the action of the blood cells themselves.  These we would keep, and be a little more consistent and persistent in the affirmations both for the general condition and for the specific effects that are desired to be created in the system.

6. We would continue the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance.  As these cause at times apparently over-nerve taxations, by passing directly through the system, we would at this period make the attachment to the extremities of the body; that is, attach the first anode to the right wrist and the other to the left ankle; the next day attach to the left wrist and right ankle; keeping this circle about the body, for there will be made a change in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that pass through the system in the circulation.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Are we becoming too reconciled to his condition, instead of taking the stand it is abnormal and to be corrected?
(A)  This would be indicated from that given, in that there should be a little more persistence in the applications, a little more persistence in the affirmations and the systematic treatments of the activities through the magnetic forces, that will bring the greater changes physically and mentally to the body.

9. (Q)  In applying the battery, should this be kept on for the same period of time, when changed to the ankles and wrists?
(A)  From thirty to forty to fifty minutes should be the time.

10. We are through with this reading.
