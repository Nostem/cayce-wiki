# TEXT OF READING 1810-4 F 63

**Reading [[1810-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/10/1942  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 8167

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1810], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC: (Comment while attempting to locate the body - "Quite a large post!")  [GD's note:  I assume he is referring to her [[Book of Job|job]] and living quarters in girls' large dormitory.]

3. Yes, we have the body here, [1810]; this we have had before.

4. As we find, while conditions are improved, there is the tendency - owing to some indiscretions on the part of the body regarding diet and the eliminations - for the reverting of the conditions as related to liver and gall duct, and the circulation in the liver, heart and lungs.

5. In the present, - we would apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs for an hour each day, three days in succession out of each week for three or four series, over the liver and gall duct area.

6. Following each three-day series of the Packs, take internally two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

7. Then the next day have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment that empties the gall duct area, - gently but decisively.

8. The next week follow the same procedure, you see, - and continue in this manner for at least three or four series.

9. Doing these, - with the precautions as to the diet, as to the general activity, - we will keep down this numbness and cause better circulation through the central nervous system, AND the blood supply of a normal reacting between heart, liver and lungs.

10. Do these, if we would bring the better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is Dr. E. M. Stafford, 529 Corcoran St., alright for the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  If he will do this in the manner as was first outlined, or take those instructions from Tucker.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)
