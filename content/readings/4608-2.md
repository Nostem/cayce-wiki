# TEXT OF READING 4608-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4608-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/29/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12512

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 29th day of March, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading                       Demopolis Highway from 
11:00 A. M.                           ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find the conditions are greatly improved throughout this body, some of the lesions as formed or caused through the pelvic regions, that produces the taxation of the nerves and muscles as govern the circulation to the lower extremities, producing the profusion of lymphatic circulation, causing the poor return circulation, or the swelling in the body, is greatly being on the improvement by the effect as produced in the system by the action of properties to overcome this profusion of circulation, yet we find that the distress to these centers often give pain to portions of the body, and were the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as first outlined been given and kept in the perfect accord, we would have entirely removed these conditions.  With the present condition we would add this, though not necessary had other conditions been followed closely - now they would be by manipulation and adjustment either through [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments and the [[Vibration|vibrations]] proper from electric forces of very high frequency without the amperage, see, or the electric treatment to the system as given by Abrams, see.  Follow these, and we will find we will relieve entirely the conditions in this body, Mrs. [4608].  Do that.

2. (Q)  Where can she get this treatment, Abrams' treatment, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Any good [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] or [[Chiropractic|Chiropractor]].  Meridian, Miss., closest here.

3. (Q)  How long will she have to take that treatment?
(A)  Three to four weeks.  She must not leave off the medicinal properties as taken at present.  Have those refilled, or take those after the other treatment, if preferable to the physician in charge.

4. (Q)  How often should she take the treatment, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  If they are electronic treatments, they will be every third.  The electric treatment would be every day.  She may take either.

5. (Q)  Which treatment would be better for her to take?
(A)  The electronic - more expensive though.

6. (Q)  Have you anything more to say about this body, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Do these or either of these as we have given, if we would bring the best conditions to this body.
