# TEXT OF READING 370-8 M 57

**Reading [[370-8]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/3/1941  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2865

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [370], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, conditions are rather serious.  With those exercises that have been indicated, - that is, the use of the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] to reduce the poisons in the system, - there needs to be adjustments made, not chiropractically but OSTEOPATHICALLY, else they would be too severe upon the heart's activity.

4. Give - but only under the suggestions and administration of a physician - those properties that tend to flow the circulation FROM the heart.  This is flooding too often.  The pressure is too high.  Use the digitol [Trade name of a fat free tincture of digitalis.] rather than digitalis, but only under a physician's direction.

5. But DO have the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], and at least every other day the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.

6. And we will find bettered conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What may be done to enable me to sleep better and to gain strength?
(A)  Get rid of the poisons through the manners indicated, and REST.  Take easily assimilated foods.  [[Beef Juice|Beef juice]], sipped - not taken by spoonsful, but take at least three to four minutes to sip a teaspoonful of this, three to four times a day - this will be strengthening.  Also fruits, vegetables raw and cooked, will bring strength and - if the pressure is reduced - will bring the ability to rest, or to lie down.

Keep away from narcotics or sedatives.  These only increase the amount of poisons, do not lessen the pressure nor take the tension from the dilating heart.

Do these.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self (two; one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], Dr. J. H. Newman, 1310 N. 24th St., B'ham.)

---

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/15/41 Letter:
> 
> ..., Ala.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce;
> 
> I have been trying to write you, to sleep, - to get myself
> treated - in fact I have been putting in the time working on
> MYSELF more since I received the reading than I have done of
> anything else.  And still trying to work a little also - in
> order (as is the custom since [[Garden of Eden|Eden]]) to eat a little - us two
> old folks at home.
> 
> As has always been true since I knew about your work, that it
> has at various times dawned upon me, when something incident
> to the work - something that was said - some conclusion - or
> something obvious - I have been able to or allowed to see the
> KINGDOM OF GOD at WORK - the thing that can be known about -
> but can't be described.  It can be illustrated as Christ did
> before his hearers many times - and some of us have to live
> a lifetime to understand what he meant.
> 
> I had summoned all the faith I could in a worried mind & weak
> body to write you the last time - and how relieved I was when
> I received your wire.  I had hardly slept any the night
> before, the day of the engagement and lay down before time -
> thinking I could be awake when the time comes - but I slept
> on through the hour.
> 
> The reading indicates I should REST.  I am doing all of this
> I possibly can.  What I do is not all burdensome (I mean the
> work), I conserve myself all the time.  Do you think it means
> I should do NOTHING at all?
> 
> When I pull through this I want to be something worth saving.
> And I know I have been carrying a lot of dead weight - and I
> can feel it bearing down on me.  But I feel better just now
> as I write and want to be cheerful and not talk about the
> disagreeable.
> 
> I want to tell you about my M.D.  I went down to see him
> Saturday evening.  I hadn't been taking his high priced
> medicine for some weeks or so - partly because I was out of
> it and partly because I could feel no benefit.  He is a busy
> man - always a large waiting crowd to see him at office
> hours - he has done good too that I know of - too a stickler
> for his science & he means to get obedience from his
> patients, but I saw him often with me Saturday while we were
> talking & after he had read the reading from you.  Instead of
> condemning him as I had felt like before - I was glad for the
> change I saw.  For some time ago I took the reading I got in
> Oct. '36 & Jan. '37 [370-4 & 370-5] to leave with him so he
> could get connectected up what I had told him of my history &
> he had expressed a desire to see them.  On my next visit he
> handed me the readings as I sat down at his consultation
> desk.  I asked him what he thought about it then & he tapped
> his head - indicating & saying - he's light up there - but he
> says there is no harm in him.  Now you & I know that this is
> his loss not ours & he is beginning to see it when I returned
> with another reading Sat.  [370-8]  I told him as I gave it
> to him - that he could, (and I rather expected him to) run me
> out but he didn't - he took quite a time to read it & you
> know how short it is - also he read the enclosures about the
> beef & paper.  Then saying what you want me to do.  I asked
> him what did he think he out [ought?] to do to me.  He really
> proceeded to treat me more sincerely & helpfully than he ever
> has.  He TOLD MORE TRUTH.
> 
> But I had pity on his ignorance with all his assumed dignity
> & knowledge - for he asked me when I was at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]
> to get that reading.
> 
> That is what I want - the DIGNITY & POWER that is in the
> abiding spirit - that I may be cleansed to be used in his
> name.  Yes like my life reading [370-3] said he strong &
> fearless like the [[Rome|Roman]] Soldier.
> 
> I must get ready to go to work.  I have seen Mr. Newman [Dr.
> J. H. Newman, D.O.] - the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - a pleasant fellow.
> 
> I didn't sleep last night - but have got 2 or 3 naps today.
> Feel very well just now.
> 
> A world of love to all of you
> 
> Sincerely, [370]
>
