# TEXT OF READING 5473-8 M 56

**Reading [[5473-8]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/10/1930  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 13681

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [547].  This we have had before under another name.  [This was the first time only his initials were used in presenting his name.]

2. In the conditions as we find here, these - we find - while they show an aggravated or an unseemly response to the administrations as have been given - are, in fact, as we find, just showing the RESULT of the ministrations for the condition wherein the body may be PHYSICALLY improved, provided there is not that worriment that brings about a temperature, that would - either through the nerve system, as for a slow brain fever - THAT should be guarded against.

3. In the relaxing of the body, it will be found that when the weak spells come from exhaustion it will be well to give a stimuli for the whole system, either in that of medicants or of that which will alter the circulation, in the usage of the stimuli of alcoholic formations, either in spirits frumenti, wine, or champagne.  These, as we find, will act better with those that are of food values, as milk, egg - or a portion of egg, or a combination of same.  Also relaxing of the body as much by GENTLE manipulation and most by the power of suggestion, when the pains in the neck and shoulders are had or experienced by the body.  These, as we find, are the reflexes from an over-sensitive, over-active body, and the mental and the nerve forces in mental [are] so exhausted as to seek to find expression, or wholly neurasthenia in action in system.  Then most by the power of suggestion as gentle manipulations are given, will aid most in same.

4. In the nausea as ensues from the weakness as comes, anything that may be given as a stimuli - either those of limewater, cinnamon water - equal parts - just a sip - or ammonia, or of any of the hydrates, or half and half of just carbonated water - ALL will aid in relieving this condition.

5. Keep the body [as] quiet as reasonable, but have as much outside, or air, as may be gained in the nominal or normal manner.  These, as we find, the best ministrations for the body.

6. In preventive measures, those of keeping drainages through the system will keep down those tendencies of inflammation in the centers as would cause the disorders.  These may be stimulated most through any of the applications as will increase the alimentary canal eliminations, so that they require an activity of the gastric forces in system, just so they are not allowed - THROUGH the diet - to become acid reactory, see?  DO NOT eat meats, nor such.  RATHER those that are alkalin, see?  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Give the cause and treatment for the dizziness.
(A)  That's why it's told to keep quiet!  for when the body attempts to become active, with the centers disturbed, dizziness is the result of incoordination, and has been seen or given.

8. That is all the questions for this body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Mrs. [310]
"    " Mrs. [4631]
