# TEXT OF READING 2-9 M 49

**Reading [[2-9]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/1/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  In some respects we find the body improved.  Others we find apparently as of irritation and less tendency of the [[Visions|vision]] reaction in system.  These conditions, these activities as appear to come and go may be expected, but the condition is in a whole ON the improve.  We would STILL insist that attention be given to those teeth in the lower portion of the jaw, as show disorders there - especially the stomach teeth and those next to same, on the left side.  These, as we find, one - at least - needs extraction.  Others may be saved by local treatment, or may be ATTEMPTED, at least - and THEN the condition, when these are relieved, may be expected to produce some irritation, but the counter-irritations as may be used at the period will be better for the body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Which tooth should be removed?
(A)  The stomach tooth.

3. (Q)  On which side?
(A)  Left.  That's been given!  [See 2-9, Par. R1.]

4. (Q)  In what order should the fume baths and sweat baths be given?
(A)  The fume baths will be the better for this body, and we would give them in this order.  Do not irritate the body too much by the frequency of same.  The body, necessarily, has to rest a great deal through such treatments.  The food values have to be necessarily considered when these are being given, as to WHAT is being made active in the system.

On Tuesdays, we would give those of the witchhazel bath, see?  fume.

On Wednesdays, those of the olive oil, and rub down afterward with that of the mixture of the Russian oil, alcohol, and the like - see?

On Fridays, (see, we have skipped a day) we would give that of the [[Iodine|iodine]] fume, see?

On Saturdays, that of the salts fume.

Then we needn't to give another until Tuesday.  Give these all fumes.  The one that's rubbed down, you see, is following one - see?

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mr. [2]'s wife
