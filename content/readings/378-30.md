# TEXT OF READING 378-30 M 57

**Reading [[378-30]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/3/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2912

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by daughter, Miss [275], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
12:00 to 12:10 Noon Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will, in the light of Divine guidance, aid in assisting [[Soul|the soul]] of [378], to seek to do that which is best and in keeping with that his Maker would have his [[Soul|soul]] WILL to do at this time.

2. EC:  (In undertone:  "Lot of water about the body this morning!")

3. Let thy inner self, the promptings of that thou knowest, be thy guide; as thou wilt put into practice that which is conscious within thee as to the directions thy Lord and Master would have thee take, so wilt thou be strengthened in Him.

4. For, as the application of that which is conscious in every [[Soul|soul]] is to put into USE, so does the strength and power of the soul grow.

5. As the development in body is kept in accord by the coordination of the factors that manifest themselves in growth, so is THIS growth in the awareness - as the soul uses that of which it becomes aware - making for the growth in the knowledge and power that comes through the USE of that thou knowest to do.

6. Let His Spirit rule thee.  Thou has put thy trust in Him. Be not afraid, for He will bear thee up as ye show forth in thine ways, in the use of that thou knowest, that He will be very near unto thee.

7. Know that He is nigh.  Know that He will direct.

8. Falter not; but keep the faith.

9. We are through.

Copy to [275]
"    "  Ass'n file
"    "  Work Book

> appearing a giant eye; which vanished as suddenly; then a
> bright curved spark for an instant in the place of that (at
> least, that was the impression).
> 
> GC said she thought she saw an arm wave over the body of
> Edgar Cayce; at first thought it was [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]], then thought
> it might have been light on her glasses - trying to reason
> it away.  The thought came to her that it might have been
> Mr. [378] rowing, as much water was indicated about the body
> in the reading.
>
