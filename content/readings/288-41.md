# TEXT OF READING 288-41 F 31

**Reading [[288-41]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1936  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 1932

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [288], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [288]; this we have had before.

2. There are those conditions as we find of rather the acute nature, arising from a congestion in the liver and through portions of the alimentary canal; rather a sort of impaction, from improper eatings.

3. We would not take more of those properties internally that have been taken (Olive Oil); for this tends to make for accumulations without the proper eliminations under the EXISTENT conditions.

4. We would, about two or three o'clock in the afternoon, take two Zilatone Tablets.  At night take two more Zilatone Tablets.

5. Of morning before arising we would take the Fleet's Phospho-[[Baking Soda|Soda]]; a glassful, WARM!  Lie on the right side, with a pillow under same; that we may drain the gall duct.

6. And these should be eliminated.

7. Rest, without too great quantities of food other than liquids.  Soups or milk or limeade or lemonade, or citrus fruit juices; with small quantities of toast, milk, tea, coffee or the like.  These would be the preferable for the eliminations for the next twenty-four hours.

8. These should eliminate the conditions.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What should be done for tiredness, irritability?
(A)  Change the way of thinking, and eliminate the poisons!

11. (Q)  Are pelvic organs functioning properly?
(A)  Very good, save those irritations through poisons - from the unbalanced eatings.

12. (Q)  May anything be done to correct or prevent sleeping with mouth open?
(A)  Close it!

13. (Q)  When asleep, how may this be done?
(A)  Make the suggestions to self as going to sleep.  Get the system balanced.  And this will be done.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
