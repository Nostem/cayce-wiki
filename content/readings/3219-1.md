# TEXT OF READING 3219-1 F 50

**Reading [[3219-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/18/1943  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 10618

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. Benj. Gimbert.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Virginia White, Stenos. Mrs. [3219]''s husband.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3219].

3. As we find, conditions are very serious.

4. There are those continued reactions from the violence of disturbance that has set up within the system itself.

5. These conditions may not be healed in the present, as we find, but may be made easier - and a prolongation of the experience in the present, if such is desired by the body.

6. To be sure, it is necessary to keep up the opiates, to keep down the pain.

7. But we would add these:

8. Each day take internally one-eighth grain of the Animated Ash (emptied dry on tongue and washed down with a little water). Ten minutes after this has been taken, use the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray over the affected areas - only for a minute or a minute and a half, and have the green glass projected between the body and the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] light.  Use only the Mercury Quartz Light, not the Carbon Light, and do not use this without the green glass.  The Light would be about forty-two inches (42") from the body - over the areas affected - and the green glass about fourteen inches (14") from the body - a piece of green plate glass about 10x12" projected between the Light and the body.

9. This will bring ease for the body.

10. First take one-eighth grain of Animated Ash, you see.  Ten minutes later apply the Light.  Do this the same time each day - don't make it one time of morning and another time of evening, but each day at the same time.

11. Also to quiet the nagging pains at times there may be used a very heavy magnet over the areas where the distresses are indicated.  Hold this very close, or upon the flesh of the body - it will, as it were, demagnetize the energies producing the irritation to the body.  Do not use this oftener than twice a day, and then only for a few minutes - three at the most; one minute in the beginning.

12. These, as we find, offer the greater help in the present.

13. (Q)  Can anything be done to relieve the headaches?
(A)  All of these as indicated will tend to relieve the pain.

14. (Q)  Can anything be done for the eyes, for [[Visions|vision]] - she is completely blind.
(A)  Only that to ease the pain, you see.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file

(See explanatory sheet [which was enclosed] regarding the Animated Ash.

An [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Mercury Quartz Lamp may be rented from Powers & Anderson, [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.  A piece of plain green plate glass will do, but if unable to obtain locally it may be ordered from The American Plate & Window Glass Co., Prospect Ave., East Orange, N.J., for $2.50 per square foot.  However, Mrs. Harvey Capps recently ordered a piece of this glass and might have an extra piece - you could call her about it.

If everything is not clear, get in touch with Mae Gimbert Verhoeven and let her help you interpret and apply the information.  GD)
