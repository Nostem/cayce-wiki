# TEXT OF READING 2206-1 M 34

**Reading [[2206-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/10/1940  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 8920

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [1747], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via telegram.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                          St. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] Hosp., 
3:40 to 3:45 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. Youngstown, [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. HLC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Conditions as we find are rather serious.

4. While there may not be a great deal of interference, with the applications that are being administered, we find that suggestions may be made to those administering to the needs of the body as to the proper basis of the disturbance, - which presents the symptoms manifested through congestions in the body, and the leakages or hemorrhages occurring internally.

5. We find that this is due to the lack of ability for coagulation, and a form of anemia which has impoverished the walls of the arterial circulation.

6. Hence it is necessary that this as the basis be considered, in minstering for the needs of the body in the present, - and that something be done to assist in the coagulation.

7. We would suggest the bovine blood, or that injections be made from same, to assist in stopping those conditions through the lungs and through the soft tissue of throat that are giving so much trouble, as well as the effects from these of the nausea and temperature.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What is cause of this condition?
(A)  Read what has just been indicated!  It is the lack of the ability for coagulation, by the thinning or the impoverishment of the walls of the arterial circulation.

11. (Q)  Is there anything else you would suggest?
(A)  This is the basis from which the reasoning should be given; for necessarily this must be considered and administered by those who are ministering to the needs of the body under the present existing conditions.

Do that.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [1747] - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file Extra copy to Miss [1747] 2/18/41, by MV

[Handwritten note:  Didn't send - EC didn't want it wired.]

WESTERN UNION - 5/10/40

Miss [1747]:

INABILITY FOR COAGULATION  ALSO FORM OF ANEMIA  THINNING WALLS OF ARTERIAL CIRCULATION  CAUSING INTERNAL HEMORRHAGES SUGGEST BOVINE INJECTIONS TO ASSIST COAGULATION AND PREVENT CONGESTION  NAUSEA TEMPERATURE.

Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc. Day Letter - Collect
