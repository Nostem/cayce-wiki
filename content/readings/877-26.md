# TEXT OF READING 877-26 M 46 (Corporation Lawyer, Protestant)

**Reading [[877-26]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/23/1938  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 5168

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Virginia, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of May, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [877], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Earthly existence in [[Poseidia|Poseidia]], [[Atlantis|Atlantis]], during first 
destructive influences used by the [[Sons of Belial|sons of Belial]]; when the 
influences of the sun were crystallized through [[Crystal (Atlantean)|the crystal]] 
that then controlled the motivative forces in the 
experience.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now called or known as [877]; those experiences in that [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth environment in what is called [[Poseidia|Poseidia]], in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]].

2. In giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, it is well that there be given something of the background - or the variations; that there is not confusion.

3. For in giving the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]]'s [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in a period as remote as the early destructive influences in that land called [[Atlantis|Atlantis]], or in [[Poseidia|Poseidia]], there is oft a confusion in the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] - as to whether Poseidia was the land or Atlantis was the land.

4. There were also other centers that were developing.  For in the projections they began as many, and in creating influences they began as five - or in those centers where crystallization or projection had taken on such form as to become what was called man.

5. Hardly could it be said that they were in the exact form as in the present.  For there were more of the influences that might be used when necessary; such as arms or limbs or feet or whatnot.

6. So, in following or interpreting the Poseidian period - or in Atlantis - let it be understood that this was only ONE of the groups; and the highest or the greater advancement in the earthly sojourning of individual [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] or [[Soul|souls]] at that particular period - or the highest that had been save that which had been a part of the [[Lemuria|Lemurian]] age.

7. Hence we find there had been the separating into groups (as we would call them) for this or that phase of activity; and those that were against that MANNER of development.

8. The [[Sons of Belial|Sons of Belial]] were of one group, or those that sought more the gratifying, the satisfying, the use of material things for self, WITHOUT thought or consideration as to the sources of such nor the hardships in the experiences of others.  Or, in other words, as we would term it today, they were those without a standard of morality.

9. The other group - those who followed [[Law of One|the Law of One]] - had a standard.  The [[Sons of Belial|Sons of Belial]] had no standard, save of self, self-aggrandizement.

10. Those [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] that were then the producers (as we would term today), or the laborers, the farmers or the artisans, or those who were in the positions of what we would call in the present just machines, were those that were projections of the individual activity of the group.

11. And it was over these then, and the relationships that they bore to those that were in authority, that the differences arose.

12. Then we find the entity, now known as or called [877], was among the [[Sons of the Law of One|children of the Law of One]]; entering through the natural sources that had been considered in the period as the means of establishing a family.  However, they were rather as a group than as an individual family.

13. For those who were of the ruling forces were able by [[Free Will|choice]] to create or bring about, or make the channel for the entrance or the projection of an entity or [[Soul|soul]], as the period of necessity arose.

14. Then such were not as households or as families, like we have today, but rather as groups.

15. Their STANDARD was that [[Soul|the soul]] was given by the Creator or entered from outside sources INTO the projection of the MENTAL and spiritual self at the given periods.  THAT was the standard of [[Law of One|the Law of One]], but was REJECTED by the Sons of Belial.

16. But this entity, [877], is the one whom we are to follow in the present; as for its application of those innate tenets, that are a portion of the aroma of the innate being, as it were, or the influence that is as the rate of [[Vibration|vibratory]] force of the entity in its relationships to the universal activity or [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces.

17. These are the abilities to which the entity may attune self, through all the various phases of its application in those directions in other experiences.

18. But that was among the first entrances, or the second entrance of the entity FROM the without, into that form which became encased as an entity, an INDIVIDUAL body, see?

19. The name then, as we would term in the present, was Deui (pronounced Dar, or D-R); and the entity was active in the recording of the messages, the directing of those forces that came with the use of the light that formed [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] upon which the influence from without was crystallized into what would become as the sound from the outer realm to the static or individual realm.

20. These were not only [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] from the sun, set by the facets of the stones as crystallized from the heat from within the elements of the earth itself, but were as the combination of these.

21. For it was these gases, these influences that were used for what we call today the conveniences as for light, heat, motivative forces; or radial activity, electrical combinations; the motivative forces of steam, gas and the like for the conveniences.

22. Then this entity, Deui, was among those who attempted to make such influences a part of the experience of those who were - as indicated - the producers of that used for food, clothing; for the MACHINES as it were for the producing of these - as we would call them today; rather than the machines used for the sources of the correlating or centralizing or crystallizing of the activity in their very forms.

23. The use of these influences by the Sons of Belial brought, then, the first of the [[Earth Changes|upheavals]]; or the turning of the etheric rays' influence FROM the Sun - as used by the [[Sons of the Law of One|Sons of the Law of One]] - into the facet for the activities of same - produced what we would call a volcanic upheaval; and the separating of the land into SEVERAL islands - five in number.

24. Poseidia, the place or the settlement of that particular sojourning of the entity - Deui - at the time, then became ONE of these islands.

25. Hence the confusion that at times is seen by those who would interpret such records.

26. As to the application of that the entity experienced, and the urges received - or that are applicable in the present, we find:

27. The entity knows innately the RELATIONSHIP of the soul - or [[First Cause|the first cause]] of self - with infinity; the relationship of infinity to each entity; and that there is no respecter of persons except as to how and why each entity, as a spark of infinity, magnifies or uses its relationship NOT for self-indulgence or gratification but for the GLORY of that which is the source of light, of all that pertains to light. For every spark of light, whether in the spiritual, the mental or the material sense, must have its inception in infinity.

28. Ready for questions.

29. (Q)  Was there any relationship in that [[Incarnation|incarnation]] with my present wife, [920]?
(A)  Because of the very relationship, and for the characterization of same, there is the necessity that there be the comparison - rather than the drawing from the individual.

30. (Q)  With my friend, [1301]?
(A)  The same would be applicable here.

As has just been indicated, ALL who were of [[Law of One|the Law of One]] were associated, see?

If they were of that group, then they were associated. Draw the comparisons, you see, from the information given for each.

31. (Q)  With my friend, Edgar Cayce.
(A)  Just as indicated.  These relationships are NOT to be drawn from those experiences that were merely two or three [[Millennium|thousand years]] back, but remember - this sojourn was nearer to fifty or five hundred [[Millennium|thousand years]] before we even have the beginning of [[Law of Moses|the LAW]] as the Law of One manifested!

32. (Q)  What was the fundamental basic principle of metaphysics and religion?
(A)  As has been indicated.

33. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
