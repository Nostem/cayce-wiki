# TEXT OF READING 2728-1 F 45

**Reading [[2728-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/15/1926  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 9855

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2728], via Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], who talked to her husband, a train conductor, about Edgar Cayce, on his recent visit to Va. Beach.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2728].]

Time of Reading 
2:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give a physical and mental reading on this body.  You will examine it thoroughly, tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find the body very good in many respects, both physically and mentally.  There are those physical conditions in the body that give some distress to the body at times, and these may be corrected.

3. Now, these conditions have to do with certain eliminations and that as causes the deflection in the manner of elimination, and the effects as produced in various portions of the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

4. Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [2728] we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find very good in its elements and constituents - the quantity is full and sufficient in the body.  At times, on account of the eliminations being disturbed, there is produced that in the pressure that causes distress to the body, for it creates a high blood pressure.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, the body is very good.  One of strong nerve in every way and manner, yet there is that pressure produced in portions of the system that gives the distress at times to the nerves, through the sympathetic system.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, brain force very good.

8. Organs of the sensory system show the deflections at times, as is produced by the pressure in circulation.  Hence that condition as is shown in the organs of sensory system, especially in the ear, as produces more of the disturbance in roaring or ringing, rather than that of an auditory depression in hearing.  The eyes show the same effect, for at times there appears rather as film, skim, or something in front, rather than the deflection of the operation of [[Visions|vision]], see?  This produced by the over pressure, as we shall see, in other portions of the system, throwing too much to this portion of the body and not eliminating used forces, for in the functioning of each organ, as the force of [[Visions|vision]], of hearing, of speech, of action of any portion of system, used forces as drosses must be eliminated.  Hence, each portion of the body in its own particular cell force must rebuild that replenishing for the functioning of each organ.

9. Throat, bronchials, lungs, larynx, very good.  The fullness as is seen in the lungs, produced rather from this pressure, as the body finds through overtaxation in any over activity produces heaviness and quick breath; yet NOT an organic condition.  Rather that of too much circulation for the whole system and the pressure produced by not keeping the equilibrium in circulation.

10. The digestive system very good.  The liver and the digestive system in the intestinal tract show how same is under the strain of the eliminations being hindered in the system, though not an organic, merely the functional condition in the body.  The greater pressure in the system produced in the hypogastric plexus, especially those as affect the kidneys in their functioning, and the organs of the pelvis in their functioning, these show how the pressure on nerve, the pressure in the body, shows the poor eliminations through this portion of the system, and the pressure as is produced gives that deflection in circulation to the lower portion of the body, especially in that of the locomotaries, or that of the lower portion of the body in the lower limbs, producing that pressure that gives distress in the limbs and joints of limbs at times.  These, as we see, then, is that of the deflected circulation, and this bearing on the portion of body through which some eliminations, and the greater portion of drosses, must be eliminated, for the whole lymphatic and [capillary] system, and those of that of the emunctories, are involved in the condition.  Not fatty portions about kidneys; not a strain in the capsule of some, or in this portion of the body; yet a PRESSURE produced in the hypogastric plexus that causes the deflection, produced by an unequalized condition existent in the body.

11. Then, to overcome this, we would apply to the body those properties as are found in the Radio-Active Appliance, to which there would be carried into the system those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], alternated with the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Chloride]]; that is, with that anode from which the application of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] would be taken into system one day, the next alternation would be (or next day) that of the Chloride Gold, see?  using the ankle as the positive and the large plate to the umbilicus as the negative, and these should be applied at least an hour each day, see?  Do that for the physical conditions in the body.  For that in the cervical, which produces the pressure to the sensory system, with the equalization and the perfect equilibrium kept in the circulation, in the nerve and in the blood supply, those of a specific exercise of head and neck, taken by the body just before retiring.  Rotary motion to head and neck - three times to left, three times to right - three times to front - three times to back - circular twice each way.  Relax - the sleep will be better, see?

12. As for the mental conditions in this body, sympathetic, motherly in every manner towards all.  One that gives much to others in its expression of self and gains much by same, finding the greater joy, peace, in the life in service to others.  The attitude of the individual, as applied in self, has and does do much toward keeping the body in the near physically fit condition as it has.

13. Keep, then, that attitude in the mental, and apply those conditions as have been given for the physical, for with a more perfect equalized system, the abilities of self expression will be broadened and assisted.

14. Do that.

15. We are through for the present.
