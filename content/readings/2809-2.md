# TEXT OF READING 2809-2 M 56

**Reading [[2809-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/10/1943  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 9999

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [635], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  What is the condition in throat, and what should be done for same?

2. EC:  This, as we find, shows congestion as well as the combined effects of a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance there.

3. We would apply warm packs of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Change these at least every hour, until there is some relief.

4. And do have corrections made in the cervical and upper dorsals to relieve the pressure.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife, Air Mail - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

APPLY WARM SEVERAL THICKNESSES GAUZE SATURATED IN [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCO-THYMOLINE]]  CHANGE EVERY HOUR UNTIL RELIEVED  HAVE [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] CORRECTIONS IN CERVICAL AND UPPER DORSAL TO RELIEVE PRESSURE.
