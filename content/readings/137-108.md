# TEXT OF READING 137-108 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-108]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/4/1928  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 498

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
7:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had before.

2. The [[Dreams|dreams]], [[Visions|visions]], impressions, as come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] come through those channels as have been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and are to be applied in the way and manner as has been given concerning same; for in application and in activity that better understanding is gained by each and every individual that would accept through those channels the impressions as received - those truths and lessons and understandings that come from day to day.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Night of June 2nd or morning of 3rd.  I was drawing lines on the sidewalk.  It seemed a combination of Sears-Roebuck and Gimbel.  Had the impression that it was high enough now.
(A)  This, as presented, shows that until there comes changes in these variations - which are to occur - that these are well to be rid of in the high mark in today - tomorrow - trading, see?

4. (Q)  Does it mean either of these, or both?
(A)  Both of these.

5. (Q)  What is the "high enough"?
(A)  The high point or pitch in today or tomorrow trading, as will be seen and indicated, and as has been indicated to the body already.  Just as is seen in that as given concerning the other various holdings of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that were not distinct.  These have been presented again in a more definite way and manner; for, as seen, these are to vary - such as that of Fox, which is near high - Tuesday or Wednesday seeing the high points for the present rise.  That in Wabash, Missouri [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]], these are again in combines, and will go higher - Missouri [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]].  Wabash - well that these be gradually disposed of so there is a profit in hand, as seen with these various others; for liquidations MUST come about - and as these various ones are presented in the definite way and manner, as were those of the two in which the marks were seen, then act in that way AT the time these were presented; for, as has been given concerning these conditions and these impressions and activities, these must come direct to self and be acted on by self at THAT time when THAT activity is given or directed DIRECTLY to the body.  Do that.

6. We are through.
