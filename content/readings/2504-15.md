# TEXT OF READING 2504-15 M 64

**Reading [[2504-15]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/1/1930  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9428

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, and Mrs. Wigal.  Also L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2504] - this we have had before.  In the general way and manner the physical conditions are still on the improve.  It will require only time, precautions as regarding the whole of the system, and the keeping up of eliminations - both as related to that of the regular eliminations through the alimentary canal, as well as drainages in the lymphatic circulation.  In the emunctory centers, where there are tendencies for fullness, these - as we find - will be well to massage with a weakened solution of [[Iodine|iodine]] and aconite, that the circulation - as it takes up same in such conditions - may eliminate through their nominal or normal channels.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What is cause and treatment for swelling in left hand?
(A)  Poor circulation from those centers in the brachial center, as related to those of the emunctories of the arm and other portion of same.  Well to massage from the centers from which these radial flexus come.

3. (Q)  What treatment for recently developed hernia?
(A)  That of the strengthening of the walls of the system as related to those conditions as exist.  Keep same bound, to be sure.

4. (Q)  What treatment for numbness?
(A)  This is the circulation, but it's gradually being aided by those of the manipulations as increase circulation from the centers from which these radial forces come.

5. (Q)  What is condition of teeth?
(A)  These need local attention as soon as it may be done.

6. (Q)  Should Ipsab be used?
(A)  This is good, and will assist in cleansing and strengthening the conditions in this portion of system.

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self
