## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/12/43 Letter from Grover C. Kemp - see in EC's personal
> folder:  "I just can't remember [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] nor any of your
> family I met the day we went to [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], you giving the
> reading on the negro boy, who stole the gold and lost a 20
> dollar gold piece down by the old spring.  It now seems such
> a short time we were together in Bowling Green...
> 
> "Remember the quail hunt where you located the old mine, and
> we had the big fried ham dinner, cooked by the fat sisters
> around 300 lbs. each, and our bird dog eating all the quails
> we killed..."
> 
> R2. 8/18/43 EC's letter to Mr. Kemp:  "Yes, I recall the quail
> hunt.  Do you know that the lady whose farm we were on that
> day is still living?  She is over a hundred years old.  That
> is one of those crazy things which came out of the readings.
> Last summer when I was there, I saw her grandson and he told
> me he had never known her to be as strong as now."
