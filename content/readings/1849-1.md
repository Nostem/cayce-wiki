# TEXT OF READING 1849-1 F 37 (Owner of Personal Therapy Salon)

**Reading [[1849-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/24/1939  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 8231

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1849], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. Thomas Garrett.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1849] ..., [[New York City|New York City]], who seeks information, advice and guidance as to her health, and also as to how to put her Personal Therapy Salon on a paying basis.  You will give her any information which may be helpful at this time, answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  [1849].

3. We have the body, the enquiring mind, [1849].

4. As to the physical health, - here we find there needs be only those precautions that are so well known by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; especially as to eliminations and assimilations.  And for the body, know that the vegetable forces will work better than the mineral forces in aiding to keep a balance in these directions.

5. And if these are kept, and the mental attitude in attune with constructive forces, we should find little disturbance in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

6. In giving advice or counsel respecting making the Personal Therapy Salon a success, - this, to be sure, is necessary if the body is to be able to serve in a manner commensurate with the [[Ideal|ideals]] and the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which this has been established.

7. There needs be those contacts with those individuals who are in the position to refer individuals who will need the service that may be administered through the offices of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

8. These as we find are to be had through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] in which there is the regular activity, or the advertising or making aware.  And for this special activity it is not necessary that there be advertising in the form of other than personal service, personal word; and that CHARACTER of such as may be had through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] for such.

9. But more in those who are in the professional service, and who are able to aid one another in personal service, - these are [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may gain the better opportunity for the working out of those things which will be and are necessary, to keep such an undertaking in the proper sphere of activity, - as well as to serve those who come under the supervision of the entity.

10. Those, then, that are of kindred thought; those that are of that activity in which such may be brought about, and these are done the better, the greater by personal contacts - or of a personal service.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Which of the publicity people shall I use?  Dr. Xern? Ruth Raphel?  Ellen Rose Dirkey?
(A)  Ruth Raphel.

13. (Q)  Or is there another that would be better than either of these?
(A)  Use this channel as the regular routine; and then make the contacts as indicated by the special service or special individuals of the profession who USE such as this service that may be given here.

14. (Q)  Is there any particular association I should make to aid me in my business at this time?
(A)  As indicated, those of the medical OR the kindred profession; the psychiatrist, the analyst, the hypnotist, the hydrotherapist, the students of every science pertaining to metaphysics.

And it would be very well if the entity would cultivate Dr. [1301].

15. (Q)  Do you refer to Dr. [(first name) [1301]]?
(A)  Dr. [(first name) [1301]].

The clientele through this association may become most helpful.

16. (Q)  Any other advice at this time?
(A)  Do these as we find; and we WILL bring the better influences and forces about this body.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file Extra copy to self, 4/4/39
