# TEXT OF READING 507-2

**Reading [[507-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/6/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 3633

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Ernest W. Zentgraf, 400 St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]''s Ave., Stapleton, Staten Island, [[New York City|New York]], this 6th day of February, 1934, in accordance with request made by those present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Ellen Horrmann, Helene, Ernest, Margret and Lilian Zentgraf.]

Time of Reading 
6:30 to 6:45 P. M.

1. HLC:  You will have before you the work of the Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., members of which are present in this room.  You will answer the questions which we will ask.

2. EC:  Yes.  Questions.

3. (Q)  What should [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]]'s report be on the sitting with Mrs. Garrett on Feb. 3rd?  [See 507-1 and 507-1 Reports.]
(A)  State those conditions that were experienced by those that acted in the capacity of the investigators as concerning that understood by such researchers in respect to same; honestly, fairly, to the best of the ability of all.

4. (Q)  What is Uvani's plane of development?
(A)  We will call him and see.  Yes.  That there may be a comprehensible presentation of what is meant by planes of development (for in most every group that has approached such subjects, PLANES are rarely understood), they are accounted as to the first, second, third, fourth, fifth plane; yet we find THESE only in the beginnings of the activities of those influences of the [[Soul|souls]] of men.

Uvani, then, is in the fifth plane; yet a man, a personality.

In seeking then, my son, study that given by Samuel as he expressed to the students in Shiloh, that when seeking let no plane enter into thine development save the plane opened by the Father for thine knowledge.  For, He has given His co- workers the activities that are necessary for those who will open their inner selves that the light of God Himself may shine into the hearts and [[Soul|souls]] of men.  For, they that seek rather for PLANES may not rise higher than those planes, unless quickened.  And what has been thine knowledge?  What has been thine understanding?  What has been thy direction? Thou mayest not be called lest God QUICKENS [[Soul|the soul]], lest He opens that thou mayest become one with Him.  Show thy activities, then, by the love He has given.  Not that such in the planes of experience are damned, but rather did HE not Himself enter hell that ALL might hear his VOICE?  They, then, that climb up some other way would make their homes, their heavens, their planes, where they choose to dwell. What, my son, is the light thereof?

5. (Q)  What is the source of the [[Visions|vision]] of the [[Persia|Persian]], [[Greece|Grecian]], [[Egypt|Egyptian]] entity that Uvani said could help and save the strength of Edgar Cayce?
(A)  On the same plane.

6. (Q)  Please explain why Uvani received the impression that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] should help?
(A)  Desire.  Companionship.

7. (Q)  What is Uvani's mission in speaking through a medium, and to whom?
(A)  That where he is others may be also.  WHAT is the mission of any teacher, minister or active force but that they may either be benefitted by or a benefit to induce those to become as one of them?  In any sphere!  This, my son, belittles not those that in all of their seeking cry aloud, yet has it not been given thee that thou shouldest come to the knowledge and the understanding and preach even, and teach even, and minister even, even to those that are still within those veils of activities?  but only through the power in the name of Him who entered same that He might save all!

8. (Q)  Is [[Crystal (Atlantean)|the crystal]] ball sent Edgar Cayce from [[India|India]] really carrying a curse, as Mrs. Garrett said?
(A)  Only that that may be accredited as for one [[Healing|laying on of hands]], or that may be blessed or may be cursed by those - and only very low may see same.  May not a curse be turned into a blessing, if it is taken in the name of the Father and the Son?  Only such may remove same in the eyes of those that are still under the cloud, but pass ye rather into the light; KNOW the blessings of the face of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] as He would come into thine experience and cast away all doubt and fear; for He is nigh unto this house.  KNOW His name as none other, for in His name alone as no other given among men whereby man may be saved - in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] - [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Spirit that came in the lowly [[Nazareth|Nazarene]], in the man called Jesus!

9. We are through.

Copy to Helene Zentgraf
"    " [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]]
"    " Ass'n file

> The world of parapsychology and psychic investigation has

> responsible for securing funds for extensive research in the
