# TEXT OF READING 137-26 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-26]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/3/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 416

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 3rd day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made through Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading as given this morning, November 3, 1925, on interpretation of [[Dreams|dreams]] of [137], especially the last paragraph of same, in which there is reference made to combinations being formed regarding future prices on L & N and Southern Railroad Stock.  In this there is given, "These may not be given through these channels as yet."  Will you please tell us just why, and what is the difference where others have obtained information and under the present circumstances.  Are [900], [137], and Edgar Cayce the ones referred to in this connection?  How may they act or conduct themselves, so as to be able to obtain this information, as under the present circumstances and conditions, as are seen from the physical viewpoint, it would be very beneficial to these to have this information.

2. EC:  For the very reason that same would be very beneficial, individually, to those as given, and the information as yet pertains to those individuals forming such a combine as regarding the sale or price of same, see?  Keep these separate.  The conditions then are in the making of man's realm as yet, see?  That which belongs to one individual is not the property of another, see?  When the combine has been completed, and the lives, the actions, the relations of either one of the three are such as to maintain that condition in which information regarding same (completed) is necessary, same will be presented to one or the other; especially through [137].  For, as has been given, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] may, through his own channel [that is, by the ability to enter into the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces] obtain from the cosmic and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] consciousnesses those conditions necessary to guide and direct those channels in which the physical may be financially benefited by same.  What relates to one relates to the other, in their different spheres and manners of manifesting same, see?  Then, for this reason, not wholly made - combine, see?  The property of another until combined, ready to be acted upon in an open market, see?  The necessity of each individual living the life necessary to obtain such information will be given when needed.

3. We are through with this reading.
