# TEXT OF READING 3291-2 F 40

**Reading [[3291-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/29/1944  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 10718

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3291], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mrs. [3291].]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3291]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there have been changes since we had the body here before.  We find that there have been periods of real improvements.  Then there have been periods when apparently even worse reactions have been experienced than the first.

4. In the present there is an abating somewhat of the disturbances, yet the coordinations in eliminations have not yet been set up properly.

5. While the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments have been very well, they have not at all times been consistent.  This is not a condition in which the treatments may be hurried through with too quickly.  For each center where there is the coordinating of the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous system should receive special attention.  These are the lumbar axis, the 9th dorsal, the 6th and 7th dorsal, the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dorsal and through the cervical area - where there are the direct associations.  Just one or two adjustments, and not relaxing the patches of the sympathetic and the emunctory systems - unless these are done properly, there is not the proper adjustments given osteopathically.

6. With the giving of the second series of the adjustments, there should have been the purifying again of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces with the taking of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, with the setting up of better eliminations by the oils.

7. If these are done properly they will bring permanent relief for this body of these disturbances.

8. These we would again do.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What causes misery in lower part of back, around base of spine, and how may this be overcome?
(A)  As just indicated, these are areas where the lumbar-sacral combine in their coordination of impulses from the nervous system, or incoordination of the nervous systems towards the activity of circulation.  With the pressures there is brought the bearing down or heaviness or aching through the areas.  The correction of these should bring better conditions and reliefs.

11. (Q)  Will wearing girdle and support relieve it?
(A)  This will aid but it will not relieve of itself.

12. (Q)  Why have I had a slight pain in region of liver since the first [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  There are the congestions that need the application of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs and the taking of the oils to bring better eliminations, better activity through the liver and gall duct area.

13. (Q)  What foods should be avoided in order to prevent this disturbance?
(A)  Fats and any great amounts of starches.

14. (Q)  Why do I invariably have swelling of eye and lips, and a great deal of itching rash, after this pain leaves?
(A)  This is the incoordination between the circulation of the liver and kidneys, and is because of pressures upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the nerve system from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] to the pineal, and those activities to the organs of the sensory system. Hence, as indicated, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments cannot be made in a hurry.  There is not required merely the adjustments, but that the osteopath do a little more work.

15. (Q)  Why am I unable to become pregnant?
(A)  These activities will come about when there has been a coordination between the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal reactions.  This is not impossible or impractical, but there must be a coordinating with the impulses that arise with the contact bringing about such possibilities and probabilities.

16. (Q)  What is the reason for the small black spot in front of left eyesight constantly, that moves as the eye moves, and sometimes also in right eye?
(A)  This is from impaired circulation.  This is not other than that which will be eliminated with the proper adjustments.

17. (Q)  Are tonsils infected, particularly right side?
(A)  Not particularly.

18. (Q)  What causes stiffness and pain in joints of hands, particularly forefinger of right hand?
(A)  Poor circulation.  Read what has been indicated as to how to set up these coordinations and not leave portions of the body-eliminations to be carried off in other than their proper channels.

Do as outlined and we will make better conditions for the body.

19. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one copy for Dr. Irvin) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(See receipt [which was enclosed] for the ten dollars.

See letter [which was enclosed] regarding information to be sought in Life Reading, which you wished returned.  Your life reading appointment we are making for December 12th, 3:30 P.M.

Under separate cover, we are sending you the pamphlet regarding Life Readings.)
