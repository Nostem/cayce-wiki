# TEXT OF READING 5438-2 M 37

**Reading [[5438-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/24/1930  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 13603

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker, nurse, [5438], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [5438].  Now, we find conditions have been, and are, on the improve regarding the whole [[Physical Body|physical forces]] or functioning of same.  While there had apparently been those periods when the progress [was] not as fast as had been seen in the first, yet these are more from the depressions produced from worry, or non-activity of the physical, and mental.  It won't hurt the body if it would go out and do some work now!  Do some exercise that will make the body physically tired!  and then the reactions through all the applications for the system would be better! If it wasn't anything but moving bricks - toting them from one side of the road to the other!  Keep the body mentally - physically - active!  Carry them over and bring them back! Anything!  Active forces, physically and mentally, to TIRE the PHYSICAL ENERGIES of the body - just so the body would KEEP MENTALLY, that the activities - whatever they be, that are necessary - "I WILL and must accomplish."  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are all treatments being given properly?  Should all be continued?
(A)  These are very good!  Should be kept up!

3. That is all the questions regarding this body.

4. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] Extra copy for self to send home
