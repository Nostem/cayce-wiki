# TEXT OF READING 294-66 M 49 (Psychic Diagnostician,

**Reading [[294-66]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/2/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2025

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., on this 2nd day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195].]

Time of Reading 
2:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC, present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, EC.  This we have had here before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]] that are presented to this body are those correlations of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in that way and manner as has been given, and should be used by entity for the development and better UNDERSTANDING of the conditions presented, physically, from time to time.

4. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

5. (Q)  Monday Morning, 3/1/26, "Regarding bookstore, Mr. Hopper, Raymond, the discussion about the money, Mr. Kitchen, the change etc."  You will recall this dream, give the interpretation and lesson to be gained.
(A)  The dream is as this:  There is seen William Hopper and Kitchen - Dixon Kitchen. [GD's note:  It seems I remember EC referring to a Dick Kitchen - perhaps the spelling is Dickson Kitchen?]  These two are cleaning grass from a plot.  When questioning someone regarding same, the reply is, they, Kitchen and Hopper, are to open bookstore in the place, which has been allowed to grow up in grass and weeds, with both being away.  And later, when seen again in store, all the affection for the individuals is shown in physical manifestations and manners.

Again there is seen, on the street, Kitchen, and question asked regarding paper for walls.  Then the reasoning as to why this individual is questioned or considered when the affection is shown for the other individual connected with the firm.

Again there is the discussion regarding change made, and the numbers of pieces of money, and the return to the store, the discussion regarding amount paid, and the amount of the purchase, and change.  These are finally corrected.

This, as we see, presents to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those conditions held in the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], as early training lessons.  It is as seen, that by neglect this is allowed to grow up in weeds and grass, and the cleansing of same is as the lesson that is necessary for the entity to gain the better understanding of the conditions as relate to the phenomena of life.

The meeting of the individual, the reasoning as to self concerning the addressing of the individual rather than that one for whom affection is felt and shown, is as but the mental forces reasoning with those trainings, those lessons, as were gained in the youth.

Again there is presented in the monies, the change, the amounts of monies, correction of same, the return to the individual and the answers as are shown, how that these lessons as have been given for the entity are the ones through which the efforts of entity are judged.

That in the spirituality of the entity are the forces of the work attempted judged, as to the masses.  That is, the masses, the peoples, judge the phenomena manifested according to the spirituality of the individual as expressed in individual life.  LEARN the lesson, for it is well.

6. We are through for the present.
