# TEXT OF READING 378-3 M 53 (Executive, Protestant)

**Reading [[378-3]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/4/1931  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 2885

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [378], through Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [378] and Mrs. [255].]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [378], present in this room, and his personal associates and associations, especially his 800 Acre tract located in Broome County at Oquaga Lake near Deposit, N.Y.  You will answer the questions regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [378], present in this room - this we have had before.  Also these associations and relations, especially as respecting the holdings, the tract, the grounds.  [459-3 on 1/15/31?]

3. As we find, with the present conditions, surroundings, circumstances, this may be turned into a channel to be made very much worth while to many, as well as an outlet for those endeavors of self and associates.  This, as  we find, would be a wonderful place for a camp, or camps, and those associations or affiliations in part would make for an opportunity for many.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  How and when can this property be sold to best advantage?
(A)  It may be sold to best advantage for the next season, not the present - but IN the present for preparations for the coming or next season, see?  Interest those who have been considering the taking, or the construction of such an outlet.  This would also make for an opportunity for the developing of such in this district; for - as the CONDITIONS as surround same would make for such an outlet - see?

5. (Q)  What is this property BEST suited for?
(A)  As we find, it would be BEST suited for such as this - see?  See, there would be both for the children's camps - boys AND girls - then the lay of the land, for that of those of the FAMILIES - which would make for a CLUB or as for a place where these would be, or would be from time to time, in closer association WITH the activities than has been set in many such a place - see?  This, then, would leave sufficient of the lands, the holdings, the surroundings, for other developments, or the upkeeps of same - see?

6. (Q)  Who shall be consulted in regard to carrying out the suggested plans?
(A)  There are those, as we have given - or have seen - as [257], [459], and associates with same - see?  [459-3 on 1/15/31?]

7. (Q)  What organization in [[New York City|New York City]] could be interested in a property of this kind either for amusement or convalescence?
(A)  As we find, this channel would be the better opportunity, see, for the developing of such a place.

8. (Q)  What kind of camps should they be - Christian or Jewish?
(A)  Would THESE [If these would be] be followed, as we have SUGGESTED here - we would find there would be a mixed group, though of course tending to the Jewish the more.

9. (Q)  How can the golf course be best disposed of?
(A)  As for the club, for the FAMILIES of those who are in camps - see?  This MAKES and OPPORTUNITY for a larger undertaking in this way and manner, see?

10. (Q)  Should the club and camps be under the same management?
(A)  To be sure, they should!

11. (Q)  Any further advice regarding this which would be of benefit?
(A)  This, as we find, would be an opportunity for not only disposing of, but putting same to a use that would be beneficial to many in many directions.

12. (Q)  Where should this be started, in [[New York City|New York]] or Binghamton?
(A)  In New York, for you see these as have been given are seeking such a location - see?  and the associates with these.  This could be made a BEAUTIFUL, an excellent site!

13. We are through for the present.
