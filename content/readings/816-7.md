# TEXT OF READING 816-7 M 53

**Reading [[816-7]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/15/1936  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 4911

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of September, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [816], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [816]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, in many respects the body-physical is very good. There are still those tendencies for toxic forces to be produced by the poor eliminations through alimentary canal. This as we find, owing to pressures in the upper portion of the transverse and descending colon, produces a disturbance in the nervous system that causes disturbances to the circulatory forces as related to the metabolism of the system.

3. If there would be those precautions about the diet, not eating too fast, not overburdening the body, keeping the Radio-Active Appliance for equalizing the circulation, making for the eliminations by the use of enemas rather than cathartics, we find that we would make much better conditions for the body; and these would produce better reactions for the system.

4. When preparing the enema (and do not make same warmer than the temperature of the body), to each quart of the water add a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and a heaping teaspoonful of salt.  Dissolve this thoroughly, to be sure, before it is used.

5. In the last of the waters - that is, after the evacuations have been complete - put to the quart of water two teaspoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] for an antiseptic and for the relieving of any strain or any reactions that might produce irritation.

6. These would be taken when necessary, whether it is every day or every other day.  Be sure that there is a regular time for eliminations and if there is not the evacuation then use the enema until this is produced, or cleanse the lower portion.

7. Use the Radio-Active Appliance each day, or those days or periods when the body is extra tired or when there are irritations.  Rest while this is being given, preferably lying prone.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is the heart functioning alright?
(A)  The only disturbance we find with the heart is a sympathetic condition and by removal of those causes as produce the irregularity at times, these should be eliminated.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
