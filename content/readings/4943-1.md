## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 5/29/51 Niece [3900]'s letter:
> 
> "Sometime before the year 1911, Mr. Cayce gave a reading
> for my aunt, [4943], who lived in Omaha, Nebraska.  At that
> time she had been diagnosed as having tuberculosis and, in
> spite of all the treatments, was growing steadily weaker.
> She would have spasms of coughing which were becoming more
> frequent.
> 
> "My father was living in Bowling Green, [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] and listened
> to the reading.  He said that Mr. Cayce called the names of a
> number of streets in Omaha until he came to the one on which
> my aunt lived, then called the house numbers until he came to
> hers.  The reading then described her condition in detail,
> giving the cause of her trouble as a pinch in the intercostal
> nerve at a certain place in her spine.  He said this could be
> corrected only by [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment, that she could take
> a barrel of the medicine the doctors had prescribed and it
> would do her no good.
> 
> "She had the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments as directed and was
> almost immediately relieved of the spasms of coughing.  She
> recovered completely.
> 
> "She died about 1917 or 1918 during an operation for gall
> stones."
> 
> R2. GD's note:  To my knowledge EC was never in Omaha, Nebraska.
