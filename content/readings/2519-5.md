# TEXT OF READING 2519-5 F 33

**Reading [[2519-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1930  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 9476

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2519], Drs. Lydic and Parker, Mrs. Dozier, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here [2519] - this we have had before. We find the body still on the improve in EVERY respect. While there are not the full eliminations from the system, and while the coordinations are not entirely in accord; yet these KEPT much in the manner as has been outlined will bring the near normal conditions for this body.  Beware MOST of cold and congestion, ESPECIALLY of getting feet damp or cold, or of drafts.  Do not overtax the body physically, nor should there be too much MENTAL worries.  While the body eliminates same much through the application of the mental and spiritual forces, as has been given - and THESE may be relied upon. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are the ovaries infected?
(A)  Sympathetically, as has been given.

3. (Q)  How much longer should treatment to uterus be given to bring back to normal?  and how often?
(A)  Once to twice each week, and should be at least for two to three weeks.

4. (Q)  Should cervix be dilated?
(A)  No.

5. (Q)  Are there any corrections to be made in sacral and lumbar that have not been made?
(A)  These need MOST to be strengthened in the sacral and lumbar.  There ARE corrections that should be made in the UPPER DORSAL.  THESE are those that have caused the most trouble.  These, while corrected often, have been in such long duration that the INTERNAL lesions are easily exercised sufficient to produce such impingement as to cause pains through shoulder and side - as they were, stitches - or the intercostal reactions from same.  These should be corrected and STRENGTHENED in their muscular reaction, in the treatments.

6. (Q)  Are teeth causing any trouble?
(A)  We use Ipsab for these and we will rid this of these conditions that have caused trouble!  This should be done, however, by one making local APPLICATIONS of same, not by a GENERAL use.

7. (Q)  Is the body absorbing poison from the teeth?
(A)  Naturally, the reflex conditions from infections as occur in these, especially in the upper back ones, cause some reactions that act as re-infection in the system. Local application - that is, cleansing of same (of the tartar), and using the solution as a cleansing solution BETWEEN the teeth, and in those places where the gums will show irritation when cleansed, see?

8. (Q)  Is body ready to return home and carry out treatments there?
(A)  The BODY is ready, mentally!  Physically, it is not!

9. (Q)  How much longer before the body can return home?
(A)  The body can return at present, but not BEST for the body.  Two weeks would add a great deal.

10. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  The GENERAL manipulation each day.  Corrections every other day.  In two weeks we would find a great deal of difference with the corrective measures for the dorsal and the STRENGTHENING measures for LUMBAR and sacral - as is also the treatments for the uterus; for with the conditions as exist, and with the warnings as of cold and congestion, we would find that a cold at the present time, and this appearing near the changes or eliminations as come in system, we would be in a more SERIOUS condition than has BEEN; for IN the eliminations, these are in the state of ADDING to the betterment of the system.  Why take CHANCES with that as has been accomplished?  See?

11. (Q)  Would you advise [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations?
(A)  These would be helpful!

12. (Q)  How often?
(A)  Not MORE than once each week.  That would be about two, in this particular case.

13. That is all the questions for this body.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
