# TEXT OF READING 4037-1 M 36

**Reading [[4037-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/6/1944  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 11729

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., for this 6th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [4037], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., via Mrs. Olga M. Etheridge of Princess Anne Draft Board.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mr. [4037].]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4037].

3. In analyzing the disturbances as we find here, many things are to be taken into consideration if there would be the perfect understanding of the disorders and disturbances.  We find that these arise from a condition which arose from hurt, hurt in the mental and spiritual self, the overanxiety of the body; and then a disappointment that, as it were, broke the spirit, broke the desire and [[Purpose|purposes]] of the body; causing, yes, physical conditions to arise.

4. But here, in analyzing, we find the close association between the [[Physical Body|physical body]], the [[Mental Body|mental body]], [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] of an individual.  While the hurt was in the [[Mental Body|mental body]] and the disappointment in the greater part was in the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]], we find that the reaction comes about in the physical being.

5. This, in its manifestation, affects the spleen as related to the blood and nerve supplies in the body.

6. Thus we have a form physically, of anemia which will, unless measures are taken, gradually grow more and more of the pernicious or violent nature in [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of the vital energies necessary for physical, mental and spiritual reaction and coordination through the body.

7. The effects are for the body to become so easily tired because of the lack of vital energies.  The addition to the body of energy-building forces has not removed the hurt, the disappointment.  For this has attacked, as it were, the [[Physical Body|physical body]] through the sensory and sympathetic nervous systems; thus causing the character of reaction.  Not that there is any mental disturbance, no; it is rather a hurt, an injury, a disappointment such that there can only come the renewing, the revivifying, by the whole trust, the whole faith, the whole renewed life within the mental and spiritual aspects of the body being put in divine hands.

8. Then physically apply the necessary energies to revivify, to replenish the energies, the energy-supplying efforts, so that even from the structural portions of body there may be the rebuilding or supplying from that assimilated in physical digestion, the red blood cellular forces to overcome the physical deficiencies and those tendencies of the energies of the body to be destroyed within the own physical self.

9. We begin first then with the mental attitude.  Do the first things first.  Begin by the closer study within self of self, as related to the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] or God.  The manifestations indicated in His word, then, must be the basis for this re-lighting, this reviving, this replenishing of the attitudes of the mind and of the spiritual self from those injuries, those hurts, those disappointments.

10. Let's begin like this:  First, don't be overanxious.  Know that it is within self and that what may be supplied by mechanical means is only the means to an end, and the good that may arise from same is only that this form of application may attune the body to that which may gain in its own mental self, by a study, by an analysis of the relation of self to the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] or God.

11. Begin, then, here:  Read [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5, then the whole 30th chapter of Deuteronomy.  Then turn to Romans, read the whole book of Romans; not analyzing but rather knowing that each of these portions refers to self.  These are the activities within self.  Then begin with St. [[John (Apostle)|John]] 14, 15, 16, 17.  Know these almost by heart, but rather as they apply to self, as they each refer to self.  For here are promises.  They apply to this body as an individual.  For the very fact that ye are conscious of yourself should be the assurance that He, the Father-God, is mindful of thee as an individual entity and that ye have work to do.  He is depending upon thee for the knowledge, for the glorifying, for the opportunity to glorify Himself through thee.  Do this first.

12. Then, after a period of some two to three weeks, apply mechanically the Radio-Active Appliance for one hour each day, or an hour and a half.  This is not as a shocking machine of any electrical forces outside of the body itself. Make the attachments to the wrist and to the opposite ankle, alternating in the usual manner, it will attune the whole body-forces themselves in their [[Vibration|vibrations]] within self to the infinite.  Be very mindful to keep the attachment plates clean, being mindful of the instructions as to how this little mechanical appliance works.  Use that period each day to [[Meditation|meditate]] and pray.

13. Then live the normal life; that is, be oft in the open.  The better exercise is walking.

14. During the hour before the Appliance is attached, apply over the spleen area - that is, across the diaphragm from the lower portion of the right rib, across the left rib; that is, the very low portion and the left side - the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs; using three to four thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Apply over this the salt heat; that is, take common table salt, spread it about one-half to an inch thick and make a pad, sewing this together so that it doesn't get in great clogs and heat by putting in the oven.  When the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs are applied, lie this hot salt over same.  Keep on for at least thirty minutes.  When this is taken off, attach the Radio-Active Appliance.  When the Glyco-Thymoline Packs are being prepared, prepare the Appliance; that is, put the Appliance in the ice.  Then connect the lead wires to be attached to the body, when ready to attach to the body.

15. In the periods of [[Meditation|meditation]], to be sure use thine own words, according to thine own reactions from what you read in the references given, but in this manner:

16. GOD, SUPPLY THAT NEEDED IN MY BODY, IN MY MIND, IN MY [[Purpose|PURPOSE]]; THAT I MAY BE THE BETTER CHANNEL TO GLORIFY THEE IN THE EARTH JUST NOW, IN EVERY WAY THOU WOULD HAVE ME GO.

17. In the diet, do include often such as liver, [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], rather than the beef or meat itself; plenty of sea foods, plenty of vegetables - especially raw vegetables.

18. Do these and we will find better conditions coming to this body.

19. Ready for questions.

20. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyco-Thymoline Packs; also directions for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|beef juice]]; and article explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/19/44 [4037]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|Association for Research and Enlightenment]], Inc.
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Sir:
> 
> In answer to your letter of July 14th, last, I would
> appreciate an earlier check-physical reading, if possible.
> The radio-active treatments that I have been applying have
> had a detrimental effect instead of soothing and relaxing my
> body.  I experienced this effect with the first battery that
> I had and thinking that perhaps the battery was defective, I
> saw Mr. Godfrey, who immediately replaced this battery with a
> new one.  The first three (3) treatments with the new battery
> had no effect, that is, it did not seem to hurt me nor
> to help, but the following treatments affected me to the
> extent that my arms and legs were weakened and ached.  These
> treatments ran me down to such an extent that I have been
> forced to discontinue same.  I have followed and applied
> all suggestions diligently and now that I have been forced
> to stop my radio-active treatments I feel as though the
> suggested procedure has been broken and that a
> check-physical reading will help to determine why my system
> will not respond to these treatments.
> 
> I would greatly appreciate an early check-physical reading
> which I think will help me.
> 
> Thanking you for your valuable time, I remain
> 
> Yours very truly, [4037]
> 
> R2. 9/7/44 [4037]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I will be at your study on Sept. 23rd at 3:30 P.M. for my
> check-physical reading which you have dated for me in your
> letter of July 25th, 1944.
> 
> You ask me for complete report of my physical case prior
> to my reading and the manner in which I have followed the
> suggestions given in my reading and the results which I
> will gladly tell you in the paragraphs below.
> 
> During the latter part of Oct. 1937 the first sign of my
> illness appeared.  One morning when I first awoke my mouth
> and tongue were just like being on fire and this lasted for
> a day.  A week later the same symptoms appeared but this time
> there was a slight tickle in my throat and right ear and this
> kept on for a few weeks until my throat felt as if it was
> on fire also.  The feeling would come and go, lasting from
> minutes to hours and at broken intervals.  When the symptoms
> would leave I felt perfectly normal.  This kept on until I
> visited a doctor for the first time in my life, he painted
> my throat and from that time it began getting worse.  Every
> attack that I had from then on was like someone pulling my
> throat apart, a depressed feeling, my muscles around my neck
> felt stiff, dizzy spells, throat like being on fire, yet my
> body felt perfectly healthy and I always had a good appetite.
> In the meantime I visited more doctors, one doctor operated
> on my throat several times, another gave me short-wave
> diathermy and [[Violet Ray|violet ray]].  A famous, out of town, specialist
> told me that I had an irritated pharynx and it would go away
> in time by taking some injections.  Now all this trouble was
> on the right side of my throat and after two years it moved
> from the right side to the left.  Further examinations showed
> that I had a spasmodic condition in my stomach and was placed
> on a diet, yet I had no relief from my throat and had no pain
> in the stomach.  During one period I was relieved for two
> months and it would start again, then relieved for four or
> five months but the trouble would start again.  Until the
> summer of 1942 this trouble was the same and then all of a
> sudden the attacks stopped.  I felt perfect for nine months
> and once in a while I had slight attacks but not enough to
> worry me because it felt as if this trouble was leaving for
> good.  Then around March 1943 was when other symptoms began
> to appear.  I had never felt anything wrong in my body during
> this throat ailment besides my throat.  Then I began to feel
> weak in my legs and arms and very very tired.  I also began
> to feel a slight burn in my bladder after urinating and this
> went on for several weeks.  One day all of a sudden my
> bladder felt as if it was on fire and I could not sit down,
> yet I was too weak to stand up.  I ran a fever of 104 for
> several days and lost 35 lbs in a short time.  Examinations
> by a urologist showed that I had a growth or projection
> in the urethra which caused this and which is known as
> Verumontanum.  I was treated for this and had some relief,
> but far from being well.  Just before I started these
> treatments, the trouble in my throat started all over again,
> my arms and legs would feel very hot, like my blood was
> boiling, my joints would ache and my fingers were numb.
> This is my condition up until today, my throat bothers me
> occasionally, the trouble in the urethra and around the seat
> is still bothering me, my legs and arms feel weak especially
> my legs, my finger tips remain numb most of the time and I
> have a tight or burning sensation around my stomach which I
> will explain below.
> 
> I had not mentioned this burning or tight sensation around
> my stomach to anyone because I had so many things that were
> wrong with me that I was afraid people would think I was
> insane.  On the day that I came to you I felt good with no
> symptoms except this feeling around my stomach.  In your
> reading you mentioned trouble in my spleen which seems to
> be in that neighborhood.
> 
> Here is my report that I followed from my reading and the
> results.
> 
> I followed the suggestions as in my reading as closely as
> possible.  At first when I began applying the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]
> and hot salt packs on the spleen area and the Radio-Active
> appliance, I did not notice any results.  After a few
> treatments when I first applied the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] packs,
> I would become sleepy and would try to stay awake in order
> to apply the Radio-Active Appliance.  After applying the
> Radio-Active Appliance for 15 or 20 minutes the sleepiness
> would wear off and would become very uneasy.  During the last
> 10 minutes of the Radio-Active it seemed like it was working
> against me, my arms and legs would become very tired and
> aching.  I tried this for 7 or 8 treatments and thinking that
> maybe something was wrong with the battery I returned it to
> Mr. Godfrey to check it.  He made another battery for me.
> During the first few treatments with the new battery I did
> not notice a thing, but after the next treatments my legs and
> arms would get very tired and aching like the first battery.
> I took 48 of these treatments and felt that they were doing
> me more harm than good and discontinued them.
> 
> Here are the questions I would like to ask in my
> check-physical reading:  1. What trouble is there around
> and near the urethra and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]?  2. What trouble is there
> around my throat?  3. What trouble is there around my left
> lower ribs and stomach that causes tightness and burning?
> 4. What causes the numbness in my finger tips and tired,
> burning, and worn out feeling in my arms and legs?
> 
> Thanking you for all the help you have given me, I am
> 
> Yours truly, [4037]
>
