# TEXT OF READING 512-1 M 5

**Reading [[512-1]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/31/1932  Sex: M  ReadingID: 3640

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 31st day of August, 1932, in accordance with request made by his father, Mr. [...] new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [959].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., West Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, the conditions physical with this body are of the prenatal conditions, and there are those incoordinations that deal primarily with the lack of the functioning of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], as well as elements that cause the deficiency in the organs' functioning through the activity of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] with the distribution of elements that make for the proper distribution of assimilated forces in the body.

2. As would be seen and understood, a physical organism is as a working machine, when perfect in its elements, for the creating and sustaining of those forces within same to supply those energies necessary for not only the physical development but the mental.  The active forces are those that work in conjunction with that as is assimilated in the developing body, and make for sustenance to that necessary to be supplied from the forces from without.

3. When there are those lacking of elements in system, and the inability of the body to produce those elements, through the inhibitions of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that make for the secretion of the forces that act with the elements of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], these then are either supplied - that there may be the coordinating forces, or there remains those active forces by the system adjusting itself without there being the coordination through the nerve and muscular forces as course with the circulatory system in the body.

4. In meeting, then, these in the present - for, as we find, would there be brought about those conditions that would be of assistance or aid in any permanent manner (for, as has been classed, this is by some considered an incurable condition, or elements are lacking wherein there may not be supplied those forces that would make for the continuity of that being properly assimilated to bring about a normal coordinating balance in the body) treatments would be begun in such a way and manner as to make for the developing forces those that would bring coordinations for the body, mentally, and physically.  Physically, of course, first, with the mental developments that will be carried by that as will be assimilated through the creating of energies with those portions of the system that do function now for any digestion at all.

5. We would prepare, then, as this:  The [[Wet Cell|wet cell]] or wet battery application.  To the nickel pole and nickel anode we would make for the application of the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] solution to be carried into the system.  Now the positive, or the one not carrying the Gold, should always be attached to the body first, when preparing to use or to give the treatment for same; and the positive, or the first anode attached, would be attached to the 2nd and 3rd dorsal center, or to the secondary cardiac plexus center - 2nd and 3rd dorsal center; while that carrying the Gold - and the larger plate - would be attached to that area over the liver, the gall ducts and the pancrean ducts, especially, or to the right and up a bit from the umbilicii plexus; so that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that will be created by the low form of the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] will carry to the organs of assimilation that which will create within the elements of same to the organs that, with their ducts and centers, will create the proper elements for the building up of those resistances necessary, and replenishing of those conditions lacking in the glands and organs of this developing body.

6. This battery treatment would be given each day in the beginning, for twenty to thirty minutes in the evening - or at the close of the day.

7. Also we would give, preferably at the beginning of the day, a general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the whole system, and especially along the cerebro-spinal system - or from the base of the brain to the end of the spine and down the limbs, from the brachial center and from the lumbar center, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with small quantities of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and [[Russia|Russian]] White oil mixed together - equal parts - with a third the quantity of witchhazel. Just sufficient quantity for the body to assimilate.  Do this each day.

8. As for the diet, keep that as will be assimilated by the body.  It will be found necessary to change, but keep sufficient quantity of the vitamins especially of A, B, D and E.  These will assist in the creating of [[Creative Forces|CREATIVE energies]], elements and forces within the body.

9. Every thirty-two days we would renew the battery charge; that is, emptying the solution and renewing same.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Can his speech be developed?
(A)  All those elements lacking for the active forces of the organs and the glands that supply the elements may be aided. Let's begin, so that we will have something like a normal development of the body, and we may have then the active forces of the organs through those activities of those elements that make for the various portions of the system towards normal development.

12. (Q)  How long should this treatment be continued before having a check reading?
(A)  Until at least the thirty-two treatments have been given.

13. (Q)  Does he have any trouble of the feet?
(A)  As indicated, there's trouble all through the whole system - for normalcy and coordination are not developing with the body!

14. (Q)  May we hope for a complete cure?
(A)  If the expectancy and the persistency and consistency of the activities are kept, we may bring almost a NORMALCY in speech, walking, sight, hearing, and the reactions in the activities of the system.

15. (Q)  Is there any further advice for those who have charge of this body?
(A)  Be persistent and consistent, knowing that those forces without may be added in the system to that of the desire of the mental forces of the body and of those supplying same, to RENEW those elements in the physical organism.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [...] [Father of [512]]
"    " File
"    " Mrs. [959]
