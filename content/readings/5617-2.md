# TEXT OF READING 5617-2 M 52

**Reading [[5617-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1930  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 13960

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5617].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Parker and McChesney.]

Time of Reading 
12:15 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Mass. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  This we have had before.  Some improvements are shown, in a general way and manner, by this body and its reactions in the system; but, would the body gain the full purport of that as has been applied - or as has been given FOR application - we would CHANGE, at least for a few weeks, the environs.  Then we will be ABLE to so add to the system as to RELIEVE this distress as is shown through the capillary and lymphatic circulation, in the character OF the irritations as appear in splotches in the system - but, under the environs, laboring as the body does - in this manner - NOT so well.  Then, go to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] for at least ten days!  Do THAT!  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
