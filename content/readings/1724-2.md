# TEXT OF READING 1724-2 M 40

**Reading [[1724-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/23/1938  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 7919

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of December, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1724], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, while there is a great improvement, there are still some disturbing factors.  There is less inflammatory influence or force in the lower end of the duodenum and those areas where there is the entrance into the jejunum.  But there is still some regurgitation from the duodenum to the stomach through the pylorus.

4. And as we find, there needs be more of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  Not too strong with the electrical forces that have been applied.  These have been somewhat severe.  Use the same KIND that has been used, but NOT so high NOR so much!

5. It is necessary that there again be more of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], but farther apart.  For where mucous is indicated in the colon, this needs to be removed; and this is the better manner, if it is properly done.  These we would give now about once in ten days or two weeks, and after the second or third make them a month apart - unless there ARE accumulations from excesses in some manner, then give them oftener.

6. Have more of the lactics to aid in the assimilations, when large quantities of foods are taken; or the Alcaroid, as indicated.  And less of sugars that make a great strain upon the activities of the liver as related with the pancreas and the activities through the gall duct area.  Little or none of anything, then, like cake, or the combinations of such.  Not that none would be taken, but none such as coconut, none such as any great quantity of preserves or puddings, or alcoholic drinks of any kind; though such carbonated waters as in Coca-Cola or the like would be HELPFUL, if they are taken moderately.

7. If all of these are kept, and keeping up the attitudes (which are much improved), these we find would bring normal forces for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What specific type of treatment should be given at Reilly's?  Please give details.
(A)  The general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments; [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] when needed; the hot and cold water - not too much of the dry cabinet but the general thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] all over, including the oil, - or the rubs with Pine Oils and the like; with, of course, the closing of the pores afterwards - and the low electrical forces, not so high as has been given.

10. (Q)  What effect does the hay fever condition have on the stomach condition, and what should be done for the hay fever?
(A)  The drippings - or the natural flow of the inflammatory forces, which when taken into the stomach produce greater irritation.  And this goes with the mucous, or aids in forming mucous; as did the cold that was had some ten weeks - yes, some twenty-eight weeks ago, see?  that formed the greater portion.

Whenever there is the inclination for sniffles, or cold, the use of an inhalant prepared in this manner, will reduce this tendency for the inflammation from nasal passages or from the throat to cause disturbance:

To 4 ounces of Pure Grain Alcohol - in a 6 or 8 ounce container, with the opening sufficiently large to put 2 vents into the cork, that may be kept closely corked when not in use - add, IN THE ORDER NAMED:

Oil of Eucalyptus..................20 minims,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.......15 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...............5 minims,
Tolu in Solution...................20 minims.

Shake the solution well and inhale mornings before any meal is taken, and evenings upon retiring, - or at other times if there is the tendency for sniffles or any erudition from the nasal or antrum passage.

11. (Q)  Please give detailed diet.
(A)  As indicated, keep away from those things which we have just outlined.  The general diet is very well, - these we would follow, rather than saying "I can't eat this," or "I can't eat that!"  Just leave off the sweets and the carbonated waters, save Coca-Cola or such, and leaving off any alcoholic beverages of any kind.

12. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these, and as conditions progress, - don't leave them off because you feel better, but keep them up sufficient to have the removal of the causes of the disturbance.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
