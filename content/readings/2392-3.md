# TEXT OF READING 2392-3 M 58

**Reading [[2392-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/30/1942  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 9215

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2392], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M., Jan. 29th. 
Taken Jan 30th, 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:  (Who was in his office ... 1/29/42, 3:30 to 4:30 P.M., EST.)

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2392]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are very good in many respects.  There are those tendencies for the thinning of the walls of the lymph flow through the body, owing to the tendency of the body to carry impurities that should have been eliminated through their normal channels - due to the lack of certain elements.  And these as we find in the present are that effect of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in the body.

4. Thus in the present, for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] condition - or the nobules that form on the neck and ear and part of the head, - we would take internally one to two drops of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (never more than two drops) in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken.  Do this for five days.  Then leave off five days.  Then repeat.  Continue in this manner until at least three or four series.

5. Each evening before retiring, on those areas where the disturbances exist, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] gently - not severely - a little [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] with sufficient [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] in same to make it as an ointment.  Cleanse the next morning with an [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] solution, - a teaspoonful to an ounce of DISTILLED WATER, and this kept as a solution for cleansing these areas before the bath of morning.

6. Then, be mindful that there is not too much grease or fats taken in the diet daily.  Little or no hog meat, unless crisp breakfast bacon.

7. Do these and we will find bettered conditions throughout this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
