# TEXT OF READING 341-3 M 17

**Reading [[341-3]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/1/1924  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 2642

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [341].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. LS:  You will answer the following questions:  Have the adjustments which have been accorded the body been made correctly?

2. What causes the breaking out condition appearing on the skin of the body, especially the feet, and what should be done to correct this condition?

3. Give specifically the condition of the eyes, and whether or not this body should wear spectacles.

4. EC:  Now, we find the general conditions in this body better than when we had them before.  The adjustments for those conditions in the spinal column and the pressure as produced by that condition has not been made fully so that they remain in the proper lineament [alignment], though the condition as was and is accorded the body at the present time is better and not as much of the strain to the system as has been. These should be carried out, as given, until the lineament is correct, and the organs that are affected by these conditions function normally through the system; as we have in the eruptions as shown in cuticle, this produced by secretions left in the system, and not eliminated through the proper channels, this producing the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] to the blood supply and creating, in the effusion of the secretions, that causing the abrasions in the cuticle.

5. The condition in the eyes are better than we had them before, and with the adjustments as given, and with the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] [[Vibration|vibrations]] and the correct exercise, taken as given, it would not be necessary for the body to assist the system through spectacles, though without these [treatments] it will become necessary to do that.

6. We would for this condition and body have adjustments correct in the body, and the organs so massaged that the elimination comes properly through the system, taking into the system this:  Though it is a poison, taken properly and correctly will assist the elimination and correct the system:

7. Fowler's solution of Arsenic, taken in this manner.  One drop each day for three days, then two drops each day for three days, then three drops each day for three days, on up, until five drops are taken.  Then, after the third day with that, rest for three days, then begin with one drop as before, and applying to those abrasions the solution as is applied, or as would be found in any of those applications for such eruptions in the cuticle.

8. Do that.  Be persistent and consistent with these.  We will bring the body to its correct [[Vibration|vibration]].

9. Be careful of diet.  More green vegetable matter.
