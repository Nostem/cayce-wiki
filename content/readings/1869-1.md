# TEXT OF READING 1869-1 F 46 (Hebrew Parentage, Religion:

**Reading [[1869-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/1/1939  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 8291

"None") This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1869], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended through [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] at the [[New York City|New York]] Psychic Research Society.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born December 21, 1892 or 1893, near Warsaw, Poland 
in Yablona. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions which she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from present - " - '29 - periods of change - some expansions, and yet fears - '27 - '20 - changes - '19 - 1893, as we find here, was the year of birth as we would count in the present.

3. We have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity called in the present, [1869].

4. In giving the interpretations from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find here, these are chosen with the desire to make the experience a helpful one in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s application of its abilities, of its emotions, and its activities in the present.

5. Rather one out of the ordinary in its reactions, and in its associations of ideas, and ideas with individuals.

6. But (without respect to the application) these are the influences that we find innate and manifested in the experience of the entity in the present:

7. One oft anxious about others.

8. One rather inclined to be secretive about its activities in associations with others.

9. One inclined to be mindful of the economic conditions in the affairs of groups, - of even nations.  Not in a political manner, but more as to the social and economic order of relationships.

10. One inclined to give too much to omens and signs, and those influences that arise from associations or affiliations with those who are reported to be occultists, - or a form of scientific research.

11. It is well that all of these be considered, but know that much that is attained through the mental concept may be misleading.  It must be rather the awareness of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], that may bring the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] and peace.

12. Know that no form of omen or signs should ever be confusing in the experience of an entity.  Thus, while [[Astrology|astrological]], [[Numerology|numerological]] or the mystic signs are important to a degree, they are merely RELATIVE as to [[Purpose|purposes]].

13. For know, as has been indicated of old, "If ye will be my people, I will be thy God" was not spoken at random, but that each [[Soul|soul]], each entity may become - as it seeks - aware of the [[Oneness|oneness]] with the promises which have been and are the birthright of every [[Soul|soul]] that comes into materialization.

14. Signs and wonders, lights and noises, - yea, even though one come from heaven and declare that He hath not come in the flesh, such an one would lead thee astray.

15. In the aspects as we find them from the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]], the entity comes close to the cusps.

16. Hence they are in that position (through the signs!) that the influences are for the entity to be both a materialist AND one given to spiritual things.

17. It behooves the entity, then, all the more to so examine self as to know not only in what ye believe but WHO is the AUTHOR of that ye profess to believe.

18. Thus the applying of self in its daily associations with its fellow man, in keeping with the tenets and [[Ideal|ideals]] of that which is [[Ideal|the ideal]] of the entity, will bring more and more awareness of the relationships to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]] ye call God.

19. For HE is mindful of all, and hath not willed that any [[Soul|soul]] should be led astray.  Seek, then, and ye shall find; knock and it will be opened unto you.

20. Study then to show thyself approved unto thy ideal, a workman not ashamed, - rightly dividing the words of truth, putting the accent where it belongs.

21. Know that all is - as Life - the gift of the Creator, and that the opportunities which present themselves from day to day are to be made manifest as a glorifying of the Father. For that is thy mission in this experience.

22. Sincerity is thy watchword, but let not thy good be evil- spoken of by accrediting to this or that sign or omen as the proof.  But know, as He hath given, "My Spirit beareth witness with thy spirit."  Not the spirit then of confusion, nor of any man-made knowledge.  For ALL may be aware, from the least to the greatest, of their relationship to the Father.

23. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth in the material [[Incarnation|sojourns]], we find that few have the direct influence upon the entity in the present, - save as the entity may make aware through the application of that which is its ideal.

24. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity, during those periods of an upheaval and the turmoil brought with the invasion of the Romans, - through those activities of the [[France|French]] dictator - or the [[Napoleon|Napoleon]], that brought desolation through the gratifying of self's own egotism, - making for material sides to be taken in the minds and heart's of many.

25. The entity, through the experience of turmoil, remained beautiful in its relationships to others.  Those hardships materially brought a peace within, - as kindness and brotherly love meted to others.

26. Then the name was Hanen Revolli.  In the experience the entity gained, - and innately others have been and are an impelling influence and force in the present experience.

27. And the abilities to write, the abilities to talk, through such associations, become outlets for the entity in the present.

28. Know, as ye experienced through that [[Incarnation|sojourn]], "He that is the greatest among you is the servant of all," ministering kindnesses, gentlenesses, patience to others.

29. Before that we find the entity was in the Promised Land, as now called; during those periods when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the earth.

30. The entity then was among those peoples who looked for, through those who were endowed with the inner [[Visions|vision]], a helper, a master, a leader, a teacher; being a student of what ye would term [[Astrology|astrology]].

31. Thus we find that all phases of such natures that tend towards [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of the universe outside of man's own material [[Visions|vision]] become conditions and experiences to be sought.  These should ever be, as we have indicated, assurances rather than those things upon which to build thy determinations or thy activities even.

32. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] the entity knew much of those things that dealt with the activities between the various religious factors, - as those termed the Sadducees, the Essenes, the Pharisees, those who worshiped beauty and those who knew of and - yea - presented those things of the mythological nature that gave to the material world concepts of the ethereal and terrestrial spheres of activity.

33. All of those were portions of the entity's experience, as Zenda Lumi.

34. There we find the entity gaining and losing; gaining during those periods in which there was the entertaining of those tenets set by the followers of the [[Nazareth|Nazarene]]; losing when those were turned to self-indulgences.

35. In the present experience, hold to those principles that were His which are combined into one, - that to love the Lord with all thy heart and mind and body, and thy neighbor as thyself, is the whole law, - and to do good is to have the smile of the Father on thee, and to do evil is to rue and regret thine own experiences.

36. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods of the turmoils and strifes there.

37. We find the entity entered with the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] who came not so much as refugees but to implant the tenets of the [[Sons of the Law of One|children of the Law of One]], and those people who attempted to give a reckoning of those activities to maintain and sustain a record.

38. Hence again we find the entity depending upon the signs of the times.  And, as He the Teacher of teachers gave, this is well in thy material life, but brings so little peace and assurance within thy spiritual self.

39. The name then was Es-el-da, - being of the daughters of the Prince of the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land; coming closer to the activities of the King in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land; becoming better established when those activities of the entity brought the influences in the Temple Beautiful that enabled the various individuals to purify their bodies and minds for channels of definite service to the various phases of man's experience in the earth.

40. The entity gained much in the latter portion, - as it may in the present, if there is the simple application of "Love ye one another," and not for self but that truth and mercy may be magnified in the earth, that the glory of the Son may be shedding its light in the lives of those ye meet day by day.

41. Ready for questions.

42. (Q)  What have been my past associations with my son [...], what are the urges in the present and what can I do for our mutual mental and spiritual development?
(A)  The daughter of that entity, as has just been indicated. The son was the Prince in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land.  The desire to see that Prince, that son again exalted into those positions of power, - let it be not of domination but let him learn - and learn WITH Him - those lessons of humbleness of [[Purpose|purpose]] in the MIGHT of God's love; and that the beauty of service for the glory of the Father - as it did to those of old - brings power that may be used in service to others.

43. (Q)  Please give such advice as will be helpful in directing and training my son, [...].
(A)  As just indicated, - no better may be given than studying to show thyself approved unto God, a workman not ashamed, serving thy fellow man.  For as ye do it unto the least of thy brethren, ye do it unto thy Maker.

44. (Q)  Toward what specific type of activity should I direct my energies?
(A)  Writing, teaching, lecturing, - as you SHOULD direct the boy.

45. (Q)  What are the first essential steps?
(A)  Self-analysis and determining WHAT is thy ideal, and WHO is the author of same.

46. (Q)  What have been my past associations with my husband and are our [[Karma|karmic]] relationships completed in the present?
(A)  These can best be answered within self.

As to the former associations and relations, these - judged by present standards - were not beautiful; yet such activities have been needed; and, remember,ye are meeting thyself!  For as ye measured, it is, it has been, it will be measured to thee!

The former associations were in the land of the nativity.

47. (Q)  What steps should I now take to conclude and clean up this relationship in relation to both my son and myself?
(A)  As given.

48. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Much of opportunity lies before thee.  Much of abilities are within thine own pursuance of truth.  Do that as seemeth good unto thee in the light of that thou hast been given, - study to show thyself approved.

49. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
