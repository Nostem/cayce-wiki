# TEXT OF READING 1158-33 F 51

**Reading [[1158-33]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/20/1941  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 6535

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.  ..., Oh.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is Dr. Nover correct that part of root from extracted third lower left side molar is imbedded in jaw bone?
(A)  As we find, this is correct - and should be the first of the attentions given, in clearing up the mouth and tooth situation.

Then, that one that needs removing - as has been indicated; and then the rest where there is indicated - from the shadow-graph or x-ray - that nerve is exposed to ANY of the fillings, these should be removed, treated in the regular way, corked, and refilled.

5. (Q)  Is it advisable to cut through jaw bone and remove broken root?
(A)  Do whatever is necessary to remove same.

6. (Q)  Should this third left molar piece of root best be removed by Dr. Nover or by Dr. Holzman, or by whom?
(A)  Dr. Nover if he is to do the rest; whoever is to do the rest of the work.

7. (Q)  How should the second left lower molar be treated?
(A)  As just indicated.

8. (Q)  Is copper-cement filling in the same molar tooth the cause of any trouble?
(A)  This is not indicated in the shadow-graph.  It is the other side or portion.

9. (Q)  Both Dr. Nover and Dr. Holzman advise that dead upper left bicuspid should be extracted.  Is it in best interest to follow this advice?
(A)  As has just been indicated.

10. (Q)  Other than the second lower left molar, will you specify where the shadow-graph indicates "Inlays" that should be removed for treating teeth?
(A)  These should be judged by the individual making the corrections!  These might be pointed out in person, but these are the judges and these are the judgments:  Where the nerve is indicated to be in touch with the filling, remove, treat, cork, refill.

11. (Q)  Can the previously suggested [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] general water treatments be given at home?
(A)  They can, but this is NOT advised.  For, too easily does everyone tend to allow circumstance to interfere with the thoroughness of such.  Take the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] in this manner:

Occasionally have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], but not each time a general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment is taken, - this only once or twice a month, you see, or just occasionally.

The general hydrotherapy otherwise would include, oftener, or two or three times a month, or the like, - a light cabinet sweat and a fume bath.  For this body we would use, in the pint of water boiling under the body, a teaspoonful each of Witchhazel and Balsam of Tolu.

This would be followed by the shower and then a thorough rubdown with oils, including Pine Oil as well as Cedar Wood Oil, and then the alcohol.

These are much preferable.

12. (Q)  Where shall I obtain these?
(A)  In a hydrotherapy place!  These should be followed by those where it is most convenient, and where these suggestions as indicated will be adhered to, see?

13. (Q)  If women followed correct diet and lived properly, could menopause be postponed till 70 or entirely eliminated?
(A)  This is a natural source as from the DIVINE forces, and is the natural consequence of those conditions from [[First Cause|the first cause]] - though by the continued effort there might be such a thing accomplished in the tenth generation.  But for what [[Purpose|purpose]], if God ordained otherwise?

14. (Q)  Are there any other helpful suggestions?
(A)  Do these things as indicated for the betterment and bettered conditions of this body.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail, Special, to ... St., [[New York City|New York City]]
"    " Ass'n file

Wire:  APPARENTLY NOVER PREFERABLE BUT SAME ONE SHOULD DO REST OF WORK.
