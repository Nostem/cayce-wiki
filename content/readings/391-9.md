# TEXT OF READING 391-9 M 24

**Reading [[391-9]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/17/1935  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 2986

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1851 Columbia Road, Washington, D.C., this 17th day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [391], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [391]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there is a return of some of those conditions that the body has been warned concerning.  There have been indiscretions in the activities of the body; not only in the STRAINING of the body but in the bringing about of the recurrent reactions that make for distresses in the activities and the discharges from the conditions that have been warned as respecting.

3. However, as we find, if the body will take those precautions and use those same injections, those same applications that have been indicated, taking the precautions as respecting the physical activities, these should soon disappear.

4. Ready for questions.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
