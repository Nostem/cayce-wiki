# TEXT OF READING 1170-4 M 64

**Reading [[1170-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/31/1941  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 6559

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of January, 1941, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [959], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               At his home in 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., West Va.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have had this body here before.

3. As we find, there have been those tendencies for too much of fats taken by the body; and this, as combined with the poor eliminations, has caused an overflow - or a regurgitation - between the circulation of the heart and the liver, or a form of thrombosis, or the thrombose became dilated.

4. To be sure, this might be called a form of heart disturbance; for, unless precautions are taken, or those activities of the nature to change these conditions through the system, it might become very serious, - flooding the heart, dilating same, and - to be sure - producing disintegration.

5. As we find, rest is the best in the present.  But we WOULD take those properties that are being administered to keep the better circulation.

6. Also we would begin immediately with the colon irrigations to reduce the engorgements there.  We would give these at least every ten days, or closer, until there is no indication - from the refuse - of any phlegm or mucous in same, or until the colon is entirely clear.  Of course, in the beginning these should not be given so much as to overcrowd or tire the body, but there should be at least five to six of these, - not merely enemas, but high irrigations.  Of course, don't attempt to drain the whole of the colon at once, but by the second or third time these are given, it should be entirely relieved.

7. Between these times of the irrigations, have a few - two or three - of the manipulations, osteopathically administered, to DISTRIBUTE the circulation below the waistline much better.  These would be most beneficial.

8. Be mindful not to overexert the body, as in too heavy lifting, or too great an exercise of any kind that is overfatiguing; and, most of all, beware of overeating.

9. Keep away from all fried foods, or from too much of meats.

10. These as we find will bring a much bettered condition, prevent the so filling of the blood vessels as to cause any leakages through the system.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Should he continue to wear truss when out again?
(A)  He should continue to wear at least some form of truss.

14. (Q)  What causes the burning in the stomach?
(A)  The regurgitation, or the lack of flow of the digestive fluids through the system.

15. (Q)  What is the eruption on lower lip?
(A)  When the poisons are more eliminated, this will gradually disappear.  As we find, it comes from the slowed circulation to the lower portions of the body.

16. (Q)  Any other suggestions at this time?
(A)  These we would do at the present time.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
