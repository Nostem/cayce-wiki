# TEXT OF READING 2015-7 F 1

**Reading [[2015-7]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/23/1940  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 8547

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of October, 1940, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [1005], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mae Verhoeven.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, improvements have been rather phenomenal; in that there is the clearing of those disturbing conditions from without.

4. But, as those warnings have been indicated, because of the poisons that are the natural effect of the burns, or the high forces of nature attempting to meet these, there is caused some temperature.

5. This should not cause any general alarm, though should necessitate real precautions being taken.

6. We would first begin with small, broken doses of Castoria, six to eight to ten drops every fifteen minutes until there is a full flushing.  Take it in the small doses, every fifteen to twenty minutes, but sufficiently long enough so that there is a GOOD reaction from it, see?  This amount should be sufficient at a dose if it is given every fifteen minutes.

7. Then the next day (when there has been the flushing, you see), give Calomel again - this is necessary; but under the direction of the physician, - don't give it self.  This with the Dover's powders is preferable.

8. Also in the first, to reduce temperature, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] gently along the spine (with the hand) with a grain alcohol solution; this not full strength, of course, but about two parts water to one part alcohol; a teaspoonful of alcohol to - two teaspoonsful of water.  Do this about twice a day.

9. And these as we find should bring about bettered conditions.

10. Keep up those applications for the burn or scald, you see.

11. (Q)  What causes swelling in hands and feet?
(A)  The lack of eliminations.  Thus the excess pressures upon kidneys and liver, causing the temperature.  Hence the two manners as indicated in which eliminations are to be set up.

12. (Q)  Please give specific diet during this period.
(A)  This should be of the general nature, rather than making any specific change.  All the diet, of course, should be body-building, but not too rich in fats.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

Wired as follows:

EIGHT DROPS CASTORIA EVERY FIFTEEN MINUTES UNTIL THOROUGH FLUSHING THEN ONLY UNDER PHYSICIAN'S DIRECTION CALOMEL AGAIN PREFERABLY WITH DOVERS POWDERS  REDUCE TEMPERATURE BY MASSAGING SPINE GENTLY WITH TWO PARTS WATER ONE PART GRAIN ALCOHOL  GENERAL BODY BUILDING DIET NO RICH FATS  FULL INFORMATION AIR MAIL.
