Date: 7/1/1936  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 2586

Student, Catholic) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of July, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [338], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [338] and Mr. [256], and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 12:10 Noon Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Mass. 
(Entity.  Detailed account of [[Incarnation|incarnation]] in the [[Persia|Persian]] 
experience when as a [[Greece|Greek]] the tendencies and talents for art 
were developed.  Advise regarding her work in art today and 
ans. ques.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, as well as that which has been given respecting [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as or called [maiden name], [338].

2. In giving that as may be helpful to the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present, well that the conditions from the material angle be understood, or as may be termed in the present from that angle which exists in the present in the nationalism of the whole world, and the nationalism that existed in the experience when [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity was]] as the [[Greece|Grecian]], with all of those experiences of those that had made for the activities in depicting in the earth of art, the various forms of art, in the statuary, in the paintings, in the markings, the drawings, and the conditions that arose from the entrance of those into the [[Persia|Persian]] land.

3. As indicated in the experiences of others, in this entity's experience also it was not the popular thing for the sojourning of individuals or of groups from the [[Greece|Grecian]] influence into the [[Persia|Persian]] land; for most of those who gathered there did so because of the very needs of the people themselves.  Yet during the early portion of the experience of the entity in the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the Persian and Arabian land, there grew a commercialism, the sectarianism as might be termed in the present of the various groups and the various activities where there were the attempts - to be sure - of the various groups to more and more inject, or more and more apply, THEIR relationships with the various groups that formed in that 'city in the hills and in the plains.'

4. Yet the entity, with the incoming, made for those expressions in the arts that are as in the experience of the entity in the present; the correlating not so much of what may be termed the thought of others in their various relationships with what had been the impelling influence in their experience, but of those things that partook of nature and its relationships to man's experience in keeping that ever before the individuals for their interpretation of the natural forces that express themselves in nature, that which expresses itself in the rhythmic of the lines in body as well as in those natural forces that manifest themselves.  These became a part of the entity.  And under the stress of those attempts on the part of many individuals and groups to express nationalism.  Hence we will find in the experiences of the entity in the present, during those periods and under the stress of the various times that are arising in the experience of the earth today, the entity may be said to be that one as may give a new interpretation in the experiences of those that may hold to the activities in the abilities for the correlating of those influences from the natural sources as well as from the influences in every [[Soul|soul]] that depict for those that seek to find the expressions in same in the present.

5. The activities of the material nature during that experience we find were in the schools of not the [origin of the] Epicurean nor the Pict, but rather what had become [in that much earlier time] a combination of same.

6. Then in the entering into the activities in the 'city in the hills,' and with the decorative forces for the commercial activities, the home, the places of service, the places of justice, the places of worship, all of these became as a portion of the activities wherein the entity was used as the means for making the artistic realizations in the experiences of those in the archaeological, in the home, in what would be termed commonly the places where the races were to be run, the places where [[Prayer|prayer]] was wont to be made, where justice was to be dealt, the activities in the home.  All of these became a part of the entity's experience.

7. And as has been indicated, they become the greater portion of the experience and developments for the entity in the present.

8. Holding then to those forces or influences in those experiences in the Persian activity, that which was depicted becomes more and more of a constructive as well as instructive influence in the rhythmic of the mind, of color, of activity, of expression, or those of a CONSTRUCTIVE nature that will make in the present for those forces that become as that upon which the entity INNATELY depends.  For THEN the entity gave of self in its activity as that of the less of self and more and more of [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

9. Hence as the expressions of self are given in the present, we will find there arising as from out of the within, the abilities to give life, to give hope, to give those things that are expressive of harmony, peace, depicted in the arts, as well as bringing same into the experience of those that behold same.

10. These then make for constructive forces in the activity of the entity in the present, and will lead to more and more the opening of the life within.  Life in its expression is that as has been given; for the essence of Life is God.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Shall I continue to study at the school I attend under the same teachers or are any others recommended?
(A)  For the present, and until the completion of that which has been set out as a goal through these particular studies in this particular association, keep the same school, the same associations.  And we will find that these will then gradually lead, in the next period of changes, to other fields of activity.  Not so much then as in a school, but as a COOPERATIVE activity with that school.

13. (Q)  Have I abilities for sculpture and should I devote part of my time to that study as well as painting for the most satisfactory expression?
(A)  This is as a portion.  They are of such a kindred nature, and were of the natural expression of the entity during those experiences, that it would be found that while sculpture will NOT be of the MAIN activities of the entity - unless given wholly or entirely to same - this should be used in the experience rather as what may be termed a rest FOR the body, see?  which will then lend a helpful means in the MANNER of expressions in the paintings.

For these, as were those expressions in the days of old that have come as legends, were so very close to the entity in those periods of activity in the Persian, that where there were made the sculpture folk or the sculptured activities, the SPIRITUAL forces took life in them, or took THEMSELVES into those influences.

Then, as the entity takes hold of same, it will find that these will, as it were, mold themselves into LIFE - and thus give in the painting a relief from what becomes oft as a bit of a grind; relieving self and thus enabling the entity, the experiences of the entity in the present, to become broader in its conception and to give more tone, more life, more expression into the painting.

14. (Q)  What phase of painting should I specialize in so that I may present those things most worthwhile?
(A)  As has been indicated, these should come as it were as MOODS for the entity.  At times those that express nature, as from the fields themselves; at times those that express the necessity for that held as a thought to be wielded into justice, yet mercy with same - those that would measure or judge between the fellowman when both perhaps are somewhat selfish and self-centered in their demands one upon another. Again there is the mood when those things which would represent the altars even of Life itself, may be depicted; not so much sacrifice as of duty, as of love, as of the expressions that find themselves from nature's influences but fill their place in the lives of individuals that may be assembled for such an activity.  These then, as we find. While this may be somewhat broad, yet it is very, very definite - when it's once begun.

15. (Q)  Where may I obtain a [[Numerology|numerological]] reading that would be reliable so that I may better determine my abilities in the present?
(A) These we find may be given through many channels.  These may be given through these influences or forces, yet these are rather as the expressions that would be given as signs and not as omens.  For [[Free Will|the WILL]] makes for that determination. But if those are sought for, as we find the better may be had from such a one as Moon Trail.  [Horace Hambling, English medium, at the time on tour in U.S.A., staying at Zentgraf home, S.I.]

16. (Q)  What associations or connections have I had with the members of my family in previous [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]]?
(A)  These have been indicated in the experiences and expressions of those that have been given as to the periods of activity when they came together.  These were then closely associated in the activities during that particular experience.  Not always the same relationships as in the present.  Some experiences are reversed, as has been indicated in the experience of the entity - as indicated in the experiences for those of the family relationships in the present.  But these become such that, as indicated in the present, there are the needs for that advice, that duty, that relationship which exists in the present - as an OUTGROWTH of those periods of activity.  And these become then the REAL reasons - if there is as a reason - rather because the brother was then the sister, or the sister rather then as the relationships of the companion or as an overseer or as an advisor, but rather than in the present the relationships are such that if the relationships are related to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], as they are expressed in the fruits of the spirit, and these exercised in the relationships, the development of body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] becomes in its proper relationship in the present.

17. (Q)  Please explain the meaning of "even as it was given as to when [[Tabernacle|the tabernacle]] was to be builded - or when it was shown even as in [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s directing when the day when each should stand as a messenger," given in a previous reading.  [See 338-3, Par. 13-A.]
(A)  As has been indicated, each - or this [[Soul|soul]] makes the application personal.  In the expressions as shown in [[Tabernacle|the tabernacle]], in the orders as given for its construction - the size, the shape, the measurements, the figures above the [[Holy of Holies|holy of holies]], the directions of the colors as indicated for the hangings, the manner in which each board was to be set, the manner in which each skin was to be used or dyed - these were not only for the physical protection but for the expressions that would come in the experience of individuals that took the service or the worship there as being a thing within themselves.  Hence became material, emblematical, and the experience of the application of same in the worship there became as a living thing in the experience of the individuals.  [[[Exodus|Exodus]] 25, 26, 27, etc.]

Now the expression as given by Him, the Maker, the Creator of the earth and all therein, was "the day arrives when not in this mountain nor in this temple, but in the hearts of men and women everywhere, is that service, that manner, that means of expression."  [[[John (Apostle)|John]] 4:21]

So as it has been given for the entity in its depicting of those expressions of things in life, those expressions of man's obligations and duties, those expressions that may hold before the beholder that [[Ideal|ideal]], that idea of the fruits of the spirit - as the entity then labors in its giving of itself in expression, it becomes even as those things given by the pattern in the mount as well as the pattern given by [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] that they become as LIVING experiences, not only in the activities of the individuals but create that atmosphere, that [[Vibration|vibration]], that environment for the beholder of those things that ARE expressions of the fruits of the spirit as manifested things in their lives and experiences, see?

18. (Q)  Please state in detail any [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in addition to those in my Life Reading that are important in the present. [See 338-2.]
(A)  These come as the growth of the entity in its activity, in its dealing with the influences in the life.  And as is found ordinarily or the more often, the expressions of same change as the application of self in the relationships to those things that are experiences in the entity's force itself.

As in those experiences that are not given, and which should become as a growth in the experiences of the entity, as has been indicated in the Persian we find not only a necessity but an expression of that which would make for a combining of the best in every influence of many peoples that were under many different thoughts.  Yet those that should come to GROW, these are but naught to expressions we may find in the entity's experience when there began first the decoration of the church in the first of Constantine's concept of the Sistine Choir, [by Michelangelo and others?] that has grown to be rather a seeking in the present; and then that as may be given in the manner and the change that will come into the experience of the entity in these directions will mean a great deal more.

19. (Q)  Did I see [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] during his time here, in order that I may paint Him as I recall Him?
(A)  Not as we might say for that [[Purpose|purpose]], but as these expressions, as these experiences find activity in the expression, then that as may be given out is the outgrowth. Or let's turn it in this manner:

There was the question asked the Master upon the occasion when the entity [338] was acquainted with the Man [Jesus should] not so oft [be] depicted as of sorrows and of grief, but [should] rather [be depicted] in the periods when there were the expressions of power, of might, of the very influences as has been given.  There was the question asked, "Who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?"  The answer, "Neither this man nor his parents, but that the glory of GOD might be manifested TODAY."  See? [[[John (Apostle)|John]] 9:3]

So in self the experience came, the activity in self brings rather then the fulfillment of same.  Depict Him as that in the giving of the Supper.  [Mark 14]  [See 5749-1.]

For there we find not only the shadow of Gethsemane, not only the sunlight of [[Crucifixion|Calvary]], but also the beauty of the glorious [[Resurrection|resurrection]] morn.  For as He gave, "This my body, broken, FOR YOU - This my blood, shed that ye might have life and have it more abundantly."

All of these that came then as the physical suffering, as the man, were pushed aside.  And in His Face there comes that as He gave, "I drink it anew with thee in my Father's kingdom." THAT depict!
