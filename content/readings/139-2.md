# TEXT OF READING 139-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[139-2]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/9/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 525

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by her son-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., La.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition at the present time, with further suggestions for the improvement of same, and you will also answer the questions I may ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had the body here before.  Now, we find the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are somewhat on the improve.  Those suggestions as have been given have not been carried out, to add to that being done those suggestions as were made.  Do that, for this we find would add to the physical betterment of the conditions of this body, see?

3. (Q)  Will it be safe for Mrs. [130] to make the railroad trip from N.O. to N.Y.?
(A)  The physical health must improve considerably from the present existent conditions, but with those improved, as we find this would bring for the body, we find no reason why this should not be beneficial eventually to the body, see? We would say, with the improvement, the body could make the trip, see?

4. (Q)  What may she do to prevent getting so terribly train sick, as she always does?
(A)  Using those properties, as has been given for train sickness; [See 106-7] equal parts lime water and cinnamon water, with the drops as per the amount as given (proportion it so) of [[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium solution and Bromide Potassium solution, for this taken without too much foods will prevent the nausea, train sickness and headaches produced from same, see?

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through for the present.
