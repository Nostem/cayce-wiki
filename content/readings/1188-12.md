# TEXT OF READING 1188-12 M 12

**Reading [[1188-12]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/21/1942  Sex: M  Age: 12  ReadingID: 6644

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office on [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - these conditions here are very fair.

3. There needs to be, with this body, the activities - consistently taken - of the head and neck exercise, especially the eyes.

4. Then occasionally, not often, but occasionally, have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments with particular reference to the supply of energies from the dorsal centers to the optic forces.  This extends, then, from the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal - not so much through the cervical; to be sure, coordinant them, but keep the upper dorsals in alignment.

5. Those corrections that are being made with teeth should be most satisfactory.

6. For this body, take Vitamins B-Complex, One A Day Brand B-Complex; not the others.  And with the diets that are the normal conditions, these should supply energies best for for the unfolding and development of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should I take Sun Ray baths; how many, how often?
(A)  Those BEST are taken in the open, though if there are long periods of dark, damp days, take the sun ray lights, take the exercise under same, only as supplementary to the out of doors or daylight, sunlight activity.

9. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these things, as we find, for the better conditions of this body.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Coulter) Copy to Ass'n file
