# TEXT OF READING 900-26 M 29

**Reading [[900-26]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/24/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5229

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 24th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by [900], himself.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                 [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [900], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the night of January 21st, or early morning hours of January 22nd, also the one had in the early morning hours of January 23rd, 1925, at his Apt. ..., [[New York City|New York City]].  The first was pertaining to Edgar Cayce and his psychic work, the one of 23rd pertaining to consciousness. You will give these in outline and the interpretation, and lessons to be gained from these, and give them in such a manner that will be understandable to the mind of [900].

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, the mind, and the [[Visions|vision]] and [[Dreams|dream]] as had by this body, [900].

3. Now, we find this first [[Visions|vision]] and [[Dreams|dream]] pertaining to that further understanding of the meaning of the psychic work, as pertaining to the mind, and of the conditions in life in the physical plane, and this [[Visions|vision]] given as would be the manner and way in which the manifestations, as seen before in December, would be manifest to the mind of the public, through the resources of the mental capacity of [900].  And through such manifestations there may come that understanding that would give many the vision, or insight, into the greater conditions in life.  And this, in the manner in which this was presented to the mental forces of [900], shows that condition in which such information, as was visioned, will come to many peoples.  And the manner of reception of same, as was seen in the group gathered about the body when the information was given, as how many about the body might partake of that [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] as described to those gathered there.  Study into these conditions, finding their co-relation, and how the development in each must come, that the greater number may gain this insight as is necessary for all to be able to partake of that [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] necessary for the developing of each and everyone in the present earth's plane.

4. As we find in that of the 23rd, in consciousness, this we find again in the edge of Borderland, and a vision of the conditions that exist in the spiritual entity as has been given a vision of to the body, [900].  And as these conditons, as these manifestations, come to this mind, little by little will the consciousness come through, that the body may gain that necessary knowledge of the [[Oneness|One-ness]] of conscious in physical, [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] in physical, conscious in spiritual, [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] in spiritual; all in one, one in all, and only manifests when [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], either spiritual or physical, is able to make each consciousness manifest in the body then occupied by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], so as to be able to manifest same in that plane in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is desirous of manifesting same at that period or time, and that sphere or space.

5. This, then, is given, that the body, [900], through the mental and the consciousness as is reached in the state when the physical consciousness is laid aside, and the consciousness in part becomes a portion of the great whole, of which each consciousness must be able to retain and gain that position necessary for the realization of same, whether in material world or in physical plane, or throughout the spheres of [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] of the physical entity's entity in the spiritual conception.

6. Now, there are many other visions and dreams, and impressions, as have been given to this body during these periods as given:  21st, 22nd, 23rd, January, 1925.  These, however, are given, in their relation to physical, metaphysical, conditions, that the mind may gather that necessary for the developing in the channel the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces are now, through the position the mind entertains and sustains to gather the knowledge necessary for the development, as has been given, for this body.

7. We are through for the present.

[1/24/25 GD's note:  Immediately following the above, before the suggestion was given to awaken him, Edgar Cayce volunteered information on [[China|China]] and the dawning of Christianity.  See it under 3976-3.]

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 1/22/25 Mr. [900]'s letter:  "As per [900-23, Par. 6-A], I
> did have certain [[Dreams|dreams]] 1/21st, but one or two of those
> manifestations which came during the night of 1/21st or early
> morning hours of 1/22nd are unintelligible to me.  I would
> greatly appreciate, therefore, your interpreting these
> manifestations to me - that is, those of the night of 1/21st
> or early hours of 1/22nd, while I was asleep in bed and which
> I saw you in the psychic state in the center of such a
> gathering as we had in Dr. Kraus' office or it might have
> been at Mrs. Bamberger's.  Anyway I seemed to be letting
> people into the gatherings and I had impression lights were
> artificial, electric and somewhat dim.  I can't remember rest
> of [[Dreams|dream]], but it meant something and I'd greatly appreciate
> your helping interpret it and what lesson is to be gained
> therefrom."
> 
> B2. 1/23/25 Letter:  "Also [[Dreams|dream]] had early morning of 1/23rd
> which I cannot recall.
> 
> "Conditions with my mother [106] are clearing beautifully and
> the barriers to our work are disappearing just as predicted
> in [900-23].  I also know now more than ever that God is a
> powerful omnipotent [[Creative Forces|creative force]], whose spirit is in all
> of us, and whose manifestation may make for the receptive
> mind, Life a great, wonderful, livable reality, that unfolds
