# TEXT OF READING 303-30 F 55

**Reading [[303-30]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/27/1942  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 2241

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [303].

3. While conditions continue to indicate the lack of vitality and energy, there are improvements.

4. We would stick rather close to those suggestions as to keeping good eliminations, and supplying the body with the vital energies through the use of the Vitamins A and D and the B-1 complex.  If there is not sufficient assimilated in the foods given, take - then - one a day of the One-A-Day brand and A and D, and One-A-Day B complex.  Take only one a day of each.  Best to take the A and D of an evening, and the B complex at the noon mealtime.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Shall I continue taking the Eno Salts, Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]] as formerly suggested?
(A)  We would keep these, but in a smaller quantity.

7. (Q)  Should I take the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] from such as Dr. Jas. Parker, or is it just as well to take enemas at home?  [James R. Parker, M.D.]
(A)  We would take enemas when necessary, using a colon tube; and take a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] about once a month or once in six weeks.

8. (Q)  Would it be necessary to take any more sweat baths or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  The massages will be well, if these are given as a thoroughly GENERAL relaxing treatment.

9. (Q)  Should the rubs be given with Peanut Oil alone, or combined with Olive Oil?
(A)  Combination here will be the better.

10. (Q)  Should rubs be given down the spine and lower limbs, or over entire body?
(A)  Over entire body will be well, but more down the spine and lower limbs.

11. (Q)  How can I get the cold and wheezing out of my right lung?
(A)  By the building of better energies and resistance with the vitamins.

Do these for the present.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
