# TEXT OF READING 5610-6 F ADULT

**Reading [[5610-6]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/19/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13951

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce this 19th day of January, 1931, location unknown.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5610].  Now, we find that there are the general normal developments under the existent conditions of the body.  There have NOT been the manipulative forces occasionally to care for the normal DEVELOPMENTS as respecting those conditions in the sensory system; that is, a general manipulative treatment - see?  This the cause of, STILL, those PERIODS of disturbance in this area.  These conditions, as we find, are better - but they will continue until there is the proper set-up of CIRCULATION through both the body and the foetus.

2. (Q)  In previous reading, the Forces mentioned a near normal condition of the hearing and activities of the sensory system would ensue after the third month, or when there begins the first movements.
(A)  There IS a nearer normal activity, and will be materially aided will there be the manipulative forces for the whole general system, with the consideration given those centers in the upper dorsal and cervical, and head and neck area - as related to the sensory, and especially the auditory centers.  Let the manipulator know and realize that STIMULI to the circulation from the 2nd and 3rd dorsal, as well as from the 3rd and 4th cervical, will work for the betterment of those conditions in the sensory system, and sensory disturbance - and, as given, when the circulation is stimulated - as is recognized - the manipulations in the dorsal and lumbar will aid in the activities of kidneys, with the general eliminations, with the general tired feeling, and relieve the nausea that comes at times to the body - by the manipulative forces; these will act in the same way and manner for the sensory system.  Follow those rather closely.

3. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be given?
(A)  Once to three times a month.

4. (Q)  Any special one you suggest?
(A)  One that knows how!

5. (Q)  What cause small blood clots in waste matter when blowing nose?
(A)  Lack of coordination in circulation to the whole sensory system.  That in itself should indicate those conditions necessary for establishing of perfect circulation in the body, that is DEPENDENT on body - as well as in self!  To the sensory system, as to the rest of the body!

6. (Q)  Can this be corrected now?
(A)  Do as we have given, if we would correct the conditions?

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
