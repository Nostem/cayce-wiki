# TEXT OF READING 302-10 F 41 (Saleswoman (in brokerage firm)

**Reading [[302-10]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/17/1933  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 2211

Christian (?)) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 17th day of March, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [302], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

..., Avenue, 
Time of Reading                          Apt. 17-G Penthouse, 
4:40 to 5:00 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [302], who is at home, ... Ave., Apt. 17-G (Penthouse), [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., in regard to her mental and material affairs.  You will give any advice counsel or guidance that would be beneficial to her along these lines, and answer the questions which she has submitted as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [302]; this we have had before.

3. Now, as we find, in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body there are many changes since we last had same here.  Most of these have been and are for the betterment of the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

4. And while there are those resuscitations gradually taking place from the renewed activity of the general system, and with the reduction of those forces or elements that made for destructive influences, with the physical and mental activities under which the body goes - or is strained even at times - the use of the Radio-Active Appliance would prove most helpful and beneficial to the general physical conditions of the body.

5. We would suggest that this be used each evening when the body is resting, or when the body returns from the activities or labors and rests; preparing and attaching it to the body for an hour to an hour and a half.  It would be found that this would rest the body much easier, and there would be more vitality and more resistive forces gradually builded.  To be sure, this is not a corrective measure or application other than through assisting the general circulation of the body. And the therapeutic effects obtained by the use of such an appliance would be the revivifying and resuscitating influences that are created by a more equalized and more coordinated circulation.

6. When the anodes are attached, these regulations should be adhered to rather severely:

7. In the first treatment we would attach the first anode to the right wrist and the other anode to the left ankle.  The next day attach the first anode to the right ankle and the other to the left wrist.  The next day to the left ankle and right wrist.  The next day to the right wrist and to the opposite ankle again.

8. Making these changes in this manner makes for the increasing of the circulation by creating the positive or negative pole to the extremities in varied portions of the system, as indicated.

9. For the general health, those conditions to which the body adheres are well; in diet, in activity, in outside exercise and the like; and, as we will find, the body should - with the proper precautions - continue to improve in the general and specific health, or healthy welfare.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Shall I make a change in my business at this time?
(A)  When there is the consideration of changes in associations and connections, as understood by self, there should be careful consideration given as to the needs, as to the desires, as to those conditions that are as necessary elements in the make-up of the associations for and of the body-mental.

There are certain, specific requirements for the body, in its mental attitude, as to the character or kind of remuneration that is necessary from the activities of the body; yet - under the existent strain (as the body has been warned oft) - there is too great an expenditure of self for the ends that to the present have been obtained through the effort expended.

As we find, there are some associations rather in line and in keeping with former interests (and present interests also), in the manufacturing end, where as much effort expended (were the proper associations or connections made for the body) would gain or maintain a great deal more pleasant, more worth while activities - and more remunerative in many ways and manners.  While leaving less channel for the purely speculative interest, intents or inclinations, such associations [with Mr. [257]?] would bring for the body mentally and materially much more satisfactory experiences.

So, if there can be made the proper associations or connections at this time, such changes we would advise - or in the near future; for, as we find, general conditions, general expansions - especially in the fields that offer opportunities for the activities of the body in that direction (that is, in the manufacture, sale, distribution, of either household furnitures or household products) will be bettered.  In this connection we would find a much more pleasant, a much more remunerative activity - in this field.

12. (Q)  Would you advise that I go into the selling of merchandise?
(A)  In the selling of ANYTHING may the body succeed, but ESPECIALLY in this field.

13. (Q)  Along what specific lines?
(A)  These are very specific that we have given here! Household furniture, or the special interests - of course - are in the manufacture, sale or distribution of a certain type of couch, or studio couch, or the like; also those of every class of household furniture, especially those lines in refrigeration that are opening and which are worthy of the best efforts of anyone with the abilities in this line, offer an opportunity, if the connections and associations are made properly.

14. (Q)  Would my contemplated and desired connection be a satisfactory one for the parties concerned?
(A)  Be very satisfactory for ALL concerned, as we find; for this includes refrigeration, and through the efforts of the body may be extended into much broader fields than is being enacted through this connection at the present time - though [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] are being opened for such connections.

15. (Q)  Would it be profitable?
(A)  As we have given.

16. (Q)  Will my present financial condition ease up this year?
(A)  That depends a great deal upon the speculative interest and many associations, and upon the abilities of the body to keep nearer in the better - or the developing rather than retardment in - the general physical health.

17. (Q)  Will money be any easier with me and worries less?
(A)  If the associations or connections are carried in the direction indicated, we would find that it would be so.

18. (Q)  Do you see any change in my residence?
(A)  Not in the immediate future.

19. (Q)  Would you advise a change in residence?
(A)  No.

20. (Q)  Are my affections placed right, [with Mr. S. A.?  See 302-2 & 302-5] and shall I keep on this same path?
(A)  As we find, these are well from many an angle or standpoint.  Associations, connections and relations, are well.

21. (Q)  As I have several matters in the hands of lawyers, please advise if I have chosen the right lawyers.
(A)  These, as we find, are beyond the ken of experimentations; and have reached the stages, in most conditions - as to whether the right or the wrong, has been chosen!  You must abide by what's to be the result!

22. (Q)  Do they look fairly successful for me and my affairs?
(A)  In most instances, very good.

23. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Weigh well those things that have been given.  Show forth self in that way and manner, that in meting out to others - that which is meted will be returned to thee in the same measure.

24. We are through.
