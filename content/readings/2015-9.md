# TEXT OF READING 2015-9 F 2 1/2

**Reading [[2015-9]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/16/1942  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 8549

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [23], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At the home of 
Time of Reading                               her parents 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        on ... Road, 
..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with special reference to the high fever, bite on ankle, the inflammation and swelling of foot and ankle; giving suggestions for corrective measures, answering the questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, this acute condition arises from a bite, but not of any very SEVERE type - though this might become so.

3. There should have been first (and this will now materially aid) this application over the bite and swelling.  Moisten salt or make it wet with [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and put on as a [[Poultice|poultice]], not binding.  Change this about every hour or so until most of the swelling is gone.

4. Then keep the body quiet.

5. Start eliminations with a good emit, as Castoria, Syrup of Figs - the Milk of Bismuth may be added with same to purify the system.  But Milk of Magnesia would be very well to keep down the temperatures.

6. Bathing the body, of course, with the weaker solution of the salt and [[Turpentine|turpentine]] will be helpful - along the spine - but this very weak.  Apply the [[Poultice|poultice]] over the bite and the ankle and foot.

7. Do not give heavy foods, only those that are almost pre-digested, when foods are given.

8. And give plenty of water.

9. These as we find are the better under the present conditions.

10. (Q)  Is the temperature from the bite?
(A)  It is from the whole combination of fright as well as a bite.

11. (Q)  Was this bite from a spider?
(A)  From a bug, not a spider.

Do this as we find, but do it right away.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

Wired as follows:  MOISTEN TABLE SALT WITH [[Turpentine|TURPENTINE]]  APPLY AS [[Poultice|POULTICE]] NOT BINDING OVER BITE AND SWELLING  MASSAGING SPINE WITH WEAKER SOLUTION OF SAME  START ELIMINATIONS WITH CASTORIA  ONLY PREDIGESTED FOODS  PLENTY OF WATER  FULL REPORT AIR SPECIAL
