# TEXT OF READING 1865-1 F 45 (Governess)

**Reading [[1865-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/11/1939  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 8274

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1865], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1646].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1865].

3. Now as we find, the disturbances are rather subtle in their nature, for these are primarily GROWING - or the giving away of active forces to that which is producing INACTIVITY in the impulse of the nerve forces as related to vitality, - in regard to voluntary and involuntary nerve flexes.

4. Thus it becomes a static condition, producing inability of locomotion; or that in the present, when locomotion is attempted, tending more toward a palsied nature.

5. This as we find primarily arises from lack of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity.  It is partially, or impulsely, produced by a disturbance which arose from those activities in the pelvic reactory system.

6. Yet these in the present have not become such that there may not be help yet gained for this body, if there are the correct applications for the creating of the proper impulse in the nerve forces, and the muscular forces as controlled by same.

7. The blood supply indicates the unbalancing of the activities through [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, as well as impoverishment in those directions in which the hormone or plasm in same produces the proper effect to those portions of the nerve plexus and ganglia.  There is the straining, as it were, from the lymph and emunctory activity of that plasm for the feeding of the ganglia between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic system that causes the proper reactions.

8. The nerve system indicates this impoverishment, due to disturbing activities.

9. As we find, the making of application for helpful forces and influences will require changes as various conditions or stages are reached in bringing helpful forces to this body.

10. First, we would use the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that would carry vibratorially into the system, alternately, the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], - one used one day, the other the next, you see.  The attachments would be made as follows:

11. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] (every other day), the small copper plate should be attached at the 4th lumbar axis, while the larger nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes (in the proportions of one grain Chloride of Gold to each ounce of distilled water), would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center.

12. The next day when using the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution, the small plate would be attached at the 9th dorsal center, and the larger plate, through which the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution passes (Spirits of Camphor, commercial strength), would be attached at the LIVER plexus, see?  That is, in the frontal portion of the body, so the electrical forces pass vibratorially through both lobes of the liver.  This would be, then, just below the right breast, but below the edge or at the edge of the lower rib area.

13. Each of these attachments would be made for thirty minutes, every other day - and should be at a regular period.  Do not leave the applicators in the Solutions when not in use.

14. The Appliance (or battery) should be recharged every thirty days, by the renewing of the solutions for recharging, you see.  In this particular instance, the Gold and Camphor Solutions may also be kept for the full period of thirty days, if the applicators are taken out of same and the Solutions are kept covered when not in use.  Hence the new solutions for FORMING the Appliance itself would be used at the end of thirty days, and also then new Gold and Camphor Solutions prepared.  Have at least five ounces of the Solution in the bottle for attaching.

15. After each application of the Appliance, have a thorough rub. This may be done by a neuropath or masseuse, but have the body THOROUGHLY rubbed with an equal combination of Olive Oil and Peanut Oil, especially along the cerebrospinal system, from base of brain to end of spine, across the diaphragm area, and especially in the lumbar and sacral area.  Massage all the body will absorb, thoroughly but gently, into the system.

16. Let a greater portion of the diet be of fruit juices and vegetable juices.  Let at least one meal each day consist principally of these, either raw or cooked - fresh, raw vegetables.

17. No fried foods at any time.  Refrain from those things that are made with corn or cornstarch, though potatoes - and potato peels, for this body - will be very well.

18. Of course, artichoke in ALL its forms would be well to be part of the diet, at least two or three times each week.

19. Have plenty of leafy vegetables, - though only fish or fowl, - no other meats, if meats are taken at all.

20. Not a great deal of pastries of any kind.

21. Whole wheat cereals, or wheat and barley cereals (as Maltex) would be taken.

22. Do these and, after the Appliance has been used for two whole periods of recharge, - that is, for sixty days, - we would give further instructions.

23. Ready for questions.

24. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Dr. Charles Goodman Taylor, ..., [[New York City|New York City]], who is studying information through the readings given on Sclerosis
