# TEXT OF READING 5294-1 M 61 (Navy Food Inspector, Religious

**Reading [[5294-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/26/1944  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 13430

Preference:  None) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [5294], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born July 23, 1883, in Medina, [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how. You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, some other curious folks born in Medina on the same day.

3. We have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now called [5294].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, these we would choose with the desire to be of a help, but [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should realize its [[Purpose|purposes]] in the earth are not that life for itself alone may be of just comfort or for gratifying the idiosyncrasies of the entity, and just because the entity doesn't do particular harm to anyone, nor does he abuse his own person or lead others astray, doesn't mean that he is accomplishing much in the earth.

5. If the [[Purpose|purpose]] is that the individual should glorify God, having [[Ideal|an ideal]], merely living a life that does not bring an incentive to someone else to glorify God is sin and just as vile in His eyes as one who in lack of thought or of judgment would err.  For this is purposefulness of self-indulgence.

6. This is not an abuse to the individual, rather as a warning to make the experiences of the life not only good but good for something, and we will find it will make the rest of the years of the entity's experience in the earth much more worthwhile.

7. That there has come the periods when the entity may take its ease - have ye not read what happened to the man who said in his heart he would do such?  May it not indeed be the same with thee?  Take warning, then.

8. There are abilities, there are urges latent and manifested, but first in self find thine own ideal, spiritually, mentally, physically.  Then study to show thyself approved; that is, act like ye knew what it's all about.  For life is earnest; life is an opportunity to show the appreciation to the divine within, with the divine in thy fellow man, and if ye act in that manner much more joy, much more worth while will be the earthly experience of this entity.

9. Of these, then, take note:  In any form, which will be seen from the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth, which has to do with color, as in decoration, as in wallpaper designing, as in wallboard designing, might the entity bring a great deal of joy, of harmony into the experience of those with whom he might work, or direct such, as a collector of stones that are colored, especially opals, emeralds, moonstone, bloodstone, what may be termed the semi-precious stones.

10. These not merely as a collector, but as one who might prepare or gather such for not merely setting, for each of these have their value in the effect, the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that they each bring about the body.

11. There being, then, individuals who when wearing a fire opal would be hard individuals to deal with when it came to sex. There would be others having different effects.  The same individual wearing or having in the apparel the moonstone might find that it would bring peace, harmony and those tendencies towards spiritual things.  There are those to whom the bloodstone brings harmony, and less of the tendencies for anger; and so with each.

12. Study them, then, with the [[Purpose|purpose]] of acquainting individuals as well as preparing same; as a pendant of the lapis lazuli or the corrosion of copper.  These are well for the entity to study, as well as considering the physical, as well as mental, [[Aura|auras]] of individuals.

13. The [[Aura|auras]] as compared to the stones, these should work in ninety-nine percent of the conditions where these are considered as those things that work with, not against, the colors seen in the [[Aura|auras]]; that is, those which indicate the fire signs in the aura of such should never wear opals, and they will even fade flowers when worn on their bodies.  But the more delicate, as would bring the nature, is preferable.

14. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, not all are given here but that there may be the pattern indicated.

15. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity during the periods following the American Revolution.  The entity was among those who were settlers from the eastern portion of the land and about portions of [[Ohio|Ohio]] where the mound dwellers had builded.

16. Then the entity was a trader in trinkets, in fire water, with the peoples of the land, to attain, then, sufficient for retirement, but when [[John (Apostle)|John]] Foster retired he lasted a very short period.

17. Have a higher purpose, as has been indicated; something creative, something helpful, something which manifests the glory of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].  For in the manner, in the purpose that ye treat thy fellow man, ye treat thy Maker. While there is that tendency to do or die, yet these should be altered.

18. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] land, among those who carried the goods from one portion of the land to another, or a caravan maker, dealing in the linens of [[Egypt|Egypt]], the pearls of [[Persia|Persia]], in the opal, [[Firestone|the firestone]], the lapis lazuli in Indo-China, yea the diamonds and rubies of some of the cities of gold.  These find an attraction for the entity in the experience in the present.  Be not rather as a hoarder but use such, then, of the knowledge of such, in the study as to the helpful force in the experience of thy follow man.

19. The name then was Taian.

20. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], when there was the entering in of the children of promise to the [[Palestine|Holy Land]].  The entity was then of the sons of Hittites who dealt with the sons of Aaron as they prepared the garments of the priest, and the entity supplied the various stones, especially the carbuncle and the agate of that particular land.

21. In the experience the entity was helpful and yet never learned, in that particular period, the value of same in the experience of individuals; not as omens, not as a sign, but, knowing the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the elements that composed such stones and what might be lacking in a body that might bring those changed [[Vibration|vibrations]].

22. As to the abilities of the entity, these have been indicated. Use them; don't abuse them!  Keep in the way that is good, but good for something.  In being helpful to thy fellow man.

23. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

> Navy was food inspection.  To secure the best food, at
> lowest possible price, for boys in Service.
> 
> "My interest in gems was life long, but not active until the
> past 10 years, when I began to plan for retirement.
> 
> "Since my retirement from the Navy a year ago, my time has
> been devoted exclusively to gems.  I feel that I have made
> great progress in gaining an understanding of the problem of
> the therapeutic value of gems, but the experimental work has
> just started.
> 
> "I regard the [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyant]] analysis in my readings most
> remarkable.  While it did not cover all points, it
> accomplished its aim of being helpful to my immediate
> future.  It is a constant source of guidance.
> 
> "Vocational guidance is as necessary at 60 as at 6.
> Retirement is as critical a period as entering school.
> The principles in my reading, I believe, are of very
> wide application today, for so many who are retired are
> perfectly capable of a long span of active, useful life."
>
