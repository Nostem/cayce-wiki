# TEXT OF READING 5706-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[5706-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/10/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14157

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at (?), this 10th day of November, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                       ..., Georgia.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have this body before, you see.  Now, we find the conditions in this body are much improved in many ways from those that we had here before.  We would make some change now for this condition, for we would with this deep [[Massage Therapy|massage]] once each five days [[Massage Therapy|massage]] well into the lumbar and sacral region and along each limb, especially the left, and about the portion of the body, though NOT ON that directly where the abrasion has been, that of balsam of sulphur, see.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this well into the body so we will have the reaction in the nerve, muscles and tissue of the locomotors and all lower portion of the body.  This we will find will tend to make the circulation very much stronger in the capillaries and if properly given by the massage over the body, - other than this you see will not cause abrasions, for it will eliminate, otherwise we may have pimples or abrasions over portions of the body, but do this, do this properly, see, as we have given.  Deep massage, see, and only give this massage of the balsam of sulphur as directed, see.  Well into the groin in the ilium and sacral plexes, along the limbs, especially the left one, see.  Not in the abrasions as is produced on the right side of the lumbar region, but around and about it, see, then so manipulate the body as to produce perfect elimination.  Balsam of Sulphur is prepared by boiling linseed oil and sulphur together.  We are through.
