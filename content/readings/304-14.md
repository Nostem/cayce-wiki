# TEXT OF READING 304-14 M 74

**Reading [[304-14]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/8/1927  Sex: M  Age: 74  ReadingID: 2267

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [304].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [304], and Miss [369].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer any questions regarding same that I may ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are much improved from those as we have had heretofore.  We find the body responding well to those conditions as have been applied through those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and manipulations through the various portions of the body, that have brought conditions to the response in the central radiation of the system itself.  These bring very much better conditions to the body.

4. Were those properties used as the inhalant much in that way and manner that has already been outlined [in 304-8], we would find many conditions would improve much faster - or these may be applied by adding the properties together in water and heating or boiling same.  This will answer in the same way and manner, though is harder to be given the system, but the response will be much deeper.

5. As for the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]], we find these only need to keep those suggestions as have been given for the body, in the diet, in the exercise, in the manipulations given as by self; for the correction as has been made has corrected that condition necessary for the improvement to keep on in the body, if the body will follow out those as has been outlined.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is it necessary for any more treatments of the manipulation from the doctor?  [M. L. Richardson, D.O.?]
(A)  Not NECESSARY for any more treatment, though of course this will only add to the forces as bring about greater resistive forces in the body, but with the exercises as have been outlined for the system, and with the manipulations as given self in the exercises, these will keep UP the correction.  The other will only add to the strength of the system.

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through with this reading.
