# TEXT OF READING 186-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[186-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/6/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 694

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by her brother-in-law, Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Calif. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find there is much that may be corrected in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, yet the abnormal conditions are of the nature that some may be brought only to the normal condition in a physical functioning organism.  The conditions have to do with many conditions of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], and from the pathological and psychological study would be very beneficial to an anatomist, or to a pathologist, for these in the psychological effects would, through the anatomical study, produce in their physical reaction that that would prove a hypothesis of the existent condition as is produced through hereditary and through enviro-mental conditions in their effect one upon the other, also prove to the psychologist that of the study of that as has given the basis of transmigration; though, as is seen in the study under this physical condition, would give rather that as of an individual entity's translation [transition?] through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], [[[Karma|Karma]]:Physical?]  [[[Reincarnation|Reincarnation]]?].  [GD's note:  Mental aberrations, perhaps?]  [It was not indicated by Mr. [195] what the mental condition was.]  [Psychosis?]

2. In the physical body, then, we find these conditions as are existent in the body.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find in an abnormal condition - that is, below normal in count in red blood, yet above normal in its pressure, produced, as we see, by anatomical condition as existent in the system, especially in that as is existent in the heart's action, through the pulmonary retractory forces as are inhibited [exhibited?] in the individual entity.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find the ganglions of many centers abnormal, through the varied condition as is existent in the derogatory condition as exists between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal, especially in those centers relating to the organs of the pelvis, and those of the thorax, for in this relation we find the condition as would be exhibited through the expressions of the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - that is, to each and every individual [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], the mental reaction from same, is at variance with others.

5. IN THE PHYSICAL FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS, in the brain forces very good, for the condition as exists through the forces of the physical body, as would be termed from a normal force of the body itself.

6. In the organs of the sensory system, many of these supersensitive at various cycles of the functioning of organs of the system.

7. In the lungs we find the greater trouble, physically, to the body, for this shows the effect of the blood stream and the nerve pressure in body; yet not organically such as would cause physical conditions to become detrimental to the functioning of same.

8. Digestive system below the normal at times, at other times super-normal in its ability to function.

9. In the liver, spleen, pancreas, these are in the abnormality of being super in their functioning.  Hence the over abundance of energy as is exhibited in the body, through the functioning of each of these their cycle; the over activity of the brain along certain centers, as is shown from the functioning of the brain itself.

10. In the functioning of the organs of the trunk portion of the body, in the blood, we find that in that of the lacteal [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], show the effect of this super heating, as it were, of the body (blood), through this portion, of the taking in of rebuilding or of plasmatic cellular force.

11. In the functioning of the hepatics, and the effect as is produced to the body in this portion, these to the body are not abnormal, to relation with other bodies, or, as would be called for a normal, ABOVE normal.

12. Then, to bring about the better conditions for this body, and to bring the normal forces as near as will be brought through these, would be through the taking of that through the subliminal forces of the [[Mental Body|mental body]], that which will build from within to that to be desired with the physical functioning of the body - that is, subjugate the normal physical body to that of the mind that would direct the physical aspectable functioning of the body [hypnosis - suggestive therapy?], and apply those [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the Radio-Active forces, when ready for sleep, keeping this [[Vibration|vibration]] for at least two and a half to three hours.  AFTER such subjugation - not unless the subjugation is given should the [[Vibration|vibrations]] be equalized in body.  Do that.  We are through for the present.
