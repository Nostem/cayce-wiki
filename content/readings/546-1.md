# TEXT OF READING 546-1 M 33 (Physician [M.D.])

**Reading [[546-1]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/11/1934  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 3866

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 11th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [601], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Providence Hosp. 
Time of Reading                              Room 138, 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [546].

2. As we find, there are serious conditions with this body, from a thinning of the walls of the circulatory system and the general weakening of the condition through the body has allowed - in the upper portion of the cardiac end of stomach - a pulling of the cords as related to the activity of the esophagus.

3. Hence this continued spasmodic condition in the throat and stomach; which activity has so inflamed as to cause an oozing of blood in throat and flooding the general system.

4. We would immediately administer a quarter grain Animated Ash in dry form, not in capsule, and give only two teaspoonsful of water to wash same down.

5. Five minutes afterwards apply the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray over the area of the 3rd and 4th dorsal center, for not longer (when really heated) than one and a half minutes; at least thirty-eight inches from body.

6. This, as we find, will almost immediately stop spasmodic condition, creating coagulation, releasing more oxygen for the system.

7. Very close attention will be required until the body has gained its strength sufficient to meet the emergencies.

8. We would on the next day repeat the dose and the application.

9. Also on the third.

10. Then skip a day.

11. And with the general applications to meet the necessities and emergencies that arise, for stimulation to the circulation, we should overcome this IMMEDIATE condition.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Does he have an injured blood vessel?
(A)  As indicated.

14. (Q)  What should be wired to Mrs. [601], the friend who requested this information?
(A)  To obtain the Animated Ash immediately and administer as given.  [See 546-1, Par. R1.]

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [601]
"    " Ass'n file

[GD's note:  Information was wired immediately to Mrs. [601].]
