# TEXT OF READING 982-2 F 34

**Reading [[982-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/13/1935  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5951

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of September, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [982], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [982]; this we have had before.

2. Conditions, as we find them, are somewhat better in some ways.  The greater quietude for the body is helpful at this time.

3. There has been the tendency for the better coagulations.  And there needs to be, as we find, the proper drainages set up in the system; and thus prevent any recurrent conditions in the formations of plethora in portions of the organism - or the forming of what would become tumorous in its nature.

4. As we have indicated, such drainages may be had not only by the proper [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations and adjustments but by the properties which would aid in purifying the blood supply.

5. The taking of so great a cathartic prevents there being the proper coagulation.  This, as we find, excites the olfactory or the emunctory and lymph circulation - through the intestinal tract.  This prevents the normal flow of gastric forces.

6. But if those properties indicated will be taken they will form with the blood supply so that the effect upon the hemoglobin, upon the urea, upon the hormones, is more in keeping with the normal system.

7. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations as indicated, with these properties - and also the addition now of electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]], as we find, would bring the near normal reaction and prevent the recurrent conditions.

8. These we would do.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Did the distractions during my first appointment account for the fact that the open wound was not mentioned in my reading?
(A)  The open wound was known; the cause of the open wound was known.  The conditions described were the general reactions that existed in the system, as we found them.

11. (Q)  Could another operation be avoided?
(A)  As indicated, the recurrent conditions may be prevented; also there may be added those elements in the system so that the present conditions will find better reaction and the [[Healing|healing]] that must ever be of the internal, of the spiritual, may be awakened.

12. (Q)  Should I continue the laxative each night as has been my custom?
(A)  This is necessary at times.  But begin with those [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, and the electrical forces from the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] - which would be about twice or three times a week, so that the coagulations, the hemoglobin, the activity of the effluvia in the blood supply will be purified and NOT make for great strains.  Then we can gradually reduce the quantity of cathartics necessary.

13. (Q)  Where should the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] be applied?
(A)  Along the cerebrospinal system is more preferable; or the area from the 9th dorsal to the 3rd and 4th cervical.

14. (Q)  Should I begin immediately with the treatments suggested in the reading?
(A)  The sooner there is the beginning, the more there may be the building up and the prevention of disturbances later.

15. (Q)  Any other advice for my welfare?
(A)  These we would do, as indicated.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self (two copies, one for Dr. Dobbins, so he can add the ultra-violet treatments to the osteopathic as given before)
"    " Ass'n file
