# TEXT OF READING 5622-2 F 51

**Reading [[5622-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/7/1930  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 13986

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Wigal, Mrs. [5622] and daughter [5621], and Miss Wright.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  In some respects we find conditions are showing the response of the applications as have been made for corrective measures in the system.  There is needed more time, and more of those applications as has BEEN outlined, would the corrections be of a permanent or of a real beneficial nature.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What foods should this body have for perfect assimilation and elimination?
(A)  This might be as a sample, though necessarily these should be changed or altered to SATISFY the cravings of food for the body:

Of mornings - these should be of gruels, and of those of the fruit juices - but never any apples, or that which will produce fermentations.  Peaches of certain characters or varieties may be taken in moderation.

In the noon - these should be rather of the vegetables that grow above the ground.

In the evenings, there may be those of the meats that have the JUICES - rather than the flesh, and those of the vegetables that carry weight; not too much starch, but that act WITH the gastric juices that make for the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] reactions in system.

3. (Q)  What laxative should be taken?
(A)  Will the manipulations and the proper diet be taken, there will only be the necessity of laxatives occasionally. These should be rather of the oils, with agar - or those that will aid the juices in the system to act in a normal manner; as an alternative between those of the caroid variety, or of the agar variety, WITH the oils that are as lubricants - rather than those that excite the gastric juices.  The olive oil, which the body will assimilate, will build as food values for the digestion and assimilating system; but be sure there is not so much taken at a time as to cause segregations [regurgitations?] within the system, but just that quantity as will be assimilated with the gastric juices of the system, whether as of that in the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] portion of system or passing through the acid reactions.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Wigal
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self

> R4. 11/24/30 Mrs. [5622] submitted questions for her reading:
> "...It means so much to know that you good people are helping
> me that I truly appreciate it...  What is the trouble with
> the pancreas?  Is it operative?  If not, what treatment is
> advised?  Specify what foods should be taken.  Is raw fruit
> harmful?  ...Little did I think when I wrote you recently
> that I would have to write you so soon about myself.  I had a
> serious attack of acute indigestion and the doctor insisted
> upon an X-ray.  They found a very cloudy pancreas, which they
> say is causing the trouble.  I don't know just what they will
> advise, but I will do nothing of any moment until you can
> give me a reading.  I feel rather upset about my condition
> and will so greatly appreciate a reading whenever you find it
> possible..."
