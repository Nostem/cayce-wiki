# TEXT OF READING 129-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[129-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 295

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of December 1923, in accordance with request made by self, via her sister, Mrs. [2790].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  There have been many changes in the body since last that we had it here.  Some of the conditions that we have to deal with have made some change in the body.

2. We would take those properties in the system that will give the [[Healing|healing]] forces and produce coagulation in the blood supply, and these would be found in these, for this body at the present time.

Ragweed 6 ounces, added to 8 ounces of water, and reduce to 4 ounces by simmering.  Strain.  While warm, add:  (when it is dissolved and cut in alcohol)

Balsam of Tolu...............3 drams,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] ...........20 minims, with
Extract of Cayenne...........2 minims.

3. The dose would be half teaspoonful in half glass of water taken 3 times each day.

4. For condition where the abrasions are, those [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with equal parts of Plantain Root Tea, and oil of Butter-fat, in and about the place, not direct upon the abrasion until the inflammation is reduced.  Do that.
