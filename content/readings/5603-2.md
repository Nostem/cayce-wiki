# TEXT OF READING 5603-2 M 13

**Reading [[5603-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/1/1930  Sex: M  Age: 13  ReadingID: 13931

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [454].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

In his room, 
Time of Reading                     ... Military Academy, 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Cornwall-On-Hudson, N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5603], this we have had before.

2. Conditions are very good physically with the body.  There are to be expected those conditions to arise, with the variations in the temperamental effect that changes of seasons, as well as the change in environmental forces, produce for a DEVELOPING body; but were those applications as has been given applied in the MANNER given, in the consistent and PERSISTENT manner, we may expect the conditions to respond - for they will; but when these are kept only haphazardly, temporarily, or altered or changed in diet, or in activity - until the system has had the opportunity to adjust itself - there may be expected recurrent conditions that may SEEM, for the time being, UNSATISFACTORY - but will these be adhered to, these SHOULD - and WILL - bring desired results.  Ready for questions.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
