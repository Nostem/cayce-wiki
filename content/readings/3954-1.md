# TEXT OF READING 3954-1 F 71

**Reading [[3954-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/5/1943  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 11600

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3954], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. Charles Clark and Mrs. Albin C. Bro. via the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Born June 15, 1872, in [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois. 
Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3954].

3. In interpreting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we are shown here, these we would interpret in this manner:  Not with the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] that we might add much to the abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], but as to give somewhat of assurance - that in that ye have manifested may be fully expressed the fourth commandment to that ye have sought and do seek.  Not thine own way but His, that He has need of thee in whatever channel ye find thyself, to contribute much to those activities that add life, light, joy to others.  For as expressed in thee, ye grow in beauty, in [[Grace|grace]], in mercy.  Thus ye become aware of being a living [[Soul|soul]]; thus enjoying - even under tribulations, His [[Grace|grace]], His mercy, His love.  For ye indeed do manifest His love in the earth, in that ye forgive in the same degree and manner in which ye ask to be forgiven. These are principles and laws in thy experience.

4. Thine own activities or [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth have had and do have much more to do with that application ye make in the present of thy thought, thy tenets in the truth, than thy [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in other consciousnesses or in other dimensions.

5. Thus ye have grown.  For it was in the earth that error first came to God's children, called children of men.  Thus in the earth ye have met, ye are meeting, ye are manifesting that beauty, that [[Grace|grace]], that long-suffering, that brotherly kindness, that gentleness which has indicated and does indicate the sowing of the seed of the spirit.  Know in self this, ever:  Ye may only sow the seed, ye may only cultivate for self.  Ye cannot cultivate the sword of truth, ye cannot cultivate the fruit for a brother, for a son, or for a friend.  Ye can sow seeds and work them in self, but God gives the increase.  He adds those that should be added from the activities of their own opportunities, such as are worthy of becoming stars, yea, even as blossoms in the garden of God, in the garden of love.

6. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth:

7. As indicated from the abilities of the entity, in the days when [[Angels|Raphael]] made for activities in the earth, ye were in close associations there, that were at times even inspirational for those activities.

8. Thus the beauties of nature, the abilities to depict the beauties of nature, are a part of thine own heritage.  And lucky indeed may be those who may some day have the works of thy hands.  For they should be one day almost as precious as [[Angels|Raphael]]'s, in the eyes of those who really enjoy God's pictures of nature.

9. In the experience ye manifested hardships and ye manifested glory.  Thus in the present, while there have oft been contributions to the sick, the needy, the wayward, as indicated in thine own thoughts and [[Purpose|purposes]], these experiences have been far from thee.

10. Then fret not thyself over others, but keep the faith.  Walk in the Way.  Keep the lights burning ever, and others will find the Way - who in the present appear to be far in arrears with appreciations of the glory and love of the heavenly Father.

11. Yea, pray oft for those who have passed on.  This is part of thy consciousness.  It is well.  For, God is God of the living.  Those who have passed through God's other door are oft listening, listening for the voice of those they have loved in the earth.  The nearest and dearest thing they have been conscious of in earthly consciousness.  And the [[Prayer|prayers]] of others that are still in the earth may ascend to [[Throne of God|the throne]] of God, and [[Angels (Revelation)|the angel]] of each entity stands before [[Throne of God|the throne]] to make intercession.  Not as a physical [[Throne of God|throne]], no; but that consciousness in which we may be so attuned that we become one with the whole in lending power and strength to each entity for whom ye speak and pray.

12. For, where two or three are gathered together in His name, He is in the midst of them.  What meaneth this?  If one be absent from the body, He is present with His Lord.  What Lord?  If you have been [[Ideal|the ideal]], that one to whom another would pay homage, you are then something of the channel, of [[Ideal|the ideal]].  Then thy [[Prayer|prayers]] direct such an one closer to that throne of love and mercy, that pool of light, yea that river of God.

13. Before that the entity was in the earth when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the land, among those that were of the holy women. In the beginning the entity was among that group of hired mourners at the passing of Lazarus.  Becoming acquainted with [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] there, having the closer association with [[Virgin Mary|Mary]], Martha, Lazarus, [[John (Apostle)|John]], James, the other [[Virgin Mary|Mary]], and [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] the Mother after [[Crucifixion of Jesus|the Crucifixion]], brought the entity close to those activities that made the entity ever as one to whom many came for counsel.  Many rely upon just being in the presence of the entity, that they might gain something from those activities, those words that the entity gives expression to.  Yet the entity is active in many realms of activities, as it was [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] in the name Martia.

14. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when there were those activities in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice.  There the entity aided those who would today be called the physicians. The entity was close in association with that one who has been the companion in the present experience.  Thus there was brought a bond of sympathy, love, hope and confidence that is still a part of the experience.  Keep then the faith.

15. The entity gained throughout that [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  Thus may the entity, if it will but look within the heart of self, know that the study of self, the study of self's [[Purpose|purposes]], and analyzing same in the light of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] Jesus, may enable one to find the beauty of love, the joy of love, the joy of living in the past and in the present.  But look ever upward, onward, to that new day in which all will come before the throne of grace and mercy.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What does my Heavenly Father want me yet to accomplish in this world?
(A)  Just sow the seeds of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]].  You needn't plow them up, you needn't try to cultivate them for others. Only cultivate them in thine own life.

18. (Q)  How can I best help my six children to become part and parcel of the Kingdom of God on earth?
(A)  By just living and being that ye would ask each of them to be.  Better by precept or example.  Not by what you say but what you are.  These are the things that will grow most in the experience of those to whom ye have given and do give spiritual and mental help.  For as the entity has found, there has been only the need to express the desire and it has come to pass in thy experience.  This is the very nature, the very practice of that He has given; that what ye ask in His name, believing, is done already.  Because it doesn't happen today doesn't indicate that it won't happen tomorrow. Because tomorrow never comes, unless today is filled with grace and love.

19. (Q)  What is meant by learning [[Law of Moses|the Law]] of Love?  How may I do this?
(A)  It is that ye make applicable in thy daily experiences. "Love me" is ever the command.  "A new commandment I give, that ye love one another even as I have loved you."  It is the willingness to sacrifice all of self, even the abilities of self, that others may know the Lord better.

20. (Q)  Why was I so recently brought back to health, after nearly [9/43] passing into the "Better Land"?
(A)  Why question ye the Father's purpose?  Rather let it be, "Use me, oh Father-God, where thou seest I may serve thee better."  But as indicated to the entity these are the assurances that He takes thought of thee and has need of thee in the lives of those who are to control the thoughts of many.

21. (Q)  How have I been associated in the past with my present friend Lillian Beath?
(A)  In the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] and in [[Egypt|Egypt]] there were close associations.  In [[Egypt|Egypt]] ye were rather close and helpful one to the other.  The associations in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] might be questioned in some portions, but parallel those.

22. (Q)  With my son, [4792] - how can I best help him?  [GD's note:  Mr. [4792] did not have a personal reading.  This number was given merely to preserve his identity.]
(A)  In the Egyptian land where ye contributed and added to the light through which, in which, he became the active force in tempering the lives of others.  In adding to it in the present, speak gently, never harshly.  For, some day he may come to thee yet in humbleness thanking you for what ye have been.

23. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
