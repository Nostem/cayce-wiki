# TEXT OF READING 1898-2 M 44

**Reading [[1898-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/26/1940  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 8330

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of March, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1898], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, conditions are improved in some directions since last we had the body here; that is, there is better ability for resistances.  However, more acute conditions have arisen with the disturbance in the gall duct area.

4. This upsets the system, causing weaknesses, indigestion, more of gas, lack of proper eliminations, and the general irritation and debilitation all over, - or the feelings of same.

5. As we find now, during this next period, or for a while, we would leave off the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

6. And twice a week, for at least two weeks, we would have the general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments as would be given at Reilly's.  First there would be the [[Hydrotherapy|cabinet bath]], you see, with the temperature (dry heat) raised not too much but just sufficient to start the perspiration.  Then have the thorough massage; then a thorough [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].  Afterwards we would have the [[Hydrotherapy|sitz bath]] and the thorough rubdown with pine oil; or pine oil would be in the water when the bath is given, and than a thorough rubdown, followed by a little of alcohol or the like.

7. After two weeks, these may be made farther apart, - that is, a week apart, see?  But as long as there is the show of mucous, and the effects of the discharges shown in the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], we would keep these up.

8. But for one hour each evening for three days preceding each of the first four hydrotherapy treatments, we would apply - at home - the heavy [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver and gall duct and the whole of the right side.  Use at least three thicknesses of flannel, saturated in the oil or wrung out (lightly) of the oil, not merely the oil poured on.  Apply as warm as the body can well stand, and then the electric pad or heat applied; not so hot as to burn or cause disturbance, of course, but this kept each evening for an hour - preferably an hour and a half - for at least three days.  Then have the first general hydrotherapy treatment in the manner indicated; then have three more evenings of applying the Packs, then another hydrotherapy treatment, and so on until at least four of the treatments have been given.  Then, when these are made a week apart, the Packs would be used only ONE evening before the massage and [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] would be taken, see?

9. After a period of four or five weeks, we would then have the GENERAL [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments again.

10. As to the diet, - keep away from too much sweets, of course.

11. Do not be OVERACTIVE, - but it is not necessary that the body give way entirely to non-activity - unless it is during the first week or two weeks of these applications indicated to relieve the acute conditions in the gall duct area.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What causes uncomfortableness at times in right testicle?
(A)  This is rather a sympathetic condition, than an acute condition.  With the massages and the some attention given to the prostate, it will be found that this will disappear. This is from pressures, nerve pressures from the general irritation in the gall duct area.

14. (Q)  Are there gall stones?
(A)  As the irritation indicates, there is some sand and gravel and some stones that will pass, if there is the use of these applications outlined.

15. (Q)  Is my continued ill health due to any organic trouble?
(A)  Well, this is organic enough, and troublesome enough! but if these suggestions will be adhered to, we should relieve these conditions!

16. (Q)  What is reason for my waking up in the night?
(A)  Nervousness from conditions indicated.  Do as we have outlined here, if we would bring the better conditions for this body.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file (See extra sheet of directions [which were enclosed] for Reilly's, R.C.A. Bldg.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)
