# TEXT OF READING 2-16 M 49

**Reading [[2-16]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/1/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 17

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, Mrs. Wigal and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2] - this we have had before.  Now, we find there has been somewhat of the tendency of a hindered assistance to the physical conditions, yet - will those of the proper drainages as for eliminations in every sense be kept, and those stimulations from the manipulations as given, or corrective manipulations osteopsthically, ranging from those of the 5th and 6th dorsal to the head, especially - these be stimulated, for the circulation - also that those of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the [[Visions|vision]] itself, as may be received through those of the eye to the optic centers STRENGTHENED, or increased in the active forces - these will MATERIALLY aid, and - again, as it were - have the active forces in a building up.  The fume baths, to be sure.  These will set the active forces in the system as to replenishing nerve energy, by the draining off of poisons from the system, or accumulations as have been; while the stimuli through manipulation will turn energies to those of the central circulation to the head, and to the sensory system, as to aid same.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What should be done about teeth?
(A)  If they were all out, would be much better!

3. (Q)  Should Degnen's lens be continued as before?
(A)  These should be stimulated, and they should be recharged.  If you will lay same in the sun, it will recharge same.  Don't leave them there too long!  In the positive sunlight three to five minutes.  In reflected sunlight from five to ten minutes.

4. (Q)  Should they be charged each day?
(A)  Every second or third day will be sufficient, but these should be WORN each day - or even twice each day - a dividing of the time.  Let the body, when same is given, be where there is not strong light, or let there be not too strong a light following same.  The body should rest during that period.  Keep out in the open as much as is possible, and keep hopeful, helpful, and mindful that the conditions WILL be corrected, will the whole accord of the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] be directed in that direction.

5. (Q)  How long should the glasses be worn at a time?
(A)  From twenty to sixty minutes.

6. (Q)  How often should fume baths be given?
(A)  Until, and as often as the strength and vitality allows, even to where - seemingly - it tends to weaken, but DO NOT allow same to become a DRAIN on the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], see? that is, we will find there will be periods when the body may take them oftener than at others, or they may be given until there is seemingly a reflex from same as tends to weaken the body, but the reactions MUST be set up, see?

7. (Q)  How often should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  Every other day.

8. (Q)  Should prescription be continued?
(A)  For the present, yes.

9. (Q)  Is it necessary to continue [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over eyeballs and along spine?
(A)  Along SPINE will be well.  We wouldn't over eyeballs at present, though they will necessarily need it a little later - when we start that of a secretion as may come from eyes.  Be well, too, to bathe the eyes each morning with a weak solution of an antiseptic for same, see?

10. (Q)  Should diet be changed, or the same as before?
(A)  That of a general building diet, which tends towards those of relaxing the body and keeping away from gases and from constipation.  We are through for the present.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self to send home
