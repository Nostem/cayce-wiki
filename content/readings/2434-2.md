# TEXT OF READING 2434-2 F 65

**Reading [[2434-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/8/1943  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 9268

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Miss [1747], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2434].

3. As we find, these are rather acute conditions, and unless there are changes may become rather serious.

4. We find that these arise from an [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] in the system, and the lack of that coordination with the eliminating system of the kidneys carrying off that they should - and the incoordination between kidney and liver elimination.

5. Thus the stress upon the heart and lungs, in circulation.

6. We would administer, daily, at least, two ounces of watermelon seed tea.  Prepare this by pouring two or two and a half ounces of boiling water over a tablespoonful of crushed or ground watermelon seed, and allowing to steep as ordinary tea.  Do not boil the seed, but pour the boiling water over same, see, and allow to steep.  Strain then and drink.

7. And then in the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, stimulate better activity of the eliminations from the 6th dorsal center. This should be from the left side and down, for the relieving of the tension there.

8. Then keep the body quiet, and keep up general eliminations well.

9. Keep up the tea two or three days, then leave off.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What is the condition of the kidneys?
(A)  As just indicated, this is an incoordination between the eliminating systems.  Thus the necessity of that character or quality of nitre as may be obtained from watermelon seed tea.

12. (Q)  Does this cause the pain in back of head?
(A)  Causes pain in back of head, as this comes to part of that circulation of heart, lungs, liver, kidneys, and naturally a block in the 6th dorsal is the area of the pressure on the brain, for there the coordination between kidneys and the liver enters to the superficial and deep circulation.

We would also, of course, put a counter-irritant back of the neck.  Musterole or Mustard to redden, not blister.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Daughter (one for Dr. Smith [Arthur E. Smith, D.O.] - Air Mail, Special) Copy to Ass'n file

(Obtain watermelon seed from a good seed store.)
