# TEXT OF READING 5215-1 F 51 (Elementary School-Teacher,

**Reading [[5215-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/12/1944  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 13351

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Miss [5215], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by book, [[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  As we find, there has existed for some time back a disturbance through the alimentary canal that has periodically brought on a great disturbance throughout the whole of the system; upsetting especially the liver, and conditions for these have been as an inflammation in the colon.

3. These arise, as we find, from the effects of influenza, or flu of the intestinal tract, especially the colon; colitis, inflammation in the area of the ascending and prolapsus in the descending colon.  This has not entirely been eliminated and the effects of the poor eliminations causing neuritic and arthritic tendencies in the extremities.

4. We would first prepare as this:  a fusion of Wild Ginseng, 2 ounces; fusion of Wild Ginger, 1/2 ounce; this we would add to three ounces of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin, with one-half ounce of grain alcohol.

5. Shake this together and take one-half teaspoonful every four hours.

6. Begin with a relaxing treatment osteopathically administered through the lumbar and sacral areas and make those specific corrections which will be found necessary in the 6th dorsal centers, coordinating, of course, the brachial centers and cervicals with corrections made.  It would require about sixteen to twenty-four of such adjustments.

7. Then be mindful of the diet that there are not too many sweets; no foods prepared with much vinegar or such natures.

8. Doing these, we will find that we will have much bettered conditions for the body.

9. Do keep the eliminations well.  These would be preferably corrected by a series of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations to clarify the whole of the colon area.  Use all of these with [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt as a purifier and [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an antiseptic in the last rinse water.  Do not give these more often than ten days apart; after the clearing of colon from mucous, then at least thirty days apart until there is no mucous in the stools. And we should find much bettered conditions for this body.

10. We are through with this reading.

2 copies to self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] of your [[Free Will|choice]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
