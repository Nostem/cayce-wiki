## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/27/21 Newspaper clipping:
> 
> SUMNER EXCITED AGAIN OVER OIL
> 
> Gallatin, Tenn., Jan. 27 [1921?] - (Special) - Excitement has
> again broken out in Sumner county over the oil situation.  It
> is reported that drillers struck oil a few days ago on the
> land of Martin Doss in the Twelfth district near Pleasant
> Grove.  The production per day has not as yet been reported.
> 
> Henry Orman of Gallatin, manager of the Normandy Oil Company
> of Bowling Green, Ky., reports that his company has just
> brought in a well, the oil shooting more than a hundred feet
> above the derrick.  This well is 150-barrel capacity.
