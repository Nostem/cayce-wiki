# TEXT OF READING 4659-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4659-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/28/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12583

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [...], his sister.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4659]''s sister and others.]

Time of Reading                           Westbrook Hospital, 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         Richmond, Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find the causes of the conditions as are produced in the functioning of the nerve system, and the coordinating of the mentality, and its actions toward the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are deep-seated, caused by a pressure and the lack of coordination in certain of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as produce, with the functioning of other organs, that pressure in the system necessary for the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal and the dissemination of the reasoning forces with the normal sensory organism of the body.  This produced by this pressure and by the taking into the system of properties that caused the lack of the body furnishing sufficient of the elements to produce the proper metabolism in the body.  With the destroying of this metabolism and of the katabolism, the nerve reaction in sensory and in the sympathetic produced an over enlarged sympathetic reaction, without dissemination in sensory. Hence the conditions as have brought about that of nerve reaction to the point of wherein the differentiation is below the normal, while the physical functioning of many organs remain near normal.

4. To bring about that in the system which will bring the proper coordination and the proper effectiveness of the nerves being centered in their proper manner will take time and effectual operation of that necessary to bring in the system those building properties that will bring about this coordination.

5. Through this means, then, should the first beginning be made (though we see little effectual work being carried on through the present surroundings.  It would necessitate, then, a changing of these conditions to bring about the more effectual normalcy for this body):  Give into the system those properties as would be found in [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] - fifteen (15) grains to fifteen (15) ounces of distilled water, with ten (10) grains [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] - beginning the dose with one (1) drop taken three (3) times each day, each day increasing the amount, until at least eight (8) drops is being taken.  Then begin again by skipping one (1) day, and with one (1) drop again, and so on.

6. At the same time give, through that of the Radio-Active Appliance, that [[Vibration|vibration]] of the Nitrate of Silver, in the ten percent (10%) solution, with alcohol added, through the anode of the [[Vibration|vibration]] to the umbilicus, see?  changing the positive anode from ankle to wrist each evening.  This should be applied for at least one (1) hour each evening.  Some nausea will be produced by the combination of the [[Vibration|vibration]] of these metals in the system, yet this will be necessary to cleanse the system and reestablish through the forces in the blood that of the proper vibration, would we bring reaction to the nerve centers of the whole cerebro-spinal system, bringing about a perfect coordination between the brain forces in the body, the sympathetic system, and the sensory organism - for all are involved in the condition.

7. When this has been carried on for at least thirty to sixty (30 to 60) days, we would give further instructions - for, as the changes come, those conditions to meet the various forces that are as those of central or local conditions are to be met - but the seat or the beginning must be made through that of the nerve system and the blood supply to same, would we bring about the normal forces for the body, first - then we will give those as the changes come for the local conditions and for the changes.  Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How long has this trouble been coming on?
(A)  Been coming on since that condition as existed in the indigestion in the body, and since the body really reached that age of puberty - for this began with these conditions, see?

9. (Q)  Will this body be normal after following these treatments?
(A)  Should be normal within one year, would these be followed out closely.

10. (Q)  What produced this pressure and caused this trouble?
(A)  First was produced by a pressure in the region of the brush end of the cerebro-spinal system.  Then, taking mercury in the system brought about this retraction in the whole nervous system, with a nerve break-down, see?  inability of coordination.

11. (Q)  Does the body realize his condition?
(A)  Only partially, for there is not the full coordination between the sympathetic and the sensory system.  Rather as a dilatory condition to the body.  At times he wakes to the realization, especially following those of hallucinations to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as regarding other people's attitude towards the body, see?

12. (Q)  Where would you advise him to be taken?
(A)  To some place where the body may be looked after closely and personally, under the supervision of one that would follow these directions as have been given.

13. (Q)  Would it be best to remove him at once?
(A)  The sooner the change is made, and these conditions followed, the sooner would be the recuperation to the body - for, as it is, only rest and quiet is really that being applied to the body, with the hopes of the system adjusting itself - which CANNOT be, without that being applied to the system as is necessary for the perfect coordination and re-vibration of the nerve centers - for, as we will see, it will be necessary to apply manipulations later, and other medicinal properties, as the changes come about.

14. We are through for the present.
