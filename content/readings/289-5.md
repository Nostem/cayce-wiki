# TEXT OF READING 289-5 M 49

**Reading [[289-5]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/11/1936  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 1951

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of January, 1936, in accordance with request made by his daughter, Mrs. [578], over phone to [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], through Active membership of his wife, Mrs. [413], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 to 12:10 Noon Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [289]; this we have had before.

2. While there are some changes, as we find, conditions are still rather serious - and are to be reckoned with.

3. If the body, as we find, would follow those suggestions that have been indicated, these WOULD make for further CORRECTIVE forces.

4. The arising of the temperature again is produced by the lack of the cooperation or coordination with the eliminating channels to the lungs and the respiratory system, where the greater portion of the congestion is caused.

5. We would find that, still, the use of the Antiphlogistine would be well, in such a way as to keep DOWN this - IF the eliminations are kept open!  If they are not kept open, then this - to be sure - makes for hardships upon the whole of the system.

6. The rubbing of the lower portion of the spine, of course, or across same, with the alcohol and the olive oil is helpful.

7. We would CONTINUE with the cathartics that have been indicated for the production of the activity with the eliminating system.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is there anything the doctor is doing that is interfering, or helpful or harmful?
(A)  Whenever there are sedatives, this only makes for a greater congestion in the eliminating system - unless those properties are taken to cause this to become active. Otherwise, very good.

11. (Q)  Please advise those who wait on the body as to how to get him to take the Liver Regulator and adhere to the other treatments.
(A)  If he won't, he won't!  Then he must pay the consequences!

Just insist - and have those done.

12. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do this, as has been indicated - as we find, is best for the body.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [413] - thru [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]]
"    " Ass'n file
