# TEXT OF READING 2094-3 M 70

**Reading [[2094-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/7/1940  Sex: M  Age: 70  ReadingID: 8739

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent. [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of June, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2094], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. There are improvements, and there are discouragements with the applications which have been made.  The body finds periods when there is a great deal of upsetting, and then periods when there are bettered conditions.

4. We find that there is the lack of the plasm which makes for the nerves' reaction, - or there is a gradual destroying of nerve impulses, which causes the muscular forces, tendons, and general activity, to become at times more and more disturbing, - or the multiple sclerosis.

5. This as we find, then, is the basic cause of disturbances.

6. The body feels that the character of the acid upsets the system.  It should not, - but this is a matter of mental reactions to the character, or manner in which it is taken.

7. Then, we would leave this off, - if there is the desire to take same in the former way and manner.  For, better assimilation is needed - as we have indicated.

8. We would continue with the use of the Appliance carrying Gold into the body vibratorially.

9. We would keep these consistently, for these offer - with the general correction osteopathically - the better activities for the body, and the improving of the conditions.  For, the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of Gold to the body should aid the assimilating system, not only to take FROM that assimilated more of those elements that aid to build bettered conditions in the nerves, but the stamina of the nerve plasm should be aided by the low electrical forces carrying this solution into the system.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Please explain why the Acigest has the irritating effect.
(A)  Just as indicated, it is more a matter of the mental self, and because of the desire to keep same in the form that it has been used.

13. (Q)  Why do all my symptoms seem worse instead of better, although I am following the advice and going to Dr. Dobbins twice a week?
(A)  As indicated, the body is really better; though there is the need for creating some inflammation that there may be better assimilated from the active forces within the body those elements which will aid in strengthening the nerve plasms of the body.

This has not attacked the spinal cord plasm, but more the locomotion and the superficial portion of the nerve plasm's circulation.

14. (Q)  Why do [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments seem to produce a sensation of burning throughout lower body and legs?
(A)  They should; for when there is brought the nerve impulse to the plasms or centers or bursa along the areas, this then tends to take from the system that which would supply strength to the nerve centers themselves.

15. (Q)  Is this condition repairable; that is, can a normal condition be restored?
(A)  As we have indicated, if there is consistency and persistency in the applications for the conditions, we may repair these disturbances.  This will take time, it will take patience, it will take persistency and consistency.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
