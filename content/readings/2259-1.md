# TEXT OF READING 2259-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2259-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1921 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/26/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8987

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the McAlpin Hotel, B''way and 34th St., [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y., this 26th day of March, 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; G. K. Nicodemus, Conductor; (?), Steno. Miss [2259].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. GKN:  Now Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep, a quiet, peaceful and restful sleep.  All the organs of your body will function perfectly.  You will be normal in every way.  You will hear me what I say and answer the questions I shall ask you, slowly and distinctly, so that we may get every word. Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep and you have before you the body of Miss [2259] who is at room 2154 McAlpin Hotel, [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.  You will see this body and go over it carefully and tell us what the trouble is, if anything, and what to do for it to make it normal again.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are some conditions abnormal to this body, [2259] we are speaking of, that to other bodies would be normal.  We find there are some things that are normal in the physical that to another body would be abnormal.  We begin here with the circulation or the life blood of the body to give the conditions as we find them here in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

3. We find the mental and spiritual developed beyond average so that the mental faculties are beyond the average in proportion to [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit force of bodies of this kind.  But in the physical we find the blood supply has lost some of its elasticity in being capable of taking on new force in the system to rebuild or carry the particles or units from which certain portions of the body should be rebuilded, that is, the hemoglobin itself in the blood becomes of such a character that it carries not sufficient of the properties in the system.  In other words, we find our system, that is of any normal body, so that when obstructions or abnormal conditions begin in any portion of the body the blood through the super-sensitiveness through the sympathetic system or solar plexus brain force and center, gathers up these particles and creates more in the system to rebuild forces that have been broken up in any portion such as we have in this body.  We find that the locomotoris [locomotories?] to the extremities both in the colon and in the arm and hands, there is an excess needed to rebuild true blood force through certain cellular force that is being used up.  This we find is not responding as it should or has been in time back.  Hence the condition we have through the action of these portions through the system.  It is a functional condition nor organic, yet becoming organic unless headed off or acted on to produce a system to rejuvenate itself and rebuild in this body.

4. The nerve force in the system is exceptionally well balanced throughout as we have given here.  Blood is normal.  Mental balance has kept nerve portions of this body well in control while in others this same condition would have wrecked much more the system than it did in this.  Because of the mental balance we have here we find the activity of the sympathetic nervous system through the solar plexus, some resistive force through the centers and repressions of their active principle through the sensory organisms so any derangement coming to these faculties have met a repression to other portions of the body in their capacity to call on vital force and throw off without detriment to some physical force as we have had in this body to the vocal organs, to the eyes, to the ears at times there has been calls from these forces through the sympathetic system.
