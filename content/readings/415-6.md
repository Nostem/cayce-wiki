# TEXT OF READING 415-6 M 6

**Reading [[415-6]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/16/1936  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 3122

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1936, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [263], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M.                                ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, [415].

2. We have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are acute conditions arising from an irritation, an upsetting of the stomach, poisons as accumulate from there, with the lack of the proper eliminations.

4. We would use the Castoria, but combined with same we would use that also to flush the system; as Citrate of Magnesia, that would follow after the use of the Castoria.

5. Keep the temperature down by rubbing along the spine with rub alcohol, rather than grain alcohol; this will also reduce the aching.

6. Keep the body quiet for a period sufficient for these to be eliminated; and we would find that the rash, the irritations over the system would be eliminated.

7. One glass of the Citrate AFTER the full bottle of the Castoria should be sufficient.

8. Do not let the body eat too much until these eliminations are entirely set up.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What can be done for soreness of throat?
(A)  This arises from the upset stomach.  Rather than from the irritations there, it's the natural poisons and accumulations that come back as a regurgitation or reaction. When eliminations are set up and the quietness is kept, we will find these will disappear.

11. (Q)  During this period what should be given the body to eat?
(A)  Broths, chicken, with rice, or grapefruit juices, pineapple juices; but no sweets.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother - Special Delivery
