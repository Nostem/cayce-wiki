# TEXT OF READING 3274-2 F 50

**Reading [[3274-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/3/1944  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 10687

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3274], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. There are some improvements.  There hasn't been the complete carrying out of the applications suggested.

4. But for the acute condition in the eye, or those tendencies for - You see, they haven't set up the drainages, or else we would - In the adjustments to be made, these are not so much corrections as to set up drainages, coordinating the areas especially as indicated where there are the closer connection with the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerve impulses; so that the drainages from the lymph as well as from the organic functions are carried off through the eliminating centers - alimentary canal, respiratory system, perspiratory system. These are [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which all of these used energies are to be eliminated.

5. As for the conditions in eye - first set up drainages, not so much corrections as drainages, in the manner indicated - then apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs on the side of the head; that is, on the temple and at the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervicals.  These, with hot salt to hold the heat, should remove these disturbances.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Are the drops now being used helpful?
(A)  Not especially helpful.  These will do, though - but the other conditions are the more necessary.

8. (Q)  What will relieve neuritis in upper arm?
(A)  Setting up drainages!  Neuritis is the expression of poisons, finding pressures in the extremities or even in torso at times.  Set up drainages through alimentary canal.

9. (Q)  What can be done about condition of throat?
(A)  All of these conditions will clear, if there will be the applications suggested.

10. (Q)  Fluctuation in blood pressure?
(A)  This comes, as indicated, from the colon condition.  Do cleanse the colon.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Coulter) Copy to Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.)
