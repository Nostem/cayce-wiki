# TEXT OF READING 599-4 M 59

**Reading [[599-4]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/25/1926  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 4144

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [599].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had the body here before.  Now, we find there are many physical and mental changes in this body since last we had this here.  There are some abnormal conditions that may be corrected in this body.  These specific conditions have to do with that as dross, as has been accumulating through those conditions as known as catarrhal conditions in the body, and the various effects as have been, and are produced in the body, have to do with the abnormal conditions, physically.

3. Then, the condition as we find in ear, in eyes, the congestion in throat, lungs, from cold, that in stomach and intestine that gives troubles at times, the headaches, as we see, from same catarrhal condition, as goes down or up, depending upon the pressure as is created in the blood supply.

4. Then, to bring the more normal condition to this body, we would have corrected, chiropractically, or osteopathically, those conditions in the 2nd, 3rd and 4th cervical, also in the 9th, 10th dorsal, and the 2nd lumbar; that principally giving the trouble being in the 2nd, 3rd cervical, and in 9th, 10th dorsal, and we would apply those [[Vibration|vibrations]] still from that of the battery as has been given for the body, [Radio-Active Appliance - see 599-1] using that same [[Vibration|vibration]], though from a two-wired battery, and two bars of iron in the same solution as has been given, attaching one to the wrist, the other to the opposite ankle, see? for hour to two hours each evening, and we will find, with this change, there will come the better conditions for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

5. In the mental there is much of that development coming to the body as has been outlined for the body, in the manner, in the way, in which the subliminal self may gain that insight into those of the unseen forces; yet [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should weigh well all these conditions, making them compatible with the tenets of the I AM from within, and the I AM that rules the Universal forces.  We are through for the present.
