# TEXT OF READING 1739-4 M 36

**Reading [[1739-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1930  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 7958

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1739], Dr. Lydic, Miss Hodges and Miss Kite.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1739].  This we have had before.  The physical and the mental and the spiritual are NEARER in coordination than they have been in MANY moons.  In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] we only need to keep for the time being those same applications as have been given for the body, to continue to keep on the improve - for the STABILITY as is NECESSARY - for the nerve forces, that had completely relaxed and gone to pieces, are constantly on the upbuild.  Do not OVERTAX body, physically or mentally - neither does that mean for the body to become a non-entity in activity, mental or physical - but USE the body-physical, the body-mental, that there is ALWAYS some reserve energy.  Be consistent, be persistent.  Keep the self, body - MENTALLY AND PHYSICALLY - CLEAN, and the way of self to apply self for the better developments will OPEN for this body, [1739].

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Would it be detrimental for body to go home for three or four days?
(A)  Not if ONLY for three or four days.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self for home
