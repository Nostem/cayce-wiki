# TEXT OF READING 1861-15 M 36

**Reading [[1861-15]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/8/1943  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 8266

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mr. [1861], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions; as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Progress is very good in many ways with this body.  There are times when [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds that the abilities in its [[Meditation|meditation]] apparently far surpass those it may become aware of in other respects.  This is a normal condition, and yet being able to be aware of and to control same is the ability to bring in the consciousness the influences for creating a better activity through the body.

3. For, it is the activity of the body when there is the consciousness given to each cellular force - that replenishes or rebuilds the ability to be at-onement with [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or God, as manifested in the Son - that brings the ability to heal one's own self.

4. Those things added are merely assistants or boosters to keep this ability of spiritual attunement, - the mechanical activities, the [[Massage Therapy|massage]]; keeping a better attunement between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous system, or the physical organism and the imaginative system, or the [[Mental Body|mental body]] and the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

5. These we would continue.

6. Vitamins have been indicated, - in that the ability of the general forces in the body, the activities and the energies of the body have been depleted or used up, - to keep the vital forces ever on that alertness for physical and mental awareness of the spiritual activities.  These we would gradually leave off, dependent upon the diet and the ability of the body to be active in the open.  But keep them up through the periods of cold, and when there is not as much sun in which the body may exercise; and we will find the mechanical adjustments not only more helpful but the physical, mental and spiritual being kept in better or closer accord.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should I continue the oil rubs?
(A)  Just as indicated.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 4/13/43 Statement prepared reporting on EC readings to date;
> read 6/43:
> 
> We look back and evaluate the past few years of our
> association with Mr. Cayce and the organization which
> sponsors his work:  Perhaps no part of our lives has been so
> important and valuable to us as that which has been gained
> from the Readings given through Mr. Cayce, beginning with
> our first experience, that of my own Physical Reading.
> 
> From the time of my birth until four years of age, I was
> almost totally blind.  During my boyhood, I went through a
> series of operations by noted specialists, who needled small
> portions of cataracts from my eyes with each operation.
> Having cut away as much as they could, without injuring the
> sight, the doctors said that they had done all they could for
> me and that I would have to rely on glasses for any further
> improvement.  However, I never lost hope and always felt that
> some means would be found in the future that would answer my
> [[Prayer|prayers]] for improved [[Visions|vision]].
> 
> Some years ago a series of radio programs dealing with
> various psychic experiences in a dramatized form, "[[Initiation|Mysteries]]
> of the Mind", were broadcasted.  Upon writing in for further
> information, I received a copy of a little pamphlet, telling
> something of the work of Edgar Cayce and [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].  I
> immediately obtained a Physical Reading.  [1861-1]
> 
> The reading gave a brief history of my eye trouble
> and diagnosed its cause, then gave suggestions for its
> correction.  It stated that certain pressures in the spine
> were causing an impingement upon the nerves of the eyes,
> hindering the normal activity of the optic nerve, shortening
> the [[Visions|vision]] and emaciating some of the matter of the eye
> itself.  It was suggested that I go to two doctors in New
> York for [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] and electrical treatments.  Immediately
> following these treatments I found that I had gained the use
> of my left eye for reading [[Purpose|purposes]].  Until then I had been
> entirely dependent upon my right eye for reading.  Treatments
> from a Norfolk [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], use of [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], [[Massage Therapy|massage]], and
> vitamins, as suggested in later readings are assisting the
> improvement.  Control of the eye muscles has become almost
> normal; the general physical health has improved one-hundred
> percent; susceptibility to colds is lower than it has ever
> been throughout my lifetime.  It might be interesting to note
> that X-Ray photographs, taken after the first reading had
> been received, verified, exactly, the trouble in the spinal
> column.
> 
> Several months after my first physical reading I had an
> opportunity to prove the accuracy of this type of reading
> beyond any possible doubt.  An epidemic of what was thought
> to be intestinal influenza caught up with me and finally put
> me abed.  I wired for a reading and received the suggestions
> for treatment by wire.  [1861-3]
> 
> Upon following these suggestions my recovery was so speedy
> that I was back at my work three days later and did not
> suffer the usual long period of weakness that is common
> to this illness.
> 
> For a number of years my wife had been suffering from poor
> digestion and elimination and was unable to eat a variety of
> foods, all of which, seriously undermined her health.  Her
> first physical reading gave the primary cause as subluxations
> in various regions of the spinal column.  This verified,
> incidentally, the results of examinations by two osteopaths
> made previous to getting the reading, but of which Mr.
> Cayce knew nothing.  [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] adjustments, [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]]
> treatments, [[Massage Therapy|massage]], and various other measures have caused
> marked improvement in her general health and she is now able
> to eat normally.
> 
> The Physical Readings proved to be of such inestimable value
> to us that we soon obtained our Life Readings as well as our
> Mental and Spiritual Readings.  These, perhaps, more than
> anything else, have helped us to discover and explore
> somewhat that which is involved in the enigmatical old saw,
> "Know Thyself" - those obscure and intricate parts of our
> personalities, minds, and [[Soul|souls]].  Why are we as we are?  Why
> do we have certain innate urges or abilities, expressed, or
> even, sometimes, repressed; why do we have certain desires,
> or attractions or repulsions to others?  Isn't there often a
> deeper meaning about some of these things than we can find in
> our daily lives to explain them?  Couldn't they be [[Karma|karmic]] and
> brought over from another [[Incarnation|incarnation]]?  The Life Readings had
> something to say!  Broadening they have been:  placing before
> us, in brief form, those progressions or retrogressions we
> had made in [[Reincarnation|past lives]] lived upon this earth,  These became,
> to us, as a foundation for this lifetime; they helped us
> understand ourselves and one another; they helped us find our
> [[Purpose|purposes]], our abilities, and our weaknesses; they gave us a
> different understanding and attitude toward all mankind.
> 
> Through my wife's reading she was enabled to find more
> happiness and freedom for expression in the home than
> she had found in the business world.
> 
> My own deep love of music and ability to play the violin were
> innate urges, I found, from a [[Persia|Persian]] [[Incarnation|incarnation]].  While
> an innate desire to discuss the War between the States and
> defend the cause of the South, and to "live" in the history
> of those times, when reading it, were from fighting in the
> Civil War during an [[Incarnation|incarnation]] previous to this.  Railroads,
> trains, everything connected with them, hold a fascination
> for me that was somewhat more satisfactorily explained when I
> discovered I had been a railroad man in that life, also.  It
> was the Life Reading that supplied the link as to why I was
> suffering with the present eye condition for something I had
> done in the past.
> 
> We found that the bonds that tied relationships or
> attractions of people to one another may have begun far back
> in the years when we lived before, It was satisfying to know
> this was not the end, then, nor the beginning.
> 
> To my wife and myself the Life Readings have been a most
> satisfactory and unquestionable proof of [[Reincarnation|re-incarnation]], and
> they have given a far deeper meaning to life, its beauties,
> its sufferings.
> 
> But perhaps this latter expression of value can be applied
> even more aptly to the value of those readings called "Mental
> and Spiritual".  We are faced daily with so many attitudes
> of mind, so many adjustments to make to life and its events.
> What should we hold to, what is firm, what is sure-footing;
> are we right, are we wrong in our attitudes; is there a God,
> a Son, Christ, who really lived?  These were the things the
> Mental and Spiritual Readings tried to help us understand.
> And in the practical application of those advices given has
> come a satisfaction in finding that "they work."  Both my
> wife and I have sought for years in philosophy, metaphysics,
> and psychical phenomena the answers to many questions of life
> and existence.  Philosophy and metaphysics can lead one into
> [[Ideal|the ideal]], the speculative, and often it is hard to reconcile
> the breach between it and the natural, normal, everyday
> things of life.  This is not so of the Readings.  We find
> them practical, applicable to our every day life, for,
> apparent throughout all of the readings is the attempt to
> bring people to normalcy, to balance, to well-being, yet
> always to that [[Last Supper|communion]] and companionship with God that He
> has promised to all who will but love Him and seek Him.
> 
> With these experiences, then, we can only feel that the
> readings have proven themselves.
> 
> So has one other!  For through the many years of personal
> contact now, the quality of the high aims and [[Purpose|purposes]], the
> character of utter sincerity, integrity and humbleness of one
> other stands out.  He has proven himself also.  He says there
> is no need to eulogize HIS character for what he has given
> us, "The Book (Bible) speaks for itself." - He has sent us
> back searching, pondering, that "Book".  It has been good for
> us!  He and the Readings have made us understand and apply it
> better.
>
