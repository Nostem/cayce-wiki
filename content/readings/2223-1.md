# TEXT OF READING 2223-1 M 24 (Music Student, Voice)

**Reading [[2223-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/21/1927  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 8940

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of August, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [2223].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2223] & Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Born October 10, 1902, in San Jose, [[California|California]]. 
Time of Reading 
5:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces, giving the conditions as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane; giving time, the place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  We have the body, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are exhibited and as have been builded, and as are latent in the present earth's plane.

3. In entering we find the entity comes under the influence of Uranus, Neptune, Venus, Mercury, and Mars, with those influences as appear in Aquarius and in Jupiter.  So we find from those latent urges many conditions that have brought and do bring peculiar conditions, situations, circumstances - peculiar surroundings - yet, will the entity [if the entity will] rely upon that of will's force as is surrounded by those influences in Venus and in the Uranian forces of good, these will bring the better conditions in the life.  Allowing those influences to be swayed by that influence in Mars and the adverse in Uranus, we find there will continue to be turmoil, strife, unsettled conditions, wayward actions at the end that would bring the entity to the less development than would be with the full application of will.  Under the Uranian influences, we find these are as double - those that are of the extremist, and as is seen from that in the application of will's forces, is either for good or for bad. More of these will be seen in the urges as are received from experiences in the earth's plane.

4. In that influence combined as between Uranus and Neptune, we find the love of water, the love of travel, the love of mystery, the love of the study of those [[Initiation|mysteries]] and mysterious conditions that have to do with rites as pertain to religion or to those conditions that bring to bear in people's lives certain lines of thought.

5. In that influence as is seen in Venus, we find those as pertain to love, and to those conditions as pertain to the influences in the lives of each that the entity contacts, and these bring many conditions that are as disappointments; for the body being high-minded - through those influences in Uranian influence - and the love of the mystery brings to bear in the life that of not understanding one that is purely secular or material-minded.

6. These, as we are giving here, are irrespective of will's influence, see?

7. That influence as seen in Mars, with Uranian, Neptune and of Venus, brings that of quick to wrath, quick to take exceptions.  This the entity - the body-mind of the individual, [2223] - has gained much control over. Much more should be gained.

8. As to those that are builded in the present earth's plane, we find are as these:

9. One that has every effort to attain that which it may use for its own development.  Get that?  One that may use every element to attain that which will develop its own element, see?  or bring to itself the highest development in this present experience, will the entity [if the entity will] apply those truths, lessons, tenets, principles, laws, understandings as are and have been gained by the entity - even in the present experience - pertaining to man's relation to man and man's relation to the Creative Energy.  Apply them, see?

10. One high minded.

11. One an extremist.

12. One that may - will - develop in many lines of endeavor - either as that of the student in archaeology and archaeological findings as pertaining to histology, biology, or any anatomical or [[Astrology|astrological]] studies, or may attain to that of physical prowess or in the development of any of the particular endowed powers of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], mechanical or of the art nature.  As to why, these will be seen in the experiences as attained and gained through the experience in the earth's plane.

13. One that has that element of attracting the opposite sex; yet this in itself often brings disappointment and discontent.

14. One that has the ability to withstand punishments, and to keep the mental balance through any [[Trial of Jesus|trial]] or tribulation, provided there is not tried to be taken unawares, or the element of love as pertaining to the opposite sex enters in, and only self restraint would then protect the best interests for the entity towards development in this present experience.

15. Beware, then, of firearms.

16. Beware of entanglements as especially pertain to the opposite sex.

17. Beware of entanglement as respecting money, estates, or conditions surrounding same - for the entity has little idea (as is called in the common parlance of the day) of dollars except to obtain that desired by the entity. Hence entanglements in any of these are not ruled save by sentiment, or by the action of Uranian forces - which may be VERY good or very bad.

18. One that will, in the application of self toward those elements as pertain to the occult or the mysterious, gain much for self.  The study of the occult would bring greater blessings than those of mystery, for these are to the entity too easily allowed to slip into the night side of such studies.

19. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and those influences in the life:

20. In the one before this we find in that period when Alfred the Great was the ruler in that land called [[England|England]].  The entity then among the counsellors to that ruler, and in the name Cohe - and the entity gained and lost through that experience, for in attaining earthly power same was diverted to the use of self.  Hence in the present experience - in [[Karma|karmic]] influence - there are seen those fears of any condition pertaining to law, or to entanglements of [[Law of Moses|the law]], or as pertaining to those who are in power - and the lack of concentration to find the source or the power of one that would threaten.

21. In the one before this we find in that period in that country known as [[Greece|Greece]] now.  The entity [was] among those in the Athenian period that were of the Theosophican [?] [sp?] rule, and the entity then [was] in the name Epiciu.  The entity gained in this experience, through the abilities to lend aid to those who would build good physical bodies and apply same in the betterment of conditions for those surrounding same. In the urge, then, is seen the care of the body, the abilities in physical prowess, and the abilities to aid others; also abilities in voice, and in the application of those things physical that attract others.  Do not apply same to excess!

22. In the one before this we find in the period when changes came in that land now known as the Utah and [[Colorado|Colorado]] country. The entity [was] among those that dwelt in the land when this was submerged through the [[Earth Changes|upheavals]] caused in the northern country.  Then in the name Eili, and the entity lost through this experience, being in that position of bringing upon others those of condemnation, for the entity taught others to apply the night-side of life - through trickery and through soothsaying, counting of stones, of beads, of carvings. Hence in the present urge we find the desire to know of such, and the fear of the influence of charm, or of that that might bring to others the thoughts of same.  WELL that the entity beware of same, and - as given - study the occult, rather than the mystery side of earth's experience.

23. In the one before this we find in that period when divisions were seen in that known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] country. The entity then [was] among those of the priests that followed that one banished [[[Pyramid Builders|Ra Ta]]], and the one that gave SO MUCH to the peoples at that period.  The entity then one that sought to be placed in charge of the temple service during banishment, and found disfavor with the ruler; and the entity suffered in body, yet in mind kept self in that way and manner that brought much help, much comfort, much of the conditions that brought reconciliation with the priest and the ruler and those that brought rebellion - being able to correlate for the various classes of peoples at that period, supplying to priest and ruler those abilities of correlating data gathered by all, which - as has been given - brought the greater lessons to the world through Christian principles, as may be seen and applied in the world to-day.  And many of those gathered there the entity contacts in the present experience.  Then in the name Aiod, and the entity gained, lost, and gained through his experience.  In this urge is seen the study of religious tenets and principles of the various peoples, the love of the occult, the love of the help the entity may give to others, the desire to get ALONG - as it were - but not at the EXPENSE of another.  This the entity should apply in the present experience in the earth's plane.

24. The abilities, as seen, lie in many directions.  As given, that in the study of occult - not "ism" but occult - and the application of development of that called psychic in self and others, will bring the greater study, the greater development for the entity in the present experience.  Keep self as in that [[Egypt|Egyptian]] or that Aaraar [Araaraart?] land, in the manner to-day, that to all must come first the knowledge of self and its relation to the [[Creative Forces|creative energies]], and in applying same to others give that as the entity would have applied to its own self.

25. Ready for questions.

26. (Q)  How are my conditions and [[Vibration|vibrations]] working in relation with [R...] and myself?  and what is his attitude toward me?
(A)  These are very good from self's own standpoint.  As to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from [R...] we have not those here.  Only that as applies to THIS ENTITY may be given.  Trust not too far in others, but doubt self before you would doubt another.

27. (Q)  How are my conditions and [[Vibration|vibrations]] working in relation with [L...], [W...], Mother, [K...]?  and what is their attitude toward me?
(A)  These, as given are the answer to the ENTITY'S vibration; not from the other.  Harken to that counsel from sister and mother.  Covet much the faith, the confidence, as is seen in [K...], but keep self in that way of development - for to gain the world and lose one's own [[Soul|soul]] is but naught.

28. (Q)  What did Dad want to tell me and couldn't tell me the last two days of his life?
(A)  This will be presented to the entity in [[Dreams|DREAM]] on Wednesday, this week.  Heed same.

29. (Q)  Should I continue my music studies, write, study religion, business?  and in what place can I do the most good?
(A)  These have been given, see?  Following the study of occult forces and [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]].  Keep in touch with those whom the entity may develop the most with, through, for, and in that way as the entity accomplished in that day accomplish in the present experience.

Well that those relations with the conditions surrounding be settled once for all - necessitating, then, hardships and struggles - in a manner - but return to that country and have this settled once for all.  Then to the mother, and then study to show thyself approved unto Him, the Giver of all good and perfect gifts.  Keep self unspotted from the world. Be ever ready to answer WITH the right word for the faith that lieth within.

30. We are through.
