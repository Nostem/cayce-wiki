# TEXT OF READING 5408-1 M 64

**Reading [[5408-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/22/1944  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 13550

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5408], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by wife and [1663].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], [[Grace|Grace]] Premo, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here [5408].

3. Now, as we find, there are disturbances here which have, for long periods, prevented the better physical functioning of the body.  These, as we find, have to do with the activity of the blood supply and eliminating forces of the body, or the functioning of the liver, as related to gall duct, or gall supply for digestion and purifying blood stream.  Here we have one gall duct almost choked by sediment, rather than stone, though the sediment is stony in its consistency.

4. As we find, so long as there is no temperature, there may be the possibility of eliminating this without operative measures.  Though operative measures would be severe upon the body, these applications, as we find, may be done with less severity to the body, so that these may eliminate, dependent upon the response of the body to the applications and as to how sincere and how consistent and persistent they are in making these applications.

5. First, we would give as foods, only liquids, semi-liquids, and those that are body-supplying, but easily assimilated.

6. Then we would begin first, and apply, for at least three hours each day, for five days in succession, [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs, over the liver and gall duct areas.  Use at least three to four thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Then apply a heavy pad of hot salt over same.  After this has been used, gently [[Massage Therapy|massage]] - not so as to rupture - not so as to displace - but so as to cause full absorption of Peanut Oil and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], equal portions, into that area and through the abdomen.

7. After the five days, rest a day, and then begin with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  Use at least three thicknesses of flannel, saturated - not just pouring on but saturated with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], and after covering with [oil] cloth to prevent soiling of linen, apply an electric pad.  Be very careful.  These should not be used more than an hour at the time, but if these are done twice a day for at least three days in succession it would be well.  Give small doses of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], but increase it gradually to all the body is able to take. Then, after the three days, rest one day.  Then begin again, but continue to give the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] at least 1/2 to 1 teaspoonful, two to three to four times a day.

8. Thus we should eliminate - empty - the whole of the greater duct.  Do that.

9. Should there be nausea or temperature, operate.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self thru Wife - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/7/44 GD's answer to [5408]'s wife's questions re. detailed
> directions:  "1. Use a small Pillow case, as for a baby
> pillow, and use sufficient salt to make a pad about half
> an inch to an inch thick.  Table salt is alright (Morton's
> iodized, preferably), or the coarse salt if you can get it -
> such as used by meat packers.  The salt should be heated in
> the oven - as in a biscuit pan - and poured into the pillow
> case and pinned or basted at the end to keep it from
> spilling.  When the pad has cooled the salt may be emptied
> and reheated or the pad itself may be put in the biscuit pan
> in the oven, just so you are careful not to get it so hot as
> to burn the cloth.  In the winter time, of course, it could
> be laid over a hot register, or the like.  When the pad is
> hot, an electric pad might be laid over it to keep it so and
> not have to reheat, though just the hot salt must be
> preferable since the reading called for it.
> 
> "2. Put half and half Peanut Oil and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] - mix a
> tablespoonful of each in a saucer to begin, then make more as
> you need it.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] each time just what will be absorbed by
> the body through the skin - or keep rubbing it in until the
> skin won't take any more and begins to accumulate on the
> outside.
> 
> "3. A small dose of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] would be about a quarter
> teaspoonful.  Begin this when the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs are begun,
> and take this dosage about every two hours through the day
> (between meals, preferably).  If it begins to regurgitate,
> decrease the amount - take it just a sip at the time - but
> keep on taking.  If it goes alright, the next day increase to
> a full teaspoonful every two hours, and so on - leaving the
> dosage at a teaspoonful if it agrees.  Whenever it begins to
> come up in the mouth, then decrease the dosage but continue
> taking - and do this from then on, not stopping when you rest
> from the Packs, but continuing until the gall duct is
> emptied.  Even then it would be well to continue the olive
> oil periodically, as the readings say that it is well for
> almost everyone to have periods of taking it.  (That reminds
> me, I think I'll take another course of it.)

> "I hope you've already gotten started and that I've made
> myself (and the reading) clear to you.  Love, and hoping for
> the best..."
