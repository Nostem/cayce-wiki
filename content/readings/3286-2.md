# TEXT OF READING 3286-2 F 27

**Reading [[3286-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/15/1944  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 10707

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3286] - Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, this we have had before.  These conditions were a long time coming on, don't be impatient.  There will come the consciousness of change, by following through with all the suggestions given.  It may require some several more rechargings of the Appliance.  Do follow with the massages after each treatment with the Appliance, as has been indicated, especially for the centers on the areas for the activities of the nerves of the limbs, along the body-forces to the limbs.  These keep regularly.  Where the knee especially is affected the oils may be massaged more than once a day, if this is desirable, and we will find as the mental-self becomes more aware of the spiritual-self, it will begin the use of the limbs gradually, not all at once but gradually.

3. Don't become impatient.  Do keep up the treatments.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Are the treatments with the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] being done correctly?
(A)  These have been done fairly well.  Don't hurry through with them.  Know that they are purposeful.  Do keep the plates a little cleaner.  Do prepare them twenty minutes before they are to be applied to the body.  Do disconnect when not in use.  Then attach together twenty minutes before they are to be attached to the body.

6. (Q)  My first Physical Reading referred to an "infection and high fever".  What kind of an infection was meant?
(A)  This was from that which is called polio.

7. (Q)  What causes fleeting pains in the right side of my head and right leg?
(A)  These are nerves attempting to recuperate or to send out "feelers."  Do [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the whole area with the oils suggested.

8. (Q)  Should my right arm and hand get direct treatments by the Appliance?
(A)  The whole body gets direct treatments by the Appliance if the massages are given along the whole area of the spine and the limbs also.

9. (Q)  What is the cause and the cure of the dark mark on the outside of my lower left leg?
(A)  Accumulation of blood under same from breaking of cells. This must gradually be diminished.  Massaging with [[Camphor|camphorated oil]] once or twice a week gradually will disseminate it.

10. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do follow through with the spiritual counsel also, and you will find bettered conditions.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
