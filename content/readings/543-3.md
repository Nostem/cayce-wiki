# TEXT OF READING 543-3 F 21

**Reading [[543-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/25/1930  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 3836

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  Those administrations for the changes in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system [Mayblossom Bitters, etc?] will be more effective [if these will] will these be given so that they coincide with the active forces in system as respecting the menstrual periods. These, as we find, would be best given, then, more of an ORDERLY nature; that is, more in quantity and more in active forces just after and just preceding the periods, and not so MUCH during that ten day interim, as is seen.  This will prevent too much of same being active with the lymphatic circulation.

2. In the manipulations as are given for the general conditions of the body, so as to keep the general coordination in the system, suppress that tendency as is felt at times in the 2nd and 3rd cervical region.  This, as we find, will aid in preventing the recurrent conditions of the reactions in the nervous system.

3. We would also find, that as the conditions PROGRESS - in the suppressing of the activity of the genitory system, there will be the tendency in those regions from which the organs of pelvis maintain their nervous energy of a swelling, or a tenderness there.  These will be aided most by the addition of those of being PAINTED with [[Iodine|iodine]], as may be absorbed in system.  This will aid the body materially.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Give cause of attacks.
(A)  As has been outlined, this produced from the active forces from the genitive system, through those conditions of a prenatal nature, as has been outlined.

5. (Q)  Would hypnotic influence be applied to body through materia-medica or through hypnotherapy?
(A)  Through materia-medica, for that as may come through the imaginative system - or hypnotherapy, or hypnotic influence is too dangerous in conditions of this nature.

6. (Q)  If body responds properly to treatment, how long will it take to completely obliterate such attacks?
(A)  From one to two, to three years.

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
