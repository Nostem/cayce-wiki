# TEXT OF READING 4905-53

**Reading [[4905-53]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/23/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12978

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
5:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick, in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]], and the operations and operators, and you will tell us how they may remove this obstruction from the hole, in such a manner that it will be understandable to the mind of Pat Moran, driller, and any other information which will assist in making this a successful and producing well.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations and operators, as we have had before.

3. Now, we find, to remove these obstructions, first is to remove that portion of the cavings as hinder the drilling, and then drill to two to five feet beside the bailer, or pump.  Then, remove the water from the hole, and in this operation we should find that pump comes to that position where the tools may take hold on same, or SHOULD this, perchance, NOT bring this from the leaning side, we would find that by running the two prong grab, this may bring this to that position in which the slip socket will take hold on the pump; then be able to remove same.  We will find there is only a few more feet to be drilled before the production will come in.  By using this, in the manner given, the sand pump should be removed in one to two days.  Three to four more, then, necessary to have production on top of ground.  Then, do that, Pat [Pat Moran].  We are through.  [See 4905-53, Par. B1.]

> R5. 2/1/26 Mr. Wyrick's letter:
> 
> 
> Morgan, [[Texas|Texas]]
> 
> Have had a conference with Mr. Pat Moran, our Head Driller.
> He does not agree with me that it would be best to close down
> a few months in order to get together enough money to run a
> string of casing.  He thinks it will be unnecessary to run
> the casing until we have reached production, then the cost of
> casing would be negligible.  He feels confident that he will
> soon have hole clear of obstruction, and after he has a clear
> hole, it will be only a short time drilling thru the lime.
> Moran says that if each one interested in the Oil Company
> could know the condition of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], and the possibilities
> it promises of big production, he feels sure none would be
> willing to shut down for lack of money to carry on, but that
> each one should make further subscription to insure the
> success of our undertaking.
> 
> We have [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] drilled to the black lime and had penetrated
> this lime to a depth of 165 feet, a total depth of 6105 feet,
> at a cost of $64,115.  This cost includes moving tools and
> building rig when we moved from old location, rebuilding rig
> and replacing large part of tools that were destroyed by fire
> last August causing a loss of $15,000 and about eight weeks
> time on fishing jobs.  Now, when we arrived on the black lime
> at 5940 feet we reached the formation we had been expecting
> for past 3000 feet.  We found it much thicker than the usual
> run in the producing fields and the rule is - more lime -
> more oil.
> 
> If you were looking for apples, you would go to that part of
> the country where apples grow, then you would proceed to
> locate an orchard or pool of apple trees.  When this is
> found, you would locate a tree, then you would reasonably
> expect to find the fruit.
> 
> The black lime in this well represents the tree.  The trip to
> this lime has cost us the aforesaid amount.  Will we stop or
> will we invest enough more to nuy [buy?] a stick to knock the
> apple?  Will not require many thousands - $5. to $25. per
> month from each shareholder for next four to six months will
> do the trick.  Are you with me?
> 
> Please do not forget that Moran is not alone in his belief:
> that oil in large volume underlies this black lime.  It is
> the firm belief of each driller working on the well and the
> opinion of five prominent practical geologists (to whom the
> samples have been submitted) operating in the State, familiar
> with all the formations, one a Consulting Geologist, two
> representing two of the largest Oil Companies in this State,
> two working independent and for leasing Companies and of all
> practical men who have inspected us.
> 
> Now, we must have money to carry on, so please let me have
> your remittance for a small amount as indicated each month
> and we will gauge our hope for ultimate reward accordingly.
> 
> M. B. Wyrick
