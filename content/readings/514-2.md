# TEXT OF READING 514-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[514-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/8/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 3646

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by self, through her sister-in-law, Mrs. [340].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [340] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Calif. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. As we find, while conditions in some respects appear for exaggeration of the specific centers being in PAIN is the adjustment or attempt of system to adjust itself to those applications being made for the betterments of the body, and we would give it TIME - but KEEPING much those applications given.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What causes tightness at shoulder blades?
(A)  As has been indicated from those of the specific centers that have been, and are yet in part under stress - from pressures in the system, with the releasing and not the WHOLLY releasing with the manipulations of those pressures, tends to accentuate weakened conditions, as indicated.  Hence we would make more of specific adjustments in the area, that the whole of the secondary cardiac segments, that have their radiation to the upper portion of the lung, receive their proper incentive from nerve and blood supply from this area.

3. (Q)  Is the deadened [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] being brought to normal?
(A)  Hasn't had time yet!  There ARE those indications, as is felt by those conditions in those portions of system, where the digestive forces are active, and their activity to the lung area, through which, into which the blood supply from same comes.  These show that there are more SENSITIVENESS, and only need that continuation - as does sore MUSCLES from exercise need the continuation, rather than the bandaging or stoppage of those incentives for their proper activity. These may be aided by the thorough relaxation by low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] over the system at times, in and about those centers from which each of these specific places receive their nerve impulse - see?  This should be with the VIOLET ray - see?

4. (Q)  How many more treatments will I need?
(A)  That will depend upon the RESPONSES of the system.

5. (Q)  Is there a more equal balance in the white blood and the red?
(A)  There is the TENDENCY towards it.  As has been given, there is hardly sufficient TIME yet for this to be made NOTICEABLE in the difference - see?  There is a tendency towards a more normal balance.

6. (Q)  What causes the intense itching of the scalp at times?
(A)  Where there has been a tendency for the cells of the cuticle to be deadened, as was indicated by the fallen condition of hair and the irritation, with circulation going BACK, it's a NATURAL consequence!  Don't scratch it, though, too much.  It may be rubbed, but when it is let it be with some oil on the tips of fingers - olive oil, or vaseline, or the like - see?

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Mrs. [340]
