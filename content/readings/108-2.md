# TEXT OF READING 108-2 M 47

**Reading [[108-2]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/10/1929  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 259

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [108].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [108] and wife [109], Mrs. Wood Gray, and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [108].  Now, we find there are those conditions physical of which the body should rather be warned, than of the conditions so abnormal that with the correction of those that are producing disturbances would not correct.  These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [108], we are speaking of, present in this room. First:

2. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, here we find some evidences of existent disturbances through the physical functioning of the system. The blood supply being that in the system which performs, through its various channels more than one function - for the supply retains and attains, from the assimilation of foods in system, for the replenishing of the body, as WELL as often acting as a carrier for elimination of used energies to their various portions or conditions in system where such eliminations may take place; performing also that function of being able to create that which acts as the coagulating forces for the body in ANY condition as may be occurrent, or concurrent, in the system.  Hence, this often may be classified as that criterion through which most any condition existent in the system may be found.  Here, in this body, [108], we find such conditions existent - that is, there is seen that the whole of the eliminating system does not function in a perfect, nominal or normal manner, and when such eliminations as should be carried, or should be eliminated through certain channels or organs, are NOT, evidences are seen in the blood stream, in the character of the cells, that ACT as carriers for various conditions in the system.  Here may well be given the condition as takes place, as to how a body organically - when the body has reached its normal development, resuscitates or rebuilds itself.  It has long been confirmed that the body in toto renews itself in ever so many periods, or so many cycles of change.  It is seen that not all portions of the system move in the same cycle, for the [[Vibration|vibration]] that is necessary to create that tissue which will replenish a heart cell, or a ventricle, will not move in the same cycle as one that replenishes the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of digestion, whether it be the salivary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] themselves or those of the lactice, or lacteal [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], or mammary glands, or the adrenal gland, or the thyroid gland, or those of the ducts as in the gall duct, or the pancreas, or that of the spleen in itself.  ALL of these in THIS particular case, are self-evident that there has been left in system such conditions as that at various stages of their cycle of replenishing or of resuscitating, have the various functionings of organs, or glands, or ducts, suffered at times under the strain put upon same by non-coordination in eliminating system.  Often we may find the body functioning nominally, or overly so, through the respiratory system, and yet the kidneys not discharging in their PROPER manner. Again we may find the kidneys overactive, or nominally active, and we find that the glands or ducts of the liver itself not discharging properly in the system, so that there are those crystallizations in the blood supply, that destroys that that, acting in the active forces as carried in blood supply through the lymphatic and through the lymphatic circulation, become changed in pigment, even to where an irritation at times may occur, where the glands of this circulation come close to the surface.  Not always, no - for the respiratory system being in a near normal state, or attempting to adjust itself, becomes OVERACTIVE.  Why? Then we find that the nervous system must be taken into consideration, as to how the very active force of the blood supply itself is taken, for with each muscular force of the body, and with practically all change in the blood supply, whether of the active force as returns the blood TO the heart or takes it FROM the heart, must receive an impulse for same to function in a normal manner.  Then, when we have hindrances - as is designated or seen here with this body, where nerves fail to RECEIVE proper INCENTIVES, the reaction from those organs or those portions of the system supplied by that nerve energy suffers in its cycle of functioning.  Why has the condition, then, not localized itself with this body of [108]?  For AGAIN there must be taken into consideration the activity or the impelling force that causes nerve energy to act.  Again we come back to that life force WITHIN each cell, that is the IMPELLING force of life itself MANIFESTED in a given physical being or body as this, itself.  Then, as we have two units of life cell forming to break into one - making a normal or an original cell - then gradually breaking into, making different forms of cells, that form into living body or organism as is seen here.  Each then forming in its active forces, as are given out by the first impulse of same, cooperate one with another, and the ability to control same through [[Free Will|the will]] force of an individual is that force or capacity wherein the life cell becomes subservient to the body through its own physical activity to stimulate certain conditions in the body, or that felt necessary by a walk, by a dip, by any active force, gives impelling activity to the energies as are necessary from those forces in themselves, and THIS has kept the body, then, in this condition yet, as seen, have gradually built into the system those wherein the incoordination has crept in.  Then it becomes NECESSARY that other measures; or another UNDERSTANDING be gained by the body ITSELF as to corrective measures to be taken to bring about a normalcy again in the physical functioning and manifesting as seen in the body itself.  Here we find then, as seen, MEDICINAL properties - as medicine would be POISON to this system - unless it persuaded itself first that it had to take medicine, and that would be a hard consciousness for this body to realize.

3. In the activities, then, there is necessary that that [[Vibration|vibration]] be created in the system that will produce an equalization between the activities of the nerve system and of the energies as are exercised in the blood supplies themselves.  This we would find by taking those properties in salts, as are created in fruits and in nuts themselves. First these are active forces with the digestive system itself.  Then, add TO that, that [[Vibration|vibration]] as would be set up through the active principles as are set forth from body to vibratory forces in nature, as are created in same through that of the Radio-Active Appliance, which - added to the various changes as come to the system from the throwing off of nerve energy, to meet the needs of conditions as are created by the vibration set up, will create within the system those properties as will bring better conditions for the whole system.  Now - as these vibrations are set up, then the exercises of various portions of the system would materially AID in bringing the equalizations AS ARE set up, to those portions of the body as is desired to be, by this body itself, [108].  As is seen for the eye, as is seen for the ear, as is seen for the fingers, and the lower extremities.  Then, those exercises as bring into play those forces exercises through the upper portion of the system, from the last cervical to the head itself.  Circular motions of the head, forward, backward, and circle.  These should be taken morning and evening.  The exercises for the body before retiring - ROTATIONS of the body from waist down, placing and bracing feet against a wall, hands on floor, circulate the body - by, stooping, circulating, stooping, circulating. These begun easily at first, gradually increased - will CHANGE the active forces in the body to near normalcy.  Ready for questions.  Taking the vibrations of the Radio-Active Appliance each evening, will also aid the body to rest better - will aid the body in digestion - in gaining better control over the nerves of the whole system, whether of the cerebro-spinal, sympathetic, or the sensory or vegetative system.

Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self "   " Ass'n office "   " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
