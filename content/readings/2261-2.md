# TEXT OF READING 2261-2 F 28

**Reading [[2261-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/14/1931  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 8990

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by her husband - Mr. [279].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2261] - this we have had before.  There are changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.  While in many respects the physical conditions are very good - some improvements are shown - there are those conditions that have arisen, that come from infection forces from within.  As is seen from conditions as exist in, or on, the Fallopian tube, as well as those in the COLON itself FROM this inflammation.  This combined with the tendency as HAS existed from toxic forces in digestive system, and from the mucus membranes in throat and bronchi, makes for a debilitation that is not so good.

2. As WE would find, these may be removed easily in their inception, or in their beginning, are there applied [if there are applied] those forces as will make for CORRECTIONS in these regions and areas, so that drainages are set up and ELIMINATIONS are carried on in the system in the PROPER way and manner, and the blood supply clarified in such a way and manner as to LOOSEN those tendencies of plethora in portions, and correct conditions.

3. We will find that, with the manipulative forces given at least twice each week, and at least THREE times each week - not on the days that the MANIPULATIONS are given - take those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the charged wet battery containing the [[Iodine|iodine]] in charge, and this attached to the body in the 4th LUMBAR and in the 4th or 5th dorsal - see?  This we would attach in this manner, when charged:  We would attach first that of the anode from the SMALLER plate, or from copper plate and copper anode, to the 5th and 4th DORSAL center (this will come with the heart's action).  Then we would attach the larger plate to the 4th LUMBAR center.  This will make for a tendency, if allowed to stay too long, of nausea - but do not keep this charge on longer than there is seen that the PULSATIONS have increased more than ten to twenty beats - see?  That will require from thirty to fifty minutes; not longer than thirty-five to forty minutes, then, would be the beat - or the application.  This is BEST to be taken as the body RESTS in the afternoons.

4. We find ALSO that were there taken internally those of the [[Atomidine|atomic iodine]] in very MINUTE doses, see, this will tend to CLARIFY the system as to its blood condition.  This we would take three drops in half a glass of water twice each day for five to ten days; then leave OFF entirely for three to five days, and we will find that - with precautions as respecting the diet, as has been outlined for that as will be a well balanced diet, easy of assimilation, and keeping eliminations properly through the alimentary canal - that there may be no more irritation in the colon than can be helped - these, we will find, will bring about a normal condition of this body, [2261].  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is an operation needed now?
(A)  As we have indicated, will [if] these changes [will] be brought about in the system, as WE would find, an operation would NOT be necessary.  To be sure, should the conditions continue to enlarge, or inflammation arise, or pain become over severe, then would be necessary for operative sources - but when the pains occur, WE would apply rather those of hot packs of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] to reduce pain, and keep up those conditions as has been outlined.

The manipulations will be for keeping the drainages well in the system, from head, lungs, in the abdominal regions, and in the pelvic regions.  Hence the manipulations OSTEOPATHICALLY will be the better treatments, twice each week.  [[Vibration|Vibrations]] three times each week, not on days that treatments are given.  Do not overtax the body by an overcharge, be mindful of the change in the pulsation - these would be charged; with the [[Iodine|iodine]] preparation, taking internally twice each day for a period of five days, and rest a period of three to five days.  Three drops in half a glass of water, morning before breakfast and before retiring at night.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
"    " Mr. [279]
