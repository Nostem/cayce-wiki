# TEXT OF READING 137-51 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-51]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/14/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 441

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, here, [137]. This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] are as that as has been given, and may be used by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] for self edification and for the building up of the mental and spiritual and physical, and financial, self.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Sunday night, Jan. 10, or Monday, Jan. 11, 1926.  Saw Mr. Alexander Stern of the firm of Herzfeld & Stern.
(A)  Now this presents to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those stabilizing conditions as may be found in self through that as of self's attitude towards this individual at the present time, and as the attitude is towards the individual, such should be [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s, [137]'s action toward those leads as are given through and by this individual.  In other words, follow up that hold as has been gained from same, see?  and there will be many developments for self in same.

4. (Q)  What reference has this, if any, to my stocks?
(A)  That as has been given.  Follow same.

5. (Q)  What should I take as this dream indicating - i.e. should I follow Mr. Stern's advice?  As regarding what?
(A)  Those same conditions as given.  Follow that advice, yes; that lead, yes.  In what?  In that as is in the way of being between each, see?

6. We have finished with this reading.
