# TEXT OF READING 900-378 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-378]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/18/1928  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5581

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of March, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House, and Thomas House, Jr.]

Time of Reading 
6:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], ... St., also [137] of ... Ave., Apt. ..., both of [[New York City|New York City]], and the line-up as set regarding the Federal Securities Corporation.  You will also have before you the [[Dreams|dreams]] the body [900] had on the dates which I will give you. You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] and [137].  These we have had before, also those relations and those contemplated conditions as regarding the Federal and the associations as respecting same.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  In the $20,000 that is planned to be paid the customer's man, Farrari is setting, for the customer's man's money? or is some of it going back in the Federal?
(A)  This is - as set up - for the customer's man, and the handling of the situation, and is in keeping with conditions surrounding such association.  Would be well to consider same.

4. (Q)  Advise what we should do regarding this, in light of stock exchange rules.
(A)  As given, this is in keeping with regulations as regarding stock exchange rules, for this is not as bonus - nor as buying the business of this exchange man - but as the use of man for those conditions regarding same.

5. (Q)  Is the plan of [195] to take a $15,000 unit and go on the Board of Directors, representing himself and [900] as a unit - [900] taking $15,000 unit with the privilege of our going on the Board when we choose - is that fulfilling the requirements as given must be fulfilled before the contracts or plans are put into force?  or must [900] be on the Board before the affiliation is made?  According to [137] it's better to be more cautious along these lines on account of the stock exchange, and work it this way.  Would this way do as well?  or is there something we can't see that absolutely necessitates our being on the Board personally or drop the thing entirely?
(A)  As has been given regarding [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of [195] and [900] and the operation of the securities corporation, this would be in keeping - were it understood that [195] representing that advice and that following that would come to [900] through [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] one with another; for, as has been given, that direction as may be received may only be given through that individual.  Act in the manner as has been outlined, would the success be assured, as has been given.

6. (Q)  Is there anything in the Federal line-up that we should know about in order that we may not get ourselves in trouble with the stock exchange, or in any other channel?
(A)  That, as given, is one of the main reasons that the bodies should be represented on the Board, so that nothing would occur that would cause any entanglements in the operation of the Federal company; for without that positive assurance that all would be known that transpires through the policies and activities of the Board, then best the associations be left alone.  Not that there is any unsatisfactory condition in present line-up or policies of same, but conditions change - even as men's minds. Being forewarned, be forearmed, see?

7. (Q)  [[Dreams|Dream]] of night of March 16th.  I was being spoken to by one on the astral plane:  "The hold-up work of hospital in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], they'll stop that, but it's success is assured nevertheless."  The same entity said to me, "So and so (somebody else on the astral plane) said General Motors is going way up."  I answered, "Yet when somebody else tells you that it in going up, that is not a direct command, and I am not to heed it."  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] answered, "But you are getting a communication from the astral plane."
(A)  Here we have two different character of presentations to the body consciousness.  That first, in the checking or stopping, representing that presentation to the physical consciousness of those conditions as are presented in physical conscious thought.  Not that the hold-up or the check would arise from sources that are in mental being's mind, but rather that the assurance of that that the success would be assured, irrespective of any conditions to prevent same.  In the second, that of the study for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of that of communications; though, as seen, there will still be advances in this particular stock; but with as much reactory force as advance, until they would be unsecure in action, unless given direct as intimated and reasoned from the inner self.

The lessons that may be gained from this condition:  That here we find two distinct character of reasoning.  One from the purely mental or physical mind.  The other from the inner self or [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]].  Heed those lessons as illustrated to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here.  Do that.

8. We are through with this reading.
