# TEXT OF READING 5744-1 F 8

**Reading [[5744-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/26/1944  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 14218

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce this 26th day of March, 1944.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:30 to 3:30 Central War Time.

1. EC:  "We don't find the body here."  [See 5744-1, Reports.]

---

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/26/44 P.M. Rdg. attempted:  "We don't find the body here."
> GD's memo:  Sorry, but probably address not sufficient.  It
> is necessary to have the exact address at which the child
> will be during the hour set.  Also preferable that the
> parents themselves write us giving this information, and
> submitting the questions they wish to have asked in the Rdg.
> If not satisfactory, we'd be glad to return the membership
> fee.  [First reading suggestion was:  "[5744] at the home of
> her parents, Mr. & Mrs. [...] in ..., Miss."]
> 
> R2. GD's memo:  Parents wrote requesting another appointment
> which was made for 7/11/44, but due to EC's illness was not
> kept.
> 
> R3. 6/44 Letter from mother:
> 
> 
> [5744] at the home of her parents, ... Plantation, 8 mi. So.
> of ..., Miss.
> 
> "From all [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] she was a strong healthy normal baby.
> Not until she was 8 mo. old did the doctors notice any
> trouble.  At that time I consulted them as I became worried
> over the fact that the child could not sit alone.  She has
> never been able to walk and I have considered sending her to
> Dr. R. F. Darnall who is head of Solasco Sanitarium School at
> Alexander, Ark., in the hope that she could be taught to
> walk.  I can't make up my mind, however, to put her in care
> of others and I'd like help on this question from you.
> 
> "I have, of course, taken her to several doctors, none of
> whom will say or know what caused her condition.  They all
> agree it could have been a birth injury."
> 
> 1. Any advice or help you can give the mother in regard to
> this child will be greatly appreciated.
> 
> R4. GD's memo:  Since the reading was attempted on 3/26/44 and
> the statement made "We don't find the body here," this case
> was given the number [5744] and included in the EC records.
> Also it occurred to me that the lack of any description of
> the child's condition may have prevented contact, indicating
> a lack of trust or seeking on the part of the parents, etc.
> EC's appointments were so far in advance, and he was becoming
> ill, so that apparently only those who were most sincere and
> cooperative were able to have their appointments filled.
