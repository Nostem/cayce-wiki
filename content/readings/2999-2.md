# TEXT OF READING 2999-2 M 1

**Reading [[2999-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/23/1943  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10303

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are some changes.  Some of these show improvement.  Some apparently aggravate the body.

3. When the convulsions begin, we find it would be well to have a cold cloth - not icy but cold cloth, wet - held at the base of the brain.

4. We would continue with the adjustments, for these are helpful.  DO break up, in the same manner, those tendencies for lesions in the lacteal duct area, or in the abdomen, just to the right - on the right side below the gall duct area. We would gently [[Massage Therapy|massage]] this area each time.

5. Keep up all of those applications which have been indicated, adding the cold cloth as given; and using at times - when the treatments are given - the magnetic treatment, especially when attempting to break up the lesion in the right side. Place the right hand under the right side of the body, and the left hand over the same on the upper side of body. Also we would apply the magnetic treatment through the area where the secondary nerve forces to the sensory forces are associated with the cerebrospinal system.  This will aid in quieting the body.  The right hand would be placed between the shoulders, and the left hand on or over the breast bone.

6. As to the [[Visions|vision]] or hearing, - these are not active in the present; though there are refractions, or there is response to light, there is some response to the hearing.  These will necessarily be developed a little later with those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of low electrical forces, if there is the ability to stop the convulsions.  Otherwise we may have such brain lesions as to set ALL of the organs of the sensory system at naught in their coordinating with brain AND those activities of the body.

7. Keep, then, what has been given.  This offers the better chances for reactions that may be helpful.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Please advise Dr. Zwicker as to whether he is doing the right thing or not.
(A)  As indicated, keep these up; adding the two variations as suggested.

11. (Q)  How long should the magnetic treatment last?
(A)  So long as it does not take too much out of the one making the application.  This would be only for a few minutes at the time, but keep it up several times during the day, or whenever there are those tendencies for the convulsions.

12. (Q)  Could the mother do this?
(A)  The mother may do it very well at some times, but not regularly - it would be too much of a strain.  For it takes vitality from a body.  Rub the hands thoroughly together, of course, before this is begun.  Keep them very clean, but let the [[Vibration|vibration]] as from the body pass through the child's body, see?

13. (Q)  He also vomits much more, and has difficulty with bowel movement.
(A)  Use enemas.  But these changes suggested should react for betterment, with these added conditions in the present.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Zwicker) Copy to Ass'n file
