# TEXT OF READING 10-1 F 20

**Reading [[10-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/21/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 41

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - through her brother-in-law, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Misses [10] and Goldstein, and Mr. and Mrs. Spandorfer.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [10].  Now, we find there are PHYSICAL disturbances in the body.  These conditions have to do with the nervous system and with the functioning of organs and ORGANIC disturbances.  In the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]], these are rather as the RESULTS than causes.

2. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [10] we are speaking of, present in this room:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find very good as to quantity and quality.  As to its circulatory forces throughout the system, there are HINDRANCES in the system as related to the CENTRAL nervous system, as has to do with the supplying of nerve nutriment, and acting as a circulatory force in the head, the neck, the throat, and to the auditory system especially.

4. IN THE NERVOUS SYSTEM, we find in the sensory system those disturbances that are both functional and organic.  These in part began first from a disorder in the system which attacked the tissue in the throat and along Eustachian tubes, and in the soft tissue of face, antrum, and in those portions of the system as have to do with the supplying of the forces that act with the auditory nerve system, or the INNER ear.  In the cerebro-spinal centers, this radiation comes from the 3rd dorsal to the 2nd cervical, and in their juncture show that of the EFFECT as has been produced by the disturbance. Reflexly, throughout the whole nervous system, these conditions are of a general nature - though the general health, the general forces in system, act in accordance with the sensory reactory forces of the body.

5. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY THEMSELVES, brain forces good.  The reactions from IMPULSES in the system, these show an extraordinary reflex force, especially as is seen in the sympathetic reactions of the body.  Head, neck, bronchi and lungs, as given - we find in the auditory forces, those of the inner central force, there are some organic disturbances - but the FUNCTIONAL disorder is that, that may be brought to bear ON the system as to produce for the body an altered or a bettered condition, through the changing of the circulatory forces and of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the nerve system as REGULATE those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the system.

6. In the throat and bronchi - in the throat proper we find the basis, or that disorder that began from the conditions as have existed in the system, as related to the attacks to the throat - especially to the tonsil, and to those nasal forces or cavities proper.  In the bronchi, only a reflex or sympathetic condition.  Lungs are very good.

7. Heart's action may be classed as an intermittent, dependent upon the sympathetic's reaction.  The body responds to sympathetic forces in system.

8. Digestive system shows the effect of a catarrhal condition as has existed, does exist, in a portion of the system - and with the change of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, these, as we find, may be corrected throughout.

9. Then, in meeting the needs of the conditions from [[Physical Body|the body physical]], first we would prepare the Radio-Active Appliance as will carry a changed vibration.  In the application of same, attach (the plain Radio-Active Appliance) the positive, or the first anode, to those portions in the system as close to the ear as possible, over the soft tissue of same - or in that just back of ear.  Then the second would be attached to the 3rd dorsal - see?  Then, in the next change - or next day - in the attaching of same, this would be changed to the lower extremity, or to the ankle - this being the negative anode, the positive being attached to those same portions of body.

10. During such applications the body should be in that position of [being] retrospective within itself, as to that as may be accomplished WITHIN self THROUGH the applications of CONCENTRATION in self.

11. In the changes as this will bring, we will find it necessary also to add those changes in corrective manipulations in the dorsal and cervical regions.

12. This being kept consistently, persistently will bring a near normal condition for the body, [10].  Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  How long should it be applied each day?
(A)  Thirty to sixty minutes.

14. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be given?
(A)  At least twice each week for two to three weeks, then REST [from them] two to three weeks and take another course of treatments or adjustments - but keep UP those [periods] of the concentration; that is, entering into the silence of self, and [in] those vibrations from the Radio-Active Appliance.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self

"    " Dr. Lydic
