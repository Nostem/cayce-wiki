# TEXT OF READING 3614-1 F 63 (Housewife)

**Reading [[3614-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/27/1944  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 11112

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3614], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
Set. bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. 
Taken 4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Maryland.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3614].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions.  These are tendencies for the lack of proper coordination in the circulation, between liver and kidneys, and those tendencies for uric acid to form poisons in portions of the extremities, and to have those conditions that cause a great deal of pain. These may become in the form of rheumatism, but tending towards arthritic-rheumatic conditions.  For there are poisons from lack of proper eliminations from the colon, as well as the uric poisons in the body-functioning.

4. We would begin applying [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs for an hour each day for three days in succession, over the gall duct and caecum area; that is, extending from the rib to the point of the hip at the caecum area - down the right side, you see.  Put these on of evening when the body is ready to retire, two or three thicknesses of flannel saturated with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]].  Then apply the electric pad or heavy salt, heated.  When removed, sponge off the area with [[Baking Soda|soda]] water.

5. After the third day, take internally two teaspoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and the next day have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

6. Then begin (not before) and take broken doses of Caldwell's Syrup of Pepsin for at least two days, half a teaspoonful every hour for almost eight to ten hours each day for two days.  Then on the third morning, take two heaping teaspoonsful of Sal Hepatica.

7. After resting then for two days, begin taking KalDak as a strengthener and body-builder.  Put a level teaspoonful in a little hot water and stir until well dissolved, then fill the glass - a small tumbler - with orange juice.  Take this once each day.

8. Occasionally, about once a week, take watermelon seed tea, prepared in this manner:  Crush or cut a teaspoonful of the seed and put in a cup, and fill with boiling water.  Let stand for thirty minutes, strain off.  When cool, drink. This should be done once a week for five to six weeks - beginning at the same time that the KalDak is begun.

9. Do these and we should find much better conditions for this body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(1/27/44 GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.

Watermelon seed for preparing the tea may be obtained from a seed store.

KalDak is distributed by The KalDak Company, Hollister Bldg., Lansing Michigan and sells for $2.50 a package.)
