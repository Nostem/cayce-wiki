# TEXT OF READING 325-24 F 56

**Reading [[325-24]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/12/1929  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2462

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of January, 1929, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Thos. B. House, and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - Mrs. [325] - this we have had before.  The body feels some better at this time; yet these are not satisfactory conditions as we find relating to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  There is the continuation of that accumulation as has been seen and given, as affects the body throughout, or that as comes to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about the breast [incipient cancer].  With the exercises of [[Physical Body|the body physical]] and mental, and whenever there are strainings of any character, whether of mental or physical, these manifest themselves in producing the strain on WHATEVER portion of the body as is under cycle change at that period.  As is seen here, the greater distress coming to the lower radial plexus or across the small of the back - and the lower limbs being cut off, in a manner, of the proper incentives through nerve condition - these bring acute conditions, through the attempt of these formations to cut off, as it were, the nutriment as should produce equalization in the system.  Hence there becomes necessary that the applications of heat, or of heat with those properties as will relax the centers so affected, sufficiently for the system to attempt to re-adjust itself under the present strain.  The applications as have been given have assisted, yet the stomach - the digestive system, especially lower portion of same - suffers still, as does now the nausea that comes spasmodically, and the headaches. These, then, are reflexes in the system attempting to adjust itself, or may be called properly retraction in system.

2. We would, then, CLEANSE the alimentary canal - not by that as would cause an accumulation in any portion of the body from the conditions as are existent throughout the alimentary canal, but rather those that will absorb or will give to the system those of sufficient energy in themselves to be active principles with the digestive system.

3. Begin, then, with a small amount of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] - not sufficient that same becomes rancid in the system, but ALTERNATE same with the [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil (in its original state, preferably), and these will be found to cooperate one with another.  Let at least two to two and a half hours intervene between the doses of each.  We would begin with a half a teaspoonful the pure [[Olive Oil|olive oil]].  Then in two and a half or three hours the tablespoonful of the [[Russia|Russian]] Oil. Continue this until there is a full reaction from the effects of this produced in the system, see?

4. THEN we would begin with manipulations - [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] and don't begin with these until this condition has thoroughly acted, see?  Gently given, and applying heat with that of the saturated solution of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] over shoulder and through that portion of the body where the pains become most severe.  The gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] - not too deep, but consistent and persistent.

5. Keep up many of those things as has been given, as in the form of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] - but these are NOT to be used unless the manipulations are given to equalize the effects produced.  Do that.  We will bring the bettered conditions for this body, Mrs. [325].

6. We are through with this reading.
