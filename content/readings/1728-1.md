# TEXT OF READING 1728-1 M 41

**Reading [[1728-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/25/1930  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 7926

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [1728] via Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1728] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  In the physical functionings of the body there are disorders that cause reactions that are NOT normal.  So, in that reaction, we find a body rather at dis-ease than diseased body.  This, then, as we would find, would have much to do with the MENTAL AND physical reactions.  To this body these conditions may best be described, possibly, in THIS manner, will these be followed closely:

2. There is the [[Physical Body|physical body]] with its attributes; the functioning organs of same considered as the physical man.

3. There is the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]] with ITS attributes; THIS also a portion of the physical man; the attributes of the [[Mental Body|mental body]] being those functioning organs known as the senses of the body.  While that condition creating any action from same is known from without, yet that on which the [[Physical Body|physical body]] becomes conscious OF same is through the attributes OF the body known as the organs of the sensory system.

4. There is also the [[Spiritual Body|SPIRITUAL body]], [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the ENTITY]], the WHOLE of the body with ITS attributes; the attributes being those of desire, of imagination, of those things builded which become akin or active with, as the functioning organs, as those of the conditions that become as the various activities or those conditions which become as habits, those ACCUSTOMED things. The attributes of same are of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], or that MIND of [[Soul|the soul]] and ITS [[Ideal|ideal]] - [[Soul|the SOUL]] of the body being that of the SPIRIT, or the life.

5. Now, how does this compare - then - in the outlook as to the physical conditions in THIS body as we have here?

6. In the comparisons, we find these become active in this manner, that - in the active forces of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], through those of the forces of the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]], become distorted in their reaction upon the physical being much in the same way and manner as habit distorts the [[Visions|vision]], the desire, the outlook, the imagination of the [[Spiritual Body|SPIRITUAL body]] - see?  In this manner, then, does these active forces upon the physical body become those conditions which bring about the distortedness of the activities, the ability to condemn self, find fault in self's own actions, in the replies or the retorts as may be made from others to the active forces of the body itself; yet in this, that the forces as are led in any active physical force OF the body must be in accord WITH those of the abilities of the imaginative and desire of body to be keeping the activities of the habits of the mind, of the sensory system, of the fleshly desires of the activities of the physical body, in accord WITH and for, and active TO, and active WITH, those in that held as [[Ideal|an ideal]] for the body.

7. In the physical forces, then, these bring about such conditions as we find, that in the active forces of the sensory system and the sympathetic nerve system the body LOSES its CONTROL of self; the physical reaction being through same to all the ORGANS of the body through those activities that are brought about between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal system.  These bring those conditions that, when the body is in its own action in keeping with those conditions being brought about in the system, become subjective TO those forces.

8. Then, to keep or to make, or to bring about the proper reactions, we would add to those of the physical forces that [[Vibration|vibration]] in the physical that would keep FOR the body the normal equilibrium between the cerebro-spinal and the sympathetic nerve system, or to the solar plexus reaction to that of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]]; adding to, then, that force that will RAISE for the body those of the forces through the mental reactions in the physical body as to keep the outlook in a POSITIVE, ACTIVE, manner.  This we may find will aid in bringing this about, WILL the body USE its OWN ABILITIES physical to assist in same, in the use of the Radio-Active Appliance - in the ordinary or plain form, but the APPLICATION being made TO the solar plexus AND to that of the first CERVICAL center.  These we would add at least once each day for at least twelve to twenty days.  THEN they may be taken whenever the body feels that feeling of that the OUTER man, or the skin itself, is SEPARATING itself from the INNER body, or that the whole of the body is as of duplex in itself.  This occurs before collapse in mental forces and in the reactions of the body.  When this is beginning to feel that this is arising, USE the appliance for at least an hour. During the first portion use it an hour each evening, as the body is ready to retire.  We will find the outlook, the changes in the physical forces, the changes in the whole of the life, changes in the whole mental outlook - but STUDY the connection in THAT manner as has been ILLUSTRATED for the body.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is meant by the first cervical center?
(A)  That position occupied between the axis of the head itself and the first and second cervical.

10. (Q)  What can be done to relieve the bronchial condition?
(A)  Change the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as come for the system as is in activity through those of making the proper coordination between that of sympathetic and cerebro-spinal, as will be seen by the application of the Radio-Active force, which RAISES the [[Vibration|vibration]] so that it makes of the portion of the body - through throat and bronchia, is relieved of that pressure as active through those of the secondary cardiac reflexes, as is known by the body.  Change these, so that the activities are through their natural channels, and this irritation disappears.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
