# TEXT OF READING 3141-1 M 83

**Reading [[3141-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/30/1943  Sex: M  Age: 83  ReadingID: 10509

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3141], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                                   At home in 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Pa.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3141].

3. As we find the analysis of this body that has been made is very good.  We would not change its applications that are being made for this body, save for the condition of the knee.

4. We would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the knee with table salt almost saturated with pure apple vinegar.  This will be most helpful.

5. We would also add low electricity to sustain and build the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body, as they are created by the administrations being made.  This should be in the form of the Short Wave or Diathermy.  This would be most helpful.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is the primary cause deficiency in vitamin B?  Is so why is this manifested only in that one nerve?
(A)  This as we find has been analyzed properly; it is not just one nerve but it shows in the one.  Hence the heat from the low form of the Diathermy will, with these massages, be beneficial.

9. (Q)  What will prevent the bronchial attacks?
(A)  These we find will come only when there is over-[[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] in the system.  Be mindful in the diet to keep to the more [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline-forming]] foods.  Eat plenty of citrus juices and fruits of all kinds, less of some characters of fruits - as apples or some characters of peaches; but the citrus fruits will keep down [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]].  Thus with the changes as will be wrought with the Diathermy, we should have less trouble with the bronchial disturbances.

10. (Q)  What causes the swelling of the legs?
(A)  This is a slowing of the circulation.  The use of the Diathermy and the heat, with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], should change all of these.

Do that.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> from the usual attacks - instead of coughing my insides out

> with very little coughing."

> correspondence.

> me with warm regards,

> Very sincerely yours,  [3141]

> infection or germs, the open spot or spots being in a
> healthful state although at times very painful.
> 
> Just now the sore spot has again closed as it did last
> February when it remained healed for some weeks and then

> I stopped taking the Vitamin B shots which I had been
> receiving into my veins.
> 
> After coming north from [[Florida|Florida]] in May I again took the
> Vitamin B shots also fluid Vitamin B complex medicine and
> the lip is almost normal.  Now I am wondering shall I
> continue the treatment which is being administered:  Namely
> the medicine after each meal and the shot into the system
> once a week.
> 
> Or is there a better procedure?  And what of my physical
> condition in general?
> 
> I also have trouble with my right knee.  Due to an injury of
> twelve years ago, it gets no sinovial [synovial?] fluid -
> anyway that is my opinion and unless I frequently manipulate
> it something gets out of place and presses on a nerve - at
> any rate that is what I think happens because of the pain
> that develops.
> 
> I really do not see how anything can be done about the knee
> - yet - who knows?
> 
> I shall be en rapport with you at the appointed hour - am
> looking forward to it full of hope.  Believe me.
> 
> Very Sincerely,  [3141]

> I presume that the questions should be something like

> b) If the primary cause is deficiency in vitamin B, why is

> c) Is the treatment now being administered the correct

> Can anything be done about my injured right knee?
> 
> Of late I notice at bed time that my legs from the ANKLE TO

>
