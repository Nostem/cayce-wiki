# TEXT OF READING 4143-2 F 25

**Reading [[4143-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/3/1932  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 11863

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 3rd day of December, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [4143], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mrs. [911].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4143].

2. Now, as we find, in the physical functionings there are caused those strains to the central portion of the nervous system, reflecting activities of the lower hepatic circulation and a strain that is felt at times for the kidneys.  This condition is more of a muscular and intercostal contraction from cold, congestion, and overtaxation of the body.

3. We would massage these portions with a counter-irritant that will stimulate the circulation, and at the same time add to the body those properties that will stimulate the elimination of used forces; then we will correct the conditions.

4. Such as:

5. For three to five days take small quantities, two to three times a day, of Eno Salts.

6. Morning and evening we would massage thoroughly with Bengay, or any counter-irritant, across the small of the back and that area disturbed.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
