# TEXT OF READING 3976-11

**Reading [[3976-11]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/6/1933  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11637

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 6th day of April, 1933, in accordance with request made by those present and by the Norfolk Study Group as a whole.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, Esther Wynne, Frances Y. Morrow, Helen Ellington, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and Leslie B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M.

1. GC:  You will give at this time a comprehensive discussion of how the people of the United States can best contribute to the spiritual development and progress of the world, answering first the question, "Through what measures and by what definite plans can we meet our own difficulties?" and second, "What definite programs should our nation sponsor to promote greater international harmony and peace?"  Please present this in such a form that it will be of help and interest to the average reader and may be presented for publication to some periodical from the Norfolk Study Group #1.  You will answer the questions which will be asked concerning this.

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find - while this is very poorly stated, to obtain any comprehensive outline, for it's stated backwards:

3. In considering conditions as they exist, and considering as to what individuals may do, and how the government should foster this or that movement, for a greater understanding, it is well that this be considered from the standpoint of what the Group represents first in the lives of individuals, and in the lives of groups, or masses, or classes.

4. Then to consider, too, as to the form of government and the policies of those who formed same, and how the people in their various walks of life have adhered to them, or how those principles have been fostered or furthered in their application in government towards the lives of the government's constituents.

5. When this, then, has been - or may be - presented from THIS angle, or view, then there may be given that of a definite nature as to what the group - as individuals, and as a group - may present as some form of application in the experience of individuals, groups, or masses, for consideration by those who are in authority.

6. For, as He has given, consider those in authority; for the powers that be are ordained from on High.

7. THEN may there be given through these channels that which may be of help.

8. We are through for the present.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 6/33 Paper by Esther Wynne:
> 
> PRESENT WORLD CONDITIONS
> 
> (A paper compiled by Esther Wynne from information given in
> the Edgar Cayce Readings 3976-8--3976-11.)
> 
> In the beginning of creation chaos existed until the Spirit
> of God moved over the face of the waters.  Out of chaos came
> our earth, our world, in all its beauty.  Nature, as the
> [[Garden of Eden|Garden of Eden]], came into existence with God's preparation
> for man's indwelling in joy and harmony.  With man's advent
> into the garden, personality asserted itself and man's
> selfish desire became a stumbling block in partaking of the
> forbidden fruit.
> 
> With personality and individuality finding expression in the
> subduing and replenishing of the earth, man, with his own
> will, not only subdued the earth but attempted to subdue his
> fellow man.  The results were the differences of opinion, as
> in the case of the rejection and acceptance of the offerings
> brought by [[Cain and Abel|Cain and Abel]], for in the acts of [[Cain and Abel|Cain]] we find
> there was a greater consideration given to his personal
> desires than service to his Maker or relation with his fellow
> man.  Hence, men began to divide themselves into various
> sects, classes, and races.
> 
> As the earth was peopled, the expansion of abilities brought
> many groups and associations into existence.  They withdrew
> into themselves and there arose in various portions of the
> world that known as periods of advancement of some particular
> group, people or race.  Abraham was called to be a blessing
> to all nations.  [[Moses|Moses]] was given training in the laws and
> teachings of the [[Egypt|Egyptians]], thus preparing him for the
> leadership of this peculiar people who were to become the
> givers of [[Law of Moses|the law]], for through this channel was to come mercy
> and [[Grace|grace]] to a tired, weary and war-ridden world in the
> Prince of Peace, of the house of [[King David|David]], of the stock of
> Judah, son of Jacob, of the seed of Abraham, the father of
> the faithful.
> 
> As the world advanced man developed various capacities and
> abilities that brought many changes in his political,
> economic and religious life.  In [[Greece|Greece]] the artistic
> appreciation of beauty in many modes of expression, language,
> architecture, sculpture, dramatic expression, reached a
> height unknown before or since that age.  [[Rome|Roman]] civilization
> gave to the world a system of laws that became the basis for
> all later judicial procedure.  The settlement of America was
> made by peoples who upheld and willingly died for the
> principle of freedom of thought and expression.  From many
> parts of the world there came to this new land people with as
> many ideas as there were various groups, but with one
> principle, one [[Ideal|ideal]] - FREEDOM:  of thought, speech,
> religion; and it has been upon this principle that the
> leading nation of the world in economic and financial circles
> built its prosperity.  Progress, real lasting progress, for a
> group, for a race, for a nation will and must come from a
> central [[Ideal|ideal]] held inviolate in the heart and mind of each
> individual.
> 
> With the advent of what may be termed this age of science and
> commercialism, men all over the world have drawn closer
> together.  Their financial, commercial and social relations
> have become more and more entangled.  The man on the other
> side of the world is today as much the neighbor as the man
> next door.  As these relations have become involved great
> turmoils have arisen in the attempt of individual leaders or
> groups to induce, force or compel one portion of the world to
> think as another.
> 
> Yet, all was not hopeless.  After the last great war there
> was a peace table and about it were gathered representatives
> of every nation under the sun.  In [[Soul|the soul]] of the
> representative of the United States sat the Prince of Peace,
> yet many in the economic and financial world would have you
> believe had this man never left his home the world would be
> better today.  Not so!  It has been written, "Ye shall pay
> every whit."  "The heavens and the earth may pass away, but
> My word shall not pass away, saith the Lord."  Did the United
> States neglect the opportunity to do all in her power to
> carry out the teachings of the Prince of Peace?  Is she not
> reaping that which she has sown?
> 
> Today, Europe is as a house broken up.  Nations are suffering
> from the terrible reaction brought upon them from harboring
> the thoughts of selfish greed and lust for power that tore
> the world asunder in 1914.  The people of one of the greatest
> nations of Europe have passed through the experience of being
> over-ridden for the gratification and satisfaction of a few.
> These peoples are being [[Reincarnation in the Bible|born again]], and are becoming the
> thorn in the flesh to many of the political and financial
> nations of the world.  Out of their trials and sufferings,
> with the [[Prayer|prayers]] and supplications of those who may pray will
> come a new light.  The hope of Europe depends upon you, in
> your home, in the same manner as did the life of Lot depend
> on Abraham's [[Prayer|prayer]] for the people of [[Sodom and Gomorrah|Sodom]] and Gomorrha.
> 
> Great [[England|Britain]] is fighting a losing struggle.  Non-resistance
> is hard to break.  Your [[Prayer|prayers]] and supplications will help
> to keep the millions of [[India|India]] from being broken and destroyed
> by internal strife, will help them to keep faith with
> themselves and with the rest of the world.
> 
> The United States faces the gravest crisis in its history.
> It cannot be expected to recover from the great economic
> depression until its basis for recovery is founded upon that
> which will bring peace and harmony.  The United States has
> been one of the leading nations in attempting to give an
> understanding of the principle, "Thou shalt love thy neighbor
> as thyself;" hence, it stands above most of the other nations
> of the world in financial and social position, yet it has
> faltered.  "KNOW YE THAT WHEN TROUBLES ARISE SIN LIES AT THY
> DOOR!"  Troubled seas lie ahead, war, famine and a greater
> economic depression are certain if the people do not rally to
> the standard of creating within their own lives as
> individuals a love of service for others.  "I will make my
> life a channel of blessing to someone today."  When the
> greater number of the people turn to this [[Ideal|ideal]] conditions
> will improve, not merely as man counts improvement but in
> contentment and understanding.
> 
> America has found that it cannot legislate goodness into the
> heart and [[Soul|soul]] of its people, nor will prohibiting a self-
> willed body keep it from sin.  When the right ideals of
> prohibition have been builded into the hearts and minds of
> the greater number of the people of the nation it will win,
> but it has already lost for the present and only waits the
> decision as to whether the people will stand as a law-abiding
> or a law-breaking nation.
> 
> With these conditions existing then:  Man has come to that
> place in his development where there must be a reckoning, for
> out of this turmoil that has arisen from social strife,
> racial differences, and economic relations, must come a
> better understanding of man's relationship to the Creative
> Forces and his relationship one to another.  You say at once
> that such a thing is impractical, impossible.  What has
> caused the present conditions throughout the world?  It is
> the realization that each is being asked from within that
> question as was asked some thousands of years ago:  "Where is
> thy brother?  ...the voice of thy brother's blood crieth unto
> me from the ground," and man is replying, "Am I my brother's
> keeper?"  The world, as a world has lost its ideal.  That
> makes for disruption and is content.  Men may not have the
> same idea, but man, all men may have the same ideal.
> 
> As the spirit of God moved to bring peace and harmony out of
> chaos, so must the Spirit now move over the earth and magnify
> itself in the hearts and minds and [[Soul|souls]] of men that they may
> dwell together in peace, harmony and understanding; for peace
> and harmony may only come from having the one ideal as set by
> Him who gave:  "Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all
> thine heart, thine neighbor as thyself."  This is the whole
> law, this the answer to the world's troubles today.  This
> ideal in the heart and soul of each will bring peace and
> plenty out of chaos.
> 
> How shall this be brought about?  As each, in his respective
> sphere, puts into action that which he knows to be the
> fulfilling of that ideal, that law of love, so will the
> little leaven leaven the whole lump.
> 
> Man's answer to everything has been POWER - power of money,
> power of position, power of this, that or the other.  This
> has never been God's way.  Rather, little by little, line
> upon line, here a little, there a little.  When individuals
> have taken thought of others they have kept the world intact.
> Where there were ten just persons, even, many a city, many a
> nation, has been saved from destruction.  We may feel or
> think the lesson given Abraham as he viewed the cities of the
> plain and plead for them is an allegorical story, a beautiful
> tale to be told children that it might bring fear into their
> hearts, but may it not warm our hearts today?  Will you make
> your own heart right and answer for your brother and
> neighbor?  Who is your neighbor?  he that lives next door, or
> he that lives on the other side of the world?  Rather, he who
> has faltered, he who has fallen by the way; he is your
> neighbor, and you must answer for him.
> 
> The question that is in every mind is, "Where can I turn for
> help?"  What will lead the world back to its ideals?  The
> responsibility of this leadership will not fall on any group,
> class, individual or sect that does not present His ideals.
> Did He not give, "The day cometh when neither in this
> mountain, nor yet in [[Jerusalem|Jerusalem]] shall you worship the Father
> ...but in spirit and in truth?"  That day is at hand.  The
> world will come out of this chaos, out of this attempt to
> grapple one with another for that which fades and dies with
> the day or years as it turns toward His teaching in, "Thou
> shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart and thy
> neighbor as thy self."
> 
> Written in June, 1933 -
