# TEXT OF READING 299-1 F 5 DAYS (Protestant Parents)

**Reading [[299-1]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/5/1931  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 2188

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of Sept., 1931, in accordance with request made by her parents, Mr. [282] and Mrs. [301] - and by her grandmother, Mrs. [255].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Born August 30, 1931, (Sunday, 6:11 P.M.), at the [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] 
Hospital, Philadelphia, Penna. 
Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those relations with the universe and universal forces as are - and WILL be - manifest in the present experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. In the naming, when all associations, relations, are considered, should be [299].  [EC named the baby.]

3. In entering, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] comes under those influences of Venus, Jupiter, Uranus, and in these respects there will be the necessity of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] BALANCED diversion for the entity AS its personality in the present experience develops.

4. This entity in this experience, as we find, will be unusual; not only to the parents, in this experience, but to others. Not strange, not curious, but UNUSUAL in its unfoldment IN its present surroundings; and during those years two and a half to four will be the periods when the greater care must be exerted as respecting its PHYSICAL, MENTAL, developments in the present.

5. In Venus will be seen that of affection on the part of the entity, and with decided likes and dislikes that will apparently of UNSEEMING ways which, unless guided in its proper manner, may make for that of a selfish and haughty air in the DEVELOPING to maidenhood and young womanhood; yet guided in a manner, not as of breaking [[Free Will|the will]], nor yet of letting the developing mind have its own way, but KNOWING that, that which may be cute or becoming in a developing mind, that grown to seed would be hauteur or selfishness, or that of any development in such a manner well to be curbed in REASON.

6. In Jupiter, as will be seen, beauty of face, figure, and mind.  These will naturally, with the incoming influence, make for one that will be inquisitive, and seeking to know the REASON, which - while not unusual ORDINARILY in a child or a DEVELOPING one - will be unusual to a marked degree in THIS entity as for CAUSES, as well as reason; making, then, those precautions that those who give answers to the seeking and developing mind give those that are an answer IN Truth as NEAR as IS possible to be done, under any and all circumstance!  This, as will be seen, will make for a development that will make apparent one ever OLD for its year, and years, yet the BEAUTY in the ABILITIES of the entity will repay all that are associated with same in the beauty of the life, in the development of the mental forces, in the ABILITIES of the entity in many - or all - directions. Hence nature, nature's activities, should be that as will ENABLE the body, physically, mentally, to ask its questions. Developing in a MODERATE manner the athletic body will aid in its better development in the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]], and unfoldment also.

7. In Uranus will make, as will be seen, those periods in the entity's development when there will be moods, temper will be expressed and manifest in its activities.  Well that those [[Visions|visions]], also those tendencies for [[Visions|visions]] of seeming exaggeration by the entity in such periods, be NOT curbed; rather made note OF, studied WITH the entity as its UNFOLDMENT in MENTAL developments shows in its activities. Colors, to be sure, will affect the body often.  Hence those of modes and violet would be well to have more OFTEN ABOUT the body, as WELL as those shades of BLUE that will make for a more EVEN development in the entity's activities.

8. Being in [[Sabbath|the Sabbath]] child, AS given, there will be those of the QUESTIONING often as to the variations in tenets held by those about the entity.  Hence in that environ as will be seen, in which the developments for and OF the entity most often came, will bring that BEAUTY IN the experiences of the entity for self and others, that will be most satisfactory. That, then, that has much of rote or ceremony, yet that that is an ENFOLDMENT and UNFOLDMENT of the love and bigness OF the SPIRITUAL life, the better environ for this developing entity.

9. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and their influence upon the present experience - the entity that that may be termed by the STUDENTS of psychological influences, or of psychopathic enfoldments, an OLD [[Soul|soul]].  Hence that tenderness WOULD those responsible for, those whose care [[Soul|the soul]] is with, SHOULD make in their activities WITH the budding of [[Soul|the soul]]'s awakening in this, a material and CHANGED world.

10. In the one then before this we find in that period when there were those dissensions, and those suppressions of the teachings in that land known as the [[Rome|Roman]] land.  The entity then among the children of those that were persecuted WITH their sires during that period, yet finding favor with those that saw the oppressions the entity, while growing under an environ that meant the suppression of activity, gained AND lost during that experience.  First known as Dafna, then changed in its environ and name to that of Sapina.  In the present experience will be found, from that influence, that of the tendency of hauteur and moods, and that necessary influence then that must bring those of a very POSITIVE - yet loving - directings of the entity's thoughts, as WELL as activities.

11. In the one before this we find in that land, that experience known as the [[Persia|Persian]] or Arabian Land, when there had been those developments in that land from those leading in a rebellion to the northern kingdom, and those had gathered about the leader.  The entity then an associate of, a sister TO, its OWN [present] MOTHER - in the NAME Melene.  In this experience the entity developed throughout.  The entity became one of a MUSICIAN of FAME, even in this plain.  In the development the entity applied those tenets that were given in that period to MUSIC, that it brought from the plains to those surroundings, that of the joy in service of one to another; becoming a [[Healing|healer]] in the latter experience through the [[Healing|laying on of hands]], in song and in the chants as were made by the entity and its associates.  In the present, with those directing influences, may the entity - in those of stringed instruments - bring AGAIN those of the shepherd song, again those of the mountain song, in rain, in sunshine, in the various activities in nature.  Hence those directing influences will bring that awakening that will bring joy, harmony, peace, loving understanding, to self and many.

12. In the one then before this we find in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, and following those periods when there were the rebuilding and the activities of those that gave the understanding to many of the tenets in that period.  The entity then was among those that came in the household of [[Ra Ta|the priest]] [[[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]]?] during that period, being then in the latter days an EMISSARY to the various peoples that that land took, or undertook, to disperse those teachings to.  The entity again a musician in reeds, and a worker in fine linens; yet a one of note in SONG during that period.  In the name Del-lli. In the present there will be seen that art that pertains to linens, cloths, or those of bas-reliefs, will be of interest to the entity; and - guided aright - may the song of the inner self be awakened, as was [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

13. As to the abilities, and that to which it may attain, and how:

14. As is seen in the unfoldment of a life, a soul, in each experience - that to which it may ATTAIN depends upon that which is builded in its formative years as its personality. It either conforms to the [[Ideal|ideals]] of its individuality, or is changed to become something apart - bringing adversities and contention when made separate.  When made one with that which is as continuity of Life and Force as manifests itself in the earth through the influences of the DIVINE, as through the Son, do the blessings come; as was said, "SUFFER little children to come unto me, for of such is the KINGDOM of heaven," that "He that offends one of these, my LITTLE ones, has offended me indeed."  Keep, then, inviolate, its experiences.  Thrust not thine own dominion, yet teach, train the little one to know, in HIM is peace and understanding, in Him is the way for all earth - as in heaven - to know the JOY of BEING alive!  Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Describe faults in present life and give guiding advice to parents for correction.
(A)  As has been given.  These may be developed from influences.  These depend upon the activities of those in the directions as has been outlined.

16. (Q)  Describe talents, and if there is one for music, name instrument.
(A)  As has been given, STRINGED instruments.

17. (Q)  In what church should entity be [[Baptism|baptized]] and taught?
(A)  As HAS been outlined.  In the church of the fathers, the better.

18. (Q)  For what field of service in this [[Incarnation|incarnation]] should entity be prepared?
(A)  As has been outlined, there are varied fields - as may be seen by the consideration of the abilities, and that to which the entity has attained.  Either in that of the field as of the TRAINER for others, or in that of the AID to others, in ITS ability to bring [[Healing|HEALING]] to others.

19. (Q)  What years will make periods of greatest development of entity in this [[Incarnation|incarnation]]?
(A)  Two and a half to four and a half years.  Then again there will occur in the thirteenth to the fourteenth year.

20. (Q)  What years should parents be warned as containing hazards to be guarded against?
(A)  Two and a half to four years.

21. (Q)  Has this entity been associated with [282] or [301], its present parents, in other [[Incarnation|incarnations]]?
(A)  As has been given, it has.  [9/8/31 Father's Ck. Life Rdg. 282-3, Par. 7-A indicates he was the husband of his present wife [301] and father of his present daughter [299] in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] incarnation.]

22. That is all the questions.

23. As developments come, as changes arise, as the UNUSUAL activities are seen, much may be added to THIS entity's development.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
