# TEXT OF READING 3011-1 M 68 (B.S., M.D., Writer, Lecturer)

**Reading [[3011-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/22/1943  Sex: M  Age: 68  ReadingID: 10319

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 22nd day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3011], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. [3011] and his wife, Mrs. [3248].]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3011] present in this room.

2. As we find, there are conditions that prevent the better physical functioning.

3. While in the present this is a tendency, if allowed to continue it will more and more destroy the ability of the body to control the voluntary muscular reactions, - especially as related to locomotion.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [3011] we are speaking of, present in this room:

5. In the blood supplies we find there has come the incoordination of the blood supplying to the re-energizing of the ganglia and the muscular centers through the body; especially as related to those areas where sympathetic - or vegetative nerve forces and the central nerve system coordinate.

6. Those disturbed in the present are the lumbar areas and through the brachial centers.

7. The assimilating has come to be depleted, gradually, as carried on through activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] through the alimentary canal, as well as that controlled by the activity of the digestion through the action of the secretions from the liver, the spleen, the pancreas and the gall duct.

8. Thus there is a lack of the ability of this assimilation to produce the vital forces as represented by certain elements, metals, in the body.

9. These are the effects and the reactions produced in the locomotory areas in the present.

10. The nerve forces in the body, as indicated - there is the lack of the ability of that assimilated to assist in reproducing for those ganglia controlling the lumbar axis and the sciatic, and the brachial plexus and those to the upper parts of the body.  These are the areas affected in that incoordination, producing the inability of the body to control the muscular and nerve flexes.  Thus some flexes become voluntary when they should be controlled by the plexus from the central nervous system - or brain.

11. This is NOT the effect of drain through the arterial circulation or any of that nature, but that in the assimilating system - as indicated.

12. As to the effects upon the organs of the body:

13. Under the general stress, vitality has been used up - in this body - without sufficient elements of certain characters and natures to be supplied the body.  And thus a form of deterioration, though - as we find - there may be supplied elements to the body that will aid in re-establishing better flexes, better reaction, bettered conditions in the assimilating forces of the body.

14. We find that these may be added to the body better through [[Vibration|vibratory]] reaction to the body force itself.

15. Then:

16. In the radioclast - or the building of that activity of the low electrical forces - carry the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of Gold and Silver to the body.  There should be twice the Gold to one of the Silver, if there will be the correct proportions maintained for the better supplying - or re-supplying - of that element, and elements, needed in the body. [Radioclast OR [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]?]

17. Prepare the Gold in the proportions of one grain of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of Distilled Water.  It is not necessary to use more than some ten drops of the solution at a time in the radioclast.  Do this two days in succession; then skip one day.

18. Then use in the radioclast a ten percent solution of the Silver Nitrate - a 10% solution.  Here, only use three drops, see?  Then skip a day, again.

19. Give then the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] again for the two days.

20. And so on.  Keep this up, rotating the applications in this manner; two days of the Gold, skipping one day; one day of the Silver, skipping one day; then two of the Gold, and so on.

21. Each evening, when ready to retire, apply a [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] pack - that is, two to three thicknesses of cotton cloth well saturated with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - over the lumbar axis AND over the brachial center; that is, the 2nd and 3rd dorsal, and in the 3rd and 4th lumbar centers.

22. After this has been applied for thirty minutes, then gently massage the areas - in a short circular motion throughout these areas.

23. These, as we find, will materially aid in correcting the conditions for this body, [3011].

24. Ready for questions.

25. (Q)  Should I continue the Vinobel tablets I am now taking as a palliative?
(A)  As we find, if it is necessary to use ANY of such applications (and we would leave these off until this new treatment is given a thorough try), we would use the Hyocine tablets.  These are preferable to that being taken.

26. (Q)  What strength Hyocine?
(A)  Not too strong.  But do not use these unless it becomes necessary, after the use of the applications suggested here, for at least five to six weeks.

27. (Q)  What causes the heart symptoms, and can they be overcome?
(A)  There is too great a laxness in the circulation between heart, lungs, liver and kidneys.  Drawing on this circulation causes that fullness which occurs in the heart area.  This is an attempt to create a balance between the circulation of the sympathetic (or vegetative) and the central nervous system. This is nerve as well as blood supply.

But with the use of these metals, vibratorially given, there should be a change for the better.

28. (Q)  Can anything be done for the vocal cords, to reduce the tone of voice?
(A)  All of this is a part of the sensory reaction from the area in the brachial center which controls the organs of the sensory system.  And these will become normal; not so much as to changing of tone but in the regularity - or the ability to CONTROL will be the improvement.

29. (Q)  Is it possible to regenerate my body so as to carry on my work for many years longer on this earth?
(A)  This is what we have been trying to give here!  These are the elements that are more oft lacking where the mental and spiritual forces have been expended at the expense of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], as with this body.

This as indicated is for the purely physical.

As for the mental and spiritual, - keep the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] that are a part of the experience.

30. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
