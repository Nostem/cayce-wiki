# TEXT OF READING 2655-1 F 29 (Actress, Religious Preference,

**Reading [[2655-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/21/1942  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 9725

"Metaphysics") This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2655], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [815].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born October 5, 1912, in McAllen, [[Texas|Texas]]. 
Time of Reading 
10:40 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane; giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '39 - changes - '35 - anxieties - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2655].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, it might be well to indicate those urges that are latent - and many of those influences about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

5. For, this information is given with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that it may be a helpful influence for this entity; enabling the entity to better fulfil, - materially, mentally, spiritually, - those [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this experience.

6. It would be well that the entity have about it at all times the emblem of the lotus; this in white, and in some form that would be, not as a charm but as an ornament, as well as a protective influence in the experience of the entity.

7. As we choose those portions of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] to be interpreted, these are given that the entity may minimize the faults and magnify the virtues, - in self and in others.

8. When there is considered the composite urges latent and manifested through [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]] or aspects, as well as through the material [[Incarnation|sojourns]], we find these are characteristics or latent urges apparent in the experience of the entity:

9. One very sincere, very open and very emphatic; and at times rather hard in criticism of others, or too plain-spoken.

10. These may be virtues or they may be faults.  The sincerity, to be sure, is a virtue; but minimize the faults, magnify the virtues, as ye speak of others.  For, with the measure ye mete it will be measured to thee again.

11. The entity at times is dramatic in such expression, and thus an excellent speaking voice.  And this may be used or applied in the associations or activities with others.

12. Then, these applications more in the form of mirth or comics should be stressed, rather than too much of dramatics.  For, the entity is inclined at times to be moody, and self-condemning - and may speak that which may be a hindrance, unless the ridiculous or the comic or funny side of every problem is taken into consideration.

13. The entity has an excellent voice as respecting speaking or singing.  Thus those parts including these should be stressed, to make for the greater development.

14. Choose those directions ye shall take.

15. From unsolicited and unexpected sources, there will be small or great incomes suddenly coming to the entity - before its thirty-fifth year.

16. The urges arising from [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]] should not be considered merely as influences of [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, but rather as urges in the experience of the mental and spiritual self from sojourns in those environs, - just as the study of language may make for the creating of the environ in such.

17. Then, we find urges arising from Venus, Mercury, Jupiter, Saturn and Uranus.  For, the entity will reach that experience when the Uranian influence will be the more manifested in the life - as there has been the material sojourn in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]].

18. Hence the intenseness, or the ability and the urge to be VERY - VERY good, or bad.

19. We find in Venus the beauty, art, music; the ability to attract - especially the opposite sex.  This, without urge, is the natural intenseness and tenseness that is the [[Intuition|intuitive]] influence, - expressed periodically or spasmodically.  Such should be cultivated, but know that the mental and the material must be the result of spiritual laws as well as material.  Thus those urges and those pronouncements shall be in love, in kindness, and not in criticism nor in malice nor in jealousy.

20. In Mercury, tempered with Saturn, we find the moods that are expressed, as well as the changes; and those high mental abilities, the easier retention in the mind of impressions and influences.  Thus those suggestions that are ever creative, or representations of same, should be a part of the daily environ, - as in the charm of the lotus.

21. We find in Jupiter the universality of the entity's abilities, as well as the application of same.  Hence those activities in which the entity should engage, and in which it will find the greater abilities for expression, should have to do with the general public, as masses; yet the expressions individual.  Hence in the radio first would the abilities find the outlet for the better activity of the entity, - in story, in the expressions in same - but always, the more oft, the lighter, the comic or the laughter producing vein.

22. From Uranus we find the [[Intuition|intuitive]] nature, as well as the extremes.  These will become more a part of the experience as the choices are made and as there is the development in those fields of expression indicated as an outlet for the activities of the entity.  And there needs to be maintained spirituality in all of the entity's approaches to every problem, to every condition, whether of the material or social nature or what not.

23. The entity is naturally a good politician; hence a very good leader.  Any group with which the entity may be associated will listen, because of the sincerity of the entity.  Be more optimistic than pessimistic; though the judgments should be from the spiritual angle, ever.

24. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - we find that these have been quite varied.

25. Before this the entity was in the vicinities of the land of the present sojourn, and the entity was a member of a group called barnstormers, - during the periods following the reconstruction in 1812.

26. Those experiences brought an outlet wherein the entity came in association and contact with many characters.

27. Thus the entity unconsciously judges human nature, and mostly the first impressions are correct.

28. The entity gained in those activities, though it builded the home in the latter portion of its experience that became an outlet for greater activities.

29. Then the name was Lucille Barnswell.  It may be said that the entity gained throughout that experience.  From same there are carried the latent urges in the present, - as in the abilities of stage or screen, as a speaker, as a composition writer.  And if the entity would make the application of self in those directions of writing, - of scripts that depict the story of home and its protective influences, - these may bring an outlet for expression in which great activities may be a part of the experience here and now.  These may be stories for screen or for broadcasts.  And the entity may find the outlet or the manner in which to apply self in these directions by first being IN the position of a broadcaster itself.

30. Before that the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] land, among those peoples during the period when there was the attempt of those in authority to embrace the activities of the Grecians, - and especially their spread of the acting, whether for the arena in fetes of manhood or otherwise, - and these later became - or took on - the greater expression of speechmaking, or declarations, or declamations and oratories.

31. Then the entity was in the opposite sex, - being beautiful in body, and made those experiences when the ability for its attraction for the opposite sex brought periods of turmoil. And these may be met in the present experience by the inactivity that attracts when not meant to be.

32. The abilities as the speaker, writer, also arise from that activity, as Guilfuld.  [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] the entity gained, the entity lost.

33. And in the meeting of same in the present, keep thy judgments.

34. Before that the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] and Arabian land, though the entity was of those peoples from [[India|India]] who came into the "city in the hills and the plains."

35. Thus all things pertaining to the orient, especially of [[Persia|Persia]], [[India|India]] and [[Egypt|Egypt]], have their meaning for the entity.

36. Coming under the influence of the teacher in the hills and the plains, the entity became a leader in the dance of truth, - to music that was set to bring the interpretations of the emotions of individuals.

37. Hence the present [[Intuition|intuitive]] influence of the activities that may be a part of the entity as an entertainer in many fields of service.

38. The name then was Leileu.

39. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land during those periods of reconstruction; being among the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] who came into the land with the ideas and [[Purpose|purposes]] which had been thwarted by the [[Sons of Belial|sons of Belial]].

40. Thus the contacts with the peoples in [[Egypt|Egypt]] for a greater portion of the experience brought mental hardships for the entity.  Being a priestess of the [[Sons of the Law of One|children of the Law of One]], not to be in the forefront brought consternation.  But with the associations with [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] and the coordinant factors in the [[Sons of the Law of One|children of the Law of One]] (for the entity then was a daughter of one in authority in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]], and thus a priestess as well as a princess), there was the ability to aid by the very high [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body itself.

41. And the entity has [[Healing|healing]] in its hands in the present.

42. The name then was Su-Er-To.

43. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

44. In the fields of the entertainer on stage or screen, or on radio, the entity may carry out those promptings and the abilities of the entity in the present.

45. The greater field may be in that of writing script or screen stories, or for such activities, and of that character that particularly makes for the preservation of home as the basis of creating the better environs in ANY land.

46. Then, let those [[Ideal|ideals]], spiritually and mentally, be the guiding influences, and the material things of life will come in their regular course.

47. Let that mind be the builder that is the intuitive force for the Christlike hope of man.

48. Ready for questions.

49. (Q)  What periods of time, during 1942, would you suggest I seek to expand my professional activities and in what direction or field?
(A)  In the early Spring, and especially on the radio - first.

50. (Q)  In what manner could my husband, [2706] aid my professional career in the field of Public Entertainment?
(A)  By the presentation and the instruction and direction in same.

51. (Q)  How should I start to develop the ability to write?
(A)  It is already latent.  And, as indicated, by the application of self in the radio prepared script, in the field suggested, it will be seen to be as a part of the entity's own experience.

52. (Q)  In what branch could I be most useful to the Government at this time?
(A)  As an entertainer, for there's the natural attraction to the abilities in any entertaining - of skits or plays or the like.

53. (Q)  What could I contribute to my marriage, towards its lasting success?
(A)  Know that it is a fifty-fifty proposition.  Don't build it on "What may I get?" but rather on "What may I give?"

54. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
