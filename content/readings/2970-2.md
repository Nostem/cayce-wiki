# TEXT OF READING 2970-2 F 49

## Text

Date: 6/2/1944  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10248

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2970], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Mrs. [2970].]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  Wash. 9, D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here: [2970].

3. As we find, there are tendencies which, unless corrections are made, will eventually cause conditions to become very disturbing to the body.  These, as we find, are the result of conditions becoming unbalanced in the chemical reactions, or the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, in its inability through the sources of the processes in [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reactions of reproducing the cellular forces necessary to replace normal conditions. These are indicated from conditions which exist at times in hands, arms, feet and limbs; not a stiffness, but a soreness and the lack of vital forces or energies being active in the extremities, especially.

4. These also produce those conditions where at times there is apparently a rash, or spots on portions of the body, conditions where there are apparently accumulations in swollen areas, or a [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], as indicated in portions of the head or face, as in conditions through portions of the body, as the mammary gland, as in the glands through the groin at times.  All of these are the effects of these conditions through the body.

5. We would find that there should be about twice a month a thorough [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment.  This would mean a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] rather than the dry heat, this [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] with witchhazel; and also a thorough massage with Pine Oils.

6. And then about once a week, until about ten or twelve have been taken, have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments which are relaxing to the body; not those that would stimulate activities or secretions, but the relaxing so that there is more uniform coordination between superficial and deeper circulation. Hence the specific areas, 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervicals, throughout the brachial center, 9th dorsal and throughout the lumbar and sacral areas, using the limbs as leverages at times for these centers.

7. Increase the eliminations somewhat, especially through the periods that these activities are being taken.  We would alternate in this manner:  For five days take just teaspoonful of Eno Salts of a morning before any meal is taken.  Leave it off, skip three days and through the next five days take a senna base laxative.  This taken each morning for five days.  Leave off for three days, then take the Eno Salts again for the five days.  Keep this up with this alternating until at least full corrections are made in the hydrotherapy as well as the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments. Here we will find we'll have much better coordinating and balance throughout the activities of the body and we'll rid it from this arthritic tendency.

8. In the matter of the diet do take more raw foods, especially watercress, carrots, and such foods and do include with these gelatine oft.  These grated, ground, but do use the juices of same with gelatine.  These, with the other corrections, will change conditions of eyes, throat and all of the organs of the sensory system; improve hearing, [[Visions|vision]], vocal box, as well as taste and sense of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] about the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  How can I eliminate the growth over right eye?
(A)  Massage with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] at least every evening.

11. (Q)  What causes sleeplessness?
(A)  We have been indicating the incoordinations, and through the use of these massages, setting up better eliminations, setting up better conditions through the glandular systems, we'll have bettered conditions in normal rest and relaxation.

12. (Q)  Should any of the teeth be extracted?
(A)  We would use Ipsab as a massage.  We do not find other than some local attention needed.  There's some need to be cleaned and then it will require about four fillings.

13. (Q)  How can I restore eyesight to eliminate use of glasses?
(A)  This will not be entirely eliminated but the suggestions in the diet and the massage indicated and do use head and neck exercises.  These all will improve [[Visions|vision]], it'll not eliminate entirely, for the nerves and the longation of the [[Visions|vision]] require help; these will be improved, however, a great deal.

14. (Q)  What exercises would be suggested to strengthen muscles and overcome sagging protruding abdomen?
(A)  Walking is the best exercise.  Also in the morning and at night, on hands with feet placed against the wall, do the circular motion once or twice; not easy in the beginning but these will be most helpful and strengthening for the body. Do this at least three times.  The first two or three times, don't try it more than once or twice.

15. (Q)  Am I in any danger of mental unbalance due to too exclusive study or religion and philosophy?
(A)  Do not study so much; though not any danger of unbalance in those directions.  Don't study so much, but apply it. You'll get a lot more out of it and it'll do others more good too.

16. (Q)  Any spiritual advice that would help me physically?
(A)  As ye would that others should do to you, do ye even so to them.  This is not merely a trite saying, not something, just to be said but in every activity, consider the things that you do and the things that you say.  Would you want them said about you?  Would you want them done to you?  Would you want them thought about you?  Should you want them to forget you?  These are different when they are explained to self in that manner and in using this.  For remember, as ye do it unto others, ye do to thy Maker.

17. We are through with this reading.
