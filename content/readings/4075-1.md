# TEXT OF READING 4075-1 F 65 (High School Teacher)

**Reading [[4075-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/7/1944  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 11771

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4075], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                                At home of 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M.                  his daughter, 
Taken 11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  That's a pretty big territory to look for one little house, and not your own house!

3. Yes, we find the body here [[Prayer|praying]] - this is very good.

4. We have the body here.  There are disturbing conditions that are in the greater portion the result of neglect on the part of the body as related to proper eliminations, because of the general conditions and activities of the body.

5. Then these have set up toxic conditions through the body, which is indicated by the periods of neuritic tending toward arthritic tendencies - by the swelling in portions of the body, and especially extremities at times.

6. Under the nerve strain on the body in its activities, these produce a great deal of anxiety, a great deal of pessimism and makes the body rather cross and crabbed at times.  This is not very good for those you instruct.

7. Hence there would be needs for the body analyzing itself, its [[Purpose|purposes]], its aims, its desires and hopes.

8. We would begin with the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments at least once each week, including mild sweats and a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]], with a great deal of rubbing.  Occasionally we would have also a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]]; especially should there be a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] in the beginning, and given properly.

9. In the course of two or three weeks, as these conditions are clarified, then begin to supplement the foods with vitamins, especially B and B complex.  Here we find it would be well to use the One-A-Day B vitamin complex.

10. Do these and we will bring much better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What should be done for the ear and nose condition?
(A)  Do those things indicated, for these conditions are a part of the blocking through the poor eliminations.  These disturbances should be eliminated with the sweats, the massages and the rubs that should come with the general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and the setting up of drainages through alimentary canal - and the activities through the character of treatments indicated.

13. We are through with this reading.  [See note below.]

Two copies to Self (one for hydrotherapist) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(If you experience difficulty in having these treatments followed, we would refer you to one of our members in your vicinity:  Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, 105 N. Van Ness Ave., [[California|Los Angeles]].)
