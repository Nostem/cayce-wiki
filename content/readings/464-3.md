# TEXT OF READING 464-3 F 51

**Reading [[464-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/18/1926  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 3384

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [464].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are improvements in the physical conditions of the body, and the body needs only to be careful of those conditions that would bring about those returning conditions that would cause the toxins to produce, as it were, re-infection to the muscular tissue in the locomotaries and in the tissue of muscular forces in the body.  The correct incentives for the functioning are set up - not wholly and complete, but the manipulations given occasionally would assist this in being brought near to normal.

4. High enemas will assist the body in preventing toxins forming in the colon.  These we would use occasionally, using the antiseptic in the water, and this amount:  To a gallon and a half of water, add tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Use this tepid, and we will find this will bring better conditions for the body.

5. Keep careful of the diet as has been outlined.  As much exercise as is feasible for the body in the open air.  This will ever be beneficial, see?

6. Follow in these lines, and we will bring the better conditions for this body, Mrs. [464].

7. We are through with this reading.

---

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 11/22/26 "I will go on with the tr. and if all there is to
> it is to keep it up, I can do that easy enough.  I do want to
> get well, for there are so many interesting things to do.  I
> just love to work.  For the last 14 yrs. I haven't known if
> I have been on my head or my heels most of the time, not one
> well day have I known.  My head has been like an inflated
> balloon most of the time, and my legs teeter like a drunken
> person.  Folks think I am queer.  So do I.  If I should
> recover from this dreadful condition I am afraid I am going
> to become very much alarmed.  I'll begin to worry about
> myself and think that there is something really serious the
> matter with me.  Such is habit.  Or else become lonely and
> melancholy for my dear departed miseries.  Could such a thing
> be?  like a dog without fleas.
> 
> "Many thanks to you all for your kindness to me.  I will
> follow instructions as closely as possible and you know to
> cure me will be a real victory, one you can depend on for
> future use.  I am supersensitive and we have all of the
> emotions of that type to contend with as well as the
> physical."
> 
> R2. 12/6/26 EC ans'd her request for a sympathetic correspondent
> who had experienced the benefit of the rdgs., by referring
> her to Mrs. [2457].
> 
> R3. 12/30/26 "I am sick in bed and the doctor who is taking care
> of me gave me an idea, so I am going to act on it.  I asked
> him what [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] is and he said it purifies the
> blood 'Have you been taking it?  Are you sure it is Chloride
> of Gold you got?'  [It turns black.]  So I am going to mail
> my bottle to you and have your doctor, who is with you in
> your work, tell me if I have been on a wild goose chase all
> this time or not...  I have had a siege of it this week -
> temperature 105.  My liver throwing bile into my stomach,
> seemingly, until my mouth tastes like bitter aloes, and I
> cannot take any food since Mon."
> 
> R4. 1/10/27 "You should see my pretty yellow face - ha! ha! ha!
> In your last rdg. you said that my body was not throwing
> off the poison as it should.  Don't worry.  The pills the
> doctor has given me are going after my liver with pitchforks
> and shovels.  I have no strength but I am living in hopes
> that will get around again sometime."
> 
> R5. 1/12/27 EC's letter:  "I suppose this concoction has been
> CALLED [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]].  I will get this fixed in Norfolk
> and sent to you.  Then I know it will be right."
> 
> R6. 1/17/26 "Dear Mr. Cayce, I received your letter and I am
> sorry to hear that you have been sick & glad to know that you
> are better again.  I was in bed 8 days.  I had to get up
> early every day to dress a boil Mr. [husband] had in the
> back of his neck and as soon as they see me on my feet they
> expect me to go to work.  So I am back on the [[Book of Job|job]] again.
> Well enough or not - it makes no difference, but my face is
> clearing out & I look like a ghost.  I cannot understand so
> much suffering especially among Christian people who ought to
> get the good things of life instead of that they get the
> worst of it.  I want you to send me a bill for the Gold and
> for your expenses in connection with it.  I had a feeling
> that all was not as it should be.  6 months wasted with a
> round up of the jaundice.  I had a lovely letter from Mrs.
> [2457].  I believe you folks had something to do with it.
> That proves a friendly spirit - which goes a long way with
> me.  For I find, I am sorry to say, would be friends quite
> indifferent which is a real sorrow to me.  However be that as
> it may.  Mrs. [2457] wrote a letter that pleased me very
> much & I want to tell you that she said if she did not go to
> [[Florida|Florida]] as she wants to go she has invited me to go to
> ... [Ala.] for a month to see what she could do for me, under
> your instructions of course.  I was perfectly amazed.  When
> you stop to think what all that would mean to her in trouble
> and possibly inconvenience for a perfect stranger is
> decidedly out of the ordinary and fairly took my breath away.
> For my own friends right in [[Ohio|Cleveland]] have not so much as
> darkened my door except the Minister's wife next door who
> comes in almost every day and I love her.  People are too
> busy with their own affairs to bother and I cannot blame
> them.  They have troubles of their own I dare say.  Well Mrs.
> [2457] wanted to see my readings so I sent them to her.  I
> was too sensitive.  Can you imagine how supersensitive people
> suffer trying to get through this world.  There is a
> continual clash.  In later years I have learned not to be so
> foolish but I don't always succeed.  It is often more
> sensitiveness than nervousness.  Both effect the physical in
> the same way.  Mrs. [...] has been very fine just as you
> said.  Since the rising of the sun I have been thrice
> blessed.  The sun must be my benefactor."
> Yours sincerely, [464]

> back but the last 2 wks. it feels as though it had been
> caved in...My antipathy to the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] may have something
