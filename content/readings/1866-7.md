# TEXT OF READING 1866-7 F 38

**Reading [[1866-7]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/24/1930  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 8284

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1866].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find in many respects the body is much bettered than as from those conditions as existed in body before; yet we find disorders and a distress through the activities of the sympathetic nerve forces, as related to those of the SENSORY system - as is indicated by the conditions existent with the eyes, the throat, and with the general nervous system.

2. While we will find that the manipulations, and the activities of the manipulations and corrective forces as are being applied - or may be applied to the body for these corrective measures - these will aid materially in correcting those of the blood supply and the nerve energy, as in their relation TO the sensory system - but for the eyes themselves proper, we will find that - will there be used those of the Degnen's lens for same [Made by [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] Co. in [[California|Los Angeles]].] - these will materially aid.  These, as we find, should be used for from thirty to thirty-five minutes each day.  This, we would find, would be better were they used just before retiring.  Then, when these have been used, use those of the scraped pulp of the Irish potato over the eye socket itself; bathing same off - when this is removed - with a mild solution of those acids as cleanse for the eyes themselves, see?  so that there will be no infection from same.  This we would do at least EVERY OTHER day, see?  but keep the manipulative measures, and build up the general system as related to the VITALITY of the body itself, using as much of the olive oil and those of beef or calves' liver as the system will digest.  Do not take large quantities of the oils at one time, but as much as the body WILL assimilate.  Well, or better, to take OFTEN and in SMALL quantities, that the system assimilates same and it becomes as food values for the whole of the digestive and assimilating system; so that the blood supply, and that as is assimilated, carries as much of those vitamins as necessary to replenish those that are lacking in a portion of the white blood itself in the body. Keep this up, and we will find this will bring a normalcy to this body, [1866].  Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
