# TEXT OF READING 4355-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4355-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/15/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12185

This Psychic Reading, given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by Mr. [953].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                             4 miles N. of 
4:30 P. M.                                  ..., Mo.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find there are many abnormal conditions.  Many that may be corrected and assist this body to gain the better equilibrium throughout its system and to give the better [[Vibration|vibrations]] for itself, and to those whom it may come in contact with.  Much of the condition has to do with the mental attitude toward others and that that it has (this body) brought to itself through this condition manifesting itself through this [[Physical Body|physical body]], [4355], we are speaking of.  Now these are conditions as we find in this body.

2. In the blood supply we find the body has sufficient in quantity but in that of the forces as is carried in the system or those that give much of the troubles to the body in the poor elimination as has been created by this has carried in the blood in the form of the bacteria as shown there.  In the numbers of red blood below normal at times.  Again above normal for the white blood supply carries much of the condition to produce troublesome force, especially in nerves and in ligaments that give locomotion and the action as is exercised through circulation and capillary circulation, controlled by mental or nerve forces through mental force over this body.

3. In the nerve system we find the body racked at times with pain, too much of the nerve centers, both of direct and of auxiliary centers.  Two plexuses, see?  [LS:  I see.]  This being produced by the nerve condition and the poor elimination in its effect over the system.  Both cerebro-spinal and sympathetic involved is also directly. The sensory organism, as to the hearing, in the organs proper this condition we find in the body.

4. The brain force in its action in system does not coordinate with the mental force in the care for the system throughout, though the body is good of mental forces, mental capacity, is normal for this body.  In the sensory organism this incoordination shows, as we have given, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] are not produced through auditory forces as proper they should be.  This is much of mental force, and might be controlled through this, though for this body physical conditions must take place that will create those impressions that will give the incentives for this activity, see?  [LS:  I see.]

5. In the lungs, very good.  Heart forces active to the normal forces, yet often above normal.  Digestive forces very good. The liver, spleen and digestive tract below the stomach fair for the condition in body, though gives disturbances at times.  Hepatic circulation, and the organs of same, show much of this condition that has been brought in the system itself by the forces through the reflexes of mental with physical, as manifested in the body, see?  [LS:  I see.]

6. RELIEF, to give the body the best for the rejuvenating and rebuilding of the forces we would find much that would be the best in the study of self, through that force as known as Unity, see?  [LS:  I see.]  And those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that would be proper for the body to gain the control over the lymphatic, the nerve vibration, and to produce the coordination would be found in this.  To one gallon of rain water, ADD:

Sea Salt.....................1 pound,
Copper Sulphate..............1 pound,
Sulfuric Acid C.P........1 1/2 ounces,
Common Zinc..................6 drams.

7. In this solution there would be placed two pieces of carbon steel, on either side of the solution you see, not touching. To these steels, which would be one inch in diameter, width about eight to ten inches in length.  Attach small copper wires to these wires to other end.  Small plates of nickel or [[Germany|German]] Silver.  These plates would be attached the positive side to the wrist, the negative side to the ankle, one first one side then the other, see?  [LS:  I see.]  Attach to the wrist on right side, to the left ankle, for at least thirty minutes each evening, when the body is ready to retire.

8. Do that.  We will find we will bring much relief, much change of vibration, much change of the action in physical and mental to this body for its better development, [4355].

9. We are through.
