# TEXT OF READING 3705-1 M 64

**Reading [[3705-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/17/1944  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 11210

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3705], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the Aug. ''43 Rosicrucian Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Beside Lake ..., 
Time of Reading                         four miles North of 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Maine.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - beautiful country.

3. We have the body here, [3705].

4. Now as we find while conditions are somewhat allayed from those that have existed in the general physical disturbances of the body, these depend a great deal upon the manner in which the body controls its appetites or its activities - as to whether the body will be continually on the improve.

5. In times back there has been a great deal of acidity or, owing to the position of the stomach, regurgitation.  This caused the acids of the hydrochloric formations through the duodenum to regurgitate in the pyloric portion of the stomach; thus causing first lacerations and reached those proportions of some ulcerations.  At the present we find there is little of this, yet there is the thickening of the walls of the pyloric portion of the stomach itself.  This with the applications that have been taken causes more acidity to be manifested in the upper portion of the jejunum and through the lower portion of the duodenum; thus causing the accumulations or formations of a great deal of gas in the system.  This acidity, passing on through the alimentary canal to a great extent, has caused the walls of the lower portion of the abdomen to be so relaxed at times as to allow the folds even of the abdomen to become irritated - in the caecum area and across the lower portion there.

6. These cause a hernia.  We find that these are the sources of disturbances in the main through this body.

7. About twice or three times a week for the first two weeks, and then once a week, have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections that would align the portions of the upper dorsals so that there is a better position maintained by the natural muscular forces that control the pyloric as well as cardiac portion of the stomach itself.

8. These also would be materially aided if there would be worn about the abdomen or at the area along the diaphragm and just below; this as an elastic or just a cloth adjusted to the body would be beneficial.  During the periods of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections and the correction of the position of stomach, with the adding of the properties we will indicate to reduce the caloric intake or production in body, it will be necessary that there be a relaxing so that the alimentary canal may be the better cleansed.  Use occasionally [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations to purify the colon.  Whether there are the regular evacuations daily or not, do cleanse the colon and we will find that the bacilli through same that acts with the digestion will correct the condition as related to the hernia much better than injections to cause contraction, for these measures are superficial, while those that may be brought about by the use of the corrective measures and the properties taken may aid nature to adjust itself, and the adherence in the wall of the peritoneum will be of such helpful forces as to bring much better conditions for the body.

9. We would take regularly with the meals a small quantity of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin with Milk of Bismuth; about six to ten drops of Lactated Pepsin in half a glass of water with three drops of Milk of Bismuth.  Take this regularly for a week then leave off about three to four days and then take again.

10. Also take once a day, after the heavier meal - whether this be breakfast, lunch or dinner - Willow Charcoal tablets with honey as prepared by Battle Creek Sanatarium.  This character of charcoal would be the most beneficial.

11. In the diet do not take quantities of fats but a great deal of body-building forces, and only honey as sweets.  Fish, fowl and lamb may be taken as meats.  While wild game would be well for the body, but none of the foods should ever be fried.

12. Do these and we will bring better conditions for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What is the condition of my heart?
(A)  As the conditions of the alimentary canal are better we will find there will be a better reaction in the heart activity or in the respiratory system.  This is a sympathetic and not an organic disorder.

15. (Q)  Should I then leave off the injection treatments for the hernia?
(A)  After the body has well begun the other treatments, yes.

16. (Q)  Could an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] be suggested to follow these treatments?
(A)  Any good osteopath will find the subluxations as indicated in the upper dorsal areas.

17. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the osteopath - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Loose-leaf book in Library for indexing

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 4/7/66 Mrs. [1770]'s report:  "After much searching we
> finally obtained Elixir of Lactated Pepsin for my sister
> as recommended in Case [3705] for ulcers.  The next day I
> was amazed to see this article which points up the recent
> discovery of lactose deficiency in many ulcer patients and
> the inability to digest milk, which previously has been one
> of the main foods prescribed in the ulcer diet:"
> 
> TIME, MARCH 25,1966
> 
> METABOLISM
> 
> Milk, Enzymes & Ulcers
> 
> Countless peptic-ulcer patients are put on a bland diet
> rich in milk and cream.  If they then get cramping abdominal
> pains, nausea and diarrhea, even worse than their original
> complaints, their doctors usually put them on a still blander
> diet - meaning more milk.  If such patients shirk their milk
> drinking and their symptoms diminish, the usual explanation
> is a quick, glib suggestion that they must be allergic to
> milk.  Not so, report two University of [[Colorado|Colorado]] doctors in
> the JOURNAL OF THE A.M.A.  The trouble is far more likely to
> be a shortage of the enzyme that the body uses to digest milk
> sugar (lactose).  For such patients, more milk means only
> more trouble.
> 
> To use lactose as fuel, the body must first break it down
> into two simpler sugars:  glucose and galactose.  The enzyme
> that does the cracking is lactase.  Nature intended babies to
> live on milk, and lactase deficiency is fortunately a rarity
> in the newborn, but the incidence increases with advancing
> age.  According to Georgetown University's Dr. Stuart
> H. Danovitch, writing in GP, as much as 10% of the adult
> population may suffer from lack of lactase.  [[Colorado|Colorado]]'s Dr.
> [[John (Apostle)|John]] E. Struthers Jr. says the figure may be as high as 30%,
> though many people have only a moderate shortage that causes
> them no distress.
> 
> The effects of severe lactase deficiency can be both
> distressing and dramatic.  After drinking a glass of milk,
> the victim soon doubles up with cramps that may spread
> across his entire distended abdomen.  He suffers nausea
> and flatulence, and then a profuse, watery diarrhea.  The
> mechanism of these reactions has now become clear, say Dr.
> Struthers, and his [[Colorado|Colorado]] colleague, Dr. Fred Kern Jr.
> Lactase is supposed to do its sugar-splitting work along the
> lining of the small bowel; if the enzyme is missing or in
> short supply, the undigested sugar passes into the large
> bowel, where it is promptly fermented.  In the process, it
> generates gas and draws water into the bowel.
> 
> Patients who have had part of their stomaches removed for
> gastric ulcer, along with victims of ulcerative colitis,
> diabetes, and a variety of abdominal disorders, including
> acute intestinal infections, are all especially liable to
> lactase-deficiency difficulties.  Now that the results of
> research in lactase function are being drawn to doctors'
> attention for use in their daily practice, the A.M.A. JOURNAL
> has been moved to rhapsodize editorially:  "What a joy
> to the clinician to find the arcane skills of research
> scientists directed to such matters as bloating, flatulence,
> cramps and diarrhea!"  The JOURNAL adds:  "Some patients
> will now acquire a new dignity, with the status of enzyme
> deficiency rather than neurosis."
