# TEXT OF READING 2785-1 F 67

**Reading [[2785-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/20/1942  Sex: F  Age: 67  ReadingID: 9953

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2785], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her daughter, Mrs. [...] and by Miss [2778].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2785].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2785].

3. As we find, there are disturbances which prevent the better physical functioning of the body.  These in their primary cause rise from influences or pressures brought to bear from an outside or external force, which have brought about these as results.

4. While it will take a little time, we find that a great deal nearer normal conditions may be brought for this body.

5. There is a wrench, or a twist, in the pelvic bone - at the lumbar axis; causing the right limb even to be shortened - by contraction.

6. This also, with the compound condition that exists in the 8th and 9th dorsal centers, brings about the lack of proper eliminations; tautness to the nerve forces of the body; with sympathetic reflexes to the sensory system, - as portions of the [[Visions|vision]] as well as hearing are gradually being impaired.

7. With the lack of eliminations there is gradually the bringing about of a form of anemia.  Also with the leaving of these poisons in the system, the ankles, knees, hips, elbows, shoulders, all at times cause a great deal of pain - especially when there is a great deal of certain elements in the air.

8. All of these, that make for a reflex condition to the organs, are the basic sources of the disturbance as we find for this body.

9. In making applications as would be the more helpful, or the most beneficial, -

10. First, we would have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

11. Then, at least twice in one week, we would have a thorough relaxing through the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  These should include, first, a gentle relaxing with a very light [[Hydrotherapy|cabinet bath]], but more of the [[Hydrotherapy|fume bath]].  And for this body use an [[Iodine|iodine]] fume bath; that is, put nearly a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|ATOMIDINE]] (which is [[Atomidine|atomic iodine]]) to a pint of water that would be so heated as to allow the fumes or steam from same to settle over the body while it is in the cabinet.

12. Following such a fume bath there should be a thorough rubdown with a combination of three parts Peanut Oil and one part [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] - for this body.  Let this be principally along the cerebrospinal system, across shoulders, across the hips, and especially across the area at the top of the pelvic bone, see?

13. After these two hydrotherapy treatments have been given, not before, we would begin very slowly to make corrections osteopathically.  Let Irvin make these.  Have two or three adjustments, - not hard, not all attempted at once, but have [[Trinity|three in one]] week, see?

14. Then rest from the applications for five days.

15. Then have another series of four or five of the hydrotherapy treatments, - these about once or twice a week.

16. Then have another series of six or eight [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, - these about twice a week, - this time include the corrections in knees, and especially in feet and limbs. Then include those in the area of the 8th and 9th dorsal, and in the lumbar and sacral areas.  Then coordinate the upper dorsal and cervical area TO same.

17. In the matter of the diet, this should be such as to supply a great deal of energies for the body.

18. At times there are the needs for sedatives, for the headaches - that are from this unbalanced condition as well as the excruciating pain, especially if there is any dampness. Hence we would take Aspirin, but do take the [[Baking Soda|Soda]]-Mint tablet with same - the [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] in pellets, see?  Two Aspirin tablets and one [[Baking Soda|Soda]]-Mint tablet - only if these become necessary, when there are the needs for reducing the pain.

19. As the corrections are made, we find that the stiffness in limbs, the shortness even in breath at times, the pain and nervousness through the body will gradually disappear.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  What should be done for insomnia?
(A)  The removing of these pressures will allow the body to rest much easier.

When there is the second series of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments (not before), we would begin with a little electricity every evening - using the hand machine [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], with the bulb applicator.  Use this about three minutes across the shoulders, shoulder blades, down at least to the waist, but not over those corrected areas in the pelvic portion, or the sacral - but down to the waist line and then up to the neck, around the head, around the ears.  This will relax the body.  This should not be taken until there is the beginning of the second period or series of the adjustments, see?

22. (Q)  What can be done to improve memory?
(A)  These corrections should be taken.

23. (Q)  Frequent dizziness?
(A)  All of these conditions arise from the same sources of disturbance.

24. (Q)  What produced the exterior pressure?
(A)  Accident of some nature.

Do these things.

25. (Q)  What causes the skin condition?
(A)  Poor eliminations!  When these conditions are cleared up, and the better vital forces are supplied in the diet as there are better eliminations, this will be much improved. In fact, we should have little or no cause for the skin trouble after the first [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] - and one should be sufficient.

26. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self (two copies; one for Dr. C. W. Irvin, Bankers Trust Bldg., Norfolk, Va., when you are ready for his treatments)
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] for Dr. James R. Parker's nurse, in the Bankers Trust Bldg., who is to give you the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]].  This is to be your very first treatment.

After resting a little from the colonic, - take the sheet of hydrotherapy directions to Miss Jane Witcher, in the Taylor Building, Norfolk, Va.

When Miss Witcher has given you a week's treatments - that is, two full hydrotherapy treatments as indicated - then take the entire copy of your Reading to the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], Dr. C. W. Irvin.  You do not go to Dr. Irvin until you have had one colonic and two full hydrotherapy treatments.

When Dr. Irvin has given you the first week's treatments - [[Trinity|three in one]] week, then leave off everything for five days.

Then have another series of four or five treatments from Miss Witcher.

Then return to Dr. Irvin for another series of six or eight osteopathic adjustments, in the manner indicated.

If everything is not clear, please call us.

When the time comes for the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], we suggest that you obtain a hand machine and use the bulb applicator at home - as it should be used of evening, and to have the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] in the doctor's office would not produce the relaxation needed in the evening.  Some beauty parlors carry this type of [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], or it can be obtained from any good drug or electrical supply house.)
