# TEXT OF READING 1208-19 M 6

**Reading [[1208-19]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/4/1943  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 6736

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of February, 1943, pursuant to the request made by the Aunt, Miss [288], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  [After repeating the suggestion in an undertone]  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are those effects of an acute condition with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the throat, the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and the Eustachian tube - and ears.

4. These, as we find, produce temperature, and irritation, upsetting of the appetite and the like.

5. Necessary to keep the body quiet, and to prevent - especially - the getting of the feet wet.  This does not mean not taking a bath, but do not get out of doors or in the open, see?

6. Then a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the back of the neck, between the shoulders, with Minit-Rub, would be given following a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the same areas with an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]] - this between the shoulders and back of neck, especially.  Put the [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] on first, you see, then the Minit-Rub; else it would be too hot.

7. Around the neck or throat or around the ears [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with a rag or flannel dipped in kerosene, all the body will absorb - of evening.  Well to tie such a rag (but this not flannel, rather gauze with kerosene on same), and then a cotton cloth over same, about the throat and the ears.

8. This, with keeping good eliminations, keeping the body quiet and the massage (which may be given at least twice a day), should better the condition.

9. (Q)  Any particular diet?
(A)  That which is body-building and is agreeable, but not too much sweets - and of course NO acids.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Aunt [288]
"    " Ass'n file
