# TEXT OF READING 360-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[360-2]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/12/1933  Sex: F  ReadingID: 2793

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1933, in accordance with request made by Edgar Cayce himself, for Associate Member Mrs. [360].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mass. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [360].

2. As we find, too severe have been the applications of the Fuller's Earth with oil; too large a quantity in too small an area.

3. We would, then, discontinue the use of this in the present, and use [[Camphor|camphorated oil]] to release the tendencies that have been created in the cervical area.

4. When this condition has been reduced sufficiently that the treatments and adjustments may be begun, we would begin with these now - that is, when it is reduced, you see, the inflammation.

5. When the adjustments have then been taken for a period of three to five weeks, we would give the necessary changes for the correction of those disorders for the body.

6. The applications were given to produce a superficial circulation or stimulation to those areas from which the eye and the other portions of the sensory system obtain their nerve and blood supply impulse; not only from the cervical but from the dorsal area also - see?

7. Hence the necessity of these for effective treatments by the absorption method, produced through manipulation or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

8. We are through for the present.
