# TEXT OF READING 1058-4 F 24

**Reading [[1058-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/16/1937  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 6197

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of February, 1937, in accordance with request made by [1058], through her friend, Miss [954], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Manhattan General 
Time of Reading                             Hospital Ward, 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 520 Second Ave. 
& 18th, 
[[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, while conditions are serious, there is some slight response to the applications that are at present being made.

2. As we find while this may necessitate eventual operative forces, or further operative forces, if following same there would be used low electrical forces as from static electricity or galvanic, these would materially aid in creating the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body.

3. Little may be changed under the existent conditions, but insist - when these conditions have arisen - that this at least be given the opportunity for its expression in the body-physical.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is the operation advisable?
(A)  As has been indicated, this apparently must be part of the condition as is arising; though if the body responds as it has at times, no.

6. (Q)  Is present treatment right?
(A)  As has been indicated.

7. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Keep the attitudes in the right.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [954] - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

Wire 2/16/37:  OPERATION ADVISABLE UNLESS RESPONSE.  KEEP RIGHT ATTITUDES.  FULL INFORMATION MAILED.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 2/18/37 Miss [954]'s letter to EC:
> 
> My dear Teacher:
> 
> Thank you so much for your inquiry about [1058].  Both of us
> appreciate it so much...just as we got such comfort from the
> telegram and the reading.  The surgeon expected to operate
> yesterday...and I hope to see her at 2 o'clock today and
> shall append a note at that time to this letter.
> 
> I shall append a note about the reading to this letter so
> that [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] can file it as a report and will not have to
> retype.  Of course, you can imagine what we have been going
> through.  I know it is faulty of me to suffer as much as I do
> when she is ill like this...but I seem to lose a large part
> of the control I have built up.  My projection even goes so
> far as physical...pains in the ear and tight [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]...if you
> please!  Quite ludicrous, of course.  I have had to take a
> hard riding from Dr. Hardwicke about it...but truth to tell
> he is not perfect himself in the matter and has confessed it.
> You see the child looks so bad...the swelling at the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] is
> enormous...so that her head inclines toward it...and the
> sympathetic [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] structure swells, too...so that the whole
> neck is enlarged.
> 
> Taken from the viewpoint of [[Soul|soul]] chastisement the entire
> condition is most interesting to me.  If, as we tend to
> believe, the body is subject to the individual will...she did
> some unique picking.  I was contrasting it last night with my
> own [[Free Will|choice]] (if we can go so far as to admit that this is
> [[Free Will|choice]]...and I tend that way)...  And I see that in my own
> [[Free Will|choice]] the injury was deeper but was so secret...so hidden
> ...that even today after nearly forty years of life and
> severe operative procedure my personal, bodily
> characteristics are much the same as they were at sixteen.
> Is not this all interesting?  On the other hand the child
> chooses an outward sign...producing scars on her face and
> neck where everyone will notice them.
> 
> I do not want you to think that her previous reading did not
> help her.  It improved her enormously for a time.  But like
> all of us she fell by the wayside in the daily care that was
> needed.  She becomes a victim of circumstances apparently...
> so often, too...even having dropped her family behind, she
> got into a roommate situation in which she lost much sleep.
> But all of us are determined to do better in the future.  If
> she will stick with us, Dr. Hardwicke will jack her up...and
> we hope to retain the strong personality of the surgeon in
> the [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]].  We were so much pleased that he endeavored to
> clear up the infection without operative procedure in the
> beginning.
> 
> Believe me, [1058] is growing and striving spiritually.  It
> is most interesting to note how she responds.  Her family,
> for instance, descends in force at such a time although their
> only part in the [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] otherwise is to collect the weekly
> allowance she gives them.  Both father and mother descend on
> the hospital...weep actual tears and moan over the
> misfortune.  [1058] this time is trying to arrange (and so
> kindly it quite touched me) to have them come less often.
> She sees what they do to her...that they pull her down.
> 
> Then there is a rather nice aunt who comes...nice up to a
> certain point.  She is the more prosperous one of the family
> and has been very kind at times giving [1058] care after she
> got out of the hospital.  But her technique is to
> criticize...whether the kid has a new hat or has chosen a
> new hospital.  Her thought is that [1058] got it all free
> last time at Medical Center...why didn't she return there
> and get it all free again!  One of the things she got free
> last time was an extra gash in the cheek which had never
> been punctured before!
> 
> So there's a bit for the funny papers...for you.
> 
> What a mixture of kindness, of "good intentions" and of
> mistakes we human beings are!
> 
> The first opening for which she did not have to go under an
> anesthetic was most successful...and there was to be a
> further one yesterday.  I look forward to finding her much
> improved today.  I feel a change in my own spirits and rather
> take that as a sign.
> 
> Love to all of you...and many thanks, especially for the
> effort made in getting the emergency check to us...and the
> telegram.
> 
> [Following added in handwriting]
> 
> Dear EC - The operation seems to have been successful
> although my personal impression of the moment is that another
> may be necessary.
> 
> I shall be hoping and [[Prayer|praying]] this is not so...and I know all
> of you will, too...
> 
> Love [954]
>

>

> remove these at a later date.  See in the reading we have

> at least be given the opportunity for its expression in the

> 
> The further advice "Keep the attitudes in the right" was much

> 
> We should much appreciate an answer to the electrical
> question...  Is the radio-active appliance which we have and
