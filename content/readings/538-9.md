# TEXT OF READING 538-9 F 44 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[538-9]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/7/1925  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 3745

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [538].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born February 22, 1880, at [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky. 
Time of Reading 
12:40 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the Universe, and the Universal Forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities latent and exhibited in the present life.  Also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and to that [that to] which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those relations with the Universe, and those conditions as relate to [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the present earth plane and those in the earth plane in other [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], with the urges and conditions as created and merited in those relations, with abilities and the how.

3. Many of these conditions we have had before.  These, as we have at present, show the relation as approached from a different viewpoint, or angle.

4. In this then, we find the relations in the present sphere taken from those of Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and of Uranus, and the entity then has those urges as are made with the relations of these conditions in earth's plane, and in the urges as received by accentuation of conditions as received from other sphere than earth's sphere or plane.

5. Then, we have one given to the conditions that are exceptional in many ways.  One not understood by many.  One that brings much joy to many through the influence of the forces in Venus and in Jupiter.  One who has mental forces far beyond that as has been developed in the present plane.

6. One that brings much of the forces of antagonism to many through some conditions as received through urges, as will be found, from appearances in earth's plane.

7. One who is decided in likes and dislikes, whether of individuals or of places, things or conditions.  One that under such conditions often brings more worry to self than to others.  One tending to be in a manner recluse in self, often keeping many things that would prove beneficial in manner, were they expressed to others.  This pertains to mental abilities, the urges, the worries, the many conditions.  One capable in many lines, either in that of artistic forces as applied to inanimate conditions, or of those pertaining to the improvement in other forces as applied from others' work.  [She spent yrs. doing retouching in her husband's photographic studio.]  One, then, a good critic in many ways.  One that is very bombastic, however, in dislike in criticism.  One that finds in the joy of the elemental forces of earth's plane the greatest enjoyment in the earth's plane.  One showing much patience under conditions where the relation is to self and to others.

8. As to the appearances, these we have much to deal with in the present plane, and these conditions under which the development was made in other planes has its effect in the present.  Hence we find there are many conditions relating to those through which the entity passes in this present time.

9. Then, in the one before this, we find in the land now [[France|France]], and during the period when the English ruler [Charles II] was in [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]] in that land on account of conditions that existed in that field.  The entity then in that of Lurline [Maezie? in 538-5] Annose [?] in the courtesan to the exiled ruler, and assisted in his return to the land, and in this found favor in the eyes of many.  In the present sphere we find this is shown in the ability of the entity to appear and to gain in that being in a manner extra in attention to strangers, and that such brings much to the entity.  As to the personality gained and exhibited in the entity, the love of the finer things in earth's plane of the beautiful, and of playing the courtesan to many.

10. In the one before this we find in the period of the rule as was shown in [[Greece|Greece]], when [[Law of Moses|the law]] or understanding was given by the Socrates, and the entity then in that household and of that family, and suffered much by the persecution as was brought on by the position that the entity occupied in that household, yet the greater urge for knowledge and understanding comes in the present plane from that [[Incarnation|incarnation]] in earth's sphere.  The name then was Normaline, and the entity was the second daughter to that philosopher of the day.  In the urge and the personality, the seeking to-day of those philosophic questions as propounded, and such easily understood, for each must be passed on that same principle as was then set - right for all and no exception in the rule.

11. In the one before this [See 538-31, Par. 13-A], we find in the land of the Normads [nomads], or in the outer edge of now Arabian deserts, [[[Persia|Persia]]-Arabia:Uhjltd period] and the entity then the follower in that of the Bedouins, and given as the most beautiful of that group to which the entity then belonged.  The name then in that of Inxa, and the entity then ruled many through the power of the eye over the condition created in the minds of those whom this would subjugate to her way of reason.  [See 538-64 on 5/7/40 in re iritis, and blindness developing in right eye - physical [[Karma|karma]]?]  In the personality as exhibited to-day, we find in the patience and persistence in the condition, thought or felt to be aright, still able to control many through the eye and expression of same.  [See 7/16/32, 7/19/32, Ck. Life Rdgs. on above [[Incarnation|incarnation]], 538-31, Par. 13-A and 538-32.]

12. In the one before this we find in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] forces, [[[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] Period] and the entity sought, after [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] entrance a sudden return again, or sought quick entrance again into earth's plane.  [Arabian period of Uhjltd just mentioned, thus indicating a date of perhaps 8 or 9 thousand B.C.?] In [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] forces the entity was of the household of the assistance [assistant priest] to the one becoming the leader in the religious cult, [294] and being of beautiful figure and form, and giving the condition to him who was the leader brought through her seductive forces much troubles to self and that entity.  This bringing banishment to the group brought distress and physical suffering to each, in that the entity set about to bring the enmity of many, and in the present we find grudges easily held and that the entity must overcome to reach the development that will bring the best, whether of mental, physical or spiritual forces.  As to the name at that period, we find in Isris [later known as Isis - see 294-147 thru 294-153.  Also see 341-9, Par. 14-A on 6/2/25 giving further details; also 341-10, Par. 20-A on.] and there are found even to this day many of the plastic forms in that country of the entity, though we find this was 10,996 earth years before the Prince of Peace came.

13. The abilities of the entity lie in those spheres of easy to acquire the musical ability, the ability to symmetrize body and music in dance, to give much to peoples through that manner, yet in that as has been set in the present plane these yet may be acquired in no small degree.  Time will soon approach when this may be effected, and the entity will through [[Free Will|the will]] of self have the building of will's forces in self to acquire development in this earth's plane.

14. Then, to use all these, let the entity gain that understanding that self must be made as nought, would we [if we would] manifest the spiritual elements in the physical plane, and that it is hard to kick against the pricks [Acts 9:5; 26:14] that would bring only the condemnation of self, that each thought, action, deed, must be met, and that paid for.  With the mind, [[Soul|soul]] and body must earth's inhabitant magnify Him, the Giver of all good and perfect Gifts, and in making [[Free Will|the will]] one with His, even as Him who became the Light, the Truth, the Way, may we approach the Heavenly [[Throne of God|throne]].

15. We are through for the present.
