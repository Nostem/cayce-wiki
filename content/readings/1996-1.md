# TEXT OF READING 1996-1 M 19 (Clerk in a Trust Co.,

**Reading [[1996-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/11/1939  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 8503

Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of September, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1942].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born May 10, 1920, in [[Canada|Montreal]], Quebec,  [[Canada|Canada]]. 
Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[Canada|Canada]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces, giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [1996].

3. Now, as we find, those conditions which exist in the attitudes of the body - [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] - are not in conformity, except here and there, with the attitudes of the [[Mental Body|mental body]].

4. And thus there are physical disturbances of the physical nature as to the reactions to the emotional body, that should be attended to, in the present; lest we will find there will be disturbances of a nature that would cause a derangement in the ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]] of this entity.

5. Physically, there are hindrances in the coccyx and in the lumbar axis; which cause a reaction to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]], - in the sensory or sense emotions a disturbance.

6. These should be corrected mechanically, - that is, osteopathically.

7. Also there should be suggestions to the body in the unconscious state, - or through hypnosis; making suggestions for POSITIVE reactions - with directions for greater creative influences, and the adherence to [[Creative Forces|creative forces]].

8. And thus we will find we will change the whole outlook of the body.

9. As to [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, - we find one that is versatile in many things, as would pertain to ways and manners of managements of affairs.  And with those corrections indicated, as to bring a normal physical reaction, much might be expected of the entity.

10. As we find, then, - until such corrections are made, we would not attempt to interpret the life records; for with the corrections made physically, as indicated, there may be a better ability of EVALUATING the emotions that are associated with conditions, places, environs, and even to the mental and spiritual life.

11. And in the suggestions, there should be included not those that would attempt to set a rule without [[Purpose|purpose]], but those suggestions should be creative in their very nature and [[Purpose|purpose]].

12. When these are done, and within then the next six to nine months, we may give the better evaluations for those suggestive forces as to the relative relationships to its former activities, as well as the [[Astrology|astrological]] urges that are unleashed in the experiences of the entity under the present environ.

13. These corrective measures physically, as indicated, should be the mechanical or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; coccyx, lumbar and sacral axis.

14. AND then hypnosis, with the directions for greater creative influences and the adherence to [[Creative Forces|creative forces]].

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(See extracts [which were enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] and Psychiatrist.)
