# TEXT OF READING 3905-1 F 21

**Reading [[3905-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/20/1944  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 11538

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3905].

3. Now as we find there are disturbing conditions that prevent the normal reflexes in the physical, the mental and the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]].

4. In considering that which may be helpful for this body, we find that the [[Physical Body|physical body]], the [[Mental Body|mental body]] and the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] must be taken into consideration; and not applications made that would permanently make a dissociation of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s [[Purpose|purposes]] in the earth.

5. For here we have a body and a mind that may be made, or may come to be a contributing factor to the betterment not only of a home, a community, a state, but a contributing factor to the material, the spiritual and mental welfare of many.

6. Thus we find a body well endowed with faculties and yet in the present on the verge of a disintegration of the unity of [[Purpose|purpose]].

7. There are pathological conditions and psychological conditions.

8. We find that it will be well to give [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - now MIND, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the ENTITY]], the shocks with overdoses, even of Insulin; to cause reflexes in the brain reaction to the emotions of the body.

9. But when these have been given it will be well to coordinate these by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] correction of subluxations existing in the body-forces.

10. At the time of birth there was a pressure brought about in the areas of the coccyx and the lower end of the spine that makes, even yet, a dissociation in that area of the coccyx and the end of the cerebrospinal nerve segments.

11. Correct these.

12. Give the shocks first, yes; else the body-mind will not be controlled in relation to these.  Then make the coordination in the coccyx with the lumbar axis, the 9th dorsal, the 3rd cervical and the 1st cervical.

13. Thus we will bring near to normal reactions for this body.

14. Don't give so many of the shocks as to upset the pancrean reaction entirely.

15. As to the number of these that should be given before beginning the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections, this should be determined by the response.  For remember, as indicated, the physical, the mental and the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] should be considered in making the applications.  Consider it as an entity, not merely as a [[Physical Body|physical body]].

16. For the body has a mind, a [[Soul|soul]] and a [[Physical Body|physical body]]; they war one with another oft because of this dissociation, or this open end of the spine which came at birth.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Has the environment been a factor in bringing about this condition?
(A)  Every phase might be a factor but this is the pathological condition.  The others are the result of the attempt of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of physical and mental reactions in body-forces.

19. (Q)  How can the environment be made more desirable for her better development?
(A)  This will change entirely.  It will not be the same in reaction when these changes are brought about.

Do that.

20. (Q)  Please suggest foods to be stressed, and foods to be avoided in the diet.
(A)  There should be a well balanced diet for this body when these changes have been wrought.  Even an x-ray will show this dissociation indicated.

21. (Q)  Any mental and spiritual advice?
(A)  We would prefer a life experience (as in a Life Reading) with the mental and spiritual advice when corrections have been made and the body herself seeks same.

22. (Q)  Who should make the corrections?
(A)  Such as Coulter.

23. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 E. 41st Street, [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 11/5/43 Mother's letter:  "[3905] is emotionally very
> unstable.  Sometimes when in a highly nervous state she
> trembles all over and momentarily loses the power of
> locomotion.  At times she has temper tantrums or violent
> rages during which she is apt to become destructive and break
> objects, strike and kick and tear up her clothes and to give
> way to loud and prolonged screaming.  She has at various
> times threatened self destruction.  At times her conduct
> seems wholly irrational or abnormal but psychiatrists who
> have examined her say she is sane."
> 
> B2. 2/11/44  Mother's letter to EC:  "I have an appointment for a
> reading for my daughter, [3905] for June, 13 but I
> desperately need your advice before that time.
> 
> "She has been in the [[New York City|New York]] Psychiatric Hospital at 168 St.
> and the Hudson River for observation since Jan. 1st.  Today I
> was informed that they have concluded she is not psychopathic
> but is in need of some sort of psychotherapy.
> 
> "They say she cannot remain at the hospital as it will take
> from five to seven years to effect a cure.
> 
> "The problem is this.  If she leaves the hospital I fear she
> will return to her husband even against the advice of the
> doctors.  He was discharged from the army because of a mental
> condition which is really serious.  If they continued to live
> together it might end in tragedy.
> 
> "Anxiously awaiting your help, I am"  [...]
