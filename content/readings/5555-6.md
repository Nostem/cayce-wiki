# TEXT OF READING 5555-6 F 51

**Reading [[5555-6]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/3/1930  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 13840

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - Now, we find a return of those conditions, or accumulations of poisons.  These cause distresses through pressure as is created in system.  We would find, it would be better were the body to change to where the applications in manipulations, in the eliminations, may be increased or set about in such a way as to eliminate, before there is a settling OF the conditions as to cause the metabolism to become so unbalanced.  These would be the most beneficial for the body, or the return to the Beach.

2. That is all the questions regarding this body.

3. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Son
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
