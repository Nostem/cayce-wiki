# TEXT OF READING 1632-1 F 37

**Reading [[1632-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/8/1938  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 7713

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1632], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her brother, Mr. [1568].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1632].

2. Now as we find, disturbing conditions or influences make reactions that at times cause anxiety to the body.

3. But that as we find needing correction would be rather in the direction of warnings, or measures to prevent disturbances.

4. And with the correcting of those influences as we find that produce the inclinations, we would find much of that which has been the cause of the anxiousness would be relieved and removed from the body as disturbing forces.

5. These than are the conditions as we find them with this body in the present, [1632]:

6. In the blood supply we find indications of there being at times very poor eliminations.

7. And with the subluxation that exists as a part of the disturbance, in the lumbar, - from conditions that have existed from the birth of the second child - these produce then a nerve reaction and cause activities in which the eliminations cause an excess amount of drosses or poisons or eliminant forces that are as accumulations.

8. Then these through portions of the body cause the pressures between the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal nervous system.

9. Thus we find anxieties through the abdominal area at times. At other times we find the effects are wholly as pressures upon the nervous forces of the body.  Then again we find there are at times anxieties as to the effect or conditions produced in the superficial circulation.  At other times the reaction is upon the organs of the sensory forces.

10. All of these, as we find, may be eliminated through the activities as would bring first the corrections through the lumbar-sacral areas, as well as coordinating these with the dorsal and throughout the cervical areas; but more specific corrections made in the lumbar and lower dorsal areas.

11. These we would make osteopathically.  In the beginning, these would be given about twice or three times each week; GENTLY but positively, see?

12. Also with these we would use the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the Radio-Active Appliance, to make for better resting for the body.  This would preferably be used not in the evening but in the afternoon, as the body rests; for the body should rest during a portion of the afternoon.  Use each day for at least forty minutes.  Circulate the attachments about the body in this manner:

13. The anode marked to be attached first would ALWAYS be attached first to the body; the first day to the right wrist, the other anode to the left ankle.

14. Next day, the first attachment would be to the left wrist, while the other would be to the right ankle.

15. Third day, attach first to the left ankle, last to the right wrist.

16. Fourth day, make the first attachment to the right ankle, the last attachment to the left wrist.

17. Thus a circle has been made of the body.  Then commence over again, alternating the attachments each day in this manner. Continue for two or three rounds, then leave off about a week, and then use again.

18. Use these periods each afternoon as the periods for [[Meditation|meditation]] upon CONSTRUCTIVE and creative influences; not only for the physical but the mental and spiritual activities of the body.

19. Do these consistently.

20. In the matter of the diet, keep to those things that are more of the alkalin nature; that is, keep closer to those things that are alkalin-producing, or about twenty percent of the acid-producing foods to eighty-percent of the alkalin-producing.

21. Do not use white breads or potatoes, spaghetti or macaroni at the same meals; that is, no two of these at one meal.

22. Do not combine fried foods ever, or very seldom, with the diets.

23. Meats, if these are taken, should consist principally of fish, fowl and lamb, rather than other meats.

24. If these applications are kept, and especially the mental attitudes towards [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] and spiritual activity, we will find better conditions coming to this body.

25. Ready for questions.

26. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> congestion in the chords of the neck.  Also, pain in the
> sacro-iliac joint.

> I have been to many doctors, & have had all sorts of tests

> a definite cause, or cure, for the headaches.

> years.  Last month my eyes were tested, & found that the
> ocular [[Photography (Atlantean)|images]] of an unequaled size (Aniseikonia) and am now

>
