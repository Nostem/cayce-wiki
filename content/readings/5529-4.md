# TEXT OF READING 5529-4 F 66

**Reading [[5529-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/26/1929  Sex: F  Age: 66  ReadingID: 13789

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35 Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5529].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
7:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find somewhat improved conditions from those as we have had.  The body in the physical manner is bettered.  The body in the mental manner is bettered.  The conditions, the relationships of the life mentally to the surroundings and experiences of the body have been made more in accord with that for the nominal development of the body.  Hence much of the distresses and the worry, as has been that of the body's surroundings, has shown improvement.  As for the physical, there needs be only those following up of those conditions as have been given as would assist the body in its decline, or in its age - as IT terms so.  There are in the mental some of the conditions that would be well not to assume without due consideration, for there is the need of the body's - physically and mentally - taking consideration of the cost to self in mind and in health.  Remain much in the same surroundings, then - though the [[Mental Body|mental body]] and the physical following same may go far afield in assistance to others and to self.  We are through.

Copy submitted to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
