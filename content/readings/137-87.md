# TEXT OF READING 137-87 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-87]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/9/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 477

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 9th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
6:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., New Jersey.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we  have the body, the enquiring mind, [137]. This we have had here before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Night of July 15 or morning of 16th.  "Bought a pair of riding breeches at auction.  They seemed in very good condition until I examined them closely, when I found they were worn and too large for me."
(A)  In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those conditions as concerning those endeavors, or efforts, which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has been contemplating, and which have been taken up in some part, the riding for the health, and exercise for the body, and, as is seen and indicated through this condition for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], that while this would be very good, and would prove beneficial, yet this may be over done and wear the body more than the good it would do, see?  Hence, then, in moderation should such things be approached.

4. We are through with this reading.
