# TEXT OF READING 4703-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4703-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1910 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/31/1910  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12658

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 31st day of October, 1910.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                           ..., Ky.

1. EC:  Mrs. [4703], ..., Ky.  Yes, we have her here.  Suffers with the headache.  Now begin here from the whole system here, [4703] - the trouble is here - the reflex that goes to the head here has begun along at the sixth cervical, second cervical in the facial muscles, around from here on through side to [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] and the head, through the eyes and temple from the reflex below at the eleventh dorsal down below and solar plexus nerve.

2. System comes in contact with the nerves from the spleen with a lesion from the strain with the spine.  The spine comes in contact with the solar [plexus] system, and spine and pancreas with the nerves and tissue here to the liver and the stomach throws back into itself the pancreas juice, which is needed to aid in the digestion of the food that is taken into the stomach, not enough to produce this, here.

3. It is from the tie-up here in the nerve center and dorsal. We have reflex from the sympathetic lower lumbar, second and third, that produce this, of course, and state at times.  As we have a reflex from the solar plexus comes in contact above the liver and cardiac plexus produce the flushes of blood and we have pains in the shoulder and between the shoulders, we have that tired, stretchy feeling, especially in the morning, and pains through the head, sick at the stomach because this comes back into the stomach, followed by a better feeling.

4. Remove the condition at the eleventh dorsal, give something to produce the pancreas juice.  Peptone will be alright to remove a portion of the trouble.  First remove cause, you see.

5. Use a [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], the [[Vibration|vibration]] movement, manipulation of the hands, or static electricity.  Manipulation of the hands, [[Vibration|vibration]], and static electricity.  Cleanse the stomach, not enough to produce to the intestinal track a sloughy feeling and secretions themselves.  Cleanse the system and cleanse the stomach, put plenty of water in it, plenty of fresh water, nothing in it but pure water.  Eat, of course, we do not want to get starved, but everything taken in must be well masticated first in the mouth.

6. First take peptone or pepsin in the stomach as this sweetens the stomach.  After a reaction from this becomes hungry again, produces in the stomach a feeling of hunger, want to eat.  Then take into the stomach an acid, hydrochloric acid which acts on the pancreas, itself, from here, see?

7. This manipulation won't do it in a minute.  Remove this trouble, take the peptone to first sweeten the stomach, get all these juices in the stomach her.  When we have this feeling want to eat use acid here use it vice versa until we get these conditions removed from this pancreas.  Nothing has formed, it has not formed anything organic yet.  Remove this trouble along the spine, the trouble from the stomach.  Do not have to cut into it to get it out, nothing organic has formed in itself.  It could form but it is not there now.
