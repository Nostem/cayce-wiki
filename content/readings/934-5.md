# TEXT OF READING 934-5 F 28

**Reading [[934-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/6/1938  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 5782

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of September, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [934], through husband [391], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [391], Edmond Jones and Lucy Clifton.]

Time of Reading 
6:00 to 6:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we have the body here, [934].

2. As we find, these are rather acute conditions arising from poisons in the system from the effect of that which is VERY severe upon the system.

3. We would keep that being applied in the present.

4. But begin with very small doses - and often - of Castoria, to ELIMINATE, see?  This means not more than ten to twenty drops every fifteen to twenty minutes, until the body is thoroughly under the influence - whether it takes two, three or four bottles!  And let THIS act as a part of the sedative.

5. It will be necessary to keep the sedative; necessary to keep the applications.

6. But we will find that with even the cold application, a WARM [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the abdomen with the equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] will be most helpful!

7. And if there had NOT been the sedative and the cold, we find that salts, [[Turpentine|turpentine]] and [[Camphor|camphor]] taken internally WOULD have been the most beneficial.  However, do not take it now! unless there becomes rather an acute condition again.

8. DO NOT take solid foods.  Use liquids.  And those things that are as a stimulant of the nature of alcohol will be VERY detrimental to the body.  Hence only keep such things as broths or [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] - and all of these sipped; not gulped but taken very lightly.  Keep the body quiet and rest as much as possible.

9. Use enemas as WELL as the eliminants.

10. Ready for questions.

11. Do that, as indicated - and if there will be the precautions we should not have greater disturbance.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
