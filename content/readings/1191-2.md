# TEXT OF READING 1191-2 M 58

**Reading [[1191-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/4/1936  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 6650

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 4th day of November, 1936, in accordance with request made by the daughter, [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Reiss and Mrs. Engel.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:55 to 4:00 P. M. 
Taken 5:00 to 5:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1191] this we have had before.

2. As we find conditions are not so well in many respects.

3. There should have been the closer adherence to the manner, the character of administering the applications through the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]].  These as we find have only in part been done.  This has allowed or did allow for the return of those hindrances in the locomotion's activity and in the circulatory system.

4. We would then have applied those activities as indicated, with a rub that would stimulate the circulation through the the torso and trunk portion of the body; especially along the lower lumbar and through the area above the kidneys. This with the combination of oils that would be absorbed by the body.  As combined in this manner, these would have been the better as we find:

5. To 2 ounces of the Oil of Pine Needles as the carrier, added:

Oil of Wintergreen...............1/2 ounce,
Oil of Cedar Wood................1/2 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root............1/4 ounce,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.......1 ounce.

Shake these together, for they separate.  Morning and evening would have been the better for the application. They would rest the body.

6. The quieting of the digestive forces would have been the better with that combination of equal portions, or an ounce each, of Cinnamon Water and Lime Water, to which should have been added 10% solution Bromide of Potassium twenty minims, 10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium ten minims.  A teaspoonful of this two hours apart would keep and make for better digestion and assimilation.  These we would do, or would have done.

7. Keep the body quiet with the necessary forces to prevent the greater suffering.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Should the electrical appliance be used in the present?
(A)  Be used AS indicated.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [...], Daughter
"    " Ass'n file
