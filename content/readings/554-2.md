# TEXT OF READING 554-2 M 44 (Furniture Buyer, Hebrew)

**Reading [[554-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/6/1928  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 3897

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [554].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Pantlind Hotel, 
Time of Reading                           Room ..., 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        Grand Rapids, Mich.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind and the business associations of [554], who is at the Pantlind Hotel, Room ..., Grand Rapids, Michigan.  Should he accept proposition in Brooklyn or [[New York City|New York]]?  Brooklyn gives no contract to merchandise.  A group of buyers N.Y. will give contract to buy for him.  Which is best and most acceptable for him?  Give any advice and guidance that will help this body regarding this.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [554].

3. This we have had here before - those of business associations and propositions of those that have been offered, whether Brooklyn or [[New York City|New York]].  We find the [[New York City|New York]] proposition the better; yet - as has been given - there are other propositions that are in formation that would present even greater opportunities than this.  We would give that the best would be not to accept EITHER of these just at the present time.  We are through with this reading.
