## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 10/29/19 Mother's ltr. to EC:
> 
> ..., Ky.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> I have waited a long time to express my thanks, in a very
> feeble way, to you for the great service rendered.  Words are
> so inadequate in a vital case like this.  The wonderful
> message you sent seems perfectly marvelous to me, and I sent
> it on to Dr. House, after making a careful copy of it, for
> fear the original might get lost.  I haven't heard from Dr.
> House but Mrs. House replied immediately, saying he would
> write in a few days.  Of course I am all eagerness to have
> him take up the case, but they are all stirred up at the
> hospital over the coming election and the changes that may
> accrue from political change.  Also the Superintendent is
> quite ill in the hospital in Paducah and Dr. House may be
> very busy.  I CAN'T believe he will be indifferent, and that
> [[Revelation (Book)|revelation]] of yours ought to be worth - be of inestimable
> worth to the medical world.  I believe every word of it.  I'm
> just as sure that God has spoken through you as I KNOW that
> He revealed Himself to [[Prophets|the prophets]] of old.  It only remains
> for US to see that no blunder is made in handling the case.
> The boy is going so fast I shudder for fear delay may mean
> loss irrevocable.
> 
> Mr. Cayce, I thank you from the bottom of my heart for what
> you have done for us, and I will be sure to let you know the
> outcome of this case.  May God bless you and yours, and may
> you live long and be a blessing always to suffering humanity.
> 
> Your true and humble friend,
> 
> [signed] Mrs. [...], ..., Ky.
