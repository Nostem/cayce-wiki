# TEXT OF READING 5592-2 F 66

**Reading [[5592-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/13/1932  Sex: F  Age: 66  ReadingID: 13916

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 13th day of June, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5592], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions about same, Mrs. [5592] - this we have had before.

2. In the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]], these we find much improved from that as we last had same.  There are specific disorders that have not as yet wholly responded to the applications for improvement or correction.  These, however, show improvement, as indicated in the manner of functionings of the system. Those conditions that arise in portions of the stomach, and especially in the cardiac end of same, as given, are from the thickening of the walls and the pressure produced there, as well as that which comes from the upper portion of the transverse colon.  It is not in the form of a growth; rather the thickening of the walls of tissue and the muco-membranes, both in the stomach proper and those portions lying between the stomach and the pressure as made from dilation in the colon, which make for pressures and pains that come about the region of the heart, causing the tendency or feeling of shortening of breath, as well as a depression in the heart and cardiac area.

3. We will find that the manipulation deeply given, and a continual use of the violet ray about these portions, will make for continued absorption of the thickened conditions, as the general conditions of body show improvements.

4. We would also add at this time more olive oil, that the muco-membrane in the colon and intestinal area may have more activity or food value for the digestive system itself, as well as aiding the colon in the eliminations and assisting peristaltic movements, which will overcome the tendency for constipation in the system.  This we would take, all the body will assimilate.  Better that same be taken in small doses and as often as assimilation permits.  Take half to a teaspoonful every two to four hours.  Should there become the tendency for regurgitation, then reduce the quantity of the dosage but continue with same.

5. For the manipulations and other suggestions, we would keep these up - as well as that of the violet ray.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file
