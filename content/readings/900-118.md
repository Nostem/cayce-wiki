# TEXT OF READING 900-118 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-118]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/28/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5321

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-116] on [[Dreams|Dream]] of [900], given on August 27, 1925, 2:15 P.M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.  You will please answer the questions regarding same, that we may understand same in a more perfect manner.

2. EC:  Yes, we have same here.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  To what stock (give name please) does the 108 refer to and is he to buy or sell?
(A)  This, as we see, is of the body's own intent and [[Purpose|purpose]].  Is that stock then that the body has been making study of and through which there may be those advantages received at this time, see?  for it will soon pass the mark, see?  Act upon that already given.

4. (Q)  To what stock does 58 & 57 & 7/8 refer to, and is he to buy or sell?
(A)  This, as we see, refers to some stock in which both, with many others, are interested, and the closing or disposing of such is to be consummated during this week - 57 & 7/8 or 58, see?

5. We are through.
