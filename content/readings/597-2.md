# TEXT OF READING 597-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[597-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/2/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4138

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 2nd day of July, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mr. [333], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, Mr. [333] and Mrs. [602].]

Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [597]; this we have had before.  Not a great deal of change in the immediate.

2. As we find, if there would be brought the better condition of this body, there would be the immediate application of those things and elements in the body that will release in the system the element to combat those influences in the blood stream that have allowed the activities to become apparent which show destructive forces in the body.

3. And these, as we find, are more active in that which has been given, and with the activities make for the ability for the dissemination.

4. We would do this.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Why did the reading -
(A)  (Interrupting)  Would you question the reading?  Then WE are through!

Copy to Mr. [333]
"    " Ass'n file

> them from a very high source, though they didn't know it..."

> R2. 7/22/34 Mr. [333]'s letter:  "...I have not heard anything
> from Mr. [[...] Mrs. [597]'s husband].  He, of course, is in
> [[Germany|Germany]] for an indefinite stay and those of his family who
> are here have not received any news from him and they do not
> know the address..."
> 
> R3. 8/25/34 Mr. [333]'s letter:  "...I heard indirectly of the
> passing on of Mrs. [597] and I understand that the plan was
> to leave the body in [[Germany|Germany]].  Mr. [[...] [597]'s husband]
> will arrive in N.Y. on September 7th, so I expect that I
> will see him soon.  I have not seen nor heard from him since
> he went away, just before our coming down to the Beach..."
> 
> R4. 8/31/34 EC's letter to Mr. [333]:  "...We were sorry to hear
> of Mrs. [597]'s death.  This was not unexpected, of course,
> but we are never prepared - nor are we always understanding
> in such things, though in such cases I'm sure most of us
> should feel that she is rid of her suffering and will soon be
> in that stage of development where [[Soul|the soul]] will be advancing
> more and more..."
