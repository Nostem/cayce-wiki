# TEXT OF READING 1188-5 M 7

**Reading [[1188-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/24/1937  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 6637

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 24th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1158].]

... School 
Time of Reading                                  on ... Rd., 
12:55 to 1:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion was given for the entire five members 
of the family at beginning of reading for [1179].)

1. EC:  Yes, here he is coming in now - we have the body, [1188].

2. Here again, as we find, there has not been the beginning of the applications that have been indicated for the body. These as we find we would follow along those lines that have been indicated.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should [1188] take [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]]?
(A)  [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] is very good, not too much; and not with the Iron, if those others as indicated are to be taken. Take it plain, or WITHOUT the Iron - not with the Iron.

As we would find for THIS body, a level teaspoonful given each day for three days, and left off for five, then taken for three days again, would be preferable - for this particular body.

The plain [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]], not with Iron - if the other properties as indicated are to be taken.

5. (Q)  Any further information for this body, or advice?
(A)  As we find, these are the developing periods and the formative years for this body as related to the mental and spiritual activities.

Hence the spiritual and mental relationships should be begun to be impressed or suggested, and put before the body.  For the mental unfoldment will be such that we will find the body developing rapidly along those lines or expressions and activities as have been indicated for same [in Life Reading 1188-3].

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother (two copies, one for Dr. Schoelles)
"    " Ass'n file
