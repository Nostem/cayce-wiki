# TEXT OF READING 3580-1 M 40

**Reading [[3580-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/19/1944  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 11070

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3580], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [3027].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:55 to 12:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.   [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, conditions here are mostly [[Karma|karmic]].  While there are pathological conditions, we find that the correction of these with the greater or better application of the spiritual [[Ideal|ideals]] and [[Purpose|purposes]] as may be practiced in the life in relationships to others, will bring a great deal of difference in the life experience of this body.  While there will not be at first a great deal of change in the [[Visions|vision]], we find that it will be materially aided as there is the adjusting of self.

3. The conditions in the spinal system, as well as in the mouth and gums, have had much to do with the condition in the optic forces.

4. Then, as we find, there should be first the general mental attitude of manifesting the fruit of the spirit as set forth by many of those in the application of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness in the experience of the body. Practice, then, brotherly love, kindness, patience, longsuffering gentleness.

5. Also have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments especially to the 4th, 3rd, 2nd, 1st dorsals, the 3rd cervical and the 1st, 2nd and 4th cervicals.  The adjustments here should be made in the orders indicated, and then those nerve centers that supply to the teeth - especially at the area just under the ear connecting with the mastoid areas of the head should have particular attention.

6. These treatments should be taken rather in series eight to ten, and then a rest period from these of at least three to four weeks.

7. During the rest period, apply for thirty minutes each day the radiations from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium (in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water).

8. The large nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct area.  This will be, for this body, the width of two fingers from the navel center to the right and three fingers up from that point.

9. The small copper plate would be attached to the 3rd cervical.

10. Apply this daily during the whole month when the body is resting from the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

11. Keep the attachment plates very clean.  Polish with the emery paper each time when taken off and each time before applying to the body.  Put the connections in the Solution and connect to the Appliance at least twenty minutes before it is to be attached, and remove the connections from the bottle when not in use.  Use at least three ounces of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] for each charge.

12. When the Appliance has been used for a month, leave off and take another series of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments - once or twice a week.  And these should be general, then, over the whole cerebrospinal system, with special reference to the areas about the jaw, the gums, [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] and the back of the head.

13. After six to eight of these treatments have been taken, leave off and have another series of the Appliance treatments.

14. Doing these the body will gain, if the mental attitudes are kept in accord.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  What is the cause of occasional sharp pains in his legs and stomach?
(A)  These areas from the poor circulation, which will be materially aided - even through the first series of the osteopathic adjustments.

17. (Q)  What should be done for his nerve system?
(A)  All that we have given is for this.

Do these.

18. We are through with this reading.



Two copies to Self (One for Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 East 41st St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(1/19/44 GD's note:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this particular case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)
