## Text

Date: 5/1/1923  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11277

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Washington Hotel, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois, this 1st day of May, 1923 [?], in accordance with request made by [3751]''s parents.

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                       [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, you see we have the body of [3751] [who is at] ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].  The [[Vibration|vibrations]] of electronic forces are those as given through the Abriam [Abrams] forces to equalize the circulation and nerve energy to produce the perfect elimination, see.  These are the kind and character of forces to be used with the applications, [atropine] injections and [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces for this body, to bring the body to its normal forces as near as possible - not by operation or entirely absorption, but by all of the forces as given, and when correctly applied are not experimental but real.  Do that, see.

2. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, have these treatments been carried out correctly?
(A)  Not in all instances, as we have given before.  We are correcting these.
