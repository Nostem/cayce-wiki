# TEXT OF READING 2426-2 F 27

**Reading [[2426-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/15/1941  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 9257

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2426], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, this has been one of those periods of a very bad time.

4. However, we would continue the treatment as outlined to follow the purifying of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  Take the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] extract about three times each week; also sufficient of the vitamins in the ADIRON compound indicated each day for the energies, so as to give the right proportions of A, B, B-1, D, E and G.

5. Keep the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.  These have not been given wholly in keeping with that suggested.  Make these more of a RELAXING than of a stimulating nature.  If there will be the aid from such relaxing treatments as to set up better drainages, then the stimulation through the addition of the vital energies indicated will be more beneficial to the body.

6. Do these, - continuing in that manner as we have formerly outlined.

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of notation for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]].  Give him this along with the last sheet of directions outlined.)
