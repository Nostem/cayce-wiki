# TEXT OF READING 3521-1 M 37

**Reading [[3521-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/18/1943  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 11005

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - through the wife, Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., via Coronet article.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3521].

3. As we find, there are definite disturbances here and these produce at times specific reactions; and at others they produce only a reflex disturbance causing weakness and indecisions in the body-mind.

4. In times back we find there was an injury to the upper portion of the coccyx end of spine; not on the lower - though the lower portion, of course, is pressing upon the sphincter centers.  These have caused a pressure in such a manner as for lesions to be formed in the gall duct and the lacteal duct areas, or the larger patches of these.

5. These are the sources of the dizziness, the tendencies for constipation, the convulsive reactions to base of brain - when these cause a plethora condition in the circulation of blood and the nerve forces through that area of the medulla oblongata at the base of the brain or at the 1st and 2nd cervical.  These we find, are the sources of the disturbance. There are other reflexes, yes, but with the corrections of these we will find that conditions will improve.

6. First, then, we would apply wet heat to the areas in the lumbar and sacral and coccyx; wet heat, preferably towels wrung out of very hot water; and then corrections made there. Then the lesions or adhesions in the lacteal duct and gall duct area may be relaxed sufficiently as to remove the disturbances that reflexly cause the activities in the liver and the lower portion of brain centers.  Reduce all of these osteopathically, if we would bring the better conditions for this body.

7. In the matter of the diet, not too much of solid foods but semi-solids and liquids during the periods that the cleansings are being brought about.  For eventually, as corrections are made, the body should have the gall duct drained osteopathically.  This should be after the relaxations are made in the sacral and lumbar areas and the coccyx conditions reduced and the gall duct area, and the lacteal ducts stimulated to better activity.  Then we may drain these.

8. Then the foods should be gradually changed, but keep away from carbonated waters, chocolates or sweets - do not take these during or any time soon after the periods of these treatments.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Do these pressures cause the double [[Visions|vision]]?
(A)  These cause all of the distresses indicated.  For with the pressures in the upper dorsal and upper cervical areas, these reflect upon the nerves of the sensory system.  They also make for the hesitancy in speech, alterations in the taste at times, as well as the acute hearing, and the periods when it is blank - as well as in the [[Visions|vision]].

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Wife (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
