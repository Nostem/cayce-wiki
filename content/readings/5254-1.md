# TEXT OF READING 5254-1 M 42 (Teacher, Presbyterian)

**Reading [[5254-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/16/1944  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 13390

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of June 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5254], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born February 14, 1901, in St. Louis, Missouri. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Mo.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how. You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  You like not to have been born here, didn't you?  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [5254].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]], there is much from which to choose, which has been made upon the skein of time and space by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Not all of this is beautiful, nor is all of it bad.  But we would magnify the virtues; and we would minimize the faults.

4. Remember, in the analysis of the urges which are latent and manifested, which arise from activities in the earth or the realms through which the entity passed, none of these urges surpass [[Free Will|the will]] of the individual entity; that which is the gift of the Creator, that ye may make yourself one with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], and thus indeed a child of the living God.

5. The abuse of the abilities separates self from the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]].

6. In giving the interpretation, then, the urges, as we find, these are the composite, as it were, of the manifestations during the interims between the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, and the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth.

7. One who is very pleasing in its personality, one who has learned or gained that concept of patience in such manners or measures as to become a very good instructor or teacher, and one who hasn't gotten the "big head" or too "self-conscious" because of knowledge, but has learned that a little knowledge may be a dangerous thing, and that knowledge not applied in the daily life easily becomes sin.

8. These are the personalities, as we find, in this particular experience.

9. As to the individuality from the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the realm of consciousness outside of the material we find one with the ability to write and one with the ability of a legal mind, or judgments, or the drawing of parallels, and the abilities to write would be very well to use; in giving the histories of a movement, organization, a particular place or a particular type of individual, or the types of books or of articles which might be written by the entity.

10. As to the appearances of the entity in the earth, these have been quite varied.  While all are not given, these may show the pattern of the entity and as to how it may apply its abilities in the present to the greater unfoldment and understanding of self, to understand others.

11. Know thyself and know thy body, thy mind, thy [[Soul|soul]] are as the three dimensions in which ye find thyself.  They are represented in the same concept of the Godhead:  Father, Son and [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]].

12. Then, in the analysis of these and of self:  Know that each has its limitations and yet they're all one; even as the Father and Son and [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] are one, as the body, the mind, [[Soul|the soul]] are one.  We see the body with those attributes.  We can conceive of what the mind is, or see it in action, but never find the mind, in the body.  [[Soul|The soul]], one hopes one has it, and yet from the mind, or the individuality of a soul, expressions are given; and thus we understand soul-mate, soul-love, soul-experience.  For these find companionship one with another.  These would be well to analyze, to write about.  They will give you experience.  But know that all which comes to the entity is from the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]], or God, that ye make manifest His love in the earth. That is thy [[Purpose|purpose]] in the earth.

13. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity during the early settlings in the land.  The entity then first was the storekeeper, one of those who made for the general place of exchange, for the needs of the groups of individuals, and thus gradually, as there were the applications of self the entity became the teacher, as may be the experiences in the present.

14. And yet it must supply the needs, not merely of the mental man, but also to the mental and spiritual man.

15. The name then was Clint Matthews.  The entity gained through the experience, but don't let purely commercial [[Ideal|ideals]] draw that from the abilities in the mental as may be indicated.

16. Before that the entity was in the activities in what is now [[Alexandria|Alexandria]], [[Egypt|Egypt]], when there were those rebellions which arose between the peoples who were followers after Islam, when [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of the libraries brought great distress to the entity.

17. The entity may, through the present experience, desire within self much of the wisdom which was lost through [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of the manuscripts in that particular period, and these are the fears which arise in the entity from the marble mobbed rule.  Thus ever the lawyer, or justice principle, is a part of the consciousness.  In the name then of Isadoran.

18. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Yucatan|Yucatan]] land when there were those activities from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, when individuals came into that portion to set up or establish new regimes from the lessons gained in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land.  The entity was with the natives, in the name Kuzan.

19. In the experience the entity gained, and thus in those things from other lands, other peoples, other groups may the entity still find lessons for self, the unfoldment of things to be given to more peoples.

20. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when there were those preparations being made through their activities in the Temple Beautiful.  The entity was among those who were training for the [[Purpose|purposes]] of making contributions to the knowledge of the young, or the teacher of that particular period.

21. The name then was Endo-Alarn.

22. As to the abilities of the entity, then, that to which it may attain and how:

23. First find self and its relationships to the Creative Forces. Then in and through those channels which have been indicated, give to others the message which will be a helpful influence to the world, making the world a better place because of ye having lived in it.

24. Ready for questions.

25. (Q)  What location in the country can I best carry on my work?
(A)  Near Washington, D.C., in the environ of [[Alexandria|Alexandria]] would be well, or in Maryland or Virginia.

26. (Q)  Where can I find a publisher if writing is my field?
(A)  This depends upon the type which is chosen, but there are plenty of publishers if ye prepare the correct manuscript.  You will find it very well in the Falls City Publishing Company or McClurg (?).

27. (Q)  What power do I possess that will help me in constructive endeavor?
(A)  Read that which has been indicated.  The power is latent within self, because ye are conscious of these relationships. If ye understand it, or understood it, you would get it more with the divine.

28. (Q)  How long should I remain in my work?
(A)  For the duration of the present differences.

29. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for Indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born Mo. 2/14/01; res. Mo.; married; no children.
> 
> B2. 7/4/43 Letter:  "Recently I finished '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]',
> and it has made a vital impression on me.  Your work has been
> important, and it proves an assertion I have always made:
> that history incorrectly records.  The truly great are not
> the generals, statesmen and leaders of commerce and industry,
> but the men who silently aid their fellowmen by serving their
> God.  I, too, had a fervent desire to enter [[Ministry of Jesus|the ministry]],
> but through lack of [[Free Will|will power]] or [[Karma|karma]], I did not reach my
> goal.  I studied law which I never followed.  I worked for
> railroads, insurance companies, as an investigator, and did
> some newspaper work.  Today, I am teaching school at a boys'
> school, but I have not found myself, and I am not satisfied.
> I feel I am not doing the work that is necessary.  I have
> reached an impasse, and feel that I am a failure; yet I go
> on feeling that there is something I must do.  I have been
> troubled by a severe sinus condition, and sometimes use that
> as an alibi.  I, too, have been attracted by the ocean, and
> when near the ocean have a feeling that at one time I resided
> near the sea.  Your life has given me hope...  If I have
> a mission to perform, I should like to know about the work
> before it becomes too late...  My favorite [[Psalms|psalm]] is the
> 121st."
> 
> B3. 7/26/43 Letter:  "...I am the seeker and should have
> patience enough in my search.  As soon as I am in a position
> to do so, I will send for 'A [[Search for God Study Groups|Search for God]]', as I desire to
> be in complete en rapport with the '[[Universal Consciousness|Universal Mind]]' by the
> date of my reading."
> 
> B4. 1/20/44 Letter:  "...Since writing to you last year,
> I have taught school at a boys' school, and have helped an
> insurance organization at ... for 3 months...  Two weeks
> ago, I made a trip to ..., [[Arizona|Arizona]], seeking employment with
> insurance companies, but up to this time nothing materially
> has developed.  I am still confused and want to go and desire
> to get into something where my work will do the most good...
> (1) Shall I try my hand at writing; if so, what type of
> writing?  (2) Would it be well for me to take up some type of
> reporting, say court reporting?  (3) Shall I continue working
> toward a better post-war world with some organization?  (4)
> Is there some type of business that I am best suited for, and
> would you advise entering business?  I was very successful
> while at ..., Ill., and made a record for the number of cases
> settled, but I do not like the work due to the methods used.
> I do not hesitate to confide in you and inform you that as
> far as standards go, I have been a failure.  My ideas have
> been high and in the past few months, I have seriously
> thought of abandoning my [[Ideal|ideals]] so that I may be able to
> conform to a pattern.  However, I still have faith and
> cannot run away.  I await your report with faith..."
