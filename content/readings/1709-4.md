# TEXT OF READING 1709-4 F 20

**Reading [[1709-4]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/15/1939  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 7859

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1709], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., Virginia.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1709]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while conditions are very satisfactory in many ways, there are still those disturbances which have been indicated that cause anxiety, - concerning the eliminations, that produce the disturbances to the face.

4. We find that the condition is better in some respects, in that only in this portion of the circulation is the disturbance apparent so much (the face); but it arises more from the lack of the coordinations between the circulation in the lower portion of the system.

5. Thus, by the very nature of the disturbance, it is manifested to the exposed portions of the cuticle.

6. As we find now, we would take the Fleischmann's Yeast; not the ironized, but that carrying the vitamins necessary for better activity through the eliminations, as well as the building or replenishing to the body.  Two bars each day would be the correct amount.  Take these for at least a week at a time, and then rest from same a week, and then take again; and repeat this procedure for at least three or four times.

7. In the adjustments, - these should be made more specific in the lumbar and sacral axis; this once a week, and three or four SHOULD be sufficient.

8. This number should be sufficient IF, after the completing of the three or four adjustments (not after each one, but after the three or four), there would be used regularly the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] about twice or three times a week.

9. Also, when the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] treatments are begun, we would apply following same the violet ray (hand machine, bulb applicator), especially around the neck and back portion of the head; not too much over face, but portions of the face, see?  and then along the area between the pelvic bone and the rib area; these areas, especially, though the area all up and down the spine may be covered.

10. Keep precautions as to the diets, of course, in relation to the fats and greases and the like.

11. Also follow the massages that would be given for the face and neck at times.  Of course, the mud packs will be beneficial; the first time these would apparently give a great deal of disturbance, but after that the reaction will be much better. Give these for self, you see, and we will find better conditions for the whole of the complexion, as well as the general health of the body.  The mud packs would only be given about once or twice a month, you see.

12. Do these, and we will find we will bring about and keep the better forces for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See copy [which was enclosed] for Dr. Hudgins.)
