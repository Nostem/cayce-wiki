# TEXT OF READING 1377-10 F 72

**Reading [[1377-10]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/15/1937  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 7085

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of June, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1377], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Carrie S. House.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1377].

2. As we find, there are changes for the better.  However, these accumulations, these pressures and the tendencies for the inflammation that arises from the accumulations in areas where scar tissue becomes involved, are in those stages where it would be well that we keep especially the manipulations and the drainages as may be set up by same.

3. Keep the eliminations somewhat to the excess.

4. We would not need to give the oil packs so much now, or so often, unless there are acute conditions arising - or the tendencies.

5. As to the treatments and the breaking up of the lesions, these may be best found by the manipulations being given in an [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] manner.

6. These we would do.

7. As for the rest, we would keep much in the same way that has been indicted for the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  How shall I know when the adhesions are removed?
(A)  As indicated, this can be told by the manipulator, the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], as the manipulations are given over the areas where these have existed - through the abdominal area and the caecum area and across the whole of the abdominal area.

10. (Q)  I still have a bearing down pain -
(A)  There is the necessity then, as indicated, to keep the eliminations a little in excess, that the poisons that are broken up - and that have been released - may be carried off from the system, and that inflammation doesn't set up again.

If these pains become severe, necessity demands, of course, that the Packs be applied again; but these can be put farther and farther apart.

11. (Q)  Should I continue with the electrical treatments?
(A)  These are well.

12. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  These we would do, and keep in as constructive frame of mind as possible under the circumstances.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
