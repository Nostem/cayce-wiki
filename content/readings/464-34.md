# TEXT OF READING 464-34 F 65

**Reading [[464-34]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/4/1941  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 3415

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [464], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there continues to be the upsetting through the alimentary canal - especially those areas of the duodenum more than in the stomach and through the jejunum.

4. Since there has not been the ability to make the applications as indicated, we would use a [[Poultice|poultice]] of a small quantity of Tartaric Acid.  Put about a level teaspoonful of Tartaric Acid on gauze, then dampen same - gently; put another gauze over same and apply this over the abdominal area.  Let this remain for at least fifteen to twenty minutes.  Best to apply cool, though not cold.

5. It may be necessary to repeat this every four or five hours for a day or two days, until there is full evacuation.

6. Then preferably of a morning take first a teaspoonful of Milk of Magnesia stirred in half a glass of water.  If this is taken in the morning, then in the evening take a tablespoonful of Milk of Magnesia again; then follow same with half a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth and fifteen drops Elixir of Lactated Pepsin stirred together in a glass of water.

7. Do that and we will make bettered conditions for the body.

8. Be mindful of the diet, - not too much sweets nor too much violent exercise.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  How long after the second dose of Magnesia before the Bismuth and Pepsin should be taken?
(A)  At least six hours.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
