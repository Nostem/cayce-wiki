# TEXT OF READING 900-365 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-365]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/9/1928  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5568

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.

3. [[Dreams|Dreams]] - many - come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through the various channels as have been outlined, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] does well in differentiating between that that may be used as study and that as may be applied in the various undertakings of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  While all are to be applied, yet some mentally, some materially.  The differentiation is the understanding; and in all thine getting, get understanding.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  Of recent date.  Interviewer:  "Nash Motors and all connected with it."
(A)  This an indication of the study the entity should make of the motor situation; Nash being as one that will be of the middling or mediocre nature in its change.

5. (Q)  Meaning the motor stocks in general are going up now?
(A)  In general, yes.

6. (Q)  I said to my mother [See 106-12 Reports]:  "Now I am going to give you this [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] treatment myself." I gave it more as Dr. House does, kneading and spreading the cervicals gently, rather than roughly cracking them.
(A)  The indication to the entity - through that of the mental forces of the body showing that needed by the one the entity would apply such treatment to and the character of treatment needed.  Morrison would be well with this - Dan Morrison.

7. (Q)  Will you give the address?
(A)  [[New York City|New York]].  Look in telephone book - put self to some trouble.

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through with this reading.
