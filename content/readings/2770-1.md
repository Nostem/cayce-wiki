# TEXT OF READING 2770-1 M 57

**Reading [[2770-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/25/1942  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 9919

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the brother - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2770].

3. As we find, there are administrations being made that are in the greater measure in keeping with the needs for removing those conditions of the physical nature.

4. The body, [2770], is meeting much of self, in the present.

5. While changes must be wrought, - as to that which will create or bring about a better coordination in the spiritual, the mental and physical well-being, - it would not be well to attempt any material change in the present.

6. We would follow through with these; making those minor changes that will enable bettered conditions to come to the body.

7. Later, if there is the reestablishing of this coordination, help may be given through these channels.

8. Keep the present administrations.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is it advisable for body to return to his work?
(A)  Not just yet.  At least some three to five weeks as yet.

11. (Q)  Is he having the right diet?
(A)  In the main this is very good.

12. (Q)  What is causing sleeplessness and how can this be overcome?
(A)  That inclination as indicated, that there is not the full coordination between the mental, the physical and the spiritual self.  This naturally finds expression in the activities of the nerve forces; and thus the insomnia. Before retiring of evening, for at least twenty to thirty minutes along the whole length of the spine, especially in those areas across the 9th dorsal, base of brain, and the sacral and lumbar axis, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body with Peanut Oil; using ONLY Peanut Oil here.

13. (Q)  Any specific exercise?
(A)  Walking is the better exercise, keeping in the open as much as practical.

14. (Q)  Where would be the best place for body to go on a vacation?
(A)  Where it would be in the open the more; that is, as to the mountains rather than the seashore.

15. (Q)  Should he go alone or with his wife?
(A)  Better with companion.

16. (Q)  Any other advice or information for the body?
(A)  These as we find, - keeping those things now.  Do coordinate the [[Purpose|purposes]] or aims, or the [[Mental Body|mental body]], with the physical reactions.  But do have these from a SPIRITUAL source of [[Free Will|choice]].

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Brother
"    " Ass'n file
