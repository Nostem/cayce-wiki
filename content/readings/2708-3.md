# TEXT OF READING 2708-3 F 50 (Widow, Manufacturer, Protestant)

**Reading [[2708-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1930  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 9817

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made the by self - Mrs. [2708].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have these conditions, those things that surround the body and the various phases of same. Ready for question.

2. (Q)  The body desires to know what time she should be at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] in order to meet the man she was to meet at this place?
(A)  The time was given, that would be between those of June and the 15th of July.

3. (Q)  Is there any other advice that will be helpful for this body at this time?
(A)  There are other conditions as have troubled the body, that there is being sought information concerning.  These are of the speculative nature, and are regarding stocks.  In considering investments and speculations, there are varied conditions as have [[Resurrection|risen]] in the affairs of the peoples and in business, especially in those lines in which the body is especially interested.  In some of these there will be great changes in the quotations of the output or stocks of the output of those various industries as are IN the same line, especially those of the DuPont interests.  These bought, or even speculated IN, after June 1st until the middle or latter part of July, will be GOOD investments, or speculative investments.  Also there are those of the nature that have to do with transmission and the changes in same.  Also a speculation may be made in that of radio - FOR a holding, but buy on the breaks and not at highs.  There will be seen, however, that in the next ten days these, as we see, will be the lowest for at least sixty to ninety days.

4. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
