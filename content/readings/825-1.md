# TEXT OF READING 825-1 F 43

**Reading [[825-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/12/1935  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 4929

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1867 Kalorama Road, Washington, D.C., this 12th day of February, 1935, in accordance with request made by Dr. Flora Taylor, Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [825], Flora Taylor (Dr.), and Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [825].

2. Now, as we find, the disturbing forces or factors in this body are both pathological and psychological.  Hence we have an incoordination from a pathological condition in the locomotories, or the pelvis, and those pressures that are created between the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal system that create for the sympathetic and imaginative forces a distorted condition in the repressions that arise from such incoordination.  Thus there are produced to the active forces of the [[Mental Body|mental body]] the irrational reactory forces.  These arise from the conditions that we find existent in this body, [825], we are speaking of, present in this room.

3. We find the BLOOD SUPPLY below normal in its reactions through the body, and from the lack of proper assimilation of those forces or influences that affect the circulatory system of the body.  The pressure producing this we find in the 3rd and 4th dorsal area; while those repressions and those activities in the axis and in the lower lumbar and in the coccyx area, where adhesions have formed from the poor circulation, and those activities that make for a hypochondriac reaction to the mental forces of the body, these come through the 4th lumbar and the coccyx center. These make for those repressions in the system.

4. As to the NERVOUS FORCES of the body, we find these do not coordinate in their reactions to the system.  Hence we have those activities that bring for the mental and the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] an exaggerated reaction in the body.

5. In meeting the needs of the conditions in this body, as we find, the exterior applications rather than those influences from medications may be the more helpful to this body.

6. First relieve those pressures that are indicated in the coccyx, by pressure upon those areas in that portion of the system, coordinating same by pressure upon the cerebrospinal centers from which these find their varied end reactions, and specifically in the lumbar, the 9th dorsal and the more specific in the 3rd and 4th dorsal as indicated.

7. We would also use the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], with the vaginal applicator, for relieving those tendencies for adhesions in the pelvic organs of the body.  This we would give for a minute and a half to three minutes at each treatment, about three times each week.

8. Every other day we would use those applications of the magnetic forces, that may be applied from the positive portion of the body of such a treatment in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical, while the negative or the left hand on the 4th lumbar area will create for the body a quieting effect and thus assist in alleviating and making for the better coordinations with the nerve energies and applications to the body as received from the adjustments made.

9. These we would do.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is there any medicine that she needs?
(A)  We would not make medicinal applications for this body; for, as indicated from the mental reactions, these would become a portion of the body as dependent upon same; while with the mechanical applications and the adjustments the body will naturally grow towards a more coordinant and a more normal condition - though it may require longer.

12. (Q)  How long would it be before she would see an improvement?
(A)  The first reaction would be in about ten days; then we would have a period of apparently returning to the first conditions, or as of an obeisure (?); [oophoromania?] then we find the next would be a response in five to six days. These, we find, would reoccur from time to time.  We should see a great deal of improvement in two and a half to three months.

13. (Q)  You think she is safe with the children and at home?
(A)  PROVIDED these applications are made as suggested. If the conditions are allowed to continue, and to grow; of course, she will not be!

14. (Q)  Is there any tear in the uterus?
(A)  More of an adhesion.  This draws, you see, from the left side.

Do these things, and we would bring the better condition for this body, [825].  We are through for the present.

Two copies to Dr. Flora Taylor [D.C., Ph.T.], (one for patient) Copy to Ass'n file
