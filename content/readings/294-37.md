# TEXT OF READING 294-37 M 48 (Psychic Diagnostician,

**Reading [[294-37]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/18/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1996

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 18th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  Now you will have before you the reading as given on [[Dreams|dreams]] of EC on 7/28/25, and will answer the following questions as I ask them, regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the reading as given, with the information given regarding same, the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this [[Visions|vision]] as we have here.

3. Ready for questions on same.

4. (Q)  In this [[Dreams|dream]], EC, GC, and MHB were seen fishing, indicating separate duties for each, as pertains to the work of applying psychic force for the good of all.  What should these duties for each be?
(A)  As indicated by the position in which the three are seen in the [[Dreams|dream]].  MHB in center, giving an explanation and interpretation and applying way of that given to the individual seeking the assistance through the phenomena, see?

That is, each individual applies for that which may be attained through the forces of EC, assisted by GC, in the position of the negative force that brings the questions to the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] force in a manner that is capable of being applied to the individual case in question.

The work, then, as given, to be explained, made applicable to the individual need of those through work of MHB.  And as seen in the catching of the fish by GC, it is seen how same should be studied by this individual, as to make self in that way in which the best [may be obtained].  For as is seen, through this channel the information for all is obtained, see?  The recognition of the fish as Jew fish, the lesson then from same coming, as it were, to the promised peoples, and the abilities of the body, MHB, in recognition of same and of the ability of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to make same applicable to all.  Jew first, then to the Gentiles, as has been given, must the lessons as come be given.  When rejected of one, accepted by another, little by little may the way be given to the peoples, for as has been given aforetime, in the lessons of the Promised One to the Jew, to the World, for as was said of old, "In Him shall all the nations of the earth be blessed."  Then, as is seen, the duties of each:

Keep self in that way that the lessons, the work of each who is a part may together be in an acceptable manner given to all peoples.

5. (Q)  Explain what is meant by surprise awakenings coming to those directly interested in operations of work.  Is that EC, his wife, and MHB.  Explain what this is.
(A)  That to each, in their separate work, and to all interested, there is to come with the setting of the work in that place which has long been given [as that place] from which the radiations are more in the channel of operation the surprise to each in the amount of good that may be accom- plished through each applying self to their portion of work, see?  Applicable, then, to each, applicable then to each in their separate channel, their work, as a unit; that is, each working then for the good of mankind in their respective manners toward one goal, that all may know that there is yet hope in Israel.

6. That is all the questions.

7. There will be much that may be said regarding these and the various channels in which there will be found help.  For as we see, the viewing of the surroundings, the conditions as seen from the [[Visions|vision]], the peoples seen in their various positions, all will have their part from time to time in the various manners and ways, [if each] will but use their best inner self toward making themselves one with the mighty force, as is being set in action, through these forces.

8. We are through for the present.
