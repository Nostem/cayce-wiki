# TEXT OF READING 1387-3 F 41

**Reading [[1387-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/9/1940  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 7105

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of October, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1387], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1387]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while there are many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here, all of these are not towards improvements.

4. These conditions we find existing in the present:

5. There have been congestions of long standing through the antrum, nasal passages; and in the attempts to eradicate these through the circulatory forces, and stimulating activities to same, these have tended to be removed in some ways - yet the disturbance has been rather distributed through the system.

6. Also there are pressures upon centers throughout the system, and we have a resultant effect at times - especially in periods of low pressure, atmospheric pressure - of more severe headaches, more disturbance with the organs of the sensory system, - the eyes, ears, nose, nasal passages, throat at times; and the tiredness.

7. This as we find is not of an arthritic nature, but rather neuritic tendencies; that is, the stiffness, the acute pains at times in wrist, arms, portions even of the lower limbs and through the body.  These are from the poisons distributed through the system, from the breaking up of those tendencies for congestion; so that the nature of these is to become at times as a neuralgic or nerve pressure.  At other times the indications are more of an inflammatory nature causing the disturbance.

8. Then, in aiding to remove these disturbances, now:

9. We would change the administrations.  We would have such as Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller.  To be sure, these treatments in the most part would be [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] adjustments, but a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with same; USING a low electrical heating current for this body. And we will find that these as combined with the White Light will prove the more helpful for the body.

10. Stimulate the centers as associate with the eliminating system; as the upper dorsal, through the upper cervical, and the muscular forces all along the cerebrospinal system.

11. Give the electrical forces in relays; that is, give them two to three periods close together, then have the reaction in the system from same - and use more [[Massage Therapy|massage]] than electricity. Then again have more of the electricity and just sufficient of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and reaction from corrections to aid the system to eliminate.

12. These as we find will bring the better forces for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What is the condition of the kidney?
(A)  At times overtaxed, owing to those excesses of poisons as indicated.

15. (Q)  Can the cause of the headaches be removed entirely?
(A)  The cause may be removed entirely.  It will take a little time; it will take series - not too close together, but periods when there will be the applications and periods of rest, see?

16. (Q)  How long should the periods of application and periods of rest be?
(A)  About three weeks of application, and about two weeks of rest.

17. (Q)  What specific electrical treatment is referred to?
(A)  As indicated, the low heat form.  This will be understood by the one making the applications.  But have them made by that one suggested!

18. (Q)  Will I ever regain my strength enough so that I can live a more normal life and not be eternally tired?
(A)  You should, if there will be the use - and the keeping of those applications suggested for the better normal balanced activities.

Do not overtax the body with the raising of the high [[Vibration|vibrations]] mentally [psychic sessions?], with so much disturbance in the body.

19. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  This should be well balanced, with plenty of Vitamins B-1 and G, and A and B, see?

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self (two copies; one for Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, 205 West 57th St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].)
"    " Ass'n file
