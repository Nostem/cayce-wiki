# TEXT OF READING 1352-7 F 24

**Reading [[1352-7]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/2/1943  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 7035

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1352], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1352].

3. There are many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that we have had before.

4. While there IS conception in the present, there must be precautions and some measures taken, else there may be a miscarriage at the next full moon; or those conditions with the body such as to be a drain on the vital forces of both the mother and child.

5. As we find, first there should be taken at least two or three douches, about five days apart; using only the Fountain Syringe.  Prepare the solution with a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to a quart of tepid water, the temperature being about seventy-five, - not hotter, not colder.

6. And then have an adjustment made, osteopathically, in the 3rd cervical, the 9th dorsal, and in the 4th lumbar.  One or two would be sufficient.

7. Do these, and we will find those alterations such that the irritations will subside that are causing drainage and irritation to the organs of the pelvis, - if done properly.

8. Then take sufficient exercise, - not overdoing it, but, - to keep the normal developments.  The best exercise is walking.

9. Keep plenty of [[Calcios|Calcios]] as a part of the diet, especially through the first four months of the period.  Twice each week spread [[Calcios|Calcios]] on a whole wheat cracker and eat, just that quantity as would be lightly spread on the cracker.

10. Also [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] would be helpful in keeping a purification of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  Take one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water before the morning meal, each day for three days.  The next week take it three days.  Then leave it off two weeks. Then take it again three days each week for two weeks.

11. After the fifth month, have at least one series of adjustments, osteopathically administered, if the period as well as the activities of the body would be better prepared for motherhood.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  When needed, what kind of laxative should be taken?
(A)  Senna Tea.

14. (Q)  Who would be the best physician in ... for the delivery?
(A)  Haynes.  [Phillip Haynes, M.D.]

15. (Q)  Would it be alright to take daily sun baths?
(A)  Be very careful about taking too much sun baths until there has been quite a different change about in the body. Especially not until after the fourteenth of July.  Then they may not be taken too much directly in the sun, but in the reflected rays - or part sun and part shade, or early morning sun and the like.

Be mindful of keeping the diets and the exercise.

16. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Beard) Copy to Ass'n file
