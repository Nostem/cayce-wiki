# TEXT OF READING 2521-2 F 62

**Reading [[2521-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/18/1941  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 9483

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2521], in accordance with request made by the daughter, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [...], daughter and M. H. Bloodworth.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2521].

3. As we find, there has been SOME response, but this - as the condition indicates - has been the feeling some easier for a time, and then again as much or more distress.

4. However, as we find, and as we have indicated, - if there will be the persistent, the consistent use of all those applications suggested, - there will be the better opportunities for response.  For, these have been done in part, and then part NOT used, and then again parts left off and other parts used.

5. Keep the applications rather in that order and manner indicated; for these as we find offer the better opportunities for response.  And, as has been given, the response will depend upon the consistency with which the suggested applications are made.

6. Do that.

7. (Q)  Should anything else be added now?
(A)  As just indicated, DO ALL that has been indicated, - consistently, persistently.

8. (Q)  Just what IS this condition?
(A)  As we have given, it is a complication of disturbances arising from a subluxed gall duct and the effects upon the whole of the system.

9. (Q)  It was indicated in the last Reading that later low electrical treatments should be given.  When should these be begun?
(A)  When there is more uniformity created in eliminations and assimilations.

10. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these things, consistently - if we would gain health for this body, at this time.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
