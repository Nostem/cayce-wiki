# TEXT OF READING 1004-2 F 46

**Reading [[1004-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/14/1936  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 6060

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of December, 1936, in accordance with request made by the sister-in-law, Mrs. [313], through Active Membership of her husband in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1004].

2. As we find, while the conditions that cause the disturbance are advancing, helped by the use of the [[Radium|Radium]] to be sure, the application of the X-Ray as treatments now would be more effective than to use greater amount of the [[Radium|Radium]].  For to burn tissue in this condition, and with the applications that have been given, is only to break it down in such manners and measures as to shorten the resistance, to weaken the strength of the body.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Would an operation at this time be advisable, or would it spread the disease?
(A)  There is not sufficient resistance or proper quantity of coagulations in the system to prevent its spreading, if operative measures were taken.

We will find that longer resistance may be maintained without operative measures, though should the blood tests show that greater quantity of the gluten in the hemoglobins of the blood the operative forces might be preferable.

5. (Q)  Is there danger of a hemorrhage?
(A)  Always, where tissue breaks and is coming always nearer and nearer to not only the veins but to the arteries.  These become problems and must be, as has been indicated, seared off yet not burned or destroyed (the tissue) either with measures to overcome same or by the X-Ray.

6. (Q)  Advise how to get doctors to cooperate with reading.
(A)  These are problems for individual consideration.  The conditions that are existent may be given.  As to how to change individuals' minds, or to point them in given directions, is an individual [[Free Will|CHOICE]] - and God Himself does not overcome will!

7. (Q)  Would it be wise to change doctors?
(A)  Rather UNWISE in the existent conditions.

8. (Q)  Should any [[Radium|Radium]] be given?
(A)  Not unless there is a greater amount of spread of the disease, as we find.  The X-Ray is the better application in the present.

9. (Q)  Could she be cured at the Astor Memorial Hospital in N.Y. City?
(A)  These become problems; as we find she would not be able to stand even the removal to same in the present.  And the condition is so far advanced there IS no cure unless there may be had other sources or applications than being administered in the present.  We are only delaying.

10. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Each that administers, each of those that are dependent, each of the loved ones materially should know, should feel, the Divine doeth all things well.  Remember that thy Pattern through suffering became the Savior.  Through suffering, individuals meet themselves, and fit themselves for the Divine expression in their [[Soul|soul]] selves.  The bodies that cause disturbance by the disobeying of the laws and activities in a material world are but troublesome things to [[Soul|the soul]] at times.  LIFE IS CONTINUOUS!  [[Soul|THE SOUL]] MOVES ON, gaining by each experience that necessary for its comprehending of its kinship and relationship to Divine.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Husband
"    " Mrs. [313]
"    " Ass'n file
