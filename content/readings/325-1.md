# TEXT OF READING 325-1 F 50

**Reading [[325-1]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/7/1922  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 2439

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala., this 7th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M.                                         ..., Ky.

1. EC:  Now for the conditions we have with the body of Mrs. [325], ..., [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].  The conditions that arise are very much the same force as we have given before.  The pains through the arms, though, have changed somewhat in their action, but are produced from the same condition arising again in this body [Neuritis?].  Until the mental forces are changed, the conditions will continue to arise from time to time [Financial worries?].  We would take much of the same properties into the system as we have given before.

2. Do not use too much high seasoning in the diet that is taken; with the clary water we would take a very low form of electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] over this body.  Violet ray hand machine, bulb applicator?]

3. As to the financial conditions, we would, as we have given before, gather those into one fund rather than so many small deals to be dealt with; that is, sell off the small properties and combine them into one larger.

4. The body will go South and West in the latter part of the year or the first part of the next.

5. We are through.
