# TEXT OF READING 2-3 M 49

**Reading [[2-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/7/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 4

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [...], and also by Edgar Cayce and those in charge of hospital.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker, Nurse, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Questions)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.

2. (Q)  Is present treatment properly given?
(A)  No.

3. (Q)  Is condition of eye progressing or getting worse?
(A)  Getting worse.

4. (Q)  Would you advise hospitalization at the [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] for him?
(A)  Be rather a hard case to handle, though he may be MATERIALLY-physically, mentally - aided.  We would advise same.

5. (Q)  What else would you advise for the body's general good?
(A)  Let's commence first - and don't get it all beforehand. As has been given for this condition, there has been a gradual accumulation in the system from those of the character of PRESSURE in system, causing this film, this inflammation, over and about the eyeball itself.  These have NOT, as yet, caused distress other than sympathetically to the optic nerve proper - though the SYMPATHETIC nerves of same, especially those of the back portion, or the lens of the eye itself - have RECENTLY shown distress.  The SUFFERING produced by the body, or in the body, may be relieved - as has been given.  Not by applying [[Vibration|vibrations]] directly to the eye, save those of the ORDINARY [[Violet Ray|VIOLET ray]] applied DIRECT over a CLOSED ball or lid, see?  but the application of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ULTRA-violet]] over that region of the upper dorsal and cervical, FROM which the RADIATION COMES TO the nerves of the eyes and face - and by the proper manipulation and adjustments in the system - these will aid and these may be brought to where surrogation or separation may be brought about.  Now there HAD been, or has been the condition when by absorption these conditions might be removed.  Taken soon enough, this may be POSSIBLY brought about yet - though it may be found necessary for an operation later on.  But aid as far as possible through these sources.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office and [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
