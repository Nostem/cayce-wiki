# TEXT OF READING 1117-1 F 65

**Reading [[1117-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/8/1936  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 6373

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of February, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1117], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1010].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1117].

2. As we find, the disturbing conditions arise from adhesions and lesions; that are caused from the lack of the proper care when these were first happening in the body.  These have been effective, of course, through the organs of the pelvis, and then - becoming somewhat infectious - have, carrying on through the system, caused a humor in the blood that has entered into the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system.

3. Thus disturbances are produced that become very aggravating to the body at times, throughout the activities of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

4. In the BLOOD SUPPLY irritations are produced that, affecting through the organs themselves, become such as to cause less and less of the ability to create activities that would become other than irritating to already disturbed and irritated tissue.

5. We find in the NERVOUS SYSTEM there is rather a RACKING upon the nervous portions of the system, affecting the body both in the physical and at times even in the mental; producing strains throughout the whole of the body itself.

6. Then, in the present, as we find:

7. We would first begin with taking internally small quantities of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; one minim of morning before meals, one minim of evening before retiring, in half a glass of water.  Increase this amount each day one minim (that is, one minim added each morning and each evening) until five minims are being taken twice a day - morning AND evening.

8. Then leave off same for three days.  Then begin over again with the one minim, increasing each day as indicated.

9. At least once a week, or oftener if the irritations become so aggravated as to cause distresses through those portions of the system, we would use a douche - or a vaginal douche - in these proportions:  To half a gallon of lukewarm water add half a teaspoonful of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  This should be used not in a spiral douche but rather in a Fountain douche - or Fountain Syringe, see?  This should be kept or held as long as possible (that is, for a few moments) before being expelled.

10. Also it would be well to use the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] solution (in the same strength as for the douche) to sponge off those portions of the body where there are irritations; about the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], or those portions where there are swellings - as seen in the groin and under the arm - the irritations there.  This will be found helpful; not done too often, but just sponge off with same.

11. We would begin with the NEUROPATHIC treatments for an equalization of the whole circulation, and to remove the tensions upon the nerve forces of the body.

12. Be mindful of the diet, that there is a refraining from fats - especially.  No butter.  Little of coffee or any stimulants.  Rather stick to vegetables and fruits than nuts.

13. And we would find we would make for a much nearer normal condition for this body.

14. Do these.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Will I be able to keep up my activities?
(A)  If these will respond in the system, we will find the body will be able to keep up activities.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - % Mrs. [1010] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(See booklet [which was enclosed] on Atomidine.)
