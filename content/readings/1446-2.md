# TEXT OF READING 1446-2 F 43

**Reading [[1446-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1937  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 7224

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of December, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1446], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1446], Mrs. [667], and mother of Miss [1652].]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1446].

2. Now, as we find, conditions show improvements in the general reactions.

3. While those conditions of more specific nature still produce irritation through the tube, the clitoris, and to the bladder, we find that these are as much from the reflexes or from the BURNINGS as used in the measures that have been taken, as from the condition itself.

4. As we find, the stimulations that have been given through applications should be continued; though they may be made a little farther apart.

5. The diets that have been outlined should be kept, too; and not so much of those that make a heaviness or drain upon the activities of the kidneys for the eliminations.  Not that the ORGANS themselves are affected so much, rather the specific conditions.

6. As we find, the taking of the ANIMATED Ash would be most beneficial for the body, provided the electrical forces as from the violet ray are used following same.  Use not only across the area of the lower portion of the abdomen and just above the pubic bone, but across the lower portion of the lumbar and sacral area; but for not MORE than two minutes - the whole treatment of the electrical forces; and these not given until about ten minutes AFTER an eighth grain of the Ash is taken, and this given about twice each week - like Tuesdays and Fridays.  But preferably take these in the evening; one-eighth grain Animated Ash; not more than two minutes, a minute and a half to two minutes, with the plain bulb applicator of the violet ray.  This is to centralize the activative forces of the Ash (the violet ray treatment), releasing oxygen to affected portions in the tube and the bladder itself.  Be sure to empty the Ash dry on tongue and swallow with water; DO NOT take IN THE CAPSULE!

7. And these as we find would be most helpful in relieving the irritation, the itching sensation - and the pain sensation that comes at times.

8. The rest we would keep much as has been outlined.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What was it that passed from the bowels about six inches long, looked like an intestine?
(A)  This was an accumulation of feces in portions of the colon itself.

11. (Q)  What causes the bowels to not move naturally?
(A)  Lack of peristaltic movement owing to the high tension that has been produced by the application of those things that caused a nerve reaction to same.

Now:  The stimulation of these, by the use of the electrical forces, is to stimulate the lymph and emunctory reaction which is a portion of the peristaltic movement; and thus will assist in gradually restoring more normalcy in that direction.

Hence the necessity for the precautions as to those influences that would cause the activities of the system to be clogged by lack of activity, or that would become too absorbent in its nature.

12. (Q)  What are the lumps that rise in the mouth and have such bad odor?
(A)  They are gas from the digestive forces or influences from the lack of the proper balance of the lacteals and the acid or the alkalin and acid forces in the lower digestive force itself.

13. (Q)  Is it harmful for this body to do housework and when it does, what causes pressure in lower back?
(A)  The natural inclinations for a great deal of activity upon the feet are to produce pressure through the areas where the disturbances have been and are, and hence these would require that the body rather budget its activities. Work a little and REST a little, and not try to do it all at once!

14. (Q)  Have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments been given correctly?
(A)  These are very good.

15. (Q)  What causes discharge?  and should douches be continued?
(A)  The natural effect of the infectious forces and tissue that have been involved by the burning or cauterizing same still causes the overflow or the elimination of this extra flow.  Yes, keep the douches.

16. (Q)  Any other advice for this body?
(A)  Keep the mental attitude in a less resentful manner and we will find the much better outlook.

For let each [[Soul|soul]] know - the activities of the mental are a part of the whole.

17. (Q)  How much improvement do you see in the body?
(A)  About thirty percent.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
