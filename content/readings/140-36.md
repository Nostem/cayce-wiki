# TEXT OF READING 140-36 F 27

**Reading [[140-36]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/17/1930  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 572

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 17th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by her brother-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [140] - this we have had before. In some respects we find the conditions are on the improve. There is near the perfect position, or NEARER the more NORMAL position of the conception, and the discharge or the hemorrhages as have been coming from the organs SHOULD SOON be eliminated entirely, with the applications being made in the MANNER as they have been made more recently.  There should NOT be - if ANY more - more than one MORE of such discharges.  Then, the normal position being attained, the normal ACTIVITY may be better for the body.  This is dependent entirely upon the whole attitude, and of the applications as would be given to the body as respecting the condition.  If there will be no more given of the properties as tend to make for the dilation and extenuation OF the organs of the pelvis, the BETTER the condition will be for the body - for the applications, the [[Vibration|vibrations]], these have been bettered - see?  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are the treatments now being given properly?
(A)  MORE properly than they have been heretofore, and the conditions SHOW the result of same.

3. (Q)  What is the bleeding itself still coming from?
(A)  It's just been GIVEN as to what!  The conception not taking place normally, the discharge coming then from the system in general - see?

4. (Q)  When will life be felt?
(A)  This will depend upon MUCH the attitude OF the body, and as to what is GIVEN the body to sustain and to DEVELOP life as related to PHYSICAL activity.  The life, as a SPIRITUAL force, is EXISTENT.  The life, as the MENTAL, is wholly acted upon BY the attitude OF the body [140].  The PHYSICAL conditions will be the RESULT of those combined efforts, see?

5. (Q)  She asks, then, if the child is definitely alive.

(A)  Dependent upon what is called alive!  Physically it isn't existent.  Only mentally and spiritually.

6. (Q)  If the child is born, and when it is born, will it be perfectly normal and healthy?
(A)  IF it is born, and WHEN it is born, it will be perfectly normal and healthy.

7. (Q)  Is there any chance in this conception of twins?
(A)  No.  Not as at present existent.

8. (Q)  The sex has not been changed, then?
(A)  Has not been altered, as far as the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are concerned.

9. (Q)  Would it be well to go to Deal, [N.J.] and when?
(A)  As soon as the body may resume its nominal or normal activities.  This, as we find at present - that should be in proper condition - the body - in two weeks.  Ten days given as a change.  It has occurred.  Two weeks the body should be able, UNLESS there is given - or unless there are other ACCIDENTAL conditions to arise.

10. (Q)  What causes the headache, and what may be done about it?
(A)  This is more of a retraction from POSITION of the body in the activity given same.  This will be relieved when the NORMALCY of position is assumed.

11. (Q)  As it now stands, when will the child be born?
(A)  In due season and in due time.

12. (Q)  What is the full interpretation of [302]'s experience regarding the baby, and four months, and named [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]]?
(A)  This rather a combination of experiences, and would best be interpreted from the mind of [302], or [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of the [[Mental Body|MENTAL BODY]] and ITS association with the conditions existent, and in THAT light would mean little [as far as [140] is concerned].

13. (Q)  Is there any further general advice?
(A)  None other than keeping the body-physical, the body-mental, in an at-onement and an attunement to the real desire OF the body as respecting the condition and its ultimate outcome.  That's a whole mouth full!  but that is that as would be given.

14. That is all the questions.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
