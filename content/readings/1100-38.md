# TEXT OF READING 1100-38 F 48

**Reading [[1100-38]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/20/1944  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 6325

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1100], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Mrs. [1100], Mr. [470], and others.]

Time of Reading                                   ... Hotel, 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Ind.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1100]; this we have had before, you see.

3. As we find, conditions in the main are better than the last we had same here.  There are, of course, those tendencies and some warnings which would bring about or make for bettered conditions in the body.  The variations which have existed in the altitude have caused those tendencies for an engorgement of the circulation between heart and lungs and between the liver and the heart.  There is not as yet, though, any organic heart disorder.  The aorta artery is engorged.  This, as we find, should then prevent the body from having too violent exercise as there are changes in the altitude, but we would find that walking as an exercise should be taken by the body each morning early with the deep breathing.  If there isn't more than a block or five blocks walked, do have this early and when taking these do breathe deeply.  Take the time for the exercise of the arms at such times.  Not that there needs to be the waving of the arms but a regular lifting in unison with the step, so that there is the bringing of coordination between lungs, heart and liver and we will improve the conditions much.  We would also indicate that there should be more regular intervals of taking the [[Grape Juice|grape juice]], systematically as has been indicated.  Not that the body is too heavy, but just don't get too heavy for the bettered conditions of heart, lungs, liver and kidneys.

4. Those inclinations in the throat and the bronchi and trachea will be much improved by the following of that which will produce more of those tendencies for an equal balance in the acidity and the alkalinity of the body.  These precautions taken, then, we should keep [[Physical Body|physical forces]] good for the body.

5. The exercise of the hand should be more on the side as if directing a choir or waving up and down, see?  These should be gentle but as using the arms to raise the body, as it were, from the ground.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What length periods should the [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] be taken?
(A)  At least for a month or six weeks.  Then leave it off a while, then again after two to three weeks, take another six weeks period.

8. (Q)  Any further advice or counsel for the body at this time?
(A)  The attitudes, of course, as the body understands, have much to do with keeping the coordination.  When there comes the disturbing conditions in mental relationships or attitudes, just don't let it upset you.  Know:  that which is, if used, is best.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

> R5. 4/65 "I have been in the hospital for 3 wks.  Had to have
