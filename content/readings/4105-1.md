# TEXT OF READING 4105-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4105-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/2/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11803

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 2nd day of July, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M.                                 Pittsburgh, Pa.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, while we find this body very good throughout, there are some conditions that may be corrected, or assisted in their correcting themselves, that the body may be better and more capacitated in giving that which is best in itself.

2. Now these are conditions as we find in this body here. [4105] we are speaking of, present in this room:  First, in the blood supply in the body, while we find the blood itself very good in its quantity and its quality, yet in the system we find it carrying those of the conditions as thrown off through the capillary when they should be eliminated through the emunctory forces in the body, or we have a high circulation just above the normal, and we have a low hepatic circulation below the normal, and we have the elimination through improper channels produced by the nerve relaxation and taxation in the system.

3. In the nerve systems we find as these conditions:  Through the condition of the body, especially, in the mental attitude toward irritations as exist both from within and without, the center especially in the lumbar region first, then the secondary cardiac plexus forces governed by the nerve energy in both cerebrospinal and sympathetic forces relax or contract these centers so as to produce the condition in the circulation and the taxation to the system through the nerve reaction to mental forces in the body.  The pressure caused by these show in the pneumogastric forces and centers and also in the action of the blood flow through capillary circulation, especially, and the depression of the hepatic circulation give some trouble through the whole portion of the system from the kidneys, the liver and the action of the forces through the intestinal tract, the spleen becoming engorged and causing pain at times on the left side, nearer the center though, you see.

4. In the functioning of organs as affected by the nerve strain of the high capillary circulation.  The head, we have the forces there as very good.  The throat, larynx, bronchials, epiglottis at times show dilation of the muscular and ligament forces being over-charged or weak, and produce not so much distress, as of a full choky feeling.  These are spasmodic conditions and not one existing at all times. The lung forces, very good.  The digestive tract, very good except as is shown in the pneumogastric at times.  The liver inclined to be of a submerged functioning and the emunctories and excretory forces do not throw off as they should as do the kidneys at times show their reaction.  Through the organs of the pelvis and their functioning we find the strain that first produces the pains to the body, but through reflex conditions and nerve strain on other centers.

5. To overcome this condition we would take this into the system:

Tincture of Valerian.............2 ounces,
Bromide of Potassium.............3 grains,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium............1/2 grains,
Elixir Calisaya..................2 ounces.

6. Sufficient simple syrup to make six (6) ounces.  The dose would be when we have these depressed feelings, teaspoonful every hour.  At other times, once or twice each day until the body feels the reaction in the system.  Use [[Vibration|vibration]] forces from electrical driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] across the abdomen and over the whole length of the spine, especially, in the lower solar plexus region and across the lumbar and sacral.  Do that. This would be well to be taken each evening so the body will find rest easier.

7. (Q)  Why does this body have irregular menstruation periods?
(A)  Nerve taxation and with the change as comes to the body. The correction in this we will find in that as given in the [[Iodine|Iodine]] with its affect of the blood supply.  [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium, see.

8. (Q)  Is this body changing life?
(A)  We have given it.

9. (Q)  How long will it take this body to change life?
(A)  Depending upon how the body is treated and the strain under which the body goes without the relief through this period.  Do as we have given if we would bring the best for this body.

10. (Q)  Why does this body have pimples on her face?
(A)  Secretions being thrown off through capillary circulation when it should be eliminated through other portions of the body.
