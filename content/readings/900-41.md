# TEXT OF READING 900-41 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-41]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/4/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5244

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
12:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you the bodies [137] and [900], and also the reading given [900] on March 3, 1925, in which it gave that he would benefit financially from May 26th to June 15th.  Correlating this and the present business condition surrounding the body [900] and [137], you will tell this body how these individuals may now best proceed in their financial affairs to bring about the best to themselves.  You will answer questions regarding this.

2. EC:  These conditions that now arise with these bodies, as individuals, have [reached] and do reach these propositions as have been outlined in other conditions for these bodies, and the acceptance or rejecting of propositions of others in their relations to the intended proposition by and between these individuals and others will be consummated in a manner satisfactory to all.  These we see will happen in ten days after the conditions are accepted.

3. As to the relations that will appear in the time as given between June and May, we find those -

[GD's memo:  Mr. Cayce stopped and would not continue with the reading.  See continuation at 5:00 P.M. in reading 900-42.]
