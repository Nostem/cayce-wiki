# TEXT OF READING 378-39 M 57

**Reading [[378-39]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/14/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2921

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
12:15 to 12:20 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.       ..., N.Y. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Quite a lot of green about the body; more spirituality coming into the understanding of the self, as to those things that disturb and have disturbed the body in the days just passed.

2. Let that mind be in thee that will make thy going in, thy coming out, in keeping with that He would have thee do just now.

3. Let the glory of the Father through the Son, and the promises that have been made to the children of men, be such that there may be in thine experience those channels, ways, manners and means that will make thee know and comprehend that which is best in the light of divine guidance that thou shouldst go in thy seeking, in thy wanderings, in thine making manifest in material affairs, in the lives, minds and hearts of those that seek to know what thou hast chosen to do.

4. For, no man lives to himself.  No [[Soul|soul]], save one that has separated itself from the love of the Father, wanders alone.

5. Keep thy paths straight.

6. In His name we seek.

7. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file
