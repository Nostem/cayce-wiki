# TEXT OF READING 1308-3 M 63

**Reading [[1308-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/27/1943  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 6947

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1308], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. Here again the body is not gaining the use of itself nor in the ability to refrain from the disturbance in the heart's activity or the general conditions that cause the weakness. Hence the body has been unable to again take up activities normally.

4. If the body had followed those applications suggested for it, much better results might have been obtained.  That the body has been persuaded that little or nothing may be obtained from such applications is a pity.

5. The body should learn that in self is the force and power through which [[Healing|healing]] may come - if there are the preparations for same.

6. Again we would give, as first indicated for the body:

7. Cleanse the body - not so much by large quantities of purgatives - but by the use of laxatives AND [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.  These [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] must be given professionally, so as to watch the heart's activity.  For these to be done thoroughly, it will require at least that two or three [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] be given - preferably a week apart.  Take one every seven days, or just vary depending upon the conditions in the colon, until three or four have been taken.

8. As laxatives we would go rather on a semi-liquied diet; plenty of such as oatmeal or other whole grain cereals, and citrus fruit juices - as oranges, grapefruit, lemons and limes.  When taking orange juice, add a little lime juice with same.  When taking grapefruit, add a little lemon juice.

This is the preferable manner.  However, DO NOT take the cereals AND the citrus fruits on the same day, to say nothing of the same meal; but alternate these, using one on one day and the other on another day, or have two or three days straight-running when only cereals would be used for the morning meal, and then several days when only citrus fruits would be used, and so on.

9. Vegetable soups or broths may be taken, not too much grease in them.  Also eat plenty of water cress - and you're in a good country for it!  Eat a big plateful every day, raw, with mayonnaise or oil dressing if preferred.  Do not include anything else with this, but just the water cress alone, fresh, daily.

10. When the system has been thoroughly cleansed, begin with the use of the Appliance as has been suggested that will aid in equalizing the circulation and in adding the [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary for the betterment of the body.

11. Each day following the Appliance treatment, give the body a good, gentle massage; using any good powder or oil as a means for carrying out the massage.

12. Do these and we will make bettered conditions for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].)
