# TEXT OF READING 3457-1 F 38

**Reading [[3457-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/4/1943  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 10930

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3457], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Born September 13, 1905, at Braddock, Pa. 
Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  Now you will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [3457] ... Street, Michigan - who seeks a Mental and Spiritual Reading regarding her relationship with her daughter [...].  And you will give the information which will be most helpful at this time, answering the questions which she has submitted as I read them:

2. EC:  Yes.  In giving that which will be helpful for his [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], many things are to be taken into consideration.  Know in self that the greater part is ever within self when trouble arises between self and daughter, between self and neighbor, between self and husband.  For that we think, that we give voice to in condemnation of others, we will find within our own selves.  For the Lord thy God is one.  And where ye condemn, so are you condemned.  As ye forgive, so are ye forgiven.

3. If ye would find self, look within.  Know what is thine own ideal - spiritual, mental, material.  Not according to what ye would judge thy neighbor, thy husband, thy daughter.  For judge not if ye be not judged - this is [[Law of Moses|the law]].  When ye judge, the same judgement ye are passing to thyself.

4. Look within.  For thy body is indeed the temple of the living God.  Thy [[Purpose|purpose]] in life, in the experience in the earth, is to magnify that glory which may be thine when ye have attained to same.

5. It is not that the daughter has taken judgement against you. You have taken judgement against that which she worships, which she adores.  The trouble lieth within self, as it does concerning thy husband.  It is within self that ye must find thy answer.  For [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord is perfect, it converteth [[Soul|the soul]] - and until [[Soul|the soul]] is set in the order, in the desire of filling that [[Purpose|purpose]] for which ye entered, of being one with Him, it may not find peace, it may not find joy - in self, nor in the earth.

6. Try the Lord.

7. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> fog, and that she feels that she has been some places before

> tormented by thought that a terrible mistake has been made by
> her marrying at such an early age [16], to a man 30 yrs. old.
> 
> The doctor at one time told me that she has a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]]
> disturbance and that she is always in a daze, or that her
> mind is far away.  She has always been a peculiar child, and
> for that last 2 years, she felt and acted towards me very
> strange, and even struck me, when I tried to correct her.
> Would you give her a special reading if she agreed to it her
> self.  She tells me that she can't concentrate, at all, and I
> suffer along with her and I would do anything to be able to
> help her find her self.
> 
> Kindly let me know just as soon as possible for you what you
> can do for me and my child.  I will be very grateful to you
> for your help in this case.  This case should prove to be
> interesting.
> 
> Very sincerely, Mrs. [3457]
> 
> P.S.  Mr. Cayce could it be possible to give me a reading
> sooner, as I am suffering greatly over this, and the daughter
> is very nervous too.
