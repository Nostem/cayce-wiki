# TEXT OF READING 4790-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[4790-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/12/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12773

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of February, 1925, in accordance with request made by his son - Mr. [270].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                             ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading as given for the body, [4790], on February 10, 1925, at 9:00 P.M.  How long at each time should the battery [[Vibration|vibration]], as given in the reading, be applied to the body, and how often?

2. EC:  For at least one hour, or until the [[Vibration|vibrations]] show the body is gaining rest and equalization from [[Vibration|vibrations]].  For the first three to five days, this may be applied thirty minutes in morning, thirty minutes in evening.  After that time (three to five days) one hour and no more during the day.

3. These, we find, will bring equalizing in circulation, stimulation in nerve supply, ability of coagulation to be assisted, ability of the resistive force to be creative in system, ability of storage energy to be revibrated in the physical centers in nerve system, in blood supply, in the incentives for digestion and assimilation.

4. Thirty minutes morning, thirty minutes evening, for at least three to five days.

5. We are through.
