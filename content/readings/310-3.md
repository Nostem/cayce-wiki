# TEXT OF READING 310-3 F ADULT (Protestant, Minister's Widow)

**Reading [[310-3]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/22/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 2346

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va, this 22nd day of January, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [310].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [310] ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].  You will give a physical, mental and spiritual reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], with suggestions and advice for the correction and upbuilding of each of these.  You will also answer the questions which she will ask you regarding all phases of her life.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body; the enquiring mind, [310].

3. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these - while very good in many respects - have disorders and distresses that precautions taken as respecting them will aid the body in better functioning throughout the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Especially is this true respecting the tissue, soft tissue in throat, face, head, and nostril.  These, as we find, would be benefitted most by the use of an inhalant that would be prepared in this manner:

4. Put 4 ounces of grain alcohol in an 8 ounce rather wide-mouth glass-stoppered container or bottle.  To this add, and IN THIS MANNER add:

Eucalyptol..............................20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...................10 minims,
Benzosol................................10 minims,
Tincture of Benzoin.....................10 minims,
Canadian Balsam..........................5 minims,
Tolu in solution, or Tincture of........40 minims.

Shake the solution together when this is to be inhaled, which should be inhaled into the throat, bronchial and lungs, and through the nostril.  This will relieve this tension, and the tendency for cold; also those conditions that make for tautness in head, face, and nasal cavities.  Use deep breathing when inhaling same.

5. We would also, for that general debilitation as produces at times an irritation to the capillary circulation, to the internal organs, the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] or pad - Double X in strength.

6. Be mindful that the body keeps the feet dry.  Do not over expose self to colds and congestions, or fevers.

7. In the matter of the diet, keep those of a general and full building; plenty of the proteins, and especially of such that carry the Vitamin C.  These, as we will find, will be aided in their materialization - by the character of the food - when the [[Radium|Radium pad]] is worn.

8. In the [[Mental Body|mental body]], this keep in accord with those tenets that have been set before self, keeping in accord with that attitude which has so long been held as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s relationships to individuals, and conditions existent in the body-physical, the body-mental, and the social, physical, and material relationships with others.  Strive not against those things that apparently irritate in the mental attitudes of others, for God's [[Purpose|purposes]] are not defeated in the services rendered.  Let the yeas be yea, and the nays be nay - for GOD giveth the increase.  God worketh His own wonders to perform, using individuals in their various spheres when they approach every attitude with "Thy will be done, O Lord, not my will."  Project not thine self into others' forces or others' lives, other than as a reflection of Him.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Where should [[Radium|Radium pad]] be worn?
(A)  Across the solar plexus of days - that is, the 8th, 9th and 10th dorsal.  Of evenings it may be laid for a few minutes across the chest or head, or across the abdomen.

10. (Q)  Should this pad be worn constantly or certain hours of the day?
(A)  Be worn at least four to six hours during the day.

11. (Q)  What is the cause and remedy for numbness in shoulder area?
(A)  Poor circulation.  We have given the reason.  The remedy will be the appliance, and the diet.

12. (Q)  Does this cause the stiffness also in right hand?
(A)  Causes the stiffness also.  Poor circulation.  Poor eliminations.

13. (Q)  What causes the intense pains at times in right side, causing discomfort at seasons in rectal area?
(A)  These are pains of a reflex nature, from the vitality being used up, and the system's attempting to adjust itself through these various periods with those losses of conditions in system.  Hence the RADIUM reflex, or Radium Active forces for the system, is to clarify the conditions of body, giving vitality and strength, and acumen in the effectiveness of each [[Vibration|vibratory]] force.

14. (Q)  Is appendix affected?
(A)  No.

15. (Q)  What is the cause of swelling in feet?
(A)  Circulation!  Remedy given!

16. (Q)  Regarding business and other phases:  Should other funds be invested in Nashville or ...?
(A)  Nashville would be better.

17. (Q)  Give any helpful suggestions regarding this.
(A)  Through those same channels in which others have been, well that these be IN the same disposition - though not exactly in the same things, see?

18. (Q)  What other would be best to invest in?
(A)  In those lines as outlined here!  Through these same sources or channels, see?  which is of the accumulative forces from interests and investments in the various real estates and properties that have been held or are held by these so acting in this capacity.

19. (Q)  In view of present conditions of N. T. L. and State Boards, does it seem best now to plan for work under either of them?
(A)  In the Spring should be the period, as has been outlined, for these conditions are going to be better - through those activities of the various boards in their next meeting, which should be in April or May, when there should be those associations and connections made.  These, as we should find, will be the most helpful.

20. (Q)  Does place now exist, or will it develop in time?
(A)  Develop!  Of course, there has been the curtailment of the work, or the laborers in these fields, rather than additions - but these are to be developed in these next meetings of these various boards, see?

21. (Q)  What definite steps for securing it?
(A)  This, as has been outlined, make known to those various members of whatever department of the work [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] decides or chooses to take hold of, and go to work on same!

22. (Q)  When should business course be started?
(A)  May be taken immediately, if so desired.  This should be in the form of the companionate clerical work; that is, some stenography, some bookkeeping, some correspondence, see? Secretarial status - so that in the work in the general way, these may be the most helpful, see?  made to fit in with any, so that - should the occasion arise - which will, with these endeavors that should be in the state work, or in the National work - the entity would be acting in the capacity of an aide to the ministers in the various fields, through various periods of its work in the various departments.

23. (Q)  Give definite suggestions for getting roomers.
(A)  These, as we find, may be best by word of mouth, and the various characters that are necessary or needed.  Just as one may attract others by the thought being sent out, by word of mouth and to others, THIS the best way and manner.

24. (Q)  Should they be men or women?
(A)  They may be both!

25. (Q)  Should Nancy Martin, Carol Strickler, or Olive Moseley become members of household?
(A)  Either, or all.  Olive Moseley would possibly present the better of any, in the capacity of companion and assistant in the various endeavors for the present period.

26. (Q)  Under what terms or plans?
(A)  These had best be worked out between themselves.  The varied circumstances or the changes that might be necessary in the affairs of each may be best met by the comparison of that necessary in the affairs of each, and that they expect to accomplish, that they expect to do with same.

27. (Q)  Is best being done at present in methods of living, thinking, or how could improvements be made?
(A)  These have been referred to in the mental attitude of the individual.  These are the better.  Do that.

28. (Q)  In view of future, should there be a gradual disposition of house furnishings as opportunity affords? or should all be disposed of, or stored when move is made?
(A)  Stored or disposed of when move is made.  Let this be a development of the conditions as they develop in the efforts as to be put forth, rather than bringing one to bear that the other may happen - see?

29. (Q)  Any other advice that will be of help for this entity at this time?
(A)  Keep the face toward the light and the shadows will fall behind.  We are through.

Copy to Self Two copies to Ass'n file
