# TEXT OF READING 341-38 M 25

**Reading [[341-38]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/4/1932  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 2677

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 4th day of October, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - [341], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and [341].]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. The general physical conditions and reactions are very good in system.

2. As we find, those corrections necessary for the activities in the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic, with the coordinating of the sensory system, especially as related to those conditions in neck, shoulders, upper dorsal, show a great improvement.

3. After a rest of a month to six weeks we would take a period of one or two weeks more treatments; then in six to eight weeks we change the glasses, for they will be improved!

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is the cause of the drowsiness, and how can it be overcome?
(A)  Keeping regular hours!

6. (Q)  Any other suggestions for general conditions?
(A)  See that there is kept the proper eliminations, so that poisons do not accumulate, and keep the balances of elements for the structural portions of the body in a normal reactive manner.  Keep plenty of those foods and food values that carry the Vitamin D; plenty of those that make for sufficient activities in the muscular forces, especially as towards the activities of the liver and the spleen.  These, as we find, are in those foods that particularly are of the cereals, or heart of cereal, with plenty of vegetables that are grown above the ground and meats that are of the wild game, fish or mutton; not much beef.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
