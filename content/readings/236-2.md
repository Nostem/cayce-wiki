# TEXT OF READING 236-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[236-2]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/21/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 1076

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [236].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ga. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, we find there are some changes for the better in this body.  While we have had some conditions that have arisen since we had this here before, that are out of the ordinary for the body, yet the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] show the improvement in the conditions, especially those that we have given, that which would bring about the correction for, in this physical force of the body.

2. Now, when these properties as have been given [in 236-1] in the entirety have been completed, we would rest from same five (5) days.  Then have this prepared and begin and take another full quantity - beginning, however, AT ONCE, with the use of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]].  With the body lying prone (on face), just before retiring, follow out the whole cerebro-spinal nervous system, beginning at the base of the brain, going slowly to the full length of the spine, retracing slowly in the same manner and way, using the bulb - the larger bulb. After this has been gone over four, five, to six times, slowly, each center, then follow out the line of the brachial plexus, along arms, see?  Then down to the solar plexus, and this rather heavy.  Then from that of the sciatic nerves, or the lower plexus - locomotary plexus, down each limb, see? under side, or with the body lying prone - just this portion.

3. We find this, kept up each evening for at least thirty (30) days, will show the difference in the blood and in the nerve reaction, ridding the system of nerve headaches, assisting in digestion and assimilation, and stimulating all of the circulatory system to a near normal property.

4. After the five days rest from the medicinal properties, begin and take the full quantity again.  We will see, then, that the conditions will have begun to disappear where they show in the cuticle, for this will have given sufficient time for the blotches or spots to have had their full cycle of development, and the repairing within the system, from the centers from which emanate the rebuilding forces in the body, will have builded better all these conditions.  Follow these out, systematically, consistently, and persistently, if we would rid the system of the condition.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What causes the pain in the back of the neck, between the shoulders?
(A)  The incoordination of the brachial plexus centers. Hence this condition as has been given here - to be treated with these conditions, see?  nerve disturbance and circulatory system.

Best that this be given, of course, in the home, when the body is ready for retiring, see?  Hence obtain the [[[Violet Ray|violet ray]] hand or high frequency] machine and use self, for self, see?  or by one in home.

6. That is all the questions.

7. We are through with this for the present.
