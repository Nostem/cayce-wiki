# TEXT OF READING 758-15 M 8

**Reading [[758-15]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1930  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 4632

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Boyd Davis and Bryant Speed.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [758].  Very good are the changes as are gradually taking place, and that being builded in the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, the correlations as are being created properly throughout the system, and their activities with the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]], are good.  We would not change particularly from the conditions as being applied.  Be consistent with all that are applied.  Do not be lax in the application, whether mental, physical, or whether those of the purely mechanical - these all have their place, their time, in the general and whole developing forces for the body.  Ready for questions.

2. No questions.

3. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Parents
