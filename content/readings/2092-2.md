# TEXT OF READING 2092-2 F 72

**Reading [[2092-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/24/1932  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 8735

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 24th day of September, 1932, in accordance with request made by her foster-son, [53], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2092].  While we find some improvements in the general conditions of the body from that as we had here before, there has not been the response wholly as there should have been.  This, as we find, in GENERAL, is the lack of the wholehearted cooperation in the application of those measures given for the improvements.

2. We would follow out more closely those suggestions that have been given, and add with same a stimuli for the digestive forces and also use at times - about once or twice a week - the mud baths, or the mud massage along the spine, over those centers that radiate to those affected portions in the limbs. This, of course, should be the kind of mud that is prepared for such, see?

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What should be taken to make bowels regular?
(A)  This, as we find, would require rather these measures:

One day, beginning first, we would take two tablespoonsful of Milk of Magnesia.

The next evening, or day, take two teaspoonsful of Milk of Bismuth.

The next day we would take a whole bottle of Fletcher's Castoria, taken half a teaspoonful at a time about every thirty minutes, see?

The next day we would rest.

The next day use a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]], with at least a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to every PINT of warm water used, see?
Then rest a day after the enema.

Then begin and go through the whole routine.

These will make for regulations, and will add to the strength of the system without reducing the body by straining, and with the various properties will supply necessary elements for bringing about the absorption of poisons in system by the toxic forces and inactivities of the body; yet making the eliminations so as to be helpful, as well as strengthening the peristaltic movements of the system.

5. (Q)  In what manner or way, or what part of last reading was not carried out satisfactorily?
(A)  Those applications for the relieving of congestions in the cerebrospinal or general system.

6. (Q)  How long before body will be normal again?
(A)  After these have been carried out systematically, for at least three to four weeks, we should be almost normal again.

7. (Q)  What stimuli for the digestive forces?
(A)  Haven't we just outlined what should be done for that? Take these, and it will be some stimuli!

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [53]
