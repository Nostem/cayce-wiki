# TEXT OF READING 3768-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[3768-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/9/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11303

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 9th day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  We have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find, the conditions a great deal different, at present time from that we had before.  The condition is improved throughout the system, those effects as produced to the nerve centers as govern the distribution and assist in the blood flow in the assimilation, are still strengthening. Those along the cardiac region, or those that govern the nerve or muscle about the cardiac end of the stomach, as affecting the conditions through the digestive tract itself, the whole condition through the alimentary canal is improved.

2. Those properties necessary to heal and relieve the lacerations:  Keep for the time being, and to prevent recurrence in the system - and there must not be taken into the system those properties that will tend to make a fermentation begin, or to break the tissue again.  Follow the lines as given here before, but gradually diminishing the amount and the stimulus and the adjustments from time to time, see.  Make all farther and farther away, and let the system rebuild from its own incentives as is being created in the system, see.  This will not return.

3. The parts remain weak, as yet, and must be kept up to the same recuperative forces.

4. The water for the condition, the stimulus for the assimilation, the [[Vibration|vibration]] or [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and adjustments for the stimulus for the muscular forces and nerve tissue to keep place for the condition that has been and is being in the body.  Do that.

5. (Q)  How much longer should she take treatment?
(A)  As we have given.  Skip a time for the medicinal properties and adjustments.  Then after two weeks skip twice, until the condition is entirely overcome, which should be in two to three moons, now.
