# TEXT OF READING 4623-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4623-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/18/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12535

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 18th day of October, 1932, in accordance with request made by Dr. A. J. Foerter, Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, H. L. and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., Illinois. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body and those conditions that surround and disturb the body, Mrs. [4623].

2. Now, as we find, conditions in many manners show improvements.  There is less tendency for the body to lapse into periods of unresponsive activity; less of those reactions that make for creating of temperature of the nature that destroys tissue and resistance; the assimilations for the body are better.

3. The properties for the releasing of the oxygen, that assists in making for better resistance in the physical sense from the application of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, with the elements that are responsive to same in the system that break down those forces that are destructive and give life or active forces to principles within the system, these are of the most material benefit at the present time.

4. We would, with the applications being already administered, apply the magnetic forces also for the body.  Sitting on the left side of body, place the right hand at the brachial plexus center, and the left hand on the centers on body through which will make for the currents of the forces from body to body pass from the brachial center to the centers through which the inflexes from the lacteal or assimilating [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] function.  These currents will run in such application through the active forces of the lymph circulation.  This will bring quietness and strength to the body, through such administrations.  These we would give for at least twenty to thirty minutes at least every other day.  We would also give with such administrations those suggestions that will act with the power or the force of suggestion in the [[Mental Body|mental body]], as this:

5. THERE IS BEING CARRIED THROUGH THE LIFE STREAM (OR BLOOD) THAT THAT WILL ENABLE THE BODY TO BECOME RESPONSIVE TO THE LIFE GIVING FORCES IN MY BODY, TO MAKE FOR RESUSCITATIONS AND ENLIVENING OF ALL PORTIONS THAT HAVE HAD DISORDERS OR DISRUPTION FROM THEIR PROPER FUNCTIONING.

6. Let the body repeat this, and feel in self that consciousness that this is BEING conducted through those applications.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What spiritual and mental help is to be rendered here, and how?
(A)  In the manner that has been outlined, this gives that mental aptitude and attitude for the body, so that all the principles and forces that are being applied will coordinate more properly in the system.

In the spiritual aspects, well that there be given those suggestions in the conversation, that there is that life force through the spiritual elements of the body from within and from without making for cooperation and coordination in the material and [[Physical Body|physical body]].

9. (Q)  May we have light on the causative factors from a mental and physical viewpoint?
(A)  This basis, as indicated from the source that the vital force of energy is to be expended in the body, comes from those destructive forces that produced the first reaction to the circulation, as related to that assimilated in the body - see?  With the inability of those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to use that which WAS assimilated, there were brought those destructive forces to that portion of the system as exercised through the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about assimilation, or the lacteals, with those of the secondary cardiac, and those elements in the pulmonaries that clarify and give the proper resuscitating forces to the blood supply itself; hence, with the proper expansion of the system through the change as created in the effect of carbon deoxidized, loosened within the system in life forces by [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]], there is released in the energies of the system those forces that act directly to the active forces of the blood stream.

10. That is all the questions regarding this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Foerter

> practically an invalid who requires constant administration
> by nurses and attendants.  Her body is contracted and almost
> doubled up forwards from a deforming type of rheumatism.
> Both hands are subject to a tremor; and walking is a very
> arduous task for her,  She has, however, a wonderfully sweet
> smile, and is always glad to express words indicating her
