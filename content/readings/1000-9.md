# TEXT OF READING 1000-9 F 41

**Reading [[1000-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/9/1931  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 6025

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of February, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - through her sister and brother-in-law, Mrs. [1192] and Mr. [417].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1192] and Mr. [417], Lillian Barclay and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1000] - this we have had before.  In many respects we find the conditions are continually, or gradually, on the improve.  There are, however, some conditions that bring disorders at the present time, due considerably to the character of, or the manner in which the manipulations have been given; for when these are for corrective measures without keeping the drainages proper in the system - and with a tendency for accumulation of drosses in a body (as in this), and with specific conditions arising through the mental attitudes builded by body also - then we have, as seen especially in the upper dorsal and the lower cervical regions, such conditions that make for - with the central nervous system, especially to the face, to the head - accumulations that make for effects created, especially in the present condition in the nasal cavity, as is seen by the abrasions or the breaking out on neck, head, face; the tendency of accumulations in throat, with prickly sensations in tonsil or in the regions thereabouts; tendency for accumulations of water or drosses from accumulations in the nasal cavities.

2. In the keeping of drainages, were the proper manipulations given these conditions would NOT have occurred.  These have made for an infectious force as respecting these conditions, and while to meet the needs of the conditions we must consider the local condition as well as that producing it, the proper manipulations being given in some portions of system, the drainages in the upper portion - through the dorsal, upper dorsal and cervical - should be so that these pressures will be removed.

3. Then, apply locally - in nostril - for that tendency of soreness - the Yellow Oxide Ointment, small quantities.

4. We would use also an inhalant that would be made in this manner.  This may be used in a spray, as well as inhaled in throat AND lungs.  Make in this manner.  Then, when ready to be used as a spray, add distilled water equal the amount that is put in spray:

Grain alcohol 85%...............4 ounces,
Eucalyptol.....................20 minims,
Benzosol.......................15 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...........5 minims,
Tincture of Benzoin.............5 minims,
Tolu in solution...............30 minims.

For the spray, take at least a teaspoonful and add a teaspoonful or two teaspoonsful - of distilled water; use in spray in throat and nostrils.

5. In the cleansing of the mouth, the teeth, and those conditions that have distorted the mental forces in body - use the Ipsab, WITH the RUB; that is, cleanse with the finger.  They will need the use of this only occasionally, or the rinsing of the mouth with this.

6. Keep these up - that is, the general treatments over the system for the other conditions, and for this local condition that has arisen; setting up the drainages better.  We will bring about normal forces.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is the submaxiliary [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] affected?
(A)  The nature of the condition in face, naturally, produces some inflammation to the submaxiliary - but with the correction of the pressures in the dorsal and cervical region, that removes pressure - with the application of local conditions, these will clarify themselves.

8. (Q)  What causes gnawing pains in lower left jaw bone at times?  and how can this be helped?
(A)  Remove these pressures that exist, setting up drainages from the whole system - especially to this portion.  The body has been fearful for the applications for conditions above the diaphragm, or through the head and neck, that would make for drainages in system - but if these are kept in accord we will find these conditions will be alleviated.

9. (Q)  Are tonsils affected?
(A)  No; only sympathetically.

10. (Q)  Are teeth in good condition?
(A)  As given, there should be the cleansing of these with the properties given, DONE by SELF - at the present.  This, as we find, will create a much better condition in the mouth and for the teeth.

11. (Q)  When can body have bridge made for lower left jaw?
(A)  In the spring or early summer.

12. (Q)  As this part of body was seat of much trouble, who will be the proper man to go to when the time comes?
(A)  One that would be rather in sympathy with the outline of suggestions that have been given as respecting the body through these sources or channels for information.  Do THAT.

13. (Q)  Is Dr. Daly giving [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments correctly?
(A)  If he had been we wouldn't have said changes!

14. (Q)  Will the mentalphysics work be any help to the body?
(A)  Naturally.

15. (Q)  Is change of climate necessary?
(A)  Not absolutely necessary, though changes would be beneficial to the body at times.  ANY change to the body is beneficial at times.

16. (Q)  Why does body become exhausted so often?
(A)  Poisons and accumulations from lack of drainages in the system.  Nerve exhaustion.

17. That is all the questions regarding this body.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office (2 copies)
"    " Mrs. [1192]
