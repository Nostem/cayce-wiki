## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. GD's note:  In approximately 1921 a reading was listed under
> [5771] which was actually a reading for his wife [5421].  Her
> given name was never used and evidently the stenographer
> mistakenly typed just the initials into the reading rather
> than preceding the name by "Mrs." as it appears everywhere
> else in subsequent readings.  The gist of the reading
> bears out the fact that it was the wife's check-up rather
> than Mr. [5771]'s.  However, it is possible that Mr. [5771]
> did have personal readings but we have no copies of them in
> the [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] files.  See 5421-1, the reading which was put on
> the film for preservation as that of Mr. [5771]'s.  Mr.
> [5771] in subsequent years requested six other readings for
> his wife [5421]* and never one for himself.  He could not
> have been so ill as mentioned in this reading [5421-1] and
> not have referred to his condition subsequently.  Everything
> points to the fact that it was his wife's reading and not
> his.  See his subsequent correspondence typed into his wife
> [5421]'s case.
> 
> 
> 
> * [Correction:  On examining his correspondence it was found
> that the sixth reading requested was for his second wife
> [5775] and not for Mrs. [5421].  Apparently Mrs. [5421] died
> after he obtained for her 5421-6 on 6/1/26, and he requested
> the reading for his next wife, Mrs. [5775] on 4/2/30.  Since
> this reading [5775-1] refers to previous readings it was
> assumed at first that she was the same person, and this
> second wife of Mr. [5771] has this reading listed on the film
> for preservation as 5421-7 instead of 5775-1 as it should be,
> because they are two different people.  The mix-up occurred
> because in both cases the ladies were referred to [as] Mrs.
> [husband's initials] instead of by their first names.]
