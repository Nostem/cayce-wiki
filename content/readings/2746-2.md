# TEXT OF READING 2746-2 M 31 (Naval Officer)

**Reading [[2746-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/11/1943  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 9883

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [2746], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2746], Mrs. [2454], Mrs. [3407] and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [2746] present in this room, together with the [[Physical Body|physical body]], his present and future conditions pertaining to his work, and changes of stations, environment and home conditions, mental and spiritual growth.  You will advise and counsel [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in regard to these for future activities, and answer the questions pertaining to his Life Rdg. given May 8, 1942, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [2746] - this, as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]], we have had before.

3. In analyzing conditions in the present, and those that portend from present activities, there are many problems to be considered.  The [[Ideal|ideals]] and [[Purpose|purposes]], to be sure, come first.  These, as indicated from [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s activity in the earth, have been good.  There have been periods when there has been confusion, or stumbling, and yet ever with [[Ideal|ideals]] as toward the light.

4. The entity was an [[Atlantis|Atlantean]], as indicated.  This experience was indicated because there had been the opportunity in self, in which there was the use of spiritual [[Ideal|ideals]] for self-indulgence.  Hence such entities, in each period of activity in the material plane, will either make reparations or - rather should it be said - turn to [[Law of Moses|the law]] of [[Grace|grace]] instead of [[Law of Moses|the law]] of [[Karma|karma]], and will come out of the experience as a wonderful success or a miserable failure. They are extremes, just as the entity itself has found in the present.  There are periods when there is the decision that something is desirable and you don't stop until you have it! These are extremes.  Time and space and patience are most needed oft, that few [[Soul|souls]] or individuals are willing to pay the price for - until they grow to be such, you see.

5. Just as has so oft been indicated, one doesn't fall out of a tree into heaven, or an airplane, or fly into heaven, but one grows in [[Grace|grace]], in knowledge, in understanding, in perfecting within self those applications of tenets and truths that bring to the activities the spiritual, the mental growth.

6. Then the physical results are in keeping with the [[Purpose|purposes]]. For it is in Him, the Giver, that we live and move and have our being.  Individuals realize, or must realize, there is little they may do save help others to help themselves.  In so doing they add to their own stature in spirit, in mind, in body-[[Purpose|purposes]].

7. So this entity in the various experiences in the earth has had those periods of constant unfoldment.

8. In [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience through those periods of turmoil the entity gained, as related to the manner in which individuals of various groups, in various portions of those activities in many lands, needed to be helped.

9. So in the present experience, the entity is passing through those periods that may be represented (figuratively, to be sure) by those periods of turmoil in that land.

10. Hence in making practical application, while all those activities need to be used, it should be for the greater unfoldment for self, for home, for those dependent upon same - when the changes have come about and again peace is found in self and in the earth.  Then row the waters no more. Rather gather from them, from the associations, those activities in keeping with the influences which prompted thee in thy activity as may be reflected from [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience; and aid in building that which in body, in mind, in purpose, may bring to many others an awareness of their relationship also.  Though they may grovel in the soil, though they may count over the money changers, though they may have their various forms of activities, let each be made aware of the fact that all must be messengers of the hope, the faith that is needed in Him for the salvation of the earth.

11. For, as He has given, He is ever at the door of the heart, of the mind.  Open and He will enter.

12. Be ever aware of those ideals and purposes that He, the Savior, may be the prompting influence.  If He be on thy side, what have thee to fear?

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  In view of the uncertainty of existing conditions, did I act wisely in establishing my home in [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]]?
(A)  It's a mighty good place, and a safe place when turmoils are to arise; though it may appear that it may be in the line of those areas to rise, while many a higher land will sink. This is a good area to stick to.

15. (Q)  Where should I buy a home?
(A)  Anywhere in the area of what may be called the Tidewater section.

16. (Q)  Is it indicated as to the time when I can expect my next change in duty, and where?
(A)  This as we find would come rather in the regular channels, that any specific period set in the present.  These activities are running in their regular order in the present. The change would be about six months from now.  But at the completion of the present unpleasantness (called by some; disturbance, called by others; horrors, called by others), go on dry land and stay there!

17. (Q)  At completion of my yard period, where is it indicated that my field of operations will be?
(A)  That depends upon the choices made.

18. (Q)  How can I find interest in the subjects I am required to study?
(A)  Make the practical application of any subject - this is the only manner in which interest may be acquired.  Unless the studies are used, they become abuses - in whatever direction they may be.  If one even attempts to study spiritual truths and makes no application of same, they become millstones about the neck!  If one studies navigation and navigates none, it will mean little.  He may have knowledge, but knowledge in such becomes as that of knowing good and evil.

19. (Q)  How may I better fulfil my duties to myself and country?
(A)  As indicated.  When present disturbances have ceased and the change may be made with all impunity to self and country, get out of the sea service.

20. (Q)  Clarify admonition in Life Rdg. that great stress exists for me in the month of May?  How and in what direction is this indicated?
(A)  Those are the periods when there will be the greatest stress in the present activities, as well as anxieties in other directions.  Those are the natural periods through which the entity experiences external hardships, mental disturbances, and when spiritual discernment is needed.

21. (Q)  What causes me to grit my teeth when sleeping?
(A)  These activities are from purely physical causes, coming from the condition in the digestive system.  Take occasionally those properties that will cause better assimilation, such as the combination of Willow Charcoal with Honey; and a few drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin after the heavier meal of the day - and this effect will disappear.

22. (Q)  How may I overcome what appear to be statical spells at times?
(A)  These are only the steadiness in self needing to be grounded well in the faith that is the motivating force of every individual entity.  Static spells are those in which there is the reasoning with the mental and spiritual ideals of self.  Listen for the voice within.

23. (Q)  How may I gain more initiative?
(A)  By depending more on Him and less on self.  This is not contradictory, though it sounds so until one puts it to the test.  Walk with Him often and He will ever give thee initiative.

24. (Q)  Should I seek a permanent commission in the Navy or not?
(A)  And stay on dry land?

25. (Q)  What work should I follow when the war is ended?
(A)  As indicated, that type or class of work in which the entity was engaged in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience.  The entity has learned much of the needs of peoples of many lands. There will be the needs for the understanding in many lands of what the American SPIRITUAL ideals are!  You can carry such an understanding to them in ways that few people may! As to whether ye yourself work in the soil, or attend to those activities about a yard, or whether in the home and the preparation of those things for others to attain in various lands, it matters not - but let the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] direct!

26. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
