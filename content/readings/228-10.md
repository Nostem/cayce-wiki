# TEXT OF READING 228-10 M 49 (Accountant, Protestant)

**Reading [[228-10]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/9/1937  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 1064

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of October, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [228], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Fla. 
(Body and enquiring mind, who seeks information, advice and 
guidance as to his life and work.  Ques.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [228]; this we have had before.

2. In considering the life's work, the activities, there should be kept ever in the activities that which will be the more constructive in the application of the [[Ideal|ideals]] in a spiritual manner.  These must be ever the basis for the activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], if there would come the greater opportunities for the material and the social, the mental and spiritual developments.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  [...] Public Accountant, who is now at Chipola Hotel, Marianna, offers me monthly salary of $225 and wants answer Monday.  What do you advise?
(A)  As we find, when all conditions are considered, this would be more in keeping with a CONSISTENT development and consistent advancement in the activities and from the abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

The State associations are desirable, and in a manner keep [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in contact and in touch with much that is desired from every angle; yet these depend so much upon the movements of political factions that the associations with [...] as we find would be more in keeping with the stabilizing of self in those activities.

While there will be in the new associations those things that are not altogether at times agreeable, we find that the very abilities of the entity [228] will be able to advance and to give those counsels and those activities that would make for the greater and better development.

Also we find that the environs after a bit; that is, after a few months - will be more satisfactory.

We would accept the offer.

5. (Q)  Any further advice for the body at this time?
(A)  As has been indicated.  Keep constructive; that is, let the promptings of the activities be from the desire to manifest those things that are the fruits of the Spirit.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

WIRED - INFORMATION AIR MAIL SUGGESTS THAT [...] OFFER BE ACCEPTED
