# TEXT OF READING 3825-4

**Reading [[3825-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/12/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11435

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195].]

Twp. 15, 
Time of Reading                        Range 117 West, 
11:30 A. M.                            SECTION 8, 
Uinta County, Wyoming.

1. LS:  Now, you have before you the lands located in Uinta County, Wyoming, Township 15, Range 117 West, SECTION 8.  You will give the conditions of this land for the [[Purpose|purpose]] of drilling an oil well, giving the best location, the water levels, the depth of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] and the amount of production to be obtained.

2. EC:  In this, we find we have had these before also.  In this, we find both levels productive.  The Southwest quarter of the Southwest half being the better place for the deep well, and the lands there show the production would be of the greater value in the deeper sands, reaching there at 1735 to 2200 feet.  In this the upper strata of oil would be very nominal, while the deeper would be very strong.  In this we find the rolling land, and the upper or ridge in the Southwest edge being the better place.  In this we will find two water stratas 89 - 1208.  These we find will not be hard to handle by casing.  The production here will reach 7 to 750 - 7,000 to 7,500 barrels of oil per day.  Gravity 38.
