## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 10/14/24 Miss [9]'s typed letter, even the signature being
> typed:  "Words cannot express my appreciation of your visit
> to my home with your friends.  I can never thank you enough
> for taking a reading for me while I was present and
> convincing me so strongly of your work.  No doubt, you could
> see by my manner how happy your presence made me.  As I
> witnessed your reading a heavy load was lifted from my mind.
> Never in my life did I have such an enjoyable evening.  My
> only regrets were that the evening passed so very swiftly.
> 
> "My trust in God is greater now, than ever.  As I talked
> with your friends and listened to the reading the truth of
> your work dawned on my mind very clearly.  My belief in God
> can never be shaken for helping me to understand your work
> in every way.  My mental [[Visions|vision]] God made as clear as
> crystal.  With all my afflictions I have much to thank God
> for.  I am sorry that I cannot see you now and again, so
> that we could discuss mind.  I would consider it a great
> privilege to hear your opinions on this subject.  I am,
> however, very glad that I had the opportunity of meeting
> you.  I wonder if our meeting was, really, due to my
> concentration of mind.  I am interested in the clubs that
> are being started to carry on your work about which
> Mr. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]] spoke to me.  Please write me if you would
> care to have one of your clubs in Paterson."
> 
> Thanking you for your visit, I am Very truly yours [9].
> 
> R2. 10/22/24 EC's letter:  "Have yours of the 14th and was indeed
> very glad to hear from you and to know that you really
> felt that the few minutes visit I had with you in Paterson
> were worth while.  The enthusiasm, or over-love to talk,
> whichever it may be called, of Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] and Mr. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]]
> both was such that I did not have an opportunity to talk
> with you very much, nor half express to you the real interest
> that I feel in trying to be of assistance to you.  Not only
> for the love and friendship that I have for [8] but seeing
> you, and realizing what a wonderful fight you have put up
> against seemingly insurmountable difficulties, I desire to be
> of help if possible.  Not, please understand, because I claim
> some power within myself, for KNOW I do not lay claim to any
> such power or force, merely the experience of hundreds of
> people who claim they have received untold benefits through
> these readings, when the suggestions as given are carried out
> fully, wholly, and entirely.  I'm in hopes that your hearing
> the reading will really make it the more possible for you to
> make personal application of the good that may be acquired
> through the readings.
> 
> "Now, I would certainly insist that the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] carry out
> these suggestions as were given.  If he will not, I'm sure
> there are two at least in [[New York City|New York]] that WILL carry them
> out."
> 
> R3. 10/28/24 MHB's letter:  "I called up Miss [9] and found she
> had not received her reading and that Dave [[[David Kahn|Kahn]]] had wrongly
> addressed same.  She has it by now."
> 
> R4. 1/21/26 EC's letter to her brother [8]:  "MHB went with me
> to see your sister; in fact he conducted the reading 9-6."
> 
> R5. 1/25/26 See 9-7.
