# TEXT OF READING 2187-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2187-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/6/1940  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8895

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 6th day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self through friend, Miss [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her sister, Mrs. [1152] and N.Y. meeting of A.R.E. which Miss [...] attended.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mass. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now, as we find, conditions-physical have grown so serious - from the forces that are as growths, and the lacerations in the stomach area - that they have caused the scientific or medical world to say that little or nothing may be done, - from the SCIENTIFIC manner.

3. Thus, - we find that there may be MUCH accomplished, if there will be the use of [[Prayer|prayer]] and the electromagnetic forces, or the hand electrical forces from one whom this body has served, - that is:

4. Have one of those who has been aided or helped by the activities of this body (young would be better, to be sure, but sufficiently old to know the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] of such), to sit by the body and give the constant hand magnetic treatment, in this manner:

5. Place the left hand under that area almost between the shoulders, and the right hand over the area where the pain and spasmodic reaction occurs at times.  This would be direct to the skin, of course.  Keep this position for twenty to thirty minutes at a time, then rest for a period of not more than thirty to fifty minutes, and begin again.  Keep this up constantly.  Of course, the one who makes the application may be changed from time to time, - just so it is one who has been aided by the body and is not too old a person, but one sufficiently minded to know the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] of such.

6. Let all whom the body has aided make supplication to the divine within each, and to those influences of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness, for [[Healing|healing]].

7. When there are those paroxysms of vomiting, give a teaspoonful of this combination in a tablespoonful of water, but let it be sipped slowly, - prepare same in this way and manner:

8. To 1 ounce of Lime Water as the base, add - in the order named:

Cinnamon Water.......................1 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium....10 drops,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium...10 drops.

Be sure to add the Cinnamon Water TO the Lime Water, and then the other ingredients in the order named.

9. As to the foods, - those that have been a part of the diet. Of course, the greater portion of the diet should be liquid.

10. Keep up the magnetic applications and the supplication constantly, - by all those whom the body has aided.

11. Do that.  These as we find offer the hope for helpful experience to the body, not only in the physical but in the mental reactions also.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [...], Air Mail Special
"    " Ass'n file

> min. following half an hr. of prayerful hands on her?
> 
> "Very gratefully yours for the message 'MUCH may be
> accomplished' - so be it."
> 
> R2. 5/9/40 EC wrote:  "The magnetic tr. can be made by anyone
> close to her.  Some have a great deal more magnetism than
> others, but naturally one for whom she has given much would
> exert more help for her.  No, it would not be kept up
> continuously, but would be for 30-50 mins. at a time several
> times a day or night.  From the experiences I have seen with
> others where such was suggested, I feel sure she will find
> much ease in the tr."
> 
> R3. 5/15/40 Miss [...] wrote:  "Our dear neighbor is still with
> us though how she lingers is amazing, she is so weak.
> 
> "The spasms of pain in the abdomen cleared 5/13th, after a
> night when her husband & sister were up with her, as
> breathing was so difficult. The family dr. was called & gave
> something to ease the distress.
> 
> "Instructions from you reached us in the late p.m. of 5/14th,
> & it was nearly midnight before I could get in touch with the
> husband, for various reasons.  He was off on business when I
> was over there & later could not get away until very late.
> Naturally, he said it was worth trying.  Wed. a.m., early
> 5/15th, I was there, and as Mrs. [2187] was awake & perfectly
> conscious, I told her about it, then read the instructions to
> her & she was very much pleased with it all.  I left a copy
> with her for her husband & sister to consult, & gave her a
> tr., at once, leaving her quiet & at ease.  She says I have
> that effect on her, & she loves to have me slowly stroke her
> forehead, as she finds it soothing to feel the touch of my
> hand.
> 
> "A long time friend of the family, a young student minister
> taking graduate work at Cambridge, was there in the P.M. and
> was very pleased at the whole story & gave her several trs.
> 
> "The several friends who have dropped in have also been asked
> to pray for her before they left & the student has been there
> several times till he had to go to the Cape for his week-end
> preaching engagement.
> 
> "Two violent attacks of nausea have been very hard on her,
> though the first time the Cinnamon Water gave help.  The
> husband & the 18 yr. daughter also gave trs., and the hypos
> have been less frequent.
> 
> "Her father arrived this p.m. from the Provinces (the wire
> having failed to be rec'd) & that was a great happiness but
> was followed rather soon by a severe attack of nausea - which
> was hard on the old gentleman as well as the patient.  I feel
> we have helped a goodly lot & am grateful."
> 
> R4. 5/19/40 Miss [...] wrote:  "Our dear, brave friend sank into
> a coma the last hr. here, and quietly 'crossed the bar', Fri.
> night 5/17th.  Hers was a gallant fight indeed, in every way
> - while the family feel we did a real service for her."
> Gratefully yours, [...]
> 
> R5. 5/24/40 EC wrote Miss [...]:  "We can only say 'His will be
