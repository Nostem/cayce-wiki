# TEXT OF READING 75-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[75-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/7/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 177

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. W. J. Twigg, Smoke Run, Pa., this 5th day of July, 1923 (?).

[Edgar Cayce; DEK, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                          ..., Pa. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Now we find the abnormal condition in this body are shown from effects of conditions that existed in the body in times back, and the condition that now exist becomes the result rather than conditions to be treated to remove the trouble now existing in the body, that is, the conditions as have been treated have been for existing conditions and not for the seat of the troubles, incoordination being the principal condition to be met with at the present time.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [75], now we are speaking of, ..., Pa.  First, in the circulation we find the blood supply of the seat of the blood supply does not give the incentive to the system as necessary to replenish all of the forces in the body to produce coordination throughout the body on account of the nerve and muscular forces that have become impinioned or misdirected in [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which they should properly flow, see, the functioning of the organs producing the blood supply force, the condition, of a very low rebuilding force in the blood.

3. In the nerve system, we find that many of the centers, especially about those of the ilium plexus, have become so distraught in their connection as to produce the incoordinating forces in this portion of the body, in the effect is to keep the action of all locomotory nerves in the distraught condition - this being produced first by lack of proper elimination in the system and the effect was to the kidneys and the whole hepatic circulation.  In the functioning of the nerves of the sensory organism:  This has produced the inability of the body to bring perfect coordination through any of the organs of this system and the effect to the sympathetic is to bring the whole locomotory system, the effect to the clarification of all blood forces, and all emunctory action to a very low state and only the incentive as given by the mind force direct any of the action of the body to keep the rebuilding force.  The body has been very weak, some better just at present, but in the very low replenishing forces for the system as yet.

4. In the functioning of the organs themselves, we find all under the action of the forces through the sympathetic reaction, in the body, see?

5. To give the [[Vibration|vibration]] to the system necessary to produce that vitality necessary to produce reaction to overcome these conditions would be to give that of the electric forces to the seventh dorsal vertebra know as Abrams' force or mode of eliminations and assimilation in the body.  Do that.

6. (Q)  Now Mr. Cayce, how did this trouble begin?
(A)  Just as we have given here, first produced by toxins that produced the overtaxation of the body, then this began.

7. (Q)  In giving treatment for this body would you advise to give medicines, or what medicine would you advise to give?
(A)  None, only of the electronic forces as we have given, and these will be at the seventh dorsal vertebra.

8. (Q)  Electronic forces, what do you mean by that?
(A)  Those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from electronic forces produced by Abrams' treatment.*

9. (Q)  Do you mean Dr. Abrams who is now in [[California|California]]?
(A)  Dr. Abrams now in [[California|California]].

10. (Q)  Do you know of one near Phillipsburg who would give this treatment?
(A)  We will find them, there are many different places, we will find.  These we do not find here at present at Phillipsburg, that are using Abrams' treatment.

11. (Q)  Will this relieve this body?
(A)  Give this electronic [[Vibration|vibration]] to this body, [75], if we would give any relief from this condition.  Donovan-Altoona and Denny, Plattsburg.

12. (Q)  Now Mr. Cayce, this body has been receiving some sort of electrical treatment, shall that be continued?
(A)  The electronic forces only for this body that are applied through the mode of the Abram forces and it will be found that the vibration for this body must be given at the seventh dorsal.  Do as we have given.

13. We are through.



* [Albert Abrams, M.D. Booklet entitled DR. ABRAMS AND THE ELECTRON THEORY BY William F. Hudgings was published by New Century Company, 55 Liberty Street, [[New York City|New York]], 1923, with introduction by the author dated June, 1923.  Dr. Abrams was then said to be sixty years old.
