# TEXT OF READING 136-65 F 22 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[136-65]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/6/1927  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 372

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of June, 1927 in accordance with request made by self - [136].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[King David|David]] and Lucille [[David Kahn|Kahn]].]

Time of Reading 
5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the information in interpretation of [[Dreams|dream]] of [136] in reading given May 25, 1927, and you will answer questions regarding same that I will ask.

2. EC:  We have the information and those conditions as surround same, and, as has been given, would the body, [136], desire to recover - intact - that as has been removed from the household, or the pin which is lost, do not be hasty in those things that would scare - or cause those who have been in the charge, or who have purloined same - AWAY from the services. For, as has been given, the manner which has been held toward such is correct.  Rather would this injunction apply under the present circumstances and conditions:  WATCH, as well as pray.  Do THAT, and we will find this will be returned intact, and in PLACE.

3. Yes, [as though answering a question in the mind of the body], put it into the heart, the mind, of those about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that the fear of the LORD (not law) is the beginning of wisdom.

4. We are through.
