# TEXT OF READING 4302-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[4302-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/1/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12112

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of December 1923, in accordance with request made by himself - [4302].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M.                                       ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see.  There have been some little unusual happenings, you see, about here recently.

2. Now, we find the conditions in this body are greatly improved from those when we had this before.  There still in the blood forces those elements that assist in creating, or in allowing the creation of that bacilli that is destructive to the cuticle itself proper in that it separates itself, becoming those portions that cause irritation to parts of the body.

3. The suggestions as given for the relief have not been carried out in full, as given.  For the condition at the present, we would take those same medicinal properties again in the system, varying them only in the quantity as taken in the system.  That is, now we would only take teaspoonful four times each day, and as directed, just after the meals, or before retiring, and we would give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the body, and affected parts, of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] forces. Especially to the fifth cervical center and solar plexus center, as well as the affected parts.  Do that, [4302].
