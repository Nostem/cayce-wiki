# TEXT OF READING 325-22 F 56

**Reading [[325-22]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/24/1928  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2460

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1928, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, many of the conditions physically show some improvement from what HAVE existed in the body.  While the conditions are not wholly satisfactory, yet - if the body were able to adhere, mentally and physically, to many of the suggestions as has been given, and if the surroundings were made much after the fashion as has been outlined, BETTERED indeed would be the conditions and the responses of the body, mentally and physically, to those as may be administered for the betterment of the conditions.  The condition as exists, as has been given regarding the tissue centralizing [in the breast], has NOT gone farther.  While pain occurs from same occasionally, the massage of the portion about the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] involved with the Iodex will prove effective, helpful and beneficial to the condition.

2. That bitterness as occurs occasionally [in mouth] is nerve reaction through the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus, and is more nervousness than any SPECIFIC condition caused from improper retraction in body.

3. Pain in head, arm, and neck, shoulders [neuritis] - tiredness - will be MATERIALLY benefited, if the spine - from base of brain to at least the middle of the spine (or to the 10th, 11th dorsal) - would be massaged at least twice each week (or each second DAY would be better) with equal parts olive oil and tincture of myrrh.  Heat the oil and add the myrrh, only using sufficient for each treatment - tablespoonful of each would be sufficient.  Do this, and keep the surroundings much in the manner as has been outlined, and we will find still bettered conditions for this body.  We are through for the present.
