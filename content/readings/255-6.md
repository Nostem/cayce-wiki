# TEXT OF READING 255-6 F 48

**Reading [[255-6]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/23/1931  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1260

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of February, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [255].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P.M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., N.Y. 
(Physical, mental and spiritual - with questions)

1. EC:  Yes, we find the body here - well.

2. In many respects we find conditions are continuing on the improve.  While there are times when the body feels mostly irritated with self, rather than with its physical condition or its physical reactions, the spiritual attitudes of the body, the abilities of the body to give out much, makes for that of the replenishing that is NOT found in other directions.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How often will coccyx have to be treated to remain in place?
(A)  This will depend a great deal upon the activities of the body.  While those conditions are of long standing, and easily the muscular forces as pull in improper directions are strong, and weak in those that pull in or for the PROPER correction, these will require some time - but what is time, to having the proper corrections made?

4. (Q)  What is the advice from others, as to diet, that makes body "lopsided" as given in reading?
(A)  There has existed in the mind as respecting certain characters of diet, that was held for such a period until other things, other conditions, other meals or foods proved to be of detriment to the body, to the body's own undoing. That's why called lopsided!  An even well BALANCED condition throughout, NOT being too severe in ANY direction!  To say that ANY diet that adds proper forces to the body for blood, muscle and nerve building, is in error, is to cut self off - even as to say, "This I may do, this I may NOT do", see?

5. (Q)  As given in reading, what was strange about body's advent into this life?
(A)  The strangeness was in the manner, the how, the wherefore.  FEW, as was indicated, are as badly, or as sincerely, or as lovingly desired - and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the ENTITY]] may be said to have taken ADVANTAGE of this.  Hence the expression.

6. (Q)  What [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] or table in music room shall body sell to help hospital?
(A)  That had BEST be determined in self, when those conditions arise that the body desires to aid at THAT PARTICULAR TIME!  for with desire (we get all phases of desire) there arises a channel through which that desire MAY operate.  See?  Little happens JUST by chance - though there are accidents, even in creation.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Dept
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
