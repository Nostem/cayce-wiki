# TEXT OF READING 532-1 F 51

**Reading [[532-1]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/12/1934  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 3707

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 12th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by herself - Mrs. [532], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [391].

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [532] and daughter.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [532].

2. As we find, there are conditions that disturb the better physical functioning of this body.  These, as we find, have to do with conditions that have gradually been brought on by impoverishment to portions of the system through the lack of elements necessary to keep an even balance.  And these have affected, then, those portions of the system and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that are impoverished by the lack of these elements in the body.

3. These, then, are the conditions as we find then with this body, [532] we are speaking of, present in this room:

4. First, as to the BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this has become impoverished by the lack of those necessary forces being assimilated by disturbances in the body as to produce a perfect coordination or balance in the red blood, the white blood, and the urea or lymph that makes for proper coagulation.

5. These conditions, as may be indicated from the character of the disturbances, are effects; and the causes are from other sources.  Yet the effect of such disturbance produces, as we find, in the nervous system and in certain organs - as we shall see in the body - the lack of activity.

6. Hence we have in the NERVE REACTIONS a nerve reaction that makes for incoordinations, and the inability for the body to carry through those impulses in the voluntary reactions to extremities, or to the body portion itself.

7. We also find that such impoverishment in the blood force, from the disturbances in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the stomach, in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], in the adrenal, produces softening, unsteadiness, unstableness in the muscular reactions and the nerve forces of the body.

8. As to the ORGANS THEMSELVES - Brain forces and their reflexes are very good.

9. The organs of the sensory system show their responses still, yet the lapsus in the reaction of those effects as PRODUCED by same shows the effect in the system.

10. In the glands about the thyroids we find the lack of those distributions through the system as to produce the proper functioning, or their effect upon all portions of the system that are affected by this incoordination, producing a katabolism and a metabolism that is abnormal for the conditions of the body.

11. Lung forces are very good save for the inability to produce proper coagulation through the activities of the respiratory system.

12. Hence we find the activities of the lymph and the emunctory are very low, and produce those effects in portions of the system that are disturbing.

13. In the digestive area, here we find again much of that in times back which has been the cause; or that which has produced same.  For, in a period some years previous, there were very strong medicinal properties given that destroyed an activity in the glands in the secretions of the system as to prevent the distribution of those forces that would keep an even balance; especially in those that prevent the ability of assimilation of elements that would be necessary to keep an even balance.  And this then caused or produced those conditions in the activity of the spleen, pancreas, and the upper hepatic circulation, that has gradually builded these disorders that exist in the body in the present.

14. As we would find, then, we may add that to this body which will materially aid in bringing a much nearer normal balance for the body.  And if there will be the persistence, and the consistent application of these forces and influences for the body, we will find there will be a ridding of these conditions, and a bringing about of a much nearer normal balance.

15. First we would begin taking into the system small quantities of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], that will make for a functioning of all the glands in the system; and supplying those elements in the digestive system that will open the ducts that will make for a better balancing in the blood supply.  In the beginning we would take three minims three times each day, in a half glass of water, just after each meal.

16. We would also begin immediately with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] that may be had in the Radio-Active Appliance to the extremities of the body, in the evening or - preferably when the body rests in the afternoons.  The first day attach one anode first to the right wrist and the other to the left ankle.  The next day attach the first anode to the left wrist and the other to the right ankle.  And so on, reversing each day.  Apply for a period of at least fifty to sixty minutes.  While there will not be felt any [[Vibration|vibration]] from these applications, there will be very soon a material change in the ability of the body to rest normally and to make for a better pulsation, a better reaction.

17. As for the diets, these have been outlined well for the body. These we would not alter or change.

18. At least twice each week we would have a general distribution of the forces through the activities of the muscles and nerves in the system, as from the NEUROPATHIC application - which follows the nerve forces throughout.  Or these may be taken each day, not so long a period of application. Or there may be taken the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation, that may be a GENERAL massage; not as a correction of the segments along the spine, but as to make for organic distribution of energies as the functioning organs are aided to not only create a balance in themselves but a coordination of the gland circulation and gland functioning through those properties as given.

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  An operation would not be necessary?
(A)  Not if these are followed; for, as indicated, the body itself is not in a condition to survive or stand an operation in the present.  If there are those applications made as we have indicated, we would find the drainages and the stimulations in the whole system would bring about much preferable conditions to any that may be had from other things - as we find.

21. (Q)  Any further advice for the body?
(A)  If these will be followed, as we find, we will bring to this body a much nearer balance in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.

Do that.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
