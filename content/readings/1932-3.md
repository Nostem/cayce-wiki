# TEXT OF READING 1932-3 M 31

**Reading [[1932-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/5/1934  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 8384

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Sun Air Farm, Oak Ridge, N.J., this 5th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the wife, Mrs. [778], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                  Box 187, 
6:10 to 6:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        Atlanta, Ga.

1. HLC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1932] who lives in ..., Ga., for whom information was given through these sources last Friday, Nov. 2, 1934.  As he has been threatened unjust dismissal from present [[Book of Job|job]], should he send letter of protest to General Manager of Company in New Jersey, exposing local office manager's threats and tactics?  Would such a letter help him retain his [[Book of Job|job]]?  You will give any advice or guidance that would be of help to him at his time.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions that confront the body in the present.

3. As we find, while these conditions assume rather alarming proportions in the beginning, there are those experiences and surroundings that do not bespeak of such drastic changes in the present.

4. Then, if there are the abilities of the body to adjust the conditions WITHOUT appealing to those higher up or in higher authority, then we would settle same.  But in the light of those things, should it seem or appear necessary, then be well for this to be done in decency and in order; not railing; not in a manner of detrimental outtake on anyone, but rather that which would be consistent with good judgements as respecting the general situation.

5. (Q)  Any other advice for his guidance at this time?
(A)  As we find, these are the conditions as regarding that being sought; remembering, as we mete it is measured to us again.

6. We are through.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file
