# TEXT OF READING 294-172 M 57

**Reading [[294-172]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/5/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2131

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. Edgar Cayce, Honorary Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before. General conditions in the physical force of the body are very good save from the effects of too much acidity in the system, producing toxic poisons in the whole general body.

2. Well that the eliminations be kept well balanced with the use of those forces that have been indicated, and by those activities of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that may be used over portions of the body to prevent the centralizations in portions of the system.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Just how and where should the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] be applied?
(A)  From the first and second cervical to the lower lumbar or coccyx, and then over the front portion of the body, around the clavicle and in those portions about the shoulders and neck and head.  Three to ten to fifteen minutes each evening would be well.

Every other day, of course, as indicated, take the small quantities of Milk of Bismuth with the Lactated Pepsin in same and water.  On the other days take the Milk of Magnesia, and on the other days - as indicated - take either the Phenolphthalein or the wafer tablets that carry same, with the properties that are of the Upjohn make, that reduce acid. [Upjohn's Citrocarbonate]

Don't overeat.

We are through with this reading.

5. That is all for the present.

6. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
