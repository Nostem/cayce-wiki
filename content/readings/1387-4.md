**Reading [[1387-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/1/1941  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 7106

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1387], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (After mumbling over the address - "Something wrong with the address."  Then, after quite a pause:)

3. We have those conditions and the desires, and the conditions surrounding same for this body, [1387].

4. As we find, there are much greater resistances in the body than we have had heretofore; though there continues to arise a great deal of distress from the lack of proper assimilation - now - in the body.

5. This is rather from an improper chemical balance in the body.

6. While there is a deficiency of the acids, this had best not be corrected with the taking of acids - UNLESS they be taken in certain ways or manners.

7. We would continue with the adjustments occasionally, and the white light treatments, - but these varied to meet those resistances AND weaknesses indicated as existent in the system.

8. At the present time we would take ACIGEST, about once or twice each week.  This would be taken ONLY in raw milk; a teaspoonful to a full glass of milk, preferably taken at the evening meal when taken.

9. Do not overtax the system with sub-acid foods, nor with too much [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] foods, nor with those carrying any [[Baking Soda|soda]].

10. Each day with preferably whichever is the heavier meal of the day, take half a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth in a full glass of water with six drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin stirred in same.

11. These properties as we find should reduce this sickness from the lack of digestion, and from the pressures produced through the alimentary canal causing sick headaches and poor elimination.

12. The lack of this balance has caused some irritation to the eliminations - and their channels - through the kidneys.

13. With the first one or two doses of the Acigest there may be caused some burning at the time of urination.  If so, then reduce the next dose to about half the quantity but keep on taking in the manner indicated.  Do this for at least the period until these channels have been clarified, or purified. But do not take the Acigest oftener than indicated, nor larger quantities; and do not take the acid in other forms than in the Acigest, see?  but take it only as we have indicated.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What produces, and what should be done for, pain in back?
(A)  As indicated, it is the strain upon the eliminating systems by this unbalancing in the body.  We would take the properties indicated, to produce a better balance; changing a little in the diets in such ways as indicated, - though don't overeat at any time, of course.

Do not take too much of sweets but a little of same.  Take those food values needed; don't have too much of the raw nor too much roughage, but a well balanced diet, see?

16. (Q)  What is the condition of the kidney?
(A)  As has been indicated.

Do these things as we have indicated here, if we would now make for better conditions with this body.

The body is better in many respects.  This unbalance causes anxiety.  With the chemical changes, the conditions should be corrected.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Miller) Copy to Ass'n file
