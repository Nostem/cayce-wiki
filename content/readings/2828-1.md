# TEXT OF READING 2828-1 M 40

**Reading [[2828-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/15/1942  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 10029

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2828], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1861].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2828].

3. Now, while we find that there are particular conditions in this body that prevent a better physical functioning, all influences and forces - in natures or conditions of this character - are to be taken into consideration.

4. So, if there would be brought about the more perfect physical condition, much of the sources of life, of health and of nature should be taken into consideration; and those expectancies, those dependences; the faith in the sources of life must be taken into consideration.

5. For, in part [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is meeting self, from those of its experiences in the earth.

6. Then, know - deep within self - deep within thy consciousnesses - WHO healeth thy diseases, WHO giveth thee opportunities.  And the trust and the hope, the understanding as may be gained by that analyzing of self from the careful study of the 30th of Deuteronomy, the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]], should be the life.  As we find, not as rote but as living, hoping, believing, knowing that as ye may ask - in His name - it may be done in the body.

7. Pathologically, we find there is the lack of energies in the body to supply those influences in the structural portion of the spine; there have become infections.

8. As we find, there may be added an oversupply of vitamins A and D, and also the necessary elements in the structural portions, or condensations.

9. Thus we would take internally the Vitamins A and D as compounded by the Miles Laboratories, or the One A Day brand of A and D, and take only one a day.

10. Also we would take [[Calcios|CALCIOS]].  This we would take three times each week, sufficient to cover a whole wheat wafer.  Skip a day between these, you see, - or take three times each week.

11. And each evening, or when the body takes the bath of evening, have gentle rub, - not severe but gentle, - along the whole length of the spine with Peanut Oil, - all the body will absorb along the spinal area.

12. Do these and be consistent.

13. Have activities in the open as much as practical, but not so severe as to strain the body - until there are improvements in same.

14. These as we find offer and will bring the better forces for this body, [2828].

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  How long has this condition been coming on?
(A)  For about ten years.

17. (Q)  Approximately how long will it be before I will improve?
(A)  How long before tomorrow, Mr. [2828]?  How long is the power of God in self?  How long does it take thee to do His biddings?  How long hast thou kept in the way beyond reproach of thine own self, as well as in the way of being a helpful influence in the experience of each one ye meet day by day?

Do as has been indicated, if we would bring the better forces for this body.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

([[Calcios|Calcios]] is manufactured by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., N.Y.  It is a syrupy paste that sells for $2.50 a jar.  According to the readings, it is the best way of assimilating free calcium.)
