# TEXT OF READING 2963-4 M 5

**Reading [[2963-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/8/1943  Sex: M  Age: 5  ReadingID: 10236

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [3339], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2963].

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is it satisfactory to give him the new medicine called Nucleice Acid in a powder form, in addition to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] pills which you approved?
(A)  Yes, this will be satisfactory if those precautions are kept, by those who have experimented with this preparation; and follow those suggestions rather closely.

5. (Q)  Is any change in treatments suggested or should they be continued the same?
(A)  We wouldn't change any of the treatments just yet.  More quietness is being shown.  While outwardly only at times does there appear to be a great deal of improvement, yet gradually there are improvements in the basic disturbances - or those that would cause disturbances through the body forces.

6. (Q)  Should we continue to mash his food, or just cut it?
(A)  Little by little let it be prepared by him as much as possible, or masticated.

Gradually handle those situations so that more and more [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds it must depend on itself.

7. (Q)  Any suggestions regarding the eyes, which cross occasionally?  Is there any weakness?  What suggestions about this?  Should we see an eye doctor?
(A)  If there were not weaknesses there, there would not be this slipping, as it were, in the coordination of the centers in the 3rd cervical, the 1st and 2nd cervical as well as in the 3rd and 4th dorsal.  There would not necessarily be needed an oculist.  If those regular treatments are kept as suggested, the daily massages etc., these should gradually build and correct themselves.

8. (Q)  What about his schooling?  Should we wait about this, or start him in public school, or should we try and find a private school for him?
(A)  We would give rather that the nurse should take care of this, at least until the next term - or the next year.

9. (Q)  Is it good for him to be around children?
(A)  We would have [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] or influence of others around the body just as normal as possible.

10. (Q)  What causes him to be frightened when near the family dog?  Should we dispose of the dog, or try to overcome his fear?
(A)  This is part of a [[Karma|karmic]] condition.  As we find it would be well to get rid of the dog, for this creates not only a very unfortunate environment for the body, but for the dog also - as to its usefulness.

11. (Q)  Is there any suggestion regarding the more nervous condition recently?
(A)  Just keep the massages and the other suggested treatments, and we will find this should respond.

12. (Q)  Should he be vaccinated now or wait until Spring?
(A)  Wait until about the 21st or 22nd of February.

13. (Q)  Please advise as to handling the child, such as guiding and teaching and helping him overcome his nervousness?
(A)  The nurse, guiding, may make suggestions to the body as it is resting or as it is going to rest.  These will help and maintain a better equilibrium in the whole general nervous system than all the medicinal properties that may be administered.

14. (Q)  Is it indicated that he will "snap out of himself" or will it be a long drawn out procedure?
(A)  Be a long drawn out procedure for a while, then we may expect him to snap out, and then have reverses or relapses, and then gradually come back to a normal condition.

15. (Q)  Any other advice for the parents in aiding this body?
(A)  Be patient, - with the body and with those environs; that we may make for the better conditions for all concerned in same.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
