# TEXT OF READING 3396-1 M 50 (Protestant Missionary)

**Reading [[3396-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/10/1943  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 10849

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Mrs. [5112].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Here we find conditions physically very serious. The course here is run.  [[Prayer|Prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]] by those interested, and those that have the interest of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] close at heart, may add that necessary for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] to meet those problems within the mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

3. Gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] - not that to cause greater stress - and the keeping of eliminations, preferably with enemas (though some will object), will make it easier for the body.

4. Preferably using an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil for the massaging, but these are not great stimulations to the body.

5. Stay off any further congestion, for the inability for the body to use its limbs will gradually increase.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 11/10/43 Wife's letter to EC:
> 
> ..., [[Florida|Florida]]
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> I am sure it is quite common for you to receive letters from
> people of whom you have never heard.
> 
> My husband is critically ill and I wrote to a very saintly
> friend in [[California|California]] who in turn wrote to friends for [[Prayer|prayer]]
> in his behalf.  So I have just received a letter from a
> friend, Mrs. [5112], ..., Ills., who served on the Mission
> field in the Belgian Congo with us.  She told me about you
> and of your great help to many, many people.
> 
> My husband suffered an accident while in Mission Service in
> the Congo.  He later developed a brain tumor.  We came back
> to America and he was operated on for brain tumor, but the
> operation was not a surgical success.  He has had four
> different series of deep X-ray therapy treatment in the last
> four years.  Suddenly the tumor has began to grow and X-ray
> therapy is not proving useful.  As the tumor grows he is
> gradually losing the use of his right side and speech.  I
> am inclosing a letter from his doctor from whom he took
> treatment at the Veterans Hospital in Atlanta - so that you
> might know how serious his condition is.
> 
> I don't think he could come to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] for a reading -
> and because he needs me so, and because of the seriousness of
> his condition I feel that I should not leave him.  He suffers
> agony untold at times because of headaches caused by the
> growing tumor.
> 
> Mrs. [5112], our friend, to whom I referred at the beginning
> offers to give us her date of December 10" that she has with
> you.  I hesitate to wait that long since the tumor seems to
> be growing rapidly and taking its toll of his right side
> and speech.  Sometimes he can hardly walk although he is
> still up and around.
> 
> I am [[Prayer|praying]] that if it is God's will that somehow there may
> be an opening in which you can help us.  I feel that we have
> done all that the doctors have advised.  They have now given
> up and say it is only a matter of time.  If there is anything
> that can be done while there is still life I hope it can be
> done - for my husband's only desire is to live and return to
> the mission field to help in the great task of taking God's
> word to the people who live in darkness out there.  If your
> reading should indicate that all has been done that human
> hands can do I shall feel resigned that it is God's will.
> 
> My husband's people call him [3396] - but for the last 20
> years or more he has been known as [3396] - using only his
> initials for he isn't fond of the name [3396].
> 
> I hope that I have not gone into too much detail.  I only
> wanted you to know the great urgency of the case.  I hope
> I can hear from you soon.
> 
> Sincerely yours
> (Mrs. [...])
