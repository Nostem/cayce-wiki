# TEXT OF READING 137-53 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-53]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/20/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 443

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137]. How often [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] changes in its mental, or in its physical aspect toward mental conditions as take place in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]!  Ready for the [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Thursday night, Jan. 14, or Friday morning, Jan. 15, 1926.  Dreamed something about [143] (daughter).
(A)  In this we find, as has been presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], that when the mental forces of the body-mind are held in that position wherein the inmost self gives intent and [[Purpose|purpose]], or gives strain and seeks knowledge, the inmost self presents same in the submerged condition of the physical.  This, then, presents as the body-mind seeks to satisfy that desire, or that love intent toward the body, [l43], for its physical condition, see?   Not that this is that the entity to be exceedingly or very sick.  Entity should rather USE same as a lesson to same, that those truths as have been presented are as die-set.  That is, they occur according to [[Law of Moses|the law]] regarding same.  The next?

4. (Q)  Saturday night, Jan. 16, or Sunday morning, Jan. 17. Man who either resembled or was [[Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]], was walking on air, or in space.  He was carrying a canoe on his back. He was stooped under the weight of the canoe.  I saw him walking on the sky - in the air - in space.  I tried but could not be sure as to whether he was making progress or not.
(A)  This again, we find, presents that as a lesson to be gained by the entity.  This:  As has been given, with that closer application of self to the truths that are being gained, there will be presented to the entity those [[Visions|visions]], those understandings, that the entity may go far beyond that position as is occupied by many in the present earth plane towards phenomenized force, for, as is seen in this, there is presented to the entity those conditions as have been visioned by the entity in an earth's plane existence, and these are as that the entity may gain the consciousness of the force as is exercising that which will produce those lessons, as have been outlined should be gained by the entity.

As is seen in the figure bowing under the weight, yet in space, in the cloud, in the sky, the PLACE occupied by THAT FORCE which to the entity is UPWARD.  That is, this force manifested in earth plane LIFTS UP, for as we see, "And I, if I be lifted up, will draw all men unto me," for "Even as [[Moses|Moses]] lifted up [[Serpent (Eden)|the serpent]] in the wilderness, even so must the Son of Man be lifted up."

5. We are through for the present.
