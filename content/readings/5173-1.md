# TEXT OF READING 5173-1 F 35 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[5173-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/30/1944  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 13308

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5173], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, this is rather a pitiable condition.

3. As we find, in the administrations made for the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], not a very good [[Book of Job|job]] was done.  There are the activities in the body with the desire, as it were, for the activity of the thyroids to function and the thyroids are not in their active form.  Hence the depressions, suppressions of weakness, suppression of hot flashes, suppressions through roof of the mouth and top of the head, feeling at times as if it must get its breath or get air.

4. As we find, there would be prepared the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] which will carry in combination [[Iodine|Iodine]], and this should be charged in the Appliance, three ounces to each charge and use the same over and over again.  But the Gold we would prepare one grain to one ounce of distilled water.  This is to be attached on wrist and ankle, see?  thirty minutes each day. These we may make altogether on the right wrist and left ankle.  Both plates should be made small.  Place that which passes into the wrist as from, or passes through, the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], see?  Keep this on thirty minutes each day at a regular hour, preferably just before the body retires or is prepared for retiring.

5. Follow this with a massage especially in the areas of the base of brain, down to the end of the spine, with Peanut Oil.

6. In the diet:  Do keep stimulations for the body-building forces.  Take plenty of Vitamins A, D and B complex.  These especially should be supplemented for the body, and we will bring better conditions for this body of [5173].

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What causes pulse to slow down?
(A)  As has been indicated, this variation in the activities in circulation because of the attempt for activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] which are only partially in working actions.

Do as has been outlined and we will have better conditions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 1909 in [[New York City|New York]]; resides in Illinois; married;
> 2 children; housewife; Jewish; religion not indicated.

> B2. 9/14/43 Letter:  "I just read an article about you...  I have
> been ill for some time, and although I'm up and around, I
> can't resume all my domestic chores and I really need to.  I
> have a couple of children that require attention, and would
> be very grateful if I could get back to my old self again..."
> 
> B3. 5/8/44 Letter:  "...I am hoping you can give me one now.
> I'm desperate.  Please help me!...  Have been ill for over
> 4 years when I was operated on for [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]].  Had a breakdown
> before that, and the illness of my daughter continued for 3
> years.  Right now, am so weak can hardly get through the day.
> If you wish any more particulars, please let me know.  The
> difficulty mainly is the weakness in my chest and that faint,
> stand-still feeling.  Please help me."
