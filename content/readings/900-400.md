# TEXT OF READING 900-400 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-400]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/14/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5603

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you the body of [900], and the advice and counsel that has come through [[Dreams|dreams]] (so called) from Gary and DePew regarding financial matters, or stocks on exchange.  You will answer the questions I will ask you, and interpret these [[Dreams|dreams]] and [[Visions|visions]] as I give them to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [900] - this we have had before. The [[Dreams|dreams]], the [[Visions|visions]], those communications as have come, do come, and as are correlated with the various aspects of conditions arising from time to time, and the mental attributes of the body attuning self to those conditions related to those in the cosmic plane, bring the various conditions as appear in the mental consciousness of the body. These, as seen, appear in varying forms and manners.  As the mental development of the body consciousness attunes itself, and is capable of physically shutting out that which hinders the expression of such [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]] - in that varying manner does the activity of the [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]] appear. Hence, as has been given by the varying ones - we find Gary appearing rather as distinctive, positive, commanding, yet ever forceful, without intruding upon the activity of the individual - while DePew representing rather the timid, uncertain manner; yet as DEFINITE as may be presented in such a consciousness as attained.  One, then, is the development of a very active, positive, distinctive leader; the other the counsellor with those that counsel.  Ready for dream.

3. (Q)  The one of recent date in which I was addressed, "[900] - three times three."
(A)  This presenting that definite correlation of information given respecting the particular stock or investment that this relates to, see?  Three - three - three, or three times three - that very definite effectiveness of that given.  This representing or presenting, then, a different phase from that as ordinarily seen in directing the information respecting activity in particular stocks, save as to ACTIVITY - see? Get the variations in this being presented, [900].  In that presented DO ACT NOW - BIM - BAM - BUM, this presenting rather that of activity.  This in three times three following nearer the activity of that force that IMPELS conditions respecting same.  Hold to it, then.

4. (Q)  What correlation has this with others, such as come in the shake downs - "Now buy Pennsylvania."  Then again last night regarding, "You won't get the most out of the rise, I'll wager," and again regarding, "Too high a debit balance."
(A)  This the three factors in the activity as presented - three times three; that the fear in self of that given, and the manner  of the action physically through such fear, preventing the full obtaining of all that would be possible in such.  Again in that shake down as seen there was GIVEN the varying condition that could be acted upon, that was not taken full advantage of.  Again in the debit, there carried the activity IN the way that prevented its full action - see?

5. (Q)  I carried the stocks given by Gary - that list.  Is it meant to hold this list?  Hold on irregardless [irrespective] [GD's note:  I suspect Mr. [900] "got" this term of expression from Mr. Cayce.  He often used the term "irregardless" in speaking.  I cannot find it, even in old dictionary under obsolete words.] of the shake down?
(A)  In this particular one, as given - and as just referred to - there was given when to turn loose and when to take hold again.  In the INDUSTRIALS - these present a different condition.  Rather that as has been given, that in the varying conditions as appear in the activities from day to day, THESE should be taken advantage of, and [as] when presented.  Just as has been given oft respecting these.  Do not attempt to always take ALL, irrespective of being sage within self, yet when there is the direct command - BE commanded.

6. (Q)  I was directed to buy Pennsylvania, and bought 300 at 63 1/2, practically the 9 mark.  Is it indicated, then, this stock - with other rails - should be held, and the industrials - such as Sears, Roebuck - Woolworth, and others, should be sold?
(A)  Just as is given.  These, as seen, find their variations in that presented from time to time.  Then sell THOSE as directed.  Hold those as directed.

7. (Q)  Any information of what is to be expected?
(A)  (No answer)

8. (Q)  The dreams of last night regarding appearing before the business conduct committee with Walter Hess, and being fined $25,000 for something done.
(A)  This, as has been given, respecting that appearance before committee - the variations as presented should be held as the principals (?).  As to that as may be expected in the varying activities - these, as have and as will be and as are given from time to time, in the direct command, will bring those returns as are given.  Then the general rise, led by those specifics as were set forth, will be the activity for the next three to four weeks, with the greater varying in those industrials as has been given.

9. (Q)  This morning on the train.  Some trouble with a check.
(A)  There appears there has been presented check that there will be some discussion about; yet, as seen, through this discussion will be brought a closer relationship that will be turned into worth while material position by the body.

10. (Q)  This check was presented by me or to me?
(A)  To me.

11. (Q)  Any further information I should know on this now?
(A)  Use that which has been given.  Don't be too harsh about it!

12. (Q)  Now, along these same lines, what might be a general business outlook for the near future?
(A)  This would depend upon what VIEWPOINT or aspect business would be viewed from.  As a general whole, there is seen that while there are varying groups, from that including steel, coal, and the lentil directions, a general impression of decrease in the activity of these movements, or these elements of business; in those general lines as pertaining to motors, and some industrials, these show the greater increase, and a more lenient activity toward a general bettered condition.  In the money market, at home and abroad, these show more leniency to the general reports than is ordinarily seen under the existent conditions, of times, and conditions in a whole.  Hence we would say, for the next ninety days, the GENERAL business outlook (This will be quoted from the magazine on same) as relating to industrials is good.  As to steel, rails, and those GROWING commodities, not so well.  Short crops in corn and wheat - and the reserve in same shorter than the world has known, in the next fifteen months.  We are through.
