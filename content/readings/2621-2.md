# TEXT OF READING 2621-2 M 30

**Reading [[2621-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/8/1941  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 9665

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - [2621], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2621]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions show improvements.  While there is much to be desired, do not be overanxious nor make too great a struggle; that is:

4. Do not apply so much without giving all that which has been suggested the full and complete time to react.

5. Do have the treatments with the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]], and do have these with the green light interposed at all times.  Do have a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] afterward with Peanut Oil - while the body is still on the table after taking the light.

6. This causing of the tingling sensation, as of being partially electrified when the body moves limbs, indicates the attempt of the system to replenish and react.

7. DO keep up the diets that have been indicated.

8. Keep ALL the treatments in the way that has been outlined.

9. Do not allow changes in ANY administrations until this course of treatment outlined has been given full consideration, for this will bring help - if there is kept by the body, and those about same, that prayerful, expectant attitude of using the strength, the abilities of the body, in a spiritually, mentally constructive way and manner in dealing with others.

10. Keep at that given.  These applications will bring help, if they are consistently carried on.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self - one for Dr. Reilly Copy to Ass'n file
