# TEXT OF READING 5510-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[5510-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/16/1930  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13752

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self, through her mother and - Dr. W. H. McChesney.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [5510], Dr. and Mrs. McChesney, Mrs. Miller, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are conditions physical that disturb the body, but the greater warning should be given as respecting the verge of conditions as exist, and that steps be taken for the correction of what are apparently minor conditions at present - that there may be the better and the more perfect functioning of the organs, and of the proper physical reaction with this body, [5510] we are speaking of.

2. These, then, are the conditions as we find them at this time:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, here we find the evidences of the lack of the proper distribution of forces as are assimilated in the system.  These are produced, as we find, by the functioning of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as are not in accord with the proper changes as should be brought about in the system.  This, especially, having to do with that of that assimilated, and as the changes are effected by the functioning of the liver and the organs as have to do with the digestion, and in this portion, and these conditions should the body take the warning and the proper precautions - for these, allowed to become in the nature of a constitutional, or the system even allowed to build that which would for the time being ward off the conditions, we may expect greater distresses to come from the organs as are affected in this disturbance.

4. Naturally, there are in the NERVE SYSTEM those reflexes as are produced by the changes as wrought in system in this direction, and these cause at times a taxation to the system that produces that of inability to carry on in a NORMAL manner - either physical OR mental, in the reaction.

5. In the functioning of the ORGANS themselves:  These, as we find, all show the effect of these disturbances as have to do with those of the distribution of that assimilated in the system.  While the digestion proper, in the stomach itself, takes place in the UPPER portion as near NORMAL, that as it passes into the system - and that distribution of that assimilated in the system - through the lower portion, or through the duodenum and through the jejunum - these form the conditions that are to be warned of, and are to be guarded against; for the circulation being disturbed, the lungs show the effect, as does the heart's action - though not ORGANIC in either direction - as does also the breaking down of the conditions as are to be normal in the lower HEPATIC circulation.

6. To meet the needs of these conditions, and to ELIMINATE same from system - these, we find, may be accomplished by a little consistency in the diet, and of persistency for the change of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as may be created in the system by that as would be carried INTO same; NOT so much as through medicinal properties, but of that [[Vibration|vibratory]] force FROM same as would aid IN CORRECTING those forces in the system.

7. We would add, then, first:  In the diet, let this be of the alkalin reaction, with sufficient of the proteins as to build NOURISHMENT, carrying in same those of the iron.

8. Also we would change the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the system by the addition through that of the Radio-Active Appliance carrying that of the [[Camphor|camphor]], or [[Camphor|spirits of camphor]] - attaching the first anode, or the positive first, to the wrist - and the next application to the ankle, and the negative (with the larger plate) being attached to the right and upper part at the umbilicus.  In the taking of this, DO NOT take same LONGER than thirty minutes at a time in the beginning, and when this is taken over time NAUSEA would be the effect, and this may produce SOME - after the third day in taking this.  This, however, will be aided in elimination by the use of those of the Infra red, and this should be particularly over the cerebro-spinal system and along those particular centers where all of the BONES carry for the blood in its reaction; so that, not only the torso but the extremities - both front AND back - should be exposed to those of the Infra red.

9. Sufficient of the manipulations should be given that the DISTRIBUTION throughout the system is kept up, and that the circulation may not become one-sided in any portion of the distributing centers, either of blood supply or of nerve impulse.

10. Do that, and be consistent and be persistent - and we will find the better conditions will come for this body.  Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What can be done to improve nearsight?
(A)  When we change the vibratory forces in the system, and the manipulations or the corrections are made in the cervical and upper dorsal - as are found necessary - and we have more blood supply, this will be overcome.  Not by glasses would we change this, but rather by the flow of the blood supply to the face, and head.

12. (Q)  What method shall be employed to adjust a misplaced metatarsal bone, if the bone really be misplaced?
(A)  There is necessary for the adjustments OF ALL of the whole system.  This may be either done osteopathically or chiropractically.

13. (Q)  Are slight headaches due to bad sight?  If not, what can be done to eliminate them?
(A)  We are correcting these conditions through that of the proper assimilation.  Most of this is due to that pressure as produced from the sourness, or from the improper assimilation in the system - and the pressure is produced through the sympathetics, in the secondary cardiac, which reflects to the brain forces and the facial centers, which cause - WITH that pressure as caused from the eye.

14. (Q)  What can be done for dysmenorrhea?
(A)  This will be corrected with the change of the vibrations in the system.

15. That is all the questions.

16. Take the vibratory forces from the Radio-Active Appliance every other day, the Infra red those days when the appliance is not given.  Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
