# TEXT OF READING 140-9 F 23 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[140-9]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1925  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 545

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by her brother-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. (This the [[Dreams|dream]] - not read:  Wednesday, December 2. "Dreamed [136], my sister-in-law, had a new diamond flexible bracelet.")

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  We have had this before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come in the manners as we have given, and should be used in the manner as we have given.  For, as seen in this regarding the jewels on the arm, the bands in same, it is representing then to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that the preparations for the greater bonds that bind self to the commendation of self, to the magnifying of spirit forces in earth plane, may be manifested through self, in the way and manner that is most acceptable to those directing forces of the higher elements that manifest through [[Physical Body|physical forces]], that give self in service to the higher forces.

3. We are through.
