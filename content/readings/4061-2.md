# TEXT OF READING 4061-2 M 2

**Reading [[4061-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/22/1944  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 11757

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Dolores Harding and others, including Harmon H. Bro.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here:  [4061]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are real improvements, but as has been indicated this will require patience and persistence.  For the conditions have been and are hindrances of those natures which require time, patience, [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]] and doing all those things which have been indicated.

4. To be sure, these may appear hopeless.  They might be considered so without there being the [[Prayer|prayer]], the [[Meditation|meditation]], the desire, the hope.

5. When there is the application of the low [[Vibration|vibrations]] which carry those properties to the body-forces, don't neglect this period, for then there is the opportunity for making those contributions or those suggestions to the very [[Soul|soul]]-body and this is where the [[Healing|healing]] or the help begins.  To be sure, it is necessary that the mechanical and the other treatments be carried forward but don't neglect the [[Prayer|prayer]] and the suggestions, and these have been partially.  Be sincere and it will take time, but we will get results.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Are the treatments being correctly given?  Attaching the plates in the right position?
(A)  Read just what has been indicated, that these must be followed through with very closely and there hasn't been as much care in the suggestions as there should be.  But these can be corrected.

8. (Q)  Are the massages being done correctly?
(A)  Do just what has been given.  If they hadn't, these would have been referred to.

9. (Q)  May anything be done to overcome difficulty in massaging around his eyes and head?
(A)  These will just take time and patience, but follow through just as much as practical.

10. (Q)  Will the bunches or growths on his eyes eventually disappear?
(A)  This depends upon the responses.  It depends upon the response to the inner consciousness as much as to physical consciousness.  It depends, then, as to whether it will be - it is like asking if tomorrow will come.  For about three million it will not come, it will be eternal.  For [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] it may, it may not, dependent upon just how sincerely, how thoroughly the suggestions are adhered to.  Don't grow impatient.  Suppose God did with thee, what opportunity would there be.

11. (Q)  Were the operations that caused them necessary and if not have they done any harm or disfigured them permanently?
(A)  Why worry self with this?  These have been done.  Why berate or belittle those who attempted to do the best?  Would you berate the one who first used that which was not well in the cleansing of the eyes, with boric acid mixed with others which caused physically disturbances?  But don't be like Lot's wife, who may be scorned also.

12. (Q)  How much does he see at this time and which eye is more useful to him at present?
(A)  The right eye is the one needing the most.  The light in the left causes [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to shield same.  There is about ten percent in the one and about fifteen to twenty in the other.

13. (Q)  Why does he rock back and forth continually?
(A)  If you had this trouble you'd do more than rock back and forth!

14. (Q)  Does it relieve the tension for him to stand on his head?
(A)  Read the first information as to where the sources of the disturbance are, between sympathetic and cerebrospinal, and the sensory system and the activities which may be determined.  Anyone having such a disorder needs the closer cooperative activity, and the body unconsciously gains or goes to that position.

15. (Q)  Is there any trouble in his ears or why does he put his fingers in them?
(A)  There is trouble, but as has been indicated, the trouble is in the lower portion of that space in the palate and above where eyes, ears, nasal passages, all of the organs of the sensory system coordinate.  Read what has been given and analyze it.  To gain the help do as has been given, consistently, persistently.  Be patient, be prayerful.

16. We are through.

Two copies to Mother Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
