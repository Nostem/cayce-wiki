# TEXT OF READING 3751-7 M 3

**Reading [[3751-7]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/30/1923  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11274

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]] on the 30th day of March, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M.                                    [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  We see, the conditions in this body are not as good as when we had them before, not enough of the eliminating elements with the condition in the system, especially, along the left side of the body and that about the facial muscles and those about the thyroidal artery.  It shows the plethora from inflammation and shows the ravages of the bacilli that must be overcome by destructive forces from without and from within.  The body must create sufficient of the warriors from within to eliminate and there must be enough of the applications of that which will resist these conditions, see, along the arm and the left portion of the body.

2. We would apply with stronger vehemence, the elements as we have given in the Plantain and the fat.  We would also give the electric violet ray?] [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the very high voltage to the body, see.  We would also give the electronic forces [Abrams Machine?] as would be from electrical forces, driving this through the system, you see.  Let the inhalants from the electronic forces carry the heavy Turp from White Pine, Balsam of Tolu and the Balsam of the Cinchona.  Keep the diet as outlined.  Keep the bowels open and be sure the cleansing forces as applied to the abrasions are of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] rather than acid astringents, see.  Do that.  The injections must be kept, of course, in the system, else we will have a reaction with the readded [re-added] electrical violet ray?] forces, see.  The electronic, [Abrams Machine?] as those as breathed into the lungs and give the incentive to the blood, as well as the electrical violet ray?] forces to spread with the elimination and shall be applied with the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], to eliminate through the proper channels.  Keep to this and be vigilant, see.
