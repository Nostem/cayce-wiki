# TEXT OF READING 308-13 F 20

**Reading [[308-13]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/26/1944  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 2342

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the Mother, Mrs. [585], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; Jeanette Fitch, Steno. Mrs. [585].]

Time of Reading                                   ... Avenue, 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, yes we have the body here; this we have had before. As we find, most of the disturbance through the injuries, or the distress from the fall, have been aided.

3. We would find now that a regular exercise would be helpful, but don't start this and then do it for a day and then skip two or three days and then try it again; either do it regularly or don't begin; an exercise the body may take itself.  This take for about two minutes morning and evening; evening after it has prepared for retiring.  On the floor with the hands, the feet to the wall, raise and then lower the body; not as taut, but relaxed, just raise and lower body three or four times; then a circular motion of the body on the hands, see?  This isn't easy, but it will strengthen the whole condition of the spine, keep the abdominal muscles well as to general position of the body and keep the limbs in shape as to strengthen the muscles without being detrimental to any portion of the body.  This will help the circulation, aid the digestion and improve the general conditions of the body.  This do at least three times; that is, raise and lower the body three times and circle the body at least three times, morning and evening.  Do this regularly and it'll keep this body physically fit.  It'll get away from those headaches, those tendencies for foods to remain in stomach or undigested too long.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is small lump on right hip?
(A)  This is an accumulation of lymph and with these exercises should disappear.

6. (Q)  Do I have fallen arches?
(A)  You have a weakness but we have given you exercise for muscles in limbs and feet.

7. (Q)  How may big pores on my nose be eliminated?
(A)  Keep the eliminations better and use Black and White cleansing cream.

8. (Q)  What is the best way to remove warts?
(A)  This one on the right knee is gradually leaving.  Put equal portions of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] and [[Baking Soda|Soda]] on the finger tip, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] this, it'll make it sore but it'll take it away also.

9. (Q)  Will I be happy married to [...], a Catholic?
(A)  No, you won't marry him - don't marry him!

10. (Q)  How can I be sure when I meet the right one?
(A)  This should answer as to that within self.

11. (Q)  In what line of work should he be engaged?
(A)  Whether he's a street sweeper or the president you'll know him!  It isn't the line of work you choose, it's as heart answers to heart and body to body.

12. We are through with this reading.

2 Copies to mother (one for self) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
