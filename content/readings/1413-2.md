# TEXT OF READING 1413-2 F 20

**Reading [[1413-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/20/1937  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 7160

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of August, 1937, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before, [1413].

2. As we find, there are decided improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body in many ways.

3. We would not, then, change a great deal in the administrations that are now being made; we would rather impress upon those administering same, in all of the phases of it, the necessity of those things and those manners as we would indicate for the improvement.

4. Consistency and persistency is the need in this type and these characters of conditions.

5. The manipulations or the administrations or the diet, then, are not to be as rote but rather as in a purposeful manner, with the EXPECTANCY of results from same - each and every one.

6. Keep, in the administrations of corrections, the more perfect alignment in the upper dorsal and through the cervical areas; relieving pressures upon the centers from which the trachea retains its impulse and activity; also in the vagus and brachial centers where the branches of the tubes are receptive to the nerve pressures in same.

7. The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] we would keep until there is a decided [[Iodine|iodine]] reaction in the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body as well as in the pulsations, and the activity of the whole circulatory system.

8. Keep the diet, not as rote but in the manners that have been indicated.

9. These we find are the better administrations for this body, [1413].

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  How may the sufficient reaction of the [[Iodine|iodine]] be determined?
(A)  By that just indicated; the change in the pulsations, heart beat, as well as in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reaction - or the clearing of the activities through the abrasions.

And these as we find must mean, then, that there is somewhat an excess of eliminations through alimentary canal.  These may be aided or stimulated by the change-about in the diet, as well as by the administrations or manipulations to the abdominal area and the cerebrospinal system - or the whole of such administrations.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Nona Grapes, Selma, Ala.) Copy to Ass'n file
