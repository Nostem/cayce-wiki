# TEXT OF READING 248-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[248-2]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/22/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1132

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 22nd day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see.  Now we find that the conditions in this body are improving in many ways from that we had before while there is some exaggeration between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal system, especially through the connection between same about the pneumogastrics that are thrown off by the nerves near the first cervical, see.

2. We would keep the stomach itself emptied, more so that reaction from these would not occur.  With the ragweed tea in the system this should keep the system cleansed.

3. Preferably to that which has been used we would use the preparation made by Simmons - known as Simmons Liver Regulator, composed of licorice compound; added cascara.

4. The half quantity would be used and given only every second day for a time; say five or six days, then three days apart for ten days, then everyday in small doses.

5. Also give and keep most of the drinking water carrying extract or tincture of may-pop; all drinking water carrying one-half teaspoon full to glass.

6. Keep up diet and exercise.  Keep salines to the body.
