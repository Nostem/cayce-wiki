# TEXT OF READING 3650-1 M 66

**Reading [[3650-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/2/1944  Sex: M  Age: 66  ReadingID: 11152

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [3650], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Dr. [3650].

3. As we find, there are conditions of which the body should take cognizance.  Yet if there is too great a stress, or if there is held in the mental self a fear, there will come those conditions that are spoken of by the [[Psalms|psalmist]] "That which I feared has come upon me."

4. Then, apply in nature that which will meet the needs for eliminating any infection that may have been carried in the circulation from those disturbances which have existed.  This will in the physical self eliminate the sources, if the fear is eliminated from the mental self that blocks such activity in the living organism in the body.

5. While not settled in any areas of the body, there is a contaminated element carrying destructive forces in its body-cells.  For these are units of infection not entirely eliminated.  They are in the blood flow, but with this application should be entirely eliminated from the blood system and no portion of the body, then, would become contaminated.

6. Take these properties rather in series, each day for at least a week, then leave off three to four days for the reaction. Then begin again and take for seven days, then have a rest period of three to five days, and then take again.  Then rest a period of some six weeks, and then take another course. Prepare in this manner:

7. Obtain the common Plantain, fresh, and make into a tea.  Cut the fresh leaves and fill a cup measure - not mashed down, but fill with the fresh leaves cut very fine.  Use only china, enamel or glassware.  Then fill the cup with boiling water, within half an inch of the top.  Let stand for thirty minutes, covered.  Then strain and drink this fusion - this amount each day.  Make it fresh each day.

8. At least twice each week apply the ultra violet light with the green light [glass?] projected between the light and the body.  To be sure, some may question as to the feasibility of diverting the ultra violet ray from the body, but it is the ozone and the type of light deflected by the green glass that is needed.  Green in itself is [[Healing|healing]].  Preferably use a single strength colored glass, not painted, but the green colored glass - preferably plain, not frosted; such as prepared by The American Plate Glass Company.  Use this a minute to a minute and a half, especially on the hand and arm and from the back - over the lumbar-sacral areas.

9. Do these and be consistent with the diet.  Never too much of meats of any kind.  Only fish and fowl.  No other characters of meats.  Occasionally a little lamb may be taken, but not fried foods.

10. Do these and we will prevent, we will eliminate the sources of infection that have existed in the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is there active trouble in the gall-bladder at this time?
(A)  Will not be when these properties have been taken in the system.  For this is a blood purifier and as the blood is purified and the light rays produced their radial activity on superficial circulation, as well as the deeper circulation, we will eliminate these disturbances - unless there are foods taken that cause greater trouble; as too much sweets, too much fats.

13. (Q)  What causes the symptoms in the upper chest of a feeling of constriction with some pain, which has been present ever since I was in Munich, [[Germany|Germany]] in 1931?
(A)  This is a suppression caused by some of those experiences of the [[Mental Body|mental body]] through that particular period.  This is psychopathic, not a condition of the physical being - yet it reflects, to be sure, upon the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  But not a true pathological condition.

14. (Q)  Would intravenous treatment with penicillin for chronic tooth infection or for any other infectious pathology be advisable when penicillin is available?
(A)  It would be very advisable, not necessarily for this particular body - if we use the properties that have been indicated - but for other bodies in a general manner, good.

15. (Q)  Why do I have a very severe ache in the lower part of my back while I am in bed - around 5:00 or 6:00 o'clock in the morning - especially if I lie on my back?
(A)  This is from those tendencies that are to be eliminated by the use of the ultra violet treatment - not more than a minute and a half.  Begin at first with a minute.

16. (Q)  What causes the bad breath and disagreeable taste in the mouth shortly after eating - especially after eating ice-cream, bread, cream or pumpkin pie?
(A)  The inability of the stomach to eliminate all of digested foods or those in the state of digestion.  But with a better circulation set up, this should be eliminated.

Use a little charcoal once in a while.  This will absorb the disagreeable conditions in the system.  Preferably use that character prepared at Kellogg's.

17. (Q)  Would you advise me to go to Dr. W. B. Dakin for preventive treatment for my prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] at this time?
(A)  Not necessarily, if we will do these others in the present - unless other conditions arise that are not existent in the present.

Do those things indicated and we will have better conditions for this body.

18. We are through with this reading.
