# TEXT OF READING 1565-5 M 44 (Merchant, Hebrew)

**Reading [[1565-5]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/13/1944  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 7595

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1565], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, this we have had before.

3. Here it might be said "We told you so."  Had you followed some of those suggestions more closely that we have indicated for the body heretofore, there would not be these segregations, these tendencies for accumulations of lymph pockets through the body and the tiredness resulting from same, the general "all-over-ness" and the puffiness through portions of the lymph circulation; as in the prostate, in the abdominal area and in the activity of the heart itself.

4. There are two courses that may be pursued, either of which will give results.  As to whether these conditions are eliminated will depend very much upon the diet and upon the general activity of the body following same.

5. The body may in its present environment take the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] light with the green glass projected between the light and the body.  Apply this for only a minute, along the area of the spine and in the front portion of the body, twice a week for a period of six weeks.  Following each of the light treatments (not before but following each treatment) give a gentle, thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with pine oils.

6. The alternate course would be for the body to take a complete rest, where it may be in water and in the sun, so as to obtain the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] directly; but the body should be careful that there are not blisters from being in water and then in sun.  However, the body should be wet before it rests on the sand.  The east coast of [[Florida|Florida]] for this body would be very well, or the lower coasts of [[California|California]].  If this course is taken, there should be a complete rest for three to four weeks, and following this rest period there should be the series of massages.

7. Either of these methods would make a great deal of change and help for the body.

8. In any case, do refrain from any form of carbonated waters, hard drinks or the like; and do keep better eliminations.

9. Keep better eliminations through the general diet and activity.

10. Ready for questions.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Reilly) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
