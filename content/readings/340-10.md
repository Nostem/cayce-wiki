# TEXT OF READING 340-10 F 42

**Reading [[340-10]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/24/1930  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 2602

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker, nurse, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we find conditions somewhat on the improve.  The better forces are active.  Well, though, would the body - during the periods when on feet - to have that so fitted in the belt, as to hold the stomach in better position.  Also that there be the eliminations from the system, even the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] would be not too severe now, were the oil used at first - or oil enema, and then later use [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations. See?

2. The body may become active a little at the time, but be sure that there is the support to the stomach and the whole of the system - see?  torso.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is body ready to go home?
(A)  If it will take care, and rest sufficiently - and be sure that the support is used when walking, or when up on feet.  Don't stand too much.

4. (Q)  How can Benzosol in crystalline form be converted into solution?
(A)  We haven't that in this body!  [See inhalant in 340-5 which did NOT include Benzosol!]  This may be accomplished best by using that of the Benzosol solution FOR the converting of same.  [GD's note:  Evidently this question was asked at the end of Mrs. [340]'s reading for the benefit of another patient who was having difficulty getting her inhalant prescription filled.]

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
