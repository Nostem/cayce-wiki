# TEXT OF READING 1788-10 M 3

**Reading [[1788-10]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1942  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 8078

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [480], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1788]; this we have had before.

3. There are some improvements.

4. As we find, the acute conditions arise from an upsetting of the digestive system.  And the body, being sensitive, responds to those anxieties and anticipations about the body.

5. The eliminations should be set up with alternated broken doses of Castoria AND Syrup of Figs; about half a teaspoonful about every hour apart.  This is being done, you see.

6. After there has been good elimination, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body - after a tepid bath - with a weakened solution of GRAIN alcohol; not the rub alcohol but grain alcohol, especially along the spine - base of brain to the end of the spine.

7. THEN [[Massage Therapy|massage]] especially between the brachial centers and the central portion of the back, and across the hips, or the sacral and lumbar, with Cocoa Butter.

8. As we find, keep the body quiet, and the food mostly that of a pre-digested nature - or milk and cereals.

9. These should bring very soon the normal forces for this body.

10. Beef tea would be well to give also, to supply strength.

11. Do this.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Does the temperature come from upset stomach?
(A)  It comes from the upset stomach, as indicated.

14. (Q)  Was there something specific he ate that caused the upset?
(A)  As just indicated, a combination of the sensitiveness of the body to the excitement about it, and the anticipations of changes in activities, - these brought about the temperature.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother, Air Mail, Special - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file

> "Thank you again and our love to you all..."
