# TEXT OF READING 538-41 F 55

**Reading [[538-41]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/24/1935  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 3777

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of June, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 to 3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are not so good.  While that which has arisen is rather of the nature of ACUTE conditions, this involves much that is [[Astrology|astrological]] - and in a condition that unless there are changes will make for a reversal to much that has been helped in the past.

3. This, as we find, is rather a congestion in the mucous membrane of the duodenum [Gastroduodenitis?  Stomach colic? Catarrhal gastritis?], and involves - through the circulatory forces - the circulation through the jejunum.  Hence it produces not only nausea but a very high temperature, and must eventually - unless eliminated - cause congestion through the colon.

4. We find that we would in the present begin with these:

5. To half a glass of water we would add:

Paregoric.....................1/2 teaspoonful, Almost saturated solution
of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]].............1/2 teaspoonful,
[[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]............1/2 teaspoonful.

Take this every hour until there is not only the alleviation but a full activity from the bowels.

6. Then, as soon as this has acted, we would begin with the Castoria and take same until IT has acted; half a teaspoonful every half hour.

7. Or the treatment may be reversed in THIS order:

8. Begin with the Castoria; half a teaspoonful every fifteen minutes, until there is a full activity.  And put the Salts Pack of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] with the Paregoric and [[Camphor|Camphor]] in same across the abdomen; making for the activity by absorption.

9. These, as we find, would be the better conditions for the body, in either direction.

10. We would also bathe the feet in warm water to take the temperature from the head, that is producing inflammation in the soft tissue of face and head - and the pressure there by temperature.  To the lungs, the throat and the head this produces a great distress.

11. Orange juice, citrus fruit juices, should be all that should be taken; save a little coffee with toast - until this has been reduced.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Is the whole half glass of the solution of Paregoric and [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] be taken -
(A)  Be taken as has been given; half a teaspoonful in half a glass of water.  Take it until there is the action.  Or take it the other way round; that is, the Castoria and the Epsom Salts, Paregoric and [[Camphor|Camphor]] PACKS.  Either way, whichever is more preferable.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    "  Ass'n file
