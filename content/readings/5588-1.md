# TEXT OF READING 5588-1 F 30

**Reading [[5588-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/16/1929  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 13908

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by self, through her sister - via Mr. [417].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, the conditions, as we find, have reached such inroads on the vital tissues of the body respecting the lymphatic and the emunctories, as to cause such distress as to prevent the normal, or a semi-normal, reaction in same. It will be necessary, then, to bring relief to, at least, segregate the destructive forces or tissue by the releasing of more carbon in the system, or more oxidization, that there may be SOME semblance of proper coagulation taking place. The inroads begin from destructive tissues being involved from outside influences, coming through those reactions of the physical as well as of the organic reactions as are seen in the system.  We would, then, first give those properties of the medicated ash; at least a quarter grain in CARBONATED water, or half carbonated, half plain - two ounces of water, one ounce carbonated and one ounce plain - quarter grain medicated ash.  This should be given at least twice each day, and apply the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray to the body, especially over the solar plexus center, for one minute, at least thirty-eight inches the light should be from the body.

2. Then, in eight days we would give further instructions, if this will but respond to the reactions.  Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
