# TEXT OF READING 563-4 F 19

**Reading [[563-4]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/29/1934  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 3980

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 29th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [325], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:25 to 5:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion - Body & enquiring mind.  Questions.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the impressions here, and those conditions that surround the body from the physical, the mental and the material welfare of the body, [563].

2. As we find, there have been and are tendencies for the clogging of the ELIMINATING systems, so that what should be eliminated through the alimentary canal and respiratory system has made rather confusing conditions, owing to the inactivity of the bile or gall ducts.

3. And this is partly from those things the body has added in the way of not cosmetics exactly (and everyone should be very precautious about any type or character used), but rather those things that have been as a stoppage for the respiratory activity.  This has in part affected the EMUNCTORY circulation, and has made the tendency for conditions in the superficial circulation that are unsatisfactory.

4. While it has not created as yet such an unbalanced condition as to produce any great character of disorder, save as to the mental forces feeling at times the conditions in face, head, and even in the soft tissue of the face - as in the antrum, as in the sinews, as in even the head portions about ear, the throat, and the activities that have made for sort of a dullness and laggardness and inactivity.

5. If these conditions are allowed to continue, we may bring detrimental effects to the body.

6. First we would leave off the use of the preparations that are in the nature of depilatories, and use rather the pure activity of water with a more ALKALIN reaction; such as the soap cosmetics used.

7. We would begin first with a thorough elimination of the alimentary canal, stimulating all of the respiratory system, especially from the centers along the cerebrospinal system, with the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].  And after the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] treatment we would add the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], to STIMULATE the superficial circulation to coordinate with the effects created by stimulating the activity of the liver, pancreas and the spleen, and eliminating the poisons from the system.

8. These for the general physical condition, as we find, will bring better conditions for the body.

9. Be mindful that not too large a quantity of fat, or butter, or any greases of any kind, would be used in the diet.

10. However, [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] taken internally would be very effective, in small quantities; quarter of a teaspoonful three or four times each day would be helpful to the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  How is the blood condition?
(A)  As indicated, irritated more especially in the superficial circulation than from the internal or organic disturbances.  This is rather from those activities as indicated, in the EMUNCTORY circulation.

13. (Q)  Have I [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] trouble?
(A)  We do not find it so.  [[Glands (Esoteric)|Glands]] are disturbed rather by the inabilities of the system to create the activities necessary for coordination in the eliminations of the body.

14. (Q)  What causes blisters on my foot, and how can I prevent them?
(A)  The poor circulation and the poisons attempting to eliminate themselves through the respiratory system, or sweat [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  Cleanse the system, purifying the blood, stimulating the circulation as we have indicated; and we find this should rid the body of these conditions.  To be sure, the diet should be kept well balanced when this is being carried on; as indicated, not using large quantities of fats of any kind - though the stimulation for the food values necessary to the emunctory and lymph circulation, through the alimentary canal, by the use of the [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], will be most beneficial.

15. (Q)  Please outline a proper diet for me.
(A)  As indicated, do not use fats or highly seasoned foods, but those that tend to be more of an ALKALIN nature are the better for the system.  No large quantities of sweets at any time, especially of cane sugar, but rather sufficient of that necessary to keep proper digestion.

First we would cleanse the system as indicated; stimulating the superficial circulation, keeping an even balance in the diet.  Not that the body should just eat this, or just eat that, but keep consistent activity in the system - in an ALKALIN PRODUCING reaction.

16. (Q)  Is there any special advice to me as to the young man with whom I am keeping company?
(A)  As indicated for this body, the associations and relations in this direction should culminate in those activities that we have heretofore indicated for the body. Choose within self.  If there is that response within self as to the character and nature, the associations of the body, then it would be well for marriage.

17. (Q)  If so, will it be congenial for me to live with his mother?
(A)  For the first three years, yes; after that, not so well.

18. (Q)  Any other advice that would be helpful to me just now?
(A)  If there are those considerations in these directions, all the more reason that there should be the proper precautions as to the activities in the directions indicated - respecting the general [[Physical Body|physical body]].

The organic conditions are very good.  The functional conditions, as indicated, are very much disturbed.

Produce, as it were, a balance; and keep a coordinating in the regular directions of the body-mental and physical.

19. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
