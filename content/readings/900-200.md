# TEXT OF READING 900-200 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-200]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/24/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5403

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, with the enquiring mind, here. The [[Dreams|dreams]] as we see that come to the body are as have been given, a correlation of physical and mental conditions in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], acted upon by the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and are the correlation of these conditions, under the stress or mind action of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] entity, and vary according to that suggestion given by the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] when the physical consciousness is laid aside, or may be that consciousness awakening in the entity when such is heard, as a voice, or as an impression to the entity, as an existent condition present with the [[Physical Body|physical body]]. Such are the [[Dreams|dreams]] of [900].  Ready for dream.

3. (Q)  Saturday Night, Feb. 20, or Sunday Morning, Feb. 21, in Room 83, White House Hotel, Biloxi, Miss.  "I threw some glass or flakes at someone, then taught them as follows: Saw myself in space surrounded by a mist or cloud.  This cloud I reasoned was what we called space and its various forms made up the various forms of creation - life - worlds of the entire infinite Universe, and the various activities of which made up the different realities our sensuous mind compared one with another - calling one activity matter, another animal, etc."
(A)  This, as we see, that condition as has been described for the entity, in how that the thought, study, or mind of the entity, presents to the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of entity those conditions to be reasoned, and are presented, then, in this manner, as an experience to the entity, to give the more perfect understanding to same, that the full concept and consciousness of the existing conditions as are being studied may be better understood and explained to those whom have these lessons to gain.  Then, as we see, this should be taken as given, and studied, for as is seen, the subconscious is that experience of the entity, plus the study in all physical phases of the entity, correlated with the spiritual entity's experiences, plus.  See?

4. (Q)  Again but the reproduction of my physical concept. Is it putting the O.K. on this concept?
(A)  Just, as we see, putting the O.K. on that concept of the physical consciousness, that the better understanding may be gained by the entity.

5. (Q)  Is it pointing out the best mode of teaching?
(A)  Pointing out the mode of teaching that will be UNDERSTANDABLE to those who attempt to gain the concept of the entity's presented lessons and truths.

6. (Q)  "[137] and I seemed to be talking to Herbert Son and he seemed to have taken some examinations and passed all but the automobile test.  I thought that peculiar and he seemed peeved and angry at someone."
(A)  This, as we see, that of the mental attempting to correlate to the subconscious forces that study the entity, these entities as are seen, is making of motor stocks, and specifically of certain motor stocks - namely Hudson, and as is presented through the tests being passed by an individual to whom the entity gives, or holds in the physician mind as one capable of understanding many conditions.  Well, then, that the three study together those concepts of the mental forces of each, concerning this individual stock and its activity, for, as we see, and as has been given, these should be studied in their NATURAL RELATION to conditions.  There is seen that soon there is to be some special change in the management, and in the activities of this motor stock - new dividends of a nature declared, which will, just before same occurs (9th to 10th of March), bring lower prices - to 117, 115, 113, 112 1/2 - from 19, see?  Then going up, up, GRADUALLY, with the sliding off, to 250 - 237 1/2, 240, 242 - sliding off again, see - then advance to 250, see?

7. (Q)  "I asked Son about Hudson Motor stock and he said he had asked his examiner about that also and that the latter had told him that Hudson Motors would likely sustain a good rise in price but might break - go down sharply first.  Then [137] turned to me and said 'Yes, Herbert told me that so I sold 200 shares at 100.'  'At 100' I said, 'Why it was up to 119 - why didn't you sell it then?'  'We have 600 shares left,' [137] replied."
(A)  Then, this is as seen, that those conditions as have been outlined must be followed, see?  Study together, the three, see?  The conditions as we have given will follow along those lines.

8. We are through for the present.
