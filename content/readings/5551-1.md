# TEXT OF READING 5551-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[5551-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/19/1929  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13828

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of September, 1929, in accordance with request made by his grandparents, Mr. [119] and Mrs. [120].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  Now, we find there are conditions as may be aided, and there are conditions that - corrected - will assist the body much in its developments, physical and mental.  While these conditions as disturb the body have to do with coordination, this coordination rather the lack of the ability of the nerves to function with the muscular tissue.  This produced by destroyed tissue in the system.  These will all be aided by a general treatment, as well as by specific conditions as would be changed from time to time as conditions develop; for, as we find, many of the tissues are wasted from the lack of coordination between the nerve ends and the tissue that such nerve fibre and ends are to supply throughout the system.

2. Now, those conditions that may be aided first, materially, that will also aid the body in the general condition, have most to do with the building up of the body as respecting its blood supply and the ability of the system to store energy in the system, and with this abundance of the nerve energy stored in system there may be carried out - through the proper coordination of [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the system, and the manipulations as would be necessary to assist the nerve ends to feed through, or to re-establish resuscitating energies or lines of connection - which would gradually aid the body better in its locomotion, in its activities, in its resultant forces of the general building up of the body.

3. Then, first, we would put the body under those environs where there may be the possibility of the tissues of the system receiving such [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as may be accorded through those of the wet batteries, as would carry in same those of the [[Iodine|iodine]] - pound - tincture of same - suspended and charged in same.  These will aid the system in receiving the proper [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in body, and then we may add those properties as developments come, from the manipulations - with the general activities - as would be accorded the body under those varying surroundings - and we will bring about the proper coordination between the systems, in as near as the body will allow those activities to act with the mental, physical and spiritual being of the body.

4. Then, do that - and we will find we may bring much better conditions for this body, [5551].  We are through for the present.

Copy to grandparents
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
