# TEXT OF READING 4905-57

**Reading [[4905-57]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/29/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12982

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick, in Bosque Co., [[Texas|Texas]].  You will tell us condition of operations at the present time. You will give us any information that will assist in financing the operations and in the work on [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] itself, that will insure the success of same, and you will answer any questions that may be asked you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], the operations here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, as to the success of the operations, from the standpoint of the work itself, we find that everyone should be very optimistic about same at the present time, for we find that soon there will be no obstruction in the way of the drilling operations, and that in a very few feet the formations will show that the producing area is reached, and the bringing in of same will then be assured, for while much time, patience and money has been expended in fighting the cavings, and the conditions have been unfavorable in a manner; yet these now are soon to be past, for, as we see, sufficient of the cavings are removed that they now begin to make hole in the formation, about and below the bottom of the hole as once reached.  In the removal of the obstruction, by the drilling by or removing either; though, as we find, this NOW may be pushed to the side, with this drilling, or taken out.  In either case, this will be overcome.

4. As to the financing of the operations:

5. We find there are many that are still interested in the operations, and willing to contribute, still, all possible to the completion of this hole.  Each that are associated should know that it is NOT sending good money after bad to keep putting money in the hole at the present time.  There are others associated with those so interested in this operation, in St. Louis, in place close to [[Chicago|Chicago]], and in [[New York City|New York]] and in [[Ohio|Cleveland]], that will still assist, were they approached on same.  Each that are interested should be willing to ask. "Ask and ye shall receive, knock and it shall be opened." Ask for the money.  Keep hammering, and it will open this well.  See?  Ready for the questions.

6. (Q)  Would it be best to remove the pump or drill by same?
(A)  This, as we see, depends upon the conditions as arise. The conditions in the well at present are that the indication would be best that this be drilled by, rather than to attempt the removal and causing more cavings in the well, for the pump is now straight sufficient so that the drilling would go obliquely by same.

7. (Q)  Considering conditions about when may they expect to reach production?
(A)  In 15 to 20 days of operations.  By the 15th of April, see?

8. (Q)  Are there any conditions of which the operators in any way should be warned, regarding the operations or tools?
(A)  None we find at the PRESENT time.

9. (Q)  Any further information that would be of assistance to the operators?
(A)  There may much be said.  This should be sufficient at the present time.  We are through, then, for the present.

---

> My dear Mr. Wyrick:-

> I have yours of the 27th.  I promised to write you when I had

> reading that conditions were on the improve at [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], but

> like very much for you to, right away, make up a list of
> questions, and let's try it on the questions again.  I
> certainly appreciate your sending me Mr. Martin's letter, for
> of course such things are bound to make a fellow feel better,
> even though it doesn't seem to be working out as fast as we
> feel like it should.  Perhaps it is as Mr. Crews said the
> other day; "It would be a good thing if we could find out
> who's the Jonah."
> 
> Yes, I received the books yesterday from Mr. Collier, and
> have read perhaps half of them already.  It's surprising to
> me, to all of us, to read them, for there is so very much of
> it sounds so very much like so many of the readings.  As the
> boy said, it is really bewildering.  I appreciate, too, your
> sending me his letter.  I'd like to get in touch with him
> personally, if I might.  I wrote him, thanking him for his
> promptness.  Perhaps I may hear again.
> 
> I, myself, have been very much under the weather for the last
> few days, and am about "all in" today.  It doesn't sound good
> for one trying to do what I am, but nevertheless, such things
> happen.  I'm in hopes though I'll be able to get hold of
> myself and be as good as new in a few days.  We have had a
> few people in for Life Readings from [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] an adjoining
> vicinity.  The peculiar thing about it, we find that everyone
> that has been in was born in North Carolina, and practically
> everyone of them in their appearance before this was in the
> early settlement of this country.  That has impressed us as
> being rather extraordinary.  I'm sure Miss [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] will be
> glad to write you off any of these that we feel like would be
> especially interesting to you, especially of [[Egypt|the Egyptian]]
> history.  We have certainly had some very wonderful and
> extraordinary good letters from various people over the
> country.  One of the most extraordinary conditions, I
> believe, is from Missouri.  A lady [Mrs. [5700]] whom we gave
> the first reading for, perhaps three years ago, for nearly
> two years they were preparing for an operation (I mean the
> readings were preparing her).  When she finally went to the
> hospital, she showed the doctor all of the readings that had
> been given on her during the whole time, so AFTER the
> operation the doctor in charge asked that another be given.
> Now, others in that vicinity, that we have given the physical
> readings for, the doctors are asking that they come to the
> hospital, where they may observe the results, free of charge
> to the patient.  They want to see what the outcome will be.
> This is slow, but it's getting somewhere.  It may never be
> worth anything directly to me, but I'm in hopes will be worth
> a great deal to the future generation.  Things have not been
> going the best in the world, to getting the things started
> here, but I'm in hopes when we can get started to sending
> these pamphlets out, should they impress people in the manner
> as they did that lady, a copy of the letter I sent you, I
> hope we will be able to do better; provided we don't get
> pushed out by the rush that is very apt to start here by the
> 15th of May.
> 
> Thanking you again for the books, and for letting us hear
> from you, hoping this finds you feeling physically better
> than you have ever been, and more optimistic about the final
> outcome of our well, and with kindest personal regards from
> all here,
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
