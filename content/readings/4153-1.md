# TEXT OF READING 4153-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[4153-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/15/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11876

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[Morton Blumenthal|Morton Blumenthal]], 161 West 75th Street, [[New York City|New York City]], this 15th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by grandmother, Mrs. [...] and physician, Dr. J. L. Rothfeder.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Morton Blumenthal|Morton Blumenthal]], Conductor; Bella B. Sidelman, Steno. Dr. [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] L. Rothfeder, Mr. and Mrs. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Mrs. [[Morton Blumenthal|Morton Blumenthal]], and Mr. Edwin Blumenthal.]

Time of Reading 
5:25 P. M. Daylight Saving Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find that while there are conditions that need correction, these are in a manner through the development of the individual being corrected through natural inclinations as are being present and presented in the body for the correction.  As to the source and the troubles of such conditions these as we find would present that as must have existed even before [[Physical Body|the body physical]] was presented in the physical way and manner and also in such conditions as surrounding that presentation. For in the inhibitions of the physical being there were presented those conditions which prevented the full coordination in the distribution of that potentially valuable in bringing about the development of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the body.  Especially did those conditions exist in those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that give to [[Physical Body|the body physical]] the development toward this reaction necessary in assimilation.

2. In the administering then to these physical defects there has been brought to the body here those conditions that in the physical present the better normal development for the body.  As to the manner, the way in which these have and are being presented, these are in a way very good.  Yet, as would have been given for the existent condition in that as has been experienced we would not have given as has been supplied.  Yet in reaching that as is necessary for the physical development of the body in the present, we would apply that as has been started for these are naturally of the natural growth.  These conditions present themselves in the physical being at the present as we find them.

3. In the blood supply in this we find the body efficient and sufficient at the present in the needs of the physical development.  In the nerve system not wholly normal in its equilibrium of distribution of used forces or as are necessary, the needs of locomotion though the incentives from some of the ganglions of the body.  Especially is this seen in that of that as is supplied through those of the plexus as radiates from the fifth dorsal center for these as seen is the radiation from that as is supplied through those forces as are given out through the pancreas and the spleen reacting in brain centers.

4. In the effect as is produced in these is being brought about by that administering of those physical forces as are being supplied to the body to bring the normal assimilation.  In the functioning of the organs under these physical conditions these we find in the radiation in the plexus from same, are very good and can bring about the normal forces for same would be as given, continuing in that as being administered with that exception that there should be supplied with these administrations those conditions as would give the more better coordination between the sympathetic and the cerebral spinal nerve system by producing in the system that connection through the manipulation as would be administered by an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] through the adjustment in the cerebral spinal system especially in the dorsal region.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How long will this condition last?  Is it chronic?
(A)  Chronic in that it is prenatal in its effect as has been given.  Not chronic in as to the body to its development and should [be] enabled for these conditions to with its present ground would better itself in 12th to 13th year. [GD's note:  The steno missed something here.]

6. (Q)  What is the name of the disease as known to medicine?
(A)  Call it what it is.  It is a prenatal condition and that effect as has been [word(s) missing] in the condition as is of that as has been given in its description here.

7. (Q)  Is any pathology of lungs?
(A)  Those only as is affected through that of the poor assimilation and as is seen from the plexus as is affected in the upper dorsal region.

8. (Q)  What should the diet be?
(A)  The diet should be [words missing] applied and blood forces those that supply the fullest amount of equalized vitamins with that of a balance in the [word(s) missing] as will give the better stimulus in the corrected condition. The diet as has been supplied has been very good.  Give more of the A & B vitamins.

9. (Q)  The condition to medicine is called 'Asthma'.
(A)  The condition as exists is of prenatal condition with that of the predisposition of the inebriety of the body before gestation and during same.  The condition as is produced in the dorsal region produces the pressure in those portions of the body.  Build the system as to overcome these with that stimulation necessary as we have given, we will overcome these conditions.  Ett? [See?]

10. (Q)  We have here three questions, one regarding this information that is now being given.  May that be asked? How is this information on this body being given?
(A)  Through those forces as are present to those [word(s) missing].

11. (Q)  Are cosmic forces transmitting or having any part in the transmission of this information?
(A)  Insofar as the desire of those seeking information are connected with the cosmic forces and have knowledge of same, yes.  As sofar as those of the forces as applied through the material forces of same, no.

12. (Q)  That is, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] force connecting the two [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] being and that being given here, does it bear any relation to cosmic mind in the cosmic plane so far as the information being given to the materialized plane is concerned?
(A)  The case in question not an example of either for these are of only relative conditions under the present condition. Apply those forces as we have given would we bring those better conditions for this body.  That as is sought may be gained through application.  We are through.
