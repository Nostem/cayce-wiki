# TEXT OF READING 3158-1 M 37

**Reading [[3158-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/16/1943  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 10529

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the sister, Miss [3282], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, here we have a lesion in the brain centers. This causes not only these spells of lapse of control of the body but the inability for the body to control itself in an emotional manner.

3. This is a [[Karma|karmic]] condition.  While the body might be benefited, it would require long, persistent and consistent effort on the part of some very loving, very careful individual.

4. These as we find are those conditions which disturb, through the inability of proper coordination between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal nervous centers.  Hence oft the things the body would do seem apparently unable to reach the consciousness.  Things the body in itself promises not to do, it does.  The voluntary and involuntary reaction or impulse, as carried in the white and gray matter of the nervous systems tends in certain centers to run together and become confusing to the body.

5. We find that there should be regular periods of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] coordination by gentle manipulations; not deep, in segment corrections, but stimulating the areas in the 2nd, 3rd and 4th cervicals, in the 3rd and 4th dorsals, and the areas where the patches of plasm between the active force of the lymph centers connect impulses by the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system.

6. These treatments we would have at least once each week until at least eighteen are taken before a rest period.

7. Then leave off the adjustments for a month and then begin again.

8. Throughout the whole period we would use each evening when ready to retire, for at least thirty minutes, the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold.  The [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] would be in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water.  Using at least three ounces.

9. The large plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibrationally to the body would be attached to the umbilical center and lacteal duct plexus; that is, a hand's breadth (including the thumb) from the navel center to the right and two fingers up from that point.  The small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal center.  Take the connections out of the Solution when not being used.  Connect to the Appliance about fifteen minutes before it is to be attached to the body.

10. Recharge the Appliance about every thirty days.  For this body the Gold Solution would only need to be changed every thirty days.

11. Make these attachments each day consistently in the manner indicated.  Polish the plates with emery paper each time before and after using.

12. When there is the beginning of the second series of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, we would give further instructions. But keep up the Appliance with the Gold Solution throughout the whole period and through the rest period.  We would give the instructions at the beginning of the rest period for adjustments.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Sister (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - let us know which one you choose) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(GD's note at time of reading:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], to be used in this case.  Also see article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of this Appliance.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 5/2/43 Sister [3282]'s letter:  "He is 37 yrs. old [Born
> 3/7/06].  At about 13 yrs. he joined the Missionary Baptist
> Church.  When he was [[Baptism|baptized]] he was stricken with a headache
> which lasted for years.  Along about the same time his mind
> went dim, as he calls it, much the same as a light being
> turned down.  Since that time he has suffered more or less
> with fears and illusions of various kinds - part of the time
> not able to work at all for lack of mind & nerve strength
> - part of the time doing light work & odd jobs that require
> little mental effort & responsibility...can't do much
> learning books.  Has had 2 breakdowns, being so tormented
> in mind that he was hysterical & could not control himself.
> The last being last year & from which he has only partially
> recovered.  Some doctors say his weakness is a result
> of self-abuse or masturbation when a boy, others say no.
> Sometimes I think fear of hell in the next life is partly
> if not all the cause, & if there is such a thing as a person
> being possessed of evil spirits I'm almost persuaded that
> he must be.  He is obsessed with the fear that he is not a
> Christian, that he will do wrong and go to hell when he dies.
> He gets the idea often that the most innocent & trivial
> things are wrong & is led to do the most silly things to
> cancel this wrong.  And even when we persuade him & he can
> see the foolishness of it, many times, yet he hasn't the
> strength to ignore the thoughts but is tormented until
> he straightens, as he calls it, by doing even the most
> uncalled for things.  He is a firm believer in the orthodox
> interpretation of the Bible & any teaching I suggest that
> seems to conflict with that he won't listen to because he
> thinks that is 'just something' I've read and is wrong.
> Sometimes I think if something or someone could prove to him
> that he was not in danger of hell fire, he would get well,
> which I don't believe he is, but in the condition he is in
> his life is being almost, if not quite, wasted, it seems
> to me, which might put him in an unfavorable position in
> the next world.  I'd give most anything to see him well &
> satisfied & able to lead a useful life, & also free us from
> responsibility for his care so we could serve ourselves &
> others better...  He is afraid of saying something wrong.
> He often won't express himself in answers to many questions,
> even as to what he believes, & if he does he is continually
> taking back what he says, as he calls it, even to the extent
> of imagining he may have said something wrong & saying he'll
> take back anything he ought to, & is always asking us to
> forgive him any harm even when he has done & said nothing -
> just gets it in his mind he might have done wrong.  Sometimes
> he gets on the fence, so to speak, one thought says do thus
> & so, another says don't, and he is afraid to move either way
> & gets all nervous & hysterical, suffering terribly.  Afraid
> to & afraid not to.  I do sincerely hope & trust you can help
> us.
> 
> "He has never done anything the world would consider wicked."
