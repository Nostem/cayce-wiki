# TEXT OF READING 738-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[738-1]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/18/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 4579

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Hirn home on 6844 Nansen St, Forest Hills, L.I., N.Y., this 18th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. George Hirn, Sr., and Mr. R. N. Ladd.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [738] and parents, George, Minnie, Irene, George Jr. & Claire Hirn, Percy & Madeline Hirn Woolum, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] Costa, R. N. & L. V. Ladd, [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], LeRoy Coombs, Dora Alport, Mr. [675], Mrs. [662], Mr. [527], and many others (approximately seventy-five).]

Time of Reading 
4:40 to 4:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [738].

2. Now, as we find, there are abnormal conditions.  These have to do with the structural portions of the body, with the locomotions and with the activities that pertain to same. As we find, these have begun to show improvements from what have been the existent conditions; and there is hope to those that minister; there are resuscitating forces being brought.

3. These present applications we would not hinder in any measure; neither would we falter from keeping that which has been begun.

4. Yet, as we find, these may be materially aided with those applications that would assist those formations in the structural body, in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] secretion, that will enable those portions of the body in their development to bring about the more perfect coordination AS these developments progress.

5. We find, then, these conditions for this body of [738]:

6. The BLOOD SUPPLIES are maintaining a normal circulation under the existent conditions, and the superficial and the internal is aided; yet, as we find, the stamina is lacking in the muscular forces, in the tendons, in the structural body itself.  Why?

7. In those periods when there were those activities within the system of the destructive forces, the incoordination was begun or brought about by those activities because of the lack of the proper elements.  For, the body is made up of the elements in which the [[Physical Body|physical body]] moves.  Then, these must be kept in a normal coordinative force for the proper activity of each organ, each [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], each system to produce or reproduce within itself that which will bring about the keeping of a normal balance within same.  And when such is done, if there is kept the mental attitude of a body in its proper channel, there is kept NORMALCY in a physical and in a [[Mental Body|mental body]].

8. Then, in adding that to this body which we find would be of the greater aid, combined with the applications already being made:

9. We would add the life [[Vibration|vibration]] to the body itself in the form of the lowest electrical [[Vibration|vibration]], that may carry to the whole system an equalized circulation; aiding or adding to the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in their functioning for the body as to produce that necessary stimuli to bring about a nearer NORMAL condition for this body, [738] we are speaking of, present in this room.

10. So, we would add the Radio-Active Appliance that will make for such [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the system as to aid that being administered to the body for the corrections.

11. Also we would advise the use of a compound for massaging, to give the stamina especially to the lumbar, the lower dorsal and the whole of the lower limbs - prepared in this manner:

12. Equal parts of Olive Oil and Tincture of Myrrh, with a third the quantity of Oil of Cedar Wood; heating the oil to add the other ingredients - that is, heating the Olive Oil before adding the other two.

13. This would be massaged in the lower portion of the cerebro- spinal system, over the lumbar area and the limbs, specifically.

14. Keep those other vibrations, those adjustments, those massages that are being given.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  How often should the Battery Appliance be given, and where should the applications be made?
(A)  To the extremities of the body, opposite sides.  Attach one day first to the right wrist, then to the left ankle. Attach the next day first to the left wrist, then to the right ankle.  This would be attached for a period of thirty minutes to an hour each day.

17. (Q)  Should this be a plain appliance, or carry some solution?
(A)  Plain appliance; the low electrical vibration, to aid the other applications.

When these treatments have been carried for thirty to sixty days, then we may change; for there are constant changes taking place.

Keep the prayerful attitude.  For each [[Soul|soul]] should know: All power, all [[Creative Forces|creative force]], comes from the One Source. How will YE use same?  In the aggrandizement of thine own motives, thine own bodily desires, or in bringing hope, faith, understanding, to those that seek?

18. (Q)  Will it be necessary to put braces on the limbs, or advisable?
(A)  NOT advisable.  Of course, AS conditions progress it will be helpful to the walking.  But in sixty days we would give further instructions; for, as we have indicated, conditions will be mightily changed in this body - even in sixty days.  If braces are then advisable - or as the conditions are necessary they may be given through these channels.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file

(See letter [which was enclosed] to The Reilly Service giving specifications for Radio-Active Appliance.  They will make this up to order, when you submit the letter asking them to do so; and I'm sure arrangements can be made to rent or buy the appliance on very reasonable terms.  Mr. Ladd will no doubt be glad to help you arrange this.

The Oils for the massaging might be obtained from most any drug store, and compounded at home in the manner given.

After following these for sixty days, let us know so that we may make an appointment for a check reading.

Also let us hear if everything is not clear to you.  GD.)

> Dear Friend:-

> I would have written to you before in answers to your letter
> of January 1, but as I understand you have been advised of
> our son [738]'s recent illness.  For that reason we were
> unable to carry out the treatments.  Therefore we could tell
> very little if there had been any improvements.  But ever

> understand there has been considerable improvement in his

> to refuse him to make use of his limbs as he has the ambition
> to use them.  Although, right now it seems as though he is
> again developing a cold.  He has a slight fever and cough.
> We are rather worried for fear there will be a renewal of his
> last illness but hope that won't happen.  Outside of that we
