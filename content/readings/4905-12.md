# TEXT OF READING 4905-12

**Reading [[4905-12]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/3/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12937

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:35 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  Now you have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on the land owned by Madison Byron Wyrick in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]]. You will please give us the present depth to which this well is drilled, and the depth, with the log, that will be necessary to drill before they reach production.  You will also answer questions relative to this that I may ask you.

2. EC:  Now we find the present depth is drilled to 4082.  This, we see, has passed through many such formations as has been given, reaching the stratas where production should be found, and we have the indications of same.  That in the present depth in which the formations will be, and in which the strata that produces the oil at present time will be found in 4242.  The variations as has been given are the variations of the conditions existent in times back.  This we find is of the condition that exists in the present time, and will be that depth and conditions being those as we see.  In this we find at present a softer strata.  Then changing to the harder, gradually going to a very hard blue-gray lime, and in this we will begin the gas pressure, coming to the production of present live oil, and gas, in this depth as given.

3. Keep following corrections, and drill to this depth, would we obtain production, in this field.

4. Do that, for this is given as the ensample of conditions existing in the material, the mental, the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]].

5. We have finished.
