# TEXT OF READING 3253-1 F 44

**Reading [[3253-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/30/1943  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 10660

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3253], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3253] - real [[Vibration|vibrations]] about the place here.

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical functioning of the body.  These, we find, are [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] in their nature.  And those results from this in the present are poor eliminations, incoordination between the circulations in the body, especially in the variation between the lower extremities and the upper part of the body, and the tendencies for the torso to become heavy.

4. These then are the conditions as we find them with this body:

5. The blood supply is irregular; as to its quantity too much; as to its balance in the chemical forces in the body, impoverished somewhat.  And this impoverishment in the circulation slows up that especially to parts of the alimentary canal.  Thus the toxic forces resulting from the poor eliminations.

6. The suppressions in the dorsal area also prevent the correct circulation through the alimentary canal, causing those tendencies towards a fullness in a portion of the colon, and a prolapsus in the lower end; causing extreme pain at times in the circulation through the lower sphincter centers.  This also gives rise to those conditions existent in the soft tissue of throat, head and neck, causing rather the tired feeling, even with the eight to nine hours of rest.  The body does not ever feel quite rested.

7. In the activity of the body forces themselves, the mental and spiritual combinations, - the body too oft feels sorry for itself and self-pity can ofttimes get in the way of the better circulation, the better relationships in the moral activities with home and those conditions that surround same.

8. As to the activities of the organs themselves, - these are becoming more and more involved.  But if the body will begin soon with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrective measures as would eventually be given thoroughly, we would have many of those problems answered much sooner concerning the activities of such individual groups through hours of [[Trial of Jesus|trial]], or [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] that will make for the releasing of same into the activities indicated in the book itself.

9. Then as to the applications necessary, - for this body, first we would begin with those activities that would contribute to the general welfare of the body; giving the body, then the mild treatments with corrective forces osteopathically to reduce the pressures in the body and to cause better assimilations to take place.

10. Begin taking internally two drops of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before breakfast for ten days.

11. Then begin with [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  These would include a mild cabinet sweat followed by a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]]; using one quarter ounce of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in the eight ounces of water boiling in the cabinet to produce the fumes.  While all of this would not be used up in one [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]], this we would keep to use each week for the Fume Bath for at least five or six such treatments; massaging the body afterward.

12. For the condition in the rectal area, we would prepare this:

13. In one ounce of Glycerine put two drops of Carbolic Acid. Stir well.  Use an ear syringe or baby syringe to inject this into the rectum so that there may be the absorbing of those conditions that cause the disturbance and when there are excess eliminations.

14. Do take such a saline solution as Upjohn's Citro Carbonates; a teaspoonful in a glass of water, dissolved thoroughly, about every other day for two to three weeks; and we should find a much better condition for this body.

15. Ready for questions.

16. The hydrotherapy treatments should be given about once every week.  We will get rid of this tired feeling.

17. Do that.

18. The [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] should stress particularly the treating of the lower dorsal area and the lumbar and sacral area.

19. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] and physiotherapist) Copy to Ass'n file

(We would refer you to Dr. [[John (Apostle)|John]] R. Pike, 90 State St., Albany, N.Y., who has had some experience with following Mr. Cayce's Readings.  He also no doubt will be able to refer you to a good physiotherapist.  Be sure that the Fume Baths are given in the manner indicated.

[[Atomidine|Atomidine]] - to be used in the Fume Bath - may be secured from The Atomidine Co., Att:  Mr. Harold S. Bisey, 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y.)
