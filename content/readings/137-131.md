# TEXT OF READING 137-131 M 31

**Reading [[137-131]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/15/1930  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 521

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [900] and [195].]

Time of Reading 
12:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion - in vicinity of [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]])

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137]. This we have had before.

2. In the [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL forces]] of the body, these we find SOMEWHAT improved from that we have had before.  The abilities of the self to innately, and to through self raise the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the system, keep an even balance.  More would the body find self able to rest, were the body to learn - or attempt to RELAX self two to three times each day.

3. As to the conditions as cause disorder at times through the kidneys, and the alimentary canal - these will be controlled best by the body taking more thought of what is accomplished by the foods taken, and PRODUCE for self that needed.  Ready for questions.

4. In the desires of self, as relationships to others and for [[Purpose|purposes]], causes, as will bring into the lives of others the consciousness of the indwelling of the divine that may be applied IN the ACTIVITIES of individuals' affairs - keep self in that at-onement that comes through oft communing with the divine from within; for to him that hath much, much of him is required; to him that lacketh in understanding, stand fast and sure in the faith of the fathers.  Let him that THINKETH he standeth take heed lest he fall; for in Him IS light, and the light is the light of the world.  Naught may destroy that as is FOUNDED in Him.

5. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
