# TEXT OF READING 4383-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[4383-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/21/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12222

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno. Mr. [287] (?).]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Had this before, you see. Now in most ways the body is not as good as when we had it before.  The conditions through the system with blood supply is involved, and those that have been of the bacilli or bacteria that have been carried there show the effect as produced on the system.  The nervous forces have become more involved in some ways, you see.  The strain over the system has been greater.  We need to follow out closer to those as we have given in the system.  We will change for the conditions as exist at the present time.

2. The body needs the rest, outdoors, oxygen, carbons, those elements that will meet the needs of the system and produce the resistive forces in the body.

3. (Q)  What change should be made in the treatment?
(A)  She isn't taking any right now.  We will give these. Will be well if this is changed to the high altitude too, you see, for the time being.  We would take for the forces in the system to remove the seditives and to give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the blood supply that will be sufficient to overcome the conditions in the system.  We would take as a stimulant to the system:

Gum [[Camphor|Camphor]].............20 grains,
Sulphate of Morphia....1/2 grain,
Muriate Ammonia.........30 grains.

This we will make into twenty pellets.  One would be taken each evening before retiring.  For the properties for the rebuilding in the system we would make in this manner:

To one gallon of rain water, add:

Sarsaparilla Root........2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root.........1 ounce,
Burdock Root.............1 ounce,
Black Snake Root.........1 ounce,
Dogwood Bark.............1 ounce,
Mandrake Root...........15 grains,
Buchu Leaves............20 grains.

Reduce this by simmering, not boiling, to one quart, strain, while warm, add:

Grain Alcohol............6 ounces, with
Balsam of Tolu...........3 drams, with
Balm of Gilead...........3 drams.

The dose would be two teaspoonfuls three times each day. Let the diet for this body be not any of the stimulants that affect the kidneys or the liver in its reaction, preferably, only the green vegetable matter well cooked, seasoned highly, yet not too much grease in any of them.  Drink hot water mornings before breakfast.  Follow these, and we will find relief for this body, [4383], ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

Take medicinal properties, sarsaparilla root, etc. before meals.  We are through.
