# TEXT OF READING 487-23 M 20

**Reading [[487-23]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/12/1938  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 3582

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of August, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [487], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [487].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  (Special reference to boil on back)

2. Yes - we have the body here, [487]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions in the general are very, very good.

4. Those conditions from the boil - or the infection - not a boil proper - were not caused by the blood stream but by a mosquito bite that carried infection from carrion.

5. This made for those poisons in the area, and the system in attempting to prevent spreading created segregation by the accumulations of the lymph and the leucocyte about same.

6. We find that it is on the improve.

7. Use those that have been used - that is, the cleansing solutions (Bichloride, see?); then apply the ointment of D.D.D.  This as we find would eliminate the conditions entirely.  Apply it about twice each day.  Apply it to cotton and then place, with an antiseptic gauze over same - and then an adhesive, see?

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Would this be the liquid or the salve, D.D.D.?
(A)  As indicated, it would be applied as a lotion.  It is the salve, of course.  [D.D.D. Cream]

10. (Q)  Should the body continue taking the compound of Sulphur, Cream of Tartar and Salts?
(A)  Not necessary.

11. (Q)  About how long should it take to remove this condition?
(A)  How long before you're going to begin!  It would take about thirty-six to forty-six hours.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
