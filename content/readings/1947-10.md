# TEXT OF READING 1947-10 F 35

**Reading [[1947-10]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/23/1943  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 8414

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1947], Associate Member of the Ass''n for research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1947], etc., and you will answer the questions she submits regarding her health, life and work.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1947]; this we have had before.

3. There are many changes that have taken place, and are taking place with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]-body.

4. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body many are on the improve, many are not so good; some have not changed a great deal.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What are the blue and white lights I see with my right eye?
(A)  At times these are reflections from the mental with the spiritual forces of the body.  This is both a physical - or a pathological - and a psychological condition.

If there is the desire to correct this from the physical angle, have more of those treatments as suggested for the centers in the upper dorsal and through the cervical, as well as those that control the throat and those to the optic centers.  These should be stimulated for a better coordination in the circulation between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous system.

This implies, then, that the specific character of treatments mechanically (osteopathically) be given.  Some of those centers that enter the skull from the side, as in that area just below where the fifth nerve center enters, - merely by this being rotated, gently; not so as to bruise but as to stimulate the superficial circulation, will aid in a better condition for the eyes as well as stimulating the 3rd and 4th dorsal and the 2nd and 3rd and 4th cervical.  In each of these, it should be where the segments make for their multiple activity with the cerebrospinal and superficial nerves, - those in the back of the head, which extend to that area below the ear; these, too, should be stimulated for these corrections physically.

Psychologically, - much depends upon what [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] accredits this to, and what it seeks.  Know there is a way that oft seems right, but the end thereof isn't beautiful.

7. (Q)  What is the pelvic condition?
(A)  This is some better, but there have been some activities that have caused a pressure in the coccyx centers, as to give irritation.  The treatment for this with the others for the head will aid in correcting and alleviating this heaviness and distress through the pelvis, as well as aiding in relieving those tensions that arise in a neuritic reaction to arms, under the arms, the elbows, knees at times.  It is neuritic, not arthritic, but is more in the superficial. Better eliminations are needed, which have been prevented - the better evacuations or eliminations from colon - by those pressures in the coccyx center and the lower lumbars. [[Colon Hydrotherapy|Colonic]] irrigations would be most helpful in keeping better eliminations; in combination with those corrections physically.

8. (Q)  Is Dr. Tucker (woman) [[[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] H. Tucker, D.O.] in Durham alright for treating me?
(A)  If she will do it as indicated!  If not, don't do it!

9. (Q)  Suggestions for hearing.
(A)  Any stimulation to the optics will also stimulate the auditory forces.  These will be almost one and the same. Only extend this to that area between the 3rd and 4th cervical more for hearing, as well as around the frontal and back portion of the ear; so that the drainage from ear to the mouth - or in the Eustachian tubes - may be kept active so as to produce cleansing.

10. (Q)  Teeth and gums?
(A)  Keep a good [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  A good stimulation for the gums would be the use of Ipana, with Ipsab as a gum [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

11. (Q)  Regarding my work:  Should I write a book on Physical Developments for Adolescents?  If so, when?
(A)  As soon as you can write it!  This, as indicated, should be a channel or an outlet for the body, in its abilities towards physical education for the teen age - or those from ten to twenty, especially.

12. (Q)  What company would be best to submit it to?
(A)  Depends upon how well it is written!  Any company that deals in such.  It may be published even by one of the school presses, if it is properly presented; as the Harvard Press, the Princeton Press, or the Columbia Press.  For, any of these would accept such as this for a general improvement to a needed condition for educational [[Purpose|purposes]].

13. (Q)  Any further suggestions on this?
(A)  You'll have to commence first!  Thinking of it isn't beginning!  It's only just thinking as yet.

14. (Q)  Is this what was meant by a previous reading as being a plan that would be used in a national capacity, or has that opportunity passed?
(A)  As just indicated.

15. (Q)  Should I return next summer to the girls camp to teach this work?
(A)  As we find, this would be well.  But this is not sure, for it appears that there are choices by self and circumstances by others that will have to do with it, or that will control.  But it would be well to choose this, if it is desired by the body.  Do not force self to do something unless it is creative in its nature, that is not wholly in sympathy with the [[Purpose|purposes]] and [[Ideal|ideals]] of the body.

Remember:  Thy body, thy mind, thy [[Soul|soul]] are as individuals. They are one, but there may oft be cross-[[Purpose|purposes]] (and who should know better than you!) between the [[Ideal|ideals]] and what others say, and what you really think.  But two must agree, even when they pray about it.

16. (Q)  Should I continue teaching this physical work in the school now, or go to [[Colorado|Colorado]], due to the illness of my aunt, or go there before camp or afterwards?
(A)  Circumstances should govern such, and choices should be made by self.  This would not be given from here.  This must be according to the purpose, [[Ideal|the ideal]].

17. (Q)  Why have the readings' prediction of my marriage not materialized?
(A)  Because you haven't accepted those who have asked, or who have indicated - you have prevented those from asking!

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
