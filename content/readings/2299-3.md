# TEXT OF READING 2299-3 M 1

**Reading [[2299-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/29/1940  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 9047

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of December, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - some little better from the acute conditions, which as we find have arisen from overacidity and the general strain that makes for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance with the throat, chest and bowels.

3. In the present, we would first give half a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth with six drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin in same.

4. Then, within an hour, begin with the broken doses of Castoria.  Give ten drops every thirty minutes.

5. When this has been given for about three to four hours (keep this up afterward, but) begin with six to eight drops of Syrup of Squill, about every two hours apart; but keep up the Castoria until there are full eliminations.

6. Start the eliminations well by giving a douche or enema, using body-temperature water, of course, with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in same; this to be given after about the first to second hour of giving the Castoria, see?

7. Massage the throat and chest with [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]], followed with the Musterole - this of course weak strength for children of this age, and this especially over the sides of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the throat, and will be well if this is massaged also across the stomach.

8. Keep the feet warm by massaging them with warm sweet oil or [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]], and keep them wrapped well.

9. As to the feeding, - let there be little of those things hard to assimilate.  Use rather the regular foods, but not too much of same.

10. These are the better conditions for the body.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

Wired as follows:

GIVE SIX DROPS ELIXIR LACTATED PEPSIN IN HALF TEASPOONFUL MILK BISMUTH WITHIN HOUR BEGIN TEN DROPS CASTORIA EVERY THIRTY MINUTES UNTIL FULL ELIMINATIONS  IW TWO HOURS GIVE ENEMA BODY TEMPERATURE [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCO-THYMOLINE]] WATER  IN FOUR HOURS BEGIN EIGHT DROPS SYRUP SQUILL TWO HOURS APART  MASSAGE THROAT CHEST WITH [[Camphor|CAMPHORATED OIL]] FOLLOWED WITH WEAK MUSTEROLE OVER THROAT [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] AND ACROSS STOMACH  KEEP FEET WARM BY MASSAGING WITH CAMPHORATED OIL AND WRAP WELL  REGULAR FOODS EASILY ASSIMILATED BUT NOT TOO MUCH.
