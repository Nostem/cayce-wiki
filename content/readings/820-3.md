# TEXT OF READING 820-3 M 25 (Government Clerk)

**Reading [[820-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/20/1935  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 4924

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Warshawsky home, 3046 Webb Avenue, Detroit, Michigan, this 20th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [820], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Guilda Warshawsky Krause, and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                              ... St., N.W., 
11:40 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C. 
(Body and enquiring mind, ... St., N.W., Washington D.C., 
who seeks guidance as to his mental and material affairs 
and associations.  You will give him such counsel as will 
be most helpful at this time and answer the questions he 
will submit.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [820]; and that information which has been given the body regarding his mental, his physical and material welfare.

2. In giving that which we find would be the more helpful and the most worthy of consideration in these directions in the present:

3. While the general physical health is very good, there are those hindrances that would and do prevent the body's activity and engagement in those fields of service that would not only bring the greater contentment to the body but the greater remunerative interests also.

4. It would be well, then, first, that the body consider those suggestions that have been given in those directions respecting the health; that there may be the better body for the material manifestations of the mental and spiritual abilities for the body in those directions regarding its work in this material experience.

5. Do the first things first, then.  Make those preparations and activities in the directions that will bring for the body the better facilities in self mentally for the activities in those directions given.

6. Then in those fields that have been intimated, either in the service of flying or in those activities pertaining to the associations of governmental activities in various departments in relationships with other lands, would be [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which the activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would bring about the greater satisfaction for the body.

7. Making those preparations first, then, we would find that these channels would - through the present associations or affiliations, that have been an association with the body - make for the opening of opportunities in these directions.

8. Necessity demands that preparations be made in these fields, but there are those connections in which the activities may be engaged immediately through which the preparations can be made.

9. And then very soon, as those changes come about in the early part of the coming year, when there are those advancements or changes in these fields of activity, there may be the opportunity for service.

10. In the meantime this leaves the opportunities for the disposal of the holdings in the fields of service or activity in which there may be the settlings of a portion of those holdings through the sale of the oil productions, through either Farrish [?] in [[New York City|New York]] (as associated with the former associations) or the Sterling interests in [[Texas|Houston]].  These, of course, are cooperative interests, but through these channels - and through those advisory to same (which connections, as we find, may be easily made by or with one Wilson, in the oil service of the government activities in former associations) - these operations may be carried on, within the next few weeks - if the activities are carried out in those directions.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Just when and what connections -
(A)  (Interrupting)  How soon does he desire to dispose of same?  Make the connections immediately, then, with Wilson; if it's necessary for a go between - which will make for introductions to those interests in which Farrish [?] is at present engaged - which is with the Standard Oil of New Jersey, while the Sterling interests (which are associates or are cooperative interests with the Farrish [?] interests) would be with the Humble Oil - which are upon the ground and are active in the west [[Texas|Texas]] field.

Do that.

13. We are through with this reading.
