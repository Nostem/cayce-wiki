# TEXT OF READING 5566-4 F 7

**Reading [[5566-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/3/1926  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 13870

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [4631].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House and Mrs. [4631].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer any questions regarding same that I may ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, this we have had before.

3. Now, we find the physical conditions in this body much improved, and near to that of normal well-being throughout the physical functioning of the system.  The eliminations, as have been hindered by subluxations and pressure in the sacral and lumbar regions, have been improved, and are GAINING in the normal functioning and the normal incentive as received through the effect of those nerves and tissue as have been released by removing pressure on same.

4. To keep the normal conditions, then, there should be the occasional manipulation given, to keep the system, the lower portion of the torso, especially, functioning in the normal way and manner.  This may be done now through those of the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and manipulations, for corrections have been made, and if these massages are given in the way and manner as to keep the general system in its normal condition, re-activity will set in throughout, and be kept in the normal place.  Were these given in an improper way and manner, we would find that hindrance, rather than assistance, might be received from same, see?

5. Keep the diet in that way and manner.

6. Keep OUTDOORS as much as possible, see?  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be given now?
(A)  Once each week would be sufficient, if done good and proper, see?

8. (Q)  Can the manipulations be given in the right and proper way by Mrs. [2457] [Mrs. [2457] had studied neuropathy technique at Southern Neuropathic College, Montgomery, Ala.], of ... St., ..., Ala.?
(A)  Yes - very good!

9. (Q)  Is it safe for the body now to start to school this fall?
(A)  Start whenever it gets ready - for the normal functioning of the body, in its blood and in its activity, will be better than being kept housed up, or being kept from it.  The body needs to be out.  It has been housed too much, you see.

10. That is all the questions.

11. We are through with this reading.
