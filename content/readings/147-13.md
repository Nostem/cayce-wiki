# TEXT OF READING 147-13 F 50

**Reading [[147-13]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/20/1924  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 615

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in this body are improved in more than one way from that we had before.  While we have some disturbing factors in the physical at the present, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that have been accorded recently to the body [via the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]] are in accord with those as given, and the system is [[Vibration|vibrating]] better to those conditions.  This solution and [[Vibration|vibration]] is at the present time very good.  There will be the necessity in fifteen to twenty-one days of renewing these ingredients for this vibration, and this should be done in the same manner [as given in 147-12], and to the same amount ofdischarge as given when this was first given in this body, and now we find the charge is at the correct ohmage.  The body needs only to keep these properties.

2. Keep the mental fit.  Be regular with the meals more than the body has been in the past.  Do not take the meats, but the juices of meats, and take regular diet that will give the building of blood and tissue in the system, that the stored energy as being accorded and assisting the body at the present time may have wherewithal to vibrate with. Unless this is done the body need not expect the spended energy to restore vibrations to accord with the auditory forces.

3. Do this.  We will bring the correct vibrations for this body, [147].

4. (Q)  Is there an instrument made to tell when the solution should be recharged?
(A)  There is.  Ohmeter.

5. (Q)  Should the contents of the battery be changed each time the battery is recharged?
(A)  It should, save the [[Iodine|iodine]] solution.

6. (Q)  Is the container containing the [[Iodine|iodine]] alright?
(A)  If it had not been we would have given it to you.
