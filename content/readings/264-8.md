# TEXT OF READING 264-8 F 39

**Reading [[264-8]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/18/1930  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 1539

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [264], and Drs. Lydic and Wigal.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  We find the general physical conditions of body still show improvement, as to the cooperative forces and the retention of those conditions which will aid and abet the physical conditions becoming nearer to that of normalcy.  Keep same. Keep up those conditions as have been and are being applied for the corrective forces in the body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is diet correct?
(A)  In the general, yes.

3. (Q)  What is the condition of the blood?
(A)  This continues on a near even keel; though we find variations at times, this is keeping NEARER in a proper balance.

4. (Q)  Is Armour's Liver Extract of benefit to the blood in this body?
(A)  Very good.

5. (Q)  What causes contraction of the growth in body, and of slight pains in lower abdomen and in back, similar to labor pains?
(A)  The attempting of those forces within same to eject or to eliminate.

6. (Q)  What treatment for same?
(A)  Gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], with that of heat.

7. (Q)  What kind of heat?
(A)  Pad, or salt packs.

8. (Q)  Under the present conditions is an operation necessary?
(A)  Not under present conditions.

9. (Q)  Is growth being absorbed and eliminated?
(A)  It's reduced in size.

10. (Q)  Should the dosage of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom salts]], the [[Castor Oil|castor oil]] packs and salts packs be continued?
(A)  They should.

11. (Q)  Should there be any change in any of the treatment now being given?
(A)  Only the change when those pains, as has been indicated.

12. (Q)  Any spiritual or mental advice to this body?
(A)  Keep in that at-onement as has been outlined and indicated for this body.  This will aid most in keeping that even keel, and consciousness of the divine within, to bring about the desire of the mental, the physical, and [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]].

13. (Q)  How long before growth will be absorbed or eliminated?
(A)  How long is tomorrow?

14. That is all the questions regarding this body.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
