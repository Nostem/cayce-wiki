# TEXT OF READING 3471-2 F 55 (Widow Gov't. Clerk)

**Reading [[3471-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/24/1944  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 10948

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self [3471], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3471]; this we have had before.

3. While there are some improvements, the body is continuing to have a great deal of disturbance.

4. As we find, we would keep the adjustments but we would use once more the Packs, cleansing the alimentary canal.

5. Then we would take, following this, each day one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] for five days in succession.  On the fifth day take five drops, and then have a saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath.  This pretty warm.  Massage thoroughly into the body while it is in the bath, using at least forty gallons of water and at least twenty pounds of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]].

6. Then we would rest three or four days.  During the time have at least two of the adjustments and then take the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] again for five days.  Then have the bath again.

7. Use at least three of these and we should find help.

8. Do have the oil rubs after the baths have been given.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What is the reason for this peculiar weakness that has always tortured me?
(A)  The poisons in the system.

11. (Q)  Is [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] the correct treatment?  (Dr. Musselman is giving me [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] and describes it by saying the body has no "tone".)
(A)  We don't find that as one of the necessary treatments in this condition.

12. (Q)  Is it best, then, to discontinue?
(A)  These are detracting rather than aiding.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Musselman) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

> R1. 9/26/44 [3471]'s letter to EC:
> 
> "I suppose the little notice in the Bulletin I received
> yesterday means that my appointment for a reading, September
> 15, was cancelled.
> 
> "I was depending so completely upon the advice I was to get
> from it that I hardly know what to do or where to turn.
> 
> "I have been holding on at this work trying to wait for it.
> The production requirements have been made so stiff and they
> hold 'demotion' and 'no promotions' over us as whips, as they
> are trying to 'freeze out' we older workers and get in 'new
> blood' as the head of our section phrases it.  I have derived
> so much help from the physical check ups that I was resting
> on the thought of help in my other problems.
> 
> "If that means it is cancelled and I am not to receive it at
> all, please tell me or if I am to receive it at some later
> date, please give me definite information as to what or when.
> The nerve strain of not knowing definitely wears on me.  I am
> enclosing an airmail stamped addressed envelope and I would
> appreciate deeply hearing as soon as you possibly can tell
> me.
> 
> "My health is already improving more under the treatment you
> gave me than it had in four years of treatments from
> different doctors.  I seemed to be slowly and painfully dying
> until you helped me.
> 
> "If I am not to have the reading I am going to try to find
> other work.  I may be dismissed after the emergency and
> thought I should know what to do.  I seem to be where I can
> hardly judge what to do..."
> 
> R2. 10/15/44 [3471]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> "Thank you for the letter and check enclosed.  I am surely
> sorry about your ill health.  It is so very discouraging to
> wish to do so much good and not be able to do it.  I do hope
> you improve soon.  I am so happy over the improvement that
> has already taken place in my health.  I feel as though I had
> been in a dark cellar and had come up to the light.
> 
> "I surely wish to have the Life Reading when your health has
> improved enough that it is possible.  I know it will be a
> wonderful thing.  I shall keep in touch and write as
> suggested. "
> 
> "I am sending for some of the booklets.  Surely wish I could
> help a lot but maybe I can help some as soon as I get my
> doctor bills paid for the good they did NOT do me.
> 
> "Thanks so very much for the help you have given me..."
>
