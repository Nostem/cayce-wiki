# TEXT OF READING 341-29 M 21 (Student)

**Reading [[341-29]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/25/1929  Sex: M  Age: 21  ReadingID: 2668

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by self, through his mother, Mrs. [538].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
5:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Ck. Physical - special reference to teeth and gums)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body [341] - this we have had before. Now, we find the general physical condition of the body very good in many respects.  There is an indication in the mental forces of the overtaxed [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]], and this is produced much by the STRAIN as is experienced through the [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL forces]] - especially in the nerves, as is SEEN with teeth. Many conditions have existed here that are retractions, yet there is seen - especially in the upper right molar - ONE that will need OPENING and TREATING the nerve exposed.  Use in same [[Iodine|iodine]] and aconite, equal parts, and we will correct the condition.  This will be necessary for the relief of that portion of the system.  In the strain these have produced, the eyes suffer some - and headaches ensue, and those applications through the contraction of the nerve system from sedatives tend to produce bad eliminations through the alimentary canal.  Watch these, that there is not the recharging of system from poisons or dregs as may be left by the non-elimination.  Use, then, oils - preferably the [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil, or [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], to cleanse this portion of the system.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Regarding the overtaxed condition -
(A)  This has been outlined, and when we remove the strain from the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and produce the proper eliminations these adjust themselves.

3. (Q)  Not necessary then to leave off some of the mental work?
(A)  We haven't given it so, have we?

4. (Q)  Any other advice for this body?
(A)  Much may be given.  We have been asked about the physical.  We are through for the present.
