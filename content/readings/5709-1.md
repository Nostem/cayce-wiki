# TEXT OF READING 5709-1 F 43

**Reading [[5709-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/21/1922  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 14160

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 21st day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.                                    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now the abnormal conditions as we find in this body are not those of an organic nature and functional only in as a specific condition has to do with the functioning of the system, and those effects this has over the organs in their relationship with the functioning of each other at their various cycles.

2. Now these are conditions as we find in this body, Mrs. [5709] we are speaking of:

3. The blood is very good, save the effect the circulation through the hepatics has in its return toward the heart forces.  This is not a condition of the blood itself, but a specific condition produced by the circulation caused by conditions as are reached as we come to those here.

4. The number of red blood corpuscles are sufficient as also, the number of white, while the lucosite [leukocyte] force and element of the white does not act in proper balance always, hence the specific indifference to perfect coagulation at certain periods in the body's functioning organs causing pain and action over portions of the body.  The nervous system, as we see, is very good.  The brain forces show their action of balance of force between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal are well balanced, the body through mental forces are above the normal on certain subjects, see.  The action of the nerve forces in the system, that is, the physical, we find in the lower dorsal and upper lumbar, lesions produced by adhesions within the system through the abdomen, for through some of these organs we find there is produced the action to force through nerve and muscular, which govern the muscular by the nerves to the hepatic to increase the circulation, hence the high pressure we have at times of the circulation, see, in a condition of the nerves that govern the action of the heart, by the reflexes from a condition produced in the nerve ganglion by that which produced insensibility to the nerve ganglion of the solar plexus and which, with the lesion formed in the lumbar and lower dorsal produced the reaction as yet to the circulation.  The nerves of the sympathetic are very good.  The body through mental and sympathetic forces are well balanced, being neither too sentimental nor too cold, see.  The nerves of the sensory organism need to be looked after before we have this reaction from this condition in the system, or we will have some trouble with the finer sensibilities especially of the eye and ear.  The organs themselves and functioning as organs are very good.  The lung, heart, as we have given.  The digestive tract is better than it has been, some time ago, the liver presenting the action of high hepatics and the effect as produced over the intestinal digestion and the effect it has in the circulation, both of blood supply and the lymphatics and ganglia over the functioning of the organs of digestion. Through the pelvis, the adhesions have produced to an extent the effect of the circulation and nerves only in their sensibilities of their present condition, though no organic disarrangement other than necessary at present, you see.  The kidneys and the organs of the pelvis present the reflex only in their action.  The secretions as are thrown off by the pelvic organs only express the nerve temperament of the body, see.

5. Now to bring the perfect equilibrium to the body, as this body's present conditions - are to be corrected without being a sick body you see, we would give that to the nerve and muscular system that would bring the perfect equilibrium of distribution of nerve energy and of the circulation by bringing it to its normal flow, best by use of the muscular system or by exercises rather than by medicinal properties. To overcome these conditions though we must have some medicinal properties to balance the system.  We would take exercises morning and evening of a specific character for each organ in its regular order of cycle development, that is, the exercises for the sensory system involves those of the brachial plexus and those of the cervical plexus.  Those would be taken regularly.  Those exercises for the abdomen and for the ileum plexus would be of a different nature to those of the sensory organism.  The circular motion for the head, neck, and arms, the twisting and bending motion for the pelvic organs.  This should be kept up for ten minutes twice a day for several moons.  Take into the system this:

6. Plain phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]], one half teaspoonful in half a glass of water to which there is added five drops of Syrup of Sarsaparilla, two drops every other day to this of a 10% solution of [[Iodine|iodide]] of potassium.  It should be taken each morning before breakfast for a week, and then rest a week. Do that.

7. (Q)  How long should that treatment be taken where you take it a week and then rest a week?
(A)  From two to three months until the system has overcome this intensity of pain through the intestinal tract, and we will relieve this condition through the pelvis, both by the medicinal properties and exercises.  They are taken together, you see.  Follow this and we will have this body normal, you see.
