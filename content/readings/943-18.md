# TEXT OF READING 943-18 M 40

**Reading [[943-18]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/8/1932  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 5818

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 8th day of February, 1932, in accordance with request made by [943] and Dr. [2119], Active Members of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [943] and wife, Dr. [2119], [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce, and Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will give the genealogical history of Thomas [[Woodrow Wilson|Woodrow Wilson]], born in Staunton, Va., Dec. 28, 1856, dating back to the year one A.D., giving the full names of the Father and Mother and the names of the offspring in each generation; also the date and place of burial of each; also the outstanding accomplishments of the Father in each generation and giving detailed description of the coat-of-arms of this family.  When possible, give information as to how the facts given covering each generation may be verified by records.

2. EC:  These facts, as we find, HAVE been verified to the sixth generation, and ARE the property of an individual.  Seek there.  Then we may give the rest that is sought, if this is desired in or through that same channel.

3. (Q)  Who is the individual that has these records?
(A)  The wife.  Part of the recent trips were made for the [[Purpose|purpose]].

4. GC:  You will dismiss this suggestion and information from your mind, etc.  [See 943-18, Par. R1.]
