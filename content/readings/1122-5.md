# TEXT OF READING 1122-5 F 21

**Reading [[1122-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/7/1930  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 6388

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2502], Drs. Lydic and Parker, Nurse, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
12:05 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.      [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Questions)

1. EC:  Yes - Yes, we have the body here, [1122].  This we have had before.

2. (Q)  What is the cause and treatment of mucous flow from the eye?
(A)  The cause is improper handling when this removed.  The correction will be the cauterization, osteopathically, of those centers from the 4th and 5th cervical as carry MUCOUS to that portion of the system.  Slow, but will materially aid those of the whole of the nerve reflexes to the head and neck.  Also aiding the speech, the auditory centers, the vocal box even itself.  Do that.  The cauterization, in this manner, means restricting the upper portion and relaxing the center in those centers.  May be delicately done, yes - certainly - but may be accomplished, AND osteopathically!

3. (Q)  What is the cause and treatment of pain in lower left abdomen?
(A)  That from the restriction produced by the condition existent in the coccyx and sacral.  By heat and by relaxation these may be materially aided.  When pain is severe, relax same with heat - heat that is wet, not just dry heat.  See?

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading for the present.  We may bring changes for this body that may prove very, very, beneficial.  Slow at times, to be sure, but the basic forces as are being accomplished are helpful to the physical and to the mental.  Keep the mental more in that attunement with those forces of the divine within.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mrs. [mother]
