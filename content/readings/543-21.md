# TEXT OF READING 543-21 F 25

**Reading [[543-21]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/30/1934  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 3854

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of August, 1934.  [This Reading was volunteered by Edgar Cayce, without a request being received for an appointment, follow ing a check Life Reading 338-3, on 8/30/34, after [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] had given the waking suggestion.]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. Cayce and Chester Robinson.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M.

1. EC:  Calomel - Colocynth - Rhubarb.  These should be given to [543]; for the mercury in the Calomel will rid system of the poisons from the burn, and the Colocynth will prevent the too great a strain upon the secretions from the liver.  The Rhubarb will tone the activity of the lacteal ducts and thus aid in preventing these great strains at this time.

2. Three tablets - C.R.C.  One each day for three days.

[Mr. Cayce then resumed the normal breathing in response to previous suggestion given by [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] and woke up - without it being necessary to give him another waking suggestion.]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/31/34 EC's ltr.:  "I have yours of the 28th...  I notice
> what your mother wrote on the back of your letter.  For some
> unknown reason to us, though it possibly might have been
> quite necessary or it might have been at a time when you
> needed it, but she asked about your bilious attacks, and
> yesterday afternoon in getting a reading for someone else, as
> we were through the enclosed came through.  I wondered if it
> was at a time that you were having one of the seizures or one
> of the times that you were thinking or feeling that you would
> like to know what the reading would say.  You will notice
> the time on it, and if you can remember this, don't forget
> to let us know about it."
> 
> R2. GD's note:  Miss [543] told me that her letter had been
> written in the morning of 8/23/34 but her mother had added
> the P.S. in the afternoon about the time the reading was
> given.  The letter did not get mailed until the next day.
> Mr. Cayce did not receive it until the morning of 8/31/34.
> Miss [543] explained that her mother was under quite a
> strain because of [543]'s illness and extra care due to the
> leg burn.  Apparently Mr. Cayce had picked up this need and
> answered it at the same moment the mother was expressing it
> on paper.
> 
> R3. 9/11/34 "Many thanks for the last letter and for enclosing
> the information you rec'd in a rdg. for someone else.  I
> appreciate also the interest you've taken in sending some
> remedy for my burn [See EC's letter 8/21/34 under 543-19
> Reports].  But I'm glad to say the latter is mending nicely
> and I shall soon take off the bandage...
> 
> "I feel that the Mayblossom Bitters will help me.  It, the
> reading's suggestions, are doing just as they came through.
> I'm having more severe seizures but in the end I'll be the
> better, if Mother dear can just stand up under the strain of
> it all."
> 
> R4. 11/21/34 "I certainly had some hard seizures when [since] I
> last wrote you, but they have checked up considerably...
> 
> "Brother, I can see a vast difference since taking the
> Bitters.  Whether or not the family can as yet I cannot say.
> Of course I realize it will take time, and likely a long time
> at that."
> 
> R5. 1/6/35 She submitted questions for a ck. rdg., mostly about
> diet.
> 
> R6. 1/14/35 "When the rdg. is rec'd I will get either Daddy
> [1212] or Mother to send you a check for it and the Bitters
> together.  I will reimburse them [from my allowance] but they
> can write checks and I cannot...  I shall not become
> discouraged.  Rather I believe I'm becoming more optimistic
> about my condition as the years go on."
> 
> R7. 1/16/35 See 543-22.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 8/25/34 See 543-20 for epilepsy, also for severe electrical
> burn on leg which she suffered during an attack about a month
> ago.
> 
> B2. 8/28/34 Letter expressed disappointment that all her previous
> questions were not ans'd.  Her mother added a P.S. later when
> mailing the letter, asking what [543] should take when
> bilious:  "Having to stay quiet on account of the intense
> burn on her leg, her liver has become clogged, consequently
> improper eliminations."
> 
> B3. 8/30/34 P.M. EC volunteered a reply, without the suggestion
> being given to obtain it, although he had not rec'd above
> letter and did not get it until the following morning,
> 8/31/34.
