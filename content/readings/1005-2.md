# TEXT OF READING 1005-2 M 16 (Student, Protestant)

**Reading [[1005-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/23/1925  Sex: M  Age: 16  ReadingID: 6062

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23rd day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [1005], via [341].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born Nov. 21, 1908, in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                     ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the Universe, and the Universal Forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life.  Also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and to that which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, with those relations with the Universe and Universal Forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present entity, with those relations with the Universal Forces as give those as urges from the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane and how they affect the individual of the present day.

3. In the entering, then, we find with this entity there are many contending influences from the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspect. Hence the various elements through which the entity may expect to pass in the present earth's plane.  The position, we see, is taken from that of Venus and Uranus, with Mercury and Jupiter, with adverse influences in Saturn and Neptune.

4. Then, we find these as influences or urges, as are latent and manifested at the present appearance:

5. One who is very quick tempered and unreasonable when such occurs, without thought of self or others, yet one who does not hold malice or resentment against any individual.  Then beware of that environment.

6. One who feels the call and urge of all the pleasures in earth's forces.  Hence one that will need much counsel and advice in the gaining of the aspects of the results of same in individual life.  One who has high ennobling influences in those with whom the entity contacts that the entity attempts to help, yet one too often harkens to those that should not be the leader of the entity.  One that has the ability to become leader, yet ever in the position of desiring to be led than to be leader.

7. One we find who has turn towards things mechanical, from the viewpoint of the application of same for use of man.  One we find who will through such bring much to self.  One we find who can prepare self to become a salesman of any condition pertaining, especially, to machinery.

8. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]]:

9. In the one before this we find in that of the entity who was the one to become the King of [[France|France]], when the father [5618] and mother [760] [Louis 16th and Marie Antoinette] were at that time beheaded, and the entity then only gained in the knowledge of the earth's forces through the things which the entity suffered, for the life at that time was only half a score years plus five.

10. The entity then finds that urge from this to partake of all the sports or pleasures as may be found - yet developed in that plane.

11. In the one before this we find in the days of the Crusade. The entity then in that of the second in command of the Crusaders, and in the second Crusade.  In the name then of Adoirs, and was among those who first planted the flag of the Crusade in [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]].  In the urge from this we find the latent desire to know more of religious truth and facts, yet there is the ever urge to see rather what others will do with same than to apply same to self, yet never forsaking the ennobling influence of same.

12. In the one before this we find in the rule of the peoples in the [[Peru|Peruvian]] country (now known) when [[Peru|the Incas]] were in the rule there.  [See Source File Key #1005 for related papers presented at the 1964 Geological Society of America meeting in [[Florida|Miami]] Beach, re 1005-2]  The entity then among those of [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Worship and [[Ra Ta|the Priest]], in the name of Kaat, and the entity then developed in that era when the forces were given to the peoples through the teachings of the entity of their relation to fellow man and the relation to Him whom people served in the Creator.  In that we find the entity has the urge as pertaining to the mechanical forces in machinery, for the entity in that day prepared many of the pitfalls in the mountains, where the machineries were prepared for lifting and lowering the precious stones as were gathered under the entity's guidance.  Also preparing the waterways for protection and for assistance in gathering such jewels.

13. In the one before this we find in that of now the Northern country of the America, when the first lands were being inhabited in that region.  Then in the name of Ooul, and the entity then was the first of the household of the peoples who established dwellings there, and gained much knowledge for usage of others who dwelt in that land in the latter day.

14. Then, the abilities of the entity in the present life:

15. In that field of gaining a better knowledge of self and the relations to pleasure and labor to the individual development of self, and applying same in a manner in which the Word of the Jehovah may be first and foremost in the entity's endeavors, and applying same to mechanical forces or toward the salesmanship of same, for much may be done to develop many through the application of these rules in such endeavors.  Then keep self in that way and ever keep the rules of the highest forces in the entity's life, for through Him we move and have our being - so sayeth -

16. We are through.
