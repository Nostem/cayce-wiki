## Text

Date: 2/14/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 8117

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of February, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the formula for Pyorrhea Cure, as given in a reading.  You will answer questions regarding this, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the formula here.  Now, we find that Pyorrhea, as is determined in the human body, comes from a specific condition, and is a germ that may be separated, and takes the form of coconut with the head, and the formula as given as a specific is of such a nature as to destroy this germ and the seat of same.  The properties as are contained in this solution, when prepared as given, work directly with the structure of this portion of the human anatomy, the calcium with calcium in coating of teeth, and especially with that near the gum.  The vegetable matter as in solution a specific for the tissue about the gum, combined with the calcium, to destroy and build at the same time.  While that of the acid and alkaloid (not [[Alkaline Diet|alkali]]) gives the destructive forces, a carrier preventing the irritation to nerve composed in such tissue, but rather enlivening them and creating the coagulation necessary in destroyed, or of tissue in part destroyed, through activity of such bacilli as is seen in Pyorrhea.  Hence we have in this a specific, unless the condition is such that the root or nerve proper has become so involved that we have a separated condition within the system, and poison being given to the condition from the system through improper channel of assimilation and elimination.  When under such conditions, we find the body may become the toxin center from the decayed matter, or decayed matter may become toxin center giving poisons in the system, working visa [vice] versa, according to the cycle of condition in body.  This, we find, in the greater number of cases, would be a specific for that dread disease, to the human family dis-ease.

3. (Q)  If put in bottles and allowed to stand, does the mixture lose its potency?
(A)  No, for we have that in the soluble form.  An addition of water may renew such condition, through the action of hydrogen, oxygen, with calcium and the alkaloid.

4. (Q)  Can the contents of the mixture, as now made, be determined by a chemist through analysis?
(A)  Provided the chemist knows what he's looking for.  Could be determined in part.  Not quantity, or wholly determined.

5. (Q)  How would it be made, either by method of compounding or by adding other harmless ingredients, so that properties could not be determined by analysis?
(A)  Already, as is given.

6. (Q)  Could it be successfully put on the market?
(A)  As given.

7. (Q)  Is this prescription a specific cure for all cases of Pyorrhea?
(A)  Where the condition is Pyorrhea, yes.  Where the condition is as outlined - this may not be a specific, yet a great benefit, and local treatment to individual molars, or teeth, may be necessary.

There may be much said as regarding the teeth in the human family.  The care and the use to which same are put.  Also the development in the human family, as to numbers and as to the length of time these may be necessary to the bodies, for we find gradually these are losing the necessity for the condition as has existed, and this condition arising that will be met through this agency of Pyorrhea Cure.  One that will bring about the gradual change in this condition.

8. We are through for the present.

> First, let me ask if you have read [[Jess Stearn|Jess Stearn]]'s book, EDGAR
> CAYCE:  [[Jess Stearn|THE SLEEPING PROPHET]]?  If not, have you read THERE IS

> [[Gina Cerminara|Cerminara]]?  [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]] is available in paperback books.
> It is a biography of Edgar Cayce.
> 
> The [[Jess Stearn|Jess Stearn]] book is among the top non-fiction best
> sellers and I do urge you to read it.
> 
> As you would learn from [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]], [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis Turner]]
> was Mr. Cayce's secretary and took down in shorthand the
> great majority of reading he gave in trance.  [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] is a
> good friend of mine, whom I see at least once a week.  About
> a year ago when I was going through my files, I took out the
> history of the SBPCo to show her, because I know Mr. Cayce
> advised the use of many unusual raw and crude drugs as
> remedies in his Physical readings.  To my amazement, she said
> that many, many people were advised to write to the company,
> then only in Asheville, to obtain drugs they could not obtain
> elsewhere.  This happened as early as 1925, as you will see
> from [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]]' note at the bottom of the first of the 3
> reading-samples I am enclosing.
> 
> Forgive me for bothering you if I am mistaken in thinking all
> this would be of interest to you or the others.
> 
> I never knew Mr. Cayce.  As you may or man not know, I moved
> down here to do writing for [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] (for Research &
> Enlightenment, Inc., abbreviated as [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]]), because the
> readings came through in archaic, King-James-Bible version
> English, and I thought should be presented to the public in
> better English.  Also many subjects needed researching.  The
> first book I did was on the subject of diet, titled THE
> NORMAL DIET.  I will send you a copy of you want to see it,
> though I doubt it.
> 
> If ever you or others of the Penick tribe are at Virginia
> Beach and don't look me up, I certainly will take it very
> unkindly.  Your Mother would be very unhappy that we don't
> keep in closer touch with one another.  She was very
> family-conscious, as are all of us children of my mother.
> Bill would just adore seeing you and entertaining you in
> Bristol.  (He has a granddaughter, did you know?  Jane's
> first baby.)
> 
> I think you should trek down to your birthplace sometime and
> point it out to Evie.  Also Turner's Drug Store - or where it
> used to be.  Bristol is growing like a house afire, and is a
> very beautiful spot.  Also you could show Evie 324 Sixth
> Street, our old homeplace, where your Mother and Father did
> all their courting.
> 
> If, when I pass on, anybody up there has an inclination to
> send flowers, I hope that instead there might be a
> contribution to the ARE Library building fund.  We have the
> largest collection on esoteric subjects to be found in the
> United States, and it is severely crowded now.
> 
> I hope you all stay well and that this Easter season is one
> of joy for you all.
> 
> Affectionately, Margaret
