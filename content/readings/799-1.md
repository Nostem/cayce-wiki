# TEXT OF READING 799-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[799-1]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/24/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4847

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [799], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [317].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have not had before. As we find, the conditions are rather serious; yet there may be a great deal of relief brought to this body without the administration of narcotics or hypnotics.  But we find there are pressures that have been a contributory cause to a very much rundown and depleted condition, with the lack of the elements in the blood stream and in the functioning of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that secrete for the activity; so there has been produced a thinning of the walls of the blood streams themselves.  Also there are the poisons that have been accumulating from the effects of constipation, a very much disturbed gall duct and the general depleting; so that there are nodules - sometimes taking on very much the form of sarcoma nodules, yet these are not AS YET true sarcoma; they are rather the inflammation of the lining of the ducts, the veins, the arteries themselves.  Hence the pressure, as we find, arises from the 3rd and 4th dorsal centers - yes, the 4th, 3rd and 2nd dorsal centers, the 5th, 4th and 3rd cervical centers.  And the pressure may be relieved by adjustments, which - we would have preferably given in the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manner.

2. Now, such treatments will not KEEP down the condition, for the tendency has been for the system to react in an abnormal manner - from the general accumulations.  So, they will be simply palliative measures for a while, and there will be required a CONTINUED treatment for a period of time, and that there be a general CLEANSING of the system - which will require a much longer period of activity.

3. We would begin to use the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations to cleanse the colon.  In the first three weeks we would have about two each week.  Don't undertake to cleanse the caecum in the first two or three or four irrigations; only first the descending and first portion of the transverse, in the first, second and third irrigation, and then we would be able to cleanse the transverse area and then finally the caecum and ascending area.

4. We would be very mindful of the diet; and while it should not be too rich, it should be particularly nourishing.  So we find that beef juices, the dried milks - as Dryco, or as even Mellins' Food for this body would be particularly well; for it, of all such properties, may be so altered by the addition of water - rather than boiled or heated milk - in such ways and manner as to CONTROL the activity of the liver and its tributary reactions to the whole of the hepatic circulation.

5. When the body is being treated by the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] (and we would prefer Dobbins in these particular conditions), we will find quite a tender area in the gall duct area.  While most of the condition is in the head and neck, from the cutting off of the circulation to these portions of the body, these centers indicated should be stimulated; and after a bit - when there has been two or three or four of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations - there should be the emptying of the gall duct osteopathically, which will be most helpful.

6. For those places where there are BROKEN places in the head and neck and portions of the body, we would use a combination of Iodex and Animated Ash.  To one ounce of the Iodex thoroughly mix ten grains of the Animated Ash.  Use this as an ointment, gently massaging around the nobules, around those places and around the activities that cause so much distress.

7. The relief of the pressure in the cervical area, particularly, should relieve the conditions for the eyes. While these are localized, it will be necessary that there be used a mild antiseptic; preferably as would be given by the physician who would make the manipulations - that would cleanse these.

8. And we will find these will be helpful, at least.  If these conditions are helped, or when they are helped, in three to four weeks we would give further instructions or changes; for, as indicated, we will have very good days and we will have some mighty bad ones.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Did the two injections supposedly of calcium cause any change in my blood and body?
(A)  Not so well for the body.

11. (Q)  Is there any trouble in abdomen?
(A)  Good deal of trouble!  That's what we are trying to get rid of, through these conditions as indicated.  These pressures from the organs of the pelvis, we find, are rather from the pressures in the colon.  These removed, and with the blood stream aided in being revivified, should remove a great deal of this.  These will depend, of course, upon the reactions of the body.

Be rather consistent and persistent with this.  Have two or three treatments or adjustments osteopathically each week for the first two or three weeks, and then they may be had a little less often - but by that time we should make further suggestions as to the responses.

Do these.  We are through with this reading.

Two copies to self (one for Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file

(See letter [which was enclosed] for The Reilly Service, with directions as to [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.

See directions [which were enclosed] for making pure [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].

The Animated Ash, to mix with the Iodex, may also be obtained from the Reilly Service

Let us know when you are ready for the check reading.  GD.)
