# TEXT OF READING 3627-1 F 49

**Reading [[3627-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/31/1944  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 11126

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3627], new Associate member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, with the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the conditions here with this body, [3627].

3. These, as we find, are the result of impulses begun by subluxations, and then the effect produced in the disturbance brought about in the coordination of eliminations from the body.  Thus drosses and disturbances that should be eliminated through alimentary canal are attempted to be thrown off through the superficial circulation.  With the character of circulation externally, these find effects in the extremities, where the slowness of the circulation to pick up the return forces in the veins, causes this irritation and rash on the extremities.

4. These are the characters of disturbances that we find in this body.

5. While in some respects the conditions in blood supply are very good, with the slowness - especially when it is cold or when it changes from warm to cold, or cold to warm, or when there are great variations in pressures, we find that the pressures upon the dorsal and lumbar areas and the reflex from this to the brachial centers cause disturbances in the extremities.

6. These are the sources, and there is little disturbance in the organs or in their functions that will not be corrected as measures are taken to set up a better coordination in the eliminating channels of this body.

7. We would then begin first, with such as Waters, to have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections.  While the whole cerebrospinal system should be included, there are to be specific corrections in the lumbar and sacral areas, and the reflex conditions in the upper dorsal and especially in the 3rd cervical areas.

8. After these have been taken for about two to three weeks, do begin having a course of treatments to set up better eliminations.

9. This should be done by the body taking such as Castoria for at least a day or day and a half; about a quarter to half a teaspoonful every hour until there are good eliminations from the alimentary canal.

10. Then there should be taken two heaping teaspoonsful of either Rochelle Salts or Sal Hepatica to flush the system.

11. Then have these properties prepared to cleanse or purify the system from that disturbance.  Do prepare these in the order indicated.

12. To one-half gallon of distilled water put, in the order named:

Yellow Dock Root.........1/2 ounce,
Prickly Ash Bark.........1/2 ounce,
Buchu Leaves..............10 grains,
Podophyllin................1 grain,
Elder Flower...........1 1/2 ounces.

13. Let these boil, only in an enamel or glass container, until - when strained - there is only a pint left.  To this add one and one-half ounces of grain alcohol in which has been cut two drams of Balsam of Tolu.  The dose would be a teaspoonful once each day when ready to retire.

14. Do this, with the others - don't do these backwards.  Do the first things first, and do be persistent and consistent, and we will correct these conditions with the body.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Would this treatment relieve the itching inside of the ear?
(A)  It'll relieve all of the itchings.

17. (Q)  Would this treatment relieve the nervous tension for no apparent reason?
(A)  This will be relaxed through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

Do these things and we will have better conditions.

18. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, 1707 Columbia Rd., Washington, D.C.) Copy to Ass'n file

(1/31//44 GD's note:  See sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to Mr. T. R. Wirsing, 1001 Third St., S.W., Roanoke, Va., for preparing the compound in case you cannot obtain same locally.)
