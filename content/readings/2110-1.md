# TEXT OF READING 2110-1 F 60

**Reading [[2110-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/30/1932  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 8761

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 30th day of August, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2110], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Born June 27, 1872, in Peoria, Ill. 
Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Illinois. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, that are latent and exhibited in the personalities of the present entity, [2110].

2. In entering, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] astrologically, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] materially, mentally, spiritually, quite an altered experience from that as would be given by those adhering to those inclinations from the purely [[Astrology|astrological]] influence. While those tendencies as to the petite niceties, that are influences in June births, are apparent, those mental forces of the body - that influence [[Soul|the soul]] and entity's development - are much different from those things that would be termed influences or [[Astrology|astrological]] experiences of the entity, from the purely [[Astrology|astrological]] standpoint.

3. Yet those influences are innate that are manifested from the [[Incarnation|sojourn]] of the entity in its experience through the astrological planes; that is, the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] of the entity in the interim of earth's experience make for much that influences impulse of the entity, especially during those periods when the physical consciousness is laid aside.  The experiences in this particular phase, when studied or meditated upon by the present entity, will be noteworthy, in that many decisions, many impulses that have arisen in the present experiences of the entity, have been reached or concluded upon in or during the periods of the activity of sleep reached or concluded upon the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  As has been oft the expression of the entity, "Sleep on that, and then I will give my decision."

4. Also there have been influences in the entity's experiences in the earth that make for those things that have become a portion of the mental and material experiences in the present, from influence of earth's [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

5. This entity, then, and its experiences - both as to the sojourns in the interims and the experiences in the earth in the material things - will make for the entity a study that will be worth while in the entity's present experience, as well as ensamples in the minds of those who would study the true atmosphere of the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] of an entity, or through psychological influence the basis of analyses of any active force in an entity's experience.  With such there may be found much that would enjoin self, as well as all, that there be more dependence, faith, hope, confidence, real consciousness of the divine influences that are manifest through the experience, and sojourn of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Consciousness in the earth, and His redeeming love to earth's sojourners.

6. As to the sojourn then of the entity in other spheres, that influence in the present, these - as will be seen - have changed in their influences, just as sojourn or an earthly body in one environ partakes of that environ until there is seen much in the manner of expression that bespeaks the sojourn in that particular clime or environ; as to the manner of movement of body, in the choosing of the vocabulary in expressing a material, a mental, a spiritual idea.

7. So, in the sojourn of the entity in Venus there has come those forces in the manifestations of love, hope, peace, harmony, as sought in expressions of that that makes for the associations of [[Soul|souls]] in whatever sphere or environ is found for the experience of such contacts.  Not that there are never likes or dislikes, or expressions of much love, of friendship, of paternal love, and the like, that vary in the experience of the entity; for with the influences also of the entity in Mercury, with its mental forbearance - as well as mental expansion, there is that ability of the entity to reason and oft give rise to the expressions that some thought, some time, and oft sleep, must pass before decisions are to be made that must influence all; yet that all-inclusive love, even as manifest by Him who took upon Himself the burdens of the world that those that were under the shadow of that brought by the experience of the Son of man might in the [[Sons of God|Son of God]] find [[Healing|healing]], hope, comfort, cheer, even in the dark trials, the temptations, or the besetting of those that would make life and its experiences as hardships to others.

8. The entity, then, is oft governed by those experiences where oft has it been said, while the tempering of [[Lamb of God|the lamb]] to the wind ["God TEMPERS the wind," said Maria, "to the shorn lamb."  STERNE, Sentimental Journey (Maria).  [Bartlett's Quotes]] is as the balm to many who have come to know the real heart of the entity, others have at times taken advantage of these influences in the experience of the entity; yet these advantages, even, have ever been tempered by that which is innate in the entity's experience, "Father, be patient with them, they know not how they burden me even now."

9. Those experiences also in Jupiter have brought, and do bring in the present, the larger [[Visions|vision]] in mental, in material, in spiritual influences.  As these recedings and approachings brought in the present experience those periods when the shadows grew deep about the entity - and other periods when the [[Visions|visions]] as from the mountain heights have been the experience; yet ALWAYS the same - for that makes for that expression oft to the inner self, "Though He slay me yet do I trust Him.  He alone has the words of life.  To whom else may I go?"

10. As to the influences in the earth's sojourn, combined with those in the interim - the turning of the ear to the cries oft has brought in the physical much that is at times apparently lost in MATERIAL things; yet the spiritual harmonies, the spiritual music of the spheres, is rather that upon which the entity bases its hope, its pleasure, its desires.

11. From these also there have been made the abilities as the companion, whether in the home, the mother, the friend, or the wife.  These make for those abilities to be TRUE to that in which self in the present has pinned its faith, and the entity has drawn near to the Door that will not so long be opened again.

12. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, and the influences upon the present experience of the entity:

13. In the one before this in that land now known as the [[France|French]], and during those periods when there were the turmoils from the sojourns in the land of that one from the near shore, when there were the attempts to many to aid in the land for the return of this ruler to his place in the material affairs of the earth.  The entity was then among those who aided in the counseling to that leader, among those in the spiritual influences that were at times listened to and at others cast aside for rather the little pleasures of the present.  The entity experienced hardships and disappointments through the experience, yet gained in the influence and through the experience, - in the name then Curei.  In the present there is seen rather those abilities to adjust self, whether to the ups and downs or the ins and outs, so as to coordinate self's surroundings to the needs of the environs; yet aware - more often than accredited with - of that which is passing through the minds and experiences of the associates, whether those that are close or those afar.

14. In the one before this we find during that period when the dissemination of the truths were among those peoples of the [[Greece|Grecian]] period and land, when that minister from Tarsus entered into the land of the Lystrians.  In the city of Macedonia did the entity then reside, and - coming under the influences of that experience - gained and lost, and gained, through the experience; for the entity was among those that heard readily, and when the hardships and physical persecutions arose the entity faltered - for the pain to the body-mind, the pain to the physical associations, brought fear that was not easily overcome; yet when the final tests came, through the persecutions, there arose those that aided in strengthening the entity, through the associations from those peoples that had once dwelt in the Arabian land, and the entity gained - in the name Lydiaus.  In the present there is seen from that experience the desire that there be burned from the material forces all that would make afraid. This brings much of the hardships in the associations, yet in Him is there light, life, and immortality, and in Him may the entity in the present find that sustaining force and power - even as was gained through that experience, that there may come the joy of service in Him that passeth all understanding, yet makes for the joy and beauty of the life in such a way and manner that all who come may read the blessed feelings and experiences of the entity, even in the present.

15. In the one before this we find in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], and during that period when there were the turmoils from the uprising from the banishing of [[Ra Ta|the priest]].  The entity was then among those of the household of the leader, and a sister to Arart in this experience, gaining through those associations; for the entity burned out much that hindered in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful, and aided in setting those chosen ones in the right way and manner.  In the name Asmiis. The entity gained through the experience, losing only in the first portion, and the entity - as the princess of those lands that were so blessed by the banishing of [[Ra Ta|the priest]] - came into power through the return, and being rejuvenated with that priest in the upbuilding of the land there in that place and in the close surrounding lands.  In the present there has remained ever that as gained by the entity in the early experiences; that there is faltering oft in man, there is faltering oft in woman, yet in Him is that cleansing power, as was [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] found by the burning - through those experiences of the flesh, by the consecration of the abilities to the higher forces, that partook of the [[Creative Forces|creative energies]] in manifesting [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

16. In the present the abilities, then, lie in setting - by example and by precept - that which will become the lining and the light to many, even as [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] as Asmiis these were brought to many that faltered - in both material and mental, and the misconception of the spiritual attributes.  The entity in the present may bring the joy and peace to many, even by the [[Healing|laying on of hands]] - and seeking in [[Prayer|prayer]] [[Throne of God|the Throne]] of [[Grace|grace]], that is sufficient to all who will unburden theirselves; for the gods to the entity are the consuming forces that purge even to the dividing of the joints and marrow of a physical being!  HE is able to save. Keep in the way that leads in life, light, immortality through Him, and ever those who come under the [[Visions|vision]] of self, the associations of self, will be pointed again and again to that Cross that was raised by Him in that experience, through the associations of the entity with Him in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] period, and who carried it forward through that experience when the burdens of the world were put under His feet; for He sits at the right hand to judge the world, and He would not have any perish but all to know, to understand, the knowledge and the peace that comes with the naming of the Name that is above EVERY name!  Keep patient.  Keep sweet.  The BEAUTIES of the Lord are upon thee.  He will not leave thy hands empty, nor will He allow thine [[Prayer|prayers]] to go unanswered!

17. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
