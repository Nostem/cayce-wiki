# TEXT OF READING 2543-3 F 34

**Reading [[2543-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/30/1942  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 9538

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - [2543], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. 
Taken 11:25 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2543]; this we have had before.

3. While the changes are slow, necessarily, we find that there ARE improvements.

4. When the conditions have been existent from the congested area as to destroy or block or hinder the activities in the sensory organism, then naturally the tissue becomes clogged and blocked about the head, the eyes, the ears.  But there are indications that this is GRADUALLY being improved, by the administrations that have been made.

5. In the present, we would rest from the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations and corrections for at least thirty days.

6. And occasionally (twice during the next thirty days) we would have a general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment.  This would include a gentle cabinet sweat, not to raise the temperature too high but sufficient to make perspiration; followed by the Swedish massage or a THOROUGH rub with oils.  This pine oil in the [[Hydrotherapy|fume bath]] at such periods would be well.  Keep this up, even after the thirty day period.

7. Throughout this period we would continue the use of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] that carry into the body the solutions indicated.  Have the Appliance recharged.

8. However, about three days out of a month, during this next thirty days, take two drops of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water before the morning meal.  Don't overtake this, but take it ONLY as indicated, - that much, no more, - and three days in succession, not three days scattered during the month.

This we would do each month, you see - three days in succession during each month, but beginning it this next month, you see.

9. After the thirty days, we would AGAIN have another series or a cycle of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections; making these, then, once a week until there is the better establishment of the full activity in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and ducts about the head, the neck, the throat, the ears.

10. DO have the Eustachian tubes and their outlets GENTLY massaged.

11. Do these, if we would keep the bettered conditions for the body.

12. Be mindful of the diet.  Keep away from hog meat.  Have plenty of fish, fowl and lamb; plenty of fresh, raw vegetables.

13. And do keep that creative attitude of seeing [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] work IN thee.  And DO apply to those ye meet daily the same attitude of mind and activity ye would have thy Lord and Saviour have toward thee.

14. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Physiotherapist in giving the cabinet sweat and massage. If Dr. Kline does not do this type of treating, she might be able to refer you to a good physiotherapist.  Let us have his name and address, - the one you choose.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Dr. Kline when you are ready to go back to her for the next series.

We are advising Mr. Godfrey to send  you the re-charging solution for the Appliance, and the Gold; also another bottle of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], since if you use your three ounces in the Appliance you will not have any to take internally.)
