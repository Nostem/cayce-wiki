# TEXT OF READING 5093-1 M 43

**Reading [[5093-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/16/1944  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 13225

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [5093], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the impressions here of that entity called [5093].

3. As we find, there are disturbances here concerning which greater precautions should be taken.  These, as we find, have to do primarily with the activity through the alimentary canal and the effect upon the organs of the central nerve and blood supply, for these are all involved to a greater or lesser degree; lungs, heart, liver, kidneys, through the activities of the alimentary canal, and thus with all the stress put upon the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous systems, incoordination exists between these two nervous systems.  Thus we will find days when the body feels fairly well, and we will find days when, well, it's just an "all-overness," not particularly ill and yet not feeling good anywhere.

4. Irregularity in eliminations through the alimentary canal causes periods of hot and cold sensations and periods when headaches, eyes, throat and kidneys all give some disturbance.

5. As we find there should be these applications made:  First a good, thoroughly relaxing treatment osteopathically administered, then a few minutes with the short-wave electrical treatments, with occasional (at least three or four of these within a month) [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations or purify the activities through the colon, especially.

6. These we would do consistently, and yet not over-do it, and we will bring bettered conditions for the body.

7. Do have at least fifteen to eighteen [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments with special reference to those areas in the cerebrospinal system when there is the coordinating of the emunctory circulation, between sympathetic and cerebrospinal nerve system, which controls the emotions as well as the lymph circulation; those through the lumbar and sacral, 9th dorsal; from 6th to 1st dorsal and throughout the cervical.

8. We will be much bettered if these are done.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What can I do about the pain in the left leg?
(A)  Purify the circulation and remove pressures by the manipulations and [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], as well as reduce the [[Vibration|vibrations]], or change the [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the nerve plexus by using the short-wave.

11. (Q)  Will this relieve the stomach?
(A)  It will relieve if it is purified.  This will require a little time.  Do not take carbonated water or strong or malted drinks of any kind.  Do not take too many sweets, especially those of chocolate or those made with chocolate or any of that kind in the diet.  Do not take too much fried foods, for these are all hard on the system.

12. (Q)  Will smoking be harmful?
(A)  Smoking in moderation will not be harmful, but not over ten to fifteen smokes a day.

13. We are through with this reading.

2 copies to self (one for the physiotherapist or doctor who is to give the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, short-wave treatments and [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] - Let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(Please, at your convenience, let us have a history of your case to compare with our information - that is your case history prior to the time of your reading.)
