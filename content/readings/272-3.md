## Text

Date: 12/3/1932  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 1679

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 3rd day of Dec., 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [272], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions that have disturbed and do disturb the body; these we have had before.

2. While there are still evidences of infections in the system, the general condition in many respects is somewhat better than when we had it before.

3. As we would find in the present, in meeting the conditions for the betterment of the body:

4. Curette the disturbances in and with the organs of gestation.

5. Keep the douches to care for the discharges, using an antiseptic solution of one teaspoonful of creolin to the gallon of lukewarm water.

6. Use the foods that do not carry proteins or fats of any nature.

7. At least twice each day take internally those properties of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], that will aid in assisting the functioning of the organs to combat the inroads of these disturbing forces. Take from five to eight minims in half a glass of water at each dose.

8. Ready for questions.

9. These, we find, would bring about much better conditions for the body.  With this eradication, when there has been sufficient of the eliminations and sufficient of the stimulation to certain portions of the system from the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], these will necessarily have to be changed, that the elements in the system be kept in sufficient balance to prevent there becoming too great a strain on the organs of elimination.

10. Do these in the present.

11. (Q)  Where are the G.C. germs now?
(A)  As indicated from that given, they are affecting primarily the organs of gestation.

12. (Q)  Would another electrical diathermacy treatment of the cervix get them?
(A)  As we find, this would not be as helpful as the curetting and the douches - that DESTROY the germ, preventing any of the effluvia or discharge from producing irritation; and the taking into the system of those properties given.

The other applications later would depend upon how effective the action of the properties given has been upon the organs of elimination.  The activity of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and the excess of [[Iodine|iodine]] in system will so deaden the germ cells as to require the throwing off in elimination.  This will so stimulate some of the other [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that it will be necessary for the changing in twenty to twenty-six days.  See?

13. (Q)  Would I get rid of these germs if I had the tubes and uterus taken out?
(A)  Better that, before the system is so overrun by the poisons, the manner as outlined be carried out; for these being localized they would become too much distributed through the system.

14. (Q)  Please tell me what doctor in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], or in the U.S. for that matter, could give me entire relief?
(A)  That one who has given or will give the curettion, and that will make for the activities necessary.  The other treatments can be taken by self, see?

15. (Q)  Would the operation which the doctor spoke of for me to be able to have an orgasm at the proper time and way, be advisable; or would I be in a worse condition than I am now in?
(A)  Be in a worse condition; for, as we have outlined, it would be well to do as we have given - see?  The curettion for those conditions in the organs of the pelvis, keeping the douches at least once a day, evenings preferably, that there be no infection and that there is allowed to be kept the reactions properly; taking into the system that which will destroy - and this is not too severe, neither will it be harmful to the body.

16. (Q)  Will this treatment get rid of this condition entirely?
(A)  It will so PREPARE the body that in those applications which may be set for the BALANCING of the system it may be ridded entirely.

17. DO that.

18. We are through for the present.
