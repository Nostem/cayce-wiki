# TEXT OF READING 5618-12 M 62 (M.D.)

**Reading [[5618-12]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/15/1929  Sex: M  Age: 62  ReadingID: 13972

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by the self, Dr. [5618].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [5618]''s wife [760], Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Check Physical)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5618].  This we have had before.  Now, we find there are many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body at present from those as we have had here before.  Some are for the betterment.  Some are not so well. We find this accentuation much of those conditions as has been given that should be corrected in the cerebro-spinal nerve system, and the pressure as is produced in same, which have NOT been corrected, through the manipulations as have been and were outlined for the body.  These in their accentuations, with the changes that have come about in the system, are producing pressure on the sympathetic and on the direct cardiac and sympathetic cardiac plexus, from the 7th, 6th, 4th and 5th DORSALS, and affect the pressure as is produced on the diaphragm and especially to and through the sympathetic to that condition as produced when the body is prone, something very similar to that of asthmatic, or heart asthmatic conditions.  These, then, corrected, osteopathically, and the [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up as would be found through the wearing of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], we would find these would be removed and the body would be strengthened to near normal.  Well, too, that occasionally, as a tonic, and stimulant, when these are taken, that these properties in this combination be taken:  These may be either made into pellets or into capsules -

Gum [[Camphor|Camphor]]..................20 grains,
Muriated Iron................24 grains,
Sulfate of Morphia............1 grain.

Made into thirty pellets, or thirty capsules.  One taken possibly once to twice each week, or - if necessary - may be taken once each day, but with the application of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] and the correction of the conditions as exist in the cerebro-spinal nervous system, from the plexus especially of that in the sympathetic cardiac plexus, we will find the conditions would be overcome.

2. The diets as would be seen for the body - not too much, or not so much of the acids, or any that PRODUCES acids in the system.  Rather the juices of meats than meats.  Rather those of the vegetables that grow ABOVE the ground, and few of those that grow IN the ground.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] they have alright -
(A)  (Interrupting)  Alright!

4. (Q)  (Continuing)  or should it be re-charged?
(A)  ALL should be recharged every day.

5. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be taken?
(A)  Until the corrections are made where there are pressures, especially in the cardiac and sympathetic cardiac plexus, and that just below the brachial plexus - from which much of the pressure has been caused to lower portion of the brain and to the eyes, becoming accentuated now in those sympathetic conditions with the bronchials and the plexus as govern the pressure between the cardiacs, and the sympathetic cardiacs, with the bronchial reaction - or in the 4th and 3rd central NERVOUS plexus to the head, throat and bronchials.

6. That is all the questions.

7. We are through for the present.
