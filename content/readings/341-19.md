# TEXT OF READING 341-19 M 18 (Student, Protestant)

**Reading [[341-19]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/30/1926  Sex: M  Age: 18  ReadingID: 2658

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [341].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [341].]

Time of Reading                                     ... St., 
1:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as [341], present in this room, and the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, especially that one known as Andrew, during the time of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].  You will give an outline of the life during this period, and how [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] may use the influence from that appearance during the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], laying particular emphasis on the present life's work.  You will also answer any questions I ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those relations with the experiences of the entity in the earth's plane, and as how same may be applied in the present, through the urges as are manifested in the present by that experience.

3. This experience then, especially in that [[Physical Body|physical body]] known as Andrew, we find the entity then the second brother in a family of four, and in the early childhood one willful in many ways, taking up the physical vocation of the parents and brother, and in the days when [[John (Apostle)|John]] [the Baptist] began to teach in the wilderness, the entity, the body (physical), Andrew, became first an adherent and a disciple of that teacher, and remained close as an aide, from first conviction, until the appearance of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] to become the disciple of the entity's master.  When pointed out by [[John (Apostle)|John]] as the one that should be greater, and increase as he decreased, Andrew then followed the new leader into the wilderness, and was close with Him during the temptation, as is recorded by Matthew, and when the return to the seashore, sought out the brother, [[[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]]] telling of those ideas, [[Ideal|ideals]], as were propounded by Him who had been pointed out, and became the close disciple then of the Teacher and Master, following close throughout the whole physical career of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]; not as the chosen three, yet one as is given often the greater physical conditions to do and to carry out.  One often spoken to for the reference to others, and this is particularly seen, especially, upon two occasions:  In the feeding of the multitudes in the entrance to the city for the evening lodgement to keep [[Passover|the Passover]].  In the entering into the Garden on the last evening.

4. The entity, then, among those who held the higher attitude towards the spiritual teachings of the Master, and to understand the full conception of that conception obtained, or that full consciousness obtained or gotten in this appearance, is then necessary to understand WHY the entity has chosen oft in the physical earth plane to manifest lessons gained, or, as is to be termed in slang parlance, often has the entity chosen to seek, to know, "why the wheels go around," from the physical action and the spiritual insight that impels same.

5. After the dispersing of the followers when persecutions came, the entity, Andrew, then went into Mesopotamia, and those countries where the entity felt that the learning of the Master was obtained, during the early education of the Master, and the travels of the Master, see?

6. Then, the entity remained true to that teaching, and brought MANY to the knowledge of God that IS within every human physical being that seeks to know how same manifests through the individual.

7. The lessons, then, as obtained, and how the entity may apply same in the present physical plane:

8. There is seen, then, the urge as is given, as to how every measure of any truth is compared to that Master's teaching in the present, for, as is seen, when this is correlated with intent and [[Purpose|purpose]], and the first urge as is seen in a physical manifestation, this to the entity becomes the sin qui non of power, force, of manifesting forces in the physical, spiritual, mental world, and the application in the present is to ever weigh those experiences in the present, measuring them up to that standard as is seen and known, and felt, as is superior, and as high above all others as the heavens above the earth; as superior as the Sun's light is to the Moon's dull rays; as great as the winds are to the stench that rises from foul forces exhumed from the low places; as great as the mountains above the plains; and as the entity keeps these [[Ideal|ideals]], these principles, applied in the teaching, the writings, the labors of the day, so may this lesson, this experience, gain for the entity that necessary to draw nigh unto that drawing force that gives the great impelling entity the effect of the Master.

9. We are through with this reading.
