# TEXT OF READING 3313-1 F 58

**Reading [[3313-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/26/1943  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 10742

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3313], through her son-in-law, new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, conditions are such that there may be a staying of the disturbances, but these activities are of the consuming nature.

3. If there is the desire, there may be used the influences that we might suggest - though these will not heal, or remove causes.  For, as has been indicated oft, causes may be [[Karma|karma]]. [[Karma|Karma]] is cause oft of hereditary conditions so called.  Then indeed does [[Soul|the soul]] inherit that it has builded in its experience with its fellow man in material relationships.

4. The conditions here may be best retarded by the use at times, about once a week, of a magnet - of sufficient strength to raise a railroad spike, - this being passed over affected areas, see?  This will aid in demagnetizing or producing a [[Vibration|vibration]] that will destroy the active forces of the consuming of cells being enlivened by the infection itself.

5. Keep as normal an appetite and actions of the body as possible.

6. These offer the greater help here; not a cure, but a retarder; an easing.

7. Use the sedatives when intense pains arise.  Hypnotics are preferable to narcotics.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is an operation necessary or advisable?
(A)  More apt to lessen the longevity or length of this life's span.  May be done if it is desired.

10. (Q)  What is the cause of my condition and what treatment is recommended?
(A)  The basic cause is [[Karma|karmic]].

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Son-In-Law
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
