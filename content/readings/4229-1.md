# TEXT OF READING 4229-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4229-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/23/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11995

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23rd day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:30 P. M.                                        ..., Ind. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find we have many abnormal conditions in this body.  Many of these may be assisted and some corrected.  They have to do with the whole organism of the body, the functioning of organs as affected by the nerve forces, and in the functioning of organs and organic conditions.

2. Now these are conditions as we find in this body here, Mrs. [4229], we are speaking of, ..., Ind.:

3. First, in the blood supply we find the weakened condition of the whole system as expressed in the circulation; for, with the weakened condition, there is not sufficient of the carbons and of the elements through the ozone to give the vital forces to the body to meet the resistance as made by the conditions in the system.

4. These conditions, as we find, have been of long-standing. They began first with that that hindered in the eliminating forces of the body and affected first, that of the distribution of used forces so that with toxins left in the body, we find they began to act on the centers; producing the troublesome condition to locomotories, to the ataxia condition of centers governing the portions of the body to the sensory system as well as to the locomotories in portions of the body.  The points themselves showing the condition reflexed in the system, this condition being first through the blood forces, then affecting the organs in the eliminating and the emunctory forces, as the condition as increased to the present force.

5. In the nerve centers we find in the sensory force the responding elements of this non-eliminating through the system, and the gradual choking, as it were, of the forces that produced the condition here as we have in the head, face, eyes, ears, you see.  In the nerve about the trunk portion of body, or lower end of the solar plexus center, the effect is reached to the locomotories, as that in the portions affecting this center gave the rise to weakness in the system.

6. Those properties as given only have flushed portions of the body without lending assistance to that to give the vital forces.

7. Now we have left in the centers, from the lumbar plexus to the cervical region, that strain on cerebrospinal and sympathetic forces until the body suffers a great deal of pain to the head and eyes and to portions of the body - through the trunk portions, too, we see.

8. In the functioning of the organs themselves we find as of these conditions:  With the functioning, as it were, of the circulation of the limited blood supply with resistive forces, many of the organs become affected in their functioning; and some, as we have through the liver and in the portions of the viscera, show organic conditions.

9. We may give relief to this body by giving the forces and [[Vibration|vibrations]] that will assist in alleviating the condition without deadening the nerve reaction, as has been at times here.

10. We would give this:  In all of the centers where the emunctories pick up the circulation - that is, arm pits, the throat and neck in frontal portion, the abdomen, across the upper portion of that near the diaphragm and the groin - massage well into these portions that of Balsam of Sulphur.

11. For the head and eyes here, to relieve the pain in the first cervical and across the brow, every second evening massage - after the application of the stew or stupe of [[Mullein|Mullein leaves]]. The massage into the system of this Balsam of Sulphur should be of each evening.

12. Of mornings give the body the [[Vibration|vibration]] from this:

13. Two bars of steel, carbon steel, with a non-conductor fastened between.  To one end of these steels attach small copper wires.  To the end of the wires attach small copper plates.  The plates each morning would be attached to the ankles, one on either ankle.  The bars of steel would then be placed in a solution of very cold water, with ice in it.  The hands, while this is attached, would be, or one hand in plain water, not of cold.  Let this [Radio-Active Appliance] remain for an hour or two hours on the body, while it rests.  This will give rest to the body and more vital resistance.

14. Do that.

15. (Q)  How can the asthma of this body be relieved?
(A)  Leave the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as we have given, and we will relieve this pressure we have here, you see.

16. (Q)  Is there any change of the sight [to] be restored for this body?
(A)  It can be assisted.  It will be helped a great deal, but not restored entirely.

17. (Q)  How can this body be relieved from pain?
(A)  Just as we have given - that is, the relief to the body will come through the vibrations as are received from the forces giving the perfect elimination, and with this [[Poultice|poultice]] [of [[Mullein|Mullein leaves]]] over the eyes to the back of the neck here we will find we will bring the relief; as with the vibrations will be a great deal of relief to the mental forces in suffering.  The pressure through the lungs and abdomen forces will be overcome by the same application, - blood force rather than an organic condition.

18. (Q)  How soon will this body get relief?
(A)  She will feel almost immediate relief from the vibrations.  It will be of gradual growth to the body from the assimilation, to make the proper elimination through the system within seven days we will find a great deal of relief of the suffering.  We will find the gradual help for the body time for some to come.

19. We are through.
