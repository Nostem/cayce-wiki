# TEXT OF READING 310-5 F ADULT (Protestant Minister's Widow)

**Reading [[310-5]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/17/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 2348

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of June, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [310], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Sarah Hesson, Mildred Davis and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Ala. 
(Physical, mental and material ques.)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [310] - this we have had before.

2. In the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these we find in many respects are showing some changes for the better.  While there are some exaggerated conditions, and some that are causing some distress in the present, these are from such unusual external influences that the system is adjusting itself to them, creating sufficient of those elements necessary to make for a better condition.

3. In those general debilitations that have existed, the nervous reactions and the eliminations, these show some improvements.

4. We would keep near to that which has been outlined in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What causes intense pain in right side of abdomen at times?
(A)  This is the breaking up at times, or contraction of those portions in system where those of the scar tissue are reacting to the changing [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system.  These, it will be found, will respond to a dry heat; or, preferably, apply hot salt packs, with a wet pack placed next to the body, in those portions where the distress arises.  This is the contraction of the muscular forces that are acted upon by these changing conditions - see?

7. (Q)  Does that mean put a hot salt pack over a wet cloth?
(A)  That's just what we said!

8. (Q)  Should the battery [Radio-Active Appliance] continue to be placed as formerly given?
(A)  Continue to be attached as given, for here it is changing the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the system in accordance with the corrections for the conditions that disturb under nerve pressure.

9. (Q)  Is proper preparation in progress now for future work?
(A)  As we find, the preparations and associations and connections that are being made, retained and worked out, are in keeping with that which will bring about, with the changes, the positions that will be the most satisfactory for the body.

10. (Q)  Should steps for securing work be immediate or more aggressive than formerly?
(A)  As just indicated, those of the proper associations, proper connections, are being made.  The aggressiveness towards same, as we find, would best be in the latter portion of the present season, than just now - but rather as the year for the various associations and connections that are being maintained, or gained, are ready for the changes for the coming seasons.

11. (Q)  Should the home at 510 Church Street be retained for another year?
(A)  As we find, this had best be retained for another period.  There will be those associations and connections from time to time that will enable the body to meet those conditions that arise, or that surround same in the various connections.  These should not be, or make for worriments - but rather be in the position to meet, through the proper conditions surrounding body, those things that will arise from time to time.

12. (Q)  Is there any way at present that additional members of household might be secured for more permanent residence?
(A)  As is indicated, there are proper connections and associations being made that will enable the body to secure same.  Let it be known in connections with those already associated, and in the manner that has kept the personnel of those in the home in the same manner, and we will find there will be those that will make this their home also.

13. (Q)  In the L... - M... friendship, is best part being played by me?
(A)  In this association and connection, WE would give, the best part is being played, or the body is HANDLING the conditions, the situations, in the best manner.

14. (Q)  Is proper attitude taken in regard to L..., and what he is thinking in regard to loss of his work with Highway Department?
(A)  This, as we find, brings in some very unusual connections as to the cause, and as to the activities that were taken by the Department.  However, as we find, if the proper attitude and proper ACTIVITIES will be taken by the body, L..., and the associations and activities of those as connected with the body, there may be brought about that association and connection in those relationships in other directions that will make the more satisfactory connections.

15. (Q)  Please suggest any helpful service which may be rendered?
(A)  This has been given here.

16. (Q)  Any other advice or counsel for the body that will be helpful at this time?
(A)  There are many connections, associations and relations, that put the body, as it were, at its wits' end, in respect just what the attitude should be.  However, as that policy which has always been held is maintained in these associations, these connections, there is and will be given that proper activity that should be taken by the body from experience to experience.

17. We are through with this reading.
