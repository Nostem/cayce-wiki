# TEXT OF READING 4-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/27/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 23

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 27th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          [[England|London]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, the abnormal conditions as we find in this body has to do with the nervous forces in the system, and the affect that has been produced within the system, both to the functioning organs and over the system in a general way, yet causing specific conditions.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, [4], [[England|London]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], we are speaking of.  The body throughout as far as the circulation is able to go unhampered or unhindered is very good.  We find there are deflections from the full circulation through portions of the body, these being caused by the condition existing in the brachial plexus and the lumbar plexus.  The blood in its own elemental conditions at present are very good, though there has been at times back, conditions that caused much of the trouble now existing, so we have a condition where the affect of blood forces have produced the effect present in the system.  The nerve forces we find are very good through the trunk portion of the body, except as we have given where lesions cause deflection of nerve, muscle and blood vital force to be carried through the system in an improper manner and form, so that poor elimination from the sacral and brachial plexus cause the condition we have through the extremities as at present, for the liver and emunctory circulation has become so emeshed with the nerve supplying centers as to cause the contraction of the ligaments about the ends of the extremities of the joint of the femur from the sacral and locomotory centers in the pelvis to those that govern the tibia and anti-tibia here in the lower extremities.  The functioning of the nerves have been so hindered by this lesion as to prevent the co-ordinating forces between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal nerve forces at the sacral plexus.  The functioning of the organs themselves we find are very good at present, while each suffers in its own cycle from the conditions existing in the nervous system, yet organically they, the organs themselves, are very good in as much so, that is, not as good, through the functioning of the kidneys and the bladder as of the other organs.  Reflexly, there are some conditions that appear in the liver so that we have a laggard condition in the lower end of the intestinal tract that only needs be cared for by keeping as near as possible, perfect elimination through these channels without exciting the action of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] throughout the digestion too much.

3. To give the relief to this body from pain and assist in bringing it to its normal actions in the system, we would give two courses of treatment to this body, that to prepare the system for the action of other forces, and those forces to create the perfect connections in the nerve centers to give the proper [[Vibration|vibrations]].  We would take this into the system:

4. To on gallon of rain water, not distilled but rain, add six ounces of Sarsaparilla root, two ounces of Yellow Dock root, two ounces of Burdock root, two ounces of Snake root, two ounces of Yellow root.  This would be reduced by simmering not boiling to one quart, strain, while warm, add four ounces of grain alcohol and three drams of Balsam of Tolu. The dose would be tablespoonful four times each day an hour before meals and at bed time, that is, at 9 or 9:30 in the evening.  After this whole quantity has been taken in the system and the body rests for seven days, then we would take [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], fifteen grains in fifteen ounces of water with six grains of Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]].  When it's prepared, for the dose for the body, which would be ten drops, we would add, also, one drop of a saturated solution of borax water. The dose would be given three times each day.  Before the treatment with the violet ray each evening, one of these doses would be given thirty minutes before that time, so we would have the reaction of the metal in the system to act with the emunctory forces in this system.  Do that with this body.

5. We are through.
