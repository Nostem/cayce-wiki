# TEXT OF READING 4263-1 M ADULT (M.D.)

**Reading [[4263-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/16/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12031

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 16th day of October, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stallings, Brazelton & Hale, Court Reporters, Steno. Dr. [4263].]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep.  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep, and all of the organs of your body will function properly.  You will please hear me and answer all of my questions.

You have before you the body of Dr. [4263] and who is present in this room.  Now, Mr. Cayce, tell me the physical and mental ailments that you find in this body that you have before you, and if an operation is necessary tell me, and if not, tell me what will relieve the ailments in this body that you have before you, and which you may find needs correction. Dr. [4263], is present in this room.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  Now, throughout the physical, mental and spiritual forces within this body, we find the body well balanced, yet we find some few abnormal conditions. Now, these are the physical conditions as we find them relative to the body that we have here.  [4263] we are speaking of.  The blood supplying forces throughout the system are well balanced for the conditions existing throughout the body, yet there is at times incentives received to portions of the forces carried in the body in the form of a leukocyte force, - a condition that does not bear well for the functioning of some of the organs within the system itself.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

3. That is we find the white blood corpuscles carrying the warrior forces of the physical within their own confines, or units gathered from cellular forces that pass within the confines of ganglia of nerve force impressions that have been received from natural forces outside, and they become warriors both to blood forces and to the finer or better incentives of nerves as returned to the system.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

4. Yet the building properties of the blood supply from the red blood cellular forces is good, - above normal, we would say, of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as found in bodies of this age, or condition, or status.  The pressure of the blood force is normal at present.  At times it varies, due to the condition and number of nerves as found from both the white and red. See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

5. In the nerve centers we find a condition that excites the forces within the body as we have given.  These have been received through the symphatic [sympathetic] or super-sensitive forces of body itself.  We find this centered about the 9th or 10th dorsal region, from which they received the impression to the blood supplying forces.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

6. Hence a condition that exists throughout the system of a languid action of nerves governing the voluntary forces of the muscular action of the body.  The symphatic [sympathetic] forces arising or meeting occasionally through symphatic [sympathetic] forces are magnified to the system, and the action of the body is governed by brain forces or action, because spasmodic super-sensitiveness of certain conditions are due to conditions that arise to meet the forces in the body.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

7. So the abnormal forces or conditions that we find to exist or meet within this body itself is the balance of force arising from the nerve centers which have become over-charged about the 9th dorsal vertebrae.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

8. The action of the sensory organism is above the normal in most of the functions of the organs themselves, yet through the action of some of the forces as received from symphatic [sympathetic] conditions we find at times a response to the action of [words missing] as received through the sensory organism, because slow to act at first, with a quick return of the action as expressed to the brain force.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

9. That state we have here.  The eye or the ear, or the force of sight or sense of smell, or touch, may be slow to receive, but quick to act, as to the [[Vibration|vibration]] the brain receives from certain things.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

10. This being due to the condition existing through or by conjunction of forces, - symphatic [sympathetic] action force, and as expressed to you through the physical and the blood supplying forces in this body here.  Dr. [4263] we are speaking of.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

11. To counter-balance or to create the equilibrium to the whole nervous system, and to give the action of force necessary to bring about the correct equilibrium, not only will this be controlled by force from within and by the action of the physical with the spiritual and mental aspect as taken into the body itself, but by taking into the system those properties that would give only the incentives as a stimulant to nerve force or nerve tissue, to create the correct incentives of force.  This should be taken in very small quantities.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

12. Gum [[Camphor|camphor]] 20 grains; sulfate of morphia 3 grains; murate of ammonia 20 grains.  These should be made into small pellets, 24 to this quantity.  One only should be taken each evening just before resting time.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

13. This is all that is needed to balance the force within the body and to correct and direct the action both of the nerves, muscular action and circulation.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

14. Follow this.

15. (Q)  The treatment that you have given, Mr. Cayce, is that all the treatment that is necessary for this body?
(A)  That is all.  We are through.

16. Now, Mr. Cayce, forget all about this reading you have given. You are feeling all right, and all of the organs of your body are functioning properly, and you are feeling good, and you will wake up in two minutes just feeling fine and all right.
