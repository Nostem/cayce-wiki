# TEXT OF READING 1710-8 M 27

**Reading [[1710-8]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/5/1942  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 7873

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mr. [1710], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through his sister, Mrs. [1523].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1523], Burlynn Davis and Esther Wynne.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Maryland.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, there are changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.  These in the greater measures have been the result of influences from without.

4. Thus there has been caused a disturbance to the muscular forces, to the tendons, and - as a result - there have been those disturbances indicating internal hemorrhage.

5. These, however, have been and are being adjusted, in this particular phase.

6. For this particular phase, we would take those activities which would aid in purifying and [[Healing|healing]] through the intestinal tract:

7. Every other day take about an ounce of Saffron Tea, and every other day - through most of the day - take water three to four times a day in which there is put a few drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

8. As for the muscular forces through the shoulder and spine, - these as we find may BEST respond to the general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]].  DO NOT - unless there are other injuries - have any cutting; else we will have muscles and tendons that may be pulled entirely out of place, and a stiff member.

9. The general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment should consist of, first, a light sweat, followed by a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] of Witchhazel; about two teaspoonsful of Witchhazel to a pint of boiling water in the Fume Bath Cabinet, see?  This should be followed by a Pine Oil Rub; and this, while not too severe, should be through the shoulder, through the area along the sides of the spine, in shoulder, in the hip also - the deep [[Massage Therapy|massage]], but not attempting to make adjustments.  These are correct as to positions, but there ARE tensions in the muscular forces - as a fold through the muscles and tendons of the body.

10. Be mindful in the diet that there are plenty of the Vitamins A, D and B-1.  These should be supplied in the foods, preferably; though - after some two or three of the hydrotherapy treatments - there may be taken one of the B-Complex tablets each week, or one each day for a couple of days out of each week, but not longer.  Take only one, and don't take them regularly - that is, straight along - if the diet is kept any way near balanced.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Were there any injurious effects from the spinal injection given last year, especially as related to the kundaline forces; if so, please give suggestions for restoring balance.
(A)  No.

13. (Q)  Are butter substitutes injurious for general use?
(A)  Depends on the character.

14. (Q)  Any mental or spiritual advice?
(A)  The mental and spiritual attitudes in this body, in most instances, have been very good.  But keep a constructive influence, - not merely for self but for its relationships to others.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [1523]

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for Physiotherapy treatments.)
