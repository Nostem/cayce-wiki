# TEXT OF READING 409-30 F 24

**Reading [[409-30]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/28/1934  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 3073

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of September, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self Mrs. [409], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, Ruth LeNoir, B. Frank Blick and wife.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Not so good are the conditions in the present. Indiscretions with the allowing of self to come in contact with those influences from without, combined with the weakness from within, have set up very distressing conditions or reactions in the system.

2. Follow those suggestions that have been made in such conditions.  Change only in that there be the application of those things that will produce a better elimination through the activity of the kidneys and bladder, by the making of a tea in this manner:

3. Put 1/2 ounce of crushed watermelon seed in 8 ounces of water.  Let this steep for thirty to fifty minutes.  Set aside and cool.  Take a tablespoonful of this tea about three or four times each day for two or three days.

4. With the applications of the [[Mullein|mullein]], with the applications of the stimulations for the rest, this SHOULD bring about the proper reactions; though the POISONS must not be allowed to disseminate through the system.

5. Let these come to the head in the hip, and the drainages from same will be increased; but if these are kept down with the [[Poultice|poultice]], this will be overcome.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is this condition what is known as erysipelas?
(A)  Not true erysipelas; but borders on same.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
