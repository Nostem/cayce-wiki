# TEXT OF READING 5485-2 F 9

**Reading [[5485-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/8/1929  Sex: F  Age: 9  ReadingID: 13704

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by parents, Mr. and Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [5485], her parents and little brother [4820], Dr. W. H. McChesney, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5485].  This we have had before.

2. Now, we find there are improvements with the physical force and functioning of this body.  These conditions need those of the suggestions as have been made for the [[Vibration|vibration]] as is necessary - as given - to create that channel through which the auditory forces may respond in their normalcy to the organs, to the sensory, to the whole nerve system.  These are the physical conditions to be created.

3. These should be kept under that same pressure as has been outlined, changing only when the discharge is below normal, and after the next re-charging we would begin with the physical massage of the nerve centers and plexus from the 1st dorsal to the base of the brain, through the whole of the cervical region and along the nerves of the 3rd and 1st nerves of face, which come around the ear, jaw, and to the frontal portion of face - but keep continually those of the supplications in the manner that will bring the awakening of the mental and [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]]'s relations one with another.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Should the massage be given osteopathically?
(A)  Osteopathically, or nearer that neuropathically.

6. (Q)  Will it in any way be detrimental to postpone the massage until the 1st of September?
(A)  The body will necessarily be postponed in the reaction that same period.  These need the reaction that is necessary through massage, for with muscular tissue, with nerve channels awakening, the massage aids same in reaching out - just as does cultivation aid a plant in reaching out into the soil.  So does the re-awakening of nerve energy need that as will enable same to reach out.

Do that when it's possible, then.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/22/29 Mother's letter in answer to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
> questionnaire:  "...I have your letter to my mother [[5485]'s
> grandmother] asking for a report of [5485]'s progress.  There
> was a marked improvement the first three weeks but since then
> very little.  We have carried out the treatments exactly and
> with the exception of a week or so at the time of my father's
> death, nothing has been allowed to interfere with the battery
> applications and massage.  So far, all improvement has been
> in one ear, the right one, which has always been the best
> one.
> 
> "Thank you for your interest and any help you can give will
> be very much appreciated..."
> 
> R2. 11/21/29 EC gave a reading for [5485]'s little brother
> [4820].
> 
> R3. 1/31/30 Letter in answer to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] questionnaire:
> "...I can tell you very little about [5485] as she is not at
> home this winter.  She is still having the battery and
> massage treatments, but her improvement is so very slow that
> it is hard to see..."
> 
> R4. 9/15/40 Mother's report in answer to A.R.E. questionnaire,
> indicated that the diagnosis in her opinion was correct with
> both of her children, but that she had no way of knowing
> 'causes'.  "Both children born deaf, the girl [5485] almost
> totally, boy [4820] has some hearing.  Physician's analysis
> was prenatal deafness..."  She indicated that the suggestions
> had been followed about 2 years with no improvements
> whatever.
> 
> R5. 8/6/56 Brother [4820]'s letter requested copies of their
> readings, indicating he had just read the book "There Is A
> River" and his mother had told him about their readings:
> "...My mother said she did not remember all of them but she
> remembered about the wet battery and said I would not take
> the treatments like Mr. Cayce suggested.  She said I took the
> battery treatment once a week or once every two weeks,
> sometimes none at all.  The reason for that I did not know
> what it was for until only last spring.  I shall be very
> happy to send you a report on my present physical condition,
> also my sister's...  My sister [5485] was born deaf and my
> mother did not know it until I was on the way.  My mother
> said she worried herself sick about me becoming deaf before I
> was born.  That was exactly what Mr. Cayce said during the
> reading for me in 1929.  IN my opinion my sister's condition
> remains the same since her readings were made because she was
> awfully stubborn in taking the treatments.  Perhaps she did
> not know what it was all about..."  [GD's note:  See [4820]
> subsequent correspondence in his own case series.]
