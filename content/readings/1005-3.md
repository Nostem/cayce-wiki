# TEXT OF READING 1005-3 M 19

**Reading [[1005-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/18/1928  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 6063

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of March, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - [1005].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, Dr. [5618], Mrs. [760] and [1005].]

Time of Reading 
6:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and cure of same.  You will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find there are many physical changes in this body since we had this here.  In those conditions that disturb the body in the present, we find these have to do with the eliminations and the effect of congestion on conditions that are inclined towards that of lack of proper incentives to the distributing of nerve energy; namely, to the sensory system, especially.  In the action as is gained from conditions existent in the 2nd and 3rd and 4th dorsal, and the 4th and 5th cervical, we have a lack of elimination to the three nerves of the face, and to the auditory and to the optic forces.  These, then, with congestion produced - by overtaxing the system through lack of proper elimination - bring, with the congestion, that of acute conditions in the outer or external elimination centers.  Hence the inflammation in the auditory and optic forces, though not direct secondary conditions.

4. Eliminate the system through those of oil, and of those of salines, and correct the pressure in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal and 4th and 5th cervical, and conditions will be relieved. Do not get feet nor head damp.  Do that.  Keep out of cold or excessive exertion until conditions are relieved.

5. We are through for the present.
