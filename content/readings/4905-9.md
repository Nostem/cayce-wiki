# TEXT OF READING 4905-9

**Reading [[4905-9]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/9/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12934

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. Byron Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; A. M. Banning, Steno. Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                         Mexia, 
12:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  Now, we have these conditions as regarding those operations on the land of Madison Byron Wyrick in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].  In this operation, we find the present depth drilled now 3601 feet.  In the information as given, we find the line that was the oil supply has been passed, and the break in the sand reached.  Only necessary that the operations be continued through the gray shale and the encountering of hard, gray cap, and then we will encounter the line oil of present day value.  Be not troubled with the present formation but give the necessary care to drilling through this formation as we bring the necessary results for success.

2. EC:  Now, we have reached the break as given, so we will find the production as given.  Follow close any instruction of drilling into production with care, and with the necessary equipment to care for production when found.  This we will find the next few days, and will only require few days of steady, workful work to succeed.  Do that.

3. We are through.
