# TEXT OF READING 294-133 M 55

**Reading [[294-133]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/12/1932  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 2092

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 12th day of February, 1932, in accordance with suggestion made by [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] that he would answer questions regarding his own health and welfare, immediately following Life Rdg. 2121-2.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion for answering questions.)

1. (Q)  In what direction should the body Edgar Cayce face in giving Life Readings?
(A)  The South.

2. (Q)  In what direction should the body face in giving readings on [[Atlantis|Atlantis]], for lecture.  [See [364] series.]
(A)  East.

3. We are through.
