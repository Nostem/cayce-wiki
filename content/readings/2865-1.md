# TEXT OF READING 2865-1 F 31 (M.D.)

**Reading [[2865-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/7/1942  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 10087

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of December, 1942, in accordance with request made by the father - Dr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1646].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set 3:30 P. M. 
Taken 3:00 to 3:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2865], ..., Penna. You will go over this body-mind carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body and mind. You will answer the questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  (In locating the individual - "Yes - quite a building! - [2865] - ")

3. Yes, we have the body-mind here, [2865].

4. As we find, there are physical or pathological, as well as mental-psychological, disturbances.  These, while they do not work together, are caused or produced by retentions in the mind, - or that which is partially, or at times, possession.

5. These are the conditions, then, as we find with this body and mind, [2865], ..., Pennsylvania.

6. The body-mind lost control of itself through overtaxing of the body-mind, combined with a type of fever that was part of the experience when the body so taxed itself; reducing the body-forces to such an extent that in many centers along the spinal column there came to be less and less ability for the centers to coordinate between sympathetic and cerebrospinal nervous systems.

7. First there was caused absent-mindedness, the tendencies towards a little temperature, the driving of self too much, and then the hallucinations, and then debilitation in the impulses to be carried back and forth through the ganglia.

8. We find that the condition may be aided.

9. First, as an aid in purifying the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, we would give internally one to two drops of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] daily - in half a glass of water of morning before any meal is taken - for five days.  Then leave off five days.  Then take five days more.  Continue in this manner until there have been about ten series of taking the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] - or throughout the whole period as we will outline for the first course of treatments, see?

10. After the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] has been taken for a few days, or the first five-day period, we would begin subjugating the body-mind by suggestion, - or hypnosis.  Such an one as Dr. Kuhn* may be risked with this undertaking.

11. When there has been the second or third such subjugation, let the body rest from such suggestions until there has been some response in the system.

12. After there has been gained control of the body once or twice (by hypnosis), begin the use of the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold vibratorially to the body - using at least one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water, but let there be at least four ounces of the solution used in the Appliance.  Renew or change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] every fifteen days.  The attachments would be made as follows:

13. The plate through which the Gold is carried vibratorially would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center, or - on this body - four fingers from the navel center to the right and one finger up.  The small copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar, so that we may have control through the entire length of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the cerebrospinal system.  These attachments would be made each day from thirty to sixty minutes; that is, begin with thirty minutes and gradually work up to sixty minutes; not attaching it for thirty minutes one time and sixty minutes the next time, but gradually increasing daily the period of attachment.  Or make the attachment for thirty minutes for the first ten days, forty minutes for the next ten days, and so on, until it is being used for one hour.

14. These low electrical forces will aid in eliminating the tendencies for possession.  Sufficient electrical forces are created.  So, use double-strength [[Vibration|vibrations]]; that is:

Copper Sulphate................3 pounds,
Sulphuric Acid C.P.............2 ounces,
Zinc..........................60 grains,
Willow Charcoal..............1/2 pound.

Keep the anodes very clean.  Disconnect from the Appliance when not in use.  DO, each time the Appliance is attached,

* [12/7/42 GD's note:  Dr. Kuhn's address is 1225 Park Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., associated with Dr. Garrett.]

surround the body with the spiritual forces, the [[Prayer|prayer]] in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness.

15. Have the Appliance ready when the suggestive treatments have been started.

16. Continue the Appliance, in the manner suggested, for at least thirty to sixty days.

17. When there has been a rest period from the first suggestive treatments, - then, after that, the second or third period of such subjugation should bring the return of coordination.

18. It will require some six or eight months to build up, after that; but we should have near normalcy for this body of [2865] if this is done.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. [Father]
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note:  Atomidine is manufactured by The Atomidine Co., 9 South 8th Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y., though you may be able to secure it from your local druggist.  It sells for $1 a bottle.

Show Dr. Kuhn the Reading when you go to him.  He is a psychiatrist who has had some experience with Mr. Cayce's Readings, and has gotten good results with the ones who have been referred to him through this channel.

See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to the Appliance Co., 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., when having it prepared, so that the one you receive will be according to specifications in this case.  An article also enclosed explains the theory of this type of appliance.

At your convenience please let us have a history of this case to the time of obtaining the Reading.  This is valuable for our files.)
