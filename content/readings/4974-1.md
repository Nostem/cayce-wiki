> R1. 1/22/21 W. E. DeLaney's letter to [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]:
> 
> [[Kentucky|KENTUCKY]] LUMBER COMPANY
> INCORPORATED
> ROUGH AND DRESSED HARDWOOD LUMBER
> 606-608 SECURITY TRUST BUILDING
> LEXINGTON, [[Kentucky|KENTUCKY]]
> 
> Mr. [[David Kahn|David Kahn]],
> [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.
> 
> Dear Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]:-
> 
> Your brother was in my office this morning, requesting that
> I give you an affidavit as to the work done by Mr. Edgar
> Cayce in connection with the illness of Mrs. DeLaney.
> 
> Will simply make statement in this letter and place notary
> acknowledgement at the bottom.
> 
> Mrs. DeLaney has been a sufferer for a number of years; and
> several years ago I heard of the ability of Dr. Cayce, and
> requested that he come to Lexington, which he did.  He was
> the first to correctly diagnose Mrs. DeLaney's trouble;
> altho' she had been to a number of physicians throughout the
> country.  He advised several treatments, which were of
> considerable benefit to her; however, did not result in a
> cure.
> 
> As a result of my acquaintance with Dr. Cayce in connection
> with this work; I have no hesitancy in saying, that he has
> wonderful hidden power; in addition, I am sure he is a
> gentleman, whose judgement can be relied upon.
> 
> Yours truly,  [signed]  W. E. DeLaney
> 
> WED.AM
> 
> State of [[Kentucky|Kentucky]]
> SCT
> County of Fayette
> 
> On this the 22nd day of January 1921 personally appeared

> presence made oath to the truth of the statement therein
> contained.

> [signed]  A. L. Meiler
