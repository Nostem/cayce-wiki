# TEXT OF READING 5721-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[5721-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/12/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14181

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by, or through, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            Bronx, [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions with this body, and some may be assisted to give more ease and better facilitate the actions of [[Physical Body|the body physical]].

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them with this body physical.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this below the normal and also charged with those toxins, those bacilli as propagated in the system, produced by over strain on body in times back, until the condition in the eliminations and the assimilation became in functioning conditions a condition that of the malignant nature.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find weakened through this gradual strain on body.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, all under that strain of the existing conditions, in the extremities and in the whole locomotary systems.

7. Then, that which may add the better condition to the body would be, taking those small properties as in that of the [[Radium|Radium]] Water, and with the application over the system (especially in those ganglia that give distress in system) that of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]].  The [[Radium|Radium]] Water should not be taken oftener than every second or third day.  Necessitate being given under the direction of physician, see?

8. This, as we find, will give the better relief to the body.  Do that, see?

9. We are through for the present.
