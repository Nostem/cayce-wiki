# TEXT OF READING 2491-5 F 70

**Reading [[2491-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/5/1942  Sex: F  Age: 70  ReadingID: 9376

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2491], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Miss Esther Wynne.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Esther Wynne, Mae Verhoeven and Burlynn Davis.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Conditions haven't changed such a great deal.  Some are bettered, some are not so well.

4. The body becomes so easily discouraged that there comes those attitudes which cause the body to feel, and then act, "Well, it don't make much difference."

5. As we find, - we would keep those things that have been suggested; especially as to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the electrical forces, the rubs.

6. But we would have now, in the first six weeks, at least two [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] cleansings; and then about once each six weeks.  These will aid in allowing the system to purify itself better.

7. These we would do.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Why does the psoriasis appear worse?
(A)  The toxic conditions and perforations are worse, through the lack of adherence in diet.  Hence the purifying of the colon should aid the more.

10. (Q)  Why is the trembling no better, and how can it be helped?
(A)  There has not been removed as yet [[Crucifixion|the cross]] connections, or pressures, that cause the trembling; or there has not been sufficient aid to the nerve forces to steady same - or to produce coordination.

11. (Q)  How long should the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] be used before it is changed?
(A)  Whenever there is the showing of the precipitation.  As to how quickly it precipitates depends upon whether the connections are left in the solution, and as to the strength of the charge through which it passes.  Ordinarily, it should be changed very fifteen days.

12. (Q)  Has the attachment of the Appliance been correct?
(A)  The applications so far as the Appliance is concerned have been very good.

13. (Q)  Please designate the exact place of the lumbar axis.
(A)  It's the 4th lumbar.

14. (Q)  Should I have more [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments?
(A)  These might come a little later, - not until after more of the mucous has been relieved or taken from the system - through the colon.

15. (Q)  Is the disease advancing?
(A)  Rather at a standstill.  Some phases of it show advances.

16. (Q)  What causes the muscles to appear to be getting shorter?
(A)  Lack of elasticity in nerve energy to allow their stretching or strengthening.

17. (Q)  Why do I lose my strength so quickly?
(A)  Lack of the energies to supply same.  Portions of those deficiencies caused the first activity through the excretory functionings in the liver, that cut off the supply of same to the system.

18. (Q)  Is there any tonic or vitamin tablet that would help me?
(A)  The B-Complex tablets would help the body, but with the body's attitude it might come to depend on these wholly.  But these would help, as a tonic.

19. (Q)  Are the walls of the intestines being built up to any degree?
(A)  The upper intestines are bettered.  Those in the colon are not so good in the present.

20. (Q)  Is there any specific cause for the seeming lack of improvement?
(A)  As just indicated, the lack of the vital energies being stimulated to the better normalcy in the body.

21. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  These are serious conditions to be dealt with.  These require persistence and consistence.  And KNOW that all [[Healing|healing]] must come through expectation, and through the attuning of [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] for improvement - and not misjudging or deterioration.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
