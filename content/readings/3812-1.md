# TEXT OF READING 3812-1

**Reading [[3812-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/3/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11391

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. A. C. Preston.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. A. C. Preston.]

Brandywine Ford, 
Time of Reading                              near 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will have before you the information given through these sources some years ago regarding the money lost by the Federal Troops near Washington, D.C. [Culpepper County, Va.].  You will give us any information regarding this at the present time, as to how and where this may be located.

2. EC:  Near Brandywine Ford - yes, near Brandywine Ford.  On the North banks of this Ford there is, and was, a large gully that emptied into the Brandywine.  In this region and this district were the troops of the Federal forces located when the Confederate or Rebel forces came into the camp in the early morning of September 24, 1863, see?  September 24, 1863.  There was just a large washing tub full of twenty dollar gold pieces - now how many is that?  These may be located in this hill as still lies between this large log here, just above the Ford and the gully.  It was buried, not deep, but has gotten much deeper in the years as they have passed.  We are through.
