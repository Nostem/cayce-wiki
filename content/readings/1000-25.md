# TEXT OF READING 1000-25 F 51 (Business Woman, Hebrew,

**Reading [[1000-25]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/28/1940  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 6041

Christian Scientist) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of January, 1940, in accordance with request made by her sister, Mrs. [1192], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Mt. Sinai Hosp., 
Time of Reading                            5th floor, Rm. 17, 
8:05 to 8:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  100th St. & 5th 
Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, unless there is an arousing in the mental self - either through suggestions or aid - the desire to fight against those conditions (which as we find arise from general debilitation through the mental and physical reaction), there will not be much hope of bringing resuscitating forces of any nature to the body, to be of any permanent or material aid.

4. As we find, a form of an anemia is the basic disturbance.

5. We would follow those suggestions which are being made for the recuperation of the resistances, by - as we find - transfusions would be helpful.

6. But unless there can be some reaction in other directions, these will not be of any great or lasting help.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Are any organs affected and to what extent?
(A)  All of them are affected, you see.  This is rather just a "giving up" - as combined with that of an anemia which has gradually grown on the body.

And this affects the nerve system, the organs, and the whole resistances of the body.

9. (Q)  How would you suggest attempting to get a mental reaction?
(A)  This must be through someone else, as indicated, - the daughter, sister, or reader.

10. (Q)  Is it advisable to have Dr. White from Boston, and follow his method?
(A)  Not necessarily.  As we find, these conditions are understood, - unless there is the desire on the part of those administering to have other advice or counsel.

11. (Q)  What would be the medical term for her condition?
(A)  As just given - anemia!

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [1192] - Special Delivery - Phoned Also
"    " Ass'n file
