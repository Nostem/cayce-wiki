# TEXT OF READING 4313-2 F 22

**Reading [[4313-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/21/1924  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 12125

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [4313].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                          ... Park Avenue, 
11:10 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                 [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give a physical, spiritual and mental reading, with suggestions for the improvement of any or all of these. You will also answer any questions regarding the welfare of this body that I may ask you.

2. EC:  Now we find the general conditions in the physical are on the improve in this body and only need the carrying out fully and entirely the suggestions as given for the improvement of the physical conditions in and for this body.

3. As to the spiritual and mental development, this also we find is on the improve in many ways.  These need be kept in that manner as has been outlined for the development in the spiritual welfare for this body, [4313].

4. Do that.

5. (Q)  How may this body reduce weight without injury to the [[Physical Body|physical body]]?
(A)  When the body has reached that condition where the adjustments are correct throughout the body, and the eliminations are proper in the system, that weight, that condition, that is the normal, the correct, will be the condition of the body.  This, as we see, will bring a reduction in the weight, bring more symmetrical lines for the body from the physical conditions, and with this correction the trouble in limbs and in the pelvic regions, of which the body has been warned, will bring the better conditions for the body.

6. (Q)  Would it be well for the body to be married at this time?
(A)  Not under the existing physical conditions for the body, for with those duties, those conditions, that would arise from such relations, would bring at this time detrimental conditions to the body in the physical and in the mental development of same, and the condition that would arise from those relations would bring much distress in the mental for this body.  When these physical, these mental, have been put in the correct relations with itself, and with the physical manifestations in the body, and with the higher in the physical and spiritual, the body would be well in the natural state - marriage.

7. (Q)  How may the body best invest her finances?
(A)  These are not given from here, save with the difference between individual conditions that might be gained in such relations.  That is, choose between two or more investments. These may give the better, but never choosing.

8. (Q)  Is there anything special in her future, physical, mental, spiritual or financial, of which she should be warned?
(A)  There is the possibility of many conditions that may arise.  When the body has so alarmed, or forearmed, itself with perfect physical health, perfect relations in the spiritual, with these [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may meet any conditions that arise.

9. (Q)  How may she be happier in her relations to her friends and family?
(A)  There has been much of the selfish nature exhibited in the actions of the body toward those with whom it has come in relations.  This has often times brought many strained conditions.  The better condition may be found in FINDING the INDIVIDUAL SELF and making itself in that way correct with the Creator, for the individual will find there is no other peace or happiness that has not the foundation in the divine laws.  Whether it be with friend or foe, family or with acquaintances, all must be on that basis.  When these are set aright, the individual will find the better relations with everyone.

Do that.

This does not mean the individual shall be one as of a recluse in any manner, only the spiritual of the condition ever presented in these relations, for as has been given all conditions are in this injunction:

"Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thine heart, thine mind and thine increase, and thy neighbor as thyself."
