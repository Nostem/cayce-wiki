# TEXT OF READING 4906-7

**Reading [[4906-7]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/26/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13002

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4906].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Section 7, 
Time of Reading                   Township 14 N. Range 14 E. 
11:00 A. M.                       Morris, Oklahoma.

1. LS:  Now, you have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] which is being drilled in Section 7, Township 14 N. Range 14 E. situated directly N. of the town of Morris, Okmulgee County, Oklahoma.  You will give the conditions of this well at the present time, and what should be done by [4906], who is drilling this well, to obtain production.

2. EC:  In this we would complete the 2980.  The conditions are not satisfactory to many.  Complete to this depth before abandoning this.  We will find that which is sought.

3. (Q)  Has the casing been set properly?
(A)  That, that is set, yes.
