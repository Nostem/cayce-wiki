# TEXT OF READING 370-7 M 56

**Reading [[370-7]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/27/1940  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2864

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of August, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [370], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions in the present are quite different from those as we have had before; though they may be said to be an outcome of those disturbances indicated, with only partial administrations made for the corrective influences as were indicated.

4. Still there is a great deal of the toxic poisons in the the system, producing irregularity in the metabolism, in the respiratory activity, in the heart's action.

5. First, as we find, there should be eliminated a good deal of the toxic poisons with the use of the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations; and gentle manipulations osteopathically administered to reduce the blood pressure; using some GOOD eliminant to keep up the eliminations or to reduce the toxic forces through the system - that of a senna base being preferable.

6. Doing these, as we find, would bring the better conditions for this body of [370].

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  In what way have I been careless, or what is causing the palpitation of my heart?
(A)  Not keeping up the eliminations properly, nor the cleansing of the colon sufficiently as it should.

9. (Q)  What causes slight pain around my heart?  [Angina pectoris?]
(A)  The pressures produced from the general nervous taxation, as from toxic forces as well as that indicated in the blood supply itself.

Doing these, we will find there will be a much better condition in this respect.

10. (Q)  Why the waking at times in the night, when sleep is impossible and my heart beats?
(A)  This as we find is the warning, and the pressure, causing this form of insomnia.

The removal of the poisons and the use of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] will correct this.  Not just enemas, you see, - these had best be high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] and given by one well versed in such - for the first few, at least, until the mucous has been removed from the colon.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
