# TEXT OF READING 2-5 M 49

**Reading [[2-5]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/3/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 6

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor;  [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2], his wife and son, Drs. Lydic and Parker, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  In many respects we find the conditions on the improve. However, there should be those applications, both as to the physical and to the medical applications, that will INCREASE the ELIMINATIONS in the system, even to a greater extent; that is, especially the coordinating of eliminations.  These we would, with those being used, use more of those of the sweats - also of the applications that will increase the capillary circulation more.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should all former treatments be continued?
(A)  At present.

3. (Q)  Can the body get satisfactory treatment at home?
(A)  Not as we find.  That's been tried!

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    "  Ass'n office
"    "  [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
