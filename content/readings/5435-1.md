# TEXT OF READING 5435-1 M 61

**Reading [[5435-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/25/1929  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 13599

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [...], via Mrs. [5535].  Also by Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce and Gray Salter.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find these as the conditions when these were sought for, [5435] we are speaking of.

2. There being pressure in the central nervous system from the clot as has found its way in circulation to the upper portion of the medulla oblongata, into that portion that controls locomotion.

3. In the meeting of the needs - for there are many portions of the system affected by same, and these may be only aided by the setting up of drainage or proper eliminations, without destroying the cell tissue in any way or manner, or as to cause such distress as to reproduce same.  This we find might have been accomplished through this application of [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] from the ultra violet to that of the brachial plexus, particularly - and with the setting up of the proper reaction through the respiratory system, keeping the body quiet - and after the reactions had set in, we would use that of the manipulation as to cause the normal distribution of the conditions eliminated from the system.  These should have been done.  These should be done.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mr. [137]
