# TEXT OF READING 2665-4 F 45

**Reading [[2665-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/14/1944  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 9741

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2665], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

... Ave. 
Time of Reading                                    Detroit, 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, so long as there was kept the suggestions consistently at the periods indicated, help came, and we will find if there will be the recuperating influences brought again to the body, it will be by following consistently the suggestions in the manner as has been indicated.

4. After the periods, as given, do have those douches.  Do make the application of the Packs as indicated.  Not just haphazardly or skipping a period but keep them consistently. Then rest for the periods, and then continue again, and we will bring better conditions.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What causes the circulatory disturbance?
(A)  As has been indicated, the suppression of the circulation through the heart, liver, lungs and kidneys upsets the pressures through the body.  But with the massage, with the corrections, we will find better conditions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
