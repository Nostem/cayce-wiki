# TEXT OF READING 2658-1 F 58 (School Teacher, Theosophist)

**Reading [[2658-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/24/1925  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 9729

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 24th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [2658], via Mrs. C. W. Dillman.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born February 18, 1866, between 6:00 and 7:00 A. M., 
at what is now a suburb of [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill. 
Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                         ..., Indiana.

1. GC:  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], a reading giving the effects of the solar systems on the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present earth plane, naming the planet, or position, from which [[Soul|the soul]] took its last flight.  You will give the vocation in life for which this body is most adapted, or may adapt itself, giving also the high points of the life in former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], giving the name and place of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth plane, with the characteristics as brought forward in its personality in the present life.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and those conditions relating to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth's plane at the present time.  Also those co-relations [correlations?] regarding the [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in earth's plane in different spheres, and sojourn in other spheres, for in this entity we find at present many of those conditions that give of those relative conditions as is related to relativity of forces manifest in the entity.

3. In this present earth plane, we find the physical entity completed in the early morning hours.  Now, the relations in the earth's plane, as reached through the [[Destiny|destiny]] as given in position in the earth's solar system in this entity, we find it took its last flight from that of Mercury, with the influence of Mercury and of Jupiter, and Uranus, the stronger in the entity's present sphere.  Hence the relative conditions and developments toward those positions as will be reached in the relations of spiritual and physical entity.

4. Then, without respect of will, for we find this has been manifested in many ways in the present earth plane:

5. One given to understand much in the mental plane.  One that has been, is, studious along all lines pertaining to the mental development.

6. One that has many hobbies, as it were, and keeps most of these to self.

7. One that has very strong likes and dislikes, and the first impressions to the body are usually the ones upon which the body acts; the forces in the mental and in the Uranian forces giving an insight into such conditions.

8. One that studies much into the spiritual elements, and has gone far from that field in which the entity was first taught.  One that thinks well for self.  One that may be intrusted with many conditions as have to do with the mental development in many others.  One that will find the greatest joy and resources in self, in following the developments of mental forces in others.

9. One that will find the greater love in life in the declining years.  One that may be called one stable, and set in many ways.

10. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] upon the earth's plane, we find:

11. In that before this, in the reign of the [[Rome|Roman]] rule, when the Church became a part of State, and the entity then offered as one who would die for the cause espoused, and brought many to the understanding of the truths as proclaimed by the followers of that sect.

12. In the one before this, we find in the land of the peoples when the chosen people entered in that land as the givers of much understanding in the rule of the land, and the entity then in the household of him who was next to the ruler in that [[Egypt|Egyptian]] land.  [6/2/25 341-10, Par. 9-A says this was NOT the [[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] period.]

13. Then, before this, we find in the East Indian countries, when the first laws of the peoples were proclaimed in that country.

14. Then, in the personality as exhibited in the present:

15. We find from the first, again seeking those laws as would be made conscious in the earth's plane.

16. In the second, that of the desire to stand alone in many ways, or independent of others.

17. In the next, that of the desire, or the innate study of all conditions pertaining to the relation of mental with the physical.

18. Then, to use and apply these, keep in that way as was given in the last appearance:  Study well to show self well grounded in that which would make the safe passage for the mental forces into the Beyond.

19. We are through.
