# TEXT OF READING 3571-1 F 4

**Reading [[3571-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/18/1944  Sex: F  Age: 4  ReadingID: 11058

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Pennsylvania.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find there has gradually grown to be a retardment in the normal activities of the body.  This has come from pressures produced in the coccyx area and a lesion in the upper dorsal and 3rd cervical.

3. From the very nature of the pressures the flow of impulses is hindered in the coordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic system, and the effects are to the organs of the sensory system.  These are hindered from pressures in or through the coccyx and lumbar area.  These also prevent the activity, while the lesions formed by the breaking of coordinations in the upper dorsal and through the cervical area, or the 3rd cervical specifically, prevent impulses reaching the activities to speech, voice, and will in [[Visions|vision]] and hearing unless measures are taken for correction of same.

4. Though the body is in its developing stage, we would apply warm [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs for fifteen to twenty minutes, just before [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections would be made in the lumbar-sacral and coccyx, also in the upper dorsal and cervical area.

5. Use three to four thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], not only over the area from the 3rd cervical to the 4th dorsal but also through the sacral, lumbar and coccyx area.  Then apply salt heat.  Preferably make a pad about an inch thick with salt, quilting this into a pad so that it does not run so, set so that it can be bent in such a way as to fit close to the body over the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Pack.  Heat this, as in an oven, and let these Packs remain on about fifteen to twenty minutes, before each [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.  Then when the body is sponged off, immediately give the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment.  It will be easier, even for the delicate body, yet these should be made very specific.

6. These before very long should change the impulses and we will find nearer to normal development.

7. Do give a great deal of liver juice, though in very small doses at a time; not so much the liver itself, but calf's liver broiled and mashed to a pulp, with a great deal of the juices - so don't make it so hard as to remove all of these. Don't put grease with it.

8. Do prepare Beef Juices and give in very small quantities, a quarter teaspoonful twice a day, just let the body sip it.

9. Then keep the regular diet, but include all those foods that will add more A, D, and B-1.  While most of these are included in those properties indicated, it is well that these be prepared also in the milk to be taken by the body. Re-enforced D would be well to be used for the body.

10. Do these and we should find better conditions.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is she deaf or partially deaf?
(A)  Partially deaf at present, but as indicated, the speech, the hearing and the [[Visions|vision]] will be affected - unless there are measures taken.

13. (Q)  What can be done for her nervousness?
(A)  All of this arises from the same disturbances indicated. Do as outlined.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for the osteopath - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
