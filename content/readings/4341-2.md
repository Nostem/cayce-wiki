# TEXT OF READING 4341-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4341-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/26/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12165

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of October, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - [4341] (Count [4341]).

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [4341] [[New York City|New York City]], and his business associations and relations.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [4341].  This we have had before.  As has been given, much may be said respecting the abilities and that to the which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|THIS entity]], THIS body, may attain in the present experience, especially since, in the application of WILL as respecting associates, the body withdraw from conditions and surroundings that, while in the monetary standpoint were satisfactory to some, yet the policies and attitude of associates being unsatisfactory to the mind and the determination of the body [4341], [[Free Will|the will]] was applied in the manner as to make for self the better expression of that to which the body physically would attain in the present experience.

3. As given, many fields are open to the body.  Those of the character in which the body has had the better or more efficient training will be those easier to succeed IN. However, in ANY line that is in accord with the ideas, [[Ideal|ideals]], OF the body, the body may make a physical, a mental, a financial, a social success in.

4. In the contemplated council or meeting, as was given, there were hindrances brought about by some that would be unsatisfactory in the minds of some individuals, and these occurred by a mishap, or a word from a former associate. This, however, will not hinder this meeting eventually coming about, nor will it other than offer a larger, better, bigger opportunity for the body to function in.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Does this refer to the first question regarding Studebaker conference?
(A)  Refers to that conference.

6. (Q)  How should body proceed with him?
(A)  Do not be over anxious about this conference.  Merely, or formally, seek the presence of the one through whom the conference would be best fitted with; that it, through the same channel that there was the first intimation of the plausibility of such a conference.  Seek through that channel, [4341].

7. (Q)  Will there be a position for the body with the Enka Corporation in handling Rayon?
(A)  This offers a wide and varied opportunity.  Seek the conference with those establishing and opening the mills. This offers the BEST opportunity in this line.  As to which the body would choose, this [[Free Will|choice]] must be made by self, but many channels will be open, and there is not the necessity of a hurried decision.

8. (Q)  How shall body approach Enka officials?
(A)  Through those that are establishing new mills, see?

9. (Q)  What opportunities are there in the Federal or Albert Pick, Barth Co?
(A)  In MANY of these lines there are opportunities for the body.  The would be especially in that of handling that portion in which the Pick, Barth Company furnishes the full line of furnishings for buildings, equipping same; and the projection of the business in this line offers the larger opportunities.  Not only would the body be able to use the ABILITIES, but expand much in the ability of being well received, as well as being capable of meeting the situations as would arise concerning such undertaking.  In this portion, or in this line, would the body fit into such an organization the best.  Making estimates, meeting the individuals, going over such with various individuals, furnishing apartments, hotels, offices, banks, and such.  Make the approach for such through the vice-president of the company.

10. (Q)  Shall body connect in automotive or aeroplane industry as representative here or abroad?
(A)  In other fields offer BETTER opportunities, though as given, in ANY ONE the body might decide as being satisfactory (that is, the policies of those with whom the body would be associated - THIS must be the first prerequisite in making a connection; that the policies can be made one with the [[Ideal|ideals]] of self, see?), then the abilities in every direction will be as assets, and the liabilities will be nil, toward making a success financially, mentally, physically, socially.

11. (Q)  Should the body again engage in his old line of business?
(A)  Rather in a broader field, as we find.

12. (Q)  How can body assist in developing of Hospital of Association of National Investigators?
(A)  Know the policies and [[Purpose|purposes]] and aims, and do these fit with the [[Ideal|ideals]] of self, the body may lend aid in word, deed, action, and by the NATURAL tendency of the native tongue to the phenomena itself.  Does the criterion OF [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] conform with the ideals of self, lend aid.  If they do not, then do NOT lend aid.

13. (Q)  What immediate investment can body make for good returns in increased value?
(A)  None would THIS refer to stock at present would be very safe.  There are many that are sky-rocketing, but runs up and down.  But for such, with the body's ideals and ideas concerning same, study THESE somewhat FIRST, and FIRST HAND, before attempting anything in THAT direction.  We are through for the present.
