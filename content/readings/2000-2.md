# TEXT OF READING 2000-2 M 54

**Reading [[2000-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/4/1939  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 8511

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of October, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2000], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes

3. As we find, conditions are improved considerably from that as we have had here before.

4. There needs only to be the continuation of those applications in full as have been indicated or suggested.

5. The toxic poisons, as indicated through the neuritic pains, are EFFECTS.  These are not causes, - but arise from the sources of infection through the alimentary canal; especially in the caecum and that area of the system.

6. Then, there needs to be sufficient of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, and the manipulations, to rid the system entirely of these toxic forces, - and these conditions will disappear.

7. After these have been eliminated, if the body will use the Yellow Saffron Tea (American Saffron) as a drink about three times a week, we will find that it will aid in keeping better influences through the alimentary canal.

8. Do that.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
