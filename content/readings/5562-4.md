# TEXT OF READING 5562-4 F 12

**Reading [[5562-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 13853

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5563] and [5562], Dr. Lydic, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5562].  Now, we find conditions are continuing to be on the improve.  We would now relax those conditions as exist in the lower end of cerebrospinal system, in the coccyx; also TAUTNESS should be brought in the upper cervical regions.  These - a variation in the character OF the manipulation.  With the tautening of these, we will find there will be less recurrence of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as prevent their NORMAL forces in the nerve system.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Why are convulsions more frequent?
(A)  The general system being nearer in accord, and with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] coming nearer in the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the system, there is the tightening up of those muscular forces and the brain forces are reacting more nominally.  Hence we find these more recurrent, just like - why does one THINK more often than normal?  The normal development of the forces in system!  Do as we have given.  We will find reduction in these, in severity, in numbers also.  Increase the dosage also in those of the Mayblossom Bitters, and decrease the number of the sedative forces in system - see?

3. (Q)  Should prescription last prescribed be given at any time other than at the regular cycle, if convulsions are frequent?
(A)  It should!  These come more severe, as have been - you see - through this period.

4. (Q)  Is diet satisfactory?
(A)  This very good.  More exercise out in the open air would be good for the body.

5. (Q)  Are all other treatments given properly?
(A)  Now, we have made changes in some other treatments. Without these, the rest are very good.  These are being added, see?

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother to send home
