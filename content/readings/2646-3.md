# TEXT OF READING 2646-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[2646-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/27/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9702

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find that the general condition through this body is better then when we had it before, in many ways. There is the showing in the system, and the trouble as is shown through the side and trunk portion of the body and without the full effect of that as given to strengthen the ligaments so as to give the perfect circulation and nerve system as its effect on the nerve system, and their centers as the nerve from the portion of the body is, or traverse it, the system from the sides here as to the head so that we have reversed some of the conditions in the system by raising the [[Vibration|vibration]] in one portion of the body to the detriment of another as we have the condition in the head.

2. With the correct forces of [[Vibration|vibration]] to the body, we would overcome these conditions and bring the proper [[Vibration|vibration]] to the body.  We would adjust these conditions again in this system by the [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces or adjustments, and we will find them necessary in the 3rd and 5th cervical and to the right side of the 8th dorsal and 4th lumbar.  The adjustment also in muscular contraction should be made in the right antrum, face.  Do that.

3. (Q)  What is the trouble in this body's right ear?
(A)  We find with congestion in the antrum or right side, the Eustachian tube becomes involved giving off this condition in the ear from the nerve contraction as is shown in the system, and the adjustment should be through the whole system and to the antrum to relieve the strain as brought on the Eustachian tube here, why the treatment is to the face here.
