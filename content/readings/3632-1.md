# TEXT OF READING 3632-1 M 67

**Reading [[3632-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/29/1944  Sex: M  Age: 67  ReadingID: 11133

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3632], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.        Cambridge, Mass.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Otis Street - runs toward and parallel with part of the river.

3. Yes, we have the body here, [3632].

4. As we find, there are disturbances.  While some of these may be attributed to general debilitation, we find there are pressures that have brought on this debilitation; thus causing, as it were, the running down of the batteries of the sacral and lumbar areas which control the nerves and reflexes through the sciatic.  Though sciatic nerve itself has not yet deteriorated, the flexes from same in locomotory centers have deteriorated.  Thus the gradual losing of control of the lower limbs, so that in the attempt to walk, the toes drop forward, and it is necessary that there be the movement to swing these up, for they drop forward.  Hence, as indicated, they draw back upon the sciatics, and to the nerves and muscular forces under the knee, through the calf of the leg. A swelling in the Achilles bursa also makes shortness in the heel and a weakness through the assimilation.

5. In the plexus of the lumbar and sacral or a part of the ileac plexus, there needs to be the removal of the pressures so that there will be not only a better blood flow, but a releasing of the nerve impulses impinged, so that there will be carried to the extremities a greater quantity of better blood supply.

6. This done, we will find there will be gradually a rebuilding - though it will also be necessary to recharge the body, as it were.

7. These corrections should be made osteopathically.

8. Hence, to recharge the system, we would use with these corrections the Radio-Active Appliance as to charge from the central nervous system to the extremities of the body.  Keep the attachment plates very clean, polishing them when taking them off and when putting them on.  Do prepare the Appliance at least twenty minutes before the attachments are to be made to the body.  Use for at least one hour each day.

9. Use that hour as the period for definite [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]]; not only for the removal of the disorders but that the [[Purpose|purposes]] of the body and mind may be used for creative, constructive [[Purpose|purposes]] - and not merely saying so.

10. Also each day when the Appliance is removed, massage the body with this combination of oils:

Olive Oil (heated)...............2 ounces,
Tincture of Myrrh................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied)............1/2 ounce.

11. Also twice each week, preferably Tuesdays and Fridays, use the violet ray - this should be that from which the resistance is taken, so as to charge directly in the body. This should begin at about the 2nd cervical and extend to the heel and feet, going down each side of the body from the 2nd cervical - not so much on the spinal column itself, but on each side where there are the sympathetic nerves joining more specifically to the cerebrospinal system.

12. This electrical application should not be begun until most of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections have been made in the ileac plexus and in the lumbar-sacral.

13. Do these and we will make better conditions - we may add many years to the life and usefulness, if you will put it to use.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What is the cause of the cough and what treatment will relieve it?
(A)  This is an effect of the deterioration and a dropping or dropping of the palate.  Tie up a piece of hair in the center of the head.  Keep it tied up, like a wig.  Tie this tighter each day.  Every three days make it a little bit tighter, a little bit tighter, it'll soon stop the cough and raise the palate.

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one of the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 10/25/43 [3632]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> I have just read the very interesting account of your life
> and work by [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]].
> 
> I wonder if you could give me a reading.  I have two
> handicaps.  Once is a faulty articulation of my left leg, and
> from the same source, I believe, I have, when walking a
> peculiar swing to my body.  Several years ago I consulted a
> spiritualist medium and was told of a misplacement of a
> nerve in the spinal column.  Before and since this
> consultation I have had treatments by orthopedic surgeons.
> The prescription consisted of certain exercises.  No results.
> I have never suffered pain from this trouble.
> 
> The other handicap is a cough.  I am told that I had
> whooping-cough when I was three months old.  I was born on
> Nov. 17, 1876 when the whoop left the cough remained.
> Examination by x-rays give no clue to the source.  In the
> chronic state of the cough it varies as to frequency.  There
> is always phlegm raised.  In the past year I have noticed
> that after a cough the mucous is pocketed in my throat for a
> while and it requires an effort to loosen it.  The mucous
> used to be a thick yellow.  Now it is slightly thicker than
> saliva and most of the time of the same color as saliva.
> In the acute state (during a cold) the cough is more frequent
> and I find it difficult to sleep in a reclining position.
> 
> I do not wish to give an incorrect [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] of my condition.
> I am not incapacitated.  This may give you an idea - I worked
> as a clerk in the Post-Office from 1909 to 1941, being
> retired on annuity on reaching 65 years.  Since 1925 (When I
> was out for 3 weeks with tonsillitis) I accumulated at the
> rate of 10 days a year and turned back on retirement 120 days
> sick leave.
> 
> Perhaps I have told you enough.  At the Post-Office where I
> worked we handled the mail for students of the Harvard
> Business School.  There was a student from [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] named
> Cayce.  This was about 1929.  One of the family near or
> distant no doubt.
> 
> With deep appreciation of your work, in its spiritual and
> human values I am,
> 
> Yours sincerely, [3632]
