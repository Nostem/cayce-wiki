# TEXT OF READING 4414-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4414-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/31/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12258

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 31st day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4414].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4414], Treva Marshall and others.]

Time of Reading 
2:40 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find the abnormal conditions in this system and body are many, and the organs in their functioning are affected in different ways, and there are some organic conditions, yet all these may be corrected or brought to a more normal state of functioning when properly handled, or the [[Vibration|vibrations]] and conditions are set so system may produce the correct [[Vibration|vibration]] from within.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, [4414], we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, the quantity is deficient in the system from the lack of the body being able, under the conditions, to store sufficient energy and vitim [vitamin?] or vitality through the blood supply, the blood being impoverished rather than being of a blood condition, though often the conditions are shown in the blood and its effect on functioning organs, especially through this impoverishment.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find as this:

5. In the eighth and ninth dorsal segment we find that center has been pressed downward and in, until the impinionment to the hypogastric center causes the lack of sufficient nutriment to carry in the system to give the pancreas, duodenum and pyloric forces and juices in the digestion to act properly in the system.  Hence the effect this condition produces is upon the hepatic circulation, and especially in the emunctories of the kidneys proper, and this nerve reaction in the sympathetics produce in the functioning then of the emunctories in kidneys the overtaxing the system. The dregs of the system through this organ's functioning produces then the condition in the organ itself, and the condition being aggravated by those conditions as produced in the pelvic organs in their functioning, produced by age and condition of the system's functioning.  In the portion of the system that the nerve ends effect, first we find in the pancreas, and this first produced in times back that condition as was termed gastritis or that of dyspepsia, and the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of system with the sedimentary to allay conditions, and to produce lactic state through intestinal tract and the liver's functioning, produces the taxation in the hepatic circulation.  This, then, has produced the long-standing or a constitutional condition.  Reflexes from these we see affect the upper portion of the body through the secondary cardiac plexus, which reaches through to the face, head and gives the inactive forces, though not abnormal or subnormal in the sensory organism.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, the functioning of the sensory organism we find affected as given through reflexes in secondary cardiac forces.  Hence the condition the heart appears in when overtaxed and the inertia as felt rather than expressed by the body.

7. Organs of the pulmonaries show their taxation as in the blood flow being the subnormal.  Hence the bronchials condition, the effect in lungs proper of the condition as shown in blood supply.

8. In digestive tract we find still shows of the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] as forms in the stomach proper.

9. The heart's action sometimes above, sometimes below normal. More subnormal than above normal.

10. The liver enlarged in order to meet the needs in the system. In the kidneys proper, we find these shrunk, and the nerve from the portion of the system that is governed through the functioning of the organs with the hypogastric that of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve gives the enlarging effect in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about the kidneys proper, and the soreness in bladder and in the pelvis, produced from the effect of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] attempting to take care of conditions.

11. R E L I E F, to give then the relief to the body, would be first take in the system these properties that we may prepare the system so as to be benefited when adjustments are done that have produced and assist in producing present conditions.  Take those properties as found in Vinol, only taking half the quantity as prescribed on the properties, and add this:

Tincture of Valerian..................4 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium...................4 grains,
Bromide of Potassium.................12 grains,
Elixir Calisaya.......................4 ounces,
Sufficient simple syrup to make eleven ounces.

The dose would be two teaspoonsful every three days, taken preferably soon after arising.  These properties to be taken for one week before adjustments are begun in the ninth and eighth and seventh dorsal.

12. Do that.
