# TEXT OF READING 900-390 M 33

**Reading [[900-390]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/29/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5593

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor & Steno.  [GD ill.  EC typed it up?]]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind of [900]. This we have had before.  Now the physical condition, shows improvement.  While the body tires out with the exercise and strain, we do not find more complications arising at present. Keep the administrations of the antiseptic inhalant as well as that of the ointment, keeping the antrum entrance open, the nostril clensed [cleansed] using packs to reduce inflammation, taking properties as have been given, of Senna, Cascara and others, for stimulation of eliminations.

2. Well that the strength and vitality be builded by rest and stimulation.  Use at least once or twice each day the yoke of an egg well beat, then sufficient spirits frumenti poured over same to cook, then fill glass with milk, beating this together with and [an?] egg beater.  Beef iron and wine well for a tonic.  Keep in the open and away from crowds. Be careful of infection and cold or congestion.  Do not get feet wet.

3. (Q)  What would be dose of beef iron and wine?
(A)  Table spoon full 1/2 to 3/4 hour before meals.  Wyeth's preferably.  [6/1/28 See 900-391, Par. 2 in re health, expanding on above information.]

4. (Q)  Regarding information given in interpretation of [[Dreams|dream]] on stocks, that have gone down [in mail to you?] with rest. Advise body regarding same.
(A)  Would the body read that as given it will be seen these were given as various groups, but were not to be purchased for rise until there was indicated to self same should be done.  These however will come back in present and following session, and are to be handled in the manner as will be indicated directly to self.

5. (Q)  [[Dreams|Dream]] recently.  "At 41 you will be free."
(A)  Much may be given as respecting that seen and indicated in this [[Visions|vision]].  For the present know this:  Keep in the way that leads to understanding and the application of same and at 41 there will be sufficient for the body-entity-being to accomplish that as has been set before self.
