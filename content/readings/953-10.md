# TEXT OF READING 953-10 M 52

**Reading [[953-10]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/10/1924  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 5863

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 10th day of September, 1924, following 4476-1, a Physical Reading for a child in [[Chicago|Chicago]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time. 
(No suggestion was given to obtain this reading, it was 
volunteered at the end of 4476-1, although it had been 
requested previously by Mr. [953].)

1. EC:  We find there is near this place on Logan Blvd., (2546 Logan Blvd.) the shop (automobile shop) in which there has been kept and held the booty or money as was obtained from the robbery as was committed on the 12th of June, 1924, near Roundout, Northwest from [[Chicago|Chicago]], in the outer suburbs.  In this we find there were those connected with same who were associated with the messenger on this Milwaukee train. These, or this party, held for the planning of this hold-up is innocent of the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] as charged against this body.  Hamuel was the one that is [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] Mind in the robbery, who at present is in Frisco - [[California|San Francisco]].

2. The monies will soon be moved from this garage.  This lies to the West and North from the location on Logan Blvd.  Only square and a half.  Not quite that far.

3. (Q)  What is the name of this garage?
(A)  Yes, that has been changed also since this has happened - Old's Garage Company.

In this we find the perpetrators of this have brought the charges, for they have found through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of the guards that the inspector could be made to appear an accessory to the crime.

Follow this lead then, the two guards accompanying this shipment.  Question those as to the parties who left Roundout for the West shore, and who first carried (see, over $100,000 involved in this) this money to Wisconsin.  Then recently it has been brought in a Star automobile to this shop.  Follow that lead.  We are through.

---

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/2/24 Mr. [953]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> You probably heard or read of the mail robbery at Rondout
> North of [[Chicago|Chicago]] that took place the first part of June.
> The postal authorities have been making strenuous efforts to
> apprehend the guilty parties and to recover the stolen money.
> They now have in custody a party whom I feel is not guilty
> and a man whom did us a good turn a few years ago.  I fear
> that he is the victim of jealousy on the part of his
> co-workers and of a frame-up of the criminal element.
> 
> Would you care to give us a reading to see whether you can
> name the guilty parties, present location of the loot and
> names of those implicated in this.  And if such should be the
> case of the party referred to.  You probably have seen his
> name in print if not, I thought it would be as well if I did
> not give you the name and you could bring it out with others
> in the reading.  It would make the evidence more conclusive.
> 
> I have no thought of giving this information to the parties
> now working on the case for I feel that it might lead to
> complications such as you have experienced in the past.
> Neither should your name be connected with it.  But if the
> reading is tangible, I have parties here who will undertake
> an independent investigation both to apprehend those guilty,
> recover the loot and clear the name of the party referred to
> should he be innocent.
> 
> I am not sure just what, if anything, we would get out of
> this in a financial way should it be a success but it would
> be an effort in the interest of law and order and exonerating
> an innocent man if it is found that he is not guilty.
> 
> So please let me have the reading just as soon as you can.
> Kindest regards and best wishes.
> 
> Sincerely,
> 
> B2. 9/5/24 EC's ltr. from [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]] to [953]:
> 
> 
> As to the other reading you mention, under the circumstances,
> as you say, and in the way that you say, I think I'd like to
> try this reading for you, but there's hardly enough of
> information here for me to work on, and I do not remember
> having seen anything regarding the hold-up.  If you can get
> the date and the place why we'll undertake this, or maybe it
> would be well, if you are coming down the first of the week,
> that we try and take it while you are here.  I have never
> tried more than one or two such conditions as this.  The only
> thing we've ever had has been given rather in the spontaneous
> sort; that is, given when we were not expecting it.  Or, if
> you'd rather, just wire me the date and I'll get it for you
> the very first opportunity.  I have a letter from my wife
