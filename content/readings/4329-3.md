1:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. This should be worn continually, save when being recharged, and at least every other day should be laid across the brow, or the head, see?  for at least ten to fifteen minutes, while the body rests - just lay this across the brow, see?  Not over the eyes exactly, though it will not hurt to lay over eyes, for it would rest and change these. We will change these noises in the head, see?  these conditions that disturb the sensory system.  The applicator should be worn across the solar plexus - back side, see? Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  When should the applicator be used - the Radio-Active Applicator, and how applied?
(A)  This would be used after a while - that is, some six to eight weeks, we will find this will then be added to bring about the more normal equilibrium.  Discontinue its use entirely for six to eight weeks, see?  Then we will find it will be necessary to take this up at that time, then using the plain, or use the battery she has, and let the anode from the negative pass through that of other solutions. 5. (Q)  What causes hands, face and feet to bloat?
(A)  Improper circulation.  Blood carried to these portions distant, as it were, from central portion of the body, and when changed from the arterial circulation to the capillary and vein circulation, this becoming slow, or so slowed up, as we see, that this condition occurs, see?  This un-equalization in the system.  Hence the change to the [[Radium|RADIUM]] active forces.

6. (Q)  Are [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments benefitting spinal condition?
(A)  Benefitting spinal condition.  These have not been given altogether properly.  Not sufficient of the manipulation with the adjustments in the extremities, especially in the locomotaries - that is, in the sacral and in the upper portion of the body, see?  arms, brachial plexus.

7. (Q)  How many should I take?
(A)  "I should take" ten to fifteen.

8. (Q)  How often?
(A)  These may be made now once each week, if properly given.

9. (Q)  When lying on right side, especially, the act of swallowing causes a little sound in that part of throat.  One of the complications of a terrible cold a few weeks ago.  Is there any obstruction of passage of air when swallowing?  At times it seemed difficult to swallow water.
(A)  No.  A restriction in the equalization from the brachial plexus to the upper portion of body, and as the brachial plexus in its reaction to arms and hands is connected with those secondary cardiac plexus, this affects the circulation and the action of the esophagus and trachea.  Hence the condition as is felt by congestion in this portion of the body when swallowing.  When lying on the right side, this, as we see, produces pressure in the region of the diaphragm and the spleen, and the liver, see?  Now the liver has been a great factor in the condition in the body, as has been the spleen, see?  Now these being in the state of plethora, as has been given, this pressure, with this non-equalized circulation, causes these conditions, or that as a dripping, as it were, of air, or a leakage in the trachea's reaction.

10. (Q)  Does this same condition cause the roaring in ears?
(A)  Unequalized circulation, yes, to the portion of the system controlled by the sensory organism.  Hence the change in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are being given, that will be continually with the body in [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator, rather than spasmodically, and not properly given always, through the Radio-Active Forces, see?

11. That is all the questions.
