# TEXT OF READING 1745-6 M 48

**Reading [[1745-6]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/9/1942  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 7977

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1745]; this we have had before.

3. In many ways conditions are improved from those we have had before.  It is apparent that the incoordination between the eliminating channels of the body, through this crystallization, causes or indicates a disturbance - or an accumulation in the kidney.

4. That there should be operative measures becomes a question.

5. As we find, it would NOT be well for same in the present conditions; that is, with the lack of the ability of coagulations and this upsetting or incoordination of the activities between liver AND kidneys as to eliminations.

6. WE would give:

7. Build up the body.

8. Continue with the adjustments or manipulations to aid in establishing better equilibrium.

9. Do add to the diet more of the Vitamins A, D and B-1. These may be taken in the concentrated form or they may be taken in that manner alternately with the excess quantity of foods that carry the same influences or properties.

10. After there has been the building to a near normal count in the white and the red blood, and the leucocytes for the body (which should require at least six weeks), then again see what would be indicated by the fluoroscope, shadowgraph or x-ray.  And if there is an increasing in the size of the shadow, it may be well to operate.

11. But DO NOT do so at once!  DO build the blood supply for body coagulation.  For, this will not be an easy matter on the body forces, see?

12. Do these, then, in the manner indicated, and then - after a period of some five or six weeks - test the blood stream for the normal reaction in a hemoglobin and the count, as indicated.  THEN have another x-ray.

13. And then it may be determined by the body, AND those reading the shadow, as to courses to pursue.

14. DO NOT overtire the body during these weeks.  However, do not just give up or sit around and wait.  DO keep the mind active also, and that is best done in normal or regular activity.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  In what concentrated form should the vitamins be taken?
(A)  Whether in pellet or capsule or liquid, taken in water or eaten, this should be determined by self!

17. (Q)  What foods should the body eat?
(A)  This depends upon which ones are to be reinforced. This should come better under the direction of one making the adjustments, or the regular physician.

18. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. McBride) Copy to sister, Mrs. [1100], to be forwarded to her
"    " Ass'n file
