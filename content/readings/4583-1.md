# TEXT OF READING 4583-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4583-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1921 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/15/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12479

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the McAlpin Hotel, [[New York City|New York City]], this 15th day of March, 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; G. K. Nicodemus, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. GKN:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to asleep - a quiet, peaceful and restful sleep.  All the organs of your body will function perfectly, you will be normal in every way.  You will hear me what I say and answer the questions I shall ask you slowly and distinctly so that we may get every word.  Now Mr. Cayce, you are asleep and you have before you the body of Mrs. [4583] who is at ..., Ky.  You will see this body and go over it carefully and tell us what the trouble is, if anything, and what to do for it to make it normal again. You will answer any questions about this body, slowly and distinctly.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now there are conditions as we find here in this body we are speaking of, Mrs. [4583]. We find conditions that are abnormal in this body, yet we find there are some conditions in the body that are not abnormal to the individual, yet might be to other bodies. The conditions as we find began with the life blood or the blood portions of the body.  We find the blood is set for the age, condition, position of the body, fairly good.  There are at the present time some bacilli being carried that are abnormal to the conditions that should exist or be in the body.  The pressure is very good.  It is normal for the conditions, quality for the rebuilding or replenishing very good.  The nervous force and condition of the body we find the whole body taut with the nervous energy overused you see, so that many of the centers along the spine are abnormal in their action.  We do not mean to imply by this condition that the body is all the time sick or very sick at the present time but the condition or position of the body as we find, there has been at some time back a condition that applied to the mucous membranes of the system an affect that has left much of this nervous condition in the body, especially through the action of the lymphatic [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in their circulation adherent to those.  This has left, in some portions of the body, improper distribution of forces or used forces to be thrown off from functioning organs, those which we have to the head especially, to the throat and ear, so that in nature it may be to establish the equilibrium through taut muscular forces, taut by the nervous condition of the body.

3. We have a deflected circulation leaving at times at some of these ganglia as we have in the throat and head, used forces that should be eliminated from the system so that certain cells and functioning organs become thickened or impaired to where their functioning does not respond through the same [[Vibration|vibration]] as in which the body acts.  So that we have an abnormal condition existing in these portions of the body. There has been some affect towards the eye, those are different at present from what they have been.  These are in the ganglia and blood supply forces that supply to the centers of the functioning organs of the sensory system; while the responsive nerve forces are within the head or brain or cranium itself the action of the organs must take place from their respective positions, thus we have thickening of the walls of the eardrum, so that of the sound or [[Vibration|vibration]] through the brain center becomes above the normal on account of the condition of thickening of the walls of these parts of these organs.

4. To reduce or prevent from the persistent deflected circulation, taking of muscular force by nerve condition, we would increase the [[Vibration|vibration]] to these centers to bring back the normal circulation through centers affected by these conditions.  That is those in the cervical and those along the facial [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and muscles that come in conjunction with the organs themselves.

5. Use an application of electric vibratory force of the lower order but rising and deflecting or the reverse coil of electric force as applied to these portions of the body. That is, those that will act both with the muscular force or blood flow and those of high voltage that would act with the nerve center and supplying force along this portion, just along the region of the cervicals, not below the fifth dorsal but across the shoulders and back of the head would these be used.

6. As medicinal properties or those to be taken in to the system only those that would affect, or assist in carrying off normal refuse matter through the proper channels in the body, that is, just once in a while take into the system a laxative carrying the acid action to the organs rather than that would affect or produce too much of the lacteal.  As we rejuvenate these centers and increase the blood supply and force to these portions of the body we will find that the condition can be relieved and remedied in this body here, that is machine by them applied to static machines or electric force but of a low reverse coil carrying 14 cycles, 4 amperes, reversing at 90.

7. (Q)  Any special treatment for the eardrum itself, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  No, the less done with that the better it will be.

Anything through this portion of the body where thickening walls have come would destroy the forces whereby nature tends to take care.  This shall be taken away by absorption all being carried out of the system by giving proper vibrations to this portion of the body in the normal centers and ganglia governing these portions, not local.

8. (Q)  How frequently should this treatment be given, Mr. C.
(A)  Every third day. - Once a day.

9. (Q)  What shall we do to relieve the nervous condition, Mr. C.
(A)  Relieve the strain of the nerves by getting proper vibrations we have given.  We relieve the strain when we bring the body to the same vibrations from each portion of the body, then we have relieved the nervous strain and tension of the whole system.  All of this works in conjunction, each to the other.
