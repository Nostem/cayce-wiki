## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/21 Dr. T. W. Posey (D.O)'s letter:
> 
> "To Whom it May concern:  I have seen Mr. Cayce's work
> in several cases...  [See 4968-1 Reports.]...
> 
> "I saw him and heard his diagnosis in several other cases
> after that.
> 
> "Perhaps three years ago Dr. T. W. Stone and myself carried
> Mr. Cayce out to see one Mr. [4969] who had been paralyzed
> for three years prior to that time, but died last year of
> pneumonia.  To my personal knowledge Mr. [4969] nor any of
> his family was not acquainted with Mr. Cayce.  Mr. [4969]
> did not see Mr. Cayce until after the diagnosis was made.
> Mr. Cayce corroborated the diagnosis not only of Dr. Stone
> and myself but a very eminent physician, a nerve specialist
> of [[Chicago|Chicago]].  Mr. Cayce took this case up from the time of
> its incipiency to the time he was present with him.  I can
> only say it was wonderful.  I can't explain how he does it;
> I don't suppose Mr. Cayce himself can.  The best way to
> substantiate what I have said in reference to Mr. Cayce's
> ability as a diagnostician is to try him out on a case or so
> and see for yourself just what he can do."
