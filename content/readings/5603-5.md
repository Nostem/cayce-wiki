# TEXT OF READING 5603-5 M 13

**Reading [[5603-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/10/1930  Sex: M  Age: 13  ReadingID: 13934

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [454].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                              ... Park Avenue, 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5603] - this we have had before.  The general conditions of the body, as we find, still show the nearer normal and nominal development for the body.  Keep the outlined diet, at least while the body is in the ADJUSTMENT of itself to the new conditions or relationships with itself, in developments.  Keep those of the appliance, alternating at times from that of the plain to that of the [[Iodine|iodine]].  These we would have about two to one of the [[Iodine|iodine]], and these - as we find - with the normal exercises of the body in its daily relations, will bring about the best, the normal reactions and developments with the body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What treatment to stimulate eliminations?
(A)  These are best stimulated by the diet and exercise. As has been seen in those given, these in the diet should be that as is a mild LAXATIVE, rather than CATHARTICS, see? and when there is the tendency of this condition to become sluggish, those of oils - PREFERABLY much more of olive than just plain mineral oil, for olive oil - PROPERLY taken - is a food for the intestinal tract.  This would be well to be considered by many:  That, that may be ASSIMILATED by the system - of olive oil - pure - is food value for the system itself, and tends to stimulate peristaltic movements, see? Taken, then, in very small quantities - but rather often, and when found to disagree - or a tendency, from the foods or the character of drinks taken - discontinue; for nothing is more severe than rancid, or oil that has become over acted on by the hydrochlorics in the system.  Only that as will assimilate.  So olive oil, or mineral oil - in moderation; but diet and exercise the best.

3. (Q)  What is best attitude to follow in new school?
(A)  Follow much in the line as has been given, as respecting self's development; not as one that would be served, but rather study to show self worthy of the confidence, faith, and promise as is put INTO self - and study to be WORTHY of that faith.  In the action towards associates, be one of - and one with - those as will be attracted to the body.

4. (Q)  What should be guarded against?
(A)  Tendency of uppishness, or piggishness.

5. That is all the questions for this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
