# TEXT OF READING 4652-1 M 53 (Manufacturer of Pipes & Smokers

**Reading [[4652-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/28/1930  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 12574

Articles) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [2541], via [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2541] and L. B. Cayce.]

(Business:  ..., Inc., 
Time of Reading                      Brooklyn, N.Y.) 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical and Material - with questions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [4652] - also those conditions as associate, and are associated, with the activities of this body.

2. In the [[Physical Body|physical body]], we find the body very good in many respects.  There are conditions that have caused disorders, or distress in a manner physical, to the body.  These, as we find, may be met easily, were they considered in times from the whole, or the causes as are active, producing same in the physical organism.  There should be remembered in such relations and such conditions as exist here, that there is the body-physical with its attributes; there is the body-mental with ITS attributes; and there is the body-spiritual with its attributes.  One and the same in EVERY instance in any organism that functions anywhere near normal, and interlocking one with another, making for that known as an individual, or a person; and while there may be disorders that may be entirely removed from the physical through the application of forces as manifest through the body-MENTAL, FROM the body-spiritual, yet each have their portion to perform, as to the whole, and there becomes times when each become so separated as it becomes necessary to add that which will BRING a better attunement to the whole.  This is not a denial of any tenets held by the body, or that may be held by any, but made according one to another, than we may find that, that exists, even as from the beginning.

3. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] here, we find there are conditions that have to do with the eliminations, or the separations within the system that cause accumulations of forces that form either as gases from the system, in the eliminations, or as to produce to the hepatic circulation such fermentation as to bring those refuses into the system, and thence the non-coordination of the eliminations in the body.  As we could find, THESE would aid in this, and in bringing about the better coordination in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and also those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as relate from the physical and mental and [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]], with that of the activities in the physical.  Using, then, at least when the body rests of evenings, that of the Radio-Active Appliance (plain) for from thirty to sixty minutes each day.  The rest will be better, the activities of the organs themselves will become more nominal, normal.  The digestive forces better, and those headaches that at times have brought distress will be relieved.

4. As for the material conditions, and those as surround same. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Advise entity just what he should do with present business, ... Inc?
(A)  Best, for the present, to carry on.  There may be the combinations made, as there has been some tentative offers, or some tentative conditions as have existed as respecting same.  These, however, are not at the proper time for such associations or connections to be made.

6. (Q)  What steps shall body take to be financially more successful?
(A)  Carry on in the present line for the present time, and in the present surroundings and the present conditions - for these are NOT auspicious times to make changes.  These may be made later, even in the present year - but not during this PRESENT period of distress, or of DEPRESSIONS general in the land.

7. (Q)  Should entity and family remain at apartment or move? If so, where?
(A)  They may remain or they may remove, that dependent considerably upon MANY conditions that have to do with various MEMBERS, even, of the family.  There are, as seen - when taken into consideration - many of the conditions as surround this body, and the body's associations and relationships with others and family considerations.  They should be more in the open than in an apartment, or even in the present environs!

8. (Q)  Should a national campaign for increasing pipe sales be considered?  If so, how can money be obtained to do this in a big way?
(A)  Should not be considered in the present.  In the beginning of the campaigns, as ordinarily come for such conditions - or for the propaganda as might be termed for such conditions - these may be BEGUN in a SMALL way, and then gradually increased as the success OF that BEGUN shows itself.  Very seldom, very rarely, comes there the circumstance, that when an attempt is made to put on - or to create such large demand by a pressure of circumstance, whether that of advertising or that of any OTHER nature, that there ISN'T the same rebound.  Better that, in ANY such relations, this be a growth.  Remember that, all things are of a natural growth.  What happens when even the product of that which would be used in the manufacture were FORCED to maturity for the use in the manufacture?  They would become dull, or become lag - even in the distribution.  Just so with the creating of the distribution of such a product. BEGIN with not too large, and THEN there will be seen a growth that may be made expedient and with the efficient, and long LASTING.  Do THAT!  Better way!

9. (Q)  Is the Beryllium Co. going to be a success and will it benefit me?
(A)  Should be a success, and should be a benefit to all concerned.

10. (Q)  What about the following stock holding?  Shall I hold or sell:  United Gas Improvement, General Food, Radio-Keith -
(A)  (Interrupting)  HOLD!

11. (Q)  All three I've named?
(A)  No.

12. (Q)  Wabash Railroad -
(A)  Buy all you can get!

13. (Q)  [[Chicago|Chicago]] & Eastern Illinois -
(A)  Don't mess with that too much.

14. (Q)  Columbia Graphophone -
(A)  Is good, if its added with the Carbon - yes, better.

15. (Q)  Continental Oil -
(A)  Be better after a while.  Not so good at present.

16. (Q)  National Union Radio -
(A)  Not so well.

17. (Q)  Anaconda -
(A)  Coppers, huh?  These are rather - for LONG holdings are good - Anaconda Copper, or any coppers - but for the activities that occur on margin, not much good.

18. (Q)  Int. Tel & Tel -
(A)  All that can be safely guarded, hold - for there will be great changes in this.  All that can be held, hold for the combine.

19. (Q)  Shall entity remain in present business or make complete change?  If so, what to go into?
(A)  As given, don't make change at present.  Remain at present.  Then there may be some combines that may be made, that would be beneficial and helpful to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

20. (Q)  That is all the questions.  Any further advice?
(A)  Make sure that there is understood those relationships that exist between the [[Mental Body|mental body]], the physical body, and the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] - and in the application of forces, whether as applied to business relationships, social relationships, or financial - that they are one, even as the physical, mental and spiritual bodies are one, and are answerable to one source.  We are through.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
