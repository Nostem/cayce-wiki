# TEXT OF READING 1393-3 F 4

**Reading [[1393-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/9/1937  Sex: F  Age: 4  ReadingID: 7117

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 9th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 to 12:05 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.       ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, while there is progression of the disturbing conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, there has NOT been the retention in the growth of those ends upon the bone; and there would be the need of the operative measures - for the removal of these.

3. It will require precautions, it will require the consideration of the condition of the blood - if there will be kept that efficiency in same for the recuperative measures to prevent becoming so set as to prevent activity.

4. But this we would find necessary.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is the baby in condition where she can stand a double operation?
(A)  As we find, it would be more preferable for the double than to wait for longer periods.  For the requirements in the natural forces of the body towards recuperative activities are such that it would be preferable for a very close or a double operation.

7. (Q)  Who would you advise, Dr. Kleinberg or Dr. Fred H. Albee?
(A)  As we find there has been indicated Kleinberg as the preferences to eliminate these disturbances without operative forces.  But this not being accomplished, the other we find would be in keeping with that idea of the double operation - as well as the CONSISTENT suggestive forces towards watching closely - before operative forces - the ability in the system, through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reaction, to create that necessary for coagulation after such an operation.  Albee, then.

8. (Q)  What is the condition of the blood at present?
(A)  As just indicated, precautions must be taken in this direction.  Do not OVEREXCITE the body before the beginning of same.  Not by mis-statements, though it be a developing body - remember the nerve shocks that come to a system, as we have so oft given, may create in a mental and spiritual atmosphere of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] or [[Soul|soul]] a dread or fear - such as to create that which is destructive.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
