# TEXT OF READING 480-14 F 23

**Reading [[480-14]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/9/1935  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 3493

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1867 Kalorama Road, Washington, D.C., this 9th day of February, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Geo. W. Chiswell (chiropractor and naturopath).]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Detroit, Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [480]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, there have been those reactions that have not appeared altogether satisfactory; for with the attempts of concentration through the mental proclivities of the conscious mind, with those reactions of general eliminations through the system and especially in the coordinating of the nervous system with the eliminations through the pelvic organs and activities, there have been periods of incoordination and a return of the dizziness, also when the superficial circulation has become more active and the poisons that should be carried through their regular channels in not only the alimentary canal but through the menses so that eruptions show on portions of the exposed parts of the body specifically, and in the emunctory centers in other ways and manners; as the rash that is indicated in armpits, in the groin and in portions of the body, as around the diaphragm area.  These are NERVOUS reactions.

3. We find that the diathermy would be MOST beneficial, as would also the stimulations of the 9th and 10th and 11th dorsal centers for the eliminations through the organs of gestation.

4. During such periods we would leave off the [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]]; however, the Tonicine in the system would be more helpful at such periods, as also would the sitz baths and the rubbing of the feet and especially the soles of the feet if they are especially heated and with those properties that tend to make for an increased circulation through the lower extremities.

5. Keep the body warm at ALL periods, especially just before and during the period of menstruation.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Please explain loss of weight and how to correct same.
(A)  Most of this arises from the nervous irritations through the anxiety of the body as related to its physical-mental activities, or its studies.  While such studies take up the vitality of the system, as we find, a closer adherence to a diet that will supply sufficient of all the elements and vitamins necessary for the replenishing of the body will meet the needs for the replenishing forces of the body; such as would be found in the WHOLE WHEAT, that will supply heat, that will supply the Vitamins A, B, C and G that are so necessary for the proper replenishing of the body.  And in this way the weight would be increased.

We would use the juice or the extract of liver now in the place of [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]].

8. (Q)  What will relieve the itchy rash on legs?
(A)  Stearate of Zinc with Balsam will relieve the exterior itching, and we will find that these changes or the increase in the circulation through stimulating the centers indicated will carry these drosses off through the general system.

9. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Keep the mental attitude in the constructive forces, without the anxiety for the material-minded reactions in the studies; keeping those eliminations throughout the alimentary canal and being mindful of the diet - these would be the better for the body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

[Questions asked in between 8 & 9 as given in above rdg. for [480], 2/9/35]

11. (Q)  How long before this haziness will pass from her mind and thinking ability?
(A)  As we find, if the body were in those associations of wedded life it would make for the relief of those strains to the system.  And when this is accomplished we may expect better reactions in these directions; for there is a suppression through these activities in the system.

12. (Q)  Should she continue at school for the next four months until graduation?
(A)  As we find, this may be accomplished if there will be less of the ANXIETY from the mental activities of the body.

13. (Q)  Would you advise a change of climate?
(A)  Not in the immediate; for this would be inconsistent with remaining at school.

14. (Q)  Will this haziness clear?
(A)  As indicated, through those channels as indicated would make for such changes in the bodily functions as to be more helpful.

15. (Q)  Would you advise marriage, then, for this body:  and how soon?
(A)  As early as there may be had the satisfying of the IMPULSES in relationships to such associations.

16. (Q)  Then, you advise that marriage would be helpful?
(A)  Would help to overcome this condition, as indicated.

Copy to Mother - separate from rest of reading
"    " Ass'n file
