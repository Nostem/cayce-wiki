# TEXT OF READING 3560-1 F 25

**Reading [[3560-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/16/1944  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 11046

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [3560], through Dr. Robert Wever, new Associate member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:35 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Pennsylvania.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - poor child!

3. We have the body here, [3560].

4. As we find, the application that are being made are very good.  The conditions as a whole are very well understood. However, there are those applications which if added to the body would bring responses from the other administrations - so that the medication and the mechano-therapy or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments would be more beneficial.

5. Every other day we would use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs from the 7th dorsal down to the 1st lumbar, broad enough so as to cover the muscular area and also the nerves of the sympathetic nerve system.  Over such a Pack apply preferably hot salt, about an inch thick sewed in a pad sufficiently large to cover the area, or quilted - so as to remain in place without moving.  Put this salt pack in the oven and heat, not so as to burn the body, but so the radiation from the properties are absorbed or driven, as it were, into the body.

6. These will tend to make better radiation from the kidney and bladder and the whole of this area of the body where the greater distress arises - even for the face and head.

7. The rest we would follow as the physicians are doing.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Wever
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.)
