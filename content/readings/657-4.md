# TEXT OF READING 657-4 M ADULT (D.O., M.D.)

**Reading [[657-4]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/4/1936  Sex: M  ReadingID: 4384

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic, Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of May, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [657], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions about the body, those things that have arisen in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

2. As we find, they exist in this way and manner:

3. There being a tendency for a lack of resistances produced by excess acidity, and an inclination and a weakness in the supply of blood forces from an anemia to the sensory forces, we have broken down - or have a collapsing of the Eustachian tube.

4. Hence we have not only those effects from a catarrhal condition which has affected and does affect the body, in the way and manner of a neuritic condition - and tendencies towards the effects of these poisons.

5. Then, to build up the system generally, we would FIRST make for a general increase in the circulatory forces of the body, by supplying a richer and better blood supply - through addtion of greater quantities of enzymes for the system. [[[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]]?]

6. We would then have, as the body begins to SHOW improvements - through building forces and the eliminations in the system - the finger surgery; those applications osteopathically made.

7. These, as we find, would offer the greater possibilities for helpfulness for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  About how long will the treatment take to bring my hearing back to its nearest normal state?
(A)  This is almost like asking how long until tomorrow!

Dependent upon the RESPONSES that the body makes in ELIMINATING the poisons from the system, and upon the building of the circulation in such a way and manner as to be able to recuperate the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

It would require at least nine to twelve months.

10. (Q)  What treatment for the spots on areas of the body? [Vitiligo]
(A)  As indicated, change the circulation.  This has been given for the body.  [See 657-1.]

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
