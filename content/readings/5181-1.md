# TEXT OF READING 5181-1 M 31 (Pvt. U.S. Army, Catholic)

**Reading [[5181-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/6/1944  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 13317

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5181], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by "Coronet" article.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Section H Bks., 
Time of Reading                               897, ... Field, 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time. ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions and those anxieties respecting the information which may be given this body, [5181].

3. In analyzing the conditions as we find in reference to this body, there are disturbances.  Though the body is very good in many respects, there are those tendencies which are a part of the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present.

4. There has long been a lacking of sufficient activity for increase in the body-weight.  This is because of the low amount of iron and of the elements which are in B-1 complex or B-Complex vitamins for there being suspended weight for any period of time.

5. This, combined with the general condition, has caused some irritation for the body as related to functioning of the organs of the sensory system; and thus, at times the taste, the hearing, [[Visions|vision]], speech, all become as crossed one with the other.

6. But we would find with the regularity of the body, if there would be taken KalDak as a tonic, and the head and neck exercises, we will improve the general health, and we will improve those conditions which have affected the eyes. KalDak may be taken preferably in either V-8 juices or in milk, but may be taken in water, if not practical for either of the other ingredients,

7. Do that, and we will make better conditions, better regularity, bettered weight for this body of [5181].

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  How can I build up resistance to lung congestion?
(A)  Read just what we have given.  We build up general resistance in the body, and these will enrich blood supply, better the assimilation and better elimination, and bring better vitality and better [[Visions|vision]].

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(KalDak is distributed by The KalDak Co., Lansing, Michigan, and sells for $2.50 per can.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/8/43 Letter:  "Since reading the article about you...
> I have become very much interested in what you are doing.
> I think it is perfectly wonderful, and I greatly envy you
> your unusual powers.  Mostly, though, I admire the way in
> which you utilize them...  I wonder if you could tell me
> anything about myself?  A few years ago, I went to several
> doctors who did nothing whatsoever for me, and since then I
> have had several illnesses, nothing terribly serious, and I
> know I am not in very good health now.  Please do not get the
> impression I have become a hypochondriac, for I have recently
> been married, and have never been happier.  It's just that I
> would so love to really feel well and have lots of pep the
> way I used to, and I am convinced there is something wrong
> somewhere.  I understand from the article that specific
> illnesses do not have to be discussed, and I haven't, but
> could tell you if you wished to know, something more
> definite..."
> 
> B2. 1/22/44 Letter:  "...I'm very interested in a Life Reading as
> well as a Physical Reading..."
> 
> B3. 5/9/44 Letter:  "...I hope Mr. Cayce will be able to give me
> a Physical Reading then, as I expect to be shipped out of the
> country in 6 months or so, and wouldn't have another

> (2) How can I put on weight?...  If there is anything you
