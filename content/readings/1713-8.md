# TEXT OF READING 1713-8 F 26

**Reading [[1713-8]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/25/1924  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 7890

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [1713].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                            ..., Pa. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  In this body we find conditions are gradually on the improve.  There are still some lesions through the abdominal region, where broken tissue through operative conditions has caused and does cause some distresses to the body at times, especially when the body exercises too much on the feet.

2. The [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] gives the equalization to the body and should be taken each evening before retiring, for at least fifteen to twenty minutes, along the whole length of the cerebro-spinal column, and especially across the lumbar and sacral region, and across the abdomen, at the point of the hips, directly across the body.

3. The diet should be more of whole wheat gruel and bran, and the body should take high enemas of the White [[Russia|Russian]] Oil to relieve the tendency of constipation, for the toxins as absorbed in system through such conditions produce the strain on hypogastric plexuses and on kidneys, and gives the fullness to capillary circulation, such as is shown in the circles under the eyes at certain periods.

4. Swimming would be an excellent exercise for the body, for not only does the respiratory system receive good from such exercise but capillary circulation is benefited.  Not too strenuous but regular and keep the body mentally fit through the forces as have been suggested for this body.

5. As to the weight, the body when in the normal [[Physical Body|physical forces]] will be equalized in and to that proper for the frame, the build and the correct mental forces.

6. Well that this body soon consider those relations as come with the wedded life, for with these cares, these conditions, would be beneficial to the body through mental and physical, especially relating to the nerve conditions in the body.

7. (Q)  Should the tooth be removed, which has been giving the body trouble, on the lower left side of the mouth?
(A)  Not absolutely necessary that it be removed, but better, however, were it done.

8. (Q)  Have the lesions disappeared?
(A)  As we have given, not entirely, else we would not have the bearing down pains at times.
