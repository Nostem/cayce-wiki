# TEXT OF READING 4571-4 M ADULT

**Reading [[4571-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/14/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12466

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 14th day of February, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this body before you see.  There are a great many changes in the physical condition of this body than when we had it before. There is apparently more sensation over the whole body at present than when we had it before and it affects the nerves of the locomotories more.  This, though, apparently a backward tendency to the condition, is in fact a reaction over the whole system for or toward a better condition through the entire body.

2. There needs to be kept up the whole condition as we have given, you see.  The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the region of the sacrum and abdomen with this same combination of balsam and sulphur. We would now take internally this -

3. To 15 ounces of water - distilled water - we would add 15 grains of [[Gold Chloride|chloride of gold]] with 12 grains of bromide of [[Baking Soda|soda]]. The dose would be 20 drops three times each day.

4. It will be well to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body thoroughly along the spine all the way, not with the balsam, but a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over both extremities, both arms and limbs, but just across the sacrum and abdomen with the balsam.  Keep the faith and keep up the work and we will find we will bring the physical relief as well as spiritual and mental to this body, [4571].

5. We are through.
