# TEXT OF READING 4062-1 M 55

**Reading [[4062-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/3/1944  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 11758

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [4062], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. William Krafft.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

12 mi. No. 
Time of Reading                                 On Auk Bay, 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Alaska.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Juneau, Alaska - Auk Bay - beautiful!

3. Yes, we have the body here, [4062].

4. As we find, there are disturbances preventing the better physical functioning of the body.  There is a general condition and there is a specific condition that is the result of an accident.  In the general condition there is the lack of vital energies as related to better eliminations and body-building.

5. We would take KalDak either in whole milk or in fruit juices, such as orange juice or grapefruit juice.  Stir a level teaspoonful first in a little hot water and then add the whole milk or the orange juice to fill the tumbler, stirring thoroughly.  Do this daily.

6. For the conditions in the arm, where there was the tearing of muscles that injured the nerves in the upper and lower portion, as to cause the inability for holding same, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the arm and the shoulder, even to the area of the shoulder blade and to the cerebrospinal nerve forces, with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] (heated) and Tincture of Myrrh.  If this is done at least every other day it would prove helpful in the body attaining normalcy in the use of the arm.

7. Do that and we will bring better conditons for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Indexing
