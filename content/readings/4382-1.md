# TEXT OF READING 4382-1 F 70

**Reading [[4382-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/4/1923  Sex: F  Age: 70  ReadingID: 12216

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 4th day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; had it before, you see [No record of earlier readings].  Now you see there are some changes in this body and the conditions since we had it before.  There are now some bruises on the body, on the hips, limbs and hand.

2. We do not find any broken bones, but some ligaments have been bruised considerably.  We would apply to these a stew [stupe?] made from [[Mullein|mullein leaves]].  We would take the green [[Mullein|mullein]], heat it, plain water, very heavy, apply it to the bruises.

3. We would give a sedative to the body to relieve pain over the body.

4. We would apply oil ointment to the scratches we have here in one place.

5. Have the body rest well; keep cheer about it, and prevent the setting up of congestion, and we should not have trouble from these conditions, though we have had more shock to the whole nervous system, but with the proper care and attention, with a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as soon as possible to prevent the tissue and ligaments connecting improperly, should have a normal body again soon.

6. (Q)  Have you anything else to say about this body?
(A)  No, only as we have given here.  Do that.  We are through.
