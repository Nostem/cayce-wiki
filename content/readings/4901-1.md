# TEXT OF READING 4901-1 M 77

**Reading [[4901-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/17/1931  Sex: M  Age: 77  ReadingID: 12920

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of June, 1931, in accordance with request made his daughter, Miss [307].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4901].  Now, we find those disorders as cause the distresses for this body are from improper [[Vibration|vibrations]] as created in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] by the reactions from activities, as well as from those conditions as have become active within themselves by this altered [[Vibration|vibration]].  Hence we find the conditions have been diagnosed properly.

2. In meeting, then, the needs of the conditions, we would add to that as is being applied for the disorders - for the abrasions or eruption - those properties of the animated ash, with the ultra [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over those centers (that is, the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]]) from which the head, neck, shoulders, receive their impulse for the circulation through the nerve and blood supply; namely, the upper dorsal and cervical region.  The dose of the ash would be one-eighth grain each day; the ultra violet being applied every OTHER day, see?  not direct, but to those that make for impulses TO the circulation and nerve and blood supply.

3. In the matter of the diet, also, we would be mindful that there are as this:

4. Mornings - the citrous fruit diet, or stewed fruits, or the like.  No coffee, no tea, no stimuli of any kind.

5. Noon - all the vegetables (GREEN) that the body may eat, see? especially of lettuce and tomatoes.

6. Evenings - be mindful that there are no bulbous vegetables taken, and the other vegetables at this meal should be well cooked.  No meats, or fat meats.  There may be taken with this meal a cereal drink, as of the Ovaltine, or rye, or such cereal drink.

7. As we will find, these will show improvements, and with this added TO that as has been applied locally.  Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should the body follow the treatment of Mr. Boone, the cancer specialist?
(A)  As just been given, that added TO, see?

9. (Q)  Should he stop the use of tobacco?
(A)  Any stimuli that tends to make dregs for the system is harmful to the condition.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [307]
"    " File
