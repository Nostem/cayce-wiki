# TEXT OF READING 2492-7

**Reading [[2492-7]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/1/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9383

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. [182], ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of August, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195] and Mr. [182].]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. (Q)  GC:  You have before you the instruments or attachments for the elimination of static, as given in former readings. Why do these not work?
(A)  EC:  This, as we find, is not completed exactly as given, or as should be to reduce the heat and color wave as prevents the receiving set in radio from receiving the perfect [[Vibration|vibration]].  The discs should be set at least 5/8" apart and should be in the vacuum tube, so that the heat will be taken in color [[Vibration|vibration]] through discs as it passes.  In the [[Vibration|vibration]] as is set in the acoustic vibration of the sending this we find is better than when we had this before, and gives the proper acoustics to the sound wave when this is attached and adjusted in the proper tune.  Do this change and we will find we will have the perfect, as far as possible under the present radio operations, vibration unnecessary in the receiving forces, will be eliminated, for we will take the heat wave, the color wave, that is called now the static force.

2. (Q)  Shall there be any change made in the figure eight instrument?
(A)  As given, this is now in the much better position and performs that for which it is intended, the perfect acoustic to the vibration.

3. (Q)  Are the colored discs the true colors and the correct size and proper diameter?
(A)  They are very good.  Truer colors would give the more perfect vibration.  As to diameter, so they are set correctly, does not have so much change.  The larger, of course, the longer will be the lasting of the instrument when in use.  This should be, however, in that manner that the vibration may be removed and adjusted as the necessity of the vibration that produces light must be incased.  We are taking heat and color, necessary the same vibration.  Better if this is silvered, or in the mercury colored tubes.  As to colors, these may be more perfect.  As to size, this does not necessitate much change.  Larger if we will find in the greater or the larger receiving set necessitate the larger or more perfect vibration.

4. (Q)  Have you any further suggestions to make regarding these?
(A)  Not with that being attempted at present.  We find that these will work.  They will eliminate when properly set so that the vibration of heat and color will be removed that produces, for the acoustics are good.

5. We are through for the present.
