# TEXT OF READING 4586-3 F 28 (Secretary)

**Reading [[4586-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/19/1926  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 12484

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [4586].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [4586], of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this, as I read same to you, and you will speak slowly and distinctly.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of the body, [4586].  The [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to the body are as the correlation of experiences in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] conditions (that is, sleep), as weighed with the body-conscious mind and the experiences of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] entity giving to the conscious mind that of correlated conditions in experience through [[Visions|vision]] or [[Dreams|dream]], and bring to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] lessons that, being applied in the life, may bring the better concept of the truths of life as applied in the various relations with the action of mind over matter, or matter as related to the action of mind concerning elemental, spiritual or material conditions - varying as per the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], as the application of knowledge does in the material plane.  Ready for dream.

3. (Q)  Saturday, Sept. 25, 1926.  "I seemed to be in a place where there were many men and women and was escorted by a friend of mine who I have high regard for.  Suddenly he disappeared and I felt I should look around for him and after searching a little while I located him in another room.  The conditions which existed around him made me very humiliated and I expressed my feelings about the entire matter very explicitly.  He tried to explain his conduct, etc., and while I believe, as I remember the dream, I tried to see his side of it, the thought remained that this friend was insincere, and so forth."
(A)  In this we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] experiencing those conditions in which the body has been warned as regarding those of the opposite sex, and relates directly to the individual as seen - for there has appeared times in the entity's experience when such relations as were seen in the [[Visions|vision]] has held the entity aloof from the associations with entity, to the extent of bringing disappointing conditions in the life - yet, would the entity have that same understanding as is visioned, there would come the better understanding, for the entity [is] capable of seeing both sides of the question, and there should never - as the entity sees and feels an expresses self - be two standards for individuals that would be associated, either in friendship or in closer relations - yet there should never be allowed that condition to exist in an entity's mind to doubt the heart that with true believing would bless that heart with true living, see?  Better to understand than to allow that condition of doubt to rancor and become the condition that undermines the mental and the physical association with others.

This then the lesson:  Be frank.  Be open, and any condition arising between friends should be treated as friends, for with friends, and real friends, there is no NECESSITY of any condition being hidden.  To others it does not matter.

4. We are through for the present.
