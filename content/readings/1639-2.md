# TEXT OF READING 1639-2 F 38

**Reading [[1639-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/29/1938  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 7727

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of September, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1639], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, conditions are in the position where BETTERMENT may come; though there will be at times some "flare ups" still with the finger - until there is brought about the more perfect coagulation.

4. Then, we would keep the applications externally; but CONTINUE to take the Codiron in sufficient quantity to aid in building a better blood supply.

5. Keep the eliminations near normal.  Be sure there is the full evacuation of the alimentary canal each day.

6. Once a month take a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] to cleanse the system - if we would bring the better condition in the blood supply.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is condition of finger?
(A)  As we have indicated, it is in the position for less of disturbing conditions - if the better blood supply is established; though there will be recurrent disturbances.

9. (Q)  The X-Ray treatments should be given, then?
(A)  As indicated, continue the external applications.

10. (Q)  When the flare-ups occur, what should be done to relieve same?
(A)  The best thing is to produce better eliminations!

11. (Q)  Am I liable to continue having trouble with it?
(A)  As indicated, for a time - until there are better influences established in the blood supply, and the vitamin forces in the body sufficient to make for the better distribution of influences and forces assimilated.

12. (Q)  Would it be better to have finger removed?
(A)  Not necessarily at the present, unless greater disturbances arise.  If all as indicated is carried out, we would not find this necessary.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
