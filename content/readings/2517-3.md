# TEXT OF READING 2517-3 F 26

**Reading [[2517-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/9/1941  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 9467

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mrs. [2517], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes

3. As we find, the body gradually continues on the improve.

4. We find it would be well to keep all of that which has been suggested, - as to the corrections to be made, and as to the strengthening influences.

5. It is better, as we find, that the B-1 Vitamins that make for resistance be assimilated in the foods, so far as possible, and as much as practical, - such as in re-inforced cereals, plenty of citrus fruits, plenty of all vegetables cooked OR raw that add such to the body.

6. To supplement same, - once a week there may be taken by the body the B-1 vitamin, - not too great a quantity but just sufficient to aid in supplying the energies.

7. If the ovarian injections are given, these should NOT be too close together, else we may overstimulate the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as related to these balances kept in the body.

8. We may then give same once or twice a week, if necessary, but we would keep - for the time being - the massage twice a week, gradually leaving these off to one - and keep these up for SEVERAL weeks yet.

9. Do these, as we find, and we should keep the body on the improve.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  How often would you suggest the injections?
(A)  Read what has just been indicated.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self (two copies, one for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]])
"    " Ass'n file
