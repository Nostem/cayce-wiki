# TEXT OF READING 324-8 F 7

**Reading [[324-8]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/18/1938  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 2436

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of October, 1938, in accordance with request made by the grandmother - Mrs. [413], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [324], Mrs. [413] and Mrs. [578].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before. Conditions show s great deal of improvement through the system.

3. As we find in the present, to cleanse the system thoroughly first, before beginning with a general tonic, would be well; but cleansing as it were with an ANTISEPTIC.

4. Hence begin with a few drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to be given once or twice a day.  Give until there is with the stool the inclination or ODOR of same having passed THROUGH the system. This is [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] in its reaction, and will not only aid in setting the digestive forces of the stomach and the intestinal system in a state of being purified but will make for the ability of the system - in its growing state - to better assimilate.

5. When as a tonic we would take Codiron; one tablet with the morning meal, one tablet with the evening meal.  These will make for the abilities of the body to replenish itself through the activity of each of the elements of same for resistances in the system, and put on weight and give appetite, and keep in better physical condition.

6. When this has been given each day for four weeks, leave it off for a little - else she might become too fat!  Then, after a week to two weeks, begin again, - and then, throughout the winter, give at least one tablet a day.

7. These will be, as we find, the better conditions for the body.

8. Be mindful that there is not too much sweets.  But take plenty of fruits and vegetables; and nuts occasionally - once a week; but when these are taken (the nuts), be sure there is plenty of bread taken soon after or before.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What causes the white of the eyes to be bloodshot, and what correction?
(A)  The lack of sufficient of the blood supply in the circulation to give the proper distribution of the matter throughout those portions of the system.  The purifying of the system to alleviate all the effects of poisons and the disturbances, by the use of the cleansing forces indicated, will correct these.

11. (Q)  How many drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] should be taken at a dose?
(A)  Two or three.

12. (Q)  Has the poison in the stomach caused the ear condition, and will this clear up altogether with the following of these suggestions?
(A)  It should clear up altogether.

If there is a continuation, after the cleansing of the system, then have two or three ADJUSTMENTS made osteopathically through the upper dorsal and cervical area - for this disturbance.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Extra sheet of directions was enclosed for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] - "when needed - or if needed!")
