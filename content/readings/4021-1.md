# TEXT OF READING 4021-1 M 45 (District Mgr. Newspaper)

**Reading [[4021-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/25/1944  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 11710

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [4021], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], [[Grace|Grace]] Premo, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, here we have a disturbing condition with the body, and a very unusual mind to deal with.  To be sure, attitudes oft influence the physical conditions of the body.  No one can hate his neighbor and not have stomach or liver trouble. No one can be jealous and allow the anger of same and not have upset digestion or heart disorder.  Neither of these is present here, and yet those attitudes have much to do with the accumulations which have become gradually the disorders that tend to produce those tendencies towards a neuritic-arthritic reaction.  Stiffness at times is indicated in the locomotories; a nausea, or upsetting of the digestive system; headaches seem to arise from a disturbance between liver and the kidneys themselves, though usually the setting up of better eliminations causes these to be eased.

3. We would find here that there would be better conditions produced by the use periodically, in the beginning twice a week for at least three to four to five weeks, of the thorough hydro-therapy treatments.  These should include Sweat, or Fume Baths, Sitz Baths, Bubble Baths; then a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and rubdown, using Pine Oils in the rubs, to cleanse and purify the superficial circulation, and to stimulate better circulation throughout the body.

4. Include in these, occasionally, the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray treatment.  Preferably the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] would be given through green glass.  It will be more beneficial to the body. To be sure, it takes from the ray certain elements that are needed, but it changes them to a [[Healing|healing]], rather than a destructive ray, in its passage through stained - not painted - glass.

5. Do have, through the first period at least two high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.

6. Then the attitudes of the body:  Know that there is within self all [[Healing|healing]] that may be accomplished for the body.  For, all [[Healing|healing]] must come from the Divine.  For who healeth thy diseases?  The source of the Universal supply.  As the attitude, then, of self, how well do ye wish to be?  How well are ye willing to cooperate, coordinate with the Divine influences which may work in and through thee, by stimulating the centers which have been latent with nature's activities. For, all of these forces must come from the one source, and the applications are merely to stimulate the atoms of the body.  For each cell is as a representative of a universe in itself.  Then what would ye do with thy abilities?  As ye give to others, not hating them, to know more of the Universal Forces, so may ye have the more, for, God is love.

7. Do that, and ye will bring bettered conditions for yourself. Work where you are.  As was given to those who were called, "The ground upon which ye stand is holy".  Begin where you are.  If there are the needs for other environs, these will be shown - will be given thee.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. May Miller, 105 N. VanNess Ave., [[California|Los Angeles]], Calif.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

> 
> An attempt to describe my condition, I believe is unnecessary
> so will not attempt to do so.
> 
> Hoping to hear from you soon, I am   Sincerely yours

> Omnipotent power of God that prevails throughout the universe
> for good."

> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia

> Dear mr. Cayce;
> 
> Please accept my thanks for the consideration you have shown
> me.  I have been reading the weekly papers and enjoy them
> very much even tho at times some of the subject matter is a
> little too deep for me.
>

> be taking up unnecessary time but have compiled a list of the
> questions as best I could and think they cover the present.

> The questions are as follows.
> 
> What may I do to regain my health?
> As oft a man's work influences his health will you advise me
> if the work of the present is suitable and will it be the
> best for me to follow in the future?  If so could a change to
> another firm in the same business be of benefit?
> Please advise if a change of residence would be advisable.
> If my present work is suitable please advise in what way I

> improved, that is how could I become more efficient in their
> direction, or would a change to another branch of the service

> Could I develop the ability to minister to my fellow man

> work?
> 
> Hoping these questions are in order and thanking you
> sincerely for your help,
> 
> I am sincerely, your friend [4021]
