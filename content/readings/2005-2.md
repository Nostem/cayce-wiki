# TEXT OF READING 2005-2 F 7

**Reading [[2005-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/15/1941  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 8525

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1947], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.  ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2005]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in the main developments are nominal.  While there are times when there are little flare-ups, we find that the present surroundings - with the keeping of the activities more in the open - will bring a very good balance for the body; both as to its general physical condition and as to the normal development of its mind AND body - and the application of the principles that are set for the body in its activities in relationships with and among others.

4. Then, we would not alter any material things as regarding this body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
