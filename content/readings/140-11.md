# TEXT OF READING 140-11 F 23 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[140-11]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/9/1926  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 547

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 9th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by her brother-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, [140]. This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come to this body in that way and manner as has been given, and should be used by this body for that instruction and lesson, as has been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

2. (Q)  Night of Friday, January 8, or Saturday morning, January 9, 1926.  "Dreamed Mrs. [139] died."  [Sister-in-law [136]'s mother]
(A)  This, as we see, should be correlated with those conditions which have been given for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that where reference is seen that the body sees self as dead. The dream itself, the correlation of the mental mind with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, then taking the condition as presented, that one that is in that position where some might expect death, and the body sees the body as dead, see?  Then the lesson rather that as is applicable to this condition as has been given, and does not refer to the body being physically dead.  Rather that as given, the body, though physically ill, is ALIVE more than ever, and with those same conditions as have been given kept, we will find a [[Physical Body|physical body]] functioning more perfect spiritually than ever before - in Mrs. [139], see?  [See 139-9 Reports.]

3. We are through with this reading.
