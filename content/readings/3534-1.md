# TEXT OF READING 3534-1 M 61

**Reading [[3534-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/5/1944  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 11019

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3534], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here, [3534].

3. As we find, there are disturbances but these are more from fear internally than from any specific cause physically in the present.

4. There were those experiences from a strain brought on by a heavy flow in the circulation, caused by a plethora in colon; but these in the greater part have passed.

5. However, if the body will have a series of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] it will bring better conditions and insure no reoccurrence, unless there are other indulgences that are not a part - or should not be a part - of the experience; freeing the body from the hemorrhage caused by this great pressure in the circulation and the breaking of tissue that was indicated in periods sometime ago.

6. The [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] should include [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, given scientifically - about ten days apart for the first three or four, and then they may be put farther apart.  These should also include weekly the light Cabinet Sweats, followed by a thorough rubdown using an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] (heated) and Tincture of Myrrh.

7. Then, cast out fear; that is, know the spiritual forces in which you believe.  Apply them in thy relationships to thy fellow man.  These have much to do with thy mental attitude and will bring for the body bettered conditions.

8. In the diet keep close to those things that are more easily assimilated.  Refrain from any of carbonated waters or strong drinks of any kind, especially anything with hops.  For the body is allergic (so-called) to such.  For these tend to produce a filling of the lymph through the alimentary canal, as well as the lymph flow in throat.  Don't use these things.

9. Have a great deal of raw vegetables, fresh - these should be a part of the diet every day, prepared in various ways.

10. Refrain from sugars in too great a quantity.  These tend to produce too great a quantity of alcohol through body-forces - pastries, pies or sweets.  Refrain from these.

11. Do this, and we will make for better conditions for this body.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> Dear Mr. Edgar Cayce
> 
> I received your kind letter, but I was sorry to hear
> that the appointments are twelve months in advance, as
> I have to have X-Rays taken this week.  I have not received
> an application, so I do not know what the dues of the
> association are.  I received two books, "A [[Search for God Study Groups|Search for God]]"
> so I will send one back and I like to send your son or the
> association a book by A. J. Davis "The Great Harmonia" "The
> Reformer" and if he wishes, "The Physician" but the
> Physician, I would like to be returned.  I am reading "A
> [[Search for God Study Groups|Search for God]]" and I think it is one of the finest books
> I have ever started to read, living it I know will bring
> health and happiness and joy, and a blessing to all that
> come in touch with him.
> 
> May God bless you and yours in your grand work is my earnest
> [[Prayer|prayer]].
> 
> B2. 1/1/44 [3534]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce
> 
> Thanks for your kind letter, and for the appointment for a
> physical reading for the morning of January 5th 1944.  I will
> be at my home ..., New Jersey between the hours of 10 A.M. to
> 12 the morning of January 5th EWT.  I will keep the hour in
> [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]], and may God bless you and your work.
> I like to ask what caused the hemorrhages last June.
> 
> I am wondering if you received the three books I sent.
> 
> Wishing you and yours a very happy blessed New Year, I am,
> 
> Sincerely,  [3534]
