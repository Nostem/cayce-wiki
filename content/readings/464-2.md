# TEXT OF READING 464-2 F 51

**Reading [[464-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/4/1926  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 3383

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [464].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House and Mr. Frank E. Mohr.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, the body shows improvement throughout, through the application of those conditions as have been given.  While we find that the gold, with the diet as has been used for the body, has not reacted to the eliminations in the proper way and manner, this produced, as seen, by the diet.  The [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, as is set by the application of the Violet Ray, have proven beneficial throughout.  The reaction as produced - those dizzy spells - is from the non-elimination centers being stimulated by this [[Vibration|vibratory]] force, in liver, spleen, and in the intestinal system.  Hence, we would use now, added to these, those properties as will produce more eliminations - not leaving off the gold, or the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in Violet Ray, in the manner as has been given, but keep away from those diets that cause fermentation in the intestinal tract, through the lower portion, through the colon, and through that portion of the body.  Rather those to bring the laxative forces, and the elimination, with a stimulation to the liver, see?  These, we find, would be in more GREEN vegetable forces, see?  No meats - not too much broths, or things of that nature.  Beef JUICES may be used, but NOT the MEATS, see?

4. Keeping up those same conditions as have been given, and we will find we will bring the relief for these conditions in this body.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is it that disturbs my sleep?
(A)  The inability of the system to react to the vibratory forces, as we see, disturbs sleep, see?  This will be assisted by the production of more eliminations, in a normal way, and of the liver and the spleen acting in a nominal manner.

6. (Q)  Why does my stomach not digest my food?
(A)  Improper eating causes much of the condition - the liver and the activity of same are not being carried on properly.

7. (Q)  Am I taking the treatments right?
(A)  Treatments are very good, for these, we see, are bringing results in the system.  The diet has not been right, see?  as has been given.

8. (Q)  What is hindering a quicker improvement in my general condition?
(A)  Diet.

9. (Q)  Why do my legs swell just below the knee, especially the right knee?
(A)  Poor elimination, and the poor circulation as is created to the lower extremities.  These would be assisted more by the adjustments of those conditions in the lumbar and sacral region, by the action of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments as would be necessary there for same.

10. (Q)  Why is eyesight affected also?
(A)  That of the nerve vibratory forces, as is set by sympathetic system by the pressure as is created on same in the cervical and dorsal region.

11. (Q)  What causes the large white spots on my hands and forearms?
(A)  Poor elimination.

12. (Q)  Why are my arms so strong and my legs so weak?
(A)  The hindrance in the lumbar and sacral region, as needs correction for the circulation in these portions.  Six to eight adjustments and treatments osteopathically given should correct these conditions.

13. That is all the questions.

14. We are through for the present.
