# TEXT OF READING 900-377 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-377]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/10/1928  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5580

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the LaSalle Hotel, Room 901, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill., this 10th day of March, 1928, in accordance with the request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; Theresa Rosen and Maryanne Custer, Stenos. Mrs. [136].]

Time of Reading 
8:45 A. M. Central Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind - [900]. This we have had here before.

2. The various experiences as come to the body in [[Visions|vision]] or [[Dreams|dreams]] are the manifestations of those forces as has been given [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or body and may be applied in the life and in the ways and manners as has been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  In re [900]'s distrust of Mr. [4666] - see 4665-1 3/8/28.
(A)  Here we find there are those forces manifested in which the conscious mind of the body has been combated with that builded in a manner by [[Soul|the soul]] mind and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s physical action.  See?  See?

4. (Q)  That is, this is mental now?
(A)  This has been builded by the mental forces of the body between the inner-self or the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] self and the mental mind - that is, it is a mental image given back to the conscious body, the mental [[Visions|vision]] builded though by self and is then for the study of self in the mental way and not a physical application of condition.  See.

5. (Q)  I understand that.
(A)  This then presents that the varied conditions as arise as present themselves are to be treated, are to be met, are to be experienced through the various channels as these are presented to the body.

6. (Q)  Concerning [137].
(A)  In this, this same condition exists.  These are builded in the [[Mental Body|mental body]].  See.  Now when you meet these conditions, these are to be met in the same way and manner as these are experienced for in fact while these conditions as seen have been experienced but consider that the body, the bodies passing through the experience has [[Visions|vision]], have also their own mental visions of that builded by their experience.

Co-relation of the two presents a different aspect.  Not that troubles will arise from the conditions, rather that self is experiencing before hand that knowledge of conditions experienced by others; hence, is capable of meeting those mental conditions where remembering, as has been given, that the mental builds the physical, is the result of that building.  See.

7. (Q)  Concerning [91].
(A)  You would for one has to have.  These are being builded in minds of the others.  Do not over-ride or over-step self by being too heavily over-armed; by being forewarned one if forearmed as to conditions; but if one is looking or expecting a crash, one may expect to have to meet that same bad condition.  If they do continue building those conditions in their own mental abilities, their own mental vision - building, building, building.

Looking for trouble, then you find it within yourselves; looking for resentment, then resentment is within thine own self for look to thy own arms of mental for if one would read self for same that causes strife or dissension or trouble for mental builded conditions or mental vision for physical effects, then to meet these conditions keep thyself well in hand - that is, as the conditions arise there will be given thee that thou should say and the manner of activity for it has been said:  "When thy [[Purpose|purpose]], old son, is right, then, lo, I am with thee.  If the Lord be on thy side, who shall be afraid?"

8. We are through.
