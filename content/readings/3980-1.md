# TEXT OF READING 3980-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3980-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/4/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11659

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the New Southern Hotel, Parlor L, [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of September, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [3980].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno. Mrs. [3980] and others.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M.                                     ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GD:  You will give a physical reading, answering the following questions:  The doctor says this body is unable to bear children, that she is sub-normal.  Please tell us if this is true.  You will give the cause of this defect, if any, also the treatment for same.  Why is the menstrual period unnatural?  If these conditions can be corrected, how long will it be before this body can bear children?  You will speak very slowly and distinctly.

2. EC:  Now, we find the physical conditions in this body very good in many respects.  There are some abnormal conditions regarding the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of reproduction, and these have somewhat to do with the condition of the body at menstrual periods, for with the abnormal condition in the endoderm [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], [adrenal?  endocrine?] and in the lactic [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], this produces the condition as is shown in the menstrual flow, for this becomes abnormal.  The condition is such, however, that this may be corrected, and the body may be brought to the condition where conception will take place in the normal manner, for the cause of this condition dates back to a time when we had congestion produced from cold, and a neuralgia condition, in the Fallopian Tubes, and affecting the genitation, or gestation, in system.  This has left the constant flow through Fallopian Tubes of an unnatural condition as exists.

3. To give then the proper [[Vibration|vibration]], we would take the injection, hypodermically, of the gland necessary to produce the incentive of the genitation in system.  Necessary that at least three such injections be made in the body, and taking the [[Vibration|vibration]] as would be found in the violet ray, with that applicator that is used as the vaginal applicator, or that projecting into the vagina, using a low current and keeping such application at least every day for three to five minutes.  Also give the stoops [stupes], or prepare properties in this manner:

4. To a gallon and a half of very hot water in a pail, or container over which the body may sit, add one ounce of Tincture of Myrrh, and 30 grains of Balsam of Tolu.  Let this be covered until Tolu is dissolved.  Then let the body sit over this and the steam from same enter the pubes and to the parts of body.  This will add to the incentive to produce in the system the conditions necessary to bring about conception.  This we will find can be completed within four to six months.  Not necessary that this be used continually, but use consistently with the conditions as the body improves from the injection, which would be taken, and the [[Vibration|vibration]] from the violet ray.  Do that for these conditions, and we will find that the first child will be a girl that will be born to this body, [3980].
