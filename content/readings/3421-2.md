# TEXT OF READING 3421-2 F 39 (Housewife, Theosophist)

**Reading [[3421-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/15/1944  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 10885

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3421], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Mont.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  [after repeating suggestion in an undertone]  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are improvements; though there are periods when the body allows itself to slip back into that consciousness to be controlled by the formation of that which is as a positive possession - but a creation of the own mental and physical self.

4. The corrections that have been made are very good.  They need to be kept up, especially closing those segments in the areas of the coccyx and through the sacral and lumbar.

5. Do begin the use of the Radio-Active Appliance, either twice each day for 40 minutes, or once each day for one hour just before retiring.  If the body goes to sleep with the Appliance attached, have someone else disconnect it and not arouse the body after it has been used for one hour.  If allowed to remain attached too long, it may bring disturbance.  Do keep the plates very clean, polishing them with the emery paper before and after attaching to the body. Do attach the same plate first each time.  It makes a difference in the cycle set up in the applicator.

6. Do these and be persistent.  Do use the period when the Appliance is being used as the period for [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]].  Let the [[Prayer|prayer]] be, though in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s own words:

GOD, THE FATHER, MAKER OF HEAVEN AND EARTH!  IN THY LOVE, IN THY MERCY, BE THOU WITH ME JUST NOW; THAT THE [[Holy Spirit|SPIRIT OF TRUTH]] AS MANIFESTED IN [[Jesus Christ|JESUS]] [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]] MAY ABIDE IN AND THROUGH ME; THAT THERE MAY BE THE [[Healing|HEALING]] NECESSARY FOR ME (calling thine own name, personally) TO BE THE BETTER CHANNEL FOR THE MAGNIFYING OF THY LOVE IN THE EARTH.

7. We are through.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Spohr) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

> healed 'to become a better channel for the magnifying of His
> love'.  I have always had that in mind, but somehow through
> you it has become a living truth.  Thank you..."

> R2. 5/8/44 Letter:  "...I have been taking my Radio-Active
> treatments regularly every night before retiring, except for
> a few days between periods for 4 cycles each, making 6 days
> treatments in each group.  The instructions advise a few
> days' rest between.  I am now on my 3rd series of treatments

> while I am taking my treatments.  In fact there is an

> the God within.  I am confident that with the 'persistence
> and consistence' suggested in my reading, I will finally
> regain my freedom even tho it takes longer than I at first
> hoped.  We feel so sorry to hear of your illness..."

> R3. 7/11/44 Letter:  "It is time for a report on my husband and
> myself.  I hesitate to write one because it is very

> loss as to what to do for me to relieve me, and he tries so
> hard.  I don't suppose there could be another check reading?
> ...I have tried to have faith, and work with all the strength
> and wisdom I possess, but at times I feel that I cannot go on
> any longer.  It is all in the hands of our gracious Father,
> of course, so may His will be done...  We are praying...  We
> are preparing to sell our home and grocery store and move
> south in search of health..."
> 
> R4. 8/2/44 Letter:  "I gladly accept your kindness in offering
> me another check reading...  May I suggest that this one be
> at the 'life' level?  I believe we might get closer to the
> difficulty.  I have mentioned our [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyant]] friend before.

> me to the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].  Each time he has pointed out a

> osteopath hadn't yet found.  Last week he came again and this

> He went up an inch higher than the osteopath has ever gone,
> and found above the coccyx, where there had been a break at
> some time.  This place is so sore, it hurt terribly, and he
> believes that after this is worked on for a while, I might
> begin to find relief, as the 'center' down there closes up so
> the creature cannot enter.  He has seen the creature many
> times, and says it is on the etheric level, not affecting
> either my astral or mental bodies.  He said only an etheric
> creature could give my [[Physical Body|physical body]] such a reaction.  We
> have worked and tried everything these many years, and are
> so exhausted and weary of it sometimes we feel that we just
> can't do any more, tho my husband says he will keep trying as
> long as he has an ounce of energy left, and a dollar.  How
> grateful we would be to find relief.  The Appliance, from our
> experience, would irritate this creature, as does anything
> directed which affects it.  When we work with prayer in a
> powerful manner, it is so irritated that the reaction in my
> body is more violent than ever.  It resents the word of God
> or anything which might eventually mean its death.  I am
> sure the Appliance would have that lovely effect of sleep on
> my body too, if it were only a physical condition."

> The nerve trunk seems also to be improving.  I shall await
> the Check Reading with a great deal of interest.  If there is
> more that I can do, or if there is something not being done
> right, I shall be only too glad to know it.  I am heartily in
> sympathy with your work, and am hoping for the time to come
> soon when we can have this higher knowledge and guidance in
> all our [[Healing|healing]] work.  I think your explanation of the action

> feed on healthy tissue but on waste matter accumulated in the
> system, so any outside entity could only feed on stagnant
> energy and not on energy directed in constructive action.  I
> am sure there has been a suppression of sex energy in Mrs.

> also that there has been a partial break somewhere on the
> circuit (perhaps at the coccyx) of the flow of cosmic energy
