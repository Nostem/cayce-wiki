# TEXT OF READING 3790-1 F 23

**Reading [[3790-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/22/1926  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 11358

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3790].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading                             House No. 152A, 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., W. Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are those abnormal conditions with the body that may be assisted, and that these same conditions may be eradicated, were those conditions followed out in a persistent and consistent manner to overcome this physical change necessary to prevent the re-occurrence of this incoordination as exists in the body at times.

3. We find there are two specific causes that produce this condition, while there are many minor conditions connected with same.  These all must be considered, taken into consideration when those conditions are applied to the body for the correction of physical defects that are of the nature of a prenatal affection [infection?] in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that have to do with the equilibrium of the body - [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] - that runs through the body, from the base of the brain.

4. The condition that must be met, then, is to create that in the system in the rejuvenation of those portions that go to make up, from the physical standpoint, that atomic force through which each gland, or each organ, rejuvenates or rebuilds itself.  Then, as the nature of the condition is seen, the necessity of a consistent and a persistent application, with all conditions considered, would this be overcome.

5. First in the treatments, REMOVE THE TONSILS, see?  Operation. Then build the system to a general health condition through the diet, which should consist of a very mixed diet - principally of vegetables and fruits; as much GREEN as is possible for the body to assimilate.

6. Taking (all of this to come after the operation, see?) those properties in medicinal manner of Maypop Tea or Bitters, see?  that as is prepared in the same way, or that as may be prepared in this manner:  Take Maypop Root and the Maypop Bulb or Fruit when just ripe - the root and the fruit at the same stage, see?  To 6 ounces of the root and of the fruit add 32 ounces of distilled water, and reduce to 16 ounces, see?  by simmering.  Strain off.  Cool.  Then add sufficient of the alcohol to preserve same, which would be, to the 16 ounces, 6 ounces of the grain alcohol, see?  The dose would be (and this must be kept constantly taken for at least ten month), 4 times each day, teaspoonful in half a glass of water, before each meal and before retiring.

7. After these have been taken for two to three months, begin then with the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]].  Especially apply this each day to the base of the brain, or the center or seat of gland, see? Following the whole track, which is down the cerebrospinal system to those glands situated in the lower portion of body, or in and about the genitory (genital?) organs.  Following the system, however, along the cerebrospinal system, down each side, which rejuvenates, especially, those branches of the sympathetic nerves that connect in their various forms to the various organs as are affected by the sympathetic system, and as are centers for the functioning of the ductless glands, of the reproductive glands, within system.

8. Do this, and we will find that by the end of the ten months we will be free of these spells and conditions.

9. We are through for the present.

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/24/26 EC's letter:  "I feel sure that you will know by the
> experience, that if you will follow out the suggestions as
> are given you will obtain results.  I hope you will let us
> hear from you from time to time."
> 
> R2. GD's note:  No record in the file of any further contact.
> A card addressed to her 1/28/48 was returned marked UNKNOWN.
> I believe she was among a group in W. Va. who knew about EC
> through Mr. Frank E. Mohr, who had coal mining connections
> there.
> 
> It would be my guess that the treatment was not even
> attempted.  From the looks of the correspondence and
> description of her living quarters, she may not have had
> electricity in the house.  Much effort would have been
> required to follow the treatment, and much faith.
> 
> R3. 7/60 Walter N. Pahnke, M.D., noted in his survey of 96 cases
> of epilepsy from the Edgar Cayce records:  Case [3790] given
> in 1926.  23 year old female.  This case was classified in
> group 1.  The patient described her epileptic fits as a "loss
> of consciousness and chewing of her tongue".  These attacks
> could very well be Grand Mal Epilepsy.  There were no
> follow-up reports.
