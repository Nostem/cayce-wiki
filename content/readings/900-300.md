# TEXT OF READING 900-300 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-300]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/14/1927  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5503

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Halcyon Hotel, [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]], this 14th day of February, 1927, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136], also [900], both of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] they had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, of each. These we have had here before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  [900]'s, Friday morning, Feb. 4, 1927.  Saw my mother in a faint.  Conditions seemed abnormal.  I decided to take things in hand and started to massage her from feet up and also to give her [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment.  [See 106-11 Reports.]
(A)  In this there is indicated, through this [[Visions|vision]] of the body conscious and correlated with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]], conditions as regarding the individual - and, as is seen, as the individual takes things or conditions into one's own hands and begins with certain treatments for the relieving of conditions, this - added to those conditions before - would be a preventative for conditions that may prove very aggravating and detrimental, as well as alarming to many. So, being forewarned, be forearmed - and, as has been given, insist that such is carried out to prevent the occurrence of such conditions.  Not that these are NECESSARY, but that the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] - correlating conscious, body conscious conditions - has given that to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that may prevent conditions.  Why tempt ye, then, those natural elements? Rather bring them to bear in unison with conditions as to bring about better conditions for the physical, the mental, bodies of all concerned, see?

4. (Q)  Does this indicate the fainting spells will yet come, in spite of medicine being taken?
(A)  As has been given often, all things should be done consistently.  Some of the diet and actions not being consistent, the body mind, the body consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], then, seeing and knowing this, gives to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that which will prevent or bring better coordination as to prevent the occurrence of any such conditions.

5. (Q)  [136]'s, Morning of Thursday, Feb. 3, 1927.  Saw Emma, the nurse I have hired for my prospective baby.  In this relation then I dreamed that rich people leave their babies, - but that I would (or should) stay with mine.  [See 136-55 Reports.]
(A)  In this there is seen that as has often been given respecting the body consciousness and the mental mind of the individual, [136].  Through the laying aside of the full consciousness, and with the weighing of conditions elemental, in this [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or subjected consciousness the entity gains the insight into relations conceptive and retrospective, in that as has been given, that the mind of the individual, the mind of the mother, would weigh much with that prospective individual, in the way of directing same in those and during those periods when the mental forces of the individual are so near to that of the subconscious wholly, and from same may the entity not only be helpful to the individual but that inner consciousness of [136] will be enlarged by association with such consciousness.

Not that the one chosen is not capable, but that the entity would gain so much for self from that close association. Apply, then, as seems well for the individual, and as is in keeping with that for which the child will be born into this material plane; being rather that channel through which the entity would ever feel that all had been given of self to bring that body mind, that body consciousness, of the child to that correct concept of the conditions to be met in this material plane.  Much study, much thought, should be given by the entity to that lesson, that condition, presented to the entity in this [[Visions|vision]].

6. (Q)  Just what does this mean as regarding the care of my child while he is an infant?
(A)  Just that as has been explained.  Not only that the development of the child would be within the keeping of that one looking carefully after the conditions surrounding same, but - as has just been given - consider the closeness of the child to that subconsciousness the entity is studying - for why should not the study be of "From whence came this living [[Soul|soul]]?" as the study of "Whence has that living [[Soul|soul]] gone?" see?

7. We are through for the present.
