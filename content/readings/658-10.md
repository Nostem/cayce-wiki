# TEXT OF READING 658-10 F 61 (Hotel Operator, Protestant)

**Reading [[658-10]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/25/1932  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 4394

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office in Pinewood, on Lake Drive, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of March, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [658], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [658].]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)  ([[Dreams|Dream]] a week or ten days ago, regarding 
Lindbergh kidnapping case.)  [See [4191] series.]

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [658] - this we have had before.

2. In the interpretation of [[Dreams|dreams]], [[Visions|visions]] or experiences by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]], there should be some understanding of the manner in which individuals, when in the unconsciousness of sleep, enter those varied spheres from which [[Visions|visions]] or dreams may be gained by such individuals.

3. [[Entity (Cayce Term)|THIS entity]], this individual, as has been given, has that ability to gather from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces while in such state, as well as [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces and mental attributes. Much of mental attributes may oft come from the power of suggestion, as may be had from that mentally and materially visioned, or there may be wholly that mental attribute from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and the universal attributes.

4. In this visioned, we find that much as seen happened, in a manner very close to the details as were visioned by the body, as far as the body concerned.  The variations in that as visioned, as we find - in that as is ordinarily termed that ERROR, in which the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces - through the activities of the circumvolution of the earth in its axis - makes for, instead of visioning going toward the East, the activities were to the South and West - and indeed near Arlington and the Capitol, were those individuals as visioned there.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Were the two servants in the Lindbergh kitchen [[Germany|German]], and were they involved?
(A)  Rather those that were of the second household, and were associates or acquaintances only.

7. (Q)  Were the servants of the Lightfoot household involved, as seen in dream?
(A)  Very much in the manner as visioned, though UNCONSCIOUSLY a party to that as was acted - see?

8. (Q)  Was Red Johnson, friend of the Lindbergh's nurse, involved, as seen?
(A)  Involved, as seen.

9. (Q)  Was the nurse involved?
(A)  Not directly.

10. (Q)  How many gangsters were there?
(A)  ONE, as may be termed a gangster.  This has been seen and given.  The rest were those rather of the same CHARACTER, but were not associated WITH same directly - until in the present circumstance.

11. (Q)  In what way were the servants of the Lightfoot household involved without knowing the way they were?
(A)  In giving information in the manner and the acts of the nurse, as to time, position, the place, and the like.

12. (Q)  Was the car outside of Princeton waiting for the nurse, as seen in the dream?
(A)  As we find, waiting - and change made - then went North, then coming South, then coming South - and still farther South and West.

13. (Q)  Did a nurse go on the boat that night, as seen in dream, with baby?
(A)  LATER the nurse took it; not that night on boat, as seen.

14. (Q)  Is it still on the boat at the present time?
(A)  We have the dream.  We haven't the child.

15. (Q)  In the dream I saw the child in a small town in [[Germany|Germany]], by the name of Arlington.
(A)  [[Germany|German]] individuals, but Arlington on the Potomac, at the Capitol, was where it was - see?

16. (Q)  In what way may the interpretation of this dream be of help in locating child?
(A)  These are too long since passed to be of material aid in the present, unless there are other [[Visions|visions]] seen - as we see at present.  Boat involved, to be sure, at the time seen - off Arlington - see?

17. (Q)  Was the nurse named Lena Taylor, as in dream?
(A)  Partially the name; partially - not wholly, see?

18. (Q)  What was name of boat, as seen in dream?
(A)  Not a large one - a rather small one.  As name - name almost gone, but had Anne something on it.

19. (Q)  Was it Ann Harbor?
(A)  Anncova, or Cardova.

20. (Q)  Is there something this body may do -
(A)  DREAM again!  Then we will go to Puget Sound, you see.

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Cayce file
