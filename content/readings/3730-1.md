# TEXT OF READING 3730-1 F 65

**Reading [[3730-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/17/1929  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 11238

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by her husband - Dr. [2365], via Mr. & Mrs. Charles W. Dillman.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [3730].  Now, we find there are those conditions as cause disturbance in the physical functioning, that may be corrected.  The condition has much to do with the nerve system and the effect as this disturbance produces on the system through the various functionings thereof.  This, then, is the condition as we find with this body:

2. In the cerebro-spinal system we find various ganglia that do not distribute properly those incentives as should be exercised through these portions of the system.  The resultant effect is disturbances in the functioning of the organs as are controlled through same, and also on the system as is disturbed by this variation in the normal distribution of forces to be used in system as the units of energy in development; also that as is carried on in the way of eliminating from the system used forces, where the replenishing and rebuilding takes place in the various functioning systems.  These conditions, as we find, exist principally in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal plexus.  Hence that condition as is seen in arm at times, and in the head.  Lower in dorsal region, we find in that of the 10th and 11th dorsal plexus.  Also int he lower portion of the lumbar and sacral. This, then, is of nerve exhaustion in many of its reactions. Again disturbing the digestive and assimilating system.

3. To meet the needs of the conditions, then, we would, osteopathically, make those corrections as would produce the near normal alignment throughout the cerebro-spinal system, giving sufficient of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] WITH same, or one treatment for adjustment - osteopathically, and the next treatment for the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], that, as the changes in the system take place through the releasing of pressure in these centers, the system may take on the new ENERGY in a SYSTEMATIC way and manner, and not cause congestion or overflooding of the system in energy in ANY direction; keeping, then, a coordinating force throughout the body.  This, we will find, will reduce many of those conditions and bring about the near normal reaction for this body, [3730].  Do that.

4. We are through.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
