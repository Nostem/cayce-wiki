# TEXT OF READING 4905-60

**Reading [[4905-60]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/13/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12985

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will tell us the condition of operations at the present time, telling us if the casing is properly set, so as to shut off lost bailer, water and cavings, and any other information that will insure the success of this well.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had this here before.  The casing is so set that the cavings and the greater trouble of the lost bailer or pump is shut off.  The water is not entirely shut off. That necessary is to keep the casing in the manner as given, so that with the cleansing same may be lowered again for a short distance.  Then there will be no trouble in drilling into production.  Do that.

3. We are through.
