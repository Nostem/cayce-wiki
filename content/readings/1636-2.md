Also we find that with the manipulative forces there should be the more general ADJUSTMENT throughout the whole of the cerebrospinal system; not MERELY the corrections in specific centers but a relaxing of the muscular forces about the ganglia throughout those centers from which there is the activity of eliminations and of the assimilating forces of the body.

THEN we would use the lower form of the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] that will accord or attune the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the body to better coordinate with the general system, - or so that there is the same active [[Vibration|vibratory]] force in each of the extremities of the body.  And the inclinations for spots where cold areas are indicated over the abdomen as well as along portions of the cerebrospinal system may be gradually eliminated by same.

8. NOT the DEEP therapy but that which calls for more activity of coordinating the superficial and the deeper circulation.

9. These done, and as we find kept consistently, we should bring better forces and influences for this body.

10. Ready for questions.

(A)  The toxic forces from lack of the eliminations throughout the system.  As the activities call for drosses to be a part of the condition, they produce greater [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]]. Hence the type of the Salt or salts indicated (fruit salts), that may eliminate those inclinations toward arthritic reactions; dissolving the disturbing forces, - if taken CONSISTENTLY as indicated.

(A)  The reactions through strain from that eaten as a part of the diet.  Hence as indicated, take the form of the electrical forces, the character of the treatments and adjustments, and the character of eliminants indicated.

13. (Q)  What causes the sore mouth and throat?
(A)  This again is a toxic force, - hence as indicated, the character of properties needed for the body.

Do those.

14. We are through for the present.
