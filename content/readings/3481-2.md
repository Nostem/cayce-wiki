# TEXT OF READING 3481-2 F 47 (Insurance Agent, Theosophist,

**Reading [[3481-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/27/1944  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 10962

Widow) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3481], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born March 1, 1897, Born Iatt, Grant Parish, La. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., La.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here and those records - and what records!  As has been and is the experience of the entity in the present consciousness in the earth's plane, these produce in the present very exceptional characteristics.

3. One not easily interpreted by most of those who are the acquaintances or friends of the entity.  Yet those who know the entity the better and, what to many are, the idiosyncrasies, love the entity very much.

4. A most capable entity, and that which may be said of the entity in the present sex may be said of few women:  she can keep a secret even from her own husband, as she did to her undoing.

5. In giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, there is much to be considered.  There are many abilities and yet, as for the entity, who is to pass judgment?

6. We would, then, rather give the entity [[Ideal|the ideal]] manner in which the abilities may be applied, and thus the entity must choose for itself.

7. Know that ye are in a material world.  True, ye are body, mind and spirit, but do not expect spiritual things to happen in material manifestations save as a helpful experience if used so, for the entity producing that which may be as a helpful, encouraging experience, and don't look for those things to materialize save by necessity.  For did [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] even feed the Disciples when in the normal manner there could be food purchased?  Did He draw from the fish monies to buy clothing, to buy trips, to buy experiences for Himself or Disciples?  or to meet emergencies?

8. Ye, as most individuals, and ye in a little manner, observe these and yet would have others believe differently.  In the application, then, first know what ye believe, spiritually, and who is the author of thy ideal.  Remember there are not authors, it is not plural.  For the Lord, thy God, is one. Don't forget it!  He's not multiple; there are helpers, yes, but each individual [[Soul|soul]] is meant to help.

9. Are ye doing your [[Book of Job|job]] or trusting someone else to do it for thee?  It can't be done!  Each [[Soul|soul]] is just as each atom, as each corpuscle.  For, remember, ye are as corpuscles in the body of God.  Each with a duty, a function to perform if the world would be better for thou having lived in it, and this is thy [[Purpose|purpose]] in the earth.

10. Know, then, in whom ye believe and in what ye believe, spiritually; then know what is thy idea and also thy ideal mentally.  For mind is the builder.  For it partakes both of materiality and eternity and spirituality.  These know, for these are true.  The results in thy experience materially should be, will be when ye are assured, pleasing first in the sight of God and harmonious in thine own experience.

11. As to the abilities, few, as indicated, have the material abilities as the entity.  Here the entity may have a career as well as a home.  But the trouble is, ye wish to have both in one.  They must be in thy experience, but apart.

12. As to the experiences in the earth, these, while not all, indicate the pattern of the entity's activities.

13. Before this the entity was in the periods when there was the lost cause and settled in portions of the land of the present nativity, as well as in portions of [[Alabama|Alabama]] and now Mississippi, [[Alabama|Alabama]] and Louisiana.  The entity was among those [[France|French]] peoples who were parts of that activity when there was the return to [[France|France]].  The entity chose to remain and became active as a business man, hence was in the opposite sex.

14. These bring mannish activities and questionings from others because of those tendencies in the entity's activities.

15. In the name then [[Elisha|Elisha]] Eli, the entity gained materially but through the use of those means of impelling those to come under the influences; and thus we find the entity meeting the problem in self today of being mentally, or forcefully by mental means, meeting such problems in the present.  These ye must find in self from what is thy ideal.

16. Before that we find the entity was in the periods when there were the journeyings from the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] to the Chaldean Land. The entity then was a companion to one of the priests; thus versed in [[Law of Moses|the law]] which had been a part of the peoples during those eras when there was the adherence to the traditions, as well as [[Law of Moses|the law]] which had been handed down by the sons of Levi.

17. The entity used these in means and measures for obtaining much of relief for self, as well as for other members of its own household in and among the Chaldeans.  For they were superstitious people pertaining to those things particularly of what ye term today [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, or the square of the sun with the moon and the [[Trinity|triune]] of Scorpio or such.

18. These are well when they were for the universal, but when these are used for personal - as is the inclination at times in the present - these become selfish motives, these bring experiences where the greater abilities are blocked, as they would be for any individual seeking self-indulgence, self aggrandizement, gratifying of earthly appetites alone without considering as to how they may be used for helpfulness to thy fellow man.

19. For, as this entity must eventually find, thy [[Prayer|prayer]] should be "Let me be the channel for helpfulness to others Lord, for others.  Thy will, O God, not mine be done."

20. The name then was Sephrath.

21. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when there were those rebellions in the land, and the entity was among those who went to those activities in the Ibex, or when the young king's son rebelled and set up his own activity.

22. The entity was close to that young king who rebelled, but so embraced the activities of a native who was in special favor with the young king who rebelled, as to lose favor and attempt to produce in and with its own cohort and companion, its own little rebellion and was "sat upon" by the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] who aided in reestablishing the activities in that land.

23. The entity eventually, through being purified in the Temple of Sacrifice, was among those allowed to be active in the Temple Beautiful to teach.

24. The name then was Pluzar.

25. As to the abilities of the entity, that to which it may attain and how:

26. As indicated, we have shown thee a more excellent way, choose thou!  For [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord is perfect and is not altered by man's wishes.  The law of the Lord keepeth one in the right way when man makes his will one with the Father.

27. We are through with this reading.

Copy to self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n File "   for indexing
