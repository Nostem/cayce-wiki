# TEXT OF READING 3961-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[3961-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/9/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11610

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 9th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have him here.  We have had him before, you see. Now, the conditions in this body are improved a great deal from what we have had before.  The power of elimination in the system is greatly improved so we do not have so much pain in the extremities - some swelling as yet at times, the drawing of some of the muscles produced by some of the conditions in the blood force.  The sweats for the body were not carried out as full or as strong as they should have been, neither were all the properties, the medicinal properties, as were given in the system for this body.  At present, we would follow these lines very specifically. Make this and take into the system:

To one gallon of rain water add:

Sarsaparilla Root......6 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root.......2 ounces,
Burdock Root...........2 ounces,
Prickly Ash Bark.......2 ounces,
Mandrake Root..........3 grains,
Buchu Leaves...........3 grains.

2. Simmer, not boil until reduced to one quart, add four ounces of grain alcohol.  Keep this rather as a stock solution, you see.  Pour off from this solution after this is well mixed, two ounces, then add to this two ounces of Elixir of Calisaya.  The dose would be tablespoonful four times each day.  When this amount is used make more until the whole quantity is taken.

3. While this is being used, treat the extremities of the body across the hips, down the sciatic nerves, across the feet on up the body for ten minutes each day with the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] with a very high voltage and low amperage.  Do this, [3961], ..., Alabama, Route 1, Box 62.
