# TEXT OF READING 136-52 F 22 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-52]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/10/1926  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 359

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by her husband - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.                      [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [136].  This we have had here before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Morning of Wednesday, December 8, 1926.  I was [[Dreams|dreaming]] of the money [900] was to make as a result of his book "God Speaks to Clem."  The number 3,157,572 [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] me.  I went to [900], who was writing his book, and told him of my dream regarding the money he was to make from or as a result of the book he was writing.  He stopped writing and wrote down the number I dictated to him in this connection.  I repeated 3,157,572.
(A)  In this we find the body-conscious mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] dwelling upon conscious conditions which are existent in the material affairs of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  And in the subjugation of the body-mind we find the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces presenting to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] the answer to the question in a two-fold manner, as respecting the results or the popularity of the book.  The number presenting the number of books, or readers, and then, taken in consideration with that as has been given, shows to the entity the large sums of monies that would come to the entity from these efforts, as well as numbers of people that would be interested in the truths and the messages as are given in same; as well, as is seen, the number representing three, in the form of fullness, in the whole manner, or the [[Oneness|ONENESS]] of the success of the book.

4. (Q)  [900]'s interpretation is that 3 - [[Oneness|oneness]] with the universal forces -
(A)  (interrupting)  [[Oneness|Oneness]] with the fullness of the success - not of the universal forces, more than the oneness of the [[Purpose|purpose]], in gaining recognition, in gaining monies, in gaining position, in gaining those necessary audiences with minds of the nature that brings the fuller light to the entity.  A FULLNESS rather of the whole, than of a ONENESS in the matter given.  See?

5. (Q)  (Continuing)  And that in addition the financial reward will come from those in [[England|England]] interested in psychic phenomena (Lodge - Doyle, etc.), who also have shipping interests, and who will read the book and seek its author and thus gain for [900] an affiliation that will make $300,000 for him.  Is this correct - will these people read the book - seek [900] as a result and give him business?  Or is this interpretation incorrect.  If so what is meant?
(A)  That as has been given is the correct, and includes in part that interpretation as has been given, but not in its fullness - for it covers a much larger field, and is lacking in some respects.

6. (Q)  In what respect is lacking?
(A)  That as has been outlined - that instead of oneness in [[Purpose|purpose]] of the spiritual forces, oneness in [[Purpose|purpose]] of the personal forces and thought.  See?

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through.
