# TEXT OF READING 900-416 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-416]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/18/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5619

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of November, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the bodies, the enquiring minds, [900] and [137] - these we have had before.  As has been given, the [[Visions|visions]] or [[Dreams|dreams]], or impressions as come TO these, come in a different way and manner as to be used to the advantage of each, or both, but IN the way and manners as have been outlined for each.

2. In that as presented by [900] in, "Radio 275, now selling at 301", these conditions present - as has been given - the variations in the quotations of these individual industrials or stocks are in line for the combinations, as have been contemplated, and - as we shall see - will be consummated when conditions as relating to the various patents as held by individual companies that present the combinations of efforts.  As respecting Radio, as has been given, there will be declines in this particular stock, breaking near 40 to 50 points.  These we do NOT find coming before the next holiday, or near the end of present month.  As has been GIVEN, in the ways as has been outlined - would the body, or bodies, follow those impressions, or those presentations as come from one that is in accord with that contemplated, and in the manner born to know such elements, rather than listening to those that would aid - yet, as KNOWN by self, not capacitated other than as a broader [[Visions|vision]] in that which not understood in material parlance.  Then, follow self, or those that WOULD direct, and take the consequence!

3. As to those conditions as may be presented to the body itself, these we find present many different phases of mental and physical, as well as application to spiritual truths that may be given to others in a manner that would bring to others the understanding of the relations as exist between man and the Maker.  This [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the body, [900] as seen at present - Aarat [name in [[Egypt|Egypt]]], Cyntus (?), or even in that of Achilles [name in Troy], may be the foundation, in the combination of that development as seen in the present [See Life Rdg. 900-38], upon which there may be builded that which will bring to self the greater development as pertaining to a mental and spiritual development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Knowing, then, that in the material world the application of spiritual truths as pertaining to spiritual things, mental image and material being, bring and build that into the physical as may be that which will bring praise from others, assistance and aid to others, in self a satisfying, a satisfaction, a development, that passeth understanding.  Knowing, then, that these are ONE - apply self in the way and manner as to merit then that commendation of self, and the laudation of those that would bring to the consciousness of self that knowledge, that understanding, that in which others may know the truth and be free indeed.  Applying self, giving self first in the service that will merit that as is necessary in the physical being in the financial world, in the social status, to give that to the world for which the body, PHYSICALLY and MENTALLY, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the ENTITY]], has been raised up FOR.  For, this MUST be given.  Fail not in that committed unto thy care, for he that putteth his hand to the plow and looketh back is worse than the infidel.  In the glory of that that may be received, then, be not impatient in well doing; waiting rather ON the Lord that would temper the inner man to that ability to mete out that as should be, would be, may be, given by the entity. Do that.

4. As relating to stocks, and as to those associations with same, harken rather to that one from within that may guide - by the attunement of self - to that consciousness of One that may direct.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

5. (Q)  Regarding the one just given, is Radio a buy at this price?  or wait for the general decline?
(A)  For prices as would be for speculation, these may be bought for 350 - or 333 FOR 50.  As for that that may be put away for KEEPS, as has been given, wait for the general decline.  That as WE-give here, harken RATHER to that as may be given through SELF!

6. (Q)  [137]'s American Tel & Tel - general decline would come end of this month - would give chance to buy Westinghouse, Telephone, Western Union and Radio at lower levels.
(A)  As given, as seen, these present conditions as may be expected FOLLOWING (if present conditions hold as set) the Thanksgiving holiday.  Holding in fluctuations, but continuing higher until that period.  Just as is seen in that presented, that the decline (or general) would not begin before the end of the month, see?

7. (Q)  Will these stocks suffer from expansive general market liquidations?  or are the breaks to be just temporary and snubby, such as we have already seen?
(A)  These will be fluctuating in the manner seen, until - as we find - near the end, or at the END OF the holiday, as given.  Rather harken to that as may be presented in those that are in accord with that accentuation in self, and who may NOT be in accord WITHOUT that perfect accentuation, as given!  We are through for the present.
