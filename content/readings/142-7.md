# TEXT OF READING 142-7 M 22 MONTHS

**Reading [[142-7]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/10/1929  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 580

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 10th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by his father, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading                        Coronado Hotel, 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Coronado Beach, Calif. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [142] - this we have had before.  The conditions as we find, while they have given some uneasiness, are gradually correcting themselves.  The change in the atmospheric condition, climatic, and the like, and especially of water, has brought about the greater disturbances, and the body needs be only cared for as respecting these conditions.  There needs nothing be changed, especially at the present time, from that being administered - for, as seen, these are bringing about the equilibrium for the system and, as has been GIVEN, the changes will be good for the body.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Anything special regarding the water?  Should this be boiled, or -
(A)  As given, the necessary precautions and changes are being brought about for this to bring nominal forces for the body at present.  We wouldn't change anything from that being done, see?  unless other conditions were to arise.  We are through with this reading.
