# TEXT OF READING 3468-1 M 72

**Reading [[3468-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/20/1943  Sex: M  Age: 72  ReadingID: 10941

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3468], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

In his room 
Time of Reading                                   in the 
Set bet. 10:30 and 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  Lockwood 
Hospital, 
..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, while the conditions are very disturbing it would not be well to change or to add to the conditions being administered under the present circumstances.  The applications made to keep circulation, to settle the stomach, to obtain the better reactions with the whole circulation are being very well administered. While these are not contributing a great deal to the disturbance of the agitans in the nervous system, we find that this will not respond under the present environs.

3. To be sure, when changes have been made and these corrections so far as may be obtained here, better results might be obtained from the applications that may be suggested to be followed at home.  These we find would help, but cannot be attempted with those applications being made.  Hence complete directions had best be given after there has been indicated just how far these helps may be carried under the present environs - and we wouldn't change from the hospital administrations in the present.

4. We would find KalDak of a great deal of help, even in the present, taken twice a day in milk.

5. When there are improvements or when the body is out of this environ, then we would give further instructions.

6. This is not finding fault with that being administered, for as indicated we wouldn't change it - except to add Kal-Dak.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(12/20/43 GD's note:  KalDak is distributed by The KalDak Company, Hollister Building, Lansing, Michigan and sells for $2.50 a package.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/13/43 [3468]'s letter to EC:
> 
> "Thanks for your kind reply to my letter.  Please try and
> help me.  I feel so helpless, so hopeless.  I have always
> lived a clean life and I don't see why this terrible
> affliction has come on me.  I have Paralysis Agitans, shaking
> palsy.  The doctors tell me there is nothing that can be done
> for it.  I have treated with some of the best doctors in the
> country.
> 
> "Your work is very marvelous, not understandable to a common
> person.  I have been a Christian and a member of a Christian
> church for many years.  I have been a [[Prayer|praying]] man.  Surely if
> we believe in [[Prayer|prayer]], we must believe God can do these
> wonderful things.  Yes, I do believe God can help me.
> 
> "I am enclosing twenty-five cents for that booklet on his
> life and work.  I think I will order a copy of THERE IS A
> RIVER soon.  It sounds very good.  I know Dr. Hereward
> Carrington very well.  I see he recommends it very highly.
> 
> "I will remit any fee you may ask.  I am hoping and
> [[Prayer|praying]]..."
> 
> B2. 9/23/43 [3468]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> "Pardon me for writing you again.  I just finished reading
> Mr. [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]]'s book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].  I have never
> been so enthusiastic over a book before.  This subject of
> psychic research is not generally understood by the public.
> I have made a study of it for many years.  I knew Dr. and
> Mrs. Crandon [Marjorie Crandon] very well; also William H.
> Butto and J. J. Walton, and Dr. Hereward Carrington of New
> York.  I used to think Mrs. Crandon was the greatest medium
> in the world and her work was very wonderful, but not any
> more wonderful than yours.  Maybe you don't call yourself a
> medium.  But surely you are a man of God.  Yes, I am sure
> your gift is from God.
> 
> "I, like yourself, am a long time member of the Christian
> church.  I sincerely wish I could meet you and have a good
> visit.
> 
> "I am sending today my application to [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] with
> fee of twenty dollars.  I do so hope you can do something
> for me.
> 
> "You said in your last letter that you were booked up for a
> year ahead.  That looks almost hopeless for me.  Isn't there
> any way that you could reach my case before that time?
> Please try.  I have suffered with this disease so long.  I
> have Paralysis Agitans and gets a little worse as I get
> older.
> 
> "Anyway, I am putting myself in your hands.  I am sure God
> will help me through you..."
>

> 
>

> reading.  Keep the period in [[Meditation|meditation]] and prayer.  Also

> the time of the reading.  I do hope we may be the means of a

>

> years, but the real desire of the person seeking information,

> 
> "Thanking you, and hoping to be the means of a real
> service..."
> 
> B4. 10/27/43 [3468]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> "Please pardon my writing you again, but I am so miserable,
> it just does me good to write you.  I know you are very busy,
> and you have been kind enough to give me that date of
> December 20, and I am trying to be patient, but it does seem
> such a long time away.  This disease makes one so morbid.  I
> try to fight it, but it is very hard.  I am hoping so much
> that you will be able to give me some relief.  I am wondering
> if you have any cases of this Paralysis Agitans in your
> records that have been cured or relieved.
> 
> "I don't think it would do any harm for me to tell you what I
> have done.  So here it is:  I took chiropractic treatments
> for several months, no relief.  I took Phenobarbital in very
> small doses and made me so sleepy, I had to stop, no relief.
> I took rabellon tablets.  They are highly recommended for
> this trouble on account of the belladona in them, which dries
> the mouth out so bad, I had to stop.  No relief.  Dr. Jollif
> of [[New York City|New York]] prescribed Hexa-Aetalin, [sic], Vitamin B-6
> intravenously.  No results.  So you can see why I am
> discouraged.
> 
> "Now, brother Cayce, again I want to say pardon me for
> writing this:  You don't need to answer it.  It just makes me
> feel better to write.
> 
> "Weather is cool here, winter will be with us soon..."
> 
> B5. 12/3/43 Questions submitted:
> 
> 
> 1. Have you any cases of Paralysis Agitans in your records
> cured or relieved?
> 2. Is the advice given through Mr. Cayce given by some
> disincarnate personality?
> 3. Will you give your idea of the cause of Paralysis Agitans?
> 4. My doctor tells me this disease might be caused by
> degenerate nerve cells.  If this is true, is it ever possible
> to repair the nerve cells?
> 5. I have taken several nerve sedatives with no benefit.  Do
> you consider these harmful?
> 6. I am bothered lately with constipation.  Can you suggest
> anything?
> 7. I have told my local doctor about going to you.  He has
> promised to cooperate with you in any way he can.
> 8. My doctor thinks I should take strychnine, 1/60 grain,
> three times a day.  Do you agree with him on that?
> 9. I took chiropractic treatments for several weeks; no
> benefit.  I think they made me worse.
> 10. There is no [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] doctor near me.
