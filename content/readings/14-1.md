# TEXT OF READING 14-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[14-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1911 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/18/1911  Sex: F  ReadingID: 46

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 18th day of April, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:50 P. M.                         [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.

1. LBC:  You have before you Mrs. [14] of ... Albuquerque, [[New Mexico|New Mexico]].  Go over her carefully, examine her thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with her, if anything.

2. EC:  We have in this body, of Mrs. [l4], a racking of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], through the inability in its present (or what has been, and shows in the present) - the inability of the distributions of the blood building properties to replenish the thinking faculties, or the action on the nervous force in the brain, to produce a unison between the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic nerve forces.  In its action on the splenic force, or those matters produced by the action of the blood in passing, or in the circulation passing through the spleen, to produce a cellular force, used directly in the rebuilding of the cells in the brain, that produce an over-taxed [condition], or stimulation.  In the action of rejuvenating these, through mechanical means, which has been, in the system, it has produced an anemic condition through the lower cells of the lungs in themselves, until we have open cell forces.  These actions, or the forces of these on the body, then, produce an expression to the mind in itself of awful pains through the side here, - the left side; that tired feeling along the spine on arising in the morning; wakefulness and restlessness from action of the nerve force and brain matters; the start in the sleep, the expression to the stomach or the intestinal tract, or uneasiness at the time just after eating, and especially breakfast; languid feeling of the head; pains through the eyes, neck, and tickling sensation produced in the throat; blood secreted or excreted or thrown out from these, at the time; or degeneration of the nerve forces in themselves, to the supplying properties, through the lungs.  Not a fundamental trouble, except wherein it is secondary.  We have the heart and the cellular forces through the side.  We have the same condition produced from this as we have from other forces.

3. (Q)  What other conditions do you see?
(A)  We have at times, some inflammation thrown off through the abdomen or kidneys here in the action of the forces in eliminating the used matter in this system, exhibiting or showing itself and the condition of these organs through the urine or that which is secreted from it.  These are the conditions as we have them now.

To remove these conditions from the system, by the action of rejuvenating of these forces, or the expression to relieve these from the mind in itself, that is most, through forces of action of mind on matter in individual self, received by the expression from the control of mind over matter, expressed to the individual, through the sensory organism in this body.  By the climation or the rarefied air, which as we have it here, which has improved the condition to the taking in to the lungs to produce an expansion of the whole pulmonary system, or the lungs themselves, so that we have every part covered by the aid.  To produce the best action possible to the blood, to the rejuvenating of these, assisted by those of lime, and [[Iodine|iodine]] here into the system which act on to those of the pulmonary organism. Rejuvenation of the splenic action of the force of the body by those of heat expressed from one body to another, or by manipulation of the body itself.  Not by rubbing of the parts except by heat that is given out from body to body. Rejuvenation of these forces, and the body will improve. It is bound to.
