**Reading [[4389-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/29/1932  Sex: M  Age: 80  ReadingID: 12228

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 29th day of August, 1932, in accordance with request made by Dr. A. J. Foerter, Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions that have disturbed the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [4389].

2. Now, as we find, general debilitation with slowness of circulation, and with the reactions and over exercising in the reflexes, are the result to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  The nerve exhaustion that results from same.  Not wholly senility, but those weakenings of the circulations through the arterial show the condition to be rather that as may be aided, and an easier condition for the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]], than for any other reactions.

3. These, then, will be rather that as will be an assistant and an aid to the better reactions that may be brought to the body-physical, in the applying - at least once or twice each day - of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the plain Radio-Active Appliance.  These attached to the body in the extremities, on opposite sides of the body; that is, when to the right wrist, then the left ankle; when to the left wrist, to the right ankle.

4. The medicinal applications for the system would be rather these as a sedative, and also aiding in the eliminations, so that the reactions to the system may be with the advantage of all those forces that may be assimilated by the digestive system:  To 2 ounces of simple syrup, add:

Tincture of Valerian................1/2 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya.....................1/2 ounce,
10% Solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium........10 minims,
10% Solution Bromide Potassium.......60 minims,
Tincture of Capsici...................1 minim.

Shake the solution together.  The dose would be half a teaspoonful twice each day, or oftener if necessary - but with using the appliance in the evening it would not be necessary to take this oftener than twice each day.

5. The diet - rather that that is easily assimilated, with as much of the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] (plain [[Beef Juice|beef juice]]) as may be easily assimilated.  Quantities also of [[Cod Liver Oil|cod liver oil]], that is easily assimilated - preferably in the form of the [[Cod Liver Oil|cod liver oil]] tablets, twice each day.  When this is washed down, or swallowed, drop 1 to 2 drops of lactated pepsin in half a glass of water and drink it.

6. These, as we find, will bring the better conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What dosage of the [[Cod Liver Oil|cod liver oil]] tablets?
(A)  One to two tablets.  These may be taken one tablet before or after each meal.  After the meal would be preferable; or two tablets twice a day.

9. (Q)  When would be the best time to apply the battery?
(A)  As given, in the evening, as it will make the rest much better.  Best that it be attached to the wrist each time first; to the right wrist, left ankle; left wrist, right ankle.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. A. J. Foerter
