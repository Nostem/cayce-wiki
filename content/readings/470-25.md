# TEXT OF READING 470-25 M 49 (Construction Engineer,

**Reading [[470-25]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/5/1939  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 3452

Theosophist) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [470], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will have before you [470], ..., Mich., in regard to manner of approach to Henry Ford as recommended through this channel.  You will answer the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find, we would choose first a subject in which it is known that the body, Henry Ford, is interested, see?  such as any of the activities as related to metaphysical subjects.

3. Acquaint self with those special phases of same.

4. Then write directly to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] a personal letter indicating the desire to, at his convenience, discuss the phases of same as respecting other phases also of psychic phenomena.

5. Through this manner there may be arranged a meeting, and given an opening for the approach to the subject as we have indicated in which each may be WELL interested, for the mutual good of all.

6. Do that.

7. Of course, there are those periods especially in which there are set periods of [[Meditation|meditation]].  Such phases of the phenomena, - approach it in a manner, in the letter, not as dictatorial but rather as seeking advice; and this will be answered.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
