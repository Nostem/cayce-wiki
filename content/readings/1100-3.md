# TEXT OF READING 1100-3 F 32

**Reading [[1100-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/4/1928  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 6290

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Pa. 
(Suggestion for Check Physical)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now the conditions show some improvements in many ways with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, yet we find - with the character of the water as has been taken by the body, and with the manipulations as given for the correction of those accentuations as have existed in the system - we have created an incoordination through the assimilations of the body, especially with reference to the digestives system - especially as coordinating with the gastric juices of the stomach proper.

2. To meet the needs of these conditions, we would add these as conditions for the system - not changing the manipulation save as the accentuation of impulses from the radial plexuses to the stomach proper, but rather accentuation of the radiation from the lower portion of the stomach, or to the 9th, 10th and 12th dorsal, more than the 7th and 8th dorsal. Keep up these manipulations, but these variations in the strong manipulation, adding that of Petrolagar ([[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]]) - tablespoonful at least twice each day.  After each meal a few drops of Pepsin, that the assimilations may be better. These, as we find, will correct the conditions.  These have been rather of that acute nature, and we will find - these added, these corrections kept - near normal conditions coming about for the body.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Why does the heart action seem to be slow and the body have little desire to move around?
(A)  Accentuations from the radial plexus as given.  Hence that of the more activity given to the lower dorsal and less to the upper - see?

4. (Q)  How many drops of Pepsin should be taken at a time?
(A)  Just a few - dependent upon the conditions as existent - three to ten drops.

5. (Q)  What should the diet be?
(A)  Just consistent with the conditions as have been given for the body.  This has been outlined.  Those the more easily assimilated, the more easily we will find the response to these conditions for the body.
