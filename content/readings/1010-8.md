# TEXT OF READING 1010-8 F 64

**Reading [[1010-8]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1936  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 6095

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of January, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1010], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 to 12:35 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Here we have an acute condition that has arisen. And, as we find, it will work hardships - with the distresses that have been indicated through the activities in the gall duct and the tendencies for the accumulations through the system.

2. We would use [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] first, with rather an alkalin solution - or a heaping tablespoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]] to the two gallons of water, with a level teaspoonful of salt, in tepid water. This will make for an alkalin reaction through the intestinal tract.  THIS would be taken first, you see.

3. Then we would take ten grains of Aspirin with five grains of Asaphoetida - the properties that will make for an allaying of the temperatures that arise from the influence of too great an activity of the leucocytes splitting up, or the mucous membranes becoming irritated or inflamed by the cold or congestions.

4. These, as we find, taken once in the evening, again in the morning, should make for a relief.

5. Only citrus fruit juices as the diet for a day or two days.

6. And these should relieve the congestions.

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file



Wired as follows:

FIRST GIVE [[Colon Hydrotherapy|COLONIC IRRIGATION]], USING HEAPING TABLESPOONFUL [[Baking Soda|SODA]] LEVEL TEASPOONFUL SALT IN TWO GALLONS TEPID WATER.  THEN TAKE TEN GRAINS ASPIRIN WITH FIVE GRAINS ASAPHOETIDA.  REPEAT TOMORROW MORNING.  FOLLOW ONLY CITRUS FRUIT DIET FOR DAY OR TWO.
