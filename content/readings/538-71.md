# TEXT OF READING 538-71 F 61

**Reading [[538-71]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1941  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 3807

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [487].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Ky.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [538]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there is some improvement in the general conditions - and in the more specific disturbance with the eye.

4. The present applications are well.  However, the day before the treatments are to be given, we would use the Violet Ray about two to three minutes; not too strong, but applied first in the 3rd, 2nd and 1st dorsal center, then slowly up the left side of the spine to the base of the brain; then turn across to the right side and up, behind the ear, and over to the right side of the face.  Do not use it too long, nor too strong; about a minute and a half to three minutes for the whole application, - not more than half a minute to the side of the face - or from the neck or base of brain to the right side.

5. At first this will produce some tendencies towards inflammation, yet - with the treatments being applied for clearing the circulation through the optic itself - it will aid in restoring a more healthful condition there.

6. Two or three of such treatments should be sufficient, see?

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q) Any suggestions regarding diet, especially at this time?
(A)  All of those vegetables that tend to clear or purify the circulation; such as celery, lettuce, carrots, radishes and the like, as a part of the diet each day.  The body-building forces are preferable.  As indicated, beef, fish, lamb - all of these that the body will assimilate are well, or even the juices from these - though not much of the bulk or flesh itself need be swallowed.

10. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body?
(A)  These we would do.

Be mindful of the better eliminations.  Keep these regular.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
