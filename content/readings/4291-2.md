# TEXT OF READING 4291-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4291-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/4/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12094

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 4th day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find that the conditions in this body are beginning to show reaction from the properties that have been taken in to balance the system.

2. Through properties taken in we may expect a change in the lymphatics, in the knees and in the lower part of the limbs.

3. Keep up the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the body.  Keep those up, especially across the lumbars and sacral, to give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to begin the regulation of the forces, so that elimination will be carried out.  Give the water every other day.

4. As to the diet, be more careful.  Keep closer to those that we have given before.

5. Keep up the water every other day and do as we have outlined. We are through.
