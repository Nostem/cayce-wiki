# TEXT OF READING 4905-42

**Reading [[4905-42]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/24/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12967

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by no one - an involuntary reading, which came without suggestion being given to obtain it, immediately following two [[Dreams|dream]] interpretation readings for Mrs. [136] and Mr. [341].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and Miss Estella Smith.]

Time of Reading                               Bosque County, 
1:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, we have here [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick, in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]]. This we find is now progressing more satisfactorily and that the hard formation in which they have entered is that above the production that they seek.  So there is only the necessity now of keeping in that way as given, and when this formation has been passed (seems Monday again, see?), we will find there that which will bring the large production to this endeavor, producing then, for each dollar invested, fifteen hundred (1500) for each one, see?

2. Then keep in that as given, making this slow, being careful of the tools in keeping same full in their temper and in their full size, for with the setting of other conditions for production, this will be found necessary, that same be in the best of condition.  The production will come.  Keep, then, in that way as given.

3. We are through.

---
