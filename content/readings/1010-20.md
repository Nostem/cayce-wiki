# TEXT OF READING 1010-20 F 71

**Reading [[1010-20]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/19/1943  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 6107

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1010], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1010].

3. As we find, conditions are not so good; yet if there are the precautions that have been indicated, there may be the keeping of a balance through the body.

4. The tendency for the blood pressure to cause the headaches, the gall duct disturbance, all of these are tending towards accumulations and segregations in the body-forces - these, in the forming of lymph pockets, are the sources of the disturbances.

5. The necessary precautions for the balancing of the pressure, through the use of the vital forces of body itself, are good - if they are kept.  It would be well if the body would take same once or twice a day, not too long a period.  Do keep the anodes or plates clean.  Do polish them a bit more, for the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body are such that it tends to form a film on same.

6. When necessary, under the direction of the physician, take those sedatives that quiet or give the body rest.

7. But the body needs to be less active, and to rest more.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Does honey or tea cause high blood pressure?
(A)  Honey does not cause high blood pressure.  Tea, with other combinations, does.  These as we find may be moderately taken, not too strong a tea, nor too much of the honey; but these as we find in combinations - as just indicated - do cause high blood pressure.  Be mindful as to the other combinations made with same.

10. (Q)  Dr. Bragg gives me "nembutal" to make me sleep.  Does it harm me?
(A)  As just indicated, this is better to have the rest - but do take more rest.

11. (Q)  He says it is hardening of arteries of heart instead of clot; is it true?
(A)  As we find, it is more the accumulations that causes spells, see?  and these are as lymph pockets.

12. (Q)  What causes sound in left side of chest almost constantly at night?
(A)  The manner or way in which there is the flow of the blood from lungs to heart and to liver.  These, of course, are hindrances, - and is why the [[Vibration|vibrations]], or the Radio-Active, should be helpful, see?

13. (Q)  After taking three rounds of four of Radio-Active Appliance (two days between) it made me nervous and jerky. How often should I take them?
(A)  If taken for a few minutes twice a day, it is not too much!  provided the body rests before and after taking same. But take for only a few minutes at the time, see?  But do keep the plates clean.  Don't mix the anodes to the upper and lower portions of body; keep them in their regular circular motions.

14. (Q)  Is the Radio-active Appliance I have alright?
(A)  If it is kept as has just been given - if it is kept in order!  Have the connections in order, but do keep the anodes clean.

15. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Keep quiet as much as practical.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
