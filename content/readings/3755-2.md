# TEXT OF READING 3755-2 F ADULT (Writer)

**Reading [[3755-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1921 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/10/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11289

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Hotel McAlpin, [[New York City|New York City]], this 10th day [Wednesday] of August, 1921, in accordance with request may by the self - Mrs. [3755].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. G. K. Nicodemus, Conductor; (?), Steno. Mrs. [3755].]

Time of Reading 
1:00 P. M.

1. GKN:  Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep, a quiet, peaceful and restful sleep.  All the organs of your body will function normally and perfectly.  You will be normal in every way. You will hear me and what I say and answer the questions I shall ask you, slowly, very slowly, and distinctly, so that we may get every word.

Mr. Cayce, you are asleep and you have before you the body of [3755] who is here in this room.  You will see this body and go over it carefully and tell us what the trouble is, if anything, what to do for it to make it normal again.  You will answer any questions about this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, we have has this before, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

3. Now the conditions that exist in this body at the present time - [3755] we are speaking of - are somewhat different from the conditions as we found in this body when we had it here before.

4. They have more to do with the connection between the spirit forces or the action of the spirit forces on the system than there is in the physical condition of the body itself save in the action of the spiritual with or over the physical, to assist or alleviate or make more normal the condition of the body itself, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

5. The action of the nerve forces in the system through this spiritual and mental has developed a better equilibrium through the incentive forces of the physical within this body, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

6. Those especially that have to do with the circulation through the lymphatics, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

7. - is better, while the condition that existed in the blood cellular forces itself in producing a better quality in the hemoglobin that produced cellular giving force to the physical only in the system, has not aided materially in the condition that existed as yet, for the action of the spiritual with mental forces over the physical has not developed within the body itself to reach to these incentives as yet without the addition of the forces that were given to apply to the body, you see, they are still lacking within the system or within this body -

GKN:  Yes, sir.

8. To add to that portion of the body that would rebuild cellular force in a definite decided way, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

9. That is, the blood in itself, we find yet in the number of their red blood corpuscles, are deficient within themselves, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

10. So that the amount of iron and silsilic [salicylic?] acid needed to produce in their emultaries [emunctories?] and lymphatics that quality to the capillary circulation, the proper distribution through the respiratory organs of the cuticle itself has not reached its proper channel, and they come to this portion of the body, that is, to the surface here, you see, in certain centers, in an uncertain and undecided manner and way, so that we have as the condition that we gave before here, that has existed, of course, in the face here, you see?

GKN:  Yes.

11. Through the molars or some portions of the body here; the pains that have existed through here are not of the condition as would be produced as from an incentive giving off to this portion of the body, that is, to the head and the facial part, that which would be of a detrimental nature, but the impossible part of the system under its present condition to eliminate through the lymphatic and through their emultaries [emunctories?], the right or proper amount of eliminating qualities to this portion of the system, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

12. So we still have some of this condition existing through the body.  Now, the condition as we have in the nervous system through that of the sensory organism or that affects the senses of the system itself, the eye, the ear, the nose and the throat, through the five senses of the system, as commonly known, so called or termed, there is not as yet the proper connection between the sympathetic and the cerebrose spinal [cerebrospinal?] and the connections made to give off the proper circulation and power of elimination of used forces to all of these portions of the body, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

13. So that we have with action of the spiritual with the mental forces through the nerve a cerebrospinal force with the action of the sympathetic on that of the sensory organism, the fuller sensitive force expressing itself through many of the organs of the sensory system, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

14. With the added forces as were given or as is given in the body to eliminate through proper channels these conditions, we will find that the body will be able to rebuild in itself much stronger, much easier, and without any of the super sensitive forces, or without the action of forces over the system to produce the improper contraction of nerve over muscular forces in the extremities and in the facial nerves, as they do at times in this body, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

15. This condition is better than it has been before -

GKN:  Yes, sir.

16. Before we have the action of this spiritual and mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]], you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

17. Added to the system these things we have given before in their proper manner and form, and we give then to the incentive of both blood supply and of nerve tissue to act in their proper channel throughout this body here -

GKN:  Yes, sir.  (Pause)

18. (Q)  Will you give us that same prescription that you gave us before, Mr. Cayce, please?  Are there any other details pertinent to this case, Mr. Cayce?  What are the medicinal properties to be taken into this body, [3755] Mr. Cayce?
(A)  What are the medicinal properties that are to be taken into this body, [3755] Mr. Cayce?  That which will add to the blood forces in the body their proper incentive to act with the nerve giving force in the body, or that which adds in the system of the correct properties to make a perfect balance throughout the cellular building units in the system.  The body lacks some of the forces from the outside to produce the proper unit building force without calling on the other forces in the system to the detriment of other portions of the body, you see?

GKN:  Yes, sir.

We would add to the system not just as we were given here before in these -

GKN:  Yes, sir.

Which was with the addition, you see -

GKN:  Yes, sir.

Of the bromide for the nerve forces and of the action of the forces through the digestive tract to produce an efficient condition throughout the digestive forces, which has been alleviated, and help to some conditions her to some extent, but we would add now these forces here in this body at this present time, see -

GKN:  Yes, sir.

We would take into the system phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]], one dram to a half a glass of water, to which there is added three drops of syrup of sarsaparilla, with one drop of [[Iodine|iodide]] of potassium.  This will be taken once each day, you see, morning preferably -

GKN:  Yes, sir.

For a period of ten days.  Then we will leave off the [[Iodine|iodide]] and only take for ten days more that of the phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and of the syrup of sarsaparilla, you see -

GKN:  Yes, sir.

Then after this, we would apply to the system the electrical forces as used in this manner or form:  Two bars of steel, 2 x 4 inches, you see -

GKN:  Yes, sir.

To which the cord is attached, that would be attached to the wrist; the water is cold that this is placed in, and every evening, just before retiring, and this is placed in the basin of water, which is cold - not ice in it, but very cold -

GKN:  Yes, sir.

The feet will be placed in at the same time while it is attached to the left wrist.  This will be done for five to ten minutes.  We produce then the equilibrium that is necessary for the circulation throughout the portions of the body.  This would be kept up when it is necessary for the body to obtain rest through the normal conditions in the system.  If we will do that we will find the body will be relieved of all the conditions through the nerve forces, through that of their forces in the system that has been carrying some other poisons back into the system through the intestinal tract, especially with the trouble of the larger colon we have been affected with before, the trouble through the circulation to the capillaries, the action of the muscular lymphatics over the system, and the nerve balance to the cerbro [cerebro?] balance sympathetic and the sensitive organisms of the system.  Do that -

GKN:  Yes, sir.

19. (Q)  Should this body have the lower tooth extracted from the lower jaw, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Should this body have the lower tooth extracted from the lower jaw, Mr. Cayce?  It is best not to have it done at the present time, until you get the body in better shape.

20. (Q)  How long will it be before the eyes in this body are restored to their normal condition, Mr. Cayce, please?
(A)  How long before the eyes in this body will be restored to their normal condition, Mr. Cayce, please.  Ten days action for the forces on the system as given here, to replete or deplete the conditions throughout the system, ten days for the action of the forces that we have given on the body here, to act through the nerve forces, three days for the application to take effect on the sensory system and the action of the body on the principles given here.  These make the conditions that are to be acted upon.

21. (Q)  Now, Mr. Cayce, you will dismiss this body.  You will not see it any more for the present.
(A)  Now, Mr. Cayce, I will dismiss this body and I will not see it any more for the present.

22. GKN:  You are resting quietly and peaceably.  All the organs of your body will function normally and perfectly.  You will be normal in every way.  You will wake now in one minute, feeling good and all right and thoroughly rested.  You will forget what you have said.  You will be feeling good and all right in every respect and thoroughly rested in every way. Now, wake up, as I have said, in one minute, Mr. Cayce.
