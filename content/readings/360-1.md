## Text

Date: 6/26/1933  Sex: F  ReadingID: 2792

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [360], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., by her sister, Miss [...], and recommended by Miss [1377].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mass. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [360].

2. Now, as we find, there are specific conditions that are the result of a combination of disorders in the system.  And the effects of these are in the disturbances of other portions of the system.

3. In times back we find there were some injuries to the body that at the time [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] be the results of conditions in the system form dis-ease as from childhood, or from an infectious disorder - measles combined with other distresses. These in their state settled in portions of the system that hindered the circulation to portions of the body.

4. Later, from a contraction of a congestive condition in the body - or from that known as influenza, or the flu, there was an increased irritation to those centers that already hindered impulses and circulation from the general system to the specific centers in digestive area, and in those sensory reflexes to the head.

5. These, then, have gradually grown to be such as to impair the [[Visions|vision]], through strangulation and irritation to the eyes. These active in a reflex manner make an impairment through the secondary cardiac plexus to the cardiac portion of the stomach.  Hence an irritation that exists there.  At times this causes greater distress through the manner in which there is produced this choking of the organ itself as to cause regurgitation from the duodenum to the stomach proper, as to cause overacidity - or an accumulation of hydrochloric fluids in stomach proper.

6. The reactions to the sensory system, and circulation to the head and eyes - optic nerve and the orb socket - are affected through the impingements and the activity of the circulation through the secondary ganglia, and 5th and 3rd nerve impulse to the head, as to cause at times more reaction in face than pain in the eye itself - save at periodical periods.

7. The centers from which we find these conditions arise are in the 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal plexus, in the 3rd and 5th cervical plexus - and in the 2nd cervical plexus do they affect directly the conditions.

8. As we find, these may be materially aided; while not relieving the condition in its entirety from the manner or character of the impingements to the nerves to the brain and through the optic centers - or wholly those that cause irritation to lids, these may be relieved to such an extent as to increase visibility to the body to at least seventy-five percent, and through the aid of lens and through the activities that may be kept, will bring a much more active and normal result or effect to the body.

9. The removal of these impingements in the same area as affect the heart's action, to the cardiac and secondary cardiac reflexes, will make for a much better enjoyment of the foods taken - and of assimilation in the system.  And the general health will make for much easier conditions for the body.

10. In the beginning, then, we would first use those applications on that portion of the cerebro-spinal system indicated, of mud - or mud made from Fuller's Earth, with an equal amount of oil to make it pliable or applicable to that portion of the system.

11. Then, with 3 to 4 ounces of Fuller's Earth, mix an ounce of Cedar Oil, that will be capable of being applied for at least two applications.

12. This, however, should be applied directly to the body itself; not in cloth, though a cloth may cover same; that is, the 3rd and 4th dorsal, the 3rd and 4th cervical, or reaching from the 2nd to the 5th cervical - see?

13. Apply this every other day until 10 applications have been made.

14. Then begin with [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] adjustments for these portions of the system, these being made every other day for at least three or four weeks of treatments.

15. Then, in the second week of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments (not before), begin with the application of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] anode directly to the eye.

16. Cleanse the eyes each morning with a weak antiseptic solution, that will cleanse the eyelids of secretions that will form from the continued stimulation to the circulation through the applications.

17. When the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are given, there should be stimulation throughout the head and to the centers leading to the optic and auditory centers.

18. We would be mindful that the diets taken are those more easily assimilated, that consist of foods tending more towards an alkalin reaction for the system.

19. As we find, these will make for better conditions to the body.

20. When a full course has been taken of the Fuller's Earth and oil, as given, with the three to four weeks period of osteopathy manipulation AND adjustments - with the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] anode used as given, then we would have a rest period of one and a half to two weeks.

21. After the rest period we would give further instructions for this body, [360].

22. Ready for questions.

23. (Q)  How long at each period should the mud be left on?
(A)  It should be applied in the evening and removed of morning, and cleanse the body.

24. (Q)  Of what thickness should this be put on?
(A)  This quantity over these places will make for about an eighth to a quarter inch thickness, over these specific centers indicated.

These will draw, and act as irritants sufficient to allow the circulation - with adjustments - to become effective in removing drosses, and in correcting the condition.

Do this, and we may then give further instructions.

25. We are through for the present.

> I confess a certain timidity in discussing the psychic -
> particularly in connecting the psychic with the physical or

> 
> To make matters even more discouraging, we know
> physiologically almost nothing about the very common function
> of sleep itself; and we get no help from the psychic himself,
> for he admitedly knows nothing of what happened and his
> reading is entirely new to him when he returns to the
> conscious world; of course there is in this, the negative
> suggestion that the force acting through him is independent
> of his consciousness and volition; that is, he acts as a
> passive agent.
> 
> Perhaps our training also adds to these several barriers; we
> are so accustomed to tapping the chest, listening to the
> heart, testing by chemistry, and examining by microscope and
> X'Ray that anything which will not submit to this diagnostic
> 3rd degree has no length, breadth and thickness, is not
> material and hence is inconsequential.
> 
> To those interested in psychic phenomena, these difficulties
> need not be discouraging.  They can find inspiration in the
> fact that a like condition exists in other fields of human
> inquiry; the electrical engineer has for a century been
> directing electricity of chosen power at will - and yet no
> one yet knows what electricity is.  This ignorance being
> inconvenient he constructs a theory which he hopes will fit
> into the known facts.  If it does not he discards it and
> tries another.  At present he postulates; that all matter is
> fundamentally electric; composed of atoms which contain a
> positive center or protone around which is grouped a cluster
> of negative elements or eletrones.  So long as these balance
> each other all is peace and quiet.  However, if one electron
> is coaxed away by man's inventions an attempt is made to
> recover this electron by stealing one from nearby atom:  It's
> then a case of every atom for himself and the consequent riot
> is what is known as an electric current.
> 
> In the transmission of magnetic energy, light, heat and sound
> waves, it has also been inconvenient not to know the agent of
> their transmission.  Oxygen and nitrogen and the other known
> elements of the atmosphere are not the agents, so in our
> dilemna we invented the ether.  Although this ether
> penetrates all space and all matter, no one has the slightest
> idea what it is and yet its assumption fills in a void in
> human knowledge.
> 
> Life itself has been pondered over since the days of [[Garden of Eden|Eden]] and
> yet its nature is as much a mystery as ever.  A dead body
> weighs as much as a living one, yet life itself has gone from
> it.
> 
> Psychical research enjoys, in common with all branches of
> learning, this privilege of filling in the voids with
> theories.  Without them that enigma of sleep cannot be
> bridged and the psychic phenomena which take place on its far
> side cannot be approached.
> 
> The subject has always been a fascinating one to me, and the
> more so during the past ten years when I have had frequent
> opportunity to examine the physical readings of Mr. Edgar
> Cayce and also the patients concerned.  The many instances
> in which by psychic means he has revealed physical conditions
> which have later been verified by physical methods have been
> impressive.  I admit but do not explain this.
> 
> Certain thoughts however have resulted from these
> experiences:
> 
> Insomuch as sleep is interpreted between the psychical and
> the sensorial, perhaps the psychic sense springs into life
> and activity as our other senses become dormant in repose.
> 
> Perhaps one highly endowed with this psychic sense can be
> compared to a sensitive radio receiver.  He can at will
> adjust and tune himself to the energy - or lack of energy -
> from a selected source, and receives and records in the
> readings emittions from this source.
> 
> If this is true and the psychic acts as a passive medium, we
> might denominate this an actual psychic touch or contact.
> 
> I offer these thoughts for whatever use they may to those of
> you interested.
>

> noted frequently that these are fitted in and cleared up by

> 
> And since we have compared our medium to a radio receiver,
> these facts suggest:
> 
> That psychic reception is, like radio reception, variable.
> That it may have its own problem of static.
> That strong senders may create interference.
> That psychic transmission is sometimes good and sometimes
> not so good.

> The Doctors:
> 
> From all sources I hear of difficulty in obtaining
> cooperation of physicians I sympathize with you in this.
> 
> The physician has arrived at his present methods of
> examination only after years of intensive training, study and
> experience.  The system, although not perfect, has been

> 
> Your hope of cooperation must rest with the occasional

> life.  Interest and curiosity on the part of these should be
> cooperative.
>

> or extraordinary results.
