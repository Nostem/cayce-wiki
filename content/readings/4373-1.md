# TEXT OF READING 4373-1 F 57

**Reading [[4373-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/28/1925  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 12206

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions for this body.  While there are many conditions existent that are the effects of those conditions in the system that are produced by the actions of the body, yet many are brought about by outside influences on the mental forces, also the effect of the combination of these conditions producing the constitutional effect in the functioning of the physical system of body, yet this may be assisted to give a better condition for the body as exists at the present time.

3. Now these, then, are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [4373], we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this low in vitality and in the ability to replenish the used forces, or to give the vital forces necessary in the functioning of the system.

5. The nerve system is as but nil in many respects, and the effect of this is that the conditions in system are often more exaggerated than apparent, or as is complained of by the body.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, these have become gradually debilitated by the existent conditions in the system.

7. Then, to give the more stable forces, and to apply those things that would be of assistance, first we would give, then, those stimulants to the system that will build to the system in its ability to store energy and give the vitality to the whole system.  Taking, then, these properties in the body:

8. To one gallon of rain water, we would add:

Sarsaparilla Root..........2 ounces,
Wild Cherry Bark...........2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root...........2 ounces,
Burdock Root...............1 ounce,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root.............2 ounces,
Calisaya Bark..............2 ounces.

9. Reduce this by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain while warm and add four ounces pure grain alcohol, with three drams Balsam of Tolu cut in it.  Also one dram of Life Everlasting.  Shake solution well together before the dose is taken, which should be teaspoonful four times each day.

10. To give more vitality to the nerve system, and to the whole body in its nerve reaction, that should be builded with the blood supply, add those [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the Radio-Active Applicator, using that of the plain applicator, using the upper portion of the body as the negative portion, attaching then first to the ankle, then to the wrist.  This should be used at least two to three hours each day.

11. Do that, and when the first quantity of the tonic, or prescription, has been taken, then there would be given further instructions for the conditions in the body.

12. Keeping the mental abilities of body in as near that of the optimistic turn as is possible for the body to keep in.

13. The diet, being those properties as are easily assimilated in the system.  Small quantities of stimulant taken at times for the system would be well, and the whole action of the mental forces being kept alive to the conditions, the improvements, that may be given in system, and the good as yet may be accomplished in that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] desires to do.

14. We are through for the present.
