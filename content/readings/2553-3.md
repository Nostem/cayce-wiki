# TEXT OF READING 2553-3 F 26

**Reading [[2553-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/17/1923  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 9553

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 17th day of September, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M.                                        ..., Minn.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see.

2. (Q)  Is an operation necessary for the body?
(A)  It is best not for an operation at the present time. The condition is not localized sufficient to operate to be of the benefit that would be necessary to relieve the condition. The condition throughout had best be localized before the operation, if then, was performed.  By the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of body through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces give the localized, then the body strengthened sufficient to be able to withstand the shock to the system.  See, this is a centralization of the conditions as we have had through the taxation of nerve system through lack of proper elimination in the body.  This, as we have, produced by the sufficient nutriment not being given necessary to give the body the incentive to throw off those poisons that have gathered here now.  See?

Stimulate through the solar plexus and throughout all of the dorsal and lumbar centers, and give that incentive for centers to eliminate from the system, you may be able to prevent this entirely - see?  The body could not stand the shock to the system, neither has it the resistive power at present to overcome the lack of vital forces in blood force, both in the white blood and the elemental constituents of the red blood rebuilding forces - see?  but give the resistance to the body through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces rather than by the operative at present.  Do that.  We are through.
