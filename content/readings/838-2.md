# TEXT OF READING 838-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[838-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/30/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4960

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 30th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [838], through Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Conn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, while the body is responding to those suggestions, there has been scarcely sufficient period for a great deal of change save in the general FEELINGS in the system.  These, as we find, if they are continued with all of those suggestions given, should bring much better conditions.

3. While the applications of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] are more [[Massage Therapy|massage]], these are very helpful.  They are hardly so deep in some centers as they might be, yet if these are continued in the definite way and manner that has been outlined - and as has been shown for the manipulator - these will bring results for this body, [838].

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Would you advise that I have two wisdom teeth removed which are troubling me?
(A)  We would get the nerves in much better condition before undertaking it.

6. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body at this time?
(A)  We would follow those things as outlined.  Be consistent and persistent, and we will find - with the use of those applications that have been given - we will gain a much better equilibrium and feel very much better throughout.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Mr. [257]
"    " Ass'n file
