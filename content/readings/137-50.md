# TEXT OF READING 137-50 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-50]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 440

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to this body are in that way and manner as have been outlined for the body, and may be used in that way and manner, see?  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Night of Jan. 8, or morning of Jan. 9, 1926.  About National Dairy Products Stock.
(A)  We have no dream in this.  We merely have that inundation, as it were, of the mental forces, weighed with the conditions in the mental mind, and this as an idea, or as a passing thought; not as a dream.

As for the stock, National Dairy Products, WE find, as here, see, this:  This is one of the stocks in which there will be a great deal of change, and will be on the general or gradual decline from 9th, see?  until about the 14th to 15th, when there will be the rallying from 76 and fraction to 79 - 80 to 81, see?  in the few days; that is, during 15th and 16th see?  This irrespective of ANY dreams.  Rather that correlation of condition as exists in those who are preparing for a change in this production and the way of the handling or control of this organization.

4. (Q)  Saturday, Jan. 9, or morning of Sunday, Jan. 10.  Saw a profile of a face - like a cutout silhouette - only white, not blue.
(A)  BLACK, should be.  This, as we see, is of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s presentation of the [[Visions|vision]] of things, conditions, by the mind of entity, see?  Only a profile, only a side view - a one side view - [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] had - rather the whole of same, would the better lessons be gained for the body.

5. (Q)  Something of sickness in nature of hemorrhoids.
(A)  This as presentation of that condition as is existent in one whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] feels a great deal of interest in - an elderly man, see?  The hemorrhoids may be assisted in this individual and prevented from becoming in such a condition as to the accentuation of same causing dis-ease and distress, by the application of those properties, as has been given in this, as to be used for such conditions, see? That is, the injection of this salve [Tim - See [1800] series] into the anus, as to produce the [[Healing|healing]] properties for this condition, would assist.  Who is this entity?  One whom the entity knows and knows of such condition existent. Then be helpful to the body, through suggestion, and presenting such for the use of the body, BEFORE the condition becomes accentuated.

6. (Q)  Sunday evening, Jan. 10.  A joint formed by 2 pieces of cake, such as Therese Block gave me at her home.
(A)  A presentation to the mind of entity of how each and every condition in the entity's application fits in, one with another, as these joints.  That is, that when applied correctly, of the knowledge of the entity, brings each condition, building one, joining one with another, see? That is, no harum-scarum, no guess work, no condition of enigma, or of not comprehension, would entity only build one with another, for as the development of the entity is, little by little, little by little - line upon line, line upon line, and the applications must be in that same manner.

7. (Q)  Some reference to a previous dream or [[Visions|vision]] I had regarding a football wager.  Can't recall the details.
(A)  That as was given in that, is as that the entity should study same and apply to the condition in hand at the present time regarding wager, and regarding conditions that are of the moment to the entity - on January 11, 1926.

8. (Q)  Sunday night, Jan. 10, or Monday Morning, Jan. 11. Saw Ipsab Corporation as a huge and wealthy concern.  [See [1800] series.]
(A)  A presentation to the consciousness of conditions reasoned, weighed with [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and physical condition, for as the question had been turned over in the mind of the entity, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] builded that outcome which, if applied to the building up of such conditions, that COULD be the outcome of same.  Then in applying same, use that condition in hand to begin, for the beginning must be made, would same grow into large conditions and prosperous ones.

9. (Q)  Now if this is possible, then what should be my first step to bring this about?
(A)  Commence - as beginning.

10. (Q)  Again something about National Dairy Products Stock.

(A)  This as has been given, as we see, rather that of the mental forces attempting to gain some concept of that as to take place, for as is seen in the conversation in a portion of a dream or [[Visions|vision]] with an entity or individual that was NOT going to cover, and this presented then that for the time as given OFF would go the prices, until the time should come (15th - 16th) when there would be an advance, see?

11. We are through with this reading.
