# TEXT OF READING 1355-2 F 29

**Reading [[1355-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/22/1937  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 7040

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of August, 1937, in accordance with request made by the brother Mr. [790], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [790].]

At home ... 
Time of Reading                                 on Route #4, 
11:50 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1355]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, some conditions are not so well, while others show some improvements.

3. The general condition has much to do with the [[Visions|vision]]; and, as we find, the more help may be had from the deep manipulations as would relieve those pressures about the vagus center as well as those in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal, 2nd and 3rd cervical, and in the 1st cervical.

4. We would keep the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body, for these with the manipulations - would equalize the circulatory forces the much better.

5. We would keep near to the diet as outlined.

6. But the manipulations would be that as we find the most helpful, especially in the areas indicated.

7. Do that.

8. These, as we find, first, should be begun about every day for five or six periods or days, and then twice to three times a week would be the better application.

9. Necessary to be sure, as these progress, that there be the lotions for the cleansing of the secretions, but these would be naturally a portion of the applications of the one making the manipulations.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What may be done for backache and shoulder pains?
(A)  That as indicated; the relaxing of the body, releasing the pressures and the circulation.

12. (Q)  Is this also to relieve the eye condition?
(A)  As has just been indicated, this especially in the vagus center or the 1st and 2nd and 3rd cervical; coordinating the 2nd and 3rd dorsal, of course, with same.

These will aid with the general conditions, also with the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces for the circulation kept intact.

13. (Q)  Should the manipulations be [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]?
(A)  They should be preferably made by one that is a specialist in the eye and the general condition of the body, osteopathically.

Do these, as we find, for the better conditions for this body, [1355].

14. We are through for the present.

Two copies to brother, Mr. [790] (one for Dr. M. L. Copy to Ass'n file                Richardson, Bankers Trust Bldg., Norfolk, Va.)
