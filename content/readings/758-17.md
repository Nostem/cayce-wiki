# TEXT OF READING 758-17 M 8

**Reading [[758-17]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/10/1930  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 4634

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1192], Dr. Lydic, and Mr. and Mrs. [[Milton Harrison|Milton]] Brown.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now we have changed his name again, but we have the same body.

2. The conditions, as we find, are constantly on the improve. The retractions as come at times should not be a worry, for this is the best indication of all those centers so needing coordination between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal being active.  Be mindful that the stomach is not overloaded, especially during the warm or hot season.  Get plenty of the sea, salt and sand about the body.  Keep those [[Vibration|vibrations]] up in the manner as they have LASTLY been applied.  The change has been MOST beneficial.  Let those [[Vibration|vibrations]] be as the body rests, see?  Be mindful that they are kept intact, or CONTACT properly when being given; for ANYTHING that may aid will be of harm if NOT applied properly.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Are battery treatments given correctly?
(A)  Just been given!  These are being BETTERED in the change.  Keep them in positive contact while being applied to the body.

4. (Q)  How often should the sand packs be given?
(A)  These may be given - if not for too long a period - each day, but let them be given with the body feeling that it is more of play than "Well, I've got to do it!"

5. (Q)  What precautions should body take against sunstroke?
(A)  This will NOT be so much precaution, if there is plenty of activity to the body in the open and not SPASMODIC activity.

6. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments during the summer?
(A)  Every other day.

7. (Q)  Would it be well for body if mother made plans to take him home in October?
(A)  It isn't October yet!  May be that it will NOT!  May be that it will!  Depend upon the conditions!

8. (Q)  Are the eyes ready to have glasses changed?
(A)  Soon will be.  Not at present.

9. (Q)  Would it be well for mother to leave body and go home?
(A)  For a short period, yes.

10. That is all the questions regarding this body.

11. We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
"    " Father
