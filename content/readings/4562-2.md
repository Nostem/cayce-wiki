# TEXT OF READING 4562-2 F 65

**Reading [[4562-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/17/1924  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 12450

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 17th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4562].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. George S. Klingensmith.]

Born January 22, 1859, in Effingham, Illinois. 
Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as have been made, and those as have been affected by the [[Destiny|destiny]] as set in that of the solar systems and that which will in itself has set, and this has been well with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

2. As to the time [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit's forces completed this present entity, [4562] that is, we find was near the meridian, and there were and are many influences from the [[Astrology|astrological]] viewpoints and conditions in the life as lived in this present sphere.  We find there were many of the ruling planets in the heavens at the time and that the greater influence in the life is form that of Jupiter, with Mercury, and that the impelling forces have been in that of Mars with Septimus, and that the sign as under which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] was born has played a very small part in its life, save when its ruling planet, Jupiter and Mercury, were in that sign, both the Sun and Moon being in the favorable aspects of the genii [Gemini?] forces in the life of the individual; those of the contrary sign coming in Mars and in Saturn, and in Venus when in the contrary sign of the birth sign.

3. In the life then as has been found, irrespective of [[Free Will|the will]] forces, one who has enjoyed much of the pleasures of earth when in the rule of the benevolent influences of Jupiter, with that refining of Mars' influence, and when used in contrary signs or manner brought the troublesome effects in the life.  Not that the life has not been set about with worries of the secular nature, but these have more often been found as the rule or influence of others than of self, and will not having been exercised these earthly and material conditions have been attracted to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. In the influence then, we find one as this:  One whose ever ennobling forces have been the rule of life.

5. One who has ever grasped for the higher study and thought on every subject.

6. One who has ever attempted to view any subject matter from every viewpoint before either condemning or accepting.

7. One whose forces will and has been in that of the home, when the rule was in the Jupiter forces only, and one whose life as as ensample has been for many.

8. One whom in younger life was considered the leader in every group to which the entity was inclined, whether social, religious, political, home or what.

9. In the influences as have been exhibited in [[Free Will|the will]]:

10. One who has often suffered self-condemnation through the lack of exercising that which it knew to be the correct thing to do or have done at the time.

11. One whose vocation would have been best in the rule of others as directed both in science and in the ways of the better forces in ruling in land.

12. One whose influence will be felt for many days, for the influence has been on the increase, and will continue to do so through the years yet to come to this earthly [[Veil of the Temple|veil]] [vale] for this entity.

13. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], we find this has been in many, many spheres, yet long since the time this entity was in earth plane before.

14. We find in the times when the change in the rule in the forces under that of Alexander The Great was over-running that portion of [[Persia|Persia]] that this entity was then in that country, and suffered under the persecutions of the invaders. In that time, we find the name as that of Hannah, and in the household of the defender of one of the cities taken by the invaders.  As to the personality as exhibited in the present sphere, we find one every in dread of an invasion of any character, whether in physical or in mental forces, through the exercise of that influence gained in the appearance before this in this land gives in the present that of the study of those same conditions, as given.

15. In that we find in the land of [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] forces, when the rule was in the peoples as founded the cities that have long since passed into oblivion.  In this we find the entity in [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit phase in that of the Princess that ruled the land for many, many, days.

16. As to the usage of these, as given here, use will's forces for the developing of the peoples, that they may get the better understanding of the meeting of that which may be termed error in their earthly passage.
