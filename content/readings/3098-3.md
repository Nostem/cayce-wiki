# TEXT OF READING 3098-3 F 54

**Reading [[3098-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/21/1944  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10452

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3098], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3098] - we have had this before.

3. As we find there has been real improvement in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, yet in some of the diet, and the lack of eliminations being sufficient, there has come a disturbance in the area and the circulation between heart and liver, or along the thrombose area.  This as combined with the accumulation of poisons has produced a great pain in the upper portion of the muscular forces in the arm.

4. The body should be quiet, set up eliminations, and keep quiet until there is a better circulation established through the body.

5. Massage the feet, the limbs, the arms and hands and this will improve same.  This done neuropathically would be well or osteopathically.  Better, though, by a good masseuse or neuropath.

6. The quiet and the eliminations set up is that needed most.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What should be done for the teeth?
(A)  There are conditions needing local attention to remove the nerve strain, and treatment for gums.  The best would be an equal combination of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] and table salt as a massage for gums.  But do have those removed that are disturbing.

9. (Q)  Why have eyes not improved and what should be done?
(A)  This has come rather from a general condition.  You have improved, but the general conditions that have arisen have caused most of the disturbance in the present.

10. (Q)  What suggestions to help the colitis condition?
(A)  Keep the better eliminations, using a vegetable compound as a laxative or eliminant with the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.  And these given periodically will be the better way to prevent the colitis disturbances being more distressing for the body.

Do these things and we will make better conditions for this body, [3098].

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
