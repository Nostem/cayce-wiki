# TEXT OF READING 3588-1 M 54

**Reading [[3588-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/25/1944  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 11080

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - through friend, Mr. [3638], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Louisiana.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - they'd better take the body away.

3. As we find, the decisions should be for the operation, for already poisons are so distributed through the system as for this to be questionable.

4. This will necessitate following same that there be the setting up of drainages throughout the body.

5. The decisions are correct.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Would it be advisable to place the body in the care of a different physician?  Whom?
(A)  They've already made the decision - you can't change it in the present.  We would follow through.

8. (Q)  Any warnings to physicians regarding procedures?
(A)  As just indicated, there should be drainages set up, and this can best be accomplished by hot and cold applications and massages.

9. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  These, in the present, will have to come from others. All who understand should pray about it.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Friend ([3638] ... Street, ..., Louisiana - Phoned also)
"    " Ass'n file
