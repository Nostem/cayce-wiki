# TEXT OF READING 3874-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3874-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/12/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11502

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of June, 1924, in accordance with request made by her husband, via Mr. [[John (Apostle)|John]] Kunz.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Kunz and [3874]''s husband.]

Time of Reading                          28 S. Clinton St., 
2:30 P. M.                               Middletown, [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with this body, and these may be corrected.  They have to do with the functioning of organs as governed or controlled by conditions that affect the nerve system in its functioning through the organs affecting the digestive tract and the blood supply, and we find that conditions rather than that producing them have been in times back treated in the body, and the effect of these with the mental force and the nerve system has caused and does cause distress in the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3874], we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this is sufficient in quantity, yet the distribution and the effect as is produced in the distribution through the system in the organs as affect circulation, and as affect organs as are controlled through the elimination and assimilation in system, produce from this (the blood force) much of the effect in the system, though we find other conditions that are the cause and seat of trouble. The blood supply we find often carries those impressions as received from the subnormal action in elimination.  Hence from the circulation we find there are portions in the system, and that they vary from time to time, that become numb or as tingling sensation, or as an overflow of blood in portions of the body.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find in this rather the seat of the trouble, especially in that of the cerebrospinal system and in the lumbar region is the greater distress experienced from the physical condition, and in this plexus in the 3rd lumbar do we find the seat of the trouble, so that we have this effect from this condition:  The distress across the small of the back, the distress to the locomotories and to the lower portions of the body, the distress in the functioning of the kidneys at the times when eliminations are being carried in the system.  Here we find we have a lesion produced in false pelvis and this effects direct this plexus, and the system attempting to overcome produces a lesion, and this in turn makes or forms pressure on the hypogastric center and through sympathetic conditions brings the distress to the digestive organs in the hypogastric center and through sympathetic conditions brings the distress to the digestive organs in the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus center, so that in the pyloric end of the stomach the forming of the gastric juices as prevented and caused and does cause the abnormal functioning of the pancreas and the duodenum, foods pass undigested and do not digest properly or remain long, depending upon the pressure created in hypogastric center, causing then distress at [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach or at the end of the esophagus.  In the reflexes then from this we have distress in the circulation, again we see in the eliminations as created by the kidneys and the liver, and with these conditions there is formed in the system the distress in the form of lack of secretion in the intestinal tract, and the body suffers from conditions produced in colon and in the end of the colon.  Naturally inclined, then, to that of constipation, which brings infection again to blood supply, carrying in the blood the forces that should be eliminated, both through the regular dross and with the circulation impaired bringing distress to the capillary circulation, and we have hot and cold flushes in portions of the system from this reflex condition.  In the sympathetics we find how these conditions are carried, as it were, from one portion of the body to the other through reflexes, and why that we have conditions that, apparently, are the cause of the distress, when the seat rest of the trouble is in the lumbar and pelvic region.

5. The sensory organism comes under stress of these conditions, so we have cerebrospinal nerve system, sympathetic nerve system and sensory nerve system involved in the conditions in this body here.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS, themselves, brain forces very good.

7. Lungs, larynx, bronchials sympathetically suffer under strain in circulation and nerves.  The digestive tract, as we see, shows how that foods, through a nerve digestion, are prevented from giving the correct incentives to the system, passing from the stomach proper to upper digestion in intestines in an acid rather than in an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] state. This gives high hepatic circulation, distress in circulation through hypogastric ganglion in nerve and blood supply overflowing at times through kidneys and causing an action that is either subnormal or abnormal.  Either and both do occur at times in this system.  The liver we find subnormal, yet abnormal in size.  Subnormal in functioning through nerve contraction in the ganglia governing the emunctories, for eliminations often are very bad.  In the intestinal tract the assimilation of toxins gives distress through intestines, when extra secretions are carried in the system to cause the removal of dross from system.  This forming the toxin gives distress across abdomen, in lower portion of stomach, and often in bearing down pains in the organs of the pelvis.

8. RELIEF - Then to bring the relief to this body, we would first prepare the system that assimilation and elimination may be carried properly in the body.

9. We would take first in the system this:

Tincture of Valerian................4 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium................10 grains,
Bromide of Potassium...............30 grains,
Elixir Calisaya.....................4 ounces,
Syrup of Celerena...................2 ounces,
Elixir Capsici......................2 minims,
Oil of Peppermint...................5 minims.
Sufficient simple syrup to make 16 ounces.

Shake solution well before taking.  The dose would be teaspoonful in glass of water every three hours.

10. Use as the diet, while this is being taken, properties easily digested in the system.  No meats but vegetables and whole wheat.

11. For the condition in intestinal tract, use enemas until the colon is entirely cleansed, gradually making these higher until the system is cleansed of all fecal matter, using oil in the higher enemas, that of the [[Russia|Russian]] White Oils.  Small quantities of these.

12. Remove by deep manipulation the lesion in the lumbar region, applying heat, and the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] that dilates the intestinal tract, and create the incentives for secretions to be formed in their proper manner.  This we will find can be removed from the system and the correct incentives given in from six to eight weeks.

13. The manipulation and dilation of electrical forces for intestines should be taken at least every third day, until the strain is removed from system.

14. Do that.  We will bring the body to the normal conditions.
