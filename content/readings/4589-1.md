# TEXT OF READING 4589-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4589-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1921 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 0/0/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12489

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[New York City|New York City]] in 1921 (?).

[Edgar Cayce; G. K. Nicodemus, Conductor; (?), Steno. Mrs. [4589].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. GKN:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep - a quiet, peaceful and restful sleep - all the organs of your body will function perfectly, you will be normal in every way and you will hear me what I say and answer the questions I shall ask you slowly and distinctly so that we may get every word. Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep and you have before you the body of Mrs. [4589] who is in room 2154, McAlpin Hotel, [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.  You will see this body and go over it carefully and tell us the trouble, if any, and what to do for it to make it normal again and you will answer any questions we shall ask about this body, Mr. Cayce.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now this body as we find it here is very good for its place, its status, or what it should fulfill and at this time it is developing wonderfully and will for years to come both on this plane and hereafter.

3. There are some physical defects that can be relieved or made so that the functioning of all of the organs will produce a better equilibrium throughout the system and produce a better physical feeling in the body so that the work through the mental and spiritual is developing and can be better given to the waiting public.

4. These are the physical conditions as we find in the system: The body is lower in the amount of blood that is carried through the system throughout.  It is sufficient in a way, yet would be better if more of the humanet lobam [hemoglobin?] were added to the system or not so much taken through the trunk portion of the body to eliminate some congestions formed by proper distributions of vital forces thrown through the lower strata where the life giving force is thrown out through the lower portions of the body, that is, thru the whole digestive tract, through all the organs of the pelvis, through that of the upper portion of the stomach which becomes reflex at times, or a higher hepatic circulation and a lower capillary and arterial circulation to the extremities then should be in this body we are speaking of.

5. As to the nervous force and conditions we find that the lower [[Vibration|vibrations]] are not sufficient in this part of the system, that is from the solar plexus out, while as to that we have to the brain center.  That in the lung force is very good though we have some irritation at times even from this that causes unevenness to the sympathetic nerve forces.  But the trouble or distribution of this from the solar plexus forces is where we are tired between the muscular nerve and blood supplying forces to the portions of the body.  The functioning of the brain itself is good, excellent thru all the lobes, channels of same.  The functioning of the organs through the sensory organism, that is, between the brain and the distribution of [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces to throw off expression from the impression that is made or received or attached to the body and becomes part of it, in these action is good.

6. The condition that we have at times affecting the eye, the ear, is purely sympathetic and is a reaction from the forces below and not anything even bordering on an improper functioning of these organs in themselves.  As to the action of the organs of the throat and upper portion of the trunk body, the lungs in themselves act as we have given here, so that the whole scope covered by the function of these organs are good.

7. The entering in of the new supply of the rebuilding forces show the deficiency to themselves, really a reflex and comes from the lower condition as we have given here.  Through the digestive tract of the whole system we find the reflex nerve and blood supply in force in this produce at times a trouble in these portions of the body so that there are times that those things taken in to add to the rebuilding force cause troubles and irritations, especially to the digestive tract through the lower or larger intestines.

8. The functioning of the liver itself is very good.  All of the secretions or excretions thrown off well for a condition of high hepatic circulation; also through reflex condition from the kidneys as in the action and the force are thrown off at times so that there is some pain thrown out at times.  The organs of the pelvis act in their formation with the condition that we have described here.  Those are going to the lower extremities or through the locomotory nerve force. The circulation back to the heart force is not as good as it should be.  The action of the munkron [emunctory?] and lymphatic is especially good when we consider the activity of the mind impression over all of these.  To assist this to produce the equilibrium in the body we would add more to the vital or life force in this form and way into the system.  By suggestion - self and self study with some one on the outside and an addition to the system of life giving forces through the center here, that is the solar plexus, ninth dorsal, of electrical force of low [[Vibration|vibrations]] or alternating vibrations, not to excite the nerve energy above their normal vibration but to give a better vibration through low formation of electricity of the veins in their action; high voltage affects nerve, low cold voltage affects the nervous system only along that channel which they are operating against or through the veins themselves, they being the inactive force, the higher voltage breaking into positive.  Do this if we would add to this body.  With its exercise, with the other things that it takes into the system the only thing to be added low cold - not high vibrations.

9. (Q)  Will you tell us what to do to get the lower larger intestine to function properly, Mr. C.?
(A)  You see the condition as we have given here with the low hepatic circulation as produced by too much of the blood being carried to those portions of the body.  When we add to the circulation through cold voltage to the system and these center which function with the intestinal tract, we will produce more of the secretions, that is, intestinal secretions that will carry out the feces and having normal action of these, just as we have given here to do, that is the way they are carried out.  The plexus, that is the solar plexus, governs the lower trunk portion of the body in the function of these organs.  When we increase the circulation through the venous circulation, that is through the veins in their return, then we produce more salines in the intestine along which channels or parts the nerve energy is excited and the blood supply is excited to carry new force and new energy to it.  The taking away of the rebuilding force that is in the blood supply is produced by causing so much to stop through the hepatic, that is, the circulation between the liver and kidneys and larger intestine.  All of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the colon would be well if this body from time to time would take into the system something such as White [[Russia|Russian]] Oil, nothing that carries a sedative, nothing that acts on any of the juice of the intestines but what would give a natural flow or function of these.  This should not be kept up when the electrical force or cold storage is given to the body. The exercises are very good that she has taken, the kneading of the colon, gently, easily with the forces as applied to the 9th dorsal.  Do that to renew condition, put new life into the whole body, be rid of the trouble int his body for a long time through this channel.

10. (Q)  Would you suggest how this body could develop more quickly along spiritual lines, Mr. C?
(A)  We do not suggest.  We can give you what could be done. That is for her to decide herself.  This body here, [4589], we are speaking of, is highly developed along the spiritual and mental force, especially along the mental development of the body and through things pertaining to the mental side of life acting on the other forces will this body develop itself along these channels, and make the body strong and well and the other forces will develop through their natural channels. This is better than using forces from outside.

11. (Q)  Will the bill that this body is endeavoring to put through in Albany to aid women and children go through?
(A)  Modified, yes.  It will finally reach its success through further developments and further influence brought to bear by many other people on this body.  We are through.
