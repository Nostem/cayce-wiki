# TEXT OF READING 487-2 M 5

**Reading [[487-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/5/1924  Sex: M  Age: 5  ReadingID: 3561

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of January, 1924, in accordance with requests made by parents.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [538], [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and little [487].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now we have some abnormal conditions in this body.  They have to do with the eliminating and assimilating forces in the functioning organs in the system.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [487], we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the assimilating forces in the liver, duodenum and pancreas are so overcharged as to give in the blood supply to the system an excess of the elements of blood force as to impoverish other portions of the system and to prevent the storing of energy and vitim [vitamins] in the system.  There is too much of the alcoholic forces, that are created by this condition.  Hence the engorged condition of the pancreas and the lack of excretions to produce the digestion in the upper intestinal tract.  This allows the hydrochloric state to exist above normal in the duodenum, and produces then reaction through the stomach and through the pneumogastric center, giving the hepatic circulation through hypogastric center an over-taxation.  Hence inflammation produced in the hypogastric arterial force, and bringing excess functioning of the kidneys, and the condition in the capillary circulation of elimination being emptied through the respiratory system giving also an excess of action in the respiratory organs of the lungs proper.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find the hypogastric pneumogastric centers are taxed by conditions in digestive tract, and find reflexes in the centers that are branches of this center; those of the third and fourth dorsal, the eighth and ninth dorsal.  Hence the achy, dull feeling, the headaches through temples and side of head.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find as these: Cerebrospinal center, or brain forces, very good.  Over active for the development and condition from the normal, but from the spiritual and [[Soul|soul]] forces normal.

6. Sensory organism very good at present, though may become affected by the conditions in the upper dorsal region if lesions are allowed to form there.

7. Lung force, the cell shows open on account of respiratory system.

8. Digestive tract, stomach proper, as we have given, showing internally the action of the gastric juices, and hydrochloric excess with alcoholic excess in duodenum.

9. Pancreas, liver, show inactivity, though pancreas engorged. Liver, by the excess, becomes dull or leadened in activity. Hepatic circulation high.  Excess of secretions through Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and through the perineum of the center covering the lacteal [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that supply the blood in the chyle formation.

10. Through the ascending colon, through ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], some inflammation created by conditions in the system of the general nature.

11. Hypogastric center in the kidneys and the emunctories all show excess of secretions eliminated by the excess of alcoholic influences as created in the system, producing some inflammation in the neck of bladder proper, and an excess of the secretions in the emunctories of liver and the kidneys proper.

12. R E L I E F, to give the relief to this system would be for the present condition, give that in the system that will bring the soothing effect to condition in duodenum, and assist in elimination in the system.

13. Give those in broken doses properties as found in that of this:  Castoria, and with the Tea of Yellow Saffron.

14. For the diets give that of pre-digested foods; cereals, fruits.  Apples, pears and grapes may be given.  Not of oranges, or of citrous fruits.  Those of the character that does not create the alcoholic in the form as destructive, which is as ethyl alcohol.  Sugars beware.  Not too much. Not too much sweets until the condition is overcome, for the hypogastric center is affecting both the lungs and the kidneys.  Liver getting inactive.  Follow these out.  Do that.

15. (Q)  What should be the broken dose of Castoria?
(A)  Half teaspoonful twice each day until the condition is relieved, with mild Tea of Yellow Saffron from tablespoonful, two tablespoonsful, twice each day.

16. (Q)  What causes the rash or breaking out on this body?
(A)  We have just given it as the elimination, the body attempting to eliminate through the respiratory system.

17. (Q)  Should chiropractic or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment be given the dorsals in the future?
(A)  [[Vibration|Vibration]] and adjustment be very good.  Be necessary unless the conditions are removed through the functioning organs, and lesions formed would eventually cause distress to the system.

Follow these as we have given.

18. We are through.
