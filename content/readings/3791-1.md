# TEXT OF READING 3791-1 M ADULT (Actor?)

**Reading [[3791-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/12/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11359

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by self, [3791], via Miss [903].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; A. M. Banning, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       ..., Georgia.

1. GC:  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], a reading giving the effects of the solar systems on the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present earth plane, naming the planet, or position, from which [[Soul|the soul]] took its last flight.  You will give the vocation in life for which this body is best adapted, or may adapt itself, giving also the high points of the life in former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], giving the name and place of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth plane, with the characteristics as brought forward in its personality in the present life.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the conditions here as respecting the effect of the solar systems upon the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present earth plane irrespective of [[Free Will|the will]] of the entity.  The conditions also as are exhibited in the present life from those conditions as was given through former passage through earth's plane.

3. In the present plane, we find the entity comes from those conditions or places of Venus.  And while the [[Astrology|zodiacal]] conditions play some part in the present plane, these are very little when compared to the influence of other conditions that appear from force of the entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in other spheres.  The entity, we find, was completed in the afternoon of this day and with the influence of Venus, Mercury, Uranus and Mars when at the better aspect of Jupiter.  Yet adverse influence when Mars is at the square of Saturn.  Moon's influence being good when sun's influence comes with Leo, the birth sign, and with the sign of the Jupiter's forces in Leo.

4. Then we have these inclinations in the present earth's plane irrespective of wills:

5. One who is of a very determined nature.

6. One who mounts many obstacles when the mental has the incentive for good.

7. One that loves the beautiful in every form and manner.

8. One that is attracted to children especially and gives and shows much filial love for young children.

9. One that is very ambitious for self and is particularly adaptable to the present line of endeavor.

10. One that will reach the greater heights in the chosen profession near the age of 52.

11. One who could, with will, have made of himself, an excellent physician.

12. One who studies much of occult forces and of mysticism, of theological surveys in strange tongues.

13. One that feels the innate force ever present and only feels the need of the awakening to be in greater position.

14. One who mounts many obstacles through the occult forces latent in self.

15. One who would go far in the study of psychological and psychic problems.

16. One who will rule many by the love manifest in self towards others.

17. One that finds the greater joy in giving joy to others.

18. One that should take care that it does not become selfish or dictatorial when it comes to power either in position or in mental abilities.

19. One that should, with the present conditions, follow the now chosen profession and give to the peoples the results in occult, psychological and mystic forces through its endeavors in such work.

20. One who will find the greater success in present earth plane in such endeavors.

21. One who should be careful in its diet, for through digestion does the greater destructive force come to the physical.

22. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, before this, we find in the days when Cromwell ruled in [[England|England]], this entity in the armies of that leader; by force was he governed.

23. Before, this, we find in the days of Phryne [?] when syncopation ruled in that land and the entity then was in authority in that land.

24. Before this, we find in the days when the Children of Promise were in a strange land, this entity then in the capacity of a ruler under authority.

25. As to the personality exhibited in the present plane, from these we find in the first that of desires of mystic and of the strange conceptions of mental and spiritual rule over the physical; in the next, we find from this the desire of the innate force to express themselves through the beautiful, through voices, through action of hands and of feet of the body.

26. In the next, hatred toward strife of any character and the ability to allow self to be abused rather than bring strife.

27. In the uses then to which this entity may use these forces and understanding, be not fainthearted whether from physical or financial disability.  Ever pressing onward, upward, relying wholly on Him who has made the way, the path, to the everlasting, and through [[Free Will|the will]], bring to submission all those conditions that would, through selfishness or desire, destroy the better elements within self, looking to that city without foundation whose builder and maker is God.

28. We are through for the present.
