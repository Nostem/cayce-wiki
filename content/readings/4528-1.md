# TEXT OF READING 4528-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4528-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/6/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12410

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 6th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by husband, via Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions, and that which produces these conditions being rather the specific nature, though many portions of the physical are affected by the condition, and to remove trouble the cause and not the effect must be corrected.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, [4528] we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, there is the lack of vital forces carried in the blood.  Hence the dull, achy, tired feeling as is exhibited in many portions of the body, the dullness, heaviness, weariness the body experiences after rest of [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  Blood not equalized in the system, and then carrying also those toxins that are not eliminated through their proper channels, and they, the toxins, affect the condition exhibited in the various functioning organs in system.  Not that we have the abnormal forces carry the excess in blood, yet sufficient of abnormal elimination as to leave in the blood those properties that become detrimental to the functioning of organs in the system.  In the pressure, we find that which is at variance.  That is, at times we would find above, again below the normal in pressure, though not produced form organic condition of any organ as yet that the blood functions especially through, yet all coming under the bane of the condition.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, with this taxed condition in blood supply many centers become involved, especially those in the hypogastric, pneumogastric, sympathetic and ilium plexus. At times secondary cardiac, pyloric cardiac and the ends of nerve centers in the lymphatic circulation.  Hence achy feelings about the joints and over various portions of the body.  A reflex condition entirely, but one that needs attention, but from the condition producing and not from that produced in nerve or functioning organs.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find as these:

6. Brain forces function very good.

7. In the functioning of the organs of the sensory system with relaxing or overtaxing, and both occur we have congestion and the change in the functioning of the organ direct, or the coordinating improperly of the functioning organ with the central system, or in coordinating properly with the sensation as should be in the system to manifest that sensation received.  Not that of organic condition in the organs.

8. Throat, lungs, larynx, bronchials and organs of the body above diaphragm show the condition as given, and how each at the various cycles of its functioning either give or take of the condition, and are in distress accordingly.  That of insomnia being one of the conditions which function through the secondary cardiac forces.

9. In the digestive tract, stomach proper, with the taxation of nerve system, we have an appetite that at times is nil. Again is extensive, though little satisfies.  That is of a reflex nerve condition.  Liver engorged from the condition, and the excretory system not functioning properly.  The emunctories become involved with the lymphatics in its forces, and we have that of an extra secretion without the functioning of the excretory system in proper form.  Again the reverse shows in the system.  This condition then we see as is produced in the system is from the forces as found in the upper intestinal tract, and the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] suffer and the toxins are absorbed in the system through these channels. Hence affect the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] secreting the new blood supply, and we have the constitutional or the condition affecting the whole system, as we have given, you see.

10. The kidneys and hepatic circulation overactive at times. Again there is distress through the functioning of these organs, without the activity of those functionings that should throw off from the system.  Hence, we have two kinds of toxins absorbed in the system, which shall be eliminated should we wish to bring the normal and the better to the system at the present time.

11. To do this, stimulate the secretions and blood supply to all this portion of the intestinal tract by stimulating that center about the seventh, eighth and ninth dorsal and so stimulate circulation through the solar plexus and its branches to the frontal portions with its connection to the hypogastric and pneumogastric and ilium plexus.  Also stimulate this with the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] which expand and stimulate the secretions of the intestine itself, without giving the [[Vibration|vibration]] of electrical forces in the nerves.  We stimulate the nerve centers so that the contraction or the relaxing is carried to the system properly.  Do this through manipulation and electrical [[Vibration|vibration]].

12. We will find that within six to eight weeks the body will be in its normal condition.
