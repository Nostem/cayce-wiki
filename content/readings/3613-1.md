# TEXT OF READING 3613-1 F 66

**Reading [[3613-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/28/1944  Sex: F  Age: 66  ReadingID: 11111

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3613], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

At the ... home, 
Time of Reading                         Main Street, 1/4 mile 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.  from town limits, 
..., North Carolina.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3613].

3. As we find, many changes are taking place in the functionings of the organs of the body, especially as related to the general nerve strain on the body.

4. An environment or climate where the body might be in the open, with congenial companionship would add as much to the general health as anything else; where there can be walks, long walks.  These would contribute to the general health.

5. The conditions in throat and lungs are the result of a pressure existent in the 3rd and 4th dorsal and 3rd cervical. If these would be corrected regularly, with a stimulation of activities through the lumbar and sacral areas at the same time, ye should - with these general changes - bring better conditions for this body.

6. Do these and also here with this body, take each day at least three ounces, preferably when ready to retire, of white wine.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Would you suggest where to go for a change in climate?
(A)  Wherever there can be found the companionship and where the body can be out of doors most of the time.  At this particular season of the year, the west coast of [[Florida|Florida]].

9. (Q)  Am I allergic to vegetation or foods?
(A)  This is not so much an allergy as it is the result of pressures in the dorsal and cervical areas, causing an overactivity of the olfactory nerves through the soft tissue of face and head.  Make these corrections, and especially where the body can stay in the open and take walks, with congenial companionship, and we will in a few months correct these conditions.  Do have those adjustments also, however.

10. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Keep the mental attitude good.  Be good for something, as well as just being good.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
