# TEXT OF READING 3217-3 M 41

**Reading [[3217-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/20/1944  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 10616

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3217], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are improvements.  Whenever there are those activities from the digestive system, or from the areas in the spinal cord as indicated, or the pressures being produced on the lesions that exist in the caecum and the lacteal area, there are the recurrent seizures that cause those incoordinations in the 1st and 2nd cervical.

4. If there will be kept continuously the activities as indicated, having periods of the treatments suggested, both the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] breaking up of the lesions - we should bring better conditions for the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is an operation for appendicitis advisable at this time?
(A)  If the same would be used as a means for doing away with the adhesions in the lacteal duct area, it would be most advisable.

7. (Q)  Any advice or counsel regarding same?
(A)  This is just as has been indicated.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file
