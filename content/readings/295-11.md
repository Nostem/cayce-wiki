# TEXT OF READING 295-11 F 32

**Reading [[295-11]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/3/1936  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 2182

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of April, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [295], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her sister, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Okla. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [295]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while those disturbing conditions that have been indicated in the lungs, as well as in the general eliminations, are a contributory cause to be reckoned with at this time, these arise in the present from rather an acute condition as brought on by poisons in the system itself.

3. Hence the condition not only with the chest cold but through the alimentary canal.

4. As we find in the present, then:

5. First we would take, for a day or more, small quantities of Olive Oil; half a teaspoonful about every hour or as much as the body will absorb.  If this becomes more nauseating than has been indicated, or if there comes the rise of temperature from same, well that this be reduced by rubbing with rub alcohol along the spine; this from the head, neck, downward along the spine.

6. During the same period or day take about an ounce of Saffron Tea every three hours apart.  This would be made by using a dram of tea to a pint of water and allowing to steep as would regular tea, or pour hot water over same and let it stand. This quantity should be taken during a day.

7. We would also find that an antiphlogistian [Antiphlogistine [[Poultice|poultice]]] over the chest would be helpful, especially along the lower portion of the left lung.

8. And take a grain of Calcidin each evening for a period of a week, and then oftener if there is not an allaying of the cough and the soreness across the abdominal area.

9. Use the Radio-Active Appliance for the keeping of an equal circulation.  This would be helpful to be left on even for two hours at a time, during this same period.

10. Unless these conditions are cleared from the system, they may be more serious.

11. Do these in the present.

12. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  The liquid and semi-liquid, though strengthening, would be the better.  Malted milks, citrus fruit juices, [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].  Such natures for the next few days, until strength is gained - if it may be responding to those administrations.

Do that.

Let the mental attitude be for a helpfulness and purposefulness in its activities, ever.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

Day Letter to Sister, as follows:

HALF TEASPOONFUL OLIVE OIL EVERY HOUR FOR DAY OR MORE. SHOULD NAUSEA TEMPERATURE ARISE REDUCE BY RUBBING SPINE FROM NECK DOWNWARD WITH RUB ALCOHOL.  DRINK OUNCE SAFFRON TEA EVERY THREE HOURS.  APPLY ANTIPHLOGISTIAN OVER CHEST LOWER LEFT LUNG.  TAKE GRAIN CALCIDIN EACH EVENING.  RADIO APPLIANCE DAILY.  MALTED MILK.  FRUIT JUICES.  BEEF JUICES.
