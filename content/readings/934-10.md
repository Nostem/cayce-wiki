# TEXT OF READING 934-10 F 31

**Reading [[934-10]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/17/1941  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5787

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of April, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [934], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Charlie H. Capps.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here.  Conditions as we find, especially as related to [[Visions|vision]] and the eyesight, are more acute.  For, too MUCH has been applied and too often VARIED things.

3. Keep the rubs across the kidney area with the [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] compound, AND the heat occasionally applied, or at least once each day.

4. Use the warm Boracic Acid Solution, preferably in eye cup, about twice a day.

5. Twice a week - Tuesdays and Thursdays - use the Murine as a brilliant, or as a purifier to the eye.  True, the greater portion of this is also boracic acid, but the other combinations with same and the forms for clearing the skim, and for purifying the inflammation, will be helpful.

6. Do not again use the other applications that have been used.

7. Do these as indicated.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
