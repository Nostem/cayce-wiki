# TEXT OF READING 127-1 M 44 (President & Gen. Manager)

**Reading [[127-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/16/1930  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 291

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [127], via his cousin, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body, the enquiring mind, [127], ..., Ill., and the Battery Equip. & Supply Co.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [127], and the associations of same with the Battery Equipment Company. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Shall we continue our business along the lines we are now operating it?
(A)  These, with the expansions as come, as are being contemplated, as are being considered for other companies, other conditions that are to be met - these, with the changes, would be the manner and the way to conduct same, as we find it.  When there are presented those of preparing or making of special equipments for the various conditions that arise, individual or group, would be well to consider same - and this would bring the natural expansion that may come with such.

4. (Q)  What changes are recommended for this year?
(A)  That as has just been given, that equipments for individuals or for those of companies, or of individual associations - these must be the expansion, or changes.

5. (Q)  What additional production can this business make with the present equipment?
(A)  That as has just been outlined.  Those as would be related to ANY of the expansions that come natural in these lines, see?  Whether it be in the radio field, or whether in special equipment for battery appliances, or whether in that field of those as have been considered in the building UP of character of batteries for various groups or companies.

6. (Q)  Is the business being managed properly?
(A)  Very good, or else we would have made changes in these, as we find them!

7. (Q)  Shall we manufacture products pertaining to other industries?
(A)  That's as has just been given!  It should!  That's the expansion that must be in that field, in the changes as should be made!

8. (Q)  Any further advice regarding any of this?
(A)  Follow in this way would be the better, for this expansion in the battery equipment business.

9. That is all the questions.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
