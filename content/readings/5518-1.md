# TEXT OF READING 5518-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[5518-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/7/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13766

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - [5518], via [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body and those impressions of same - [5518]. Now, we find the disturbances as cause uneasiness through the system are the RESULTS of pressure along the cerebro-spinal system, and the resultant effects of a disturbed metabolism through pressure as is created in system through the lack of the coordination in eliminations.  These are the disturbances as we find.  These show the effect in the blood supply of that variation in the pressure, that dullness, that heaviness experienced across the middle portion of body, the tendency of headaches that dull and wear off - at times; at others they seem to produce a dizziness.  The bad tastes at times in the mouth, the heaviness in feet, and the pains across the lower portion of the back.  These are the EFFECTS and SIGNS of the pressures created in the system, of the lack of the proper eliminations through alimentary canal, disturbances in the colon itself.

2. To meet the needs of the conditions, we would rest and exercise more in the open.

3. We would have those manipulations following the application of the ultra violet AND Infra Red lights; a rub down to produce the stimulation in the outer portion of system; ROUGH it as to diet; and we would find in three to four weeks the body would feel near normal, near fit.

4. We would have the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations at least twice, for the eliminations through the system; being mindful of those conditions existent in the lower portion of the colon itself - but we will find the body will be much improved.  Do that. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]
