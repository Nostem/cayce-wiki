# TEXT OF READING 137-61 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-61]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/4/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 451

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that way and manner as have been outlined, and may be used by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the way as has been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Feb. 16, or 17, 1926.  Saw some sort of ridiculous wedding procession, at which I particularly noticed Stanley Halle, who is on Stock Exchange.  I attended the affair with Ma.
(A)  This, as we see, the correlation of mental conditions in the mind of entity, with that as is presented in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces as regarding the wedding or marriage of an individual, that to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] appears to be as ludicrous or ridiculous as this entity as seen, Halle, in the daily physical life at times presents to the entity. The lesson as should be gained:  Though this entity appears as ridiculous, and in an uncomely manner at times, yet there are definite results as are obtained by this entity, and as seen, then, same might be expected from a wedding.

4. (Q)  Some sort of animal was in [143]'s ball.  It was called a beetle but was a small size of a vicious animal.  I hit it with a broom or something and it made a terrible noise and was lost in bed.  I then came running down to [143] and she was crying because the animal was hid in her bed. I took her out of her bed because I was so worried regarding her safety.
(A)  Rather this as the warning as regarding the entity as seen, in the games that are played by this entity, for as seen, as ridiculous as occurs the animal in the ball, and called one thing when the entity sees same as a vicious, or one that would cause trouble, see?  Then the lesson: Be rather the more careful with the games the entity as seen is allowed to participate in, that the scare - the good health may come or be kept by this entity, and not necessitate the enduring of worry or troubles, see?  Well, then, that these be as much in open and not inside, see? for, as seen, lost in bed covered - hidden, see?  Then more outside, or as much as possible, see?

5. (Q)  Fleischman went up.
(A)  This the attempting of the mental forces to gain the impressions of the action of individuals or individual stocks, and this is rather of the mental than a correlation with conditions to exist, or as of given statements, for no prices, no change is seen of the definite nature.  Then, rather the mental than that to be used in a manner of gain for entity.

6. (Q)  Feb. 18 or 19, 1926.  Had my tonsils taken out.
(A)  With the condition as exists in the body in the physical way and manner, again we find the physical-mental attempting to present to the entity those relations with existent conditions, or something to occur, see?  yet we do not find the relation as presented with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces as of an action to take place in a definite time, or in a definite way and manner.  Rather, then, should the entity be warned to care.  Be sure that such conditions do not arise and have the troubles with same as has been experienced in times back, physically, see?

7. (Q)  Mr. Exton of N.Y. Stock Exchange was in Columbia Carbon crowd on exchange.  I have the specialist book in this stock - Columbia Carbon.  He mentioned something about his not getting any stock and as I didn't like some of his previous actions towards me.
(A)  We do not find this complete.  In this there are presented, in a manner to the entity, those conditions as are to become existent in relation to this individual stock, yet as seen is not complete in its presentation through that of the dislike, as it were, as is presented in the [[Visions|vision]], or feeling as is experienced by entity in same. This, as we see, as others, then, will show the decline in its valuation.

8. (Q)  Feb. 20 or 21.  In conversation I was comparing white silk pajamas to wide blue striped pajamas of other material.
(A)  This again a partial presentation to the entity of conditions wherein there is to be presented those conditions in the actions of the physical force of body, as pertaining to the relations of daily duties.  That is, a comparison of quotations on stocks, as is the comparison of silk pajamas to those of other materials, and as is seen, there is to be those conditions becoming as the striped, or as the change- able conditions in this association, see?  daily labors.

9. (Q)  Feb. 26 or 27.  My mind seemed to go over previous days' happenings, such as Columbia Carbon selling down from 65 1/8 to 64 1/4.  I sold 200 of my stock at 65 and was sorry I didn't buy it back when it went lower. Also saw my mother-in-law and father-in-law preparing for their European trip.
(A)  This, as is presented to the entity in that of the price in an individual stock, gives then as the reasoning with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces in the quotation or action in quotation of this individual stock, as is held by the entity, the wish, the desire that same should be back in the possession of entity, yet there is seen connected WITH same, and as a portion of same, the sailing away of other, those close to the entity, see?  Then, as is seen, this presents to the entity this sailing off AGAIN, or going lower in the price, see?

10. (Q)  What does this indicate regarding Columbia Carbon?
(A)  Off.

11. (Q)  Should I buy it back around 64?
(A)  Sell it around 64.

12. (Q)  Feb. 27 or 28.  Again recalled past events - namely my conversation with Mr. Alexander Stern on floor of Stock Exchange regarding Columbia Carbon, which I am long of. When and how much would the dividend be raised?  How high would the stock go and when?
(A)  This, as we see, as has been given, will sell off from the price as has been seen to 60 - 59 - and even lower. Then beginning about the llth - 12th of the present month with the declaring of the dividend, and the general advances in the various conditions on the Exchange, go to 69 - 68 7/8 - 69, see?

13. (Q)  Coal stocks very strong - especially Burns Bros. Stock.
(A)  This again the indication to the entity of conditions as ARE to become existent, without SPECIFIC indication of prices, or when, see?  These may be presented to the entity again, in the same way and manner as is seen by the review- ing of conditions as become existent conditions in the daily activities of the entity.

14. (Q)  You will please correlate conditions at present regard- ing Hudson Motors with information regarding same given in a reading of Feb. 24, 1926 [See 900-200].
(A)  In this there is seen there is given that, with this combination and this pooling of the stock of this motor corporation, there was given the great advance as would begin in or about the 10th to 12th of March.  In the same there is seen there is given the breaking off from time to time, yet showing a general advance.  With the existent conditions there is seen this has broken down 15 to 17 points.  Now begins on the gradual up-climb, and with the continual breaking in other conditions will go off again, with sudden advances, sudden slumps, until the 12th to 13th, when the gradual advance will begin.  The pool, however, has been shaken and partially out of attunement with conditions, so well that these conditions be followed closely.

15. We are through for the present.
