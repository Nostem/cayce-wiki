# TEXT OF READING 3700-1 M 28 (Catholic)

**Reading [[3700-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/16/1944  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 11205

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

In Mayview 
Time of Reading                              Hospital, 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.       Mayview, Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, conditions here are the result of [[Karma|karma]] as well as the abuse of self in the developing forces of the body.  These have brought and do bring the spasmodic nerve reactions or the spasms when the brain becomes as the shock center, so that the body loses control of itself through the incoordination of the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system to activities or impulses through the body.

3. Under the present environs we find that not a great deal may be added.  However, if there would be used the combination of bromides as may be found in Bromidia, as an allaying of the conditions for a period of a monthly cycle, and then the use of a hypnotic such as Luminal, the reactions may be better controlled.

4. These offer, as we find, only palliatives and not correctives for the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  When should I stop taking the present medicines?
(A)  This depends upon whether there would be the attempt of those in charge to apply these suggestions indicated.

7. (Q)  What has been the relation of the religious ecstasies that have seized me, to my health, and to the recovery from my ailment?
(A)  These are only reflexes and are portions of the imaginative forces as indicated from the [[Karma|karmic]] reactions. We find that these will be met only by the activities of [[Prayer|prayer]], [[Meditation|meditation]], and the application physically made - rather than the mental or emotional ecstasies - of just gentleness, kindness, patience, brotherly love; these applied, not preached, but applied daily.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
