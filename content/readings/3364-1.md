# TEXT OF READING 3364-1 M 42

**Reading [[3364-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/15/1943  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 10803

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3364], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) [3364] and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3364].

3. In analyzing the disturbances with this body, here we find not only the pathological conditions to be considered but the psychological.  For, these are the effects of [[Karma|karmic]] influences, thus having to do with something being met in self in the present experience.

4. Here, the complete analysis of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]]'s being might be proof of those tenets (to those who would study such) that life is a continuous experience.  And where one has met self in those activities having to do with the psychological (that is, [[Soul|the soul]]-self), as in this body, and also the physiological - or the [[Physical Body|physical body]] and its relationships to the spiritual or psychic body, as in this condition here, there is brought a homosexual disturbance that is to the body a mental and a physical condition to be met.

5. These, then, are the conditions to be met.

6. For, with the relationships in mind, in body of such, as to open the cells in the Leydigian ([[Glands (Esoteric)|lyden]]) forces, there is the flow of the kundaline in and through the body which finds expression in the organs of the sensory system; not merely to the disgust but to the shame of the body in itself - and yet these become a part of the whole body-mind and being.  Thus there is brought not merely a physical or purely pathological condition but a physiological and psychological disturbance to the body.

7. These as we find can be met, by the mind and by that which is a part of the electrical forces of the body itself.  Thus these, as we find, will be the applications.  If these are used we may correct these disturbances, in self, in mind, in body, in the physical reactions.

8. First we would begin taking internally small quantities of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], to produce excess [[Iodine|iodine]] in the body.  Taken one drop in half a glass of water each morning before breakfast for five days.

9. Then leave off three days.  Then take again for five days.

10. Then leave off five days more, and commence all over again.

11. At the same time begin using the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to produce, with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] a reaction of potassium in the body or potash, that will react upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] forces that are the cause or the source of this deflection.  This would be attached to the body on the same day that the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is taken internally.  The attachments would be made in the same manner as the Radio Active Appliance.  The charging solution should be made as strong as the body can well take, that is, to the gallon and a half of distilled water:

Copper Sulphate.................2 pounds,
Sulphuric Acid C.P..........1 3/4 ounces,
Zinc............................9 drams,
Willow Charcoal...............1/2 pound.

Both attachment plates are to be made of the same metal either the [[Germany|German]] silver or nickel.  One would be attached to the right wrist the other to the left ankle; the next day the same plates would be attached one to the left wrist, the other to the right ankle, and swap back and forth daily in this way and manner.  Attach the Appliance for thirty minutes each day.

12. When the second series of the Atomidine has been taken (not before but afterward) have an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment in the lumbar axis and the lower portion at the 9th dorsal.

13. Then, after two more series of the Atomidine, have another [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment.

14. Do these, and we should have no further trouble.

15. Live and keep normal activities.  Begin with the study of self - not anatomically but spiritually.  And the greater spiritual lesson you may gain is in the 5th chapter of Matthew.  Learn this by heart, then read the 14th chapter of [[John (Apostle)|John]] and the 12th chapter of Romans.  Then live them!  Live them in thy daily relationships to others.  Know that these words are spoken to thee.  Apply these with thy application of the mechanical and material things for the body.

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
