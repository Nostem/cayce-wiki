# TEXT OF READING 349-21 F 35

**Reading [[349-21]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/12/1939  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 2753

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of September, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [349], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Room 1640, 
Time of Reading                               ... Ave., 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, conditions have improved greater than was indicated they might in the beginning.

4. Still, there are many things which should be considered throughout the corrections.  But as we find, those activities that have been used are very good.  These are not wholly in keeping with the suggestions as we have indicated, but as these are kept closer to those suggestions, greater will be the results, or better will be the results.

5. Hence we would give, - carry on with those treatments begun.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Of what particularly should body be warned, by organs not functioning properly at the present?
(A)  Of the accumulations of waste that would form, either of conditions as to become as tumorous or of an indeterminate nature.

Hence those suggestions as indicated, that it would be well for the applications to those affected portions to remove these disturbances.

8. (Q)  Are any of the reproductive organs improperly located in body?
(A)  Not improperly located, but congestion in some of same. NOT functioning nominally.

9. (Q)  When and how can body conceive a child?
(A)  This should be determined better after the corrections have been made, rather than before, see?  For their proper relationships, these should be with the body functioning near to normal, and NOT burdened with the cares of daily activities in a physical or mental nature.

10. (Q)  Shall mole under left arm be removed and if so, what method?
(A)  If any method is used at all, use the electrical needle. But if it doesn't give disturbance, leave it alone!

11. (Q)  Is the present condition affecting eyes?  If so, what treatments?
(A)  This is merely a reflex condition, and if the proper precautions are taken, and the use of glasses or reflectors for the activities, these should find better conditions developing as the general physical health improves.

12. (Q)  Is there any danger of peritonitis?
(A)  Is there any danger of tomorrow coming?  All of these are superficial!  If the proper administrations are made, no! If they are not made, yes!

13. (Q)  Is condition of liver affecting the bronchial tubes, right and left?
(A)  This is a sympathetic condition to same.  Apply those things suggested, if we would have corrections.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
