# TEXT OF READING 2524-5 M 43 (Engineer, Christian Scientist)

**Reading [[2524-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1944  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9490

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2524], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [2524] in his apartment at the ..., [[New York City|New York City]], who seeks information, advice and guidance that will enable him to better understand all phases of his life and his associations, so that he may make more practical application of the information which has been given him through this channel.  You will answer the questions he submits, as I ask them:

2. EC:  - He has the information - he'd better commence to make application, rather than be sorry!

3. (It's best to keep quiet than even to try to read.)

4. We have the enquiring mind, [2524].

5. As has oft been indicated for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in information concerning what it has done with its abilities and in regard to the conditions existent in the body at present.

6. There must first be peace within self before there can be found even congeniality with its associates.  Few there be, to be sure, who can continue to put on a front or attempt to present a personality that is so at variance to the individuality as to be just "putting on" all that while. You fool no one but yourself and make yourself miserable. For what you are, by your relationships to others, speaks much louder than what you say to individuals.

7. Then the beginning - for the physical help as well as for the interpretations of self - is to begin with thy [[Ideal|ideals]]. Set it down in black and white.  Take yourself in hand and draw a [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]].  You're a pretty good engineer to draw pictures of other things.  Did you ever try a [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] of yourself?  How far do you span from what you would be, or from what you would have others to think you are?  How far does this stretch?

8. This should be begun, then, with the spirituality.  What is thy [[Ideal|ideal]], spiritually?  Who do you hope to see, where do you hope to go, when you leave here?  remember, He that gave the world compared man's life to a tree, in that where it falls that is where it stands.  The life goes on, only the body remains.  It returns to that which it has builded itself. Just as the rich man and Lazarus.  Because of his patience, fortitude and brotherly live, Lazarus went to the bosom of faith, or in faith.  Yet it was not apart from torment, though divided so very distinctly as to make a barrier that could never be crossed - in that realm of consciousness.

9. What is thy [[Ideal|ideal]] mentally?  Know that in the material plane, [[Thought Forms|mind is the builder]].  Are thy ideals and thy conversations with thy associates consistent?  They should be.  For as [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] gave, "Consistency, thou art a jewel."

10. These, then, are the beginnings in the finding of self.  And in the finding of self you will come closer to accomplishing that which may bring material as well as mental satisfaction in thy activities in the earth.

11. Do that, then, and you will come to know thyself better, and thy relationships with others will be more satisfactory, and there will no longer be the discontent within self or with self.  For if ye will have people interested in thee, interest thyself in people - and not by force.  Ye like associations, ye like such activities.  Why not practice it in thy life?

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Regarding health:  What is the state of my health and what measures should I take to improve it?
(A)  Take a good deal of the suggestions that have been given.  Clear the body as you do the mind of those things that have been hindered.  The things that hinder physically are the poor eliminations.  Set up better eliminations in the body.  This is why [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] and [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] come nearer to being the basis of all needed treatments for physical disabilities.

14. (Q)  What should I do for the lump that forms at base of right ear each morning?
(A)  Take the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments down at Reilly's and you'll get rid of all of it!  Change your mind and it won't come back!  Keep on thinking and worrying - it'll come back double.

15. (Q)  Are teeth in good order?
(A)  These are very good - they need some local attention. Use Ipsab occasionally with the dentifrice used - you'll find it will be most helpful to the gums and in relieving those tensions in the throat.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this on the gums with the finger.

16. (Q)  Regarding work:  What should I do to overcome a sense of confusion and unrest?
(A)  Just as indicated.  The confusion is within self, because of the presenting of one thing that you know and another that you would like for other people to think you know.

17. (Q)  Should I continue to work with the company I am now associated with or should I set up my own separate organiza- tion?  If so, please advise.
(A)  Stay through this particular period of anxiety in those activities in this land.  After another year ye may change for yourself.  You'll be big enough then for it.  You may have found yourself, if you set yourself to do it.  For remember, as He has given, "I stand at the door and knock. If you open I'll come in."  And He doesn't come in to be a drudge, He comes in to help.  He doesn't come in to be kept, He comes in to work with you, and to depart when you fail to do so.

18. (Q)  Regarding home:  Would [L...] and I be suitable life companions?
(A)  That you had best develop in your own self.  Are thy ideals one?  Can they conform?  Are ye a complement one to another, or will one lean upon the other, and the other draw upon the other?  Determine these in thine own self.  Yes, ye have been associated together before.  Read of thy activities in other lands and you'll see her standing at the door.

19. (Q)  In planning ahead, should I remain in my present home or would a change sometime this year be desirable?
(A)  If there is to be a change, it should come in the month of October or November.  We don't see a change very soon, or not best for a change very soon.

20. (Q)  Regarding my brother [2564] (who passed from the earth life about eighteen months ago), is it indicated that he is happy?
(A)  As was his [[Purpose|purpose]], so is the activity.  As indicated through the experience given thee in His word, pray for and with him if ye would help.

21. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> DOCTORS COME IN 2 MODELS
> By [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] G. Molnar, M.D.
> 
> Dear Dr. Molnar:  Please explain the difference between M.D.s
> and D.O.s.  My brother and I are having quite an argument and
> hope you can settle it. - P. B.
> 
> Together, the two groups - doctors of medicine and doctors of
> [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] - constitute the sum total of physicians qualified
> to give general health care.
> 
> Aside from them, there are others who profess themselves to
> be "doctors" but have not had adequate training.  One such
> group, for example is not permitted to give medicine or to
> perform surgery.  Obviously this limits their capability.
> There are still other groups which set themselves up to
> try to treat everything solely with diets, exercise and such.
> 
> Good diet, of course, is beneficial to all of us, and so
> is an appropriate amount of exercise, but you can't treat
> pneumonia with them, or remove a clogged gall bladder, or
> relieve other such serious conditions.
> 
> And there's one more general grouping:  people who have
> had excellent training, but only for a certain task.
> Podiatrists (for foot trouble), dentists and dental surgeons,
> optometrists (for measuring refractive errors in the eyes,
> and related matters), physiotherapists who do a great deal
> of good for people but work in conjunction with physicians.
> But these people stick to their fields of special training.
> 
> For general, comprehensive medical and surgical care, there
> are only the two main classifications:  M.D.s and D.O.s.
> 
> When [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] began, it was a cult, based on the belief
> that adjustments of the skeletal bones (mostly the spine)
> could be the answer to all illnesses.  That was back in the
> preceding century.
> 
> As of today, however, [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] schools have entrance
> requirements and provide comprehensive teaching comparable
> to that of the Medical schools.  Graduates, like M.D.s, have
> at least a year of service as interns after that before they
> become fully licensed physicians.
> 
> There are some differences, but not in fundamental health
> science.
> 
> There aren't nearly as many osteopathic schools, and not as
> many osteopaths.  There are some states which have few if any
> osteopaths, other states which have many.
> 
> In [[California|California]], for example, there has been a joining of
> forces to the extent that no distinction is made between the
> two.  In other states, however, the osteopaths are reluctant
> to give up their separate identity and "don't want to be
> swallowed up by the M.D.s."  There are various reasons for
> this, which I won't try to discuss here.  Part of it may be
> some leftover resentment from the days when the M.D.s viewed
> osteopaths scornfully.
> 
> But today the degree of "D.O." is a guarantee that the doctor
> has had rigid training and passes searching examinations just
> as the M.D. has.
> 
> I might add that half a century ago, there was a bitter
> conflict between the "allopaths" and the "homeopaths,"
> two branches of the M.D.'s, based on opposing theories
> as to the use of medicine.  It was for some years, a
> violent dispute, but it ended in agreement that the
> common cause is fighting disease.
