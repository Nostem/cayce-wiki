# TEXT OF READING 1184-1 F 39

**Reading [[1184-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/4/1936  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 6613

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1936, in accordance with request made by the husband - [...] through Associate Membership of Mr. [...] [[1069]''s son] in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions that disturb this body, [1184].

2. As we find, there are disturbing conditions which prevent the normal reactions for the body.  These as we find, while of a specific nature in some directions, also are contributory to other disturbances.

3. These arise from hindrances as we find in the impulses that supply the circulation to portions of the system.

4. From the effects of that as apparently was a character of influenza, or the flu, a condition was created in the nerve supplies to the trachea, the bronchi; and the organs of the sensory system were disturbed from the basis - that is, the ganglia from which there is the sympathetic impulse as well as the central nervous system in its supply of energies to portions of the head and the throat have been disturbed.  These have been aided and yet the very aiding of these in the MANNER of aiding became detrimental to the circulatory forces, and a condition where little of the cause has been reached though part of the condition which disturbed the body is removed; hence the condition is gradually growing worse in another direction.

5. For the impulses from the upper dorsal and through the cervical area for the supply to the auditory forces have allowed the balance or the oils and the circulatory forces through the functioning of the organs themselves to become impoverished.  And there are those tendencies for a prolapsus and for a drying Of those conditions in the organs that are supplied from the centers and ganglia themselves.

6. However, as we find, these are much worse in the one in the present; though both are becoming involved.

7. Also those conditions in the throat, the soft tissue of the face and head, while these are improved these are conditions that are disturbed through these same impulses.

8. There are also tendencies from the activity of these conditions for a disturbing in the assimilations of the system through the effect of those secretions as have entered or found their way through into the digestive system and produce the effect of catarrhal conditions as beginning in the gastric forces of the digestive system.

9. As we would find, to bring the greater aid in the immediate would be to take for the relaxation or the stimulation through corrections from the 5th and 6th dorsal to the base of the spine; also adding the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] that may be had through a stimulation of the Red Ray that will be of a deeper therapy.

10. These will make for applications to be given at least three times a week.  The manipulations and adjustments as well as the relaxing of the body.

11. As we find, the deep or the Red Ray would be given before the manipulations or corrections.  These will aid in stimulating and in making for those building ups in the system so that eliminations may be made more proper and thus bring the better conditions.

12. If there is indicated that there has been sufficient of the prolapsus when there has been the activity created by the stimulating in the upper dorsal and through the cervical and about the head and the neck, then it would be necessary for the body to have one or two corrective forces through the breaking of lesions about the eustachian tubes.

13. Do these, and we will find the better conditions for this body, [1184].

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [...], [[1069]'s son] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note at time of reading:  Dr. [[John (Apostle)|John]] R. Thompson in the Stevens Bldg. may be able to help you get lined up with the proper treatments, if you will let him read this.  GD.)
