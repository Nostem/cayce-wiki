# TEXT OF READING 760-26 F 62

**Reading [[760-26]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/22/1937  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 4706

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of May, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [760], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [760].]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [760]; this we have had before.

2. As we find the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body show improvements in most every way throughout, from that as we have had here before.

3. However, there are some localized conditions, or acute conditions, arising from infectious forces in the nostril or nasal passages and along portions of the face, from a sympathetic reaction as we find.

4. We would bathe same with a weak antiseptic, as the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - this preferably warm or hot.

5. Then on the abrasion places, or where the blister is shown in a portion of the nasal passage, we would use Yellow Oxide Ointment.

6. Thus we will find these will disappear.

7. One or two general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments would be well also.  In the main we find these general conditions, though, very good.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
