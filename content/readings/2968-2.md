# TEXT OF READING 2968-2 F 19

**Reading [[2968-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/30/1943  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 10243

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2968], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] Dillman, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[California|California]].

1. GD:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there has NOT been sufficient of the Appliance applications to materially change the effects in the body. These should be followed more closely, the outlines as to the preparation of the Appliance for its use, as well as the character of massages following same.

4. If these are done consistently, we should see definite changes for betterment of the body by the time there has been the second or third recharging of the Appliance.

5. Do not allow pessimistic remarks to keep the body from receiving all those things suggested.

6. Do that - prayerfully, consistently - we WILL find help.

7. If there is the giving up - well, you needn't to expect results.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  If I follow instructions religiously, could it be at all possible for me to start school in September instead of February?
(A)  February, as we find, will be possible - better than undertaking to do it in September.  But do it as given.  Do not get the idea that if it is suggested for an hour that two hours would make it better - it'll make it worse!  DO it as indicated.

10. (Q)  I am getting a new back brace that will hold me much straighter.  Will that be helpful?
(A)  Not necessarily; it will be better for some activities, but this will tend to prevent the complete rubs having their full effect.

11. (Q)  I shall, of course, have no manuals done in the future, but have these that have been done been detrimental?
(A)  This depends upon what use they are put to!  Of course, the suggestions made here are taking the body where and how it is in the present.

Do those things outlined, AS outlined.  Be consistent, be prayerful, if you would get results.

12. (Q)  Will the adjustments be gradual, or will I notice immediate results?
(A)  Read what has just been indicated.  At first it will appear that they are detrimental.  Then, as the massages and adjustments are made, by the changes of the circulation, changes will be gradually made for the better.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
