# TEXT OF READING 3715-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3715-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/17/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11221

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by his mother - Mrs. [...], via Mr. Frank E. Mohr.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading                               At his home in 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., W. Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, the conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body are rather serious.  Won't be much done for this body.

4. Now, these are the conditions, as we find in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body:

5. In the blood stream, this has been very good, and the conditions still show that there is some hope of recuperation still carried on by the inward [[Physical Body|physical body]], yet, with so much of the diseased tissue becoming involved along the cerebro-spinal system, and with the blood stream picking up rather than eliminating or forming resistance against same, we see this is gradually scattering throughout the system, and more and more does this hinder the locomotion of the body, for the formation is of sarcoma nature in the cerebro-spinal system proper, and with the bone structure being involved in the condition, and with the cerebro-spinal cord becoming involved in same, by the diseased tissue of the bone structure, this gradually cuts off the various incentives of locomotion, save as is seen through sympathetic system.  Reached too far a stage to be much benefits accomplished with same.  The better condition would be to apply those properties as would be found in [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes to the affected portions of the spine, see?  This, as we see, would nearer localize the condition.  Then give the body those properties internally that would give rejuvenation and assist in the building in the blood stream that as will, more and more, eliminate that that is thrown in it, and create more and more of the leukocytes that will ward off, or wall up, the dis-eased or diseased tissue.  While this will not aid in the paralysis as is produced already, yet it may prevent from spreading at the present time.

6. The [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes would be made of the green [[Mullein|Mullein leaves]] - these placed in warm water, or placed in a dish and warm water, or rather BOILING water poured over same - the leaves themselves taken out of this, see?  and as warm as is possible applied directly to these portions of the body, along the spine here, you see.

7. Also taking into the system those properties as are found:

8. Drink no water without carrying elm bark (slippery elm bark), and oils, and Magnesia, or Petrolagar - these all, of course, will cleanse the system - add to the eliminations.  Then take [[Baking Soda|Soda]] and Gold, see?  in these proportions:  15 grains of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] in 15 ounces distilled water, with 15 grains of Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]].  The dose would be 3 to 5 drops in water 2 times each day - 3 o'clock in afternoon and 9 o'clock in the mornings, see?  This again will keep elimination, and build in tissue and resistance in the blood supply.

9. Do not give meats.  Broths of meat, or of vegetables, these will be best for the body, see?

10. Do that.

11. We are through.
