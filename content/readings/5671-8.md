# TEXT OF READING 5671-8 F 34

**Reading [[5671-8]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/20/1930  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14090

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5671], Drs. Lydic and Parker, Miss Morris, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  Now, better changes have been made for this body.  The only changes as we would give in that being administered at present, would be in the manner that is being administered in the steam or [[Hydrotherapy|sitz bath]].  That we would give in the manner as has been outlined, or CHANGE to THIS specific - and put it in as given, not as you THINK it should be given!  Do it THIS way:  First, in the container, put the hot water - see?  the quantity as desired.  Then, with the tolu first dissolved, add same to the water - see?  Not soluble in water, but the quantity IS soluble in other solutions - see?  Dissolved - then add that of the tincture of myrrh, see?  then the bud or the gum, see?  NOT that this dissolves, no - see?  but NECESSARY for the betterment of the condition!  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What dosage of gum of aloes?
(A)  Five grains.

3. (Q)  Is body ready for ointment and battery?
(A)  Not just yet.  We would begin with same, though, in seven to eleven days.

4. (Q)  Should rectal treatments be resumed?
(A)  Not yet.

5. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Those as given we would do.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
