# TEXT OF READING 5627-1 F 62

**Reading [[5627-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/25/1930  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 13992

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by the self - via daughter, via Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         Asbury Park, N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, conditions, as we find, that cause distresses to the body, [5627], have to do with those effects as are created from disturbances of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  This, as we find, coming from that unbalancing of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in system, that has to do with both the blood supply and the eliminations of the system.  The thyroids, the lacteals, endrenal [adrenal?], all have their portions in the conditions as have caused, do cause, this hardening - or this distress in the physical functioning of the body.

2. In meeting the needs, as is seen, much rest, much of the proper consideration in the diet, and the changes in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as may be set up in the system.  These, as we find, may be changed, or altered, by the use of those of adrenalin, in its proper proportions to the needs; of the use of the thyroids, in their proper proportions to the needs of the replenishing in the system, and these would be given under the supervision of a physician who would also use those of the charged batteries as would carry into the system those of both [[Iodine|iodine]] and of [[Gold Chloride|chloride of gold]].  These, carried in through such charges, will aid the system in gaining its normal equilibrium.  We would also use the massage and the stimulation of the eliminating system, through those of fume baths, as would aid in capillary circulation.  Do that.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [137]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
