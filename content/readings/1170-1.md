# TEXT OF READING 1170-1 M 54 (Automobile Business, Protestant)

**Reading [[1170-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/13/1931  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 6556

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th Day of June, 1931, in accordance with request made by self, through his wife - Mrs. [959].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., W. Va. 
(Body and enquiring mind in regard to all phases of his life 
and those dependent upon him.  Questions regarding business, 
family, health, and all phases of the body's life)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1170], and those conditions and circumstances as surround the body and those dependent upon same.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  For what work am I best suited?
(A)  That of a salesman in lines as pertain to utilities, for the experiences not only in the present but in the past have fitted, or give a tendency towards such conditions in the life of the body, [1170].  Utilities, or such.

3. (Q)  Is there something else that I can do, other than I am now doing, that will be more profitable?  If so, what is it, and where?
(A)  In any place there is an outlet for such, that IS satisfactory to the body AND those that are dependent upon same; or with ones that are DISTRIBUTORS of such as utilities in any lines.  These, as we find, would offer the better opportunity, and be more profitable to the body.

In the districts as are in Tidewater, or that portion of the country WHERE the body has labored - see?

4. (Q)  It is our desire to finish [146]'s treatments and educate the three boys.  Will we be able to do this?
(A)  With their own assistance, as we find, this will be, and may be accomplished for those who will take same.

5. (Q)  Shall I continue in the automobile business?
(A)  There have been some associations, some connections recently made which, while not including this as a whole, or only in part, as we find, will keep the body close in touch with that part, or a portion of utilities.

6. (Q)  What specific utilities would be the best?
(A)  Any that's in the utility line.  As WE would find, those that have to do with a sort of combination store and auto shop, or dispensary of parts, or of those that have to do with autos.  The Ford agency at the Beach would be very good!

7. (Q)  Shall I accept the Thayer proposition?
(A)  Not until all others are weighed well together with this, and then as circumstances and conditions surround the body, weigh these well together.

8. (Q)  Advise if I should operate a filling station myself?  If so -
(A)  (Interrupting)  This would be very well, and - as given - this may be done in Tidewater, or may be done in the present surroundings.

9. (Q)  If I should change location, which would be better - Tidewater or Eastern North Carolina?
(A)  As we have given, Tidewater would be better.

10. (Q)  Would changing climate effect my health or that of any of my family?
(A)  As we find, would - in the present - be better for all concerned - in Tidewater.  The atmospheric pressure, as indicated in the conditions as respecting the respiratory system - as well as circulation - has been so altered, or so changed in the activities of the body, that if these are kept (that is, the activities) in the open, would be well in any of Tidewater.

11. (Q)  Shall we sell our home?  If so, when and how?  What price?
(A)  This, as we find, would not be - or would not be well to consummate - before the late Fall, or early next Spring.  As to the price, or as to how - these, as we find, will present themselves as the circumstances and the surroundings indicate by the activities of the body.

12. (Q)  Shall we accept a proposition from Mrs. Mourraille?
(A)  All of these are to be considered well together with the various conditions or surroundings that are brought about by these various suggestions.  There have been some determinations, or INTENTIONS builded in the body recently. These, as we find, would be well to consider - but we would not DECIDE under thirty or sixty days, and then after due consideration to all things.

13. (Q)  Under present conditions and circumstances, would it be well to make a trip to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] to look into the Ford agency?
(A)  Would be well.

14. (Q)  Is there any warning you would give to safeguard the health of the body at this time?
(A)  As we have found, and as we have given, do not consider that which would keep the body confined within doors.  That the general warning.  We are through for the present.
