# TEXT OF READING 3776-18 F 71

**Reading [[3776-18]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/18/1926  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 11331

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Miss [243].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. We find the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are on the wane.  The vitality is as used up.  The resistance in the whole system is gradually weakening.  The disintegration may be expected.  There is only that to be done that will give that resistance as is left the incentive to keep giving that as it may.  This will be found by increasing the stimulation in heart's action, and by the massage along the spine of the myrrh and olive oil. This would give better resistance or prolong the resistance as the body has, see?  Give stimulation, then, of the Spirits Frumenti, with milk and egg, and give the massage once to twice a day, see?  Give hot packs to feet and lower limbs to ease the pressure in poor circulation, and beware of any condition that upsets the intestinal system.

3. Do that for the present.

4. We are through with this reading.

10/18/26 Wire:  WIRE JUST RECEIVED DELAYED ON ACCOUNT OF PHONE GIVE HEART STIMULANT ALSO STIMULATION SPIRITS FRUMENTI WITH MILK AND EGG MASSAGE SPINE AT LEAST TWICE A DAY WITH EQUAL PARTS OLIVE OIL AND MYRRH HOT BATHS OR PACKS TO FEET AND LOWER LIMBS BEWARE OF ANY CONDITION THAT WOULD UPSET INTESTINAL SYSTEM PLEASE WIRE ME HER CONDITION THIS AFTERNOON LOVE TO ALL.  EDGAR CAYCE
