# TEXT OF READING 1384-2 F 5

**Reading [[1384-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1937  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 7100

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is her illness curable?
(A)  As we find, as has been indicated, there are those prospects, those hopes that should never be let loose of - for all complete recovery to the normal activities of a physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]].

In the present, since changes are to be made:

During this present season we would enable the body to keep in the open as much as possible.

Also, at least once each week through the next two months, we would have an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation with adjustments in the lumbar, the sacral and the lower limb; that there may be the assimilation and the stimulation for not only the growth to normalcy but the activity of the body.

Each day we would use the Radio-Active Appliance for one hour, attached to opposite sides of the body; right wrist, left ankle; left wrist, right ankle.  This is to create - by the body-[[Vibration|vibrations]], that are brought to activative forces of forcing through the whole of the circulatory forces that renewal of energy and vitality - re-ionizing of the vital forces.

After the treatment with the Radio-Active Appliance each day we would use a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Tincture of Myrrh; especially over afflicted portions - that is, the hip, the thigh, the knee and the feet; that this, too, may stimulate the circulation.

These do, and keep activities cheery.  Thus we may bring the better conditions for the body.

When there is the inability for so much outside activity, there should necessarily be changes made not only in the adjustments and manipulations and the applications but in other conditions about the body, for the better and greater development.

4. (Q)  Will she be able to walk and run normally?
(A)  This depends upon development.

5. (Q)  Is a brace necessary?
(A)  This when the body is so active outside (though it becomes somewhat cumbersome) is helpful; though as soon as strength is shown we would keep away from same as much as possible.

6. (Q)  Should she go back to Dr. Locke in [[Canada|Canada]]?
(A)  Preferably we would carry her to Dr. Dobbins in [[New York City|New York]]!

7. (Q)  Are special shoes necessary?
(A)  These ARE necessary.

8. (Q)  What specific kind would be advised?
(A)  Those that make a brace to the foot.  These will naturally be changed as developments are shown.  Let the suggestion of the manipulator be followed in this respect.

9. (Q)  About how long will it take?
(A)  How long is till next week or next year!  This depends upon the consistency, the persistency, and the application or the awakening of the better forces for normal activity.

We should say, before the season for the changing, there should be a great deal of help for the body.

Then - as to time, this may better be given then.

10. (Q)  What diet and exercise?
(A)  All the exercise and in the open, and that that makes for blood and nerve building in the diet.  Let this, too, be outlined by the manipulator.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents
"    " Mr. [257] (This copy to be given to Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].)
"    " Ass'n file
