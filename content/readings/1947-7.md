# TEXT OF READING 1947-7 F 33

**Reading [[1947-7]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/9/1941  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 8411

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1947], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1947].

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is the condition of my ears?
(A)  Owing to the undue emotional conditions, these as we find - while somewhat better - are still causing a good deal of anxiety at times.

Also the strains through the body cause the kidneys and the pelvic organs to be disturbed.

We would take at least six [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, especially in reference to the conditions from the 3rd and 4th dorsal to the base of the brain; and especially to ears, eyes and throat; and those in the lumbar and sacral for the organs of the pelvis AND the better eliminations - and the coordinations with kidneys and liver.

5. (Q)  Please give condition of gums and teeth, and suggested treatment.
(A)  As we find, we would use an equal combination of salt and [[Baking Soda|soda]] as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] for the gums twice each day.

We would have some local attention for some of the teeth, by a dentist.

6. (Q)  Is Dr. Pleasants of ... reliable in this respect?
(A)  Would be very good.

7. (Q)  What is the explanation for the unhappy professional experience I had last winter?
(A)  This was a part of the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in MEETING itself, as indicated through this channel, for [[Soul|soul]]-development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  If these experiences are used as stepping-stones, and not as conditions to make for anxieties - or conditions in which there are grudges held, we find that they will prove beneficial.

There should be no disturbance, no unpleasantness from same, if there is kept the present attitudes - and if there is the honest application of those things as for the associations with others.

8. (Q)  Will I continue to suffer, particularly in the case of creditors, because of it?
(A)  Not if there is the application as indicated.

9. (Q)  How can I help my work of physical development become a national condition, as my Reading suggested?
(A)  By the manners in which the first application of same is to be spread as a regular curriculum in the teen age students, see?  This should be through the stressing of same, and thus there will gradually be those activities as indicated becoming a part of the regular activities for betterment of the physical health, of teen age girls especially.

10. (Q)  Have I really grown in spiritual maturity?
(A)  Grown a great deal.  Do not allow those anxieties, because of what others may say, to change thy [[Ideal|ideal]] and its [[Purpose|purposes]] and its application to others.

11. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Keep close to those [[Ideal|ideals]] which prompted [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to launch the activities.  For these may be made beneficial in the experience of others.

For, if the activities that have been a part of the experience were something added to one of thine own, it would be worth the world!

12. We are through with this Reading.  [See below note re next attempted Rdg.]

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

[Check Reading attempted next for "[2005], Camp ..., N.C." Answer came back, "We do not find the entity here."

So we did not attempt [2004]'s thinking perhaps they were at another address.  We'll get these later.]

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] directions for:  Dr. A. R. Tucker, 411 Masonic Temple Bldg., Raleigh, N.C.)
