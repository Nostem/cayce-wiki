# TEXT OF READING 409-22 F 21

**Reading [[409-22]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/13/1931  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 3065

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [409].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [409], Sarah Hesson, Anne Penn, Mr.[5655], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [409].  Now, we find the body is continuing on the improve.  There are those conditions in the muscular forces, and in the abrasions themselves, that cause some disorder as yet.  This, as we find, is [[Healing|healing]], and that - with the applications of that as produces better circulation through those parts that have been so long dormant - gradually more use may be had of the limb itself.  Until there is less of the discharge, we would not put too much weight on the limb, and we would have more exercise of same in a specific manner.

2. In the application of the violet ray, as well as over the knee and portion of the limb, the sole of the foot and along the ankle, that the whole circulation may be aided in the limb, see?

3. We would keep the rest of those as outlined for the body; for, as we will find, these will be well, too - that occasionally portions are left off, but that they are applied again, that there may be the proper reactions through the system.

4. We would, for the specific exercises, make those, of mornings, that bring the blood supply to the head - as of the head and neck exercise, the arms, and the bending at the waist.  In the evening, we would take those (All of these had best be taken lying down, or sitting up and lying down) of the circular motion of the body, of the hips, of the limbs, and the bending again of the body toward the floor - see?  These will aid the body.  Then, as the discharge is lessened more and more put weight on the limb - see?  This will make for more activity in the discharge - may pain some at first, but with the applications as will bring ease for same, as has been outlined, these will bring better conditions for the body.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What causes bad breath?
(A)  Poor eliminations.

6. (Q)  Is there any pus pockets settling in hip joints?
(A)  We do not find so.  Those of the excretions and discharges as are coming from those of irritations, where so long in the flesh and about the covering or skin of the bone itself there was irritation.  Hence this going through the system, see, CAUSES the reactions that produce for poor eliminations.  Hence all of the applications are tending toward making a coordination of eliminations.

7. (Q)  Is the body naturally fat, or is it swollen?
(A)  Naturally fat!

8. (Q)  What should be done for granulated eyelids?
(A)  Use a weak solution of boracic acid.  About twice each week use those of scraped Irish potato, bound of evening over the eye.

9. That is all the questions regarding this body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n
"    " File
