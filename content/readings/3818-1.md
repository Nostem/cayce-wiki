# TEXT OF READING 3818-1 M 39

**Reading [[3818-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/17/1924  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 11414

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Washington Hotel, Room 1105, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill., this 17th day of July, 1924, in accordance with request made by self, through Mr. [2667].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno. Mr. [3818].]

Time of Reading 
12:40 P. M.                                      ..., Ill.

1. EC:  Now, we find, while this body is very good physically throughout in many ways, there are some abnormal conditions that may be corrected, that the body physically may function more perfectly in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

2. Now, we find the abnormal conditions have to do with eliminations and with the absorption of poisons in the system that hinder the proper functioning of organs.  In the mental forces there are conditions that do not at the present time coordinate with the condition of the mental forces as has been outlined.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3818] we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the red blood deficient in quantity and quality, for the poisons or toxins as absorbed or taken in the blood supply show or give that destructive forces, so that we find the body in portions of the system through this condition has the lack of the vital forces to meet the needs of the body in functioning in every organ. This we find is shown in the pelvis forces, in the hepatic circulation, in the hypogastric circulation, both in nerve and in blood supply.  White blood forces better or nearer normal but coagulation in used forces prevent the perfect union or coagulation.  Hence the dullness, the achey portions and conditions as we find through the body.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, these we find more from mental condition than from the forces in the body.  That is, the attitude of the body from the mental status shows how conditions from external forces affect the body physically.  This is part from weakened condition in blood supply and of the nerve forces, yet is of the physical a portion, a part, for has more to do with the other forces in system than physical.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, brain forces very good.  In the throat, lungs and larynx we find how that these conditions are exaggerated in the system at times, digestive tract in this, also the effect of the hypogastric centers and the circulation through same.

7. In other organs these are very good.

8. In the condition in the pelvis and in the hepatic circulation these are where we find the system absorbing the toxins that become detrimental to the body.

9. To give the best mental and to assist [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for the perfect coordination in this system, we would correct the conditions through the mental forces by the set of the mind to that the body desires the actions toward in a correct manner.

10. In the physical, we would take only those exercises in the physical and those properties in the system that will produce the equilibrium necessary to overcome these conditions, using then this:

11. This would be the one dose and these would be prepared as often as necessary, taking in the beginning at least twelve.

Eucalyptol..............1 minim,
Oil of Turp.............1 minim,
Canadian Balsam.........1 minim.

12. These may be prepared in capsules or in liquid, using Peptotone as a carrier, and would be taken once each day, and applying the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] forces over the body along the whole length of the spine for at least ten minutes each day.

13. Do that.  We will bring the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in this body to the normal conditions.
