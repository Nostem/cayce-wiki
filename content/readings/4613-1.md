# TEXT OF READING 4613-1 M 6

**Reading [[4613-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1927  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 12518

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by his father, Mr. [554].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are those physical conditions that cause the abnormal development with this body.  These conditions have to do with that abnormality as has already been created by improper functioning and distribution of the effects created in system by [[Glands (Esoteric)|pituitary]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] not functioning in a normal way and manner, and with the pressure as is created in the cerebrospinal nerve centers these will not be relieved until pressure is removed from same, and that [[Vibration|vibration]] added to the system that would bring the reaction necessary for the full development, not only of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as affecting the body at the base of the brain through the medulla oblongata, but those of the normal functioning of the central brain in the solar plexus center radiating that from the umbilicus plexus.

3. Then, to meet the needs of these conditions, and to bring about the normal forces for the body, we would prepare this to be applied to the body to carry the correct [[Vibration|vibration]] to this system.  We would build a machine like this:

4. In a three (3) gallon glass jar or crock, put two (2) gallons of rain water.  Then put:

Copper Sulfate.....................1 pound,
Sulfuric Acid C.P..................1 ounce,
Willow Charcoal....................3 ounces,
Common Zinc........................5 drams,
Heavy Sea Salt.....................6 ounces.

5. Prepare a covering for the container of wood, with two (2) holes in same, so that poles may be put into this solution. The poles should be half inch flat and quarter inch thick. One of nickel, the other of copper.  To the ends of these poles (projecting above the wooden top) attach covered copper wires.  To the end of these wires - to the one that is of copper attach a small plate of copper (size of fifty cent piece), with attachments so that same may be attached to the body of the child.  To the other pole attach a wire and a larger plate (two inches in diameter) to the end of this wire, and that of nickel.  In between the pole and the plate attach a lead wire, and same is placed through a two (2) - ounce container (glass), or through the cork, so it projects into the solution, of two (2) ounces of distilled water, with five (5) grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], see?  This should be attached to the body - these plates - for an hour each day. Attach the small plate to the base of brain, see?  and this attached first, see?  so it is in contact with same, see? The larger flat plate to be attached to the umbilicus, see?

6. After this has been used for five (5) hours - that is, an hour each day, or five (5) days, begin with the adjustments of the cerebrospinal nerve centers and the vertebraes, or adjustment of the vertebraes and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the muscular tissue surrounding the cartilaginous forces as connect same, so that the body may respond to the [[Vibration|vibration]] as is created by the electro vibration of the gold to the developing system, see?

7. When this (and the solution may be kept for this long, if covered) has been used for sixty (60) days, then we would give those necessary changes as are seen for the further development of the normal forces for this body.  Do that.

8. (Q)  Should the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] be given osteopathically?
(A)  Be given osteopathically.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] and adjustments given osteopathically, and there will be seen the changes as take place in the body from these vibrations as are set up.  Every third (3rd) day attach to the base of brain.  Every OTHER day (begin though with attaching to brain, see?) attach to wrist or ankle, either one, see?  but attach the small plate first, and the larger plate - that the current passes through the [[Gold Chloride|gold solution]] - to the umbilicus, each day.  Do that, as given.  Prepare this as given, and we will bring better conditions for this body, [4613], you see.

9. (Q)  Is this lead wire on the wires that has the nickel pole and plate?
(A)  As given, on the nickel pole and plate.  See, the lead wire passes into the [[Gold Chloride|gold solution]].  When this is taken from the body do not allow any of the plates to come in contact with each other, and each day the lead wire going into the two (2) ounce container should be taken out and cleansed, see?  and when solution shows any sedimentary condition from the vibratory forces as set up, add new solution in the gold, see?  The solution as is in jar may be changed at the end of thirty (30) days, but prepared in the same way and manner as given.  At the end of sixty (60) days we would give the necessary changes for this body.

10. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments be made?
(A)  Every other day, AFTER five days.

11. We are through for the present.
