# TEXT OF READING 340-34 F 48

**Reading [[340-34]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/29/1936  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2626

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of July, 1936, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [340], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], and Thomas House.]

Time of Reading                                   ... St., 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [340].

2. Conditions are not so good, yet as we find these acute conditions arise from indiscretions in the eating or in the combinations of those things that are known not to agree very well with the body.

3. Then with the attitude and then with the heat, and then with the general conditions, these tie-ups in the mucous membranes, throughout the alimentary canal and also through the throat and head, and the inflammation through the colon area, all arise.

4. First as we find in the present, give a cool enema, see; cleansing the system or the colon with same.

5. Follow same with at least two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the last waters.

6. Put a [[Poultice|poultice]] of grapes, cool - grapes of any character, preferably Concord but any character, crushed - except those without seed, don't use those - we want those with seed for it's the tartaric acid that we are giving that we want the reaction from.  Crush same, place between thin cloths over the abdomen, extending up to the lower end of the stomach, to overcome this nausea and tendency for the reaction to the head and throat and the irritations that arise.

7. If these are unable to be obtained, then take the Tartaric Acid to use in a pack; not a saturated solution but a little less than saturated solution; that in water or tap water will be well, for we want it that cool.  Put this then in packs three or four or five thicknesses, just wring this out of same and place over the same area.

8. Have the manipulative forces gently given along the spine.

9. After the eliminations are set up and the head is easy, then begin with those same properties as has been indicated for the removal of inflammation through the alimentary canal.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What diet should be taken during this period?
(A)  That is a semi-liquid diet would be preferable.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

Collect - July 29, 1936:

GIVE COOL ENEMA WITH TWO TABLESPOONSFUL [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCO-THYMOLINE]] IN LAST WATER  GRAPE [[Poultice|POULTICE]] OVER ABDOMEN CONCORD IF POSSIBLE. STOP  IF UNABLE TO SECURE GRAPES USE SATURATED SOLUTION OF TARTARIC ACID ON SEVERAL THICKNESSES OF CLOTH.  [[Massage Therapy|MASSAGE]] ALONG SPINE.  AS SOON AS RELIEVED BEGIN OTHER TREATMENTS PREVIOUSLY SUGGESTED.  EDGAR CAYCE.
