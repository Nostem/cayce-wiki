## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 10/11/17 F. W. Sears's letter to EC:
> 
> F. W. SEARS, M. P.
> LECTURER, WRITER, TEACHER
> 108 & 110 W. 34th ST.
> [[New York City|NEW YORK]]
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce;
> 
> Have just returned from a lecture trip west which immediately
> followed my vacation and will answer your letter now before
> I take up my season's work which begins next week.
> 
> I have not been able to obtain the "wet battery" suggested*
> and so have just began to use a Farradic battery with dry
> cells.
> 
> There are two kinds of batteries, Farradic and Galvanic,
> both of which may be used with either dry cells or wet
> batteries.  As the former and dry cells are a more recent
> invention I have been trying to get a Galvanic battery with
> wet cells.  Have tried everywhere in both N.Y. City and
> [[Chicago|Chicago]] for the latter.  Have had one promised me here in
> N.Y. City but found it was not ready when I returned last
> Tuesday so have just began a course of treatment with the
> Farradic dry cell and believe it is going to be quite
> beneficial.
> 
> Now in regard to what you call your "gift" and the inquiry
> you make in reference to it.
> 
> The world is perfectly familiar with what is known as the
> first, second, and third dimensions; i.e., length, breadth
> and thickness, but it is not familiar with the fourth
> dimension, which is the interpenetrability of matter although
> there is ample evidence of it even to the physical senses,
> such evidence may be quickly observed by dipping a lump of
> sugar, or a cloth of any kind, in water and witnessing the
> atoms of water fill the space in between the atoms of sugar
> or cloth.
> 
> Man is also familiar with the material or physical world but
> he is not familiar with the astral world simply because the
> latter is made of finer material and interpenetrates the
> atoms of the coarser material of the physical world at will,
> and also exists outside of such earth material.
> 
> At the time of the birth of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] "something"
> comes and takes possession of it otherwise we have what is
> 
> 
> 
> * [GD's note:  No copy of Mr. Sears' reading to which he
> refers is in the A.R.E. files.  Evidently EC wrote asking
> his opinion of the information and this was the reply.]
> 
> known as a "still birth" or a dead child.  This "something"
> is the [[Spiritual Body|astral body]] which is more commonly known as the
> "[[Soul|soul]]".  At the times of the "death" of the [[Physical Body|physical body]]
> this [[Spiritual Body|astral body]] simply leaves it and does not come back to
> take possession of it again, the connecting cord being
> separated.
> 
> The astral world, from which the "[[Soul|soul]]" comes at birth and
> to which it returns at death, is all around us and interwoven
> with the physical or material world.
> 
> Some persons are more sensitive to the astral world than
> are others.  This is NOT a "gift" but rather a species of
> abnormal and unconscious development which may be had by
> anyone.  Whether or not the effects are "good" depends upon
> whether such person uses such development as [[Jesus Christ|the master]] of
> it or its slave.  The tendency with more persons is to become
> the slave to it and in all such cases it not only debilitates
> the body but in time it controls the human mind.  Such
> development simply being the unconscious giving up of the
> human body and all of its faculties to some [[Spiritual Body|astral body]] other
> than the one to which the human body rightfully belongs, and
> permitting its use by such astral body.
> 
> When you become unconscious during the time you are using
> this "gift" you are simply giving up the use of your physical
> body to some astral entity and allowing it to use your body
> and its organs to express itself on this material plane.
> This astral entity is one who was a physician here in the
> material world and who is now a physician on the astral plane
> (for we have and need physicians there as well as here, but
> those on the astral plane deal with the finer material than
> do those on the physical plane; this finer material being
> what is usually called here by the name of magnetism,
> although that is not what it really is.)
> 
> When you lay down to sleep at night your astral body leaves
> the physical but remains connected with it by an astral cord.
> When it returns and takes possession of your [[Physical Body|physical body]]
> the consciousness, knowledge or wisdom of your human mind has
> not been increased or changed to any material extent; and
> this same thing is true when you lay down your physical body
> at death.  On the astral plane one uses his finer senses
> (which correspond to the five physical senses) and is able to
> determine things by reason thereof which the physical senses
> do not know.  That is why your doctor was able to tell me
> some things about my physical condition which I had sensed
> but of which I was not fully sure before.  He was not able
> to tell me a lot of other things simply because he had not
> learned them either here in the material world nor on the
> astral plane.
> 
> I am calling attention to these things in order that you may
> better understand the correct value of the information given
> during these trances of yours.
> 
> That your control, or guide (whatever one may wish to call
> it) is able to diagnose disease much better by reason of
> working with his astral senses than is the vast majority of
> physicians, there is not doubt, and to that extent can this
> be used by those in need of such assistance.  That any
> information which it might give for other [[Purpose|purposes]] would be
> of any value is another matter and would depend wholly and
> entirely upon such guide's development both here and on the
> astral plane.*
> 
> In view of the fact that to continue to permit your physical
> body to be used as the medium through which these messages
> may be given, is to devitalize it in most cases and in time
> bring on a general debilitated condition unless the work is
> done with great care and under the most harmonious
> conditions, my advice to you would be to go very slow in any
> further development of this "gift" and build up your physical
> body to the end that when you do allow yourself to be used as
> a medium that you are able, physically and mentally, to do so
> without injury to yourself.
> 
> I have had a larger experience, probably, in the study of and
> in dealing with "nervous" people and the influence of the
> astral [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] upon them than falls to the lot of the most
> experienced "nerve specialist" and know how easy it is for
> the average person to get into the currents where he becomes
> astralized or obsessed, or both, and how difficult it is for
> him to get free from these currents.  Nothing but the proper
> control of his human mind by his own [[Soul|soul]] or ego ever
> accomplishes this permanently.  It is just as necessary to
> keep out from under the control of some other astral mind as
> it is to keep out from under the control of some other human
> mind.
> 
> With all good wishes I am
> 
> Faithfully  [signed]  F. W. Sears
> 
> 
> 
> * [Handwritten at top of above letter was the word NO in
> large letters and circled.]  [GD's note:  This big pencilled
> "NO" is in EC's handwriting.  I suspect it means EC did not
> agree with Mr. Sears's interpretation of the source of "the
> gift".]
