# TEXT OF READING 379-18 F 61 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[379-18]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/22/1942  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 2950

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by self Mrs. [379], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [379], [420], [1532], Esther Wynne, Ruth Holland, Helen and Marsden Godfrey.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [379], born December 22, 1880, in Baltimore, Md.  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [379].

3. In giving the signs, the symbols that may indicate the experiences of this entity through the material [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], - to be sure, as to their beauty, or their significance in the experience of the entity, will depend much upon the artist's concept, as to how same is balanced.

4. For, in some instances it will be found that these appear to be at great variations.

5. For this entity, we would begin - in the center at the bottom - by putting the sign of Mercury; in blue and gold.  About same, top and bottom, put the sign or symbol of Aries, while upon each side put the sign of Libra.  These, to be sure, would be fine but black in their nature.

6. Upon the left side put the island of [[Poseidia|Poseidia]]; this almost rectangular in its shape, with the base being the longer edge, and in the center - in the base - would be the projection, or two curves there, upon which there would be indicated the city, or a town, of [[Poseidia|Poseidia]] - which would be written on (and this in a circle), in the color of coral pink.

7. Upon the right at the bottom put the sign of the earth; this green in the lines, shadings from these indicating their association.  Here mind or spirit, as place.  As activity, [[Poseidia|Poseidia]] and earth.  As to the manner, that accredited in Aries and Libra.

8. In the center above this put [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], or a temple; this to be in stone color, and to show an entrance to a temple - this indicated by three steps.  These should be long steps, hence covering most of the front of [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]]; indicating upon either side of the entrance - on the one the scarab (in gold), on the other the hawk, or the figure with the hawk face or head (and this in flesh, though head in brown and gray) - do not make the bill too long.

9. Upon the left side put the sign of Uranus, at an angle rather than as ordinarily set in a balance, - turned toward the central figure.  The upper portion would be in coral or pink, the lower portion in blue; and the signs about same would be of Leo - four in number.

10. Upon the right side put the sign of Venus; this with the four figures or signs of Cancer.  All of these [[Astrology|zodiac]] signs would be fine-lined in black.  The symbol of Venus would be in gray and blue.

11. The central figure would represent the place, [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], the activities indicated in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience.  The manner of expression through the period would be indicated in Uranus, and the extreme - or in the manner of the might or power, or as indicated in Leo in the heart activity of same; while in the latter portion in love that meant the sacrifice as in Scorpio.

12. Above this would be [[Crucifixion|the cross]] in the distance, - this upon the left side.  This should be indicated by the evening, and the clouds above same.  In the foreground would be the figure of [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] FLEEING from same; while to the right would be the light as from the sun - though more brilliant, even, than the golden, showing the white and gold in same.  This would be expressed with not a great deal of vegetation, though ALL grass, flowers and shrubbery shown as green in the foreground would be bending - to indicate the sorrowing in the period or experience.

13. Upon the left put the symbol of Jupiter, in white and gold, a five-pointed star at the top of same.

14. Upon the left put the symbol for the earth, in blue.  No rings about either of these symbols.  Above this also would be [[Star of Bethlehem|the star]], in gold.

15. The central figure above this would be a village, with streets, houses, - these rather in the edge of a wilderness, with a great deal of vegetation - especially trees - a little water in the foreground, in blue.  Most of the trees would be in green, and a little color here and there as of the redwood, or the dogwood - or both, as presented in blossom. Of course, the house - or houses, or village - would be in white and black.  To the sides of each would be grasses and reeds.  Above same would be the sun rising; this indicated in a great deal of color - as the little fleecy clouds are lighted by the sun's rays.

16. Upon the left from this would be the sign of quite a large question mark; this in white, with the symbol of Pisces upon each side; nothing at top or bottom.

17. Upon the right put a large G; this in gold, and the eight- pointed star upon the top AND the bottom.

18. These would indicate the place, the questions, the service given.

19. As to that to which the entity may attain:

20. At the top put the figure of the questionings of the Scriptures.  Two figures would be indicated, with the temple as a background.  This would be the Child questioning or reasoning with [[Ra Ta|the priest]].  This would be overshadowed by the golden cloud, and upon either side would be [[Crucifixion|the cross]] - not heavy, not too slender, but in gold, though light or white upon edge of same.  The stars that make the wreath should extend from the top as a curtain; at least twelve, extending down to the central portion.  These would be smaller upon the sides, and rising to the central star, and these alternate white and gold, to the larger central star above the cloud at the top - and this formed not as an arch, but forming a peak arch, or peaked arch over same.

21. This indicates the attaining of learning, the application as He taught in the earth.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [379]
"    " Miss Wynne
"    " Ass'n file
