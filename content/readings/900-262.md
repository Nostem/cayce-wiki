# TEXT OF READING 900-262 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-262]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/23/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5465

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. T. B. House, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and Thomas House.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you, and you will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had the body here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through those sources as have been outlined, and may be used or applied in the daily life, in the way and manner as has been given, for these are as experiences to the mental potential forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Thursday Morning, Aug. 19, 1926.  "I was expectorating blood into my handkerchief.  The flow increased and they told me to go up to Liberty in the mountains.  I was very frightened, fearing tuberculosis.  I tried frantically to reach Cayce for a reading to ascertain just the cause and what this hemorrhage was."
(A)  In this there are presented, in this manner, those conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body to bring the warning to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as respecting the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the body.  Well that the indications as are exhibited in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] at the present time be due consideration given in same, through the sources in which the entity is expecting, or is attempting in same to gain information concerning, for, as we see in the physical forces of this body, there are those conditions that might cause those conditions to appear, through strain, and the condition is at present only that of strain.  Use the inhalant for same, and we will find this will disappear, see?  This as the preparation:

To 4 ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol.........................20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...............5 minims,
Canadian Balsam.....................3 minims,
Benzosol............................8 minims,
Benzoin.............................5 minims,
Tolu in solution...................30 minims.

Keep solution in glass corked bottle, twice the size of the quantity.  Shake solution and inhale, both through nostril and through the mouth, for at least three times, twice each day, inhaling deep in the lungs, through nostril and through the mouth, see?  the fumes from same, see?  This will relieve the condition, for this acts as a stimulant to the mucus coating of nostril and throat.

We would also be careful of the condition as exists in [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] about the ear - the Eustachian Tubes, see?  These would be well to be manipulated again.

The diet should be looked after, for there should not be created in the system toxins to be overcome, by strains as have been produced in the mucus coating of bronchials, of the tubes, or of the nasal channels; for, as we see, the physical body is that through which the mental and spiritual forces of an entity manifest in the physical world, and the manifestations as are seen and known of other bodies or minds - they gain much of their concept from the physical forces of an entity.  Whether the spiritual or mental attributes are considered, those in close contact see the physical condition first, and are persuaded much thereby.  Hence this vehicle, this physical body, should be guarded well, that through same the greater number may be brought in that [[Last Supper|communion]] with the manifested forces of the mental and spiritual abilities of this entity, [900].

4. (Q)  "Durant seemed running 3 railroads for a Lauer joint account.  I sought the name of the roads and it seemed something like 'Bree' or maybe 'Loree.'  They should go up under these conditions I felt, yet I didn't like to buy the market at this high level."
(A)  This as the indication to the entity of [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which these various fluctuations as concerning the three individual stocks as are seen, of the Lackawana, of the Southern, and of that of the Hudson, are being manipulated through Durant, and, as is seen, these will advance, these will fall, these will change much in the next few weeks, with the unsettled market conditions.

5. (Q)  Indication that rails should be bought, particularly Loree group?
(A)  These should be bought, as we see, but we will see the fluctuations in these, see?

6. (Q)  But not now - later on a break?
(A)  Now is very good time, for the break will come and there will be much fluctuations, yet these are to advance for some time yet.

7. (Q)  "Some high priced stock, the name of which I could not ascertain, seemed to go high and then break. Something warning of a coming break in stock market."
(A)  This, as is seen, the mental forces of the entity attempting to gain, through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] that of conditions which have existed in the body-mind forces of the entity.  These will be presented again.  As seen at present, these represent steel and General Motors, see?

8. (Q)  "Saw American Linseed sell at 34."
(A)  These, as we see, again the mental attaining, through [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], views of conditions as pertaining to the market.  These will be presented again.  This, as is indicated, will sell near this figure, see.  When the same general forces as have been seen, and through those same channels as this is seen, is obtained, there will be presented as to time.

9. (Q)  Voice:  "When Fleischman breaks then SELL THE MARKET."
(A)  This given as the criterion for conditions as regarding the industrial end of the market.

10. We are through with this reading.
