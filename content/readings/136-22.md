# TEXT OF READING 136-22 F 21

**Reading [[136-22]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/5/1925  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 329

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by her husband - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same. You will also answer any questions I might ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  The physical conditions in some respects are different from those as we had here before.  The accentuation in some of the ganglions in the distresses caused have changed to those as are affected by the muscular tissue in portions of the body.  The general physical condition of body is better than when last we had same.  The mental attitude of body towards self is somewhat changed.

3. For those conditions in the muscular tissue, along the cerebro-spinal portion, we would apply equal parts Tincture of Myrrh and Fluid Extract Tolu solution, using about half an ounce of each.  Massage this well into the cuticle.  Then apply heavy salt, heated, and in a cloth container, to these portions and have as warm or hot as body can stand.  This, as we see, will soon alleviate the condition.  Also giving to the ganglions and tissue in these portions of body that relief from the congestion as is caused there.  Do that, and we will bring the relief for those physical conditions causing distress at the present time in body, [136].  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is this body pregnant?
(A)  Not at this time.

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.
