# TEXT OF READING 272-2 F 33

**Reading [[272-2]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/18/1931  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 1678

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [272].

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. (Q)  Where does the discharge from my uterus come from?
(A)  From the walls of the uterus and condition in the vagina itself, as WELL as the infection toward the [Fallopian] tube. Were those applications of those douches made, that carry an antiseptic in same as of Lysol in its proper proportions for same, with the applications of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over the centers that supply the nutriment to the pelvic organs and over the puba itself, we will find these conditions and these discharges will be relieved.  Taking at the same time a tonic that will CLARIFY these conditions from the system, as is made in THIS manner:

To 1/2 gallon of distilled or rain water, we would add:

Cinchona Bark......................1/2 ounce,
Dogfennel Flower...................1/2 ounce,
Prickly Ash Bark...................1/2 ounce,
Elder Flower.........................4 ounces.

Reduce this by simmering, or slow boiling, to one pint. Strain while warm and add 2 ounces of grain alcohol, CUTTING 2 DRAMS Balsam of Tolu in same.  Shake the solution well together before the dose is taken, which would be half a teaspoonful 4 times each day, BEFORE each meal and at bedtime.

The douches as we would take with the Lysol, or with any disinfectant for those conditions in the system of this nature, we would take at least twice each day.

With the matter of the diets, beware of meats, of butter, of any greasy foods.  No fried foods or meats of any nature. Rather those of the vegetables that grow above the ground or in pods, see?

3. (Q)  Are the tubes affected?
(A)  This has just been given you!

4. (Q)  Where is the streptococci holding the infection?
(A)  It isn't a strept!

5. (Q)  Where are the G.C. germs?  Are they in my blood stream?
(A)  They are active TO the blood stream, as are being infected FROM those conditions existent in the uterus and vagina.  Hence those conditions as given for LOCAL treatment, as well as the blood supply.

6. (Q)  Are they attacking any other organs in my body, other than the sexual ones?
(A)  If they were allowed to remain in same, they will!

7. (Q)  Are G.C. germs in the pimples on my arms?
(A)  Not yet.  These are the blood infections from the effluvia as thrown out by the too little activity through the whole of the eliminating system.  Hence the supplying of that for the blood supply, as given, as well as for the system itself.  Follow these, and we will find these will be removed from the system in their proper order.  Be sure that the body keeps itself clear from any infectious force, or any association with the opposite sex in these applications as are being given for the body.

8. (Q)  Am I going to have an operation later?
(A)  It depends upon the conditions and what the activities will be!  for it may be necessary to have an operation if these conditions are allowed to attack the organs of the pelvis farther, or should they become effective in some other portions of the system.  Allowed to run on in their regular way and manner, as they would, they would become effective - but if these are applied, this - This would be foretelling, or prognosticating.  That depends upon the activities of the body itself, as to whether it will or will not.  It would be NECESSARY, were the conditions allowed to become acute!

9. (Q)  When can I experience libido?
(A)  When the conditions in the whole general system have been clarified in such a way and manner as normalcy appears in these directions.

10. (Q)  Will it physically injure the organs under these conditions?
(A)  Unless precautions are taken, and the activity is followed to remove or relieve the system of the conditions. It MUST injure, because - and this will injure the body as to any condition where there would be the action of fetus for the system, that should never occur - for there will be a strain in the blood!

11. (Q)  Why can't and haven't I had a normal "orgasm" at the proper time?
(A)  This because of the conditions as have existed in the body, and the activities as have been indulged in by the body from time to time, in a promiscuous and in an unseemly manner.

12. (Q)  What can be done to be normal?
(A)  This has been given!  We are through!

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
