# TEXT OF READING 5731-1 F 59

**Reading [[5731-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/17/1944  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 14191

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5731], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 and 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This [address] we have had before.

3. As we find, there are disturbances quite different.  These in the present have to do with a subluxation that exists in the lumbar and sacral area, combined with the unbalanced condition in the chemical reaction, partially from this suppression in the brush end of the nerve forces and partially irregularity in the diet.

4. Thus unless there are measures taken for these corrections, in both the subluxations and elements in the assimilation through the system, we will have the development of a progressive disability for the body.

5. Then, in making the corrections, first we should have a series of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments with special reference to the lumbar-sacral and the sacro-ileac plexus; coordinating these with reflexes from 9th dorsal, brachial center, and the 3rd cervical.  When about six to eight of these have been taken, leave them off for a while.

6. Begin then with the use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that will carry, for this body, both [[Camphor|Camphor]] and Gold vibratorially into the body, through the activity of the assimilation as stimulated by these properties carried vibrationally into the circulation.

7. The Appliance would be used for thirty minutes each day, one day using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the next day using the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution.  The Gold would be in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water, and use three ounces.  Use three ounces of commercial strength [[Camphor|Camphor]].  Change the solutions every thirty days.

8. Each day the large nickel plate, through which the Solution passes vibratorially, would be attached to the lacteal duct and umbilical plexus, or for this body the width of the forefinger and the second finger from the navel center to the right and three fingers up from that point.

9. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the small copper plate would be attached at the 9th dorsal area.  When using the Camphor, it would be attached to the lumbar axis, or 4th lumbar.

10. The charging solution for the Appliance would be two pounds of the Copper Sulfate and the rest of the properties in the correct proportions.

11. Do keep the attachment plates very clean.  Do disconnect from the Appliance and take the connections out of the Solutions when not in use.  Do connect to the Appliance at least twenty minutes before the attachments are to be made to the body.

12. Each day when removing the Appliance give the body a thorough massage with an equal combination of Olive Oil (heated) and Tincture of Myrrh.  Massage from the toes toward the body, or to the 9th dorsal.  From the 1st cervical massage downward to the 9th dorsal.  This should be in a rotary motion along the cerebro-spinal system and along each side of the spinal column to the areas indicated.  Do this thoroughly, not just something to be hurried through with.  Follow along the line of the sciatic centers or nerves, and through the lumbar axis.

13. In the diet add considerable more raw vegetables.  Eliminate meats, such as hog meat or beef.  Fish, fowl and lamb may be taken, but not fried.

14. Do include a great deal of gelatin at times in preparing the vegetables that are raw, with these grated or cut or ground, or at times drink the juices of these mixed with gelatin.

15. The cooked vegetables should be more of the leafy nature than the bulbous; such as pie plant, artichoke should be included in the diet; but mostly have leafy vegetables.  Prepare these in their own juices; even when the pie plant is prepared; not too much sugar but just sufficient to make it palatable for the body.

16. Do these and we may prevent the growing of those disturbances through the lower limbs and later through the upper.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Is an operation advisable?
(A)  No.

19. (Q)  What causes my legs to have a crawley feeling and my arms to itch from my wrist to my shoulders?
(A)  Just as indicated through the centers affected by the subluxations, as well as the unbalancing in the chemical forces of the body.  Do as outlined, we may prevent multiple sclerosis.

20. (Q)  Would I be better if I stayed at home?
(A)  It will be necessary to stay at home for at least the periods indicated:  two or three months.  After the body has been using the Appliance for two months, have another series of the adjustments.

21. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] and article explaining theory of [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].)
