# TEXT OF READING 147-33 F 53

**Reading [[147-33]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/13/1927  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 772

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 13th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here and those conditions as surround the body throughout the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] at the present.  With the physical condition there is seen much change from that as we have had here before.  Much that is seen and felt by the body is the reaction which has set in from the non-application of the Radio-Active Appliance forces and of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] in its activity to the blood supply and to the whole eliminating system.  Hence, as we find in portions of the mesenteric system, there are those disturbances that are apparently causing distress to the body.  These we would remove by this manner:

2. We would use Usoline oil as a lubricant for the whole system, taking at least a tablespoonful of the oil each day.  Every OTHER day take high enemas to relieve the strain in the colon, even to the ascending and transverse and descending colon, see?  Cleanse the whole colon at least every other day.

3. Keep up the appliances - the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] and the Radio-Active Appliance.  These will bring better reactions and better conditions for this body.  Do that.

4. We are through for the present.

> 
> "My food seems to all stay right in my stomach so long in
> digesting.  There is, and has been for quite a while, a
> soreness in my left side, in my intestines just in front of
> my hip, and at times I pass such quantities of mucous.  I
> know you will find out these things without my telling you,
> but this has been so long I can't help feeling uneasy.  I
> said soreness; it isn't sore to the touch.  I can't describe
> the feeling but there is something there that shouldn't be.
> If I were so stupid in describing this to a doctor he would
> think me an idiot, probably justly so, too."
