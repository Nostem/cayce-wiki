# TEXT OF READING 1104-1 F 8

**Reading [[1104-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/14/1936  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 6342

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of January, 1936, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her sister, Mrs. [1073].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here (What a body!), [1104].

2. Now, as we find, the disturbing conditions in this body, from a normal physical standpoint, are of such natures that - if there is ANY change wrought - it will require time, patience and persistence on the part of those MAKING such applications.

3. For if these conditions had been changed or altered during those periods of the early developments in the structural portion of the body, there might have been a preventing of much of the reversal that has been brought about by the improper activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as related to the developments in the structural forces; in the coordination also of the mental AND the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

4. These conditions, as we find, from the material standpoint, came from the improper reactions or care FOR the body during those periods first of gestation - and then during those periods when there was the first giving to the body of its activities for the functioning of the organs as related to its assimilation.  And then the arising of temperatures made for the adhering of lymph tissue, owing to elemental conditions in those portions of the body - detrimental influences - during those periods of its first developments.

5. Hence we find that the functionings of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]], and those relationships of the pineal to the gestations in the sexual forces in the system, have become all engorged - or are abnormal in the body; rather than becoming gradually centered to those activities through the base portion - or through the medulla oblongata - to the cerebrospinal system where the sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems coordinate with their activity to the cerebellum and the central portion of the frontal portion of the brain forces themselves.

6. Hence all of these conditions, all of these structural forces, must be taken into consideration - if there would be offered those things that would be of help for the body.

7. And, as we find, these would necessitate - in about every six to eight months - that a CHANGE be made in the form or the MANNER of the application - of those electrical forces; or the ELEMENTAL forces, or the elementary forces, that supply to the system itself the necessary impulses and influences for creating constructive influence for a structural body, as well as for a coordination between the nervous systems of the body and their activities.

8. Begin, then, first, with this:

9. Use the Radio-Active Appliance each day for at least forty-five to sixty minutes.  While there will be little or no effect felt, there will gradually - from such applications - come a change in the activities of the system.

10. Make the cycle of the body in these manners:  Once to the right wrist and left ankle.  The next day to the left wrist and right ankle.  Then the next day begin over again, but make the attachment first to the ankle, THEN to the wrist. Then the next day the first attachment would be to the opposite ankle and the other to the opposite wrist.  Then the next day when it's attached, go to the right wrist and left ankle; then the left wrist and right ankle.  Then make the first attachment to the ankle again, etc.

11. Keep the anodes or small plates clean.  Make positive contact.

12. And after each of the treatments give the body a GENERAL massage of the OILS, with these combinations:

13. Equal parts of Olive Oil, [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], and (cut same with) Petroleum Oil or Coal Oil, see?  These, as we find, would not be rubbed hard, but along the spine and across the structural portions of the body; as along the spine across the ribs, across the pelvic bone, across the sternum or the bone just below the ribs, you see, and all portions; so that there may be just the absorption of these.  Massage these each time for five to ten minutes.

14. And then in six months, we would give those changes necessary.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [1073] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file (GD's note to mother:  See letter [which was enclosed] to Health Home Remedies Co., also article explaining theory of appliance.)
