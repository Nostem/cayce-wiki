# TEXT OF READING 195-23 M 42 (Realtor, Mfr. of Automotive

**Reading [[195-23]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/8/1926  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 736

Parts) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 8th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [195], who, on Friday morning, 3/5/26, was in Room C-10, Commodore Hotel, [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had as follows:  [GC read [[Dreams|dream]] recorded in 195-23, Par. B2.] You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [195], with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as we have had here.  This body and the enquiring mind we have had before.

3. The dreams here presented are as those conditions which are weighed with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in such a manner that the physical-mental mind may gain lessons through these experiences of the conditions that are presented in an emblematical way, and may be applied in the physical sense as to bring the better conditions for the body.

4. This, as we see, as is presented, relates to those specific conditions in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is building or applying those conditions which would prove of the beneficial influence to individuals.  And as these are builded, then, as it is seen, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should look well into the portions of such an instrument that is under the supervision of the entity, that there be no hindrances to the conditions through which the entity is attempting to be of aid, see? and in the building of same, give that which will give the more certain assistance and aid to those individuals, whether friend or foe.  And, as is seen, in this, in doing this, in this precaution that is taken, there comes no crash, no troubles, no interference, and through same the entity itself is able to outdistance them all, in every way and manner.  Then use same.  Apply same in that way as is presented.  [See [1800] series of rdgs. on the Radio-Active Appliance.]

5. We are through.
