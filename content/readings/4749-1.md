# TEXT OF READING 4749-1 F CHILD

**Reading [[4749-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/15/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12721

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of June, 1923.  Contact: [[John (Apostle)|John]] Kunz'' family (?).

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

In the home of her father, 
Time of Reading                on lot #6, ... farm, one mile 
8:30 P. M.                     east on ... Pike, R.R. #2 of 
Middletown, [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find in this body there are some abnormal conditions for this body, and while the body is in the developing stage, there must and may be things done for the body that will bring the development to the normal forces in this body, as we find these through the developing forces in the system, and they have to do with specific conditions existing from prenatal, others produced in the system or in the body at birth, and the effect as these have produced in the body itself, see.  There is not far distance between the spiritual and [[Soul|soul]] forces in the physical developing in this body, [4749] we are speaking of, as known here.

2. In the blood forces in the body we find the developing of the physical has been hindered in part by the condition as produces the supply in its digestive forces, and it produces an irritation to those parts already over-stressed or strained for their developing portion of the system, as we find for the condition as exists in the facial portion itself and in the developing of the forces to give the incentive for the elimination of abnormal forces produced by the condition existing in the body and that effect as produced the strain first.

3. As for the condition in nerve forces, they are only in their beginning of functioning forces and may be stimulated to give the incentives to assist in physical development.

4. In the functioning of the organs as we have in the sensory forces and the organs as affected by prenatal and of exterior forces, these show the reaction may develop in the body to bring the normal forces to the body in their normal way and manner, see.

5. Add to those forces and give the application of that [[Vibration|vibration]] that will give the body the incentive to overcome the condition in the system.  There will be some trouble expected with the developing of this body in some weeks and some months to come.  These will be well to be remembered and keep these in mind, for there will be a developing through the intestinal tract of a congestion, if not post-minded to this condition, that will prove serious to this body.  If the conditions arise in the body, use this as they appear.  This does not show at present but it may appear.  If so, use equal parts of Tincture of Myrrh and Balm of Gilead - rub across the groin and abdomen of this body.  This will come in some weeks unless the diet is changed in the body.  If the diet is changed to a more acceptable need, the body may escape this.

6. (Q)  Is there any other treatment for this body?
(A)  We haven't given the treatment as yet for the condition as existing.  We find those presenting themselves, but they will not come if the treatment is followed out, but they are in a way to present themselves at present.

For the application to the parts of the body here in the facial portions and eye as affected, we would use with the Boracic Acid Wash.  After the bathing off of parts of the secretions as shown, use equal parts of Boracic Acid with Balsam of Peru as a powdered form.  Also use this with the acid as a wash for the throat internally now, see, with a cloth or a small mop through the tonsil region and epiglottis and the throat proper.  In the throat use the was with the Boracic Acid Wash, that of Tincture of Ermoline [[[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]?  Listerine?].  Be well to use one to three minims each day as an intestinal disinfectant to prevent this acid state of presenting itself.  Use one to five minims [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], see.  Do that.  Stimulate with the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine from the third cervical to the end of the spine, that the circulation may have its full flow and affect, see, just the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] after bath and before it rests, see.

7. (Q)  What was the original cause of the condition in eye?
(A)  We have given it.

8. (Q)  Will the eye grow to be the same as the other eye and be absolutely normal?
(A)  If the directions as given for the development of the body are followed, it will grow to be normal, not as the other, but normal for the condition in the body see, because the eyes are going to be different colors.

9. (Q)  Is the sight destroyed in this left eye?
(A)  No.

10. (Q)  What will cure this condition in the eye?
(A)  Follow the directions as we have given.  The wash and the [[Healing|healing]] forces to exposed parts, see, and the stimulation to the centers to assist the circulation to develop the body, normally see.

11. (Q)  Is this left eye affecting the other eye?
(A)  No, and will not if these are followed.

12. (Q)  What is the condition of the throat and what can be done to relieve or cure the trouble?
(A)  Some inflammation, some over-secretions.  Do as we have given for the condition here.  Use this as the wash, see, and give these properties as we have given, see, or we will not have the rise of the condition, rather of the rise of the acid state of the condition in the intestinal tract of the condition her in the throat.  Do not over-feed the body but give plenty.

13. (Q)  Is there any treatment to be given to this body to keep the eyes from being different colors?
(A)  We will be glad to keep them this way.  With these the pigment will be changed some in this way:  The developing of the forces in the body have reached such a state, you see, that this change or difference in the color will be from the sub-normal effect as in the system at present, see.  With the gradual development and with the application of these applications to assist in alleviating secretions thrown out and to produce assimilation and elimination of the developing forces, the body will gradually grow to its normal condition, see.  Will be of a gradual condition, see, provided this is not allowed to develop in the intestinal tract, see.  Ward against that, but when it comes, use this as we have given across the abdomen and the groin, see.

14. (Q)  How long will it take for this body to be absolutely normal?
(A)  For it to grow normal, be eighteen to twenty months. For it to be perfectly normal, it will be two to three years.

15. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, will this body live to be very old?
(A)  Meet the conditions as they arise, as we have given.  We have given as to the conditions that might be met.  Don't seek too far to the future.  Do well to take care of the present, see.  Don't ask those things you shouldn't know.

16. (Q)  What should be the diet of this body?
(A)  Consider its age and use common sense.

17. (Q)  What is wrong with the diet of this body at present?
(A)  Not anything.

18. (Q)  Have you anything further to say regarding the conditions of this body?
(A)  Conditions to be wary of to arise in three weeks.  That isn't at present.  Do as we have given.  We are through.
