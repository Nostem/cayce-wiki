# TEXT OF READING 161-2 M 11

**Reading [[161-2]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/23/1930  Sex: M  Age: 11  ReadingID: 632

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and others.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, [161] - we have the body here - this we have had before.  The conditions, as we find, show that there are changes being effected in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body. WE would be sure that the drainages, ESPECIALLY as related to the draining from head, throat and torso proper, are thrown out through the ALIMENTARY canal, in the present, and that the drosses from alimentary canal are raised to an excess; so that there is not the accumulation of too heavy activity in the blood itself.

2. The rest, as we find, are VERY good.  These would only be stimulated.

3. (Q)  What would you advise for cough?
(A)  The reduction of that pressure as exists, especially in the 4th DORSAL.  This by manipulation.  Then we may use that of the expectorant as comes from the use of wild cherry, or cherry pectoral; that a combination of essence of cherry with a very SMALL amount of that as will act as an antidote for the soft tissue bronchia; for it is the tickling sensation there that produces same.  Hence the reduction in the dorsal as given, or the pressure there - and the use of same as an expectorant, see?

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this series of readings.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Parents
