# TEXT OF READING 2262-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2262-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1911 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/28/1911  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8991

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 28th day of January, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno.]

Western State Hospital, 
Time of Reading                      Right Wing, 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    Staunton, Va.

1. LBC:  You are now asleep and will be able to tell us what we want to know.  You have before you Miss [2262], Stanton [Staunton?], Va., Western State Hospital, Right Wing:  go over her carefully, examine her thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with her, if anything.  What do you find.

2. EC:  Yes, we have her here.  We have two troubles.  The beginning, or the real trouble, is from abnormal conditions of the system.  Then we have here a second infection from the troubles existing in the trunk, or the body.  We first had here troubles with the digestive organs of the system, carried into the system; trouble with the intestinal tract and pelvis.  This is the beginning of the trouble we have in this body here; through the nerve force and supply to the pelvis, from the organism of the kidneys, not throwing out of the system the poisonous matter, to be eliminated from the system through this organism, thus the whole system has become infected until we have the hepatic circulation in the body at a minimum, low.  This, carried into the system, has produced a choking up of the opposite poles to the kidneys and the liver, which throws out the excretions and secretions from the system, - the excretion from the blood force and nerve force and all matter carried into it there, to be thrown out as waste material from both ends used up by the nerve force.

3. This produced a second infection in the system; choking up the Hepatic circulation.  Then we have a forced circulation through the blood forces, until we have an overpuffed body, not throwing out from it, all the forces being used up. The blood is carrying back and forth to the system here, by forced circulation.  The blood is carried to the lungs and carried back through the hepatic circulation, and through the lower part of the heart, until we have a system all lax, or low temperature, through this part of the body.

4. The body is becoming cold in the extremities you see none of the circulation being carried through the forces, through the sympathetics, or through the capillary circulation, by that part of the system.

5. If we want to force the body to improve, put electrical force to stop these forces here, stimulating the nerve forces, overstimulation to it, you see, from normal.  Overstimulate them to their normal condition by electrical forces, which carried into the blood forces may give it you see, some stimulation to the heart action.  Give nitroglycerine and strychnine.  They force the blood out from the heart, heart action, into the circulation and into the body.  Want the electroids [electrolytes?] for that you see given into the body so that we electrify the nerve forces, along with the properties of the blood force.

6. Now, we have a nerve and blood force in the body, almost interlapping with each other.  Electrify this nerve force, with the blood supply to the system, then we carry the force to the system.  We increase thus, the hepatic circulation here in the trunk part of the body.

7. To eliminate these, we have to build the body from this. Have to treat what we have here to save life.  Give more heat into the body.  Have hot applications along the spine. We cannot keep electricity on it all the time.  Neither can you keep this all the time, but keep warm pads along here at the solar plexus, and also the helia [ilia?].  Electrify these to carry it through until we get medicinal properties into the system, to assist nature in itself.

8. (Q)  Any other treatment you would prescribe?
(A)  Do this right now, and she will get better.  Then you will have something more to do.  But do this now.

9. (Q)  What is the condition of her mind?
(A)  Not much there.  Dormant there you see.  We have a brain action from the cerebro-spinal nerve force, carried from the system.  As we keep the forced circulation, we keep the mind.  The mind is alright in itself, but it is not acting, because the sympathetics in the system carry the force in the body.  That is what is keeping it there. The strength of the mind keeps the force of the sympathetic system in action.  The mind is dormant, you see.
