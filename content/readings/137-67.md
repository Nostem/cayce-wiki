# TEXT OF READING 137-67 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-67]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/22/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 457

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                     [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the enquiring mind of this body, with the [[Dreams|dreams]] had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] as come we see are in that manner as have been presented, and as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in the physical mind, adjusts the mental proclivities of the body mind to that attunement, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] gives more and more those experiences in direct or emblematical manner, that may be used in physical conditions for the betterment and the better understanding of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s mental forces.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Saturday night, March 20, or Sunday morning, March 21, 1926.  I was on sand pile, and the pile seemed to give away. The sand seemed to be running down.  I was afraid there would be a cave-in or collapse, but there was not.  It seemed to refer to Hudson.
(A)  In this there is presented, in this emblematical manner, that condition as is seen weighed with the mental forces, and in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] manner given this emblematical condition that shows [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as to the conditions regarding Hudson Motors.  That whereas there has been those conditions in which seemingly this price would break entirely, for the constant downward movement in same brings that as of the whole thing would go to smash, but, as has been given, much of this must now be bought to deliver, and there will be seen in Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday's trading, in this week, the advance to at least 118, see?  As again the same condition is presented, in that of the snow, in seeing one apparently fall into same and disappear, yet none are missed, or none falls in; this showing how the entity, in its mental forces, has given to self's impression that it will break entirely - white, see? - snow, see? - giving away, yet does not happen.  Again the same presentation of the conditions to be seen in those rises as will occur.  Ready for questions. (After pause)  We are through.
