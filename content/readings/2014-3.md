# TEXT OF READING 2014-3 M 19

**Reading [[2014-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/10/1940  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 8539

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. [[Milton Harrison|Milton]] W. Harrison, 56 Highland Circle, Bronxville, N.Y., this 10th day of April, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2014]''s mother, Wm. D. Taylor, Edith and Leslie Savage.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.J.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them, regarding his condition:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2014]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are decided improvements in some respects. While there are a great many reactions, still, that are not satisfactory, we find the ability of coordinating activities - the voluntary as well as involuntary - shows much greater improvement than we have had before.

4. There are improvements also in the coordination of the attempts of the body to function in a coordinant manner; the impulses between the sympathetic and the central or cerebrospinal show some improvement.

5. To be sure, much is yet to be desired respecting the involuntary activities of some of the functionings, especially in the sympathetics as related to the sensory system; or as to the face, the mouth, the salivary [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] reaction, to the conditions in the assimilating system.

6. Then, as we find, these are the needed conditions, the principles, in the present.

7. There has not been as yet a full removal of the adhesions in the right portion of the abdominal area, or about the lacteals and the umbilical plexus; and extending to the activity in the caecum, with the drainages through the colon area.

8. Those same applications indicated, then, need to be kept; and then the massaging and those activities to aid same.  We will find that the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] here will aid in breaking up the lesions and also enable the nerve forces through the system to coordinate better; and then gradually produce a better building of coordination between the sympathetics, the vegetative nerve system and the cerebrospinal system, especially in the areas from the coccyx to the 9th dorsal. These areas need some stimulations.

9. We would keep, then, those activities; and these - with the general precautions - should bring the continued improvements.

10. To be sure, these may be slow, but they are sure.

11. Then, keep the mental attitudes about the body of a helpful, creative influence; the trusting within the ability of [[Creative Forces|creative energies]] to function through the body itself.  This is necessary.

12. Do that.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What is the reason for, and what can be done for the habit reaction he has; such as the spitting, drawing of the mouth down, and waving of the fingers before his nose and mouth?
(A)  These, as indicated, are reflexes through the sensory nerve system; lack of coordination between impulses and the guided or directed forces in the mental reactions of same. Keep up the applications indicated for corrections, making the suggestions - and not attempting to control by violent means!

15. (Q)  What type of suggestion would you recommend?
(A)  As just indicated, the type that is to be given continually; of the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] or God, - love manifesting through the activities of the body.  These as helpful forces will bring the bettered conditions for this body.

16. (Q)  Will the White Light be helpful?
(A)  The White Light will be helpful more when there are better coordinations between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal, or when the lesions and adhesions are broken up.  For as the functioning of the White Light is to be with the impulses through the nerve and muscular forces, these should be guided or directed through their proper channels when these administrations are given - for the better results.

17. (Q)  While eating he seems to go in sort of an absent-minded state.  What can be done for this?
(A)  This again is the lack of coordination between the impulses, and the activities of the functionings of the system.  This is a natural condition in the general animal kingdom, as would be seen by the study of same; and the variation between man and such is to CONTROL impulses rather than allowing the impulses to control the activities of the nervous system!

These, then, will be stimulated by making coordinations between the 9th dorsal plexus, you see, and the 2nd and 3rd dorsal center; that is, these as pressures, you see, first one and then the other; for these will change the impulses arising from the nervous system to the muscular reaction of the digestive forces, as well as the assimilating influences through the body.

18. (Q)  How often should the treatments be given?
(A)  About three times a week.

19. (Q)  How often should the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs be given?
(A)  These should be given at intervals of at least three days, until there is no indication of any adhesion through this area.  This can be determined by the pressures upon the area over the lacteal duct and umbilical center where they join just below the gall duct emptying into the system, see? and that upon the nerve center just below to the LEFT side of the end of the spine.

Give these Packs three days in succession, you see; then rest three to five days; then give again for three days in succession; and so on.

20. (Q)  Can anything be done at present to improve the leg, or walking?
(A)  This as we find must be the result of the general improvement of the whole system.  This will be GREATLY improved as applications in general are made for stimulating the circulation.

Do not lose patience with self nor with others.  Keep the attitude of helpfulness.  Know that conditions that exist, while from the purely material may be trying, they are that needed for thine own - and for [2014]'s - [[Soul|soul]] development!

Be patient, for in patience ye possess thine own [[Soul|soul]]!

21. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Taylor) Copy to Ass'n file
