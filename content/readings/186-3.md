# TEXT OF READING 186-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[186-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/2/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 696

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by her brother-in-law, Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House and Mr. [195].]

Time of Reading 
7:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Calif. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

2. Now, we find there are many changes in the body since last we had same here.  While the [[Vibration|vibrations]] have not been kept as often as they should, these, were they kept in this manner as has been outlined, would bring the greater change in the physical functioning of this body.  We would keep these up, then, in a more systematic, more persistent, manner, for the changes for the better in the physical functioning, and in the action and reaction through the mental forces of the body, will be for the betterment of the physical and the mental forces of this body.  Then, do that.

3. As for those conditions as are arising from that of the change in the attitude towards conditions, these need be directed by those who keep in close accord with the best conditions, physically and mentally, of this body.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  How often should the Radio-Active Applicator be used?
(A)  At least one hour every day.

5. (Q)  Should mental suggestions be kept up?
(A)  These should be kept up.  The subjugation of [[Physical Body|the body physical]] should be carried out more thoroughly.  The mental suggestions may be given by one while the body sleeps, would they begin - yet it would take longer, or require longer to accede to this, see?

6. (Q)  How many Radio-Active treatments should be given to bring back the body to normal?
(A)  At least two hundred.

7. That is all the questions on this.

8. We are through with this reading for the present.
