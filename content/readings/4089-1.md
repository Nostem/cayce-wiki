# TEXT OF READING 4089-1 M 2 1/2

**Reading [[4089-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/12/1926  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 11785

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of April, 1926, in accordance with phone request made by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], via Mr. [4171] and Mrs. [4177].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there has been an accident to the physical structure of the body, and it is affecting the body in specific manners.  The injury is to the spine in two instances, or places, and the reaction is to produce spasmodic conditions in the body - at the 1st and 2nd cervical and in the coccyx.  Hence the retraction as is shown by the reaction to the end of the spinal column nerves proper, and the drawing to the body.

3. We would, to correct same, have immediate [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments for those conditions along the spine, applying the light rays to the body to quiet the nerves and overcome narcotics in the system.  Do that immediately, and we will find the body will respond to these conditions, [if] were the corrections made [will be] in the proper manner, see?

4. We are through for the present.
