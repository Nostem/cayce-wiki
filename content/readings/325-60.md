# TEXT OF READING 325-60 F 63

**Reading [[325-60]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/9/1935  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 2498

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of August, 1935, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, conditions are about at a standstill. They are not improving greatly.  But that they are not making greater inroads is an indication for betterments.

2. As we find, it would be well that the body be quiet, and in the open as much as possible - or where there may be outside activity.

3. Well that a shield be worn for the protection of those portions where the injury is indicated.

4. Use the Plantain salve consistently.  This is not consistently used in the present.

5. It is NECESSARY, as indicated, that the eliminations and the drainages be set up from the cervical and upper dorsal areas. As we find, the manipulations osteopathically are preferable in this particular or specific condition to the [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] treatments.  We find that there would be more sympathy from the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] with the electrical treatments that have been indicated in the X-Ray or the flashes; and such electrical treatments should be conducted or be maintained by the one giving the manipulative forces, or one in accord with same, so that these are made to accord one with another.

6. DO NOT use the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] SO STRONG if the X-Ray treatments are given.  The X-Ray treatments would not be given more than once in two weeks or the like, you see.  So, three or four days after the X-Ray treatment begin with the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] hand machine (rather than the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]]).  To be sure, this is objectionable to the body, but if the bulb is placed on the body and THEN the current turned on - GENTLY - this will NOT be objectionable.  It is necessary to start eliminations, and to keep them maintained to overcome this tendency for the accumulations in the stomach of the forces that should be carried from the system - and that cause the inability for the food values to be taken.

7. Take the red wine AS a meal, with black or heavy bread.

8. The lighter wines or champagne should be SIPPED, as to make for a settling of the stomach and to strengthen the body.

9. If the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] is prepared properly, without any of the fat, and SIPPED - NOT taken in spoonsful - it will strengthen and WILL digest!

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should she go to Hay Sanitarium at Briar Cliff, N.Y., or the Sanatorium at East Aurora, N.Y., organized by Dr. Hay, or the Catholic Sanatorium at Denville, N.J.?
(A)  We do not find that a sanitarium would be the better place for the body; from the mind's activity and from many of the surroundings and conditions.  It should be where it may be in the open more, and where it may have someone of its own family to look after it.

13. (Q)  Should Dr. Dobbins, Dr. Rein [D.C.] (Ryan), or Dr. Stenberg [D.C.] of Richmond Hill, L. I., be used for the treatments?  [Gottfrid Stenberg, D.C.]
(A)  One of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] school is the better, who will cooperate with the electrical treatments as indicated. Dobbins.  [Frank P. Dobbins, D.O.]

14. (Q)  Who would be best doctor for the X-Ray flashes?
(A)  As indicated, one in cooperation with the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

15. (Q)  If X-Ray flashes are given, what should be done about the Ash, and the present lamp that she uses daily?
(A)  We have indicated that these would not be given at the same time; but three to four days after the X-Ray treatments use the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] hand machine, NOT the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, following the small doses of Ash taken.

16. (Q)  What effect will the X-Ray flashes have on the system?
(A)  Stimulate those portions to activity and DESTROY the activities that make for inroads in the condition here.

17. (Q)  Any danger of burning the flesh and causing other disorders?
(A)  Always danger with X-Ray!  This, then, should be given ONLY under the supervision of someone TRAINED in same.  Hence, as indicated, such an one should be in sympathy with the activities of the one giving the osteopathic manipulations.

18. (Q)  What will stop the pain when it arises in the right breast?
(A)  Only a sedative.

19. (Q)  Is the wound so deep that its inroads cannot be stopped?
(A)  It would be well for those who have considered this to begin from that we gave through those sources concerning this condition some fifteen to sixteen years ago, and read ALL of that given since!  that it is a growth, gradual.

20. (Q)  What will stop the further inroads?
(A)  Only keeping the eliminations bettered.  These are reaching serious conditions, to be sure.

21. (Q)  How much wine and how often?
(A)  A small wine glass full once a day is a meal, with the bread as indicated.  Sips of the others; the heavy red wine or the [[Last Supper|communion]] wine - of its own faith.  [Hebrew - Kosher wine?]

22. (Q)  Would the juice of vegetables be good for her as prepared by Foltus restaurants, or better the whole vegetables?
(A)  Better the whole vegetables, for those juices are not always prepared the best way.

23. (Q)  What can be done when she feels like jumping out of her skin because she can't let loose of her nerves?
(A)  Only the activities that will make for the QUIETING of the nerve centers, as we have indicated may be had best by the OSTEOPATHIC treatments gently given to the centers.

24. (Q)  Where should the drainages be set up in the body?
(A)  We have given that the drainages should be set up from the cervical and upper dorsals, that the eliminations from the whole of the system may be carried throughout the body.

25. (Q)  What would be best to use to prevent the chafing and irritation of the wound itself, when she wears clothes?
(A)  As indicated, use a PROTECTOR; as they used to have shields, see?

26. (Q)  Is the salve she uses properly mixed?
(A)  We have just indicated that this should be made with the equal weight of the cream or butterfat AND plantain; the plantain bruised or ground before, you see.  Boil together sufficiently long as to make a FUSION; not just COME to a boil!

27. (Q)  Should any of her teeth be removed?
(A)  Why cause more and MORE destructive forces or irritations when the body is ALREADY disturbed so much!  This should ONLY be in extreme conditions!  Just take care of these.

28. (Q)  Which teeth need attention?
(A)  Look and see!

29. Do these things that we have indicated for the better condition of this body in the present.

30. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Mr. [257] (one for Dr. Frank Dobbins) - Special Delivery Copy to Ass'n file
