# TEXT OF READING 715-2 F ADULT (Protestant)

**Reading [[715-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/20/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4534

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of December, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [715], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, there are improvements in the general physical condition of this body.  While there are still those conditions that disturb the body, and there must be those precautions taken else there will be the arising of that which would become malignant in those conditions in the pelvic organs and which would become a greater and greater strain upon the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body - if these drainages will be kept set up in the system, and the activities followed that have been and may be indicated here, we find the conditions taken in the present may be eliminated.

3. As is indicated in the general outline that has been given, as much precaution should be taken respecting keeping up the vitality and the resistance as respecting anything else; for there is the necessity of revivifying the depleted blood circulation and building more rich red blood, that there may not be the activity of infectious forces through the respiratory or pulmonary system.

4. Hence precautions must be taken as to keeping the body WARM, the feet dry at all times; and then the changes that we would outline in the present:

5. We would continue with the warm [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche, at least three times each week; a tablespoonful of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in two quarts of water would not be too severe.  This, as we find, will aid in preventing infection from discharges in congested area in the pelvic system.

6. We would have the body rubs, especially over the cerebrospinal system as well as across the diaphragm and the throat and across the lower PELVIS area.  Such a massage would be given each evening (preferably after using the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] over the whole of the same area indicated for ten to fifteen minutes), rubbing into the body what it will absorb of a compound prepared in this manner - into the cerebrospinal system, the throat and neck, across the diaphragm, across the lower portion of the abdomen:

7. To 4 ounces Russian White Oil, or Usoline, or Nujol, add - in the order named:

Oil of Wintergreen...............1 ounce,
Tincture of Benzoin............1/2 ounce,
Oil of Cedar Wood................1 ounce,
Kerosene or Coal Oil.............1 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]...............1/2 dram.

These will tend to separate when left standing.  Before ready to use, shake well together; pour only a small quantity in an open container to massage into the body as indicated.

8. Be mindful now that the body-building diet is adhered to, in this manner:

9. Mornings - citrus fruit juices or fruit juices; as pineapple juice, grapefruit, orange, lemon (orange and lemon are better together than separate), [[Grape Juice|grape juice]], or the like.  All of these, or any one of these, may be taken at the meal; with whole wheat toast, coffee or milk or Ovaltine.  A little crisp breakfast bacon occasionally.  Coddled egg; or these may be taken at other meals.

10. Say at nine-thirty to ten o'clock in the morning, between the breakfast and lunch meals, we would have a malted milk with egg - ONLY the YOLK of the egg in same; this well beaten together, and use three to five drops of LEMON - not lemon extract, but LEMON juice.  Beat together.  It may be used with ice cream or otherwise.  If preferable, the other juices may be used, but do not use synthetic flavoring for same.

11. Noons - meat broths or vegetable broths, or a whole green fresh raw vegetable salad may be taken - these may be varied.

12. In the middle of the afternoon we would again have the CHOCOLATE malted milk, or Coco-Malt, or such natures.  In THIS we would use rather a teaspoonful of spirits frumenti, or wine, or of such a nature; not MORE than a teaspoonful, but with the whole YOLK ONLY of the egg.

13. Evenings - well-balanced, well-cooked vegetable diet, with meats that are only of the wild game, fowl, fish, lamb or mutton; not beef, not hog meat.

14. Keep up the eliminations, even if it is necessary to use high enemas or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], to keep the drosses carried from the system.

15. These, as we find, would bring for the body the better conditions.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Is entire treatment being given properly?
(A)  As indicated, we are changing the conditions for the body in the present.  Take these that we have indicated here, in the present.

Keep the body WARM, and the feet dry.  This doesn't mean not to take a foot bath, but don't get the feet damp or wet in the weather!

18. (Q)  Am I overtaxing my body?
(A)  If these activities are carried on with the treatments that have been indicated, it will not be an overtaxation - as we find.

19. (Q)  What is causing loss of weight?
(A)  Those applications that have been given to reduce the activities and tendencies of elements in the system to set themselves up as resistances to BETTER [[Physical Body|physical forces]] - or CONSTRUCTIVE [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the body.  Now we will build consistently!

20. (Q)  What causes constant discharge?
(A)  As indicated, this is from congestion there - and is what is being warned against by using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche, as indicated, three times each week.

21. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these at this time.

Keep the [[Mental Body|mental body]] in a CONSTRUCTIVE manner.  Let the spiritual attitude be that there is a PURPOSEFULNESS in all of the physical and mental activities, and those [[Purpose|purposes]] not for self-aggrandizement or glorification, nor for preservation of body alone - but rather to the glory of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] that are each individual's heritage through [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-power in the earth.

22. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
