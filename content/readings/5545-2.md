# TEXT OF READING 5545-2 M 41

**Reading [[5545-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/24/1930  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 13819

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker, nurse, Mrs. Dave Levy, [5545] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Much bettered are the conditions throughout with this body.  We would use a little more of the Petrolagar in the system, and of the pure [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil with same - teaspoonful of one with a teaspoonful of the other at least once each day, or - if found necessary to keep down the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] - may be given oftener; for the STOOL now should be examined for those conditions as are being eliminated or thrown out from stomach proper, where the disorders or disposition of the refuse, or offal, show the activities as are being brought about.

2. Keep the manipulations, FOR the activities of both upper and lower portion of stomach, ACTIVE - by the manipulation that is STIMULATING.  Do not allow the cardiac end to become lax, nor to so stimulate the pyloric end as to have the stomach EMPTYING TOO quick, or food remaining too long.  The DIET is very good, though the body grows tired at times.  This should be borne WELL, and know that the system - as in respect to this - is PRACTICALLY being REJUVENATED, almost made over! It can eat nails after a while!

3. In the activities as we see for the replenishing, in the system - through that of the STIMULI as taken - be mindful that these do not become so as to cause disorders WITH the rest of the system.  No slop, or those of soft drinks of ANY kind, should be taken - but those carrying the character - in the waters - as has been given.  That of the elm and of the saffron should have their active forces with the system at all times.  Cool, or a little more ice ABOUT the ELM water will make it better and more palatable, and will ASSIMILATE with system much better.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is cause of dizziness?
(A)  When the stomach does not empty properly, the regurgitations as come from same produce that pressure in the upper portion that causes - it's more giddiness, or dizziness, or what would be termed as same, for it's re-[[Vibration|VIBRATING]] in its nature - or more as a SIMMER, as would be seen from heat rising.  That is the effect, see?  It's the revibration to the nerve system.  Hence that stimulation as was given for BOTH the upper end of stomach, also to the lower.  This has been told about.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. Keep up what we have given.  Be a little patient, but know that there is being brought about those conditions that will correct the disturbances IN this body, and that the body's strength - the body-physical and the body-mental - IS gaining.  Set before self, mentally, that the body would attain.  Make it HIGH, and keep the mental LIFTED in that direction; for to heal the physical alone, and to have the mental still distorted - would only be the return of the conditions when ACTIVITIES would be renewed physically.  But make the body physically fit, that the body-mental may act through same - and MAKE the efforts to bring about that as IS DESIRED, in a mental AND [[Physical Body|physical body]] - but make it high! Don't be satisfied with less!  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send to wife
