# TEXT OF READING 5648-4 M 55 (Accountant)

**Reading [[5648-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/29/1930  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 14044

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 29th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5648].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                              ..., Manuelita, 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.          ..., S.A.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [5648] of the office of ..., [[South America|South America]].  You will give this body advice regarding his health, his mental and material, and all phases of his life, and suggestions that would be helpful in all of these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [5648] - this we have had before.

3. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these, as we find, have altered much since last we had same.  Much has been for the betterment.  Some we find not so well.  Will the body keep for the proper eliminations and drainages in the system, while not over building up of the fleshly portions of the system, the mental and the physical will be kept clarified, and in such an element and state that resuscitation and replenishing, rebuilding, may be nominal or normal condition for the body.  The mental is for the body, for ALL bodies, ever the builder.  These should be kept as clean and as clarified as there should be for the physical; for, as has oft been said, bodily cleanliness makes for bodily godliness, and this should be comprised always in the lives and the activities of every individual.

4. Well that in the surroundings and the conditions for this body, that that be kept in accord with the affairs, the activities, the leading forces in the life of the individual from day to day.

5. As to the material conditions of life, and those changes and alterations as will come into the life - these, as we find, will bring for such developments as the body mental, [[Physical Body|the body physical]], applies within the abilities - physical and mental - of the body.  Keep in the way that leads to the understanding of the relationships of self with the divine forces as are manifest in a material plane.  Keep the applying of self in material abilities, in physical abilities, in mental abilities, in accord with that with which the individual would be endowed for self.  So endow self's own activities into the lives of others, that that as would be brought to self will be brought to those with whom the body labors for, and labors with.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
