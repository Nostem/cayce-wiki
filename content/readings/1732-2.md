# TEXT OF READING 1732-2 F 37 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[1732-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/21/1930  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 7935

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1732].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1732] and her husband, Mr. [1734].]

Born August 2, 1892, Brooklyn, N.Y. 
Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present entity, [1732].

2. In entering, we find many changing in the experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to those relations as would be termed [[Astrology|astrological]]. In the INDWELLING influences innate and manifested in the present entity's personality and individuality, these become DIFFERENT in the application by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].  Not that these are of that nature except as BUILDED BY each entity.  In this relation, then, we find the greater influences through those [[Incarnation|sojourns]] BETWEEN the experiences in the earth plane in Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, and Uranus, and under the influence of Uranus has the greatest changes come in the experience of the entity in this earth's plane; so that, will the entity find when Jupiter and Uranus were in conjunction with their influences in the earth's plane, this the period when the changes came.

3. One, then, of high MENTAL ability, and intuitively knows MANY things that others question the body personally as concerning; FEELING much in the expressions of the mental self.  Capable, through the influences of the Venus forces, of showing, manifesting, a great love in the experience, and - as seen - these builded in other experiences also, and tried almost by fire.

4. In those influences, then, as seen from Jupiterian, these combined with Mercury, Venus AND Uranus, make for an INDIVIDUALITY and a PERSONALITY as distinct in the present experience of the entity.

5. One capable of understanding even [[Initiation|mysteries]] to others.  One that hateth, or shuns those who are not sincere in their actions and their activities.

6. One that, whether the conditions be of position of low estate or of high minded, will the SINCERITY OF the individual be manifest, consideration, thought, care, and even those of love will be manifest FROM the entity toward such.

7. One that in the care of the young ESPECIALLY gifted, as FEW, in understanding the mannerisms and the conditions as arise in the mind and activities of the young, and the entity would have been a GREAT instructress in this field.

8. In those conditions as make for the personality; one amiable and kind, and showing deference where deference is due.

9. One appreciative, then, of the arts and its RELATIONSHIPS to various phases of individual life.

10. One naturally, and by the development of same, the HOME builder.

11. One that, in the ABILITIES as developed INNATE, may find much in common with those who study mental application under varied subjects as kindred to the MENTAL abilities of young or old.

12. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and THEIR effect upon the present experience of the entity:

13. In the one before this we find in that period known as the [[Greece|Grecian]], when there were discussions and dissensions among the peoples of the philosophical research as attempted by the peoples of the classes during that period known as Socrates' rule, or Socrates' thought.  The entity among those that were of those kindred to those peoples, and one accepting same. Hence in the present is seen much of the reasoning abilities of the entity, and whatever may be presented to the mental forces of entity must have some reasonable basis for the undertaking, for the entity to consider same.  In the name Elois, and these of that name have and will OFTEN make the entity think when they hear the name called.  In the present experience, those studies of that period have been somewhat of a mystery and an understanding to the entity, and applied in the present experience may be made to mean much in the present development of the mental and [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] of the entity.

14. In the one before this we find during that period when the peoples of the Promise Land returned to that land from the [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]], and under the leadership of Ezekiel did the entity return to [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]], and among those of the household of Zerubbabel.  The entity gained through this experience; giving self much in service of those who labored in the restoring of [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] and in the aiding of those who gathered of the worldly goods to make same possible, both from the house and lands of Artaxerxes and also of the lands about [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]], being of the household of those that warred with Sanballat during this rebuilding.  In the name Corienl, and there were MANY that called the entity BLESSED during that experience.  In the present there is seen the sacredness of service in any, and the tendency of the entity to consider the service as to whether the SINCERITY of [[Purpose|purpose]] is manifest by such an one GIVING service; also that of the ability TO BE what may be slangly called "broad minded" as respecting the manner is which one serves.

15. The one before this in that land now known as [[Egypt|Egypt]].  The entity came into the land with the peoples of the hill country when the land was subdued, and the rule set up where DIVISIONS arose when [[Ra Ta|the priest]] was banished.  The entity then became the companion and aide to that deposed ruler, [1734] Raai, and the entity gained THROUGH this experience in the aiding of gaining the CONFIDENCE OF the peoples that were aliens AND natives in the study of establishing the relationships of man to man, nation to nation, individual to the [[Creative Forces|Creative forces]] and to the Maker; gaining in the helpfulness of the worship as was set up during that period, making for self, in the name Isai, that of one WORSHIPED long after by the peoples who established the rule there.  In the present, textiles and those of the various ornamental conditions as have to do with making for the comfort of home, the thought as expressed in individuals' thinking on the surroundings, and that innate feeling felt by the entity of that, as the body mind thinks, so becomes the outlook of the individual upon life and ITS RELATIONSHIPS!  WITH the thought of a body respecting individuals and those of the mental and spiritual forces.

16. In the abilities of the entity, then, lie those forces of the home building, of the training of those of the developing mind, and in teaching those that WONDER, to think.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  How best may the entity help her sister, financially and mentally?
(A)  Best, in aiding anyone, to assist a body to help themselves.  In JUST financial aid often comes misunderstanding, but to help one to help themselves UNDERSTAND themselves, their relationships, their responsibilities, their OWN abilities, their effort must be expended - then the GAIN may be even given, but that the entity builds for self is the best aid.

19. (Q)  How best can entity overcome sensitiveness or inferiority complex?
(A)  Inferiority complex does NOT exist in the entity.  The sensitiveness, as may be seen, is from that of the innate feeling of ever being overshadowed by one IN power, MENTALLY and FINANCIALLY, and in the MATERIAL conditions.  In overcoming, let self's own self be the expression of that felt, and in the ACTIONS of same; that is, in SELF there is seen all force as is manifest in the whole universe, will the entity awaken in self the relationship of self to the divine influences, and knowing that the activities of self are the reflections of that FELT IN self of the God, or [[Ideal|the ideal]], as worshiped or held before self as truth.  In THIS manner, in this way, may the entity overcome that felt as timidity.

20. (Q)  Will body be as happy in another apartment than the present one in [[New York City|New York]]?
(A)  This may be a question, and is a manifestation in a material way of that timidity FELT in self.  DO NOT consider same as inferior, but rather as HUMBLENESS - and, as has been said, "He that is humble before HIS God will be exalted on high".  In considering, then, the change in the home, the natural influence of the entity is in the home building, and to change is to create for self that varying condition that is made to cause FEAR to enter in.  WILL the entity set self to study self, in a manner as has been given, and in the change (should it change) study to make THAT more manifest, as happy and as content as in the present surroundings, may be had.

21. That is all the questions.

22. Much may be said as respecting the entity's abilities as to the APPLICATION of same in that field, in that endeavor as has been given may the entity excel.  We are through for the present, though more may be given with the application of that as outlined.  We are through.

Copy to Self
