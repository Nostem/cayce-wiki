# TEXT OF READING 1380-1 M 27 (Head of Manufacturing Dept.,

**Reading [[1380-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/3/1937  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 7088

Hebrew) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of June, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1380] - new Associate Sponsored Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by:  Dr. [[Paul (Apostle)|Saul]] B. Sells, Dr. Gardner Murphy and Dr. Lucien Warner, of the Department of Psychology, Columbia University, [[New York City|New York City]].  The verification of appointment was received this morning, with membership application and written request from Mr. [1380], but not opened until just before period of the Reading.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, to give that as might be the more helpful to the body - understanding what has caused or produced the conditions that have become structural hindrances in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and the manner in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or [[Soul|soul]] might meet same - we would rather have the approach made from the angle of the experiences or [[Incarnation|sojourns]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in in the earth.

3. In meeting them physically in the present, there needs to be kept as near a normal reaction as possible through those forces where the activities are necessitated in keeping as near a normal balance as possible.

4. But for [[Soul|the soul]] or the mental and spiritual developments, these should be approached from that angle as indicated.

5. We would not suggest any alterations in those things that have been given, or the outlines that have been suggested for the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, Mr. [1380].

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. [[Paul (Apostle)|Saul]] B. Sells
"    " Sponsor (Theo B. Dobbins)
"    " Ass'n file
