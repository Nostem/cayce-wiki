# TEXT OF READING 5438-3 M 37

**Reading [[5438-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/10/1930  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 13604

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  The body physically is considerably improved.  The body mentally is considerably tangled.  In the applications for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, the body SHOULD be clarified as to the worriments as concerning physical conditions and physical surroundings. This will be as necessary as to keep the mental and the physical in its proper relationships one to another; and, as has been given, there should be those conditions as more and more bring about the NECESSARY activities for the physical and mental reactions of the body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Has the [[Visions|vision]] improved?
(A)  At times there seems to be a setback; at others, very much improved.  Sees most that it wants to see!

3. (Q)  Would you suggest any further treatment for [[Visions|vision]]?
(A)  Necessary to keep up those of the stimuli from the cervical and upper dorsal to the head.  These may be used by the deep [[Electric Vibrator|VIBRATOR]] occasionally, will be just as helpful - or MORE SO even than the deeper manipulation, adding the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces to same.  It will be like SHAKING the body into its normal activity.

4. (Q)  What treatment for blemishes on face and chest?
(A)  These will clarify themselves properly when the blood stream (and see, the mental worry causes most of this!) - when the blood stream, though, is proper in its reaction in the system, these will clarify themselves.  Very good to use locally (as it must have same) that of sulphur and lard, proportioned about one to ten, see?  Now, that should be the PURE hog lard; NOT of any added vegetable oils!

5. (Q)  What business is the body best fitted for?
(A)  Let's get well first!  Don't worry about that too much! Carrying brick may be very good for the body - but the mental and physical should be kept in accord, as the body improves the physical activities in ANY direction.  Then the body will find that these may be led or carried in the proper way or manner.

6. (Q)  Is body physically able to go home?
(A)  Hardly yet.  At least should be two, three or four weeks.

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
