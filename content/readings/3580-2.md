# TEXT OF READING 3580-2 M 41

**Reading [[3580-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/5/1944  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 11071

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3580], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A.M. Eastern War Time. [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here:  [3580]; this we have had before.

3. Helpful conditions have come to the body and yet it has not wholly or fully carried out those suggestions indicated, and there are those periods when the body feels the accumulations over eyes and the activity to the other sensory organs are active and yet not wholly coordinant, but there is some [[Visions|vision]] now.

4. We would follow closely those suggestions which have been made for the body, not so oft in the treatments osteopathically, but a little bit longer and a little bit more setting up eliminations rather than stimulations.  Let them be more of a relaxing, so that the applications of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] will carry into the system the body-energies which may be helpful to sustain and maintain a better coordination between sympathetic and cerebrospinal and the sensory organisms.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should the Radio-Active Appliance be continued?
(A)  It should be continued, as should also the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].

7. (Q)  Is paralysis starting to affect his hearing?
(A)  This is why the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold should now be used in the body.  These attachments should be the larger plate to the umbilical and lacteal duct center, which on this body would be the width of three fingers directly to the right from the navel center and two fingers up from that point.  The small copper plate would be attached at the 9th dorsal.  This should be used at least twice each week, one of these just before the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are given, one each week, see?  [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium, one grain to one ounce of distilled water, using three ounces at a time.

Do keep plates clean.  Do disconnect when not in use.  Do keep these recharged every thirty days, changing the solution at the time.

8. (Q)  What should be done to the teeth and gums?
(A)  Local attention is needed by the dentist.

9. (Q)  What causes pressure inside, going back to muscle tissues of groin and legs?
(A)  The indications as from that which is necessary for the replenishing of energy in the nerve cells by the carrying of the gold vibratorially into the body.  These are nerve ends lacking in energy activity.

10. (Q)  Are the spinal and liver injections he is getting at times good or harmful?
(A)  They are not good.

11. (Q)  Is there any throid deficiency?
(A)  No.  Do as indicated if we would gain any help.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(7/5/44 GD's note:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., for preparing the Wet Cell Appliance to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)
