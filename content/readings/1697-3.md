# TEXT OF READING 1697-3 F 62

**Reading [[1697-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 7839

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self through husband - Mr. [1757], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, Mrs. [1697].

3. As we find, conditions are not so good as when we had them the last time.  However, with the general corrections osteopathically - that have been indicated - and with the closer adherence to those suggestions made for purifying the system, as related to all activities of the body, there should be kept the nearer to normal [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

4. While the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections are needed, we would be sure to do the other things also; stimulating the eliminations as well as purifying the blood stream, adhering rather closely to those suggestions that have been made as for the the diets and the regular activity of the body.

5. As for the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, - these should be of a general RELAXING nature; with - after such relaxation - stimulating the gastric flow to EMPTY or to eliminate the forces from the stomach and upper bowel.  This means, of course, that the stimulation would be through the lower portion of the cervicals and the upper dorsals, with a stimulation to the 9th dorsal center - especially lower portion.

6. These should bring better conditions for the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Any other specific directions for Dr. Hudgins?
(A)  These are the directions, as we find best suited for keeping better conditions and preventing those segregations or any superficial activity to the outer or superficial circulation.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Hudgins) Copy to Ass'n file
