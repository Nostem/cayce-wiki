# TEXT OF READING 3517-1 F 31

**Reading [[3517-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/13/1943  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 11000

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3517], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. I. D. Wollerman.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are rather serious and rather aggravating disturbances with this body.  These have to do with impingements that have existed in cerebrospinal system, and especially the effects produced upon the general nerve system through the fifth nerve, and the tic that has been a part of the experience.  The removal of nerve has not removed the sources of pressures, and these bring a general aggravation to the body at times whenever there is a certain change in atmospheric pressure.

3. These applications as we find will aid, they will not cure - they will aid in keeping down the disturbance, if applied correctly and consistently.

4. First we would have a series of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments with special reference to the centers in the 6th dorsal, 3rd cervical and the lumbar axis.  These specifically need attention; not making adjustments all at once, but relaxing the body and making the adjustment as soon as the body is sufficiently relaxed that these may be made.

5. Begin using each day when ready to retire the Radio-Active Appliance, that will bring equalization of pressures through the body.  No [[Vibration|vibration]] will be perceptible to the body from the use of this except at times.  For it is using the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the body itself.  Do not allow the Appliance to be used on others while it is being used on self.  Make the attachments for an hour each evening.  Put the Appliance in ice before it is to be used, not letting the water come over the top of the Appliance ever.

6. Do these and we will bring better conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Can this source of disturbance be seen in the fluoroscope, or X-ray, or in other tests?
(A)  If there is the correct interpretation it will be found in the 6th dorsal, the 3rd cervical and the 4th lumbar.

9. (Q)  What causes pain in face when body is lying on right side, or upright with head turned in direct right angle for a few minutes?
(A)  As indicated, the variation in the pressure as the atmosphere is produced by the manner in which the body rests upon the bed or couch.

10. (Q)  What causes the dizzy spells and falling?
(A)  These come from the unbalanced circulation, and will be aided by the corrective measures osteopathically and by the [[Vibration|vibratory]] rate of the body itself being changed.

11. (Q)  What causes the ear to itch and crack open on the outer rim?
(A)  This is caused from too great a quantity of the flow of impulse to this portion of the body, owing to the nerve changes wrought through the manner in which the nerves were tied together in the operative forces.

The massage should help, though at times it will be found irritating, but not when once set in their correct vibrations, through the combination of the equalization in the balancing of the nerve forces in cerebrospinal and sympathetic systems.  Don't treat only the cerebrospinal system.  Do treat the patches of the sympathetic nerves also in the areas indicated.

12. (Q)  Is the periodical scalp condition extreme dandruff or eczema, has it any connection with the facial trouble, and what will relieve the condition?
(A)  All from the circulation, arising from the unbalanced condition in the body.  Use a good cleansing agent for the scalp, with a good tonic afterward.  Olive Oil shampoo would be well for this body.

13. We are through with this body.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

> Mr. Edgar Casey

> Dear Mr. Casey:

> Mr. Casey, for going on four years I have been almost out
> of my mind with pain.  I have some peculiar type of facial
> neuralgia, or an obscure case of tic douloureux, but the
> doctors have never been able to give a true diagnosis, and
> I am almost at wits end.
> 
> To give you an idea of the seriousness of my case, let me
> tell you I have been in the hospital five times.  I have
> had five minor types of surgical treatments, or operations,
> and two VERY major operations.  One, a quite serious brain
> operation, and the other a deep sinus.  Twelve specialists
> have had an active part in my case, and at least twice as
> many more have sat in on the case and done research.  None of
> them has even ever claimed to be sure they were putting their
> fingers on the basic trouble.  It has completely baffled all
> of them, each has tried in some way to help, and has hoped he
> was getting at the root of the trouble, and all were willing
> to admit they just don't know".
> 
> Mr. Casey, I am trying to show you how sincere I am in my
> plea for help.  YOU can tell us what the trouble is.  Don't
> think I am exaggerating my case to win your attention, as
> I truly am not.  The doctors tell me there are cases in the
> asylums for malady of this type while even not as severe.
> In the medical profession this pain is commonly referred to
> as the "suicide pain", and I can well understand why.  Yes,
> there was even a time when I was weak enough to consider it,
> but I am past that danger now.  I want to live to be an old,
> old woman, but I want to be able to live a useful life.  A
> life worth living, not a hell on earth.
> 
> My last operation was in June.  By December the doctors will
> know if it is going to be successful.  From all indications
> so far, it has not been.  If they decide I have not improved
> they will try another operation, only hoping it will give me
> relief.  That is why Ira Wollermann urged me to get this off
> to you so urgently, before another operation was performed.
> 
> Will you, Mr. Casey, help me?  Will you give me a reading
> so that we can know just what we are working on, instead
> of groping around in the dark?  I know you have probably
> received many requests as desperate, but I am sure none
> could be more sincere.
> 
> Also, Mr. Casey, I hope I am not being too selfish if I ask
> you to help me find myself, I know there is a place for me in
> the world.  I know I have a right to have a successful life
> as a member of this great Universe, but I can't seem to find
> myself.  There is something meant for me, I was meant to
> have a more successful, a fuller life, but I have bungled it
> somewhere.  I know my physical life has retarded me, but what
> is this urge driving me to?  I am in my thirties now, and
> if I am going to find myself I should be starting, but
> continually I seem to face solid walls.  I want to be
> a success, and here I am, just a little stenographer at
> twenty-five dollars a week.  Could you advise me?
> 
> Any, any help that you can give me will be greatly
> appreciated you can be sure, Mr. Casey, and I will
> be holding my breath till I hear from you.
> 
> Sincerely, [3517]
