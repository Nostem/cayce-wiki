# TEXT OF READING 480-32 F 24

**Reading [[480-32]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/9/1937  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 3511

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of January, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [480], through Associate Membership of her husband in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions in general are developing very well. While there are the adjustments of the body - circulatory forces to the changes that naturally arise in the body in the present, the eliminations as carried on by the lymph circulation allow the throwing off of forces that make an acute condition in the form of the rash that has been indicated.

3. This does not arise so much from diet as from the circulation's not receiving the impulses as nominally arise under other conditions in the body.

4. Hence it is a skin irritation, or a condition arising from the superficial circulation.

5. As we would find in the present, we would take each morning for seven to eight days, before the morning meal, a level teaspoonful of Eno Salts in water.  Drink while effervescing. Take same for seven or eight days, then rest for seven or eight days, then take again - if there is still the disturbance.

6. Also we would find that the bathing or sponging off of the portions with a ten percent solution of any antiseptic would be very well.  But as we find if the Eno Salts is taken, as indicated, the disturbance should be eliminated.

7. The rubbing with [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]] is also very well.  This only when there is great irritation.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Should the [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]] be used instead of the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Myrrh?
(A)  Rather than the Oil and Myrrh in the present.  But take internally the Eno Salts, level teaspoonful mornings before any meal - each morning.

10. (Q)  Any further advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Take those precautions as has been indicated regarding cold, congestion and the general activities.  These we find for the present.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    "  Ass'n file
