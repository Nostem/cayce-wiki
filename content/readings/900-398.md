# TEXT OF READING 900-398 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-398]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/7/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5601

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  Now you will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [900], of ..., Deal, N.J., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you, especially the list that was given him, he feels, by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], Elbert H. Gary.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had here before.  Also the information and those conditions surrounding the list as referred to respecting the various industrials which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has been, and is, interested in the stocks of same.  These, as seen, show those inclinations and indications that there will be the continuation of those conditions for some time.  These, as seen - and as has been given, and as has been viewed by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], by the various conditions that have come to the entity respecting same - should be followed in the way and manner as has been given.

3. Because there arises, [900], in the daily affairs, those differences of opinions that bring about misunderstandings with associates and different individuals on the Board and in the Exchange, let this not interfer with that as may be obtained through the closer association with that individually obtained, or that individually ordered that may be GIVEN from day to day, and when given to act, SO ACT, but when differences allow the concentration to be wavered, there is trouble in becoming specific or definite in the actions. Act in that manner.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  Morning of July 6, 1928.  I was hanging on to a roof. It seemed I was going to have a lot of trouble getting down, but I didn't.
(A)  This, as indicated, that the various conditions as arise in the daily activities of the body, find there are at times precarious conditions apparent, yet when the activities of the body are in keeping with those lessons as have been gained from time to time, not allowed to be wavered by the various circumstances that arise from individuals speaking, rather than from that from within - then we find, as indicated, the trouble seems to be, or does come - as seen in the [[Visions|vision]] - just worry.

5. (Q)  Does this refer to stocks?
(A)  Rather to those conditions in the daily affairs of the body.

6. (Q)  Is the list given me the proper list, and will price hold thru for several months?
(A)  As has been indicated, use and apply same in the way as has been given.  These are correct, and the correct manner and way to handle same.

7. (Q)  Will I see many ups and downs or low prices?
(A)  As indicated, these are to be given from day to day, will entity only allow self to be attuned in the way and manner as may bring these in a consistent way and manner. Remember, consistency is a jewel - but must be followed, and a portion of self's own experience in existence, - to gain the better understanding of that being sought through the various channels of approach as the entity is entertaining and gaining.

8. (Q)  Hamberg.  Moss & Ferguson, and the N.Y. Stock Exchange. What advice can entity give in relation to these?  What will I be called upon to do?
(A)  As has been seen.  This well to be followed, and keep self in that attunement that those forces as may be brought to the consciousness of entity may have the channel through which they may come.  We are through.
