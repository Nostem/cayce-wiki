# TEXT OF READING 3279-3 M 33

**Reading [[3279-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/25/1944  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 10695

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [3279], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body:  [3279], this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are some improvements since we last had same.  The taking of those properties indicated for periods of expectoration, and the tendency for the accumulations in lungs - these are well - but don't take too much, but these are the better to aid in alleviating expectorations.  Wet heat - that is, as of heavy toweling across portions of the lungs, will also aid.

4. Do keep inhalants, as has been indicated, and keep up the building for better assimilation.

5. Do give of the foods such as [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], liver, liver extract all that the body can handle, but don't overtax the body.

6. Do keep eliminations good.  This should be aided by cleansing the colon occasionally.  Do, when there is the lessening of pain in the lung, begin the massage - more massage than adjustments - and we will gain better strength for the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Are inhalants being taken correctly?
(A)  These are very good, but keep them so that there will be strengthening to the body; that is, supply more of the brandy and do have or keep the keg where a little more of the evaporation takes place but the gases are preserved for inhalations.  Keep keg in warm place, see?

9. (Q)  Do the sedatives taken inhibit progress?
(A)  Not to any great extent.  These are necessary to keep down a profuse bleeding, see?

10. (Q)  How long should bedrest be continued?
(A)  When the body feels equal to it, just a little exercise in the open will be well.  Resting in the outdoors, or walking.  These will gradually be added, for the body within six months will be able to overcome most of this.

11. (Q)  When should chiropractic adjustments be again started?
(A)  As has been indicated.  These may be begun all right now, if it is more massage than adjustments, see?

12. (Q)  What treatment should be given for sore throats, sinus and bronchial trouble experienced in the past, in fall and winter, if they reoccur?
(A)  Those that are being applied are correct.  Add more to these, and we will destroy that.  That is being accomplished.

13. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Keep those things suggested.  Be patient, be persistent, but be consistent.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
