# TEXT OF READING 281-45

**Reading [[281-45]]** · Series: 281 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/25/1940  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1828

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of February, 1940, in accordance with request made by the [[Prayer|Prayer]] Group of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Florence Edmonds, Esther Wynne, Frances Y. Morrow, Hannah Miller, Marsden Godfrey and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:35 P. M.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers Prayer Group]], members of which are present here, and their study of [[Healing|spiritual healing]].  You will give counsel and guidance for this group at this time, and answer the questions which it presents.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the group as gathered here, as a group and as individuals, and their study and application of [[Healing|spiritual healing]].

3. In the undertakings, and in the application of that which has been the experience of this group, we find that these are not at all times as consistent and as persistent with their [[Purpose|purposes]] as their name, their knowledge, their understanding, would indicate.

4. A great deal has been and may be accomplished in the work, in the study of [[Healing|spiritual healing]]; and these should be kept as a part of the experience of each individual.  Because, there are periods when - with the associations of ideas, as well as [[Ideal|ideals]] - it is necessary for the application of mechanical and medicinal properties in the experience of each.  And, there are experiences when apparently there is the failure on the part of either to bring the material help to many, according to the IDEA of an individual.  Such experiences have tended to create a laxness on the part of many, and a questioning within self as to the need, or as to the use of it.

5. Yet there has been forgotten much that is the basic principle, the basic truth that has been indicated through these channels, and as seen in the teachings of Him who is the way, the truth and the light.

6. In the study of His application of healing, in His [[Ministry of Jesus|ministry]], - were all healed only by the spoken word?  Were all healed by the laying on of hands?  Were all healed by any one way or manner?

7. Rather, as ye analyze His activities, and that which is thy own experience, ye may see the manifestation of the truth that the Lord is One, exemplified in the healing as well as in the daily relationships of man to man in all phases of His experience.

8. Thus this group, as ye analyze and study spiritual healing, - be first consistent in thy activities and in thy thoughts, and in thy [[Purpose|purposes]], in thy hopes and in thy desires day by day.  And in the application of those truths, or those examples as were given by Him, as well as in thy relationships to thy fellows, - be persistent, as well as consistent.

9. Thus there may come indeed into the experience of all the more and more awareness of His consciousness, of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]], as a living thing in thy experience.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Give the affirmations which may be sent to those on the [[Prayer|prayer]] list?
(A)  FATHER, GOD!  IN THY MERCY, IN TRY LOVE, CAST OUT FEAR, HATE AND MALICE FROM MY OWN THOUGHTS, MY OWN [[Purpose|PURPOSES]]:  THAT THY PEACE, THY LOVE, MAY WHOLY DIRECT MY LIFE.

2nd:  FATHER, GOD!  CREATE WITHIN ME A NEW PURPOSE, TO DO THY WILL IN EVERY WAY AND MANNER:  THAT FEAR MAY BE ERADICATED FROM MY LIFE, AND THAT THE PEACE AS PROMISED IN HIM MAY FILL MY CONSCIOUSNESS.

3rd:  OUR LORD, OUR GOD!  IN THE PROMISES OF [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]], [[Jesus Christ|JESUS]], WE SEEK THY COUNTENANCE - NOW:  THAT PEACE AND LOVE MAY FILL OUR OWN LIVES, THAT WE MAY SHED THAT LIGHT INTO THE LIVES OF OTHERS.

4th:  OUR LORD, OUR GOD!  LET PEACE AND HARMONY REIGN WITHIN MY BODY, MY MIND, MY [[Soul|SOUL]] - FOR [[Jesus Christ|JESUS]]' SAKE.

5th:  MERCIFUL FATHER!  REMEMBER ME IN MY WEAKNESS, AND MAKE MY PURPOSES ONE WITH THEE:  THAT ALL FEAR AND DOUBT MAY BE BLOTTED OUT OF MY CONSCIOUSNESS, AND ONLY THE LOVE AND PEACE OF LOVE DIVINE REIGN WITHIN MY LIFE.

6th:  FATHER, GOD!  HEAR ME, IN MY HUMBLE [[Prayer|PRAYER]].  TAKE AWAY FEAR AND DOUBT:  THAT MY BODY MAY BE WHOLE:  THAT MY MIND MAY BE CLEAR:  THAT MY [[Soul|SOUL]] MAY BE AT PEACE WITH THEE.  THIS I ASK IN JESUS' NAME.

7th:  MY LORD, MY GOD!  BE MERCIFUL TO ME!  KEEP FEAR, AND DOUBT, AND HATE, OUT OF MY LIFE.  LET ME FILL MY BODY, MY MIND, MY [[Soul|SOUL]], WITH THE [[Christ Consciousness|CONSCIOUSNESS OF THE CHRIST]]-LOVE, THE CHRIST-PEACE, THE CHRIST-HARMONY.

8th:  OUR LORD, OUR GOD!  HEAR, WHILE WE PRAY TOGETHER:  THAT HATE, AND FEAR, AND DOUBT, MAY BE TAKEN OUT OF OUR LIVES: THAT LOVE AND HARMONY AND PEACE MAY REIGN WITHIN OUR BODIES, OUR MINDS, OUR SOULS - FOR JESUS' SAKE!

Then, to all we would give this:

Let not your hearts be troubled; ye believe in God and in His watchful care.  Then, putting away doubt and fear, let that mind be in thee which was in Jesus, the Christ, as He prayed,

"Father, forgive them, they know not what they do."

12. (Q)  In March 15, 1939 Reading [281-40, Par. 25-A], it was advised that no publication should be presented by this group at that time.  Would it be well to prepare some type of pamphlet before the next Congress in June, and if so what should be the nature of this pamphlet.  If no pamphlet is advised, is there any other type of publication that should be prepared by the group?
(A)  This is very well, if there is more consistency, more persistency on the part of each member of the group, that they as individuals may testify as to what HAS been accomplished in their own experience.

13. We are through for the present.
