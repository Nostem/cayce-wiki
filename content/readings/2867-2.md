# TEXT OF READING 2867-2 F 36

**Reading [[2867-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/23/1943  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 10091

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2867], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2867], Mrs. [1541], and Mrs. [1523].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2867], this we have had before.

3. As find, there are improvements; though at times apparently there are those reactions that cause some distress.

4. If there will be the keeping closer to those suggestions indicated, as to the applications and as to the diet, we will find conditions will improve much better.

5. It is necessary to keep better eliminations.  If this requires the use of those activities that will make for better coordination between the liver and the kidneys, the ALCAROID with the Caroid and Bile Salts will be very good in this respect.  For, the Alcaroid is an active force upon the gastric forces in the stomach itself, and also aids in the eliminations from kidneys.  The Bile Salts tablets with this will cause a better flow of the gastric juices in the gall duct area, and a better reaction in the emunctory circulation through the alimentary canal.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Please give specific directions for Dr. Irvin to make treatments more effective.
(A)  These should be more from the right side of those centers where there have been the indications for the corrections necessary, and we will find much better response. And there should be more treating through the lumbar and the lower portion of the dorsal BEFORE the corrections are made in the upper dorsal and cervical area.  This is reverse, to be sure, to that ordinarily indicated, but it will make for bettered conditions.

8. (Q)  About how many more [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments should be taken?
(A)  Eight to ten should be sufficient, and these - if done properly - may be made a little farther apart.

9. (Q)  What causes extreme dizziness?
(A)  The lack of the flow of the gall duct area.  This regurgitating causes the dizziness to the body.

10. (Q)  What causes soreness in left breast?
(A)  This is from the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  And the gentle massage, not hard, with Cocoa Butter, should cause this to disappear; for it will allow a better circulation.  This means more under the arm, not on the breast itself at all, but the area to the left and down the side, see?

11. (Q)  Has menopause started?
(A)  It is the beginning of same; not what might be called started as yet.

12. (Q)  How may I best normalize my weight without injury to my health?
(A)  In the diet.

13. (Q)  Any other advice at this time?
(A)  Do these things, consistently, persistently, and do be mindful of the diet.  And, of course, keep the mental attitude constructive.  For, the attitude not only towards conditions and things but also toward people, will have much to do with the reactions in the body; and will become more so as there are those changes in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Irvin) Copy to Ass'n file
