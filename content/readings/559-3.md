# TEXT OF READING 559-3 F 18 MONTHS

**Reading [[559-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/23/1929  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 3953

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by her mother and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [267], Mr. [5593], Mrs. [442], Dr. Berger, Mrs. Hopp, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body - this we have had before, [559]. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What is the cause of the bloody mucous stools?
(A)  The eliminating of those conditions as have been and are causing the distresses through the alimentary canal.

3. (Q)  Have the worms passed?
(A)  Passing.

4. (Q)  What diet to correct this condition?
(A)  Be very well for any eliminating diet of those juices as are cleansing for the system; that is, those the body may be able to assimilate, and with the changing or altering of those food values in the milks - as has been outlined.  It will be found necessary to change these, under the varying circumstances.  Trouble - yes, but its WORTH it - for the welfare of the individual.

5. (Q)  Is the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] treatment producing any harmful effects?
(A)  When there is curd, reduce the quantity, as has been given.  Not harmful, or it wouldn't have been given - for corrections come, with the change of the effect produced in system, through the activity of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] created by either those of the medication, or of the manipulation, or the lights.  See?

6. (Q)  Shall the Dryco or Klim milk formula be given and for how long?
(A)  CHANGE as becomes necessary with the effect as is produced.  Dryco may be well for the time.  Then we may find that it would be necessary to change to those of other formulas.

7. (Q)  Any other changes in the treatment, or further suggestions?
(A)  We would give rather the heat light, or the Infra Red for the present, rather than that of the ultra violet - for the Infra Red will be rather more [[Healing|healing]] to the tissues as are under strain in the present conditions, and these would be given after the heat has attained its proper radiation, for only twenty minutes.

8. (Q)  What strength of the Dryco?
(A)  That as is in the formula for the disturbance in the ALIMENTARY canal, for those conditions in the colon should be cleansed with those enemas of the oil - as has been outlined.

9. That is all the questions regarding this body.

10. The body is on the improve.  Because these conditions arise, just be mindful that these DO NOT act in a manner that there is the regurgitation and this inflammation come up, rather than being eliminated.  Hence the ELIMINATING elements are necessary through the alimentary canal.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Berger (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Parker
