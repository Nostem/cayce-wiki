# TEXT OF READING 2293-4 F 31

**Reading [[2293-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/31/1942  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 9037

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2293], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. While in the main conditions we find are very good, there are still those tendencies the correction of which would enable better relaxation, and the responses to the physical and emotional reactions would be much improved.

4. Hence we find there should be the equalization of the pressures along the cerebrospinal system, where there is the closer coordination between the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic nervous systems.  This should be done osteopathically, and with particular reference given to the cervical areas, and to the 3rd and 4th dorsal, 9th dorsal, and the lumbar axis.

5. Some six or eight such treatments should be administered.

6. These as we find, with keeping the mental attitudes in those directions which have been indicated, would be the most beneficial for the body.

7. Do these, if we would keep the better forces in the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Why do I have so much dandruff?
(A)  The poor circulation to the scalp, - hence the necessity for the coordinations in the upper cervical area, as indicated.  This tends to make for accumulations.

Use the special Drene Shampoo with hair conditioner added, as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and cleanser for the hair, at least once a week for some five or six weeks; and we will find, with the treatments, this will disappear; the hair will be more lustrous and have less tendency for the graying, with more tendency for keeping near to the natural color.

Do this regularly.

10. We are through for the present - with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Dr. Shackleford, [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].)
