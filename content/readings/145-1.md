# TEXT OF READING 145-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[145-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/28/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 592

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of May, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [145].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.                    [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find many conditions in this body are very good. There are those abnormal conditions as are produced by the poor eliminations in the system, the tendency towards too much eliminations being eliminated through the capillary circulation.  Hence the condition as exists throughout the mesenteric system, the liver and the hepatic circulation. The conditions as cause the greater distress, then, being that as is seen in the change from the intestinal digestive system and the eliminations as are caused or produced during and after this digestion has taken place.  Hence the tendency towards constipation and the inactivity of the liver.  The strains as are produced in the body being from those of the existent condition in the nerve system, and the deterred effect of the incentives as supplied along these deflected nerves cause the condition as is seen in the upper portion of body.  Then the irritation as is produced by the trouble in the digestive and eliminating system, added to those conditions where the subligations show, this produces the distress to the upper portion of body.

2. Then, to bring about the normal conditions for this body, first we would take into the system those properties that would set up the normal conditions for eliminations, as would be found in using high enemas first, for two to three days.

3. Then take this a laxative.  This as one dose.  At least three should be taken.

Prodophyllum................1/2 grain,
Sanguinaria...................1 grain,
Cascara Sagrada.............1/4 grain,
Dover's Powders.............1/4 grain.

4. This should be put in capsule, and each dose taken one day apart.  As this has cleansed the system, or when this has cleansed the system, drink plenty of water - that is, keep the system FULL for at least two to three days.

5. Then begin the adjustments, through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation of those conditions as exist in the upper dorsal and the cervical, and the lower portion of the lumbars.  This should at least be adjusted, or treated and adjusted - eight to twelve such adjustments taken, every other day until at least six or eight, and then the rest once each week.  At the end of this the body should be very near the normal. Do that.

6. We are through for the present.
