# TEXT OF READING 1210-5 M 57

**Reading [[1210-5]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/21/1939  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 6742

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through Miss [954], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Lenox Hill Hospital, 
Time of Reading                          76th St. & Park Ave. 
3:30 to 3:55 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find with this body, [1210], the acute condition - arising from inflammatory forces, arising from the regurgitation in the caecum area, through the telescoping of portions of the lower part of the jejunum - would respond better to the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] as combined with hot and cold applications over stomach and abdomen.

4. Hot and cold alternately for several hours - two to three; then a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] to dilate the colon and to remove the disturbance as well as distress caused.

5. Then rest.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What nourishments, if any, should be given now?
(A)  These should necessarily, of course, be almost predigested foods for the time being, or until all of those tendencies for bloating AND for the inflammatory forces or temperature, are gone.

8. (Q)  For how long a period should he rest, after the acute condition is relieved?
(A)  Until there is sufficient strength for activity.  Day, - two days.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self through Miss [955] - Wired also Mailed special

> By now Mabel has seen you.  I had a strange experience
> expecting to send the enclosed letter to you.  I expected

> I was lead to the big old [[1210]'s mother]'s family Bible

> Dear Miss [954]-
> 
> Your friend Miss Brown was in to see me Saturday morning

> experience that you will, am sure, be interested in.

> the experience Friday after noon when went to take the
> reading; whether before or after do not know.  But either

> number of Balls - or balloons rise to where ever I was.

> know all is well - God's Love, God's Mercy is sufficient for

> entered into that rest - by service already begun.  Don't let

> 
> Already your wish has been done - and ALL IS WELL.  Am sure
> there will be times when may be able to speak.  Possibly
> understand now why have been slipping in and out so easily

> Mercy and resting on His promises to each of us will do my

> thyself of what might have been.  God is LOVE - and is able
> in same to keep all who seek - and he seeks and has entered
> in.  Miss Brown gave me a full message - tho much of same I
> knew - tho could not did not of course mention to her my

> S.S. Class yesterday - may see her again to day.
> 
> Your experience with the Book is interesting.  Yes some words

> 
> Hold fast - to HIS PROMISES - in them are strength - in them

> other - for as you do it unto the least - ye do it unto ME.

> And that is you and I.  May His love keep you.  Work work

> Our Souls we Know he is safe with Jesus.

> My love to you and from each one here.
>
