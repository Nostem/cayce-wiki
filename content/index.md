- **[Browse readings](/readings)** — look up a reading number or explore the collection in order.

- **[Read a series](/series)** — follow an existing collection of related readings.

Use **Search** to find a reading number such as `1527-2`, or a subject such as `Atlantis`. New here? Start with the [reading and citation guide](/help).

## Read with the source in view

Readings retain their identifying numbers and historical wording. Reports and Background are separate from the main transcript. Modern descriptions, topic links and generated indexes are research aids, not part of the original reading.

Before quoting or relying on an association, check the surrounding source text. [About annotations and responsible citation](/help#source-text-and-annotations).

## About this collection

This site was extracted from the A.R.E. _Complete Edgar Cayce Readings_ CD-ROM. The archive contains historical material, including medical and metaphysical claims; presenting that material does not establish those claims as fact or as current medical guidance.

Use the original reading identifier and this page's stable URL when citing the site. For formal work, verify the text against the source edition. [How to cite a reading](/help#citing-a-reading).
